### Changed
- `scripts/skill_index.py` moves the script table of a skill with more than 12 scripts out of its `INDEX.md` into a lazy `INDEX-scripts.md` next to it (`agentic-delivery`, 78 scripts, and `deep-code-review`, 24, split; smaller skills are unchanged). `INDEX.md` keeps the references table and a pointer ("Read `INDEX-scripts.md` when you need a script"). The saving applies to reference lookups only: `agentic-delivery/INDEX.md` falls from 16,962 to 4,533 bytes (about 4.2K to 1.1K tokens by the repo's chars/4 estimate). A script lookup reads `INDEX.md` plus `INDEX-scripts.md`, 4,533 + 12,834 = 17,367 bytes, 405 bytes (2.4%) more than the old single file, so such a lookup is slightly worse. The one-line purpose and arguments of every script are also in the entry index (`.claude/skills/INDEX.md`, trimmed from 12,352 to 12,344 bytes to stay in its budget). Nothing in `SKILL.md` routing changed. These are modelled byte counts, not transcript measurements. `skill_index.py --check` now also flags an orphan `INDEX-scripts.md` left behind when a skill drops under the cap; `land-release.sh`, `test-conflict-free-prs.sh` and the CI comment treat `INDEX-scripts.md` as a derived file like `INDEX.md`, and `--regen` keeps size rows for the two split skills' index files. Test: `scripts/test_skill_index_split.py` (registered in `scripts/ci-tests.d/skill-index-split.sh`).
