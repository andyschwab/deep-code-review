### Added
- Bench: `scripts/overclaim_bench.py`, a free, preregistered over-claim safety bench, with 40 new held-out fictional end-of-task transcripts (77 items, gold labels) in `docs/bench/overclaim-r2-1021/`. The model labels each item `unverified`, `partial`, `done` or `verified`; a label stronger than the evidence allows is an over-claim, scored by regex and a gold table (no judge). Driver, cases, Perun arm text and `PREREGISTERED.md` were hashed before any call; rival texts (obra/superpowers `verification-before-completion`, one addyosmani/agent-skills page) are pinned to commits and file hashes.
