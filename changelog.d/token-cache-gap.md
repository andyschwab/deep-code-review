### Added
- `token_report.py` now measures idle-gap cache rewrites and spend by model tier. Main-thread 5m cache-write tokens that follow a gap over 5 minutes are split into 5m-1h and over-1h gaps, with an upper-bound input-eq saving if a 1h cache pin held the cache (the report's formula: gap writes x (1.25 - 0.1) minus 0.75 x every other 5m write) as a share of main input-eq. The report also gives input-eq share by model for main and lanes (`UNPRICED` for a model with no rate, never zero). Text and `--json` (`idle_rewrites`, `tier_split`) carry them; all figures are upper bounds, not a bill. Two `Lever:` lines print only when material: pin the main cache to 1h when at least 30% of 5m writes follow an idle gap and the bound is at least 5% of main input-eq and less than half of cache writes are already 1h (names the `FORCE_PROMPT_CACHING_5M=1` override and the v2.1.242+ pin requirement; `host-enforcement.md` says which accounts get 1h); a negative bound prints "a 1h pin would not save". Sidechain lines are no longer counted as main turns, and orchestrator tier when a top-tier main carries over 40% of all input-eq. `/perun:cost-retro` leads with them. Tests: `scripts/test_token_cache_gap.py`.
