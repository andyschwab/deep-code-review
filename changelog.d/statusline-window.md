### Added
- Agentic-delivery: `cost_statusline.py` now also prints the active 5-hour window, e.g. `win 2h10m left | 62% | +14k/min` (time left, the host's `rate_limits.five_hour.used_percentage`, and the window's weighted-token burn per minute). It reuses `token_report.burn_blocks` through a sibling import of `agentic-ceo/scripts/token_report.py` (imported without writing `__pycache__`) and reads `transcript_path` from the statusLine JSON (key checked against the host statusline docs). From the last 1 MiB of the transcript it parses, as JSON, every whole line that mentions `"usage"` and keeps only `requestId`, `apiBlockIndex`, `timestamp`, `isSidechain`, `type`, `message.model` and `message.usage` of main-thread turns; no message content is kept, printed or sent anywhere. Time left uses the host's `rate_limits.five_hour.resets_at` when it is finite and at most one window ahead, else the modelled window. A modelled window or burn rate read from a clipped tail is marked `~`, unless the tail holds a gap of a whole window between two turns (that anchors the block chain; merely reaching back 5 hours does not). When the host sends no `prompt_cache.hit_ratio`, the cache share is computed from the same tail and shown as `tail cache N%`, distinct from the host's session-wide `cache N%`; it survives a failure of the window part. Without `token_report.py`, without a readable transcript, or on any error, the output stays the plain fields and nothing is printed to stderr. Tests: `scripts/test_cost_statusline_window.py` (registered in `scripts/ci-tests.d/cost-statusline-window.sh`), including a CPU bound (under 500 ms, import included) on a 50 MB transcript of about 330-byte lines.
