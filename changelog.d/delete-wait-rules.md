### Changed
- Agents no longer wait on local copies of CI. `pre-push-verify.sh` drops its CI re-runs (fix-class and mechanism, size-ratchet, bench_check, mustload, run-evals selftest, INDEX/SHA256SUMS, changelog fragments) and keeps conflict markers, the skip-ci token check, the privacy scan of changed files (added by the privacy-parity change), your configured `DCR_PREPUSH_CMD` tier and the warn-only lints; CI on the exact pushed sha is the single blocking gate for the rest. Deleted `scripts/test-prepush-fix-class.sh`; `scripts/test-prepush-ci-parity.sh` now pins only the kept privacy step.
- `agentic-delivery` G5/G7: local verification is the tests the change affects; CI on the exact SHA is the receipt. Removed the rule that a coordination lesson closes only when a script lands.
- `train_land.sh` gives a union proof only when the PRs overlap on paths or generated files (the `GENERATED` list in `scripts/land-release.sh`; without it the union runs); disjoint green PRs drain back-to-back through `land_train.sh` (`LAND_PRS`). `TRAIN_UNION=always` forces the union. Tests: `scripts/test-train-disjoint.sh`.
- Review tiers: skill `SKILL.md`, `INDEX.md`, `references/*` and `evals/*` are tier 2 (a `2!` row on `references/*.md`, `evals/*.json` and `SKILL.md` pins the tier over name-based globs such as `*/merge*`; other files there, e.g. `install.sh`, keep their strictest row); a `1!` row keeps nested skill `scripts/*`, `templates/*`, `CLAUDE.md` and `AGENTS.md` at tier 1, and agents and commands stay tier 1.
- `review_gate.py` accepts an older reviewed head for a newer push when the diff since the reviewed head touches no tier-1 path (renames count both paths) and leaves every changed `SKILL.md` frontmatter block (tool grants) identical; a tier-1 path or a commit missing locally needs a fresh review. Tests: `scripts/test_review_gate_rereview.py`.
