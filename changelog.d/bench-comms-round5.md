### Added
- `docs/bench/comms-r5-1017/`: a pre-registered, free-model (PCSS, no paid call) fifth `communication-structure` bench, bench only. It tested the owner decision to keep the ~150-word digest default and add a decision-brief mode (recommendation plus rationale) for messages that ask the reader to decide. Three wordings of the mode were tuned on 10 scenarios; the tune-selected one (a labelled brief skeleton) ran once against the unedited skill and humanizer on 60 fresh held-out scenarios (judge v2 via `scripts/rival_eval.py`, GLM-5.1 primary). Keep rule: paired (edit minus unedited) 95% bootstrap CI lower bound above 0. Result: -0.025, CI -0.117 to +0.067, so no SKILL.md or eval edit ships; edit versus humanizer 0.358, CI 0.258 to 0.467, not superior.
