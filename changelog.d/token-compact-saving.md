### Added
- `token_report.py` now estimates what auto-compact would save on the main thread: `auto_compact` in `--json` and a `Lever: auto-compact ...` line replay each main turn's context (input plus cache writes plus cache read), compact at 200000 (the `autoCompactWindow` `install.sh` writes) to an assumed 30000 tokens, then grow by the observed per-turn deltas; saving is 0.1 times the context removed, minus 1.25 times the restart size per simulated compaction. An upper bound, not a bill: quality lost to compaction and the summarizer call are not modelled, and a session that already compacts or never passes the window is skipped. The saving ranks in `Top levers` as `auto-compact at 200000`. `/perun:cost-retro` merges its two "lead with" instructions into one (lead with the top-ranked lever; its `Lever:` line gives the how). Tests: `scripts/test_token_compact_saving.py`.
