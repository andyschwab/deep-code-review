### Added
- `token_report.py` now prints, and `--json` carries as `lane_tail`, the share of lane input-equivalent spent beyond two tool-call caps and the p90 lane tool calls: `tail_share_over_cap` against the reference cap (default 150; `build_report(cap=)` accepts an override, but `--budget` is gate-only so CLI output uses 150) and `tail_share_over_shipped_cap` against the `LANE_TOOL_CAP` the operating-layer template ships (250, read from the template at runtime; omitted if unreadable). When the tail beyond the shipped cap is 10% or more of lane input-eq it adds a lever line: lower `LANE_TOOL_CAP` toward the p90, enforced by `lane_cap.py`. Both figures are upper bounds: the redo cost of the tail in a narrower lane is ignored.
