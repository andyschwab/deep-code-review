### Added
- `token_report.py --outcome DIR` measures whether spend nudges moved anything, from main-thread usage, timestamp and model fields only (never message text). Per UTC week it prints turns, input-equivalent tokens per turn, cache-hit %, and the share of sessions that passed a lever trigger (main context reached the 200,000-token auto-compact window; a turn on a top-tier model) and later applied it (an observed context drop of over 50%; a later turn on a lower tier). `--since DATE` adds before/after rows, `--json` emits the same data. A correlation over a changing work mix, not proof of cause. Tests: `scripts/test_token_outcome.py` (registered in `scripts/ci-tests.d/token-outcome.sh`).
