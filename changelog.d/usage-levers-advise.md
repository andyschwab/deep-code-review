### Added
- Agentic-CEO: `token_report.py --advise` (and so `/perun:cost-retro`) gains three usage-optimization rules, each fired by a deterministic trigger over usage, timestamp and model fields (never message text; the effort rule also reads settings keys, only to stay silent when one is already set): `idle-resume` (2 or more main turns that re-wrote at least half of a 100,000-token-or-larger context after a gap over 1 hour: `/compact` before stepping away, or `/clear` between unrelated tasks), `cache-bust` (2 or more same-model turns within 150 seconds where cache read was under 5% of the previous context, whatever the growth (a collapse with a net shrink stays silent because it looks like compaction; a clear of the oldest tool results can also fire): a changed cache prefix, so do model, effort, fast-mode, MCP and plugin changes at a task boundary), and `effort` (main output averaging 1,500 or more tokens per turn and 30% or more of main input-eq plus output with no `effortLevel`, `CLAUDE_CODE_EFFORT_LEVEL` or `modelSettings` set: `/effort low` or `/effort medium`; heuristic thresholds, not calibrated on a corpus). Every command and setting is confirmed on code.claude.com/docs pages fetched 2026-10-21 and logged in `docs/standards-index.md`; skill-description budget and `idleCompaction` were dropped because no transcript field measures the first and the docs fetch did not confirm the second. Tests: `scripts/test_cost_advice_levers.py` (registered in `scripts/ci-tests.d/cost-advice-levers.sh`).
