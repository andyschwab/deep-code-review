**Recommended Segment: The Cash-Strapped Indie Team (Budget Efficiency Angle)**

**Rationale:**
For a producer with only $50,000 total budget, survival is the primary job-to-be-done, not optimization for external grants. Hiring a traditional casting director at $2,000 per project consumes 4% of their entire film budget—a prohibitive line item. Your $200/month subscription represents less than 0.5% of that same budget. The immediate, tangible relief of saving $1,800 per project directly impacts their ability to fund other critical areas like equipment, locations, or post-production.

While diversity quotas are important, they are often a secondary concern compared to the existential threat of running out of money before filming begins. A producer worried about "magic" and intuition is likely a luxury concern for someone in this financial bracket; their primary fear is insolvency. The argument that you reduce casting time from six weeks to ten days further compounds the financial benefit, allowing them to start shooting sooner and burn less cash on pre-production overhead. The diversity metric (30% increase) serves as a powerful *supporting* proof point for quality, but the *primary* hook must be the drastic reduction in upfront cost and time-to-market.

**Positioning Hypothesis (to validate with your 80 current users):**
*For indie film producers operating on micro-budgets (<$100k) who need to cast quickly without breaking the bank, CastAI is an AI-powered casting assistant that reduces casting costs by 90% and timelines by 60%; unlike traditional casting directors or expensive directories, it delivers diverse, high-quality talent matches for a flat monthly fee, allowing producers to allocate more funds to actual production.*
