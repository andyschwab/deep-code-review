### Recommended Positioning: Culture Building & Belonging

**Segment Choice:** Frame EmpathyVR as a **strategic culture-building tool** focused on long-term belonging and retention, rather than litigation risk mitigation.

**Rationale:** While legal exposure is a baseline requirement for HR, positioning primarily as "risk mitigation" commoditizes your solution into an insurance policy, inviting price competition with cheap compliance workshops. Your pilot data (25% inclusivity lift, 40% drop in complaints) proves a positive behavioral shift, which aligns with the modern CHRO's strategic mandate to solve retention crises and foster innovation through belonging. Risk framing looks backward; culture framing looks forward. Use the drop in complaints as *evidence* of cultural success, not as the primary promise.
