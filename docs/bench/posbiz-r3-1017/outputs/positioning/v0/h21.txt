**Strategic Recommendation on the "Medical vs. Artistic" Dilemma:**
Do not choose between "precision" and "safety." Position **safety as the byproduct of precision**. Elite directors do not buy "safety devices"; they buy "performance tools." Framing it as a "precision tool" makes it a competitive advantage. The liability reduction is the *reason to buy*, but the *story to tell* is about achieving the impossible standard of perfection.
