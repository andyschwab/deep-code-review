**Recommended Approach: Lead with Workflow Integration**

While 99.5% accuracy is a critical table stake, leading with it creates unnecessary friction by implicitly attacking the competence of current stenographers and their trusted tools. In a profession where pride in precision is paramount, suggesting "our competitors are inaccurate" triggers defensiveness rather than interest. Furthermore, accuracy claims are often viewed with skepticism until proven in court.

The stronger reason to switch is **Workflow Seamless Integration**. The primary barrier to adoption for stenographers is not just error rates, but the fear of disrupting their physical rhythm and the high cost of retraining on new hardware postures. By positioning ScribeVoice as the tool that requires *zero* change to how they sit, hold their machine, or interact with their workflow, you remove the perceived risk of adoption. Accuracy becomes the *result* of using a tool that fits perfectly, rather than a controversial claim used to sell a difficult transition.

**Message Hierarchy for Launch Campaign**

**1. Core Message (The Headline)**
"ScribeVoice: The only voice-activated note taker that integrates into your existing workflow in under 5 minutes—no changes to your posture, your machine, or your routine required."

**2. Supporting Pillar A: Zero Friction Adoption**
*   **Claim:** Eliminate the learning curve entirely.
*   **Proof Point:** 120 firms are currently using the beta with full integration achieved in under 5 minutes.
*   **Benefit:** Stenographers can start capturing higher volume immediately without the weeks of retraining typically required for new hardware.

**3. Supporting Pillar B: Precision You Can Trust**
*   **Claim:** Achieve near-perfect transcripts that drastically reduce post-session edit time.
*   **Proof Point:** Beta data demonstrates 99.5% word accuracy in live environments.
*   **Benefit:** Focus on the flow of the proceeding rather than correcting errors later, without having to sacrifice your trusted physical setup to get there.

**4. Supporting Pillar C: The Safe Switch**
*   **Claim:** Upgrade your output without upgrading your habits.
*   **Proof Point:** Direct compatibility with workflows used by current beta firms who previously relied on traditional stenotype machines and general dictation software.
*   **Benefit:** Mitigates the fear of hardware obsolescence or physical strain associated with switching systems.

**5. Call to Action**
"Join the 120 firms already transcribing faster without changing how they work. Schedule a 5-minute integration demo."

*Note: This hierarchy assumes the "under 5 minutes" and "99.5% accuracy" figures provided are representative of your broader beta data and ready for public claim verification.*
