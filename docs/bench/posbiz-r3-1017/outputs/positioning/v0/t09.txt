**Recommendation: Frame as "Invisible Preservation"**

Do not position RetroFix as a "modern aid" or explicitly as an "AI tool" for the primary market. Doing so validates the fears of your core base (preservation purists) and forces them to choose between their values and your product. Instead, position the technology as an **invisible guardian of authenticity**.

Your differentiator is not "prediction"; it is **preventing invasive repairs**. Every unexpected breakdown forces a shop to source non-original parts or perform non-factory modifications to get a car running quickly. By predicting failure *before* it happens, you allow shops to source correct, period-accurate parts and perform maintenance on their own schedule, keeping the vehicle historically pure.

**Strategy:**
1.  **For the Shop Owner:** Sell "Peace of Mind" and "Authenticity Assurance." The tech is the means, not the message.
2.  **For the End Client (Purist):** The sensor is invisible. The result is a car that never breaks down and never requires modern "hack" repairs.
3.  **Addressing the Split:** Instruct your current users to stop "hiding" sensors and start framing them as "diagnostic monitors" similar to tools a master mechanic has always used, just more precise. If the client fears the word "AI," do not lead with it. Lead with the outcome: "We ensure your restoration remains 100% original by preventing emergency fixes."

> For **vintage restoration shops** who **struggle to maintain historical authenticity when unexpected breakdowns force emergency repairs**, RetroFix is a **predictive monitoring service** that **ensures vehicles remain period-accurate by identifying part failures before they occur**; unlike **routine manual inspections or reactive break-fix models**, it **provides the lead time necessary to source original parts and avoid non-factory modifications, all through invisible sensors that respect the vehicle's original aesthetic.**

1.  **A/B Test Messaging:** Present two landing pages to shop owners. One highlights "88% AI Accuracy" (Modern Aid); the other highlights "100% Original Part Sourcing Guarantee" (Invisible Preservation). Measure conversion.
2.  **Interview Purist Clients:** Ask current end-clients if they would object to a "maintenance monitor" if it guaranteed no emergency repairs were ever needed. Do not mention "AI" initially; gauge reaction to the *outcome*.
