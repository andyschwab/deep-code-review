### Positioning Hypothesis: The Daily Margin Saver

**For** individual street food vendors in high-heat markets who struggle with daily cash flow and spoilage, **FrostCart** is a solar-powered cold storage unit that eliminates the daily cost of ice and prevents stock loss without grid power. **Unlike** buying block ice daily or running diesel generators, it **guarantees** a net savings of $4 every single day while maintaining safe temperatures for 12 hours.

**Core Message:** Stop buying ice. Start keeping your profit.
**Proof Point:** 200 units currently deployed across Mumbai and Bangkok delivering verified $4/day operating cost reductions.
**Riskiest Assumption:** That the immediate, tangible cash saving of $4/day outweighs the abstract, low-probability fear of regulatory shutdown for the average unlicensed vendor.
**Validation Test:** Run two landing page ads or stall signage tests in one market: Ad A highlights "Pass Health Inspections Guaranteed," Ad B highlights "Save $4 Every Day on Ice." Measure click-through or inquiry rates from actual vendors.
