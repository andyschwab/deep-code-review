**Recommendation: Frame as "Invisible Preservation," not "Modern Aid"**

Do not position RetroFix as a tool for modernization or a "necessary evil" for skeptics. This framing validates the fear that technology dilutes the hobby's soul, alienating your most valuable asset: the purists who control the budget and the reputation. Instead, position the algorithm as an **invisible guardian of authenticity**. The value is not "AI" or "data"; the value is **continuity**. The product ensures the car remains original by preventing the catastrophic failures that force owners to install non-original replacement parts or miss historic events.

Your current friction (users hiding sensors) confirms that the *method* must be invisible, but the *outcome* must be celebrated. The positioning should appeal to the "Preservation Purist" by promising that RetroFix is the only way to keep a vintage vehicle truly vintage for another decade. The "modernization skeptic" segment is a trap; if they feel they are compromising the soul of the hobby, they will never champion your product. Win the purists by proving that true preservation now requires predictive insight, and the skeptics will follow once the social proof is established.
