**Recommended Approach: Lead with Workflow Integration**

While 99.5% accuracy is a critical table stake, leading with it creates unnecessary friction by implicitly attacking the competence of traditional stenography and existing tools. In a profession built on precision, suggesting current methods are "inaccurate" triggers defensiveness rather than interest. Furthermore, accuracy claims are often viewed with skepticism until proven in court.

The stronger reason to switch is **seamless integration**. The primary barrier to adoption for stenographers is not the fear of errors, but the fear of disruption—changing physical posture, relearning muscle memory, or altering a trusted workflow. By positioning ScribeVoice as the tool that requires *zero* change to how they sit or work, you remove the perceived risk of adoption. Accuracy becomes the proof that supports the claim, not the headline that challenges the status quo.

**Message Hierarchy for Launch Campaign**

**1. Core Message (The Headline)**
"For court stenographers who demand precision without disruption, ScribeVoice is the voice-activated note-taker that integrates into your existing workflow in under 5 minutes; unlike traditional stenotype machines or general dictation software, it delivers 99.5% accuracy without requiring you to change how you sit, type, or work."

**2. Supporting Pillar A: Zero Friction Adoption**
*   **Claim:** Preserve your muscle memory and physical setup entirely.
*   **Proof Point:** Beta data showing 120 firms integrated the system in under 5 minutes with no hardware swaps or posture adjustments.
*   **Benefit:** Eliminates the "learning curve" tax that stalls other tech upgrades.

**3. Supporting Pillar B: Verified Precision**
*   **Claim:** Near-perfect transcripts that reduce edit time, validated in real environments.
*   **Proof Point:** 99.5% word accuracy achieved across the current beta cohort.
*   **Benefit:** Reduces post-session editing workload without compromising the gold standard of legal transcription.

**4. Supporting Pillar C: Risk-Free Transition**
*   **Claim:** A switch that feels like an upgrade, not a replacement.
*   **Proof Point:** Adoption by 120 diverse firms who previously relied on stenotype machines or Dragon Professional.
*   **Benefit:** Social proof that peers have successfully adopted the tool without workflow interruption.

**5. Call to Action**
"See how your firm can achieve 99.5% accuracy in under 5 minutes—without changing a thing about your current setup."

Note: This hierarchy treats accuracy as the validator that makes the low-risk switch safe, rather than the primary hook that invites debate.
Ensure all "120 firms" and "99.5%" claims are backed by signed testimonials or case studies before public release to satisfy legal scrutiny.
