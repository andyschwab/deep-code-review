**Positioning Hypothesis: Environmental Responsibility as the Primary Driver**

For artisanal soap makers who struggle to justify premium pricing to eco-conscious end-consumers, EcoWrap is the biodegradable packaging solution that validates their brand's sustainability claims; unlike standard plastic or generic recycled paper, it offers a verified 90-day decomposition cycle that directly supports a higher retail price point for their finished goods.

**Recommendation: Choose the "Environmental Responsibility" Narrative**

You should lead with **environmental responsibility**, not premium brand elevation. Here is why this converts the skeptical, margin-worried artisan:

1.  **It Solves the Margin Problem Directly:** Your customer's fear is the 15% material cost increase. A "luxury look" is subjective and risky; a soap maker cannot guarantee their customers will pay more just because the wrap feels nicer. However, "eco-certified" is a tangible market signal. If their end-customers are already paying extra for green products (as some of your users noted), the EcoWrap cost becomes a pass-through investment, not a margin erosion. The narrative shifts from "this costs me more" to "this allows me to charge more."
2.  **Differentiation vs. Hygiene Factor:** In the artisanal soap niche, "looking nice" is a baseline requirement (a hygiene factor); every competitor has attractive packaging. "Biodegradable in 90 days" is a specific, defensible claim that standard plastic cannot match and generic recycled paper often fails to prove with such speed. This specific metric turns your packaging into a marketing asset they can put on their own label, directly addressing their need to stand out.
3.  **Risk Mitigation for the Buyer:** A skeptical artisan worries about wasting money on unproven aesthetics. Positioning the wrap as an "environmental upgrade" aligns with the existing values of their target demographic, reducing the risk of the purchase. They aren't buying "pretty"; they are buying "proof of sustainability" which protects their brand reputation.

**The Test:** Do not assume this works. Before rewriting the website, take your current "premium look" message and your new "90-day decomposition" message to 5 of your most margin-sensitive customers. Ask them: "Which of these two statements helps you raise your soap price by $2?" Their answer validates the hypothesis.

*Note: The "premium" angle should remain a secondary support pillar (the feel of the wrap) but not the headline, as it lacks the objective proof point needed to overcome cost objections.*
*Ensure any claim regarding the "90-day decomposition" is backed by visible certification or test data on your sales materials to avoid being marked as unverified.*
