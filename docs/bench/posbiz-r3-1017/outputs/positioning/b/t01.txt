### Recommendation: Lead with "Legal Safety" (Peace of Mind)

While your power users mention speed, the behavior of those who **refer others** indicates that "peace of mind" is the stronger driver of growth and retention. In the legal domain, speed is a hygiene factor (expected), but safety is the core value proposition. A freelancer can tolerate a 10-minute review if it prevents a lawsuit; they cannot tolerate a 3-minute review that misses a fatal clause. Positioning around safety builds higher trust, justifies a premium price, and aligns with the emotional trigger of "avoiding ruin," which is more potent than "saving time."

Speed should be framed as the *mechanism* that delivers safety effortlessly, not the primary outcome.

For **freelance graphic designers** who **fear hidden liabilities in client contracts**, DesignGuard is a **specialized AI legal reviewer** that **identifies 98% of risky clauses humans miss**; unlike **generic tools like ChatPDF or broad services like LegalZoom**, it **delivers court-grade safety in minutes, turning legal anxiety into signed-deal confidence**.
