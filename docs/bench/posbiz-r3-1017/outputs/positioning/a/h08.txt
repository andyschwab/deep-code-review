**The Decision: Why Security Wins the Headline Space**
You asked whether focusing on security sounds restrictive. In the theater industry, **security is the license to operate; fluidity is the benefit of using the tool.**

1.  **The Stakes:** Your data shows a client lost $10,000 due to a leak. A lost rehearsal hour is an annoyance; a leaked script is a legal and financial catastrophe. Producers and rights holders (the people signing the checks) care deeply about the former.
2.  **The Perception:** "Security" does not mean "restrictive" if framed as "peace of mind." If you lead with "fluidity," you sound like a nicer Google Doc. If you lead with "security," you sound like industry infrastructure.
3.  **The Balance:** The headline above anchors on the *outcome* of security ("Stop chasing versions") which implies safety, while the sub-head immediately delivers the creative benefit ("Start rehearsing"). This appeases the producer's fear of leaks without alienating the director's need for speed.
