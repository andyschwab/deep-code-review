**Recommendation: Position as "Invisible Preservation"**

Do not choose between "pure preservation" and "modern aid." Choosing "modern aid" alienates the purist core who fund the hobby's prestige. Choosing "pure preservation" without explaining *how* you achieve it ignores the reality that manual inspection fails.

Instead, frame RetroFix as the **guardian of originality**. The value is not the AI; the value is that the car never breaks down at a show and never requires non-original emergency repairs. The technology must be framed as invisible infrastructure that enables the traditional outcome. Your messaging should validate the skeptic's fear (tech changes the soul) while proving your solution prevents the greater sin (mechanical failure altering the car's history).

**Positioning Statement (Hypothesis to Validate)**

For **vintage restoration shop owners** who **must guarantee vehicle reliability at high-stakes shows without compromising originality**, RetroFix is a **predictive monitoring service** that **prevents unexpected breakdowns and non-original emergency repairs**; unlike **routine manual inspections or reactive fixing**, it **uses hidden sensors to forecast failures with 88% accuracy, reducing show-day incidents by 70% while keeping the vehicle's exterior and experience entirely traditional**.

*   **[ASSUMED]:** Clients accept hidden sensors if the exterior remains untouched.
*   **Riskiest Assumption:** That hiding the tech builds trust rather than eroding it if discovered.
*   **Test:** Run two landing pages or sales scripts. Version A highlights "AI-powered modernization." Version B highlights "Invisible protection for original vehicles." Measure conversion rates among shop owners who self-identify as "traditionalists."
