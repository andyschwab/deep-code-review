**Recommendation: Lead with "Protecting Priceless Artifacts."**

While the finance director controls the budget, the curator defines the requirement and holds the veto power in a museum setting. A generic industrial sensor (like SafeFlow) or a manual routine can technically detect water, but they cannot understand the *context* of a historic collection. Your differentiator is not the hardware; it is the specialized application to heritage preservation. Leading with cost savings commoditizes your device, inviting comparison to cheaper industrial alternatives. Leading with preservation positions AquaGuard Heritage as a specialized partner in the museum's core mission, making price secondary to risk mitigation for irreplaceable items.

Use the financial argument (insurance reduction) as the **proof** that validates the emotional mission, not as the primary hook. The narrative is: "We fulfill your duty to preserve history so completely that insurers recognize the reduced risk."
