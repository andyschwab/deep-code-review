You do not need to choose between accuracy and cost. For a skeptical city planner, **accuracy is the entry ticket, but cost savings is the closer.**
*   If you pitch *only* accuracy: You sound like a luxury expense ($12k vs. free photos). The planner asks, "Why pay this when we have photos?"
