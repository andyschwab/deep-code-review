**Recommended Audience: Team Owners and Franchise Decision-Makers**

Prioritize team owners over individual gamers. While players desire an immediate competitive edge, owners bear the financial risk of player burnout, injury-related roster instability, and the high cost of medical interventions. Your data supports a "longevity and asset protection" narrative that directly impacts the owner's bottom line, whereas cognitive claims, while attractive to players, are harder to verify instantly and may be viewed as marketing hype without third-party validation. The price point ($400) is negligible compared to the $2,000 chef alternative but significant compared to generic powders; owners will pay the premium to avoid the physical degradation that ends careers prematurely.

**Message Hierarchy**

**Core Hypothesis (Positioning Statement)**
*For professional esports franchise owners who need to protect their high-value player assets from career-ending inflammation and performance decay, NutriGame is a specialized nutrition service that extends player longevity and stabilizes peak performance; unlike generic mass-gain powders or costly private chefs, it delivers a verified 25% reduction in musculoskeletal inflammation and a 15% reaction time improvement at one-fifth the cost of culinary staff.*

**Primary Pillar: Asset Protection & Longevity (The "Why" for Owners)**
*   **Claim:** Reduce the risk of chronic injury that sidelines top talent.
*   **Proof Point:** 25% reduction in reported wrist and back inflammation within two weeks across 30 global teams.
*   **Contrast:** Generic powders ignore specific inflammatory triggers; private chefs lack clinical precision for esports-specific strain patterns.
*   **Buyer Cost of Inaction:** Continued reliance on generic supplements risks long-term player health, leading to expensive roster turnover and lost tournament revenue.

**Secondary Pillar: Stabilized Peak Performance (The "How" for Coaches)**
*   **Claim:** Maintain consistent reaction times during high-pressure tournament windows.
*   **Proof Point:** 15% improvement in reaction time scores specifically during peak tournament hours.
*   **Contrast:** Standard diets cause energy crashes; chefs provide general health but not data-backed cognitive optimization for gaming windows.
*   **Buyer Cost of Inaction:** Inconsistent player performance during critical matches due to fatigue or nutritional gaps.

**Tertiary Pillar: Operational Efficiency (The Financial Logic)**
*   **Claim:** Elite-level nutritional support without the overhead of a full culinary team.
*   **Proof Point:** $400/month per player vs. $2,000/month for private chefs, with superior targeted outcomes.
*   **Contrast:** Chefs are a broad, expensive solution; powders are cheap but ineffective for professional demands.
*   **Buyer Cost of Inaction:** Overpaying for non-specialized food services or under-investing in player health infrastructure.

**Validation Note**
This positioning is a hypothesis to be validated; the 15% and 25% figures must be backed by your internal data logs when presented to buyers. Do not claim trademark clearance or market size without external legal and research verification.
