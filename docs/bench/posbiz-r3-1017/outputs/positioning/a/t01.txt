**Recommendation: Lead with Legal Safety**

While your power users praise speed, your growth engine (referrals) is driven by peace of mind. In the legal domain, "fast" is a nice-to-have feature, but "safe" is the core job-to-be-done. Freelancers can tolerate a 10-minute review if it prevents a lawsuit that bankrupts them; they cannot tolerate a 3-minute review that misses a fatal clause. Speed is your operational advantage, but safety is your value proposition. Positioning as the "fastest" invites comparison with generic tools on velocity alone, whereas positioning as the "safest" creates a distinct category where generic tools fail by definition.

**Positioning Statement**

For freelance graphic designers who need to sign client contracts without risking their business, DesignGuard is an AI contract reviewer that identifies hidden legal liabilities generic tools miss; unlike LegalZoom or manual reading, it provides specialized protection against costly clauses, turning legal anxiety into confirmed peace of mind. [Hypothesis to validate: That freelancers prioritize avoiding catastrophic loss over saving 40 minutes per contract.]

**Why this wins over the "Speed" angle:**
1.  **Stakes vs. Convenience:** Missing a risky clause has existential consequences; taking 45 minutes is merely an inconvenience. Marketing must address the highest stake.
2.  **Referral Logic:** Users refer others to solve their friends' fears, not just to save them time. The "peace of mind" narrative is inherently more viral in professional communities.
3.  **Differentiation:** LegalZoom and ChatPDF can claim speed. Only a specialized tool can credibly claim higher accuracy for a specific niche.
