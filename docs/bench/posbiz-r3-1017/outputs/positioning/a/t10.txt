**Choice:** Lead with **Workflow Seamless Integration**.

**Reasoning:** For court stenographers, time is billable and physical ergonomics are non-negotiable. While 99.5% accuracy is a strong table stake, leading with it invites a defensive comparison against established stenotype machines (which are already highly accurate in skilled hands) and implies your competitors are flawed. This creates friction. Conversely, "integration in under 5 minutes" directly attacks the highest barrier to entry: the fear of a steep learning curve and workflow disruption. Your data shows adoption happens in under 5 minutes; this is your unique differentiator. Accuracy prevents churn, but ease of adoption drives the initial switch. You win by removing the risk of change, not by arguing about marginal gains in precision.
