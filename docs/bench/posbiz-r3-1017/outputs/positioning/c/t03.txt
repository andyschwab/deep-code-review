**Recommendation: Prioritize the Productivity-Ergonomics Angle**

Position StandTall explicitly as a **performance tool for engineering teams**, not a general wellness device. While health is the underlying mechanism, the *buyer trigger* for your highest-value segment (enterprise managers) is output and focus, not pain prevention. A wellness frame risks categorizing you as a "nice-to-have" perk competing with gym memberships, whereas an ergonomics frame positions you as essential infrastructure for remote work efficiency, directly addressing the manager's need to maintain team velocity.

**Primary Messaging Hierarchy for Landing Page & Ads:**

*   **Headline:** "Eliminate Posture-Induced Distraction: The Wearable That Keeps Remote Engineers in Deep Focus."
*   **Supporting Point 1 (Outcome over Symptom):** Frame the 60% reduction in slouching not as a health stat, but as a continuity metric—fewer micro-breaks to stretch or adjust means longer, uninterrupted coding sessions.
*   **Supporting Point 2 (The Cost of the Alternative):** Contrast against ergonomic chairs (static, expensive, one-time fix) by highlighting that StandTall provides active, real-time correction that adapts to the engineer's changing posture throughout the day, preventing the "slump" that kills afternoon productivity.
*   **Supporting Point 3 (Enterprise Validation):** Leverage the 25% pain reduction as proof of sustainability rather than the primary sell; argue that comfortable engineers code longer without burnout, making this a scalable solution for managers to deploy across entire teams immediately.

**Note:** Your current 50/50 survey split suggests you are currently capturing two different buyers; this recommendation intentionally narrows your focus to the higher-LTV enterprise channel while keeping health benefits as secondary proof. Test this hypothesis by running A/B ads where the "wellness" variant targets individuals and the "productivity" variant targets engineering managers, measuring conversion rates and demo requests rather than just clicks.
