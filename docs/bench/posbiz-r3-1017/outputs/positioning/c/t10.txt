**Choice: Lead with Workflow Seamless Integration.**

While 99.5% accuracy is a critical table stake, leading with it creates unnecessary defensiveness by implying current stenotype machines or Dragon Professional are fundamentally broken. In the legal field, where existing tools are trusted implicitly, claiming "near-perfect" accuracy invites skepticism and forces buyers to defend their current equipment. Furthermore, accuracy is an outcome they only verify *after* adoption; the immediate barrier to entry for a stenographer is the fear of disrupting their physical workflow and speed during a live proceeding.

The stronger reason to switch is the elimination of the "learning curve" risk. By positioning ScribeVoice as the only solution that requires zero change to how a stenographer sits, holds their machine, or executes strokes, you remove the primary friction point. Accuracy becomes the *proof* that the seamless integration works, not the primary hook. This approach respects the buyer's expertise and frames ScribeVoice as an evolution, not a correction of their current methods.
