**Recommendation: Lead with "Legal Safety" (Peace of Mind)**

While your power users mention speed, the behavior of those who *refer others*—citing "peace of mind"—indicates that safety is your true differentiator and retention driver. Speed is a hygiene factor; freelancers expect tools to be fast. However, the fear of a lawsuit is an existential threat that generic tools like ChatPDF cannot address and LegalZoom addresses too broadly. Positioning around "closing deals quickly" invites comparison on seconds saved, whereas positioning around "avoiding lawsuits" creates a category where you are the only safe choice for a non-lawyer.

**Primary Segment:** Freelance graphic designers who have recently encountered a confusing contract clause or know someone who got burned by a bad agreement.

For **freelance graphic designers** who **fear signing contracts with hidden traps that could lead to unpaid work or lawsuits**, **DesignGuard** is the **AI contract reviewer** that **identifies 98% of risky clauses humans miss**; unlike **generic PDF readers or broad legal services**, it **provides specific, design-industry-focused safety checks that turn legal anxiety into confidence before you sign**.
