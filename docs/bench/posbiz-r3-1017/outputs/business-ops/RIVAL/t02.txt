### Critical Data Points for Payer Contract Forecasting

To accurately forecast losses and potentially recover funds, gather these specific contract details immediately:

1.  **Denial Reason Codes & Appeal Windows:** Identify if denials are technical (fixable/resubmittable) or clinical (permanent loss). Determine the specific deadline (e.g., 30, 60, 90 days) to file appeals before revenue is permanently written off.
2.  **Fee Schedule Variance:** Confirm if the $85 rate is uniform across all payers. Some contracts may have lower allowable amounts, meaning a denial on a lower-rate payer hurts less than on a higher-rate one.
3.  **Clean Claim Rate Requirements:** Check contracts for penalties or withholding clauses triggered if your denial rate exceeds a specific threshold (often 5-10%), which could worsen cash flow beyond just the lost visit revenue.
4.  **Timely Filing Limits:** Verify the maximum time allowed between visit date and claim submission to ensure operational delays aren't causing the spike in denials.
