### Market Sizing Framework for HapticTrain

Since regulatory mandates are pending, you must model your Total Addressable Market (TAM) using a **Scenario-Based Adoption Framework** rather than a compliance-based one. This approach separates "voluntary early adopters" from "mandatory compliance buyers," allowing you to revenue forecast without relying on non-existent laws.

#### 1. Define the Unit Economics
Before sizing the market, define your **Annual Contract Value (ACV)** per firm.
*   **Hardware:** One-time cost per glove set.
*   **Software/Content:** Recurring license for simulation modules (e.g., electrical shock scenarios).
*   **Maintenance/Support:** Annual fee for calibration and updates.
*   *Action:* Establish a conservative ACV (e.g., $15,000/year) to use in the calculations below.

#### 2. The Scenario Matrix
Calculate revenue potential across three distinct scenarios using your base of **5,000 large manufacturing firms**.

**Scenario A: Voluntary Early Adoption (Current State)**
*   **Driver:** Firms seeking competitive advantage, insurance premium reductions, or proactive safety culture improvements before laws change.
*   **Assumption:** Typically 2–5% of a market adopts new safety tech voluntarily before mandates.
*   **Calculation:** 5,000 firms × 3% adoption = **150 customers**.
*   **Revenue Potential:** 150 × ACV.
*   *Strategy:* Target firms with recent safety incidents or those marketing themselves as "industry leaders."

**Scenario B: Partial Mandate (Likely Intermediate Step)**
*   **Driver:** Regulations may initially apply only to "high-risk" sub-sectors (e.g., high-voltage electrical work, heavy pressing machinery) rather than all manufacturing.
*   **Assumption:** Estimate that 40% of your 5,000 firms fall into high-risk categories. Of those, 60% comply immediately upon regulation.
*   **Calculation:** (5,000 × 40%) × 60% = **1,200 customers**.
*   **Revenue Potential:** 1,200 × ACV.

**Scenario C: Full Compliance (Mature Regulation)**
*   **Driver:** Broad mandate covering all large manufacturing facilities.
*   **Assumption:** 90% compliance rate among the total 5,000 firms over a 3-year rollout.
*   **Calculation:** 5,000 × 90% = **4,500 customers**.
*   **Revenue Potential:** 4,500 × ACV.
