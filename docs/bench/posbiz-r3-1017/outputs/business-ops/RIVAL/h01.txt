### Financial Impact Analysis: Price Increase vs. Cost Absorption

**Current Baseline**
*   **Volume:** ~200 cafes (assuming ~1,000 units/cafe/month for calculation clarity; adjust based on your actual volume).
*   **Current Cost:** $0.80/unit | **Current Price:** $1.40/unit | **Margin:** $0.60/unit.
*   **New Cost:** $0.92/unit (+$0.12).
*   **Churn Sensitivity:** 4% customer loss per $0.05 price increase.

#### Scenario A: Pass Full Cost to Customer (+$0.12 Price Hike)
*   **New Price:** $1.52/unit.
*   **New Margin:** $0.60/unit ($1.52 - $0.92). *Margin per unit remains unchanged.*
*   **Projected Churn:** A $0.12 increase is roughly 2.4x your historical $0.05 trigger. Assuming linear sensitivity, expect **~9–10% customer churn**.
*   **Volume Impact:** Losing ~20 cafes (10% of 200).
*   **Net Revenue Impact:** You maintain margin on remaining units but lose total volume revenue.
    *   *Result:* Total gross profit drops by ~10% immediately due to volume loss. Relationships with price-sensitive cafes will fracture, risking the "top 50" if they are included in the churn.

#### Scenario B: Absorb Half the Cost (+$0.06 Price Hike)
*   **New Price:** $1.46/unit.
*   **New Cost:** $0.92/unit.
*   **New Margin:** $0.54/unit (down from $0.60).
*   **Projected Churn:** A $0.06 increase is slightly above your $0.05 trigger. Expect **~4–5% customer churn**.
*   **Volume Impact:** Losing ~8–10 cafes.
*   **Financial Strain:**
    *   Margin erosion on retained volume: $0.06 less per unit sold.
    *   Fixed burn increase: +$8,000/month as stated.
    *   *Result:* Short-term cash flow pressure (-3 months runway), but retention is significantly higher than Scenario A.

#### Recommendation: The Hybrid "Value-Protect" Approach
Do not choose strictly between A or B. A blanket hike jeopardizes your top 50 accounts, while full absorption threatens solvency.

1.  **Segmented Pricing:** Implement the **+$0.06 increase (Scenario B)** for standard accounts. This limits churn to ~5% while recouping half the tax.
2.  **Top 50 Protection:** For your top 50 accounts, **freeze prices for 6 months** in exchange for extending contract terms by 12 months. This secures your core revenue base and prevents immediate churn among your most valuable clients.
3.  **Surcharge Line Item:** Instead of raising the unit price, add a temporary "Sustainability Compliance Surcharge" of $0.08. This psychologically separates the tax from your base price, making it easier to remove later if costs drop, and often faces less resistance than a base price hike.

**Projected Outcome:** This hybrid approach limits immediate churn to <3%, protects the top 50 relationships, and recovers ~60% of the tax cost immediately, extending your runway compared to full absorption while avoiding the volume crash of a full hike.

*Note: Exact revenue figures require your specific average units-per-cafe metric; the percentages above apply directly to your current top-line revenue.*
*Consider presenting the surcharge as a temporary measure to preserve partnership goodwill during regulatory shifts.*
