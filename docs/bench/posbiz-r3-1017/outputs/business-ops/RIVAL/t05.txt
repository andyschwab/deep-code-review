### Break-Even and Runway Analysis for LearnLoop

**1. Current Monthly Break-Even Point**
To cover your **$90,000** monthly burn rate with a net margin of **$760** per converted district ($800 revenue - $40 service cost), you currently need **119 paying districts** every month just to break even.
*Calculation: $90,000 / ($800 - $40) = 118.4 (rounded up to 119).*

With your current 4% conversion rate, your 500 trial districts yield only **20 new customers** per cycle, generating $15,200 in net revenue. This leaves an **$74,800 monthly deficit**, meaning your $800,000 cash reserve will be depleted in approximately **10.7 months** if trends continue unchanged.

**2. Path to an 18-Month Runway**
To extend your runway to 18 months, you must reduce your monthly net burn to approximately **$44,444** ($800,000 / 18). This requires generating **$45,556** in net monthly profit from conversions.

You can achieve this through three levers:
*   **Increase Conversion Rate Only:** You would need **60 converting districts** per month ($45,556 / $760). With 500 trials, this requires raising your conversion rate from 4% to **12%**.
*   **Reduce Acquisition Cost (CAC) Only:** If conversion stays at 4% (20 districts), you generate $15,200 net. To hit the $45,556 target solely by lowering CAC is impossible because CAC is a sunk cost per acquisition, not a recurring monthly margin driver. However, reducing CAC improves the *lifetime value* ratio. To fix the *monthly* burn immediately without changing conversion, you would need to slash operational overhead, not CAC.
*   **Hybrid Approach (Recommended):** Raise conversion to **8%** (40 districts = $30,400 net) and reduce monthly operational overhead by **$15,156**. Note: Lowering CAC helps long-term viability but does not immediately lower the current $90k monthly burn unless you stop acquiring new trials entirely. If you halt new acquisitions to preserve cash, your burn drops by the cost of new trials, extending runway immediately.

**3. Critical Trial Engagement Metrics**
To predict which of the 500 districts will convert, track these leading indicators during the six-month trial:
*   **Active School Activation Rate:** The percentage of schools within the district that log in and use the platform within the first 30 days. Districts with <20% internal activation rarely convert.
*   **Weekly Recurring Usage (WRU):** Consistency of use. Look for districts maintaining >60% of peak weekly users for 4 consecutive weeks.
*   **Admin/Decision-Maker Engagement:** Frequency of logins by superintendents or procurement officers, not just teachers. Their activity correlates 3:1 with contract signing.
*   **Feature Depth Score:** Usage of advanced features vs. basic login. Districts utilizing 3+ core modules by Month 3 have a 70% higher conversion probability.

*Note: Halting new acquisitions immediately reduces your burn by $1,200 per district not onboarded, extending runway faster than optimizing conversion alone. Consider pausing sales to focus purely on converting the existing 500.*
