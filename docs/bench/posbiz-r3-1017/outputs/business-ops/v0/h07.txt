### Lane A: Applied Arithmetic & ROI Narrative

To justify the $50,000 annual contract ($50/seat × 1,000 seats), you must demonstrate that the value created exceeds this cost. Based on your pilot data (15% stress reduction) and industry benchmarks, here is the applied math and the metrics you must track to make the calculation auditable.

**1. The Break-Even Thresholds**
Your cost to the client is **$50,000**. To break even, the solution must save the client $50,001.
*   **Via Turnover:** With a turnover cost of $15,000/person, you need to prevent **3.4 departures** annually ($50,000 ÷ $15,000) to cover your fee.
*   **Via Absenteeism:** With a cost of $200/day, you need to recover **250 lost workdays** annually ($50,000 ÷ $200).

**2. The Narrative Structure (Apply, Don't Direct)**
Do not claim the 15% stress reduction *automatically* equals specific savings. Instead, structure the narrative as a hypothesis test: *"If the observed 15% reduction in stress correlates to even a marginal decrease in turnover or absenteeism, the ROI is positive."*

*   **The Turnover Argument:** If your 1,000-person client typically sees a 10% annual turnover rate (100 people), preventing just **4 people** from leaving covers your entire fee.
    *   *Formula:* `Savings = (Baseline Turnover Count × Reduction % Attributable to CalmChat) × $15,000`
    *   *Application:* If you can attribute a 0.5% absolute reduction in turnover to CalmChat (5 people retained), Savings = 5 × $15,000 = $75,000. Net Value = $25,000.
*   **The Absenteeism Argument:** If the average employee takes 5 sick days/year, the workforce loses 5,000 days.
    *   *Formula:* `Savings = (Total Workforce Days Lost × Reduction % Attributable to CalmChat) × $200`
    *   *Application:* If CalmChat reduces stress-related absenteeism by just 1% (50 days recovered), Savings = 50 × $200 = $10,000. This alone covers 20% of your fee.

**3. Metrics to Track (The "Undeniable" Data)**
To move from "stress reduction" to "dollar savings," you must instrument the pilot to capture the *leading indicators* that correlate to the financial lagging indicators above. Track these specifically:
*   **Intent-to-Stay Score:** Survey question: "How likely are you to remain at this company in the next 12 months?" (Correlate changes in this score against your stress reduction metric).
*   **Self-Reported Avoided Absence:** In-app prompt: "Did using CalmChat help you manage a situation that might otherwise have caused you to miss work today?" (Yes/No). Multiply "Yes" counts by $200.
*   **Utilization vs. Outcome Correlation:** Segment users by usage frequency. Do high-frequency users show lower stress scores than low-frequency users? This isolates your product's impact from external factors.

*   **Read:** At current pricing, the client needs to retain ~4 employees or save ~250 days of work to break even.
