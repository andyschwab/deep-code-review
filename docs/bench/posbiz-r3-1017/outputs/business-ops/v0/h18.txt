**Lane R: Regulation-Dependent (Route Immediately)**

Your question regarding whether you can **legally market** your product as "biodegradable" and the associated risk of fines for greenwashing is a **Lane R** matter. It depends entirely on specific EU regulations and legal determinations that cannot be resolved through arithmetic or general business logic.

**The Regime:**
Your situation falls under the **EU Unfair Commercial Practices Directive (UCPD)**, specifically as amended by the **Empowering Consumers for the Green Transition (ECGT) Directive** (Directive (EU) 2024/825), and potentially the **EU Green Claims Directive** (once fully applicable or as a benchmark for current enforcement trends). These regimes strictly govern environmental claims.

**Why You Must Route to Counsel:**
1.  **Substantiation Requirement:** Under current and upcoming EU rules, explicit environmental claims like "biodegradable" generally require verification by an accredited third party based on recognized scientific methods. Making a claim without this certification creates a high probability of being classified as an "unfair commercial practice."
2.  **Risk of Fines:** The ECGT Directive empowers national authorities to impose significant fines (often a percentage of turnover) for greenwashing. Only a qualified attorney specializing in EU consumer law can assess your specific liability exposure based on the exact wording of your marketing and the jurisdictions of your 10 toy brand clients.
3.  **Definition of "Biodegradable":** The legal definition often requires specifying the environment (e.g., "biodegradable in industrial composting" vs. "in soil"). A vague claim of "biodegradable in soil" without a harmonized standard or certification is a primary target for enforcement actions.

**Action Required:**
You **must consult with a regulatory affairs specialist or legal counsel** before launching any marketing campaign or making public claims. Do not proceed with the "biodegradable" label until counsel confirms your specific evidence package meets the strict substantiation requirements of the ECGT and relevant national implementations.
