### Lane R: Regulatory & Professional Determination (Route Immediately)
Your core question—"What is the **correct** depreciation schedule?" and "How do we **justify** this to auditors?"—falls under **Lane R**.
*   **The Regime:** This involves GAAP/IFRS standards for asset impairment, useful life estimation, and salvage value determination in specialized industrial contexts.
*   **The Action:** You must engage a CPA or auditor with specific experience in **offshore energy, maritime infrastructure, or heavy industrial assets**. Standard IT auditors will likely lack the context for subsea retrieval costs and marine corrosion models.
*   **Why Route:** Only a licensed professional can determine if an 8-year life is permissible given the "inability to repair" constraint, or if the risk of total loss requires an accelerated method or an immediate impairment charge. Stating a definitive schedule here would be unauthorized practice of accounting.

### Lane A: Arithmetic & Structuring (Apply Your Numbers)
While the *decision* is Lane R, you can prepare the **arithmetic scenarios** for your professional to review. Below is the framework to apply your specific inputs ($12M total assets, $4.2M/pod, $15k/day ROV) to different hypotheses.

**1. Define the Inputs (Do not fabricate; use your estimates)**
*   **Cost Basis ($C$):** $4,200,000 per pod.
*   **Estimated Salvage Value ($S$):** Likely negative or zero. *Calculation:* Retrieve cost ($15,000 × days) minus scrap value. If retrieval costs exceed scrap, $S$ is a liability, effectively increasing the depreciable base.
*   **Useful Life ($N$):** The variable in dispute (3–5 years vs. 8+ years).

**2. Scenario Modeling (Show the Formula)**
Present these three calculations to your CPA to test sensitivity:

*   **Scenario A (Conservative IT Standard):**
    *   Method: Straight-Line
    *   Formula: $(C - S) / 5$
    *   *Application:* $(4,200,000 - 0) / 5 = \mathbf{\$840,000/year}$ expense per pod.
*   **Scenario B (Optimistic Marine Stability):**
    *   Method: Straight-Line
    *   Formula: $(C - S) / 8$
    *   *Application:* $(4,200,000 - 0) / 8 = \mathbf{\$525,000/year}$ expense per pod.
*   **Scenario C (Accelerated/Risk-Adjusted):**
    *   Method: Double Declining Balance (reflecting high early obsolescence risk).
    *   Formula: $Book Value \times (2 / N)$
    *   *Application (Year 1, 5-yr life):$ $4,200,000 \times 0.40 = \mathbf{\$1,680,000}$ expense.

**3. Decision Aid for Auditor Justification**
To support your CPA's final ruling, prepare a memo quantifying the "Physical Inaccessibility Factor":
*   **Repair Probability:** If probability of repair = 0, does the asset functionally expire upon first component failure?
*   **Retrieval Economics:** Calculate the break-even days for ROV usage. If a repair takes 10 days ($150k), does it exceed the value of the uptime gained?
*   **Environmental Stressors:** List specific corrosion/saltwater ingress data points that differ from land-based servers.
