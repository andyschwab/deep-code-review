### The Core Boundary: Control vs. Asset Protection

Your goal is to structure the relationship as a **pure equipment lease** between Acme Exo (Lessor) and the General Contractor (Lessee), ensuring the workers remain solely under the control of the General Contractor. In Texas, courts and agencies (like the TWC and IRS) generally apply a "right of control" test. If Acme Exo dictates how, when, or where the worker performs their job, you risk reclassification.

**Key Contractual Boundaries to Maintain (The "Do Nots"):**

*   **Do Not Control Schedules:** Your contract must never specify the hours the worker wears the suit or mandate break times. The General Contractor must retain exclusive authority to set work schedules.
*   **Do Not Direct Work Methods:** Avoid clauses that dictate *how* the worker performs their construction tasks while wearing the suit. You can specify how to *operate the device* safely, but not how to lift, carry, or build.
*   **Do Not Provide Direct Training to Workers:** Training should be provided to the General Contractor's supervisors, who then train their employees. If you must train workers directly, limit it strictly to technical device operation and safety warnings, explicitly stating this does not constitute job supervision.
*   **Do Not Enforce Discipline:** Acme Exo must have no role in disciplining, firing, or promoting the workers. Any removal of a suit due to misuse must be communicated to the General Contractor, who then decides the consequence for the worker.

**Clauses to Include for Asset Protection (The "Dos"):**

*   **Maintenance and Repair:** Define maintenance as the lessor's responsibility or a scheduled service performed by Acme technicians *at the convenience of the site*, not during critical work windows dictated by Acme. The General Contractor is responsible for daily inspections and reporting damage.
*   **Usage Monitoring:** You may include telemetry for **device health and safety limits** (e.g., overheating, battery failure, unauthorized modification). Explicitly state in the contract that data collected is for **asset preservation and product safety only**, not for evaluating worker performance, productivity, or attendance.
*   **Indemnification:** Include strong indemnity clauses where the General Contractor agrees to defend and hold Acme harmless against any worker classification claims arising from their use of the equipment.
*   **Scope of Use:** Clearly define the permitted uses of the exoskeleton. If a worker uses it outside these parameters, the General Contractor is liable for damages, but Acme does not step in to manage the worker.

**Specific Clause Guidance:**

*   **Avoid:** "Acme Exo reserves the right to remove suits from workers who fail to meet productivity standards."
*   **Include:** "Acme Exo reserves the right to deactivate or retrieve suits that are operated outside of manufacturer safety specifications or show signs of unauthorized modification, upon notice to the Lessee (General Contractor)."
