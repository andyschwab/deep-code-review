### Lane A: Unit Economics & Margin Sensitivity
**Formula:**
`Gross Margin % = (Selling Price − Total COGS) ÷ Selling Price`
Where `Total COGS = Manufacturing Cost + Freight + Customs Duty`.

*   Selling Price: $400
*   Manufacturing Cost: $210
*   Freight: $18
*   Duty Rate Scenario: 4% (Low) to 12% (High) applied to the customs value (typically Cost + Freight).

**Scenario Analysis:**

1.  **Best Case (4% Duty):**
    *   Duty Amount: ($210 + $18) × 0.04 = $9.12
    *   Total COGS: $210 + $18 + $9.12 = $237.12
    *   Gross Margin: ($400 − $237.12) ÷ $400 = **40.72%**

2.  **Worst Case (12% Duty):**
    *   Duty Amount: ($210 + $18) × 0.12 = $27.36
    *   Total COGS: $210 + $18 + $27.36 = $255.36
