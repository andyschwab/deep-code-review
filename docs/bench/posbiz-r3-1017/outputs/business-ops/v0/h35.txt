**The Regimes Involved**
1.  **United States:** The primary regime is the **Securities Act of 1933** and the **Securities Exchange Act of 1934**, enforced by the SEC. The defining test is the **Howey Test**, a four-pronged legal analysis determining if a transaction involves an investment of money in a common enterprise with a reasonable expectation of profits derived from the efforts of others.
2.  **European Union:** The primary regime is **MiCA (Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation, Regulation (EU) 2023/1114)**, which fully applies as of late 2024. MiCA categorizes tokens into Asset-Referenced Tokens, E-Money Tokens, and "Other Crypto-Assets" (which often includes utility tokens). However, if a token qualifies as a "financial instrument" under **MiFID II** (e.g., transferable securities), it falls outside MiCA and under stricter securities laws.

*   In the **EU**, the distinction relies on whether the token grants access to a service (utility) versus representing a claim on future value or equity. Misclassification can lead to severe penalties, including the unwinding of sales and personal liability for directors.
