### Revenue Comparison & Break-Even Analysis

**1. Studio Revenue Baseline (Monthly)**
*   **Classes/Month:** 40 classes/week × 4.33 weeks ≈ 173 classes.
*   **Max Potential Revenue:** 173 classes × 8 attendees × $25 × 5 headsets = $173,000.
*   **Scenario A (70% Utilization):** $173,000 × 0.70 = **$121,100** generated revenue.
*   **Scenario B (90% Utilization):** $173,000 × 0.90 = **$155,700** generated revenue.

**2. FitVerse Revenue Projection (Per Studio)**

| Model | Formula | 70% Utilization | 90% Utilization |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Flat Fee** | $150 × 5 headsets | **$750** | **$750** |
| **Revenue Share** | 12% × Studio Revenue | **$14,532** | **$18,684** |

*Observation:* At the stated parameters, the revenue share model yields significantly higher income per studio than the flat fee in both utilization scenarios.

**3. Break-Even Utilization Rate**
To find where Revenue Share equals Flat Fee ($750):
*   $750 = 12% × (Max Potential Revenue × Utilization Rate)
*   $750 = 0.12 × ($173,000 × $U$)
*   $750 = $20,760 × $U$
*   $U$ = $750 / $20,760 ≈ **3.6%**

*Result:* The revenue share model becomes more profitable than the flat fee at a utilization rate of approximately **3.6%**. Given your baseline assumptions (70–90%), the revenue share model is mathematically superior in every projected case.

**4. Operational Break-Even (Studio Count)**
To cover $60,000 monthly expenses:
*   **Flat Fee Model:** $60,000 / $750 = **80 studios** required.
*   **Revenue Share (at 70% util):** $60,000 / $14,532 ≈ **5 studios** required.
*   **Revenue Share (at 90% util):** $60,000 / $18,684 ≈ **4 studios** required.

### Data Validation Requests for Pilot Studios

To validate these projections before committing to a model, request the following specific data points from pilot studios:
1.  **Actual vs. Booked Attendance:** Compare scheduled attendees against actual show-ups to refine the "8 attendees" assumption (no-shows may affect revenue recognition clauses).
2.  **Class Cancellation Rates:** High cancellation rates could drastically reduce the "40 classes/week" baseline, impacting the revenue share volume.
3.  **Seasonality Trends:** Monthly attendance fluctuations over the last 12 months to stress-test the "70% vs 90%" utilization assumptions during off-peak periods.
4.  **Attribution Clarity:** Data distinguishing classes booked *specifically* through your VR software versus general studio bookings, ensuring the 12% share applies only to attributable revenue if contractually required.

*Note: The extreme difference in break-even rates suggests the $150 flat fee may be severely underpriced relative to the value generated, or the revenue share percentage is exceptionally high for this hardware margin.*
*Verify if the revenue share applies to gross class revenue or net profit, as this materially changes the studio's willingness to accept the terms.*
