### Lane A: Arithmetic & Unit Economics Application

You asked to model the transition impact on runway and compare revenue structures. Below is the math applied to your specific numbers ($180k monthly burn, 4 cities, proposed SaaS rate).

**1. Cash Flow & Runway Comparison (Year 1)**

*   **Current Perpetual Model (Status Quo):**
    *   *Assumption:* You sell 1 new city license this year (or renew/maintenance only).
    *   Upfront Cash (1 license): $600,000.
    *   Maintenance (4 cities @ 15% of $600k base? Or 15% of contract value?): Assuming 15% of the $600k ACV equivalent = $90,000/year ($7,500/mo).
    *   Total Year 1 Cash In: ~$690,000 (if 1 new sale) or $90,000 (if maintenance only).
    *   *Runway Impact:* High immediate cash injection if new sales occur; lumpy revenue.

*   **Proposed SaaS Model:**
    *   Monthly Revenue per City: $75,000.
    *   Total Monthly Revenue (4 cities): $300,000.
    *   Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR): $3.6M.
    *   *Runway Calculation:*
        *   Monthly Net Burn = Monthly Burn ($180,000) – Monthly Revenue ($300,000) = **-$120,000** (Positive Cash Flow).
        *   If fully converted, you are cash-flow positive immediately.

**2. Transition Impact (60% Conversion Rate Scenario)**

If only 60% of your 4 existing customers convert to SaaS in Year 1:
*   Converted Cities: 2.4 (approx. 2 cities for conservative cash modeling).
*   SaaS Revenue: 2 × $75,000 = $150,000/month.
*   Lost Perpetual/Maintenance Revenue: Assume the non-converters stay on maintenance or churn.
*   *Net Monthly Burn during transition:* $180,000 (cost) – $150,000 (rev) = **$30,000/month**.
*   *Runway Formula:* `Runway = Cash on Hand ÷ Net Monthly Burn`.
    *   If you have $500k cash: $500k ÷ $30k = ~16 months.
    *   If you have $0 cash: You need bridge funding or to accelerate the remaining 40% conversion.

**3. Valuation Multiple Context (Arithmetic Only)**

*   **Perpetual Model:** Typically valued at **1x–3x Revenue** (due to lumpy cash flow and lower predictability).
*   **SaaS Model:** Infrastructure software often commands **6x–10x ARR** (due to recurring revenue stability).
*   *The Math:* $3.6M ARR × 8x multiple = ~$28.8M implied valuation vs. $1M revenue × 2x multiple = ~$2M implied valuation.
*   *Caveat:* This arithmetic assumes investors accept the $75k/mo price point and the 60% conversion risk is mitigated.

**Decision Aid:**
*   **Read:** The SaaS model flips your burn from negative to positive *if* you achieve >2.4 city conversions (break-even point: $180k ÷ $75k = 2.4 cities).
*   **Flip Input:** The conversion rate is the critical variable. At <60% conversion, you remain cash-flow negative.
