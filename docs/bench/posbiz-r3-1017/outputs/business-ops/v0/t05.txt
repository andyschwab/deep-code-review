### 3. Engagement Metrics to Track

While I cannot predict your specific user behavior, standard unit economics for B2B SaaS trials suggest tracking leading indicators that correlate with willingness to pay. Focus on metrics that demonstrate **integration depth** and **active utility**:

1.  **Administrative Integration Completion:** Did the district connect their Student Information System (SIS)? Districts that complete technical onboarding usually have higher switching costs and higher conversion.
2.  **Weekly Active Users (WAU) per School:** Track the percentage of enrolled students/teachers logging in weekly. A decline here often predicts churn before the trial ends.
3.  **Feature Depth:** Measure usage of "premium" or core workflow features versus passive viewing. High depth correlates with perceived value.
4.  **Champion Identification:** Track the number of distinct administrators engaging with support or reporting tools. Multi-stakeholder engagement reduces single-point-of-failure risk.
