**Operational Context (Educational Only)**
Regulators typically examine whether a transaction meets the **Howey Test** criteria: (1) investment of money, (2) in a common enterprise, (3) with a reasonable expectation of profits, (4) derived from the efforts of others. Enforcement actions have often targeted services where the provider pools assets, selects validators, and manages the technical staking process on behalf of users who rely on the provider's expertise for returns. Differences in prior cases often hinge on whether the user retains custody, selects validators, or if the yield is derived from protocol inflation versus the provider's active management. However, applying these tests to your specific "pooling" implementation is a legal conclusion you must obtain from counsel.
