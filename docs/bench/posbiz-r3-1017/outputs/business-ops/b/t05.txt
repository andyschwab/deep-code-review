### Lane A: Unit Economics & Runway Analysis

**1. Current Break-Even Point (Monthly)**
To cover your fixed monthly burn of $90,000 using only the contribution from paid contracts:
*   **Revenue per paid district:** $800/month.
*   **Variable cost assumption:** The $40/month cost applies per *active school*. Without the number of schools per district, we cannot calculate the exact net contribution per district.
    *   *Scenario A (1 school/district):* Net = $800 - $40 = $760.
    *   *Scenario B (10 schools/district):* Net = $800 - $400 = $400.
*   **Formula:** `Break-Even Districts = Monthly Burn ÷ (Price - Variable Cost per District)`
*   **Application:**
    *   If net margin is $760: You need **119 paid districts** ($90,000 ÷ $760).
    *   If net margin is $400: You need **225 paid districts** ($90,000 ÷ $400).
*   **Current Reality:** With 500 trial districts and a 4% conversion rate, you have **20 paid districts**. You are significantly below break-even in either scenario.

**2. Runway Extension to 18 Months**
*   **Current Runway:** $800,000 cash ÷ $90,000 burn = **~8.9 months**.
*   **Target:** 18 months requires reducing net monthly burn to ~$44,444 ($800,000 ÷ 18).
*   **Gap to Close:** You must generate **$45,556** in monthly net contribution from conversions to hit this target.
*   **Required Conversions (to hit 18-month runway):**
    *   Using the $760 net margin estimate: You need **60 total paid districts** ($45,556 ÷ $760).
    *   Using the $400 net margin estimate: You need **114 total paid districts**.
*   **Conversion Rate Required:**
    *   To get 60 districts from 500 trials: **12% conversion rate** (up from 4%).
    *   To get 114 districts from 500 trials: **22.8% conversion rate**.
*   **CAC Reduction Impact:** Lowering CAC ($1,200) does not immediately extend runway unless you stop acquiring new trials. CAC is a sunk cost for the current 500. It only helps if you plan to acquire *more* districts; otherwise, focus on conversion and variable costs.

**3. Decision Aid & Next Steps**
*   **Read:** At current conversion (4%), you will run out of cash in ~9 months. To reach 18 months, you must triple your conversion rate to ~12% (assuming 1 school/district) or nearly sextuple it if districts are larger.
*   **Flip Input:** The number of schools per district is the missing variable. If average schools > 5, your unit economics may be negative (cost > $800), making break-even impossible at this price point.
*   **Threshold Rule:** If LTV (Price ÷ Churn) < 3x CAC, or if Contribution Margin is negative, stop acquisition immediately.
