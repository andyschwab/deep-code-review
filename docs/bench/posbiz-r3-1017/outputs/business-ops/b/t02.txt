### Net Revenue Model & Break-Even Analysis

**Current State (15% Denial Rate)**
*   **Gross Revenue per Visit:** $85
*   **Denial Rate:** 15% (Effective collection rate: 85%)
*   **Net Revenue per Visit:** $85 × 0.85 = **$72.25**
*   **Operational Cost per Visit:** $30 (Fixed)
*   **Contribution Margin per Visit:** $72.25 - $30 = **$42.25**
*   **Monthly Volume:** 12,000 visits / 3 months = 4,000 visits/month
*   **Monthly Contribution:** 4,000 × $42.25 = **$169,000**
*   **Net Cash Flow:** $169,000 (Contribution) - $140,000 (Burn) = **+$29,000/month**

**Scenario A: Denials Stabilize at 15%**
*   Net Revenue remains **+$29,000/month**. The model holds; runway is preserved.

**Scenario B: Denials Creep to 20%**
*   **New Net Revenue per Visit:** $85 × 0.80 = $68.00
*   **New Contribution Margin:** $68.00 - $30 = **$38.00**
*   **Required Volume to Maintain Current Cash Flow ($29k):**
    *   Target Total Contribution = Fixed Burn ($140k) + Target Cash Flow ($29k) = $169,000
    *   Required Visits = $169,000 / $38.00 ≈ **4,448 visits/month**
*   **Additional Visits Needed:** 4,448 - 4,000 = **448 additional visits/month** (approx. 112 more per week).

**Decision Aid**
If denial rates rise to 20%, you must increase volume by ~11% just to maintain current cash flow.
*   **Flip Input:** The break-even denial rate (where Cash Flow = $0) occurs when Contribution Margin covers the $140k burn.
    *   $140,000 / 4,000 visits = $35 required margin.
    *   $35 = ($85 × (1 - Denial%)) - $30 → Denial% ≈ **23.5%**.
    *   *Rule of Thumb:* If denials exceed 23.5%, the business burns cash faster than the current 6-month runway implies, regardless of volume growth.
*   **Next Step:** If you cannot secure 450 extra visits next month, consider isolating the denial cause by payer before expanding the therapist network.

### Data Points to Gather from Payer Contracts

To forecast losses accurately, gather these specific contract terms:
1.  **Timely Filing Limits:** The exact window (e.g., 90 vs. 180 days) to submit claims before automatic denial.
2.  **Appeal Windows & Requirements:** The deadline and specific documentation needed to overturn a denial; this determines if "denied" revenue is permanently lost or just delayed.
3.  **Clean Claim Definitions:** Specific coding or metadata requirements unique to each payer that trigger auto-denials.
4.  **Fee Schedule Variance:** Confirm if the $85 rate is flat across all visit types or if specific CPT codes have different reimbursement rates that are being denied disproportionately.

*Note: Your current runway calculation assumes the $140k burn is static; if volume increases to fix the denial gap, variable support costs may rise, shortening the runway further.*
*Verify the "fixed" $30 cost assumption if visit volume scales significantly.*
