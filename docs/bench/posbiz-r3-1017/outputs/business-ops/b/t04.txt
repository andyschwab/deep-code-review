**Lane R: Route to a Licensed Professional**
Your request asks for specific contractual language and financial thresholds to ensure a lease qualifies as an "operating lease" to keep liabilities off the balance sheet. This is a **Lane R** matter. Under current International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS), specifically **IFRS 16**, the distinction between operating and finance (capital) leases for lessees has largely been eliminated, requiring most leases to be recognized on the balance sheet. While lessors (your company) still distinguish between operating and finance leases, the criteria for this classification depend on whether "substantially all the risks and rewards incidental to ownership" are transferred.

Determining whether your specific terms (36-month term vs. 7-year life, residual value guarantees, ownership clauses) trigger a finance lease classification is a regulatory and accounting determination. Providing specific language to "ensure" operating lease status or defining exact financial thresholds to "remain compliant" constitutes legal and accounting advice. An incorrect classification could lead to covenant violations with your bank and regulatory penalties.

**Action Required:**
You must engage a qualified **CPA or accounting firm** familiar with **IFRS 16** and German commercial law (HGB) immediately. They will analyze:
1.  Whether the lease term covers a "major part" of the economic life (your 36 months vs. 84 months is a factor, but not the sole determinant).
2.  Whether the present value of lease payments amounts to "substantially all" of the fair value.
3.  The impact of any residual value guarantees or bargain purchase options you include.
