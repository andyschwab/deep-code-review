### Required Data for Segmentation

To refine this model before deciding, analyze these specific patterns in your database:

1.  **Documents Per User (Distribution):** Calculate the exact document count for the top 10% vs. the bottom 50%. Determine the break-even document count where $0.50/doc equals $99 (which is 198 documents). Users above 198 docs/month will see a price hike; those below see a discount.
2.  **Elasticity Clusters:** Identify users currently near the 198-document threshold. These are your highest risk for churn if prices rise, or highest potential for increased revenue if they stay and exceed the threshold.
3.  **Cost-to-Serve Variance:** Verify if server costs truly scale linearly. If heavy users cost $40/mo to serve rather than $12, losing them saves more margin than calculated above.

**Decision Aid:** If >30% of your current base exceeds 198 documents/month, a pure usage model risks massive churn. Consider a "Good-Better-Best" tier: a capped usage plan (e.g., $99 for up to 200 docs) to retain heavy users while offering a low-entry tier for new users.
