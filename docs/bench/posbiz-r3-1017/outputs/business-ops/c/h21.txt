**The Regime:**
The primary regime governing your situation is the **EAR**, administered by the U.S. Department of Commerce's Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS). Specifically, geospatial imagery and related software often fall under **Category 7 (Navigation and Avionics)** or **Category 9 (Aerospace and Propulsion)** of the Commerce Control List (CCL). Additionally, if your satellite resolution exceeds certain thresholds (historically 0.5 meters or better for commercial satellites, though rules have evolved), the data may be subject to strict licensing requirements regardless of whether the end-user is a private hedge fund or a government entity. The **end-user** (financial institution) does not automatically exempt you from export controls; the **destination country** (Eastern Europe, Southeast Asia) and the **technical specifications** of the data are the triggering factors.
