**Lane A: Unit Economics & Decision Analysis**

Your current monthly contribution margin is $35 ($60 price − $25 COGS). With a 5% monthly churn rate, the average subscriber lifespan is approximately 19 months (1 ÷ 0.05). This yields a current Lifetime Value (LTV) of roughly $665 per subscriber ($35 × 19), excluding acquisition costs.

**Option 1: Increase Price to $70**
*   **New Margin:** $45 ($70 − $25).
*   **Break-even Churn:** To maintain the same total monthly profit ($175,000), your churn could rise to ~6.4% before you are worse off than today.
*   **Risk:** If price sensitivity causes churn to exceed 6.4% (an increase of >1.4 percentage points), total profit declines. In high-inflation climates, essential goods (nutrition) often see lower elasticity than luxuries, but a 16.7% price hike is significant.

**Option 2: Reduce Dosage by 20% (Keep Price $60)**
*   **Assumed Cost Savings:** If ingredient cost scales linearly, COGS drops ~20% from $25 to $20.
*   **New Margin:** $40 ($60 − $20).
*   **Break-even Churn:** You can afford churn up to ~5.6% before falling below current profit levels.
*   **Risk:** "Shrinkflation" often triggers higher perceived betrayal than explicit price hikes, potentially spiking churn beyond 5.6% if customers notice the efficacy drop.

**Decision Aid:**
*   **Read:** Option 1 offers higher upside ($45 margin) but requires retaining more customers against a price shock. Option 2 offers a smaller margin buffer ($40) and risks brand trust if the dosage change is detected.
*   **Flip Input:** The deciding variable is **churn elasticity**. If you believe churn will stay under 6.4% with a price hike, take Option 1. If you believe dosage reduction will cause churn >5.6%, avoid Option 2.
*   **Threshold:** If LTV:CAC is currently <3x, prioritize margin preservation (Option 1) provided churn doesn't spike drastically.
*   **Next Step:** Run a "Van Westendorp" style survey or A/B test the $70 price on *new* signups only this week to gauge real-world resistance before applying it to the 5,000 existing subscribers.
