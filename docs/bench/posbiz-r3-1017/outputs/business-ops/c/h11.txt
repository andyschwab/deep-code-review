**Regime Identification:**
Your question regarding fee structures for low-balance accounts and the operation of a "robo-advisor" touches on **Securities Law** and **Investment Adviser regulations**.

**Routing Requirement:**
In the United States, offering automated investment advice generally requires registration as an **Investment Adviser** with either the SEC or state securities regulators, depending on assets under management (AUM). The structure of your fees (flat vs. percentage) and how they are disclosed falls under the **Investment Advisers Act of 1940**. Furthermore, charging fees on low-balance accounts may trigger fiduciary duty concerns regarding whether the fee is "reasonable" relative to the service provided.

**Action:**
