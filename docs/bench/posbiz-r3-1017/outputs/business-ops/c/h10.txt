**Decision Aid: Pricing Structure for Data Rights Transfer**

**One-line read:** At the client's current offer ($150/device), you realize an immediate $50 gain per unit but forfeit $200 in future value, resulting in a net loss of $150 per patient compared to your standard model; you must restructure the deal to capture a portion of that forfeited value upfront or via royalty to avoid subsidizing the client's research at your expense.

**The Arithmetic (Apply, Don't Direct)**
To determine the fair price, we must calculate the **Total Contract Value (TCV)** required to match your baseline economics, then compare it against the client's offer.

*   **Baseline Economics (Status Quo):**
    *   Upfront Device Revenue: $100
    *   Estimated Future Data Value: $200
    *   **Total Value Per Patient:** $300

*   **Client's Current Offer (Data Ownership Transferred):**
    *   Upfront Device Revenue: $150
    *   Future Data Value to You: $0 (Rights transferred)
    *   **Total Value Per Patient:** $150

*   **The Gap:**
    *   $300 (Baseline) − $150 (Offer) = **$150 shortfall per patient.**
    *   Across 500 patients, this deal leaves **$75,000** of potential value on the table.

**Sensitivity & Break-Even Analysis**
Since you need this contract to extend your runway (Burn: $75k/month), cash flow timing matters. You have two structural options to close the $150/patient gap:

1.  **Option A: Full Upfront Buyout (No Royalty)**
    *   *Formula:* `Required Price = Standard Device Price + Foregone Data Value`
    *   *Calculation:* $100 + $200 = **$300 per device.**
    *   *Trade-off:* Maximizes immediate cash to extend runway instantly. The client pays a premium to eliminate future administrative overhead and risk.
    *   *Flip Input:* If the client refuses $300, calculate their maximum willingness to pay. If they cap at $200, you are still accepting a $50/patient discount ($25k total) for the benefit of immediate liquidity.

2.  **Option B: Reduced Upfront + Data Royalty**
    *   *Formula:* `Royalty Rate = (Foregone Data Value) ÷ (Projected Client Revenue from Data)`
    *   *Application:* If you accept the $150 upfront, you are effectively lending them $150 of future value. You need a royalty clause that recoups this $150 plus a premium for risk.
    *   *Structure:* $150 upfront + $X per patient per year, or Y% of gross revenue generated from the aggregated dataset.
    *   *Trade-off:* Lower immediate cash (slower runway extension) but preserves long-term upside.
    *   *Flip Input:* If the client's projected revenue from your data is unknown, this model is **UNVERIFIED**. You must request their monetization projection to set a fair royalty percentage.

**Decision Thresholds (Rules of Thumb)**
*   **Liquidity Rule:** If your cash runway is < 2 months, prioritize **Option A** (maximize upfront cash) even if it means accepting a slight discount (e.g., $275 instead of $300) to close the deal quickly.
*   **Value Rule:** If runway is > 4 months, prioritize **Option B** to avoid permanently selling an asset worth $200 for only $50 extra today.
*   **Walk-Away Point:** If the client refuses to exceed $200 upfront *and* refuses any royalty, the deal destroys value ($100 loss/patient vs. baseline).
