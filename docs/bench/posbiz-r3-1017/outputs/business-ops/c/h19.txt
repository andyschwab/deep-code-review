#### 1. The Arithmetic Framework (Lane A)

**Formula:** `Annual Revenue = (Target Firms × Adoption Rate × Units per Firm × Price per Unit) + Recurring Services`

*   **Scenario A: Voluntary Adoption (Base Case)**

*   **Scenario B: Partial Mandate (Hybrid Case)**

    *   *Input:* `[Estimated %]` of firms falling into high-risk categories (e.g., 20% of the 5,000).
    *   *Driver:* Compliance necessity for specific job roles.
    *   *Calculation:* `(5,000 × [0.20]) × [Mandated Adoption %] × [Units/Firm] × [Price]`

*   **Scenario C: Full Mandate (Upside Case)**
