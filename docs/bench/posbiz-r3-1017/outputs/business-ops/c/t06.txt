**Lane R: Regulation-Dependent Assessment (Route to Counsel)**

Your request asks for a determination on whether your staking service constitutes an unregistered securities offering and requests specific structural changes to mitigate this risk. This falls strictly under **Lane R**. The classification of a crypto asset or staking service as a "security" depends on complex legal tests (such as the *Howey* test in the U.S. or similar frameworks in Singapore under the Securities and Futures Act) and specific facts regarding investor expectations, managerial effort, and profit derivation.

**Regime Identification:**
The primary regimes involved are:
1.  **U.S. Securities Law:** Specifically the Securities Act of 1933 and the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, enforced by the SEC. The SEC has previously indicated that staking services involving the pooling of assets and reliance on the promoter's efforts to generate yield may be deemed investment contracts.
2.  **Singapore Financial Regulation:** The Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) regulates digital payment tokens and may classify certain staking arrangements as capital markets products depending on their structure.

**Why This Cannot Be Answered with Arithmetic or General Advice:**
Determining if your specific implementation (pooling funds to validate transactions) triggers security classification is a legal conclusion, not a mathematical one. No amount of unit economics calculation can determine regulatory status. Providing a list of "operational differences" that guarantee safety or suggesting specific "structural changes" to avoid classification constitutes legal advice. An incorrect assessment here could lead to severe penalties, cease-and-desist orders, or criminal liability.
