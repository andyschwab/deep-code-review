### Scenario Analysis: Price Increase vs. Cost Absorption

**Current Baseline (Pre-Tax Change)**
*   **Cost per Unit:** $0.80
*   **Price per Unit:** $1.40
*   **Margin per Unit:** $0.60
*   **Volume Assumption:** To calculate specific revenue impacts, we must establish a baseline volume. Based on your stated burn rate increase of $8,000/month if you absorb half the cost ($0.06/unit), your current monthly volume is approximately **133,333 units** ($8,000 ÷ $0.06).
*   **Current Monthly Contribution Margin:** $80,000 (133,333 units × $0.60).

**Scenario A: Pass Full Cost Increase to Customers**
*   **New Cost per Unit:** $0.92
*   **New Price per Unit:** $1.52 (+$0.12)
*   **New Margin per Unit:** $0.60 (Unchanged)
*   **Volume Impact Assumption:** You noted a 4% churn on a 5-cent hike. A 12-cent hike is 2.4x larger. If churn scales linearly (a risky assumption, but necessary for this model), churn could reach ~9.6%. Let's model a conservative **8% volume drop**.
*   **Projected Volume:** 122,666 units (133,333 × 0.92)
*   **Projected Monthly Contribution:** $73,600 (122,666 × $0.60)
*   **Net Impact:** **-$6,400/month** vs. baseline.
*   **Risk:** High sensitivity among top 50 accounts; potential contract renegotiations.

**Scenario B: Absorb Half the Cost Increase**
*   **New Cost per Unit:** $0.92
*   **New Price per Unit:** $1.46 (+$0.06)
*   **New Margin per Unit:** $0.54
*   **Volume Impact Assumption:** Based on historical data, a 5-cent hike causes 4% churn. We apply this **4% volume drop**.
*   **Projected Volume:** 128,000 units (133,333 × 0.96)
*   **Projected Monthly Contribution:** $69,120 (128,000 × $0.54)
*   **Net Impact:** **-$10,880/month** vs. baseline.
*   **Cash Flow Note:** This aligns with your estimate of an $8,000–$11,000 monthly strain, shortening runway by the projected 3 months.

**Decision Aid**
*   **Read:** Passing the full cost preserves your margin percentage but risks an 8% volume loss, costing you ~$6.4k/month in total contribution. Absorbing half protects volume better (only 4% loss) but erodes margin, costing you ~$10.9k/month.
*   **The Flip Input:** The decision hinges on **price elasticity at the 12-cent mark**. If a 12-cent hike causes >13% churn, absorbing half becomes the mathematically superior option for total cash generation. If churn stays under 13%, passing the cost is less damaging to your bottom line.
*   **Threshold Rule:** If your runway is <6 months, prioritize cash preservation (Scenario A) unless you have written confirmation from your top 50 accounts that they will accept the hike. If runway is >12 months, prioritize retention (Scenario B) to secure long-term LTV.
*   **Next Step:** Before announcing, survey your top 50 accounts with a "good-better-best" tier proposal: offer a locked-in rate for 12 months at the higher price in exchange for a volume commitment, testing their willingness to pay without triggering immediate churn.

*Note: Volume figures above are derived from your provided burn-rate data; actual churn at a 12-cent increase is unverified and may be non-linear.*
