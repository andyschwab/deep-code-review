**3. Cash Runway Sensitivity**
You stated a 4-month runway. A write-down is a non-cash expense; it reduces reported profit and asset value but **does not** immediately reduce cash. However, it signals that the expected cash inflow from this inventory is lower than planned.
*   *Formula:* $\text{Adjusted Runway} = \frac{\text{Current Cash}}{\text{Monthly Burn} - \text{Recovered Inventory Cash Flow}}$
*   *Decision Aid:* If you assumed €840,000 in future revenue from this stock to fund operations, and you now assume €0 (Scenario A), your effective runway may be significantly shorter than 4 months unless costs are cut or new revenue found immediately.

**Decision Threshold:** If the probability of selling the 350 liters at full price is <50%, conservative arithmetic suggests modeling your runway assuming a 100% loss of this asset value to avoid liquidity surprises.
