**Net Revenue Model & Break-Even Analysis**

**Current State (15% Denial Rate)**
*   **Gross Volume:** 12,000 visits/quarter (4,000/month).
*   **Paid Visits:** 4,000 × (1 - 0.15) = 3,400 visits.
*   **Net Revenue:** 3,400 × $85 = **$289,000/month**.
*   **Total Costs:** (4,000 visits × $30) + $140,000 fixed burn = **$260,000/month**.
*   **Net Cash Flow:** $289,000 - $260,000 = **+$29,000/month** (Positive).

**Scenario A: Denials Stabilize at 15%**
You remain cash-flow positive by ~$29k/month. At this rate, your current runway extends beyond the stated six months, assuming no other cost increases.

**Scenario B: Denials Creep to 20%**
*   **Paid Visits:** 4,000 × (1 - 0.20) = 3,200 visits.
*   **Net Revenue:** 3,200 × $85 = **$272,000/month**.
*   **Total Costs:** $260,000 (unchanged volume/cost structure).
*   **Net Cash Flow:** $272,000 - $260,000 = **+$12,000/month**.
*   **Result:** You remain profitable, but monthly surplus drops by ~59%.

**Volume Required to Maintain Current Cash Flow ($29k surplus) at 20% Denials**
To regain the $29,000 monthly surplus with a 20% denial rate, we solve for the new visit volume ($V$):
*   *Formula:* `(V × (1 - DenialRate) × Price) - (V × VarCost) - FixedBurn = TargetSurplus`
*   *Inputs:* `(V × 0.80 × $85) - (V × $30) - $140,000 = $29,000`
*   *Simplify:* `($68V - $30V) = $169,000` → `$38V = $169,000`
*   *Required Volume:* **4,448 visits/month**.
*   *Additional Visits Needed:* 4,448 - 4,000 = **448 additional visits/month** (an ~11.2% increase).

**Decision Aid**
At 20% denials, you do not break even; you simply earn less. You only need to scale volume if your goal is to restore the *surplus* cash flow, not to survive. If volume cannot increase by ~450 visits/month, the 20% scenario reduces your cash buffer but does not immediately threaten solvency based on current figures.

**Data Points to Gather from Payer Contracts**
To forecast losses accurately, retrieve these specific clauses:
1.  **Timely Filing Limits:** The exact window (e.g., 90 vs. 180 days) to submit claims before automatic denial.
2.  **Appeal Windows & Requirements:** The deadline and specific documentation needed to overturn a denial (critical for recovering the 15-20% loss).
3.  **Fee Schedule Adjustments:** Whether denials trigger downstream rate penalties or audit clauses.
4.  **Clean Claim Definitions:** The specific technical or coding criteria required to avoid "technical" denials versus clinical denials.

This is educational information, not legal, tax, accounting, financial, or investment advice.
