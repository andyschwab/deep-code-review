**Net Revenue Model & Break-Even Analysis**

**Current State (15% Denial Rate)**
*   **Gross Revenue:** 12,000 visits × $85 = $1,020,000 (Quarterly)
*   **Net Revenue (85% collection):** $1,020,000 × 0.85 = $867,000
*   **Total Costs:** 12,000 visits × $30 = $360,000 (Costs incur on all visits, denied or not)
*   **Net Profit (Quarterly):** $867,000 - $360,000 = **$507,000**
*   **Monthly Net Profit:** ~$169,000
*   **Implied Monthly Burn Coverage:** Your stated burn is $140,000. At 15% denial, you are currently covering burn with ~$29,000 monthly surplus.

**Stress Scenario (20% Denial Rate)**
*   **Gross Revenue (same volume):** $1,020,000
*   **Net Revenue (80% collection):** $1,020,000 × 0.80 = $816,000
*   **Total Costs:** $360,000 (Fixed per visit)
*   **Net Profit (Quarterly):** $816,000 - $360,000 = **$456,000**
*   **Monthly Net Profit:** ~$152,000
*   **Result:** Still covers the $140,000 burn, but surplus drops to ~$12,000/month. Runway sensitivity increases significantly.

**Volume Required to Maintain Current Cash Flow at 20% Denial**
To maintain the current quarterly net profit of $507,000 with a 20% denial rate:
*   **Formula:** $Target Profit = (Visits × Price × (1 - Denial Rate)) - (Visits × Cost per Visit)$
*   **Rearranged for Visits:** $Visits = Target Profit / [(Price × (1 - Denial)) - Cost]$
*   **Inputs:** $507,000 / [($85 × 0.80) - $30]$
*   **Calculation:** $507,000 / ($68 - $30) = $507,000 / $38 ≈ **13,342 visits/quarter**
*   **Additional Volume Needed:** 13,342 - 12,000 = **1,342 additional visits per quarter** (approx. **447 additional visits per month**).

**Decision Aid**
At a 20% denial rate, your margin per successful visit effectively drops, requiring a **11.2% increase in volume** to maintain current profitability. If acquiring 447 extra monthly visits is not feasible within your current capacity, the denial rate becomes a critical leverage point rather than a volume problem.

**Data Points to Gather from Payer Contracts**
To forecast losses accurately, retrieve the following specific clauses:
1.  **Appeal Windows & Success Rates:** The specific timeframe to contest denials and historical overturn rates by payer.
2.  **Denial Reason Codes:** Breakdown of denials (e.g., eligibility vs. coding vs. authorization). Some are fixable operationally; others are contract disputes.
3.  **Fee Schedule Variance:** Confirm if the $85 is a flat fee or if specific CPT codes have different allowable amounts that might be triggering denials.
4.  **Timely Filing Limits:** Ensure denials aren't due to internal submission delays which contractually bar re-submission.

This is educational information, not legal, tax, accounting, financial, or investment advice.
