### Revenue Model Comparison & Break-Even Analysis

**Assumptions & Inputs**
*   **Hardware Margin:** $1,200 (Price) - $700 (Cost) = $500 one-time gross profit per unit.
*   **Studio Baseline Capacity:** 5 headsets × 40 classes/week × 8 attendees × $25/class = $40,000/week potential gross revenue.
*   **Monthly Multiplier:** ~4.33 weeks/month.
*   **Utilization Rates:** Applied to *attendees* (revenue driver).
    *   70% Utilization: 5.6 avg attendees/class.
    *   90% Utilization: 7.2 avg attendees/class.

**Model A: Flat Licensing Fee ($150/headset/month)**
*   Revenue per studio (5 headsets): $150 × 5 = **$750/month**.
*   *Note:* This is fixed regardless of utilization or studio growth.

**Model B: 12% Revenue Share**
*   **At 70% Utilization:**
    *   Weekly Studio Revenue: 5 headsets × 40 classes × 5.6 attendees × $25 = $28,000.
    *   Monthly Studio Revenue: $28,000 × 4.33 ≈ $121,240.
    *   Your Share (12%): **$14,549/month**.
*   **At 90% Utilization:**
    *   Weekly Studio Revenue: 5 headsets × 40 classes × 7.2 attendees × $25 = $36,000.
    *   Monthly Studio Revenue: $36,000 × 4.33 ≈ $155,880.
    *   Your Share (12%): **$18,706/month**.

**Comparison Summary**
Under both 70% and 90% utilization scenarios, the **Revenue Share model significantly outperforms the Flat Fee** ($14.5k–$18.7k vs. $0.75k). The flat fee would only be superior if the studio's total monthly class revenue fell below $6,250 (approx. 14% utilization), which contradicts your "typical studio" profile.

**Break-Even Utilization Rate**
To find where Revenue Share > Flat Fee ($750):
1.  Required Studio Monthly Revenue = $750 / 0.12 = $6,250.
2.  Required Weekly Revenue = $6,250 / 4.33 ≈ $1,443.
3.  Required Attendees per Class = $1,443 / (5 headsets × 40 classes × $25) ≈ 0.29 attendees.
4.  Utilization % = 0.29 / 8 (max capacity) ≈ **3.6%**.

*Decision Aid:* The revenue share model becomes more profitable than the flat fee at merely **3.6% utilization**. Given your pilot data suggests 70%+, the revenue share is mathematically superior unless you anticipate massive studio churn or revenue collapse. The "predictable cash flow" of the flat fee comes at a massive opportunity cost (~$14k/month per studio).

**Data Request for Validation**
To validate these projections with pilot studios, request:
1.  **Weekly Attendance Variance:** Standard deviation of attendees per class over the last 6 months (to model downside risk).
2.  **Cannibalization Rate:** Percentage of software-booked classes that would have occurred anyway without the VR integration (to isolate *incremental* revenue if your contract ties share only to new bookings).
3.  **Churn Correlation:** Does higher class volume correlate with higher headset maintenance costs or downtime?

**Operational Context**
With $60,000 in monthly OpEx, you need roughly **4 studios** on the Revenue Share model (at 70% utilization) to break even, versus **80 studios** on the Flat Fee model.
