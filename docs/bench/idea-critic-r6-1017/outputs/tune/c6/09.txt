**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **capped pilot** launching 1 March 2025 limited to **500 subscribers** (not 8,000) for 90 days, with a strict **go/no-go checkpoint on 1 June 2025**. Do not commit the full $650,000 upfront; spend only what is required to service the pilot cohort.

**Why this plan could fail:**
1.  **Revenue Math Error:** Your projection claims $2M revenue, but 8,000 subscribers × $25/week × 52 weeks equals **$10.4M**, not $2M. If you meant $2M annualized, you are actually projecting only ~1,540 subscribers, not 8,000. This fundamental discrepancy invalidates the current financial model.
2.  **Margin Collapse:** You assumed a 20% gross margin ($5.00/box) after a $0.30 packaging cost, but you excluded the **$0.08/mile fuel surcharge** for refrigerated trucks. For average delivery distances, this variable cost could erase 10–15% of your margin, potentially making every box sold loss-making before fixed costs.
3.  **Unverified Upgrade Rate:** The plan hinges on converting existing customers, yet you have **zero data** on how many of the 22,000 current users will upgrade. If the conversion rate is below 2% (requiring 400 upgrades to hit even the lower subscriber target), the $500k influencer spend is inefficient overkill.
4.  **Retention Assumption:** The belief that a 15-minute delivery reduction boosts retention is unproven. If churn exceeds your 5% monthly assumption (e.g., hits 8–10% as often seen in new food verticals), the lifetime value turns negative immediately.

**Kill Criteria (Stop/Pivot Triggers):**
At the 1 June 2025 review, **terminate or pivot** the initiative if:
*   **Churn exceeds 6.5% per month** (sustained over the 90-day pilot).
*   **Realized gross margin drops below 12%** after accounting for actual fuel surcharges and packaging.
*   **Conversion from existing base is under 1.5%** despite targeted outreach.
*   **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** for new subscribers exceeds **$150** (implying payback >6 months at your margin).

*Note: The revenue calculation error must be corrected before presenting to the board, as it suggests either a 6x overstatement of subscribers or a 5x understatement of revenue.*
