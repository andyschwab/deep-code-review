**Recommendation: Proceed with a capped pilot, not a full launch.**
Do not commit to the full $195k budget or the $675k ARR projection yet. Instead, authorize a "Go-If" pilot targeting **15 solo practitioners (10% of the market)** by March 2024. This limits upfront exposure to ~$20k while validating the critical 30% conversion assumption and the support capacity constraint before scaling.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Conversion Overestimation:** The 30% conversion rate is unverified against a paid wall; if actual conversion is <10%, ARR drops to $225k, failing to cover the $195k initial outlay and operational costs within the first year.
2.  **Support Capacity Collapse:** The assumption that current staff can absorb a 50% ticket increase without new hires is a single point of failure; if premium users demand high-touch support, service levels for all users will degrade, causing churn.
3.  **Usage Mismatch:** The "3 hours per month" usage model may be incorrect; if solo lawyers use the tool less frequently, the perceived value of a $15k annual fee diminishes, leading to immediate cancellation after the trial.

**Kill Criteria:**
Terminate the project if, by **June 30, 2024**, the pilot cohort fails to achieve either:
*   A **15% paid conversion rate** from the trial group, OR
*   A **Net Revenue Retention (NRR) of >80%** after the first billing cycle.

*Note: Recalculate the ROI model using a conservative 10% conversion rate before presenting to Maya to stress-test the break-even point. Verify current support ticket volume trends immediately to confirm the 50% surge capacity claim.*
