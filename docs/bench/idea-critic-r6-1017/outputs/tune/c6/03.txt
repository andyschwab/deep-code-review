**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" Pilot, Not a Full Launch Commitment**

Approve the $70,000 pilot starting June 1, 2025, but withhold the remaining $15,000 contingency and the commitment to the full $85,000 annual spend until specific adoption thresholds are met. The proposal currently assumes a 30% adoption rate (3,600 patrons) without accounting for the unpriced "Chronicles of Alder" licensing, which could erode the budget cushion before the pilot begins. By structuring this as a conditional pilot, you protect the library from sinking costs into a platform that fails to secure key content or attract sufficient users.

**Primary Failure Modes & Hidden Risks**

1.  **The "Content Gap" Failure:** If the "Chronicles of Alder" licensing fees exceed the $15,000 contingency, the pilot may launch without the very titles driving the projected 15% lift in checkouts. Without these popular titles, the 30% adoption assumption becomes highly optimistic, potentially resulting in a "ghost town" platform that damages patron trust in future digital initiatives.
2.  **Adoption Overestimation:** A 30% uptake (3,600 users) in year one is aggressive for a new interface. If actual adoption lands at 15% (1,800 users), the cost-per-active-user doubles, making the platform inefficient compared to simply purchasing more titles for the existing system.
3.  **Timeline Compression:** A three-month window (June–September) coincides with summer reading programs. If technical onboarding or marketing is delayed by even two weeks due to licensing negotiations, the pilot data will be skewed by the reduced runtime, leading to inconclusive results by the September 1 review date.

**Pre-Agreed Kill Criteria**

Terminate the project and do not convert the pilot to a permanent annual contract if **either** of the following conditions is met by August 15, 2025:
*   **Adoption Floor:** Fewer than 1,200 unique patrons (10% of registered base) have actively borrowed at least one title from the new platform.
*   **Cost Ceiling:** The final licensing agreement for "Chronicles of Alder" requires an additional expenditure exceeding $12,000, leaving less than $3,000 for actual marketing or operational contingencies within the pilot budget.

**Leading Metric to Watch:** Weekly active borrowers (not just registrations) during the first four weeks of the pilot. If this number does not trend toward 300 weekly users by July 1, the 30% annual goal is mathematically unreachable.

*Note: Ensure the licensing negotiation for "Chronicles of Alder" is completed before June 1 to avoid mid-pilot budget shocks. The $15,000 cushion should be treated as unavailable until the contract is signed.*
