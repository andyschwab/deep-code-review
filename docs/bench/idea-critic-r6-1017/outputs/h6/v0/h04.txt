**Main Failure Modes & Hidden Risks:**

3.  **Conversion Rate Delusion:** A 15% conversion rate is exceptionally high for cold traffic (industry average is often 1–3%). If your beta data came from warm leads, applying this to a $150k cold ad spend will result in a catastrophic burn rate with minimal sales.
