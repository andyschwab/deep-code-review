**Recommendation: GO-IF**
Proceed with the launch only if you secure a binding carrier rate card confirming shipping costs remain under $4.50 per unit before accepting pre-orders. Without this fixed variable cost, your margin model is speculative and risks negative unit economics at scale.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**
1.  **Margin Collapse from Shipping Variance:** Your current model assumes $4.50 total variable cost per unit ($3 books + $1.50 ship). If mixed-size parcels trigger dimensional weight pricing or zone surges averaging $6.00 instead of $1.50, your contribution margin drops from $10.50 to $9.00. At 800 members, this is a $1,200/month profit erosion that compounds if churn is higher than expected.
2.  **Churn Underestimation:** Assuming churn stabilizes at 5% monthly after a 30% initial drop is optimistic for physical subscription boxes, which often see "one-and-done" behavior. If churn stays at 15% monthly, you must acquire 120 new members just to maintain an 800-member base, likely exceeding your marketing budget and CAC limits.
3.  **Author Chat Scalability vs. Value:** A single monthly virtual chat for 800 members offers low perceived individual value, failing to justify the $15 price point against digital-only competitors. If attendance is low, the feature becomes a cost center without retention power; if high, technical or moderation costs may spike unbudgeted.

**Pre-Agreed Stop/Pivot Trigger**
Stop acquisition and pivot to a digital-only or local-pickup model if, by the end of Month 2, the blended cost per shipped box exceeds $5.00 OR if net member growth (new joins minus churn) is negative for two consecutive months.

**Arithmetic & Logic Notes**
*   **Revenue Math:** At 500 members, revenue is $7,500/mo. Variable costs are $2,250 (books) + $750 (ship) = $3,000. Gross profit is $4,500 before overhead. This looks healthy *only* if shipping holds at $1.50.
*   **Churn Logic Error:** You stated "30% churn rate will stay under 5% monthly." A 30% *total* early churn is distinct from a 5% *monthly* recurring churn. Clarify if you mean 30% of the *initial* cohort leaves in month 1, then 5% of the *remaining* leave monthly. If you meant 30% monthly churn continuing, the business fails immediately.
*   **Missing Cost:** No budget is allocated for payment processing fees (~2.9% + $0.30 per transaction), which would deduct ~$750/month at 500 members, reducing your safety margin.
