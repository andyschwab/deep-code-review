**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **controlled pilot** (go-if), not a full immediate shift. Launch the revenue-share model with only 20% of new clients for one quarter while maintaining the flat fee for the rest. This validates the 90% acceptance assumption and the $1,200 onboarding cost impact before exposing the entire $6.48M revenue base to competitor undercutting.

**Top Three Failure Modes:**
1.  **Acceptance Rate Miscalculation:** If client acceptance is 70% instead of 90%, the projected $7.0M revenue collapses to ~$6.6M, which fails to cover the additional $96,000 annual marketing spend and $648,000 in new onboarding costs, resulting in a net loss compared to status quo.
2.  **Competitor Price War:** Competitors charging $10,000 flat fees may aggressively target your price-sensitive clients during the transition; if they capture more than 15% of your pipeline, the volume drop will negate the higher per-placement margin.
3.  **Salary Verification Risk:** The model relies on an $85,000 average salary; if the actual average placed salary is $75,000, the 18% share yields only $13,500, eroding the margin buffer needed to absorb the new $1,200 onboarding cost and increased marketing spend.

**Kill Criteria:** Terminate the expansion and revert to the flat-fee model if, after 90 days, the **blended revenue per placement drops below $12,500** or **client acceptance of the new terms remains below 80%**.
