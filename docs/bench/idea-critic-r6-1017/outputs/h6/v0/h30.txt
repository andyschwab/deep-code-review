**Primary Failure Modes**

Archive data often contains sensitive personal information about living individuals from decades past; unlocking this without a legal review is a high-severity trigger for litigation.
3.  **Capacity Bottleneck:** Expecting three developers to handle integration, microfilm digitization oversight, *and* new compliance infrastructure without external vendors will likely delay launch by 6+ months, burning cash before revenue starts.
