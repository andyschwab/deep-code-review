**Recommendation: Do not proceed with a full replacement of physical classrooms.** Instead, run a controlled pilot converting only 20% of capacity to the app model while maintaining core in-person sites.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Revenue Collapse from Churn:** Your model assumes 80% retention and 90% completion rates identical to in-person learning. Self-paced mobile apps historically suffer significantly higher churn and lower completion than instructor-led formats. If retention drops to 60% (a common industry variance for unguided digital learning), you lose $840k in existing revenue, instantly wiping out the projected $420k gain and creating a net loss despite cost savings.
2.  **Undercapitalized Launch:** Your initial math excluded the $200k marketing spend from the total upfront investment. The true Year 0 cash requirement is $1.0M ($800k dev + $200k marketing), not $900k. Furthermore, the $120k annual maintenance is an operating cost that persists even if enrollment targets are missed, eroding margins faster than anticipated.
3.  **Irreversible Infrastructure Loss:** Replacing all 12 sites eliminates your physical asset base before validating the digital product. If the app fails to engage users, you cannot instantly reopen closed locations, leaving you with no delivery mechanism and stranded students.

**Kill Criterion:**
Terminate the project and revert to the legacy model if, after 12 months of full operation, **net new revenue (total revenue minus total operating costs) is less than $150,000 above the current baseline.** This specific financial tripwire ensures the risk of digital transformation is justified by actual profit growth, not just cost cutting or vanity metrics.
