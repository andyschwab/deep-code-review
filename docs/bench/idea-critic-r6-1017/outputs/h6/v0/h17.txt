**Main Failure Modes:**
1.  **Capacity Collapse:** Your plan assumes session length and therapist capacity remain static. If virtual visits require more administrative time or technical troubleshooting per hour than in-person visits, your therapists will see fewer patients, reducing total revenue despite the new tier.
2.  **Revenue Cannibalization:** If the 18% switching rate is accurate but the $98 virtual rate replaces higher-margin in-person visits without reducing overhead costs proportionally, your net margin per patient drops, extending the break-even point beyond viability.
