Do not authorize the full fleet replacement yet. Proceed only after obtaining fixed-price quotes for the 240V site upgrades and validating the first-year energy consumption on a pilot of two units. The current proposal lacks the critical installation cost data required to calculate true ROI, and the five-year battery life assumption carries unquantified risk beyond year three.

**Top Three Failure Modes**
1.  **Hidden Infrastructure Costs:** Site electrical upgrades (transformers, trenching, panel swaps) could exceed the hardware budget, destroying the payback period.
2.  **Battery Degradation:** If capacity drops significantly after year three, replacement costs (~$3,000/unit) would erase accumulated operational savings.
3.  **Utility Rate Volatility:** Commercial electricity rates are not fixed; a rise to $0.25/kWh would halve the projected annual savings, extending the break-even point beyond the battery lifespan.

**Kill Criteria (Stop/Change Triggers)**
*   **Installation Cost:** If site upgrade quotes exceed **$15,000 total** ($1,250 per mower equivalent), halt the project.

*   **Battery Health:** If pilot units show **>15% capacity loss** before 1,500 operating hours (approx. 9 months), terminate the contract and revert to gas.
