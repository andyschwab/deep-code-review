**Recommendation: Proceed with a conditional "Go" (Go-If)**
Launch the pilot on 1 June 2025 only if a binding insurance quote and a confirmed customs pathway for battery packs are secured by 15 April 2025. Without these, the unpriced risk of regulatory seizure or liability gaps invalidates the $2.8M investment.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**
1.  **Regulatory & Logistics Halt:** Customs delays or bans on lithium battery imports could ground the fleet indefinitely, turning fixed drone costs into sunk losses with zero operational utility.
2.  **Weather-Induced Capacity Collapse:** If actual flyable days drop below the assumed 80% due to coastal weather patterns, sortie frequency will fall short, failing to meet the $350k savings target while fixed maintenance costs remain.
3.  **Unit Economics Inversion:** At $15k/month per drone ($1.44M/year total), maintenance alone consumes 51% of the total budget. If payload efficiency drops or sortie counts rise to meet demand, operating costs could easily exceed the $350k savings, resulting in a net financial loss.

**Kill Criteria (Stop/Re-evaluate Triggers)**
*   **Financial:** If the fully loaded monthly operating cost (maintenance + insurance + amortized integration) exceeds **$235,000** after three months of operation.
*   **Operational:** If weather or regulatory constraints prevent more than **70%** of planned sorties in any rolling 30-day window.
*   **Strategic:** If customs clearance for battery replacements exceeds **14 days** at any point, triggering an immediate pause to reassess supply chain viability.

*Note: The current budget excludes insurance, which is critical for aviation liability; this must be quantified before final approval. The maintenance cost assumption ($15k/drone) is exceptionally high relative to the savings goal and requires immediate vendor validation.*
