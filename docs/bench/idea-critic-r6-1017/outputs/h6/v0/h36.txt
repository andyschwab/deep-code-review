**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" conditional approval, limited to a 6-month pilot in two diverse districts before full rollout.**

The proposal's core logic holds only if the net benefit remains positive after accounting for unmodeled labor costs and variable pipe efficacy. Currently, the projected $1.4M annual saving is threatened by the omitted $150k labor cost and the risk that older pipes yield lower leak-detection efficacy than the 8% average. A full immediate rollout exposes the Authority to a potential negative ROI if false positives trigger excessive truck rolls or if the 8% reduction fails to materialize in aging infrastructure zones. By restricting the initial phase to a pilot, you validate the false-positive rate and labor impact with real data before committing the remaining capital.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Labor Cost Overrun:** If the false-positive rate exceeds 4%, the cost of dispatching field crews ($150k baseline) could escalate rapidly, eroding the $1.4M savings and potentially turning the project net-negative.
2.  **Infrastructure Variance:** The assumed 8% water loss reduction may not apply uniformly; districts with extremely old or complex piping might see significantly lower detection rates, failing to meet the revenue avoidance targets required to service the $300k annual data fee.
3.  **Adoption Shortfall:** The financial model relies on 5% adoption of the premium alert package. If customer uptake is lower, the direct revenue or specific savings attributed to this tier will not offset the fixed data-service fees.

**Pre-Agreed Kill/Pivot Criteria:**
Execute a formal review after 6 months of the pilot. **Kill or pivot the project if:**
*   The verified false-positive alert rate exceeds **3.5%** (resulting in unsustainable dispatch costs).
*   The realized water loss reduction in the pilot districts averages less than **5%** (indicating the 8% target is unachievable across the broader network).
*   The calculated Return on Investment (ROI) for the pilot period falls below **2%** annualized.

*Note: Ensure the pilot districts selected represent the oldest and newest pipe infrastructure to properly stress-test the efficacy variance assumption. The $150k labor estimate must be treated as a variable floor, not a fixed cap, in the final board presentation.*
