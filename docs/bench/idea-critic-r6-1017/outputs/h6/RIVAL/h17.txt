**Recommendation: Proceed with a Phased Pilot, Not a Full Launch**

Do not commit the full $260,000 for a July 1st full-scale launch yet. Instead, authorize a **$65,000 limited pilot** starting July 1st targeting only 500 existing patients and 50 new leads. Your plan relies on two unproven assumptions: that 18% of existing patients will voluntarily switch to a lower-revenue channel ($98 vs. $115), and that therapist capacity remains static despite the overhead of managing hybrid schedules. A full rollout exposes Stonebridge to a potential revenue dip of ~$60,000/month if conversion exceeds expectations without corresponding efficiency gains. Validate the conversion rate and operational friction with real data before scaling the technology spend.

**Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **Revenue Cannibalization:** If >25% of high-value in-person patients switch to the lower-cost virtual tier without a net increase in total volume, monthly revenue will drop despite higher appointment counts.
2.  **Capacity Illusion:** Virtual visits often require different administrative overhead (tech support, digital intake). If therapists spend >10% of their day troubleshooting rather than consulting, the "no capacity increase" assumption fails, forcing costly hiring or causing burnout.
3.  **Churn Spike:** If new remote patients churn at >8% monthly (vs. your implicit lower assumption), the marketing spend to replace them will erase the margin on the $98 fee.

**Kill Trigger**
Terminate the project immediately if, after 90 days of the pilot:
*   **Net Revenue per Therapist Hour** drops by more than 12% compared to the current in-person baseline, OR
*   **Virtual Patient Churn** exceeds 10% in any single month.

These metrics directly protect the clinic's core margin and operational stability.
