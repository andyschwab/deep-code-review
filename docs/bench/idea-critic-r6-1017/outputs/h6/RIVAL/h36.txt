**Recommendation: Proceed with a Phased Pilot, Not Full Rollout**

Do not authorize the full $2.5M rollout on 1 Jan 2027. Instead, approve an immediate **Phase 1 Pilot** covering 5,000 meters across diverse pipe-age districts for six months. The current proposal relies on unverified assumptions regarding false-positive rates and labor costs that could erase the projected $1.1M net annual benefit ($1.4M savings minus $300k fees). A full-scale deployment without validating these variables exposes the Authority to significant operational risk and potential budget overruns. Use the pilot to establish a definitive false-positive baseline and measure actual field crew response times before committing remaining capital.

**Primary Failure Modes**

1.  **Labor Cost Explosion:** If the false-positive rate hits the vendor's upper bound (6%) rather than the optimistic lower bound (2%), field crew costs could triple from the estimated $150k to over $450k annually, turning a profit center into a net loss.
2.  **Infrastructure Mismatch:** The assumed 8% water loss reduction may not materialize in districts with aging infrastructure where leaks are already catastrophic and visible, rendering the sensors redundant in those zones while missing subtle leaks in newer pipes.
3.  **Adoption Shortfall:** The financial model hinges on 5% premium adoption. If customer uptake is below 3%, revenue will fail to offset the recurring $300k data-service fee, creating a structural deficit.

**Pre-Agreed Kill/Pivot Conditions**

Establish these specific triggers during the Phase 1 Pilot to automatically halt or restructure the project:

*   **False-Positive Trigger:** If verified false-positive alerts exceed **3.5%** over a 90-day period, **PAUSE** expansion. Require the vendor to recalibrate sensitivity thresholds at no cost before proceeding.
*   **ROI Trigger:** If the pilot data projects an annualized Return on Investment below **4%** (accounting for actual labor costs and adoption rates), **KILL** the project entirely.
*   **Operational Burden Trigger:** If average field crew response time per alert exceeds **45 minutes** due to volume, **PIVOT** to a "Customer-Only" alert model (no automatic dispatch) until automation improves.

Presenting this cautious, data-driven approach to COO Patel demonstrates fiscal responsibility and operational prudence, securing the project's long-term viability rather than risking a costly failure.
