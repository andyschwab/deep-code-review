**Recommendation: Do not launch yet.** The current plan is financially insolvent due to a critical arithmetic error in your revenue projection. You are projecting $9,000 in revenue for Q3, but 200 members paying $45/month for three months (June, July, August) generates **$27,000** in gross revenue. Even if you meant 200 total sign-ups accumulating over the quarter, the average active membership would be ~100, yielding ~$13,500. Your current math implies an average revenue of only $15/member/month, which contradicts your $45 price point. Fix this model immediately; you cannot determine profitability with a 300% variance in top-line revenue.

**Three Biggest Failure Points:**
1.  **The "Free Shipping" Trap:** You have not priced packaging and shipping. If these costs exceed $15 per box (likely for live plants requiring protective materials), your margin evaporates. At 200 members, a $5 underestimate per box costs you $1,000/month, turning your projected $1,000 quarterly profit into a $2,000 loss before accounting for plant COGS.
2.  **Conversion Delusion:** You assume a 30% sign-up rate from existing customers (1,500 members) but are capping the pilot at 200. If you only need 200 members, you only need a **4% conversion rate**, not 30%. Relying on a 30% conversion assumption suggests you haven't stress-tested the marketing funnel; if you *do* hit 30%, you will overwhelm your 1,200 sq ft greenhouse capacity and logistics immediately, causing service failure.
3.  **Churn vs. Acquisition Cost:** You budgeted $8,000 to acquire 200 members ($40/customer acquisition cost). With a $45 monthly fee and 15% churn, the average customer lifetime is roughly 6.6 months, yielding ~$297 in lifetime revenue. If your COGS (plant + pot + soil + box + ship) is $25/month, your lifetime profit is only ~$132 per customer. You are spending $40 to make $132 over six months, leaving almost no buffer for operational overhead or the "unknown" shipping costs.

**Pre-agreed Halt Trigger:**
Halt the program immediately if the **fully loaded cost per box (plant + packaging + shipping) exceeds $18.00** after the first 50 pilot shipments. This threshold ensures you maintain a minimum 60% gross margin to cover the high fixed costs of the ad spend and greenhouse overhead.
