**Recommendation: Proceed with Conditional Approval**
Approve the $2.5M rollout only if the City Council provides written confirmation that the insurance premium will not exceed $0.15 per ride and the potential traffic surcharge is capped or deferred for 12 months. Without these written guarantees, the unit economics are too fragile to support the projected margins.

**Top Three Failure Modes**
1.  **Regulatory Cost Shock:** If the municipal traffic policy passes with the full $0.05 surcharge plus the unpriced insurance hike, your margin per ride collapses from ~$1.85 to under $1.00, turning projected profits into immediate operating losses.
2.  **Ridership Overestimation:** Achieving 10,000 rides/month/city in Q1 assumes instant market penetration. If adoption ramps slowly (e.g., 4,000 rides), fixed costs (staffing/permits) will consume cash reserves 2.5x faster than modeled.
3.  **Maintenance Underestimation:** At $150/bike/month, you are assuming near-zero damage/theft. If real-world wear matches industry averages ($200+), the $800k bike capital will require premature reinvestment, starving operations of working capital.

*   **Net Operating Loss > $100,000** for the quarter.
