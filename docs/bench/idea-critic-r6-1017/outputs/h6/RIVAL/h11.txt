**Subject:** Recommendation on VoltTrim Electric Fleet Proposal

**Recommendation: DELAY**
Do not authorize the $136,800 hardware spend yet. While the projected operational savings of $102,240 annually are compelling, the proposal currently lacks a Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) validation. We cannot calculate the true ROI without the installation costs for the 240V upgrades, and the five-year battery life assumption carries unquantified risk beyond year three. Proceeding now risks capital allocation based on incomplete data.

**Top Three Failure Modes**
1.  **Hidden Infrastructure Costs:** If site electrical upgrades exceed ~$45,000 total, the payback period extends beyond two years, significantly reducing the project's net present value.
2.  **Premature Battery Degradation:** If battery capacity drops below 80% before year three (as no data exists for years 4–5), we face unplanned capital outlays for replacements that could erase accumulated fuel savings.
3.  **Utility Rate Volatility:** The model assumes a static $0.12/kWh rate. If commercial electricity rates rise to $0.20/kWh, annual operating costs per mower nearly double to $768, cutting projected annual fleet savings by roughly 15%.

**Stop/Change Triggers**
Immediately halt deployment or renegotiate the warranty if either of the following occurs:
*   **Cost Trigger:** The formal quote for 240V site installations exceeds $3,500 per station ($10,500 total).
*   **Performance Trigger:** Pilot data or vendor references confirm battery replacement is required prior to 36 months of operation.
