**Recommendation: Proceed with a Phased Pilot, Not Full Rollout**

Do not commit the full network yet. Authorize a **single-school pilot** immediately. While the theoretical five-year savings are strong (~$1.3M), the risk of implementation failure and hidden training costs is too high to bet all six schools on unproven assumptions. A pilot allows you to validate the "15% efficiency gain" and actual training duration before exposing the entire $700,000 upfront capital outlay. If the pilot confirms the vendor's estimates, trigger the full rollout in Q3.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**

1.  **Hidden Implementation & Training Costs:** The vendor's "three-week" estimate is optimistic. If staff adoption takes six weeks due to complex legacy data migration or resistance, your $108,000 labor savings could vanish or turn into a net loss due to overtime and temporary staffing needs during the transition.
2.  **HRIS Integration Breakdown:** You are assuming zero integration cost. If the new platform requires custom API work or manual data bridging with your existing HRIS, the $200,000 implementation fee will balloon, destroying the Year 1 ROI case.
3.  **Operational Disruption During Pay Cycles:** Payroll errors are intolerable. If the centralization causes even one delayed or incorrect pay run across the network during the switchover, the reputational damage with staff and the cost of emergency remediation will outweigh any efficiency gains.

**Specific Kill Condition Trigger**

Stop or pivot the project immediately if the **First-Year Net Cost exceeds $300,000.**

*Calculation for monitoring:*
(Current Vendor Spend $720k + New Platform $500k + Implementation $200k + Maintenance $80k) − (Labor Savings $108k) = **Total Year 1 Cost.**
If this final number is greater than $1,020,000 (which represents a net increase of >$300k over your current $720k baseline), terminate the full rollout and revert to regional vendors.

**Note:** Ensure the pilot contract includes a clause allowing exit without penalty if integration costs exceed $25,000.
**Note:** Verify if the $500k platform fee is a perpetual license or a multi-year prepayment; if the latter, your cash-flow risk is higher than modeled.
