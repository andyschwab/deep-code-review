**Recommendation: Pursue a Hybrid Approach (Self-Publish with a Marketing Partner)**
Do not go fully independent yet. While you retain creative control, your marketing budget ($80k) is insufficient to guarantee visibility in the crowded space-sim genre without a partner's existing audience. A hybrid deal (e.g., a publishing label that handles storefront features and influencer networks for a reduced revenue share) de-risks the "discovery" variable while letting you keep the majority of upside if the game hits.

**Three Biggest Failure Points**
1.  **Discovery Cliff:** $80k burns quickly on paid ads with diminishing returns. If Steam's algorithm doesn't pick up the game organically within the first 72 hours of launch, revenue will flatline regardless of quality.
2.  **Conversion Delusion:** You are banking on a 5% demo-to-full-game conversion. Industry average for space sims is typically 1.5%–2.5%. A 5% rate implies a cult-hit level of engagement that rarely scales to the volume needed for $500k revenue.
3.  **Platform Fee Blindness:** You mentioned "hidden platform fees." Steam takes a flat 30%. If you calculated revenue before this cut, your $500k gross is actually $350k net, turning your projected 100% ROI into a 40% loss against your $250k dev spend.

**Abort/Pivot Triggers**
*   **Demo Conversion:** If conversion is below **2.5%** after two weeks of the June 1st demo launch, pivot immediately to a publishing partner deal or delay launch to rework the core loop.
*   **Wishlist Velocity:** If you have fewer than **7,500 wishlists** accumulated by August 1st (2.5 months pre-launch), abort the self-publish plan; the algorithmic momentum required for Day 1 success will not exist.

**Critical Flaws in Numbers & Assumptions**
*   **The Math Doesn't Hold:** To hit $500k gross at a standard $25 price point, you need 20,000 units sold. At a realistic 2% conversion rate, you need **1,000,000 unique demo players**. Acquiring 1M demo users with an $80k marketing budget implies a Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) of $0.08, which is impossible in the current ad market (realistic CPA is $2.00+). You are underestimating the traffic volume required by roughly 10x.
*   **Market Growth vs. Share:** A 15% YoY market growth does not guarantee *your* share grows. In a growing market, more competitors enter, often diluting individual title performance unless you have a distinct hook.
*   **Revenue Timing:** Steam pays out ~30 days after month-end. Your "first year" revenue might not fully materialize in cash flow until month 15, affecting your ability to fund post-launch content or DLC.
