**Recommendation: Do not proceed with the current plan; execute a capped pilot first.**

The proposal relies on a fatal assumption: a 15% conversion rate (18,000 users) is industry-defying for news archives, where typical rates are 1–3%. At a realistic 2% conversion, revenue drops to ~$35,000/month, failing to cover the $250k monthly burn of the projected ad loss ($300k/year drop) plus operational costs. Furthermore, excluding legal compliance costs for GDPR/CCPA creates immediate liability risk, and the "three developers" capacity claim ignores the unpriced engineering lift for secure paywall integration and churn modeling.

**Main Failure Modes:**
1.  **Conversion Delusion:** Achieving even 3% conversion yields only ~$50k/month, resulting in a net annual loss when combined with the $300k ad revenue decline and $750k upfront CAPEX.
2.  **Compliance Gap:** Launching without budgeted legal review for GDPR/CCPA risks fines exceeding the project's total potential revenue.
3.  **Churn Cliff:** With no retention data, a standard 5–8% monthly churn could erase the subscriber base within 18 months, leaving high fixed costs and no revenue.

**Kill Criteria & Pilot Structure:**
Launch a **4-week pilot** to 5% of traffic (6,000 users) with a hard spend cap of $50k.
*   **Kill Trigger:** If Month-1 converted revenue is **below $15,000** (implying <2.5% annualized conversion) or if legal review identifies unmitigable compliance blockers, terminate the initiative immediately.
*   **Pivot Trigger:** If conversion is between 2–4% but churn exceeds 10% in the first 60 days, pivot to a voluntary donation model or metered access rather than a hard paywall.

Do not commit the full $750k or lock the entire archive until the pilot validates a conversion rate above 4% and confirms legal safety.

*Note: The current math assumes zero churn and zero legal cost, which guarantees a net loss even at optimistic conversion. The pilot must price legal work before any code is written.*
