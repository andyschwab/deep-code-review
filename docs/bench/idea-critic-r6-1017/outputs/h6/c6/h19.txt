**Recommendation: Proceed with a conditional pilot (Go-If), not a full launch.**
Do not commit the full $2.5M or the October 2025 date yet. Instead, authorize a capped pilot ($250k budget, 3 schools) to validate the sales cycle and integration costs before scaling. The current plan relies on unverified arithmetic and a flawed competitor comparison that creates a high risk of revenue shortfall.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Revenue Arithmetic Error:** The projection of $270,000/month is incorrect. 250 schools × 180 students × $6/month equals **$270,000 per year** if the price is annual, or **$270,000/month** only if every single student is billed every single month without attrition. However, the "12-month adoption" assumption contradicts the "first year revenue" target. If schools adopt gradually over 12 months due to budget cycles, you will not hit 250 schools in Month 1. Realistically, Year 1 revenue will be less than 50% of the projected $3.24M annual run rate, creating an immediate budget deficit against the $2.9M spend.
2.  **Flawed Competitive Positioning:** Comparing your $6/student/month ($72/year) to a competitor's $15/student/year is dangerous without adjusting for feature parity. If the competitor includes LMS integration and digital tools in their low fee, your higher price point requires proof of superior value. If you cannot demonstrate this quickly, sales cycles will extend beyond the assumed 12 months, stalling cash flow.
3.  **Hidden Integration Costs:** The budget excludes specific costs for LMS integration and supplemental digital tools, which are standard expectations for K-12. If these are built post-launch or require custom work per school, the $2.5M platform upgrade budget will be exhausted before reaching the 250-school target, forcing a choice between halting development or burning additional capital.

**Kill Criteria:**
Terminate the initiative if the pilot reveals a **churn rate exceeding 5% within the first six months** OR if the **customer acquisition cost (CAC) per school exceeds $4,000** (based on the $400k marketing budget divided by the 100 schools needed to validate the model before full scale). Additionally, kill the project if the sales cycle averages **longer than 9 months**, as this invalidates the FY budget adoption assumption and prevents reaching profitability within the planned runway.
