**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Margin Erosion:** New SKUs often incur higher slotting fees or lower turnover, causing distributors to demand deeper discounts. If the effective margin drops from 30% to 22%, your break-even point skyrockets from 45,000 to ~60,000 cases, making the target mathematically impossible within the pilot window.

3.  **Cannibalization vs. Incrementality:** The 60,000-case target may simply steal share from your existing high-margin products rather than generating new net revenue. If the net incremental volume is less than 50% of the reported sales, the $185,000 investment yields negative ROI despite hitting the top-line target.
