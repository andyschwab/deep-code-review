Approve the purchase of **two** drones and a 90-day proof-of-concept at Port Linton only, rather than the full eight-drone, three-harbour rollout. The current plan exposes $2.8M to unpriced regulatory and insurance risks. 2.  **Weather-Induced Capacity Crunch:** If actual flyable days drop below the assumed 80% due to coastal weather patterns, the fleet cannot meet delivery frequency targets, forcing expensive road-freight backups that erase the $350,000 savings goal.
3.  **Maintenance Cost Escalation:** The estimated $15,000/month/drone ($180k/year) is high; if marine corrosion or salt air drives actual maintenance 20% higher than projected, the total operating cost will exceed the freight savings, resulting in a net financial loss.
