**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" Pilot, Not Full Rollout**

Do not approve the full $2.5M capital outlay for January 2027 yet. Instead, authorize an immediate **bounded pilot** of 5,000 meters across three districts with varying pipe ages (old, medium, new). Commit to the full 120,000-meter rollout *only if* the pilot meets specific kill criteria after six months. This approach validates the 8% water-loss reduction assumption and the false-positive rate before risking the entire budget.

**Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **Labor Cost Overrun:** If the false-positive rate hits the vendor's upper bound (6%), field crew costs could exceed $450k annually (vs. your $150k estimate), erasing the net benefit.
2.  **Variable Efficacy:** The 8% loss reduction may not hold in districts with ancient infrastructure where leaks are already catastrophic and obvious, rendering the sensors redundant there while missing subtle leaks in newer pipes.
3.  **Adoption Shortfall:** If premium alert adoption falls below 3% instead of the projected 5%, revenue from avoided treatment costs drops to $840k, failing to cover the $300k data fees plus increased labor.

**Pre-Agreed Kill Criteria**
At the six-month pilot review (approx. July 2027), terminate or pivot the project if:
*   **False-Positive Rate:** Verified field data shows a false-positive alert rate **>4%**, triggering unsustainable labor costs.
*   **ROI Threshold:** The annualized Return on Investment (calculated as *[Avoided Costs + Premium Revenue] – [Data Fees + Actual Labor Costs]*) falls below **12%** (approx. $300k net annual gain), indicating the project cannot service the capital debt.
*   **Efficacy Floor:** Measured water loss reduction in the pilot districts is **<5%**, disproving the core 8% efficiency model.

If these thresholds are met, pivot to a targeted rollout only in districts where efficacy >6% and false positives <3%, or cancel the project entirely.

*Note: The board presentation should frame the full rollout as contingent on these pilot results to demonstrate fiscal prudence.*
*Ensure the pilot contract includes a clause allowing termination of the data-service fee if the kill criteria are triggered.*
