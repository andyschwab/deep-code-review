**Recommendation:** Do not proceed with a full rollout. Instead, propose a **90-day pilot** to 10% of clients (approx. 4–5 placements/month) while maintaining the flat fee for the rest. The current proposal contains a critical arithmetic error and an unverified adoption assumption that threatens immediate profitability.

**Top Three Failure Modes:**

1.  **Fatal Arithmetic Error & Margin Compression:** Your revenue projection assumes 100% of the 45 monthly placements convert to the new model at the higher rate. However, you also state marketing costs must rise by $8,000/month to maintain the *same* pipeline, implying volume stays flat at 45.
    *   *Current Monthly Revenue:* 45 × $12,000 = **$540,000**.
    *   *Proposed Monthly Revenue (if 90% adopt):* (40.5 placements × $15,300) + (4.5 placements × $12,000) = $619,650 + $54,000 = **$673,650**.
    *   *New Costs:* You added $1,200 onboarding per candidate (45 × $1,200 = $54,000) + $8,000 marketing = **$62,000** in new monthly costs.
    *   *Net Gain:* ($673,650 - $540,000) - $62,000 = **$71,650** theoretical gain.
    *   *The Trap:* If adoption is only 75% (not 90%) due to competitor undercutting ($10k flat fee), revenue drops to ~$641k. After the $62k new costs, your net gain vanishes to ~$39k. If adoption hits 60%, you **lose money** compared to the status quo because the fixed cost increase ($62k) outweighs the revenue lift from fewer conversions. The model is overly sensitive to the 90% adoption rate.

2.  **Competitor Undercutting & Client Churn:** Competitors charging $10,000 flat are significantly cheaper than your $12,000 current fee, let alone your $15,300 average target. Clients focused on short-term cash flow will likely reject an 18% share ($15,300) when they can pay a competitor $10,000. Your assumption that "aligning cash flow" guarantees 90% acceptance ignores that 18% of salary is objectively more expensive than both your current fee and competitor fees.

3.  **Hidden Operational Drag:** The $1,200 onboarding cost is treated as variable, but if the background-check automation requires fixed software licensing or dedicated staff time regardless of volume, this cost becomes a fixed burden that accelerates losses if placement volume dips even slightly during the transition.

**Kill Criteria:**
Terminate the initiative immediately if, after the 90-day pilot:
*   **Adoption Rate < 75%** of offered clients.
*   **Net Margin per Placement** (Revenue minus the $1,200 onboarding and allocated marketing) falls below **$11,500** (your current effective margin baseline).

*Note: The projected $7.0M annual revenue figure in your draft appears to ignore the phased adoption risk and the immediate $96k annual increase in fixed marketing costs.*
