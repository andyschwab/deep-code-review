**Recommendation: Go-If (Conditional Approval)**
Proceed with a capped pilot of **three mowers and one charging station** only. Do not authorize the full fleet purchase until the unknown site-installation costs are quoted by a licensed electrician and the pilot confirms actual energy consumption over 30 days. This limits exposure to the unpriced infrastructure risk while validating the operational savings claims.

**Top Three Failure Modes**
1.  **Infrastructure Cost Overrun:** The "few weeks" estimate for 240V upgrades could hide six-figure electrical panel upgrades or trenching costs, instantly destroying the ROI.
2.  **Battery Degradation:** If capacity drops significantly after year three (as no data exists), replacement costs could exceed the projected diesel savings for the remaining two years of the cycle.
3.  **Utility Rate Volatility:** The model assumes a static $0.12/kWh; a rate increase to $0.25/kWh would halve the projected annual savings, extending the payback period beyond acceptable limits.

**Kill Criteria (Stop/Change Triggers)**
*   **Hardware/Install:** Total installed cost per station (hardware + electrical work) exceeds **$4,500**.
*   **Energy Cost:** Verified average electricity cost rises above **$0.20/kWh**.
*   **Performance:** Battery runtime decreases by more than **15%** within the first **18 months** of operation.
