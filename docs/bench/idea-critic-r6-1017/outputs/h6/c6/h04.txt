**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" Pilot, Not a Full Launch.**
Do not commit the full $150k marketing budget or the Q2 2025 launch date yet. Instead, authorize a capped $25k pilot to validate the unit economics and conversion assumptions before scaling. The current plan relies on a 15% conversion rate (exceptionally high for e-commerce) and ignores $44k in identified costs, creating a high risk of negative margins on every sale.

**Primary Failure Modes & Hidden Risks:**
1.  **Channel Cannibalization:** The assumption that wholesale customers will remain "unchanged" while you shift 25% of production to direct sales is fragile. If your best wholesale partners see you competing directly at potentially lower effective prices (or prioritizing your own stock), they may reduce orders or demand margin concessions, eroding the stable $1.2M base.
2.  **Unit Economics Collapse:** Your projected 20% margin is likely overstated. Adding the missing $3 packaging and $8k logistics contract increases your cost basis. More critically, if the 15% conversion rate is optimistic and drops to a standard 2–3%, your customer acquisition cost (CAC) will skyrocket, turning the $250k revenue target into a loss-making endeavor once the $150k marketing spend is amortized over fewer actual buyers.
3.  **Operational Friction:** Moving from bulk wholesale shipping to individual unit fulfillment introduces complexity (returns, customer service, breakage) that your current team may not be resourced to handle, distracting from the core wholesale business.

**Kill Criteria & Pivot Triggers:**
Establish a strict checkpoint after spending $10k on marketing and achieving 50 direct orders:
*   **Kill Switch:** If the verified conversion rate is **<5%** OR if the fully loaded cost per unit (including packaging, logistics, and proportional marketing) exceeds **$85** (assuming a ~$100 average order value derived from your targets), halt the launch immediately.
*   **Pivot Trigger:** If conversion is healthy (>8%) but wholesale partners express concern, pivot to a "wholesale-plus" model where the online store only sells limited editions or overstock, preserving partner relationships.
