**Recommendation: Proceed with a capped pilot, not a full launch.**
Launch on 2025-03-01 but cap enrollment at 200 members (not 500) for the first 60 days. Your current unit economics are dangerously thin, and the shipping variable is a potential margin killer that must be resolved before scaling to 500+ members.

**Arithmetic & Logic Slips:**
1.  **Margin Error:** Your revenue is $15. Direct costs are $12 (books) + $1.50 (packaging) = $13.50. This leaves only $1.50 gross profit per user to cover payment processing (~$0.75), author chat platform fees, labor, and marketing. You are likely operating at a loss per unit before fixed costs.
2.  **Churn Contradiction:** You assume a "30% churn rate" that "stays under 5% monthly." These are contradictory. If you mean 30% *total* churn over the first quarter, that averages >10% monthly, which contradicts the <5% steady-state goal. If you mean 30% *annual* churn, that is ~2.5% monthly, which is plausible but optimistic for a new niche subscription. Clarify this metric immediately.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Shipping Cost Variance:** Since you haven't nailed carrier rates, a mere $2.00 increase in average shipping cost (common for mixed-size parcels) wipes out your entire $1.50 gross margin, turning every new subscriber into a direct cash loss.
2.  **Author Chat Scalability:** A "virtual author chat" for 800 members is logistically difficult to make feel exclusive. If it becomes a webinar, perceived value drops; if it's intimate, you cannot scale to 800 without fracturing the community or exploding costs.
3.  **Content Cost Inflation:** Sourcing 4 curated books at exactly $3 each consistently is risky. If wholesale prices rise by $0.50/book or a title goes out of print requiring a pricier substitute, your margin vanishes.

**Pre-Agreed Stop/Pivot Trigger:**
**Kill Criteria:** If, after the first 200 sign-ups, the **actual blended shipping cost exceeds $2.25 per box** OR **net retention (after churn) falls below 85% in Month 2**, immediately pause enrollment and pivot to a higher price point ($22+) or a digital-first model. Do not scale to 500 members until these two metrics are proven stable.

*Note: Your "unknown" shipping cost is the single largest risk; negotiate a hard cap with carriers before taking a single dollar.*
