**Handoff: Staging deploys fail 40% of the time (timeouts) since Jan 8; retry 2-3 times to proceed.**

**Symptoms & Impact**
Deploys to staging timeout ~40% of the time, starting Jan 8 (post-DB migration to larger instance). Each attempt takes ~25 minutes vs. normal 8 minutes. The workaround is retrying 2-3 times, which usually succeeds.

**Ruled Out**
- **Resources:** CPU/Memory <50% during failures.
- **Database:** Queries work fine when not deploying; migration itself is stable.
- **Network:** Infra team confirmed no dropped packets or partitions.

**Suspected Cause**
Deploy logs show the health check endpoint timing out, yet app containers are up. Likely a startup race condition linked to the DB connection pool increase (10 → 50 connections) on Jan 8.

**Current Status**
Incident ticket **INC-4472** is open with the platform team; no response yet.

Continue using the retry workaround to keep releases moving while waiting for Platform's diagnosis on INC-4472. Monitor if the 40% failure rate changes after their investigation.
