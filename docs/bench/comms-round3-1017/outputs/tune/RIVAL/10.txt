**Executive Summary: Last-Mile Routing Optimization Investment**

Last-mile delivery costs reached $31.2M in 2023 ($13.33 per stop), exceeding our $11.50 target by 15.9%. Performance varies significantly by region: the Southeast meets targets, while the Northeast and West Coast exceed them by 38% and 26.6% respectively due to congestion, labor rates, and regulatory costs. The Midwest and Southwest lag due to inefficient rural routing and outdated mapping data.

Root causes center on our 2021-era routing algorithm, which lacks real-time traffic integration, and a static driver app that prevents dynamic rerouting.

Four options are available:
*   **Option A:** Implement a new routing platform ($2.4M + $480K/year). This offers a 12–18% cost reduction across all regions but requires an 8-month implementation.
*   **Option B:** Shift 40% of Northeast and West Coast volume to gig drivers. This saves $3.20 per stop on affected routes but introduces quality risks and requires $800K upfront.
*   **Option C:** Deploy urban locker networks ($1.8M). This reduces costs by $4.50 per eligible stop but applies to only 22% of volume.
*   **Option D:** Maintain current operations and absorb the $1.3M annual variance.

The board has allocated $3M for 2024 improvements, though approval for ongoing operational expenses remains uncertain. Option A fits within the capital budget and provides the broadest impact, but its multi-year ROI depends on securing approval for the recurring $480K license fee. Options B and C offer faster, targeted savings but leave structural inefficiencies in other regions unaddressed.

Recommendation: Prioritize Option A to address system-wide inefficiencies, with a contingency plan to pilot Option B in the Northeast if recurring funding for Option A is denied.

Note: Exact parking violation costs may be understated as some driver out-of-pocket expenses are unrecorded.
