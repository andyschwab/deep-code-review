**Executive Summary: Customer Concentration Risk & Diversification Investment**

**The Situation**
We face immediate revenue risk. Our top 10 customers generate 71% of our $48.7M annual revenue, and contracts for our top five clients (representing 54% of total revenue) expire between June and December 2024.

**Critical Risks**
*   **Meridian Corp (18.9% of revenue):** Our champion left in November; the new VP has not engaged. They are evaluating competitors.
*   **Pinnacle Holdings (9.2% of revenue):** Contract expires in five months with no renewal discussions started.
*   **Pipeline Gap:** Our current qualified pipeline ($12.4M) projects only ~$3.5M in new revenue based on historical close rates. This is insufficient to replace potential losses from Meridian and Pinnacle alone.

**Investment Options**
We have $1.5M allocated for 2024 strategic initiatives.

*   **Option A: Accelerate Enterprise Sales.** Hire 3 account executives ($675K). Generates $2–4M new pipeline in 6 months. Fastest impact, but may not fully cover a major churn event.
*   **Option B: Expand Mid-Market.** Invest $400K in marketing and self-serve tools. Targets 50+ smaller accounts to dilute concentration over time. Lower cost, slower revenue ramp.
*   **Option C: Lock-in Top Customers.** Offer pricing incentives for 3-year terms. Secures revenue but reduces margins by 5–8%. Does not solve long-term concentration.
*   **Option D: Adjacent Verticals.** Enter healthcare/government ($1.2M). High barrier to entry; 12–18 month timeline before revenue impact. Too slow for current risk window.

**Recommendation**
Prioritize **Option A (Enterprise Sales)** combined with immediate **retention outreach to Pinnacle**.

Hiring three account executives fits within the $1.5M budget and offers the quickest path to replacing at-risk revenue. Simultaneously, leadership must intervene immediately to open renewal talks with Pinnacle before their June expiration. Option D is too slow for our current exposure, and Option C erodes margins without reducing dependency. We should defer Option B until Q3 once enterprise hiring is underway.

**Decision Required:** Approve the $675K investment for three enterprise account executives and authorize immediate executive-led engagement with Pinnacle Holdings.
