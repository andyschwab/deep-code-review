**To:** Elena, CFO
**From:** IT Asset Management
**Date:** January 18, 2024
**Subject:** Decision Required: Software License True-Up vs. Renegotiation (Deadline: Feb 28)

You must decide by February 28 whether to process an immediate license true-up or attempt a broader contract renegotiation.

**Current Exposure**
The audit reveals an $87K liability for under-licensed software, primarily in DataVista Analytics (32 seats) and CodeForge (13 seats). We have been under-licensed on DataVista since March 2023, creating a high risk of audit penalties ranging from $138K to $207K if discovered before we act. Conversely, we are wasting $165K annually on unused CloudPeak licenses (210 seats), largely due to headcount reductions in Northwind Division.

**Options**

1.  **True Up and Reduce (Recommended for Compliance)**
    *   **Action:** Pay the $87K true-up cost immediately and reduce CloudPeak seats to match actual usage.
    *   **Financial Impact:** Net savings of $78K in year one ($165K savings minus $87K cost), eliminating audit risk.
    *   **Constraint:** This locks in current terms. CloudPeak's renewal in June 2024 may still see a 15–20% price increase due to their new per-user pricing model.

2.  **Renegotiate All Contracts**
    *   **Action:** Pause the true-up to bundle negotiations for DataVista, CloudPeak, and StreamLine.
    *   **Potential Upside:** Consolidating three CloudPeak agreements could yield an 8% discount in exchange for a 3-year commitment.
    *   **Risk:** Negotiations typically take 4–6 weeks, likely pushing past the February 28 deadline. This leaves us exposed to DataVista audit penalties and potentially forfeits the option to simply true up later. DataVista has not responded to our January 5 compliance inquiry, so we do not know if an audit is already pending.

**Recommendation**
Given the high audit risk with DataVista and the uncertainty of renegotiation timelines, the safest path is to execute the true-up and reduction immediately to secure the $78K net saving and compliance safety. We can then prepare for the CloudPeak renewal separately in June.
