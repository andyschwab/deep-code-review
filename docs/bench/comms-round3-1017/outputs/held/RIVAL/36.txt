**Executive Summary: Dormant Account Cleanup**

**Recommendation:** Adopt a phased approach. Prioritize disabling former employee accounts and securing orphaned service accounts immediately (5–6 weeks with two engineers). Implement the auto-disable policy concurrently. Schedule the broader cleanup of inactive and shared accounts for Q2.

**Context & Risk**
We currently lack a policy for disabling inactive accounts, leaving 3,616 dormant accounts (12–20% per system) across our infrastructure. This creates specific security and compliance exposures:
*   **Security:** 234 accounts belong to former employees, representing direct entry points. More critically, 12 orphaned service accounts hold admin-level access in AWS or SAP; disabling these without testing risks breaking production jobs.
*   **Compliance:** Our SOC 2 auditor flagged this as a control deficiency. Failure to remediate by the March 2025 audit will result in a formal finding.

**Scope & Effort**
Total estimated effort is 440 hours.
*   **Phase 1 (Immediate):** Disable former employee accounts (40 hrs) and trace/secure service accounts (120 hrs). This addresses the highest security risks and satisfies the core auditor concern.
*   **Phase 2 (Policy):** Configure auto-disable rules for Okta, AD, and AWS (80 hrs).
*   **Phase 3 (Q2):** Audit and disable remaining inactive and shared accounts (200 hrs). This phase requires manager sign-off for nearly 2,000 accounts and investigation into legitimate uses for shared logins.

**Decision Required**
Approve the phased plan to ensure we meet the March audit deadline without risking production stability. A "full cleanup" attempt before March is not feasible due to the unknown dependencies of orphaned service accounts and the volume of manager verifications required.
