**Subject: Decision needed: Redesign traffic drop and rollback options**

**Status**
The redesign launched March 12 (homepage, product, pricing, about). Organic traffic is down 18% week-over-week compared to pre-launch levels. Search Console shows indexing delays, but this does not explain the full drop. We likely lost backlink equity due to 301 redirects and the new URL structure on product pages.

**Constraints**
*   **Paid Campaign:** A major campaign launches April 1, directing traffic to the new URLs.
*   **Effort:** Engineering estimates a full rollback will take two days.
*   **Budget:** The $340k redesign budget is already spent.

**Uncertainty**
It is unclear if traffic will recover as indexing resolves or if the drop is structural due to the URL changes.

**Options for Approval**

1.  **Full Rollback:** Revert all pages to the pre-launch version.
    *   *Pros:* Stops the bleeding immediately; preserves SEO equity.
    *   *Cons:* Requires 2 days of dev time; conflicts with the April 1 paid campaign (landing pages would need to be remapped or delayed).
2.  **Wait and Monitor:** Keep current site and fix indexing issues.
    *   *Pros:* No dev cost; aligns with April 1 campaign.
    *   *Cons:* Risk that traffic does not recover; continued 18% loss during the critical campaign launch.
3.  **Partial Rollback:** Keep the new homepage but revert product pages to old URLs.
    *   *Pros:* Targets the specific area with URL structure changes; may recover product traffic.
    *   *Cons:* Mixed user experience; still requires dev time; complex routing for the April 1 campaign.

**Recommendation**
Given the April 1 campaign deadline, a full rollback creates significant friction for paid media. If the drop is primarily on product pages, Option 3 offers a middle ground. However, if engineering confirms the indexing delay is the primary driver, Option 2 minimizes disruption to the upcoming launch.

Please advise which direction to take so engineering can begin work immediately.
