**Node Autoscaling Trial – Daily Update**

We completed a 72-hour trial starting April 8 at 09:00 UTC. Cluster costs dropped from $2,340/day to $1,890/day, a 19.2% reduction.

However, we observed 47 service interruptions during scale-down events, concentrated in the order-processing service. During these windows, p95 latency increased from 120ms to 340ms. We adjusted the scale-down thresholds at the 36-hour mark, which reduced interruptions to 12 over the remaining 36 hours.

Two key unknowns remain:
*   We have not yet tested under peak load; the next predicted peak is Monday, April 15.
*   Spot instance behavior in us-east-1 may differ from the us-west-2 region used for this trial.

**Decision Needed:** Should we proceed with a partial rollout now, or wait until after the peak load test on April 15?
