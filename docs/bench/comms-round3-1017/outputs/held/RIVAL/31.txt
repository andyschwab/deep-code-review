**Recommendation:** Do not approve a full company-wide rollout for Q1. Instead, expand the pilot to include the Sales team and extend the timeline by one quarter.  

**Current Status**  
The Q3 survey (71% response rate) shows engagement gains in Engineering and Product, where the pilot ran from July to September. Combined interventions (weekly pulse checks and manager coaching) lifted engagement by 11 points in these groups. However, Sales engagement dropped to 62, and their survey response rate was only 54%, making their data unreliable for decision-making. Customer Success scores were flat but likely skewed by a mid-quarter reorg.  

**Financial Impact**  
Scaling the current pilot company-wide would cost an estimated $1.36M per quarter ($340 per employee).  

**Key Risks to Full Rollout**  
1. **Unproven Durability:** We have only one quarter of post-stabilization data. It is unclear if the 11-point gain will hold beyond six months.  
2. **Variable Effectiveness:** The pilot succeeded in high-performing teams (Engineering/Product). We do not know if teams with different baseline issues, such as Sales or Customer Success, will see similar improvements.  
3. **Data Gaps:** The low response rate in Sales prevents us from diagnosing their specific engagement drivers accurately.  

**Next Steps**  
Given the budget review on November 15, I propose we allocate funds to expand the pilot to the Sales team immediately. This will allow us to test the interventions in a lower-engagement environment and improve our data quality before committing to the full $1.36M quarterly spend. We should revisit the company-wide decision in Q2 once we have six months of durability data and results from a broader range of team types.
