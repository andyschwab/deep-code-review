Subject: Chatbot Pilot: 2-Week Status & Decision Needed

We are two weeks into the six-week chatbot pilot. While wait times for bot-handled queries dropped from 8 minutes to 2 minutes, the bot is missing its core targets. The deflection rate sits at 34.7% against a 45% goal, and customer satisfaction (CSAT) for bot interactions is 3.2/5, significantly lower than the 4.1 average for live agents.

The primary driver of low satisfaction is the bot's inability to resolve complex account issues, which currently forces users to repeat their query three times before escalating to a human. Additionally, we have not yet tested Spanish language support because the translation files are not ready; that phase was scheduled to begin March 1.

You have three options:

1.  **Stop the pilot now.** The current CSAT score suggests the bot is frustrating customers more than it helps, and it is failing to meet deflection targets.
2.  **Adjust escalation rules immediately.** Reducing the threshold from three retries to one could improve satisfaction by getting users to humans faster, though this may lower the deflection rate further.
3.  **Extend the pilot.** This allows time to fix the complex issue handling and complete the Spanish testing, but risks continued low satisfaction scores in the interim.

Recommendation: Adjust the escalation rule to one retry immediately to protect CSAT, then decide on an extension once we see if that stabilizes satisfaction scores.

Note: Spanish testing cannot proceed until translation files are delivered. Current data covers only English interactions.
