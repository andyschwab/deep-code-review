**Executive Summary: Q4 Quality Investment Prioritization**

**Recommendation:** Prioritize immediate investment in **Line B (Welding)** and **Line E (New Finishing)**. This combination costs $342K, staying within the $400K budget, and addresses the two highest defect drivers.

**Rationale:**
Line B is the largest source of defects (3,891 units) with a clear, fixable root cause: temperature inconsistency and worn fixtures. A $275K investment yields a rapid return (5-week timeline) and cuts the defect rate from 2.31% to roughly 1.0%. This single fix removes nearly a third of our total quality issues.

Line E, while newer and smaller in volume, has the highest defect rate at 2.89%. The issues stem from uncalibrated tooling and training gaps. For $67K, we can recalibrate and train staff within two weeks. Although a learning curve remains, ignoring a new line's foundational setup risks cementing bad habits and higher long-term costs.

**Deferred Projects:**
We must delay action on **Line C (Assembly)** and **Line A (Stamping)** this quarter.
*   **Line C:** Requires supplier cooperation from Vantage Components, our sole source for critical parts. Since we cannot switch suppliers and cost/timeline for their compliance is unknown, investing in internal process redesign ($110K) now carries high risk of failure.
*   **Line A:** Currently performs best (1.12%) and is closest to the 0.8% target. The proposed $210K die replacement is a scheduled maintenance item best planned for the next fiscal cycle rather than an emergency fix.

**Next Steps:**
Approve the $275K for Line B controllers/fixtures and $67K for Line E recalibration/training immediately. Procurement should initiate the Line B order to meet the 4-week parts lead time. We will revisit Line C once negotiations with Vantage Components clarify their willingness to tighten tolerances.
