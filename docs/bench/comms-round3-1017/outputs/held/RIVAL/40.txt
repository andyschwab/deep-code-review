**Executive Summary: Model Monitoring Rollout Decision**

**Current State:** Of our 67 production models, only 27% have full monitoring (performance, drift, and data quality). Critical gaps leave 58 models without feature drift detection and 26 without automated prediction quality checks. Notably, 4 of our 14 revenue-impacting (Tier 1) models lack drift detection, and zero models monitor for bias.

**Business Impact:** In 2024, two undetected degradation incidents lasted over three weeks; one resulted in an estimated $340K loss from mispriced recommendations. With EU AI Act requirements phasing in during 2025, regulatory exposure is increasing alongside operational risk.

**Proposed Rollout:**
*   **Phase 1 (Q1 2025):** Secure all Tier 1 models with drift, data quality, and prediction monitoring.
    *   *Investment:* $85K + 320 engineering hours (8 weeks).
*   **Phase 2 (Q2 2025):** Extend coverage to Tier 2 models.
    *   *Investment:* $60K + 280 engineering hours (6 weeks).
*   **Phase 3 (Q3 2025):** Cover Tier 3 models and pilot bias monitoring.
    *   *Investment:* $45K + 240 engineering hours (8 weeks).

**Total Program Cost:** $190K infrastructure + 840 engineering hours (~5.25 FTE-months).

**Key Risks & Unknowns:**
1.  **Capacity:** Two of our six MLOps engineers may be reassigned to a platform migration, threatening Q1 start dates.
2.  **Compliance:** The bias monitoring pilot may require legal review, potentially adding 4–6 weeks to Phase 3.

**Decision Required:**
1.  **Approve Full Rollout:** Begin Phase 1 in Q1 assuming current capacity holds.
2.  **Delay to Q2:** Wait to confirm engineering availability before committing.
3.  **Approve Phase 1 Only:** Secure Tier 1 models immediately, then reassess capacity and legal requirements before proceeding.

***

*Recommendation: Given the $340K incident cost and upcoming EU regulations, approving Phase 1 immediately mitigates the highest financial and compliance risks while buying time to resolve capacity constraints.*
