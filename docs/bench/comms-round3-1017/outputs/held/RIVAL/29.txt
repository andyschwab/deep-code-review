**Executive Summary: Travel & Expense Policy Review**

**Recommendation:** Approve the proposed policy updates and fund the new expense management platform.

**Business Case**
The Q3 2024 audit revealed $342,000 in policy violations across $2.87 million in spend (11.9% violation rate). Key issues include hotel bookings exceeding limits ($134k), missing receipts ($78k), and off-channel bookings ($52k). Sales, Engineering, and Marketing account for 64% of these violations.

Current manual processes are inefficient, with an average report processing time of 12.3 days versus the 5-day industry benchmark. Three managers currently review over 200 reports each quarter, and 23% of reports require rework.

**Financial Impact**
Enforcing the recommended changes—specifically 14-day advance booking, preferred hotel programs, channel consolidation, and strict per diem enforcement—projects **$353,000 in annual savings**. This exceeds the total value of current violations and covers the cost of the new platform.

**Risk Mitigation**
Immediate action is required to address compliance risks:
*   **Audit Exposure:** $78,000 in missing receipts affects three jurisdictions.
*   **Card Agreement:** $13,000 in personal charges on corporate cards threatens our banking relationship.
*   **Regulatory Compliance:** Two unapproved international trips are under legal review for potential export control violations.
*   **Fraud:** Seven reports totaling $8,900 are flagged for potential duplicate submission.

**Proposed Actions**
1.  **Policy Updates:** Mandate 14-day advance airfare booking, require use of the corporate travel platform, and implement automated pre-approval for trips under $1,500.
2.  **Platform Implementation:** Deploy automated policy checking at submission to reduce manual review and eliminate receipt gaps.
3.  **Governance:** Institute quarterly compliance reporting by department.

**Decisions Needed**
To finalize the rollout, please confirm:
*   Whether to increase hotel limits in specific high-cost cities.
*   The approval threshold for VP-level international travel.
*   Adoption of a per-diem-only model for meals to remove receipt requirements.

Approving these measures will reduce violations, cut processing time by more than half, and generate immediate net savings.
