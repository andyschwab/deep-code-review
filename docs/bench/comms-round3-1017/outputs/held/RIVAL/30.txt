**Executive Summary: Competitor Pricing Analysis**

Northwind holds 8.7% of the enterprise project management market, ranking fourth. While our customer retention metrics (7.7:1 LTV:CAC) outperform the industry average, we face immediate risks: Vertex is undercutting us in the SMB segment, and missing native time tracking and resource forecasting costs us an estimated $18 million annually. Additionally, 34% of departing customers cite pricing as the primary factor, and only 42% of IT decision-makers view us as "good value."

Three viable paths forward emerge from the analysis:

1.  **Selective Increase (Recommended):** Raise Professional tier prices to $20/user/month and Enterprise to $27/user/month, while keeping the Starter tier unchanged. This aligns us closer to the market average where we currently underprice our value.
    *   *Impact:* +$28 million annual revenue.
    *   *Risk:* Minimal, with an estimated 1.2% additional churn.

2.  **Restructure with Add-ons:** Lower base prices by 10% across all tiers but bundle currently paid add-ons (time tracking, forecasting) into the base package. Monetize heavy users via a new premium automation package.
    *   *Impact:* +$12 million annual revenue; potential 1.5–2% mid-market share gain.
    *   *Risk:* Higher execution complexity; requires product repositioning.

3.  **Aggressive SMB Play:** Match Vertex's Starter tier at $9/user/month to stop share loss.
    *   *Impact:* -$8 million annual revenue on the Starter tier; potential 3–4% SMB share gain.
    *   *Risk:* Significant margin compression; does not address revenue growth.

Maintaining the status quo poses the highest long-term risk, with projected losses of 1–2% market share in the SMB segment alone.

**Recommendation:** Pursue **Option B (Selective Increase)**. Our strong support ratings and integration marketplace justify a price correction in the Professional and Enterprise tiers where we are currently underpriced relative to competitors. This approach maximizes immediate revenue ($28M) with contained churn risk. We should simultaneously accelerate the roadmap to integrate time tracking and forecasting natively to close the feature gap driving lost deals.

A secondary initiative should address contract structure; shifting our mix from 62% annual contracts toward the industry standard of 71% could further improve LTV and reduce churn.
