**To:** Anika Patel, CTO
**From:** Engineering Team
**Date:** Nov 4
**Subject:** Decision Required: Cloud Region Migration Feasibility

We recommend pushing back on the current migration plan to explore alternatives. While the move to a us-west-2 primary and eu-west-1 secondary architecture is feasible, the execution risks and costs outweigh the immediate benefits under the proposed timeline.

The migration requires 14–18 months and $2.37M in one-time costs, plus an additional $168K annually. The engineering burden is significant: 2,140 hours across four phases, drawn from a team already at capacity with no dedicated migration staff. This creates a high likelihood of burnout and roadmap slippage.

The critical blocker is database risk. Our largest database (2.3 TB) has never been migrated. Testing shows inconsistent replication lag (2s to 47s under load), making the estimated 45–90 minute cutover window unreliable. A failure here could exceed our 99.95% SLA, violating stricter terms in un-audited customer contracts. Additionally, legal has not yet confirmed compliance certification for eu-west-1; proceeding without this exposes us to regulatory risk.

Given the November 8 deadline for next year's budget, we cannot guarantee a safe cutover window or compliance clearance in time. We should pause this specific plan to:
1. Audit customer contracts for strict SLA constraints.
2. Complete the legal compliance review for eu-west-1.
3. Evaluate lower-risk alternatives, such as a single-region failover strategy or delaying the EU expansion until the replication lag issue is resolved.

Approving the current plan commits us to a high-probability risk of extended downtime and compliance gaps.

---
*Note: Decision needed by Nov 8 to impact next year's budget. Legal compliance status for eu-west-1 is the immediate gating item.*
