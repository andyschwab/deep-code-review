No cost estimate or timeline exists because no team has accepted responsibility.

**Decision Required**

Please select one of the following paths:

1.  **Accept Current Timeline:** Allow the in-progress items to finish as scheduled (through Q1 2025) while Internal Audit continues to mediate the ownership dispute for data retention. This carries the risk of further delay on the unowned item.
2.  **Accelerate In-Progress Items:** Direct management to prioritize the eight open items to close them before Q1 2025. This requires additional resources but does not solve the ownership deadlock for data retention.
