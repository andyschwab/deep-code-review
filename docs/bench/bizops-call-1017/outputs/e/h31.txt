**The Call**
Do not switch to a flat subscription yet; instead, adopt a "Base + Overage" model with a Service Level Agreement (SLA) credit for false positives exceeding 5%. Your current 15% monthly churn driven by an 8% false positive rate is an immediate existential threat that outweighs the theoretical risk of regulatory fines; you must lower verification thresholds to stop the bleeding, using the SLA to share the compliance risk with clients rather than absorbing it entirely via a penalty clause.

**The Numbers**
*Current Unit Economics:*
Formula: `Contribution Margin = Price - Variable Cost`
Your Figures: $1.20 - $0.45 = **$0.75 per check** (62.5% margin).

*Churn Impact (ASSUMED):*
If you lose 15% of your client base monthly due to friction, your recurring revenue halves in roughly 4.5 months regardless of margin.
*Scenario A (Status Quo):* Margin $0.75, but Revenue Base shrinks by 15%/month.
*Scenario B (Lower Thresholds):* False positives drop to ~3% (industry standard), churn stabilizes at <3%.
*Risk Cost (ILLUSTRATIVE):* If a compliance fine is £50,000 (approx. $63,000) and occurs once every 2 years due to relaxed thresholds, the monthly risk cost is ~$2,600. If you have 1,000,000 checks/month, that is $0.0026 per check—negligible compared to the revenue loss from churn.

*Proposed Pricing Structure:*
1.  **Base Fee:** $0.90 per check (guarantees margin coverage).
2.  **Performance Credit:** If false positives > 5%, credit client $0.50 for every excess false positive.
3.  **Volume Tier:** Clients committing to >100k checks/month get a 10% discount on the Base Fee.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The deciding input is the **elasticity of churn relative to false positive rates**.
*Trigger:* If lowering thresholds reduces false positives from 8% to 4% but increases regulatory inquiry probability by >20%, pause. However, given your 15% churn, the break-even point for retention is likely a false positive rate under 5%.
*Rule of Thumb:* Retention is worth 10x the marginal compliance risk in early growth stages.
*Action this week:* If your false positive rate is >5%, consider A/B testing a relaxed threshold on 10% of traffic for one major client with a signed waiver/SLA credit agreement to measure churn reduction vs. compliance noise.
