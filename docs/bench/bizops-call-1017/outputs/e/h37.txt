**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The binding variable is the **legal classification of the error**. If the sensor failure is deemed "gross negligence" (e.g., you knew of the bug and deployed anyway), no contract clause in Texas will protect you, regardless of what you sign.
*Trigger:* If a second similar incident occurs before you update firmware or issue a safety bulletin, presume gross negligence.
*Rule of Thumb:* Liability caps are generally enforceable for ordinary negligence in Texas B2B contracts, but never for bodily injury caused by gross negligence.
