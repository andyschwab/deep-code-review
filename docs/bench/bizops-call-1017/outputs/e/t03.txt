**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is **the source of the token's value**. If the token's value is derived *solely* from the consumption of the voting service (utility) and cannot be resold or speculated upon, the security risk lowers. However, if buyers (HOAs) pay the $2,000 fee *expecting* the tokens to appreciate in value or become tradable later, it triggers the "Expectation of Profit" prong of the *Howey* test.
*   **Trigger:** If any marketing material, whitepaper, or sales conversation hints at future token tradability, liquidity events, or value appreciation, you are already in violation territory.
*   **Stop Rule:** Do not onboard the next 50 HOAs. The threshold is zero new token issuances until counsel reviews your sales scripts and tokenomics.
