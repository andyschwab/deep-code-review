**Liability Landscape & Safeguards (General Rule):**
Typically, the provider (clinic) holds the ultimate legal responsibility for the accuracy of submitted claims. However, if your software systematically suggests higher codes due to algorithmic bias or training errors, your company could face liability for "causing" the false claim. There is no settled precedent specifically for "algorithmic billing errors," creating a high-risk gray area where regulators may treat systematic AI errors as evidence of negligence.

**Required Compliance Safeguards (Engineering/Process):**
1.  **Audit Trails:** Log every AI suggestion, the doctor's final selection, and the timestamp. You must prove the human made the final choice.
2.  **Confidence Scoring:** The UI must display a confidence level for suggestions; low-confidence codes should trigger mandatory manual review.
3.  **Disclaimer UI:** Every screen must state: "AI suggestions are for administrative assistance only and must be verified by a certified coder."
4.  **Bias Testing:** Regularly audit your model against known correct coding datasets to detect systematic upcoding drift.
