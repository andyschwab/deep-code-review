**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The deciding factor is not a specific MWh number, but the **legal definition of "control"** and **compensation**. In California, the CPUC has specific proceedings for Distributed Energy Resource Providers (DERPs) and Demand Response Aggregators.
*   **The Trigger:** If you dispatch the batteries (decide when to sell) and take a cut of the sale, you are likely acting as an aggregator. California has no "safe harbor" threshold based solely on participant count for this activity; even small aggregators often require certification or exemption.
*   **The Rule of Thumb:** If you touch the dispatch signal or the money flow for energy sales, assume you are regulated until an attorney confirms an exemption.
*   **This Week's Step:** If you plan to sign up 100 more homes next month, consider pausing new enrollments until counsel reviews your operational control flow against CPUC Decision 14-08-050 (regarding demand response) and newer DERP rules.
