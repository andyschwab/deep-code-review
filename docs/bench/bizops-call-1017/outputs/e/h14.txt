**The Call**
Do not adopt a blanket local-storage mandate; it likely flips your unit economics from profitable to loss-making per unit unless you reprice the hardware by +35% or charge a distinct "sovereignty premium" on the SaaS fee. The current 65% margin cannot absorb a 40% support cost increase plus fixed annual insurance hikes without turning negative on a per-client basis.

**The Numbers**
*Current State (Cloud):*
Assuming your $8,500 hardware price and $200/month fee yield a 65% gross margin, your current Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) is roughly $3,655 per unit (hardware + initial setup) and ~$70/month for cloud/support.
*Proposed State (Local):*
1.  **Support Cost:** Increases 40%. If current monthly support is part of the $70 variable cost, a 40% jump adds ~$28/month.
2.  **Insurance:** €15,000/year per client = ~€1,250/month (~$1,350/month).
3.  **New Monthly Variable Cost:** $70 (base) + $28 (support lift) + $1,350 (insurance) = ~$1,448/month.
4.  **Revenue:** $200/month.
5.  **Result:** You lose ~$1,248/month per client on recurring revenue alone, ignoring the one-time hardware margin erosion from on-site auditing.

*Runway Check:*
With €200k cash and €45k burn, you have **4.4 months** of runway. Adding one local-storage client immediately increases monthly burn by ~€1,150 (insurance), shortening runway further unless priced correctly.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The **insurance premium allocation** is the killer. At €15k/year per client, the fee covers only 7.5% of the cost ($200 vs $1,350).
*Trigger:* If you cannot negotiate a bulk insurance rate or pass ≥€1,100/month of this cost to the client, reject the local-storage requirement.
*Rule of Thumb:* Any fixed annual cost assigned to a single client must not exceed 25% of that client's Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR). Here, it is 625%.
*This Week's Step:* If clients insist on local storage, present a revised quote: $12,000 hardware (one-time) + $1,500/month software fee. If they reject, consider Y: declining the deal until your cash runway exceeds 12 months.

**Routing Note (German Legal & Compliance)**
You must consult a German attorney specializing in IT law and insurance before signing any local-storage contract.
1.  **Data Privacy (GDPR/BDSG):** While local storage addresses sovereignty, German law (BDSG) has strict rules on *biometric* data processing. You need a legal determination on whether your specific "on-premise" architecture still classifies you as a "processor" requiring a specific Article 28 agreement, or if the client becomes the sole controller, shifting liability. Do not rely on general reports for biometric specifics.
2.  **Insurance Validity:** Confirm with a German insurance broker whether your current cyber policy voids coverage if data is stored on client-managed hardware outside your secure cloud, and whether the €15k estimate accurately reflects the risk exposure for facial recognition systems in critical infrastructure (data centers).
