**The Call:** Reject the current "$2 per verification charged to universities" model for the launch phase; it misaligns incentives by taxing the supply side (universities) before demand (employers) is proven, creating the exact adoption barrier you fear. Pivot immediately to a "Freemium Supply, Paid Demand" model: offer universities free unlimited issuances to remove friction, and charge employers a premium fee ($5–$15) per verified check or sell them subscription bundles.

**The Numbers:**
*Current Model Projection:*
Revenue = Verifications × Price per Unit
Base Case (3,000 requests/mo): 3,000 × $2 = **$6,000/mo**.
*Assumption (ASSUMED):* University registrar budgets for new, unproven vendor pilots are typically <$500/mo without a dedicated procurement cycle.
*Result:* At $6,000/mo implied cost (if volume scales) or even a fixed monthly equivalent, you exceed the informal spending limit for 4 of your 5 pilot universities, stalling onboarding.

*Alternative Model (Employer-Pays):*
Revenue = Employer Checks × Price per Check
Low Case ($5/check, 10% conversion of current traffic): 300 × $5 = **$1,500/mo**.
High Case ($15/check, 30% conversion): 900 × $15 = **$13,500/mo**.
*Assumption (ASSUMED):* Employer cost-per-background-check ranges from $30–$100; a $15 crypto-verification fee is <50% of standard alternatives, creating high willingness to pay.

*Unit Economics Sensitivity:*
If University Churn > 20% due to cost, Network Value drops disproportionately (Metcalfe's Law).
Break-even on University Acquisition Cost (CAC) requires Lifetime Value (LTV) > $2,000 per university.
Under current pricing, if a university processes only 500 checks/year ($1,000 revenue), you lose money on integration/support costs.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes this call is **Employer Verification Volume**.
*Trigger:* If employer-paid verifications do not reach 500/month within 90 days of switching to the free-university model, reconsider a hybrid "freemium" tier where universities pay only after exceeding 1,000 free checks/month.
*Rule of Thumb:* In two-sided marketplaces, subsidize the side that is harder to scale (universities/registrar inertia) and monetize the side with immediate budget authority (employers/HR).
*Step this week:* If your current 3,000 requests came from employers, contact the top 10 requesting organizations and propose a pilot subscription at $500/month for unlimited checks; if they agree, you have validated the demand-side pricing.
