**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The determining factor is the **economic substance** of the token, not its name. If the token grants ownership rights, profit sharing, or voting power, it is likely a security (equity). If it is purely a pre-payment for future software access with no secondary market speculation encouraged, it *might* be treated as deferred revenue (liability) rather than immediate income, though KRA may still tax upon receipt.
*   **Trigger:** The moment you market the token with promises of value appreciation or list it on an exchange before the platform is fully functional.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If the buyer expects profit from the efforts of others, it is a security.
