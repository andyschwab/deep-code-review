**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is the **"Claims-Made" vs. "Occurrence" trigger** and the presence of an **"Extended Reporting Period" (Tail)** endorsement in your specific contract.
*   **Trigger Rule:** If your policy is "claims-made" (standard for tech E&O) and the claim was filed after the policy expired or outside the reporting window, coverage is void.
*   **Decision Threshold:** If the time between audit completion and claim filing exceeds your policy's reporting limit (often 30–60 days post-engagement unless extended), assume denial.
*   **Actionable Step:** If your current policy lacks a "discovery period" >6 months, consider purchasing a specialized "cyber liability" rider or establishing a reserve fund equal to 20% of annual revenue ($60,000) by next quarter.
