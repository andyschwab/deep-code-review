**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The determining factor is not the lack of humans, but whether your software provides **individualized advice** or **discretionary management**.
*   **Trigger:** If your algorithm analyzes specific client data (risk tolerance, deposit size) to execute trades *on their behalf*, you are providing discretionary investment advice.
*   **The Flip:** If the software is merely a "tool" where the client explicitly approves every trade before execution, you might argue for a software exemption. However, your description ("automated rebalancing... to maximize returns") suggests discretionary authority.
