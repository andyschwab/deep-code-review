**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that changes your regulatory risk profile is **custody of funds**.
*   **Trigger:** If your platform holds buyer funds for **>0 hours** (even 24 hours) before settling with the farmer, regulators often view this as acting as a principal or broker-dealer rather than a passive conduit.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Pure technology facilitators typically never touch the principal; funds move directly from buyer to seller via a licensed third-party payment processor, with the fee deducted separately.
*   **Action This Week:** If you currently hold funds in escrow, **consider** integrating a direct settlement partner immediately to remove custody from your balance sheet, then document this architectural change before engaging regulators.
