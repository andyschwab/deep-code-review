**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The deciding factor is the specific "territorial scope" clause in your doctors' individual malpractice policies.
*   **Trigger:** If a single doctor treats a patient in a state where their policy does not explicitly cover telehealth delivery *into* that jurisdiction, you face uninsured liability.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Licensure compacts (like PSYPACT for psychologists or IMLC for physicians) grant the *legal right* to practice across borders but rarely mandate or provide *insurance* coverage. Insurance follows the policy language, not the license.
*   **This Week's Step:** If you have doctors already seeing patients across state lines, pause those specific cross-state sessions until you receive a certificate of insurance confirming coverage for the patient's location.
