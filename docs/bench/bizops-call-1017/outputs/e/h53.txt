**The Legal Landscape & Numbers:**
The core conflict lies in the intersection of the **Supremacy Clause** (federal law overrides state law) and the **FAA Modernization and Reform Act of 2012**, which preserved state authority over non-safety issues but generally preempted state laws regulating aviation *safety*.

*   **The Preemption Test:** Courts typically ask: Is the state law regulating *safety* (usually preempted if conflicting with FAA) or *privacy/liability/land use* (usually not preempted)?
