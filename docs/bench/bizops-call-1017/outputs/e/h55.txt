**The Call:** Reject the 2% settlement bonus immediately; this structure creates a high probability of violating Colorado Rule of Professional Conduct 1.5(e) (fee sharing with non-lawyers) and Rule 5.4 (professional independence), exposing both your firm and the client attorneys to disciplinary action. Pivot to a fixed "success fee" capped at a dollar amount or a tiered usage surcharge unrelated to the settlement percentage.

*   **Current Model:** Base ($500/mo) + 2% of Settlement.

    *   *Result:* Aligns price with value/effort without tying to the client's recovery %.
*   **Assumption (LABELLED ASSUMED):** Colorado follows the ABA Model Rules strictly regarding non-lawyer fee sharing (which it does).

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is the **method of calculation**. If the fee is calculated as a *percentage of the recovery*, it is likely impermissible fee sharing. If the fee is a *fixed amount* triggered by a win, it is generally permissible as a standard commercial bonus.
*   **Trigger:** Any contract clause stating "X% of settlement" must be removed before the next billing cycle.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Non-lawyers cannot share in the *profits* of a legal case, only charge for *services* rendered.
*   **Step this week:** If your current contracts contain percentage language, consider drafting an amendment to convert the Q4 $30,000 entitlement into a flat "consulting success fee" of equivalent value (if ethically defensible for past work) or waive the percentage portion to mitigate risk, then switch future contracts to a capped fixed bonus.
