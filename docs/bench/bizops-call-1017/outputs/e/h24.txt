**The Call**
Do not alter your per-bed pricing model to share savings; instead, immediately engage healthcare counsel to validate that your $150/bed/month rate constitutes "Fair Market Value" (FMV) via an independent appraisal and to structure your contracts under the "Electronic Health Records (EHR) Items and Services" or "Outcomes-Based Payment" safe harbors. Your current concern is valid: pricing tied explicitly to a percentage of savings generated can trigger Anti-Kickback Statute (AKS) liability, but charging a flat FMV fee for software that *incidentally* produces savings is generally permissible if properly documented.

**The Numbers**
There is no formula to compute "legal safety"; the metric here is the gap between your price and an independent FMV assessment.
*   **Current Revenue:** 300 beds × $150/bed × 12 months = **$540,000 ARR**.
*   **Runway:** $800,000 cash / ($540,000 ARR + estimated operating burn). *Assumption:* If your burn matches revenue, runway is ~18 months; if burn is higher, it shortens.
*   **Compliance Cost (ASSUMED):** $15,000–$25,000 for a one-time FMV appraisal and legal contract review.
*   **Risk Exposure:** Potential treble damages + civil penalties per violation (statutory amounts change; do not estimate without counsel).

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that flips this from "compliant SaaS" to "illegal inducement" is **linking your fee directly to the dollar amount of savings or bonuses the hospital receives**.
*   **Trigger:** If a contract clause states "VitalLink receives 20% of readmission penalty avoidance," you have crossed into prohibited remuneration territory unless strictly structured under the specific Outcomes-Based Payment safe harbor (which requires rigorous methodology and risk-sharing).
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Fees must be set in advance, not fluctuate based on the volume or value of referrals or federal healthcare program business generated.
*   **Step this week:** If your current drafts contain language tying fees to savings percentages, consider pausing signature and inserting a "subject to legal review of AKS safe harbors" clause.
