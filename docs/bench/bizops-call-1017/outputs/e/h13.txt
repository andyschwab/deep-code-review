**The Call**
Structure your premium tier as two distinct line items: a "Real-Time Streaming & Certification Service Fee" (uncapped, market-based) and a "Certified Transcript Production Fee" (strictly capped at $4.25/page). Do not bundle these into a single per-page or per-hour aggregate price, as commingling uncapped services with capped statutory fees creates a high risk of the entire package being re-characterized as a transcript fee by regulators.

**The Numbers**
Your revenue model relies on separating capped vs. uncapped components.
*   **Formula:** Total Booking Value = (Hourly Appearance Fee) + (Streaming/Tech Fee) + (Pages × $4.25).
*   **User Figures:** $175/hr (appearance), 15% commission, $85k quarterly revenue.
*   **Assumption (ASSUMED):** A standard 2-hour deposition generates 40 pages of transcript.
    *   *Current Model:* ($175 × 2) + (40 × $4.25) = $350 + $170 = $520 total. DepoTech take: $78.
    *   *Premium Model (Compliant):* Add a flat $150 "Real-Time Streaming" fee. Total = $350 + $150 + $170 = $670. DepoTech take: $100.50.
    *   *Risk Model (Non-Compliant Bundle):* If you charge $20/page for a "bundled premium transcript" (covering streaming + text), the effective rate ($20) exceeds the $4.25 cap. Regulators may view the $15.75 excess as an illegal overcharge on the statutory product.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The triggering input is **commingling**. If your invoice or marketing materials present the streaming and transcription as a single indivisible unit priced per page or per hour of testimony, the $4.25 cap likely applies to the whole.
*   **Trigger Rule:** If the line item description includes the word "transcript" alongside "streaming" with a single price, stop.
*   **Actionable Step:** This week, revise your invoice template to ensure the streaming fee is listed as a separate "technology/service" line item, distinct from "transcription services," before launching the premium tier.
