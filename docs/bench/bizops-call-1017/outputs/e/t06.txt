**Mechanics & Unknowns**
The mechanics generally require sourcing income to the physical location where the service was performed. For a worker moving between states, income must be split by time or mileage based on state-specific rules. The unknown variables include whether CA and NY recognize each other's credits for taxes paid, and if Texas imposes any withholding despite having no state income tax (it may still require reporting that triggers federal or other state actions). There is no single "interstate compact" that automatically solves gig-worker proration; most compacts apply to traditional employees with fixed bases.
