**Lane R: Regulation-Dependent (Route to Counsel)**

Your core question—whether generating standardized regulatory reports constitutes the unauthorized practice of law (UPL) in Singapore or the EU—is a **legal determination** that cannot be resolved through arithmetic or product logic alone. This falls strictly under **Lane R**.

**The Regime:**
1.  **Singapore:** The issue centers on the **Legal Profession Act** and interpretations by the Law Society of Singapore regarding what constitutes "practicing law." The boundary often hinges on whether the service applies legal knowledge to specific facts to provide a conclusion on rights/liabilities, versus merely automating a clerical formatting task.
2.  **EU Expansion:** Entering the EU introduces the **EU Services Directive** and individual member-state bars' rules on UPL. Additionally, because your product handles compliance data for financial entities, you must triage obligations under **MiFID II** (if reports influence investment decisions) and the **Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA)** regarding ICT third-party risk.

**Why You Must Route:**
No amount of standardization automatically grants immunity. Regulators and courts look at the **substance of the output**, not just the delivery method. If your software interprets ambiguous transaction data to determine *which* rule applies or *how* a specific regulation classifies an activity, it may be deemed legal judgment, regardless of whether the code is identical for every client. Conversely, if the software strictly maps user-provided data fields to a static government form without interpretation, it is more likely to be viewed as a clerical tool. Only a qualified attorney licensed in Singapore (and later, relevant EU jurisdictions) can review your specific algorithm, output samples, and terms of service to render a verdict on this boundary.

**Immediate Action Plan (Pre-Counsel):**
Before engaging counsel, prepare the following to make the consultation efficient and cost-effective:
*   **Sample Outputs:** Provide redacted examples of the PDF reports generated for different transaction types.
*   **Logic Flow:** Document exactly where human judgment enters the process versus where the code makes deterministic mappings.
*   **Terms of Service:** Bring your current disclaimer language stating the reports are "for informational purposes" or "not legal advice."
*   **Time-Sensitive Item:** Do not market or sell to EU clients until counsel confirms the UPL risk and any required local entity structures, as penalties for unauthorized practice can include injunctions and fines.

**Note:** While you wait for legal counsel, you can proceed with **Lane A** arithmetic on your unit economics (e.g., calculating runway based on your $50k MRR and burn rate) since those figures do not depend on the legal classification. However, do not finalize the EU expansion budget until the legal route is clear, as compliance costs may vary drastically based on the regime.
