**The Regime:**
The relevant regime is **State Unauthorized Practice of Law (UPL) Statutes and Bar Association Rules**. Each state (e.g., California Business and Professions Code § 6125; New York Judiciary Law § 478) defines the practice of law differently, though common elements often include:
1.  Applying legal principles to specific facts.
2.  Drafting legal instruments with binding effect.
3.  Advising on legal rights or obligations.

Regulators and courts typically analyze software tools using a functional test: Does the tool merely provide static forms (generally permissible), or does it engage in a dialogue, select clauses based on user inputs, or customize documents in a way that requires legal judgment (high UPL risk)?
