**Lane R: Regulation-Dependent Routing**

Your question regarding whether your governance tokens constitute an unregistered security is a **Lane R** matter. It depends entirely on legal determinations under U.S. securities law (specifically the **Securities Act of 1933** and SEC enforcement frameworks like the **Howey Test**). I cannot provide a "clear breakdown" that concludes whether you are safe or liable, nor can I tell you if the lack of a secondary market protects you. Only licensed counsel can interpret these facts against current case law and SEC guidance.

**Regime Name:** U.S. Securities Law (SEC Jurisdiction).
**Action Required:** You must consult a **securities litigation or compliance attorney** specializing in digital assets/blockchain before onboarding new users or distributing further tokens.

**Critical Unknowns to Resolve with Counsel**
Do not attempt to resolve these internally; bring these specific questions to your attorney:
1.  **Expectation of Profits:** Does the "future platform utility" you mentioned create an expectation of profit derived from the efforts of others (your development team), even without a secondary trading market? The SEC often argues that appreciation in token *utility* or *value* driven by the issuer's work satisfies this prong.
2.  **Investment of Money:** Does the $2,000 setup fee, when bundled with token receipt, constitute an "investment of money" where the token is the asset being purchased, rather than a fee for a service?
3.  **Common Enterprise:** Is there a coupling of fortunes between the HOA board members and VoteChain's success such that their returns (voting power/utility) depend on your managerial efforts?
4.  **Secondary Market Nuance:** While you state there is no secondary market, does the *potential* for future tradability or the ability to transfer tokens between board members create a de facto market that regulators could scrutinize?

**Immediate Next Steps**
1.  **Pause Distribution:** Do not distribute tokens to the next 50 HOAs until counsel reviews the current model.
2.  **Gather Documentation:** Prepare your whitepaper (if any), tokenomics model, marketing materials, and the specific language used in the HOA contracts regarding the tokens for your attorney's review.
3.  **Identify Specialist:** Seek a law firm with a dedicated "Digital Assets" or "Fintech Securities" practice group. General corporate counsel is likely insufficient for this specific regulatory boundary.
