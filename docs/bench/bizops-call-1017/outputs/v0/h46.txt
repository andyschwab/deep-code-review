**Regimes Involved:**
1.  **GDPR (UK/EU):** Voice recordings used for identification or unique characterization are often classified as **biometric data**, a "special category" of data requiring heightened protection. For children, the legal basis for processing is stringent. Retention is governed by the principle of "storage limitation" (data kept no longer than necessary), but "necessary" is a legal test based on your specific purpose, not a fixed number I can assign.
2.  **COPPA (US Federal):** Governs online collection of personal information from children under 13. It requires verifiable parental consent before collecting voice recordings. Retention is limited to "as long as reasonably necessary" to fulfill the purpose, but the definition of "reasonably necessary" for AI model training versus service delivery is a legal determination.
3.  **California Laws:** California has specific biometric privacy obligations and the Age-Appropriate Design Code, which imposes default high-privacy settings and limits on data retention for minors.
