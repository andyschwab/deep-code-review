**Regulatory Landscape Overview (For Discussion with Counsel)**
When you meet with an attorney specializing in non-profit law, you should discuss the following regimes and concepts that govern your situation:

1.  **IRS Prohibition on Contingency Fees:** The IRS generally prohibits paying fundraisers (including grant writers) a percentage of the funds raised. This is often viewed as "private inurement" (benefiting an individual over the charitable mission) or an impermissible use of charitable assets. Your counsel needs to evaluate if your 10% success fee triggers these prohibitions under federal tax law.
2.  **Foundation Guidelines:** Most major foundations explicitly forbid using grant awards to pay for fundraising costs, which often includes contingency fees. If your clients pay your fee directly from the grant award, they may be violating the grant agreement, potentially leading to clawbacks or disqualification.
3.  **State Solicitation Laws:** As a Chicago-based entity operating nationally, you may be subject to state charitable solicitation registration laws, some of which have specific rules about compensation structures for fundraising professionals.
