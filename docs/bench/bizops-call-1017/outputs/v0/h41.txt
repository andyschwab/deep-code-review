### Lane R: Regulatory Compliance (Route Immediately)
Your core question—whether you can market therapeutic efficacy claims without clinical trial data in a direct-to-consumer (DTC) model—falls strictly under **Lane R**. This is a regulatory determination involving the **U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)** and potentially the **Federal Trade Commission (FTC)**.

*   **The Regime:** Because NeuroStep is a virtual reality tool intended for the "rehabilitation of stroke victims," it likely meets the FDA definition of a **medical device** (possibly Class II). The intended use (treating a disease/condition) dictates regulation, not the sales channel (DTC vs. Insurance).
*   **The Risk:** Making therapeutic claims (e.g., "improves motor function after stroke") without FDA clearance (510(k) or De Novo) or substantial clinical evidence can trigger FDA warning letters, seizure of products, injunctions, and significant civil penalties. The FTC also prohibits unsubstantiated health claims in advertising.
*   **The Constraint:** I cannot tell you if your specific claims are permissible, what class your device is, or if an exemption applies. These are binding legal conclusions.
*   **Action:** You must engage regulatory counsel specializing in digital health/FDA law immediately. Do not launch marketing materials until counsel confirms your claim substantiation requirements.

*   **Unit Price:** $2,500 (one-time) + $150/month (recurring)

**Break-Even Analysis (Simplified):**
To cover your monthly burn solely through new hardware sales (ignoring subscription revenue for conservative estimation):
$$ \text{Units Needed per Month} = \frac{\text{Monthly Burn}}{\text{Hardware Price}} = \frac{45,000}{2,500} = 18 \text{ units} $$

If you include the first month's subscription ($150), the revenue per user is $2,650:
$$ \text{Units Needed} = \frac{45,000}{2,650} \approx 17 \text{ units} $$

**Sensitivity & Decision Aid:**

*   **The Reality Check:** If your COGS is $500/unit and CAC is $300/user, your net contribution per unit drops to $1,850 (hardware only).
    $$ \text{Adjusted Units Needed} = \frac{45,000}{1,850} \approx 25 \text{ units/month} $$
