### Lane A: Financial Implications (Arithmetic Applied to Your Numbers)

Your current unit economics show a contribution margin of **$170 per box** ($450 price − $280 variable cost). With a 4% monthly churn, your approximate customer lifetime is 25 months, yielding a gross Lifetime Value (LTV) of ~$4,250 per pharmacy before fixed costs.

Your DEA cap limits you to 60% of existing orders. Assuming your current volume matches your burn coverage, this supply shock effectively cuts your revenue-generating volume by 40% while your fixed burn ($35,000) likely remains static in the short term.

**Path 1: Raise Prices for Remaining Slots**
*   **The Math:** To maintain total contribution margin dollars with only 60% of the volume, the required price ($P_{new}$) solves: $0.6 \times Volume \times (P_{new} - 280) = 1.0 \times Volume \times (450 - 280)$.
*   **Result:** $P_{new} - 280 = 170 / 0.6 \approx 283$. Therefore, $P_{new} \approx \$563$.
*   **Implication:** You must raise prices by ~25% to $563 just to break even on contribution margin, assuming zero additional churn from the hike. If price sensitivity causes churn to rise above 4%, you will need an even higher price.
*   **Decision Aid:** If your customers view these ingredients as critical and non-substitutable, a price test on renewals may work. If LTV:CAC drops below 3 or payback exceeds 12 months under the new price, the model is broken.

**Path 2: Pause New Sign-ups**
*   **The Math:** This preserves cash but does not fix the margin deficit caused by the volume cap.
*   **Runway Check:** Current cash ($120,000) ÷ Monthly Burn ($35,000) = **~3.4 months runway**.
*   **Implication:** Without increasing margin per unit or reducing fixed burn, pausing sign-ups only delays the inevitable if the 40% revenue loss persists. You must reduce the $35,000 burn or increase the $170 margin immediately.

**Path 3: Seek a Waiver (Cost Unknown)**
*   **Financial Impact:** This path has high uncertainty. If successful, you restore 100% volume. If failed, you incur legal fees (Lane R) with no revenue recovery.
*   **Sensitivity:** If legal fees exceed $15,000, your runway drops to <3 months. This is a high-risk bet given your current cash position.

**Recommended Next Step:** Calculate the exact price elasticity with your top 10 customers immediately. If they accept $563, implement it for renewals only. Simultaneously, model a "survival burn" scenario where you cut the $35,000 fixed cost by 30% to extend runway to 5+ months while resolving the supply issue.
