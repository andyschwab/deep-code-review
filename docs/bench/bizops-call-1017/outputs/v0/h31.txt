### Lane A: Unit Economics & Pricing Structure (Apply)

**Current State Arithmetic**
*   **Gross Margin per Check:** $1.20 (Price) − $0.45 (Cost) = **$0.75** (62.5% margin).
*   **Churn Impact:** 15% monthly churn implies an average customer lifespan of ~6.7 months. High false positives (8%) are the stated driver.
*   **The Trade-off:** You cannot calculate the "optimal" price without quantifying the cost of a false positive (lost client revenue) versus the cost of a fine (unknown variable).

**Pricing Model Application**
Do not set a price; instead, structure the decision using these methods applied to your data:

1.  **Outcome-Based Pricing (Risk Sharing):**
    Instead of a flat $1.20, structure the fee as **Base + Penalty Credit**.
    *   *Formula:* `Effective Price = Base Rate − (False Positive Count × Credit Amount)`
    *   *Application:* If you charge $1.50 base but credit $10.00 for every verified false positive that causes a player block, you align incentives.
    *   *Sensitivity:* If your false positive rate stays at 8%, calculate the break-even credit amount where your margin hits zero.
        *   *Example:* At 1,000 checks, 80 are false positives. Gross profit before credits = $750. To maintain $0.50 margin/check ($500 total), total credits cannot exceed $250. Max credit per error = $250 / 80 = **$3.12**.
    *   *Decision Aid:* If clients value a blocked player at >$3.12, this model reduces churn. If regulatory fines exceed the cost of these credits, this model is dangerous.

2.  **Good-Better-Best Tiers (Volume & Thresholds):**
    Offer tiers based on risk tolerance rather than just volume.
    *   *Tier 1 (Strict):* Lower false positives (e.g., <2%), higher price ($2.00+). Target: High-compliance clients.
    *   *Tier 2 (Balanced):* Current stats (8% FP), current price ($1.20).
    *   *Tier 3 (Lenient):* Higher false positives, lower price, with a contractual cap on liability.
    *   *Application:* Map your current 15% churn to clients who need Tier 1 but are stuck in Tier 2.

**Decision Thresholds & Next Step**
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If LTV:CAC < 3 or Payback > 12 months, the current model is unsustainable. With 15% monthly churn, your LTV is severely compressed regardless of CAC.
*   **The Flip Input:** The variable that changes the answer is the **Cost of Regulatory Fine**. Without this number, you cannot optimize.
*   **Actionable Step:** This week, survey 3 churning clients: "What is the estimated revenue loss to you when one legitimate player is blocked?" Use this figure to model the "Credit Amount" in the outcome-based scenario above.
