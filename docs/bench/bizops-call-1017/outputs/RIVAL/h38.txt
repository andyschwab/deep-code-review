**Strategic Recommendations**

1.  **Renegotiate Staff Contracts Immediately:** If coaches are currently paid hourly regardless of attendance, the model is insolvent. You must shift to a "per completed session" compensation model or a lower base rate + performance bonus to align costs with realized revenue.
2.  **Mitigate No-Shows:** A 25% no-show rate is critical. Implement automated SMS reminders, confirmation calls, and a strict cancellation policy (where permissible under Medicaid rules) to push attendance above 85%.
3.  **Billing Code Investigation:** Standard fee-for-service codes often do not reimburse for missed appointments. You must investigate:
    *   **Care Management Codes:** Are there monthly care management codes (e.g., CCM, PCM) that pay a flat fee per patient per month regardless of session count? This would de-risk the no-show variable.
    *   **Administrative Add-ons:** Some state Medicaid plans allow specific modifiers for telehealth platform fees or care coordination, though these are rare for smoking cessation specifically.

**Critical Compliance Questions for a Specialist**
Do not proceed with billing changes without answers to these specific questions from a healthcare reimbursement attorney or specialist:
1.  **No-Show Billing:** Does Ohio Medicaid explicitly prohibit billing for missed appointments, or are there specific modifiers (e.g., for telehealth failures) that allow partial reimbursement?
2.  **Supplemental Payments:** Are there Ohio-specific "Health Home" or "Value-Based Care" incentives for smoking cessation outcomes that provide lump-sum bonuses separate from per-session fees?
3.  **Contractor vs. Employee:** If we restructure coach pay to "per session," does this change their legal classification from employee to independent contractor under Ohio labor laws, and what are the tax/liability implications?
4.  **Overhead Allocation:** Is there a valid billing code for "remote patient monitoring" or "digital therapeutic platform access" that covers the $10 overhead independently of the human coaching session?

**Conclusion**
Under your current structure (paying for scheduled time), you are losing money on every patient. The business is only viable if you can shift coaching costs to a "pay-per-completion" model or secure alternative monthly care management billing codes that ignore session-level volatility.
