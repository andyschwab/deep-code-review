Your current $2,000/hour model implies a high-stakes environment where procedural perfection is non-negotiable.

**1. Pre-Session Technical Verification (The "Competency" Check)**
Courts may overturn judgments if a party claims they couldn't participate effectively due to technical confusion. You must mandate a **Technical Competency Certification** 48 hours before any hearing.
*   **Action:** Require all participants to complete a standardized 15-minute simulation in your VR environment that tests audio clarity, movement, evidence viewing, and objection protocols.
*   **Deliverable:** Generate a signed "Technical Readiness Certificate" for each participant, logged on the case file. If a party fails this, the hearing cannot proceed, shifting liability for delays to the unprepared party rather than your platform.

**2. Immutable Session Logging (The "Record")**
A claim of "lag" or "missing testimony" requires an indisputable record. Standard video recording is insufficient for VR.
*   **Action:** Implement **Volumetric Session Recording**. Unlike 2D video, this captures the 3D spatial data of the session, allowing a reviewing judge to "re-enter" the room from any angle to verify sightlines and audio proximity.
*   **Action:** Deploy **Network Integrity Logging**. Continuously log latency (ping), packet loss, and frame rates for every user every 5 seconds. If a user's connection drops below a defined threshold (e.g., >150ms latency), the system must automatically pause the proceedings and log the interruption.
*   **Deliverable:** A cryptographically hashed "Case Ledger" that combines the volumetric recording, network logs, and chat transcripts into a single tamper-evident file stored on immutable storage (e.g., WORM storage or blockchain-anchored hash).

**3. Identity and Presence Verification**
To prevent claims that an unauthorized person participated or that a participant was not attentive:
*   **Action:** Integrate **Continuous Biometric Authentication**. Use VR headset data (hand tracking patterns, voice biometrics) to continuously verify that the registered attorney is the one speaking and acting, not a substitute.
*   **Action:** Implement **Gaze and Attention Tracking**. Log where participants are looking during evidence presentation. If a lawyer claims they "didn't see" a document, the logs can prove their avatar was facing it.

**4. Procedural Safeguards for "Virtual Objections"**
*   **Action:** Create a dedicated, low-latency "Objection Channel." When an objection is raised, the system must instantly freeze the opposing counsel's audio/video feed while preserving the objector's ability to speak to the arbitrator.
*   **Deliverable:** An automated "Ruling Log" that timestamps every objection, the immediate system response (freeze/unfreeze), and the arbitrator's verbal ruling, ensuring no prejudice occurred during the delay.

**5. The "Fair Hearing" Affidavit**
*   **Action:** At the conclusion of every session, require the Arbitrator and all parties to sign a digital affidavit confirming that no technical impediments prevented their full participation, referencing the specific Session ID and Network Log hash.

By shifting from a passive hosting model to an active **procedural guardian** model, you transform potential bugs into documented procedural events. This data provides the evidentiary basis for a judge to dismiss appeals claiming unfairness, as the record will objectively prove whether due process was maintained or if a party failed to meet technical requirements.
