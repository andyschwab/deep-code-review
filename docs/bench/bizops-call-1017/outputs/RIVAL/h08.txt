**Critical Compliance Warning:** Your current flat-fee model (£500 per case regardless of complexity) presents a **high risk of non-compliance** with UK solicitor conduct rules, specifically the SRA Standards and Regulations regarding fee transparency and the prohibition of misleading charges.

**1. Compliance Assessment**
Under the SRA Code of Conduct, solicitors must ensure fees are "fair and reasonable" and clearly reflect the service provided. A rigid flat fee that ignores a 20x variance in resource consumption (2 hours vs. 40 hours) creates two distinct violations:
*   **Overcharging:** Charging £500 for a 2-hour automated task may be deemed disproportionate to the value delivered and the cost incurred, violating the principle of fairness.
*   **Misleading Pricing:** If the fee implies a standard level of human review or complexity that does not exist for simple cases, it breaches transparency rules. The regulatory warning you received confirms this exposure.

There is **no specific "safe harbor"** in UK regulations that exempts AI-driven legal tech from these principles simply because it is technology-assisted. Fixed fees are permitted, but only if they are a genuine reflection of the *average* cost and risk, or if the variation in complexity is negligible. A 20x variance is not negligible.

**2. Required Structural Changes**
You must move away from a single blind flat fee. To maintain compliance while preserving pricing simplicity, adopt one of the following models:
*   **Tiered Fixed Fees:** Create three distinct bands (e.g., Standard, Complex, High-Complexity) based on objective criteria (e.g., number of applicants, visa type, evidence volume). This aligns price with foreseeable work.
*   **Hybrid Model:** A lower base technology fee (£150) plus a variable component based on actual AI compute time or human review hours triggered.
*   **Capped Hourly:** An hourly rate for the AI/human hybrid time with a hard cap that acts as the "fixed" quote for the client.

**3. Mandatory Disclosures for End-Clients**
To ensure transparency and protect your partner firms, the following disclosures must be explicit in the client care letter or engagement terms:
*   **Service Definition:** Clearly state that the service is "technology-assisted" and define the extent of human involvement (e.g., "AI-driven analysis with solicitor oversight only for complex flags").
*   **Fee Basis:** Explain exactly how the fee is calculated. If using tiers, list the criteria determining the tier. If using a flat fee, explicitly state what is included and what triggers additional charges.
*   **Variability Warning:** Disclose that while the fee is fixed, the underlying computational work varies, and the fee represents an average cost across similar cases.
*   **Complaints & Redress:** Provide clear pathways for clients to challenge the fee if they believe the case complexity did not justify the charge.

**4. Immediate Action Plan**
Do not finalize next quarter's contracts under the current model.
1.  **Pause** the single £500 fee immediately for new engagements.
2.  **Implement Tiers:** Segment your 1,200 annual cases into complexity buckets and assign appropriate fixed fees to each.
3.  **Update Contracts:** Rewrite terms to explicitly define the "scope" of the fixed fee, ensuring it matches the tiered service level.
4.  **Partner Indemnity:** Ensure your contracts with law firms indemnify them against regulatory action resulting from your pricing structure, though this does not stop the SRA from investigating the firms themselves.
