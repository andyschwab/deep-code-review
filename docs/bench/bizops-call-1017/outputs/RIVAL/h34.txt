**Immediate Operational Steps to Mitigate Risk**

1.  **Pause and Isolate:** Immediately implement a temporary hold on processing any document flagged by your system as containing potential PII (Social Security Numbers or National IDs). Do not process these manually until a secure protocol is established.
2.  **Implement Secure Redaction (Do Not Reject):** Do not build a filter to reject documents. Rejecting leases disrupts your clients' critical workflows and damages trust. Instead, build an automated secure redaction workflow. When sensitive data is detected, the system should automatically redact the specific data points (e.g., masking all but the last four digits) and process the remainder of the document.
3.  **Notify and Educate:** Proactively inform your existing enterprise clients about this new security feature. Frame it as a value-add enhancement to your compliance posture, not a reaction to a failure. This turns a liability risk into a trust-building opportunity.
4.  **Data Segregation:** Ensure that any redacted data logs are stored in a separate, highly encrypted environment with strict access controls, distinct from the main lease abstraction data.

**Legal and Compliance Counsel**

Your current Terms of Service are almost certainly **insufficient** for this specific risk. Standard SaaS terms often disclaim liability for user-uploaded content, but they rarely cover the specific nuances of *processing* and *potentially exposing* regulated PII like SSNs or UK National Insurance numbers.

You must engage specialized legal counsel immediately. You operate in two high-stakes jurisdictions:
*   **New York (USA):** Subject to NY SHIELD Act and potentially GLBA depending on the financial nature of the data.
*   **London (UK):** Subject to GDPR, which has extremely strict requirements for processing special category data and heavy fines for breaches.

General corporate counsel is not enough; you need a firm specializing in cross-border data privacy and PropTech. They must draft specific Data Processing Addendums (DPAs) and update your privacy policy to explicitly state how PII is detected, redacted, and stored. Do not launch the redaction workflow until these documents are reviewed.
