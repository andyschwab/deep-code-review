3.  **Bridge Financing:** Use the amended contracts (showing $75k–$150k in receivables) to secure a debt line or bridge round to cover the remaining 6–8 month gap.

**Accounting Rules & CPA Requirements:**

*   **Standard Startup Practice:** Hardware startups typically recognize revenue on physical delivery if title and risk of loss transfer to the customer, regardless of software status, provided the hardware has standalone value.
*   **Requires CPA Input (ASC 606):**
    1.  **Separation of Obligations:** Determining if the dispenser and monitoring service are distinct performance obligations. If bundled incorrectly, you may be forced to defer *all* revenue until the software is active.
    2.  **Variable Consideration:** If refunds are likely due to the delay, revenue may need to be constrained.
    3.  **Inventory Capitalization:** Ensuring storage costs are expensed immediately (period costs) rather than capitalized into inventory value, which affects your P&L timing.

Do not recognize revenue based on "pre-sales" alone if the goods are not delivered; this violates GAAP and risks audit failure.

*Consult a CPA immediately to draft the contract language that legally separates hardware delivery from software activation to enable earlier revenue recognition.*
