**Compliant Fee Structure Breakdown for DepoTech**

Based on California regulations, the $4.25 per-page cap applies **strictly to the original certified transcript**. It does not cap appearance fees, real-time streaming, or immediate certification services, provided these are billed as distinct line items. To introduce your premium tier without violating statutes, you must unbundle the package value.

**Recommended "Premium" Structure:**
1.  **Appearance Fee:** Bill the reporter's hourly rate (e.g., $175/hr) separately. This is uncapped.
2.  **Real-Time Streaming:** Charge a separate hourly or flat fee for the remote connection and instant feed. This is a technology/service fee, not a transcription fee, and is exempt from the per-page cap.
3.  **Expedited/Immediate Certification:** Charge a separate flat fee or percentage surcharge for the turnaround speed.
4.  **Certified Transcript:** Bill the final pages at or below $4.25/page.

**Crucial Compliance Rule:** Never present a single "all-in" price per page that exceeds $4.25. If you bundle these into one lump sum divided by page count, regulators may interpret the excess as an illegal surcharge on the statutory rate. Your invoice must clearly delineate: *Service A (Uncapped) + Service B (Uncapped) + Transcript (Capped).*

**Revenue Strategy:**
Since you operate on a 15% commission, your revenue grows by increasing the *uncapped* components. Encourage reporters to sell the "Remote Real-Time Experience" rather than just the transcript. By shifting value perception to the streaming technology and immediate access (which have no price ceilings), you increase the total booking value while keeping the statutory transcript line item compliant.

**Statutory Clarity vs. Legal Interpretation Required**

*Explicitly Stated in Public Statutes (No interpretation needed):*
*   The maximum charge for an **original certified transcript** is fixed ($4.25/page).
*   Appearance fees for attending a deposition are permissible and not subject to the per-page cap.
*   Charges for copies of transcripts (non-original) have their own separate, distinct caps.

*Requires Legal Expert Interpretation:*
