**Unknown Variables in Interstate Compacts**
There is no single "interstate compact" that standardizes gig worker withholding across CA, NY, and TX. The unknowns are:
*   **De Minimis Thresholds:** How many days/hours trigger withholding in NY for a non-resident? (Often 1-2 days, but varies).
*   **Reciprocity Agreements:** CA and NY generally do not have reciprocity for gig work; TX has no income tax but may have other fees.
*   **Sourcing Rules:** Whether income is sourced to the customer's location, the worker's location, or the platform's server location.
