The deciding factor is the **insurance carrier's specific definition of "practice location."**

*   **Rule of Thumb:** Licensure compacts (like PSYPACT for psychologists or IMLC for physicians) allow a doctor to *practice* across borders, but they rarely mandate a unified *insurance* product.
