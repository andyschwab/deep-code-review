**Recommendation: Prioritize completion rates; do not roll out the 6:00 AM time globally yet.**

The data reveals a classic vanity trap: the 6:00 AM slot drives **engagement** (opens) but fails to proportionally drive **value** (workouts). A 12% lift in opens yielding only a 3% lift in completions indicates that the earlier time captures attention but not action. Users are likely opening the notification out of habit or reflex upon waking, then dismissing it without exercising, or perhaps working out later without logging it immediately as you suspected. If you optimize for open rates, you will successfully annoy 50,000 users earlier in the morning without significantly increasing their fitness outcomes.

Your assumption that early openers are "more motivated" is contradicted by the flat completion curve. If they were truly more motivated, the conversion from open to workout would match or exceed the 7:30 AM group. Instead, the 6:00 AM slot appears to be generating "false positive" engagement—users interacting with the app but not deriving the core value. For a fitness product, the North Star is completed workouts, not notification opens. Scaling a strategy that increases noise (opens) without moving the needle on value (completions) risks increasing churn due to notification fatigue.

Regarding the "snoozing" hypothesis: if 7:30 AM users are simply working out later without immediate logging, your current event taxonomy is blind to this behavior. However, until you instrument a way to capture delayed logging, you must treat the recorded completion rate as the only verified measure of value delivery. You cannot optimize for a behavior you are not measuring.

**This-week steps:**
1.  **Instrument "Delayed Completion":** Add an event property to track workouts started within 2 hours of notification open but logged later, to validate the snoozing hypothesis.
2.  **Cohort Analysis:** Segment the 6:00 AM group by those who opened but didn't complete; survey a sample to ask if they worked out offline or forgot to log.
3.  **Decision Trigger:** If delayed logging accounts for >8% of the missing conversion gap, reconsider the 6:00 AM slot; otherwise, stick with 7:30 AM or test intermediate times.

The VP should halt the global rollout until the gap between opens and completions is explained or closed.
