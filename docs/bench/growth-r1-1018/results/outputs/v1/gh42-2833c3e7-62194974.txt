**Recommendation: Adopt "Weekly Completed Intensity Sessions" as your North Star Metric.**

Do not use "Total Minutes" (vanity; rewards low-intensity loitering) or a "Composite Score" (unverifiable; 50% sensor gap creates data noise that investors will reject). Your North Star must rise only when a user receives core value. For FitVerse, value is a completed, effective workout, not just time spent in a headset.

**Definition:** A "Completed Intensity Session" is a user-initiated workout lasting ≥20 minutes (adjustable based on validation) where the user maintains a target heart rate zone for at least 60% of the duration.
*   **Why this works:** It filters out casual users who put the headset on but don't sweat, directly addressing your suspicion that duration alone is misleading. It aligns with your hypothesis that finishing a session drives renewal.
*   **Handling the Sensor Gap:** Do not exclude the 50% without sensors. For these users, define a proxy "Intensity" using in-app performance metrics (e.g., controller velocity, range of motion, or game-level completion) calibrated to match the heart-rate data of your sensor-equipped cohort. This ensures the metric covers 100% of your DAU.
