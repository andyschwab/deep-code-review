**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Listeners completing a "Value Session" (defined as listening ≥10 minutes to a room where ≥2 distinct speakers contribute). This rejects vanity metrics like total rooms or raw downloads, focusing strictly on the delivery of core value: meaningful social audio consumption.

**Input Metrics:**
1.  **Listener Retention Cohorts:** % of users returning in Week 2 after their first "Value Session."
2.  **Content Signal-to-Noise Ratio:** % of created rooms that achieve ≥3 listeners within 15 minutes of creation.
3.  **Creator Activation:** % of new room creators who host a second room within 7 days.

**Diagnosis (Bottom-Up AARRR):**
Stop analyzing acquisition or total room count immediately. Your data indicates a **retention leak** driven by a supply-quality mismatch. Doubling room supply while session duration drops 36% (22m → 14m) suggests you have flooded the ecosystem with "noise" (empty or low-quality rooms), making it harder for listeners to find value. In a two-sided network, more supply only helps if demand can discover quality; currently, discovery friction is likely spiking. Prioritize **listener satisfaction** signals over creator activity volume. If listeners leave faster, creator activity becomes futile regardless of volume.

**Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only These Questions):**
Do not instrument "everything." Deploy these specific events to answer named questions:
*   `room_quality_signal`: Fires when a room hits ≥3 concurrent listeners. *Question: Is new supply discoverable and engaging?*
*   `session_depth`: Fires at 5m, 10m, 15m marks with `room_creator_id` and `listener_id`. *Question: Are specific creators driving the duration drop?*
*   `search_to_join_latency`: Time between search/opening feed and joining a room. *Question: Is discovery friction increasing due to noise?*
*   `repeat_creator_action`: Fires when a user hosts a 2nd room. *Question: Are we retaining creators who actually build audiences?*

**Identifier Seam:** Ensure `listener_id` and `creator_id` are stable pseudonymous IDs to track cross-session behavior. Do not use raw PII; route consent and data retention policies to your privacy compliance review.

**This Week's Steps:**
1.  **Segment the Drop:** Query session duration by "Room Quality Signal" (rooms with <3 listeners vs. >3). Hypothesis: Duration collapsed specifically in low-traffic rooms, confirming the "noise" theory.
2.  **Cohort Retention Check:** Pull Week-2 retention for users who joined only "low signal" rooms vs. "high signal" rooms in the last 30 days. If the former is <20% of the latter, your roadmap must prioritize discovery algorithms or room gating over creator tools.
3.  **Define the Tripwire:** If <40% of new rooms achieve the "Quality Signal" (3 listeners) within 24 hours, halt any features encouraging mass room creation and shift engineering resources to feed ranking or "featured room" curation.
