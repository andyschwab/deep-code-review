1.  **`step_repeat_count` (Integer):** Capture how many times the user requested the *same* instruction before marking it complete.
    *   **Why:** This is the direct signal of friction in a hands-free context. If users cannot hear or understand the command due to kitchen noise or cognitive load, they will ask for a repeat. A high average here invalidates the "short and direct" assumption.
    *   **Question Answered:** "Are users struggling to hear or process instructions while cooking?"

2.  **`inter_step_duration_seconds` (Integer):** Capture the elapsed time between the audio output of the previous step and the voice command completing the current step.
    *   **Why:** This distinguishes between active cooking (natural delay) and abandonment (extreme delay or session end). Unlike raw timestamps, this delta isolates the specific step's friction.
    *   **Question Answered:** "Did the user abandon the recipe mid-flow, or did they simply take time to cook?"

3.  **`completion_confidence_score` (Float/Enum):** Capture the system's confidence level that the user's voice command matched the expected completion phrase (e.g., "Next," "Done," "Step completed").
    *   **Why:** In noisy kitchens, false positives (system thinks you said "Next" when you didn't) or false negatives (system ignores you) break the flow. This metric isolates environmental noise issues from content issues.
    *   **Question Answered:** "Is ambient kitchen noise causing the system to misinterpret completion commands?"
