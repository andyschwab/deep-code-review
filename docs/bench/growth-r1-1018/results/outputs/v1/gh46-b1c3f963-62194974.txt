Here are the five critical events to instrument for ScribbleAI's MVP, prioritized to measure real value (retention and core action) rather than vanity. Each event includes the specific question it answers.

1.  **`suggestion_accepted`**
    *   **Definition:** Triggered when a user clicks "Accept" or uses the keyboard shortcut to insert an AI-generated suggestion into their document without further modification.
    *   **Question Answered:** "Is the AI output immediately usable enough that users trust it on the first try?" (Measures baseline model utility).

2.  **`suggestion_edited_then_accepted`**
    *   **Definition:** Triggered when a user modifies the text of an AI suggestion (e.g., deletes words, rephrases sentences) *before* finalizing/inserting it into the document.
    *   **Question Answered:** "Are users engaging deeply to co-create value, indicating the tool is a partner rather than just a generator?" (Your hypothesized stronger engagement signal).

3.  **`core_session_return`**
    *   **Definition:** Triggered when a unique user ID initiates a new session containing at least one interaction (view, edit, or accept) within 7 days of their previous session.
    *   **Question Answered:** "Are users retaining and returning to the workflow?" (This is your primary retention metric; without this, acquisition is wasted).

4.  **`workflow_mode_start`**
    *   **Definition:** Triggered at the start of a session with a property `mode: "drafting"` or `mode: "editing"`, determined by whether the document was empty or had >50 words upon opening.
    *   **Question Answered:** "Which use case (drafting vs. editing) drives our actual retention?" (Resolves your 50/50 beta uncertainty to focus product development).

5.  **`suggestion_rejected`**
    *   **Definition:** Triggered when a user explicitly clicks "Reject," "Dismiss," or closes a suggestion box without inserting the text.
    *   **Question Answered:** "How often does the model fail to provide value?" (Essential for calculating the net success rate and identifying model degradation).

**Implementation Notes:**
*   **Identifier Seam:** Ensure every event carries a stable, pseudonymous `user_id` to track cohorts across sessions; do not use raw PII.
*   **Vanity Exclusion:** Do not instrument `page_view`, `time_on_site`, or `total_suggestions_shown` yet; these rise regardless of value and will distract from the core retention signal.
