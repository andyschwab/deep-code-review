### Balanced Scorecard for Business License Renewal Portal

To satisfy taxpayer accountability and citizen experience, replace the single metric of "cost reduction" with four outcome-based measures. These metrics distinguish between true efficiency and process abandonment.

**1. Completion Rate (The Truth Metric)**
*   **Definition:** The percentage of users who start a renewal or application and successfully receive their license.
*   **Why it matters:** A drop in call volume is only good if completions stay steady or rise. If completions drop while calls drop, users are silently abandoning the process.
*   **Target:** Maintain or exceed the legacy system's completion rate. Any decline requires immediate investigation.

**2. End-to-End Processing Time**
*   **Definition:** The average time from when a business starts the application to when the license is issued.
*   **Why it matters:** This directly measures the speed benefit to the citizen. It captures both the time spent filling out forms and any backend processing delays.
*   **Target:** A measurable reduction compared to the previous manual or legacy timeline (e.g., "Renewals completed within 24 hours").

**3. First-Time Applicant Success Rate**
*   **Definition:** The specific completion rate for new businesses creating an account for the first time.
*   **Why it matters:** Your migrated users know the process; new users do not. This isolates whether the new portal is accessible to growing businesses or creating a barrier to entry.
*   **Target:** Parity with the rate for existing users. A significant gap indicates onboarding friction.

**4. Assisted Digital Support Ratio**
*   **Definition:** The number of help requests (calls/emails) per 100 successful completions.
*   **Why it matters:** This refines your "call volume" metric. Raw call drops could mean fewer users or silent failure. This ratio tells you how much human support is still required to achieve one successful digital outcome.
*   **Target:** A steady downward trend as the system matures, indicating increased self-sufficiency.

**Reporting Recommendation for the Audit**
Present these metrics as a cohesive story: "We reduced costs (Support Ratio) only because citizens are finishing faster (Processing Time) without getting stuck (Completion Rate)." If call volume is down but Completion Rate is also down, the narrative must shift to "We have a hidden abandonment problem," rather than claiming success.

**Note:** These metrics require instrumenting the specific steps of the application flow to distinguish a "start" from a "finish."
**Note:** Ensure all user tracking uses pseudonymous identifiers to comply with privacy standards while allowing cohort analysis.
