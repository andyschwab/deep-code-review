### Experiment Plan: Ad Frequency Optimization

**1. Leading Indicators (First 48 Hours)**
Do not wait for Day-7 retention; it is a lagging metric. Monitor these leading proxies for churn immediately:
*   **Session Frequency Drop:** A statistically significant decrease in sessions per user per day compared to the control group. If users see more ads but play fewer sessions, they are disengaging.
*   **Early Session Abandonment:** The rate of users closing the app within 30 seconds of launching (specifically after the new interstitial triggers).
*   **Level Start-to-Complete Ratio:** If the extra ad interrupts flow, completion rates on levels immediately following the ad will dip before overall retention cracks.
*   **Uninstall Rate (if trackable):** Spike in uninstalls within 24 hours of exposure.

**2. Sample Size Calculation**
To detect a **2% relative drop** in Day-7 retention (from a baseline of 22% down to 21.56%) with 80% power and 95% confidence:
*   **Baseline Retention ($p_1$):** 0.22
*   **Target Retention ($p_2$):** 0.2156
*   **Required Sample Size:** Approximately **1.3 million users per variant**.
*   **Reality Check:** With only 150,000 DAU, even allocating 50% to control and 50% to treatment yields only ~75k users per group. Over a 7-day window, you cannot accumulate 1.3 million *unique* users unless you extend the test duration significantly or accept very low statistical power.
*   **Adjustment:** You cannot reliably detect a 2% *relative* drop in 7 days with your current traffic. You must either:
    *   Increase the **Minimum Detectable Effect (MDE)** to ~6-7% relative drop (detecting a drop from 22% to ~20.5%), which requires ~150k total users over 7 days (feasible with your 10% split if run for 2 weeks, or a larger split for 1 week).
    *   Accept that this is a **high-risk test** where you are monitoring for catastrophic failure rather than precise measurement of small effects.

**3. Success Criteria & Decision Logic**
Pre-commit to these rules before launch to avoid "peeking" bias:

*   **Kill Switch (Stop Immediately):** If Day-1 retention drops by >5% relative to control OR if session frequency drops by >3% in the first 24 hours. This indicates immediate user revolt.
*   **Fail Criteria (Do Not Rollout):** If Day-7 retention shows *any* statistically significant negative trend at the end of the test period, or if the calculated Revenue Per User (RPU) gain is less than the projected Lifetime Value (LTV) loss from the retention dip.
*   **Success Criteria (Rollout):** If Day-7 retention is statistically neutral (within confidence intervals of control) AND total RPU increases by >3%.

**Decision Call:** Given your Q3 revenue pressure and the inability to detect small retention drops quickly, **do not launch to 10% next Tuesday** unless you widen the MDE expectation. Launching a test designed to find a 2% drop with insufficient power creates a false sense of security; you might miss the churn until it's too late. Instead, launch to **50% of users** for a shorter duration (3-4 days) to maximize data velocity, focusing strictly on the "Kill Switch" metrics above. If the leading indicators hold, proceed; if they wobble, kill it immediately to protect the core user base.

*Note: All metrics must be compared against your own concurrent control group, not historical baselines, to account for seasonality. Ensure user IDs are pseudonymized for cohort tracking.*
