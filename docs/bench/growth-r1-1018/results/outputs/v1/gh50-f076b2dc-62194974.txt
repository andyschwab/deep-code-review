### Retention Funnel Design: SeoulPlay RPG (December Launch)

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Users completing ≥3 PvP battles (value delivered), not raw installs or tutorial starts.

**Diagnostic Funnel (Bottom-Up Retention Focus)**
Track these time-bound milestones to isolate friction between "Tutorial Start" and "First PvP Win." Do not instrument generic "screen views"; only track events that answer specific friction hypotheses.

| Funnel Step | Event Name (Trigger) | Time Bound | Diagnostic Question | Input Metric to Move North Star |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- :--- |
| **1. Tutorial Engagement** | `tutorial_step_complete` | Per step (0–10 min) | Is the tutorial too long or boring? | Tutorial Completion Rate |
| **2. Tutorial Exit** | `tutorial_quit` | < 15 min | Where exactly do they drop? (Map to step ID) | Drop-off % per step |
| **3. First PvP Entry** | `pvp_queue_join` | < 60 min | Is the barrier to entry (UI/Matchmaking) too high? | % of Tutorial Completers entering queue |
| **4. First PvP Outcome** | `pvp_battle_result` | < 65 min | Is the difficulty spike causing immediate failure? | Win Rate (Target: >30% for new users) |
| **5. Activation Threshold** | `first_pvp_win` | < 60 min | **Core Hypothesis:** Does this predict D7 retention? | **Activation Rate** (Current: 30%) |
| **6. Early Retention** | `session_start` | Day 1, Day 3, Day 7 | Did activation stick? | D1/D3/D7 Retention Cohorts |

**Instrumentation Rules**
*   **Properties Required:** Every event must include `user_id` (pseudonymous/stable), `session_id`, `tutorial_step_id` (if applicable), and `pvp_match_id`.
*   **The Identifier Seam:** Use a stable, pseudonymous user ID to track cross-session behavior. Do not log PII (names, emails) in analytics events; route consent management to privacy compliance protocols.
*   **Hypothesis Testing:** To distinguish between "length," "difficulty," and "rewards," add a `quit_reason` property to `tutorial_quit` if explicit exit buttons exist, or correlate `tutorial_step_id` with `average_playtime` to find the exact step where the 12-minute average accumulates.

**This Week's Actions (For Sprint Planning)**
1.  **Define Event Schema:** Finalize the JSON schema for the 6 events above, ensuring `tutorial_step_id` is granular enough to pinpoint the exact screen where the 12-minute drop-off clusters.
2.  **Build Activation Cohort Query:** Create a dashboard query comparing D7 retention of users who hit `first_pvp_win` < 60 min vs. those who did not, to validate the core hypothesis before launch.
3.  **Instrumentation Gap Check:** Verify current logs capture `pvp_queue_join`; if missing, prioritize this engineering ticket to distinguish matchmaking friction from gameplay difficulty.

*Note: The 45% D1 baseline is historical context; do not use it as a success threshold for the new title without validating the new funnel's baseline post-launch. All metric targets (e.g., 55% D1) must be validated against live post-launch data, not pre-launch assumptions.*
