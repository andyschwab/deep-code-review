**Experiment Design: Pricing Tier A/B Test**

**Primary Metric:** Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) across the entire trial cohort.
**Guardrail Metric:** Conversion rate to the $99 Enterprise tier (must not drop >5% relative to control).
**North Star Input:** Weekly Active Galleries uploading inventory (ensures paid users derive value).

**Test Structure**
*   **Control (50%):** Current binary choice ($19 Basic, $99 Enterprise).
*   **Treatment (50%):** Three-tier choice ($19 Basic, $49 Professional, $99 Enterprise).
*   **Duration:** 6 weeks (Feb 1 – Mar 15). Do not peek at results before Mar 15. Early reads inflate false positives; a "win" on day 10 is `UNVERIFIED`.

**Sample Size & Power**
With 2,000 users total (1,000 per arm) and typical SaaS conversion variance, you can reliably detect a **15–20% relative lift in ARPU**.
*   *Calculation logic:* If current ARPU is ~$30 (weighted), a 20% lift is +$6. Detecting smaller lifts (e.g., 5%) requires significantly more traffic than you possess.
*   *Decision Rule:* If the Treatment ARPU is higher than Control AND the Enterprise conversion guardrail holds, ship. If ARPU rises but Enterprise drops >5%, the mid-tier is cannibalizing high-value revenue; do not ship.

**Risk Mitigation**
1.  **Cannibalization Check:** Explicitly track migration intent. If users who *would* have bought $99 now choose $49, your net revenue declines even if conversion volume rises. The guardrail metric catches this.
2.  **Choice Paralysis:** Monitor "No Purchase" rate. If the third option causes decision fatigue and total conversions drop, the test fails regardless of ARPU.
3.  **Cohort Stability:** Ensure the 2,000 users are randomized strictly by user ID upon trial start. Do not segment by gallery size unless you have 500+ users per segment, which you likely do not.

**Required Instrumentation (Event Taxonomy)**
Instrument only these events to answer specific questions:
*   `pricing_page_viewed`: Did they see the options?
*   `plan_selected`: Which tier was clicked? (Properties: `plan_tier`, `user_id`).
*   `payment_completed`: Did the transaction finish? (Properties: `plan_tier`, `revenue_amount`).
*   `core_action_completed`: (North Star input) Did they upload 5+ artworks post-purchase?

**This Week's Steps**
1.  **Define the Break-Even Tripwire:** Calculate the exact Enterprise churn rate that negates the $49 revenue gain. (e.g., "If Enterprise conversions drop by 12 users, the $49 gains are wiped out"). Set this as your hard stop.
2.  **Lock the Analysis Plan:** Pre-commit to reading the data **once** on March 15. Document this date and the primary/guardrail metrics in your experiment tracker now to prevent "peeking" bias.
3.  **Verify Randomization:** Confirm your engineering team can assign users to Control/Treatment buckets persistently before Feb 1.

**Note:** No benchmark for "good" ARPU is provided; success is defined strictly by outperforming your own Control group.
**Note:** Ensure user IDs used for cohorting are pseudonymous; route any PII handling to your privacy compliance protocol.
