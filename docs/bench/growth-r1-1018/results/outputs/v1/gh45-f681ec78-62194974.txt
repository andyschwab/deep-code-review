### Trust Health Dashboard: Leading Indicators & Composite Metrics

Your current "dispute rate" is a lagging failure metric; it tells you when trust broke, not when it is forming. To monitor community health in real-time, you must measure the **micro-commitments** users make before a transaction occurs. Trust in a high-stakes marketplace like PeerRent is built during the discovery and negotiation phases, not after the gear is returned.

**1. The North Star: "Verified Conversion Delta"**
Replace the static "8% acceptance lift" with a dynamic cohort metric. Track the **acceptance rate of rental requests** segmented by the *specific combination* of trust signals present in the lender's profile.
*   **Metric:** Acceptance Rate for (Verified ID + Insurance Badge + >5 Reviews) vs. (Unverified + 0 Reviews).
*   **Why:** If the delta remains flat at ~8% despite strong signals, your "Verified ID" badge has low signal value to renters, or your insurance messaging is unclear. This isolates which specific signal drives the decision.

**2. Leading Indicator A: "The Hesitation Ratio" (Behavioral)**
Measure the friction in the request flow. High trust reduces friction; low trust causes users to stall.
*   **Event to Instrument:** `profile_view_duration` and `request_draft_abandonment`.
*   **Definition:** The percentage of users who view a lender's profile for >45 seconds (indicating scrutiny) but do not send a request, compared to those who send a request within <15 seconds.
*   **Action:** If this ratio spikes for a specific category (e.g., cinema lenses), your trust signals for that category are insufficient.

**3. Leading Indicator B: "Pre-Transaction Inquiry Density"**
Users who don't trust the listing ask more questions before committing.
*   **Event to Instrument:** `message_thread_length` prior to `booking_confirmed`.
*   **Definition:** Average number of messages exchanged before a booking is confirmed.
*   **Baseline:** Establish your current average (e.g., 4 messages). A sudden increase suggests rising anxiety or confusion about safety/insurance terms.

**4. Composite Metric: "Trust Signal Weighting"**
You asked what users care about (reviews vs. speed vs. insurance). Do not guess; calculate the weight of each signal using your existing data.
*   **Method:** Run a logistic regression (or simple correlation analysis) on historical bookings where the outcome is `request_accepted`.
*   **Inputs:** Binary flags for `has_verified_id`, `has_insurance_badge`, `avg_review_score`, and `response_time_<1hr`.
*   **Output:** A coefficient for each factor showing its actual impact on acceptance. This tells you exactly which badge to promote in your UI.

**5. Sentiment Proxy: "The Post-Booking NPS"**
Since disputes are rare (94% success), you need to measure *positive* trust, not just the absence of failure.
*   **Survey Question:** Trigger a one-click survey immediately after the booking is confirmed (not after return): *"How confident do you feel about this rental?"* (Scale 1–5).
*   **Why:** Confidence at the moment of payment is the purest measure of trust. If confidence is low but the transaction completes, you have a future dispute risk.

**Implementation Plan (By Thursday):**
1.  **Instrument:** Add events for `profile_view_duration`, `message_count_pre_booking`, and `booking_confidence_score`.
2.  **Query:** Segment current acceptance rates by the four trust signals (ID, Insurance, Reviews, Response Time) to find the true driver.
3.  **Dashboard:** Build a single view showing the "Hesitation Ratio" and "Confidence Score" trended daily.

**Note:** Do not instrument "everything." Only track these specific events to answer the named questions above; unused data creates noise.
**Note:** Ensure user IDs used for cohorting are pseudonymous; route any PII collection for surveys to your privacy compliance protocol.
