### Proposed Core Metrics for CareConnect

**1. North Star Metric: Weekly Active Patients with Verified Adherence**
Replace "appointments completed" with the count of unique patients over 70 who complete a appointment **and** demonstrate a verified adherence signal within 7 days.
*   **Why:** "Appointments completed" is a vanity metric; it rises even if patients are confused or unsupported. True value occurs only when the visit leads to action.
*   **Measurement Strategy:** Since digital logs are complex and survey response is low (10%), triangulate adherence via **low-friction proxy events** already in your system:
    *   *Proxy A:* A scheduled follow-up call initiated by the patient or caregiver within 7 days (indicates engagement, not confusion).
    *   *Proxy B:* A binary "Plan Understood" confirmation captured by the clinician at the end of the call (clinician-verified, not self-reported).
    *   *Proxy C:* Medication refill requests or pharmacy integration signals (if available) within 14 days.
*   **Target:** Establish a baseline using current data; do not invent an industry benchmark. The goal is to increase the *ratio* of adherent patients to total appointments, not just the raw volume.

**2. Input Metric: The "Caregiver-Assisted" Activation Rate**
Measure the percentage of successful appointments where a secondary participant (caregiver/family) joins the session or is listed as a contact.
*   **Why:** For patients over 70, "ease of use" is often a function of support networks, not just UI design. High activation with caregiver involvement correlates with higher retention and adherence in this demographic.
*   **Measurement:** Count sessions with >1 participant or a linked caregiver account. This answers: "Are we enabling the support system required for success?"

**3. Quality Guardrail: The "Confusion Leak" Rate**
Track the percentage of appointments resulting in an unscheduled inbound contact (call/chat) within 24 hours labeled "technical issue" or "instruction clarification."
*   **Why:** This objectively measures "ease of use" and "clarity" without complex logging. A spike here indicates the treatment plan was not understood or the platform failed the user, directly countering the "appointments completed" vanity metric.
*   **Measurement:** Tag existing support tickets or inbound call logs with these two specific categories. No new software needed.

**4. Retention Cohort: 30-Day Return Rate by Age Bracket**
Instead of aggregate retention, measure the percentage of patients aged 70–75, 76–80, and 80+ who return for a scheduled follow-up within 30 days.
*   **Why:** Aggregates hide friction specific to the oldest users. Reading retention *before* acquisition prevents wasting marketing spend on users the product cannot yet support.
*   **Measurement:** Use existing appointment timestamps to cohort users by age group and calculate return rates.

**Implementation Note for the Advisory Board**
Do not wait for perfect data. Begin tracking the "Confusion Leak" and "Caregiver-Assisted" rates immediately using existing logs and clinician notes. Treat the current 10% survey response as `UNVERIFIED` for strategic decisions; rely on the behavioral proxies above until survey participation exceeds 40%.
