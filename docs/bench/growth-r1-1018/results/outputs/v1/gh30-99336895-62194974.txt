### Experiment Design: Welcome Offer A/B Test (Wanderlust)

**Objective:** Determine whether a 10% discount or a free cancellation upgrade drives superior long-term customer value without eroding margins, specifically for users who download but do not book within 24 hours.

**Test Parameters**
*   **Start Date:** February 1st.
*   **Duration:** Minimum 3 weeks (through Feb 21st) to capture the full initial booking cycle and early retention signals.
*   **Population:** New users, Day 1 non-bookers.
*   **Variants:** Control (No Offer) vs. Variant A (10% Discount) vs. Variant B (Free Cancellation).

**Primary Metric (North Star Input)**
*   **Adjusted Contribution Margin per Acquired User (ACM/AU):** This is the only metric that reconciles your assumption (discount = conversion, upgrade = trust) with finance's need for margin safety.
    *   *Formula:* `(Total Revenue from Cohort - Cost of Goods Sold - Offer Cost) / Total Users in Variant`.
    *   *Why:* Raw conversion rate is a vanity metric here; a 10% discount may spike bookings but destroy profit. ACM/AU captures both the lift in volume and the cost of the incentive.

**Secondary & Guardrail Metrics**
1.  **Activation Rate (Bookings within 7 days):** Measures immediate friction reduction.
2.  **Average Order Value (AOV):** Critical guardrail. Ensure the discount doesn't train users to only book cheap trips, or the upgrade doesn't encourage risky, high-cost bookings that are later cancelled.
3.  **Repeat Booking Rate (Day 14–21):** Proxy for the "trust" hypothesis. If Variant B wins here, it validates the long-term value assumption.
4.  **Cancellation Rate:** Specific guardrail for Variant B. If "free cancellation" leads to speculative booking and high churn, the trust benefit is negated by operational noise.

**Analysis Plan & Finance Approval**
Do not peek at results before Feb 22nd. Reading significance on Day 3 or 7 inflates false positives and will be rejected by rigorous review. The analysis must compare the **ACM/AU** of each variant against the Control.

*   **Decision Rule:** Ship the variant with the highest statistically significant ACM/AU.
*   **Tripwire:** If either variant shows a >5% decrease in AOV or a >10% increase in cancellation rate compared to control, halt the offer regardless of conversion lift.

**Required Instrumentation (Event Taxonomy)**
To calculate ACM/AU, your analytics must track:
*   `offer_viewed` (properties: offer_type)
*   `booking_completed` (properties: revenue, cost_of_goods, offer_applied)
*   `booking_cancelled` (properties: reason, days_since_book)
*   `user_id` (pseudonymous, stable across sessions for cohort tracking).

**This Week's Step:** Define the exact `cost_of_goods` logic for your data team to ensure the "Offer Cost" is deducted correctly in the ACM calculation before Feb 1st. Without this, the finance team cannot approve the budget.

*Note: All metrics must be compared against Wanderlust's own baseline; no external industry benchmarks are used. PII handling for user IDs must route to privacy compliance protocols.*
