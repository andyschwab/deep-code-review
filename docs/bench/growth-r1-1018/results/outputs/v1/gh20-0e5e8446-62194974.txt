To evaluate the AR feature's impact on sales, instrument a linear funnel that answers one specific question per event, moving from access to value realization. Do not instrument "just in case"; every event below must map to a named hypothesis about user intent or drop-off.

**1. Entry & Activation (Hypothesis: Users can access the core value)**
*   **`ar_session_started`**: Records when a user enters the AR view from a product page.
    *   *Question answered:* Is the entry point visible and clickable?
    *   *Properties:* `product_id`, `entry_source` (e.g., search, category page), `user_cohort`.
*   **`camera_permission_granted`**: Records successful access to the device camera.
    *   *Question answered:* Is technical friction blocking activation?
    *   *Properties:* `device_model`, `os_version`, `failure_reason` (if denied).

**2. Engagement & Intent (Hypothesis: Manipulation time correlates with purchase intent)**
*   **`product_rendered`**: Confirms the 3D asset is loaded and visible.
    *   *Question answered:* Is latency causing early exit before interaction?
    *   *Properties:* `load_time_ms`, `asset_complexity`.
*   **`interaction_performed`**: Records specific manipulations to distinguish "playing" from "evaluating."
    *   *Question answered:* Are users actively testing fit/color vs. passively watching?
    *   *Properties:* `action_type` (e.g., `shade_switch`, `rotate`, `zoom`), `session_duration_at_event`, `cumulative_interactions`.
    *   *Note:* Aggregate these into a "Depth of Engagement" metric rather than treating every click as equal. High frequency of `shade_switch` combined with long duration suggests high intent; short duration with random actions suggests novelty.
*   **`capture_created`**: Records when a user saves a snapshot or video.
    *   *Question answered:* Did the user see a result worth keeping? This is a strong leading indicator of purchase.
    *   *Properties:* `capture_type` (photo/video), `products_in_frame`.

**3. Conversion & Drop-off (Hypothesis: The path from AR to checkout has a specific leak)**
*   **`ar_to_cart_clicked`**: Records the click on the "Add to Cart" or "Buy Now" button within the AR interface.
    *   *Question answered:* Does the AR experience successfully drive the next step?
    *   *Properties:* `time_in_ar_before_click`, `interactions_count`.
*   **`checkout_initiated_from_ar`**: Records when the user reaches the checkout screen after an AR session.
    *   *Question answered:* Where is the drop-off between intent and payment?
    *   *Properties:* `cart_value`, `shipping_method_selected`.

**Critical Measurement Rules**
*   **Stable Identifier Seam:** Ensure every event carries a pseudonymous `user_id` that persists across sessions to calculate retention cohorts (e.g., "Did users who tried AR in Week 1 return to buy in Week 2?"). Do not use raw PII; route identity mapping to your privacy compliance protocol.
*   **North Star Metric:** Define your primary success metric as **"AR-Assisted Purchases"** (transactions where `checkout_initiated_from_ar` is true), not total AR sessions. Session count is a vanity metric if it does not convert.
*   **Baseline Comparison:** Since this is a new launch, your initial baseline is zero. Compare Week 1 retention and conversion rates against Week 1 of other major feature launches or general site benchmarks, but do not assume external "good" rates.
*   **Drop-off Analysis:** Calculate the conversion rate between each adjacent step (e.g., `camera_permission_granted` → `product_rendered`). The step with the largest percentage loss is your primary optimization target.
