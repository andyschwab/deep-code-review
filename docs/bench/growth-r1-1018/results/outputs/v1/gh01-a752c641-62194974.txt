### Immediate Action Plan: Isolate Friction and Validate Fix

**1. Specific Data Points to Request from Engineering**
Do not ask for "all logs." Request these four specific event properties to distinguish technical failure from behavioral confusion:
*   **`document_upload_attempt`**: Count of users who clicked "Upload."
*   **`upload_error_code`**: Breakdown by specific error (e.g., `413 Payload Too Large`, `timeout`, `invalid_format`). A spike here indicates a **technical** block.
*   **`upload_abandonment_stage`**: The exact step where the user exited (e.g., "file selector opened" vs. "file selected but not submitted"). If users drop before selecting a file, the UI is likely **confusing** (behavioral).
*   **`time_on_step`**: Average duration on the verification page. A sharp increase suggests users are struggling to understand the new interface; a sharp decrease suggests they are bouncing immediately due to an error.
