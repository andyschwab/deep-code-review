**Activation Definition for NovaPay Executive Steering Committee**

**Proposed Metric:** "Compliance-Adjusted Time-to-First-Transfer (T3+)"
**Definition:** The percentage of registered users who complete identity verification (KYC) AND execute their first peer-to-peer (P2P) transfer within **5 days** of registration.

**Rationale:** Your current 3-day window assumes instantaneous regulatory clearance, which contradicts the reality of Vietnam and Thailand's compliance landscapes. By extending the window to 5 days, you distinguish between users blocked by systemic friction (regulatory delay) and users who simply do not value the product (product failure). A user who verifies on Day 4 and transfers on Day 5 has realized value; counting them as "failed activation" under a 3-day rule creates a false negative that obscures product-market fit.

**Daily Thresholds to Track:**
1.  **KYC Completion Rate (Day 1):** Target >60%. If lower, the friction is purely regulatory/onboarding UI, not product value.
2.  **Activated Cohort % (Day 5):** Target >20% (adjusting from current 12% based on the extended window). This is your true North Star input.
3.  **Drop-off Point:** The specific step where users abandon the flow (e.g., "ID Upload Started" vs. "ID Verified").

**Decision Framework for Tuesday's Meeting:**
Do not expand marketing spend to Indonesia yet. Pouring acquisition into a funnel where 88% of users fail to transact is capital destruction. Instead, apply this diagnostic:
*   **Scenario A:** If extending the window to 5 days lifts activation from 12% to >18%, the issue was an arbitrary time threshold. **Action:** Update dashboards, maintain current spend, and optimize KYC UI speed.
*   **Scenario B:** If activation remains ~12% even with the 5-day window, the leak is product or trust-related, not just regulatory timing. **Action:** Pause Indonesia expansion immediately. Focus engineering on the specific drop-off step identified in the "Drop-off Point" metric above.

**Immediate Next Step:**
Query your analytics today for the cohort registered March 15–20. Recalculate the activation rate using the 5-day window. If the number does not move significantly by tomorrow morning, the onboarding flow itself is broken, and the decision to pause expansion is made.

*Note: This metric assumes your analytics stack links the "KYC Verified" event to the "First Transfer" event via a stable, pseudonymous user ID. Ensure PII handling for KYC data routes strictly to your privacy compliance protocols.*
