### Framework for Fairness, Effectiveness, and Incremental Impact

**1. Fairness Audit: Segments to Analyze**
Do not rely on the aggregate 78% accuracy. To detect bias before the Wednesday deadline, slice the model's performance (precision, recall, and false positive rate) across these specific cohorts:
*   **Demographics:** Age brackets, gender, and location (France vs. Germany).
*   **Subscription Tenure:** New users (<3 months) vs. loyal users (>12 months). *Critical risk:* The model may flag loyal users with temporary usage dips as "high risk," triggering unnecessary discounts that erode margin without preventing churn.
*   **Plan Type:** Free, Premium, and Enterprise tiers.
*   **Usage Intensity:** Power users vs. casual users.

If the false positive rate (loyal users incorrectly flagged) is significantly higher in any specific group (e.g., German Enterprise users), the model is biased. Flag these segments as `UNVERIFIED` for automation until retrained or threshold-adjusted.

**2. Measuring True Incremental Impact (Holdout Design)**
You cannot measure the model's value by simply observing churn rates after intervention; you must isolate the *incremental* lift caused by the discount.
*   **The Design:** From the 15,000 weekly high-risk users, randomly withhold the discount offer from a **10–20% control group** (the holdout). These users receive no email.
*   **The Metric:** Compare the churn rate of the *treated* group vs. the *holdout* group.
    *   *True Lift* = (Churn Rate Holdout) – (Churn Rate Treated).
    *   If both groups churn at the same rate, the discount is ineffective, and the model is triggering wasted spend.
*   **Pre-commitment:** Define the sample size and duration (e.g., 4 weeks) before starting. Do not peek at results daily to avoid false positives.

**3. Cost Analysis: False Positives vs. False Negatives**
Quantify the business impact of errors to set the optimal probability threshold:
*   **False Positive Cost:** A loyal user receives a discount they didn't need.
    *   *Cost* = Discount Value + Potential Brand Devaluation.
*   **False Negative Cost:** A high-risk user receives no offer and churns.
    *   *Cost* = Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) lost.
*   **Decision Rule:** If LTV is high, tolerate more false positives. If margins are thin, tighten the threshold to reduce false positives, even if it misses some at-risk users.
