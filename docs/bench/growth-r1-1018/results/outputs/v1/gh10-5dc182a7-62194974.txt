**Diagnosis Plan & Decision Framework**

**1. Immediate Data Triage (Do this tonight)**
Stop guessing; isolate the leak in the activation funnel. Since heatmaps are unavailable, query your event logs for these three specific flows comparing the 7 days pre-Sept 28 vs. post-Sept 28:
*   **Visibility Check:** Ratio of `dashboard_view` to `upgrade_cta_click`. If views are stable but clicks dropped >30%, the CTA is visually obscured or cognitively missed.
*   **Intent Check:** Ratio of `upgrade_cta_click` to `pricing_page_view`. If clicks are stable but pricing views dropped, the click target exists but the immediate context (e.g., a modal or redirect) is broken.
*   **Completion Check:** Ratio of `pricing_page_view` to `trial_converted`. If this is stable, the issue is purely top-of-funnel (visibility); if this also dropped, the new layout may have altered value perception or introduced a technical bug in the checkout flow.

**2. Addressing Seasonality (The External Factor)**
You cannot "adjust" for seasonality without historical data. Do not invent a benchmark. Instead, run a **cohort comparison**:
*   Compare the Sept 28 cohort against the same weekday cohorts from the previous 4 weeks.
*   If *all* recent cohorts show a similar downward trend independent of the UI change, it is likely seasonal or market-wide.
*   If only the post-Sept 28 cohort deviates sharply while prior weeks were stable, the UI refresh is the primary driver. Given a drop from 14% to 9.5% in 3 days, the magnitude suggests a structural break (design/bug) rather than gradual seasonal drift.

**3. The Call: Revert or Test?**
Do not revert immediately unless the `upgrade_cta_click` rate is near zero (indicating a broken link or invisible element). A 9.5% conversion rate, while degraded, indicates the system still functions. A full revert loses the opportunity to learn *why* it failed.

**Recommended Action: Segmented Canary Rollback**
*   **Strategy:** Route 50% of German traffic to the old UI (Control) and 50% to the new UI (Treatment) effective immediately.
*   **Duration:** Run for 48 hours to gather statistical significance given your volume (~1,200 users).
*   **Success Metric:** If the Control group returns to ~14% and Treatment stays at ~9.5%, the UI is the confirmed cause. If both groups remain at ~9.5%, the cause is external (market/seasonality), and reverting will not help.
*   **Guardrail:** Monitor `support_ticket_volume` regarding "billing" or "upgrade" issues. A spike here confirms user confusion regardless of conversion stats.

**4. Tomorrow's Status Update Script**

We have initiated a 50/50 A/B test against the previous version to definitively separate UI impact from market seasonality. We expect a final verdict on whether to rollback or iterate by [Date/Time]. No full revert is recommended yet to preserve learning velocity."
