**Framework for Interpretation: The Value-Adjusted Funnel**
Weigh these signals using a bottom-up retention and revenue lens, not a top-line volume lens:
1.  **Primary Metric (Tasks Created):** This is a vanity metric in isolation if it does not correlate with long-term retention or revenue. An 18% increase driven by "gaming the system" (creating empty tasks to maintain streaks) creates database noise without delivering customer value.
2.  **Guardrail Metric (Session Duration):** The 12% drop suggests the "fun" element reduced genuine engagement. Users are completing the mechanical action faster but deriving less utility, contradicting the hypothesis that gamification increases engagement.
3.  **Revenue Signal (Premium Upgrades):** Even with a small sample, a downward trend in upgrades is a leading indicator of churn in the monetization funnel. If the free experience becomes "good enough" via badges, the incentive to upgrade diminishes.

**Decision Logic**
You cannot ship a feature that increases low-quality volume while degrading engagement depth and threatening revenue. The trade-off is negative: you are exchanging high-intent session time for low-intent task spam. The "success" of 18% more tasks is likely a measurement artifact of users optimizing for badges rather than productivity.
