**Recommendation:** Prioritize **Weekly Active Teams Completing a Conflict-Free Merge After a Real-Time Session** as your North Star metric for the first three months.

Do not prioritize concurrent session counts (vanity), raw time-to-merge (noisy), or qualitative scores (lagging). Your stated value hypothesis is "reduced merge conflicts." Therefore, the metric must rise *only* when a team successfully uses the feature to avoid a conflict and merge code. Session counts rise even if the feature is useless; time-to-merge fluctuates due to code complexity unrelated to collaboration; satisfaction scores are subjective and often disconnected from actual workflow efficiency.

**Why this metric:**
1.  **It validates the value hypothesis:** It directly measures the outcome you promised (fewer conflicts), not just the activity (editing together).
2.  **It filters noise:** It ignores teams that try the feature once and abandon it, focusing on those who integrate it into their actual shipping workflow.
3.  **It drives the right behavior:** Engineering will optimize for stability and conflict-resolution logic, not just connection uptime.
