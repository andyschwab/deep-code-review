### GreenCart MVP Tracking Plan (London Launch)

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Users completing a **Successful Delivery** (Order placed → Delivered).
*Rejects vanity metrics like "App Opens" or "Items Viewed" which rise regardless of value delivery.*

**Funnel Diagnosis Strategy:** Read bottom-up. Before optimizing acquisition (ads), verify that users who receive groceries actually return (Retention). If retention leaks, acquisition spend is wasted.

#### Essential Event Taxonomy
Instrument only these events to answer specific business questions. Do not instrument "just in case."

| Event Name | Business Question Answered | Essential Properties |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `session_start` | **Acquisition/Reach:** Are users entering the app? | `user_id` (pseudonymous), `borough`, `device_type`, `campaign_source` |
| `product_view` | **Browse Friction:** Can users find what they need? | `user_id`, `category`, `search_query`, `item_availability_status` (in-stock/out-of-stock) |
| `cart_add` | **Activation Intent:** Is the "core action" initiated? | `user_id`, `item_count`, `cart_value`, `missing_items_count` (critical for availability hypothesis) |
| `checkout_start` | **Checkout Friction:** Do users drop off before paying? | `user_id`, `estimated_delivery_time`, `delivery_fee`, `borough` |
| `order_placed` | **Conversion:** Did the transaction complete? | `user_id`, `order_id`, `total_value`, `item_count`, `scheduled_delivery_window` |
| `order_fulfilled` | **Value Delivery:** Did the user actually get the goods? | `order_id`, `actual_delivery_time`, `substitution_count`, `missing_item_count`, `driver_id` |
| `app_reopen` | **Retention:** Do they come back after the first delivery? | `user_id`, `days_since_first_order`, `borough` |

#### Critical Implementation Notes
1.  **The Identifier Seam:** Product cohorts require a **stable, pseudonymous `user_id`** to track a single user across sessions and devices. This overrides standard observability rules that avoid high-cardinality labels; however, do not send PII (names, addresses) to this analytics stream. Route all PII and consent flags to your privacy compliance system.
2.  **Testing the "Availability" Hypothesis:** You suspect item availability is a friction point. The `cart_add` and `order_fulfilled` events must explicitly capture `missing_items_count` and `substitution_count`. Without these properties, you cannot correlate availability issues with churn.

#### This Week's Steps
1.  **Define the Schema:** Engineering to finalize the JSON schema for the 7 events above, ensuring `user_id` is persistent and pseudonymous.
2.  **Validate the Hypothesis:** Once live, run a cohort analysis comparing retention rates of users with `substitution_count > 0` vs. `substitution_count = 0` in Week 2. If the former group churns significantly faster, availability is your primary blocker, not delivery time.

*Note: No benchmarks for "good" retention or conversion are provided as GreenCart has no historical baseline in London. All success criteria must be derived from your own Week 1–4 trends.*
*Do not instrument pageviews or click-depth; they are vanity metrics that do not map to the North Star.*
