**Evaluation Plan: Net Revenue Impact & Risk Mitigation**

Do not accept the 5% lift and 18% drop as final verdicts yet; these are unverified mid-stream observations subject to "peeking" bias. To justify this to the CFO, you must reframe the analysis from a simple trade-off to a **Net Lifetime Value (LTV) simulation** based on pre-committed data.

**1. The Core Metric & Calculation**
Stop optimizing for "subscription lift" alone. Your North Star for this experiment is **Net Revenue Per Acquired User (NRPAU)**.
*   **Formula:** `(New Premium Subscriptions × Premium LTV) + (Remaining Free Users × Free Tier Ad/LTV) - (Churned Users × Lost Potential LTV)`.
*   **The Trap:** Your assumption that churned users were "low value" is unverified. If the 18% drop includes users who would have converted in Month 3 or acted as viral referrers, the net impact is negative. You cannot calculate the six-month projection without isolating the *counterfactual*: what would those churned users have done had the features remained free?

**2. Leading Indicators (Weekly Monitoring)**
Monitor these three inputs weekly to detect brand erosion before it hits the six-month revenue line:
*   **Free-to-Premium Conversion Rate (Day 30+):** If Day-7 retention drops but Day-30 conversion holds steady, your assumption holds. If Day-30 conversion also dips, you are killing future revenue, not just low-value traffic.
*   **Viral Coefficient (K-Factor) of Free Tier:** Track invites/shares per active free user. A drop here confirms the "brand perception" risk; gated features reduce the product's shareability, starving future acquisition.
*   **Support Ticket Volume & Sentiment:** Specifically tag tickets mentioning "paywall," "greedy," or "pricing." A spike here is a leading indicator of long-term brand damage and app store rating declines.

**3. Segmentation Strategy**
Do not aggregate data. Segment the 18% churn by:
*   **Usage Intensity Prior to Gate:** Did heavy users of the now-gated features churn, or was it casual browsers? Heavy user churn signals a value proposition failure.
*   **Acquisition Channel:** Did organic users react differently than paid ad users? Organic users often have higher sensitivity to perceived "bait-and-switch" tactics.
*   **Geography/Device:** Ensure the drop isn't isolated to a region with lower purchasing power where the price point is simply prohibitive, which is a pricing error, not a feature value error.

**4. The Decision Framework**
Run this analysis for a fixed duration (e.g., 4 weeks from Nov 10) before drawing conclusions. Do not stop early because the numbers look "good enough."
*   **Ship (Keep Change):** If NRPAU is positive AND the Viral Coefficient remains within 5% of baseline.
*   **Iterate:** If NRPAU is positive BUT Viral Coefficient drops >10%. Consider ungating one high-shareability feature to restore growth while keeping revenue.
*   **Stop (Revert):** If NRPAU is negative OR if brand sentiment metrics spike significantly, indicating long-term customer acquisition costs will rise due to reputation damage.
