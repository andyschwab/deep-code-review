### TutorMatch Metric Hierarchy: Balancing Liquidity and Quality

**1. North Star Metric: Weekly Successful Matches Retained**
*Definition:* The count of unique student-tutor pairs who complete a booking in a given week AND book again within the subsequent 30 days.
*Rationale:* This rejects vanity metrics like "total bookings" or "tutor signups," which can rise while the business decays (as seen in your declining repeat rates). It forces optimization for actual value delivery (a successful match) and longevity (retention), balancing both sides of the marketplace.

**2. Input Metrics (The Drivers)**
These are the levers that move the North Star, categorized by marketplace side:
*   **Student Side (Demand):**
    *   *Activation Rate:* % of new students who book within 48 hours of sign-up.
    *   *Repeat Booking Rate (Cohort-based):* % of students from a specific start month who book a second time. Track this by cohort to isolate whether newer vintages are worse than older ones.
    *   *Price Sensitivity Index:* Ratio of search-to-book conversion for sessions priced above vs. below the median.
*   **Tutor Side (Supply):**
    *   *24-Hour Fill Rate:* % of student requests receiving a confirmed tutor within 24 hours. (Your current hypothesis).
    *   *Tutor Utilization:* % of active tutors with at least one booking per week. (Prevents over-supply where tutors churn due to lack of work).
*   **Matching Quality (The Bridge):**
    *   *Post-Session Rating Delta:* Average rating difference between the first and third session for the same pair. A drop indicates deteriorating quality over time.
    *   *Re-match Rate:* % of students who request a different tutor after a completed session.

**3. Operational Diagnostics (Detecting Liquidity Early)**
To answer your open question on price vs. quality and detect liquidity cracks:
*   **Liquidity Heatmap:** Segment fill rate by *Subject* and *Location*. Aggregate fill rates hide local shortages; a 70% overall fill rate might mask a 20% rate in "A-Level Maths in Leeds," which drives student churn.
*   **Funnel Leakage Analysis:** Compare the drop-off rates at three specific stages:
    1.  Search initiated → Profile viewed. (Low here = Price/Profile issue).
    2.  Profile viewed → Booking request sent. (Low here = Availability/Price issue).
    3.  Request sent → Confirmed. (Low here = Supply/Tutor response issue).
*   **Churn Attribution:** For students who do not return, tag the last known state: "No Match Found" (Liquidity), "Price Too High" (Sensitivity), or "Low Rating" (Quality).

**4. Guardrail Metrics**
*   **Tutor Churn Rate:** Ensure optimizing for student fill rate doesn't burn out tutors with low-paying or high-friction requests.
*   **Cancellation Rate:** A spike here often precedes a drop in repeat bookings, signaling a mismatch in expectations.
