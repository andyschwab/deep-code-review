### Experiment Design: Welcome Offer A/B Test

**Objective:** Determine whether a 10% discount or a free cancellation upgrade drives superior long-term customer value (LTV) without eroding margins beyond acceptable limits.

**Test Parameters**
*   **Start Date:** February 1st.
*   **Duration:** 21 days minimum (3 weeks) to capture the full initial booking cycle and early retention signals.
*   **Population:** New users who download the app but do not book within 24 hours.
*   **Variants:**
    *   **Control:** No specific welcome offer (or current standard flow).
    *   **Variant A:** 10% Discount Code.
    *   **Variant B:** Free Cancellation Upgrade.

**Metric Framework**
To satisfy finance requirements, we must measure immediate revenue against long-term value and margin impact.

1.  **Primary Metric (North Star for this test): Adjusted Net Revenue per User (NRPU) at Day 21.**
    *   *Definition:* (Total Booking Value - Discount Cost - Operational Cost of Upgrades) / Total Users in Cohort.
    *   *Why:* This captures both conversion lift and the direct margin cost of the offer. It prevents optimizing for "bookings" that lose money.

2.  **Guardrail Metrics (Must not degrade):**
    *   **Gross Margin %:** Ensure the discount variant does not drop below the break-even threshold defined by finance.
    *   **Cancellation Rate:** Specifically for Variant B, ensure the "free cancellation" does not lead to excessive booking churn that clogs inventory or support lines.

3.  **Secondary Metrics (Leading Indicators & LTV Proxies):**
    *   **Conversion Rate (Day 1–7):** Measures immediate activation lift.
    *   **Average Order Value (AOV):** Checks if the discount incentivizes smaller baskets or the upgrade incentivizes higher-risk/higher-value trips.
    *   **Repeat Booking Rate (Day 21+):** Early signal of whether the "trust" hypothesis (Variant B) holds for a second transaction.

**Analysis Plan & Rigor**
*   **Pre-commitment:** We will calculate the required sample size based on the Minimum Detectable Effect (MDE) on NRPU before launch. We will **not** stop the test early if significance is reached on Day 3; peeking inflates false positives. The read-out occurs only after the 21-day period concludes.
*   **Segmentation:** Results must be sliced by "intended trip value" (low vs. high) to see if the upgrade works better for expensive bookings as hypothesized.
*   **Decision Rule:**
    *   If Variant A wins on NRPU but Variant B shows statistically significant higher *Repeat Booking Rate* (even if Day 21 NRPU is flat), recommend extending the test to 45 days to capture the second booking event before finalizing.
    *   If neither variant beats Control on NRPU, kill both offers.

**Deliverable for Finance (Due Next Wednesday)**
A one-page brief containing:
1.  The pre-calculated sample size required for statistical significance on NRPU.
2.  The projected budget cap based on maximum exposure (worst-case conversion lift × discount cost).
3.  The specific "break-even" conversion lift required for the 10% discount to remain profitable.
