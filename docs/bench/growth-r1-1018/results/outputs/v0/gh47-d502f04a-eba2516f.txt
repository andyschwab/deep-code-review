**Japan Launch Scorecard: Early Success Indicators**

**1. North Star Metric: Weekly Active Ordering Users (WAOU)**
Reject "Average Order Value" (AOV) as your primary metric for this launch. In high-density markets with smaller basket sizes, AOV is a vanity metric that rises artificially if you force larger orders, potentially killing the frequency behavior you seek. Your North Star must be **WAOU**: the count of unique users placing at least one order per week. This rises only when users derive recurring value.
*   **Input Metrics:** Order Frequency (orders/user/week) and Activation Rate (users completing first order within 24h of signup).

**2. The Funnel: Read Bottom-Up (Retention First)**

*   **Priority 2 (Activation):** Measure time-to-first-order. If users sign up but don't order immediately, your variety/fee balance is wrong.
