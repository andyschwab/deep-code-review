### Non-Negotiable Event Taxonomy
Instrument only events that answer specific survival questions. Do not track "everything."

1.  **`session_start`** (Properties: `user_id`, `is_new_user`, `app_version`, `timestamp`)
    *   *Question:* Are users able to establish a session under load?
    *   *Why:* Distinguishes between app crash, network timeout, and successful entry.
2.  **`content_play_started`** (Properties: `content_id`, `content_type` [Sleep Story/Anxiety Session], `user_id`, `load_latency_ms`)
    *   *Question:* Is the core value (audio playback) initiating despite the surge?
    *   *Why:* Validates the "Sleep Stories" assumption and tests new "Anxiety Sessions" activation.
3.  **`content_play_completed`** (Properties: `content_id`, `duration_listened_sec`, `completion_rate`)
    *   *Question:* Does heavy load cause drop-offs mid-session (buffering/latency issues)?
    *   *Why:* Differentiates content quality issues from infrastructure failure.
4.  **`error_event`** (Properties: `error_code`, `feature_context`, `user_id`)
    *   *Question:* What specifically is breaking when the system is stressed?
    *   *Why:* Critical for immediate triage during the surge.

### Sampling & Data Integrity Strategy
Since you cannot store 100% of data, apply **deterministic sampling** based on `user_id` to ensure a single user's journey remains intact for cohort analysis (e.g., New vs. Legacy).

*   **Strategy:** Hash `user_id` modulo 10. Capture 100% of data for users where `hash % 10 == 0`. Drop all events for the other 90%.
*   **Rationale:** Random per-event sampling breaks session reconstruction. User-based sampling preserves the funnel (Start → Play → Complete) for a statistically significant subset, allowing you to compare New User behavior on Anxiety Sessions vs. Sleep Stories accurately.
*   **Priority Override:** Always capture 100% of `error_event` regardless of sampling. You cannot sample away critical failures.

### Validation Steps (Pre-Surge)
1.  **Load Test Verification:** Simulate 3x traffic in staging. Verify that the sampling logic holds (exactly ~10% of users appear in the dataset) and that `error_event` volume matches injected faults.
2.  **Latency Budget Check:** Ensure the instrumentation pipeline adds <50ms to the critical path. If the logger blocks the main thread, it will cause the very drop-offs you are trying to measure.
3.  **Backfill Protection:** Confirm that if the queue fills, the system drops *oldest* non-critical events first, preserving real-time `error_event` and `session_start` data.
