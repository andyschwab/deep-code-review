### Experiment Design: Ad Frequency Optimization

**1. Success Criteria & Decision Rule**
Do not rely on 48-hour data for a final "ship/kill" decision on retention; day-7 retention cannot causally exist in two days. Instead, use a **pre-committed sequential design**:
*   **Primary Metric:** Day-7 Retention (measured at full term).
*   **Guardrail Metric:** Session frequency (sessions per user per day) and early churn proxy (users who do not return within 24 hours).
*   **Decision Tripwire:** If the treatment group shows a **>5% relative drop in session frequency** or a **>10% relative increase in 24-hour churn** within the first 48 hours, **stop the experiment immediately**. This is your early warning system for the "quit threshold."
*   **Global Rollout Condition:** Proceed only if Day-7 retention shows no statistically significant negative lift (non-inferiority) AND revenue per user increases by >3% at the end of the test.

**2. Leading Indicators (First 48 Hours)**
Watch these specific signals to detect early friction before Day-7 data matures:
*   **Immediate Churn Proxy:** Percentage of users who launch the game but fail to complete a single level session after seeing the new ad load.
*   **Session Depth Decay:** Average number of levels played per session. A sharp drop indicates ad fatigue disrupting the core loop.
*   **Uninstall Rate (if available via SDK):** Spike in uninstalls within 24 hours of exposure.
*   **Ad Load Failure Rate:** Technical errors or timeout rates on the additional ad slot (ensuring revenue loss isn't due to fill-rate issues).

**3. Sample Size Calculation**
To detect a **2% absolute drop** in retention (from 22% baseline to 20%) with 80% power and 95% confidence:
*   **Required Sample Size:** Approximately **23,000 users per variant** (46,000 total).
*   **Feasibility Check:** With 150,000 DAU, allocating 10% (15,000 users) to the test group is **insufficient** to detect a 2% absolute drop with statistical confidence in a short window.
*   **Adjustment:** You must either increase the test allocation to ~30% of DAU, extend the duration to accumulate enough user-days, or accept that you can only detect larger effects (e.g., a 4-5% drop). Do not declare a "win" or "safe" status on a 10% sample if the minimum detectable effect is larger than your risk tolerance.

**4. Execution Protocol**
*   **Pre-commitment:** Define the analysis date now. Do not peek at Day-7 retention results before the cohort matures. Peeking inflates false positives.
*   **Variance Reduction:** Use CUPED (Controlled-Experiment Using Pre-Experiment Data) by conditioning on each user's pre-experiment retention probability to increase sensitivity without needing more users.
*   **Revenue vs. Retention:** If revenue rises but the guardrail (session frequency) breaks, **kill the feature**. Short-term revenue gains from churned users are vanity; lifetime value collapses if retention leaks.

**Note:** Your current 10% allocation plan is underpowered for a 2% retention shift detection; increase exposure or adjust expectations. Never treat 48-hour retention as a proxy for Day-7; use session depth and 24-hour return rates as the actual leading indicators.
