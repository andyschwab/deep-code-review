**North Star Metric: Weekly Active Users with ≥10 Meaningful Connections**

Reject "density," "inbound message rate," and "viral coefficient" as your primary North Star. These are input metrics or vanity proxies. Density is hard to define across sub-industries; message rate measures noise, not value; viral coefficient measures acquisition, not the retention payoff you hypothesized. Your North Star must be the single metric that rises *only* when a user receives the core value you promised: a robust professional network. Therefore, track **WAU (Weekly Active Users) who have completed the "10 Meaningful Connections" threshold**. This directly couples activity with your specific network-effect hypothesis.

**The Measurement Strategy (Bottom-Up Funnel)**

Do not pour more into acquisition (2,000/month) until you prove the bucket holds water. Diagnose retention first, then activation, then acquisition.

1.  **Retention Cohort Analysis (The Proof):**
    *   **Question:** Do users who hit 10 connections in Week 1 retain at a significantly higher rate at Day 90 than those who do not?
    *   **Action:** Segment users by "Connections Made in First 7 Days" (0–2, 3–9, 10+). Plot Day 30, 60, and 90 retention for each cohort.
    *   **Investor Narrative:** If the "10+" cohort retains 3x better than the "0–2" cohort, you have quantitative proof of the network effect. If retention is flat across cohorts, the hypothesis is false, and adding users won't fix value.

2.  **Activation Event Taxonomy:**
    *   Instrument only events that answer named questions. Do not track "everything."
    *   **Event:** `connection_made` (Properties: `user_id`, `timestamp`, `industry_match`).
    *   **Event:** `meaningful_interaction` (Define this rigorously: e.g., message exchange > 3 replies, or profile view followed by connection).
    *   **Event:** `threshold_crossed` (Triggered when `meaningful_interaction` count ≥ 10 for a user).
    *   **Identifier Seam:** Use a stable, pseudonymous `user_id` to track cohorts across sessions. Do not use raw PII here; route consent/PII handling to your privacy compliance protocol.

3.  **Input Metrics (The Levers):**
    *   Once the North Star is established, track these inputs to move it:
        *   **Activation Rate:** % of new signups reaching 10 connections within 14 days.
        *   **Invitation Acceptance Rate:** Quality of invites (better than raw viral coefficient).
        *   **Time-to-10:** Average days to reach the threshold.

**Experiment Rigor for Series B**

When testing features to improve these metrics (e.g., a new "suggested connections" algorithm):
*   **Pre-commit:** Define the sample size and duration *before* launching. Do not peek at Day 2 and declare a win; this inflates false positives.
*   **Primary vs. Guardrail:** Primary = % reaching 10 connections. Guardrail = Unsubscribe rate or Spam reports. A win on activation that spikes spam is a loss.
*   **Status:** Any result read before the pre-committed end date is `UNVERIFIED`.
