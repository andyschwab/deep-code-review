### Top 5 Events for ScribbleAI MVP Launch

To validate product-market fit without vanity noise, instrument only these five events. Each answers a specific question about user value and retention.

**1. `session_started`**
*   **Definition:** Fired when a user successfully loads the editor interface with a document (new or existing).
*   **Properties:** `document_type` (new_draft vs. existing_edit), `user_id` (pseudonymous stable ID).
*   **Question Answered:** Are users actually entering the workflow? (Filters out landing page bounces).

**2. `suggestion_generated`**
*   **Definition:** Fired every time the AI returns a completed suggestion block to the user.
*   **Properties:** `suggestion_length`, `context_type` (continuation vs. rewrite), `latency_ms`.
*   **Question Answered:** Is the engine running often enough to provide value opportunities? (Denominator for acceptance rate).

**3. `suggestion_accepted`**
*   **Definition:** Fired when a user inserts an AI suggestion into the document without modification.
*   **Properties:** `suggestion_id`, `time_to_accept_ms`, `document_type`.
*   **Question Answered:** Is the raw output useful enough to ship immediately? (Your baseline value signal).

**4. `suggestion_edited_then_accepted`**
*   **Definition:** Fired when a user modifies an AI suggestion (adds/deletes/changes words) and then commits it to the document.
*   **Properties:** `edit_distance` (approximate % of tokens changed), `suggestion_id`, `document_type`.
*   **Question Answered:** Is the AI providing a valuable "first draft" that saves time even if not perfect? (Your hypothesized stronger engagement signal).

**5. `return_session`**
*   **Definition:** Fired when a `user_id` initiates a `session_started` event more than 24 hours after their previous session.
*   **Properties:** `days_since_last_session`, `total_sessions_count`.
*   **Question Answered:** Are users retaining? (The ultimate verdict on product-market fit; read this before looking at acquisition).

### Implementation Notes
*   **Identifier Seam:** Use a stable, pseudonymous `user_id` for all events to track cohorts across sessions; do not use raw PII. Route any consent logic to your privacy compliance protocol.
*   **Vanity Check:** Do not track `page_views`, `clicks_on_menu`, or `time_on_page`. These rise regardless of value and will obscure the retention signal.

### This Week's Step
Hand this list to engineering Monday with the instruction: "If an event does not answer one of the five questions above, do not instrument it." Once live, calculate your **Core Value Rate**: (`suggestion_accepted` + `suggestion_edited_then_accepted`) / `suggestion_generated`. If this rate is <20% in week 2, the model quality or placement is broken; do not spend on acquisition until it rises.
