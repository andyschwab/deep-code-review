**Experiment Design: Pricing Tier A/B Test**

**Primary Metric:** Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) across the trial cohort.
**Guardrail Metric:** Conversion rate to the $99 Enterprise tier (must not drop >5% relative to control).
**Hypothesis:** Introducing a $49 "Professional" tier increases overall ARPU by capturing users currently churning between $19 and $99, without significantly cannibalizing Enterprise upgrades.

**Test Structure**
*   **Control Group (50%):** Current binary choice ($19 Basic, $99 Enterprise).
*   **Treatment Group (50%):** Three-tier choice ($19 Basic, $49 Professional, $99 Enterprise).
*   **Duration:** 6 weeks (Feb 1 – Mar 15). Do not peek at results before the end date; early reads inflate false-positive rates.
*   **Unit of Randomization:** Individual trial user account.

**Sample Size & Power**
With 2,000 total users (1,000 per arm) over six weeks, you can reliably detect a **15-20% relative lift in ARPU** with 80% power, assuming a standard deviation typical of SaaS pricing experiments. If your expected lift is smaller (e.g., 5%), this sample size is insufficient, and the result will be `UNVERIFIED`. You must pre-commit to this minimum detectable effect (MDE); do not extend the test dynamically based on interim significance.

**Risk Mitigation**
1.  **Cannibalization Check:** The guardrail metric specifically monitors if the $49 plan merely downgrades users who would have paid $99. If Enterprise conversion drops significantly while total ARPU remains flat, the test fails.
2.  **Choice Paralysis:** Monitor the "time-to-purchase" and drop-off rate at the pricing page. If treatment users take significantly longer to convert or abandon the page more often, the extra option is causing friction.
3.  **Cohort Consistency:** Ensure the 2,000 trial users are randomized strictly upon entry to the pricing page to avoid selection bias.

**Decision Framework**
*   **Ship:** If ARPU increases ≥15% AND Enterprise conversion decline is <5%.
*   **Stop:** If ARPU is flat/negative OR Enterprise conversion drops >5%.
*   **Extend:** Only if the result is inconclusive (confidence interval crosses zero) AND you have additional traffic available beyond the 2,000 users. Do not extend simply to chase significance.
