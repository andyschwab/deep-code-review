### GreenCart MVP Tracking Plan (London Launch)

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Users completing a **Successful Delivery** (Order placed + Delivered without cancellation/refund).
*Rejects vanity metrics like "App Opens" or "Items Viewed" which rise regardless of value delivered.*

**Funnel Strategy:** Diagnose bottom-up. Prioritize **Retention** of the core action (re-ordering) over Acquisition volume. If users do not return after week 1, increasing ad spend in Camden, Hackney, or Islington is wasted.

#### Essential Events & Business Questions

Instrument **only** these events to answer specific hypotheses. Do not track "just in case."

**1. `session_start`**
*   **Properties:** `user_id` (pseudonymous), `borough`, `device_type`, `timestamp`.
*   **Question:** Are users in specific boroughs failing to load the app or bounce immediately due to service area confusion?

**2. `product_view`**
*   **Properties:** `user_id`, `category`, `item_id`, `is_in_stock` (boolean).
*   **Question:** Does low item availability (`is_in_stock: false`) correlate with session abandonment before cart addition? (Validates the "hidden friction" hypothesis).

**3. `cart_add`**
*   **Properties:** `user_id`, `item_count`, `cart_value`, `estimated_delivery_time`.
*   **Question:** At what cart value or delivery estimate do users hesitate? Is the "aha moment" reaching a specific basket size?

**4. `checkout_initiated`**
*   **Properties:** `user_id`, `payment_method`, `delivery_slot_selected`.
*   **Question:** Where is the drop-off between intent and payment? Is it payment friction or slot unavailability?

**5. `order_placed`**
*   **Properties:** `user_id`, `order_id`, `total_value`, `item_count`, `borough`, `promised_delivery_window`.
*   **Question:** What is the true conversion rate from browse to paid order? How does this vary by borough?

**6. `order_fulfilled`** (Critical for Retention)
*   **Properties:** `user_id`, `order_id`, `actual_delivery_time`, `promised_delivery_time`, `items_substituted_count`, `items_missing_count`, `customer_rating` (if captured).
*   **Question:** Does actual delivery time vs. promised time drive satisfaction? Do substitutions (availability issues) negatively impact the likelihood of a second order?

**7. `app_open` (for Retention Cohorts)**
*   **Properties:** `user_id`, `days_since_first_order`.
*   **Question:** Do users return in Week 2? (The primary retention metric). Compare return rates of users who experienced substitutions vs. those who did not.

#### Identifier Seam & Privacy
*   **Stable ID:** Use a consistent, pseudonymous `user_id` across all events to track the journey from browse to repeat order.
*   **Privacy:** Do not log PII (names, addresses, raw card numbers) in analytics events. Route consent management and data retention limits to your privacy compliance protocol.

#### This Week's Steps
1.  **Define the "Successful Delivery" schema:** Agree on the exact property set for `order_fulfilled` to ensure `actual_delivery_time` and `items_substituted_count` are captured accurately.
2.  **Build the Retention Query:** Draft the SQL/analysis to compare Week-2 return rates for users with `items_substituted_count > 0` vs. `0`. This validates your friction hypothesis without needing external benchmarks.
3.  **Reject Vanity:** Explicitly exclude `page_view` counts from the executive dashboard; replace with `checkout_initiated` to `order_placed` conversion rates.

*Note: No benchmarks (e.g., "good retention is X%") are provided; compare your Week 2 data against your Week 1 baseline only. If data is missing at launch, mark metrics as UNVERIFIED rather than estimating.*
