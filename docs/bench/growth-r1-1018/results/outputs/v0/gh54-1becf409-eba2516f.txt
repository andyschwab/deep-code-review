### Investigative Steps & Hypotheses: StreamLine Revenue-Engagement Divergence

**Core Diagnosis:** Your North Star metric is currently misaligned. Daily Active Users (DAU) is a vanity metric in this context; it measures traffic, not value. The true North Star must be **"Weekly Active Users completing a high-value content session"** (e.g., watching >20 mins of premium-exclusive content). The 18% DAU growth with flat conversion indicates you are successfully acquiring users who consume low-value content but fail to perceive the upgrade incentive.

**Hypothesis 1: The "Free Content Trap" (Content Mix Issue)**
The February content library expansion may have added high-volume, low-retention content (e.g., short clips, ad-supported older titles) that satisfies free-tier users without exposing them to premium value.
*   **Test:** Segment retention cohorts by "Content Type Consumed." Compare the 4-week retention rate of users who only watch new February content vs. legacy premium content. If the new cohort retains well on the platform but never converts, the content is cannibalizing the upgrade path.

**Hypothesis 2: Activation Friction (The "Aha!" Moment is Missing)**
Users are active but not hitting the activation threshold required to feel the pain of the free tier limits.
*   **Test:** Define the specific action correlating with conversion (e.g., "3 episodes of an original series in 7 days"). Calculate what percentage of the new 18% DAU surge actually hits this threshold. If <10% hit it, your onboarding or recommendation engine is failing to drive users to premium hooks, regardless of traffic quality.

**Hypothesis 3: Monetization Flow Leakage**
The assumption that "engagement leads to upgrades" is broken by a specific friction point in the paywall experience.
*   **Test:** Funnel analysis from "Premium Content Click" → "Paywall View" → "Checkout Start" → "Subscription." If drop-off spikes at "Paywall View," the value proposition is unclear. If it spikes at "Checkout," there is technical or pricing friction. Do not assume; measure the exact step where the 95.5% leakage occurs.

**Immediate Actions for Earnings Call Preparation:**
1.  **Stop reporting raw DAU growth** as a success metric to investors without the conversion context. It signals inefficient capital allocation.
2.  **Run a cohort analysis** comparing pre-February and post-February users on "Premium Content Exposure Rate." This isolates whether the new library is the culprit.
3.  **Instrument the "Upgrade Intent" event:** Track clicks on premium locks/trailers. If these are flat while DAU rises, your content strategy is misaligned with monetization.

**Verdict:** You are likely attracting "low-intent" traffic or satisfying them too well with free content. The problem is not traffic quality per se, but a failure to transition engagement into perceived value scarcity. Fix the activation funnel before spending more on acquisition.
