### Diagnostic Plan: Month-Two Retention Cliff

**1. Define the North Star and Core Action**
Reject "monthly active users" or "logins" as primary metrics; these are vanity indicators that rise even when value delivery stalls. Establish the North Star as **"Weekly Budget Reconciliation"** (the specific action where a user categorizes transactions against their set budget). Retention must be measured as the percentage of users completing this core action in Week 5–8 post-signup, not merely opening the app. If the app is working, users should be reconciling; if they stop reconciling, they have lost value, regardless of login frequency.

**2. Event Taxonomy: Answer Named Questions Only**
Instrument only the events required to distinguish between your three hypotheses (lack of features, financial stabilization, or forgetfulness). Do not track "clicks" broadly.
*   **Question:** Do users stop because they achieved their goal?
    *   **Event:** `budget_target_met` (Boolean: Did the user's actual spending stay within the planned limit for two consecutive months?).
    *   **Property:** `variance_percentage` (How close were they to the limit?).
*   **Question:** Do users stop because the data became stale or useless?
    *   **Event:** `manual_categorization_count` and `auto_categorization_confidence_score`.
    *   **Property:** `days_since_last_transaction_sync`.
*   **Question:** Is the "second bank account" correlation causal or a proxy for power users?
    *   **Event:** `second_account_connected`.
    *   **Property:** `time_to_connect` (Days from signup).
    *   **Analysis:** Compare retention of users who connected a second account *early* (Week 1) vs. *late* (Week 6). If early connectors retain better, the feature likely drives value. If only late connectors retain, the feature is likely a marker of users already engaged by other factors.

**3. Cohort Analysis: Bottom-Up Funnel Read**
Analyze retention from the bottom up. Do not look at acquisition sources yet.
*   **Segment A (Stabilized):** Users who hit `budget_target_met` in Month 1. Did they churn in Month 2? If yes, the hypothesis of "financial stabilization" is confirmed; the product needs a "next goal" workflow.
*   **Segment B (Struggling):** Users who exceeded budgets or had low `auto_categorization_confidence_score`. Did they churn? If yes, the drop-off is due to friction or lack of utility, not success.
*   **Segment C (Passive):** Users with zero transactions synced in Month 2. This indicates "forgetfulness" or disconnection from financial reality.

**4. Experiment Rigor for Q3 Roadmap**
Before prioritizing new features, validate the "second bank account" lever. Do not simply build more integrations based on the 20% correlation.
*   **Design:** Run an A/B test on new signups. Group A sees a prompt to connect a second account on Day 3; Group B sees no prompt.
*   **Pre-commitment:** Define the sample size and duration (e.g., 4 weeks) before starting. Do not peek at results on Day 2.
*   **Metrics:** Primary metric is Week 6 `Weekly Budget Reconciliation`; Guardrail metric is `support_ticket_volume` (to ensure confusion doesn't spike).
*   **Verdict Rule:** If Group A does not show a statistically significant lift in the *core action* (not just connection rate) by the end of the test, the correlation is non-causal, and building more integration features will not fix the churn.
