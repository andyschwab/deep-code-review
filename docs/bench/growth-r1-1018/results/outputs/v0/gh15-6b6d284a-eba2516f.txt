To answer your retention questions and validate the hypothesis that false positives drive churn, your event schema must link every automated detection to a specific user decision. Do not instrument "everything"; capture only the events that answer whether the system delivered value or noise.

**1. Core Event Categories**
Define three distinct logical event types. Each must carry a stable, pseudonymous `household_id` to enable cohort analysis without exposing PII.

*   **`motion_detected`**: Fired by the algorithm when movement occurs.
    *   **Required Properties**: `detection_id` (unique), `object_type` (person, pet, vehicle, unknown), `confidence_score` (0–1), `timestamp`, and `zone_id` (e.g., "front_door", "backyard").
    *   **Purpose**: Establishes the baseline volume and the raw error rate of the classifier.

*   **`alert_delivered`**: Fired when a notification is successfully pushed to a user's device.
    *   **Required Properties**: `detection_id` (foreign key to the motion event), `delivery_channel` (push, sms, email), `timestamp`.
    *   **Purpose**: Confirms the user had the opportunity to react. Filters out backend detections that were suppressed by user settings before reaching the user.

*   **`user_response`**: Fired when the user interacts with the alert.
    *   **Required Properties**: `detection_id` (foreign key), `action_type` (verified_safe, confirmed_threat, dismissed, ignored_timeout), `time_to_respond` (seconds elapsed between `alert_delivered` and this event), `timestamp`.
    *   **Purpose**: This is your critical retention signal. "Ignored_timeout" (no action within a set window, e.g., 30 seconds) combined with `object_type: pet` validates your false positive hypothesis. "Verified_safe" indicates a false alarm that required manual labor, which also drives churn.

**2. Logical Relationships and Retention Logic**
The schema must enforce a one-to-one relationship between a `motion_detected` event and its subsequent `alert_delivered` and `user_response` events via the `detection_id`. This chain allows you to construct a **Retention Funnel** read bottom-up:

*   **Step 1 (Value Leak)**: Calculate the rate of `dismissed` or `ignored_timeout` responses grouped by `object_type`. If "pet" events have a 95% dismissal rate, your hypothesis is confirmed: the algorithm is noisy for pet owners.
*   **Step 2 (Friction Cost)**: Measure the `time_to_respond` for `verified_safe` actions. High friction here (users having to constantly check and clear alerts) predicts churn even if they don't explicitly dismiss.
*   **Step 3 (Cohort Analysis)**: Group `household_id`s by their primary noise source (e.g., "High Pet False Positives"). Compare the Day-7 and Day-30 active rates of this cohort against households with low false positive rates. If the high-noise cohort retains significantly worse, you have causal evidence for your roadmap.

**3. Implementation Guardrails**
*   **Instrument for the Question**: Do not log raw video frames or continuous telemetry in this schema. Only log the discrete decision points listed above.
*   **Identifier Seam**: The `household_id` must be stable across sessions to track user behavior over the 30-day beta, but it must be pseudonymous. Route any mapping of this ID to real-world addresses or names to your privacy compliance layer, not this analytics schema.
*   **Baseline Dependency**: Do not assume a "good" dismissal rate. Your benchmark is the trend of your own beta data. If dismissal rates drop week-over-week after an algorithm tweak, that is success.

**This Week's Step**: Before Monday's modeling session, define the exact enumeration values for `action_type` (specifically distinguishing between an explicit "dismiss" swipe and a passive "ignore" timeout), as conflating these two will obscure whether users are actively annoyed or just habituated to noise.

Note: This schema design assumes the MVP stage where validating the core value proposition (accurate alerts) is the primary driver of retention.
Do not fabricate benchmark thresholds for "acceptable" false positive rates; derive these solely from the correlation between your beta dismissal rates and actual churn.
