For the SkyDrop Austin pilot, reject "FlightInitiated" and "PackageSecured" as primary success metrics; they measure operational output, not customer value or trust. Your North Star must be **CustomerHandoff Completed**. This event only fires when the recipient physically accepts the package, directly validating your hypothesis that trust (safety/reliability) is the adoption bottleneck. If a drone flies and lands safely but the customer refuses the handoff due to perceived risk, "SafeLanding" would falsely count as success while "CustomerHandoff" correctly identifies a trust failure.

To distinguish operational failures from external factors like weather, implement a three-tier event taxonomy with specific properties:

1.  **Primary Value Event:** `CustomerHandoff_Completed`
    *   **Definition:** The moment the customer scans/confirms receipt.
    *   **Properties:** `delivery_id`, `scheduled_time`, `actual_time`, `weather_condition_code` (e.g., clear, rain, wind_speed), `delay_reason_code` (e.g., none, weather_hold, mechanical_fault, customer_unavailable).
    *   **Insight:** A low completion rate despite high `FlightInitiated` counts indicates a trust or last-mile reliability issue.

2.  **Operational Integrity Event:** `SafeLanding_Confirmed`
    *   **Definition:** Drone touches down within geofence without incident.
    *   **Properties:** `landing_status` (success, hard_landing, abort), `abort_reason` (weather, sensor_error, low_battery).
    *   **Insight:** Use this to calculate the "Operational Success Rate." If `SafeLanding` is high but `CustomerHandoff` is low, the issue is customer trust or handoff logistics, not flight safety.

3.  **External Factor Marker:** `Weather_Hold_Initialized`
    *   **Definition:** System pauses dispatch or recalls drone due to weather thresholds.
    *   **Properties:** `trigger_metric` (wind_gust, precipitation), `duration_minutes`.
    *   **Insight:** Do not count these as failures in your reliability metric. Instead, create a derived metric: **Adjusted Reliability Rate** = `(CustomerHandoff_Completed) / (Total Orders - Weather_Held_Orders)`. This isolates system performance from uncontrollable environmental factors.
