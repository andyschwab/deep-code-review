**Prioritized Event Taxonomy for NexusAI Beta (5 Events Max)**

To distinguish user confusion from genuine disinterest and enable a valid go/no-go decision by January 30th, instrument these five events. This selection prioritizes **retention of the core action** over vanity metrics or system health, adhering to the MVP stage requirement of measuring only what answers a named question.

1.  **`core_action_completed`** (Properties: `document_length`, `time_to_complete`)
    *   **Question Answered:** Did the user actually derive value (a finished document), or did they abandon the flow?
    *   **Rationale:** "Document created" is a vanity metric if the document is empty or abandoned mid-stream. This event defines your North Star: value delivered. Low completion rates indicate either confusion (UX friction) or disinterest (product mismatch). Without this, you cannot measure retention.

2.  **`first_value_realized`** (Properties: `time_since_signup`, `action_trigger`)
    *   **Question Answered:** How long does it take a new user to reach their first "aha" moment?
    *   **Rationale:** You explicitly questioned "time-to-first-value." In an MVP, activation speed predicts retention. If users take >2 minutes to generate their first useful output, they will churn before forming a habit. This distinguishes confusion (high time-to-value) from disinterest (no attempt).

3.  **`suggestion_interaction`** (Properties: `suggestion_id`, `action_taken`: [accepted, rejected, ignored, edited])
    *   **Question Answered:** Is the AI output actually useful, or are users fighting it?
    *   **Rationale:** "Suggestion accepted" is insufficient. Knowing the ratio of **accepted vs. edited vs. ignored** reveals product quality. High "edited" rates suggest the AI is close but confusing; high "ignored" rates suggest the feature is irrelevant. This is your primary input metric for the North Star.

4.  **`session_return`** (Properties: `days_since_first_session`, `session_count`)
    *   **Question Answered:** Are users coming back to the core action without prompting?
    *   **Rationale:** Retention is the only true validator of product-market fit. Do not track "daily active users" (vanity); track specific returns to the core action. If users do not return within 3-5 days, the product has not solved a persistent pain point. This is the bottom-up funnel check required before analyzing acquisition.

5.  **`critical_flow_abort`** (Properties: `step_failed`, `error_code`, `last_action`)
    *   **Question Answered:** Where exactly are users getting stuck due to bugs or UX confusion?
    *   **Rationale:** Do not instrument generic "error rates" (system health/ops observability). Instrument specific aborts in the *user journey*. If 40% of users abort at the "tone selection" step, that is confusion. If they abort after seeing the price, that is disinterest. This targets engineering bandwidth to the highest-leakage point.

**Implementation Note:** Do not instrument generic system logs or cumulative signups; they are noise at this stage. Ensure all user identifiers are pseudonymous to separate product cohorts from ops observability.
