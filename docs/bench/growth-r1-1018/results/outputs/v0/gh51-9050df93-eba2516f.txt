**Activation Metric Definition: "Compliance-Cleared First Transfer"**

Do not measure activation as "first transfer within three days" in isolation. Given the regulatory environment in Vietnam and Thailand, this current definition conflates product friction with compliance latency, artificially depressing your activation rate to 12%.

**Proposed New Definition:**
A user is **Activated** when they complete their first peer-to-peer transfer **within 7 days of completing Identity Verification (KYC)**.

This shifts the "clock" for your core value metric. The user journey now has two distinct gates:
1.  **Verification Rate:** % of registrants who complete KYC.
2.  **Post-KYC Activation Rate:** % of verified users who transfer within 7 days.

**Why this balances speed and compliance:**
*   **Regulatory Reality:** In emerging crypto markets, KYC approval can take 24–48 hours due to manual reviews or bank API delays. Your current 3-day window starts at signup, penalizing users who are ready to transact but are waiting on compliance.
*   **Value Realization:** The "aha moment" is the transfer, not the signup. By starting the clock post-KYC, you measure the product's ability to deliver value once the legal barrier is removed. If the Post-KYC Activation Rate is high (>40%) but Verification Rate is low, your bottleneck is compliance/onboarding, not the wallet utility.

**Daily Thresholds to Track:**
Since you have no historical baseline for this new metric, do not use industry benchmarks. Track these three figures daily against your own trailing 7-day average:

1.  **KYC Completion Rate:** Target >60% of registrants. (If <50%, the drop-off is pre-product).
2.  **Post-KYC Day-1 Transfer Rate:** Target >15% of *newly verified* users transferring within 24 hours of approval. This is your leading indicator of immediate value.
3.  **Post-KYC Day-7 Activation Rate:** Target >35% of *newly verified* users transferring within a week. This is your robust activation metric.

*   **Pause Marketing (Fix Onboarding):** If KYC Completion Rate is <50% OR Post-KYC Day-1 Transfer Rate is <10%. Spending on acquisition now pours users into a leaky compliance or utility bucket.
*   **Expand to Indonesia:** If KYC Completion Rate is >60% AND Post-KYC Day-7 Activation Rate is >30%. This proves that once users are cleared, the product delivers value, and the previous 12% figure was a measurement error caused by the timer starting too early.
