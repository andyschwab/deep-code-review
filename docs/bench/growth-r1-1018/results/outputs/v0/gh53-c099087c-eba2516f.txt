### North Star & Measurement Framework for EchoRoom

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Listeners completing a "Value Session" (defined as listening ≥10 minutes to a room where ≥2 distinct speakers contribute).
*Reject vanity metrics:* Total rooms created and total downloads are rejected; they rise regardless of ecosystem health and currently mask the retention leak.

**Diagnosis (Bottom-Up AARRR):**
Your data indicates a **Retention/Activation leak**, not an Acquisition problem. Doubling room supply while session duration drops 36% (22m → 14m) suggests you have increased noise, not value. Pouring effort into creator activity (supply) while listener retention flattens is inefficient; listeners are the constraint in a live audio network. If listeners do not stay, creators have no audience, creating a negative feedback loop.

**Event Taxonomy (Instrument only these questions):**
1.  **Question:** Does new content drive repeat listening?
    *   *Event:* `listener_return_cohort` (Properties: `user_id`, `days_since_first_listen`, `room_category`).
2.  **Question:** Is the " Value Session" threshold being met?
    *   *Event:* `value_session_completed` (Properties: `duration_seconds`, `unique_speaker_count`, `room_type`).
3.  **Question:** Are creators retaining an audience?
    *   *Event:* `creator_audience_retention` (Properties: `creator_id`, `avg_listener_duration`, `listener_return_rate`).

**Two-Sided Network Health Read:**
Do not measure creators and listeners in isolation. Measure the **match quality**:
*   **Supply-Demand Ratio:** Active listeners per active room (segmented by room topic). If this ratio drops while room count rises, you are fragmenting the audience.
*   **Creator-Led Retention:** Cohort retention of listeners who follow a specific creator vs. those who browse public rooms. If follower retention is high but public room retention is dropping, your algorithm is surfacing low-quality public content.

**Experiment Rigor Warning:**
Do not A/B test the next UI change by peeking at daily dashboards. If you run a test to fix session duration, pre-commit to a sample size or duration (e.g., 14 days) and define a **guardrail metric** (e.g., creator churn rate). A "win" on listener duration that causes creators to leave is a net loss. Without a pre-committed design, any observed lift is `UNVERIFIED`.

**This Week's Steps:**
1.  **Query:** Segment session duration by "rooms with ≥2 speakers" vs. "rooms with 1 speaker" to confirm if multi-speaker dynamics correlate with the 22-minute baseline.
2.  **Define:** Instrument the `value_session_completed` event immediately; current "session duration" is too blunt to distinguish between engaged listening and background noise.
3.  **Decision Rule:** If the "≥2 speakers" cohort shows flat retention while single-speaker rooms drive the volume increase, pause public room promotion and shift Q3 roadmap to tools that help creators invite co-hosts, not just broadcast.
