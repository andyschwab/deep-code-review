### Proposed Core Metrics for CareConnect

**1. North Star Metric: Weekly Active Patients with Verified Adherence**
Replace "appointments completed" with a metric that only rises when a patient receives value. Define this as the count of unique patients per week who complete an appointment **and** demonstrate a verified adherence signal within 7 days.
*   **Why:** "Appointments completed" is a vanity metric; it increases even if patients are confused or unsupported. Adherence proves the treatment plan was understood and actionable.
*   **Measurement Strategy:** Since survey response is low (10%) and digital logs are complex, use **proxy events** that require minimal interaction:
    *   *Pharmacy Fill Confirmation:* Integration with partner pharmacies to flag when a prescribed medication is picked up (binary yes/no).
    *   *Caregiver Acknowledgement:* A single-click SMS link sent to a designated family member/caregiver confirming "Plan Reviewed," rather than asking the patient to log in.
    *   *No-Show Reduction:* Track the rate of kept follow-up appointments as a passive indicator of engagement and understanding.

**2. Input Metric: The "Friction-Free" Completion Rate**
Measure "ease of use" not by clicks, but by **assistance independence**.
*   **Definition:** The percentage of appointments where the patient completes the entire flow (join, consult, end) without triggering a "help" event or requiring a staff-initiated callback.
*   **Why:** For patients over 70, needing human intervention to navigate the tool indicates a failure in "ease of use." High independence correlates with confidence and comprehension.
*   **Data Source:** Operational logs of staff callbacks or "technical support" flags during the session window. This avoids complex interaction tracking while capturing the outcome of confusion.

**3. Retention Cohort: 30-Day Return for Chronic Management**
Read the funnel bottom-up. Acquisition (new signups) is irrelevant if the patient does not return for necessary chronic care.
*   **Definition:** The percentage of patients who had an initial visit and successfully returned for a scheduled follow-up within 30 days.
*   **Why:** In elderly care, retention is the primary proxy for trust and perceived support. If they do not return, the initial experience likely failed to convey safety or clarity.
*   **Baseline:** Compare this quarter's return rate against last quarter's internal baseline. Do not compare to industry averages.

**4. Qualitative Signal: The "Clarity" Subset**
Augment the low-response survey with a high-signal micro-question.
*   **Strategy:** Instead of a full survey, trigger a one-question SMS post-visit: "Did you leave the call knowing exactly what to do next? (Yes/No)."
*   **Goal:** Increase response volume by reducing friction. Even a 20% response rate on a single binary question provides a more statistically significant trend line than a 10% rate on a complex survey.

**Immediate Next Steps (This Week)**
1.  **Define the Adherence Proxy:** Select one objective data source (e.g., pharmacy fill data or caregiver SMS acknowledgment) to pilot as the primary adherence signal.
2.  **Instrument the "Help" Flag:** Ensure operational logs capture every instance where staff must intervene to help a patient connect or navigate, establishing the baseline for "Friction-Free Completion."
3.  **Draft the Micro-Survey:** Finalize the single-question SMS script for the medical advisory board review.

*Note: All metrics must be calculated using CareConnect's own historical data; no external healthcare benchmarks are applied here. Patient identifiers used for cohort tracking must remain pseudonymous and routed to privacy compliance protocols.*
