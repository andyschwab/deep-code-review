**Decision: Do not deploy globally. The test result is inconclusive and potentially harmful due to conflicting primary signals.**

The 25% lift in click-through rate (CTR) is invalidated by the 10% drop in pages read per session. In a book club context, "pages read" is a stronger proxy for customer value than "clicks." A rise in clicks coupled with a fall in consumption indicates the new algorithm is likely optimizing for curiosity or click-bait rather than genuine engagement. Deploying this now risks degrading content quality and eroding trust with your editorial standards.

**Evaluation Plan: From Vanity to Value**

To validate long-term impact before the September update, shift your measurement framework immediately:

1.  **Redefine the North Star:** Stop using CTR as the primary success metric. Adopt **"Weekly Active Readers completing a book chapter"** or **"Average Pages Read per User per Week"** as your North Star. CTR becomes an input metric only; if it rises while the North Star falls, the change is a failure.
2.  **Diagnose Bottom-Up (Retention First):** Do not measure acquisition or total clicks yet. Analyze **Week-2 and Week-4 retention cohorts** for users exposed to the new algorithm versus the legacy system.
    *   *Question:* Do users who experienced the new recommendations return to read again within 7 days?
    *   *Action:* Instrument an event `book_session_started` with properties `source_algorithm` and `pages_read_count`. If retention dips, the "diverse titles" hypothesis is incorrect; the recommendations are irrelevant.
3.  **Measure Community Health:** Since community discussion is a core value pillar, track the ratio of **Comments/Reviews per 1,000 Pages Read**.
    *   *Hypothesis Check:* If pages read drop but discussion volume stays flat, users are frustrated but vocal. If both drop, the algorithm is killing engagement.
4.  **Experiment Rigor (Pre-commitment):**
    *   Run a new A/B test with a **pre-committed duration** (e.g., 4 weeks) to capture full reading cycles. Do not peek at daily results.
    *   **Primary Metric:** Retention rate (Day-7 return).
    *   **Guardrail Metric:** Average pages read per session (must not decrease by >2%).
    *   **Stop Rule:** If the guardrail breaches -2% with statistical significance, halt the test immediately regardless of CTR gains.
