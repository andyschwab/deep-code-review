Implementing now carries a high risk of a "false positive"—rolling out a change that provides no actual value while potentially introducing unforeseen friction or technical debt.

However, due to lower traffic volume, our test lacked the statistical power to confirm this wasn't just random noise. Argue that implementing an unverified change risks destabilizing the pricing page performance right before a critical quarter.
