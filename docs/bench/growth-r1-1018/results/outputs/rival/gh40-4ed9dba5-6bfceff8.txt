# NicheCRM Pricing A/B Test Plan

**Objective:** Determine if introducing a $49 "Professional" tier increases Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) without significantly cannibalizing the $99 "Enterprise" tier.

### 1. Hypothesis
Because independent galleries perceive a value gap between the $19 and $99 plans, we believe introducing a $49 "Professional" tier will increase overall ARPU by 15% among trial users. We will know this is true if the test group shows a statistically significant lift in ARPU compared to the control group, with Enterprise tier adoption remaining within 10% of the control baseline.

### 2. Test Structure
*   **Control Group (50%):** Sees current binary options ($19 Basic, $99 Enterprise).
*   **Variant Group (50%):** Sees three options ($19 Basic, $49 Professional, $99 Enterprise).
*   **Population:** 2,000 active trial users (1,000 per variant).
*   **Duration:** 6 weeks (February 1 – March 14).

### 3. Metrics Definition
*   **Primary Metric:** **Average Revenue Per User (ARPU).** Calculated as total monthly recurring revenue (MRR) generated from the cohort divided by the total number of users in that cohort. This accounts for both conversion rate and plan selection.
*   **Guardrail Metric:** **Enterprise Plan Conversion Rate.** If the rate of users selecting the $99 plan in the Variant group drops by more than 10% relative to the Control group, the test indicates harmful cannibalization.
*   **Secondary Metric:** **Overall Conversion Rate.** Percentage of trial users upgrading to *any* paid plan.

### 4. Sample Size & Power Analysis
With 1,000 users per variant, this test is powered to detect meaningful shifts in plan distribution rather than tiny incremental changes.
*   **Detection Capability:** Assuming a baseline conversion rate of ~20% (industry standard for SaaS trials), a sample of 1,000 per group allows us to detect a **20-25% relative lift** in conversion with 80% power at 95% confidence.
*   **ARPU Sensitivity:** We can reliably detect an ARPU increase of approximately $3-$5 per user. If the true lift is smaller, the 6-week duration may yield inconclusive results, which is an acceptable risk given the traffic constraints.

### 5. Risk Mitigation Strategy
*   **Cannibalization Check:** We will monitor the Enterprise selection rate weekly. If the $99 plan selection in the Variant group drops precipitously (e.g., >15% drop) in the first two weeks, we will pause the test to prevent irreversible revenue loss.
*   **Confusion Monitoring:** Track "time to purchase" and support ticket volume regarding pricing. If users in the Variant group take significantly longer to convert or submit more billing inquiries, the additional option may be causing decision paralysis.

### 6. Decision Framework for CFO
*   **Go (Launch $49 Plan):** Variant ARPU is statistically higher than Control AND Enterprise cannibalization is <10%.
*   **No-Go (Keep Binary):** Variant ARPU is lower or statistically insignificant, OR Enterprise cannibalization exceeds 10% without sufficient volume compensation from the new tier.
*   **Iterate:** If conversion lifts but ARPU is flat, consider adjusting the feature set of the $49 tier to justify a higher price point ($59) before a full launch.

This plan prioritizes revenue integrity over speed, ensuring that any new tier adds net value before the board review.

*Note: Ensure billing system configuration allows for mid-cycle upgrades should users need to switch plans post-test. All revenue figures must be recognized based on the effective date of the upgrade.*
