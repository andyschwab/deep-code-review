For CodeSync's real-time collaboration feature, **reduction in time-to-merge** is the only metric that directly validates your core value hypothesis: eliminating the four hours per week currently lost to merge conflicts. Only a decrease in the time engineers spend resolving conflicts demonstrates true product value.

Similarly, satisfaction scores can be inflated by the "novelty effect" of new features without reflecting actual workflow improvements.

Segment by team size and project complexity to understand where the feature delivers most value.
