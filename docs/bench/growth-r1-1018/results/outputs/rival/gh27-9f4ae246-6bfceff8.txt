# Mobile vs. Web Activation Gap: Investigation Plan

**Objective:** Isolate whether the 23% activation gap (Web 45% vs. Mobile 22%) stems from technical performance (load times, crashes) or UX design (screen real estate, checklist visibility).

## 1. Data Normalization Strategy
To address shorter mobile session frequencies, we will shift from "per-session" to "per-user cohort" analysis over a fixed 7-day observation window.
*   **Cohort Definition:** Group users by signup date and device type.
*   **Session Aggregation:** Sum all events per user within the 7-day window. A user who completes step 3 on day 1 and step 4 on day 3 is treated as a single continuous journey, eliminating bias from frequent session restarts on mobile.
*   **Exclusion Criteria:** Exclude users with <2 seconds total time on app (bot/accidental install) to clean the denominator.

## 2. Comparative Funnel Analysis
We will construct a step-by-step funnel for the onboarding checklist, comparing drop-off rates at each specific action.

**Metrics to Track per Step:**
1.  **Entry Rate:** % of installs reaching the checklist view.
2.  **Completion Rate:** % of viewers completing the specific step.
3.  **Time-to-Complete:** Median time between step start and success event.
4.  **Error Rate:** % of sessions triggering error logs or rage clicks (rapid repeated taps) at that step.

**Hypothesis Testing Matrix:**
*   **If Technical:** Look for high drop-off correlated with slow load times (>3s) or specific OS versions at the *start* of the flow. High crash rates before step 1 indicate performance issues.
*   **If UX/Design:** Look for high drop-off at specific interaction points (e.g., "Upload Avatar" or "Connect Calendar") where mobile completion time is significantly higher than web, despite successful loading. High "rage click" rates on small buttons confirm screen real estate issues.

## 3. Segmentation for Root Cause Isolation
Break down the mobile cohort to pinpoint the failure mode:
*   **By Connection:** Compare activation on Wi-Fi vs. Cellular. If cellular drops significantly, latency is the culprit.
*   **By Device Age:** Compare high-end vs. low-end devices. If low-end devices fail early, performance rendering is the issue.
*   **By Step Complexity:** Categorize checklist items by interaction type (text input vs. button tap vs. file upload). If only complex inputs fail on mobile, it is a UX form-factor issue.

## 4. Deliverable Timeline
*   **Days 1-3:** Query event logs to build the normalized 7-day cohort funnels.
*   **Days 4-7:** Correlate drop-off points with performance logs (load time, errors) and interaction heatmaps (rage clicks).
*   **Days 8-10:** Synthesize findings into a "Blocker Map" identifying the exact step and cause (Tech vs. UX).
*   **Day 15:** Draft presentation for Steering Committee.

## Recommended Immediate Action
Implement a "Quick Win" test: If the data points to UX, propose a simplified mobile-specific checklist (fewer steps, larger touch targets) for an A/B test to validate the hypothesis before a full redesign.

*Note: Ensure event tracking names are consistent across iOS, Android, and Web before querying; mismatched naming will skew the funnel comparison.*
*Data must be pulled from the last full quarter to match the reported 45%/22% baseline.*
