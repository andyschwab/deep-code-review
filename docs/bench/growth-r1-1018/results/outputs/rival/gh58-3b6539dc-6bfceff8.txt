# Ontario Business License Portal: Success Metrics Scorecard

To satisfy both taxpayer accountability and citizen experience, we recommend tracking four outcome-based metrics. These measure whether the portal is faster, easier, and more effective than the legacy system, without relying on technical implementation details.

### 1. Renewal Completion Rate (Effectiveness)
**Definition:** The percentage of businesses that start a renewal application and successfully submit it.
**Why it matters:** A drop in call volume is only a success if renewals are actually finishing. If this rate is low, businesses may be frustrated and abandoning the process, shifting the burden from phone calls to non-compliance.
**Target:** Match or exceed the historical completion rate of the legacy system within 90 days.

### 2. Average Time-to-Submit (Efficiency)
**Definition:** The median time a user spends actively completing a renewal, from start to finish.
**Why it matters:** This directly measures the "faster" promise of digital transformation. It distinguishes between a quick successful transaction and a user struggling silently.
**Target:** Reduce average completion time by 40% compared to the combined time of previous phone/mail processing.

### 3. First-Time Applicant Success Rate (Equity & Growth)
**Definition:** The percentage of new businesses (without legacy accounts) who successfully complete their initial licensing application on the first attempt.
**Why it matters:** Your current data only reflects migrated users. This metric reveals if the system works for new entrepreneurs, who face the highest friction and represent the province's economic growth.
**Target:** Achieve an 85% first-attempt success rate for new applicants.

### 4. User Effort Score (Satisfaction)
**Definition:** A single question asked immediately after submission: "How easy was it to complete your renewal today?" (rated 1–5).
**Why it matters:** Efficiency means nothing if the process feels difficult. This provides direct citizen feedback for the Public Accounts Committee, replacing abstract "satisfaction" surveys with immediate, transaction-specific data.
**Target:** Maintain an average score of 4.2 or higher.

---

**Reporting Note:** Present these metrics as a trend line since the February migration to show trajectory, not just a single snapshot.
**Audit Readiness:** These four outcomes directly link reduced operational costs (Efficiency) to verified citizen success (Completion & Satisfaction), addressing both fiscal and service mandates.
