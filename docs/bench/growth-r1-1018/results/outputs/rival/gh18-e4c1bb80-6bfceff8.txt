# Proposed Core Success Metrics for CareConnect

To balance business viability with patient well-being for our 70+ demographic, we propose shifting from a pure volume metric ("appointments completed") to a **Value-Realization Framework**. This framework measures not just access to care, but the efficacy and accessibility of that care.

## 1. Primary Outcome Metric: The Adherence Proxy
**Definition:** The percentage of patients who successfully complete a prescribed post-appointment action within 48 hours.
**Rationale:** This replaces "appointments completed" as the north star. It directly correlates to health outcomes without requiring complex clinical data integration.
**Measurement Approach (Hybrid):**
*   **Digital Signal:** Track simple binary events that require minimal friction, such as clicking a "Confirm Medication Received" link in an SMS or answering a single-question automated voice call ("Did you pick up your prescription? Yes/No").
*   **Human Signal:** For the 30% of patients who do not engage digitally, care coordinators will log adherence during routine check-in calls.
*   **Target:** Establish a baseline using current self-reported data, then aim for a 15% increase in verified adherence actions quarter-over-quarter.

## 2. Accessibility Metric: The "Zero-Friction" Rate
**Definition:** The percentage of appointments that begin without technical assistance or rescheduling due to connectivity issues.
**Rationale:** For patients over 70, "ease of use" is best measured by the *absence* of struggle rather than the speed of interaction. High friction indicates a failure in product design for this specific demographic.
**Measurement Approach:**
*   Track the ratio of successful joins vs. failed join attempts.
*   Log instances where a patient calls support *before* or *during* an appointment specifically for connection help.
*   **Target:** Maintain a "Zero-Friction" rate above 92%. A drop here signals a need for UI simplification or better onboarding, regardless of appointment volume.

## 3. Qualitative Dimension: The "Clarity & Confidence" Index
**Definition:** A micro-survey metric capturing patient sentiment immediately post-consultation.
**Rationale:** Current surveys have low response rates because they are likely too long or complex. We need a high-frequency, low-burden signal.
**Measurement Approach:**
*   Deploy a single-question automated voice or large-text SMS survey within 1 hour of the visit: *"On a scale of 1 to 5, how clear are your next steps?"*
*   Supplement this with a monthly "Deep Dive" call sample (N=50) conducted by non-sales staff to gather narrative feedback on "feeling supported."
*   **Target:** Achieve a 40% response rate on the micro-survey (due to reduced friction) with an average score of 4.5/5.

## 4. Business Viability Metric: Continuity of Care
**Definition:** The percentage of patients who return for a scheduled follow-up or chronic care management session within the recommended clinical window.
**Rationale:** This balances well-being with revenue. Patients who understand their plan and feel supported are more likely to return. It validates that the platform is sustaining health, not just treating acute episodes.
**Measurement Approach:**
*   Compare the date of the initial visit against the date of the next scheduled interaction.
*   **Target:** Increase retention/follow-up booking rate by 10% year-over-year.

## Summary for the Advisory Board
These metrics move CareConnect from a "transactional" model (counting visits) to a "relational" model (counting health progress). By focusing on **Adherence**, **Frictionless Access**, **Clarity**, and **Continuity**, we align our financial goals directly with the physiological and psychological safety of our elderly patients.

*Note: Implementation requires updating our current logging to capture "failed join" reasons and scripting the single-question post-visit survey.*
