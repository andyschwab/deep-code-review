### Priority Tracking Plan for NexusAI Beta Launch

To distinguish between user confusion and genuine disinterest with only five events, you must track the full activation funnel and specific failure points. Do not track aggregate "error rates" as a single event; instead, instrument the specific blocking errors that prevent value realization.

**1. `onboarding_step_completed`**
*   **Properties:** `step_name`, `time_elapsed`
*   **Rationale:** This establishes your baseline drop-off before the product is even used. If users vanish here, it is a positioning or signup friction issue, not a product-market fit issue. It separates "can't get in" from "don't like it."

**2. `document_created`**
*   **Properties:** `template_type`, `time_to_create`
*   **Rationale:** This is your primary activation metric. A user who creates a document has overcome the "blank page" fear. Low volume here indicates the core value proposition isn't compelling enough to trigger action, signaling genuine disinterest.

**3. `suggestion_accepted`**
*   **Properties:** `suggestion_type`, `confidence_score`, `edit_distance`
*   **Rationale:** This is your core "aha!" moment and proxy for value. High creation but low acceptance suggests the AI output is poor or irrelevant (product failure). High acceptance validates the utility of the automated writing feature.

**4. `generation_error_encountered`**
*   **Properties:** `error_code`, `step_location`, `user_intent`
*   **Rationale:** Instead of a generic error rate, this captures specific blocking failures during generation. If users create documents but hit this event repeatedly and then churn, the drop-off is due to **confusion/technical failure**, not disinterest. This tells engineering exactly what to fix.

**5. `session_abandoned_prematurely`**
*   **Properties:** `last_action`, `session_duration`, `documents_count`
*   **Rationale:** Triggered when a user leaves without a successful `suggestion_accepted` after spending >2 minutes. This differentiates between a quick bounce (disinterest) and a struggle session (confusion). If this rate is high immediately after `document_created`, users are trying to work but failing to find value.

**Decision Framework for Jan 30th:**
*   **Go:** High `document_created` rate + High `suggestion_accepted` rate.
*   **Fix & Delay:** High `document_created` + High `generation_error_encountered` (Technical debt, not PMF failure).
*   **Pivot/Stop:** Low `document_created` + Low `session_abandoned_prematurely` (Genuine disinterest; the problem isn't worth solving for this audience).

**Note:** Ensure `user_id` is attached to all events to calculate user-level retention later. Do not implement "time-to-first-value" as a separate event; calculate this metric downstream using the timestamps from `onboarding_step_completed` and `suggestion_accepted`.
