### Experiment Plan: Ad Frequency Optimization

**1. Leading Indicators (First 48 Hours)**
Since Day-7 retention cannot be measured in 48 hours, monitor these proxy metrics to detect early churn signals:
*   **Session Frequency Drop:** A statistically significant decrease in the average number of sessions per user compared to the control group. If users return less often immediately, they are likely churned.
*   **Level Start/Completion Rate:** Monitor the funnel drop-off between "Level Start" and "Level Complete." A sharp decline suggests frustration with ad interruptions causing users to abandon levels or close the app mid-session.
*   **Uninstall Events:** Track explicit uninstall events (if available via SDK) or sudden cessation of activity (users active yesterday but zero sessions today).
*   **Ad Load Failure Rate:** Ensure the technical implementation isn't causing crashes or freezes, which would mimic churn.

**2. Minimum Sample Size Calculation**
To detect a **2% relative drop** in Day-7 retention (from a baseline of 22% down to 21.56%) with 80% power and 95% confidence:
*   **Baseline Conversion (p1):** 0.22
*   **Target Conversion (p2):** 0.2156
*   **Required Sample Size:** Approximately **265,000 users per variant**.

*Critical Constraint:* With only 150,000 DAU, allocating 10% (15,000 users) to the test group means it will take **~18 days** just to accumulate the necessary sample size for the treatment group, excluding the 7-day observation window. You cannot achieve statistical significance for a 2% retention lift/drop within a short Q3 window using this allocation. You must either:
*   Increase the traffic allocation (e.g., to 50/50 split) to finish faster.
*   Accept detecting only larger effects (e.g., a 5-10% drop), which requires far fewer users (~4,500 per variant).
*   Extend the testing timeline beyond Q3.

**3. Success Criteria & Decision Matrix**
Define the rollout decision based on the net impact on **LTV (Lifetime Value)**, approximated here as *Retention × ARPU*.

| Outcome Scenario | Retention Impact | Revenue Impact | Decision |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Win** | No significant drop (<1% relative) | Increase >5% | **Rollout Global** |
| **Loss (Churn)** | Drop >2% relative | Any increase | **Kill Feature** (Long-term LTV damage outweighs short-term gain) |
| **Loss (Revenue)** | No drop | No significant increase | **Kill Feature** (No upside to justify risk) |
| **Inconclusive** | Confidence interval spans zero | Mixed signals | **Extend Test** or **Kill** (Given Q3 constraints, prefer killing to avoid opportunity cost) |

**Guardrail Metric:** If Day-1 retention drops by >3% in the first 48 hours, **stop the test immediately**. This is a leading indicator of catastrophic Day-7 failure.

*Note: Given your traffic volume, detecting a subtle 2% retention change is statistically impossible within a standard sprint without increasing exposure risk. Consider testing for a larger effect size or extending the timeline.*
*Ensure your analytics pipeline attributes revenue to the specific cohort exposed to the extra ad, not just the session.*
