# PeerRent Trust Health Dashboard: Leading Indicators & Composite Metrics

To move beyond the lagging "dispute rate," you must measure **trust signals** (inputs) and **trust actions** (behaviors) that precede a transaction. The low 8% lift from ID verification suggests users weigh other factors more heavily or do not understand the badge's value.

## 1. Composite Metric: The Trust Velocity Score (TVS)
Create a single health score for every user and the overall marketplace, updated daily. This predicts dispute likelihood before gear changes hands.

**Formula Components (Weighted):**
*   **Response Latency (30%):** Time between rental request and first response. (Fast response = high trust).
*   **Profile Completeness (20%):** Binary check: Verified ID + Insurance Linked + 3+ Photos + Bio > 50 words.
*   **Acceptance Ratio (20%):** % of requests accepted vs. ignored/declined.
*   **Repeat Request Rate (30%):** % of renters who book the same owner twice (the ultimate trust signal).

**Dashboard View:** Track the platform average TVS weekly. A dropping TVS predicts a future rise in disputes 2–4 weeks out.

## 2. Leading Behavioral Indicators
Track these specific micro-conversions in your analytics pipeline immediately:

*   **The "Profile Deep Dive" Ratio:**
    *   *Metric:* % of users who click "View Full Profile" or expand "Read Reviews" after seeing a listing thumbnail.
    *   *Insight:* High clicks indicate users are *seeking* trust signals because they don't feel safe yet. If this drops while conversion rises, your surface-level trust badges are working.
*   **Verification Click-Through:**
    *   *Metric:* % of users who click the "Verified ID" badge vs. those who click "Read Reviews."
    *   *Insight:* Your data suggests ID matters less than reviews. If users ignore the ID badge click, the 8% lift is explained; they are prioritizing social proof over identity proof.
*   **Insurance Upsell Acceptance:**
    *   *Metric:* % of renters who voluntarily add extra insurance coverage.
    *   *Insight:* Willingness to pay for protection is a proxy for perceived risk. High uptake means low trust in the base protocol.
*   **Message-to-Booking Conversion:**
    *   *Metric:* % of conversations that result in a booking request.
    *   *Insight:* Trust is often built in chat. A low rate suggests the profile isn't answering safety questions, forcing friction.

## 3. Essential Survey Questions (Micro-Surveys)
Deploy these triggered surveys (max 1 question) to capture sentiment without fatigue:

*   **Post-Message (to Renter):** "What was the primary factor that made you feel comfortable requesting this gear?"
    *   *Options:* Owner's Reviews, Response Speed, Verified Badge, Insurance Coverage, Price.
*   **Post-Decision (to Owner who declined):** "What stopped you from accepting this request?"
    *   *Options:* Renter had no reviews, Renter unverified, Gut feeling, Equipment value too high.
*   **Post-Rental (NPS Style):** "How safe did you feel during this transaction?" (Scale 1–5). Correlate this specifically with the *pre-transaction* Trust Velocity Score.

## 4. Operational Dashboard Layout
Build your real-time view with these three panels:

1.  **Friction Heatmap:** A funnel showing drop-off rates at "View Profile" → "Click Verification/Reviews" → "Send Message" → "Book." Identify where trust breaks down.
2.  **Signal Correlation Table:** A live regression showing which variable (Reviews, ID, Response Time, Insurance) has the highest correlation with *Booking Accepted*. Re-evaluate monthly; if ID stays low, stop promoting it as a primary trust lever.
3.  **Community Health Alert:** Flag any user whose Trust Velocity Score drops below the 10th percentile for manual review *before* they transact.

By shifting focus from "did a dispute happen?" to "are users exhibiting high-trust behaviors?", you can intervene operationally before assets are at risk.

*Note: Ensure your analytics implementation tracks click events on specific profile elements (badges vs. reviews) to validate the correlation data.*
