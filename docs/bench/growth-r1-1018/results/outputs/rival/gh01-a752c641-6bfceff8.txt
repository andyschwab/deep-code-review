### Immediate Data Request for Engineering

To isolate whether the drop-off is technical (bugs/errors) or behavioral (confusion/friction), request the following event logs segmented by **date (pre- vs. post-March 12)** and **device type (iOS vs. Android)**:

1.  **Step-Level Funnel Counts:**
    *   `vehicle_verification_started`
    *   `document_upload_viewed` (Did they see the new UI?)
    *   `document_upload_attempted` (Did they try to select a file?)
    *   `document_upload_failed` (Crucial: Capture error codes like "timeout," "format_invalid," or "server_error")
    *   `document_upload_success`
    *   `verification_completed`

2.  **Error & Latency Metrics:**
    *   Count of API errors returned during the upload step.
    *   Average time spent on the upload screen (a sharp increase suggests confusion; a sharp decrease suggests immediate abandonment due to errors).
    *   Drop-off rate *between* specific sub-steps (e.g., selecting file vs. hitting submit).

**Diagnosis Logic:**
*   **Technical Issue:** High `document_upload_failed` rates, specific error codes spiking post-March 12, or crashes logged during the session.
*   **Behavioral Issue:** Low `document_upload_attempted` rates despite high `document_upload_viewed` counts, or increased time-on-step without errors.

### Quick Validation Test Structure

Since you cannot roll back immediately without executive approval, run a **5% Canary A/B Test** starting tomorrow:

*   **Hypothesis:** Because the new interface introduced friction (assumed), reverting 5% of traffic to the legacy interface will increase verification completion rates by at least 10% compared to the new interface.
*   **Variant A (Control):** Current new interface (95% of traffic).
*   **Variant B (Treatment):** Legacy interface (5% of traffic).
*   **Primary Metric:** `verification_completed` rate.
*   **Guardrail Metric:** Support ticket volume related to uploads.
*   **Duration:** Run for 48–72 hours to gather ~200 completions in Variant B for directional significance. If Variant B outperforms A by >15%, you have data to recommend an immediate rollback.

### Communicating Revenue Impact to the CFO

Do not present the "14% gap" as a static percentage. Translate it into **lost weekly contribution margin** using this formula:

1.  **Calculate Lost Drivers:** (Historical Rate 35% − Current Rate 21%) × 4,200 starts = **588 lost drivers/week**.
2.  **Apply Lifetime Value (LTV):** Multiply 588 by the *net* LTV of an active driver (not gross revenue; subtract insurance, support, and acquisition costs).
    *   *Example:* If Net LTV is $500, the weekly burn is **$294,000**.
3.  **Project Quarterly Run-Rate:** Multiply the weekly burn by 12.
    *   *Example:* **$3.5M quarterly risk.**

**Executive Summary Phrasing:**
"The interface update is currently costing us ~588 verified drivers weekly. Based on current unit economics, this represents a **$294k weekly erosion in contribution margin**, projecting to a **$3.5M annualized revenue leak**. We recommend a 72-hour canary test to confirm the fix; if validated, a rollback recovers this margin immediately."

*Note: Ensure engineering logs include specific error strings to distinguish between "user gave up" and "system rejected file."*
*Do not wait for full statistical significance on the canary test if the lift is massive (>20%); the revenue risk justifies faster action.*
