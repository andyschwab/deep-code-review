# NovaPay Activation Metric Definition

## Recommended Activation Definition
**"Compliance-Adjusted Activated User"**: A user who completes their first peer-to-peer (P2P) transfer within **7 days** of registration, provided they have completed identity verification (KYC) at any point prior to the transfer.

### Rationale for 7-Day Window
The current 3-day window fails to account for Vietnam and Thailand's regulatory realities:
- **Vietnam**: KYC verification typically takes 24-48 hours during business days
- **Thailand**: Bank integration verification can take 2-3 business days
- **Weekend delays**: Registrations on Friday-Sunday face inherent 2-3 day verification delays

Extending to 7 days captures users who are genuinely engaged but delayed by compliance processes, while still measuring speed of value realization.

## Daily Tracking Thresholds

| Metric | Target | Alert Threshold | Critical Threshold |
|--------|--------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Day-1 KYC Completion Rate** | ≥85% | <75% | <60% |
| **Day-3 KYC Completion Rate** | ≥95% | <90% | <80% |
| **7-Day Activation Rate** (P2P transfer / registrations from 7 days prior) | ≥25% | <18% | <12% |
| **Time-to-First-Transfer (median)** | ≤4 days | >5 days | >6 days |
| **KYC-to-Transfer Conversion** (users who complete KYC and transfer within 48 hours) | ≥60% | <45% | <30% |

## Segmented Tracking

Track these metrics separately by:
1. **Country** (Vietnam vs. Thailand) – regulatory differences may require country-specific targets
2. **Registration day** – identify if weekend registrants show systematically lower activation
3. **KYC verification time** – segment users by verification duration (0-24h, 24-48h, 48-72h, 72h+)

## Decision Framework for Indonesia Expansion

**Proceed with expansion if:**
- 7-Day Activation Rate ≥20% for two consecutive weeks
- KYC-to-Transfer Conversion ≥50%
- Day-3 KYC Completion Rate ≥85%

**Pause and fix onboarding if:**
- 7-Day Activation Rate remains <15% after implementing tracking
- KYC-to-Transfer Conversion <35% (indicates friction post-verification)
- Significant drop-off (>40%) between KYC completion and first transfer attempt
