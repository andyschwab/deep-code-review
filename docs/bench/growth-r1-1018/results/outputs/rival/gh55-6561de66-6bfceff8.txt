# TutorMatch Metric Hierarchy: Balanced Marketplace Health

## Level 1: North Star & Business Health
*Goal: Measure sustainable two-sided value creation.*

**Primary North Star:** **Successful Matches per Active Week**
*Definition:* A "successful match" is a booking where the session occurs and both parties rate the experience ≥4 stars. This replaces "total bookings" to prioritize quality over volume.

**Guardrail Metrics (The "Do No Harm" Layer):**
*   **Student Retention Rate:** % of students booking a second session within 45 days. (Current concern: Declining).
*   **Tutor Churn Rate:** % of active tutors failing to accept any request in 14 days.
*   **Net Revenue Retention (NRR):** Revenue growth from existing students minus churn.

## Level 2: Marketplace Balance & Liquidity
*Goal: Detect supply/demand imbalances before they impact the North Star.*

**Liquidity Health Score (Composite Metric):**
Instead of relying solely on 24-hour fill rate, track this triad:
1.  **Request Fill Rate (24h):** % of student requests matched within 24 hours.
    *   *Threshold:* Alert if <85% for 3 consecutive days.
2.  **Tutor Utilization Rate:** % of tutor available hours actually booked.
    *   *Threshold:* Alert if <40% (indicates oversupply/inefficiency) or >85% (indicates supply constraint).
3.  **Search-to-Book Ratio:** % of student searches resulting in a booking.
    *   *Diagnostic:* Low ratio + High Fill Rate = Price/Quality mismatch. Low ratio + Low Fill Rate = Supply shortage.

**Segmentation Strategy:**
Break all Level 2 metrics by **Subject** (Math vs. English) and **Region** (London vs. Remote). Aggregate numbers hide local liquidity crises.

## Level 3: Operational Drivers & Hypothesis Testing
*Goal: Isolate the root cause of declining repeat rates (Price vs. Quality).*

**Hypothesis A: Price Sensitivity is Driving Churn**
*   **Metric:** **Price Elasticity of Demand**
    *   *Track:* Conversion rate at different price points relative to the student's previous booking.
    *   *Track:* % of requests cancelled by students after viewing tutor rates but before booking.
*   **Metric:** **Discount Redemption Rate**
    *   *Track:* Correlation between promotional discount usage and second-book retention.

**Hypothesis B: Matching Quality is Deteriorating**
*   **Metric:** **First-Session Success Rate**
    *   *Track:* % of first bookings that result in a repeat booking *with the same tutor*.
    *   *Diagnostic:* If overall repeat rate is down but "same-tutor" repeat rate is stable, the issue is initial matching, not ongoing satisfaction.
*   **Metric:** **Mismatch Flag Rate**
    *   *Track:* % of sessions where a student requests a different tutor for the next session despite the previous tutor being available.
*   **Metric:** **Profile View-to-Request Ratio**
    *   *Track:* Are students viewing more profiles before committing? (Indicates difficulty finding the "right" fit).

## Early Warning System for Liquidity Issues

To detect issues before fill rates crash, monitor these leading indicators weekly:

1.  **The "Ghost Supply" Ratio:** Tutors marked "available" who have not logged in for 48 hours. (Prevents inflating supply numbers).
2.  **Request Aging Curve:** The average time to fill is a lagging indicator. Track the *percentage of requests remaining unfilled after 1 hour*. A spike here predicts a 24h failure before it happens.
3.  **Tutor Response Latency:** Average time for a tutor to accept/decline an invite. Increasing latency often precedes tutor churn.

## Implementation Note
Shift the operations team's daily standup focus from "How many bookings yesterday?" to "Where is the Liquidity Health Score red, and is it a price or quality issue?" Use the Mismatch Flag Rate to immediately audit matching algorithm changes.

This hierarchy ensures that growing tutor supply (40% MoM) does not come at the cost of student experience, directly addressing the decline in repeat bookings.
