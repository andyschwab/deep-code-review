### Melodia Paywall Impact Evaluation Plan

**Objective:** Determine the net six-month revenue impact of gating three premium features, balancing subscription lifts against free-tier retention losses and potential long-term brand erosion.

#### 1. Core Revenue Model (The "North Star" Calculation)
To justify the strategy to the CFO, you must model **Total Net Revenue Impact**, not just subscription lifts. Calculate this weekly using the following formula:

$$ \text{Net Impact} = (\Delta \text{Subs} \times \text{ARPPU}) - (\Delta \text{Free Users} \times \text{LTV}_{\text{free}}) - (\text{Projected Viral Loss} \times \text{Acquisition Cost}) $$

*   **$\Delta$ Subs:** Incremental premium subscriptions attributed to the paywall.
*   **ARPPU:** Average Revenue Per Paying User.
*   **$\Delta$ Free Users:** The volume of users lost due to the 18% retention drop compared to the pre-Nov 10 baseline.
*   **$\text{LTV}_{\text{free}}$:** The estimated lifetime value of a free user (via ads, data, or future conversion). *Do not assume this is zero.*
*   **Projected Viral Loss:** Estimate the reduction in new user acquisition caused by fewer free users sharing music (see Leading Indicators below).

**Critical Adjustment:** Ensure your observation windows match. Do not divide November's full marketing spend by partial-month conversion data. Restate all metrics over the same closed weekly windows to avoid skewed efficiency rates.

#### 2. Weekly Leading Indicators
Monitor these metrics weekly to detect negative trends before they hit the six-month revenue report:

*   **Viral Coefficient (K-Factor):** Track invites sent per active user. A decline here confirms the hypothesis that losing free users hurts organic growth.
*   **Free-to-Paid Conversion Rate (Organic):** Are the remaining free users converting at a higher rate, or has interest stalled?
*   **Churn Reason Analysis:** Specifically track "Price" or "Features Removed" in cancellation surveys. A spike indicates brand perception damage.
*   **App Store Rating & Review Sentiment:** Monitor for spikes in 1-star reviews mentioning "greedy" or "paywall." This is a lagging indicator of brand health but leads to lower conversion rates.
*   **Day-1 and Day-3 Retention:** If Day-7 is down 18%, check earlier cohorts. If Day-1 is also dropping, the paywall is blocking initial value realization, signaling a critical onboarding failure.

#### 3. Strategic Segmentation
To understand if specific demographics are reacting differently, segment all data by:

*   **User Acquisition Source:** Compare organic vs. paid users. Paid users may have higher intent and tolerate paywalls better; organic users might be more sensitive to friction.
*   **Geography:** Revenue sensitivity varies by region. A 18% drop in a high-LTV market (e.g., US/UK) is more damaging than in a market where free users primarily drive ad revenue.
*   **Device Type:** iOS vs. Android users often have different spending behaviors and retention patterns.
*   **Music Consumption Intensity:** Segment by "Heavy" (>2 hrs/day) vs. "Light" users. If heavy users are churning, you are losing your best potential brand advocates.
*   **Pre-Change Behavior:** Compare users who *would* have used the gated features (based on historical behavior of similar profiles) vs. those who wouldn't. This validates if you successfully targeted "low-value" users or accidentally alienated high-potential ones.

#### 4. Recommendation Framework
When presenting to the CFO, argue from the stated goal of **maximizing total company revenue**, not just premium subs.
*   **If Viral Coefficient drops >10%:** The loss of free users is actively starving the top of the funnel. The trade-off is likely negative long-term.
*   **If Churn Reasons shift to "Value":** The brand perception issue is real. Consider un-gating one feature as a "teaser" to restore value perception while keeping two gated.
*   **If Heavy Users are churning:** Immediate reversal is recommended; these users drive the network effect essential for a music platform.

**Note:** Ensure your analytics events for "paywall_view" and "feature_blocked" are firing correctly to attribute causality accurately. Reconcile subscription numbers from your payment processor with internal event data to rule out tracking errors before finalizing the report.
