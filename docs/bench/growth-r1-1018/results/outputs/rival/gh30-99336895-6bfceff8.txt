# A/B Test Plan: Welcome Offer Optimization

**Test Window:** February 1 – February 21 (3 weeks) + 7-day post-exposure observation for final conversion attribution.
**Audience:** New app users who have not booked within 24 hours of download.

## 1. Hypothesis
Because price sensitivity drives immediate action while risk aversion blocks high-value consideration, we believe offering a **10% discount** will increase immediate booking volume, whereas a **free cancellation upgrade** will increase average booking value (ABV) and repeat booking rates. We will know this is true if the discount variant shows higher Day-7 conversion but the upgrade variant shows higher Day-30 Revenue Per User (RPU).

## 2. Primary Metric (Decision Driver)
**Metric:** **Incremental Revenue Per User (RPU) at Day 30**
*Definition:* (Total Gross Booking Value attributed to the variant – Estimated Cost of Offer) / Total Users in Variant.
*Why:* This single metric captures both the conversion lift (volume) and the margin impact (cost of discount vs. cost of upgrade). It prevents optimizing for "bookings" that lose money or "high value" bookings that never happen.
*Finance Approval Note:* We will model the break-even conversion lift required for the 10% discount to match the control RPU.

## 3. Secondary Metrics (Diagnostic)
These metrics explain *how* the primary metric moved and validate the behavioral assumptions.

| Metric | Purpose | Expected Direction (Discount) | Expected Direction (Upgrade) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | : |
| **Day-7 Conversion Rate** | Measures immediate friction reduction. | Increase | Neutral/Slight Increase |
| **Average Booking Value (ABV)** | Measures if users book more expensive trips. | Neutral/Decrease | Increase |
| **Offer Redemption Rate** | % of users who actually use the code/upgrade. | N/A (Auto-apply) | % who book refundable fares |
| **Cancellation Rate** | Validates the actual cost liability of the upgrade. | N/A | Monitor for abuse |

## 4. Guardrail Metrics (Stop Conditions)
Monitor these daily. If any drop significantly (>5% with p<0.05), pause the test immediately.
*   **Net Margin %:** Ensure the cost of the free upgrade (insurance cost) or discount does not exceed gross profit per booking.
*   **App Uninstall Rate:** Ensure aggressive push notifications about the offer do not drive churn.
*   **Customer Support Ticket Volume:** Specifically regarding "how to apply code" or "cancellation policy confusion."

## 5. Long-Term Value (LTV) Tracking Plan
Since the test duration (3 weeks) cannot fully capture LTV, we will establish a **Cohort Watch** for these specific users.
*   **Action:** Tag all users in Variant A (Discount) and Variant B (Upgrade) in your data warehouse.
*   **Measurement:** At Day 60 and Day 90, compare the **Second Booking Rate** and **Total Lifetime Revenue** of these cohorts against the Control group.
*   **Assumption Validation:** If the Upgrade variant shows a higher Second Booking Rate, the "trust" hypothesis is confirmed, justifying a higher CAC payback period for that segment.

## 6. Sample Size & Duration Logic
*   **Duration:** 3 weeks (Feb 1–21) ensures coverage of three full weekly booking cycles (including weekend browsing behavior).
*   **Observation Window:** We will attribute conversions occurring up to 7 days after the offer is served (Feb 28 final data pull) to account for users who receive the offer on Feb 21 but book later in the week.
*   **Requirement:** To detect a 10% relative lift in RPU with 80% power, we need approximately 15,000 users per variant (assuming a baseline RPU of $5.00 and standard deviation typical of travel apps). If traffic is lower, we will extend the test duration rather than reduce sample size.

## 7. Finance Deliverable for Wednesday
To secure budget approval, present the **Break-Even Analysis**:
*   *Scenario A (Discount):* "We need a X% increase in conversion volume to offset the 10% margin loss."
*   *Scenario B (Upgrade):* "We need a Y% increase in Average Booking Value to offset the $Z cost of the insurance upgrade."
*   *Recommendation:* Run the test to determine which lever (volume vs. value) is more elastic for our specific audience.
