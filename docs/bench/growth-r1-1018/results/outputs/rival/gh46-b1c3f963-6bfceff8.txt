### ScribbleAI Launch Tracking Plan (Top 5 Events)

Hand this list directly to your engineering lead. These events prioritize value realization over vanity, focusing on the "aha" moment where the AI successfully integrates into the user's workflow.

| # | Event Name | Trigger Definition | Critical Properties | Why This Matters |
|---|------------|--------------------|---------------------|------------------|
| 1 | `suggestion_generated` | Fires immediately when the AI returns a completed suggestion to the user interface. | `context_type` (drafting vs. editing), `suggestion_length`, `generation_time_ms` | Establishes the denominator for all acceptance rates. The `context_type` property resolves your unknown about drafting vs. editing usage without needing separate events. |
| 2 | `suggestion_accepted` | Fires when a user clicks "Accept" or tabs through to adopt the AI suggestion **without** modifying the text first. | `context_type`, `suggestion_id`, `time_to_decision_ms` | Measures immediate trust and low-friction value. A high rate here indicates the AI understands intent perfectly on the first try. |
| 3 | `suggestion_edited_then_accepted` | Fires when a user modifies the AI-generated text (adds/deletes/changes words) and *then* finalizes the acceptance. | `context_type`, `edit_distance` (percentage of text changed), `suggestion_id` | **Your North Star Metric.** As hypothesized, this signals deep engagement. It proves the user found the suggestion useful enough to build upon, even if not perfect. Track `edit_distance` to see if users are tweaking syntax or rewriting half the content. |
| 4 | `suggestion_rejected` | Fires when a user explicitly clicks "Reject/Dismiss" or deletes the entire suggestion block without incorporating any part of it. | `context_type`, `suggestion_id`, `rejection_reason` (if UI allows selection, e.g., "irrelevant", "tone mismatch") | Crucial for calculating the net value rate. High rejection rates on specific `context_types` will tell you exactly where the model is failing (e.g., great at drafting, terrible at editing). |
| 5 | `session_workflow_completed` | Fires when a user exports, copies to clipboard, or saves a document containing at least one accepted AI suggestion. | `total_suggestions_accepted`, `document_length`, `session_duration` | Moves beyond single interactions to measure completed work. This confirms that the AI didn't just generate text, but actually helped finish a task. |

**Implementation Notes:**
1.  **Naming Convention:** Ensure engineers use snake_case exactly as written above to match standard analytics schemas (e.g., GA4/Mixpanel).
2.  **Property Consistency:** The `context_type` property must be populated on *every* event above to enable the drafting vs. editing segmentation analysis immediately upon launch.
