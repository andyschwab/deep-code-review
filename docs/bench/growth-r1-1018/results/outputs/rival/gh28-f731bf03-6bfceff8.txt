### Proposed Success Indicators for GreenLedger Dashboard

To validate your hypothesis that cost savings drive engagement more than raw environmental data, and to quantitatively measure "perceived value" without daily surveys, implement the following metric framework. These metrics distinguish between *outcome value* (the financial benefit) and *engagement value* (the habit formation).

#### 1. Primary Value Metric: The "Savings Realization Rate"
Instead of displaying total carbon reduced (which is abstract), prioritize the **Estimated Cost Avoidance** calculated from user inputs.
*   **Definition:** The sum of projected utility, waste, and fuel savings derived from the specific sustainability actions a user logs or completes.
*   **Quantitative Proxy for Value:** Track the **"Savings-to-Action Ratio."** If users who view the "Cost Savings" tab log 3x more data entries than those who only view the "Carbon Footprint" tab, your hypothesis is confirmed.
*   **Dashboard Display:** Show a running total of "$ Saved/Projected" prominently at the top, with "CO2 Reduced" as a secondary, supporting statistic.

#### 2. Engagement Metrics (Quantifying Perceived Value)
Since you cannot ask users daily if they feel the tool is valuable, use these behavioral proxies:
*   **Data Completion Velocity:** Measure the time between account creation and the first complete "Scope 1 & 2" report. High perceived value correlates with speed to first insight.
    *   *Target:* < 48 hours for 50% of new signups.
*   **The "Return-to-Savings" Frequency:** Track how often users return to the dashboard specifically within 24 hours of a utility bill upload or data entry.
    *   *Hypothesis:* If the tool is valuable, users will return immediately to see the financial impact of that specific entry.
*   **Feature Adoption Depth:** Monitor the percentage of users who connect at least one automated data source (e.g., utility API, accounting software) versus manual entry.
    *   *Rationale:* Users only endure setup friction if the perceived downstream value (savings) is high.

#### 3. Validation Experiment: The "Framing Test"
Before finalizing the UI for Tuesday's sprint, run a rapid A/B test on your landing page or onboarding flow (if traffic allows) or a segmented email campaign to existing waitlist users.
*   **Variant A (Cost Frame):** Headline: "Reduce your overhead by 15% through sustainability tracking."
*   **Variant B (Impact Frame):** Headline: "Track and reduce your carbon footprint by 20%."
*   **Success Signal:** Compare click-through rates (CTR) and signup conversion. If Variant A outperforms B by >20%, proceed with the cost-savings-first UI.

#### 4. Alignment with Growth Goal (1,000 Active Businesses)
To hit 1,000 active users by June 2025, focus on **Retention via Value Realization**:
*   **Metric:** "Monthly Active Reporters" (users who log data or view reports ≥2 times/month).
*   **Leading Indicator:** The "% of users who generate their first 'Savings Report' within Week 1."
    *   *Action:* If this number drops below 40%, the onboarding flow is failing to demonstrate immediate financial value, threatening your June 2025 goal.

**Recommended Dashboard Hierarchy:**
1.  **Hero Metric:** Total Estimated Annual Savings ($) – *Dynamic, updates with every data point.*
2.  **Secondary Metric:** Carbon Footprint Trend (vs. last month) – *Contextualizes the savings.*
3.  **Actionable Insight:** "Top 3 Opportunities to Save" – *Directly links behavior to financial outcome.*

This approach shifts the product narrative from "compliance/reporting" to "profit optimization," directly addressing the small business owner's primary motivation while providing you with hard data to validate your strategy.

*Note: Ensure your legal team reviews any "Estimated Savings" language to avoid misleading claims; use ranges or "projected" qualifiers.*
*Since your August interviews were inconclusive, prioritize instrumenting these events before the UI sprint ends to capture baseline behavior immediately.*
