2.  **Revenue Per Open (RPO):** Divide total revenue by the number of unique opens.
    *   Variant A: ~$12.90 per open

3.  **Conversion Rate (Post-Click):** Of those who clicked, how many purchased? If Variant B's traffic bounced or failed to convert at the same rate as A, the issue may lie in the landing page relevance or the specific audience segment triggered by Variant B's wording.
