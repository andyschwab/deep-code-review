### Revised Pilot Success Funnel: LeadScope Enterprise

To accurately measure value consumption for contract renewal, replace the single-step "login" metric with a four-stage **Value Realization Funnel**. A session is only "meaningful" if it reaches Stage 3.

#### **Stage 1: Access (The Gate)**
*   **Definition:** Unique user successfully authenticates.
*   **Metric:** `login_count`
*   **Purpose:** Measures technical access and initial curiosity.
*   **Status:** Necessary but insufficient for success.

#### **Stage 2: Intent (The Search)**
*   **Definition:** User executes a search query with at least two filters applied (e.g., industry + revenue) OR views a saved list.
*   **Metric:** `search_executed` or `list_viewed`
*   **Threshold:** Must occur within 10 minutes of login.
*   **Insight:** Distinguishes active selling intent from passive browsing or administrative checks.

#### **Stage 3: Value Consumption (The Core Action)**
*   **Definition:** User exports lead data (CSV, Excel, or CRM push) OR saves a lead to a personal pipeline.
*   **Metric:** `lead_exported` or `lead_saved`
*   **Threshold:** Minimum 5 leads per session to filter out testing behavior.
*   **Insight:** This is the definitive "aha" moment where data leaves the platform and enters the sales workflow. **Only users reaching this stage count toward your 60% WAU goal.**

#### **Stage 4: Integration (The Habit)**
*   **Definition:** User returns to perform Stage 3 actions in at least 3 distinct days within a 7-day rolling window.
*   **Metric:** `weekly_active_exporter`
*   **Purpose:** Validates stickiness and workflow integration.

---

### Implementation Logic for Renewal Decisions

**1. Redefine "Weekly Active User" (WAU)**
Stop counting logins. A user is **Active** only if they complete **Stage 3** within the week.
*   *Current False Positive:* User logs in 5 times, searches once, exports nothing. (Previously: Active. Now: Churned).
*   *New Reality:* Only reps moving data to their pipeline are active.

**2. Account Health Scoring (For April Renewals)**
Categorize your 15 pilot accounts based on the % of their assigned sales team reaching **Stage 4**:
*   **Green (Renew Ready):** >60% of seats are `weekly_active_exporter`.
*   **Yellow (At Risk):** 30–59% of seats are `weekly_active_exporter`. Requires CS intervention to identify blockers.
*   **Red (Churn Likely):** <30% of seats are `weekly_active_exporter`. High risk of non-renewal despite high login counts.

**3. The "Meaningful Session" Definition**
For all future reporting, a session is **meaningful** if:
`(Login) + (Search with 2+ filters) + (Export/Save ≥ 5 leads)` occurs in a single visit.

*Note: Ensure "exports" include CRM pushes if your platform supports direct integration, as this is often higher value than CSV downloads.*
