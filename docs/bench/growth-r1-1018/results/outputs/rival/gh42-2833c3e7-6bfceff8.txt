### Recommended North Star Metric: "Effective Workout Minutes" (EWM)

**Definition:** The sum of minutes spent in sessions where the user achieves a verified moderate-to-vigorous intensity threshold, regardless of total session duration.

**Formula:**
$$ \text{EWM} = \sum (\text{Minutes in Session} \times \text{Intensity Validity Flag}) $$

*   **Intensity Validity Flag = 1** if:
    *   Heart Rate (HR) data is present AND average HR > 60% of estimated max HR; **OR**
    *   HR data is missing BUT the user completed a "High-Intensity" tagged program (proxy validation).
*   **Intensity Validity Flag = 0** otherwise (e.g., low-intensity exploration, menu browsing, or unverified low-effort sessions).

#### Why This Metric Wins for Series A
1.  **Solves the "Noise" Problem:** It explicitly handles your missing sensor data (50% of users) by using program completion as a secondary proxy for intensity, preventing you from discarding half your user base from the metric.
2.  **Aligns with Value:** Unlike "total minutes" (which rewards idle time) or "30-minute completion" (which penalizes effective 20-minute HIIT workouts), EWM rewards *physiological effort*, which is the core value proposition of VR fitness.
3.  **Investor Narrative:** It shifts the story from "time spent in app" (a vanity metric) to "verified health output" (a outcomes metric), directly addressing investor concerns about health efficacy.

### Validation Plan: Correlating EWM with Retention

To validate this metric before your due diligence next week, execute the following retrospective analysis on your last 3 months of data:

**Step 1: Cohort Construction**
Segment users into weekly cohorts based on their signup date. For each user, calculate their average weekly EWM during their first 14 days.

**Step 2: Define Retention Outcome**
Define "Retained" as a user who subscribes/renews or remains active (>1 session) in Day 30–60.

**Step 3: Correlation Analysis**
Run a logistic regression or calculate Pearson correlation coefficients for three candidate metrics against the Retention Outcome:
*   **Candidate A:** Total Minutes (Current Metric)
*   **Candidate B:** 30-Min Session Completion Rate (Your Hypothesis)
*   **Candidate C:** Effective Workout Minutes (Proposed Metric)

**Step 4: Select the Winner**
The metric with the highest correlation coefficient ($r$) and the clearest separation between retained/churned users in a decile analysis is your North Star.

**Expected Outcome:**
You will likely find that Candidate B (30-min completion) has a high correlation but low coverage (only applies to 12% of users), making it unstable as a primary KPI. Candidate A will show weak correlation due to noise. Candidate C should demonstrate a strong, monotonic relationship where higher EWM in week 1 predicts higher retention, while covering 80%+ of your active user base.

**Implementation Note for Investors:**
Present the metric as "Minutes of Verified Effort." Show a chart plotting Day-14 EWM against Day-60 Retention rates. Explicitly state the proxy logic used for the 50% without sensors to demonstrate data rigor.

*Note: If the regression shows program completion is a poor proxy for intensity, refine the flag to require only HR data but weight those users separately in reporting until sensor adoption increases.*
