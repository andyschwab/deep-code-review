**The Critical Threshold Change**
Prior to July 2021, each EU country had its own distance-selling threshold (ranging from €35,000 to €100,000). **This system no longer exists for cross-border sales.** There is now a single, union-wide threshold of **€10,000** in total annual cross-border sales to all EU consumers combined.

You cannot wait to hit a country-specific limit because those individual limits no longer apply to your volume. Since you are a non-EU entity, you likely have no "domestic" sales in any EU country to count toward the threshold; your very first sale to an EU consumer counts toward the €10,000 cross-border total. **How to Comply (The OSS Solution)**
You do **not** need to register in every single country. Instead, you should utilize the **Import One Stop Shop (IOSS)** or the standard **One Stop Shop (OSS)** depending on how you ship:

1.  **If shipping directly from the US (Value < €150):** You should register for **IOSS**. This allows you to collect VAT at the point of sale on your website and remit it via a single monthly return in one EU member state. This ensures your customers do not face surprise fees upon delivery.
2.  **If shipping from the US (Value > €150) or holding stock in an EU warehouse:** You generally register for **OSS**. You collect VAT based on the customer's location rate but file a single quarterly return in one chosen EU country.
