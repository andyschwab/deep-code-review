In the context of state taxation, "nexus" is the connection between a business and a state that obligates the business to register and pay taxes. This is a classic physical presence. Most states, including GA, AL, and TN, do not offer a "grace period" of days for physical operations. The moment your staff administers a vaccine on their soil, you are likely conducting business there. The "4-week" duration is sufficient to trigger filing obligations; it is not short enough to be considered a transient activity exempt from taxation.

Since you have a physical presence, those safe harbors generally do not apply to your corporate income tax obligations.
