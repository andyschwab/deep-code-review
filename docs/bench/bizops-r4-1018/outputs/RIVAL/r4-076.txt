### Realistic Market Sizing Framework for VaultSecure

Do not present the raw number of 5,000 entities as your immediate opportunity; this ignores your specific constraints and will appear naive to sophisticated investors. Conversely, discounting the total market solely by a historical 2% adoption rate may undersell the specific urgency of secrets management in the current security landscape. Instead, use a **Velocity-Adjusted SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)** framework that layers procurement reality over the theoretical total.

**Step 1: Define the Serviceable Available Market (SAM) by Urgency, Not Just Regulation**
Not all 5,000 regulated entities move at the same speed. Segment your 5,000 potential clients into three buckets based on "trigger events" that bypass standard procurement drag:
*   **High Velocity (15%):** Companies undergoing recent security audits, SOC2/ISO certification drives, or those who have suffered a near-miss breach. These buyers have budget urgency.
*   **Medium Velocity (35%):** Companies actively modernizing DevOps pipelines or migrating to cloud-native infrastructure.
*   **Low Velocity (50%):** Stable legacy environments with no immediate trigger.

**Step 2: Apply a "Procurement Friction Coefficient"**
Instead of a flat 2% conversion rate, apply a time-phased capture rate. Your 2% historical figure likely represents a 12-month cycle. For a 3-year investment horizon, your obtainable market expands as cycles complete.
*   *Year 1 SOM:* High Velocity segment × Your current win rate (e.g., 40 customers / reachable high-velocity prospects).
*   *Year 3 SOM:* (High + Medium Velocity) × Accumulated win rate over 3 cycles.

**Step 3: Calculate the "Value-Weighted" Market**
Investors care about revenue, not just logo count. Your current ARPU is $30,000 ($1.2M / 40).
*   **Raw TAM:** 5,000 companies × $30k = $150M (Theoretical ceiling).
*   **Realistic SOM (Year 1):** 750 (High Velocity) × 5% capture rate × $30k = $1.125M (Validates your current traction).
*   **Realistic SOM (Year 3):** 2,500 (High + Med) × 12% cumulative capture × $30k = $9M ARR potential.

**Narrative Strategy for Investors**
Present the **$150M TAM** to show the ceiling, but immediately pivot to the **$9M Year-3 SOM** as your actionable plan. Explicitly state: "While 5,000 entities exist, our serviceable market is defined by procurement velocity. We are targeting the 15% with immediate compliance triggers, allowing us to bypass typical 18-month sales cycles." This demonstrates you understand the friction rather than ignoring it.

**Critical Data Points to Gather**
To defend this model, you must gather:
1.  **Trigger Event Correlation:** Data showing what percentage of your 40 current customers bought within 6 months of a specific event (audit, breach, cloud migration). This proves you can identify the "High Velocity" segment.
2.  **Sales Cycle Variance:** Breakdown of sales cycle length by industry sub-sector (e.g., Fintech vs. Traditional Banking). If Fintech closes in 4 months while Banks take 18, size them separately.
3.  **Budget Cycle Alignment:** Evidence of when security budgets are released in your target verticals to forecast cash flow accurately.

By framing the market through "velocity segments" rather than a flat discount, you turn a constraint (slow procurement) into a strategic filter that highlights your operational sophistication.

*Note: Ensure your $30k ARPU is scalable; enterprise deals in banking often exceed this once usage scales. Use your current 40 customers as case studies to prove you can navigate the very procurement hurdles you cite.*
