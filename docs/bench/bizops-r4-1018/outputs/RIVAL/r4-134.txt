**The Regulatory Reality**
The FDA clears devices based on safety and efficacy for medical use. The FCC regulates radio frequency (RF) emissions and spectrum interference. 1.  The device must be used under the supervision of a licensed practitioner (which may not apply to your direct-to-consumer model).
