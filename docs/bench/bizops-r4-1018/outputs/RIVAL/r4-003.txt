**2. Runway Sensitivity Analysis**
To determine the runway loss, we must estimate your current monthly volume to calculate the total cash impact.
*   **Current Monthly Gross Profit:** Revenue - (COGS + Operating Expenses).
    *   We know Operating Expenses (Burn) = $45,000.
    *   Current Gross Profit per unit = $48.00 ($120 - $72).
    *   *Implicit Volume Calculation:* To sustain a $45k burn with a 40% margin, you are likely selling roughly **1,563 pairs/month** (assuming you are currently breaking even on cash flow before the tariff, or simply using the burn rate as the fixed cost baseline).
    *   *Correction based on prompt data:* The prompt states a **$45,000 monthly burn rate**. In startup finance, "burn" is typically Net Cash Outflow. If you are currently surviving on reserves, your Net Loss is $45k.
    *   Let's isolate the **incremental cost** of the tariff.
    *   Estimated Monthly Volume: If your total cash burn is $45k, and assuming you are not yet profitable, the volume is hard to pinpoint exactly without revenue data. However, we can model the **direct cash drain** based on the margin erosion.

*Scenario A: Absorbing the Cost (No Price Increase)*
*   **Cost Increase per Unit:** $10.80
*   **Estimated Monthly Volume:** Based on a $120 price point and typical sneaker startup metrics, let's assume a conservative volume of **1,000 pairs/month** (approx. $120k revenue). *Note: If your volume is higher, the impact scales linearly.*

**3. Strategic Recommendation**
Absorbing the cost reduces your gross margin from 40% to 31%, which is dangerous for a hardware/retail brand. More critically, it accelerates your cash burn by **24% to 48%** depending on volume, cutting your runway by **1 to 2 months**.

With only ~4 months of runway remaining under the tariff scenario, you are in the "danger zone" for raising emergency funds (which typically takes 3-6 months). **Do not absorb the cost.** You must pass at least a portion of this to consumers immediately. A $12 price increase (10%) restores most of your margin and preserves cash, extending your runway back toward the 5-month mark while you secure new funding.
