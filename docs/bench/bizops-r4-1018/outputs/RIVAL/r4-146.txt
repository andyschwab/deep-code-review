**Immediate Compliance Warning: Your Current Model Carries Significant Legal Risk**

Your concern is well-founded. Charging a fixed per-patient fee ($250) for referrals to hospice providers who bill Medicare creates a high risk of violating the **Federal Anti-Kickback Statute (AKS)** and potentially the **Civil Monetary Penalties (CMP) Law**. While your worry about the "Medicare hospice benefit cap" is related, the primary legal danger is not the cap itself, but the classification of your fee as an illegal inducement for referrals.

**The Core Legal Problem**
Under the AKS, it is a federal crime to knowingly offer, pay, solicit, or receive any remuneration (anything of value) to induce referrals of items or services reimbursable by a federal healthcare program (like Medicare).
*   **Per-Patient = Per-Referral:** A fee triggered specifically by the enrollment of a patient is viewed by regulators as payment for the referral itself, not for a legitimate service.
*   **The "Cap" Connection:** You are correct that this impacts the cap. Hospice providers receive a fixed daily rate from Medicare. If they pay you $250 per patient out of their revenue, they are effectively reducing the funds available for patient care in exchange for the referral. Regulators view this as siphoning federal funds.

**Consequences**
If investigated, the consequences include:
1.  **For Providers:** Loss of Medicare certification, fines, and exclusion from federal programs.
2.  **For CarePath:** Civil monetary penalties, criminal charges, and forced disgorgement of all profits generated from these fees.

**Required Strategic Pivot**
To scale safely, you must decouple your revenue from the *act of referral* or the *volume of patients*. You cannot charge based on success (enrollment). Consider these compliant alternatives:

1.  **Flat Subscription (SaaS Model):** Charge providers a flat monthly fee (e.g., $500–$2,000/month) for access to the platform, regardless of how many patients they enroll. This must be set at **Fair Market Value (FMV)** for the software utility, not based on projected referral volume.
2.  **Cost-Based Service Fees:** Charge for specific, verifiable administrative services (e.g., credentialing verification, data reporting) at a fixed rate or hourly rate that reflects the actual cost of providing that service, ensuring the total compensation does not vary with the volume or value of referrals.
3.  **Family-Facing Model:** Shift the payer to the families seeking care. Charge families a convenience fee or subscription for care coordination services. *Note: This requires careful review of state laws regarding charging beneficiaries for services that should be covered by the hospice benefit.*
