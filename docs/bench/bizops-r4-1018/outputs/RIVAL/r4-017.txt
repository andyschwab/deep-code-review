### General Principles for Mixed Bundles

In jurisdictions like California, tax authorities typically analyze mixed bundles (taxable + exempt items) using one of two primary frameworks:

**1. The "True Object" Test**
Authorities examine the primary purpose of the transaction. If the buyer's main objective is the taxable item (the hydroponic unit) and the exempt item (seeds) is merely incidental to its use, the **entire bundle** is often taxed at the higher rate. Since your seeds are specifically designed for your hardware, regulators may view them as an inseparable part of the taxable electronic system rather than a standalone agricultural product.

**2. The "Separately Stated" Rule**
To preserve the exemption for the seed portion, most tax codes require that:
*   The exempt item must be **separately stated** on the invoice with its own distinct price.
*   The customer must have the option to purchase the taxable item *without* the exempt item (even if you discourage it).
*   The allocation of price between the two items must be reasonable and reflect fair market value.

If you sell the bundle as a single SKU for $199 with no breakdown, tax auditors will likely assess sales tax on the full $199.
