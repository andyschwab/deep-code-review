### Market Sizing & Revenue Path Analysis for CareTrain

**1. Total Addressable Revenue (TAM) Comparison**

*   **B2B Channel (Nursing Homes):**
    *   **Market Size:** 15,000 facilities × $2,000/year = **$30 Million TAM**.
    *   **Realistic 3-Year Capture:** With a 6-month sales cycle and 3 salespeople, assume each rep closes 2 deals/month once ramped (optimistic for high-touch). Year 1: ~100 accounts ($200k). Year 2: ~400 accounts ($800k). Year 3: ~800 accounts ($1.6M).
    *   **Constraint:** Sales capacity is the bottleneck. You cannot scale faster than your reps can close 6-month cycles.

*   **DTC Channel (Family Caregivers):**
    *   **Market Size:** 5,000,000 caregivers × $15/month × 12 months = **$900 Million TAM**.
    *   **Realistic 3-Year Capture:** At $10k/month ad spend and 3% conversion, assume a Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) of ~$50–$80 (industry standard for health/education ads). This yields ~125–200 new subscribers/month.
    *   **Churn Factor:** DTC churn is typically high (5–8% monthly). Net growth stabilizes quickly unless retention improves.
    *   **Projection:** Year 1: ~1,500 active subs ($270k ARR). Year 2: ~3,000 active subs ($540k ARR). Year 3: ~5,000 active subs ($900k ARR), assuming reinvestment of revenue into ads.

**2. Path to $1 Million ARR**

*   **B2B Timeline:** **~3.5 to 4 years.**
    To hit $1M ARR, you need 500 nursing home contracts. With 3 reps and a 6-month cycle, closing 500 deals requires roughly 150 rep-months of *closed* deals. Given ramp time and pipeline generation, this exceeds your current team's capacity within 3 years without hiring more sales staff.

*   **DTC Timeline:** **~3 years (conditional on churn).**
    To hit $1M ARR, you need ~5,600 active subscribers. At a net growth rate of ~150 subscribers/month (after churn), this takes roughly 37 months. However, this path is fragile; if churn exceeds 6% or CPA rises above $90, the timeline extends indefinitely.

**3. Recommendation: The Faster Path**

Given your constraints (3 salespeople, $10k/mo budget), **neither channel guarantees a fast path to $1M ARR within 3 years**, but **DTC offers a slightly faster theoretical ceiling** if unit economics hold, while **B2B offers higher certainty per dollar earned.**

However, the **hybrid approach** is the only realistic winner:
1.  **Prioritize B2B for immediate cash flow:** $2,000 ACV is significant. Focus your 3 reps entirely here. Closing just 42 deals gets you to $1M ARR. While the cycle is long, the contract value is high and churn is likely lower than DTC.
2.  **Use DTC for brand validation, not primary revenue yet:** $10k/mo is insufficient to scale DTC to $1M ARR quickly against high churn. Use it to gather data on content engagement to strengthen the B2B pitch ("families are asking for this").

**Verdict:** Stick to **B2B** as the primary engine. You need 42 clients, not 5,600. With 3 reps, hunting 42 whales is more manageable than managing the churn and support load of thousands of $15 users. The 6-month cycle is painful, but the path to $1M is clearer: 14 deals per rep over 3 years.

*Note: DTC requires a significantly higher marketing budget ($50k+/mo) to overcome churn and reach $1M ARR quickly. B2B requires patience but fits your current team structure better.*
