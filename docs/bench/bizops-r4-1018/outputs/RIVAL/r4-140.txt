### The Math: Does a Sales Rep Make Sense for ErgoScan?

**Current Unit Economics (Product-Led)**
*   **Annual Contract Value (ACV):** $5,000
*   **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** $800
*   **Close Rate:** 15% (from demo)
*   **LTV:CAC Ratio:** ~6.25:1 (assuming conservative 1-year retention)
*   **Verdict:** Highly efficient. You are spending $0.16 to earn $1.00.

**Proposed Unit Economics (Sales-Led)**
*   **Rep Fixed Cost:** $90,000 (base) + ~$45,000 (commission/benefits overhead) = **$135,000/year**.
*   **Break-even Deals:** To cover just the rep's cost, you need **27 additional deals** per year ($135k / $5k ACV).
*   **Activity Required:** At a 15% close rate, the rep needs **180 qualified demos** annually (15/month) just to break even. If the close rate improves to 25% via sales expertise, they still need **108 demos** (9/month).

**The Verdict: Do Not Hire Yet**

The math does **not** support adding a dedicated enterprise sales representative at your current $5,000 ACV.

1.  **The Efficiency Trap:** Your current inbound model is exceptionally efficient. Introducing a human sales layer increases your blended CAC drastically unless that rep can close significantly more than 3 deals per month *incrementally*.
2.  **The ACV Threshold:** Standard SaaS economics suggest a dedicated sales rep becomes viable when ACV reaches **$25,000–$50,000**. At $5k, the margin per deal is too thin to absorb the $135k fixed cost without unrealistic volume.
3.  **The Volume Mismatch:** Mid-sized tech firms likely do not generate enough inbound volume to keep a dedicated rep busy *and* profitable. If the rep spends time prospecting cold, your CAC will skyrocket, destroying your current 6:1 efficiency.

**Strategic Recommendation**

**Remain Product-Led with a "High-Touch" Overlay.**
Do not hire a full-time rep. Instead:
*   **Founder-Led Sales:** Have the founders or current team handle the top 10% of inbound leads personally to test upsell potential without fixed costs.
*   **Raise Prices First:** Use your high close rate (15% is healthy for inbound) as validation to increase prices to $10k–$15k. If conversion holds, the unit economics shift in favor of sales.
*   **Commission-Only Pilot:** If you must test sales, hire a commission-only contractor to target enterprise accounts specifically. Only convert to a full-time role once they generate $250k+ in pipeline consistently.

Wait until your ACV hits $20k+ or your inbound lead volume exceeds 50 qualified demos/month before adding fixed sales headcount.

*Note: Your current low CAC suggests strong product-market fit; prioritize scaling inbound channels over fixing a sales process that isn't broken.*
