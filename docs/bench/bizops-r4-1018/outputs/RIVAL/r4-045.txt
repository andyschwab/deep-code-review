**Critical Warning: Your request contains a fundamental regulatory mismatch.**

The FDA structure-function claim notification process you are asking about applies **only to dietary supplements**, not conventional foods. Protein powders are frequently classified as **conventional foods** (represented as "food for ordinary consumption") rather than supplements, depending on packaging, marketing, and formulation. If GreenPump is classified as a conventional food, **you cannot make structure-function claims at all**, regardless of notification. Doing so would render your product misbranded immediately upon launch.

**If (and only if) your product is legally classified as a dietary supplement:**

1.  **Submission Timeline:** You must submit the notification to the FDA **no later than 30 days after** the product is first marketed. You do **not** need approval prior to launch, but the claim must be truthful, not misleading, and substantiated by evidence you possess *before* you make the claim.
2.  **Required Disclaimer:** Your label must display the following exact disclaimer in a prominent box:
    *"This statement has not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."*
3.  **Pre-Labeling Steps:**
    *   **Confirm Classification:** Verify with a regulatory expert that your product meets the definition of a dietary supplement (21 CFR 101.93) and not a conventional food. This is the single most critical step.
    *   **Substantiation:** Ensure you have competent and reliable scientific evidence supporting "supports muscle recovery" and "aids in energy metabolism" specifically for your formulation.
    *   **Notification Content:** Prepare the notification including the exact claim, the basis for the claim, and your contact information.
