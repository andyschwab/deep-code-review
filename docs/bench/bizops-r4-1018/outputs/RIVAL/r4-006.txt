**Executive Summary: Cut Influencer Spend Immediately**

Your current strategy is mathematically unsustainable. You are burning cash to acquire customers who do not generate enough profit to cover their acquisition cost within a safe timeframe. At your current burn rate of $50,000/month and cash reserve of $120,000, you have **2.4 months of runway** left if you continue this pace. Without an immediate pivot, Lumina Candles will be insolvent by month 3.

**Unit Economics Breakdown**

*   **Gross Profit per Unit:** $63 ($85 Price - $22 Cost)
*   **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** $75 ($30,000 Spend / 400 Customers)
*   **Contribution Margin per Customer:** **-$12** ($63 Profit - $75 CAC)

**The Critical Flaw:** You are losing $12 on every single new customer acquired through influencers before accounting for overhead, salaries, or shipping. Your "90-day payback period" calculation is flawed because it assumes a second purchase that your data explicitly states is not happening. With a 1.0 purchase average, there is no future revenue to pay back the initial loss. You are not just slow to break even; you are structurally unprofitable on the first transaction.

**Break-Even ROAS Analysis**

To simply break even on ad spend (ignoring overhead), your Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) must cover the inverse of your gross margin percentage.
*   **Gross Margin %:** 74% ($63 / $85)
*   **Break-Even ROAS:** 1.35x (1 / 0.74)

Your current performance:
*   **Revenue Generated:** $34,000 (400 units * $85)
*   **Ad Spend:** $30,000
*   **Current ROAS:** **1.13x**

You are operating at a 1.13x ROAS when you need a minimum of 1.35x just to cover the cost of goods and the ads. You are effectively subsidizing every influencer campaign with your limited cash reserves.

**Strategic Recommendations**

1.  **Pause Influencer Spend Today:** Stop the $30,000/month bleed immediately. This single action reduces your monthly burn from $50,000 to $20,000, extending your runway from 2.4 months to **6 months**. This buys you time to fix the model.
2.  **Fix Retention Before Acquisition:** Your unit economics only work if the Lifetime Value (LTV) increases. Since customers currently buy only once, you must implement a post-purchase email flow, a loyalty program, or a subscription model to drive a second purchase. You need the second purchase to happen within 30 days, not 90.
3.  **Re-evaluate Pricing or Costs:** If retention cannot be improved quickly, your price point ($85) is too low relative to your CAC ($75). Either raise prices to increase gross margin or find cheaper acquisition channels (e.g., organic content, partnerships) where CAC is under $40.

**Conclusion**

Do not chase vanity metrics like "400 new customers" when those customers lose you money. Your current velocity is a path to bankruptcy, not growth. Preserve cash, fix the repeat purchase rate, and only resume paid marketing once your LTV:CAC ratio exceeds 3:1.
