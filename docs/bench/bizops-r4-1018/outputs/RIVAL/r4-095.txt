### Financial Stress-Test: CloudGuard Runway Scenarios

**Current Baseline**
*   **Cash Reserves:** $1,200,000
*   **Monthly Burn:** $85,000
*   **Current Runway:** ~14.1 months

Let $R_{total}$ be your current total monthly revenue. Your largest customer contributes $0.35 \times R_{total}$. The following models assume your $85,000 burn rate remains static initially, though immediate cost-cutting is recommended.

#### Scenario A: Total Revenue Loss (Customer Churns)
If the competitor bundles the feature for free and you lose the account entirely:
*   **Revenue Impact:** Loss of $0.35 \times R_{total}$.
*   **Net Burn Increase:** Your net burn increases by the amount of revenue lost.
    *   *New Monthly Burn* = $\$85,000 + (0.35 \times R_{total})$
*   **Runway Calculation:** $\$1,200,000 / \text{New Monthly Burn}$
*   **Critical Threshold:** If this customer accounts for more than ~$26,400/month in revenue (approx. 31% of your current burn), your runway drops below 12 months immediately. If they represent a larger portion of revenue relative to your burn, the impact is severe.
    *   *Example:* If that customer pays $50,000/mo, your net burn rises to $135,000. New Runway: **8.9 months**.

#### Scenario B: 50% Price Discount (Customer Retains at Half Price)
If you retain the customer but must discount by 50%:
*   **Revenue Impact:** Loss of $0.175 \times R_{total}$ (half of the 35% share).
*   **Net Burn Increase:** Your net burn increases by half the revenue lost.
    *   *New Monthly Burn* = $\$85,000 + (0.175 \times R_{total})$
*   **Runway Calculation:** $\$1,200,000 / \text{New Monthly Burn}$
    *   *Example:* If that customer pays $50,000/mo, you lose $25,000/mo. New net burn: $110,000. New Runway: **10.9 months**.

#### Immediate Contingency Plans

**1. Hiring & Cost Freeze (Day 1)**
*   **Hiring:** Implement an immediate freeze on all non-essential hiring. Delay any planned Q3/Q4 hires.
*   **Variable Costs:** Cut all discretionary spending (travel, conferences, software subscriptions not critical to core delivery) by 20–30% immediately. This could reduce burn by ~$5k–$8k/month.
*   **Contractor Review:** Audit all contractor agreements; terminate those not directly tied to retaining the at-risk customer or critical product stability.

**2. Strategic Pivot Actions**
*   **Diversification Sprint:** Reallocate sales resources immediately to target mid-market enterprises less likely to be impacted by the cloud provider's bundle. The goal is to replace 50% of the at-risk revenue within 6 months.
*   **Product Differentiation:** Accelerate development of features the cloud provider cannot easily bundle (e.g., multi-cloud policy management, advanced compliance reporting specific to regulated industries).
*   **Customer Negotiation:** Prepare a "loyalty tier" offer for the at-risk customer that locks in a 12-month contract at a 20% discount now, rather than waiting for the 50% threat next quarter.

**3. Cash Preservation Target**
To maintain a safe 12-month runway in the **Total Loss Scenario**, you must reduce your net burn to $100,000/month.
*   Required Action: Identify $15,000–$20,000 in immediate monthly cost reductions (personnel or operational) to offset the revenue shock.

**Note:** These models assume no additional capital infusion. If the revenue loss exceeds $40k/month, consider initiating a bridge round discussion immediately while cash reserves are still healthy.
