**2. Unit Economics & Volume Required**
To offset the new $45,000 monthly cost, we must determine the revenue required per consultation.
*   *Assumption:* Based on 300 consultations/month and typical tele-dermatology reimbursement rates, we estimate an average net revenue of **$125 per consultation**. (If your actual average differs, adjust the final volume proportionally).
*   **Gross Margin per Consult:** $125 × 65% = **$81.25**.
*   **Monthly Volume Needed to Cover New Costs:** $45,000 / $81.25 = **554 additional consultations/month**.
*   **Total Target Volume:** 300 (current) + 554 (new) = **854 consultations/month**.
*   **Growth Required:** You need an **185% increase** in total volume to maintain the original 18-month runway immediately.
