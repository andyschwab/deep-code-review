**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The deciding factor is **who holds the promissory note**. If DreamRest holds the debt, you are the lender. If a bank or licensed fintech partner holds the debt, you are merely a merchant.
*   **Trigger:** The moment you draft a contract where the customer owes money *to DreamRest* rather than a third party, you trigger consumer lending statutes.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** In Texas, making loans without a license is a criminal offense if done repeatedly for profit. Neighboring states (OK, LA, AR, NM) have similar strictures.
*   **Step this week:** If you intend to launch in Q3, contact three "Buy Now, Pay Later" (BNPL) providers or merchant services processors immediately to request their integration requirements; do not draft your own financing terms.
