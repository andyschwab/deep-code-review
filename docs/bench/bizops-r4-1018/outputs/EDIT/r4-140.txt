**The Call**
Do not hire the dedicated sales representative yet; the unit economics break only if the new rep closes at least 38 deals annually (over 3x your current implied volume), which is unrealistic for a single hire without a proven outbound pipeline. Remain product-led until you either increase the Average Contract Value (ACV) to ~$12,000 or validate that a sales rep can sustain a close rate above 45% on qualified leads.

**The Numbers**
*Formula:* Annual Breakeven Deals = Total Annual Sales Cost / (ACV – Variable Cost per Deal).
*Current State:* ACV $5,000; CAC $800; Contribution Margin $4,200.
*Proposed State Assumptions:*
1.  **Sales Rep Cost:** $90,000 base + $20,000 estimated commission (20% of revenue at breakeven) + $15,000 overhead/tools = **$125,000 Total Fixed Cost** (ASSUMED).
2.  **Variable Cost:** The $800 inbound CAC is largely marketing spend. For a sales-led model, assume demo/demo-engineering costs remain, but marketing spend per deal drops. Let's assume variable cost drops to $300/deal (ASSUMED).
3.  **New Margin:** $5,000 - $300 = $4,700.

*Breakeven Calculation:*
$125,000 / $4,700 margin = **26.6 deals/year** just to cover the rep's cost, ignoring the loss of existing inbound efficiency.

*Volume Reality Check:*
At a 15% close rate, generating 27 deals requires **180 qualified demos** per year (15 per month).
If the rep works 22 days/month, they need ~0.7 qualified demos every single day, every day, with no ramp time. If the close rate improves to 25% (optimistic for new outbound), you still need 108 qualified demos/year (9/month).
*Upsell Impact:* To justify the hire with only 15 deals (a manageable load), the ACV must rise to **$13,300** (ASSUMED target) via upsells, effectively tripling the deal size.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes this call is the **Qualified Demo Volume**. The math only works if you already have a backlog of 15+ qualified demos per month that are currently going uncontacted.
*Trigger:* If you cannot generate 10 qualified demos per month for the rep within 60 days of hiring, the fixed cost will burn 2.5 months of runway per year with no return.
*Rule of Thumb:* A dedicated sales rep is viable only when ACV × Close Rate × Monthly Leads > (Monthly Salary + Commission) / 0.5 (targeting 50% gross margin on sales effort).
*This Week's Step:* If you have fewer than 30 warm leads in your CRM right now, consider running a 4-week outbound pilot using a founder-led sales process before committing to a $125k fixed cost.
