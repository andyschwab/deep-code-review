*Formula for Target Price:*
$$P_{target} = \frac{C_{variable}}{1 - \text{Target Margin}}$$

*Formula for Break-Even Volume (under new model):*
$$V_{BE} = \frac{\text{Fixed Costs}}{\text{Base Fee} + (\text{Variable Rate} - C_{variable})}$$
