**The Call**
Do not announce this change next month under your current contracts; reclassifying "seats" to "active users" for existing customers is a material alteration of the billing metric that exposes you to breach of contract claims if applied unilaterally. You must hire an external attorney to redraft the core definitions and construct a grandfathering or amendment strategy before proceeding.

**The Numbers**
The financial impact of the shift depends on the utilization gap you identified.
*   **Current Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR):** 1,200 teams × (Avg Seats × $29). *Note: Average seats per team is missing.*
*   **Hypothetical Active User MRR:** If a customer pays for 100 seats but only 40 are active daily:
    *   *Current:* 100 × $29 = $2,900/month.
    *   *Proposed:* 40 × $29 = $1,160/month (assuming same unit price).
    *   **Result:** A 60% revenue reduction per affected enterprise account unless the per-unit price is increased significantly to offset the lower volume.
*   **Churn Impact:** With 3% monthly churn, losing enterprise accounts due to contract disputes could spike this rate. If 10% of customers dispute the change and leave, that is an immediate loss of 120 teams.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is the **contractual definition of "Fee" and "User"** in your existing Master Service Agreements (MSAs).
*   **Trigger:** If your current MSA defines the fee as "$X per Seat" where "Seat" is defined as a named user or license key regardless of activity, switching to "Active Users" (a dynamic metric) violates the pricing schedule attached to the contract.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Changing *how* you measure the billable unit is a material term change; changing the *price* of that unit is a commercial negotiation. Doing both without consent is a breach.
*   **Actionable Step:** If your current contracts lack an explicit right to modify billing metrics unilaterally with notice, consider pausing the rollout for existing customers and applying the "active user" model only to new contracts signed after the legal update.
