**The call:** Pursue the B2B channel to reach $1M ARR faster; the DTC path requires unattainable traffic volume given your current $10k/month budget and 3% conversion rate, whereas B2B requires securing only 500 accounts (3.3% market penetration) which is feasible with three salespeople over 24–30 months despite the 6-month cycle.

**The numbers:**
*Formula:* Total Addressable Revenue (TAR) = Total Units × Penetration Rate × Annual Price.
*Formula:* Time to $1M = $1,000,000 / (New Accounts per Month × ACV).

**B2B Segment (Nursing Homes)**
*   **Total Units:** 15,000 homes.
*   **Price:** $2,000/year.
*   **Theoretical Max TAR:** $30,000,000/year.
*   **Sales Capacity Assumption:** 3 reps × 4 deals closed/rep/year (conservative given 6-month cycle) = 12 deals/month once ramped.
*   **Year 1 Revenue (Ramp):** Months 1–6 = $0 (sales cycle). Months 7–12 = ~6 deals/month avg → 36 deals × $2,000 = $72,000 ARR.
*   **Year 2 Revenue:** 12 deals/month × 12 months = 144 new deals. Total base ~180 deals × $2,000 = $360,000 ARR.
*   **Year 3 Revenue:** 144 new deals. Total base ~324 deals × $2,000 = $648,000 ARR.
*   **Time to $1M:** At 12 deals/month ($24k new ARR/month), you need 500 total customers. Starting revenue accumulation in Month 7, you hit ~$1M ARR in **Month 34** (early Year 3), assuming steady close rates and no churn.

**DTC Segment (Families)**
*   **Total Units:** 5,000,000 caregivers.
*   **Price:** $15/month ($180/year).
*   **Theoretical Max TAR:** $900,000,000/year.
*   **Traffic Requirement Assumption:** To get 1 subscriber at 3% conversion, you need ~33 visitors. To get 1,000 subscribers ($180k ARR), you need ~33,000 targeted visitors converting.
*   **Budget Constraint:** $10,000/month.
*   **CAC Assumption (UNVERIFIED):** If Cost Per Click (CPC) is $2.00 (industry avg for health/education), budget buys 5,000 clicks/month.
*   **Projected Growth:** 5,000 clicks × 3% conversion = 150 new subs/month.
*   **Revenue Math:** 150 subs × $15 = $2,250 MRR added/month.
*   **Churn Assumption (UNVERIFIED):** DTC content often sees 5-10% monthly churn. If churn is 5%, net growth stalls quickly.
*   **Time to $1M ARR:** Requires ~5,555 active subscribers ($1M / $180). At 150 net new subs/month (ignoring churn for best case), this takes **37 months**. With realistic 5% monthly churn, you may never reach $1M ARR on this budget as churn equals acquisition around 3,000 subs.

**What flips it, and the trigger:**
The **DTC Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** is the flip variable. If your ad creative drives CPC below $0.60 or conversion above 10%, DTC becomes viable.
*Trigger:* If your next $5,000 ad spend yields a CAC over $60 (4 months of subscription value), pause DTC immediately.
*Rule of Thumb:* LTV:CAC must exceed 3:1. With $180 LTV (assuming 12-month tenure), CAC must be under $60.
*Step:* This week, run a $2,000 test campaign to measure actual CPC and conversion; if CAC > $60, reallocate the remaining $8,000 to B2B lead generation tools or sales enablement.
