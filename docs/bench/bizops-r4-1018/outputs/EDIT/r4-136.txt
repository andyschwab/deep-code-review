**The Numbers & Technical Gap:**
*   **Requirement:** FIPS 140-2 (or its successor FIPS 140-3) validated cryptographic modules.
*   **Current State:** AES-256 encryption (algorithm only).
*   **The Gap:** FIPS validation certifies the *implementation* of the algorithm within a specific hardware or software module, not just the algorithm itself.
*   **Data Scope:** 4 TB across 200 clinics.
*   **Cost Implication:** Migration costs (downtime, data transfer, re-architecture) are high. Enabling FIPS mode on major cloud providers (AWS, Azure, GCP) in US regions is typically a configuration toggle or a specific instance type selection, costing $0 in data migration fees.
*   **Assumption:** Your Virginia cloud provider is a major hyperscaler (AWS us-east-1, Azure East US, etc.) which maintains FIPS 140-2 validated endpoints. If you are on a smaller, non-compliant host, migration is required.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes this call is your cloud provider's specific region capability.
*   **Trigger:** Check your provider's compliance documentation for "FIPS 140-2" in the "us-east-1" (or specific Virginia) region.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If the provider offers "FIPS endpoints" or "FIPS-enabled instances," you can satisfy the contract by reconfiguring your encryption keys and connection points. If they cannot provide a valid FIPS certificate number for their modules, you must migrate.

**Routing Note:**
