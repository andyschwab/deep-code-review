**The Call**
Do not recognize the $60,000 upfront. Under standard accounting principles (ASC 606 / IFRS 15), you must defer the revenue and recognize it ratably over the period the customer receives benefit. Given your facts, the safest baseline is to recognize the $12,000 annually on a straight-line basis over the 12-month license term, treating the three-month implementation as a pre-service activity that does not delay the start of revenue recognition unless the contract explicitly states service begins only after go-live.

**The Numbers**
*Formula:* Monthly Revenue = Total Contract Value / Recognition Period (Months).
*User Figures:* $60,000 total value; 5 contracts; $12,000 each.
*Assumption (Timing):* We assume the "service period" is the 12-month license window starting October 1, regardless of the 3-month setup, because the customer is contractually bound and the setup is a cost to fulfill, not a separate product.
*Result (Base Case - 12 Month Ratiable):*
- Monthly Recognition: $12,000 / 12 = **$1,000 per contract/month**.
- Q4 Recognition (Oct, Nov, Dec): $1,000 × 3 months × 5 contracts = **$15,000**.
- Deferred Revenue (Balance Sheet at Year-End): $60,000 - $15,000 = **$45,000**.

*Assumption (Alternative - Setup Excluded):* If your legal counsel confirms the service period legally excludes the 3-month setup (i.e., the clock starts in January), the period is 12 months starting January.
*Result (Alternative):*
- Q4 Recognition: **$0**.
- Monthly Recognition (Jan onwards): $1,000 per contract.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is whether the implementation is a **distinct performance obligation**.
- *Trigger:* If Sam's work is highly customized such that the client could not use the software without it, and you sell similar setup services separately, you may need to allocate part of the $12,000 to "setup" and recognize that portion upon completion (after 3 months).
- *Rule of Thumb:* If setup is <10% of total contract value and not sold separately, most companies expense it as incurred and recognize the whole license ratably.
- *Action:* If the setup fee is implicitly >$1,200 per deal or sold as a standalone item to other clients, consider allocating price before the board meeting.

**Routing Note**
Revenue recognition for SaaS with implementation is a high-stakes accounting determination. Bring the specific contract language regarding "commencement date" and "acceptance criteria" to your CPA to finalize the deferral schedule and ensure compliance with ASC 606.
