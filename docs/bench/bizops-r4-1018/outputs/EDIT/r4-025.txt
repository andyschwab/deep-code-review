**The Numbers & Risk Factors:**
Antitrust liability under Article 101 TFEU does not rely on a specific revenue threshold or a "safe harbor" market share percentage for algorithmic collusion.
*   **Market Share:** Your estimated 2% share is well below the 15% threshold often cited in EU Block Exemption Regulations for vertical restraints, suggesting you lack the market power to unilaterally distort competition. *Assumption:* Your 2% figure is accurate across the relevant geographic and product market definition.
*   **The Trigger:** The legal risk is not the *dynamic* nature of the pricing, but the *input data*. If your algorithm uses non-public or real-time specific competitor prices to align your pricing (even indirectly), regulators may view this as a "hub-and-spoke" conspiracy or tacit collusion.
*   **Scenario Analysis:**
    *   *Low Risk:* Algorithm adjusts prices based solely on your inventory levels, your historical sales velocity, and seasonality.
    *   *High Risk:* Algorithm scrapes competitor sites hourly and adjusts your price to be exactly €1 lower or matches their increase automatically.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that changes this from a compliance exercise to a violation is **data granularity regarding competitors**.
*   **Trigger Value:** If the algorithm ingests *specific, real-time* competitor prices rather than *aggregate, delayed* market indices.
*   **Stop/Pivot Rule:** If your legal counsel cannot certify that the data feed excludes individual competitor pricing logic, halt the "competitor pricing" module immediately.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** "If the bot knows what Competitor X charges right now, it is too smart for comfort."
*   **Actionable Step:** If you proceed, consider building a "clean room" architecture where the pricing engine sees only anonymized, aggregated market data, not specific rival SKUs.

**Routing Note:**
EU antitrust enforcement is principles-based, not purely threshold-based; the European Commission and national authorities (like the German Bundeskartellamt) are actively investigating algorithmic collusion. You must engage EU antitrust counsel to review the specific logic of your algorithm and the source of your competitor data before deployment. Do not rely on market share alone as a defense.
