**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes this from a routine commission to a transfer pricing investigation is **control or relatedness**. If any partner is partially owned by your founders, employees, or investors, or if you exert significant control over their operations (beyond standard quality assurance), they cease to be "unrelated third parties."
*   **Trigger Value:** >0% ownership overlap or de facto control.
*   **Stop/Pivot Rule:** If you plan to increase the commission rate above 25% or offer equity alongside cash, pause and get a formal opinion.
