**The Call:** Your current model loses $0.50 on every single-bag order shipped; you are subsidizing small buyers with margin needed for overhead. Immediately switch single-bag shipping to a pass-through rate (at least $8.50) or enforce a $45 minimum order value to restore positive unit economics.

**The Numbers:**
*Formula:* Contribution Margin = (Price + Shipping Revenue) – (COGS + Actual Shipping Cost).
*Current Single-Bag Unit Economics:*
*   Revenue: $22 (bean) + $6 (shipping charged) = $28.00
*   Variable Cost: $9 (COGS) + $8.50 (actual shipping) = $17.50
*   **Result:** $10.50 Contribution Margin per bag.

*The Hidden Loss:*
While the contribution margin is positive ($10.50), you are losing **$2.50 per transaction** on the shipping line item alone ($6 charged vs $8.50 cost).
*   Monthly Volume: 1,200 bags × 85% single-bag rate = 1,020 single-bag orders.
*   Monthly Shipping Subsidy Loss: 1,020 orders × $2.50 loss = **$2,550/month**.
*   Remaining Multi-bag Orders (15%): 180 orders. Assuming these average 2 bags (conservative estimate), shipping cost per order drops relative to revenue, but the single-bag bleed dominates.
*   Total Monthly Contribution from Single-Bag Orders: 1,020 × $10.50 = $10,710.
*   Less Shipping Subsidy Leak: ($2,550).
*   Net Contribution from Single-Bag Segment: $8,160.
*   Less Fixed Overhead: $4,000.
*   **Current Estimated Net Profit:** ~$4,160 (before marketing/taxes).
*   *Note:* If multi-bag orders are also underpriced on shipping, profits vanish faster. The $2,550 leak represents 64% of your fixed overhead coverage.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The flip input is the **Shipping Surcharge**.
*   *Trigger Value:* Charge at least $8.50 for single bags.
*   *Impact:* Eliminates the $2,550 monthly leak immediately, increasing net profit potential by ~64%.
*   *Rule of Thumb:* Never offer flat-rate shipping below your 90th percentile actual carrier cost unless the Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) justifies the initial loss (unlikely for commodity coffee).
*   *Action Step:* If your cart allows it, implement a dynamic shipping rule this week: "Single bags: $9.00 flat; 2+ bags: $6.00 flat." This covers costs and incentivizes larger baskets.
