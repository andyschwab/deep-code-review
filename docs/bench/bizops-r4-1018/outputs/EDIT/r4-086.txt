**The Call**
Adopt an **incremental CAC** model for channel decisions and a **fully-loaded blended CAC** only for board-level runway math; do not allocate fixed salaries arbitrarily to channels, as this distorts profitability signals. Your conference channel is currently break-even on cash spend alone, while your digital channel is highly efficient; adding salary allocation will artificially depress the digital channel's performance without changing actual cash outflow.

**The Numbers**
*Formula:* $\text{CAC} = \frac{\text{Channel Spend} + \text{Allocated Variable Labor}}{\text{New Customers Acquired}}$

*Inputs & Assumptions:*
*   **Digital Channel Spend:** $60,000 (Given)
*   **Conference Channel Spend:** $40,000 (Given)
*   **Customers (Digital):** 400 (Given)
*   **Customers (Conference):** 400 (Given)
*   **Fixed Salaries:** Unknown total (Let $S$ = total annual salary cost).
*   **ASSUMED Allocation Method:** Since you lack time-tracking data, we will test two scenarios: (A) Ignore fixed salaries (Cash CAC) and (B) Split salaries 50/50 (Arbitrary Blended).

*Results:*

| Metric | Digital Channel | Conference Channel | Blended (Total) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cash Spend** | $60,000 | $40,000 | $100,000 |
| **Customers** | 400 | 400 | 800 |
| **Cash CAC** | **$150** | **$100** | **$125** |
| *Revenue per User* | $200 | $200 | $200 |
| *Immediate Margin* | +$50 | +$100 | +$75 |
| **Blended CAC (50/50 Salary Split)** | $150 + (0.25 \times S)$ | $100 + (0.25 \times S)$ | $125 + (0.125 \times S)$ |

*Analysis:*
Your conference channel appears *more* efficient on a cash basis ($100 CAC vs $150 CAC) despite the high touch nature. This suggests the booth fees are driving volume effectively. However, if your two marketing staff earn $100,000 each ($200k total), a 50/50 split adds $50,000 to each channel.
*   *Digital CAC becomes:* $150 + $125 = $275 (Loss of $75/customer).
*   *Conference CAC becomes:* $100 + $125 = $225 (Loss of $25/customer).
This arbitrary split makes both channels look unprofitable immediately, which is likely false if the salaries support brand equity or retention beyond just acquisition.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The deciding input is **Variable Labor Hours**. If your team spends >70% of their time on conferences, the conference CAC rises sharply.
*   **Trigger Rule:** If the sales team cannot log specific hours spent per channel within 14 days, revert to **Cash CAC** for tactical decisions.
*   **Threshold:** If Cash CAC exceeds $160 (80% of LTV assuming 1-year retention), pause the channel. Currently, both are under this.
*   **Action:** If you cannot track time by Friday, stop allocating salaries to channels entirely and treat them as fixed overhead; judge channels solely on media/event spend vs. new logo count.
