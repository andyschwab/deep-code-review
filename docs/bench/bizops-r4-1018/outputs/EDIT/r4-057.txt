**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The deciding factor is whether your training data includes works where rights holders have explicitly reserved their TDM rights.
*   **The Trigger:** The EU AI Act and updated Copyright Directive (Directive (EU) 2019/790) create a strict liability environment. Article 4 of the Copyright Directive allows TDM for commercial purposes *unless* rights holders have expressly reserved their rights (often via machine-readable tags like `robots.txt` or repository metadata).
*   **The Flip:** If your 50 million lines of code include repositories with "All Rights Reserved" or specific opt-out mechanisms you failed to filter, your defense collapses.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If you cannot prove an automated filtering process existed *at the time of scraping* to exclude opt-out notices, assume liability is high.
