**The Call**
Reject a pure sales-led motion immediately; the math fails because one year of rep cost exceeds your total customer lifetime value. Pursue a product-led growth (PLG) hybrid where you invest in self-serve onboarding to reduce churn, using the savings from avoided sales salaries to fund that investment.

**The Numbers**
The viability hinges on the LTV:CAC ratio and Payback Period.
*Formula:* $LTV = \frac{ARPA \times Gross Margin}{Churn Rate}$ (simplified for monthly churn).
*Formula:* $Payback = \frac{CAC}{Monthly Contribution Margin}$.

**Scenario A: Sales-Led (Your Current Fear)**
*   **Inputs:** Rep Cost $120k/year ($10k/month). Assuming 1 rep closes 4 deals/year (optimistic for complex infra), CAC per deal = $30,000.
*   **LTV Calculation:** $45,000 (given).
*   **Result:** LTV:CAC = $45k / $30k = **1.5x**.
*   **Verdict:** Unviable. Standard SaaS requires >3x. You lose money on every hire unless you drastically increase deal size or close rate.

**Scenario B: PLG with High Churn (Status Quo Risk)**
*   **Inputs:** CAC = $0 (organic/self-serve). Churn = 5% monthly.
*   **LTV Calculation:** $\frac{\$45,000 \times 0.80}{0.05} = \$720,000$ (Wait, this formula assumes ARPA is annual. Let's correct based on your LTV figure).
*   *Correction:* You stated LTV is projected at $45,000. If this is the *gross* LTV before margin:
    *   Contribution LTV = $45,000 \times 0.80 = \$36,000$.
    *   If Churn is 5% monthly, implied Average Life = $1/0.05 = 20$ months.
    *   Implied Monthly ARPA = $45,000 / 20 = \$2,250$.
*   **Result:** If you keep 5% churn, LTV is capped at $36,000 (contribution). If CAC is near zero, the ratio looks infinite, but the *absolute profit pool* is too small to support any future paid acquisition or heavy R&D.

**Scenario C: Hybrid (Invest in Onboarding)**
*   **Assumption (ASSUMED):** Reallocate $60k/year (0.5 rep cost) to customer success automation/content to drop churn to 2.5% monthly.
*   **New LTV:** Average life extends to 40 months. New LTV $\approx \$90,000$ (gross) or $\$72,000$ (contribution).
*   **Result:** You double your asset value per customer without adding $120k fixed cost.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is **Monthly Churn**.
*   **Trigger Value:** If churn remains >3.5% monthly after onboarding improvements, the LTV drops below $25k (contribution), making even low-cost acquisition difficult to scale.
*   **Decision Rule:** If you cannot reduce churn to <3% within 6 months using self-serve tools, the market may be too complex for PLG, requiring a pivot to high-touch enterprise sales with much higher pricing ($100k+ ACV) to justify the rep cost.
*   **This Week's Step:** If your current activation rate is <40%, consider building a guided "sandbox" environment instead of hiring a sales rep.
