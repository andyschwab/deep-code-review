Regarding contract clauses: While I cannot provide legal advice, typical distribution agreements often contain "Most Favored Nation" (MFN) clauses (requiring you to give them your lowest price) and "Slotting Fees" or "Markdown Money" (charges for unsold inventory or promotions).
