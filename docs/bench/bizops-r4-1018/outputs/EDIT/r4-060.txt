**The Call**
Do not recognize revenue for the 30% of usage occurring in the final three days until the measurement period closes; defer this portion to the next period. You likely do not need an external auditor immediately unless you are preparing for an audit, raising institutional capital, or banking covenant requires it, but you must engage a CPA to formalize your policy before closing the quarter.

**The Numbers**
Under US GAAP (ASC 606), revenue for usage-based fees tied to a distinct service (each workflow run) is generally recognized as the usage occurs, provided the price is fixed per unit. However, if the total consideration is variable or the final count is unknown at period-end, you must estimate the transaction price or wait until the uncertainty is resolved.

*   **Total Runs:** 200,000
*   **Price Per Run:** $0.50
*   **Gross Potential Revenue:** $100,000
*   **Current Month Recognizable (Days 1–27):** 140,000 runs × $0.50 = **$70,000** (Recognize now)
*   **Deferred Revenue (Days 28–30):** 60,000 runs × $0.50 = **$30,000** (Defer to next month)

*Assumption (ASC 606 Constraint):* This calculation assumes you cannot reliably estimate the final 3 days' usage without significant risk of reversal. If you have stable historical data predicting the month-end spike with high confidence, you might accrue the $30,000 now, but given this is your *first* month of this model, the conservative approach (deferral) is the standard practice to avoid restatement risk.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The input that changes the call is **historical predictability**. If, after 3–6 months, your month-end usage variance drops below 5%, you may switch from deferring to accruing the estimated final days' revenue in the current month.
*   **Trigger Date:** End of Q3 (assuming Q2 is your first month).
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If the difference between your estimate and actuals is consistently <5% for two consecutive months, consider updating your policy to accrue.
*   **This Week's Step:** If your month-end close process takes longer than 3 days, consider implementing a real-time usage dashboard to lock the "cut-off" number exactly at midnight on the last day, allowing you to recognize 100% of the month's revenue in that same month's books once the count is final.
