**The Call**
Reject the current $200 CAC immediately; while the unit economics are theoretically profitable long-term, the 11-month payback period exceeds your 6-month cash runway, creating a high risk of insolvency before you recoup acquisition costs. You must pivot to lower-cost channels or increase upfront pricing to survive.

**The Numbers**
*Formula:* Contribution Margin = Price - Variable Cost; LTV = Margin × Lifetime; Payback = CAC / Margin.
*Inputs:* Price ($45), Variable Cost ($8), Lifetime (14 months), CAC ($200).
*Computed Figures:*
1.  **Monthly Contribution Margin:** $45 - $8 = **$37/user**.
2.  **Lifetime Value (LTV):** $37 × 14 = **$518/user**.
3.  **LTV:CAC Ratio:** $518 / $200 = **2.59x** (Generally healthy, >3x is ideal).
4.  **Payback Period:** $200 / $37 = **5.4 months**.

*Cash Flow Reality Check (The Trap):*
While you recover the $200 in 5.4 months, you burn $200 upfront.
*   **Current Burn Rate:** 4 staff + ops. Assuming a lean $15k/month total burn (salary + infra), your $150k runway lasts 10 months *if* you stop spending on ads.
*   **Growth Burn:** To grow, you spend $200 per user. If you acquire 100 users/month ($20k ad spend), your total monthly burn rises to ~$35k.
*   **Runway at Growth Pace:** $150k / $35k ≈ **4.3 months**.
*   **Result:** You will run out of cash in ~4 months, but you won't have recovered the ad spend for those users until month 9.4. You die before you get paid.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that changes this verdict is the **Payback Period**.
*   **Trigger Value:** Your payback must drop below **3 months** to safely grow on a 6-month runway with limited capital.
*   **Required Action:** Reduce CAC to **$110** (at current margin) OR increase upfront cash collection (e.g., annual prepay discount) to cover the CAC immediately.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If Payback > 50% of Runway, stop paid acquisition.
*   **This Week's Step:** If you cannot lower ad costs by 40% this week, consider launching an annual plan at $450 (2 months free) to collect $450 upfront, covering the $200 CAC and $74 in first-year variable costs immediately, turning cash-flow negative growth into cash-flow positive.

**Metrics to Track Weekly**
Do not wait for monthly reports. Track these every Monday:
1.  **Blended CAC:** Total marketing spend / New customers acquired (Target: < $110).
2.  **Cash Runway:** Cash in bank / (Fixed Costs + Planned Ad Spend).
3.  **Payback Period:** CAC / Monthly Contribution Margin (Target: < 3 months).
4.  **Churn Rate:** % of subscribers cancelling this week (any spike >0.5% weekly kills LTV).
