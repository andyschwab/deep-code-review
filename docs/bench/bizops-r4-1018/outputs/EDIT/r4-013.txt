**The Call**
You must open **four** new locations to break even on a consolidated basis, assuming they reach your current mature revenue levels. However, because new stores take six months to ramp up, you will operate at a net loss for approximately the first 4–5 months of this expansion. The biggest variable that could invalidate this plan is **gross margin erosion**; if rising labor and rent force you to discount prices or if waste increases, your contribution margin per store drops, potentially requiring 6+ locations to break even instead of 4.

**The Numbers**
*Formula:* Break-Even Units = Total Fixed Costs / Contribution Margin per Unit.

**Current Fleet (3 Stores) Monthly P&L:**
*   Revenue: $66,000 ($22k × 3)
*   COGS (38%): -$25,080
*   **Gross Profit:** $40,920
*   Rent: -$17,400 ($5,800 × 3)
*   Labor: -$36,000 ($12k × 3)
*   Overhead: -$35,000
*   **Current Monthly Net Loss:** **($47,480)**

**Per-Store Unit Economics (Mature):**
*   Revenue: $22,000
*   Variable Costs (COGS @ 38%): -$8,360
*   **Contribution Margin (CM):** $13,640
*   *Note: Rent and Labor are treated as fixed costs per location in this model.*

**Total Fixed Costs Required to Cover:**
*   Existing Fleet Loss: $47,480 (This is the hole the new stores must dig out of)
*   *Correction:* To break even, Total Contribution Margin must equal Total Fixed Costs (Rent + Labor + Overhead).
*   Total Fixed Costs (3 existing + N new):
    *   Rent: $5,800 × (3 + N)
    *   Labor: $12,000 × (3 + N)
    *   Overhead: $35,000 (Assuming no increase yet; if you hire staff, add here).
*   Equation: $13,640 × (3 + N) = [$5,800 + $12,000] × (3 + N) + $35,000
*   $13,640(3+N) = $17,800(3+N) + $35,000
*   *Wait, calculation check:*
    *   Current CM (3 stores): $40,920.
    *   Current Fixed Loc Costs (Rent+Lab): $53,400.
    *   Current Overhead: $35,000.
    *   Total Fixed: $88,400.
    *   Gap: $88,400 - $40,920 = $47,480 (Loss).
    *   CM per *new* store: $13,640.
    *   Fixed Cost per *new* store: $17,800.
    *   **Net Contribution per new store toward overhead:** $13,640 - $17,800 = **($4,160)**.

**Critical Correction:** Your current mature stores are **losing money** on a cash basis before overhead.
*   Store Revenue: $22,000
*   Store Direct Costs (COGS + Rent + Labor): $8,360 + $5,800 + $12,000 = $26,160.
*   **Store Level Net:** -$4,160 per month.

**Verdict Revision:** You cannot break even by opening more stores under these specific numbers. Each new mature store **increases** your monthly loss by $4,160.
*   Current Loss: $47,480.
*   With 4 new stores (7 total): Loss increases by $16,640.
*   **Result:** There is **no number** of new stores that will help you break even unless you raise prices, reduce COGS, or lower rent/labor per store. The unit economics are negative.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes this call is **Average Revenue Per Store**.
*   **Trigger Value:** You need monthly revenue of **$26,161** per store just to cover direct store costs (Rent + Labor + COGS).
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If you cannot drive revenue above $26.2k/month per location, expansion accelerates bankruptcy.
*   **Action Step:** If your traffic assumptions are slightly optimistic but don't hit $26.2k revenue, **halt expansion immediately** and focus on increasing ticket size or cutting labor hours at existing locations.

**Routing Note**
Since your unit economics are negative, do not spend the $85,000 capital per store. Consult a turnaround specialist or CPA to restructure the cost base before seeking additional funding.
