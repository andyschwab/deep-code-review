**The Call**
Reject the 50 million "total users" figure as your TAM; it represents gross installs, not unique people, and will be flagged by sophisticated Series A investors as inflated. Instead, define your TAM as the **unique individuals willing to pay for digital wellness**, then apply your observed overlap rate to derive a defensible Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM). Do not pivot to "households" unless your content is explicitly family-shared; B2C meditation apps are priced and marketed per individual user, and switching metrics mid-pitch signals a lack of category understanding.

**The Numbers**
To adjust for double-counting, you must treat the 50 million figure as a pool of *instances* rather than *identities*.
*Formula:* $Unique Users \approx \frac{Total Installs}{1 + (Overlap Rate \times (Avg Apps Per Multi-User - 1))}$

Since you lack the industry-wide "average apps per multi-user," we must bracket this using your internal data as a proxy assumption.
*   **Input A (Industry Installs):** 50,000,000 (Reported)
*   **Input B (Your Overlap Rate):** 15% (0.15) — *ASSUMED to be representative of the broader market.*
*   **Input C (Avg Apps per Multi-User):** Unknown. We will test Low (2 apps), Base (2.5 apps), High (3 apps).

*Computed Unique Market Size:*
1.  **Low Overlap Impact (2 apps/user):** $50M / (1 + 0.15 \times 1) = 43.5M$ unique users.
2.  **Base Case (2.5 apps/user):** $50M / (1 + 0.15 \times 1.5) = 40.8M$ unique users.
3.  **High Overlap Impact (3 apps/user):** $50M / (1 + 0.15 \times 2) = 38.5M$ unique users.

*Paid Conversion Subset (SOM):*
Apply your conversion hypothesis to the Base Case unique users. If the industry average paid conversion is ~5% (standard for freemium wellness):
$40.8M \times 5\% = 2.04M$ paid subscribers.
At $9.99/mo, the revenue-based SOM is ~$245M annually.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is the **Average Apps Per Multi-User**. If power users average 4+ apps, the unique market shrinks below 37M, tightening competition for the remaining wallet share.
*Trigger:* If your next cohort analysis shows multi-app users holding >3 subscriptions, revise your TAM down to the "High Impact" scenario ($38.5M) immediately.
*Rule of Thumb:* Investors accept a 20-30% deduction from gross install figures for overlap in saturated app categories.
*This Week's Step:* If your overlap rate exceeds 20% in Q3, consider bundling or annual discounts to increase switching costs before competitors target your dual-users.
