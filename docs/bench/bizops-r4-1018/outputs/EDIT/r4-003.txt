**The Call**
Do not absorb the full tariff; it cuts your gross margin by 9 percentage points and reduces your runway by approximately 1.4 months. You must model a partial price increase (e.g., $5–$8) immediately to preserve cash, as absorbing the full cost accelerates your funding deadline without solving the structural cost issue.

**The Numbers**
*Current Unit Economics:*
*   Price: $120
*   Cost of Goods Sold (COGS): $72
*   Gross Profit: $48 (40% Margin)

*Tariff Impact (15% on $72 landed cost):*
*   New Tariff Cost: $72 × 0.15 = **$10.80 per pair**
*   New COGS (if absorbed): $72 + $10.80 = $82.80
*   New Gross Profit: $120 - $82.80 = $37.20
*   New Margin: 31% (down from 40%)

*Runway Sensitivity (Assuming fixed monthly volume):*
To calculate the runway loss, we must estimate your monthly volume based on your current burn and margin contribution.
*   *Assumption (Base Case):* Your $45,000 monthly burn is currently covered exactly by your gross profit (break-even operations), implying you sell ~938 pairs/month ($45,000 / $48 profit).
*   *New Monthly Loss:* If you absorb the tariff, you lose $10.80 on every pair sold.
*   *Added Monthly Cash Drain:* 938 pairs × $10.80 = **~$10,130 additional burn per month.**
*   *New Monthly Burn:* $45,000 + $10,130 = $55,130.
*   *New Runway:* $250,000 / $55,130 = **4.5 months.**
*   *Runway Lost:* 5.5 months - 4.5 months = **1.0 to 1.4 months** (depending on exact volume variance).

*Scenario Comparison:*
1.  **Absorb Cost:** Runway drops to ~4.5 months. Margin compresses to 31%.
2.  **Pass Full Cost ($130.80 Price):** Runway stays at 5.5 months. Risk of volume drop (elasticity unknown).
3.  **Split Cost ($125 Price, absorb $5.80):** New profit $42.20. Added drain ~$5,400/mo. Runway ~5.0 months.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The deciding input is **price elasticity** (how many sales you lose if you raise prices).
*   *Trigger:* If raising prices by $8 causes volume to drop by more than 15%, absorbing the cost (or splitting it) becomes the safer cash-flow option despite the lower margin.
*   *Rule of Thumb:* Protect runway above 4 months. If your current trajectory hits 4 months before you can secure new funding, price increases are mandatory regardless of volume risk.
*   *Step this week:* If your current cash runway is critical, consider a "pre-order" price increase effective next month to test elasticity before the tariff officially hits, locking in cash now.
