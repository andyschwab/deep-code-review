**The Call**
Proceed with the deployment but treat the four-week onsite stint as a high-risk trigger for a German Permanent Establishment (PE); do not finalize the Statement of Work (SOW) without a specific "limited activity" clause and a quick validation from a German tax specialist. The risk is not zero, and the €150,000 upfront billing does not mitigate the nexus exposure created by physical presence.

**The Rules and Triggers**
Under the Germany-France and Germany-UK Double Taxation Treaties (aligned with the OECD Model), a foreign company creates a taxable Permanent Establishment (PE) in Germany if it has a **fixed place of business** or if a **dependent agent** habitually concludes contracts there. For service-based tech firms, the critical threshold is often the **"construction or installation project"** clause or the **"service PE"** provision.

*   **Duration Threshold:** Many treaties define a PE for installation/services if activities exceed **6 months** within a 12-month period. However, Germany's domestic law and specific treaty interpretations can be stricter. Some treaties trigger PE status after **183 days** of aggregate presence by employees. Your **4-week (20 working days)** duration is likely *under* the automatic treaty time-threshold for a "service PE," but it is not a safe harbor.
*   **Activity Nature:** The risk escalates if your French developers are not just "configuring" but are effectively **managing** the project, making binding decisions, or if the client's site is deemed a "fixed place of business" at your disposal. If the work is deemed "preparatory or auxiliary," it may be exempt, but core configuration of a €250k platform often fails this test.
*   **The "Deemed PE" Risk:** Even without a time-based PE, if your staff habitually exercise authority to conclude contracts or negotiate terms onsite, you create an **Agency PE**. Since you are billing 60% (€150,000) upon delivery, ensure no onsite staff have the authority to alter contract terms or accept delivery formally, as this could bind the company locally.

**What Flips It**
The single input that changes the call from "monitor" to "immediate filing obligation" is the **interpretation of "fixed place of business"** by the German Finanzamt (tax office). If the client provides a dedicated office, desk, or server room exclusively for your team for those four weeks, some authorities argue this constitutes a fixed place regardless of duration.
*   **Trigger:** If the SOW grants your team **exclusive control** over a specific workspace at the client's Berlin facility, the 4-week safety margin vanishes.
*   **Step:** Before signing, add a clause stating the client provides workspace on a **non-exclusive, revocable basis** and that your staff have **no authority to conclude contracts** or accept final delivery onsite.
