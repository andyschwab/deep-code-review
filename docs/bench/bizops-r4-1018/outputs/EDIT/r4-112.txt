**The Call:** Your current 15% percentage-of-revenue fee structure creates a high-risk exposure under the Federal Anti-Kickback Statute (AKS) because it directly correlates your compensation to the volume or value of referrals; you should immediately pivot to a Flat-Fee-Per-Transaction or Cost-Based subscription model to align with Safe Harbor provisions while preserving revenue.

**The Numbers:**
*   **Current Model:** $200 (Avg Session Price) × 15% (Fee) = **$30 per visit**.
    *   *Risk Factor:* The $30 varies directly with the session price and volume. If a therapist refers a $250 session, your fee rises to $37.50, creating a financial incentive to upcode or increase volume.
*   **Proposed Flat-Fee Model (Illustrative):** Charge a fixed administrative fee per booked session regardless of the session's clinical cost.
    *   *Formula:* Target Revenue / Estimated Volume = Flat Fee.
    *   *Base Assumption:* To maintain ~$30/visit revenue, set the flat fee at **$30 per booking**.
    *   *Result:* Whether the session is $150 or $300, your fee remains $30. The therapist's net revenue changes, but your compensation does not fluctuate based on the referral's value.
*   **Proposed Subscription Model (Illustrative):** Monthly platform access fee.
    *   *Base Assumption:* Average therapist books 10 sessions/month. Current revenue = $300/month.
    *   *Result:* Charge **$300/month flat**. This decouples compensation entirely from per-patient volume.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes the legal risk profile is the **compensation methodology**. Under current enforcement trends, any fee calculated as a *percentage* of the cost of the item or service ordered is presumed problematic.
*   **Trigger:** If your fee is calculated as `Session Price × %`, you are in the danger zone.
*   **Pivot Rule:** If `Fee = Fixed Amount` (per booking or per month) AND that amount reflects **Fair Market Value (FMV)** for the actual administrative services provided (software, billing, scheduling) without regard to the volume of federal healthcare program business generated, you move toward the "Personal Services and Management Contracts" Safe Harbor.
*   **Actionable Step:** This week, calculate the fully loaded cost of your platform services (hosting, support, payment processing) per therapist. If your proposed flat fee exceeds this cost significantly without a commercial justification unrelated to referrals, lower it to FMV.
