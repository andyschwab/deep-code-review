**The Call:** Do not front-load all 50 units; slow deployment to a "just-in-time" schedule that lands you at 30 units by month 7, preserving roughly $240,000 in cash to ensure you enter fundraising with >3 months of runway remaining. Front-loading this capex destroys your runway and signals distress, which outweighs the growth signal of rapid deployment in a Series A context where survival probability is the primary valuation driver.

**The Numbers:**
*   **Current Runway:** $720,000 cash (derived from $80k/mo × 9 months).
*   **Scenario A (Front-Load 50 Units):**
    *   Capex: $600,000 (50 units × $12k).
    *   Remaining Cash: $120,000 ($720k - $600k).
    *   New Runway: **1.5 months** ($120k / $80k burn).
    *   *Result:* You hit your 30-unit milestone but run out of cash 5 months before your planned raise.
*   **Scenario B (Phased Deployment to 30 Units by Month 7):**
    *   Capex Schedule: Deploy ~4-5 units/month to hit 30 units by Month 7.
    *   Total Capex by Month 7: $360,000 (30 units × $12k).
    *   Operating Burn (7 months): $560,000 ($80k × 7). *Note: Assuming burn includes current ops; if capex is separate, add it.*
    *   Total Cash Outflow (7 months): $920,000.
    *   *Correction on Burn Assumption:* If the $80k burn *excludes* the hardware capex, the math is:
        *   Operating Burn (7 mo): $560,000.
        *   Capex (30 units): $360,000.
        *   Total Outflow: $920,000. This exceeds your $720k cash.
    *   **Revised Strategy (Assumption: Burn includes some ops, Capex is extra):** You cannot afford 30 units *and* 7 months of ops on $720k if burn is purely operational.
    *   **Let's re-calculate based on strict cash flow:**
        *   Available Cash: $720,000.
        *   Required Ops Cash (9 months safety): $240,000 (3 months buffer).
        *   Available for Capex + Growth: $480,000.
        *   Max Units Affordable while keeping 3-month buffer: ($480k - (8 months * $80k ops? No, you raise in 8 months)).
        *   Let's target the raise date (Month 8). You need 3 months runway *after* Month 8? No, you need runway *until* the money hits.
        *   **Critical Constraint:** To survive 8 months to the raise: $80k × 8 = $640,000 needed for ops.
        *   Remaining for Capex: $720k - $640k = **$80,000**.
        *   **Max Units Deployable:** $80,000 / $12,000 = **6.6 units**.

*   **The Gap:** You cannot deploy 30 units and survive 8 months on current cash/burn.
*   **The Fix:** You must either (a) reduce monthly burn to ~$50k, (b) raise a bridge note now, or (c) deploy only 6 units and reset investor expectations to a "pilot" milestone rather than "30 units."

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes this call is **Monthly Burn**. If you can cut burn from $80k to $50k immediately, you free up $240k over 8 months ($30k × 8), allowing you to deploy ~20 additional units (total ~26) while maintaining safety.
*   **Trigger:** If you cannot sign letters of intent (LOIs) with customers to pre-pay for kiosks or reduce burn by 25% within **14 days**, you must abandon the "30 units by Series A" plan.
*   **Step:** If you cannot cut burn or secure pre-payments this week, consider pivoting the Series A narrative to "validated unit economics on 6 units" rather than "scale at 30 units," as running out of cash negates any deployment metric.
