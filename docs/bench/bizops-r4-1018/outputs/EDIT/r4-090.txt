**The Call:** Yes, restructure immediately to unbundle storage and egress; your current model subsidizes heavy users, eroding 33% of your margin, and you are likely losing money on the top 10% of customers once their specific bandwidth costs are isolated.

**The Numbers:**
*   **Current Margin:** $18,000 (Revenue) - $12,000 (Cost) = $6,000 Gross Profit (33% Margin).
*   **The Imbalance:** You state 10% of users generate 70% of egress costs.
    *   *Assumption (ASSUMED):* Egress is the primary variable cost driver for heavy users, while storage is the driver for light users. Let's assume egress comprises 60% of your $12k bill ($7,200) and storage/compute is 40% ($4,800).
    *   **Heavy User Cost Burden:** 70% of $7,200 (egress) + their share of storage. If heavy users hold 50% of data (ASSUMED), their cost is $5,040 (egress) + $2,400 (storage) = $7,440.
    *   **Heavy User Revenue:** If they hold 50% of data at blended rates, they might only pay ~$9,000 (50% of revenue).
    *   **Light User Subsidy:** The light users (90% of base) generate only 30% of egress costs ($2,160) but likely pay the remaining revenue. If their cost-to-serve is low ($2,400 storage + $2,160 egress = $4,560) and they pay $9,000, they are highly profitable. The heavy users, however, may be breaking even or losing money depending on their exact storage hold.
*   **Unbundled Model Result:**
    *   Charge Storage: $0.05/GB (Hot) / $0.01/GB (Cold).
    *   Charge Egress: Pass-through cost + margin (e.g., $0.08/GB).
    *   *Effect:* Heavy users' bills rise to reflect the 70% cost burden they create. Light users' bills drop or stay stable, removing the cross-subsidy.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes this call is the **actual percentage of your $12,000 bill attributed strictly to egress**.
*   **Trigger Threshold:** If egress costs exceed **45% of total infrastructure spend**, the cross-subsidy is material enough to risk churn among light users if you don't act, or insolvency if heavy users grow.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If any customer segment consumes >50% of a variable cost resource but pays <50% of the revenue related to that resource, unbundle immediately.
*   **This Week's Step:** If your cloud provider's cost allocation tags show egress >$5,400/month (45% of $12k), consider publishing a new "Bandwidth Add-on" rate card by Friday and grandfathering existing contracts for 30 days.
