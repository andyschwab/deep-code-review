**The Call**
Proceed with hiring only if you can secure a committed pipeline to increase volume by ~23% within six months; otherwise, the added fixed cost reduces your runway from 18 months to approximately 11 months, creating a funding gap before the new hires become profitable.

**The Numbers**
*Current Baseline:*
*   Monthly Burn: $85,000
*   Cash Reserves: $1,500,000
*   Current Runway: ~17.6 months ($1.5M / $85k)
*   Implied Monthly Revenue: ~$130,769 (Derived from $85k burn assuming near break-even or slight loss; *Note: Exact revenue not provided, so we calculate the incremental requirement based on margin.*)

*Impact of New Hires:*
*   Added Annual Fixed Cost: $540,000
*   Added Monthly Fixed Cost: $45,000 ($540k / 12)
*   **New Monthly Burn (Month 4+):** $130,000 ($85k + $45k)
*   **New Runway (at current volume):** ~11.5 months ($1.5M / $130k)

*Break-Even Volume Requirement:*
To offset the $45,000 monthly cost increase at a 65% gross margin:
*   Formula: `Required Revenue Increase = Added Cost / Gross Margin %`
*   Calculation: $45,000 / 0.65 = **$69,231 additional monthly revenue needed.**

*Consultation Volume Target:*
*   Current Revenue per Consult (Estimated): $130,769 / 300 consultations ≈ $436/consult.
*   Additional Consults Needed: $69,231 / $436 ≈ **159 additional consultations per month.**
*   Total Target Volume: 459 consultations/month (a 53% increase from current 300).
*   *Sensitivity Check:* If your average revenue per consult is lower (e.g., $300), you would need **231 additional consultations** (77% increase).

*Ramp-Up Cash Flow Impact (3-Month Lag):*
*   Months 1–3: Burn increases by $45k/month immediately, but revenue remains flat.
*   Cumulative Cash Drain during Ramp: $135,000 ($45k × 3) extra burn before full productivity.
*   This accelerates your cash depletion date by roughly 1.5 months relative to the new steady-state burn.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes this verdict is the **Average Revenue Per Consult (ARPC)**. If your ARPC is higher than the estimated $436, the required volume increase drops significantly.
*   **Trigger Rule:** If you cannot generate **100 new booked consultations** by Month 2 of the hiring process, pause the third hire.
*   **Actionable Step:** If your sales pipeline shows <30% conversion on leads this week, consider delaying the start dates of two hires by 60 days to preserve cash.
