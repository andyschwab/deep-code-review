**The Numbers:**
To determine if the hike works, we calculate the **Maximum Tolerable Churn Increase**.
*Formula:* $\text{Max New Churn Rate} = \frac{\text{Price Increase \%}}{1 + \text{Price Increase \%}} \times \text{Current Volume Equivalent}$
*Simplified Break-Even Logic:* You need the revenue gain from the hike to exceed the revenue lost from customers leaving.
*   **Current Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR):** $1,000,000 / 12 \approx \$83,333$.
*   **Proposed Price Increase:** 30%.
*   **Break-Even Churn Threshold:** To maintain the same revenue immediately after the hike, you can afford to lose roughly **23%** of your customer base *if* the remaining 77% pay 30% more. However, since churn is a rate over time, we look at the monthly impact.
*   **Monthly Sensitivity Analysis (ASSUMED):**

    *   *Failure Case (Backlash):* If churn spikes to **25%** (startups flee), you lose $20,833. The hike on remaining 75% generates $\approx \$18,750$. **Net Loss: -$2,083/mo.**
