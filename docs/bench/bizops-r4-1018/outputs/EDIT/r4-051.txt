**The Call**
Reject the deal as structured; at $0.0012 per request, you remain profitable on a marginal basis, but the tier creates a "cliff" where total revenue drops if Acme's volume crosses a specific threshold without a corresponding drop in your blended costs. Do not sign until you model the break-even volume where the discount triggers, and verify if your fixed overhead scales linearly or steps up at 100M requests.

**The Numbers**
*Formula:* Contribution Margin = Price per Unit – Variable Cost per Unit.
*Current State:* $0.0020 – $0.0008 = **$0.0012 margin/unit**.
*Proposed Tier:* $0.0012 – $0.0008 = **$0.0004 margin/unit**.

*Assumption (Fixed Costs):* You stated $15,000/month fixed overhead. We assume this does not increase until capacity is breached.
*Scenario A (Acme stays under 100M):* Margin remains $0.0012. Viable.
*Scenario B (Acme hits 150M requests, all discounted):*
If the discount applies to *all* units once the threshold is crossed (common in bad contracts):
Revenue = 150M * $0.0012 = $180,000.
Variable Cost = 150M * $0.0008 = $120,000.
Contribution = $60,000.
Net Profit = $60,000 – $15,000 = $45,000.
*Result:* You do not lose money on additional requests; the margin shrinks from 60% to 33%, but remains positive.

*The Trap (Blended Rate vs. Marginal Rate):*
If the contract implies a retroactive discount (price drops to $0.0012 for *all* units once 100M is hit), compare revenue at 99M vs 101M.
At 99M: 99M * $0.002 = $198,000 Revenue.
At 101M (retroactive): 101M * $0.0012 = $121,200 Revenue.
*Verdict:* You lose $76,800 in revenue by processing 2 million extra requests. This destroys value.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The deal flips from viable to catastrophic if the discount clause is **retroactive** (applies to all units) rather than **incremental** (applies only to units >100M).
*Trigger:* If the contract language says "price drops to $0.0012" without specifying "for usage above the threshold," assume retroactive.
*Rule of Thumb:* Never accept a volume tier that reduces total revenue when volume increases.
*Step this week:* Redline the contract to specify the discount applies **only to incremental units** above 100M. If Acme refuses, walk away.
