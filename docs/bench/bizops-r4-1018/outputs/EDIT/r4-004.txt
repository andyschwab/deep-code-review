**The Call**
Stay with the weekly subscription for now; switching to a per-meal credit system is likely to worsen your unit economics by increasing food waste beyond your current 8% and eroding contribution margin, unless you can prove churn drops below 6% monthly to offset the operational inefficiency. The predictability of the weekly model is currently your primary lever for covering your $8,000 fixed costs.

**The Numbers**
We evaluate the Contribution Margin (CM) per customer per month to determine which model covers fixed costs faster.
*Formula:* `Monthly CM = (Orders/Month × (Price - Variable Cost)) - Waste Cost`
*Current Inputs:* Price/meal $12, Food Cost $5, Waste 8% of food cost ($0.40/meal), Fixed Costs $8,000.
*Assumption:* "Orders/Month" is 4 for weekly subscribers. For the credit model, we assume volume stays flat initially but waste increases due to forecasting difficulty.

| Scenario | Orders/Mo | Gross Margin/Meal | Est. Waste/Meal | Net CM/Meal | Monthly CM/Customer |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Current (Weekly)** | 4 | $7.00 | $0.40 (8%) | $6.60 | **$26.40** |
| **Proposed (Credit)** | 4 | $7.00 | $1.00 (ASSUMED: 20% waste) | $6.00 | **$24.00** |
| **Proposed (Credit)** | 3 | $7.00 | $1.00 (ASSUMED: 20% waste) | $6.00 | **$18.00** |

*Break-even Analysis:* To cover $8,000 fixed costs:
*   Current Model requires **304** active customers ($8,000 / $26.40).
*   Proposed Model (flat volume, higher waste) requires **334** active customers ($8,000 / $24.00).
*   Proposed Model (volume drop + higher waste) requires **445** active customers.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes this call is the **reduction in churn**. If moving to credits reduces monthly churn from 12% to **below 6%**, the increased Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) may justify the lower monthly margin per user.
*Trigger:* If you pilot the credit model for 30 days and food waste exceeds **12%** or active order frequency drops below **3.2 meals/month**, revert to weekly subscriptions immediately.
*Rule of Thumb:* Flexibility only pays if it significantly extends tenure; it rarely pays if it increases variable costs.
*Next Step:* This week, run a "soft credit" test with 10% of your user base where they can skip a week without cancelling, measuring the actual change in waste percentage before changing the pricing architecture.
