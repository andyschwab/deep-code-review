**The Call**
Do not treat the bundle as a single taxable item; instead, you must separately state the value of the seeds on your invoice to preserve the agricultural exemption for that portion. If you fail to break out the seed cost, California tax authorities will likely classify the entire $199 as taxable electronic hardware, triggering the liability you fear.

**The Numbers**
The risk is the difference between taxing the full bundle versus only the hardware component.
*Formula:* `Potential Liability = (Total Units × Bundle Price × Tax Rate) − (Total Units × Hardware Portion × Tax Rate)`
*User Figures:* 5,000 units projected; $199 bundle price; 7.25% CA sales tax rate.
*Assumption (Hardware Value):* Since the seeds cost you $12, we assume a fair market value allocation of $12 for seeds and $187 for hardware (ASSUMED: cost approximates value for allocation).

*Scenario A: Entire Bundle Taxed (No separate statement)*
$199 × 5,000 × 0.0725 = **$72,137.50**

*Scenario B: Only Hardware Portion Taxed (Separately stated)*
$187 × 5,000 × 0.0725 = **$67,787.50**

*Tax Savings from Separation:* **$4,350.00**
*Note:* Your estimate of $70,000 liability aligns with Scenario A. The savings calculated here ($4,350) reflects the seed portion only. If your internal allocation values the seeds higher, or if the "exempt" status is lost entirely, the exposure remains near the $72k figure.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the outcome is **invoice presentation**.
*Trigger:* If your invoice line item reads "Smart Garden Kit - $199" without breakdown, the entire amount is taxable.
*Rule of Thumb:* In California, when a sale includes both taxable and exempt items, the burden is on the seller to separately state the exempt portion. Failure to do so results in the "true object" test often defaulting to the taxable character of the dominant item (the hardware).
*Action Step:* If your current invoicing system lumps the price, consider updating your SKU structure this week to list "Hydroponic Unit" and "Seed Bundle" as distinct line items with assigned values totaling $199.
