**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes the verdict from "stop" to "proceed with caution" is the **Lawful Basis for Processing**. Currently, you likely rely on "legitimate interest" or "contract performance" for fall detection (movement within a room). Geolocation tracking of vulnerable adults almost certainly shifts the requirement to **explicit consent** (from a legal guardian, not the patient) or a specific **public interest/health mandate** under Article 9(2)(i) or (j) of the GDPR.
*   **Trigger:** If you cannot secure written, explicit consent from the legal guardians of *every* resident in a facility before activation, or if a local Data Protection Authority (DPA) in Germany (strict on employee/subject monitoring) or France determines this exceeds "necessary care," you must halt.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If the data can identify a specific person's location outside a controlled environment in real-time, it is high-risk.
