**The Rules and Landscape:**
In Florida, the practice of medicine is legally defined as occurring where the **patient is physically located** at the time of the telehealth encounter, not where the provider sits. Consequently, a physician licensed only in Texas cannot legally treat a patient in Florida without specific Florida authorization.

1.  **Licensure Requirement:** The Florida Board of Medicine mandates that physicians providing telehealth services to patients in Florida must hold an active Florida medical license. There is no automatic reciprocity or "shadow licensing" that allows immediate practice upon hiring.
2.  **Telehealth Registration (The Pathway):** Florida Statute 456.47 offers a specific **Telehealth Provider Registration** for out-of-state physicians. This is not a full license but allows practice limited to telehealth.
    *   **Constraint:** This is not an instant "temporary permit." It requires an application, fee payment, and verification of the home-state license. While often faster than full licensure, it is not effective until the Board processes and approves it. You cannot legally bill or treat under this status until the registration number is issued.
3.  **Penalties for Non-Compliance:** Practicing medicine without a license in Florida is a **third-degree felony**. Penalties include criminal prosecution, fines up to $5,000 per incident, and permanent injunctions against the practice. For your business, this risks immediate shutdown, loss of merchant processing capabilities, and exclusion from insurance networks.

**What Flips the Decision:**
The only variable that changes this verdict is the **status of the application**. If your three doctors have *already* submitted their Telehealth Provider Registration applications to the Florida Board of Medicine and have received written confirmation of approval (or a temporary practice permit explicitly issued by the Board during processing), they may proceed. If they have not applied, or if applications are pending without explicit written authorization to practice interim, the trigger to stop is active.
