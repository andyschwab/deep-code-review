**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The deciding factor is the classification of the error by Customs and Border Protection (CBP). While minor paperwork errors often result in fines (like your $4,200 demurrage), **food safety and country-of-origin labeling (COOL)** are high-priority enforcement areas. If CBP determines the labeling discrepancy prevents the end consumer from identifying the true origin or compromises food safety tracking, they have the authority to refuse entry or order destruction (seizure) without a monetary penalty option.
*   **Trigger:** Any discrepancy involving "Country of Origin" on food products.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If the error prevents a consumer or inspector from immediately verifying the source nation, the risk shifts from "fine" to "seizure/refusal."
