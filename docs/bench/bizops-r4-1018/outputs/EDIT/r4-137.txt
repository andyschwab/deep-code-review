*   **The Rule:** Physical presence nexus (having employees or equipment in the state) generally triggers filing obligations immediately upon entry, unlike "economic nexus" which relies on revenue thresholds (often $100k+ or 200+ transactions).
*   **The Assumption (UNVERIFIED):** You are assuming a *de minimis* threshold exists for temporary medical clinics similar to trade show exemptions.

*   **Computed Figure:** If nexus is triggered, you do not owe tax on the full $250k in each state. You would apportion income.
