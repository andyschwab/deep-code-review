**The Call:** Do not halt the fundraising round, but immediately pause closing any new commitments until you issue a written supplemental disclosure to all current and prospective investors detailing the supply chain disruption and its impact on your unit economics and timeline. You do not need to formally "amend" past statements if you proactively correct the record before funds are exchanged, but failing to disclose this material change now creates significant legal risk for securities misrepresentation.

**The Numbers:**
Your current gross margin is 55% on a $2,200 AOV, implying a Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) of $990 per unit.
*   **Formula:** New COGS = Current COGS × (1 + Cost Increase %)
*   **Input:** $990 × 1.30 (30% increase due to air freight) = $1,287.
*   **New Gross Margin:** ($2,200 - $1,287) / $2,200 = **41.5%**.

This 13.5 percentage point drop in margin directly increases your cash burn rate. If your original model assumed reinvesting gross profit into growth, you now have significantly less cash per unit sold to cover operating expenses.
*   **Revenue Timing Impact:** Shifting from a 4-week to a 10-week delivery window delays revenue recognition by approximately 6 weeks per order. If you planned to recognize $1.5M over 18 months based on immediate fulfillment, your cash inflow curve shifts right, potentially creating a liquidity gap in months 3–9 where expenses outpace delayed revenue.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The deciding factor is **investor reliance**. If an investor writes a check tomorrow based on the old 55% margin and 4-week delivery assumptions without knowing about the Vietnam delay, that is a actionable misrepresentation.
*   **Trigger:** The moment you solicit or accept funds while aware of the material adverse change.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** In securities fundraising, you have an affirmative duty to update material facts until the transaction closes. Silence is not neutral; it is misleading.
*   **Action This Week:** Draft a one-page "Supplemental Information Memo" summarizing the supplier delay, the revised 41.5% margin scenario, and the updated cash flow timeline. Send this to all parties who have seen the deck. Frame it as "stress-testing our model against real-world volatility," which demonstrates operational maturity rather than failure.
