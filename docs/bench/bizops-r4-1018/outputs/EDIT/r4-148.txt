**The Call**
Your monetization model alone does not automatically transform SoberPath into a broker or guarantor of quality under California law, but it significantly increases your exposure to negligence claims if you imply verification without performing it. To mitigate liability, you must immediately decouple the fee from any suggestion of vetting and explicitly disclaim that listings are unverified paid advertisements, not endorsed recommendations.

**The Numbers & Risk Factors**
There is no arithmetic formula for liability, but your risk profile is driven by the ratio of **implied trust** to **actual verification**.
*   **Current State:** 150 listings × $199/month = ~$30k MRR.
*   **Liability Trigger:** The legal danger arises not from the $199 fee itself, but if your marketing, UI, or customer support leads a user to believe the fee purchases *verification* rather than *visibility*.
*   **Assumption (High Risk):** If your site uses language like "verified partners," "certified homes," or if you manually curate the list without a clear audit trail, a court may find you assumed a "duty of care."
*   **Assumption (Low Risk):** If the platform operates as a pure classifieds model (like Craigslist) where the fee buys a slot, and you prominently display "Paid Listing – Not Verified," you generally retain Section 230-style protections and avoid broker status.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes your legal standing is **representation**. If you represent the homes as "vetted," "screened," or "approved," you flip from a passive directory to an active guarantor.
*   **The Trigger:** A lawsuit filed by a family alleging reliance on your "seal of approval" after an incident at an unlicensed home.
*   **Stop/Pivot Rule:** If you cannot verify 100% of licenses monthly, you must stop any language implying you do.
*   **Actionable Step:** This week, audit your homepage and checkout flow. If the words "verify," "check," or "trusted" appear near the pricing or listing description, replace them with "paid placement" and add a visible disclaimer: *"SoberPath is a paid directory. We do not verify licensure or quality. Users must confirm credentials directly with the California Department of Health Care Services."*
