**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that changes the outcome is the **nature of the technical uncertainty**. If your team was simply assembling known components (standard API integrations, basic forms), the credit is zero regardless of allocation. If they were solving unsolved problems (e.g., low-latency sync of medical records on unstable facility Wi-Fi), the credit is maximized.
*   **Trigger:** If >50% of dev time was spent on routine feature assembly vs. experimental problem solving, pause the claim.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If you cannot describe a specific hypothesis your team tested and failed at before succeeding, that portion of work is likely ineligible.
