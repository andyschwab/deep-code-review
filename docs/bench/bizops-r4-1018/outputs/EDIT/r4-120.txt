**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is **custody duration**. If you reduce the time funds sit in your controlled account to near-zero (real-time pass-through) or eliminate custody entirely (direct payer-to-payee), you may fall under exemptions for "commercial agents" or simply cease to be a money transmitter.
*   **Trigger:** The moment your payment processor freezes funds or a regulator issues a cease-and-desist (which they can do immediately upon detecting unlicensed aggregation).
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If money enters your bank account and sits there for *any* duration before you manually or automatically push it out, you are likely transmitting. If the money goes from User → Licensed Processor → Healer (with you only receiving a separate fee invoice), you are likely safe.
