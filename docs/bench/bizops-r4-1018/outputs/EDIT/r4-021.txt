**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is the **number of discrete transactions** processed through your Shopify store into Texas.
*Trigger Value:* 200 transactions in any 12-month period.
*Rule of Thumb:* If you sell more than ~17 soaps/month directly to Texas customers via Shopify, you likely hit the transaction threshold even if your revenue remains under $500k.
*Action Step:* If your Shopify order count for Texas addresses is >150 in the last 12 months, consider pausing direct TX marketing until you consult a tax professional to confirm the "marketplace facilitator" deduction rules for transaction counts in Texas.

**Routing Note**
