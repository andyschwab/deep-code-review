**The Call**
Do not hire the dedicated sales representative yet; the math shows you would need to nearly triple your current close rate just to break even on the base salary, ignoring commissions and benefits. Remain product-led until you either increase your Average Contract Value (ACV) to at least $12,000 or validate that a sales-led motion can reliably achieve a 40%+ close rate.

**The Numbers**
We compare the contribution margin of your current model against the required volume to cover a new sales hire.

*Formula:*
`Break-Even Deals = (Base Salary + Benefits + Commission) / (ACV - Variable Costs)`
`Required Close Rate = Break-Even Deals / Leads Generated`

*Inputs & Assumptions:*
*   **ACV:** $5,000 (Given)
*   **Current CAC (Marketing):** $800 (Given)
*   **Current Close Rate:** 15% (Given)
*   **Sales Rep Base:** $90,000 (Given)
*   **Benefits/Overhead:** ASSUMED 20% of base ($18,000) for payroll taxes/insurance.
*   **Commission:** ASSUMED 10% of ACV ($500/deal).
*   **Variable Cost per Deal:** ASSUMED $0 beyond commission (SaaS COGS negligible for this calc).

*Scenario A: Current Product-Led Unit Economics*
*   Contribution Margin per Deal = $5,000 - $800 = **$4,200**
*   Margin % = 84%

*Scenario B: Sales-Led Break-Even Analysis*
*   Total Fixed Cost (Base + Benefits) = $90,000 + $18,000 = $108,000
*   Net Revenue per Deal (after 10% commission) = $5,000 - $500 = $4,500
*   **Break-Even Volume:** $108,000 / $4,500 = **24 deals/year** (2 deals/month).

*The Feasibility Check:*
To sell 24 deals at a 15% close rate, you need **160 qualified demos/year**.
If your current inbound marketing generates enough leads to close 24 deals at 15%, you are already doing ~3.6 deals/year organically.
*   **Current Organic Contribution:** 3.6 deals * $4,200 = $15,120.
*   **Sales Team Cost:** $108,000 fixed + commissions.
*   **Gap:** You need to generate an *incremental* 20.4 deals solely from the rep's effort to cover their own cost.
*   **Required Close Rate Shift:** If lead volume stays constant, the close rate must jump from 15% to roughly **45%** to justify the fixed cost on the same lead flow.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes this call is **ACV**. At $5,000, the deal size is too small to support a $90k+ fixed cost unless volume is massive.
*   **Trigger Threshold:** If you can raise ACV to **$12,500** (via upsells/bundles), the break-even volume drops to ~10 deals/year, making a sales hire viable at current close rates.
*   **Actionable Step:** If you cannot secure commitments for a $12k+ tier this quarter, consider a "Sales Development Representative" (SDR) model ($50k base) to book meetings for founders, rather than a full Account Executive.
