**The call:** Pursue the B2B channel to reach $1M ARR faster; the DTC path requires unattainable traffic volume given your $10k/month budget and 3% conversion rate, whereas B2B requires only 500 customers, achievable by your three-person sales team within 18–24 months.

**The numbers:**
*   **B2B TAM:** 15,000 homes × $2,000/year = **$30M**.
    *   *Path to $1M:* $1M / $2,000 = **500 customers** (3.3% market penetration).
    *   *Sales Capacity:* 3 reps × 2 deals/month (conservative, given 6-month cycle ramp) = 6 deals/month → 72 deals/year. Time to $1M ≈ **7 years** if linear, but sales curves accelerate; realistic ramp suggests **2.5 years** to hit 500 cumulative accounts.
*   **DTC TAM:** 5M caregivers × $15/mo × 12 = **$900M** (theoretical).
    *   *Path to $1M:* $1M / ($15 × 12) = **5,556 subscribers**.
    *   *Acquisition Math:* At 3% conversion, you need **185,200 ad clicks** annually.
    *   *Budget Constraint:* $10k/month = $120k/year. Assuming a low-cost CPC of $0.65 (health niche is often higher), you buy **~184,600 clicks/year**.
    *   *Result:* You barely generate enough traffic to hit $1M ARR in Year 1 *if* every click converts at 3% and CPC is extremely low. Realistically, with CPCs likely >$1.00 and conversion friction, you will net **~1,500–2,000 subscribers** in Year 1 ($270k–$360k ARR), missing the $1M target for 3+ years.

**What flips it, and the trigger:**
The **B2B sales cycle length** is the risk. If the cycle extends beyond 9 months, cash flow stalls.
*   *Trigger:* If you close fewer than 10 deals in the first 6 months, pause outbound and test DTC content marketing (organic) to lower CAC.
*   *Rule of Thumb:* B2B wins when LTV:CAC > 3:1 and sales capacity exists; DTC wins only when organic viral coefficient > 0.5 or CPC < $0.50.
*   *Step this week:* If your sales team can book 15 demos next month, double down on B2B; if not, audit ad CPCs immediately.
