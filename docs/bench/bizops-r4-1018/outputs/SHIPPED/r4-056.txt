**The Numbers & Factors**
There is no arithmetic formula for antitrust violation; the "calculation" is a qualitative test of two variables: **Market Power** and **Predatory Intent**.
*   **Market Power (The Threshold):** In EU competition law (Article 102 TFEU), a market share consistently above 50% creates a *rebuttable presumption* of dominance.
    *   *Your Figure:* 80% (ASSUMED based on your statement).
    *   *Implication:* You are likely already "dominant" in the eyes of a regulator unless the "relevant market" is defined much broader than "orchestration for European startups" (e.g., global orchestration or general DevOps tools).
*   **Predatory Pricing (The Conduct):** For a dominant firm, pricing below cost is abusive if it aims to eliminate competitors rather than compete on merit.
    *   *The Test:* Is the free tier priced below your **Average Variable Cost (AVC)**?
    *   *Your Input Needed:* Calculate the marginal cost of serving one additional free user (compute, support, bandwidth). If Price (0) < AVC, you are technically selling below cost.
    *   *The Defense:* If you can prove the free tier is a promotional loss-leader with a clear path to profitability via upsells (the "freemium" model) and not a tactic to starve out a specific rival, it is usually defensible. However, dominant firms face a stricter burden of proof than non-dominant ones.
