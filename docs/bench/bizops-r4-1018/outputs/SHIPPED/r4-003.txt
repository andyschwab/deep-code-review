**The Call**
Do not absorb the full tariff; it cuts your gross margin by 9 percentage points and reduces your runway by approximately 1.4 months. You should immediately model a price increase of $10–$12 (roughly 8–10%) to preserve cash, as absorbing the cost accelerates your funding deadline from late month 5 to early month 4.

**The Numbers**
*Current Unit Economics:*
*   Retail Price: $120
*   Landed Cost: $72
*   Gross Margin: $48 (40%)

*Tariff Impact (Assuming 15% on the $72 landed cost):*
*   Tariff Cost per Unit: $72 × 0.15 = **$10.80** (ASSUMED: Tariff applies to full landed cost of rubber-soled shoes).
*   New Landed Cost: $72 + $10.80 = $82.80
*   New Gross Margin (if price held at $120): $120 - $82.80 = **$37.20**
*   New Margin %: 31% (a drop from 40%).

*Runway Sensitivity (Absorbing the Cost):*
*   Current Monthly Burn: $45,000
*   Estimated Monthly Volume: To derive this, we must assume your current burn includes COGS. However, without your specific sales volume, we cannot calculate the *exact* new burn.
*   *Alternative Approach (Contribution Margin Impact):* Every shoe sold now contributes $10.80 less to covering fixed costs.
*   *Scenario A (Low Volume - 500 pairs/mo):* Extra cost = $5,400/mo. New Burn = $50,400. New Runway = $250k / $50.4k = **4.96 months** (Loss of ~0.6 months).
*   *Scenario B (High Volume - 1,000 pairs/mo):* Extra cost = $10,800/mo. New Burn = $55,800. New Runway = $250k / $55.8k = **4.48 months** (Loss of ~1.1 months).
*   *Scenario C (Very High Volume - 1,500 pairs/mo):* Extra cost = $16,200/mo. New Burn = $61,200. New Runway = $250k / $61.2k = **4.08 months** (Loss of ~1.5 months).

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is your **monthly sales volume**. The higher your volume, the faster the tariff drains cash.
*   **Trigger:** If you sell more than 800 pairs per month, absorbing the cost reduces your runway by over 1 month, pushing you into a critical "emergency raise" zone before month 5.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If a cost increase reduces gross margin by >5 percentage points, pass at least 50% of the cost to the consumer immediately.
*   **Step this week:** If your volume exceeds 800 pairs/month, consider raising the retail price to $129.99 to offset $9 of the $10.80 tariff, preserving most of your original margin and runway.
