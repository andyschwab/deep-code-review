**The Call**
Adopt an **incremental CAC** model for channel decisions and a **fully-loaded blended CAC** for runway planning; do not allocate fixed salaries arbitrarily to channels, as this distorts profitability signals. Your conference channel is currently inefficient compared to ads unless conference customers have significantly higher retention or upsell potential.

**The Numbers**
To calculate realistic efficiency, separate variable spend from fixed overhead.

*Formula:*
`Incremental CAC = Channel Specific Spend / New Customers from Channel`
`Blended CAC (Cash) = (Total Marketing Spend + Total Sales Spend) / Total New Customers`

*Your Figures:*
1.  **Google Ads/Content (Low-touch):**
    *   Spend: $60,000
    *   Customers: 400
    *   **Incremental CAC:** $150/customer
2.  **Conferences (High-touch):**
    *   Spend: $40,000
    *   Customers: 400
    *   **Incremental CAC:** $100/customer (Variable only)
3.  **Fixed Overhead (The Confusion Source):**
    *   Two marketing staff salaries (Unknown variable, let's call it $S).
    *   Total Customers: 800.

*Analysis:*
If you allocate 50% of salaries to each channel, you artificially inflate the CAC of the lower-volume channel or distort the high-touch channel depending on time spent.
*   **Scenario A (Exclude Salaries for Channel Efficiency):**
    *   Ads CAC: $150
    *   Conference CAC: $100
    *   *Result:* Conferences appear cheaper.
*   **Scenario B (Include Salaries for True Profitability):**
    *   Assume annual salary burden for 2 staff is $200,000 (ASSUMED for illustration).
    *   Total Spend = $60k + $40k + $200k = $300,000.
    *   Total Customers = 800.
    *   **Fully-Loaded Blended CAC:** $375/customer.
    *   *Result:* At $200 revenue per customer, you are losing $175 on every customer in Year 1 before hosting costs or COGS.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The deciding factor is **Lifetime Value (LTV)**, not CAC allocation.
*   **Trigger:** If conference-acquired customers churn slower or upgrade faster than ad-acquired customers, the higher touch cost is justified. If LTV is identical across channels, the conference channel is burning cash relative to the ad channel once staff time is factored in.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** LTV:CAC must exceed 3:1 for sustainability. With a $200 price point, if your gross margin is 80% ($160), you need customers to stay >2.4 years just to break even on the *blended* $375 CAC (ignoring COGS).
*   **Action:** If the conference customers do not show 20% higher retention than ad customers by Q3, consider replacing one conference with a targeted webinar series to reduce variable spend.
