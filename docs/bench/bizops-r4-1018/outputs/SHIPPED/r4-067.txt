**The Call**
Do not book the €5,000 implementation fee as immediate revenue; under standard accounting rules (IFRS 15, applicable in Dublin), it must be recognized over the expected customer lifespan or contract term. While this model improves immediate cash flow and extends your runway, it distorts LTV calculations if the fee is treated as recurring value, so you must report it separately to avoid signaling weak product stickiness to investors.

**The Numbers**
To assess the impact, we separate Cash Flow from Recognized Revenue.

*Formula:*
`Cash Impact` = Upfront Fee + (New MRR × Months)
`Recognized Revenue (Monthly)` = (Upfront Fee / Amortization Period) + New MRR
`LTV` = (New MRR × Gross Margin % × Avg Lifespan) + Upfront Fee (one-time)

*User Figures:*
Current MRR: €500 | New MRR: €300 | Upfront Fee: €5,000 | Avg Lifespan: 18 months | Customers: 100.

*Scenario A: Customer stays 6 months (Churn Risk)*
*   **Cash Collected:** €5,000 + (€300 × 6) = **€6,800**
*   **Revenue Recognized (straight-line over 18mo):** (€5,000 / 18 × 6) + (€300 × 6) = €1,666 + €1,800 = **€3,466**
*   **Distortion:** Cash looks strong (€6.8k), but earned revenue is half that.

*Scenario B: Customer stays 18 months (Base Case)*
*   **Cash Collected:** €5,000 + (€300 × 18) = **€10,400**
*   **Revenue Recognized:** (€5,000 / 18 × 18) + (€300 × 18) = €5,000 + €5,400 = **€10,400**
*   **Result:** Cash and Revenue align only at the exact average lifespan.

*Scenario C: Customer stays 36 months (Upside)*
*   **Cash Collected:** €5,000 + (€300 × 36) = **€15,800**
*   **Revenue Recognized:** €5,000 (fully amortized) + (€300 × 36) = **€15,800**

*Runway Impact (ASSUMED: Monthly burn remains constant)*
The €500k potential inflow (100 customers × €5k) adds **2.5 months** of runway to your current 12 months, extending you to ~14.5 months immediately upon conversion.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the investor narrative is **Churn Rate**. If churn increases because the upfront fee creates friction, the "cash boost" becomes a liability refund risk.
*   **Trigger:** If new customer acquisition drops by >20% or churn exceeds **4% monthly** within 90 days of the change, the fee is suppressing growth.
*   **Step:** If churn spikes in the first quarter, consider splitting the fee into two payments (onboarding and 90-day success milestone) to reduce friction while maintaining cash flow.

**Routing Note**
The determination of the exact amortization period (contract term vs. expected life) and the specific IFRS 15 compliance for your contracts is a binding accounting decision; confirm the amortization schedule and deferred revenue liability setup with your Irish CPA before issuing new invoices.
