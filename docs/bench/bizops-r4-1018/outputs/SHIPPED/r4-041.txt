**The Call**
Do not launch the trade-in program next month without first confirming your sales tax nexus status; the physical return of hardware from customers in specific jurisdictions creates a high risk of triggering immediate registration obligations in those counties, even without a warehouse.

**The Numbers**
Your exposure scales directly with the volume of returns.
*   **Formula:** Potential Nexus Events = Units Sold in Jurisdiction × Trade-in Participation Rate.
*   **User Figures:** 2,000 units planned; $150 credit per unit; $250k cash reserves.
*   **Assumptions (ASSUMED):**
    *   *Low:* 5% participation (100 returns scattered across many states) → Low individual county density, but broad monitoring required.
    *   *Base:* 15% participation (300 returns) → Likely clusters in specific CA counties or key expansion states.
    *   *High:* 30% participation (600 returns) → High probability of exceeding "slight presence" thresholds in multiple jurisdictions.
*   **Result:** If 50 units are returned to a single county where you have no other presence, that physical activity may constitute a "physical presence" nexus, obligating you to collect and remit sales tax for *all* sales into that jurisdiction, not just the trade-ins.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The deciding factor is whether a state's Department of Revenue interprets the temporary possession of returned inventory (even for immediate shipment to a recycler) as maintaining a "stock of goods" within their borders.
*   **Trigger:** The moment the first returned unit crosses a county line into a jurisdiction where you are not currently registered.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Many states treat any regular physical presence of company-owned inventory (including returns) as a nexus trigger, regardless of duration.
*   **Step this week:** If you plan to process more than 10 returns in any single non-registered jurisdiction, consider pausing the program in those specific zip codes until counsel reviews your logistics flow.

**Routing Note**
You must consult a **state and local tax (SALT) attorney** or a **CPA specializing in multi-state sales tax nexus**.
*   **Exact Question:** "Does the temporary physical presence of trade-in appliances returned by customers in [Specific State/County], prior to their shipment to our central recycler, constitute a 'physical presence' or 'stock of goods' that triggers sales tax nexus and registration requirements under current state statutes and economic nexus laws?"
*   **Time-Sensitive:** This must be resolved before the first return is processed, as retroactive penalties and interest can accrue from the date of the first triggering event.
