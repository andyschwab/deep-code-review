**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The critical input is the **destination state's specific quarantine list** for *Solanum lycopersicum* (tomato). California and Arizona have strict quarantines against pests like the Tomato Pinworm and various fruit flies; New York monitors for Gypsy Moth and other pathogens.
*   **Trigger:** If a destination state requires a *per-shipment* inspection rather than accepting a *nursery stock license* with periodic inspections, **stop** shipping live plants to that state immediately.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If the compliance cost exceeds 15% of the Average Order Value ($5.25), the direct-to-consumer model for live plants is unsustainable without raising prices significantly.
*   **Step this week:** If your current Florida nursery license does not explicitly list "interstate shipment" endorsements, consider pausing live plant marketing and selling only seeds while you apply for destination-state licenses.
