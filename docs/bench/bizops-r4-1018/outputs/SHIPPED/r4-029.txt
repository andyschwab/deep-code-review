**The Numbers:**
To visualize liquidity, build a **6-Month Rolling Cash Flow Waterfall**.
*Formula:* `Ending Cash = Starting Cash + (0.5 × New Orders × Price) + (0.5 × Delivered Orders × Price) − (Monthly Fixed Costs + Variable Cost per Suit × Active Production Volume)`

*Assumptions (Labelled ASSUMED):*
1.  **Variable Cost per Suit:** Estimated at $600 (50% of price) for fabric and tailor labor, paid steadily.
2.  **Fixed Costs:** Rent, utilities, and admin estimated at $10,000/month (ASSUMED).
3.  **Production Flow:** Orders placed in Month 1 deliver in Month 2.5 (averaged to Month 3 for cash flow).

*Scenario A: Steady State (20 suits/month)*
*   **Inflow (Upfront):** 20 × $1,200 × 50% = $12,000/mo.
*   **Inflow (Completion):** 20 × $1,200 × 50% = $12,000/mo (starting Month 3).
*   **Outflow:** $10,000 (Fixed) + (20 × $600) = $22,000/mo.
*   **Net Monthly Change:** ($12k + $12k) − $22k = +$2,000.
*   **Result:** Positive cash flow; no credit needed.

*Scenario B: Surge (40 suits/month starting Month 1)*
*   **Month 1 Inflow:** 40 × $600 (upfront) = $24,000. No completions yet.
*   **Month 1 Outflow:** $10,000 (Fixed) + (40 × $600) = $34,000.
*   **Month 1 Net:** -$10,000. (Cash drops from $60k to $50k).
*   **Months 2–4 Dynamics:** You continue paying $34k/month in costs. Upfront inflow stays at $24k. Completion inflows ($24k/mo) only begin trickling in during Month 3.
*   **The Crunch:** In Months 2 and 3, you are paying out $34k but only bringing in $24k (upfront) + partial completions. You will burn roughly $10k–$15k per month until the delivery wave catches up in Month 4.
*   **Projected Low Point:** By end of Month 3, cash could dip below $30k. If the surge sustains, you break even later; if it stops, you are left with high fixed costs and low volume.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The **Variable Cost Ratio** is the flipper. If your actual material/labor cost exceeds 55% of the sale price, the upfront deposit covers less than half the variable cost, accelerating the cash drain.
*Trigger Rule:* If `Cash Balance < (3 × Monthly Fixed Costs + Total Active Production Variable Costs)`, secure a line of credit immediately.
*Action:* If your projected cash balance in Month 3 falls below $40,000 under the surge scenario, consider securing a $25,000 revolving line of credit this week to bridge the gap between paying tailors and collecting final payments.
