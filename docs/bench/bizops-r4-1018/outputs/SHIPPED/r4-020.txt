    *   *Your Status (Assumed):* Ingredients = Human-Edible; Facility/Process = Pet-Food Only.

*   **The Flip Input:** Certification of your *manufacturing facility* for human-food production (not just ingredient sourcing).
*   **The Trigger:** If you cannot produce a current certificate or inspection report showing your *final packaging line* is approved for human food, the claim flips from "differentiator" to "liability."
*   **Decision Threshold:** If the cost of upgrading your facility to human-food standards exceeds the margin gain from the price premium, abandon the "human-grade" claim entirely.
*   **This Week's Step:** If your facility is not human-food certified, consider changing the label to "Made with USDA-Inspected Beef" or "Human-Quality Ingredients," which accurately reflects your input cost without implying full process certification.
