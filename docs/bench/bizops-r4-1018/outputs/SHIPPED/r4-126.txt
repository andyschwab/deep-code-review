**The Call:** Reject any shift where the premium exceeds 35% of the base rate unless it is a strategic loss-leader for a new hospital contract; immediately implement a "good-better-best" pricing tier that explicitly passes overtime costs to the client for requests under 24 hours.

**The Numbers:**
To offset one high-premium shift, we calculate the contribution margin loss and divide it by the standard margin per shift.
*Formula:* `Offset Shifts = (Premium Shift Margin Loss) / (Standard Shift Margin)`

*Assumptions (marked ASSUMED due to missing average shift value):*
1.  **Average Bill per Shift:** $1,466 (Derived from $220k / 150 nurses).
2.  **Standard Cost of Goods Sold (COGS):** 80% of bill (to achieve 20% target margin).
3.  **High-Premium Scenario:** Premiums consume 30% of the *base rate* (not total bill), effectively raising COGS.

*Scenario A: Standard Shift (20% Margin)*
*   Revenue: $1,466
*   Nurse Pay (ASSUMED 80%): $1,173
*   **Contribution Margin:** $293

*Scenario B: High-Premium Shift (30% premium on base pay)*
*   Revenue: $1,466
*   Nurse Pay: $1,173 + ($1,173 * 0.30 premium) = $1,525
*   **Contribution Margin:** -$59 (Loss)

*Result:* To offset one Scenario B shift (-$59), you need **0.2** of a Scenario A shift to break even on that specific transaction. However, to cover your **$12,000 fixed costs** while absorbing these losses:
*   If 20% of shifts are high-premium (loss leaders): You need **55 standard shifts** to break even overall.
*   If 50% of shifts are high-premium: You need **103 standard shifts** just to cover fixed costs, leaving zero profit.
*   **Current Runway:** $60,000 reserve / $12,000 burn = **5 months** if revenue stops; currently, your 8% margin ($17,600 gross profit - $12,000 fixed) yields only $5,600 net profit/month, extending runway indefinitely but stalling growth.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The **mix ratio** of standard vs. premium shifts flips the call.
*   *Trigger:* If premium shifts exceed **25% of total volume**, your net margin drops below 5%, threatening cash flow stability.
*   *Rule of Thumb:* If a specific hospital requests >3 urgent fills per week, switch them to a "Priority Tier" with a 1.5x multiplier.
*   *This Week:* Audit last month's 150 shifts. If >30 were last-minute fills, send a rate card update to those specific clients tomorrow stating: "Shifts requested <24hrs incur a 35% urgency fee."
