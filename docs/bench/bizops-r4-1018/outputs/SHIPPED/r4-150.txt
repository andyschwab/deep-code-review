**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call from "safe aggregator" to "unlicensed insurer" is the **presence of risk transfer or outcome guarantees** in your vendor or customer contracts.
*   **The Trigger:** If any agreement implies BenefitHub pays the cost of care if a vendor fails, or guarantees a specific health/legal outcome rather than just access to a network, you are likely transferring risk.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Insurance involves pooling risk and indemnifying against loss; service aggregation involves coordinating access. If you act as the payor of last resort, you are insuring.
*   **Step This Week:** If your standard contract contains language promising "outcomes" or assuming liability for vendor non-performance beyond simple breach of contract, consider engaging specialized insurance counsel to rewrite these clauses to strictly limit your role to "administrative facilitation" before your next renewal cycle.
