**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The decision flips only if the $20,000 internal effort can be specifically traced to the *development* of new security software modules required for the scan, rather than the *implementation* or *maintenance* of the control environment.
*   **Trigger:** If >50% of the CTO's time was spent coding new features vs. configuring policies or gathering evidence, consult your CPA to isolate those coding costs.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Audit fees for compliance certifications are almost universally expensed as incurred because they provide no future economic benefit beyond the current certification period.
*   **This Week's Step:** If your effective tax rate is positive and you need to lower this year's tax bill, elect to expense the full $65,000; if you are in a loss carryforward position where current deductions provide no immediate cash value, discuss capitalization with your CPA to smooth future earnings.
