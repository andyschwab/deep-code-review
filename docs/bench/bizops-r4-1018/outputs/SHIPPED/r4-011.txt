**Hidden Cost Factors**
1.  **Freight & Logistics:** Moving goods from a co-manufacturer often adds inbound freight costs not present in a local pilot kitchen.
2.  **Minimum Order Quantities (MOQs):** Co-manufacturers often require large raw material purchases, tying up cash flow.
3.  **Slotting Fees:** Scaling to new stores to hit volume targets often incurs retailer slotting fees.
4.  **Quality Control Rejects:** You may pay for units the co-man produces that fail your quality check but are still billed.
