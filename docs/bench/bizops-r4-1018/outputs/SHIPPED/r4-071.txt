**The Call**
Do not announce this change next month under your current contracts; reclassifying "seats" to "active users" for existing customers is a material alteration of the billing metric that exposes you to breach of contract claims and requires external counsel to redraft the core definitions and transition logic before rollout.

**The Numbers**
The financial impact of this shift relies on the utilization gap you identified.
*   **Formula:** `Revenue Lift = (Total Seats × Price) × (1 – Utilization Rate)`
*   **Your Figures:** 1,200 teams, $29/seat, 40% utilization (meaning 60% of paid seats are inactive).
*   **Assumption (Base):** If "active users" perfectly matches the 40% usage, your revenue from these specific enterprise accounts drops by ~60% unless you raise the per-unit price significantly.
*   **Assumption (High):** If you re-price to capture the full value of the enterprise (e.g., tiered pricing), the math changes, but the *legal* obligation to existing fixed-price contracts remains the primary blocker.
*   **Result:** Without a price increase, switching metrics on existing contracts reduces revenue from low-utilization accounts. With a price increase, you face immediate resistance and potential legal action for unilateral modification.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is the **contractual definition of "Fee" and "User"** in your current Master Service Agreement (MSA).
*   **Trigger:** If your current MSA defines "User" as "any individual authorized to access the service" (static) rather than "any individual who actually logs in" (dynamic), changing the billing logic without a contract amendment is a breach.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** In US commercial law, a change that increases cost or alters the fundamental basis of payment for an existing term is rarely enforceable without mutual consent.
*   **Step:** If your current contracts lock in a "per seat" definition for the contract term, consider pausing the metric change for existing customers until renewal, while applying it only to new logos.
