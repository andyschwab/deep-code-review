**The Call**
Do not market the "premium badge" as an investment or a guarantee of financial return; reframe it immediately as an administrative fee for access to a recruitment service, and remove the word "guaranteed" from all employment claims. While selling a certification with job-placement assistance is typically a service (Tier 2), explicitly promising "future income potential" or "guaranteed interviews" in exchange for an upfront fee creates a high risk of being scrutinized as an unregistered securities offering or engaging in deceptive trade practices (Tier 3).

**The Numbers**
Your current model relies on conversion volume, not investment returns.
*   **Base Revenue:** 2,000 students × $500 (Cert) = $1,000,000.
*   **Upside Scenario (50% uptake):** 1,000 students × $100 (Badge) = $100,000 additional revenue.
*   **Risk Exposure:** If classified as an unregistered security, penalties can exceed 100% of funds raised plus rescission rights (returning all money to buyers).
*   **Assumption (ASSUMED):** You have the operational capacity to deliver actual interviews to 1,000 candidates. If your network cannot fulfill "guaranteed" interviews, you face immediate liability for false advertising regardless of securities law.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that flips this from a safe service fee to a potential securities violation is **marketing language that frames the $100 fee as a purchase of future profit or income rights** rather than payment for a present service.
*   **The Trigger:** If your marketing materials suggest the buyer is "investing" in their career with an expectation of profit derived from the efforts of others (the Guild's network), you trigger the *Howey Test* analysis.
*   **Stop/Pivot Rule:** If you cannot contractually guarantee an interview within 30 days of purchase, you must stop using the word "guaranteed."
*   **This Week's Step:** If your current copy mentions "income potential" or "investment in your future," consider rewriting it to describe "access to a recruiter database" and "application processing services" only.
