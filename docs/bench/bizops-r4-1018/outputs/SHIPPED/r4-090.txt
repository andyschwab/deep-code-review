**The Call:** Yes, restructure immediately to unbundle storage and egress; your current model forces light users to subsidize heavy users, creating a hidden loss on your most active segment and capping your margin expansion.

**The Numbers:**
Your current unit economics show a 33% gross margin ($6,000 profit on $18,000 revenue), but this aggregate hides the distortion.
*   **Formula:** `Segment Profit = Segment Revenue - (Segment Storage Cost + Segment Egress Cost)`
*   **Current State (Aggregate):** Revenue $18k - Infra $12k = $6k Net.
*   **The Distortion (Illustrative Assumption):** Assuming your $12k bill is split 40% storage ($4.8k) and 60% egress ($7.2k) based on typical registry profiles:
    *   **Heavy Users (10% of base, 70% of egress):** They likely pay only ~10-15% of total revenue (if their storage volume is proportional to user count) but consume 70% of the egress cost (~$5,040). If their revenue contribution is under $5,040 plus their storage share, you are losing money on every byte they pull.
    *   **Light Users (90% of base):** They pay ~85-90% of revenue but consume only 30% of egress (~$2,160). Their effective margin is artificially inflated by covering the heavy users' bandwidth.
*   **Result:** Under the current bundled or loosely tiered model, Heavy Users have a negative or near-zero contribution margin, while Light Users are overcharged relative to their cost-to-serve.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is the **Egress-to-Revenue Ratio per Segment**.
*   **Trigger:** If Heavy Users generate >40% of your total revenue, the current model might sustain; if they generate <30% (likely, given they are only 10% of users), the subsidy is critical.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** In infrastructure, price must track the variable cost driver. If egress is 60% of your cost but not 60% of your price mix, the model is broken.
*   **Actionable Step:** This week, segment your last month's logs by user ID. Calculate `(User Egress GB * $0.04 estimated cost/GB)` vs. `(User Total Bill)`. If the top 10% of users show a negative delta, draft a new "Pro/Enterprise" tier that charges $0.08/GB for egress separately from storage, grandfathering existing light users but applying the new rate to new heavy sign-ups or overage.
