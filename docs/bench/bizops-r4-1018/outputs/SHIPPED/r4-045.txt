**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that changes this from a manageable startup cost to a business-ending event is the **timing of the FDA notification**.
*   **The Rule:** For structure-function claims (e.g., "supports muscle recovery"), you must notify the FDA **no later than 30 days after** you first market the product. You do *not* need pre-approval, but you must have the submission proof before selling.
*   **The Trap:** If your claim is deemed a "disease claim" (e.g., implying treatment of a deficiency or disease), the product is misbranded from day one.
*   **The Trigger:** If you print labels without the exact statutory disclaimer or submit the notification late, you trigger a "misbranded" status.
