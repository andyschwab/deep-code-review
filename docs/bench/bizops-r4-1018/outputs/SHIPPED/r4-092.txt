**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call from "safe software provider" to "unlicensed practice of law/accounting" is the **specificity and prescriptiveness of the language** in your reports.
*   **Trigger Value:** If your report uses imperative verbs like "must," "shall," or "required to" regarding specific legal statutes, or if it provides a definitive "Pass/Fail" legal conclusion rather than a "Risk Score" or "Observation," you cross the line.
*   **Stop/Pivot Trigger:** If any customer support ticket or sales call includes a phrase like "Can your tool tell us exactly how to fix this to avoid a fine?", you must pivot immediately to requiring human professional review for that client.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Software identifies patterns; professionals interpret laws. If your output interprets the law, you are practicing.
*   **This Week's Step:** If your current report templates contain definitive legal conclusions, consider replacing them with probabilistic risk indicators (e.g., "High Probability of Non-Compliance with HIPAA Section X") and adding a bold header: "Data for Professional Review Only."
