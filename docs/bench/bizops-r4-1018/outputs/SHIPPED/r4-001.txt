**The Call:** Your current model loses money on 85% of your orders; you are subsidizing single-bag shipments by $2.50 each, turning a healthy product margin into an overall monthly loss. You must immediately decouple shipping fees from flat rates or enforce a two-bag minimum to stop the bleeding.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that threatens sustainability is a drop in average order value (AOV) or an increase in carrier rates.
*   **Trigger:** If carrier rates rise by **$2.51** (making the subsidy $5.01) OR if your product margin drops below **$2.50** (e.g., bean costs spike), single-bag orders become net-negative.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Never subsidize shipping by more than 15% of the product margin unless LTV (repeat purchase rate) is proven high. Your current subsidy is ~19% of your margin ($2.50/$13.00).
*   **Actionable Step:** If your carrier rates increase next month, consider raising the flat shipping rate to $9 immediately or implementing a "Free Shipping on 2+ Bags" threshold to force higher AOV.
