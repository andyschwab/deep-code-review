**The Call:** Proceed with the deployment but treat the four-week onsite stint as a high-risk trigger for a "service permanent establishment" (PE) in Germany; do not finalize the Statement of Work (SOW) until a German tax attorney confirms whether your specific configuration activities count as "technical services" under the UK-Germany tax treaty.

**The Numbers & Rules:**
Germany generally asserts corporate tax nexus (Permanent Establishment) on foreign firms if they maintain a fixed place of business or if personnel provide services onsite for more than **183 days** within a 12-month period. However, the UK-Germany Double Taxation Treaty (and OECD Model Article 5) contains a specific "service PE" clause that can lower this threshold significantly.

*   **Standard Rule:** Physical presence < 183 days usually avoids a "fixed place" PE.
*   **The Trap (Service PE):** If your employees are performing "technical services" or "consulting" rather than mere "preparatory or auxiliary" activities, a PE can be created **immediately** or after a much shorter duration (often **3 to 6 months**, depending on the specific treaty interpretation and activity type), regardless of having a formal office.
*   **Your Figures:**
    *   Contract Value: €250,000.
    *   Exposure: If a PE is triggered, Germany may tax the profit attributable to those services.
    *   Duration: 4 weeks (20 working days).
    *   **Assumption (UNVERIFIED):** That "configuring a custom platform" constitutes core revenue-generating technical service rather than auxiliary setup. If this assumption holds, the 183-day safe harbor may not apply, and the risk exists from Day 1.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is the **legal characterization of the work**. If the work is deemed "preparatory/auxiliary" (e.g., minor installation), you are likely safe. If it is deemed "technical consulting/customization" (core value delivery), you likely have a PE.
*   **Trigger:** Signing the SOW without a clause explicitly defining the work scope to align with "auxiliary" exemptions or without a professional ruling.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Any onsite activity where your staff modifies the core product logic for the client is often viewed as a service PE.
*   **Step this week:** If the SOW describes the work as "custom configuration and logic implementation," consider pausing signature to engage counsel for a treaty analysis.
