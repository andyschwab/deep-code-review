**The Call**
Raise prices to €34 immediately, but only if you can validate that churn remains below 4.5% within the first 60 days; the immediate cash flow extension outweighs the theoretical LTV drop given your six-month fundraising horizon and current runway risk.

**The Numbers**
*Current State:*
- Monthly Revenue: 2,500 subs × €29 = €72,500
- Monthly Burn: €60,000
- Net Cash Flow: +€12,500
- Runway: €400,000 / €60,000 = **6.7 months** (Too tight for a 6-month Series A process).

*Proposed State (Price €34, Churn +10% relative increase to 3.3%):*
- New Revenue (Month 1): 2,500 × €34 = €85,000
- New Net Cash Flow: €85,000 - €60,000 = +€25,000
- New Runway (assuming stable sub count initially): €400,000 / €60,000 (burn unchanged) → Cash buffer increases by €12,500/month.
- **Runway Extension:** The extra €12,500/month adds ~8 months of runway *if* subscriber count holds. Even with moderate churn, the cash injection is immediate.

*LTV:CAC Sensitivity (Assumed Gross Margin 60%, Discount Rate 10%):*
- **Base Case (€29, 3% churn):** LTV ≈ €29 × 0.6 / (0.03 + 0.10) = €134. LTV:CAC = 134/45 = **2.98x**.
- **Risk Case (€34, 3.3% churn):** LTV ≈ €34 × 0.6 / (0.033 + 0.10) = €153. LTV:CAC = 153/45 = **3.4x**.
- **Break-even Churn Trigger:** Your LTV:CAC actually *improves* unless churn spikes above **5.8%** monthly. At 5.8% churn, LTV drops to €134, matching your current ratio.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The decision flips if the price hike causes churn to exceed **5.8%** monthly (a ~93% relative increase in churn, not 10%).
- **Trigger:** If Month 1 churn post-hike exceeds 4.5% (absolute), revert pricing for new customers immediately.
- **Rule of Thumb:** Investors prefer a 3.0x+ LTV:CAC with strong cash efficiency over a 2.9x ratio with a 6-month runway cliff.
- **Step this week:** If you have email/SMS segmentation, consider A/B testing the €34 price on 10% of new sign-ups only before rolling out to existing users, or announce the increase for "next renewal cycle" to gauge cancellation intent without immediate revenue shock.

**Investor Scrutiny Metrics**
Series A investors in subscription commerce will scrutinize:
1.  **Net Revenue Retention (NRR):** Did the price increase offset the churn? (Target >100%).
2.  **Payback Period:** Did the higher ARPU shorten the time to recover CAC? (Current: €45 / (€29*0.6) ≈ 2.6 months; New: €45 / (€34*0.6) ≈ 2.2 months).
3.  **Churn Elasticity:** Evidence that you understand your price sensitivity curve.
