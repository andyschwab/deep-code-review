**The Call**
Yes, bundling distinct performance obligations (software vs. services) in a single contract requires you to allocate the transaction price based on standalone selling prices (SSP) under IFRS 15 (the standard applicable in Ireland). You do not need to halt sales, but you must engage your auditor or a qualified accountant immediately to document your SSP methodology and allocation logic before closing more deals or recognizing revenue for the current batch.

**The Numbers**
Under IFRS 15, you must identify distinct goods/services and allocate the total contract value proportionally to their SSPs.
*   **Total Contract Value:** €15,000 (€10k Software + €5k Services).
*   **Standalone Selling Prices (SSP):**
    *   Software SSP: €10,000 (Assumed: This is your observable standalone price).
    *   Services SSP: €5,000 (Assumed: This is your observable standalone price).
*   **Allocation Logic:** Since the bundle price (€15,000) equals the sum of the SSPs (€10,000 + €5,000), no discount allocation is required. The revenue splits exactly as priced.
    *   Software Revenue to defer: €10,000 per contract.
    *   Services Revenue to recognize on completion: €5,000 per contract.
*   **Quarterly Impact (25 deals):**
    *   Total Deferred Software Revenue: €250,000 (recognized ratably over 12 months).
    *   Total Services Revenue: €125,000 (recognized upon workshop/template completion).

*Assumption Check:* If you ever sell the software alone for €9,000 (a discount) but keep the bundle at €15,000, your SSP for software becomes €9,000. You would then allocate the €15,000 bundle proportionally (9/14 to software, 5/14 to services), changing the recognition amounts.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The complexity flips from "simple split" to "complex allocation" the moment you offer a discount on the bundle that does not apply to the individual components, or if you cannot establish an observable SSP for one component.
*   **Trigger:** If you sell a bundle for €13,500 (10% off) while selling components individually at €10k and €5k, you must allocate the €1,500 discount proportionally unless you have evidence the discount belongs entirely to one component.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If the bundle price ≠ sum of standalone prices, you must mathematically reallocate.
*   **This Week's Step:** If you have not formally documented your SSP evidence (e.g., historical standalone sales data, margin-plus estimates, or competitor benchmarks) for both the API and the workshops, consider pausing revenue recognition on the services portion until your accountant validates your estimation method.

**Routing Note**
IFRS 15 compliance is a binding accounting determination. Bring this specific question to your external auditor or Irish chartered accountant: "Does our current evidence support our asserted Standalone Selling Prices for the professional services component, and is our proposed allocation method for bundled discounts defensible for our year-end audit?" Do not finalize your year-end accounts without this sign-off.
