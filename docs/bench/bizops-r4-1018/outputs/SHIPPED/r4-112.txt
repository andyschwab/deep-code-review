**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that changes your legal exposure is the **method of calculation**. If the fee is a percentage of the clinical reimbursement or varies based on the number of patients referred, it is likely an illegal inducement. If the fee is a fixed amount determined by a separate valuation of administrative services (FMV) and does not fluctuate with referral volume, it may qualify for the "Personal Services and Management Contracts" safe harbor.
*   **Trigger:** If your legal counsel cannot document a third-party FMV opinion supporting your fee within 30 days, cease the percentage-based model immediately.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Fees tied to *volume* or *value* of referrals are prohibited; fees tied to *fixed* administrative costs are generally permissible.
