**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes this call is **Churn/Cancellation Liability**. If customers cancel early and you must refund pro-rated amounts, your cash evaporates.
*   **Trigger:** If your historical annual churn exceeds **15%**, the risk of refund liabilities outweighs the runway gain.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Only sell multi-year upfront if you are confident in >85% retention.
*   **Step this week:** If churn is <15%, consider drafting a "no-refund" or "credit-only" cancellation clause for these specific deals to protect the cash.
