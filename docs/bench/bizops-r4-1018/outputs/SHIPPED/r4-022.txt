**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The trigger is the **laboratory analysis of the finished goods**. If a single bottle from a batch tests >0.5% ABV, that specific batch loses its non-alcoholic status. The "flip" occurs if you continue to label and file as non-alcoholic despite known variance.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If your process cannot guarantee <0.5% at the point of sale (accounting for fermentation in the bottle), you are operating outside the non-alcoholic definition.
