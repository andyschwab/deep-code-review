**The Call**
Reject the assumption that a four-week campaign triggers immediate corporate income tax filing obligations in Georgia, Alabama, and Tennessee; while you likely create "nexus" (the legal presence required to be taxed), most states provide statutory exemptions for businesses present solely for short-term projects under specific day or revenue thresholds. You must register as a foreign entity to operate legally, but you can likely file for an exemption from income tax filing for this specific period if your activity stays under the safe-harbor limits.

**The Numbers & Rules**
The determination relies on "Physical Presence Nexus" rules versus "Temporary Presence Exemptions."
*   **Formula:** Nexus Exists = (Days Present > State Threshold) OR (Revenue > State Threshold).
*   **Your Inputs:** 4 weeks (~20–28 business days), $250,000 total revenue (approx. $83k/state if split evenly).
*   **Assumptions (ASSUMED):** Revenue is split equally; "days" count only business days where staff/equipment are on-site.
*   **State Specifics (General Rule):**
    *   **Georgia:** Generally requires filing if nexus exists, but offers exemptions for temporary presence if activities are limited and duration is short (often interpreted as < 30 days for specific construction/service projects, though strict interpretation varies).
    *   **Alabama:** Has a "temporary presence" exemption for businesses present for less than 30 days in a 12-month period for specific activities.
    *   **Tennessee:** Similar temporary exemptions exist for short-term projects, often aligned with a 30-day window.
*   **Result:** Under the base assumption of ~20 days and <$100k revenue per state, you likely fall within temporary exemptions for *income tax*, but you still trigger the requirement to *register* as a foreign entity to legally do business.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The one input that changes the call is **duration**. If your setup, teardown, and actual clinic days exceed **30 calendar days** in any single state, the temporary exemption likely vanishes, requiring full corporate income tax registration and filing for that state immediately.
*   **Trigger Date:** If your campaign in any single state extends beyond **Day 30** of physical presence in 2024, stop and file.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Treat 30 days as the hard cliff for temporary exemptions in the Southeast.
*   **Step this week:** If your schedule shows < 25 days per state, consider drafting a "Notice of No Taxable Nexus" or temporary exemption claim for each state's Department of Revenue while proceeding with foreign qualification registration.

**Routing Note**
This is a jurisdiction-specific determination of "nexus" and "foreign qualification." You must engage a local CPA or tax attorney in GA, AL, and TN to confirm the exact day-count methodology (calendar vs. business days) and to file the foreign registration documents; failing to register as a foreign entity can result in penalties even if no tax is owed.
