Instead, adopt a **Modular Good-Better-Best pricing structure** where the initial "Core Security" license is priced at £9,500 (capturing immediate cash flow), with optional "Advanced Fintech Compliance" modules billed as separate, value-add line items or triggered by usage milestones after 90 days.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The deal flips if the client's procurement policy defines "total contract value" across all related vendors or consecutive contracts within a fiscal year.
*   **Trigger Value:** If the client flags "related party" or "split POs" during the Base Tier review.
*   **Stop/Pivot Rule:** If legal/compliance asks for a "total scope of work" letter, pivot immediately to a single £20k contract and negotiate *payment terms* (e.g., 50% upfront, 50% net-30) rather than price splitting.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Never split contracts for the *same* deliverable; split only for *distinct* functional modules with independent utility.
*   **This Week's Step:** If your sales team hears "we need to bundle this," consider offering a 90-day "Pilot Program" at £9,500 that automatically converts to the full enterprise suite with a pre-agreed change order, rather than two static contracts.
