**The Numbers:**
The trigger for "economic nexus" is typically defined by a state-specific threshold of gross revenue or transaction count over a 12-month rolling period.
*Formula:* If (12-Month Gross Sales > State Revenue Threshold) OR (12-Month Transaction Count > State Count Threshold) → Filing Obligation Exists.

*Your Data (Annualized from last quarter):*
*   **California:** 60 transactions/quarter → ~240/year. Revenue: 60 × $2,800 = $168,000/quarter → ~$672,000/year.
    *   *CA Threshold:* $500,000 gross revenue.
    *   *Result:* **EXCEEDED** ($672k > $500k).
*   **New York:** 45 transactions/quarter → ~180/year. Revenue: 45 × $2,800 = $126,000/quarter → ~$504,000/year.
    *   *NY Threshold:* $500,000 gross revenue AND 100 transactions.
    *   *Result:* **EXCEEDED** ($504k > $500k; 180 > 100).
*   **Florida:** 30 transactions/quarter → ~120/year. Revenue: 30 × $2,800 = $84,000/quarter → ~$336,000/year.
    *   *FL Threshold:* $100,000 gross revenue.
    *   *Result:* **EXCEEDED** ($336k > $100k).

*Assumption:* Sales velocity remains constant; no large returns deducted from gross sales.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes this call is a sharp decline in sales volume over the next two quarters. However, because nexus is usually calculated on a rolling 12-month basis, you cannot "wait and see."
*Trigger:* You crossed the threshold in the most recent quarter. The obligation generally begins the month after the threshold is exceeded.
