**The Call:** Reject the raw "50 million users" figure as your TAM; instead, define your TAM as the total US population interested in mindfulness, then apply a "unique household" filter and a "willingness-to-pay" conversion rate to derive a defensible Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM). Investors care about the revenue you can capture, not the inflated count of app installs.

**The Numbers:**
To adjust for double-counting and saturation, use this funnel logic:
1.  **Raw Industry Figure:** 50,000,000 (US meditation app users).
2.  **Overlap Adjustment Factor:** Your data shows 15% of users have ≥1 competitor. Industry reports often count *installs*, not *people*. If the average user has 1.4 apps (a common industry heuristic for this sector), the unique user count is roughly $50M / 1.4$.
    *   *Assumption (ASSUMED):* Average apps per user is 1.4 (based on your 15% multi-app data point suggesting moderate overlap, though likely higher in the general population).
    *   *Estimated Unique Users:* ~35.7 million.
3.  **Willingness-to-Pay (WTP) Filter:** Not all free users convert.
    *   *Assumption (ASSUMED):* Industry average conversion from free to paid is 3–5%.
    *   *Low Case (3%):* 1.07 million paid users.
    *   *Base Case (5%):* 1.78 million paid users.
