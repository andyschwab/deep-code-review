**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is the **specific phrasing of the marketing promise**.
*   **Trigger:** If your marketing materials, terms of service, or sales scripts use the words "financial advice," "investment recommendation," or "fiduciary," you trigger the **Investment Advisers Act of 1940** (US) and **FSMA/FCA regulations** (UK).
*   **Threshold Rule of Thumb:** Providing *general* data and algorithms is typically exempt; providing *specific* recommendations tailored to an entity's financial portfolio with an expectation of compensation for that advice constitutes registration-worthy activity.
