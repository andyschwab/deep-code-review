**The Call**
Adopt a hybrid pricing model immediately: a base fee covering baseline usage plus a defined overage rate for traffic spikes, rather than building a buffer into flat fees or relying solely on flat rates. This structure directly aligns your non-linear cost curve with revenue, preventing the margin erosion you fear when signing new large customers.

**The Numbers**
Your current baseline gross margin is 70% ($2,000 Revenue - $600 Cost). However, under peak conditions, your margin collapses to 25% ($2,000 Revenue - $1,500 Cost).
*Formula:* Gross Margin % = (Revenue - Variable Cost) / Revenue.
*Assumption (ASSUMED):* You define "peak" as the 95th percentile of customer traffic behavior.
*Scenario Analysis:*
1.  **Status Quo (Flat Fee):** If you sign 10 new large customers who all hit peak usage simultaneously, your blended margin drops significantly. With 50 customers at baseline and 10 at peak, total revenue is $120k, but costs rise to $45k (50 * $600) + $15k (10 * $1,500) = $60k. Blended margin falls to 50%. If more existing customers spike, you approach your feared 40% threshold.
2.  **Tiered/Overage Model:** Base fee covers up to 1.5x baseline traffic. Overage charged at $0.0X per GB/unit above that.
    *   *Result:* Revenue scales with the cost spike. If cost triples, revenue increases proportionally, maintaining the ~70% margin target regardless of volume spikes.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that changes this recommendation is your **churn sensitivity to price complexity**. If enterprise clients refuse any variable component, you must revert to flat fees but strictly cap liability in the contract (e.g., "Service credits only, no unlimited scaling").
*Trigger:* Monitor your "Cost-to-Revenue Ratio" weekly. If it exceeds 0.35 (35% cost) for two consecutive months due to spike handling, you must activate the overage clause for all new contracts immediately.
*Rule of Thumb:* Never sign a contract where the theoretical maximum cost exceeds 40% of the contracted revenue without a corresponding variable revenue mechanism.
*Action Step:* If your next sales cycle includes a customer with >2x expected growth, consider presenting a "Base + Overage" quote alongside a "Premium Flat" quote priced at the peak cost level to anchor value.

**Routing Note**
While this models the economics, the specific legal language required to enforce overage charges, define "traffic spikes," and limit liability for infrastructure scaling must be drafted by legal counsel to ensure enforceability in your customer contracts.
