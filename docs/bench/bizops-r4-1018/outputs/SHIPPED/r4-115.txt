**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The **Average Revenue Per Consultation (ARPC)** is the missing variable that dictates the volume target.
*   **Trigger:** If your actual ARPC is below $500, the required volume increase (>138 consults/mo) may exceed market demand capacity for three new providers immediately.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If new hire utilization < 60% by Month 3, pause further hiring.
*   **Step:** Before signing contracts, calculate your exact ARPC from last month's ledger. If ARPC < $600, consider a phased hire (1 provider now, 2 later) to match organic demand growth.
