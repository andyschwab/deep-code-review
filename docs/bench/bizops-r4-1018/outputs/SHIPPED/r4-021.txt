**The Numbers**
Economic nexus is triggered by exceeding a state's specific revenue or transaction count in direct sales. Marketplace facilitator laws generally shift the collection duty to the platform (Etsy), meaning those sales often do not count toward *your* nexus threshold for registration, though rules vary by state.

*Assumptions:*
1.  **Etsy Sales:** Treated as facilitated sales where Etsy collects/remits tax (standard practice for CA/TX).
2.  **Shopify Sales:** Treated as direct sales counting toward your nexus.
3.  **Projection:** Your $40k projected out-of-state sales are split proportionally to current direct sales (approx. $20k CA, $14k TX) or evenly; either way, they remain below thresholds.

*Threshold Comparison (Direct Sales Only):*
*   **California:** Threshold is $500,000 in gross receipts.
    *   Your Direct Sales: ~$12,000 (Current) → ~$20,000 (Projected).

*   **Texas:** Threshold is $500,000 in gross revenue. (Note: Texas repealed its 200-transaction count threshold in 2019; it is now revenue-only).

    *   Status: **Below Threshold.**

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes this call is a massive spike in **direct Shopify revenue** exceeding $500,000 in a 12-month period in either state.
*   **Trigger:** If your rolling 12-month direct sales in CA or TX cross $500,000.
