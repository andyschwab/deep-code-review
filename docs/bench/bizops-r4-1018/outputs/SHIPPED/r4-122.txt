The single input that changes the risk profile is **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Verification**.
*   **Trigger:** If a Registered Dietitian (RD) or Licensed Nutritionist reviews and signs off on every algorithm-generated plan before it reaches the user, the service shifts from "automated practice" to "clinical support tool."
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If the output is actionable medical/dietary advice derived from private health data without human review, you are likely practicing without a license.
