**The Call:** Do not bind internal cost savings to customer performance credits; this structure creates unmanageable liability and conflates your operational margins with service delivery. Instead, guarantee specific performance metrics (e.g., scan completion time) with credits defined as a percentage of the subscription fee, unrelated to your internal cost basis.

**The Numbers:**
Your proposed liability is uncapped and volatile because it ties a customer credit to an internal variable (your cloud bill) that the customer cannot audit and you cannot fully control.
*   **Current Model:** Price $400/mo. You claim a "savings" pass-through.
*   **The Risk Formula:** `Liability = (Number of Breaches) × (Promised Savings Credit)`.
*   **Assumption (Low):** 1 breach/month, credit = $50. Monthly exposure: $50.
*   **Assumption (Base):** 5 breaches/month (e.g., during a global supply chain attack or cloud outage), credit = $100 (aggressive marketing claim). Monthly exposure: $500. This exceeds the revenue from a single client if the credit is per incident.
*   **Assumption (High):** A systemic infrastructure spike causes widespread delays. If you promised to pass through "35% savings" as a credit, and your costs spike 50%, your margin turns negative while you simultaneously owe credits.
*   **Result:** Your liability becomes a function of your own inefficiency plus market volatility, rather than a fixed cost of doing business.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The trigger is the decoupling of *internal cost* from *external service level*.
*   **Flip Input:** Changing the credit definition from "equal to internal savings" to "10% of monthly subscription fee per hour of delay."
*   **Threshold Rule of Thumb:** SLA credits should never exceed 20–30% of the monthly recurring revenue (MRR) for that client in a given month, regardless of your internal costs.
*   **Actionable Step:** If your marketing team insists on highlighting the 35% efficiency gain, state it as a case study or "typical customer benefit" in marketing copy, but strictly exclude it from the binding SLA text. Draft the SLA this week to cap total credits at 50% of one month's fee.
