**The Call:** Your current $250 per-patient fee structure creates a high-risk exposure to federal Anti-Kickback Statute (AKS) violations and potential Medicare hospice cap complications; you must immediately cease charging per-referral fees and restructure to a flat-rate SaaS model or fair-market-value (FMV) service agreement before scaling.

**The Numbers & Risk Mechanics:**
The issue is not a mathematical breach of the Medicare hospice cap itself (which limits the total reimbursement a *provider* receives from Medicare per beneficiary, roughly $30,000–$35,000 annually depending on the year and adjustments), but rather the legal characterization of your fee under fraud and abuse laws.
*   **Current Model:** $250 × 300 patients = $75,000 revenue.
*   **The Violation Vector:** Under the AKS, offering or paying anything of value to induce referrals for services payable by a federal healthcare program (Medicare) is a felony. A "per-patient" fee directly correlates your compensation to the volume of referrals, creating a presumption of inducement.
*   **The Cap Interaction:** While your fee doesn't mathematically break the provider's cap, if a provider argues that your fee forced them to reduce care quality to stay under the cap, or if the fee is viewed as a portion of the Medicare payment being kicked back to you, it triggers scrutiny. The Office of Inspector General (OIG) views per-unit referral fees in this context as suspect regardless of the cap math.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
*   **The Flip:** Changing the pricing basis from "per patient enrolled" to "per month platform access" or "per hour of coordination service" at Fair Market Value (FMV).
*   **The Trigger:** The moment you sign a new provider contract or renew an existing one with the current per-patient language.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If your revenue goes up solely because referral volume goes up, without a corresponding increase in services rendered by your platform, the arrangement is likely an illegal kickback.
*   **Immediate Step:** If you have active contracts, consider pausing billing under the current terms and engaging counsel to draft an amendment converting the fee to a flat monthly subscription or an hourly rate for specific administrative tasks, documented by a written services agreement.

This is a Tier 3 regulatory determination. You must engage a healthcare attorney specializing in federal fraud and abuse (AKS/Stark Law) immediately. Do not rely on general business counsel. The specific question to bring is: "How do we restructure our existing $250 per-referral agreements into a compliant FMV SaaS or service arrangement that satisfies the OIG's safe harbors for personal services and management contracts?" Time is sensitive as ongoing violations can lead to exclusion from Medicare and criminal liability.
