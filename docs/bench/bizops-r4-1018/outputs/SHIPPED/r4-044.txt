**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes this call is the **legal determination of "consumer product" status** for an unfinished item sold with the intent of being made into a skateboard.
*   **Trigger:** If the Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) or a court determines that an unfinished blank is a "component part" intended for a regulated end-use, you remain liable for safety standards (e.g., lead content, phthalates, structural integrity).
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If the marketing, packaging, or product design implies the item is for skateboarding, regulators often treat it as the finished good regardless of the final paint step.
*   **Step this week:** If you can segregate sales channels so blanks are sold strictly as "craft wood" with no skate branding, consider a pilot run of 50 units; otherwise, halt the raw blank strategy pending legal review.
