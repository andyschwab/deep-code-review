**The Call**
Your recurring revenue structure justifies targeting a 6x–8x revenue multiple, significantly higher than the 1x–2x typical of traditional toy hardware companies, provided you can prove churn is low and cohort margins are expanding. Do not lead with the 4% net margin; lead with the 8% month-over-month growth and the predictability of the £144k MRR.

**The Numbers**
*Current Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR):* £144,000/month × 12 = **£1.728M**.
*Valuation Scenarios (Revenue Multiple Method):*
*   **Traditional Hardware Multiple (1.5x):** £1.728M × 1.5 = **£2.59M** (Base case if sold as a product company).

*Unit Economics Check (ASSUMED inputs marked):*
*   **LTV (Lifetime Value):** Formula = (ARPA × Gross Margin %) / Churn Rate.
    *   ARPA: £45.
    *   Gross Margin: **ASSUMED 50%** (Hardware heavy; verify actual BOM + shipping).
    *   Churn: **ASSUMED 5% monthly** (Industry avg for boxes; verify your actuals).
    *   *Calculation:* (£45 × 0.50) / 0.05 = **£450 LTV**.
*   **LTV:CAC Ratio:** £450 LTV / £60 CAC = **7.5x**.
    *   *Verdict:* A 7.5x ratio is exceptional (standard is 3:1). This is your strongest lever for the 8x multiple.
*   **Payback Period:** CAC / (ARPA × Gross Margin %) = £60 / (£45 × 0.50) = **2.6 months**.
    *   *Verdict:* Extremely fast capital recycling, supporting aggressive growth arguments.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that collapses your multiple from 8x to 2x is **Churn**. If your monthly churn exceeds 8-10%, acquirers will reclassify you as a "leaky bucket" hardware seller rather than a stable subscription asset.
*   **Trigger:** If your next cohort analysis shows >8% monthly churn or declining gross margins due to shipping/hardware costs, pivot your narrative from "high-growth platform" to "efficient direct-to-consumer brand" and expect a 3x–4x multiple.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Subscription multiples compress rapidly when growth is funded by acquisition rather than retention.
*   **This Week's Step:** If your churn is <5%, prepare a cohort retention chart showing stability over 6+ months; if X (churn) is unverified, consider Y (running a cancellation survey immediately) before entering negotiations.
