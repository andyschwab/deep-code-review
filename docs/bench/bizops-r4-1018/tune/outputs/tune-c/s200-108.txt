Runway is calculated as: `Cash Balance / Monthly Burn Rate`.
Monthly Burn is derived from: `Fixed Operating Costs - Monthly Gross Profit`.

Actually, a simpler approach for Burn: `Burn = OpEx - Gross Profit`.
Let's derive OpEx first.
Current Gross Profit = $159,200 × 0.40 = $63,680.
Current Burn = $60,000.
Therefore, OpEx = Burn + Gross Profit? No. Burn is cash outflow exceeding inflow.
Correct Formula: `Net Cash Flow = Gross Profit - OpEx`.
`Burn = -(Net Cash Flow)`. So, `$60,000 = OpEx - $63,680`.
