**The Call:** Restrict free usage immediately; your current model is a leaky bucket that will drain your $900,000 reserve in approximately 10 months before reaching profitability. A viral coefficient of 1.4 is strong for top-line growth but fatal here because it scales your losses faster than your revenue.

**The Numbers:**
*   **Current Burn:** $60,000/month (given).
*   **Free Tier Cost:** 50,000 users × $0.15 = $7,500/month.
*   **Paid Tier Revenue:** $40,000/month (given).
*   **Implied Paid ARPU:** $40,000 / 2,000 = $20/user/month.
*   **Contribution Margin (Paid):** Assuming variable costs for paid users are similar to free ($0.15), margin is ~$19.85/user.
*   **Net Monthly Cash Flow:** $40,000 (Revenue) - $60,000 (Total Burn) = -$20,000/month.
*   **Runway:** $900,000 / $20,000 = 45 months *if growth stops*.
*   **Growth Impact (The Leak):** With a viral coefficient of 1.4, your user base grows exponentially. If 96% of new users are non-paying (current ratio: 50k free vs 2k paid), every 1,000 new users adds ~$150 in monthly costs but negligible revenue.
    *   *Scenario A (Status Quo):* If the user base grows just 10% next month (conservative for k=1.4), you add 5,200 free users. Cost increases by $780. Revenue stays flat unless conversion improves. Burn rises to $20,780 negative.
    *   *Scenario B (Restrict Free):* Cap free tier or introduce friction. If you convert just 4% of the 50,000 free users to paid ($20/mo) and reduce the rest to zero cost:
        *   New Revenue: 2,000 users + 2,000 converted = 4,000 × $20 = $80,000.
        *   New Cost: 4,000 users × $0.15 = $600 (variable) + fixed costs.
        *   Result: Potential profitability.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is the **Free-to-Paid Conversion Rate**. Currently, it is 4% (2,000 paid / 52,000 total).
*   **Trigger Threshold:** You need a conversion rate of roughly **15-20%** to sustain the current viral growth without external funding, assuming costs remain linear.
*   **The Math:** To cover the $7,500 free-tier cost solely through new conversions from that pool, you need 375 new paying users ($7,500 / $20 ARPU). That requires converting 7.5% of the free base just to break even on *their own support costs*, ignoring your $52,500 other burn.
*   **Actionable Step:** If your conversion rate does not jump from 4% to >8% within 30 days after introducing a "paywall" or usage cap on the free tier, consider pivoting to a purely paid trial model or shutting down the free tier entirely.
