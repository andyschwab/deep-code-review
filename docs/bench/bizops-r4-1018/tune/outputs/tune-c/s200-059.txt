**The Call:** Do not match the competitor's flat rate; immediately pilot a three-tier "Good-Better-Best" model with volume discounts for your top 10 users while maintaining the current per-minute rate for smaller accounts. A blanket 15% price cut destroys your contribution margin too aggressively without guaranteed volume retention, whereas a tiered model protects yield from price-insensitive small users and secures the 60% of usage currently at risk.

**The Numbers:**
*Current State:*
*   Revenue: $100,000 (2M mins × $0.05)
*   Variable Cost: $40,000 (2M mins × $0.02)
*   Contribution Margin: $60,000
*   Fixed Costs: $45,000
*   **Net Burn:** $-15,000 (Profitable operation)
*   **Runway:** $300,000 / $15,000 = **20 months** (Note: You are currently cash-flow positive, so runway is technically infinite until burn turns negative).

*Scenario A: Blanket 15% Price Cut (Price drops to $0.0425)*
*   Assumption: Volume remains flat at 2M minutes (ASSUMED: Low elasticity).
*   New Revenue: $85,000
*   Variable Cost: $40,000
*   New Contribution Margin: $45,000
*   Fixed Costs: $45,000
*   **Net Burn:** $0 (Break-even)
*   **Risk:** If volume drops even 5% due to churn or lack of growth, you immediately burn cash. You sacrifice $15k/month in profit for no strategic gain.

*Scenario B: Tiered Model (Targeted Discount)*
*   Assumption: Top 10 customers (1.2M mins, 60%) get 20% discount ($0.04); Small users (0.8M mins) stay at $0.05.
*   New Revenue: (1.2M × $0.04) + (0.8M × $0.05) = $48,000 + $40,000 = $88,000
*   Variable Cost: $40,000
*   New Contribution Margin: $48,000
*   Fixed Costs: $45,000
*   **Net Burn:** $-3,000 (Still profitable)
*   **Upside:** If tiered pricing attracts 10% more volume from mid-market (ASSUMED), revenue rises to ~$96,800, increasing profit buffer.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The deciding input is **churn rate among the top 10 customers**. If losing one major client reduces volume by >8% (160k mins), the lost contribution ($1,600–$3,200/month) outweighs the cost of the discount.
*   **Trigger:** If any of the top 10 customers request a quote comparison or mention the competitor by [Date: 30 days from now], execute the tiered discount offer immediately.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Never discount below your fully loaded cost plus 20% margin unless the lifetime value (LTV) extension justifies it.
