**The Call**
Reject the raw $12.5M top-line figure; your viable immediate market is likely 60–70% of your total count unless you pivot to a hybrid hub model, in which case you can recover up to 90% of the opportunity. Do not size the market by total farms; size it by "completable enrollments."

**The Numbers**
*Formula:* Viable TAM = (Total Farms × Connectivity Success Rate) × Price Per Unit.
*Loss Formula:* Revenue Leakage = (Total Farms × Dropout Rate) × (Price + Support Cost).

Using your inputs (5,000 farms, $2,500 price, $300 support cost, 40% dropout):
*   **Current State (Online Only):**
    *   Assumed Success Rate: 60% (Base case, derived from 100% - 40% dropout).
    *   Viable Students: 3,000.
    *   **Viable Revenue:** $7,500,000.
    *   **Direct Loss (Failed Students):** 2,000 students × ($2,500 lost revenue + $300 support cost) = **$5,600,000** in total value destruction (lost sales + wasted ops).

*   **Hybrid Hub Scenario (Assumed):**
    *   Assumption: Partnering with co-ops lifts success rate to 90% (only 10% dropout due to non-connectivity issues).
    *   Viable Students: 4,500.
    *   **Potential Revenue:** $11,250,000.
    *   **Recoverable Opportunity:** $3,750,000 additional revenue vs. current state.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is the **Connectivity Success Rate**. If you can validate that local co-ops or community centers can host offline download/viewing sessions, your addressable market expands from 3,000 to ~4,500 units.
*   **Trigger Rule:** If you can secure agreements with just 5 major regional co-ops to act as "offline hubs" within the next 60 days, consider pivoting your delivery model to "Download-at-Hub, Learn-on-Farm."
*   **This Week's Step:** If 3 out of 10 surveyed farmers confirm they visit a co-op with Wi-Fi weekly, consider launching a pilot "offline packet" program instead of building more online support tools.

**Routing Note**
While market sizing is arithmetic, structuring partnerships with agricultural co-ops to host your content may involve commercial licensing agreements or liability waivers regarding data usage on their networks; consult a commercial contracts attorney before signing hub agreements.

*Note: The 40% dropout rate is treated here as a hard connectivity constraint; if churn is partly due to course quality, the "recoverable" number above is overstated.*
