**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes this from "proceed with caution" to "stop immediately" is the **destination state's specific embargo list**. California (CDFA), Arizona (DACA), and Florida (FDACS) maintain strict "quarantine pest" lists that frequently include tomato seeds due to risks like the Tomato Brown Rugose Fruit Virus (ToBRFV).
*   **Trigger:** If *any* of your five varieties appear on a target state's prohibited list, your ability to ship there drops to zero regardless of federal permits.
