**The Numbers:**
To determine if the market supports 300 units, calculate your required Conversion Rate (CR) against estimated Traffic (T):
*Formula:* `Units = Traffic × Conversion Rate`
*Target:* 300 units.
*Your Constraints:* COGS 85€, Price Point (Assumed Base) 325€ (mid-range of competitors).
*Gross Margin per Unit:* 240€.
*Break-even Marketing Spend:* 300 units × 240€ = 72,000€ potential gross profit. Your 15,000€ budget covers ~62 units at a 100% marketing efficiency ratio (MER) of 1.0, meaning you must rely heavily on organic reach or achieve a very high MER (>4.0) to hit 300 units solely via paid channels in Q1.

**Data Sources & Method (Bottom-Up):**
Do not use top-down fashion reports. Build your model using these specific, scrapable proxies for *active* demand in France and Germany:

1.  **Search Intent Volume (The "Pull"):**
    *   *Source:* Google Trends (free) and SEMrush/Ahrefs (paid trials).
    *   *Action:* Scrape monthly search volumes for exact long-tail keywords: "vegan leather handbag luxury," "cactus leather bag," "mushroom leather purse" filtered by FR/DE.
    *   *Calculation:* Take the sum of monthly searches for these terms in FR/DE. Apply a conservative click-through rate (CTR) of 2% for organic presence or 1.5% for ads to estimate *visitable* traffic.
    *   *Gap:* You cannot determine *commercial intent* (willingness to pay €300+) from search volume alone. You must layer this with price-filtered data if available, or assume a 10% qualification rate (UNVERIFIED assumption).

2.  **Event Density (The "Push"):**
    *   *Source:* Eventbrite, Meetup, and Instagram Geotags.
    *   *Action:* Count attendance at "sustainable fashion," "vegan lifestyle," and "ethical luxury" pop-ups in Paris and Berlin over the last 12 months.
    *   *Calculation:* Total Attendees × 5% (ASSUMED overlap with luxury spenders) = Potential Local Customer Pool.
    *   *Gap:* Attendance does not equal purchase intent. This requires a survey intercept (see below).

3.  **Competitor Social Proof:**
    *   *Source:* Instagram/TikTok comments on the 12 identified competitors.
    *   *Action:* Scrape comments containing "price," "buy," "shipping to [FR/DE]," or "waitlist."
    *   *Calculation:* Volume of buying-signal comments ÷ 12 competitors = Average unmet demand signal per brand.

**What Flips It:**
The **Conversion Rate** is the flip input. If your site converts at 1.0% (industry average for new luxury DTC), you need 30,000 qualified visitors to sell 300 bags. With a 15,000€ budget, your Cost Per Click (CPC) must be under 0.50€ to generate that traffic, which is unlikely for "luxury" keywords.
*Trigger:* If your initial landing page test (spending 500€) yields a CPC > 2.00€ or a CR < 0.8%, pivot immediately to a pre-order/waitlist model to validate price sensitivity before full production.
