3.  **Revenue per Student:** Assuming 30 credits/year @ $350 = $10,500/student; Total Potential Revenue ≈ $3.15M (if fully enrolled/capacity utilized), but current revenue is likely lower based on actual credit load.

*   **High Case:** If hidden costs (faculty salary adjustments to meet accreditation standards) rise, the burden could exceed 30% of your current budget.
*   **Low Case:** Minimal external consulting keeps initial costs near $50k, but annual overhead remains mandatory.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is **secular partner willingness to accept "religious-only" accreditation**.
*Trigger Value:* If a target university explicitly signs a memorandum of understanding (MOU) accepting your current religious accreditation *before* you spend on regional accreditation.

*Rule of Thumb:* Secular institutions rarely accept credits from non-regionally accredited schools unless a specific state articulation agreement exists (unlikely for private religious schools across state lines).
*Step this week:* If you have no signed LOIs from secular partners contingent *only* on curriculum review (not accreditation), consider pausing partnership outreach to focus on a feasibility study for regional accreditation.
