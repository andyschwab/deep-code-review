**The Call**
You cannot launch with current reserves; you are $30,000 short of covering the initial server build before acquiring a single user. Do not proceed until you secure at least $90,000 in additional funding or reduce the upfront infrastructure commitment. Even with perfect adoption (500 users in six months), you will not break even on a cash-flow basis until Month 7, assuming no scaling costs.

**The Numbers**
*Formula:* Break-Even Users = (Upfront Cost + Monthly Burn × Months) / (Cert Fee + Monthly Fee × Months Active).
*Inputs:* Upfront $90k; Monthly Fixed $4k; Price $200 one-time + $20/mo.
*Assumptions:*
1.  **Adoption Rate:** Linear growth to 500 users by Month 6 (ASSUMED).
2.  **Scaling Costs:** Variable server cost per user is $0 (ASSUMED; high risk).
3.  **Churn:** 0% (ASSUMED).

*Projection (Cumulative Revenue vs. Total Cost):*
*   **Month 1:** Cost $94k | Rev $1.7k (83 users) | Gap: -$92.3k
*   **Month 3:** Cost $102k | Rev $14.5k (250 users) | Gap: -$87.5k
*   **Month 6:** Cost $114k | Rev $69k (500 users) | Gap: -$45k
*   **Month 9:** Cost $126k | Rev $138k (500 users + new) | **Surplus: +$12k**

Under these assumptions, you cross into profitability in **Month 8 or 9**. The high initial fixed cost creates a "valley of death" where early revenue barely dents the $90k hole while the $4k/month burn continues. Compared to a standard low-capex SaaS model, your break-even is delayed by approximately 4–6 months because every dollar of early revenue must first repay the infrastructure debt before covering operating expenses.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that destroys this model is **variable server scaling cost**. Your assumption that the $4,000 monthly fixed cost covers unlimited user growth is likely false. If video processing or data storage scales linearly with users, a sudden spike in adoption could double your monthly burn before revenue catches up.
*Trigger:* If user acquisition exceeds 100 users/month, immediately audit variable infrastructure costs. If variable cost per user exceeds $5, your path to profitability extends indefinitely.
*Action:* If you cannot raise $90k this week, consider shifting the $90k upfront cost to a usage-based cloud model (pay-as-you-go) to lower the initial cash barrier, even if it raises long-term unit costs.
