**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is **Control over Method**. If your platform mandates specific teaching curricula, requires instructors to use proprietary materials, or penalizes them for setting their own rates/schedules outside your narrow bands, the relationship shifts from "facilitator" to "employer."
*   **Trigger Rule:** Under the common law test (used by CRA and Ontario courts), if you control the *means* and *manner* of work (not just the result), you are likely an employer.
