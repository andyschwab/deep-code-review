**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that changes this call is **retailer requirement**. If you are selling Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) only, you *might* survive on internal data with heavy disclaimers (though FDA "misbranding" risk remains). However, if you intend to sell to any retailer (REI, Whole Foods, etc.), they will demand a Certificate of Analysis (CoA) from an accredited lab before listing.
*   **Trigger Value:** Signed Letter of Intent (LOI) from a major retailer.
*   **Decision Rule:** If you have an LOI contingent on shelf-life data, you have no choice but to test. If you are 100% DTC, you still face FDA scrutiny on "misleading labeling."
