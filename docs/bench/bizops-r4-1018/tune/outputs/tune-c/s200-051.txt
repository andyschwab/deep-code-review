**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that breaks your model is the **percentage of traffic hitting peak rates**.
- *Trigger Value:* If peak traffic exceeds **23%** of total volume, your blended cost rises above $1.80, dropping your gross margin below 60%.
- *Computed Date:* At a steady 15% peak rate, you are safe. If you anticipate a product launch or seasonal spike next quarter where peak traffic might hit 25%, your margin target fails.
- *Rule of Thumb:* Treat any month where peak traffic >20% as a "margin alert" requiring immediate infrastructure optimization.
