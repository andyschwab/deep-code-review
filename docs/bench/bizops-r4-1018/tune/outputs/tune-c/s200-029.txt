**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes the tax treatment is the **timing of the price reduction**. If you change the mechanism to an "instant discount" where the customer pays only $85 at the register, the sales tax base drops to $85 immediately.
*   **Trigger:** If your cash flow analysis shows the $5,437 extra tax remittance (per 3,000 units) critically impacts operations, consider switching to an instant discount model.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Manufacturer rebates mailed after purchase rarely reduce sales tax bases; retailer instant discounts almost always do.
