**The Call:** Do not cut marketing by half immediately; instead, pivot your sales strategy to target smaller, faster-declosing deals while simultaneously reducing non-marketing operating expenses. A 4.5 burn multiple is a critical warning sign for any B2B company, including deep-tech infrastructure, indicating that every dollar of growth costs $4.50 in cash burn. With only ~8.3 months of runway remaining and a 9-month sales cycle, you face a near-certain cash crunch before new enterprise revenue lands. Cutting marketing alone risks starving future pipeline without solving the immediate liquidity gap caused by the mismatch between your sales cycle length and your cash reserves.

**The Numbers:**
*   **Runway Calculation:** Cash / Net Burn = $1,500,000 / $180,000 = **8.33 months**.
*   **Burn Multiple Assessment:** 4.5x is significantly above the "healthy" benchmark of <1.5x for Series A/growth stages and even the "acceptable" <2.0x for early seed. For context, a 4.5x multiple implies extreme inefficiency in converting capital to revenue.
*   **Scenario A (Cut Marketing 50%):**
    *   *Assumption (ASSUMED):* Marketing is 60% of OpEx. If Net Burn ($180k) = OpEx - Revenue ($40k), then OpEx = $220k. Marketing = $132k. Cutting 50% saves $66k.
    *   *New Net Burn:* $180k - $66k = $114k.
    *   *New Runway:* $1,500,000 / $114k = **13.1 months**.
    *   *Risk:* Enterprise sales cycles (9 months) rely on consistent top-of-funnel activity. Halving spend now likely collapses revenue in 2–3 quarters, extending runway temporarily but killing long-term viability.
*   **Scenario B (Pivot to SMB/Faster Deals + Moderate Cuts):**
    *   *Assumption (ASSUMED):* Shortening sales cycle to 3 months accelerates cash inflow. Reducing non-marketing OpEx (e.g., hiring freeze, contractor reduction) by 15% saves ~$13k.
    *   *Goal:* Reduce burn multiple to <2.0x. To achieve this with $40k revenue, Net Burn must drop to ~$80k (2.0 x $40k).
    *   *Required Action:* Total cost reduction of $100k/month. This requires both marketing efficiency (better targeting, not just less spend) and significant OpEx reduction.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is the **Sales Cycle Length**. If you cannot compress the time-to-cash below 6 months, austerity is mandatory regardless of strategy.
*   **Trigger Threshold:** If you do not secure at least two deals with a sales cycle <4 months within the next **45 days**, you must enact immediate austerity (layoffs/contract termination) to extend runway to 18+ months.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Runway should always be >1.5x your sales cycle. Currently, 8.3 months runway < 9 months cycle = Failure state.
