**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is **Title Transfer Timing**. If title transfers *only* after the final payment and the customer has a genuine right to terminate early without penalty (returning the chair), it leans toward a lease. If title effectively passes at signing or the "lease" is non-cancelable with a nominal buyout, it is a loan.
*   **Trigger:** If your contract includes a "$1 buyout" or automatic title transfer clause, stop launch immediately.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** In California, if the lessee is bound to pay substantially the full value of the goods and has an option to become owner for nominal consideration, it is a secured sale (Cal. Com. Code § 10103).
*   **Step this week:** If your draft contract lacks a clear, non-penal early termination right where the customer can return the chair and owe nothing further, consider pausing the draft and flagging "secured transaction" risks.
