**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The decision flips if your annual revenue exceeds **$2.5 million** or if you expand into highly litigious states with specific statutory liabilities for educational providers that pierce standard corporate veils.
*   *Trigger Value:* If a single lawsuit threat exceeds **$50,000** in potential damages (legal defense costs alone), the insurance limit becomes the bottleneck, not the entity structure.
*   *Stop/Pivot Trigger:* Re-evaluate entity structure if you hire employees in those states (creating nexus and employment law exposure) or if a specific state board mandates local registration for content providers.
*   *Rule of Thumb:* Structural separation is for isolating distinct business lines with different risk profiles, not for geographic segmentation of the same product.
*   *This Week's Step:* If your current Terms of Service do not explicitly limit liability to the price paid for the course and mandate binding arbitration, **consider engaging a lawyer to draft these specific clauses immediately**, as they are your first line of defense.
