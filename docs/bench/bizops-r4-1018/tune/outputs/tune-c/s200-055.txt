**The Call**
Your unit economics have broken: at $7.50 variable cost against $15 revenue, your contribution margin has collapsed from 73% to 50%, slashing monthly profit from $12k to $3k and reducing your runway from ~9 months to ~6 months. You must immediately introduce a "Good-Better-Best" tiered pricing model that separates standard code repositories from high-volume binary storage, grandfathering existing small repos while charging overage fees or higher tiers for the new heavy users.

**The Numbers**
*Formula:* Monthly Profit = (Price − Variable Cost) × Repos − Fixed OpEx.
*Current Reality (Base Case):* ($15 − $7.50) × 2,000 − $18,000 = **$3,000 profit**.
*Previous State (For Context):* ($15 − $4.00) × 2,000 − $18,000 = $14,000 profit.

*Break-Even Analysis (When margin turns negative):*
To find the tipping point where Profit = 0:
($15 − Cost) × 2,000 = $18,000 → $15 − Cost = $9 → **Cost = $6.00 per repo**.
Since your cost is currently $7.50, you are already operating below the break-even variable cost threshold required to cover fixed expenses at this volume. If costs rise further, you bleed cash faster; if they drop below $6.00, you return to profitability.

*Pricing Sensitivity (ASSUMED: 10% churn if prices rise >20%):*
If you raise prices to $22 for heavy users (covering the $7.50 cost + margin) and keep 1,800 users at $15:
(1,800 × $11 margin) + (200 × $14.50 margin) − $18,000 = $19,800 + $2,900 − $18,000 = **$4,700 profit**.
This assumes you can segment the 2,000 repos accurately.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is the **percentage of repos exceeding 50MB**.
*Trigger:* If more than **40%** of your repositories exceed the 50MB threshold (currently implied at 100% based on your average), your current flat rate becomes unsustainable regardless of minor price tweaks.
*Rule of Thumb:* Variable costs should never exceed 30-40% of LTV in SaaS; you are currently at 50%.
*Action Step:* If your data shows >25% of repos are >80MB, consider implementing an immediate "Binary Asset Surcharge" or migrating those users to a "Pro" tier at $25/mo by next billing cycle.

**Routing Note**
Your customer contracts likely define "active repository" without size limits; modifying terms for existing customers may require legal review to avoid breach-of-contract claims or violating consumer protection laws regarding unilateral price changes. Consult counsel to draft the notification language and verify the notice period required to implement tiered pricing legally.
