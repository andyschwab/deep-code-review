**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The deciding factor is not the **25%** revenue share (there is no statutory "safe harbor" percentage where income automatically becomes "unrelated"), but whether the IRS views selling job listings as *substantially related* to your exempt purpose.
*   **The Rule:** Income is "Unrelated Business Income" (UBI) if it is from a trade or business, regularly carried on, and *not* substantially related to the exempt purpose.
*   **The Analysis:** A job board for people with disabilities is arguably the core mechanism of your mission (employment advocacy). If the service provided to employers is identical to the benefit provided to candidates (matching), it is likely **Related Income**, meaning it is fully tax-exempt and does not threaten your status, regardless of the dollar amount.
*   **The Trigger:** If the service shifts to "premium branding" or "data sales" that do not directly facilitate hiring, it flips to Unrelated.
*   **Actionable Step:** If the legal review confirms the activity is "related," file Form 990 reporting the revenue as program service revenue. If "unrelated," you must file Form 990-T and pay corporate tax on the net profit; you only need a subsidiary if the *scale* of this unrelated activity becomes larger than your related activities (the "primary purpose" test).
