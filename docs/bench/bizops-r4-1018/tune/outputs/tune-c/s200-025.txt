**The Numbers:**
*   **Revenue Recognition (Book/Tax):** Under ASC 606 (the usual standard for bundled goods/services), you must allocate the transaction price based on relative standalone selling prices (SSP).
    *   *Formula:* Allocated Price = (Item SSP / Total SSP) × Bundle Price.
    *   *Your Figures:* Hardware SSP $120 + Treat SSP $35 = $155 Total SSP.
    *   *Bundle Scenario:* First month bundle = ($120 + $35) × 0.80 = $124.
    *   *Allocation:*
        *   Hardware: ($120 / $155) × $124 = **$96** (Recognize immediately).
        *   Subscription: ($35 / $155) × $124 = **$28** (Recognize over the month).
    *   *Result:* Even with a discount, you recognize a portion of revenue upfront. The $90,000 total revenue figure must be split between "Product Revenue" (immediate) and "Deferred Revenue" (liability until treats delivered).
*   **Sales Tax (Colorado):**
    *   *Rule:* Colorado generally taxes tangible personal property (collars) but exempts most food for home consumption (treats).
    *   *Bundle Treatment:* If sold as a single unitemized price, the entire amount is often taxable as a "true bundle" of taxable goods. If itemized, you tax only the collar portion.
    *   *Assumption:* ASSUMED you are not itemizing on the invoice currently.
    *   *Impact:* You may be over-collecting tax on the treat portion if not itemized, or under-collecting if the state views the bundle as wholly taxable.
