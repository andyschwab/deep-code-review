**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The classification of the data flips the requirement from "standard privacy policy" to "explicit consent + DPIA."
*   **Trigger:** If your algorithm extracts unique geometric identifiers to distinguish one individual from another (which bespoke fitting requires), the data is **biometric** under GDPR Article 4(14).
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If the data can re-identify a user without a name, it is special category.
*   **Stop/Pivot Date:** Before the first beta scan is processed.
*   **This Week's Step:** If your current architecture stores raw images or 3D models on non-EU servers, **consider** pausing data ingestion and drafting a DPIA scope document to present to counsel.

**Routing Note:**
You must engage a UK/EU data privacy counsel to confirm whether your specific scanning algorithm meets the "specific technical processing" threshold for Article 9 biometric data and to validate your Data Processing Agreement (DPA) with cloud providers. This is time-sensitive before any user data is collected.

**Mandatory Disclosures & Retention:**
*   **Disclosures:** You must explicitly state the *purpose* (bespoke fitting), the *legal basis* (explicit consent, not legitimate interest), and the *retention period* in a standalone notice, not buried in Terms of Service.
*   **Retention:** GDPR requires data minimization. You must define a specific deletion timeline (e.g., "deleted after shoe delivery" or "30 days") and automate it. Indefinite storage is non-compliant.
*   **Storage Location:** While GDPR does not strictly mandate *local* EU storage, transferring data outside the EU (e.g., to US servers) requires valid transfer mechanisms (like SCCs). Given the sensitivity, local EU storage is the safest architectural choice to avoid transfer complexities.

**Classification Summary:**
Foot geometry used for unique identification is generally treated as biometric data. This mandates **explicit opt-in** (a separate tick box, not pre-checked) and prohibits processing until that consent is given. Standard terms are insufficient.
