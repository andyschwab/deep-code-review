**The Call**
Your shift to leasing likely reclassifies your revenue from a one-time sale of tangible personal property to a taxable lease of equipment, which generally triggers ongoing sales/use tax obligations in every state where a unit is physically located, creating immediate nexus and filing requirements you did not have under a pure shipping model.

**The Numbers**
*   **Current Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR):** $10,000
*   **Units Active:** 25
*   **Implied Lease Rate per Unit:** $400/month ($10,000 / 25)
*   **Manufacturing Cost per Unit:** $40,000
*   **Taxable Base Shift:** Instead of taxing the full $40,000 once at the point of sale, most states tax the $400 monthly lease payment repeatedly over the life of the lease.
*   **Illustrative Tax Impact (Assumed 8% avg rate):**
    *   *Old Model (One-time sale):* $40,000 × 8% = $3,200 collected once per unit.
    *   *New Model (Lease):* $400 × 8% = $32 collected **every month** per unit.
    *   *Cumulative Effect:* Over a 5-year lease, you collect $1,920 in tax per unit, but you must file returns monthly or quarterly in every jurisdiction where a unit sits.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The specific definition of "tangible personal property" versus "service" in each state's code flips the obligation; however, hardware leases are almost universally treated as taxable leases of property, not exempt services.
*   **The Trigger:** Physical presence of the leased asset. Placing a simulator in a flight school in Texas creates nexus in Texas immediately, regardless of where your headquarters are.
*   **Stop/Pivot Trigger:** If you have units in more than 5 states, the compliance cost of multi-state filings may exceed the margin on the lease unless automated.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If a unit is physically located in a state for more than 30 days, assume you have nexus and must register.
*   **This Week's Step:** If you have units in multiple states, consider engaging a sales-tax automation provider (like Avalara or TaxJar) to map your current 25 locations before your next filing deadline.

**Routing Note**
You must consult a CPA or sales-tax attorney to determine the exact "situs" rules for your specific lease contracts in each state (e.g., some states tax the lessor, some the lessee) and to handle the voluntary disclosure agreements if you have already accumulated liability in states where you failed to register upon delivery.
