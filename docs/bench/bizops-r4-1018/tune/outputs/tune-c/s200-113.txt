**The Call**
Do not attempt to maintain your original margin targets by simply selling more units; with only 9 months of runway and a 12-month development cycle, you will run out of cash before the product ships. Your immediate priority is not a revised sales target but a capital bridge or a scope reduction to survive the gap. Selling more units is mathematically impossible if the product is not cleared for sale.

**The Numbers**
Your current unit economics per device are:
*   **Price:** $299
*   **Variable Cost (Hardware + CAC):** $85 + $60 = $145
*   **Contribution Margin:** $154 per unit (51.5%)

To calculate the new break-even volume, we must account for the fixed cost increase caused by the delay.
*   **Current Monthly Burn:** $83,000
*   **Delay Duration:** 6 additional months (12 months total - 6 months original)
*   **Estimated Cash Gap:** $83,000 × 6 = $498,000 (This assumes burn stays flat, which is unlikely given increased engineering hours).

Let $A$ be the unknown cost of the third-party security audit.
Let $L$ be the additional labor cost for the 6-month extension (8 engineers × 6 months).
*   **Total New Fixed Costs:** $498,000 (base runway gap) + $L + $A$.

**Break-Even Formula:**
$$ \text{Additional Units Needed} = \frac{\text{Total New Fixed Costs}}{\$154} $$

*Scenario Analysis (Assumptions marked ASSUMED):*
1.  **Low Impact:** Audit ($A$) = $25k; Labor increase ($L$) = $100k (ASSUMED: overtime/contractors).
    *   Gap = $498k + $25k + $100k = $623,000.
    *   **Additional Units:** ~4,046 units.
    *   *Result:* Total required sales = 5,000 (original) + 4,046 = 9,046 units. This is an 81% increase in volume, likely unachievable in year one for a delayed hardware startup.

2.  **High Impact:** Audit ($A$) = $75k; Labor increase ($L$) = $250k (ASSUMED: full team burden + consultants).
    *   Gap = $498k + $75k + $250k = $823,000.
    *   **Additional Units:** ~5,344 units.
    *   *Result:* Total required sales = 10,344 units. This doubles your original target.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the outcome is **Runway Extension**, not sales volume. You cannot sell your way out of a cash gap created by a regulatory delay before product launch.
*   **Trigger:** If you do not secure an additional $600k–$900k in funding or reduce monthly burn by 40% within the next 30 days, the company will cease operations before the FDA documentation is complete.
*   **Action:** If the audit quote ($A$) exceeds $50,000, consider pausing non-essential engineering features to focus solely on the minimum viable compliance package required for submission.

**Routing Note**
The specific FDA cybersecurity documentation requirements (e.g., SBOM, vulnerability management plans) and the exact scope of the third-party audit required for your device class are binding regulatory determinations; consult a regulatory affairs specialist or FDA counsel to define the precise cost ($A$) and timeline, as this dictates your survival strategy.
