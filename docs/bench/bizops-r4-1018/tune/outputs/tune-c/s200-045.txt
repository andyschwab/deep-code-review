**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is the **geographic concentration of your DTC customers**. Even with high total revenue, nexus is state-specific.
*   **Trigger Value:** $100,000 in gross sales *or* 200 separate transactions into any single state within a 12-month period (rules vary; some states use only revenue, some only transactions, some both).
*   **Pivot Trigger:** If any single state's DTC sales exceed $85,000 year-to-date, pause marketing spend in that state and consult a tax professional immediately before crossing the $100k line.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Treat wholesale sales as "invisible" for nexus calculations only if you have a valid resale certificate on file; otherwise, they count fully.
