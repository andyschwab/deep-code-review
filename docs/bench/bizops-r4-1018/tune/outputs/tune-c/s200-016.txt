**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The critical input is **invoice presentation**. In California, shipping charges are generally exempt from sales tax if: (1) the shipment is via common carrier, (2) title passes to the buyer at the seller's location (FOB origin), or the shipping is optional, and (3) the charge is stated separately on the invoice. If you bake the cost into the subscription price, the entire $20 becomes part of the taxable sales price.
*Trigger:* If your Q3 gross receipts (including shipping) exceed $500,000 (which they already do), you must register. The "flip" is purely financial: separating the fee saves you collecting tax on the $5 portion.
*Rule of Thumb:* If shipping is optional and separately stated, it is often non-taxable. If mandatory and bundled, it is taxable.
*Step this week:* If your invoice currently bundles the fee, redesign it immediately to list "Subscription" and "Shipping" as distinct line items to attempt to exclude the shipping portion from the taxable base.
