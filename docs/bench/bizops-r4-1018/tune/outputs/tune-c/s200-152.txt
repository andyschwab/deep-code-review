**The Call**
Your target is mathematically impossible with your current pipeline; you must cut costs immediately and treat fundraising as a parallel, urgent track rather than a backup plan. Relying on closing 29 deals from 10 leads to save the company is a strategic failure waiting to happen.

**The Numbers**
To extend runway by 18 months at a $40,000 monthly burn, you need **$720,000** in *profit*, not just revenue.
*Formula:* Required Profit = Target Months × Monthly Burn.
*Assumption (CRITICAL):* This calculation assumes 100% gross margin (zero cost of goods sold, implementation, or commissions). If your margins are 80% (a common SaaS benchmark), you actually need **$900,000** in revenue.

*Scenario A (100% Margin - Best Case):*
*   Deals Needed: $720,000 / $25,000 = **29 contracts**.
*   Leads Required (at 20% close rate): 29 / 0.20 = **145 qualified leads**.
*   Current Pipeline Gap: You have 10 leads; you need 135 more immediately.

*Scenario B (80% Margin - Realistic):*
*   Revenue Needed: $720,000 / 0.80 = $900,000.
*   Deals Needed: $900,000 / $25,000 = **36 contracts**.
*   Leads Required: 36 / 0.20 = **180 qualified leads**.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes this outcome is **Gross Margin**. If your delivery costs are higher than assumed, the revenue target explodes.
*Trigger:* If you cannot add 135+ qualified leads to your pipeline within 30 days, the "sales-only" rescue plan fails.
*Rule of Thumb:* A sales cycle of 4 months means revenue booked today arrives in 4 months. You will run out of cash in Month 6. Even if you close deals in Month 3, the cash hits in Month 7—too late.
*Action:* If you do not have $500k+ in committed capital access by next Friday, consider an immediate 30% reduction in burn (furloughs or layoffs) to extend the organic runway while you pitch investors.

**Routing Note**
*Securities/Fundraising:* Since you are likely to miss the organic target, preparing to sell equity or SAFEs constitutes a securities offering. Consult counsel immediately to structure a bridge round compliant with SEC regulations before soliciting funds.

*Missing Assumptions Derailing Plan:*
1.  **Cash Timing:** You assumed booking a deal equals cash in hand. With a 4-month cycle, you cannot solve a 6-month cash crisis with new sales alone unless deals close *and* pay upfront in the next 60 days.
2.  **Pipeline Velocity:** You assumed 10 leads can convert to 29 deals. Mathematically, you need to triple your lead volume instantly.
3.  **Margin Reality:** You treated revenue as profit. Any cost to serve (hosting, support, sales commissions) increases the deal count required.
