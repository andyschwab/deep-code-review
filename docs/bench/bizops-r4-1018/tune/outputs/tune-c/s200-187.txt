**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is **Behavioral Control**. If your platform mandates specific coaching methods, sets strict availability windows, or prohibits coaches from working on competing platforms, the IRS will likely deem them employees regardless of your contract language.
*   **Trigger:** The moment you enforce a standardized curriculum or discipline coaches for refusing specific time slots, the risk shifts from "audit possibility" to "probable violation."
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If you control the *how* and *when* of the work, they are employees. If they control the method and schedule, they are contractors.
*   **Step this week:** If you have any written policies dictating coaching scripts or mandatory login hours, consider suspending enforcement of those policies immediately while you seek counsel.
