**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is the **applicability of the "essential office functions" exemption** under the UK Public Sector Bodies Accessibility Regulations 2018.
*Trigger:* If the RFP or legal counsel confirms the training is "not essential for the performance of tasks" (e.g., optional DEI training vs. mandatory safety training), you may submit an accessibility statement explaining the limitation rather than building to full AA standard.
*Rule of Thumb:* Government digital services default to WCAG 2.1 AA unless a specific, documented exemption is filed.
*Action this week:* If the RFP is silent, submit a formal clarification question asking: "Does this training content qualify for the 'essential office functions' exemption under the 2018 Regulations, or is full WCAG 2.1 AA compliance a pass/fail requirement?"
