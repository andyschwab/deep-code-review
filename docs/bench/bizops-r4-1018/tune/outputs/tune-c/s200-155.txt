**The Call**
Reject the 2% flat conversion assumption for your investor deck; it likely overstates your immediate Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM) by ignoring intent segmentation and cannibalization of existing premium subscribers. Instead, present a segmented TAM/SAM model that isolates "tutoring-ready" users from general power users, using your 2% figure only as a long-term ceiling, not a launch baseline.

**The Numbers**
To size the market without double-counting, apply this funnel to your specific data:

1.  **Total Registered:** 200,000
2.  **Daily Active Users (DAU):** 200,000 × 10% = 20,000
3.  **Existing Paid Subscribers (Exclusion):** *Unknown Variable.* You must subtract users already paying for *any* premium feature to avoid double-counting revenue.
    *   *Assumption (Base Case):* If 5% of DAU are already paid (per your historical feature conversion), that is 1,000 users.
    *   *Addressable Free DAU:* 20,000 - 1,000 = 19,000.
4.  **Tutoring-Intent Segment:** *Unknown Variable.* Not all DAU want live tutoring.
    *   *Formula:* Addressable Free DAU × Intent Filter (%)
    *   *Scenario A (Optimistic/Your 2%):* 19,000 × 2% = **380 users**.
    *   *Scenario B (Conservative/0.5%):* 19,000 × 0.5% = **95 users**.
5.  **Annual Revenue Potential:**
    *   At 380 users × $30/hr × 4 hrs/month × 12 months = **$547,200/year**.
    *   At 95 users × $30/hr × 4 hrs/month × 12 months = **$136,800/year**.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is the **Intent Filter** (the percentage of DAU specifically seeking live conversation vs. self-study). Your current 2% assumption treats "feature interest" as "tutoring demand," which is risky for high-touch products.
*   **Trigger:** If a micro-survey of 500 DAU reveals less than 1% explicitly want live tutoring, pivot your launch strategy from a broad rollout to a waitlist model to manage tutor supply/demand.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** High-touch services (tutoring) typically convert at 1/4 to 1/10 the rate of low-touch features (content unlocks).
*   **This Week's Step:** If you cannot isolate intent data, consider running a "fake door" test (a non-clickable "Book Tutor" button) on the dashboard to measure actual click-through rates before hiring tutors.
