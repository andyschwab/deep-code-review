**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is **Price Elasticity of Demand**.
- **Trigger Value:** If your actual patient loss exceeds **16.5%**, the price hike destroys revenue.
- **Rule of Thumb:** In high-ticket, cash-pay healthcare, demand is often elastic (sensitive). A 20% hike causing >17% loss is probable if competitors remain at $15k.
- **Actionable Step:** If you cannot validate via a survey that <17% of patients will leave, **do not raise prices**.
