**The Call:** Do not frame the nonprofit tier as a revenue leak; position it as a verified, low-churn distribution channel that de-risks customer acquisition for your paid B2B or premium B2C segments, provided you explicitly track and report the "subsidized LTV" and conversion funnel from free to paid.

**The Numbers:**
Your current runway is a hard constraint before any narrative framing matters.
*   **Formula:** Runway = Cash Reserves / Monthly Burn Rate
*   **Inputs:** $600,000 / $30,000
*   **Result:** 20 months.

To raise a Series A ($4M) in 12 months, you must demonstrate growth within this 20-month window. The impact of the 40% free tier on unit economics depends on the **Subsidy Ratio** (how many paid users support one free user).
*   **Metric:** Effective CAC Blended = Total Sales & Marketing Spend / (Paid New Users + *Weighted* Free Users).
*   **Assumption (UNVERIFIED):** If your paid conversion from free nonprofit users is <2%, treat them as a marketing expense, not revenue users. If >10%, treat them as a top-of-funnel lead gen engine.
*   **Scenario:** If you acquire 1,000 free nonprofit users at $0 marginal cost, but they refer 50 paid users who would have cost $500 each to acquire, your effective CAC drops significantly.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes the investor verdict is **Paid Conversion Rate from the Nonprofit Tier**.
*   **Trigger:** If conversion from verified nonprofits to paid individual/family tiers exceeds **5%** within 6 months, the narrative flips from "giving away value" to "efficient community-led growth."
*   **Stop/Pivot Date:** By Month 14 (6 months before your target raise), if conversion is <2%, you must pivot to restricting features or introducing a time-limit on the free tier to prove monetization capability.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Canadian VCs in the social-impact space often prioritize **Total Active Users (TAU)** and **Retention** over immediate revenue if the path to monetization is clear, but pure revenue-growth funds will discount your valuation if >30% of your base is unmonetized without a clear upsell path.
*   **This Week's Step:** If your current nonprofit conversion is unknown, consider instrumenting a specific "upgrade prompt" within the support group flow to measure willingness-to-pay immediately.

**Routing Note:**
Structuring the "verified nonprofit" eligibility and ensuring the free access does not inadvertently create a taxable benefit or charitable status issue for your corporation requires a brief review by an Ontario corporate lawyer; specifically ask, "Does providing free services to registered charities constitute a non-arm's length transaction affecting our future tax credits or share structure?"

Canadian investors typically look for a clear "land-and-expand" story; if the nonprofits are the "land," ensure your deck shows exactly how they expand into paid seats or data insights.
