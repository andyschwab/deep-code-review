**The Call:** Unbundle professional services immediately into a separate line item or usage-based add-on; do not raise the base subscription price yet, as this penalizes low-touch customers and obscures the true cost of your high-touch cohort.

**The Numbers:**
Your current model hides a significant margin leak. We calculate the annual cost of service per customer and its impact on Contribution Margin (CM) and Payback Period.

*   **Formula:** Annual Service Cost = Hours × Rate; Adjusted Annual CM = (Monthly Price × 12 × Gross Margin %) − Annual Service Cost.
*   **Current State (Base Case):**
    *   Revenue: $30,000/year ($2,500 × 12).
    *   Gross Profit (before services): $21,600 ($30,000 × 72%).
    *   Service Cost: $3,000 (20 hours × $150). **(ASSUMED: Current burden)**
    *   **Adjusted Annual CM:** $18,600.
    *   **Effective Margin:** 62% (down from 72%).
*   **Option A: Raise Price to $3,100 (to cover cost):**
    *   New Revenue: $37,200.
    *   Service Cost: $3,000.
    *   **Adjusted Annual CM:** $23,844 (assuming margin % holds on base, minus fixed service cost).
    *   *Risk:* High churn sensitivity; you lose price-sensitive, low-touch clients.
*   **Option B: Unbundle Services (Charge $200/hr or package at $4,000/yr):**
    *   Subscription Revenue: $30,000 (CM: $21,600).
    *   Service Revenue: $4,000 (Cost: $3,000). Service CM: $1,000.
    *   **Total Adjusted Annual CM:** $22,600.
    *   *Benefit:* Protects base margin; high-touch clients self-select and pay for their burden.
*   **Option C: Reduce Included Hours to 5 (Charge overage):**
    *   Similar to Option B but creates friction at onboarding.

**Lifetime Value (LTV) & Payback Impact:**
*   **Churn:** 3% monthly ≈ 31% annual churn. Implied Average Lifespan ≈ 3.2 years.
*   **Current LTV (with hidden cost):** $18,600 × 3.2 = **$59,520**.
*   **Unbundled LTV:** $22,600 × 3.2 = **$72,320** (assuming churn remains stable).
*   **Payback Period:** If your blended CAC is $15,000:
    *   Current: $15,000 / ($18,600/12) = **9.7 months**.
    *   Unbundled: $15,000 / ($22,600/12) = **8.0 months**.
    *   *Note:* If raising prices causes churn to spike to 4%, LTV collapses to ~$45k, making Option A dangerous without validation.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The deciding input is **Price Elasticity of Demand** for your enterprise tier. If more than 8% of customers churn solely due to a price increase, unbundling is superior.
*   **Trigger:** If your next cohort's service hours exceed **12 hours**, your effective margin drops below 65%.
*   **Action:** By the end of Q3, if average onboarding hours remain >15, implement a mandatory "Implementation Fee" for new contracts.

**Weekly Metrics to Track:**
1.  **Services Burn Rate:** Total consultant hours sold vs. delivered per week.
2.  **Net Revenue Retention (NRR) by Cohort:** Separate customers by onboarding hours (<5 vs >15) to see if high-touch clients actually stay longer.
3.  **Contribution Margin per Account:** Calculate this weekly per new logo, explicitly subtracting actual service hours × $150.
