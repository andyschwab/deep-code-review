**The Call**
Do not alter your fee structure, but immediately implement explicit, multi-point disclosure statements on your submission portal and in author agreements confirming that payment is conditional *only* upon acceptance after peer review. Your current model (APC charged post-acceptance) aligns with standard industry practice, but the *perception* of conflict requires proactive transparency to satisfy EU research integrity expectations and avoid reputational damage; statutory mandates for specific wording are currently limited to general consumer protection and unfair commercial practices laws rather than a dedicated "APC disclosure statute."

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the regulatory risk profile is your **acceptance rate relative to your burn rate**.
*   **Trigger:** If your acceptance rate exceeds 60% while you remain unprofitable, regulators or critics may infer *de facto* "pay-to-publish" behavior, triggering scrutiny under the **EU Directive on Unfair Commercial Practices** (misleading omissions) or violating the **European Code of Conduct for Research Integrity**.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** An acceptance rate >50% for a fee-based OA journal is a red flag for predatory practices.
*   **Step this week:** If your acceptance rate is >45%, consider publishing your quarterly acceptance statistics on the homepage to demonstrate editorial rigor independent of revenue needs.
