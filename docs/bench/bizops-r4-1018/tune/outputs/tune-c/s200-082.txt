**The Criteria and The Override Question:**
Under the EU AI Act, AI systems intended for use in recruitment (specifically filtering, ranking, or selecting natural persons) are explicitly listed as **high-risk** (Annex III). The regulation targets the *purpose* of the system, not just the degree of automation.
*   **Automated Decision-Making:** The fact that your system filters 60% of applicants before human review confirms the system's substantial influence on the outcome.
*   **Human Override:** The ability for a human to override the decision **does not** lower the risk classification from high-risk to limited/minimal. The law recognizes that human reviewers often suffer from "automation bias" (trusting the machine's ranking). Therefore, the presence of an override mechanism triggers *additional* requirements for human oversight procedures, but it does not exempt you from the high-risk conformity assessment, data governance, and technical documentation mandates.
