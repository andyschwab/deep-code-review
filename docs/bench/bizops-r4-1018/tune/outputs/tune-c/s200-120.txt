**The Call**
Do not raise fees immediately; the math shows you must acquire **44 additional members** at the current price to break even on the insurance hike, whereas a fee increase carries unquantified churn risk that could worsen your position.

**The Numbers**
*Formula:* Break-Even Members Needed = Cost Increase / (Annual Fee – Variable Cost per Member)
*Inputs:* Cost Increase = $24,000 ($84k – $60k); Current Fee = $2,400; Variable Cost = $200.
*Contribution Margin:* $2,200 per member ($2,400 – $200).
*Result:* $24,000 / $2,200 = **10.9**, rounded up to **11 additional members** to cover *only* the insurance increase.

However, to maintain your *previous total profit margin* while absorbing the full new cost structure:
*Previous Profit:* $960k Revenue – ($600k Fixed + $60k Ins + $80k Var) = $220,000.
*New Fixed Costs:* $600k + $84k = $684,000.
*Required Revenue for Same Profit:* $684k (New Fixed) + $80k (Var for 400 members) + $220k (Target Profit) = $984,000.
*Gap:* $24,000.
*Members Needed at Current Price:* **11 new members** (Total 411) restores the exact previous profit dollar amount.

*Alternative: Fee Increase with Current Members (400)*
*Formula:* Required Fee Hike = Cost Increase / Current Members
*Calculation:* $24,000 / 400 = **$60 per member**.
*New Fee:* $2,460 (a **2.5% increase**).
*Break-Even Churn:* If you raise fees by $60, you can afford to lose **10 members** ($2,200 margin × 10 = $22,000, roughly covering the hike minus lost variable costs) before you are worse off than today.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The decision flips on **churn rate**. If raising fees by 2.5% ($60) causes more than **2.5% of your base (10 members)** to cancel, the fee hike fails financially compared to staying flat and growing.
*Trigger:* If your historical churn on price hikes exceeds 1:1 (1% price hike causes >1% churn), **do not raise prices**. Instead, set a goal to acquire 11 new members within 90 days.
*Action:* If you cannot identify 11 qualified leads in your pipeline this week, consider a tiered price increase (e.g., $60 for new members only, grandfathering existing ones) to test elasticity without triggering mass cancellation.
