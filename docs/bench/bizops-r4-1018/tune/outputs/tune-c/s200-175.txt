**The Call:** Allocate the $100,000 to the asynchronous video segment; your current unit economics (800 clients vs. 40) and the non-linear scaling constraints of live facilitation suggest video offers a significantly larger Total Addressable Market (TAM) for a capital-constrained expansion into the US.

**The Numbers:**
To size the market accurately, do not start with "global remote workers" (too broad) or "total HR budget" (too vague). Use a bottom-up model based on **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)** scaling to **Serviceable Available Market (SAM)**.

*Formula:* `TAM Segment = (Target Companies in US) × (Penetration Rate %) × (Average Contract Value)`

Since you lack the specific US penetration rate, we must bracket it using your current Canadian performance as a proxy, labelled **ASSUMED**.

*Assumptions:*
1.  **Target Universe:** ~30,000 mid-sized US tech/remote-first firms (derived from ~10x Canadian market size, **ASSUMED**).
2.  **Video Penetration:** Your current 800 clients represent a specific saturation. If Canada has ~3,000 target firms, your penetration is ~26%. Applying this to the US: 30,000 × 26% = 7,800 potential clients.
3.  **Live Penetration:** High-touch sales cycles limit this. If you have 40 clients in Canada (~1.3% penetration), US potential is 30,000 × 1.3% = 390 clients.

*Projected 3-Year Revenue Potential (Base Case):*
*   **Asynchronous Video:** 7,800 clients × $2,000 = **$15.6M ARR**.
*   **Live Facilitated:** 390 clients × $15,000 = **$5.85M ARR**.

*Growth Constraint:* Video scales with server costs (near-zero marginal cost); Live scales with headcount (linear marginal cost). To capture the Live TAM, you would need to hire ~350 new facilitators, consuming far more than your $100k budget.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The one input that changes this call is **Enterprise Willingness-to-Pay (WTP) for Live Interaction**. If US enterprises prove they will pay 3× your current rate ($45k+) for live sessions due to compliance or culture mandates, the Live TAM revenue could match Video.
*Trigger:* If you secure 5 US pilot contracts at >$25k each within 90 days, reconsider the split.
*Rule of Thumb:* If Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) for Live exceeds 30% of the first-year contract value, the segment is inefficient for early expansion.
*Next Step:* This week, run a targeted outreach to 50 US HR directors offering a "Live Pilot" at $20k; if conversion is <2%, double down on video.

**Routing Note:**
Expanding from Toronto to the US creates immediate nexus obligations for state sales tax (economic nexus thresholds vary by state, often $100k revenue or 200 transactions) and potential employment law issues if you hire US-based facilitators as contractors. Consult a US-qualified tax attorney to determine which states require registration before you invoice your first US client.
