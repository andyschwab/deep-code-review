**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The **Watt-hour (Wh) rating** of your battery is the single input that dictates the compliance tier.
*   **Threshold:** If your battery is **≤ 100 Wh**, it ships under Section II of IATA regulations (lighter burden). If **> 100 Wh**, it requires full Class 9 Dangerous Goods handling (heavy burden).
*   **Trigger:** Check your battery spec sheet today. If >100 Wh, your costs could double again, and some passenger aircraft may ban them entirely, forcing cargo-only flights.
*   **Action Step:** If your battery is <100 Wh, contact three freight forwarders specializing in "Section II Lithium Batteries" this week to lock in rates; if >100 Wh, consider redesigning the battery pack or switching to sea freight for the initial 2,000 units to preserve cash.
