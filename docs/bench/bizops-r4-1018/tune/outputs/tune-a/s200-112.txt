**The Call**
Proceed with the sliding-scale model immediately; formalizing income-based discounts is a standard charitable activity that strengthens, rather than jeopardizes, your current 501(c)(3) status, provided the scale is written and applied uniformly. **What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call from "safe" to "risky" is **discretionary application**. If therapists grant discounts ad-hoc without documented income verification, the IRS may view this as private benefit to specific individuals rather than a charitable class.
*   **Trigger:** If more than 5% of discounted cases lack signed financial eligibility forms, pause the expansion and audit immediately.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Charitable care must be offered to a "charitable class" (e.g., anyone under 200% of the Federal Poverty Level), not selected individuals.
*   **Next Step:** This week, draft a written "Financial Assistance Policy" defining the income brackets and required documentation (e.g., tax return or pay stub) for the sliding scale; do not offer a single discounted session until this policy is board-approved.
