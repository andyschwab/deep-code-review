**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The deciding factor is not the *percentage* of total revenue (there is no fixed "25% rule" that automatically revokes status), but whether the activity is **"substantially related"** to your exempt purpose.
*   **The Test:** Does charging $200 contribute importantly to the accomplishment of your charitable purpose (employment for disabled individuals) beyond merely raising funds? If the job board is your primary method of advocacy, it may be related (non-taxable). If it functions like a generic commercial job board (e.g., Indeed) that just happens to be run by a non-profit, it is unrelated (taxable).
*   **The Trigger:** If your net profit from this activity exceeds **$1,000** in a tax year, you must file Form 990-T.
*   **Stop/Pivot Rule:** If a tax counsel determines the activity is "unrelated" and the effective tax rate (approx. 21% corporate rate) reduces the net benefit below the administrative burden, **consider** spinning this into a taxable subsidiary or restructuring the fee as a "sponsorship" (which may be non-taxable if no substantial return benefit is provided to the payer).
