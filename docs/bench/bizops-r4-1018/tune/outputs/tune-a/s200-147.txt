**The Call:** Do not absorb the full cost to target a 10% margin; this strategy destroys more absolute profit than passing the price increase, even with a significant volume drop. Your baseline is to pass the €7.50 to the customer immediately, while simultaneously validating the prescription exemption with French counsel to potentially zero out the liability for a segment of your sales.

**The Numbers:**
*Current Baseline (Monthly):*
Revenue: 10,000 units × €65 = €650,000
Net Profit (25%): €162,500
Implied Total Cost (COGS + OpEx): €487,500 (€48.75/unit)

*Scenario A: Absorb Cost (Price €65, Margin ~10%)*
Assumption: You reduce net margin to 10% of revenue.
New Net Profit: €650,000 × 10% = €65,000
**Profit Impact:** -€97,500/month vs. baseline.
*Note:* This assumes volume holds at 10,000 units. If volume dips even slightly due to market noise, profits erode further.

*Scenario B: Pass Full Tax (Price €72.50, Volume -30%)*
New Price: €65 + €7.50 = €72.50
New Volume (Assumed): 10,000 × 0.70 = 7,000 units
New Revenue: 7,000 × €72.50 = €507,500
Total Costs (Assumed Fixed COGS €20 + Variable OpEx scaled):
To compare fairly, we assume the €48.75/unit cost structure includes variable operating expenses that scale with volume.
Adjusted Total Cost: 7,000 × €48.75 = €341,250
Tax Liability: 7,000 × €7.50 = €52,500
New Net Profit: €507,500 - €341,250 - €52,500 = €113,750
**Profit Impact:** -€48,750/month vs. baseline.

*Comparison:* Passing the tax (Scenario B) retains **€48,750** more monthly profit than absorbing it (Scenario A), despite losing 3,000 customers.

*Break-Even Volume for Scenario B:*
To match the current profit of €162,500 under the new tax and price:
Required Contribution = Fixed Costs + Target Profit + Tax
Using unit economics: (Price - Variable Cost - Tax) × Volume = Target Profit + Fixed Portion of OpEx.
Simplified: You need to sell approximately **9,150 units** at €72.50 to match your current absolute profit. Your feared 30% drop (to 7,000) misses this target, but absorbing the cost misses it by a wider margin.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is the **prescription exemption status**.
*Trigger:* If >18% of your current volume can be converted to "prescribed" status (exempting them from the tax), the "Pass Full Tax" strategy becomes superior even with lower volume, as the effective price for those users remains €65.
*Action:* If your legal review confirms OTC products are taxed but prescribed ones are not, consider launching a "Medical Verification" flow on your checkout this week to segregate these customers.
