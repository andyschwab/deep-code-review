**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is the **legal classification of the Income Share Agreement (ISA)**.
*   **Trigger:** If the SEC or a court determines your ISA meets the *Howey Test* criteria (specifically, if students are investing money with an expectation of profits derived from your entrepreneurial efforts), you are operating an unregistered securities offering.
*   **Threshold:** The cost of non-compliance (fines, cease-and-desist orders, restitution) exceeds the $50,000 registration cost you fear.
*   **Actionable Step:** If you cannot obtain a formal legal opinion within 14 days stating your specific ISA structure is *not* a security, consider switching to a standard tuition model with a "pay later" clause that does not contingent on your success in placing them, but rather on their enrollment.
