**The Call**
Keep your current ratable recognition policy; do not accelerate revenue to improve reported financials for your Series A. Under US GAAP (ASC 606), revenue for SaaS subscriptions must be recognized over the period the service is provided, regardless of when cash is received or when implementation occurs, unless the implementation constitutes a distinct performance obligation. Accelerating revenue without a distinct separable service component is non-compliant and creates significant audit risk that could derail your fundraising.

**The Numbers**
*Current Policy (Ratable):*
Formula: `Annual Contract Value (ACV) / 12 months`
- Single-year deal: $15,000 / 12 = **$1,250/month**
- Multi-year deal: $135,000 / 36 months = **$3,750/month**

*Hypothetical Accelerated Policy (Incorrect):*
If you attempted to recognize 50% upfront due to implementation:
- Multi-year deal: ($135,000 * 0.50) = $67,500 Month 1 + ($67,500 / 35) = $1,928/month thereafter.
- **Result:** This creates a artificial spike in Month 1 revenue that does not match the transfer of control of the software service over time.

*Runway Context:*
Cash: $1,200,000
Burn: $80,000/month
Current Runway: **15 months**
*Note:* Changing revenue recognition affects the *income statement* (profitability), not the *cash flow statement*. It will not extend your 15-month runway since the cash from multi-year deals is likely already collected or billed, and recognition is a non-cash accounting entry.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The only factor that changes the call is if your "implementation work" is a **distinct performance obligation** separate from the software access.
- **Trigger:** If the implementation is highly customized, sold separately to other customers, and the customer could benefit from it without the ongoing subscription, it *might* be recognized upon completion (point in time).
- **Rule of Thumb:** If the implementation is standard onboarding required to make the SaaS usable (which is typical for API testing tools), it is bundled with the subscription and must be recognized ratably.
- **Action Step:** If your implementation involves custom code development specific to one client rather than standard configuration, document the scope immediately; otherwise, assume it is bundled.

**Routing Note**
Revenue recognition under ASC 606 is a binding accounting determination. Before finalizing your Series A audit or changing any policies, you must consult your external auditor or a CPA specializing in SaaS. Specifically ask: "Does our specific implementation workflow qualify as a distinct performance obligation under ASC 606-10-25, or is it a fulfillment cost bundled with the subscription?" Do not make this change without written confirmation from your auditor, as restatements during due diligence are fatal to fundraising.
