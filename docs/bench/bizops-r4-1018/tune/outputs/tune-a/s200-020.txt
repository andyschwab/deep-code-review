**Routing Note (Securities & Legal):**
Presenting unfulfilled pre-orders as "revenue" in a pitch deck is a material misrepresentation under securities laws (SEC Rule 10b-5 in the US), potentially constituting fraud. A SAFE note is a security; selling it requires strict adherence to disclosure rules. You must engage a securities attorney to:
1.  Draft specific risk factors regarding the unfulfilled nature of the orders.
2.  Ensure your pitch deck language distinguishes between "bookings" and "GAAP revenue."
3.  Confirm that standard SAFE templates (like Y Combinator's) are sufficient or if side letters are needed to address the hardware-specific fulfillment risks. Do not use a generic template without counsel review given the magnitude of the pre-order book relative to your raise.
