**The Call**
Do not allocate the $100,000 based on global remote worker counts; that top-down approach is too noisy to distinguish between video and live formats. Instead, build a bottom-up model using "HR Training Budget per Employee" as your primary multiplier, applied separately to the specific sub-segments of remote teams that historically buy asynchronous vs. live solutions. Your current data suggests live services have 7.5x higher ARPU but 20x lower volume; the market sizing must reveal if the pool of buyers willing to pay $15k is large enough to justify the sales effort compared to the $2k volume game.

**The Numbers**
Use a bottom-up TAM formula: `TAM = (Target Companies) × (Penetration Rate) × (Average Contract Value)`.
Since you lack the specific count of US remote-first companies, you must derive the inputs:
1.  **Target Companies:** Instead of "global remote workers," estimate the number of US companies with 50–500 employees (your likely fit) that are >40% remote.
    *   *Assumption (ASSUMED):* There are ~30,000 mid-market US firms with significant remote populations.
2.  **Segment Split:** Divide the target by willingness to pay for format.
    *   *Assumption (ASSUMED):* 80% prefer asynchronous (video) due to scale; 20% prefer live facilitation for culture building.
3.  **Average Contract Value (ACV):** Use your current pricing as the ceiling, adjusted for US market premiums.
    *   *Video ACV:* $2,000 (Base) to $2,500 (High).
    *   *Live ACV:* $15,000 (Base) to $18,000 (High).

*Computed Illustrative TAM:*
*   **Video Segment:** 30,000 firms × 80% × $2,000 = **$48 Million**.
*   **Live Segment:** 30,000 firms × 20% × $15,000 = **$90 Million**.

*Note on Reliability:* The "HR Budget" data point is more reliable than "worker count" because it filters for intent to pay. However, without verified US-specific firm counts, these figures are illustrative only.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The deciding input is the **Sales Cycle Length and Cost of Acquisition (CAC)** for the live segment. If acquiring a $15k live client takes 6+ months and costs >$4k in sales effort, the effective net value drops below the video segment despite the larger theoretical TAM.
*   **Trigger:** If your sales team cannot close 2 live deals per quarter within the first 6 months of US entry, pivot the $100k budget entirely to video localization and automated sales funnels.
*   **Step this week:** If you have <5 qualified US leads for live sessions, consider running a $5k targeted ad campaign to test demand elasticity before building new features.
