**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The deciding factor is the **refundability and contract term certainty**. If the chains can delist you with 30 days' notice and keep the fee, you must expense the $60,000 immediately because the future economic benefit is not guaranteed.
*Trigger:* Review the "Termination" and "Refund" clauses in your signed contracts. If fees are non-refundable regardless of performance, stick to the 24-month amortization. If fees are refundable pro-rata upon early termination, switch to immediate expensing or amortize over the minimum non-cancellable period.
*Rule of Thumb:* Match the expense recognition to the period you actually expect to sell the product.
*Step this week:* If your contracts are non-cancellable for 12 months, consider amortizing over 12 months instead of 24 to be conservative for investors.
