**The Call**
Do not establish a German subsidiary yet; for a single $60k contract, the cost ($40k) destroys unit economics. Proceed by deploying infrastructure to the AWS Frankfurt region (`eu-central-1`) and executing a robust Data Processing Agreement (DPA), but treat the "local entity" requirement as a specific legal negotiation point, not an automatic regulatory trigger.

**The Numbers**
*   **Contract Value:** $60,000/year.
*   **Subsidiary Setup Cost:** $40,000 (one-time).
*   **Immediate Impact:** Setting up the entity consumes **26.7%** of your total cash reserves ($40k / $150k) against only **40%** of one year's revenue from this deal.
*   **Break-even on Entity Cost:** You would need to retain this customer for **8 months** just to recoup the setup fee, ignoring ongoing local administrative/tax filing costs (assumed **$15k/year**, marked ASSUMED).
*   **Alternative Cost:** AWS data transfer and region premium (assumed **10%** increase over us-east-1, marked ASSUMED) + Legal review of DPA (assumed **$5k**, marked ASSUMED) = ~**$11,000** total first-year compliance cost.
*   **Result:** The "local entity" path burns cash 3.6x faster than the "local hosting + contract" path for this specific deal size.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes this call is **customer leverage regarding "Permanent Establishment" (PE) risk**. German tax authorities may deem you to have a taxable presence if your sales activities in Germany are habitual, not just because you host data there.
*   **Trigger:** If the customer's legal team insists that *only* a local entity can sign the DPA or indemnify them under local law, or if you plan to hire sales staff in Germany.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Data residency (where bits live) rarely mandates corporate residency (where the company sits) for B2B SaaS unless local consumer protection or specific sector laws (e.g., health/finance) apply.
*   **Step this week:** If the customer rejects a Delaware entity signing a German-law DPA, consider engaging a "Employer of Record" or local fiscal representative instead of a full subsidiary.
