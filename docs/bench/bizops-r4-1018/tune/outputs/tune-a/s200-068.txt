**The Call**
Do not proceed with the immediate $600k expansion; your current cash reserves cannot support the aggressive move without a bridge round. The math shows a cash deficit within 7–8 months under the expansion scenario, well before the new revenue can offset the increased burn. Secure a bridge round *before* signing leases or hiring, or delay expansion until organic growth increases your cash buffer.

**The Numbers**
*Current State (No Expansion):*
- Cash: $1.2M
- Burn: $75k/mo
- Revenue: $90k/mo (growing 10% MoM)
- *Note:* You are currently cash-flow positive ($90k - $75k = +$15k/mo). Runway is effectively infinite assuming growth continues, as you are generating cash.

*Expansion Scenario (Immediate Hire):*
- Upfront Cost: $600k (Month 0)
- New Monthly Burn: $135k/mo
- Revenue Lag: ASSUMED 6 months before new hires generate incremental revenue (standard engineering ramp).
- Month 0 Cash: $1.2M - $600k = $600k.
- Months 1–6 Cash Flow: $90k (base revenue, growing) - $135k (burn).
  - Month 1 Net: -$45k
  - Month 6 Net (approx): -$30k (due to 10% organic growth on base revenue)
  - *Estimated 6-month cash drain:* ~$225k (conservative average).
- Cash Remaining at Month 6: $600k - $225k = $375k.
- Post-Lag Burn: $135k. To survive 6 more months (to reach Month 12), you need $810k. You have $375k.
- **Result:** You run out of cash in Month 8–9, not 12.

*Assumptions:*
- Revenue from new engineers starts Month 7 (ASSUMED).
- No additional churn or cost overruns.
- 10% MoM growth applies only to existing base until new team ramps.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes this call is the **revenue ramp time**. If your new engineers can generate billable value in **3 months** instead of 6, the cash drain reduces by ~$100k, pushing failure to Month 10—still short of 12 months, but closer.
*Trigger:* If you cannot close a bridge round of at least $1.5M within 60 days, you must delay the expansion.
*Rule of Thumb:* Never commit to a fixed cost increase that reduces your runway below 18 months when entering a new market.
*Step this week:* If you have term sheets or investor interest, initiate the bridge round immediately; if not, freeze the hiring plan and model a "soft launch" with 1–2 hires instead of 5.
