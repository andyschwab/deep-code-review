**The call.** Reject the dedicated sales team; the market math shows it drains your runway in under five months even at 100% market penetration, making a self-serve model the only viable path.

**The numbers.**
*Total Addressable Revenue (TAR):* 1,200 candidates × $1,500 price = $1,800,000 (theoretical max).
*Projected Annual Revenue (Self-Serve):* 1,200 candidates × 5% conversion × $1,500 = **$90,000**.
*Projected Annual Revenue (Sales Team):* Even assuming a sales team doubles conversion to 10% (optimistic ASSUMED), revenue = $180,000.

*Unit Economics & Runway:*
*   **Self-Serve Model:**
    *   Revenue: $90,000/year ($7,500/month).
    *   Costs: $10,000/month (ops) + $0 (sales) = $10,000/month.
    *   Monthly Burn: $2,500.
    *   Runway: $100,000 / $2,500 = **40 months**.
*   **Sales Team Model:**
    *   Revenue: $180,000/year ($15,000/month) [Assumes 10% conversion].
    *   Costs: $10,000 (ops) + $15,000 (sales team: $180k/yr) = $25,000/month.
    *   Monthly Burn: $10,000.
    *   Runway: $100,000 / $10,000 = **10 months**.
    *   *Reality Check:* If the sales team only achieves the base 5% conversion ($90k revenue), monthly burn rises to $17,500, destroying runway in **5.7 months**.

**What flips it, and the trigger.**
The single input that changes the call is the **conversion rate**. To justify the $180,000 sales cost, you must generate at least $270,000 in annual revenue ($22,500/month) to cover the $25,000 monthly burn and break even.
*   *Break-even Conversion:* $270,000 / ($1,500 × 1,200 candidates) = **15% conversion**.
*   *Trigger:* If you cannot prove a path to 15% conversion (tripling your current estimate) within 90 days, the sales team is mathematically impossible.
*   *Rule of Thumb:* In niche B2C education, sales teams rarely exceed 2-3x organic conversion without enterprise contracts.
*   *Step this week:* If your current funnel data shows <8% conversion, consider pausing all hiring and optimizing the self-serve landing page for the 5% baseline.
