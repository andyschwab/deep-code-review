**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that flips this from "profitable" to "existential threat" is **coverage denial**. If an adverse event occurs and the insurer denies the claim based on "off-label combination" exclusions or "marketing misrepresentation" (using the word "accelerated"), your liability shifts from the insurer to your clinic's assets.
*   **Trigger:** If your insurance carrier provides written confirmation that the rider *explicitly* covers the specific drug combinations you intend to use AND your attorney approves the term "accelerated" as non-misleading under Florida Board of Medicine rules, proceed.
