Reject the universal 40% price hike immediately; it mathematically guarantees insolvency given your concentration risk. Instead, implement a "Good-Better-Best" packaging shift: grandfather the 30% beta cohort at current rates (capped) while applying a 25% increase to the remaining 70% and all new logos. This captures ~80% of the required revenue uplift while keeping aggregate churn below your 5% survival threshold.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that changes this call is the **price elasticity of your non-beta customers**. If their churn exceeds 6% in response to a 25% hike, the strategy fails.
*   **Trigger Date:** End of Month 1 post-announcement.
*   **Stop/Pivot Rule:** If cancellations from the standard cohort exceed 8 nodes (approx 6%) in the first 30 days, freeze pricing and pivot to upselling "Enterprise" features (SSO, SLA) instead of raising base seat costs.
