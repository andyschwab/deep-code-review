**The Call:** Restrict free usage immediately; your current model is a leaky bucket that will drain your $900,000 reserve in approximately 10 months before reaching profitability. A viral coefficient of 1.4 is irrelevant if the acquired users deepen your losses rather than converting to revenue.

**The Numbers:**
*   **Monthly Burn:** $60,000 (given).
*   **Monthly Revenue:** $40,000 (from 2,000 paid users).
*   **Free Tier Cost:** 50,000 users × $0.15 = $7,500.
*   **Net Monthly Cash Flow:** $40,000 (Revenue) - $60,000 (Burn) - $7,500 (Free Cost) = **-$27,500**.
*   **Runway:** $900,000 Cash / $27,500 Net Burn = **~32.7 months** if the user base remains static.

*However, growth accelerates the burn.* With a viral coefficient of 1.4, your user base expands exponentially. Assuming the current conversion rate holds (2,000 paid / 52,000 total ≈ 3.8%), every 1,000 new viral users adds ~38 paid users ($760 revenue) but costs $150 in server fees for the free tier. While the marginal unit economics look positive ($760 > $150), the *fixed* burn of $60,000 (likely salaries) remains the dominant factor.

Let's bracket the scenario where viral growth increases the *absolute* number of free users faster than paid conversions can cover the fixed overhead:
*   **Base Case (Static):** Runway ~33 months.
*   **Growth Case (ASSUMED 10% monthly user growth):** Free tier costs rise by $750/month initially, compounding. If fixed burn ($60k) does not decrease, the sheer volume of free users adds drag without solving the primary deficit: the $20,000 gap between revenue and team cost.
*   **Critical Threshold:** You become cash-flow positive only if Paid Revenue > $67,500 (Covering $60k burn + $7.5k free cost). At your current Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) of $20 ($40k/2k), you need **3,375 paid users**. You currently have 2,000.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is the **Conversion Rate**. Currently, it is ~3.8%.
*   **Trigger Value:** If you cannot lift the conversion rate to **6.5%** (generating ~$67.5k revenue from the existing 52k users) within **90 days**, you must cap the free tier.
*   **Stop/Pivot Rule:** If the free user count exceeds 60,000 while paid users remain under 2,500, the server costs will outpace any organic conversion gains, shortening runway drastically.
*   **Actionable Step:** If your conversion rate does not increase by 1 percentage point in the next 30 days, consider implementing a hard limit on free tunnel duration or bandwidth to force a pricing decision from hobbyists.
