**The Call:** Unbundle professional services immediately; do not raise the base subscription price yet. Your current model subsidizes high-touch onboarding with recurring revenue, destroying unit economics. At 20 hours, your service cost ($3,000) exceeds your first-year gross profit from the subscription alone, making the payback period infinite under the current structure.

**The Numbers:**
*   **Current Unit Economics (Per Customer):**
    *   Annual Subscription Revenue: $2,500 × 12 = $30,000.
    *   Subscription Gross Profit (72% margin): $30,000 × 0.72 = $21,600.
    *   Service Cost (20 hrs × $150/hr): $3,000.
    *   *Adjusted* Annual Gross Profit: $21,600 - $3,000 = $18,600.
    *   Monthly Churn: 3% (implies ~28-month average lifetime).
    *   **Lifetime Value (LTV):** $18,600/year ÷ 12 months × 28 months ≈ **$43,400**.
    *   **Payback Period:** Since the $3,000 service cost is incurred upfront (Month 1), and monthly contribution margin is $1,800 ($21,600/12), you recover the service cost in **1.7 months**. *However*, this assumes the service cost is the only acquisition cost. If you have sales commissions or marketing spend, add those to the numerator.

*   **Option A: Increase Base Price to $3,500/mo**
    *   New Annual Revenue: $42,000.
    *   New Gross Profit (72%): $30,240.
    *   Less Service Cost: $3,000.
    *   New Annual Contribution: $27,240.
    *   *Risk:* Price elasticity unknown; may increase churn beyond 3%.

*   **Option B: Unbundle Services (Recommended)**
    *   Keep Subscription at $2,500/mo.
    *   Charge for Onboarding: 20 hrs × $200/hr (blended rate) = $4,000 one-time fee.
    *   Subscription LTV (no service deduction): $21,600/year ÷ 12 × 28 ≈ **$50,400**.
    *   Service Profit: $4,000 - $3,000 = $1,000 immediate profit.
    *   *Result:* Protects recurring margin and monetizes the complexity driving the hours up.

*   **Option C: Reduce Included Hours to 5**
    *   Forces customers to pay overage or succeed with less.
    *   Risk: Failed onboarding leads to higher early churn (>3%), collapsing LTV.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The deciding input is **Churn Rate**. If unbundling causes churn to spike above **5.5% monthly**, the loss in lifetime value outweighs the service recovery.
*   *Trigger:* Monitor churn weekly for the next two cohorts. If new cohort churn >1.5% in their first 30 days post-unbundling, pause the fee and revert to a capped inclusion model.
*   *Action this week:* Draft a "Standard Onboarding Package" definition (5 hours included, remainder billed at $200/hr) and present it to your top 5 newest clients as a pilot before rolling out broadly.

**Weekly Metrics to Track:**
1.  **Services Hours per New Logo:** Track actuals vs. the 20-hour trend.
2.  **Time-to-Value:** Days from contract to first successful dashboard usage (correlates with churn).
3.  **Net Revenue Retention (NRR):** Ensure upsells aren't masking the service bleed.
