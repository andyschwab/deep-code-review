**The Call:** Reject the flat fee increase to $35 as the primary strategy; instead, launch the tiered model ($15/$35/$75) immediately, but only if you can convert at least **13.5%** of your base to the Elite tier while migrating 50% to Basic. The flat hike risks a catastrophic churn spike among price-sensitive users that the tiered model mitigates by offering a down-sell option, preserving your top-line revenue even with lower average prices for some.

**The Numbers:**
*Current Baseline:*
Revenue: 8,000 members × $25 = **$200,000/mo**.
Variable Costs: Assumed near-zero per user (digital community); Fixed Costs (Server/Content) = $12,000. (Team costs are fixed overhead, excluded from contribution margin per unit).
Current Contribution Margin: **$188,000/mo**.

*Option A: Flat Fee Hike ($35)*
Formula: $35 × (8,000 × (1 - Churn Spike)).
To beat current revenue ($200k), you need >5,715 members.
Break-even Retention: You can lose up to **28.5%** of your members and still match current revenue.
Risk: If churn spikes >28.5% due to price shock, you lose money vs. status quo.

*Option B: Tiered Model ($15 Basic, $35 Premium, $75 Elite)*
Assumptions (marked ASSUMED):
1.  **Basic Migration:** 50% of users (4,000) drop to $15 (price-sensitive retention).
2.  **Premium Stay:** 30% of users (2,400) stay at $35 (status quo equivalent).
3.  **Elite Upgrade:** X% of users upgrade to $75.
4.  **Churn:** Negligible initial churn due to down-sell option.

Formula for Revenue: $(4,000 × 15) + (2,400 × 35) + (8,000 × X% × 75)$.
Current Revenue Target: $200,000.
Fixed Revenue from Basic+Premium: $60,000 + $84,000 = $144,000.
Gap to Fill: $200,000 - $144,000 = $56,000.
Required Elite Revenue: $56,000.
Users needed at $75: $56,000 / $75 = 747 users.
**Minimum Conversion Rate:** 747 / 8,000 = **9.3%**.

*Refined Scenario (Your 20% Hypothesis):*
If 20% (1,600 users) hit Elite:
Revenue = $60k (Basic) + $84k (Prem) + $120k (Elite) = **$264,000**.
New Contribution Margin: $264,000 - $12,000 = **$252,000**.
Upside: +34% margin increase over status quo.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that flips the decision is the **Elite Conversion Rate**.
*Trigger Threshold:* If your pilot survey or soft-launch shows <**9.3%** uptake for the $75 tier, the tiered model underperforms the current baseline (assuming the 50% drop to Basic holds).
*Rule of Thumb:* If <10% of users value "Elite" features at 3x the base price, your segmentation is too weak; revert to a simple $30 flat fee instead.
*Action This Week:* Run a Van Westendorp price sensitivity survey specifically on the "Elite" feature set. If >15% say $75 is "cheap" or "a bargain," proceed with the full tiered launch.
