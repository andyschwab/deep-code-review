**The Call:** Do not raise the base price yet; instead, implement a "Standard vs. Rush" tiered pricing model with adjusted payment terms to isolate the cost of delays from your core value proposition. Raising the base price by 6.7% (to ~$4,800) mathematically covers your fixed administrative increase assuming current volume, but it penalizes all customers for a bottleneck that only affects timing, risking churn in a price-sensitive travel market.

**The Numbers:**
Your current unit economics per student:
*   **Revenue:** $4,500
*   **Margin (20%):** $900
*   **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS):** $3,600

**The Problem:** You have a new fixed cost of $1,500/month. To cover this purely through a base price hike, we must assume a monthly cohort size.
*   *ASSUMED Volume:* 10 students/month (Low), 20 students/month (Base), 30 students/month (High).
*   *Required Price Increase per Student:* $1,500 / Volume.
    *   Low (10): +$150/student (New Price: $4,650)
    *   Base (20): +$75/student (New Price: $4,575)
    *   High (30): +$50/student (New Price: $4,550)

**Scenario Modeling:**
1.  **Base Price Increase:** Add $75–$150 to all bookings.
    *   *Result:* Covers admin costs immediately.
    *   *Risk:* Reduces competitiveness against programs without visa delays.
2.  **Rush Processing Fee (Recommended):** Keep base at $4,500. Add a $250 "Expedited Visa Handling" fee for students needing confirmation in <8 weeks.
    *   *Logic:* If 6 out of 20 students pay this, you generate $1,500, covering the admin hike without alienating the 14 price-sensitive planners.
3.  **Payment Terms Adjustment:** Shift from deposit-based to 100% upfront at booking (9 weeks prior).
    *   *Cash Flow Impact:* Eliminates the "squeezed cash flow" by holding the capital longer, offsetting the opportunity cost of the delay without changing the sticker price.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The deciding input is your **monthly booking volume**. If your volume drops below 10 students/month, a fee-based model fails to cover the $1,500 fixed cost unless the fee exceeds $150, which may be unacceptable.
*   *Trigger:* If next month's bookings are <12 students, switch to a mandatory base price increase of $125 immediately.
*   *Rule of Thumb:* If administrative costs exceed 5% of total cohort revenue, the base price must change.
