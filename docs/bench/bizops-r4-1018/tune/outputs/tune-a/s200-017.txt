**The Call**
Collect a valid resale certificate from each designer before their first tax-exempt order and verify it immediately; do not rely on a one-time collection without validation, as you are legally liable for uncollected sales tax plus penalties if the certificate is invalid. You must consult a tax professional to establish your verification protocol before onboarding your first ten partners, specifically to address multi-state nexus rules.

**The Numbers**
*Liability Exposure Formula:* `Total Exposure = (Uncollected Tax Rate × Total Taxable Sales) + Penalties + Interest`
*User Figures:* 50 orders/month × $2,500 AOV = $125,000 monthly volume.
*Assumptions (ASSUMED):* Average combined sales tax rate of 8% across your five target states; penalty rates of 10–25% depending on state negligence rules.
*Computed Results:*
*   **Base Case (One invalid certificate ignored for 1 year):** $125,000 × 12 months × 8% = $120,000 uncollected tax.
*   **Penalty Impact:** $120,000 + (20% penalty) = **$144,000 total liability** for a single bad account over a year.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes your risk profile is the **state of the buyer**. If a designer is in a state where you have "nexus" (physical presence or economic threshold), you are strictly liable for verifying their exemption.
*   **Trigger:** If you process $125,000 in sales to a single state in a quarter (hitting economic nexus thresholds in many states), your verification burden increases from "good practice" to "audit priority."
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Treat every out-of-state reseller as if you have nexus until a pro confirms otherwise.
*   **This Week's Step:** If you have not yet signed a resale certificate verification policy, consider pausing tax-exempt sales until a CPA reviews your current certificate forms against the laws of your five target states.
