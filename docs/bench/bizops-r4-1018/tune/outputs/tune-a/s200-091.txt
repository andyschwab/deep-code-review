**The Call**
You cannot justify a 10x valuation leap on last year's 2x revenue growth alone; the narrative must shift from "historical performance" to "future monopoly potential" supported by unit economics that prove the 4% monthly churn is a temporary friction point, not a structural leak. If your LTV:CAC ratio is below 3:1 or your net revenue retention (NRR) is under 100%, the $100M valuation is mathematically indefensible regardless of the story.

**The Numbers**
*   **Revenue Growth:** $1.2M → $2.4M (100% YoY).
*   **Valuation Multiple Shift:** Pre-money ~$8M (8x ARR) → Post-money $100M (41.7x ARR).
*   **Burn Rate Estimate:** 15 employees × $180k = $2.7M/year (ASSUMED: no other major costs).
*   **Churn Impact:** 4% monthly churn compounds to ~38% annual churn.
    *   *Formula:* Annual Retention = $(1 - 0.04)^{12} \approx 0.61$.
    *   *Result:* You lose 39% of your starting revenue every year just to churn.
*   **Net Revenue Retention (NRR) Threshold:** To justify a 40x multiple, your NRR must exceed 130% (expansion revenue must outpace the 39% churn significantly).
    *   *Calculation:* If you start with $1.2M, lose $468k to churn, you must add >$948k in upsells/new sales just to hit $1.7M organic growth, let alone double.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)**.
*   **The Trigger:** If your NRR is <110%, the valuation is unsupported. If NRR >130%, the market may accept the multiple based on "land and expand" dynamics.
*   **Decision Rule:** A SaaS company commands a 40x ARR multiple only if it demonstrates >100% YoY growth *projected* for the next 24 months, driven by expansion revenue, not just new logos.
*   **Step This Week:** Calculate your expansion revenue (upsells/cross-sells) separately from new logo revenue. If expansion is <20% of total new ARR, pivot the narrative to "product-market fit in a new vertical" rather than "efficient scaling."

**Narrative Arguments for the Investor**
Since the math currently favors the skeptic, your narrative must rely on three non-revenue indicators:
1.  **Proprietary Moat:** Demonstrate that your rate-limiting technology handles throughput competitors cannot match at the same cost basis, creating a switching cost that will naturally suppress churn from 4% to <1.5% within 12 months.
2.  **Pipeline Conversion:** Present signed Letters of Intent (LOIs) or pilot conversions that are not yet in ARR but represent $5M+ in committed future revenue, effectively arguing that the "real" ARR is already $5M+.
3.  **Market Expansion:** Argue that the $2.4M ARR captures only a niche; the Series A funds entry into a TAM (Total Addressable Market) 10x larger, where the current tech stack provides a first-mover advantage that justifies the premium.
