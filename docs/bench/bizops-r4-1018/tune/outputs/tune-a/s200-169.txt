**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is **transaction initiation**. Under UK law (PSR 2017), providing information (AIS) is regulated but often exempt for pure data; initiating payments (PIS) or holding funds strictly requires FCA authorization.
*   **Trigger Value:** If your API integration allows a "Send Money" button that moves funds without the user logging into the bank's native app to authorize the specific transaction via the bank's own interface, you are likely acting as a Payment Institution.
*   **Stop/Pivot Date:** Before your next funding round due diligence (typically 4-6 weeks out). Investors will not fund a company with an unresolved regulatory classification risk.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If you touch the money flow, you are regulated. If you only touch the data stream, you may be an exempt agent.
*   **This Week's Step:** If your engineering team has implemented any function that constructs a payment order rather than just fetching a balance, consider flagging that feature as "beta/restricted" until counsel reviews the specific API endpoints against the FCA's "agent" guidance.
