**The Call**
Your unit economics have broken: at $7.50 variable cost, your contribution margin is only 50%, leaving $15,000 monthly to cover $18,000 in fixed costs, resulting in a $3,000 monthly loss. You must immediately introduce a tiered pricing model (Good-Better-Best) based on storage volume or commit depth to restore profitability; flat pricing is unsustainable under current usage patterns.

**The Numbers**
*Formula:* Monthly Profit = (Price − Variable Cost) × Repositories − Fixed Costs.
*Current State:* ($15.00 − $7.50) × 2,000 − $18,000 = **−$3,000**.
*Break-Even Analysis:* To cover $18,000 fixed costs with 2,000 repos, you need $9.00 contribution per repo.
*Threshold:* Margin turns negative when Variable Cost exceeds **$6.00 per repo** (assuming $15 price).
*Runway:* With $110,000 cash and a $3,000 monthly burn, you have **~36 months** of runway if costs/revenue remain static, but this ignores growth or further cost inflation.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is **Variable Cost per Repo**.
*Trigger:* If average repo size grows another 33% (to ~133MB) without a price increase, assuming linear cost scaling, your variable cost hits $10, making the loss $6,000/month.
*Rule of Thumb:* Never let variable costs exceed 40% of price in SaaS infrastructure models.
*Action This Week:* If your average repo size is >80MB, consider implementing a "Standard" tier cap at 75MB and an "Enterprise" tier with overage fees for binary assets.
