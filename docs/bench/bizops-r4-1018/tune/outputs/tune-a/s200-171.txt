**The Call**
Accepting this term sheet implies a **20% dilution** of your company; this falls squarely within the standard 15–25% range for Series A rounds, making the equity loss mathematically reasonable provided your unit economics hold up during the planned international expansion.

**The Numbers**
Dilution is calculated based on the relationship between the new investment and the post-money valuation.

*   **Formula:** `Dilution % = Investment Amount / (Pre-Money Valuation + Investment Amount)`
*   **Your Figures:**
    *   Investment: €4,000,000
    *   Pre-Money Valuation: €16,000,000
    *   Post-Money Valuation: €16,000,000 + €4,000,000 = €20,000,000
*   **Calculation:** €4,000,000 / €20,000,000 = **0.20 (20%)**

If you currently own 100% of the company (assuming no prior investors), your stake drops to 80%. If you have existing investors, everyone's percentage ownership is reduced by a factor of 0.8.

*Context on your runway:* With a €120k monthly burn and 8 months of runway, your current cash reserve is **€960,000**. The new €4M capital extends your theoretical runway to roughly **41 months** (€4.96M total / €120k), assuming burn remains flat. However, since you plan an "aggressive marketing campaign," your burn will likely increase. To maintain a healthy 18–24 month runway post-raise, your new monthly burn can safely rise to between **€200k and €275k**.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The deal flips from "reasonable" to "dangerous" if your **LTV:CAC ratio drops below 2.5x** during the expansion. Entering Southeast Asia and Latin America often requires lower pricing (reducing Lifetime Value) and higher education costs (increasing Customer Acquisition Cost).
*   **Current Unit Economics Snapshot:**
    *   Monthly Revenue per User: €89
    *   Churn: 3.5% (implies average life of ~29 months)
    *   Rough LTV (uncounted discount rate): €89 * 29 ≈ **€2,581**
*   **Trigger:** If your blended CAC in new markets exceeds **€1,030** (assuming a target 2.5x LTV:CAC), the growth strategy destroys value faster than the capital can support it.
*   **Action:** Before signing, model your CAC sensitivity. If CAC in target regions is >€1,000, consider piloting in one region first rather than an simultaneous aggressive launch.

**Routing Note**
While the dilution math is standard, the **term sheet itself is a securities offering document**. Do not sign without legal counsel reviewing: (1) liquidation preferences (ensure they are 1x non-participating), (2) board composition changes, and (3) any anti-dilution clauses that could worsen your position in a down round.
