**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call from "proceed with caution" to "halt immediately" is the **promise of outcomes**. If your marketing claims specific developmental results (e.g., "Guaranteed sleep training in 3 days") rather than access to expertise, you trigger strict scrutiny under the UK Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008.
*   **Trigger:** If your current ad copy or landing page promises a specific result rather than access to a service, pause the campaign.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Marketing must sell the *service* (access to experts), not the *outcome* (child behavior changes).
*   **Step this week:** Audit all landing pages and ads. If any phrase guarantees a result, replace it with "access to expert guidance" and add a disclaimer: "Advice is for educational purposes and does not replace medical diagnosis."
