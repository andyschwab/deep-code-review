**The Regulatory Line & Criteria:**
The FDA distinguishes supplements from drugs based on **intended use** and **claims**, not just the physical act of mixing.
1.  **Intended Use (The "Drug" Trigger):** If your quiz implies a diagnosis (e.g., "You have a magnesium deficiency") or if your packaging/marketing claims to treat, cure, or prevent a specific disease, the product is legally a drug. Supplements can only support general structure/function (e.g., "supports bone health").
2.  **Compounding vs. Manufacturing:** Traditional compounding (pharmacy-level, patient-specific, based on a practitioner's prescription) is distinct from mass-market customization. If your algorithm generates recommendations without a licensed healthcare provider reviewing each case, the FDA may view this as manufacturing a new drug formulation for each user without approval.
3.  **Dosage & Ingredients:** Using ingredients at dosages significantly higher than the Tolerable Upper Intake Level (UL) or including ingredients not on the FDA's "GRAS" (Generally Recognized As Safe) or approved supplement lists triggers immediate enforcement.
4.  **The Quiz as Diagnosis:** If your quiz output tells a user they *have* a condition or *need* a specific therapeutic dose to fix a symptom, this constitutes a medical diagnosis. A compliant model frames outputs as "suggestions for nutritional support" based on lifestyle factors, not medical needs.

**What Flips It & The Trigger:**
The single input that changes your status from "risky supplement" to "unapproved drug" is **the presence of disease-specific claims or diagnostic language in your quiz logic or marketing materials.**
*   **Trigger:** If your current copy uses words like "treat," "cure," "diagnose," "deficiency," or names specific diseases (e.g., "for arthritis" or "to lower blood pressure"), you are already non-compliant.
*   **Stop/Pivot Rule:** If your legal review confirms your quiz acts as a diagnostic tool without physician oversight, you must pivot immediately to a "wellness-only" recommendation engine or integrate a licensed provider review step (telehealth model) before your next funding round.
