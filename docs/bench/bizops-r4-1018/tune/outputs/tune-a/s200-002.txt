**The Call**
Classifying nutrient pods as a "subscription" versus a "recurring order" generally **does not** change your sales tax collection rate or filing frequency in California, Texas, New York, Florida, or Illinois, because the tax liability is driven by the physical nature of the goods (tangible personal property), not the billing cadence. Your advisors are likely conflating *nexus* thresholds (which depend on total sales volume) with *taxability* rules (which depend on the product type). For physical goods like nutrient pods, you collect sales tax in all five states once you cross their economic nexus thresholds, regardless of whether the customer buys monthly or annually.

**The Numbers & Rules**
Since you provided no sales volume figures, I cannot calculate your specific nexus status. Instead, here are the economic nexus thresholds that trigger the *obligation* to register and collect tax in each state. Once registered, the tax rate applies to the transaction price of the pods whether billed as a subscription or individual item.

| State | Nexus Threshold (Sales) | Nexus Threshold (Transactions) | Tax Treatment of Pods |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **California** | $500,000 | None | Taxable as tangible property. |
| **Texas** | $500,000 | None | Taxable as tangible property. |
| **New York** | $500,000 | 100 transactions | Taxable as tangible property. |
| **Florida** | $100,000 | None | Taxable as tangible property. |
| **Illinois** | $100,000 | 200 transactions | Taxable as tangible property. |

*Assumption:* The nutrient pods are physical items shipped to the customer. If they were purely digital codes, the analysis would shift to digital goods taxability, which varies by state.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The one input that changes the call is your **gross revenue into each specific state**.
*   **Trigger:** If your sales into New York exceed $500,000 *or* you complete 100 separate transactions there, you must register.
*   **Pivot:** If you mistakenly treat the subscription as a "service" (non-taxable) rather than the sale of tangible goods (taxable), you will under-collect tax. The billing label ("subscription") does not exempt physical goods from sales tax in these jurisdictions.
*   **Step:** If your year-to-date sales in any of these five states exceed 50% of the thresholds listed above, consider engaging a tax automation tool or CPA immediately to prepare for registration before the next quarter ends.

**Routing Note**
While the general rule is that physical goods are taxable regardless of billing frequency, the specific application of "bundling" (hardware + pods) at the point of sale versus separating them can alter the taxable base in states like Illinois and California. You must bring the exact invoice structure and shipping terms to a licensed CPA or sales tax attorney in each jurisdiction to confirm if the initial hardware shipment creates immediate nexus or if the recurring pods alter the "true object" of the transaction.
