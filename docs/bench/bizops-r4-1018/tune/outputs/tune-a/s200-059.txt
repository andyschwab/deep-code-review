**The Call**
Do not match the competitor's flat rate broadly; instead, implement a tiered volume discount immediately for your top 10 customers while maintaining current pricing for the rest. A blanket 15% price cut destroys your contribution margin too aggressively without a guaranteed volume spike, whereas a targeted tiered model protects your core yield while securing your largest revenue block. Double down on parallel testing as your differentiator for the mid-market to justify the per-minute price.

**The Numbers**
*Current State:*
Revenue: $100,000 (2M mins × $0.05)
Variable Cost: $40,000 (2M mins × $0.02)
Contribution Margin: $60,000
Fixed Costs: $45,000
**Net Monthly Cash Flow: +$15,000**
**Current Runway:** $300,000 / $15,000 = **20 months** (infinite if growth continues, but baseline is 20).

*Scenario A: Blanket 15% Price Cut*
New Price: $0.0425/min
New Revenue (assuming flat volume): $85,000
Variable Cost: $40,000
Contribution Margin: $45,000
Fixed Costs: $45,000
**Net Monthly Cash Flow: $0**
**Runway:** $300,000 / $0 = **Undefined (Cash flow neutral, no buffer for errors)**.
*Break-even Volume Required:* To maintain $15k profit at $0.0425 price, you need ~2.7M minutes (+35% volume).

*Scenario B: Tiered Model (Targeted Discount)*
Assumption: Top 10 customers (60% of usage = 1.2M mins) get a 20% discount ($0.04/min). Remaining 40% (0.8M mins) stay at $0.05.
New Revenue: (1.2M × $0.04) + (0.8M × $0.05) = $48,000 + $40,000 = $88,000
Variable Cost: $40,000 (unchanged)
Contribution Margin: $48,000
Fixed Costs: $45,000
**Net Monthly Cash Flow: +$3,000**
**Runway:** $300,000 / $3,000 = **100 months** (based on current volume).

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes this call is **churn among the top 10 customers**. If you lose even two of these top clients (representing ~12% of total volume) due to lack of discounting, your revenue drops to ~$88,000 naturally, pushing you toward break-even anyway.
*Trigger:* If any of the top 10 customers request a pricing review in the next 30 days, execute the tiered discount immediately.
*Rule of Thumb:* Never discount below your fully loaded cost plus 20% margin unless the volume increase is contractually guaranteed.
*Step this week:* If a top client mentions cost concerns, consider offering the 20% volume tier contingent on a 6-month commitment.
