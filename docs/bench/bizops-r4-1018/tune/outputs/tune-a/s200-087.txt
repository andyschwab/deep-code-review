**The Call:** Raise the $750,000 seed extension immediately; bootstrapping the $450,000 build by halving marketing while growing 25% YoY creates a cash deficit that threatens solvency before the product ships.

**The Numbers:**
Your current annual revenue is $600,000. Assuming a standard SaaS gross margin of 80%, your gross profit is $480,000. To sustain 25% growth, you likely reinvest most of this into the 8-person team and existing marketing.
*   **Bootstrap Scenario:** You need $450,000 for development over 9 months ($50k/month). Cutting marketing by 50% risks collapsing the 25% growth engine. If revenue flattens or dips due to reduced acquisition, your $200,000 cash reserve covers only 4 months of the development burn ($200k / $50k), leaving a $250,000 funding gap before launch.
*   **Raise Scenario:** Raising $750,000 covers the $450,000 build cost plus $300,000 for operating runway and continued marketing. This preserves the growth trajectory required to justify a higher valuation in the next round.
*   **ASSUMED:** Monthly operating burn (excluding new dev costs) is roughly $40,000–$50,000 based on 8 employees in Toronto and current revenue reinvestment. If your actual burn is significantly lower, the bootstrap math improves, but the growth risk remains.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The deciding input is **customer churn relative to marketing spend**. If cutting marketing by 50% causes monthly churn to exceed new acquisitions (net negative growth), the bootstrap path fails.
*   **Trigger:** If your next two monthly reports show growth dropping below 1.5% month-over-month after the marketing cut, you must pivot to fundraising immediately to avoid a "down round" later caused by stalled traction.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Do not sacrifice top-line growth for product development unless you have 18 months of cash on hand; you currently have less than 6.
*   **Step this week:** If your current CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) is under 3x LTV (Lifetime Value), consider pausing only low-ROI marketing channels rather than a blanket 50% cut, and draft a seed extension deck targeting a 12-month runway.
