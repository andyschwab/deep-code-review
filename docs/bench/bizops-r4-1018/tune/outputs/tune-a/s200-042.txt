**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The audit risk flips from "unreliable" to "robust" the moment you stop grouping items by batch and start tracking them by **Unique ID**.
*   **The Trigger:** Implement a ledger where every item acquired gets a unique SKU/ID linked to its specific purchase invoice date and cost.
*   **Threshold Rule of Thumb:** If you cannot match a sold item to a specific purchase receipt within 5 minutes, your current method is audit-risky.
*   **Actionable Step:** If you have more than 50 unsold items without a unique ID linked to a cost basis, consider migrating to inventory software that supports serial/lot tracking for individual units this week.
