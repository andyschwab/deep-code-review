**The Call**
Proceed with the launch; your 15,000€ budget is sufficient to test the 300-unit goal, but you must validate demand via a bottom-up proxy model before committing the full production run, as generic reports will obscure your specific niche.

**The Numbers**
To size your Specific Addressable Market (SAM) without generic data, calculate the "Active Seeker" pool using this formula:
`SAM = (Search Volume × Intent Conversion Rate) + (Event Attendance × Purchase Propensity)`

*   **Input 1 (Search):** Scrape Google Trends/Keyword Planner for "vegan leather bag," "mushroom leather," and "cactus leather" filtered to France/Germany.
    *   *Assumption (ASSUMED):* 5,000 monthly high-intent searches across both regions.
    *   *Assumption (ASSUMED):* 2% click-through to luxury alternative sites.
    *   *Result:* ~100 potential buyers/month from search.
*   **Input 2 (Events):** Aggregate attendance from "sustainable fashion pop-up" events in Paris/Berlin (Eventbrite, Meetup, press releases).
    *   *Assumption (ASSUMED):* 2,000 total attendees in Q1 across relevant events.
    *   *Assumption (ASSUMED):* 5% purchase propensity for items >250€.
    *   *Result:* ~100 potential buyers from events.
*   **Total Addressable Pool (Year 1):** (100 × 12) + 100 = **1,300 active seekers**.
*   **Your Goal:** 300 units.
*   **Required Market Share:** 300 / 1,300 = **23%**.

*Unit Economics Check:*
*   Revenue (300 units × 325€ avg price): 97,500€
*   COGS (300 × 85€): 25,500€
*   Gross Margin: 72,000€
*   Marketing Budget: 15,000€
*   **Result:** Positive cash flow if the 23% share is achievable.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The **Intent Conversion Rate** is the flip variable. If the actual conversion from search/event interest to a >250€ purchase is below 1.5% (instead of the assumed 2-5%), your accessible market shrinks below 300 units.
*   **Trigger:** If your pre-launch landing page or pop-up trial yields a conversion rate under 1.5% after spending 3,000€ of your budget, **stop** the full production run and pivot to a lower price point or different material narrative.
*   **Step this week:** Scrape the top 12 competitors' social engagement rates on "vegan" posts; if average engagement is <1%, consider delaying production to refine the value proposition.

**Routing Note**
No jurisdiction-specific legal or tax filing is required for this market sizing exercise; however, if you proceed to sales, ensure your "vegan" and "sustainable" claims comply with the EU Green Claims Directive to avoid regulatory penalties.

*Data Gaps:* You can scrape search volumes and event lists internally. You cannot internally verify the *purchase propensity* of event attendees or the *income bracket* of searchers; hire a micro-research firm (or run a paid survey ad) to validate these two specific coefficients before scaling.
