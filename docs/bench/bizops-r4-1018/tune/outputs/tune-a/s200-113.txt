With 9 months of runway and a 12-month development cycle, you will run out of cash 3 months before launch unless you reduce burn by $28,000/month or raise funds immediately. *Break-Even Analysis with Unknown Audit Cost:*
Let $A$ = Cost of third-party security audit.
Let $Q$ = Quantity of units to sell.
Original Fixed Costs (implied by burn minus variable costs) are currently absorbed in the $83k burn. The delay adds engineering labor and the audit fee to fixed costs before revenue starts.

To maintain original targets, you must cover the *additional* fixed costs incurred by the 6-month delay.
*   Additional Labor Cost (ASSUMED): You have 8 engineers. If the delay is purely extra time, the cost is 6 months of their fully loaded salary. Without specific salary data, we label this **$L_{delay}$**.
*   Additional Audit Cost: **$A$** (Unknown).
*   Total New Fixed Burden: $L_{delay} + A$.

*Additional Units Required:*
$$ \Delta Q = \frac{L_{delay} + A}{154} $$

*Scenario Bracketing (Illustrative Only):*

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**

*   **Trigger Value:** You must reduce burn from $83,000 to **$62,500** immediately to stretch runway to 12 months ($750k / 12 = $62.5k).
