**The Call**
Construct a bottom-up TAM using the global sports nutrition market as your ceiling, then apply a conservative "sustainable alternative" penetration rate derived from plant-based protein adoption curves rather than inventing insect-specific data; do not pitch a top-down number based on a hypothetical 0.5% of the total market without first validating that the "insect" sub-segment exists at that scale.

**The Numbers**
Since specific insect protein data is missing, we derive the figure using a proxy model.
*Formula:* `TAM = (Global Sports Nutrition Market Size) × (Proxy Adoption Rate)`

1.  **Global Sports Nutrition Market (Base Input):** Industry consensus (Grand View Research/Statista aggregates) places this between **$50B–$60B USD** annually. Let's use **$55B** as the base.
2.  **Proxy Adoption Rate (The Missing Variable):** You cannot assume 0.5% of the *total* market immediately. Instead, look at the plant-based protein share within sports nutrition, which is roughly **10–15%**. Insect protein is a subset of "alternative/sustainable" proteins.
    *   *Assumption (Low):* Insect protein captures 1% of the *alternative* protein share (0.1% of total market).
    *   *Assumption (Base):* Insect protein captures 5% of the *alternative* protein share (0.5% of total market).
    *   *Assumption (High):* Insect protein captures 10% of the *alternative* protein share (1.0% of total market).

*Computed Results (Annual Revenue Potential):*
*   **Low Case:** $55B × 0.1% = **$55M**
*   **Base Case:** $55B × 0.5% = **$275M**
*   **High Case:** $55B × 1.0% = **$550M**

*Current Run-Rate Check:* Your current UK revenue is 2,000 units × £35 = £70k/month (£840k/year). To hit the "Base Case" global TAM of $275M (~£215M), you would need to scale volume ~250x from your current UK baseline, assuming price parity.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is the **definition of the "addressable" consumer**. If you define TAM as "everyone who eats protein," your number is inflated and untrustworthy. If you define it as "early-adopter sustainable athletes in North America/Asia," the number shrinks but becomes defensible.
*   *Trigger:* If credible market research publishes a specific "edible insect" CAGR exceeding 20% by late 2025, upgrade the Proxy Adoption Rate from 5% to 10% of the alternative segment.
*   *Step this week:* If you lack a specific North America adoption statistic, consider running a small-scale ($500) survey on platforms like Prolific targeting US gym-goers to generate your own "willingness to try insect protein" percentage to replace the generic plant-based proxy.
