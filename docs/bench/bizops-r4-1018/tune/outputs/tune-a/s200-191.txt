**The Call:** Adopt a 10% early-bird discount; it is the only modeled tier that clears your 70% upfront staffing hurdle while preserving a viable break-even target under 300 campers. Anything deeper than 15% erodes margins too quickly relative to the cash flow gain.

**The Numbers:**
*   **Upfront Need:** You must cover 70% of $120,000 staffing = **$84,000** by April 1.
*   **Fixed Costs:** $35,000 (incurred regardless).
*   **Base Tuition:** $650/week.
*   **Scenario A (No Discount):**
    *   Price: $650.
    *   Cash in April (assuming 100% early payment for model): $650 × Campers.
    *   *Break-even Enrollment (Total Profit > 0):* Fixed Costs ($35k) + Staffing ($120k) / $650 = **239 campers**.
    *   *April Cash Gap:* To hit $84k, you need **129 campers** paying early. If historical early payment is low, you fail the cash test.
*   **Scenario B (10% Discount - Recommended):**
    *   Price: $585.
    *   *Break-even Enrollment:* $155,000 Total Costs / $585 = **265 campers**.
    *   *April Cash Target:* To hit $84k, you need **144 campers** to commit early.
    *   *Margin Impact:* You sacrifice $65 per camper, but you unlock the cash needed to operate.
*   **Scenario C (20% Discount):**
    *   Price: $520.
    *   *Break-even Enrollment:* $155,000 / $520 = **298 campers**.
    *   *April Cash Target:* To hit $84k, you need **162 campers** to commit early.
    *   *Risk:* You now need nearly 300 total campers just to break even, a 25% increase over last year's volume.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The deciding input is your **early-commitment conversion rate**. If fewer than 32% of your projected total enrollment (144 out of a conservative 450) sign up by April 1 at the 10% discount, you will miss the $84k cash target.
*   **Trigger Rule:** If early-bird sign-ups are below 100 campers by March 15, consider adding a "sibling bundle" or payment plan fee rather than deepening the discount, as deeper discounts raise your total break-even point unsafely.
*   **Step this week:** If your current early-bird landing page converts under 5% of visitors, consider A/B testing a "lock-in price" message rather than explicitly stating the percentage off.
