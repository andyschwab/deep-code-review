**The Call:** Transmitting insurance codes does not automatically classify CareConnect as a "healthcare provider," but your current activity of storing session notes likely already defines you as a **Business Associate** under HIPAA, not a simple technology vendor. You should proceed with Business Associate compliance immediately; do not wait for a partner warning or scale to 200 therapists before addressing this, as the risk of federal penalties exists now if a breach occurs.

**The Numbers:**
*   **Current Exposure:** You process $45,000/month. A typical HIPAA violation penalty ranges from $100 to $50,000 per violation category per year, with a maximum annual cap of $1.5 million per violation type.
*   **Compliance Cost vs. Risk:**
    *   *Estimated Upgrade Cost:* $25,000 (one-time).
    *   *Potential Penalty (Minimum Scenario):* $100+ per record exposed. If just 10 session notes are breached due to lack of encryption, the fine ($1,000+) exceeds the cost of basic preventative measures, excluding legal fees and reputational damage.
    *   *Scaling Impact:* Moving to 200 therapists (approx. 3.3x growth) increases your data volume and attack surface. If you are found non-compliant at scale, penalties are often calculated based on the duration of non-compliance and the number of individuals affected.
*   **Assumption (ASSUMED):** The $25,000 estimate covers the gap between your current security posture and full HIPAA Security Rule compliance (encryption, access controls, audit logs).

**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The determining factor is not the *transmission* of codes, but the *creation, receipt, maintenance, or transmission* of Protected Health Information (PHI). Since you admit to storing session notes, you are already handling PHI.
*   **Trigger:** The moment a therapist uploads a session note containing patient identifiers to your server, you are a Business Associate.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If you touch PHI, you are subject to HIPAA. There is no "lighter burden" tier for small platforms; the Security Rule applies based on data type, not revenue.
*   **Action Step:** If you store session notes, consider executing a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with your cloud hosting provider and drafting a standard BAA for your therapists this week. Do not market yourself as "HIPAA compliant" until an audit confirms it, but operate *as if* you are bound by the rules immediately.
