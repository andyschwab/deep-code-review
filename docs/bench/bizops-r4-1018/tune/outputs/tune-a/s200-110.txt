**The Call:** Elect accelerated depreciation (likely Section 179 or Bonus Depreciation) to maximize immediate tax shields and improve current cash flow, provided you anticipate being in a similar or higher tax bracket soon; the risk of "hurting you later" is a timing difference, not a permanent loss, but it will reduce future deductions when revenue potentially plateaus.

**The Numbers:**
*   **Asset Basis:** $300,000 (Medical equipment).
*   **Current Monthly Revenue:** $45,000 ($540,000 annualized).
*   **Loan Payment:** $8,000/month ($96,000 annualized).
*   **Method A (Accelerated):** Assume 100% bonus depreciation or Section 179 expensing in Year 1.
    *   Year 1 Deduction: $300,000.
    *   Years 2–5 Deduction: $0.
    *   *Result:* Lowers Year 1 taxable income by $300,000. Defers tax payment to later years.
*   **Method B (Straight-Line):** Assume 5-year MACRS life (standard for medical equipment).
    *   Annual Deduction: ~$60,000 ($300k / 5).
    *   *Result:* Lowers taxable income by $60,000 annually for 5 years.
*   **Cash Flow Impact (Illustrative):**
    *   If your marginal tax rate is **25%**:
    *   Accelerated saves **$75,000** in taxes Year 1 vs. **$15,000** under Straight-Line.
    *   **Net Cash Benefit Now:** +$60,000 in hand immediately with Accelerated.
    *   **Future Trade-off:** In Years 2–5, you lose the $60k deduction, increasing taxable income by that amount relative to the straight-line path.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The decision flips if your taxable income is currently low (due to other losses) or if you expect tax rates to rise significantly before you can utilize the deduction.
*   **Trigger:** If your projected Year 1 taxable income *before* depreciation is less than $300,000, you cannot fully utilize an immediate $300k deduction unless you carry it back/forward (rules vary).
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Take the deduction now if your current marginal tax rate is equal to or higher than your expected future rate.
*   **Step this week:** Calculate your estimated Year 1 taxable income before depreciation; if it exceeds $300,000, prepare the election forms for immediate expensing.
