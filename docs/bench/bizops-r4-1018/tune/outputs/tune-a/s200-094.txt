**The Call**
A 45% gross margin is not an automatic dealbreaker for Seed-stage infrastructure, but it is a critical warning signal that your current partner economics are unsustainable without a clear path to expansion revenue. Do not abandon the partner strategy yet; instead, reframe the narrative from "margin erosion" to "customer acquisition efficiency," provided you can demonstrate that these referred customers have a high Lifetime Value (LTV) beyond year one. Your immediate priority is to renegotiate the fee structure to a declining scale or secure multi-year contracts to amortize the upfront cost.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the verdict is **Retention Rate (NDR)** and **Contract Duration**. Venture capitalists in infrastructure tolerate lower initial margins if the LTV:CAC ratio exceeds 3:1.
*   **The Trigger:** If your partner-referred customers churn at >40% before Year 2, the model is broken. If they retain >80% into Year 2 (where margins jump to ~70%), the model is investable.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** You are investable at 45% margin only if the blended margin over 3 years exceeds 65%.
*   **Actionable Step:** If your current renewal rate is unknown, consider implementing a "clawback" clause or a multi-year commission cap immediately. For the board meeting, present a sensitivity analysis: "At 80% retention, our 3-year blended margin is 68%; at 50% retention, it is 42%."
