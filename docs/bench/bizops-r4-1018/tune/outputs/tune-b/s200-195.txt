**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single factor that flips this from "risky" to "catastrophic" is **commercial derivation**. Courts weigh four factors for fair use:
1.  **Purpose:** Educational use helps, but *commercial* subscription use hurts significantly. You are selling access to content featuring their IP.
2.  **Nature of Work:** Creative fiction (cartoons) gets stronger protection than factual news.
3.  **Amount Used:** Using the "heart" of the character (the visual identity) weighs against you.
4.  **Market Effect:** This is your biggest liability. You admitted these images drove a **20% sign-up spike**. This proves you are capturing market value that belongs to the IP holder, directly harming their potential licensing revenue.
