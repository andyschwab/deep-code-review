**The Call:** Your current flow—holding funds in a merchant account before disbursing to tutors—almost certainly triggers money transmitter licensing (MTL) obligations in California, New York, and Texas, regardless of the 24-hour holding period. You must cease this specific fund-flow model immediately or engage specialized fintech counsel to structure a compliant exemption or partnership.

**The Numbers & Mechanics:**
The regulatory trigger is not your commission rate (15%) or transaction volume ($240k/month), but the legal definition of "receiving money for transmission."
*   **The Rule:** In most US states, if you accept funds from a payer (parent) with the obligation to pay a third party (tutor), you are transmitting money.
*   **The "24-Hour" Myth:** There is no universal federal or state exemption based solely on a <24-hour hold time. While some states have narrow exemptions for agents of payees or very short-term holds, they are fact-specific and rarely apply automatically to platform models without explicit legal opinion.
*   **Jurisdictional Risk:**
    *   **New York:** Has the strictest regime (BitLicense/MTL). Operating without a license here is a criminal offense. The "limited purpose trust company" or agent exemptions are narrow and require prior approval.
    *   **California:** Requires a license if you control the funds. The Department of Financial Protection and Innovation (DFPI) actively enforces against platforms settling via merchant accounts rather than direct peer-to-peer rails.
    *   **Texas:** Requires a license if you receive money for transmission. The Texas Department of Banking scrutinizes "marketplace facilitators" closely.
    *   **Delaware:** As your home state, you likely need a license here too, even if you have no local customers, because the transmission originates from your entity.

**What Flips It & The Trigger:**
The single input that changes this assessment is **custody**. If you can re-architect the payment flow so funds move directly from Parent to Tutor via a third-party processor (using split payments or instant payouts where the platform never takes legal title or possession of the funds), you may qualify for an exemption in some jurisdictions.
*   **Trigger:** The moment your merchant account settlement hits your operating balance sheet before you manually or automatically push it to tutors, you are transmitting.
*   **Immediate Risk:** Cease and desist orders, freezing of assets, and civil penalties ranging from $10,000 to $25,000 per unlicensed transaction in aggressive states like NY.
