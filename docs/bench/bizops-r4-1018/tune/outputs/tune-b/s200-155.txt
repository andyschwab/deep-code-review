**The Call**
Reject the 2% assumption as your base case for an investor deck; it likely overstates demand by ignoring intent and cannibalization. Instead, present a segmented TAM showing a "Conservative" scenario (0.5% conversion) and an "Upside" scenario (2.0%), explicitly labeling the 2% figure as contingent on validated user intent for live tutoring.

**The Numbers**
To size the Specific Addressable Market (SAM) for tutoring without double-counting, use this funnel:
`SAM = (Total Users × Free %) × Daily Active Rate × Tutoring-Intent Filter × Net Conversion Rate`

Using your provided figures:
*   **Total Users:** 200,000
*   **Free Tier Pool:** 200,000 × 85% = 170,000
*   **Daily Active Users (DAU):** 170,000 × 10% = 17,000

*Scenario A: Your Proposed 2% Conversion (Upside)*
*   **Assumption:** 2% of DAU convert immediately to tutoring.
*   **Calculation:** 17,000 × 2% = **340 users**.
*   **Revenue Impact:** 340 users × (assumed usage) × $30/hr.

*Scenario B: Conservative Intent-Adjusted (Base)*
*   **Assumption:** Only 25% of DAU actually want live tutoring (Intent Filter), and 2% of *that* group converts. Effective rate = 0.5%.
*   **Calculation:** 17,000 × 0.5% = **85 users**.

*The Overlap Risk (Double Counting)*
If you already have paid subscribers (the 15% non-free users), you must subtract those who might switch from a flat subscription to hourly tutoring.
*   **Unknown Variable:** % of existing paid users who would cannibalize their subscription for hourly tutoring.
*   **Adjustment:** If 10% of your 30,000 paid users (3,000 people) switch to hourly, your *net new* market shrinks, even if gross tutoring users rise.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is the **Tutoring-Intent Filter**. Currently, you are assuming all daily active users are potential tutoring customers. In reality, language learners often prefer self-study.
*   **Trigger:** If a survey of 500 DAU shows less than 15% express interest in live tutoring, discard the 2% conversion model entirely.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** For niche features (live human interaction), conversion rates are typically 10x lower than broad feature unlocks (like "offline mode").
*   **This Week's Step:** If <15% of surveyed users say "yes" to tutoring, consider pivoting the launch to a "group class" model to lower the $30/hr price barrier before building the 1:1 marketplace.
