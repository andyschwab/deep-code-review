**The Call:** Do not aggressively shift revenue to direct channels before your Series A if it slows overall growth; instead, frame your marketplace strategy as "capital-efficient distribution validation" while presenting a concrete, low-cost plan to diversify. Investors prefer a narrative of "leveraged scale with managed risk" over "premature sales spend with poor unit economics." Your current data shows direct sales are currently inefficient ($30k revenue vs. likely high burn), whereas marketplaces delivered $160k with zero acquisition cost. Killing growth to fix a concentration risk that doesn't yet threaten your runway is a strategic error.

**The Numbers:**
*   **Current Runway:** $45,000 monthly burn implies $540,000 annual burn. With $800,000 ARR, you are roughly cash-flow positive or very close to it (assuming minimal other costs), giving you significant leverage.
*   **Channel Efficiency:**
    *   Marketplace: $160,000 revenue / $0 incremental sales cost (estimated) = Infinite CAC efficiency.
    *   Direct Sales (Last Year): $30,000 revenue. If you employed even one sales rep at $100k fully loaded, your CAC was >3x revenue.
*   **Concentration Risk:** 20% of revenue ($160,000) is channel-dependent.
    *   *Worst Case:* Loss of 100% of channel revenue drops ARR to $640,000.
    *   *Impact:* New ARR ($640k) still covers annual burn ($540k). You remain viable even in a total channel blackout.

**Assumptions:**
*   **ASSUMED:** The $45k burn includes all salaries and overhead.
*   **ASSUMED:** The "20%" figure refers to the portion of total ARR derived from marketplaces ($160k is exactly 20% of $800k).
*   **ASSUMED:** Direct sales attempt involved at least one dedicated headcount or significant founder time opportunity cost.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The call flips if marketplace revenue concentration exceeds **40-50%** of total ARR *and* you lack cash-flow positivity.
*   **Trigger Value:** If channel revenue hits $400,000 (50% of $800k growth) while direct revenue remains flat.
*   **Pivot Date:** Re-evaluate in 6 months (or upon hitting $1.2M ARR).
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If a single channel represents >30% of revenue, investors require a mitigation plan, not necessarily immediate revenue diversion.
*   **This Week's Step:** If your churn rate is <2% monthly, consider building a "direct convert" program (offering incentives for marketplace customers to migrate to direct billing after year 1) rather than hunting new direct logos.

**Investor Narrative Strategy:**
Investors do not fear marketplace reliance; they fear *lack of control* and *inability to scale beyond the platform*.
1.  **Reframe the Dependency:** You didn't "fail" at direct sales; you identified a channel with 10x better unit economics and doubled down. This shows discipline, not weakness.
2.  **The Mitigation Plan:** Present a "Direct Motion 2.0" that focuses on *up-market* enterprise deals (where marketplaces often friction) rather than competing on SMB volume. Show that you can acquire $50k+ ACV deals directly, even if volume is low.
3.  **The Ask:** Use the Series A to hire a Head of Sales specifically to build the enterprise layer, using marketplace cash flow to fund the experiment.

**Routing Note:**
While this is a strategic go-to-market question, ensure your marketplace contracts (AWS/Azure/GCP) do not contain "most favored nation" clauses or restrictions on direct solicitation of those specific customers; have counsel review the specific terms of your marketplace agreement before launching a direct migration incentive.
