**Top Three Regulatory Hurdles:**
1.  **State Food Facility Registration:** Many states (including CA and FL) require out-of-state manufacturers to register as food facilities *in that state* and may require an in-state agent. Your Wisconsin license generally only covers production, not the right to distribute into other jurisdictions without their specific endorsement.
2.  **Grade "A" Pasteurized Milk Ordinance (PMO) Compliance:** Interstate shipment of dairy often requires compliance with the FDA's PMO. If your artisanal cheeses are raw milk or not graded according to specific state-adopted versions of the PMO, states like Texas may have outright bans on entry.
3.  **Labeling & Cold Chain Documentation:** States have divergent labeling laws (e.g., specific font sizes for ingredients, allergen warnings, or "keep refrigerated" icons). Furthermore, you must prove the cold chain was unbroken during transit; failure to provide temperature logs upon inspection can lead to immediate destruction of the shipment.
