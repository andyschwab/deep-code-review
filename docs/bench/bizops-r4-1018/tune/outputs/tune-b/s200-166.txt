**The Numbers:**
Your current unit economics per student at the proposed $2,100 price point versus the status quo:
*   **Formula:** `Profit = (Price - Variable Cost) × Enrollment`
*   **Current Variable Cost:** $450 (software) + Other Costs.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The deciding input is **price elasticity at the $1,950 threshold**. If raising the price by $150 causes enrollment to drop by more than 8% (falling below 37 students), the hybrid strategy fails financially compared to the full hike.
*   **Trigger Rule:** If enrollment in the first month of the new price falls below 3 students/week, revert to the $1,800 price and cut fixed costs elsewhere.
*   **Step this week:** If competitors do not announce a permanent price increase within 14 days, consider formalizing the $1,950 price as the new base and bundling a "FAA Compliance Certificate" to justify the premium.
