**Contract Risks & Hidden Costs:**
1.  **Minimum Annual Guarantee (MAG):** Most airport concessions require paying the *higher* of the percentage rent or a fixed minimum. If the MAG is set based on optimistic traffic models, you could owe rent even if sales are zero.
2.  **CAM Charges (Common Area Maintenance):** Airports often pass through security, cleaning, and terminal maintenance costs. These can add 2–5% to your effective rent.
3.  **Exclusive Pouring Rights:** Check if the airport mandates specific beverage suppliers at inflated costs, which would increase your effective COGS.
