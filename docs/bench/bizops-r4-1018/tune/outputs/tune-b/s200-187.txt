**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The deciding factor is **Behavioral Control**. If your platform mandates specific hours, requires coaches to use proprietary methods, or prevents them from working for competitors, they are employees.
*   **Trigger:** If your contracts or operations dictate *when* a coach must log in or *how* they must teach (beyond basic safety/quality standards), the "independent influencer" label fails.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Independent contractors control their own schedule and method. Employees follow instructions.
*   **Action This Week:** If you currently assign shifts or dictate lesson plans, consider pausing new coach onboarding and engaging employment counsel to rewrite contracts to emphasize coach autonomy (e.g., coaches set their own rates, hours, and methods).
