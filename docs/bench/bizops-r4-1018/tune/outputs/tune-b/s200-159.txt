**The Call:** Do not delay the launch; a two-month delay pushes you into a negative cash position in Month 5, whereas launching now keeps you solvent through the eight-month horizon. However, your cash buffer is dangerously thin regardless of timing. You should secure a short-term line of credit immediately to cover the inevitable dip, rather than delaying revenue generation.

**The Numbers:**
*Formula:* Ending Cash = Starting Cash + (Monthly Revenue – Monthly Expenses) – One-time Expansion Cost.
*Assumptions:*
1.  **Current Ops:** Net cash flow is +$10,000/month ($30k rev - $20k exp).
2.  **New Location Costs:** $85,000 upfront at Month 0 (if launched now) or Month 2 (if delayed).
3.  **New Location Revenue:** $0 for the first 6 months of operation (recruitment period).
4.  **New Location Expenses:** ASSUMED $0 additional operating expense during the 6-month recruitment phase beyond the upfront $85k. If there are monthly burn costs (rent/utilities) before students arrive, the situation is critical.

*Scenario A: Launch Now (Month 0)*
*   **Start:** $120,000
*   **Month 0:** -$85,000 (investment) + $10,000 (ops) = $45,000 remaining.
*   **Months 1–5:** +$10,000/month net.
    *   Month 1: $55,000
    *   Month 2: $65,000
    *   Month 3: $75,000
    *   Month 4: $85,000
    *   Month 5: $95,000
*   **Month 6:** New cohort starts (revenue increases).
*   **Result:** Lowest cash balance is $45,000 (end of Month 0). You remain positive.

*Scenario B: Delay Launch by Two Months (Invest at Month 2)*
*   **Start:** $120,000
*   **Months 1–2:** +$10,000/month net.
    *   Month 1: $130,000
    *   Month 2: $140,000
*   **Month 2 (End):** -$85,000 (investment). Balance: $55,000.

    *   Month 3: $65,000

    *   Month 5: $85,000

    *   Month 7: $105,000

*Correction on "Negative Cash Flow":*
Your fear of negative cash flow likely stems from **unaccounted monthly burn** at the new location. The $85,000 covers deposits and equipment, but if the new location incurs **$15,000/month** in rent, utilities, and instructor salaries during the 6-month recruitment phase (a realistic assumption for a vocational school):

*Revised Scenario A (Launch Now, with $15k/mo new burn):*
*   **Month 0:** $120k - $85k + $10k (old net) - $15k (new burn) = **$30,000**.
*   **Months 1–5:** Net flow = $10k (old) - $15k (new) = -$5,000/month.
    *   Month 1: $25,000
    *   Month 2: $20,000
    *   Month 3: $15,000
    *   Month 4: $10,000
    *   Month 5: **$5,000** (Critical Low).
*   **Month 6:** Cohort starts, revenue offsets burn.

*Revised Scenario B (Delay 2 Months, with $15k/mo new burn starting Month 3):*
*   **Months 1–2:** Build cash to $140,000.
*   **Month 3:** -$85k (invest) + $10k (old) - $15k (new) = **$50,000**.
*   **Months 4–8:** Net flow -$5,000/month.
    *   Month 4: $45,000
    *   Month 5: $40,000
    *   Month 6: $35,000
    *   Month 7: $30,000
    *   Month 8: $25,000.

**What Flips It:** The magnitude of **monthly operating burn** at the new location before revenue starts. If monthly burn exceeds $16,600, launching now depletes your cash to zero before Month 6.
**Trigger:** If your projected monthly burn for the new site is >$15,000, you must secure a line of credit covering at least $75,000 (6 months of negative flow) before signing the lease.

**Recommendation:** Launch now to start the 6-month clock sooner, but obtain a line of credit immediately. Delaying only postpones the revenue start date while exposing you to potential cost inflation. The risk you are overlooking is that "six months to recruit" could extend to eight months; if recruitment slips by two months under the high-burn scenario, you insolvency occurs in Month 7 (Launch Now) or Month 9 (Delayed).
