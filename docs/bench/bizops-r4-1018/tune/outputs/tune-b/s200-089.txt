**The Numbers:**
Your current unit economics show a critical break-even point.
*   **Current CAC Efficiency:** At 8% conversion, your effective CAC per *paid* user was $15,000 ($1,200 / 0.08). At 4.5%, it has spiked to ~$26,666 ($1,200 / 0.045).

Your $90k estimate likely includes the lifetime value (LTV) of those lost customers, which is valid for strategic decisions.
*   **Runway Impact:** $800k cash / 10 months = $80k burn/month. Losing $90k/month in efficiency means you are effectively burning double your cash reserve rate relative to growth.

**Assumptions for the New Model (Labelled ASSUMED):**
*   **ASSUMED:** A simplified 3-band usage model (e.g., $500, $2,000, $10,000 caps with clear overage) will restore conversion to at least 6.5% (midpoint between current and historical).
*   **ASSUMED:** Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) drops 10% initially due to fewer add-on upsells but stabilizes as volume increases.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes this call is **churn**. If simplified pricing attracts low-quality users who churn within 30 days, your LTV:CAC ratio collapses.
*   **Trigger:** If Day-30 churn on the new pricing plan exceeds 15% (vs. your historical baseline), pause the rollout.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Transparency improves conversion but can attract price-sensitive segments. Monitor cohort retention, not just signup rate.
*   **This Week's Step:** If you can isolate 500 trial users, serve them the simplified pricing page tomorrow; if conversion lifts above 5.5% in 48 hours, commit to the full redesign.

**Testing Methodology:**
1.  **Design:** Create three clear bands (Starter, Pro, Enterprise) based on 80% of your current users' usage patterns. Remove all 15 add-ons from the initial view; move them to a "post-purchase" settings page.
2.  **Split:** Randomly assign 50% of incoming trial traffic to the current complex page (Control) and 50% to the simplified band page (Variant).
3.  **Metric:** Primary metric is Trial-to-Paid Conversion Rate. Secondary metric is Time-to-Checkout.
4.  **Duration:** Run for 14 days or until 200 conversions are reached per variant to ensure statistical significance.
