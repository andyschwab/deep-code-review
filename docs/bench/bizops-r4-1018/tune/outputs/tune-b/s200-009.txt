**The Call:** Assume your current General Liability (GL) policy excludes product recall costs and that without specific Product Recall insurance, you are personally exposed to the $150,000+ logistics and destruction costs if your corporate veil is pierced or if you have signed personal guarantees. Secure a standalone Product Recall policy and confirm your GL includes "products-completed operations" immediately; do not distribute the pilot batch until a broker confirms these specific coverages are bound.

**The Numbers:**
*   **Potential Exposure:** $150,000 (estimated recall/destruction) + unknown regulatory fines + reputational damage.
*   **Coverage Gap:** Standard GL typically covers third-party bodily injury/property damage *caused* by the product but explicitly excludes the *cost* of withdrawing the product (logistics, notification, destruction).
*   **Estimated Cost (ASSUMED):** For a pre-revenue infant nutrition startup, Product Recall insurance premiums often range from $5,000 to $15,000 annually with a retention (deductible) of $10,000–$25,000. *Note: Infant formula is a high-risk category; rates are highly sensitive to your clinical test results and manufacturing certifications.*
*   **Investor Requirement:** Series A term sheets usually require "customary" insurance, but sophisticated investors in regulated consumables often mandate specific recall coverage prior to closing if distribution has begun.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is the **policy exclusion language** regarding "food/beverage" or "infant products." If your current GL carrier classifies infant formula as an excluded high-risk category, your exposure is total.
*   **Trigger:** If you cannot bind a recall policy with a limit of at least $500,000 within 14 days, halt the pilot distribution.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Never distribute consumables for infants without recall coverage equal to at least 3x your maximum batch value.
*   **Step this week:** If your current broker cannot provide a written confirmation that recall costs are covered or offer a standalone recall policy within 5 business days, consider engaging a specialty insurance broker who focuses on life sciences or food/beverage immediately.
