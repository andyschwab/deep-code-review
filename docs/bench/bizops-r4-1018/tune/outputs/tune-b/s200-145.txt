The deciding factor is **scientific uncertainty**. If your nutritionists were applying known biological principles to mix standard ingredients, it is routine quality control (non-qualifying). If they were attempting to solve a bioavailability problem where no established method existed and the outcome was technically uncertain, it qualifies.
*Trigger:* If your project documentation does not explicitly record the "unknowns" you faced at the start of the formula project and the failed experiments you ran to resolve them, exclude the £150,000 from the claim immediately.
