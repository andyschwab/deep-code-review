**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that changes this call is the **legal classification of your data**. If a qualified attorney certifies in writing that your dataset meets the HIPAA "Expert Determination" standard (45 CFR § 164.514(b)(1)) such that it is no longer Protected Health Information (PHI), your regulatory burden drops significantly.
*   **Trigger:** If the cost of obtaining this Expert Determination letter plus required safeguards exceeds $50,000 (likely), abandon the current plan.
