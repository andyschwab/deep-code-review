**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes this call is **Permanent Establishment (PE) risk**. If your sales activities, contract signing authority, or customer support operations within Germany are substantial enough to create a taxable presence, the German tax authority may deem you to have a PE regardless of your corporate structure.
*   **Trigger:** If the customer insists that only a German *GmbH* can sign the contract due to their internal procurement policies (not law), or if your team plans to hire employees physically located in Germany.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Data residency (GDPR) rarely mandates a local *entity*; it mandates local *processing*. Tax presence (PE) mandates local *registration*.
*   **Step this week:** If the customer's legal team cites "data sovereignty" as the reason for needing a local entity, present the AWS `eu-central-1` architecture diagram and DPA; if they cite "tax invoicing" or "local liability," consider a local fiscal representative instead of a full subsidiary.
