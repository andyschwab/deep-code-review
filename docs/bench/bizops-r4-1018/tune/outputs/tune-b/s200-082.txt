**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that confirms your status is whether your system falls under **Annex III, Section 4** of the EU AI Act (AI systems intended for recruitment, selection, and promotion).
*   **The Rule:** Systems making or substantially influencing decisions on access to employment are high-risk.
*   **The Override Myth:** The Act explicitly states that high-risk classification applies regardless of whether the output is reviewed by a human. If your tool filters 60% of candidates automatically, it is "substantially influencing" the decision. A human reviewing only the top 40% does not negate the risk posed to the filtered 60%.
