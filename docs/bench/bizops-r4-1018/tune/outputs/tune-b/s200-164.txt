**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The determining factor is not the transmission of codes, but the creation, receipt, maintenance, or transmission of **Protected Health Information (PHI)**. Since you admit to storing session notes, you are already handling PHI.
*   **Trigger:** The moment you store a session note linked to a patient identity, you are a Business Associate under the HIPAA Omnibus Rule.
*   **Threshold:** There is no "small business" exemption for HIPAA if you handle PHI.
*   **Actionable Step:** If you are storing notes today, consider executing a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with your cloud hosting provider and drafting BAAs for your therapist clients immediately. Do not wait for the 200-therapist milestone; the liability exists at 60 therapists.
