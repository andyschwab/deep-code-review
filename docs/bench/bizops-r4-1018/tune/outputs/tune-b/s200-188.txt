**The Call:** Reject the dedicated sales team; the market math shows a self-serve model is the only viable path. A three-person sales team would require capturing over 16% of the entire annual market just to cover their salaries, leaving no margin for product delivery, marketing, or profit, whereas a self-serve model breaks even with only 80 customers annually.

**The Numbers:**
*   **Total Addressable Revenue (TAR):** 1,200 candidates × $1,500 price = **$1,800,000** (theoretical max).
*   **Projected Organic Revenue (Self-Serve):** 1,200 candidates × 5% conversion × $1,500 = **$90,000/year**.
*   **Sales Team Cost:** $180,000/year (fixed).
*   **Operating Costs:** $10,000/month × 12 = $120,000/year.

*Scenario A: Dedicated Sales Team*
To justify the $180,000 sales cost alone, you must sell 120 additional courses ($180k / $1,500).
*   **Required Market Share:** (120 sales + 60 organic sales) / 1,200 total = **15% total market penetration**.
*   **Resulting Burn:** Revenue ($270k) - Sales Cost ($180k) - Ops ($120k) = **-$30,000 annual loss**.
*   **Runway Impact:** With $100k funding and a $30k annual loss (plus ramp-up time where revenue is zero), the team drains cash before proving unit economics.

*Scenario B: Self-Serve Model*
*   **Revenue:** $90,000 (based on 5% of 1,200 candidates).
*   **Costs:** $120,000 (Ops only).
*   **Gap:** -$30,000.
*   **Break-Even Analysis:** To cover the $120,000 ops cost at $1,500/unit, you need **80 customers**. This represents a **6.7% conversion rate** of the total market (80/1,200).
*   **Sensitivity:** Increasing conversion from 5% (60 users) to 7% (84 users) flips the business from a $30k loss to a $6k profit without adding fixed headcount.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes this call is the **conversion rate**. The sales team model only works if you can realistically capture >20% of the entire US maritime bar exam market, which is statistically improbable for a new entrant.
*   **Trigger Rule:** If you cannot validate a conversion rate above **7%** via self-serve channels within 6 months, the business model is unsustainable regardless of sales headcount.
*   **Actionable Step:** If your current funnel yields <60 sales in the first cycle, consider **raising the price to $2,500** (testing price elasticity in a niche professional market) rather than adding sales staff, as this lowers the break-even volume to 48 customers.
