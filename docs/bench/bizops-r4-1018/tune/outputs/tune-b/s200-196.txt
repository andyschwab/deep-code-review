**The Call**
Do not launch with current reserves; you face a $70,000 cash deficit before acquiring the first user. The $90,000 server cost acts as a massive barrier that delays profitability by approximately 18–24 months compared to a standard lean SaaS model. You must secure at least $100,000 in bridge funding or reduce upfront infrastructure costs by 60% to survive the first year.

**The Numbers**
*Formula:* Break-Even Users = (Fixed Costs + Cumulative Operating Expenses) / Revenue Per User.
*Inputs:* Upfront Fixed Cost = $90,000. Monthly OpEx = $4,000. Price = $200 one-time + $20/month.
*Assumptions:*
1.  **Adoption Rate:** 500 users over 6 months (linear ~83 users/month).
2.  **Churn:** 0% (Optimistic/ASSUMED).
3.  **Scaling Costs:** Constant $4,000/month (ASSUMED; see "What flips it").

*Scenario A: Base Case (500 users by Month 6)*
*   **Month 6 Cumulative Revenue:** (500 users × $200) + (Avg 250 active users × $20 × 3.5 months avg tenure) ≈ $100,000 + $17,500 = $117,500.
*   **Month 6 Cumulative Cost:** $90,000 (upfront) + ($4,000 × 6) = $114,000.
*   **Result:** Theoretically breaks even on *cash flow* in Month 6, **BUT** requires $114,000 cash upfront. You have $60,000. You run out of cash in Month 4.

*Scenario B: Required Runway to Profitability*
To cover the $90k sunk cost + $4k/mo burn while waiting for recurring revenue to compound:
*   **Month 12 Projection:** ~1,000 users total. Revenue ≈ $200k (one-time) + $120k (recurring). Costs ≈ $90k + $48k. Net Positive.
*   **Cash Gap:** You need to fund ~$138,000 in total costs over 12 months before recurring revenue stabilizes the burn.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The **variable server scaling cost** is the critical unknown. Your model assumes $4,000/month is fixed regardless of user count. If video processing or data storage scales linearly with users (e.g., +$5/user/month), your margin evaporates.
*   **Trigger:** If variable costs exceed $8 per user/month, the $20 maintenance fee becomes unprofitable.
*   **Stop/Pivot Rule:** If user acquisition exceeds 100 users/month, re-audit server logs immediately. If cost-per-user rises above $8, pause marketing and renegotiate infrastructure contracts.
*   **Action this week:** If you cannot raise $100k, consider switching to a usage-based infrastructure model (e.g., AWS Lambda/Cloudfront) to convert the $90k fixed cost into variable costs, aligning expenses with revenue.

**Routing Note**
The $90,000 capitalization of server infrastructure may require amortization over 3–5 years rather than an immediate expense deduction, affecting your taxable income and cash flow projections; confirm this treatment with a CPA before finalizing the budget.
