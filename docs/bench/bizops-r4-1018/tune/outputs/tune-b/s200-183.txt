**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that changes the compliance call is **when the fee is collected**.
*   **Trigger Value:** If you collect *any* portion of the €1,200 *before* the final editorial decision, you cross from standard Open Access practice into potential "pay-to-publish" territory, triggering scrutiny under the **European Code of Conduct for Research Integrity** and possibly national consumer protection laws regarding unfair commercial practices.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Payment must be strictly contingent on a formal "Acceptance" letter.
