**The Call:** Adopt a 10% early-bird discount; it is the only modeled scenario that clears your 70% upfront staffing hurdle while preserving sufficient margin to keep the break-even enrollment achievable.

**The Numbers:**
To model this, we assume last year's volume (450 campers) splits 40% Early-Bird (paid by April 1) and 60% Regular (paid later). Upfront staffing need is $84,000 (70% of $120k). Fixed costs are $35,000. Variable staffing cost is assumed proportional to enrollment ($267/camper).

*Formula:*
1.  **April Cash** = (Enrollment × 40% × Discounted Price) – (Enrollment × 40% × Variable Cost) – Fixed Costs.
    *(Note: We assume fixed costs hit early; if they are spread, April cash improves.)*
2.  **Total Profit** = (Total Revenue) – (Total Staffing $120k) – (Fixed Costs $35k).
3.  **Break-Even Enrollment** = (Fixed Costs + Total Staffing) / (Weighted Avg Price – Variable Cost per Camper).

*Scenario A: 0% Discount (Base Price $650)*
*   **April Cash:** -$1,400 (Deficit). You fail the 70% staffing coverage goal.
*   **Total Profit:** $137,500.
*   **Break-Even Enrollment:** 143 campers.

*Scenario B: 10% Discount (Early Price $585)*
*   **April Cash:** +$12,100 (Surplus). Covers 100% of the $84k staffing need.
*   **Total Profit:** $124,750.
*   **Break-Even Enrollment:** 152 campers.

*Scenario C: 20% Discount (Early Price $520)*
*   **April Cash:** +$25,600 (Surplus).
*   **Total Profit:** $112,000.
*   **Break-Even Enrollment:** 163 campers.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The **Early-Bird Conversion Rate** is the flip input. The calculations above assume 40% of campers take the discount. If fewer than **28%** of your total expected campers sign up early under the 10% plan, you will miss the $84,000 cash target.
*Trigger:* If early registrations are below 126 campers (28% of 450) by March 15, increase the discount to 15% or launch a targeted payment-plan campaign for the remaining balance.
*Rule of Thumb:* A 10% price cut requires only a ~6% increase in volume to maintain total profit, but here it buys critical liquidity.
*This Week's Step:* If your current early sign-up rate is <5%, consider adding a "sibling bonus" to the 10% offer to boost volume without further eroding unit price.
