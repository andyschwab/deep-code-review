**The Call**
Your unit economics have broken: at $7.50 variable cost against $15 revenue, your contribution margin has collapsed from 73% to 50%, slashing monthly profit from $14k to $3k and reducing your runway from ~7 months to ~4 months. You must immediately introduce a "Good-Better-Best" tiered pricing model that separates standard code repositories from high-volume binary storage, grandfathering existing small repos while charging overage fees or higher tiers for the new heavy usage.

**The Numbers**
*Formula:* Contribution Margin = Price − Variable Cost; Monthly Profit = (Margin × Repos) − Fixed OpEx; Runway = Cash / Monthly Profit.
*Current State (Base Case):*
- Price: $15/repo
- Variable Cost: $7.50/repo (driven by 100MB avg size)
- Volume: 2,000 repos
- Fixed OpEx: $18,000
- **Result:** Margin = $7.50; Total Contribution = $15,000; **Monthly Profit = -$3,000** (Loss); **Runway = Negative** (burning cash).

*Scenario Analysis (ASSUMED Variable Cost scaling linearly with size):*
- **Low (Optimization):** Cost drops to $6.00/repo via compression. Profit = $4,200/mo. Runway = 26 months.
- **Base (Status Quo):** Cost stays $7.50/repo. Profit = -$3,000/mo. Runway = N/A (insolvent trajectory).
- **High (Growth in Binaries):** Cost rises to $9.00/repo (120MB avg). Profit = -$9,000/mo. Runway = 12 months.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that flips this from loss to profit is the **Variable Cost per Repo**.
- **Break-even Cost:** $6.00 per repo. (Calculation: $15 Price − ($18,000 OpEx / 2,000 Repos) = $6.00).
- **Trigger Threshold:** If average repository size exceeds **80MB** (assuming linear cost scaling from the 50MB->$4.00 baseline), you lose money on every additional customer.
- **Actionable Step:** If your average repo size is currently >80MB, consider implementing an immediate surcharge for repositories exceeding 75MB or migrating heavy users to a "Pro" tier at $29/month by the next billing cycle.
