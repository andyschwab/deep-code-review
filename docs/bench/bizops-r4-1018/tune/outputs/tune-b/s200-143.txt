**The Regulatory Landscape & Licensing Requirements:**
Insurance licensing is strictly line-of-authority specific. While your P&C license covers travel cancellation (typically classified as travel insurance or a form of property/casualty), it does not extend to accident and health coverage.
*   **The Bundle Problem:** Regulators generally view a single-premium bundle containing both P&C and Health lines as a composite product. To sell it, the entity or individual must hold *both* lines of authority. You cannot "hide" the health component inside a P&C wrapper to avoid licensing.
*   **Duration Irrelevance:** The five-day duration of the retreat does not exempt the product from health insurance licensing. Short-term accident policies (even those covering specific events like a retreat) are still classified as accident and health insurance. There is no standard "under seven days" exemption in CA, TX, or FL that allows a P&C agent to sell health coverage.
*   **State Specifics:**
    *   **California:** The Department of Insurance (CDI) strictly enforces line-of-authority. Selling health coverage without the proper license is a misdemeanor and can result in license revocation.
    *   **Texas:** The Texas Department of Insurance (TDI) requires a specific Accident & Health license. Bundling does not waive this requirement.
    *   **Florida:** The Florida Office of Insurance Regulation similarly mandates distinct licensing for health lines.

**Penalties for Non-Compliance:**
You are correct that penalties are severe. In all three states, selling insurance without the proper line of authority can result in:
1.  **Cease and Desist Orders:** Immediate halt to sales.
2.  **Fines:** Often calculated per violation (per policy sold), potentially reaching tens or hundreds of thousands of dollars given your projected 500 policies.
3.  **License Revocation:** Loss of your existing P&C licenses, effectively shutting down your entire brokerage.
4.  **Criminal Charges:** In some jurisdictions, knowing violation can be a criminal offense.
