**The Call:** Raise the $750,000 seed extension immediately; bootstrapping the $450,000 build by halving marketing while growing 25% YoY creates a cash deficit that threatens solvency before the product ships.

**The Numbers:**
Your current annual revenue is $600,000. Assuming a standard SaaS gross margin of 80%, your gross profit is $480,000.
*   **Implied Operating Burn:** To grow at 25% with 8 employees in Toronto, your operating expenses (OpEx) likely consume nearly all gross profit, leaving minimal net cash flow.
*   **Bootstrap Scenario:** You need $450,000 for dev. Cutting marketing by 50% risks collapsing the 25% growth engine. If revenue flattens or dips due to reduced acquisition, your $200,000 reserve covers less than 6 months of net burn (assuming a conservative $35k–$40k monthly net burn for an 8-person team). You run out of cash in Month 5, mid-development.
*   **Raise Scenario:** Raising $750,000 adds to your $200,000 reserve = $950,000 total. At a $40k monthly burn, this provides ~24 months of runway. This covers the 9-month build plus 15 months for go-to-market.
*   **Dilution Math (ASSUMED):** If your pre-money valuation is $3.0M (conservative "funding winter" estimate), a $750k raise dilutes you by 20%. If you bootstrap and fail to ship, equity value goes to zero. 20% of a living company is superior to 100% of an insolvent one.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The decision flips only if you can reduce the development cost from $450,000 to under $150,000 without delaying the launch.
*   **Trigger:** If you cannot scope the MVP to be built within 4 months using existing staff (absorbing the cost into current OpEx), you must raise.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Never deplete >50% of cash reserves on a single R&D bet without new capital committed.
*   **This Week's Step:** If your engineering lead confirms the $450k/9-month estimate is fixed, draft your seed extension deck targeting a 18–24 month runway; if they confirm a $150k/4-month "survival" scope is possible, pause fundraising and execute the cut.

**Milestones for a Viable Seed Extension:**
To justify the raise at a reasonable valuation, you must demonstrate:
1.  **Retention Proof:** Net Revenue Retention (NRR) >100% on the current legacy tool (proving the market needs the migration).
2.  **Design Validation:** Signed Letters of Intent (LOIs) from 3–5 current customers committing to upgrade to the new version upon release.
3.  **Unit Economics:** Clear data showing CAC payback is <12 months, proving that the marketing spend you are protecting is efficient.
