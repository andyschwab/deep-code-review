**The Call:** Do not cut marketing by half immediately; instead, pivot your sales strategy to target smaller, faster-declosing deals while reducing marketing spend by 20% to extend runway. A 4.5 burn multiple is a critical warning sign for any SaaS business, including deep-tech infrastructure, indicating that every dollar of new revenue costs $4.50 in burn. While early-stage deep tech can tolerate higher multiples than standard SaaS, 4.5 combined with a nine-month sales cycle and only 8.3 months of runway creates an immediate liquidity crisis that requires strategic redirection, not just austerity.

**The Numbers:**
*   **Runway Calculation:** Cash / Net Burn = $1,500,000 / $180,000 = **8.3 months**.
*   **Burn Multiple Context:** Your multiple is 4.5x.
    *   *Benchmark (Early Stage SaaS):* < 2.0x is efficient; 2.0–4.0x is acceptable for high growth; > 4.0x is inefficient.
    *   *Benchmark (Deep Tech Infra):* Often tolerates up to 5.0x *if* ARR growth is > 100% YoY and sales cycles are shortening.
*   **Scenario A (Cut Marketing 50%):**
    *   *Assumption (ASSUMED):* Marketing is 60% of OpEx. Cutting 50% of marketing reduces total OpEx by ~30%.
    *   *New Burn:* ~$126,000/month (assuming revenue holds static).
    *   *New Runway:* ~11.9 months.
    *   *Risk:* Halving lead generation for a 9-month sales cycle likely causes revenue to collapse in 6–9 months, accelerating the crash.
*   **Scenario B (Pivot to Smaller Deals):**
    *   *Assumption (ASSUMED):* Smaller deals close in 3 months vs. 9 months, improving cash flow velocity.
    *   *Impact:* Reduces CAC payback period and stabilizes monthly revenue sooner.
    *   *Combined Action:* 20% marketing cut + pivot extends runway to ~10.5 months while preserving pipeline quality for the new strategy.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that changes this call is **Net New ARR Growth Rate**. If your Annual Recurring Revenue is not growing at least 15–20% month-over-month despite the high burn, the 4.5 multiple is unjustifiable regardless of sector.
*   **Trigger Threshold:** If next month's Net New ARR is < $6,000 (15% of current revenue), execute the pivot to smaller deals immediately.
*   **Stop/Pivot Date:** You must see improved cash flow velocity within **60 days**. If the sales cycle does not compress below 6 months by then, you will run out of cash before the enterprise deals close.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** For a company with < 12 months runway, the burn multiple must trend toward 2.0x within two quarters.
*   **This Week's Step:** If your current pipeline contains fewer than 5 deals with a close probability > 70% and a cycle < 4 months, consider pausing all enterprise marketing campaigns and reallocating that budget to sales engineering resources for self-serve or mid-market trials.
