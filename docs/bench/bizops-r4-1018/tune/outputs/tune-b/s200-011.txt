**The Call:** Proceed with the launch; your 15,000€ budget is sufficient to test the 300-unit goal, but you must validate demand via a bottom-up "search-and-event" model rather than relying on industry reports. Do not hire a research firm yet; your two-person team can execute the data collection internally using the specific public signals outlined below.

**The Numbers:**
To determine if the market supports 300 units, we calculate the required conversion rate against your estimated reach.
*   **Goal:** 300 units sold.
*   **Revenue Target:** 300 units × 325€ (mid-point of competitor range) = 97,500€.
*   **COGS:** 300 × 85€ = 25,500€.
*   **Gross Margin:** 72,000€ (before marketing).
*   **Marketing Budget:** 15,000€ (Q1).
*   **Break-even Marketing Efficiency:** You can afford a Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) of up to 50€ per bag (15,000€ / 300 units) to hit your volume goal within the budget.

**Bottom-Up Market Sizing Method (Internal Execution):**
Instead of top-down reports, build your Total Addressable Market (TAM) slice using these three specific data layers:

1.  **Search Intent Volume (Digital Demand):**
    *   **Data Source:** Google Trends and Keyword Planner (free tiers).
    *   **Action:** Scrape monthly search volumes for specific long-tail terms in FR/DE: "vegan leather handbag luxury," "cactus leather bag," "mushroom leather purse." Exclude generic terms like "vegan shoes."
    *   **Calculation:** Sum the monthly search volume for Paris and Berlin metro areas. Apply a conservative 0.5% click-through rate (CTR) to estimate addressable traffic, then a 2-4% conversion rate to estimate potential sales volume.
    *   **Gap:** If total relevant searches < 5,000/month combined, the organic demand may be too thin for pure inbound; you will need heavy outbound influencer seeding.

2.  **Event Foot Traffic (Physical Demand):**
    *   **Data Source:** Public attendee lists and post-event reports from "Neonyt," "Première Vision Paris (Vegan section)," and local sustainable pop-ups (e.g., "Green Showroom").
    *   **Action:** Contact organizers for exact footfall numbers of *sustainable* specific zones (not total fair attendance).
    *   **Calculation:** Estimate 1-3% of attendees are high-intent luxury buyers. Multiply by average basket size.
    *   **Gap:** Organizers often withhold granular data. If they refuse, send a team member to count foot traffic at the next pop-up for 4 hours and extrapolate.

3.  **Competitor Social Proof (Share of Voice):**
    *   **Data Source:** Instagram/TikTok engagement on the 12 identified competitors.
    *   **Action:** Count comments indicating purchase intent ("Where to buy?", "Price?") on their last 20 posts.
    *   **Calculation:** This serves as a proxy for unsatisfied demand or market churn you can capture.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is your **actual Cost Per Click (CPC)** on luxury-sustainable keywords.
*   **Trigger:** If your Q1 ad spend exceeds 60€ per acquired customer (blended), pause scaling immediately.
*   **Threshold:** A CPC > 2.50€ in this niche suggests the "luxury alternative" audience is more expensive to reach than your margin allows.
*   **Step this week:** Run a 500€ test campaign targeting "vegan luxury handbag" in DE/FR. If CPC < 2.00€ and CTR > 1.5%, the market slice is large enough to proceed to the full 15,000€ spend.
