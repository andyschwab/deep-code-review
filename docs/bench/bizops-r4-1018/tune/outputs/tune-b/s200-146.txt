**The Call:** Do not frame the nonprofit tier as a revenue leak; position it as a verified, high-retention distribution channel that lowers your blended CAC and validates product-market fit, provided you explicitly model the "Paid Conversion Path" from individual users influenced by these groups. Investors will penalize you for hiding the 40% figure, not for giving it away, if you fail to articulate the strategic unit economics behind the subsidy.

**The Numbers:**
Your current runway is a hard constraint: $600,000 cash / $30,000 monthly burn = **20 months**. This is sufficient to reach a Series A raise next year, assuming burn remains flat.
Regarding the valuation narrative, the critical metric is not "Total Active Users" vs. "Paid Conversion" in isolation, but **LTV:CAC on the paid segment** adjusted for the **subsidy ratio**.
*Formula:* Blended CAC = (Total Sales & Marketing Spend) / (Total New Paid Users).
*Assumption (Base Case):* If the nonprofit tier drives organic referrals that reduce your paid marketing spend by 20%, your Blended CAC drops even if the denominator includes only paying customers.
*Result:* If you treat the 40% as "zero value," your LTV looks artificially low. If you treat them as "channel partners" that reduce acquisition costs for the remaining 60%, your unit economics improve.
*Illustrative Figure:* If your average paid user generates $500/year LTV and your CAC is $150 (3.3x ratio), adding 40% free users who cost $0 to serve but reduce overall marketing spend by 15% effectively lowers your CAC to ~$127, improving your ratio to 3.9x.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The narrative flips from "viable social impact" to "lack of product-market fit" if your **Paid Conversion Rate** from the non-subsidized 60% segment falls below **5%** (a common SaaS benchmark for early-stage health/wellness).
*Trigger:* If, by Month 12, your organic conversion from individual users (non-nonprofit) is <5%, investors will view the nonprofit tier as a crutch masking weak demand.
*Action Step:* If your current conversion is unknown, implement a tracking cohort this week to isolate individual sign-ups originating from nonprofit referrals versus direct traffic. If referral-driven conversion is >2x direct conversion, double down on the nonprofit strategy in your deck.
