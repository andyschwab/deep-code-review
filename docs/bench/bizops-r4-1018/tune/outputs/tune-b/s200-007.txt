**The Call:** Opening a buying office in Quito with three local staff members creates a **high probability** of establishing a Permanent Establishment (PE) in Ecuador, exposing the portion of your global profit *attributable* to that office to Ecuadorian corporate income tax. You cannot rely on the US-Ecuador tax treaty for protection because **no such treaty currently exists**.

**The Numbers & Criteria:**
*   **Tax Base Scope:** If a PE is established, Ecuador taxes only the **profit attributable** to the Quito office (e.g., margins on sourcing activities performed there), not your entire global profit from US sales.
*   **PE Triggers in Ecuador:** Under Ecuador's Domestic Tax Law (LORTI), a PE is typically triggered by:
    1.  **Fixed Place of Business:** Maintaining an office, branch, or factory (your proposed buying office qualifies).
    2.  **Dependent Agent:** Employees in Ecuador who habitually conclude contracts or negotiate terms on your behalf.
    3.  **Service PE:** Providing services for more than 183 days in a 12-month period.
*   **Treaty Status:** **UNVERIFIED/CRITICAL:** There is **no active Double Taxation Treaty** between the US and Ecuador. The 1939 treaty was denounced by Ecuador in 2011. You have no treaty-based shield to limit PE definition or cap withholding rates.
*   **Operational Cost Impact:** The $120,000 annual cost does not change the *legal* risk of creating a PE (presence triggers it), but it establishes a **deductible expense base**. If taxed, you can offset Ecuadorian revenue with these local costs to calculate the net taxable profit attributed to the office.

**What Flips It & The Trigger:**
*   **The Flip:** The specific **activities** your staff perform. If they only perform "preparatory or auxiliary" activities (e.g., quality control, market research, storage for delivery *by others*), you may argue against PE status. If they **negotiate or sign contracts** with farmers, PE status is almost certain.
*   **Trigger Rule:** If your staff sign purchase agreements > $0, assume PE status immediately.
*   **Actionable Step:** If you proceed, consider structuring the Quito team as independent contractors or limiting their authority strictly to quality assurance and logistics coordination, ensuring all contracts are signed in Vermont.
