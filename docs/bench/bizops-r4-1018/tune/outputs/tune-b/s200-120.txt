**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The decision flips on **churn rate**.
*Trigger:* If raising the fee by $60 (2.5%) causes **more than 11 members** (2.75% of your base) to cancel, then acquiring new members is the superior financial path.
Given your concern about a >10% hike, a 2.5% hike is likely safe, but if you anticipated a larger hike, the math changes.
*Rule of Thumb:* If your historical churn is <3% annually, raise the fee. If churn is volatile or >3%, focus on adding 11 members.
