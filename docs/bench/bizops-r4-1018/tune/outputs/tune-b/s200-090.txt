**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The deciding factor is whether a court views "storing encryption keys linked to biometric templates" as possessing biometric data.
*   **Trigger:** If a plaintiff argues that the server-side key allows reconstruction or verification of the biometric trait, the "on-device" defense fails.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If you store *any* token, hash, or key that is useless without the specific biometric input, you are likely in scope.
*   **Actionable Step:** If your current Terms of Service do not have a standalone, written release specifically mentioning "biometric data" and "retention schedule" signed *before* the first scan, consider pausing the feature for new Illinois and Texas users until counsel drafts compliant workflows.
