**The Call:** Allocate the $100,000 to the asynchronous video segment. Your current unit economics show video drives 80% of revenue with significantly lower delivery friction, and the Total Addressable Market (TAM) for self-serve video scales globally without linear headcount constraints, whereas live facilitation is capped by your ability to hire and schedule experts.

**The Numbers:**
To size the market accurately, do not use "global remote workers" (too broad) or "total HR budget" (too vague). Use a bottom-up model based on **Target Accounts × Penetration Rate × Average Contract Value (ACV)**.

*Formula:* $TAM = (\text{Total Target Companies}) \times (\text{Estimated Penetration \%}) \times (\text{Your ACV})$

**Asynchronous Video Segment (Base Case):**
*   **Target:** US companies with 50–500 employees (approx. 600,000 firms).
*   **Penetration:** Assume 5% are actively buying self-serve soft skills training (ASSUMED).
*   **ACV:** $2,000 (your current price).
*   **Result:** $600,000 \times 0.05 \times \$2,000 = \$60\text{M}$.

**Live Facilitated Segment (Base Case):**
*   **Target:** Same 600,000 firms, but only 10% have budget for high-touch live workshops (ASSUMED).
*   **Penetration:** Assume 1% conversion due to sales cycle and scheduling friction (ASSUMED).
*   **ACV:** $15,000.
*   **Result:** $600,000 \times 0.10 \times 0.01 \times \$15,000 = \$9\text{M}$.

*Note on Data:* Reliable data points for "Target Companies" come from databases like Crunchbase or LinkedIn Sales Navigator filtered by headcount and industry. "Penetration rates" must be derived from your current conversion metrics or industry benchmarks for SaaS vs. Services, not guessed.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The one input that changes this call is **Churn vs. Capacity**. If your video churn exceeds 25% annually (indicating low engagement) OR if you can secure enterprise live contracts at $50k+ with a sales cycle under 3 months, the live model becomes viable.
*   **Trigger Date:** Review Q3 financials (90 days from now).
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) for live clients exceeds 40% of the first-year contract value, the service model is inefficient at scale.
*   **Step this week:** If your current video renewal rate is >85%, consider building a "hybrid" tier (video + quarterly live Q&A) to test price elasticity before committing the full $100k.

**Routing Note:**
Expanding from Toronto to the US creates nexus obligations for state sales tax and potential employment law issues if you hire US-based facilitators as contractors. Before signing your first US client or hiring US staff, consult a US-qualified tax attorney to determine which states require registration and to review your independent contractor agreements for compliance with local classification tests (e.g., California's ABC test).
