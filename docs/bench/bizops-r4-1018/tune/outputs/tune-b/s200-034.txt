**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that flips this from a "lease" to a "loan" is the **certainty of title transfer**. If your contract guarantees ownership at the end of the term for a nominal fee (e.g., $1) or automatically transfers title without further action, regulators and courts (particularly in California) will likely view the transaction as a credit sale, not a true lease.
*   **Trigger Rule:** If the customer has no meaningful economic alternative but to purchase (because the final payment is nominal), the "lease" label fails.
*   **Action:** If your draft contract includes an automatic title transfer or a "$1 buyout" clause, **stop** and rewrite the termination/purchase options before seeking legal review.
