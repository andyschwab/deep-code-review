**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**

*   **Regulatory Constraint:** Nonprofits face "Unrelated Business Income Tax" (UBIT). If your paid training programs are deemed too commercial or not directly related to the exempt educational purpose, that revenue could be taxed, eroding margins. Scaling paid programs requires careful structuring to ensure they remain "related" to the mission.
*   **This Week's Step:** If you have < $10,000 in committed donations, consider launching as a C-corp with a "social mission" clause and plan to spin out a nonprofit subsidiary later once revenue scales.
