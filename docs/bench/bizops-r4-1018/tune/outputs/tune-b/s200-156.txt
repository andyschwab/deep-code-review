**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes this call is **Physical Nexus**. Unlike economic nexus (based on revenue), holding an event and physically transferring tangible goods (workbooks) in a state can create an immediate filing obligation regardless of revenue volume.
*   **Trigger:** If any of these states define the act of selling tangible personal property at a temporary event as creating physical nexus, you owe tax on the $30,000 materials immediately.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If you have physical presence, revenue thresholds often do not apply.
*   **Step this week:** If you have not registered in these states, consider pausing the physical handover of workbooks (ship them separately from a non-nexus state) until counsel confirms your physical presence status.
