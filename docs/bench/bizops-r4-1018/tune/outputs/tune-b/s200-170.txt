**The Call**
Reject the flat €45,000 fixed fee as currently structured; it caps your upside and exposes you to catastrophic margin erosion if a client scales to 1,000 seats. Instead, adopt a **tiered fixed-fee model** (e.g., up to 300 seats) with a defined overage rate, or stick to per-seat pricing for enterprises exceeding 400 employees. The predictability of revenue does not outweigh the risk of unlimited variable costs without a usage cap.

**The Numbers**
*Formula:* Profit = Revenue − (Cost Per Seat × Number of Seats).
*Your Inputs:* Per-seat price €150; Fixed fee €45,000; Variable cost €40/seat.

| Scenario | Seats | Per-Seat Model Profit | Fixed-Fee Model Profit | Difference |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Small Client** | 200 | €22,000 | €37,000 | Fixed wins +€15k |
| **Average Client** | 250 | €27,500 | €40,000 | Fixed wins +€12.5k |
| **Break-Even Point** | **375** | **€41,250** | **€45,000** | **Equal** |
| **Growth Client** | 500 | €55,000 | €45,000 | Per-Seat wins +€10k |
| **Enterprise Risk** | 1,000 | €110,000 | €5,000 | Per-Seat wins +€105k |

*Assumptions:* Costs remain linear at €40/seat; no volume discounts applied in per-seat model.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that flips the recommendation is **client headcount growth**.
*   **The Trigger:** If any single client exceeds **375 seats**, the fixed-fee model becomes less profitable than per-seat pricing.
*   **The Risk:** At 1,000 seats, your margin drops from €110,000 (per-seat) to just €5,000 (fixed), effectively giving away 95% of your potential profit while absorbing 40x the delivery cost relative to revenue.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Never sell unlimited usage on a high-variable-cost service without a cap.
*   **Action This Week:** If your average client is 250 seats, consider a fixed fee of €45,000 **capped at 350 seats**, with a €120/seat overage rate thereafter. This secures the baseline revenue while protecting against the 1,000-seat scenario.

**Routing Note**

BGB on General Terms and Conditions) to avoid clauses being deemed void for lack of transparency. Consult a German commercial lawyer to draft the specific wording for the tiered pricing structure.
