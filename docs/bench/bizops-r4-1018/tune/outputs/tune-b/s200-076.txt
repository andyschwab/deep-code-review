**The Call**
Immediately restrict the free tier with hard usage caps or credit limits; the current model is mathematically insolvent because your infrastructure cost per user vastly exceeds any realistic lifetime value (LTV). You are subsidizing non-paying load testers, not acquiring qualified leads. Do not pivot to trial-only yet; first, segment the free users to see if any exhibit paying behavior patterns.

**The Numbers**
Your unit economics are currently inverted.
*   **Current MRR:** $3,500 (120 users × $29).
*   **Current Infrastructure Cost:** $30,000/month.
*   **Net Burn from Ops:** -$26,500/month (excluding salaries/other overhead).
*   **Runway:** ~7.5 months ($200k / $26.5k), assuming zero other expenses. In reality, with salaries, you likely have <4 months.

**Cost Per Free User (CPUFU) Analysis:**
*   Total Free Users: 4,880 (5,000 total - 120 paid).
*   Assumed Cost Allocation: If 90% of the $30k bill is driven by free users (typical for load testing abuse), that is ~$27,000 / 4,880 = **$5.53 per free user/month**.
*   **LTV Comparison:** To justify a $5.53/month acquisition cost, a user must convert.
    *   Conversion Rate: 2.4% (120/5,000).
    *   Effective Cost Per Paid Acquisition (via free tier): $5.53 × (1/0.024) = **$230.40**.
    *   Payback Period: $230.40 / $29 = **7.9 months** just to recover infrastructure costs, ignoring support, sales, and server costs for the *paid* users themselves.

**Assumptions (Labelled):**
*   **ASSUMED:** 90% of infra costs are attributable to free-tier load testing (Base Case). If this is only 50%, the cost per user drops, but the payback period remains >3 months.
*   **ASSUMED:** Churn is standard for SaaS (~5% monthly). If churn is higher, LTV drops below the acquisition cost entirely.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that changes this call is the **conversion rate of "high-intent" vs. "load-test" users**.
*   **Trigger:** If you analyze logs and find that users who hit >80% of a proposed credit limit convert at >10%, then a credit-limit model works.
*   **Stop/Pivot Rule:** If <1% of users who exceed a specific API call threshold convert within 14 days, they are not leads; they are cost centers. Block them immediately.
*   **Actionable Step:** This week, implement a hard cap (e.g., 1,000 requests/day) on the free tier. Monitor if the $30k bill drops proportionally. If the bill drops 80% and MRR stays flat, you have confirmed the free users were purely parasitic.
