**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is **how you access the bank data**.
*   **Trigger Value:** If you access data via the banks' official **Open Banking APIs** (regulated by the FCA under PSD2) as an AISP, you need registration but *not* the $50k reserve capital required for deposit-taking or payment execution. If you are scraping credentials or executing transfers directly without being an authorized PISP (Payment Initiation Service Provider), you are operating illegally.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** "Data read-only" via Open Banking = Lower barrier (AISP). "Money movement" or "Credential handling" = High barrier (PISP/Bank).
*   **Stop/Pivot Date:** Before signing any term sheet for your next round (estimated 4–6 weeks out). Investors will conduct legal due diligence on this exact point.
*   **Actionable Step:** If your engineering team is using credential scraping instead of certified Open Banking APIs, consider pivoting immediately to a certified TPP (Third Party Provider) model or restricting features to read-only referrals.
