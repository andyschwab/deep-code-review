**The Call:** You cannot justify a 10x valuation leap on last year's 2x revenue growth alone using standard unit economics; the narrative must shift immediately from "historical performance" to "future market dominance" and "strategic scarcity," specifically highlighting a Total Addressable Market (TAM) expansion or a proprietary moat that promises non-linear growth in the next 12 months.

**The Numbers:**
*   **Current Burn:** 15 employees × $180k = $2.7M/year (~$225k/month).
*   **Current ARR:** $2.4M.
*   **Net New ARR Needed to Break Even:** With 4% monthly churn (~40% annual), you lose ~$960k/year in revenue. To just hold steady, you need $960k in new sales. To cover your $2.7M burn and grow, you need roughly $3.7M in *new* ARR this year (a 150% growth rate on the existing base).
*   **Valuation Math:** A $100M post-money valuation on $2.4M ARR implies a **41.7x ARR multiple**.
    *   *Standard SaaS Benchmark:* High-growth (100%+ YoY) companies typically trade at 10x–20x ARR.
    *   *The Gap:* You are priced at double the top-end benchmark despite only achieving 100% growth (2x) historically.
*   **Assumption (Marked ASSUMED):** To justify 41.7x, investors must underwrite a projection where you hit $10M–$12M ARR within 18 months (getting the multiple down to a standard 8x–10x on *forward* revenue).

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is **Contracted but Unrecognized Revenue (Pipeline Conversion)**. If you have signed letters of intent or committed contracts worth >$5M that start billing in Q1/Q2, the "2x growth" narrative disappears, replaced by "5x imminent growth."
*   **Trigger:** If you cannot demonstrate a qualified pipeline converting to $300k+ Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) within 90 days, the valuation is mathematically indefensible on fundamentals.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** A 40x multiple requires a path to $10M ARR in <24 months.
*   **Step this week:** If your pipeline is <3x your current ARR, consider restructuring the round as a convertible note with a valuation cap rather than a priced equity round, deferring the valuation justification until the next milestone.
