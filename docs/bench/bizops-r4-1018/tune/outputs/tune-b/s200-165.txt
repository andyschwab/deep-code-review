**The Call**
Do not treat the question of whether your model is a registered security as a math problem you can solve internally; you must immediately pause new enrollments under the current structure and engage securities counsel for a formal opinion before proceeding. While your model resembles an Income Share Agreement (ISA) rather than a traditional equity offer, the SEC's application of the *Howey* test to contingent repayment obligations in education is fact-specific and unsettled; operating without a definitive legal opinion exposes you to existential enforcement risk that outweighs the $50,000 registration cost you fear.

There is no formula to compute "security status" because it is a legal determination, not an arithmetic one. However, we can look at your unit economics to understand the stakes of a pivot:

**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is the **presence of an "expectation of profits derived from the efforts of others."**
*   **Trigger:** If a regulator or court determines that students are investing money (or deferring payment) with the primary expectation that *your* marketing and placement efforts will generate their ROI (the job), the arrangement likely qualifies as an investment contract under the *Howey* test.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If the repayment is purely a debt obligation contingent on income (like a loan), it is less likely to be a security. If the repayment feels like buying a share of future success driven by your platform, it leans toward a security.
*   **Actionable Step:** This week, draft a "cease and desist" to yourself on new enrollments under the current terms, and contact a securities attorney specializing in ISAs or education finance. Ask them specifically: "Does our specific contingency clause create an expectation of profit from our efforts, triggering *Howey*?"

Only a licensed securities attorney can provide the definitive answer you requested. You cannot continue enrolling students under the current model until counsel confirms the structure is not a security.
