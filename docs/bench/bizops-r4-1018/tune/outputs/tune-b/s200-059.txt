**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The deciding input is the **churn rate of the top 10 customers** if you do not discount.
*Trigger:* If any of the top 10 customers give notice to leave within the next 30 days, the tiered discount becomes mandatory immediately.
*Rule of Thumb:* If losing one top customer reduces revenue by more than the cost of discounting all ten, discount immediately. (Losing 10% of volume = $10k revenue loss vs. $12k cost of discounting everyone).
