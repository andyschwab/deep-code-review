**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The deciding factor is the specific word "Certified." Under the **FTC Green Guides (16 CFR Part 260)**, an unqualified claim of certification is deceptive if no recognized third-party organization has verified it.
*   **Trigger:** If your packaging says "Certified," you are legally exposed regardless of whether the product actually composts.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** "Certified" = Third-Party Proof Required; "Compostable" = Competent and Reliable Scientific Evidence Required (which can be internal but is risky without standards like ASTM D6400/D6868).
