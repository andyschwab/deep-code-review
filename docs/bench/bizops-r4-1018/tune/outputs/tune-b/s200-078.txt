**Investor Defense Data Points:**
To defend any valuation, gather:
1.  **Cohort Analysis:** Show that newer cohorts churn less than older ones (proving product-market fit improvement).
2.  **LTV:CAC Ratio:** Calculate Lifetime Value (ARPA / Monthly Churn) vs. Customer Acquisition Cost. With 4% churn, LTV is artificially suppressed unless ARPA is very high. You need an LTV:CAC >3:1.
3.  **Differentiation Metric:** Quantify a technical moat (e.g., "50% lower ingestion cost" or "10x faster query speed") that directly translates to customer savings or stickiness.
