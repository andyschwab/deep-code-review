**The Call**
Construct your TAM using a "substitution proxy" model anchored to the total sports nutrition market, then apply a conservative "early-adopter penetration" factor (0.5%–1.0%) rather than seeking nonexistent insect-specific baselines. Investors will trust a logical derivation from a known large number (total sports nutrition) more than a fabricated niche statistic.

**The Numbers**
*Formula:* TAM Revenue = (Global Sports Nutrition Market Size) × (Target Capture Rate).

Since you lack the specific global baseline, we must derive it using a proxy multiplier from adjacent sectors, as specific insect protein reports are indeed sparse.
*   **Input A (Global Sports Nutrition Market):** *UNVERIFIED/ASSUMED*. Industry consensus for the broader sports nutrition market (protein powders, bars, RTD) typically ranges between $45B–$55B USD annually. For this model, we will use a conservative **$45B USD** (~£35.5B GBP) as the base.
*   **Input B (Target Capture Rate):** **0.5%** (as requested).
*   **Input C (Insect Adoption Proxy):** *STRATEGIC ASSUMPTION*. Since direct data is missing, validate the 0.5% by comparing it to plant-based protein's share of total protein (~10–15% currently) and applying an "early-stage discount" (insect is ~1/20th the maturity of plant-based). This makes 0.5% defensible as a "conservative early-adopter" slice.

*Computed Results (Annual Revenue Potential):*
*   **Base Case:** £35.5B (Market) × 0.5% = **£177.5 Million**.
*   **Low Case (Conservative Market Size $35B):** £27.6B × 0.5% = **£138 Million**.
*   **High Case (Aggressive Capture 1.0%):** £35.5B × 1.0% = **£355 Million**.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is the **Global Sports Nutrition Market Size (Input A)**. If the actual market is stagnant or shrinking due to economic downturns, your 0.5% slice yields less revenue.
*   **Trigger:** If credible 2024/2025 reports (e.g., from Grand View Research or Statista) place the global market below $40B, reduce your TAM projection by 15% immediately.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** In pitch decks, a "bottom-up" sanity check often beats top-down. If your 0.5% implies selling more units than there are gyms in your target regions, the number is implausible.
