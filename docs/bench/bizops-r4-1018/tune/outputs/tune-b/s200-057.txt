**Clarification on Regulations:**
Your concern about ITAR (International Traffic in Arms Regulations) may be misplaced; most commercial encryption falls under EAR (administered by BIS), not ITAR (administered by State Department), unless the software was specifically designed for military use. However, EAR controls are still strict. The fact that you are in Texas (US) means US laws apply to the export of the code itself, regardless of where the customer is located. The "analysis" function does not automatically exempt the underlying cryptographic library from control.
