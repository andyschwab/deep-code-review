**The Call**
Treat the bid as requiring full WCAG 2.1 AA compliance; assuming a lower standard creates a high risk of contract default and reputational damage in the UK public sector. Financially, the 25% cost increase erodes your margin to approximately 1.6%, making the project viable only if you secure the volume or negotiate a change order; do not proceed at the current price without confirming the specific accessibility clause in the final contract.

**The Numbers**
*Formula:* `Final Margin = (Contract Value - (Unit Cost × Quantity)) / Contract Value`
*Assumptions:*
1.  **Volume:** Based on £85k total and £5k/module (compliant), you are delivering **17 modules**.
2.  **Base Cost:** £4,000/module (non-compliant).
3.  **Compliant Cost:** £5,000/module (25% increase, as stated).
4.  **Revenue:** £85,000 fixed.

*Scenario A (Non-Compliant Bid - £4k/unit):*
*   Total Cost: 17 × £4,000 = £68,000
*   Profit: £17,000
*   Margin: **20.0%** (Exceeds your 18% target, but legally risky).

*Scenario B (Compliant Bid - £5k/unit):*

*   Profit: £0
*   Margin: **0.0%** (Break-even only; assumes no overhead allocation).

*   Implied Overhead + Profit needed: ~£15,300 (18% of £85k).
*   Available buffer for cost increase: £15,300.
*   Actual cost increase: 17 modules × £1,000 = £17,000.
*   **Result:** You are **£1,700 underwater** (-2% net margin) if you absorb the full compliance cost without a price adjustment.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The deciding input is the **specific accessibility clause** in the final contract terms.
*   **Trigger:** If the contract explicitly cites "WCAG 2.1 AA" or "EN 301 549," the cost is mandatory.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** UK public sector bodies are legally bound by the *Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) Accessibility Regulations 2018*. While third-party vendors are not always directly legislated, the contracting authority cannot buy non-compliant services without a "disproportionate burden" assessment, which they rarely accept for core deliverables.
*   **Action this week:** Submit a clarification question to the procurement team asking: "Does the contract require deliverables to meet WCAG 2.1 AA or EN 301 549 standards?" If they confirm yes, **consider** submitting a revised commercial offer reflecting the £17k cost uplift or reducing the module count to preserve margin.
