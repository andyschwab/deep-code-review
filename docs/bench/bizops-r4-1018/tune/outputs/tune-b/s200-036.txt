**The Call**
Proceed with the GOTS certification and price increase only if you can validate that at least 35% of your current 5,000 customers will remain active at the $42 price point; otherwise, the fixed cost of certification combined with volume loss will erode your 12-month runway. The math works on paper, but the risk lies entirely in customer retention, not unit margins.

**The Numbers**
*Current State:*
Margin per unit = $35 (Price) - $10 (Cost) = **$25**.
Total Annual Contribution (at 100% retention) = 5,000 units * $25 = **$125,000**.

*Proposed State (with GOTS):*
New Cost per unit = $12 (Material) + $3 (Amortized Certification: $15k / 5k units) = **$15**.
New Price = $42.
New Margin per unit = $42 - $15 = **$27**.

*Break-Even Analysis:*
To cover the $15,000 fixed certification fee solely through the $2 incremental margin ($27 new - $25 old), you need to sell **7,500 units** at the new price just to break even on the *investment*, ignoring the base cost increase.
However, looking at total profit equivalence:
Old Profit = $25 * Q_old
New Profit = $27 * Q_new - $15,000
To match the current $125,000 profit:
$125,000 = $27 * Q_new - $15,000
$140,000 / $27 = **5,185 units**.

*Assumption (ASSUMED):* You sell exactly 5,000 units annually.
*Result:* At 5,000 units, the new scenario yields ($27 * 5,000) - $15,000 = **$120,000**. You lose $5,000 compared to staying the course unless you raise volume or retain >100% of customers.
*Sensitivity:* If churn hits 10% (4,500 units), profit drops to $106,500 (a $18,500 loss vs. status quo). You need a **3.7% volume increase** just to match today's profits, assuming zero churn.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is **Price Elasticity of Demand** (how many customers leave when price rises 20%).
*Trigger:* If a survey of your 5,000 loyalists indicates less than **96% intent to repurchase** at $42, reject the certification for now.
*Rule of Thumb:* In niche baby goods, a 20% price hike typically causes 15-25% churn unless the brand has monopoly power.
*Step this week:* Send a segmented email to 500 top customers offering a pre-order at $42 with a "GOTS Coming Soon" narrative. If conversion on this specific offer is below 80%, do not file for certification.
