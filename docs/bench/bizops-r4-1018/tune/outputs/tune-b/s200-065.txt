**The Call**
Reject the single blended CAC number for your board deck; it obscures the fact that your SMB unit economics are likely efficient while your enterprise efficiency is currently unproven. Present two distinct CAC figures (SMB vs. Enterprise) alongside a "Blended Payback Period" metric to justify the budget increase without hiding the long sales cycle risk.

**The Numbers**
A blended CAC formula ($Total Spend / Total Customers) fails here because the $350,000 total spend (Marketing $150k + Sales $200k) supports two fundamentally different motion costs.

*   **SMB CAC (Computed):**
    *   *Assumption:* Marketing drives SMB leads; Sales time is split 20/80 between SMB/Enterprise based on deal complexity.
    *   *Attributed Spend:* $120k Marketing + $40k Sales = $160,000.
    *   *Customers:* 80.
    *   *Result:* **$2,000 per SMB customer**.
    *   *Efficiency:* $2,000 CAC vs. $1,200 ACV implies negative first-year margin unless churn is near zero or expansion revenue is immediate.

*   **Enterprise CAC (Computed):**
    *   *Assumption:* Remaining spend attributed here.
    *   *Attributed Spend:* $30k Marketing + $160k Sales = $190,000.
    *   *Customers:* 12.
    *   *Result:* **~$15,833 per Enterprise customer**.
    *   *Efficiency:* $15,833 CAC vs. $45,000 ACV is a healthy 3.0x LTV:CAC ratio (assuming standard margins), contradicting your fear of it being "unsustainably high."

*   **The Distortion:** Your manual calculation of $25,000 Enterprise CAC likely over-allocated marketing spend or under-counted the closed deals relative to the specific sales effort. The real issue is not the cost, but the **cash flow gap**: you spend $15k today to get $45k nine months later.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The decision to increase the sales budget by 50% flips on **Enterprise Sales Cycle Variance**. If the 9-month cycle slips to 12 months, your cash burn during the acquisition period increases by 33%, potentially breaking runway before revenue recognizes.
*   *Trigger Rule:* If the average time-to-close for new enterprise deals exceeds 10 months in Q3, freeze the budget increase and audit the sales pipeline velocity.
*   *Actionable Step:* If your current cash runway is less than 18 months, consider shifting 20% of the proposed enterprise sales budget to SMB retention/expansion programs to shorten the payback period.
