**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that changes this from a margin win to an existential threat is the **definition of "triage"** performed by non-lawyers.
*   **Trigger Value:** If a care coordinator suggests a specific legal strategy, interprets a statute for a user, or drafts any document beyond a blank form, you have crossed into UPL.
*   **Stop/Pivot Rule:** If your proposed workflow requires non-lawyers to determine *which* legal remedy applies (rather than just collecting contact info), stop immediately.
*   **Actionable Step:** If you cannot define a "firewall" where non-lawyers act strictly as administrative schedulers without offering any legal opinion, consider retaining the outsourced model or creating a completely separate legal subsidiary with distinct branding and billing.

**Regulatory Red Flags & Required Routing:**
In California, two primary doctrines endanger your plan:
1.  **Unauthorized Practice of Law (UPL):** Only active members of the State Bar of California may practice law. "Practicing law" includes giving legal advice, selecting legal forms, or negotiating legal rights. If your care coordinators (non-lawyers) listen to a client's employment dispute and say, "You should file a wage claim," that is UPL. Even "triage" that involves analyzing facts to recommend a legal path is prohibited.
2.  **Corporate Practice of Law:** California generally prohibits business corporations (like your EAP) from employing lawyers to render legal services to the public. Law firms must typically be owned by lawyers. While there are exceptions for "in-house" counsel serving the *corporation*, employing lawyers to serve your *clients* (the employees of your customers) under your brand may violate this rule unless structured as a registered legal corporation or under a specific exemption.

**Activities Care Coordinators Must Avoid:**
*   **Never** interpret laws, regulations, or contracts for a client.
*   **Never** recommend a specific legal course of action (e.g., "sue," "settle," "file this form").
*   **Never** draft legal documents or fill in legal forms on behalf of a client.
*   **Never** represent that the EAP provides "legal advice" if the person speaking is not a lawyer.

**Absolute Requirements for Legal Opinion (Tier 3 Routing):**
Before hiring staff or changing invoices, you must engage California counsel to answer:
1.  Can a non-lawyer EAP entity employ attorneys to provide direct legal services to third-party beneficiaries (employees) without violating the corporate practice of law doctrine?
2.  Does billing "legal and mental health" as a single line item constitute holding out the non-lawyer entity as a law firm?
3.  What specific script can care coordinators use that remains strictly administrative and avoids the "gray area" of legal triage?
