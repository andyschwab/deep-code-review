**The Numbers:**
Your current pricing is $0.05 per virtual user hour (VUH). Your volatility stems from fixed infrastructure overhead versus variable compute costs.
*Formula:* Gross Margin = (Revenue - Cloud Costs) / Revenue.
*Current Data:* Revenue $40k; Costs $8k–$22k.
*Computed Margins:*
- **Best Case (Low Demand/Cost):** ($40k - $8k) / $40k = **80%** (Matches your 85% observation roughly).

- **ASSUMED Base Case:** Average cloud cost of $15k (mid-weighted toward spikes due to your flash-sale niche). Result: ($40k - $15k) / $40k = **62.5%**.

*Unit Economics Skeleton:*
