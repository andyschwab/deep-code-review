### Evidence Gathering Plan & Decision Framework

**1. Immediate Evidence to Gather (Next 7 Days)**
Do not adjust pricing yet. Your assumption that the price page caused preemptive churn is untested. Gather these three specific data points to distinguish between "perceived value" and "competitor switching":

*   **Cancellation Reason Segmentation:** Tag the last 60 days of cancellations by stated reason (if collected) or support ticket context. Look specifically for keywords: "too expensive," "found alternative," "budget cut," or "renewal shock."
    *   *Source:* CRM/Support logs.
    *   *Metric:* % of cancellations citing price vs. competitor.
*   **The "Grandfather" Cohort Analysis:** Compare the churn rate of customers on the *old* annual plan (who saw the new prices but kept their rate) vs. those who were actually up for renewal and faced a price hike (if any existed) vs. monthly users.
    *   *Hypothesis:* If grandfathered users are churning at the same rate as others, the issue is **not** the new price tag on the website, but likely product value or a broader market shift.
*   **Exit Survey / "Win-Back" Interviews:** Contact 10 recently canceled annual subscribers. Ask: "What specific event triggered your decision to cancel now?" and "Did you see our new pricing, or had you already decided to leave?"
    *   *Method:* 15-minute calls (Mom Test style: ask about past actions, not future intentions).

**2. Decision Matrix: Adjust Now vs. Wait**

| Evidence Scenario | Verdict | Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Scenario A:** >50% of leavers cite "found cheaper competitor" or specific competitor names. | **Competitor Switch** | **Wait.** Do not lower prices. Prepare competitive battle cards for Sales. |
| **Scenario B:** >50% cite "price too high" but mention *no* competitor; grandfathered users are *not* churning. | **Perceived Value Shock** | **Test Adjustment.** Consider a temporary "loyalty discount" for renewals rather than a global price cut. |
| **Scenario C:** Grandfathered users are churning at the same high rate; reasons are vague ("business changes"). | **Product/Market Issue** | **Wait.** A price cut will not fix a product value gap. Investigate product usage drops prior to cancellation. |
| **Scenario D:** Data is inconclusive or sample size < 20 interviews. | **Insufficient Data** | **Wait.** Present the "Unknown" status to the CFO. Risk of revenue loss from a premature price cut outweighs the churn risk for now. |
