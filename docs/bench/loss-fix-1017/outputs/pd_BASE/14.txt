### Interview Plan: CodeForge Trial Conversion

**Objective:** Validate whether "unclear value perception" drives the 70% trial churn without exhausting the 87-user pool.

**Sample Strategy: Stratified Micro-Sampling**
Do not interview randomly. Select **8 users total** (approx. 9% of base) across four distinct behavioral segments to isolate the "value perception" variable from product gaps.

| Segment | Criteria | Count | Why Interview? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | : |
| **A. The Silent Drop-offs** | Started trial, 0 PRs reviewed, churned. | 2 | Tests if they never understood *what* the tool does (pure value perception). |
| **B. The Active Churners** | Started trial, >3 PRs reviewed, churned. | 2 | Tests if they saw value but didn't see *enough* to pay (pricing/fit vs. perception). |
| **C. The Converters** | Successfully paid. | 2 | Establishes the baseline: what specific moment triggered their "aha"? |
| **D. The Non-Starters** | Signed up, never installed/configured. | 2 | Tests friction in the "first mile" setup vs. value prop confusion. |

**Selection Protocol (Anti-Bias)**
To avoid interviewing only the "vocal/available":
1.  **Filter by Behavior, Not Response:** Pull the lists from your analytics database first based on the criteria above.
2.  **Randomize Within Segments:** If Segment A has 20 users, assign them numbers 1–20 and pick two via a random number generator. Do not let account managers suggest "interesting" candidates.
3.  **Blind Invites:** Use a generic subject line ("Quick feedback on your recent trial") rather than "We want to discuss why you cancelled," which attracts only those with strong negative emotions.

**Interview Design (The Mom Test)**
Since you suspect "unclear value," do not ask "Did you understand the value?" (They will lie to be polite). Ask about past specific behaviors:
*   "Walk me through the last time you tried to set up a code review workflow before using CodeForge."
*   "What was the specific task you were trying to complete when you opened CodeForge for the first time?"
*   "At what point did you decide to stop using the trial? What were you doing immediately before that?"

**Preservation of Pool**
*   **Cap:** Stop at 8 interviews. Look for pattern saturation (if the last 2 interviews repeat the same reasons as the first 6).
*   **Follow-up Gate:** Do not ask these 8 users to validate solutions yet. Tag them as "Discovery Only." Reserve the remaining ~79 users (minus natural churn) for future solution testing.

**Deliverable for Team Review:**
Present this matrix and the 3 behavioral questions above. Agree on the "Kill Criteria" before scheduling: If 6/8 churned users cite a specific missing feature (not perception), the hypothesis is false, and you must pivot to product development.

*Note: Ensure the "Active Churners" actually completed a full review cycle; if they abandoned mid-process, move them to "Silent Drop-offs" for accurate segmentation.*
*Schedule interviews for 15 minutes max; respect their time to maintain goodwill for future re-engagement.*
