### Discovery Plan: Validating Enterprise Reporting Demand

**Objective:** Determine if low usage at Meridian stems from mismatched report content (fixable) or a lack of genuine workflow need (non-starter) before building for new prospects.

**1. Retrospective "Switch" Interviews (Meridian Corp)**
*Target:* The 3 active users and 5 inactive power-users (finance leads).
*Method:* Conduct 30-minute interviews focusing on past behavior, not future desires.
*Key Questions:*
*   "Walk me through the last time you needed expense data for a stakeholder. What specific question were you trying to answer?"
*   "What did you do instead of using our dashboard? (e.g., exported to Excel, asked an analyst)."
*   "Show me the spreadsheet or slide you eventually produced."
*Goal:* Identify the specific "job" the dashboard failed to hire for. Did they need compliance audits, budget variance, or vendor spend analysis?

**2. Problem-First Sales Intercepts (New Prospects)**
*Target:* The two enterprise prospects currently in the pipeline.
*Method:* Sales to pause the "feature demo" and run a diagnostic script.
*Script:* "Before we show you reporting, tell me about the last board meeting where expense data was requested. What format did they want it in? Who currently builds that?"
*Constraint:* Do not show the current dashboard. Record the specific data points and formats requested.
*Goal:* Verify if the prospects' needs match the gaps found at Meridian or if they are asking for a different class of reporting entirely.

**3. The "Concierge" Test (Cheapest Validation)**
*Action:* Offer to manually generate the specific report the new prospects need using raw data exports, delivered via email within 24 hours.
*Success Metric:* Will the prospect accept a manual PDF/CSV over "waiting for the feature"?
*Kill Threshold:** If prospects refuse the manual report or fail to provide specific criteria for what they need, demand is likely low. Do not build the automated dashboard.

**4. Quantitative Signal Check**
*Action:* Analyze support tickets and sales call recordings from the last 6 months for keywords like "export," "CSV," "custom report," or "finance team."
*Goal:* Triangulate interview findings with organic demand signals across the broader base.

**Decision Gate:**
Commit engineering resources only if:
1.  Meridian interviews reveal a specific, high-value question the current dashboard misses.
2.  New prospects define a concrete use case and accept the concierge test.
3.  Support/sales data shows >15% of enterprise leads raise this unprompted.

If these conditions are not met, the finding is "checkbox feature," and the recommendation is to stop development and focus on core expense flow efficiency.

*Note: This plan prioritizes behavioral evidence (what they did/accept) over stated intent (what they say they want).*
*Timeline: 2 weeks for interviews and concierge tests before any roadmap commitment.*
