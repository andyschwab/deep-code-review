**Recommendation: Run a "Fake-Door" Pricing Test Before Building**

Do not commit engineering time yet. Your riskiest assumption is not technical feasibility, but **willingness to pay**. The cheapest way to disconfirm this is a behavioral test where users attempt to purchase the feature before it exists.

**The Test Plan**
1.  **Setup (2 days):** Add an "AI Meeting Summaries" toggle in your settings for eligible accounts (e.g., teams with recurring meetings >45 mins).
2.  **The Hook:** When a user enables it, display a modal: *"AI Summaries are coming soon. Unlock automatic transcripts and action items for an additional $10/month."*
3.  **The Action:** Include a "Start Free Trial" or "Pre-order Now" button that leads to a payment flow (or a "Coming Soon – Join Waitlist" page if legal/compliance blocks fake charges).
4.  **The Metric:** Track the **conversion rate** of eligible users who click the button.
5.  **The Kill Threshold:** Define this number *before* launching.
    *   *Example formulation:* "If fewer than **[X]%** of exposed users attempt to upgrade within 14 days, we kill the feature or pivot to a lower-cost implementation."
    *   *How to set [X]:* Calculate the minimum adoption needed to cover the $4,000 monthly compute cost at your proposed price point. If you need 400 upgrades to break even, and you expose the offer to 1,000 users, your threshold is 40%. If the real number is 5%, you have saved two months of engineering time.

**Execution Timeline for Next Wednesday**
*   **Today/Tomorrow:** Define the specific kill threshold based on your break-even math.
*   **Wed–Fri:** Implement the UI toggle and modal (no backend audio processing required).
*   **Monday:** Expose to 10–20% of your target segment.
*   **Wednesday Review:** Present the click-through and conversion data to the CEO/Eng Lead.

**Decision Logic**
*   **High Conversion:** Proceed to build the MVP, confident in ROI.
*   **Low Conversion:** You have validated that users *say* they want it but *won't pay* for it. Do not build. Investigate if a lighter version (e.g., manual upload only) works, or drop the idea entirely.

**Notes**
*   Ensure your legal team approves the "fake door" copy to avoid billing confusion; a "Reserve Spot" pre-order is a safe alternative.
*   Do not survey users about this; their stated intent ("Yes, I'd pay") is unreliable compared to this behavioral signal.
