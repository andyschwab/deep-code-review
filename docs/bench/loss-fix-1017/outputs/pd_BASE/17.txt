**Recommendation: Do not commit to either model yet. Run a "Riskiest-Assumption Gate" test first.**

Your current data (5 customers) is too small to validate a scaling strategy. Four sales-led successes and one self-serve attempt is statistically inconclusive. Your core risk is the assumption that "mid-size companies *require* sales relationships for this price point." If this is false, hiring sales reps now burns cash on a problem you could solve with product. If it is true, building self-serve features now wastes engineering time on a channel that won't convert.

**Immediate Action Plan (Next 2 Weeks):**

1.  **Test the Assumption:** Isolate the "sales requirement" variable.
    *   **The Test:** Create a "fake door" or concierge experiment on your website targeting the 50–500 employee segment. Drive traffic (via ads or content) to a pricing page showing the $800/mo plan with a "Start Free Trial" button (self-serve) vs. "Book Demo" (sales-led).
    *   **Metric:** Conversion rate to sign-up or demo request.
    *   **Kill Threshold:** Define this *before* running. Example: "If fewer than 2% of visitors click 'Start Free Trial' despite clear value prop, we assume self-serve is dead for now." Or, "If we get 10 self-serve signups but 0 convert to paid without human touch, the assumption holds."

2.  **Interview the Self-Serve Customer:**
    *   Conduct a "Jobs-to-be-Done" switch interview with your one self-serve customer. Ask specifically: *What prompted you to look for a solution? Why did you choose us without talking to anyone? What nearly stopped you?*
    *   Compare their profile against the four hand-held customers. Is the self-serve user a different persona (e.g., technical buyer vs. compliance officer)?

3.  **Decision Matrix (Fill these blanks with real data):**
    *   **Option A (Sales-Led):** Proceed only if [X]% of new leads reject self-serve options explicitly asking for a human.
    *   **Option B (Self-Serve):** Proceed only if [Y] new customers sign up and activate without human intervention in the next 30 days.
    *   **Option C (Hybrid):** If data shows distinct segments, route enterprise leads to sales and SMB/tech-savvy leads to self-serve.

**Non-Goals for This Stage:**
*   Hiring full-time Account Executives.
*   Major engineering overhaul of onboarding flows.
*   Declaring "Product-Market Fit."

Your strategy session should focus on designing these tests, not choosing a path based on intuition. The cost of hiring the wrong team or building the wrong product far exceeds the cost of two weeks of experimentation.
