### Riskiest Assumption Gate & Testing Roadmap

You hypothesized that **host fee tolerance (15%)** is the riskiest assumption. However, based on your metrics (340 hosts, 90 chefs, only 47 bookings), the data suggests a more fundamental failure: **conversion friction** or **lack of supply liquidity**, not necessarily price sensitivity. If hosts aren't booking even once, testing whether they would pay *more* (or accept a fee) is premature. The assumption that would collapse the model immediately is likely **"Chefs want this type of gig"** (supply side) or **"Hosts care more about cuisine than price/convenience"** (demand side fit).

If chefs do not actually want these gigs (due to logistics, pay, or effort), you have no product. If hosts care more about price/convenience than cuisine, your value proposition is misaligned.
