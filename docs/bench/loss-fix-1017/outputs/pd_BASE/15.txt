**Recommendation: Do not invest in full localization yet. Run a riskiest-assumption test first.**

Your core assumption—that 14 requests signal sufficient market demand to justify a $90k, four-month investment—is currently unvalidated. Building the full solution now violates the **riskiest-assumption gate**: you are about to spend significant engineering capacity on a hypothesis (viability/desirability) that has not been disconfirmed or confirmed with behavioral evidence.

**The Required Next Step: The Cheap Test**
Before writing code, you must validate **willingness to pay** and **actual switching behavior** in these specific markets. Translation alone does not prove revenue potential.

**Test Design:**
1.  **Objective:** Disconfirm the assumption that retailers in Germany, Japan, and Brazil will pay for a localized ShopEase.
2.  **Method:** "Fake Door" or Concierge Test.
    *   Create localized landing pages (German, Japanese, Portuguese) describing the specific value prop for their market.
    *   Include a clear pricing tier in local currency.
    *   Drive targeted traffic (via low-cost ads or direct outreach to the 14 leads + similar prospects).
    *   **Metric:** Click-through to "Start Trial" or "Contact Sales" *after* seeing the price.
3.  **Kill Threshold (You must define this number):**
    *   *Example structure:* "If fewer than [X]% of visitors from these regions attempt to sign up at the listed price, or if fewer than [Y] of the 14 original leads commit to a pilot contract conditional on localization, we **drop** the initiative for this quarter."
    *   *Action:* Set the minimum conversion rate that would mathematically justify the $90k cost within your target payback period. If the test result is below this number, do not build.

**Why this approach?**
*   **Cost:** A few hundred dollars in ads and design time vs. $90k and four months of team velocity.
*   **Evidence:** It measures what users *do* (attempt to buy), not what they *say* (request a feature).
*   **Decision Clarity:** It provides the CEO and Finance with a binary go/no-go signal based on revenue intent, not speculation about "tip of the iceberg" demand.

**Immediate Action Plan for Quarterly Planning:**
1.  Present the $90k build as **conditional** on passing the test above.
2.  Propose a 2-week sprint to run the cheap test.
3.  Commit to the full build *only* if the kill threshold is met. If not, redirect the team to deepen English-market features where demand is proven.
