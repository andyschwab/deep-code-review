### Decision Framework: Cresta CRM Integration Sunset

**1. Quantify the Trade-off (The "Keep" Case)**
*   **Revenue at Risk:** $210,000 ARR.
*   **Engineering Cost:** 3 sprints/year. (Calculate internal cost: 3 sprints × [Avg Sprint Cost]).
*   **Opportunity Cost:** Value of 3 sprints applied to your highest-priority roadmap item (e.g., new feature revenue or retention lift).
*   **Net Value:** Revenue - (Eng Cost + Opportunity Cost). If negative, the financial case for sunset is strong.

**2. Validate the Riskiest Assumption (The "Churn" Fear)**
*   **Assumption:** "Migrating these 14 accounts will cause significant disruption leading to churn."
*   **Test:** Conduct 5–7 "Switch Interviews" (Jobs-to-be-Done style) with the primary admins of the 14 accounts.
    *   *Ask:* "Walk me through the last time you considered switching CRMs. What stopped you?" and "If we provided a concierge migration service to [Modern CRM], what would be your biggest fear?"
    *   *Goal:* Determine if the barrier is technical disruption or simple inertia.
*   **Kill Threshold:** If >50% of interviewed accounts indicate they are already planning to migrate or would accept a managed migration, the churn risk is lower than assumed.

**3. Define the Decision Matrix**
| Scenario | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **High Churn Risk + High Revenue** | **Maintain.** Build a "legacy lock" to stop new adoption but support existing users. |
| **Low Churn Risk + Low/Negative Net Value** | **Sunset.** Offer a paid/concierge migration path to a supported CRM. |
| **Unclear Risk** | **Pause Build.** Run the interviews before allocating more sprints. Do not build new features for this integration. |

### Recommendation & Next Steps

**Recommendation:** Treat the integration as a **sunset candidate** pending validation. The low adoption (0.16%) and high maintenance burden suggest the opportunity cost of those 3 sprints likely exceeds the $210k revenue, especially if those sprints could drive growth elsewhere. However, do not cut the service until the churn risk is quantified.

**Immediate Next Steps:**
1.  **Calculate the exact engineering cost** of 3 sprints and compare it directly to the $210k revenue to establish the baseline net value.
2.  **Schedule interviews** with 5 of the 14 active accounts within the next 10 days to test the "disruption/churn" assumption before presenting the final plan to leadership.
