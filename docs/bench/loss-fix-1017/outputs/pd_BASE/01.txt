**Recommendation: Do not prioritize building offline mode yet. Prioritize validating the "regular use" hypothesis first.**

Your current data confirms high *stated interest* (12 calls) and one confirmed *loss* (450 technicians), but it does not confirm *frequency of use* or *retention impact*. Building a robust offline sync engine is a high-cost infrastructure shift. If only 5% of your 2,300 customers operate in dead zones regularly, the ROI may not justify the Q3 opportunity cost. If 60% need it daily, it is existential. You currently cannot distinguish between a "nice-to-have safety net" and a "daily workflow blocker."

**Immediate Investigation Plan (Run these before coding):**

1.  **Quantitative Triage (The "How Many" Question)**
    *   **Action:** Send a 3-question survey to your 2,300 existing customer admins.
    *   **Questions:**
        1.  "In the last 30 days, how many times did a technician report being unable to complete a job due to lack of connectivity?" (0, 1-5, 5+)
        2.  "What percentage of your total work hours occur in areas with no cellular signal?" (<5%, 5-20%, 20%+)
        3.  "If we launched offline mode next month, would you increase your seat count?" (Yes/No/Maybe)
    *   **Decision Threshold:** If <15% of customers report weekly connectivity blockers, deprioritize for Q3. If >30% report daily blockers, proceed to build.

2.  **Qualitative Deep Dive (The "Why" and "Where" Question)**
    *   **Action:** Conduct 5 "Mom Test" interviews with the 12 prospects who asked, plus 3 existing customers in rural industries (utilities, agriculture, mining).
    *   **Focus:** Do not ask "Do you want offline mode?" Ask: "Tell me about the last time your team couldn't finish a ticket because of signal." Dig for specific behaviors: Do they carry paper backups? Do they drive to find signal? What happens to the data?
    *   **Goal:** Determine if the pain is a minor annoyance or a workflow stopper.

3.  **Loss Analysis**
    *   **Action:** Interview the lost prospect (450 technicians).
    *   **Focus:** Specifically ask how they use the competitor's offline mode. Is it for viewing schematics, logging time, or capturing signatures? This defines your Minimum Viable Product (MVP) scope if you proceed.

**Reasoning for VP of Product:**
"We have evidence of a sales barrier, but no evidence of usage frequency. Building full offline sync requires significant engineering resources. By running a 1-week survey and 5 interviews, we can quantify the 'regular use' segment. If the data shows sporadic need, we can explore lighter solutions (e.g., caching specific views) rather than a full architectural overhaul. If the data shows critical daily blockage, we have the justification to re-prioritize the roadmap with confidence."

*Note: Do not estimate market size or technical effort until the survey returns real numbers.*
*Note: If the survey response rate is low (<10%), treat the risk as unverified and assume the churn risk is higher than currently measured.*
