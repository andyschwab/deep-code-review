### Validation Plan: Advanced Version History Add-On

**1. Name the Riskiest Assumption**
*Assumption:* Power users at enterprise accounts are willing to pay $15/seat/month for branching/comparison views, rather than expecting this in the base plan.
*Status:* Untested. Building now risks low adoption or churn.

**2. The Cheap Test (Fake-Door + Concierge)**
Do not build the feature. Instead, instrument the existing UI to measure intent and conduct high-fidelity sales interviews.

*   **Test A: The "Fake Door" (Behavioral Signal)**
    *   **Action:** Add a "Advanced Version History (Beta)" button in the workspace settings for your top 20 enterprise accounts only.
    *   **Flow:** Clicking the button opens a modal: *"This feature is coming soon. Join the waitlist for early access at $15/seat/mo?"* with two buttons: ["Join Waitlist"] and ["Not Interested"].
    *   **Metric:** Click-through rate (CTR) on the button vs. conversion to "Join Waitlist."
    *   **Kill Threshold:** If <5% of exposed power users click the button, or <2% opt-in to the paid waitlist, **do not build**. The perceived value is too low.

*   **Test B: The "Mom Test" Interview (Willingness to Pay)**
    *   **Action:** Schedule 15-minute calls with the 3 accounts who mentioned iteration struggles + 5 other power users.
    *   **Script Focus:** Do not pitch the solution. Ask: "Walk me through the last time you lost work or struggled to compare designs. What did you do to fix it? How much time did that cost?"
    *   **Pricing Validation:** Ask: "Have you tried to solve this with other tools? What do you pay for them?" If they haven't tried to solve it, they won't pay $15/mo.
    *   **Kill Threshold:** If 0 out of 8 interviewees mention having a budget for this or currently paying for a workaround, **do not build**.

**3. Non-Goals for This Stage**
*   **Building the branching engine:** This is a non-goal until the kill thresholds are passed.
*   **Polishing the comparison UI:** Irrelevant if no one clicks the entry point.
*   **Broad rollout:** Restrict testing to enterprise accounts only; free users are not the target.

**4. Decision Matrix**
| Outcome | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **High Intent** (>5% click, >2% waitlist, users confirm budget) | Proceed to MVP spec. |
| **High Interest, Low Price Sensitivity** (Users want it but balk at $15) | Re-test at $5/seat or bundle into higher tier. |
| **Low Intent** (Below thresholds) | Kill idea. Keep basic history free; explore lightweight alternatives. |

**Next Step:** Implement the fake-door tracking snippet and schedule the 8 interviews by Friday.

*Note: Do not estimate market size or revenue yet; those numbers are fabricated without test data.*
