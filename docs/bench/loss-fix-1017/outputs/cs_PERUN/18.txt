**Fund the $45k record recovery and mandate data migration for Acme Analytics and DataSync Corp to avoid $3.2M in fines and audit failure.**

**The Decision**
You must approve two actions by November 30 to meet the December 15 GDPR deadline:
1.  **Authorize $45,000** to recover missing transaction records (5.2 years vs. 7-year policy).
2.  **Require Acme Analytics and DataSync Corp** to migrate data from non-compliant regions or face contract termination.

**Trade-offs: Transaction Records**
*   **Recover ($45k):** Restores 1.8 years of data lost in migration. Prevents financial audit failure and tax penalties. Risk: Archives are 5+ years old; recovery may be incomplete.
*   **Accept Gap ($0):** Saves immediate cost. Guarantees policy violation (5.2 vs 7 years). High risk of audit failure and regulatory penalties during CCPA review (Jan 20, 2025).

**Trade-offs: Non-Compliant Vendors**
*   **Migrate Data:** Maintains vendor utility while achieving compliance. Cost borne by vendor or shared. Risk: Vendor refusal or technical infeasibility.
*   **Terminate Contracts:** Eliminates compliance risk immediately. Risk: Operational disruption to analytics and sync services; requires finding replacements before Q1 2025.

**Context & Risks**
*   **Exposure:** 34,200 customer records held beyond the 24-month limit (oldest from 2019).
*   **Liability:** Over-retention risks fines up to 4% of annual revenue ($3.2M). Unencrypted backups add $150k–$500k per incident risk.
*   **Deadlines:** GDPR corrective actions due December 15, 2024 (78 days remaining).
*   **Total Remediation:** $183,000 one-time + $16,000/year required to close all gaps (DSAR automation, litigation hold, backup encryption).

**Recommendation**
The $45k recovery cost is 1.4% of the potential revenue-based fine. Accepting the gap creates a definitive audit failure. For vendors, migration preserves business continuity while curing the breach; termination is the fallback if they refuse.
