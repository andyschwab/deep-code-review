### Evidence Gathering Plan & Decision Framework

**1. Immediate Evidence to Gather (Next 7 Days)**
*   **Exit Survey Data (Quantitative):** Implement a mandatory one-question exit survey for cancellations: "What was the primary reason for canceling?" with options: "Price increase perceived," "Found cheaper alternative," "Lack of usage/value," "Other." *Target: 50+ responses to establish a baseline split.*
*   **Support Ticket Analysis (Qualitative):** Review all support transcripts from March 14–present containing keywords "price," "cost," "competitor," or "cancel." Tag them as "Price Shock" vs. "Competitor Mention." *Goal: Identify specific competitor names or pricing thresholds mentioned.*
*   **Usage Cohort Check:** Compare the usage frequency (logins/reports run) of the canceling cohort in the 30 days prior to cancellation against those who renewed. *Hypothesis test: If usage is high, it's price sensitivity; if low, it's value mismatch.*

**2. Decision Matrix: Adjust Now vs. Wait**

| Condition | Evidence Required | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Adjust Pricing Now** | >60% of exit surveys cite "Price" AND <15% mention specific competitors. | **Action:** Offer a "loyalty discount" or freeze rate for 12 months to at-risk renewals immediately. The issue is perception, not market reality. |
| **Wait / Investigate** | >40% mention specific competitors OR usage data shows low engagement before cancel. | **Action:** Do not cut price. Conduct "switch interviews" with 5 lost customers to understand the competitor's offer. Price cuts won't fix a value gap. |
| **Hybrid Approach** | Split signal (e.g., 40% price, 40% competitor). | **Action:** A/B test a targeted retention offer (e.g., "Stay at old rate if you renew this week") to a small segment before rolling out broadly. |
