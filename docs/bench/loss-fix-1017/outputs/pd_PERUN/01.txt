**Recommendation: Do not commit to building offline mode yet. First, run a "Riskiest-Assumption Gate" test to validate usage frequency versus "safety net" desire.**

Your current data confirms *desirability* (prospects ask for it) and *viability risk* (you lost a 450-tech deal), but it does not confirm *actual usage frequency*. Building a robust offline sync engine is a high-cost infrastructure change. If 80% of customers only need it as a rare safety net, a lighter-weight solution (e.g., better error handling, local caching of read-only data) might suffice. If 40% use it daily, you must build the full sync. You cannot decide based on sales calls alone, as prospects often conflate "nice to have" with "must have."

**Immediate Investigation Plan (Complete in 2 weeks):**

1.  **Quantitative Survey (Existing Customers):**
    *   **Ask:** "In the last 30 days, how many times were you unable to complete a task because of poor connectivity?"
    *   **Segment:** Cross-tabulate responses by customer industry and geographic region (rural vs. urban) to test your assumption about rural areas.
    *   **Metric:** Identify the % of customers with >5 incidents/month. This is your "daily active offline" segment.

2.  **Qualitative "Mom Test" Interviews (5 Lost Prospects + 5 At-Risk Customers):**
    *   **Ask:** "Tell me about the last time connectivity was an issue. What specifically were you trying to do? How did you solve it?"
    *   **Goal:** Distinguish between *blocking* issues (cannot submit timesheet) and *annoyances* (slow logo load). Verify if the "safety net" is actually a critical workflow blocker.

3.  **Define the Kill Threshold (Decision Rule):**
    *   **Proposed Rule:** Proceed to full build only if >25% of existing customers report >5 connectivity blockers/month OR if 3 of the next 5 enterprise deals list it as a mandatory requirement.
    *   **Alternative:** If <10% report frequent issues, prioritize a "lite" caching feature instead.

**Reasoning for VP:**
We have evidence of revenue loss, but no evidence of the *scale* of the engineering effort required versus the value returned. By running these tests, we avoid over-engineering a solution for a "safety net" need while gathering the specific data required to justify the investment if the need is indeed widespread.

*Note: Do not estimate market size or competitor feature depth internally; route those specific questions to sales intelligence or external reports.*
