Your assumption that higher completion equals better long-term outcomes is the riskiest variable here. In employee learning, delayed "first course" often correlates with lower retention because the immediate utility signal is weak, even if the onboarding flow feels more engaging.

3.  **Kill Threshold:** If the difference in 30-day activation between the two groups is <2%, default to Test A. Faster time-to-value reduces support costs and engineering load, providing a clearer ROI unless the retention lift is significant.
