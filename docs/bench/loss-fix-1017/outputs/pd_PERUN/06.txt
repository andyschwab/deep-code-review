### Sunset Decision Framework: Cresta CRM Integration

**1. Quantitative Threshold (The "Kill" Math)**
*   **Current Cost:** 3 engineering sprints/year. (Assign your internal fully-loaded cost per sprint to get $Cost_Eng).
*   **Current Revenue:** $210,000/year.
*   **Opportunity Cost:** Value of the highest-priority feature those 3 sprints could build instead.
*   **Decision Rule:** If ($Cost_Eng + Opportunity_Cost) > $210,000, the integration is net-negative *unless* churn risk exceeds the surplus.

**2. Qualitative Risk Assessment (The "Disruption" Test)**
Do not assume migration pain; verify it. Run a **Jobs-to-be-Done "Switch" Interview** with 5 of the 14 active accounts.
*   *Ask:* "Walk me through the last time you considered changing your CRM. What specific workflow in Redwood+Cresta would have broken?"
*   *Metric:* Count how many cite "existential business disruption" vs. "minor inconvenience."
*   *Threshold:* If >50% indicate existential disruption, a forced sunset risks immediate churn. If <50%, they are likely滞留 (stranded) by inertia, not dependency.

**3. The Experiment (Concierge Migration)**
Before sunsetting, run a **concierge test** with 3 willing accounts.
*   *Action:* Offer white-glove migration support to a modern CRM at no cost in exchange for a case study.
*   *Signal:* If they refuse free help, the "disruption" is real or they are dormant. If they accept, your churn risk is low.

### Recommendation & Next Steps

**Recommendation:** **Prepare to sunset, but validate churn risk first.**
The revenue density ($15k/account) is likely lower than the fully-loaded cost of 3 sprints plus the opportunity cost of delayed innovation. However, do not announce a sunset date yet.

**Next Steps:**
1.  **Calculate the exact burn:** Confirm the dollar value of 3 sprints + opportunity cost. If this exceeds $210k, the financial case is closed.
2.  **Run 5 Switch Interviews:** Determine if the barrier to leave is *pain* or *inertia*.
3.  **Execute Concierge Migration:** Attempt to move 3 accounts voluntarily.
    *   *Success:* Proceed with a 6-month sunset notice and migration guides.
    *   *Failure (Refusal/Churn Threat):* Isolate these accounts on a legacy maintenance tier (no new features, minimal security patches) rather than burning core sprint capacity.

**Note:** This framework relies on your internal sprint cost data and direct customer evidence; do not proceed based on the assumption of "legacy status" alone.
**Note:** Present the "Concierge Migration" results to the VP as the definitive proof of churn risk before setting a final sunset date.
