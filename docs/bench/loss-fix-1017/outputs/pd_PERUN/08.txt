### Validation Plan: Advanced Version History Add-on

**1. Riskiest Assumption & Kill Criteria**
*   **Assumption:** Power users at enterprise accounts will pay $15/seat/month for branching/comparison views rather than expecting it in the base plan.
*   **Cheapest Test:** A "Fake Door" landing page + Concierge pilot with the 3 interested accounts.
*   **Metric:** Conversion rate from "Learn More" to "Start Pilot Waitlist" (commitment to pay).
*   **Kill Threshold:** If <2 of the 3 interested accounts commit to a paid pilot, or if <5% of cold traffic from similar segments clicks "Buy" on the pricing page, **do not build**.
*   **Sample Size:** 3 specific accounts + 500 targeted visitors to the landing page.
*   **Deadline:** 2 weeks.

**2. Discovery Interviews (The Mom Test)**
Do not ask "Would you pay for this?" (hypothetical). Instead, schedule 30-minute calls with the 3 accounts who mentioned iteration struggles.
*   **Ask:** "Walk me through the last time you lost track of a design iteration. What specifically happened? How much time did you spend fixing it?"
*   **Ask:** "What tools or workarounds are you currently using to manage versions? (e.g., Figma files named 'v2_final_final', Slack screenshots, external docs)."
*   **Ask:** "Have you ever paid extra for a plugin or tool specifically to solve version control? How much?"
*   **Goal:** Quantify the cost of their current pain. If the pain costs them $500/month in labor, $15/seat is easy. If they just "prefer" better history but manage fine, they won't pay.

**3. Behavioral Experiment (Fake Door & Concierge)**
*   **Fake Door:** Create an internal-only or beta-feature page describing "Advanced Branching & Comparison" with a clear "$15/seat/month" price tag and a "Start Free Trial" button.
    *   *Action:* Share this link directly with the 3 interested accounts and a segment of 50 similar power users.
    *   *Signal:* Track clicks on "Start Trial." When they click, show a message: "This feature is coming soon. Join the priority waitlist?" (Capture email).
*   **Concierge Pilot:** Offer the 3 interested accounts a manual "white-glove" service.
    *   *Action:* "We will manually generate branch comparison reports for your team for 2 weeks for $15/seat."
    *   *Signal:* If they agree to pay for the *manual* service, the demand is real. If they hesitate, the perceived value is too low for the price.
