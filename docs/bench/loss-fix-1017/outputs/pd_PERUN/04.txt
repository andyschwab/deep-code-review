### Discovery Plan: Validating Enterprise Reporting Demand

**1. Riskiest-Assumption Gate**
*   **Assumption:** Low usage at Meridian is due to irrelevant report content (desirability), not poor discoverability or workflow fit.
*   **Cheapest Test:** "Switch Interviews" with the 3 active Meridian users and 5 non-users to identify the specific "job" they hired the dashboard to do.
*   **Kill Threshold:** If fewer than 2 of 5 non-users can articulate a specific financial question they currently solve manually (outside Clearwater), pause feature expansion. The issue is likely workflow, not content.
*   **Decision Rule:** If specific manual workflows are identified, proceed to concierge testing. If not, investigate onboarding/discoverability before building new reports.

**2. Qualitative Discovery (The "What" and "Why")**
*   **Activity:** Run 6 "Mom Test" interviews (3 Meridian active, 3 Meridian inactive, 2 prospect champions).
*   **Focus:** Do not ask "Do you want advanced reporting?" Instead, ask: "Walk me through the last time you needed a custom expense view. What data did you pull? Where did you put it? Who did you send it to?"
*   **Output:** An Opportunity-Solution Tree mapping specific financial questions (opportunities) to potential reporting solutions, separating "nice-to-have" from "must-have" compliance needs.

**3. Behavioral Validation (The "Will They Use It")**
*   **Activity:** Concierge Experiment for the two new prospects.
*   **Method:** Before building the dashboard, offer to generate their required reports manually via CSV export and email for 2 weeks.
*   **Metric:** Track if they open the files, forward them, or ask for modifications.
*   **Kill Threshold:** If prospects do not request a modification or fail to use the manual report in a decision meeting within 14 days, demand is likely a "checkbox" rather than a core need. Do not build the automated dashboard.

**4. Quantitative Signal (The "How Many")**
*   **Activity:** Analyze support tickets and sales call transcripts from the last 6 months for keywords related to "export," "custom view," or "audit."
*   **Metric:** Frequency of specific data requests vs. generic "reporting" mentions.
*   **Goal:** Determine if the demand is fragmented (many unique requests) or concentrated (80% of users need the same 3 views).

**Next Steps for Product Director Approval:**
1.  Authorize 1 week for interview recruitment and execution.
2.  Approve manual concierge service for the two prospects (no engineering time).
3.  **Non-Goal:** No new engineering resources allocated until the concierge test proves a repeatable, valuable workflow.
