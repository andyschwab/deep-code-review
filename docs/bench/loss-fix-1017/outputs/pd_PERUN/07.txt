### Discovery Plan: Waypoint Retention (Q2)

**1. Current Evidence Summary**
*   **Churn Volume:** 68 lost customers (90 days).
*   **Interview Sample:** 22/68 churned users (Feb–Mar).
*   **Reported Themes:** Price (14), Low Engagement/Abandonment (8), Missing Calendar Sync (5).
*   **Constraint:** Interviews were semi-structured; data is self-reported, not behavioral.

**2. The Riskiest Assumption Gate**
*   **Assumption:** "Building calendar sync will retain customers who cited it as a reason for leaving, and price is not the primary driver for the 'low engagement' group."
*   **Decision:** Do **not** build calendar sync yet. The sample size (5) is small, and self-reported reasons often mask the true cause (e.g., they didn't value the app enough to miss the sync).
*   **Cheapest Test:** A "Fake Door" or Concierge test on the *existing* churned list or current at-risk users.
    *   **Action:** Email the 5 who mentioned sync (and a control group of 10 who mentioned price) with a specific offer: "We are piloting a manual calendar concierge service for 2 weeks. If we manually sync your team's schedule to your Google/Outlook calendar daily, will you reactivate your subscription?"
    *   **Metric:** Reactivation rate / Commitment to pay.
    *   **Kill Threshold:** If fewer than **2 of the 5** sync-complainants reactivate even with the manual fix, the feature will not solve the retention problem. Drop the idea.
    *   **Secondary Signal:** If the "price" group reacts at a similar rate to the "sync" group when offered a workaround, price is likely the real barrier for everyone.

**3. Validation Steps Before Building**
*   **Step A: Re-interview for Behavior (The Mom Test)**
    *   Re-contact the 8 who "stopped using after month one." Do not ask "Why didn't you like it?" Ask: "Walk me through the last time you tried to schedule a shift. What specific tool did you use instead? How much did it cost?"
    *   *Goal:* Determine if "lack of sync" was a polite excuse for "didn't see value."
*   **Step B: Quantitative Triage**
    *   Survey the remaining 46 churned customers (who weren't interviewed) with one question: "Which single missing feature would have forced you to stay?" (Include Calendar Sync, Price Discount, Other).
    *   *Goal:* Validate if the 5/22 ratio holds across the whole population.

**4. Success Criteria for Q2**
*   **Go:** Manual concierge test yields >40% reactivation among sync-complainants.
*   **No-Go/Pivot:** Reactivation is <40%, or price-sensitive users reactivate only when offered a discount. Pivot to pricing model or core value proposition redesign.

**Non-Goals for this Stage**
*   Building native two-way calendar synchronization infrastructure.
*   Redesigning the pricing page (until price is proven as the sole blocker).

**Note:** This plan prioritizes behavioral evidence over stated intent to avoid building a feature nobody pays for. Share the "Kill Threshold" with your co-founder before starting the test.
