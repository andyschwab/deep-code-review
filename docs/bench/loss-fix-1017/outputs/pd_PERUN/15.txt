**Recommendation: Do not invest in localization yet.**

The current evidence is insufficient to justify a $90,000 investment and a four-month team diversion. You have 14 expressions of interest (1.2% of your base) but zero validated willingness to pay or behavioral commitment. Building now violates the **Riskiest-Assumption Gate**: you are assuming these 14 requests represent viable market demand rather than polite curiosity.

**Immediate Action Plan (Run before building):**
1.  **Isolate the Riskiest Assumption:** "Retailers in Germany, Japan, and Brazil will switch to ShopEase and pay our fees specifically because of localization, despite us lacking local brand trust."
2.  **Execute the Cheapest Test (Concierge/Fake Door):**
    *   Contact the 14 leads individually. Do not pitch; ask about their *current* solution for language/currency and what they pay for it.
    *   Create a localized landing page (or manual mockup) for each region. Drive targeted traffic (small ad spend) to measure sign-up intent.
    *   **Kill Threshold:** If you cannot secure **5 Letters of Intent** (signed commitment to purchase upon launch) or **3 prepaid pilot contracts** from these regions within 3 weeks, the project is paused.
3.  **Decision Rule:**
    *   **Go:** Only if you hit the threshold above.
    *   **No-Go/Pivot:** If you fail to get committed pilots, the $90k is better spent deepening English-market features where your 1,200 active customers already validate demand.

**Required Data for CEO Presentation:**
Do not present "potential market size." Present the results of the test above. If the test yields fewer than the required commitments, the data proves the revenue hypothesis is unproven, protecting the company from building a feature no one pays for.
