### Recommendation: Scale Test B (Interactive Demo) with a Hybrid Optimization

**Decision:** Proceed with scaling the **Test B (Interactive Demo)** approach, but immediately initiate a hybrid experiment to reduce the "time-to-first-course" friction.

**Rationale:**
While Test A was faster, its lower completion rate (18%) suggests users are dropping off before experiencing core value. In learning platforms, **activation** is strongly correlated with completing the first meaningful action (finishing onboarding). Test B's 22% completion represents a **22% relative increase** in activated users. Even if these users are slower initially, a user who finishes onboarding is statistically more likely to return than one who abandons the flow early, regardless of speed. The 40% longer duration is a usability friction to be solved, not a reason to reject the higher-converting model.

**Immediate Action Plan (4-Week Sprint):**
1.  **Adopt Test B as the baseline:** Roll out the interactive demo to 100% of new traffic.
2.  **Launch "Hybrid Test C":** Dedicate the 4-week engineering capacity to modifying Test B. Add *optional* progress indicators or "skip to first course" shortcuts within the free exploration mode.
    *   *Hypothesis:* We can maintain Test B's high completion rate while reducing the time-to-value by offering structured exits for power users.

**Additional Data Needed to Validate:**
To confirm this decision and refine the hybrid approach, request the following data from your analytics team immediately:
*   **Day-7 and Day-30 Retention Cohorts:** Compare retention between Test A completers vs. Test B completers. If Test B completers retain at equal or higher rates, the extra time spent is an investment in engagement, not a penalty.
*   **Drop-off Heatmaps for Test B:** Identify exactly *where* users spend the extra 40% of time. Are they exploring valuable features (good) or stuck in confusion (bad)?
*   **"Aha!" Moment Correlation:** Determine if reaching the first course quickly actually predicts long-term retention for your specific user persona, or if *understanding the platform layout* (which Test B facilitates) is the true predictor.

**Metric to Watch:**
Shift your primary success metric from "Time to First Course" to **"Onboarding Completion Rate weighted by Day-7 Retention."** This balances volume of activated users with quality of activation.

*Note: Ensure the engineering team tracks "time spent in demo" vs. "courses started" in the hybrid test to isolate the impact of the new shortcuts.*
*Do not revert to Test A unless Day-30 retention for Test B users is significantly lower than Test A users.*
