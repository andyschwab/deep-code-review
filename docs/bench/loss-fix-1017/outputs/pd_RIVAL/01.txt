### Recommended Investigation Plan for Offline Mode Decision

To move from assumption to evidence before committing Q3 roadmap resources, execute these three targeted investigations:

**1. Quantitative Signal Analysis (Internal Data)**
Audit your CRM and support tickets for the last 6 months. Tag and count every instance where "offline," "no signal," "sync failed," or "connectivity" was mentioned.
*   **Goal:** Determine if the 12 recent sales objections are an outlier or part of a growing trend.
*   **Key Metric:** Calculate the percentage of total churn or lost deals in the last year explicitly attributed to connectivity issues versus price or feature gaps.

**2. Targeted Customer Discovery (Qualitative)**
Conduct 5–7 rapid interviews with two specific groups:
*   **Lost Prospects:** Contact the 12 recent deals where offline mode was raised. Ask: *"Walk me through the specific scenario where you needed offline access. What happened when you couldn't connect? How did that impact your technician's day?"*
*   **At-Risk Existing Customers:** Interview 3–5 current enterprise customers who operate in known low-connectivity zones (rural utilities, mining, remote construction). Ask: *"Tell me about the last time your technicians worked without signal. What workaround did they use (paper, screenshots, delayed entry)? How much time did that cost?"*
*   **Goal:** Validate your assumption about rural areas. You may find the need is actually about underground work (basements, tunnels), airplanes, or simply unreliable corporate Wi-Fi in urban centers.

**3. "Skin in the Game" Validation**
In your next 5 sales demos involving field operations, change your response to the offline question. Instead of saying "It's on our roadmap," say: *"We are evaluating building a robust offline mode for Q3. If we delivered that, would it change your decision today, or are there other blockers?"*
*   **Goal:** Distinguish between a "nice-to-have" safety net and a true deal-breaker. If they say "it would help, but we still need X," it's not the primary driver.

**Decision Framework for the VP**
Present your findings using this logic:
*   **Prioritize Offline Mode If:** >20% of lost enterprise revenue is directly linked to this gap, OR existing customers report >2 hours/week of wasted labor due to workarounds.
*   **Deprioritize Offline Mode If:** Requests are isolated to specific niches <5% of your TAM, or if prospects admit it's a "checkbox" item but not the primary reason for churn.

**Recommendation:** Do not build yet. Spend 1 week on the investigation above. The cost of building the wrong feature is higher than the cost of delaying the decision by one week.

*Note: Focus on quantifying the labor cost of current workarounds; this translates technical needs into business value for leadership.*
*Ensure the product trio (PM, Design, Eng) reviews the interview findings together to assess feasibility constraints early.*
