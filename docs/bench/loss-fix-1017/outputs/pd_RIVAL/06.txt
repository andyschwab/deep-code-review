### Sunset Decision Framework: Cresta CRM Integration

**1. Quantitative Viability Analysis**
*   **Revenue Retention:** $210,000 ARR (2.5% of total account base, but revenue impact depends on total company ARR).
*   **Maintenance Cost:** 3 sprints/year. Assuming an average fully loaded engineering cost of $25k/sprint, the annual cost is ~$75,000.
*   **Opportunity Cost:** 3 sprints could deliver ~6–9 medium-sized features or significant technical debt reduction. Estimate the potential revenue uplift or churn reduction from alternative uses of these sprints.
*   **Net Value:** Currently positive ($210k - $75k = $135k net), *unless* the opportunity cost of the lost features exceeds $135k.

**2. Qualitative Risk Assessment (The "Churn" Variable)**
*   **Migration Friction:** Interview the 14 customers. Determine if they are stuck due to lack of resources, specific unique workflows, or contractual lock-ins.
*   **Disruption Cost:** Estimate the engineering effort *they* must expend to switch. If high, they may churn rather than migrate.
*   **Strategic Alignment:** Does supporting a 2018-era API align with Redwood's current architecture and security standards?

**3. Decision Matrix**
| Scenario | Recommendation | Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High Churn Risk + Low Opp. Cost** | **Maintain Temporarily** | Freeze feature development; allocate only critical security patches. Set a 6-month review. |
| **Low Churn Risk + High Opp. Cost** | **Sunset Aggressively** | Announce deprecation with a 12-month timeline. Offer concierge migration support (funded by savings). |
| **High Churn Risk + High Opp. Cost** | **Hybrid Approach** | Build a lightweight "maintenance mode" adapter or partner with a third-party integration platform (e.g., Zapier) to offload maintenance. |

### Recommendation & Next Steps

**Recommendation:** Do not sunset immediately. The integration is currently net-positive in direct cash flow ($135k margin). However, the *opportunity cost* is the hidden killer. Proceed with a **"Concierge Migration" strategy**.

**Immediate Next Steps:**
1.  **Customer Discovery (Week 1-2):** The PM and CS Director must interview all 14 accounts. Ask: "What specifically prevents you from migrating?" and "If we ended support in 12 months, what would you do?"
2.  **Opportunity Audit (Week 2):** Engineering Lead to list the top 3 initiatives blocked by lack of sprint capacity and estimate their revenue impact.
3.  **Proposal (Week 3):** Present a deprecation plan offering white-glove migration assistance to these 14 accounts in exchange for a contract extension on the new stack, mitigating churn risk while freeing up engineering resources.

*Note: If the opportunity cost of the 3 sprints exceeds $135k in potential value, the decision shifts immediately to sunset regardless of churn risk.*
