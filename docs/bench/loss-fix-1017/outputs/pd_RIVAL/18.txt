### Assessment: Granular Admin Permissions at TeamSync

**Verdict:** Current evidence strongly supports your assumption that Meridian Corp is an outlier. There is insufficient signal to treat this as a widespread enterprise need at this stage.

**Key Evidence:**
*   **Signal-to-Noise Ratio:** Zero requests from 339 other active accounts over 18 months suggests the current binary model satisfies 99.7% of the base. In product discovery, silence is often data; if this were a critical blocker, other scaling teams would likely have flagged it.
*   **Cost of Delay vs. Cost of Build:** A six-week commitment for two senior backend engineers represents ~480 hours of opportunity cost. Diverting this resource from the core roadmap to solve a single-customer problem creates significant technical debt risk if the feature sees low adoption.
*   **The "Loud Customer" Bias:** Meridian's vocal presence in forums and sales calls creates an availability heuristic, making the problem feel larger than it is. Their specific mention of "compliance requirements" hints at a niche vertical (e.g., finance, healthcare) rather than a general enterprise workflow issue.

**Recommended Action Plan for Thursday's Meeting:**

1.  **Do Not Build Yet:** Reject the immediate six-week build request. The risk of building unused complexity (Viability risk) outweighs the potential value.
2.  **Conduct Targeted Discovery (1 Week):** Before any code is written, validate if this is a silent majority issue or a niche requirement.
    *   **Interview 5–7 Large Accounts:** Specifically reach out to your largest 10 customers (by seat count). Ask about their current admin workflows and any internal security policies they struggle with. Do not mention Meridian or the specific feature yet.
    *   **Survey the Base:** Send a single-question survey to all 340 accounts: *"What is the biggest risk you face with your current team permission settings?"* This quantifies the pain without leading them to a solution.
3.  **Interim Solution for Meridian:** Work with Customer Success to manually manage Meridian's access needs or create a specific process for them. If they cannot operate without this feature, they may be a poor fit for the product's current stage, or they represent a specific "Enterprise Tier" opportunity that requires a different pricing/model strategy entirely.

**Conclusion:** Treat Meridian's request as a hypothesis, not a requirement. Validate the prevalence of the problem across your top 5% of customers before committing engineering resources. If fewer than 2–3 other large accounts express similar friction, this remains a niche customization, not a core platform feature.
