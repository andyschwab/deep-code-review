### Strategic Interpretation & Investigation Plan

**The Core Insight:** Your data reveals a classic "product-market fit divergence." Solstice likely fits the *workflow* of small teams (high satisfaction) but fails the *governance, scale, or integration* requirements of enterprises (low satisfaction). Since 70% of revenue comes from the at-risk enterprise segment, this is a retention emergency, not just a feature gap.

**Hypothesis Refinement:**
Your assumption that small teams succeed due to "simpler workflows" is likely only half-true. It is equally probable that enterprises are failing because your tool lacks necessary controls (permissions, audit logs, reporting) or creates friction in complex, cross-functional dependencies, rather than just lacking "features." The gap between 89 and 61 suggests a fundamental mismatch in how value is delivered at scale, not merely a training deficit.

**Immediate Investigation Steps (Next 2 Weeks):**

1.  **Conduct "Lost & At-Risk" Enterprise Interviews:**
    *   Target 5–7 customers from the >500 employee segment who gave low scores or recently churned.
    *   **Goal:** Distinguish between "missing capabilities" (Value risk) and "unable to adopt" (Usability/Onboarding risk).
    *   **Key Question:** "Walk me through the last time your team tried to roll out Solstice to a new department. Where did the process stall?" (Focus on past behavior, not opinions).

2.  **Analyze Usage Telemetry by Segment:**
    *   Compare activation rates and feature adoption between the <50 and >500 cohorts.
    *   Look for drop-off points: Do enterprise users fail to invite >10 members? Do they stop using the tool after the first month? Low adoption of specific "power features" may indicate they are too hard to find (onboarding) or don't solve the enterprise problem (value).

3.  **Support Ticket Sentiment Analysis:**
    *   Review the last quarter's enterprise support tickets. Categorize them into "How do I?" (training gap) vs. "I need X to work with Y" (feature/integration gap). A high volume of integration requests signals a feasibility/strategy issue, not a training one.

**Building the Executive Case for Q3:**
Frame the investment not as "fixing bugs" but as "protecting 70% of revenue." Present two potential paths based on your findings:
*   **Path A (Adoption Fix):** If the issue is onboarding, propose a dedicated Enterprise Success track and customized implementation playbooks.
*   **Path B (Product Pivot):** If the issue is missing governance/integrations, propose a "Enterprise Readiness" sprint focused on API depth, SSO, and advanced permissions.

Do not commit resources until you validate which path addresses the root cause.

*Note: Prioritize qualitative interviews immediately; survey data explains the "what," but only conversations will reveal the "why."*
*Ensure your Product Trio (PM, Design, Eng) joins these interviews to witness the friction firsthand.*
