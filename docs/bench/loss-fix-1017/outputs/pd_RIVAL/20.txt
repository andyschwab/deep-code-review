**Recommendation: Do Not Build Multi-Language Report Generation in Q2**

Prioritize **Automated Data Import (ERP)** as your primary initiative and **Dashboard Builder** as secondary. Defer Multi-Language support to Q3.

**Rationale for Leadership:**

1.  **Churn Prevention vs. Revenue Retention:** Your data indicates manual data entry drives 40% of churn. With 150 customers, losing even a small fraction due to this friction likely exceeds the $120K risk from your largest account. ERP integration is a defensive "must-have" to stop revenue leakage across the entire base, whereas multi-language is an offensive "nice-to-have" for a single segment.
2.  **Market Expansion:** Automated data import was requested by 18 customers (12% of your base), representing the broadest demand signal. Solving the top frustration for the majority unlocks growth potential and reduces support costs associated with manual onboarding.
3.  **Strategic Negotiation:** Instead of building the multi-language feature immediately, engage your largest account directly. Explain that while multi-language is on the roadmap, the Q2 focus is on stabilizing core data integrity (ERP) which benefits them too. Offer a manual concierge service for their specific multi-language reports in the interim. This maintains the relationship without consuming 8–10 story points of engineering capacity.
4.  **Team Capacity:** Delivering two complex features (ERP + Dashboard) already stretches your 24-point sprint capacity. Attempting three guarantees failure on all fronts. The Dashboard Builder (12 requests) addresses the need for flexibility after data is imported, creating a logical workflow: Import Data → Customize View. Multi-language breaks this flow and serves a niche need.

**Proposed Q2 Plan:**
*   **Sprint 1-3:** Automated Data Import (MVP for top 3 ERPs).
*   **Sprint 4-5:** Dashboard Builder (Core widgets).
*   **Sprint 6:** Buffer, bug fixes, and manual concierge support for the large account's language needs.

This approach maximizes retention, addresses the widest pain point, and keeps the team focused on deliverable outcomes rather than over-committing.

*Note: Validate the "concierge" option with the large account before finalizing the roadmap to ensure it satisfies their immediate compliance needs.*
