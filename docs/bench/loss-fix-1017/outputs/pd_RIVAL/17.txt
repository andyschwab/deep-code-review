**Recommendation: Pursue a Hybrid "Product-Led Sales" Model**

Do not choose strictly between sales-led or self-serve yet. Your data indicates you have a viable product but an unproven scaling engine. The fact that 80% of your revenue required 12 hours of founder hand-holding suggests your current onboarding is not ready for pure self-serve. However, the single self-serve customer is a critical signal that cannot be ignored as a mere outlier without further testing.

**Immediate Action Plan:**

1.  **Productize the Onboarding (Weeks 1-4):** Before hiring sales staff, you must reduce the 12-hour setup time. Document every step you took for the four hand-held customers. Convert these steps into in-app guides, automated workflows, or template libraries. Your goal is to get the "time-to-value" for a new customer under 2 hours without your direct involvement.
2.  **Run a Controlled Self-Serve Experiment (Weeks 5-8):** Stop using your personal network for new pilots. Direct all inbound traffic to the new self-serve flow. Set a success metric: If you can convert 3 new customers with less than 2 hours of support each while maintaining the $800/month price point, the self-serve model is viable.
3.  **Validate the Sales Hypothesis:** Your assumption that mid-size companies *expect* sales relationships may actually mean they expect *support*, not necessarily a negotiated contract. Test this by offering a "Concierge Onboarding" add-on ($500 one-time fee) within the self-serve flow. If customers buy it, they value the help but prefer choosing it. If they churn without it, your product usability is the blocker, not the sales model.

**Decision Criteria for Next Month:**
*   **Go Sales-Led:** Only if the self-serve experiment fails completely (0 conversions) AND you confirm that prospects explicitly refuse to buy without a demo/negotiation.
*   **Go Self-Serve:** If you achieve 3+ conversions with minimal touch.
*   **Stay Hybrid:** If customers sign up self-serve but frequently purchase the onboarding add-on. This allows you to scale marketing while monetizing the necessary hand-holding.

Hiring account executives now is premature and risky; it locks you into high fixed costs before solving the unit economics of customer acquisition. Fix the product experience first to prove the model works without you.

*Note: Focus your strategy session on defining the specific metrics for the self-serve experiment rather than debating long-term philosophy.*
