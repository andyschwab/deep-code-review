### Interview Selection Plan: CodeForge Trial Conversion

**Objective:** Identify why 70% of trials do not convert without exhausting the user pool or introducing selection bias.

**1. Sample Size & Segmentation**
Do not interview randomly. With only 87 users, every conversation counts. Target **8–10 interviews** total. This is usually sufficient to reach saturation on *why* a behavior occurs without burning through your validation pool.

Split your selection into two distinct cohorts to avoid bias toward the "vocal" or "available":
*   **Cohort A (The Drop-offs):** 5 users who started a trial in the last 45 days and did *not* convert.
    *   *Selection Criteria:* Do not pick the most recent. Pick a mix of industries/team sizes from your 12 companies. Avoid picking only those who replied to previous emails.
*   **Cohort B (The Converters):** 3 users who *did* convert recently.
    *   *Purpose:* To contrast their "aha moment" against the drop-offs. If converters saw value in Day 2 and drop-offs didn't see it until Day 15, you have found your leverage point.

**2. Recruitment Strategy to Prevent Fatigue**
*   **The "One-and-Done" Rule:** Mark anyone interviewed now as "Discovery Only." Do not approach them again for solution validation for at least 60 days. This preserves ~77 users for future testing.
*   **Incentivize Heavily:** Since the pool is small, offer a significant incentive (e.g., $100 gift card or 3 months free if they eventually convert) to ensure high show-up rates and goodwill.
*   **Outreach Script:** Be transparent. "We are researching why teams succeed or struggle with code review workflows. We are not selling anything, and this will not affect your trial status."

**3. Interview Focus (The Mom Test Approach)**
Do not ask "Did you find value?" or "Was the tool clear?" These invite polite lies. Instead, reconstruct the timeline of their trial:
*   "Walk me through the first code review you attempted with CodeForge. What triggered it?"
*   "What was happening in your sprint that week?"
*   "When did you stop using it? What was the specific task you were trying to do right before you closed the tab?"
*   "How did your team handle that specific review after you stopped using the tool?"

**4. Execution Timeline**
*   **Days 1-2:** Select participants and send invites.
*   **Days 3-7:** Conduct interviews (aim for 30 mins each).
*   **Day 8:** Synthesize findings. Look for the specific moment where the "value perception" broke down. Was it onboarding? A missing integration? A social hurdle in getting the team to join?

**Note:** If you cannot find 5 non-converters who are willing to talk, expand the window to trials from the last 90 days, but prioritize recency.
**Note:** Record these sessions (with permission) so the whole product trio can listen, reducing the need for you to take detailed notes during the call.
