My proposed change addresses a valid segment of users who need an in-process priority bypass to meet urgent processing needs without the overhead of managing additional infrastructure. There is a genuine demand for a "simple mode" that allows immediate handling of critical tasks within the existing scheduler, serving as a pragmatic alternative for teams that cannot justify the operational cost of a separate service.

Would you be open to reconsidering this feature if it were optionally gated or configured strictly for these simpler deployment scenarios? I believe the project could benefit from supporting this middle ground, offering flexibility for users who find the full architectural separation over-engineered for their current scale. I am happy to refine the implementation to align better with the project's standards, perhaps as an opt-in extension rather than a default behavior, to ensure it complements rather than conflicts with the long-term roadmap.
