## Summary
Fixes a persistent memory leak in the VideoFlow buffer release logic, reducing steady-state memory usage by ~95% (4GB → 200MB) during standard streaming workloads.

## What
- Refactored `BufferPool::release()` to ensure immediate deallocation of orphaned frames.
- Added explicit scope guards to prevent early-exit leaks in the decoding pipeline.
- Introduced a regression test (`test_buffer_release_stress`) that asserts heap stability over 10k iterations.

## Why
Under continuous load, unreleased buffers accumulated linearly, causing OOM crashes after ~20 minutes of streaming. This patch enforces strict ownership semantics, eliminating the accumulation.
