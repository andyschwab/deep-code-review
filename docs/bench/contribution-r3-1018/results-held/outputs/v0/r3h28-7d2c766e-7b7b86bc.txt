**Title:** Feature Request: Geo-aware configurable health check intervals

**Problem Statement:**
Current load balancing logic often assumes uniform network conditions or relies solely on simple round-trip time (RTT) metrics. In complex, multi-region deployments—particularly those spanning diverse cloud providers, edge locations, or hybrid on-premise setups—latency characteristics can vary drastically between zones independent of geographic distance. Fixed or purely RTT-based health check intervals may lead to premature node ejection in high-jitter zones or sluggish failure detection in stable but distant regions.

**Proposed Solution:**
Introduce a configuration option allowing users to define custom health check intervals keyed by geographic region or logical topology groups. This enables operators to tune sensitivity based on known network behaviors of specific zones without altering the core balancing algorithm.

**Generalized Use Cases:**
1.  **Hybrid Cloud Environments:** Where connections between private data centers and public clouds traverse unpredictable public internet paths.
2.  **Edge Computing:** Where nodes are distributed across locations with widely varying backhaul qualities.
3.  **Legacy Integration:** Facilities operating with older networking gear that exhibits higher jitter or processing delays.

**Benefit:**
This adds flexibility for operators managing non-uniform networks, improving stability and reducing false-positive health failures without imposing complexity on users with standard, uniform deployments (via sensible defaults).
