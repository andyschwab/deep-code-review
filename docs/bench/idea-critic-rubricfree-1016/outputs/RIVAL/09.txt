# Weaknesses in the plan

1. **The 6-month migration is an assumption, not a finding.** Dec 1 to Jun 30 gives about 7 months, so you have roughly one month of slack. Nothing in the plan says any customer has confirmed it can hit that.
2. **The hardware customer is a hard failure.** If v1 calls are baked into deployed devices that can't be updated remotely, a shutoff bricks them. The fix would be field visits or replacements, with contract, liability and reputation consequences. The plan has no fallback for this.
3. **The plan has one date and no exception path.** Consider a minimal v1-to-v2 translation proxy for unmigratable clients, a paid extended-support tier, or a per-customer sunset date. Any of these could cost far less than 1.5 engineers.
4. **The cost case is lopsided.** You're weighing 1.5 engineers against at least $400K/year from one customer, plus the other four. The plan doesn't quantify revenue at risk against savings, so Hana can't judge the tradeoff.
5. **Contract exposure is unchecked.** Some agreements may promise API availability or require 12 months' notice, which could make June 30 non-binding or costly.
6. **Concurrent migrations strain the solutions engineers.** Five large migrations in the same window, with no stated capacity or ownership.
7. **There are no graduated steps.** Brownouts, rate limits, warning headers and per-customer usage reporting would reveal who isn't ready before the cutoff.
8. **Sequencing risk.** If customers learn of the deprecation from a public announcement before a personal conversation, the relationship takes damage.
9. **Unexplained remainder.** If the 5 customers are "most" of the 6%, who generates the rest? It may include unknown integrators you can't reach.

# Questions for the account managers

For each of the 5 customers:
- What is on v1 today (endpoints, volume, which teams or systems), and who owns the integration technically?
- Is there a v2 roadmap or engineering freeze that affects their capacity to migrate in H1 2027?
- What does their contract say about API availability, deprecation notice, and SLAs? Are renewals coming up in the window?
- How would they react to this timeline, and has anyone mentioned v2 concerns before?

For the hardware customer specifically:
- Which customer is it, and is it the $400K account?
- How many devices are deployed, where, and what is their lifespan?
- Can the devices be updated over the air at all? If not, what does a physical update cost, and who pays?
- Do the devices call v1 directly, or through a gateway or the customer's backend we could redirect?
- Is there a replacement hardware cycle already planned that could carry v2?

Across accounts:
- Who accounts for the remaining v1 traffic outside the 5, and can we identify and contact them?
- Would any customer pay for extended v1 support?
- Is anyone in a competitive evaluation where a forced migration could tip them?
- Who should tell each customer, and how soon before the public announcement?

*Before the meeting, get per-customer v1 usage by endpoint and a firm cost estimate for a v1 translation proxy, so you can offer Hana a concrete alternative.*
