## Where your build case is weakest

1. **The saving is thin.** Roughly $40k over 3 years sits well inside the error of an unpriced estimate. A 30% overrun erases it.
2. **The biggest unknown is one-sided.** If finance wants self-serve ad hoc querying, build cost rises while buy cost likely doesn't. Your case is cheapest in the scenario where finance asks for nothing more.
3. **Hosting and access control are unpriced.** Sales, inventory and margin data likely need role-based or row-level restrictions, especially margin. This is often where internal BI projects expand.
4. **The maintenance figure is optimistic.** 0.25 engineer assumes stable scope, no turnover, no data-source changes and no support burden from 60 users. Dashboards that work tend to generate new requests.
5. **Key-person risk.** If one of the two builders leaves, the tool may become hard to maintain.
6. **Opportunity cost.** 8 engineer-months are not spent on other data work, and that cost isn't in the comparison.
7. **No track record behind the estimate.** The 4-month figure doesn't show whether data modeling, testing, rollout and training are included.

## What Marcus will most likely push back on

- **"What happens if this overruns again?"** This is his main objection. Expect him to ask for a hard cap, who owns the overrun, and when the project would be stopped.
- **"What is the all-in 3-year cost?"** He will want loaded salaries, hosting, security and maintenance, not just the build.
- **"Why not negotiate the vendor price?"** At 60 seats, a multi-year commitment or fewer seats might lower the quote. Expect him to treat $96k as an opening number.
- **"What else would these engineers do?"**
- **"Will finance's ad hoc needs blow this up?"** He will want that answered before he approves anything.

## How to strengthen the case

- Ask finance now whether they need ad hoc querying, and get the answer in writing.
- Price hosting and access control before presenting.
- Propose a phased build: deliver the first 8–10 reports in about 6–8 weeks, with a go/no-go gate and a fixed budget ceiling.
- Get the vendor's implementation fees, contract terms and renewal price increases, so the comparison is complete.

If finance does want ad hoc querying, buying is probably the safer recommendation.
