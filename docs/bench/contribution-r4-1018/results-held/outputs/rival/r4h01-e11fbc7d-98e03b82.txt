You cannot and should not generate a "synthetic" dataset based on real patient patterns, even if you anonymize it. In healthcare contexts, the *pattern* of appointments (e.g., specific sequences of dates, frequencies, or correlations with holidays) can itself be sensitive or re-identifiable. Furthermore, attempting to engineer data that "mimics" real patient behavior introduces unnecessary risk and complexity.

The correct solution is to construct a **minimal, deterministic, and purely artificial** test case that targets the *logic error* directly, completely decoupled from any real-world observation.

Since the bug is in leap year calculation, you do not need a schedule of "appointments." You only need to prove the date math fails. Replace your complex dataset with a single, hardcoded assertion using obvious, fake dates that no human would ever mistake for real data.
