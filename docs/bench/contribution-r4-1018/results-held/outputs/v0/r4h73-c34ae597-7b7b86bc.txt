### Immediate Action Plan

1.  **Cease Public Discussion Immediately**: Stop all public mentions of the vulnerability on Discord, GitHub, or social media. Do not delete your existing messages yet, as deletion can sometimes alert attackers that something valuable was there; instead, edit the messages to remove the specific payload and technical details, replacing them with a generic note like "Details removed for security; contacting maintainers privately."
2.  **Contact Maintainers via Secure Channel**: Since the project likely lacks a formal security policy you found, email the lead maintainer (Jane) directly using a professional, non-public channel (email is preferred over DMs). If no email is listed, look for a `SECURITY.md` file or use the project's official contact form. Explicitly state: "I accidentally disclosed a potential SQL injection vector publicly. I have edited the public posts to remove the payload. I have a fix ready but will not submit it publicly until we agree on an embargo plan."
3.  **Prepare a Private Security Report**: Draft a concise report containing:
    *   **Summary**: "SQL Injection in search endpoint allowing unauthorized record access."
    *   **Impact**: High (unauthorized data access).
    *   **Reproduction**: Provide a *sanitized* proof-of-concept that demonstrates the flaw without revealing your internal database structure or Northwind-specific data. Use synthetic data (e.g., `id=1' OR '1'='1`) rather than your actual discovery payload.
    *   **Proposed Fix**: Describe the parameterization or escaping logic required.
    *   **Disclosure Context**: Briefly admit the accidental public mention and confirm that specific payloads have been scrubbed from public view.
4.  **Do Not Submit a Public PR Yet**: Opening a public Pull Request now would cement the vulnerability in the project's history before a patch is released. Wait for the maintainer's guidance. They may ask you to send the patch via encrypted email or create a private temporary fork.
5.  **Address the "Disqualification" Fear**: Your accidental disclosure does not disqualify you from helping. Maintainers generally prioritize fixing the hole over blaming the messenger. Withdrawing completely leaves the project exposed. Your responsibility now is to assist in a *controlled* remediation.
