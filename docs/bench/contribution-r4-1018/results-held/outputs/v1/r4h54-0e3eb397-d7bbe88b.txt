You cannot fully sanitize the edit history yourself; only repository administrators can delete the PR or scrub server-side logs. Your best mitigation is immediate, voluntary disclosure to the maintainers via a private channel. This demonstrates integrity and often prevents a "ban" outcome, as you are acting to protect them, not hide the error.

**Fallback if Maintainers Are Silent**
If you cannot reach them privately within 30 minutes, post a comment on the PR itself: "Urgent: I accidentally included an internal link in the original description. I have edited it out, but the history remains. I am attempting to contact maintainers privately to resolve this privacy concern immediately." This puts the ball in their court publicly while showing proactive responsibility.

**Note:** The fact that the link requires authentication limits the actual data exposure, but the *existence* of the internal structure is the privacy leak. Honesty is your only shield against a permanent ban.
