Do not open a pull request. Submitting code that the maintainers have explicitly stated will be "closed immediately" violates the project's contribution rules and wastes their time reviewing a change they cannot accept. It is not considered "spam" in a malicious sense, but it is disregarding their current workflow constraints.

Instead, follow these steps to share your work respectfully without violating the freeze:

1.  **Preserve Your Work Locally:** Keep your branch on your personal fork. You can continue to refine the palette, add tests, or update documentation there. This ensures your work is safe and ready when the freeze lifts.
2.  **Comment on the Pinned Issue (Optional but Recommended):** Post a single, polite comment on the existing pinned issue. State that you have developed a dark theme meeting accessibility standards and have it ready in your fork. Ask if the maintainers would appreciate a link to your fork for future reference, or if they prefer you simply wait until the rendering engine rewrite is complete.
    *   *Draft Comment:* "Hi, I've designed an accessible dark theme palette and implemented the changes in my local fork. I see the note about the rendering engine rewrite and the freeze on new themes. I will hold off on opening a PR. Would you like a link to my fork for future reference once the rewrite is done, or should I just wait for a new issue to be opened?"
3.  **Wait for the Signal:** Monitor the pinned issue or the project's roadmap. Once the core rendering engine is rewritten and the maintainers indicate they are accepting theme contributions again, you can rebase your branch (if necessary) and open your PR immediately.

**Direct Answer to Your Uncertainty:**
You should **wait**. Opening a PR now guarantees immediate closure and creates noise in their tracker. Sharing a link in a comment on the *existing* discussion thread is the only appropriate way to signal your readiness without violating the "discuss before coding" rule or the specific freeze instruction.

**Fallback if Silence Persists:**
If the rendering engine rewrite takes longer than expected (e.g., several months past "next quarter") and there is no update, you may politely bump the pinned issue once to ask for a status update, mentioning your ready-to-go contribution again.

**Note:**
Your proactive work is valuable; timing its submission to align with the maintainers' capacity is the key to getting it merged.
Always prioritize the project's stated contribution guidelines over your own readiness to ensure a positive community interaction.
