# Memo draft: one company-wide metric for 2027

**Proposed top-line metric: Collaborating Workspaces**
The number of paying workspaces in which at least three people edited a board in the last 30 days.

Today, 62% of paying workspaces meet this bar. That is our starting point. We should judge progress against it and against our own trend, not against outside figures.

**Why this metric**
- **It measures value delivered, not activity.** Northwind is a team tool. A workspace where three or more people are building on boards is getting what it paid for, and those are the accounts most likely to renew and expand.
- **It rises only when the business is healthier.** Sales growth raises it by adding new workspaces that start collaborating. Product and support raise it by keeping existing workspaces active. Seats sold, ticket volume and daily active users each capture only one department's view.
- **It improves on daily active users (about 18,000).** That number counts individuals, so it can rise while teams drift away, for example if a few heavy solo users log in more.
- **It is easy to explain:** "How many paying teams are really working together in Northwind?"

**Supporting metrics (the levers underneath)**
1. **Activation:** the share of newly paying workspaces that reach three editing members within their first 30 days. This is the lever for Sales and onboarding. No baseline has been supplied, so it needs to be measured.
2. **Retention of collaboration:** of the workspaces collaborating today, the share still collaborating 90 days later. This is the lever for Product and Support, and the first place to look if the top-line stalls.
3. **Seat utilization:** the share of purchased seats that edited a board in the last 30 days, reported by account size. This is the lever for Sales and Customer Success, and it covers large accounts, where "three people" may be too low a bar.

**What we have not confirmed**
- That collaboration drives value is currently our assumption. We have not tested it with customers.
- We have never split usage by account size, so we do not know whether large accounts behave like small ones.
- "Three members" is a placeholder threshold, not a proven one.

**Next steps this week, before the memo is final**
1. Pull the 62% figure split by seat band (for example 5–20, 21–50 and 51–200 seats). If large accounts sit far below small ones, set the threshold by size band, such as a percentage of seats instead of a flat three.
2. Compare renewal and expansion over the past year for collaborating versus non-collaborating workspaces. If there is no clear gap, the assumption fails and we should pick a different metric.
3. Interview five to eight customers across sizes, asking what they would lose if the team stopped using boards together.

**Decision we are asking for:** adopt Collaborating Workspaces as the working top-line metric from January 2027, subject to step 2 confirming that these workspaces renew better.

*Note: ticket volume works better as a Support health indicator than as a top-line metric, since a rise can mean more usage or more problems.*
