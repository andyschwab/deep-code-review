# Onboarding A/B test: feasibility plan

**Bottom line:** The test is feasible, but only for a sizeable lift (about 3 to 4 points). A lift of 2 points or less can't be detected before December 15. Your manager is right about small effects and wrong that we can't learn anything.

**Assumptions:** 1,400 signups a week split 50/50 (700 per arm), 38% baseline activation (3 notes in 7 days), 5% significance (two-sided), 80% power. All figures are approximate.

| True lift (38% → ) | Users per arm | Total | Weeks at 1,400/wk |
|---|---|---|---|
| +5 pts (43%) | ~1,500 | ~3,000 | ~2 |
| +4 pts (42%) | ~2,300 | ~4,650 | ~3.3 |
| +3 pts (41%) | ~4,150 | ~8,300 | ~6 |
| +2 pts (40%) | ~9,400 | ~18,800 | ~13 |

**Timeline.** Activation needs 7 days to mature, so the last enrolled signup must arrive by about Dec 1 to be readable around Dec 9.
- Start Oct 26: 5 weeks of enrollment (~7,000 users), able to detect about +3.3 pts.
- Start Nov 2: 4 weeks (~5,600 users), able to detect about +3.7 pts.
- If exam season cuts signups by a quarter, a 5-week test detects about +3.8 pts.

Each week of build delay costs sensitivity, so lock the design now.

**Design (commit before launch)**
- Randomize each new signup 50/50 and keep them in that arm.
- Primary metric: the share of users who create 3 notes within 7 days.
- Guardrail: a pre-chosen measure such as week-2 return or onboarding completion. Pick it now and don't swap it later.
- Fixed end date, read once. No mid-test peeking. During the test, report only enrollment counts.
- Ask Priya to write down her expected lift now. That tells us whether it falls above or below what we can detect.

**Decision rules (written in advance)**
- Ship if the confidence interval for the lift is above 0 and the guardrail holds.
- If the interval includes 0 but excludes a harmful drop, report "inconclusive: we can rule out effects larger than X." That is not "no effect."
- If the guardrail regresses, don't ship, even if activation is up.

**If the numbers don't work** (for example, Priya expects +1 to 2 pts, or signups fall sharply)
1. Test a bolder variant, since big changes are detectable and subtle ones aren't.
2. Pre-declare a faster proxy such as "first note in 24 hours" as a secondary measure. It is a supporting signal, not a substitute for the primary.
3. Present Dec 15 as an interim status (enrollment on track, no results) and let the test run past it to its pre-committed size.
4. Pair a smaller test with usability sessions on the new flow, and treat the results as directional, not proof.

**This week**
1. Pull weekly signups and 7-day activation rate for the last 8 to 10 cohorts. If the rate moves by more than about ±2 pts week to week, our sample sizes are too optimistic. Rerun the table with that spread.
2. Confirm that the events for note creation and signup are logged per user with a stable pseudonymous ID, and that assignment to the arm is recorded.
3. Get Priya's expected lift in writing, and agree the guardrail and decision rules with your manager.
