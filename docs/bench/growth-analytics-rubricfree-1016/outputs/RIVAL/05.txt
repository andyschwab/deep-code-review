# Quillstack onboarding test: feasibility plan

**Bottom line:** The test is feasible if the redesign lifts activation by about 3.5 points or more (38% to 41.5%+, roughly a 9% relative gain). It can't reliably detect smaller effects before December 15. Your manager is partly right, but the test isn't hopeless. It is sized for a big win, which is what Priya expects.

**Assumptions:** 50/50 split, 1,400 signups per week (700 per arm), 38% baseline, 80% power, 5% two-sided significance, one fixed analysis at the end. Activation takes 7 days, so the last users enrolled need a full week before they count.

**Sample needed per arm, by true lift**

| Lift (points) | Per arm | Weeks of signups |
|---|---|---|
| +2 (40%) | ~9,300 | ~13 |
| +3 (41%) | ~4,200 | ~6 |
| +4 (42%) | ~2,350 | ~3.4 |
| +5 (43%) | ~1,500 | ~2.2 |
| +8 (46%) | ~600 | ~0.9 |

**What fits before December 15:** Enrollment must stop around December 1 to leave the 7-day window plus a few days to analyze. Launch date is the biggest lever, and I don't know your build time, so here are two cases:

- **Launch Nov 2:** about 4.5 weeks, ~3,100 per arm, detects ~3.5 points.
- **Launch Oct 26:** about 5.3 weeks, ~3,700 per arm, detects ~3.2 points.

Every week of launch delay costs about 700 users per arm and makes the smallest detectable lift larger.

**Plan**
1. Randomize all new signups at signup, so both arms see the same exam-season shifts.
2. Primary metric: 3 notes within 7 days. Guardrails: week-1 retention and support tickets.
3. Fix the end date and sample size in advance. Don't stop early on a good-looking result.
4. Agree on the decision rules now:
   - **Ship:** the confidence interval excludes zero and the guardrails hold.
   - **Drop:** the interval excludes any lift of 3 points or more.
   - **Inconclusive:** anything else. Keep the test running into January, or follow up with a second test.

**If the numbers don't work**
- **Ask Priya what lift she expects.** If she believes in 5+ points, the test is well powered. If the honest guess is 1 to 2 points, it isn't, and we should say so now.
- **Make the redesign bolder** rather than a polish pass, since bigger changes are easier to detect.
- **Add an early secondary metric**, such as first note within 24 hours, for directional reading. Don't swap it in as the primary metric after seeing results.
- **Accept an inconclusive readout on Dec 15** and label it that way, with the observed interval. "No significant difference" would not mean "no effect."
- **Don't shorten the window or lower the bar** to force a result. That produces false wins.

**Notes:** If signups fall during exam season, the detectable lift grows, so recheck the weekly count in mid-November. Also confirm that activation tracking works for both flows before launch.
