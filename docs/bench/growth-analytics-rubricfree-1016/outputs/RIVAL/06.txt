# Tallyhouse pre-launch instrumentation, in priority order

I'm assuming the data that can't be rebuilt later is user-level, timestamped history. Everything below is server-side where possible, so ad blockers and consent banners don't drop it.

**Must ship before Nov 6 (~2 dev-days, Marco to confirm the estimate)**

1. **Persistent `user_id` plus `signup_completed` with acquisition source stored on the user record.** Capture UTM values and referrer at first touch and save them permanently. Every retention or revenue cohort by channel depends on this, and it can't be added retroactively.
2. **Tagged links for the newsletter and each partner placement** (lowercase, hyphenated, one link per placement; medium `newsletter` or `partner`). This is nearly free, and it separates curious launch-day traffic from the people who stay. Create a custom GA4 channel group so these mediums don't land in Unassigned.
3. **`invoice_created` and `invoice_sent`, with `user_id`, timestamp and invoice count.** Sending an invoice is the moment a freelancer gets value, so it serves as both the activation event and the definition of an "active" week. Retention can be derived from it later, as long as the raw timestamped events exist.
4. **Billing events from the payment provider's webhooks:** `trial_started`, `subscription_started`, `plan_changed`, `subscription_cancelled`, each with `plan` and `value`. These cover investor revenue questions (MRR, conversion, churn). First confirm your billing system can export its full history. If it can, spend dev time here only on the events it doesn't already hold.
5. **`beta_user` flag on the existing 340 users.** It keeps beta behavior out of launch cohorts. It's a one-time script.
6. **A QA pass** in GA4 DebugView and on the server log: no duplicate events, no PII in properties (no emails or client names), and consent handling for EU/UK visitors.

**If time remains**

7. `signup_started` and one event per onboarding step, to locate funnel drop-off. Landing page views already exist, so visit-to-signup is measurable now.
8. `invoice_paid` (whether the freelancer's client paid), if it's a product feature. It's a strong long-term stickiness signal, but it can wait a sprint.

**Can wait until after launch**

Button-click and feature-usage detail, session replay and heatmaps, A/B testing infrastructure, a customer data platform, and dashboards. None of these loses irreplaceable history if added in December.

**Notes**
- For January, only cohorts that have finished their observation window should count toward retention. A Nov 6 cohort will have roughly eight weeks of data, and late-December signups will still be pending. Label them pending rather than counting them as churned.
- Decide the definitions now and share them with the board: "active" means one or more invoices sent in a week, and "retained" means active in week N.
