The spec reveals several unmitigated output harms. First, **HALLU** is present: profile pages present answers as "confirmed fact with no source shown," fabricating authority without evidence. Second, **DISC** is unaddressed: the in-app chat suggests AI-written practice questions but "does not tell users the content was written by AI." Third, **AUTO** is a critical failure: the matching engine "executes irreversible actions... with no human approval," specifically auto-booking sessions, which violates the requirement for a human-in-the-loop on high-stakes actions.

Regarding **BIAS**, the spec notes an 11% flag rate for both older and younger users during QA. While this indicates monitoring occurred, the text does not confirm the disparity was resolved, only that they "ship with that calibration." However, since the spec explicitly mentions the metric and calibration effort rather than ignoring disparate impact, I will not flag BIAS as an *unhandled* harm based strictly on the text provided, though it remains a risk. **RELY** is partially mitigated by the "one tap" override, though the lack of uncertainty markers on "facts" weakens this. **DARK** patterns are explicitly denied (no timers, plain opt-in).

The combination of undisclosed AI content, unsourced facts presented as truth, and irreversible automated booking without human review constitutes sufficient risk to block launch.
