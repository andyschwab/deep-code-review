The spec explicitly addresses most harm categories: AI disclosure is present (visible label), over-reliance is mitigated (confidence labels, sources, one-tap override), dark patterns are avoided (opt-in, no timers), and high-stakes actions have a human gate.

However, the spec reports a specific disparity: "the bot flagged 18% of older users but 9% of younger users." This describes a systematic disadvantage based on age group where the output (flagging for intervention) applies disproportionately to older members. The spec presents this data point but does not describe a mitigation, investigation, or threshold adjustment to address this disparity before launch. This fits the definition of **BIAS**.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
