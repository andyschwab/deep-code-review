The spec explicitly addresses the defined harm categories through concrete controls. Hallucination (HALLU) is mitigated by sourcing every claim and attaching confidence labels. Over-reliance (RELY) is countered by enabling one-tap overrides and appeals. Lack of disclosure (DISC) is resolved via visible AI-generated labels on all outputs. Deceptive patterns (DARK) are avoided through opt-in consent, unticked defaults, and the absence of urgency timers. Unsafe automation (AUTO) is prevented by requiring human sign-off for irreversible actions like renewals or discounts.

Regarding Bias (BIAS), the spec states that pilot error rates were "within one point across every group tested." This indicates the feature was evaluated for disparate impact and found equitable within the pilot scope; it does not describe a present systematic disadvantage. As the spec describes safeguards for every listed harm category rather than unaddressed risks, no categories are flagged.
