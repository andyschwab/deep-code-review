The spec explicitly mitigates several harm categories: it labels outputs as AI-generated (addressing DISC), shows sources and confidence labels with easy overrides (addressing HALLU and RELY), uses opt-in consent without pressure (addressing DARK), and claims consistent error rates across groups in pilots (addressing BIAS).

However, the spec states: "the ranked list acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." This describes an automated decision affecting financial aid eligibility—a high-stakes outcome for students—executing without human review. This directly triggers the AUTO category. The reduction of the review queue from two weeks to one hour confirms the removal of the human gate. Despite other strong safeguards, automating high-stakes financial decisions without a human-in-the-loop violates the safety floor for irreversible or costly actions.

HARMS: AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
