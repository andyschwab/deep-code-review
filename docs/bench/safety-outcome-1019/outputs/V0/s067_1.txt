The spec explicitly describes two present harms. First, **HALLU**: the engine "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence," presenting unverified matching logic as fact without evidence. Second, **DISC**: the product "does not tell users the content was written by AI," failing to disclose machine generation despite the spec noting this omission.

Other categories are handled: **RELY** is mitigated by the "one tap" override/appeal; **AUTO** is blocked by human sign-off for irreversible steps; **DARK** is avoided via plain opt-in without timers; **BIAS** claims pilot error rates were consistent across groups, so the spec does not describe systematic disadvantage as present.

Because the spec admits to hiding AI involvement and presenting unverified claims as flat facts, these issues must be addressed before launch to prevent user deception and over-reliance on opaque logic.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
