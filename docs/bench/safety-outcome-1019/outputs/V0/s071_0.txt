The spec reveals three active harms. First, **DARK** patterns are present: the default is "on," consent is "pre-ticked," and a "ten-minute timer" manufactures urgency to pressure users against their own interest. Second, **HALLU** exists because the charity's impact statement is presented as "confirmed fact with no source shown" and lacks links to financials for verification. Third, **BIAS** is indicated by the system flagging equal percentages (11%) of older and younger users for re-prompting after decline; without knowing the baseline population distribution, this equal rate suggests a potential systematic disadvantage or disparate impact that requires investigation before launch.

Conversely, **AUTO** is handled (human approves irreversible actions), **DISC** is handled (outputs labeled AI-generated), and **RELY** is mitigated (one-tap override available). However, the presence of dark patterns, unverified facts, and potential bias creates unacceptable risk for a public launch.

HARMS: DARK, HALLU, BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
