The spec reveals three unmitigated harms. First, **BIAS** is explicitly present: the system has a known error rate "twice as high" for plates with temporary paper permits, systematically disadvantaging that group. While tuning is planned, the pilot launches with this documented disparity active. Second, **RELY** is triggered because users "cannot override or appeal the result," removing human judgment from potentially flawed automated flags. Third, **HALLU** is a risk because the system presents a "stated confidence level" alongside citations; without defining these levels as coarse, qualitative tiers (e.g., "Verified" vs. "Unverified") rather than precise percentages, this invites over-trust in uncalibrated probabilities.

Other categories are handled: **DISC** is addressed via visible AI labels; **AUTO** is mitigated by the human sign-off requirement for fines; and **DARK** patterns are avoided through opt-in, unticked consent without timers. However, the combination of known bias, lack of appeal, and potential false precision in confidence reporting creates unacceptable residual risk for an enforcement tool.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
