The spec reveals two active harms requiring remediation before launch.

First, **BIAS** is explicitly present. The testing noted an 11% failure rate for both older and younger users. While the spec claims "consistency," identical high error rates across demographic extremes suggest a systemic disadvantage for age groups compared to a presumed middle baseline. The presence of explanations and appeals mitigates impact but does not eliminate the biased outcome itself.

Second, **DARK** patterns are described in the enrollment flow. The interface employs a countdown timer (manufactured urgency) and a pre-ticked consent box (hidden default) to pressure users into agreeing to data storage against their own interest. These are classic deceptive patterns that undermine informed consent.

Other categories appear handled: **HALLU** is addressed via cited sources; **RELY** is mitigated by visible confidence levels and one-tap overrides; **DISC** is covered by visible AI labels; **AUTO** is prevented by human sign-off on irreversible actions. However, the confirmed presence of Bias and Dark Patterns constitutes a failure of the safety floor.

HARMS: BIAS, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
