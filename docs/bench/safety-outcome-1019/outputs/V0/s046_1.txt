The spec reveals two present harms requiring remediation before launch.

First, **BIAS** is explicitly described. The internal test showed disparate impact where "11% of older users" were flagged compared to younger users for identical keywords. The team's dismissal of this as a "normal distribution" indicates the bias is unaddressed and currently active in the system logic.

Second, **RELY** (Over-reliance) is present by design. The specification states the ranker "shows no source or confidence" to enable fast skimming. While an override exists, hiding uncertainty and sourcing invites automation bias, leading recruiters to trust scores beyond their reliability without visible cues to verify them.

Other categories are handled: **DISC** is covered by the visible AI label; **DARK** is mitigated by opt-in consent and lack of pressure patterns; **AUTO** is prevented by the human sign-off requirement for bulk actions. However, the unmitigated bias and the intentional removal of confidence signals constitute significant safety failures.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
