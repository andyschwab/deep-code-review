The spec explicitly presents several unmitigated harms. First, profile pages present answers as "confirmed fact with no source shown," creating a high risk of **HALLU** (hallucination surfaced as fact). Second, the in-app chat suggests AI-written practice questions without disclosing their machine origin, triggering **DISC** (missing AI disclosure). Third, the matching engine executes "irreversible actions" like auto-booking sessions "with no human approval," which is a critical **AUTO** failure for high-stakes commitments.

While the spec notes an override for tutor selection, it does not describe an override for the auto-booked session before it becomes irreversible, nor does it address the lack of uncertainty indicators on the "confirmed facts." The mention of calibration statistics for age groups suggests awareness of bias, but the other three issues are described as current, shipped behaviors without controls.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
