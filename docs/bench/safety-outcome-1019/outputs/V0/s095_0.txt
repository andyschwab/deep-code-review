The spec explicitly flags several critical harms. **BIAS** is present: claiming error rates were "within one point across every group" based on a tiny pilot (three universities) is insufficient evidence to rule out systematic disadvantage; this asserts safety without rigorous measurement. **DARK** patterns are admitted: a "ten-minute timer" creates manufactured urgency, and "pre-ticked consent" hides defaults, pressuring users against their own interest. **AUTO** is a definitive failure: the spec states "irreversible actions [disbursement]... fire immediately" with "no human approval." Financial aid disbursement is high-stakes and costly; full automation here violates the human-in-the-loop floor.

Conversely, the spec indicates **HALLU**, **RELY**, and **DISC** are handled: sources and confidence labels are shown, overrides/appeals are easy, and AI disclosure is visible. However, the presence of unmitigated DARK patterns, unsafe AUTO execution, and unsubstantiated BIAS claims blocks launch.

HARMS: BIAS, DARK, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
