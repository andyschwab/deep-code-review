The spec explicitly describes two present harms. First, **BIAS**: the system flagged 11% of older and younger users differently for identical inputs, yet the team dismissed this as a "normal distribution" rather than investigating potential systematic disadvantage based on age proxies. Second, **HALLU**: the ranker presents answers "flatly" with "no source or confidence," presenting unverified scoring as fact without indicating uncertainty, despite the high stakes of employment screening.

Other categories are handled: **DISC** is covered by the visible AI label; **RELY** is mitigated by one-tap overrides/appeals; **DARK** patterns are absent due to unticked consent and no timers; **AUTO** is prevented by human sign-off on bulk irreversible actions. However, the unaddressed bias finding and the presentation of hallucinated/unverified scores as flat facts constitute significant risks requiring remediation before launch.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
