Beacon's spec reveals three critical output-safety failures requiring immediate remediation.

First, **BIAS** is explicitly present: the team acknowledges an error rate "twice as high for one group of hotel chains," indicating systematic disadvantage, yet relies only on passive weekly log checks rather than active mitigation or subgroup testing before launch.

Second, **HALLU** and **RELY** are unaddressed risks. The system states answers "flatly" with "no source or confidence" despite known accuracy gaps. This design invites over-reliance by masking uncertainty, preventing users from assessing the validity of fabricated or unverified claims.

Third, while **AUTO** (irreversible actions) is correctly gated behind human sign-off and **DISC** is handled via visible labels, the combination of known bias, hidden uncertainty, and a "final" result with "no appeal path" creates a dangerous environment for high-stakes financial commitments. The current controls are insufficient given the admitted failure modes.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
