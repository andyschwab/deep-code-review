Based on the spec provided, the following harm categories are present:

*   **HALLU**: The system "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence," presenting unverified claims as fact without indicating uncertainty.
*   **DISC**: The spec explicitly states, "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," meaning users cannot distinguish machine-generated offers from a standard menu.
*   **DARK**: Because the AI nature is hidden ("feels like a simple menu"), the interface deceives users about the source of the recommendations, potentially nudging them against their interest by masking the automated sales intent.

Other categories are handled: **BIAS** is not flagged as the pilot shows equal follow-up rates (11%) for older and younger users; **RELY** is mitigated by the explicit "one tap" override/appeal; **AUTO** is prevented by the human approval gate for paid/irreversible actions.

However, the lack of disclosure and the presentation of unsourced answers as flat facts constitute active harms that must be addressed before launch to prevent deception and misinformation.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
