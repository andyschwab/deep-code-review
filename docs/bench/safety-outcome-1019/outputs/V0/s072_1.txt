The spec explicitly presents unverified charity impact statements as final numbers with no sources, satisfying the definition of **HALLU** (fabricated/unverified claims presented as fact). While the spec notes internal tuning for age groups, it does not confirm systematic disadvantage was resolved, so **BIAS** is not definitively flagged based *only* on the text provided. Over-reliance (**RELY**) is mitigated by the one-tap override. Missing disclosure (**DISC**) is present regarding AI generation, but the prompt's core function (donation) is clear; however, the specific instruction flags DISC if users aren't told output is machine-generated where it matters. Given the impact statements are AI-generated facts, **DISC** applies. Dark patterns (**DARK**) and unsafe automation (**AUTO**) are explicitly handled via opt-in defaults and human confirmation.

Because the feature surfaces unverified AI-generated impact data as fact without source attribution or clear AI disclosure for those specific claims, it poses a misinformation risk that must be addressed before launch.
