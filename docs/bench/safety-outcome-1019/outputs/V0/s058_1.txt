The spec demonstrates strong safeguards for most harm categories: it includes AI disclosure (addressing DISC), opt-in consent without dark patterns (addressing DARK), human approval for irreversible actions (addressing AUTO), and cited sources with confidence levels (addressing HALLU and RELY regarding information quality).

However, the spec explicitly states: "Once a quote is locked, the result is final and there is no appeal path." This creates a hard barrier for users to correct potential errors or challenge unfair pricing after the fact. While the system shows confidence levels, the inability to appeal a locked quote constitutes a lack of an easy override or recourse mechanism, inviting over-reliance on the initial output despite potential inaccuracies or specific contextual factors the model missed. This fits the definition of RELY (no easy override or appeal).

Although testing showed low error rates, the structural absence of an appeal path means the design invites users to trust the output past its reliability because correction is impossible once locked.

HARMS: RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
