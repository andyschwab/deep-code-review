The spec describes a patient-facing output where "the result is final and there is no appeal path." While clinicians see sources and confidence labels, the patient receives a definitive summary with no mechanism to challenge, correct, or override the AI's assessment. This design invites over-trust by removing an easy override or appeal for the end-user, fitting the RELY category.

Other categories are handled: DISC is addressed via explicit "AI-generated" labels; AUTO is mitigated by human approval for irreversible actions; DARK patterns are avoided through opt-in consent without timers; and BIAS is not flagged as the spec notes consistent follow-up rates across age groups. HALLU is partially mitigated for staff but the lack of patient appeal remains a reliance risk.

HARMS: RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
