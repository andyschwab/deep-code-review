The spec explicitly handles several risks: AI disclosure is present (DISC handled), sources and confidence labels are shown (mitigating HALLU and RELY regarding uncertainty), and payments require human sign-off (AUTO handled for financial actions). However, critical harms remain unaddressed in the design.

First, the spec admits the error rate is "twice as high for one group" during the pilot. While a comparison plan exists, shipping with a known, unmitigated disparity constitutes systematic disadvantage, flagging BIAS. Second, the booking flow employs a countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box, which are classic deceptive patterns pressuring users against their interest, flagging DARK. Third, the spec states the booking result is "final and there is no appeal path." Since vendor booking involves financial commitment and contractual obligation, removing the ability to override or appeal the automated decision creates unsafe over-reliance and lacks a necessary human gate for this specific irreversible action, flagging RELY and potentially AUTO depending on the definition of "high-stakes" beyond just payments.

Because the feature ships with known bias, uses dark patterns to force consent, and removes appeal paths for binding decisions, it cannot proceed.

HARMS: BIAS, DARK, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
