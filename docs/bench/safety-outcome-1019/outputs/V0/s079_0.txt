The spec explicitly flags two present harms. First, **HALLU**: the system presents outputs as "confirmed fact with no source shown" and treats them as "definitive" based on limited beta data, risking unverified claims surfacing as authority. Second, **DISC**: the spec states the product "does not tell users the content was written by AI," failing to disclose machine generation despite the potential for user confusion regarding the source of the quote logic.

Other categories are handled: **RELY** is mitigated by the "one tap" override/appeal; **AUTO** is gated by human sign-off for high-value moves; **DARK** patterns are absent due to opt-in consent and no timers; **BIAS** is claimed addressed via testing (though the "definitive" stance remains risky).

Because the feature ships with undisclosed AI involvement and presents unverified outputs as absolute fact without sourcing, it violates the transparency and hallucination safeguards required before launch.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
