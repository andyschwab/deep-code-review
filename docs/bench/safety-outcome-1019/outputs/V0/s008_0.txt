The spec explicitly flags **BIAS**: error rates are "twice as high" for evening-only guards. While a "targeted review pass" is added, the root disparity exists in the shipped system, requiring verification that the review actually mitigates the harm before launch.

The spec describes **DARK** patterns: a "countdown timer" manufactures urgency, and a "pre-ticked consent box" hides defaults, pressuring users against their own interest during onboarding.

Other categories are handled: **HALLU** is addressed via source labeling; **RELY** is partially mitigated by confidence labels (though the "no appeal path" is risky, swaps are allowed); **DISC** is covered by AI labeling; **AUTO** is gated by human sign-off for irreversible steps.

However, the presence of confirmed bias (even with a planned review) and active dark patterns constitutes a failure to meet safety floors. The bias mitigation is a promise, not a verified result, and dark patterns are prohibited by design.

HARMS: BIAS, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
