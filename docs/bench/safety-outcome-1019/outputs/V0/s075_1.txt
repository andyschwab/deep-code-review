The spec reveals three unaddressed output-safety harms. First, **BIAS**: The system flags older and younger users at identical rates (11%) for extra verification, indicating age-based differential treatment that systematically disadvantages these groups compared to others. Second, **DISC**: The interface displays "verified" without disclosing that a model generated the output, violating transparency requirements for automated decisions. Third, **RELY**: The design presents answers with "stated confidence levels" (implying false precision rather than defined coarse tiers) and explicitly states "there is no appeal path" once a manager confirms the result, removing user recourse and inviting over-reliance on the initial automated flag.

While **AUTO** is handled (irreversible actions require human approval) and **DARK** appears mitigated (consent is plain with no timers), the combination of undisclosed algorithmic profiling, lack of appeal, and missing AI disclosure creates significant risk before launch.

HARMS: BIAS, DISC, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
