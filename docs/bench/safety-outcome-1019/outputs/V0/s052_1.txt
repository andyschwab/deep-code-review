The spec explicitly describes several present harms. Under **HALLU**, the interface presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown. Under **RELY**, users cannot override or appeal results even if they believe the date or dosage is wrong. Under **DARK**, the design uses a ten-minute expiration timer and pre-ticked consent for auto-refill enrollment, pressuring users against their own interest.

While the spec notes that **DISC** is handled (visible AI-generated label) and **AUTO** is mitigated (human approval for irreversible actions), the unaddressed issues regarding factual presentation, lack of user override, and deceptive patterns constitute significant risks. The claim of low error rates in simulation does not negate the structural inability for users to correct errors in production.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
