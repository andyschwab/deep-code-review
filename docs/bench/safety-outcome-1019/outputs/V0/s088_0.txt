The spec explicitly handles several potential harms: AI disclosure is present (mitigating DISC), overrides are easy (mitigating RELY), human sign-off gates irreversible publishing (mitigating AUTO), and bias is acknowledged with a plan to tune (addressing BIAS).

However, two harms remain present per the spec's own description. First, the system presents placement recommendations "flatly" with "no source or confidence," inviting over-reliance despite known accuracy gaps; while an override exists, the lack of uncertainty signaling constitutes RELY. Second, the export flow uses a "countdown timer" and a "pre-ticked consent box," which are classic deceptive patterns pressuring users against their interest, flagging DARK.

HARMS: RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
