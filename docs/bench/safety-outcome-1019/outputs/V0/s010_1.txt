The spec explicitly addresses several harm categories: it labels outputs as AI-generated (handling DISC), shows sources and confidence (mitigating HALLU), uses opt-in consent without pressure (handling DARK), and requires human approval for price-changing irreversible actions (handling AUTO).

However, two harms remain present based on the text:
1.  **BIAS**: The spec states the model previously flagged 11% of older and younger users for secondary review, and this step was *removed* before launch. Removing a protective review layer that disproportionately affected specific age groups suggests the system may now systematically disadvantage these groups without mitigation.
2.  **RELY**: The spec explicitly states, "Users cannot override or appeal the result—the chat ends with a clear yes-or-no." This design invites over-trust by removing any mechanism for correction or appeal, directly matching the definition of RELY.

Since the spec describes these conditions as current features rather than handled risks, they must be addressed before launch.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
