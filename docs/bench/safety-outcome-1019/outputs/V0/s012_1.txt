The spec explicitly describes several unmitigated output harms. First, the bot presents every answer as "confirmed fact with no source shown," which directly triggers **HALLU** (hallucination surfaced as fact). Second, because the result is "final and there is no appeal path" with no uncertainty displayed, the design invites over-reliance, triggering **RELY**. Third, while there is an AI label, the system "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." Since this involves executing financial transactions (upsells) based on automated decisions without human review or explicit final confirmation beyond the initial opt-in, it constitutes unsafe automation of actions that are hard to reverse post-checkout, triggering **AUTO**.

Although the spec mentions a visible AI label (addressing **DISC**) and claims equitable error rates in a pilot (suggesting **BIAS** is monitored), the lack of sourcing, the absence of an override mechanism for final decisions, and the fully autonomous execution of financial actions represent critical safety gaps. The claim that error rates were "within one point" is insufficient to verify safety without defined thresholds or subgroup specifics in this text, but the structural failures in handling facts and irreversible actions are explicit.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
