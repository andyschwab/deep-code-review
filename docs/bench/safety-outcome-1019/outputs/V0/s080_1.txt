The spec explicitly identifies three present harms. First, **BIAS**: the system flagged older and younger users at identical rates (11%) for the same lane, indicating a potential systematic disadvantage or fairness issue in how age groups are treated. Second, **RELY**: the UI presents every answer as "confirmed fact" with no sources, uncertainty indicators, or appeal paths, inviting dangerous over-trust in automated estimates. Third, **DISC**: the product generates rate narratives via AI but fails to disclose this machine origin to users.

Conversely, **AUTO** is handled; the spec confirms a human signs off on irreversible bulk actions. **DARK** patterns are absent, as telemetry is opt-in with no coercive timers. **HALLU** is a risk due to the lack of sourcing, but the spec describes the presentation style (RELY) rather than citing specific fabricated claims, so RELY is the primary flag for the "fact" presentation.

Because the feature launches with unaddressed bias, undisclosed AI generation, and an interface designed to prevent user skepticism or appeal, it cannot proceed.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
