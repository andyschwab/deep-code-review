The spec explicitly addresses several harm categories: AI disclosure is present (DISC handled), confidence and sources are shown with easy overrides (RELY handled), and irreversible actions require human sign-off (AUTO handled).

However, the spec describes two active harms:
1.  **DARK**: The interface uses a "countdown timer" and a "pre-ticked consent box." These are deceptive patterns that manufacture urgency and hide defaults, pressuring users against their own interest.
2.  **BIAS**: The spec claims error rates were "within one point across every group tested." This phrasing implies aggregate similarity but fails to confirm the absence of systematic disadvantage for specific protected groups. Without explicit data showing no disparate impact, the risk of bias remains present in the description.

Because the spec admits to using dark patterns and provides insufficient evidence regarding bias mitigation, the feature cannot proceed.

HARMS: DARK, BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
