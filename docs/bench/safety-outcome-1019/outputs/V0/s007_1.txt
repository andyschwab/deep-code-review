The spec reveals three present harms. First, **DISC**: "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," violating the requirement for AI disclosure where it matters to users. Second, **DARK**: The interface uses "pre-ticked" consent for auto-accepting shifts and imposes a "ten-minute timer," creating manufactured urgency and hidden defaults that pressure users against their interests. Third, **RELY**: While confidence levels are stated, the spec notes they are "drawn from past coverage gaps" without defining the coarse tiers (e.g., Verified vs. Unverified) or warning against over-trust in the specific "11%" flagging metric, which lacks context on whether this represents a bias or a valid control.

However, **BIAS** is not flagged because the pilot explicitly tested older and younger users equally (11% each), showing an attempt to measure fairness rather than a described systematic disadvantage. **AUTO** is handled, as "a human signs off on each irreversible step." **HALLU** is mitigated by cited sources.

The combination of missing disclosure, deceptive patterns (pre-ticked boxes, timers), and potential over-reliance on undefined confidence metrics creates unacceptable risk for launch.

HARMS: DISC, DARK, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
