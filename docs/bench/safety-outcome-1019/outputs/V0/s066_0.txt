Based on the provided spec, the following harm category is flagged:

**HALLU**: The spec explicitly states Matchly "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence." Presenting ranked recommendations without indicating uncertainty or evidentiary support creates a risk of unverified claims being perceived as established fact, even if internal error rates were low.

All other categories appear addressed: **BIAS** is claimed handled via testing; **RELY** is mitigated by the "one tap" override/appeal; **DISC** is covered by explicit AI labeling; **DARK** is avoided via opt-in consent and no timers; **AUTO** is prevented by human sign-off for irreversible actions.

However, the lack of uncertainty signaling (HALLU) in a ranking system presented "flatly" constitutes a present harm that should be resolved before launch to prevent users from treating probabilistic outputs as absolute truths.

HARMS: HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
