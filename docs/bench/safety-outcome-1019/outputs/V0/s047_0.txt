The spec explicitly presents unverified ranking rationales as "confirmed fact with no source shown," satisfying the definition of **HALLU** (hallucination surfaced as fact). While the spec notes an override exists, presenting fabricated or unverified claims with absolute authority constitutes the harm regardless of the ability to correct it later.

Additionally, the spec states the tool "does not tell users the content was written by AI," which directly triggers **DISC** (missing AI disclosure). The spec attempts to mitigate **BIAS** by citing an audit showing flat scoring across age bands, and it handles **AUTO** by requiring human approval for irreversible actions. **RELY** is partially mitigated by the one-tap override, though the "confirmed fact" framing undermines this. **DARK** patterns are explicitly denied in the beta design (opt-in, no timers).

Because the feature ships with unverified claims presented as facts and lacks AI disclosure, it fails the safety floor for output integrity and transparency.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
