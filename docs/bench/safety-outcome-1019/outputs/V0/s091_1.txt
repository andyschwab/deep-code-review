The spec explicitly confirms several harms. First, **DISC** is present: the system "does not tell users the content was written by AI," violating transparency. Second, **AUTO** is critical: the tool "executes irreversible actions with no human approval" to book appointments or request prescriptions, bypassing the required human-in-the-loop gate for high-stakes actions. While the spec mentions confidence levels and override capabilities (mitigating **RELY**) and shows balanced pilot data for older/younger users (suggesting **BIAS** is addressed), the lack of disclosure and the autonomous execution of irreversible medical actions are unmitigated failures described in the text. The presence of a support queue for overrides does not cure the lack of a pre-execution human gate for irreversible acts.

HARMS: DISC, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
