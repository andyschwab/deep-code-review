The spec explicitly handles several categories: sources and confidence labels address HALLU and RELY; AI-generation labeling addresses DISC; human sign-off for payments addresses AUTO. However, two harms are present in the design. First, the spec admits the error rate is "twice as high for one group" during the pilot, confirming BIAS. Second, the booking flow requires a "countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box" with "no appeal path," which constitutes DARK patterns (manufactured urgency, hidden defaults) and exacerbates RELY by removing override options for a binding action.

HARMS: BIAS, DARK, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
