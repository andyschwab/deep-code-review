The spec explicitly handles several categories: DISC is addressed via visible AI labels; RELY is mitigated by cited sources and confidence levels (though the tier definition is unspecified, the mechanism exists); AUTO is gated by human sign-off for irreversible fines; and DARK patterns are avoided through opt-in, unticked defaults without timers.

However, two harms remain present per the spec's own admission. First, BIAS is confirmed: the spec states the error rate is "twice as high for one group" (temporary paper permits), systematically disadvantaging them. While tuning is planned, the pilot launches with this known disparity active. Second, RELY is partially triggered because, despite showing confidence, "Users cannot override or appeal the result—the flag stands." This removes the easy override necessary to prevent automation bias when the system is known to be fallible for specific groups.

Because the feature ships with a known, unmitigated disparate impact on a specific user group and removes the ability to appeal erroneous flags, it poses a direct risk of unfair enforcement during the pilot.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
