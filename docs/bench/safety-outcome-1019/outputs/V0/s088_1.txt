The spec explicitly addresses several harm categories: DISC is handled via visible AI-generated labels; RELY is mitigated by allowing one-tap overrides and appeals; AUTO is controlled by requiring human sign-off for irreversible publishing steps. However, two harms remain present. First, HALLU is flagged because the system states placement recommendations "flatly" with "no source or confidence," presenting unverified suggestions as absolute facts without uncertainty indicators. Second, BIAS is admitted in the spec itself, which notes the error rate is "twice as high" for true-crime episodes with heavy sound design, indicating a systematic disadvantage for that specific content group. Additionally, DARK patterns are evident in the export flow, which uses a "countdown timer" (manufactured urgency) and a "pre-ticked consent box" (hidden default) to nudge producers toward automatic re-insertion against their potential interest. Since the spec confirms these harms exist without stating they are resolved, they must be addressed before launch.

HARMS: HALLU, BIAS, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
