The spec explicitly acknowledges a known disparity: the error rate is "twice as high for one group," confirming **BIAS** is present and not yet mitigated. While the pilot aims to tune the model, shipping with a known, unquantified disproportionate failure rate constitutes a flagged harm.

Regarding **HALLU**, the interface presents answers as "confirmed fact with no source shown." Although users can override this, presenting unverified suggestions as absolute fact without source attribution fits the definition of hallucination surfaced as fact.

However, the spec indicates several controls are active: **RELY** is addressed via "one tap" override/appeal; **DISC** is handled by labeling outputs as AI-generated; **DARK** patterns are avoided through clear consent and no timers; and **AUTO** is prevented by requiring human approval for irreversible actions.

Because the feature launches with an admitted, unmitigated bias affecting a specific group and presents unverified data as confirmed fact, it cannot proceed without addressing these specific inventories.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
