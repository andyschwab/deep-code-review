The spec explicitly identifies three unmitigated harms. First, under **DISC**, the system "does not tell users the content was written by AI," intentionally obscuring the machine-generated nature of the intake. Second, under **AUTO**, the tool "executes irreversible actions with no human approval," specifically booking appointments or sending prescription refills automatically upon user confirmation, violating the requirement for a human-in-the-loop gate on high-stakes actions. Third, under **RELY**, while overrides exist, the combination of hidden AI authorship and irreversible automated execution invites over-trust before the user realizes the stakes.

Although the spec notes equal flagging rates across age groups (addressing **BIAS**) and includes cited sources (mitigating **HALLU**), the lack of disclosure and the absence of a human gate for irreversible medical actions are critical failures. The "user confirms" step for prescriptions does not constitute a clinical human-in-the-loop review required for safe automation in this context. These issues must be resolved before launch.

HARMS: DISC, AUTO, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
