The spec reveals three unaddressed harms. First, **DISC**: recommendations are presented as "our picks" with no disclosure that a model generated the output, hiding the automated nature of the decision from users. Second, **RELY**: while confidence levels are shown, the spec explicitly states users "cannot override or appeal the result," removing the necessary human control loop for corrective action. Third, **BIAS**: the engine flagged equal percentages of older and younger users for follow-up, yet the lack of an appeal mechanism means these groups are systematically disadvantaged by the inability to correct potential misclassifications.

Although **AUTO** is handled (human approval exists for deposits) and **DARK** is mitigated (no timers, easy cancel), the combination of hidden AI agency, removed user agency, and unappealable segmentation creates significant risk. The product invites over-trust in a system that users cannot correct, while obscuring its machine origins.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
