The spec reveals three critical harms requiring mitigation before launch.

First, **HALLU** is present: the product presents every answer as "confirmed fact with no source shown." Despite internal tracking, surfacing unverified claims without sources or uncertainty indicators misleads users.

Second, **RELY** is triggered by the same behavior. Presenting outputs as absolute fact without showing uncertainty invites over-trust, even though an override exists. The design fails to communicate the limits of the AI's reliability to the user.

Third, **BIAS** is explicitly admitted. The spec states error rates are "twice as high" for itineraries involving international codeshares. While extra review is planned for the pilot, shipping a feature with a known, systematic disadvantage for a specific subgroup constitutes a bias harm that must be resolved, not just monitored.

Other categories are handled: **DISC** is addressed via labeling; **AUTO** is mitigated by human sign-off on irreversible steps; **DARK** is less clear due to the pre-ticked consent and expiration timer, but the one-tap appeal offers a strong counter-balance, so it is not flagged as definitively present compared to the explicit admissions above.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
