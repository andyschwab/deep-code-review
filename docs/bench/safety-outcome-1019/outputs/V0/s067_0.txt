The spec explicitly reveals three present harms. First, **HALLU**: the engine states answers "flatly" with "no source or confidence," presenting fabricated or unverified claims as fact. Second, **DISC**: the product "does not tell users the content was written by AI," violating the transparency requirement for machine-generated outputs. Third, **RELY**: by showing "no uncertainty" alongside flat assertions, the design invites over-trust, even though an override exists; the lack of visible limits encourages automation bias.

Other categories are handled. **BIAS** is addressed via pilot testing showing consistent error rates across groups. **DARK** patterns are absent due to plain opt-in, no timers, and easy cancellation. **AUTO** risks are mitigated by human sign-off on irreversible scheduling steps.

Because the feature ships with unaddressed hallucination risks, missing AI disclosure, and design elements fostering over-reliance, it cannot proceed without remediation.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
