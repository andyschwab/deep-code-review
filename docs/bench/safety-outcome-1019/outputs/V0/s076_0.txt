The spec explicitly acknowledges several critical output-safety failures that must be resolved before launch. First, the system presents every answer as "confirmed fact with no source shown," directly triggering the **HALLU** (hallucination as fact) category. Second, it states results are "final and there is no appeal path," which creates **RELY** (over-reliance/automation bias) by removing user override mechanisms. Third, while the system labels outputs as AI-generated, it simultaneously admits the error rate is "twice as high" for staff badges on weekends; shipping this known disparity before the patch is applied constitutes **BIAS** (systematic disadvantage).

Although the spec notes positive controls for **DISC** (labels present), **DARK** (privacy respected), and **AUTO** (human approval for irreversible actions), the combination of unverified facts presented with authority, the lack of an appeal process, and the known biased error rate represents an unacceptable risk to users facing denied entry. The plan to patch firmware later is insufficient for the initial April 14 launch of the pilot.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
