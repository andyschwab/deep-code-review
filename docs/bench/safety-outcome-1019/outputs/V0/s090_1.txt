The spec explicitly handles several harm categories: bias is measured with consistent results across age groups (BIAS handled); hallucination is mitigated by showing sources and confidence labels (HALLU handled); disclosure is present via "AI-generated" labels (DISC handled); deceptive patterns are avoided with opt-in consent and no timers (DARK handled); and high-stakes actions require human approval (AUTO handled).

However, the spec states: "For patient-facing output, the result is final and there is no appeal path." This directly indicates that users cannot override or appeal the output, inviting over-trust without an easy correction mechanism. This matches the RELY (over-reliance) definition.

HARMS: RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
