The spec explicitly addresses most harm categories: it labels outputs as AI-generated (mitigating DISC), requires human approval for irreversible actions (mitigating AUTO), provides one-tap overrides (mitigating RELY), uses unticked consent with no timers (mitigating DARK), and reports balanced error rates across groups (mitigating BIAS).

However, the spec states the assistant "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence." For a concierge booking real hotels and negotiating upgrades, presenting fabricated rates, non-existent rooms, or false upgrade promises as absolute facts without uncertainty markers or sources creates a direct risk of users acting on hallucinations. The spec describes this behavior as a feature ("so users know exactly what they're getting") rather than a mitigated risk, effectively confirming the presence of unverified claims presented as fact.

HARMS: HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
