The spec reveals two critical output-safety failures. First, under **HALLU**, the bot "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown." Surfacing unverified recommendations as absolute truth without uncertainty markers creates a high risk of hallucination being accepted as fact by users. Second, under **DISC**, the spec explicitly states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output." Since the bot generates personalized recommendations and decisions, the lack of AI disclosure prevents users from understanding the nature of the interaction.

Other categories appear addressed: **BIAS** is mitigated by the reported equal flagging rates across age groups; **RELY** is handled by the one-tap override for staff; **DARK** patterns are absent due to clear opt-in and cancel mechanisms; and **AUTO** risks are blocked by the mandatory human sign-off for irreversible actions. However, the combination of presenting unverified outputs as facts and hiding the AI's involvement constitutes a significant safety gap that must be resolved before launch.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
