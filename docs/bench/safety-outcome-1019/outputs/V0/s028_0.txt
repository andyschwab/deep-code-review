The spec reveals three present harms requiring mitigation before launch.

First, **BIAS** is explicitly admitted: the model flags older users for check-ins at double the rate of younger users (18% vs. 9%), indicating systematic disadvantage based on age.

Second, **DARK** patterns are described in the auto-renewal flow. The use of a "countdown timer" manufactures urgency, and a "pre-ticked consent box" hides defaults, both nudging users against their own interest.

Third, **AUTO** is triggered. The spec states that enrolling a member in auto-renewal—an irreversible financial action—"executes... with no human approval." While limited to a pilot, the mechanism lacks the required human-in-the-loop gate for high-stakes actions.

Other categories are handled: **HALLU** is mitigated by showing sources; **RELY** is addressed via overrides and appeals; **DISC** is covered by visible AI labels. However, the presence of Bias, Dark Patterns, and Unsafe Automation constitutes a failure of the safety floor.

HARMS: BIAS, DARK, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
