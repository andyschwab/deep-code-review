The spec explicitly handles several harm categories: DISC is addressed via visible AI-generated labels; AUTO is mitigated by requiring human sign-off before irreversible pauses; RELY is covered by cited sources, stated confidence, and one-tap overrides.

However, two harms are present in the current design:
1.  **BIAS**: The spec admits the error rate is "twice as high for one group." While an adjustment is planned for the "next sprint," the system currently ships with this known disparate impact.
2.  **DARK**: The offboarding flows include a "countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box," which are deceptive patterns pressuring users against their interest. The spec notes these will be revisited "before broad rollout," but they remain in the pilot launch described.

Since the feature ships with confirmed bias and deceptive interface elements, even for a pilot, these must be resolved to prevent immediate harm to the affected driver group and ensure fair consent processes.

HARMS: BIAS, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
