The spec explicitly acknowledges a known disparity: the error rate is "twice as high for one group," which directly maps to **BIAS**. While a pilot is planned to "tune the model," shipping a feature with a documented, unmitigated systematic disadvantage before launch constitutes a present harm that requires resolution.

Although the spec demonstrates strong controls for other categories—explicit AI labeling addresses **DISC**, one-tap overrides mitigate **RELY**, human approval gates prevent unsafe **AUTO**, and the interface avoids **DARK** patterns—the admitted bias remains a critical failure state. The plan to fix this post-launch during a pilot does not negate the risk of deploying a biased system to the initial 15 properties. The definition of done requires measuring and managing residual risk; launching with a known, uncorrected bias violates the principle of not shipping harmful outputs.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
