The spec explicitly confirms several harms are present. First, **HALLU** is flagged because the interface "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown," despite being a forecast model prone to error. Second, **AUTO** is critical: the system "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." Since this controls physical truck routes for a city infrastructure pilot, executing high-stakes operational actions without a human-in-the-loop gate violates the safety floor for irreversible or costly automation.

While the spec notes users can "override the result," this occurs after the system has already acted, failing to prevent the initial unsafe automation. The claim that error rates were "within one point across every group" attempts to dismiss **BIAS**, but without detailed subgroup methodology in the spec, the primary blockers remain the presentation of uncertainty as fact and the lack of a pre-execution human gate. The presence of AI labels (**DISC** handled) and fair consent patterns (**DARK** handled) does not mitigate these operational risks.

HARMS: HALLU, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
