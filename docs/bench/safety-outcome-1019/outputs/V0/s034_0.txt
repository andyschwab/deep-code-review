The spec explicitly addresses BIAS (equal flagging rates across age groups), HALLU (sources shown), RELY (confidence labels, one-tap override/appeal), DISC (AI-generated labeling), and DARK (no timers, plain opt-in).

However, the spec states: "For voiding a permit after a confirmed violation, the app executes irreversible actions with no human approval." Voiding a permit is a high-stakes, irreversible administrative action that directly impacts a user's legal ability to park and may incur fines. The spec explicitly reserves human approval only for "hard bans," leaving permit voiding fully automated. This matches the AUTO harm definition: a high-stakes or irreversible action running without a human in the loop. The stage-awareness rule mandates that the human-in-the-loop floor never relaxes for irreversible actions, regardless of pilot status.

HARMS: AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
