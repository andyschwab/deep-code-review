The spec reveals three critical output-safety failures. First, **BIAS** is present: the system flags older users for follow-up at double the rate of younger users (18% vs 9%), indicating a systematic disadvantage. Second, **RELY** is unmitigated: the design explicitly states users "cannot override or appeal the result," removing human agency despite the tool's advisory nature. Third, **DISC** is missing: the spec notes "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," violating transparency requirements for AI-generated recommendations.

While **HALLU** is addressed (sources and confidence labels are shown), **DARK** patterns are absent (consent is opt-in with easy cancellation), and **AUTO** risks are managed (human sign-off exists for irreversible actions), the combination of unexplained bias, forced reliance, and hidden AI origin creates unacceptable risk for a healthcare feature. The disparity in flagging rates requires investigation before exposing more patients to potential mis-triage.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
