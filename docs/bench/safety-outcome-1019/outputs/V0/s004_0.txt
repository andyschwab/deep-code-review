The spec reveals three present harms requiring attention. First, **BIAS** is explicitly admitted: the beta flagged 18% of older users versus 9% of younger users for specialist review, indicating systematic disadvantage. Second, **DARK** patterns are described: a countdown timer creates manufactured urgency, and a pre-ticked consent box hides defaults for data sharing, pressuring users against their interests. Third, **DISC** is confirmed missing: the spec states the interface "does not tell users the content was written by AI," despite generating coverage determinations.

Other categories are handled. **RELY** is mitigated by visible confidence labels, source attribution, and one-tap overrides. **AUTO** is prevented by the requirement that a human approves every irreversible action. **HALLU** is partially addressed by showing sources, though the lack of disclosure complicates how users interpret those sources.

Because the feature ships with confirmed bias, deceptive interface patterns, and missing AI disclosure, it cannot proceed without remediation.

HARMS: BIAS, DARK, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
