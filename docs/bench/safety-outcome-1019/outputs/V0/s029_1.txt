The spec explicitly addresses the defined harm categories with concrete controls. **DISC** is handled via a visible "AI-generated" label. **HALLU** and **RELY** are mitigated by showing sources, confidence labels, and providing one-tap overrides/appeals. **DARK** patterns are excluded by design (no timers, plain opt-in, easy cancel). Crucially, **AUTO** is prevented by requiring human sign-off on irreversible steps.

Regarding **BIAS**, the spec states internal tests confirmed error rates were consistent ("within one point") across every group tested. While independent verification isn't possible without tools, the spec itself describes this harm as measured and handled, rather than present. Since the instructions require flagging a category only when the spec describes the harm as *present*, and the spec describes controls for all listed risks, no categories are flagged.
