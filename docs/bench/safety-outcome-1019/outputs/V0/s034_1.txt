The spec explicitly addresses BIAS (equal flagging rates across age groups), HALLU (sources shown), RELY (confidence labels, one-tap override/appeal), DISC (AI-generated labels), and DARK (opt-in consent, no timers, easy cancel).

However, the spec states: "For voiding a permit after a confirmed violation, the app executes irreversible actions with no human approval." Voiding a permit is a high-stakes, irreversible administrative action that directly impacts a user's legal ability to park and may incur fines. Automating this without a human-in-the-loop gate violates the safety floor for irreversible actions, regardless of the preceding confirmation logic. This matches the AUTO harm category.

HARMS: AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
