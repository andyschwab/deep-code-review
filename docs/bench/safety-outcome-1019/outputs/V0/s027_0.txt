The spec explicitly confirms age-balanced targeting (no BIAS), provides one-tap overrides for staff (no RELY), uses clear opt-in consent without pressure (no DARK), and gates irreversible actions behind human sign-off (no AUTO).

However, two harms are present. First, the bot "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown," creating a risk of unverified claims being treated as truth (HALLU). Second, the spec states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," meaning users and potentially staff are unaware the advice is machine-generated (DISC). While staff have control, the lack of AI disclosure violates transparency norms for automated recommendations, and presenting unsourced answers as fact invites hallucination risks.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
