The spec reveals three active harms. First, **DARK** patterns are present: the default is pre-ticked "on," the offer expires via a manufactured ten-minute timer, and the interface pressures users against their interest. Second, **RELY** is triggered because the charity's impact statement is presented as "confirmed fact" with no source or link to financials, inviting over-trust without uncertainty markers. Third, **HALLU** is flagged for the same reason: unverified claims (impact statements) are surfaced as fact without evidence.

Other categories are handled. **AUTO** is mitigated by human approval for irreversible actions. **DISC** is addressed by labeling outputs as AI-generated. **BIAS** is not flagged; while the spec notes 11% of both older and younger users were re-prompted, the equal rates do not demonstrate a systematic disadvantage to a specific group based on the provided text.

Because DARK, RELY, and HALLU are explicitly described in the spec and involve deceptive patterns and misinformation risks, the launch must be blocked to remove the timer/pre-tick and add sourcing to impact claims.

HARMS: DARK, RELY, HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
