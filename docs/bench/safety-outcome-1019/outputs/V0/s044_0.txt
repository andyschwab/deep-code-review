The spec reveals three critical harms. First, **BIAS** is explicitly present: testing shows an error rate twice as high for itineraries involving international codeshares, systematically disadvantaging that group. Second, **HALLU** exists because the product presents every answer as "confirmed fact" with no sources shown, despite internal tracking only being available for post-hoc audits. Third, **DARK** patterns are designed into the interface: the offer expires via a ten-minute timer and consent is pre-ticked, pressuring users against their own interest by manufacturing urgency and hiding defaults.

While the spec notes human sign-off for irreversible steps (mitigating AUTO), clear AI labeling (mitigating DISC), and easy overrides (mitigating RELY), the identified harms regarding biased error rates, unsubstantiated factual claims, and coercive interface design must be addressed before launch.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
