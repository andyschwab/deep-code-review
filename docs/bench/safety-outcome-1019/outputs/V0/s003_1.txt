The spec reveals three critical harms. First, **DISC**: The product intentionally hides that a model produced the output ("nothing in the product says a model produced the output"), preventing users from understanding the nature of the decision support. Second, **DARK**: The interface uses a countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box for payouts, manufacturing urgency and hiding defaults to pressure adjusters against their own interest. Third, **AUTO**: While a human signs off, the spec describes a "pre-ticked consent box" for irreversible financial actions ($500+), which undermines meaningful human control by encouraging blind acceptance of high-stakes automation.

Although the spec claims error rates are consistent across groups (addressing BIAS) and allows overrides (mitigating RELY), the active concealment of AI usage, deceptive UI patterns, and weakened human gate for irreversible actions constitute immediate safety failures. The claim of being "confident" without providing raw data or subgroup specifics also borders on HALLU if those specific metrics aren't verifiable, but the three flagged categories are sufficient to block.

HARMS: DISC,DARK,AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
