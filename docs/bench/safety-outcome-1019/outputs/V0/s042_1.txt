The spec explicitly addresses several potential harms: it labels outputs as AI-generated (mitigating DISC), provides cited sources and confidence levels while allowing one-tap overrides (mitigating HALLU and RELY), uses plain opt-in without pressure (mitigating DARK), and reports equal follow-up rates across age groups in beta (no evidence of BIAS).

However, the spec states the assistant "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first" to rebook flights, change seats, or issue refunds. Rebooking and refunds are high-stakes financial and logistical actions that can be costly or difficult to reverse if incorrect (e.g., routing errors, fare differences, or unwanted itinerary changes). Executing these irreversible actions fully autonomously without a human-in-the-loop gate or explicit user confirmation before execution constitutes an unsafe automation risk. The staged rollout does not eliminate the fundamental lack of a gate on the action itself.

HARMS: AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
