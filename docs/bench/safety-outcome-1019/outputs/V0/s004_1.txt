The spec explicitly reveals several unmitigated harms. First, **DISC** is present: the system "does not tell users the content was written by AI," violating the requirement for machine-generated disclosure. Second, **DARK** patterns are built into the interface via a "countdown timer" (manufactured urgency) and a "pre-ticked consent box" (hidden default), pressuring users against their own interests. Third, **BIAS** is evidenced by the beta run where 18% of older users were flagged for specialist review versus only 9% of younger users; while the team plans to "track" this, shipping with a known, uncorrected disparate impact without a stated mitigation plan constitutes an active harm.

Other categories appear handled: **AUTO** is mitigated because a person approves every irreversible action. **RELY** is addressed by showing sources, confidence labels, and allowing one-tap overrides. **HALLU** is partially addressed by showing sources, though the lack of AI disclosure complicates trust.

Because the feature ships with confirmed deceptive patterns, missing AI disclosure, and an unmitigated known bias affecting a protected demographic (age), it cannot proceed.

HARMS: BIAS, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
