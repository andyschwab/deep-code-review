The spec reveals three unaddressed harms. First, **DISC**: The interface presents recommendations as "our picks" with no statement that a model produced the output, violating the requirement for AI disclosure where it matters to users. Second, **RELY**: While confidence levels are shown, the spec explicitly states users "cannot override or appeal the result," creating a dangerous over-reliance trap with no escape hatch. Third, **BIAS**: The engine flagged equal percentages of older and younger users for follow-up, yet the lack of an appeal mechanism (noted above) means this potential disparate impact cannot be corrected by the affected groups.

Although **AUTO** is handled (human approval exists for deposits) and **DARK** is mitigated (no timers, easy cancel), the combination of hidden AI authorship, impossible appeals, and uncorrected filtering creates significant risk. The "closed beta" status does not exempt the feature from these fundamental safety floors; shipping without an override mechanism or clear disclosure invites immediate user harm and distrust.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
