The spec explicitly describes two present harms. First, under **HALLU**, the system "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown," surfacing unverified claims with undue authority. Second, under **RELY**, the result is "final and there is no appeal path," inviting over-trust by removing uncertainty indicators and blocking overrides for denied entries.

Other categories are handled in the spec: **BIAS** is acknowledged and patched before the second site launches; **DISC** is addressed by labeling outputs as AI-generated; **DARK** is mitigated via opt-in consent without timers; and **AUTO** is controlled by requiring human approval for irreversible actions.

Because the design ships with outputs presented as absolute fact and denies users any appeal mechanism, these issues must be resolved before launch to prevent user harm from erroneous denials.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
