The spec explicitly addresses several harm categories: it labels outputs as AI-generated (handling DISC), shows sources with confidence labels and allows overrides (handling RELY and HALLU), and uses opt-in consent without pressure (handling DARK). While it mentions error rates were similar across groups in a pilot, this does not fully prove the absence of BIAS in a larger rollout, but the spec does not describe bias as a present, unaddressed failure mode in the design itself.

However, the spec clearly describes a critical failure in the AUTO category. It states: "the ranked list acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." Since this ranking determines scholarship eligibility—a high-stakes decision with significant financial and academic consequences—executing this action without any human review or gate before it "acts" constitutes unsafe automation of a high-stakes decision. The reduction of the review queue to "under one hour" by removing human review confirms the lack of a human-in-the-loop gate for the initial decision.

HARMS: AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
