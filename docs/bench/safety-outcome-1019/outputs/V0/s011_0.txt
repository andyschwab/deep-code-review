Based on the provided spec, the following harm categories are flagged:

*   **HALLU**: The spec states Kiosk "shows no source or confidence" while presenting answers flatly. This creates a risk of unverified claims being accepted as fact without context.
*   **DISC**: The spec explicitly notes, "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," meaning users cannot distinguish machine-generated recommendations from a static menu.

The spec indicates that **BIAS** is not currently flagged because pilot data shows equal follow-up rates (11%) for older and younger users. **RELY** is mitigated by the "one tap" override and appeal mechanism. **DARK** patterns are absent due to the lack of timers and the presence of easy cancellation. **AUTO** risks are handled by the requirement for human approval on irreversible, paid actions.

However, the lack of AI disclosure (DISC) and the presentation of unsourced information as fact (HALLU) constitute present harms in the current design. Per safety guidelines, missing AI disclosure where it matters to the user and the potential for hallucination without uncertainty markers require remediation before launch.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
