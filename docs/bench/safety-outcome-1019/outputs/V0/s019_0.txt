The spec explicitly confirms controls for several categories: error rates were tested across groups (mitigating BIAS), overrides and appeals are available (mitigating RELY), an AI-generated label is visible (mitigating DISC), and irreversible steps require human confirmation (mitigating AUTO). The core validation is described as complete.

However, the spec admits two active harms. First, the interface "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown," which directly enables HALLU by surfacing unverified claims as authoritative facts without evidence. Second, the sign-up flow uses a "countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box" to nudge quick completion; this constitutes DARK patterns by manufacturing urgency and hiding defaults, pressuring users against their own interest.

Because the specification describes these harmful behaviors as current features rather than planned fixes, the product cannot proceed to launch in its stated form. The presence of deceptive patterns and the presentation of unsourced outputs as fact violate the safety floor regardless of the pilot timeline.

HARMS: HALLU, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
