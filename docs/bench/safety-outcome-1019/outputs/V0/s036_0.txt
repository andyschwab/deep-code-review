The spec explicitly flags several critical output-safety failures. First, **BIAS** is present: the team admits the error rate is "twice as high" for vehicles with temporary paper permits, systematically disadvantaging that group. Second, **AUTO** is a hard failure: the spec states the result is "final," there is "no appeal path," and the system executes "irreversible actions" (ticket issuance) with "no human approval." This violates the mandatory human-in-the-loop gate for high-stakes decisions. Third, **RELY** is triggered because the design offers "no easy override or appeal," inviting over-trust in a known-flawed system.

While the spec correctly handles **DISC** (visible AI label) and **DARK** (no timers, easy cancel), the combination of known bias, irreversible automation, and lack of appeal creates an unacceptable risk profile. The plan to "tune the scanner" before rollout does not mitigate the immediate absence of a human gate for irreversible actions.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
