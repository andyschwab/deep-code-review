The spec reveals two present harms requiring attention before launch.

First, **HALLU** is flagged. The system "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence," presenting unverified predictions as absolute facts without indicating uncertainty. While overrides exist, the lack of visible confidence invites users to treat probabilistic outputs as certainty.

Second, **DISC** is explicitly confirmed as a gap. The spec states, "It does not tell users the content was written by AI." Even though the users are internal staff, the absence of disclosure regarding machine-generated content constitutes a transparency failure under the defined categories.

Other categories are handled: **RELY** is mitigated by the "one tap" override/appeal; **AUTO** is prevented by the human sign-off on irreversible steps; **DARK** is avoided via plain opt-in without timers; and **BIAS** is acknowledged and being tracked (though the disparity exists, the spec indicates active management rather than an unaddressed design flaw, but the primary blockers here are the unmitigated presentation of facts and missing disclosure).

Because the feature presents unverified claims as flat facts and hides its AI nature from users, it fails the transparency and uncertainty floors.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
