The spec reveals two unaddressed harms. First, **BIAS**: The model testing explicitly flagged an 11% discrepancy affecting older and younger users. While consistency was tested, the spec does not state this disparity was resolved or mitigated before launch; shipping with a known, quantified disadvantage for specific age groups constitutes a present bias harm.

Second, **DARK**: The enrollment flow includes a "countdown timer" (manufactured urgency) and a "pre-ticked consent box" (hidden default). These are classic deceptive patterns that pressure users into agreeing to data storage against their own interest or without active, informed consent.

Other categories appear handled: **HALLU** is addressed via cited sources; **RELY** is mitigated by visible confidence levels, explanations, and a one-tap override; **DISC** is covered by the visible AI label; and **AUTO** is prevented by the human sign-off requirement for irreversible actions. However, the unresolved bias and the active use of dark patterns require fixes before launch.

HARMS: BIAS, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
