Beacon's spec reveals three critical output-safety failures. First, **HALLU**: the system states answers "flatly" with "no source or confidence," presenting unverified claims as fact. Second, **RELY**: the design invites over-trust by showing no uncertainty and explicitly providing "no appeal path" after confirmation. Third, **BIAS**: the spec admits the error rate is "twice as high for one group of hotel chains," indicating systematic disadvantage, yet relies only on passive weekly log checks rather than active mitigation before launch.

While the spec correctly handles **DISC** (visible AI label), **DARK** (no timers, easy cancel), and **AUTO** (human sign-off on irreversible steps), the unaddressed hallucination presentation, lack of override mechanisms, and acknowledged biased error rates constitute unacceptable residual risk for a feature executing financial transactions.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
