The spec explicitly confirms several harms. **HALLU** is present: the system presents every ranked answer as "confirmed fact with no source shown." **DISC** is present: the tool "does not tell users the content was written by AI." **DARK** is present: beta consent is "opt-in and unticked," a deceptive pattern that pressures users against their interest by hiding the default.

Other categories are handled or absent. **BIAS** is addressed via an audit ensuring flat scoring across age bands. **RELY** is mitigated because results can be "overridden or appealed in one tap." **AUTO** is controlled since a person approves every irreversible action.

However, the presence of hallucination presented as fact, missing AI disclosure, and deceptive consent defaults constitutes significant risk. Presenting unverified rankings as confirmed facts without sources invites severe over-reliance despite the override button, and the unticked default violates basic transparency norms. These issues must be resolved before launch to prevent misleading users and regulators.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
