The spec reveals three unmitigated harms. First, **BIAS**: the system flags older and younger users at identical rates (11%) for extra verification, creating a disparate impact based on age that disadvantages these groups compared to others. Second, **RELY**: the process offers "no appeal path" once a manager confirms an answer, removing the user's ability to correct errors or challenge decisions. Third, **DISC**: the interface displays "verified" without disclosing that a model generated the output or confidence level, hiding the automated nature of the decision from the user.

While the spec notes human approval for irreversible actions (mitigating AUTO) and simple consent for location entry (mitigating DARK), the combination of demographic flagging, lack of recourse, and missing transparency creates significant risk. The absence of an appeal mechanism for a system that disproportionately targets specific age groups is particularly critical. These issues must be addressed before the pilot launches to prevent unfair exclusion and ensure users understand how decisions affecting their access are made.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
