The spec reveals three unaddressed harms. First, **DISC**: the interface "reads like a simple scheduling form" with no indication that a model produced the output, violating the requirement for AI disclosure. Second, **DARK**: the design employs deceptive patterns, specifically a "ten-minute timer" creating manufactured urgency and "pre-ticked" consent for auto-accepting shifts, pressuring users against their interests. Third, **RELY**: while an override exists, the system displays a "stated confidence level" without defining the tiers (e.g., what specific probability or evidence "high confidence" represents), risking false precision and over-trust.

Although **BIAS** was measured (showing equal 11% flag rates across age groups) and **AUTO** is properly gated by human sign-off for irreversible actions, the presence of undisclosed AI generation, deceptive interface mechanics, and undefined confidence metrics creates immediate user risk. These issues cannot be ignored for launch.

HARMS: DISC, DARK, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
