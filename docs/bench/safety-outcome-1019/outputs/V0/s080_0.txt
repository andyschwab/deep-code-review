The spec explicitly describes multiple unmitigated harms. First, **BIAS** is present: the system flagged different user age groups for the same lane, indicating systematic disadvantage. Second, **HALLU** and **RELY** are critical failures; the UI presents every answer as "confirmed fact" with no sources, uncertainty, or appeal path, inviting dangerous over-trust in potentially fabricated rates. Third, **DISC** is violated because the generated rate narratives are not identified as AI-produced.

Conversely, **DARK** patterns are not flagged; the spec notes consent is opt-in, unticked, and easy to cancel. **AUTO** risks are mitigated; the spec confirms a human signs off on irreversible bulk actions before execution.

However, the combination of biased outputs presented as absolute fact without disclosure or recourse creates unacceptable risk for financial decision-making. The lack of uncertainty indicators and appeal mechanisms for spot rates constitutes a severe safety failure.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
