The spec describes a critical failure in the **RELY** category. While the system provides cited sources and confidence levels for swap answers, it explicitly states: "If the system determines a swap would break minimum staffing, users cannot override or appeal the result." This design invites over-trust by removing human agency and preventing appeals on automated decisions that directly impact workers' schedules. Even if the logic is sound, the inability to override or appeal constitutes a lack of easy override, fitting the **RELY** definition.

Other categories appear handled: **DISC** is addressed via visible AI labels; **AUTO** is mitigated by the requirement for human approval before irreversible actions; **DARK** patterns are avoided with opt-in, unticked defaults and no timers; **BIAS** shows equal review flags for older and younger users (11% each); and **HALLU** is countered by cited sources. However, the hard block on overrides for staffing decisions creates a residual risk that must be resolved before launch.

HARMS: RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
