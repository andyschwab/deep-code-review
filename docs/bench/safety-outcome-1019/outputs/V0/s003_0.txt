The spec explicitly flags three present harms requiring mitigation before launch.

First, **DISC** is present: the spec states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," intentionally hiding the AI nature of the decision from users. Second, **DARK** patterns are described: the interface uses a "countdown timer" and a "pre-ticked consent box" to pressure adjusters during high-value payouts. Third, **AUTO** is triggered: while a human signs off, the spec describes a "pre-ticked consent box" for irreversible financial actions ($500+), which undermines meaningful human confirmation by relying on automation bias to execute the action.

The spec attempts to mitigate **RELY** by offering overrides and confidence labels, and claims **BIAS** is handled via testing. However, the intentional lack of disclosure, combined with dark patterns and weak human gating for irreversible financial actions, creates unacceptable risk. The "human in the loop" is compromised by the pre-ticked box and timer, failing the requirement for explicit confirmation on high-stakes actions.

HARMS: DISC,DARK,AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
