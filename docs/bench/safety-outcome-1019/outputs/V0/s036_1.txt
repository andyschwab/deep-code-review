The spec explicitly flags several unmitigated harms. First, **BIAS** is admitted: the error rate is "twice as high" for vehicles with temporary paper permits, and the fix ("tuning") is pending, meaning the feature ships with known discriminatory performance. Second, **AUTO** is present and untreated: the spec states the result is "final," there is "no appeal path," and ticket issuance (an irreversible, high-stakes action) executes with "no human approval." While the spec addresses **DISC** (visible label) and **DARK** (no timers, easy cancel), the combination of known bias affecting a specific group and fully automated irreversible enforcement without a human gate creates unacceptable risk. The confidence level mentioned does not mitigate the lack of an override or appeal mechanism for the biased outcomes.

HARMS: BIAS,AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
