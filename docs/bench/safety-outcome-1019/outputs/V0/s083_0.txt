The spec explicitly presents unverified forecasts as "confirmed fact with no source shown," satisfying the definition of **HALLU** (hallucination surfaced as fact). While error rates were low in testing, presenting probabilistic forecasts as absolute facts without uncertainty indicators creates a risk of misleading users, regardless of past performance.

Furthermore, the system "acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first" to dispatch crews. In a physical logistics context involving 2,000 bikes and 150 stations, automated dispatch constitutes a high-stakes action with real-world costs and operational disruption. The spec confirms there is no human review before execution, triggering **AUTO** (unsafe automation of high-stakes actions). Although an override exists post-decision, the lack of a pre-execution gate for irreversible physical deployment violates the human-in-the-loop floor for high-stakes automation.

The spec indicates **BIAS** is addressed (error rates consistent across groups), **DISC** is handled (visible AI label), **DARK** is mitigated (no timers, easy cancel), and **RELY** is partially addressed via the one-tap override, though the "confirmed fact" framing undermines this. However, the presence of unmitigated HALLU and AUTO requires intervention.

HARMS: HALLU, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
