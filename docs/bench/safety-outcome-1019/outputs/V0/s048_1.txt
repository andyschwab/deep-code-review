The spec explicitly handles several potential harms: it addresses bias (BIAS) by noting the disparity and shipping an audit trail; it mitigates over-reliance (RELY) via source visibility, confidence labels, and appeal mechanisms; and it prevents unsafe automation (AUTO) by requiring human approval for irreversible actions.

However, two critical harms remain present in the design:

1.  **Missing AI-disclosure (DISC):** The spec states, "nothing in the product says a model produced the output—the interface reads as a simple sorting tool." Users and recruiters are not informed that these rankings and justifications are machine-generated, violating transparency expectations.
2.  **Deceptive patterns (DARK):** The onboarding flow includes a "countdown timer" (manufactured urgency) and a "pre-ticked consent box" (hidden default), which pressure users into agreements against their own interest or without active consent.

Since the feature ships with undisclosed AI generation and manipulative interface patterns, it cannot proceed.

HARMS: DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
