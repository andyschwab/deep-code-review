The spec reveals two unmitigated harms. First, **HALLU**: charity impact statements are presented as "confirmed fact with no source shown," despite being AI-generated. This surfaces unverified claims with unwarranted authority. Second, **DISC**: the spec explicitly states the prompt "does not tell users the content was written by AI," failing to disclose machine-generated content where it matters for trust.

Other categories are addressed: **RELY** is mitigated by one-tap overrides; **AUTO** is prevented by mandatory user confirmation; **DARK** patterns are absent due to opt-in defaults and lack of urgency; **BIAS** testing showed equal dismissal rates across age groups, indicating no systematic disadvantage in the described metric.

However, presenting fabricated or unverified charity impacts as fact without disclosure creates a significant risk of misleading users before they commit funds. These issues must be resolved before launch.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
