The spec explicitly states that error rates were low during beta, leading the team to treat outputs as "definitive" and present every answer as "confirmed fact with no source shown." This design invites over-trust by removing uncertainty indicators, satisfying the RELY category. Although an override exists, the presentation of estimates as absolute facts without sourcing encourages users to rely on them blindly.

Regarding DISC, the spec notes the tool does not disclose AI involvement because calculations are deterministic and copy is templated. Since the core function is estimation based on inputs rather than generative content creation, and the logic is described as deterministic, this does not clearly meet the threshold for undisclosed machine-generated content in a deceptive manner under this specific definition.

The spec confirms human sign-off for high-value moves (addressing AUTO), opt-in consent (addressing DARK), and no evidence of systematic disadvantage (BIAS) or fabricated claims (HALLU) is presented as a feature; rather, the confidence in low error rates is the issue. The primary harm is the unwarranted confidence instilled in the user.

HARMS: RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
