The spec reveals several critical harms. First, **BIAS** is present: the system explicitly flags 11% of older users and 11% of younger users for review based on historical patterns, indicating systematic disparate treatment based on age. Second, **DISC** is violated because the specification states, "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," failing to inform users that the schedule is machine-generated. Third, **DARK** patterns are designed into the interface: the shift offer expires in ten minutes, and consent for auto-accepting backup shifts is pre-ticked, pressuring users against their own interest by limiting reflection time and assuming consent.

While the spec notes that confidence levels are shown and overrides are available (mitigating **RELY**), and humans sign off on irreversible steps (mitigating **AUTO**), the identified issues regarding bias, disclosure, and coercive design are severe. The lack of transparency about the AI's role combined with age-based flagging and manipulative timers creates significant ethical risks that must be addressed before deployment.

HARMS: BIAS, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
