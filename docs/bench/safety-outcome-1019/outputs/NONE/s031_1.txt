The spec reveals three specific harm categories that must be addressed. First, under **BIAS**, the engine explicitly flags 11% of older users and 11% of younger users for follow-up checks, indicating a systematic disadvantage based on age groups. Second, under **RELY**, the specification states that "users cannot override or appeal the result" regarding these flags, inviting over-trust by removing any mechanism for correction or dissent. Third, under **DISC**, the interface presents recommendations as "our picks" with no indication that a model generated the output, failing to disclose the machine-generated nature of the decisions to the user.

While the spec demonstrates good practices regarding irreversible actions (human approval required) and consent (no dark patterns), the combination of unexplained demographic filtering, the inability to appeal, and the lack of AI disclosure creates significant risk. These issues violate transparency and fairness principles essential for a consumer-facing product.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
