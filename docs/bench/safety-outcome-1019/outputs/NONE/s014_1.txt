The spec indicates that RenewRight handles several potential harms proactively: outputs are labeled as AI-generated (addressing DISC), confidence levels and sources are provided to mitigate over-trust (addressing RELY and HALLU), and human approval is required for irreversible actions (addressing AUTO). Pilot testing showed consistent error rates across groups, suggesting bias (BIAS) is currently managed.

However, the onboarding flow presents a clear issue. The specification states the process includes a "countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box for data usage." This design pattern pressures users into agreeing to data usage without active, informed consent, leveraging time pressure and default settings to secure agreement against the user's potential interest in reviewing terms carefully. This fits the definition of a dark pattern.

HARMS: DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
