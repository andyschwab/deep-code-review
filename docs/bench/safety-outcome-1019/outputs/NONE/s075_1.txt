The spec reveals several critical harms. First, the system flags older and younger users at identical rates (11%) for extra verification, suggesting a potential **BIAS** where age groups are systematically disadvantaged compared to others, even if the rates match each other, the implication is that age is a factor in flagging. Second, the interface displays "verified" or "needs review" without disclosing that a model generated the output, violating **DISC** requirements. Third, once a manager confirms an answer, the result is final with "no appeal path," creating a **RELY** risk where users cannot challenge potentially erroneous decisions. Finally, while irreversible actions like badge revocation require human approval, the initial flagging and verification process lacks uncertainty indicators for the user during the automated step, further reinforcing **RELY**.

However, the spec notes that answers carry cited sources and confidence levels (mitigating **HALLU**), irreversible actions have human approval (mitigating **AUTO**), and consent is plain opt-in (mitigating **DARK**). The primary unresolved issues are the lack of disclosure regarding machine generation, the absence of an appeal mechanism, and the age-based flagging pattern.

HARMS: BIAS, DISC, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
