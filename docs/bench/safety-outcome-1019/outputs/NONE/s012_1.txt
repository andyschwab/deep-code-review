The spec indicates several potential harm categories. First, HALLU is present because the bot "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown," risking unverified claims being treated as truth. Second, RELY is a concern since "the result is final and there is no appeal path," inviting over-trust without an easy override mechanism for incorrect suggestions. Third, AUTO applies because "the system acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first," executing actions without human oversight, though the stakes here (upsells) may be debatable, the lack of review fits the definition. 

However, DISC is handled since "each output carries a visible AI-generated label." DARK is not evident as the spec describes "plain opt-in consent with no timers and one-tap cancel," avoiding pressure tactics. BIAS is not flagged because pilot error rates were consistent "across every group tested, covering age brackets," suggesting no systematic disadvantage was observed in the data provided.

Given the presence of HALLU, RELY, and AUTO, where users receive unverified facts as certain with no recourse, these issues should be addressed to prevent user confusion or frustration before launch.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
