The spec reveals several critical issues requiring attention before launch. First, the admission that "the error rate is twice as high for one group" indicates a systematic disadvantage, triggering the **BIAS** category. Although an adjustment is planned for the next sprint, shipping with known disparate impact is a present harm.

Second, the offboarding flow includes a "pre-ticked consent box." This interface pattern manipulates users into agreeing to terms they might otherwise reject, fitting the definition of **DARK** patterns. While the team plans to revisit this before "broad rollout," it is part of the current design specified for the pilot.

Other categories are adequately addressed: outputs have visible AI labels (mitigating **DISC**), confidence levels and overrides are available (mitigating **RELY** and **HALLU**), and human sign-off is required before irreversible actions (mitigating **AUTO**). However, the presence of confirmed bias and dark patterns necessitates a halt to ensure safety and fairness.

HARMS: BIAS, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
