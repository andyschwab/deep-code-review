The spec reveals several critical harms. First, the tool systematically flags older users for follow-up at double the rate of younger users (18% vs. 9%), indicating potential **BIAS**. Second, the design explicitly states that "users cannot override or appeal the result," which creates a **RELY** risk by inviting over-trust without an easy mechanism for correction. Third, the specification notes that "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," failing to disclose the machine-generated nature of the triage advice, which constitutes **DISC**.

While the spec mentions that humans sign off on irreversible actions (mitigating **AUTO**) and that consent is handled carefully (mitigating **DARK**), the identified issues regarding bias, lack of appeal, and nondisclosure are present in the current design description. The presence of source citations helps mitigate **HALLU**, but does not resolve the other flagged categories.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
