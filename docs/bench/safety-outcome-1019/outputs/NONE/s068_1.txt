The spec reveals several critical harm categories. First, under **HALLU**, profile pages present answers as "confirmed fact with no source shown," risking the presentation of unverified claims as truth. Second, **DISC** is evident because the in-app chat suggests AI-written practice questions without informing users that the content is machine-generated. Third, **RELY** concerns arise as the interface presents outputs as confirmed facts without showing uncertainty, potentially inviting over-trust despite the appeal mechanism. Finally, **AUTO** is triggered because the matching engine executes "irreversible actions" like auto-booking sessions without human approval, bypassing a necessary human-in-the-loop for high-stakes commitments.

While the spec notes calibration for age groups (addressing potential BIAS) and easy cancellation (mitigating DARK patterns), the identified issues regarding fabrication, undisclosed AI generation, over-trust design, and irreversible automation constitute significant risks. These flaws directly violate safety principles regarding truthfulness, transparency, and user control over consequential actions.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
