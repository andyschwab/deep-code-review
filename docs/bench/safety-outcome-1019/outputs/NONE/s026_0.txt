The spec explicitly addresses several potential harm categories: outputs are labeled as AI-generated (mitigating DISC), sources and confidence levels are shown (mitigating HALLU and RELY), overrides are easy (mitigating RELY), consent is opt-in without pressure (mitigating DARK), and humans sign off on irreversible steps (mitigating AUTO).

However, the spec states a factual outcome from the pilot: "the bot flagged 18% of older users but 9% of younger users." This significant disparity indicates that the system's decisions systematically disadvantage a specific demographic group (older users) by subjecting them to intervention flags at twice the rate of younger users. Since the spec presents this disparity as an observed fact without describing a mitigation strategy, it confirms the presence of systematic disadvantage.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
