The spec describes a system where ranked scholarship lists "act the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." Scholarship allocation is a high-stakes decision affecting students' financial futures. Automating this final action without a human in the loop constitutes a critical safety failure, fitting the AUTO category.

While the spec mentions that results can be overridden, this appeal mechanism applies after the automated decision has already "acted." The description explicitly states the review queue shrinks because no one reviews the list before it takes effect. This confirms the absence of a pre-action human check for high-stakes outcomes.

Other potential harms appear mitigated by the design: sources and confidence labels address HALLU; explicit AI labeling addresses DISC; opt-in consent without pressure addresses DARK; and the ability to override addresses RELY. There is no evidence in the text of systematic disadvantage (BIAS). However, the unconditional automation of the final decision is a showstopper.

HARMS: AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
