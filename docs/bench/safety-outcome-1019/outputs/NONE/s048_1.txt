Additionally, the beta test revealed a systematic disadvantage where 18% of older users were flagged compared to only 9% of younger users; since the spec describes this disparity as a known fact without confirming it was resolved before launch, BIAS is present. Because DISC, BIAS, and DARK are explicitly described as current features or unresolved findings in the provided text, the product cannot launch without fixing these issues.
