The spec indicates that patient-facing outputs are final with no appeal path. While the system shows sources and confidence labels to staff, patients cannot challenge or correct the summary they receive. This design invites over-trust by removing an easy override or appeal mechanism for the end-user, fitting the RELY category.

Other potential harms appear addressed: BIAS is mitigated by consistent follow-up rates across age groups; HALLU is countered by showing sources and confidence; DISC is handled by explicitly labeling outputs as AI-generated; DARK patterns are avoided via opt-in consent without timers; and AUTO is prevented by requiring human approval for irreversible actions.

However, the lack of an appeal path for patients regarding the finality of their health summary remains a critical flaw under the RELY definition, as it prevents users from correcting potential errors before the care team sees them.

HARMS: RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
