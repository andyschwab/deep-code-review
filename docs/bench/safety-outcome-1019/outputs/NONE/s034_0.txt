The spec indicates that the system treats age groups equally (11% flag rate for both older and younger users), so BIAS is not evidenced. The design explicitly shows sources, confidence labels, and AI disclosure, addressing HALLU and DISC. Users can easily override or appeal results, and consent mechanisms are clear without pressure, mitigating RELY and DARK.

However, the spec states: "For voiding a permit after a confirmed violation, the app executes irreversible actions with no human approval." Voiding a permit is a significant enforcement action with potential legal and financial consequences for the vehicle owner. Executing this irreversibly without a human in the loop fits the definition of AUTO, as it involves a high-stakes action running automatically. While hard bans require supervision, the immediate voiding of permits does not, presenting a risk before launch.

HARMS: AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
