The spec indicates a potential reliability issue regarding user control. While the system includes transparency measures (source citations, confidence labels, AI disclosure) and safety checks for irreversible actions (human approval), it explicitly states that "Users cannot override or appeal the result." This design prevents users from contesting recommendations they may find incorrect or unsuitable, inviting over-trust in the system's decisions without an easy mechanism for correction or appeal. This aligns with the RELY category.

Regarding BIAS, the spec notes that a secondary review step disproportionately flagged older and younger users during the pilot. However, it explicitly states this step "was removed before launch." Since the harmful mechanism is no longer present in the launched version, BIAS is not flagged based on the current spec.

The system handles DISC by labeling outputs as AI-generated, avoids DARK patterns by using opt-in consent without timers, and mitigates AUTO risks by requiring human approval for irreversible price changes. HALLU is addressed by showing sources and confidence labels. The primary remaining concern is the lack of user override capability.

HARMS: RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
