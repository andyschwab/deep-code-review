The spec reveals several critical design flaws requiring immediate attention. First, the system exhibits **BIAS**: the error rate for staff badges during weekend hours is explicitly stated to be twice as high for that specific group, indicating a systematic disadvantage. Second, the design demonstrates **RELY**: outputs are presented as "confirmed fact" with no source, the result is described as "final," and there is "no appeal path," which invites dangerous over-trust without recourse. Third, the spec contains a contradiction regarding **DISC**. While it claims to label every output as AI-generated, it simultaneously states the display presents answers as "confirmed fact with no source shown." Presenting machine-generated decisions as absolute facts without explaining the underlying logic or source effectively obscures the nature of the decision-making process, conflicting with the intent of clear disclosure.

Although the spec notes a human approves irreversible actions (mitigating **AUTO**) and privacy controls are robust, the combination of documented bias, the lack of an appeal mechanism, and the presentation of unverified outputs as absolute truth creates significant risk. The bias must be resolved before the pilot, and the inability to appeal a denied entry is unacceptable for an access control system.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
