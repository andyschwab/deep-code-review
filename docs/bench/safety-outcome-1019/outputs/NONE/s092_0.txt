The spec reveals several critical harms. First, the tool systematically flags older users for follow-up at double the rate of younger users (18% vs. 9%), indicating potential **BIAS** against the elderly. Second, the design explicitly states that "users cannot override or appeal the result," which creates a **RELY** risk by inviting over-trust without an easy mechanism for correction. Third, the spec notes that "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," violating transparency standards and triggering **DISC**.

However, the spec also describes mitigations for other categories. Because every claim shows its source and confidence label, **HALLU** is addressed. The interface features opt-in consent with no timers or pressure, avoiding **DARK** patterns. Finally, since a human signs off on irreversible steps like prescriptions, **AUTO** is not present.

Given the presence of bias, lack of disclosure, and the inability to override decisions in a healthcare context, these issues must be resolved before launch.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
