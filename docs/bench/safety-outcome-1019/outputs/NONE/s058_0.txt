The spec describes a pricing engine with several safeguards: it cites sources, states confidence levels, labels outputs as AI-generated, requires opt-in consent for auto-negotiation, and mandates human approval for irreversible actions. These features explicitly address potential harms related to hallucination (HALLU), disclosure (DISC), dark patterns (DARK), and autonomous high-stakes actions (AUTO).

However, the spec states: "Once a quote is locked, the result is final and there is no appeal path." This design prevents users from challenging or correcting a decision even if they believe it is erroneous or unfair after locking. By removing any mechanism for review or override once a specific state is reached, the system invites over-trust in the initial output and removes user agency, fitting the definition of RELY (no easy override or appeal).

The spec mentions testing across groups with low error rates but does not provide evidence that systematic disadvantage against specific groups has been evaluated or ruled out, so BIAS cannot be confirmed as present based solely on the text provided. Similarly, since the system labels outputs as AI-generated, DISC is handled.

HARMS: RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
