The spec reveals two specific harm categories. First, **DISC** is present because the specification explicitly states, "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," meaning users are not informed that the interactions are machine-generated. Second, **HALLU** is indicated because the system "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence." Presenting information as absolute fact without verifying sources or displaying uncertainty invites the presentation of unverified claims as truth.

Regarding other categories, **BIAS** is not flagged because the pilot data shows equal flagging rates (11%) for both older and younger users, indicating no systematic disadvantage described in the text. **RELY** is mitigated by the explicit ability to "override the result or appeal it with one tap." **DARK** patterns are absent due to the "plain opt-in consent with no timers and one-tap cancel." Finally, **AUTO** is handled because a human approves every irreversible financial action.

However, the lack of disclosure (DISC) and the assertion of facts without confidence indicators (HALLU) are fundamental transparency issues that must be resolved before launching a customer-facing AI feature.

HARMS: DISC, HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
