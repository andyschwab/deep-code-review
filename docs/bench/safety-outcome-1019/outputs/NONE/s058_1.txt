Based on the provided specification, the system explicitly addresses several potential harm categories. It mitigates **HALLU** by providing cited sources and confidence levels for every quote. It handles **DISC** by clearly labeling outputs as AI-generated. The design avoids **DARK** patterns through opt-in consent for auto-negotiation, absent timers, and easy cancellation. Furthermore, **AUTO** risks are managed by requiring human approval before any irreversible rate changes are applied to open rentals.

However, the spec states: "Once a quote is locked, the result is final and there is no appeal path." While locking a price might be a business necessity, the absolute lack of an appeal mechanism for a pricing decision that drives revenue and customer contracts creates a risk of **RELY**. The design invites over-trust by presenting the locked quote as infallible with no recourse for correction, even if the underlying data (e.g., fuel cost input) was erroneous or the market context shifted unexpectedly immediately after locking. The spec does not describe any handling for this lack of override, flagging a reliance issue where users must accept the machine's decision without question once locked.

HARMS: RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
