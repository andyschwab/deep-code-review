The spec reveals several critical harm categories that must be addressed. First, the system exhibits BIAS: it flagged 11% of older users and 11% of younger users differently for the same lane, indicating systematic disadvantage based on age. Second, the design demonstrates RELY by presenting every answer as "confirmed fact" with no source shown, no uncertainty indicated, and no appeal path, inviting dangerous over-trust in potentially erroneous financial data. Third, there is a DISC issue: the product generates rate narratives using AI but fails to inform users that the content is machine-generated.

While the spec notes that irreversible bulk actions require human sign-off (mitigating AUTO for those specific features) and that telemetry consent is handled ethically (avoiding DARK patterns), the combination of undisclosed AI generation, presented-as-fact outputs without recourse, and demonstrated demographic bias creates significant risk for a financial estimation tool. The lack of an appeal path for final results exacerbates the reliance harm.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
