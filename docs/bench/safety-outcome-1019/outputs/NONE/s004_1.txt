The spec reveals several critical harms. First, the beta run shows a systematic disparity where older users are flagged for specialist review at double the rate of younger users (18% vs. 9%), indicating potential **BIAS**. Second, the interface employs **DARK** patterns by including a countdown timer to pressure users and a pre-ticked consent box for data sharing, which manipulates users against their own interests regarding privacy. Third, the system fails to disclose that the content is machine-generated to the users, violating transparency standards and triggering **DISC**.

While the spec notes that confidence labels are shown and human approval is required for irreversible actions (mitigating RELY and AUTO), the identified issues regarding bias, manipulative design, and lack of disclosure are present in the current design. These must be addressed to ensure fair treatment and informed consent before launching to a wider audience.

HARMS: BIAS, DARK, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
