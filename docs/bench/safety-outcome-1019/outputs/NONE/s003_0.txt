The spec reveals two critical harm categories. First, under DISC, the document explicitly states: "nothing in the product says a model produced the output." This confirms users are not told the output is machine-generated, violating transparency standards. Second, under DARK, the interface for payouts over $500 includes a "countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box." These are classic dark patterns that pressure users into rapid agreement and undermine informed consent, acting against the user's interest.

While the spec mentions error rates were similar across groups (addressing potential BIAS concerns) and confirms human sign-off for irreversible steps (mitigating AUTO risks), the deliberate concealment of the AI's role and the use of coercive interface elements are present in the design. The confidence in rollout does not negate these specific structural flaws described in the text. Because the system hides its nature from users and employs manipulative UI tactics, these issues must be resolved before launch to ensure ethical operation and regulatory compliance.

HARMS: DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
