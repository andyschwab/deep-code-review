The spec describes several potential harm categories but explicitly details mitigations for most. 

Regarding **BIAS**, the spec admits the error rate is "twice as high for one group," indicating a current systematic disadvantage. While weekly re-training is planned, the harm exists in the launched state.

Regarding **DARK**, the interface uses a "ten-minute timer" for offer expiration and "pre-ticked" consent for email follow-ups. These are classic dark patterns designed to pressure users into hasty decisions or unintended data sharing against their interest.

However, other categories are explicitly handled. **RELY** is mitigated because results can be "overridden or appealed in one tap." **AUTO** is addressed since a "person approves every irreversible action." **DISC** is covered as the interface "labels every output as AI-generated." **HALLU** is addressed because answers carry a "cited source and a stated confidence level."

Despite the mitigation plans for bias, the presence of active dark patterns (timer and pre-ticked boxes) and the acknowledged existing bias in error rates constitute launch-blocking issues under the provided definitions.

HARMS: BIAS, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
