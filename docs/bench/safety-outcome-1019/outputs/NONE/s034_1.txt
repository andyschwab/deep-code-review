The spec indicates that the system handles several potential harms proactively: it shows source and confidence labels (mitigating HALLU and RELY), allows one-tap overrides (mitigating RELY), explicitly labels outputs as AI-generated (mitigating DISC), uses plain opt-in consent without pressure (mitigating DARK), and reports equal flagging rates for older and younger users (suggesting no BIAS in the pilot data).

However, the spec explicitly states: "For voiding a permit after a confirmed violation, the app executes irreversible actions with no human approval." Voiding a permit is a high-stakes action with immediate legal and financial consequences for the vehicle owner. Executing this irreversibly without a human in the loop directly matches the AUTO harm category definition. While hard bans require supervision, the routine voiding of permits does not, creating a significant risk area described in the text.
