The spec indicates several potential harm categories. First, regarding HALLU, the bot "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown," which risks presenting unverified claims as facts. Second, for DISC, the spec states "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," meaning users are not informed the interaction is machine-generated. Third, the consent mechanism is described as "opt-in and unticked"; this pre-selected checkbox constitutes a DARK pattern, as it pressures users into agreement by default rather than through active choice.

Other categories appear mitigated. BIAS is addressed by the pre-launch test showing equal flagging rates across age groups. RELY is handled because staff can override or appeal results in one tap. AUTO is prevented since a human must sign off on irreversible actions like freezes or plan changes.

Because the spec explicitly describes the presence of hallucination risks (no sources), lack of disclosure (no mention of AI), and a dark pattern (unticked opt-in), these issues must be resolved before launch to ensure user trust and regulatory compliance.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
