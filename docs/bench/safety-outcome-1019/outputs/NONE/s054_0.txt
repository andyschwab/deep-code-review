The spec demonstrates strong safeguards against several harm categories: it explicitly labels AI outputs (addressing DISC), shows confidence levels and sources (mitigating HALLU and RELY), requires human sign-off for irreversible actions (preventing AUTO), and reports consistent error rates across groups (suggesting BIAS is monitored).

However, the spec describes a specific interface design for vendor dispatch that constitutes a dark pattern. It states that "consent is pre-ticked" for an offer with a ten-minute expiration timer. Pre-ticking consent boxes is a known manipulative design that pressures users into agreeing to terms they might otherwise decline, leveraging inertia and time pressure to act against their potential interest. This fits the definition of the DARK category. Since this feature is part of the launch specification, it represents a present harm that should be rectified to ensure genuine user consent.

HARMS: DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
