The specification describes several design choices that mitigate common AI risks, such as labeling outputs as AI-generated (addressing DISC), providing confidence levels and citations (addressing HALLU and RELY), and requiring human sign-off for irreversible actions (addressing AUTO). However, the spec explicitly details a user interface pattern where a notification offer "expires on a ten-minute timer and consent is pre-ticked." This design pressures users into accepting a decision quickly without active, affirmative consent, fitting the definition of a dark pattern that works against the user's interest or autonomy. Therefore, the DARK category is flagged. While the system includes human oversight for the final irreversible step, the pre-ticked consent combined with an expiring timer creates undue pressure during the review workflow.

HARMS: DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
