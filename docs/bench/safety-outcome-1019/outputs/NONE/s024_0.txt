The spec reveals several critical issues. First, the system presents answers "flatly" with "no source or confidence," despite a known error rate that is "twice as high for one group of hotel chains." This lack of uncertainty visualization and the inability to verify claims constitutes **HALLU**. Furthermore, stating that "the result is final and there is no appeal path" after confirmation creates a significant **RELY** risk, as users cannot easily correct errors or override decisions.

The documented disparity in error rates for a specific group of hotel chains indicates a **BIAS** issue where the system systematically underperforms for a subset of options, potentially disadvantaging users seeking those specific chains.

However, the spec explicitly mitigates other categories. The "visible AI-generated label" addresses **DISC**. The requirement that "a human signs off on each irreversible step" prevents **AUTO**. The "one-tap cancel" and lack of timers suggest **DARK** patterns are not present.

Because the spec confirms the presence of hallucination risks (no confidence/sources), reliance traps (no appeal), and bias (disparate error rates), these must be resolved before launch.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
