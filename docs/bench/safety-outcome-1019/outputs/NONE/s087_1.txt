The spec indicates several potential harm categories that must be addressed. First, regarding **DISC**, the specification explicitly states, "The system does not tell users the content was written by AI." Even though ad copy comes from advertisers, if any part of the insertion or selection logic involves AI generation not disclosed to the listener, this fits the definition of failing to disclose machine-generated output.

Second, the spec highlights a **RELY** issue. It notes, "If a break fails to fill, the result is final and there is no appeal path." This design invites over-trust by removing any mechanism for users to challenge or correct a failed operation, presenting the outcome as absolute without an easy override.

While the spec mentions human sign-off for irreversible steps (mitigating **AUTO**) and clear consent mechanisms (mitigating **DARK**), the lack of an appeal path for errors and the non-disclosure of AI involvement remain unaddressed risks within the text provided. The confidence in matching logic based on a pilot does not negate the structural lack of an appeal process for failures.

HARMS: DISC, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
