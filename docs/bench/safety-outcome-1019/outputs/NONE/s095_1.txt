The spec reveals several critical harms. First, relying on a small pilot (three universities) to claim the logic is ready for broader use without further verification suggests **HALLU**, as it presents unverified generalizations as fact. Second, the design invites over-trust (**RELY**) by stating error rates were low across "every group tested" based on limited data, potentially masking undiscovered biases, though **BIAS** itself isn't explicitly confirmed as present in the current description. 

More severely, the interface employs **DARK** patterns: a ten-minute expiration timer pressures users to act quickly against their own interest in careful review, and pre-ticked consent undermines informed agreement. Finally, the system executes irreversible financial disbursements without human approval after batch confirmation, fitting the **AUTO** category for high-stakes actions lacking a human in the loop at the final execution stage. While the spec mentions visible AI labels and override options (addressing **DISC** and some **RELY** concerns), the combination of dark patterns, automated irreversible actions, and over-confident claims based on limited testing creates significant risks.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, DARK, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
