Based on the provided specification, two harm categories are present. First, **HALLU** is flagged because the spec explicitly states the engine "shows no source or confidence" and presents answers "flatly" as absolute facts (e.g., "This tutor fits") without indicating uncertainty or providing reasoning. Second, **DISC** is flagged because the spec admits the product "does not tell users the content was written by AI," failing to disclose the machine-generated nature of the recommendations.

Other categories are not flagged because the spec describes mitigations: **RELY** is addressed by the "one tap" override and appeal mechanism; **AUTO** is handled by requiring human sign-off for irreversible scheduling steps; **DARK** is avoided via plain opt-in consent without pressure tactics; and **BIAS** is not indicated as the pilot showed consistent error rates across groups.

However, the presence of undisclosed AI generation and the presentation of unverified matches as absolute facts constitutes a significant transparency and accuracy issue that must be resolved before launch.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
