The spec explicitly describes several critical issues. First, the interface presents answers as confirmed facts without sources, and users cannot override or appeal results even if they believe data is wrong; this invites over-trust and lacks an easy override, flagging **RELY**. Second, the design uses a ten-minute expiration timer and pre-ticked consent boxes to pressure users into auto-refill enrollment, which constitutes **DARK** patterns. While the spec notes a visible "AI-generated" label (addressing **DISC**) and requires human approval for irreversible actions (addressing **AUTO**), the identified reliance and dark pattern issues remain present in the described design. The claim about error rates being "within one point" is presented as a factual result from a simulation, but without context on what that metric means, it borders on **HALLU**, though the primary clear violations are reliance and dark patterns.

HARMS: RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
