The spec reveals several critical issues. First, the model flags 18% of older users versus 9% of younger users for check-ins, indicating systematic disadvantage based on age (BIAS). Second, the auto-renewal flow uses a pre-ticked consent box and a countdown timer, which are interface patterns designed to pressure users into agreements against their potential interest (DARK). Third, this same auto-renewal process executes an irreversible financial action without human approval (AUTO).

While the spec notes that other outputs include confidence labels, override options, and AI disclosure (addressing RELY and DISC for those specific features), the identified harms in the auto-renewal flow and the demographic disparity in flagging are explicitly present in the design. The pilot gating does not mitigate the inherent harm of the design patterns themselves.

HARMS: BIAS, DARK, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
