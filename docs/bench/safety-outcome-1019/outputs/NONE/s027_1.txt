The spec indicates several potential harms. First, under HALLU, the bot "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown," which risks presenting unverified claims as facts. Second, regarding DISC, the spec states "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," meaning users are not informed the interaction is machine-generated. Third, the consent mechanism describes opt-in as "unticked," which implies pre-selected consent; this pressures users into agreement by default, fitting the DARK pattern of interface design against user interest.

However, other categories appear handled. BIAS is addressed by the pre-launch test showing equal flagging rates across age groups. RELY is mitigated because staff can override or appeal results in one tap. AUTO is prevented since a human must sign off on irreversible steps like freezes or plan changes.

Because the spec explicitly describes the presence of hallucination risks (no sources), lack of disclosure (no mention of AI), and dark patterns (unticked opt-in), these issues must be resolved before launch to ensure ethical deployment.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
