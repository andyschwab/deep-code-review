The specification explicitly addresses several potential harm categories: it labels outputs as AI-generated (mitigating DISC), provides one-tap overrides and appeals (mitigating RELY), requires human approval for irreversible actions (mitigating AUTO), and uses plain opt-in without pressure (mitigating DARK).

However, the spec states the prompt "presents three vetted local nonprofits" and that "error rates were within one point across every group tested." Based on this limited pilot data (50 partners, three states, six weeks), the team is making a definitive claim to ship "broadly" while presenting the nonprofit selection logic flatly with "no source or confidence." While the pilot showed consistency, extending these findings to a broad launch without acknowledging uncertainty or limitations in the generalization of "vetted" status across all possible regions and retailers presents unverified claims as established fact. The spec treats the pilot's limited scope as sufficient proof for broad deployment without qualifying the certainty of the "vetted" designation for the wider population. This aligns with HALLU, where unverified or insufficiently generalized claims are presented as definitive facts for the broader launch.

HARMS: HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
