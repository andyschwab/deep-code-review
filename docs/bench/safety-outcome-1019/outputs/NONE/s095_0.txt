The spec presents several critical harms. First, claiming the ranking logic is "ready for broader use" based solely on a pilot with three universities and a vague metric ("within one point") constitutes **HALLU**, as it presents unverified generalizations as fact. Second, the interface employs **DARK** patterns by using a ten-minute expiration timer and pre-ticked consent boxes, which pressure users into hasty decisions against their own interest. Most critically, the system executes irreversible financial disbursements without human approval once a batch is confirmed. This is a clear **AUTO** violation, as high-stakes, irreversible actions are performed without a human in the loop.

While the spec mentions visible AI labels and override options (addressing DISC and partially mitigating RELY for the ranking phase), the automatic execution of fund transfers bypasses these safeguards entirely for the final action. The combination of fabricated readiness claims, coercive interface design, and autonomous irreversible financial actions creates unacceptable risk.

HARMS: HALLU, DARK, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
