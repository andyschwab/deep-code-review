The spec indicates two critical harm categories. First, under HALLU, the system "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown." Presenting probabilistic forecasts as absolute facts without citing sources constitutes presenting unverified claims as fact. Second, under AUTO, the spec states, "The system acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." Since this system dispatches crews and determines truck routes for city infrastructure, it qualifies as a high-stakes action running without a human in the loop.

While the spec mentions users can override results (mitigating RELY) and includes visible AI labels (mitigating DISC), the combination of presenting outputs as absolute fact and executing actions without prior human review creates significant operational risk. The claim that error rates were "within one point across every group tested" is presented as a justification for removing uncertainty indicators, but this does not negate the harm of presenting forecasts as confirmed facts in the live interface.

HARMS: HALLU, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
