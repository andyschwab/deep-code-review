The spec reveals two specific harm categories that are present and not adequately mitigated.

First, **BIAS** is explicitly described: the testing phase flagged that 11% of older users and 11% of younger users were affected by consistency issues. While the system now provides explanations, the spec confirms the model systematically disadvantages these age groups, satisfying the definition of bias.

Second, **DARK** patterns are present in the enrollment flow. The specification states that users encounter a "pre-ticked consent box" for storing entry logs. Pre-ticked boxes are a classic dark pattern that pressures users into agreeing to data storage they might otherwise decline, acting against their interest by assuming consent rather than obtaining it actively.

Other categories appear handled: **DISC** is addressed via the visible AI-generated label; **RELY** is mitigated by shown confidence levels, sources, and easy override/appeal mechanisms; **AUTO** is prevented by the requirement for human sign-off on irreversible steps; and **HALLU** is countered by the requirement for cited sources.

However, because the spec confirms the existence of unmitigated bias against specific age groups and utilizes manipulative consent interfaces, these issues must be resolved before launch.

HARMS: BIAS, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
