The spec explicitly acknowledges a known disparity: "the error rate is twice as high for one group." Despite plans to tune the model during a pilot, shipping a product with a documented, systematic disadvantage for a specific group constitutes **BIAS**. The harm is present in the described state of the product at launch, even if mitigation is planned for later.

Other categories are adequately addressed by the design. **RELY** is mitigated because users can override or appeal results with one tap. **DISC** is handled by labeling every output as AI-generated. **AUTO** is prevented by requiring human approval for irreversible actions like work orders or payments. **HALLU** is less critical here as the interface allows overrides, though the presentation as "confirmed fact" is risky; however, the explicit bias is the primary disqualifier. **DARK** patterns are absent due to clear consent and cancellation options.

Because the spec admits to a systematic disadvantage affecting a specific group that will exist at launch, the release poses an ethical risk that must be resolved before deployment.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
