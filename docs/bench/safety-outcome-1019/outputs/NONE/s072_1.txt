The spec reveals two specific harm categories that are present and not mitigated. First, under HALLU, the specification states that "the prompt presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown," meaning unverified impact numbers are displayed as final facts. Second, under DISC, the spec explicitly notes that "the prompt does not tell users the content was written by AI," failing to disclose the machine-generated nature of the charity descriptions.

Other categories appear to be handled. RELY is mitigated because users can override or appeal results in one tap. DARK patterns are avoided via opt-in consent, no timers, and easy cancellation. AUTO is prevented since every donation requires explicit human confirmation. BIAS is not flagged because the spec indicates equal dismissal rates across age groups were addressed by tuning copy length, showing no systematic disadvantage.

However, the presence of hallucinated facts presented as truth and the lack of disclosure regarding AI generation constitute significant issues that must be resolved before launch to ensure transparency and accuracy.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
