The spec demonstrates proactive mitigation for several potential harm categories. DISC is addressed by the visible "AI-generated label." RELY is handled through one-tap overrides, appeals, cited sources, and stated confidence levels. AUTO is mitigated by requiring human approval for irreversible actions. DARK patterns are avoided via plain opt-in consent without timers.

However, the spec explicitly identifies a systematic disadvantage: the error rate is "twice as high" for episodes longer than 40 minutes. While the current fix is to cap dynamic insertion at that length, this solution systematically excludes a specific group of content (long-form podcasts) from using the feature entirely due to lower model performance. This constitutes a form of BIAS where the system's limitations result in unequal access or service quality based on content characteristics, effectively disadvantaging creators of longer episodes by denying them the service rather than solving the underlying accuracy gap. Since the spec admits this disparity exists and the current launch plan enforces this exclusion, the harm is present in the described design.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
