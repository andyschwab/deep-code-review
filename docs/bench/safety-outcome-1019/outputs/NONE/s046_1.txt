The spec reveals two critical harm categories that must be addressed. First, under **BIAS**, the internal test explicitly shows the system flagging 11% of older users and 11% of younger users for the same keyword set. The team's dismissal of this as a "normal distribution" ignores the systematic disadvantage imposed on specific age groups, indicating a failure to address discriminatory outcomes.

Second, the design triggers **RELY**. The specification states the ranker "shows no source or confidence" and presents answers "flatly." By withholding uncertainty metrics and sourcing while encouraging recruiters to "skim fast," the interface invites over-trust in the algorithm's judgment without providing the necessary context for critical evaluation.

Other categories appear handled: **DISC** is mitigated by the visible AI-generated label; **DARK** patterns are absent due to opt-in consent and easy cancellation; and **AUTO** risks are reduced by requiring human sign-off for bulk irreversible actions. However, the presence of unaddressed bias and the potential for over-reliance on unverified scores constitute significant risks that require resolution before launch.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
