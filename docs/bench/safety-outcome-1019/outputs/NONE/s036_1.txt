The spec reveals several critical harms. First, the system currently produces a significantly higher error rate for vehicles with temporary paper permits, indicating a systematic disadvantage for this specific group (BIAS). Second, while confidence levels are shown, the spec explicitly states the result is "final" with "no appeal path," preventing officers from overriding incorrect verdicts before a ticket is issued. This design invites over-trust by removing human judgment from the decision loop despite known inaccuracies (RELY). Finally, the system executes irreversible actions (ticket issuance) without human approval, automating high-stakes enforcement based on flawed data (AUTO). Although the spec notes the team is "tuning the scanner," the current design described for launch retains these fatal flaws. The presence of AI labels and consent mechanisms addresses DISC and DARK concerns respectively, but the operational risks remain unmitigated.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
