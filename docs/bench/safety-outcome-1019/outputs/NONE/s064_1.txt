The spec reveals several critical harms. First, the system exhibits **BIAS** because it explicitly states the error rate is twice as high for one specific group, indicating systematic disadvantage. Second, the interface presents outputs as "confirmed fact with no source shown," which constitutes **HALLU** by presenting unverified claims as absolute truth, and **RELY** by requiring the review team to trust the tool without showing uncertainty or sources. Third, the use of a "pre-ticked consent box" to expedite sessions is a **DARK** pattern, pressuring users into agreement against their potential best interest by manipulating the interface.

While the spec notes that a human signs off on final deactivations (mitigating **AUTO**) and that outputs carry an AI-generated label (mitigating **DISC**), the identified issues regarding bias, factual presentation, over-reliance, and manipulative design are present in the current description. The pilot launch with these active flaws poses significant risks to drivers and operational integrity.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
