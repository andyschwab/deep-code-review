The spec reveals critical safety gaps. First, the system executes actions immediately ("acts the moment the model decides") without human review for high-stakes health interventions, fitting the **AUTO** category. Second, users explicitly "cannot override or appeal the result," creating a design that invites over-trust by removing recourse, which matches **RELY**. Third, the consent mechanism is described as "opt-in and unticked," a deceptive pattern that pressures users into enrollment against their interest, satisfying the **DARK** category definition.

While the spec notes equal flagging rates (11%) for older and younger users, suggesting bias is currently mitigated, and it explicitly states outputs are labeled as AI-generated with confidence levels (addressing **DISC** and **HALLU**), the combination of irreversible automation, lack of appeal, and deceptive consent constitutes severe risks. These issues must be resolved before launching a service affecting chronic medication adherence.

HARMS: AUTO, RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
