The spec describes several safeguards: outputs include cited sources and confidence levels (mitigating HALLU and RELY), every result is labeled as AI-generated (mitigating DISC), users can override or appeal in one tap (mitigating RELY and AUTO concerns regarding irreversibility), and signup uses plain opt-in without pressure (mitigating DARK). Additionally, beta data shows equal flagging rates for older and younger users (11% each), suggesting no current evidence of systematic disadvantage (mitigating BIAS).

However, the spec explicitly states: "The assistant acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." While low-stakes rebooking might seem benign, flight changes can have significant financial or logistical consequences (e.g., missing connections, visa issues, or unwanted layovers). The system executes these changes automatically without a human confirming the specific selection before it happens. Although an override exists post-action, the initial execution is fully automated for a decision that alters a user's travel itinerary immediately. This fits the AUTO category definition where a potentially high-stakes action runs without a human in the loop prior to execution. The presence of an appeal mechanism does not negate the fact that the action occurs automatically first.

HARMS: AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
