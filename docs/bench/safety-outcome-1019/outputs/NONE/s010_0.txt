The spec indicates a **RELY** harm. While the system provides sources and confidence labels, it explicitly states that "Users cannot override or appeal the result," forcing a binary yes-or-no conclusion without recourse if the recommendation is incorrect or unwanted. This design invites over-trust by removing user agency in the decision loop.

Other categories are addressed in the spec: **BIAS** is mitigated (the secondary review flagging age groups was removed); **HALLU** is countered (claims show sources); **DISC** is handled (outputs are labeled AI-generated); **DARK** patterns are avoided (opt-in consent, no timers, easy cancel); and **AUTO** risks are managed (human approval required for irreversible price changes). Only the lack of an override mechanism remains a critical flaw.

HARMS: RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
