The spec explicitly reveals several critical issues. First, the beta test showed the system flagged older users at double the rate of younger users (18% vs. 9%), indicating systematic disadvantage based on age. This confirms **BIAS**.

Second, the spec states, "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," meaning users are unaware the rankings are machine-generated. This is a clear violation of transparency, flagging **DISC**.

Third, the onboarding flow uses a "countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box." These are classic dark patterns designed to rush users and assume consent rather than obtaining it freely, which constitutes **DARK**.

While the spec mentions an audit trail, override capabilities, and human approval for irreversible actions (mitigating RELY and AUTO), the identified harms regarding bias, disclosure, and manipulative interface design are present and unresolved in the current description.

HARMS: BIAS, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
