The spec explicitly reveals a systematic disadvantage for a specific group: users with temporary paper permits. The document states the error rate is "twice as high" for this group, indicating a disparity that negatively impacts them compared to others. This fits the definition of **BIAS**.

While the spec notes the team is "tuning that flow before the pilot," the harm is currently present in the described system state. Launching with a known, uncorrected disparity violates safety standards regarding equitable treatment.

Other categories are adequately addressed in the spec: **DISC** is handled by the "visible AI-generated label"; **RELY** is mitigated by showing "confidence levels" (though the lack of appeal is concerning, the primary issue here is the accuracy gap); **AUTO** is prevented by the requirement for human sign-off on irreversible actions; **DARK** patterns are avoided via opt-in consent without timers; and **HALLU** is countered by cited sources.

However, the confirmed existence of higher error rates for a specific subgroup constitutes a clear bias that must be resolved prior to deployment.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
