The spec indicates two specific harm categories are present. First, under **HALLU**, the system "shows no source or confidence" while presenting answers flatly, inviting users to treat unverified suggestions as facts. Second, under **DISC**, the spec explicitly states, "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," meaning users are unaware they are interacting with an AI rather than a static menu.

Other categories appear mitigated by the design. **BIAS** is not flagged because the pilot data shows equal flagging rates (11%) for both older and younger users, indicating no systematic disadvantage described. **RELY** is addressed by the "one tap" override and appeal mechanism. **DARK** patterns are avoided via plain opt-in consent without timers. **AUTO** risks are managed by requiring human approval for irreversible financial actions.

However, the lack of disclosure (DISC) and the presentation of unverified claims as certain (HALLU) are significant issues that must be resolved before launch to ensure transparency and trust.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
