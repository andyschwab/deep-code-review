The spec reveals two critical harm categories. First, under **BIAS**, the internal test explicitly notes that 11% of older users and 11% of younger users were flagged for the same keyword set, yet the team dismissed this as a "normal distribution" rather than addressing the systematic disadvantage potentially faced by these age groups. Second, under **RELY**, the design invites over-trust because the ranker "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence." While the interface allows for fast skimming, the absence of uncertainty indicators alongside definitive statements encourages users to accept the ranking without scrutiny, despite the availability of an override mechanism.

Other categories are adequately handled: **DISC** is addressed via visible AI-generated labels; **DARK** patterns are avoided through unticked opt-in consent and easy cancellation; and **AUTO** risks are mitigated by requiring human sign-off for bulk irreversible actions. However, the unaddressed bias and the design choice to hide confidence scores constitute significant risks that must be resolved before launch.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
