The spec indicates a potential bias issue where the system flags 11% of older users and 11% of younger users for review due to time-off requests overlapping with critical shifts. While the percentages are equal, the mechanism systematically disadvantages specific age groups based on their requests, fitting the BIAS definition if the criteria for "critical shifts" disproportionately impact these demographics without justification.

More critically, the spec states: "If the system determines a swap would break minimum staffing, users cannot override or appeal the result." This design invites over-trust by removing human agency and providing no easy override or appeal mechanism for decisions that directly affect workers' schedules. This clearly falls under the RELY category.

However, the spec explicitly addresses several other potential harms. Since the RELY issue (no override/appeal for staffing decisions) and the potential BIAS (systematic flagging of age groups) are described as present features rather than handled limitations, they represent unresolved harms.
