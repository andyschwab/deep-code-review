The specification for RefillLoop demonstrates robust safeguards against the defined harm categories. Disclosure is handled via visible "AI-generated" labels on outputs, addressing DISC. Over-trust (RELY) is mitigated by displaying confidence levels, citing sources, and providing one-tap overrides or appeals. The design explicitly prevents automatic high-stakes actions (AUTO) by requiring human sign-off before any irreversible order placement. Dark patterns (DARK) are avoided through unticked opt-in consent, absence of timers, and easy cancellation.

Regarding BIAS, the spec notes that 11% of older users and 11% of younger users were flagged for possible duplicate prescriptions during the pilot. Since the flagging rate is identical across these age groups, the spec does not describe a systematic disadvantage based on age; rather, it suggests consistent detection rates. There is no evidence in the text of unverified claims presented as fact (HALLU), as dosage adjustments include cited sources and confidence levels. As the spec describes mechanisms that handle these potential harms rather than exhibiting them, no categories are flagged.

HARMS: NONE
DECISION: PROCEED
