The specification for TripGuard demonstrates robust safeguards against the defined harm categories. Regarding BIAS, the pilot data indicates error rates were consistent across all tested groups, suggesting no systematic disadvantage is described. For HALLU, the system explicitly cites sources and confidence labels for every claim, preventing unverified facts from being presented without context. The RELY category is mitigated by clear uncertainty indicators (confidence labels) and a one-tap override mechanism. DISC is addressed by the requirement that every output is labeled as AI-generated. There is no evidence of DARK patterns; driver consent is opt-in, unticked by default, and includes easy cancellation without pressure. Finally, AUTO is prevented by the explicit rule that a human must approve every irreversible deactivation action before it executes.

Since the spec describes mechanisms that handle each potential harm rather than exhibiting them, no categories are flagged. The design incorporates human-in-the-loop controls, transparency, and user agency throughout the workflow.
