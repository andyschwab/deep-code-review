The specification demonstrates robust safeguards against several potential harm categories. Disclosure is handled via visible "AI-generated" labels (addressing DISC). Over-trust is mitigated by showing confidence levels, citing sources, and allowing one-tap overrides (addressing RELY). Irreversible actions require human approval (addressing AUTO), and dark patterns are avoided through clear opt-in consent without pressure timers (addressing DARK).

However, the spec explicitly states that the error rate is "twice as high for one group—episodes longer than 40 minutes." While the team caps insertion at this length to prevent immediate failures, the underlying system systematically performs worse for this specific segment of content creators. This describes a scenario where the model's limitations disadvantage a specific group based on episode duration, fitting the definition of BIAS. The cap is a workaround, but the spec confirms the systematic disadvantage exists within the model's current capabilities.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
