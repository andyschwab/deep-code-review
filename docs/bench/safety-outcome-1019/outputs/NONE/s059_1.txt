The spec explicitly addresses several potential harm categories: it labels outputs as AI-generated (mitigating DISC), provides confidence levels and sources (mitigating HALLU and RELY), allows overrides and requires human approval for irreversible actions (mitigating RELY and AUTO), and retrains the model to address higher error rates for a specific group (actively mitigating BIAS).

However, the spec describes two features that constitute DARK patterns. First, the "ten-minute timer" creates artificial urgency to pressure users into quick decisions, potentially against their best interest. Second, "consent is pre-ticked for email follow-up," which manipulates users into agreeing to communications they might not otherwise choose. These interface designs actively pressure users against their own interests.

HARMS: DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
