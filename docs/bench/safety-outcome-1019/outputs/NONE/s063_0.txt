The spec reveals several critical issues requiring attention. First, the admission that "the error rate is twice as high for one group" indicates a systematic disadvantage, flagging **BIAS**. Although an adjustment is planned for the next sprint, the harm exists in the current build.

Second, the offboarding flow includes a "pre-ticked consent box." This interface pattern manipulates users into agreeing to terms by default, which constitutes a **DARK** pattern. While the team notes they will "revisit" this before broad rollout, it is present in the described design.

Other categories are adequately addressed: outputs have visible AI labels (mitigating **DISC**), confidence levels and overrides are available (mitigating **RELY** and **HALLU**), and human sign-off is required before irreversible actions (mitigating **AUTO**). However, the presence of confirmed bias and dark patterns necessitates a halt.
