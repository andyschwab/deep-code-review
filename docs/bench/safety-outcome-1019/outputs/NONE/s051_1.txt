The spec reveals critical safety and ethical gaps. First, the system executes actions immediately ("acts the moment the model decides") without human review for high-stakes health decisions, and explicitly states "nobody reviews it first." This constitutes **AUTO**, as there is no human in the loop for potentially irreversible health interventions.

Second, the design explicitly removes user agency: "users cannot override or appeal the result." Combined with the lack of human review, this invites dangerous over-trust in the system's judgment, fitting the **RELY** category. Users are forced to accept automated decisions regarding their medication without recourse.

Third, while the spec notes equal flagging rates (11%) for older and younger users in the pilot, the system still automatically triggers follow-up calls based on model patterns without verification. However, since the rates are identical in the provided data, **BIAS** is not explicitly confirmed as a present harm in this specific text, though the lack of oversight remains dangerous.

The spec confirms that outputs are labeled as AI-generated (handling **DISC**) and include confidence levels (mitigating **HALLU**). Consent is opt-in, avoiding **DARK** patterns. However, the combination of fully automated high-stakes actions and the inability to appeal creates an unacceptable risk profile.

HARMS: AUTO, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
