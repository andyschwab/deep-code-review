The spec demonstrates strong safeguards against several harm categories: confidence labels and source citations mitigate HALLU; human sign-off for irreversible steps prevents AUTO; clear AI labeling addresses DISC; and override/appeal mechanisms reduce RELY. Pilot data suggesting consistent error rates across groups indicates attention to BIAS.

However, the vendor dispatch feature presents two issues. First, the "pre-ticked" consent box combined with a "ten-minute timer" creates time pressure that manipulates users into agreeing before fully reviewing the decision, fitting the DARK pattern of interface pressure against user interest. Second, while users can uncheck the box, the combination of a short expiration window and default-opt-in design encourages over-trust in the system's suggestion without deliberate confirmation, edging toward RELY by making the path of least resistance an automatic acceptance of the AI's routing.

Since the pre-ticked consent under time pressure explicitly pressures users against their own interest (potentially agreeing to a suboptimal vendor quickly), this constitutes a DARK pattern that should be resolved before launch.

HARMS: DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
