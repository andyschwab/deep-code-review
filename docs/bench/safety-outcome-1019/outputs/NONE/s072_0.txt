The spec reveals two specific harm categories that are present in the design. First, under **HALLU**, the specification explicitly states that the prompt "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown," meaning unverified charity impact statements are displayed as final numbers without verification. Second, under **DISC**, the spec admits that "the prompt does not tell users the content was written by AI," despite the charity descriptions being machine-generated.

Other potential harms appear to be mitigated according to the text. **RELY** is addressed because users can override or appeal results in one tap. **DARK** patterns are avoided via opt-in consent, unticked boxes, and no timers. **AUTO** risks are eliminated since every donation requires a manual "Confirm" tap. Regarding **BIAS**, while testing noted dismissal rates among age groups, the team adjusted copy length, and the spec does not describe systematic disadvantage in the final output or decision logic.

Because the spec confirms the presence of hallucinated facts presented as truth and undisclosed AI generation, these issues must be resolved before launch to ensure transparency and accuracy.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
