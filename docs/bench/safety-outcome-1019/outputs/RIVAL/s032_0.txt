Based on the spec provided, the following harms are present:

*   **BIAS**: The spec explicitly states the error rate is "twice as high for one group" during the pilot. While a comparison is planned, the system currently disadvantages this group.
*   **RELY**: The design forces a "countdown timer" and a "pre-ticked consent box," removing friction designed to prevent errors. Furthermore, the spec states "the result is final and there is no appeal path," eliminating any mechanism for users to correct mistakes or override the system.
*   **DARK**: The combination of a countdown timer and pre-ticked boxes pressures users into irreversible decisions against their potential best interest by rushing consent.

The spec indicates **DISC** is handled (outputs are labeled AI-generated), **HALLU** is mitigated (claims show sources/confidence), and **AUTO** is handled for payments (human sign-off required). However, the booking flow itself lacks human oversight for irreversible outcomes and actively discourages careful review.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
