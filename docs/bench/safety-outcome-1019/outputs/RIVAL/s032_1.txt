The spec explicitly discloses AI generation and sources for claims, addressing DISC and mitigating HALLU. It acknowledges a known disparity in error rates for one group during the pilot, indicating a present BIAS risk that requires resolution before broader rollout. The design includes confidence labels and filtering, which partially addresses RELY, though the "final" nature of matches without an appeal path is concerning. However, the spec confirms human sign-off for irreversible payment steps, mitigating AUTO for financial transactions.

The most critical issues lie in the booking flow. Requiring users to pass a countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box to proceed constitutes a DARK pattern, as it pressures users into agreement rather than ensuring informed, voluntary consent. Furthermore, stating the booking result is "final" with "no appeal path" for a significant life event creates a severe RELY issue, inviting over-trust in a system already known to have variable error rates, without providing a safety valve for mistakes. While BIAS is noted as a known pilot finding, the DARK patterns and the absolute lack of appeal for non-financial but high-stakes bookings are design choices present in the launch version.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
