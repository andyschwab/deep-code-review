The spec explicitly describes several present harms. First, **BIAS** is evident as the system flagged different user age groups differently for identical lanes, indicating systematic disadvantage based on age. Second, **HALLU** occurs because the UI presents answers as "confirmed fact" with no sources, despite being estimates based on adjusted data. Third, **RELY** is triggered by the design offering no uncertainty indicators, no source visibility, and no appeal path for final results. Fourth, **DISC** is confirmed since the product generates AI content but fails to inform users it is machine-generated.

The spec indicates **DARK** patterns are not present; telemetry is opt-in and unticked by default, and cancellation is easy. Similarly, **AUTO** harms are mitigated because the spec requires human sign-off for irreversible bulk actions before execution. However, the combination of biased outputs, fabricated certainty, lack of disclosure, and inability to appeal creates significant risk for a financial tool.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
