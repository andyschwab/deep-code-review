The spec explicitly addresses several potential harm categories: DISC is handled via visible "AI-generated" labels; RELY is mitigated by showing sources, confidence labels, and one-tap overrides/appeals; DARK patterns are avoided through unticked opt-in consent and no timers; and AUTO is prevented by requiring human sign-off for irreversible steps.

However, the spec provides data indicating a potential BIAS issue: "In the pilot's first month, the bot flagged 18% of older users but 9% of younger users." This significant disparity in flagging rates between age groups suggests the system may be systematically disadvantaging older users, perhaps by subjecting them to more frequent interventions or labeling them as disengaged at different thresholds than younger users. The spec acknowledges this outcome but does not describe any mechanism to investigate, correct, or mitigate this disparity before the full launch. Since the spec itself presents evidence of systematic disadvantage based on age, the BIAS category is flagged.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
