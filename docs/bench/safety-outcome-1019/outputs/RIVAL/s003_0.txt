The spec explicitly flags several critical harms. First, **DISC** is present: the design intentionally hides that a model produced the output ("nothing in the product says a model produced the output"), violating transparency. Second, **DARK** patterns are described: the interface uses a "countdown timer" and a "pre-ticked consent box" to pressure users during high-value payouts, undermining informed consent. Third, **RELY** is triggered by the claim of uniform error rates ("within one point across every group") combined with aggressive speed goals, which may invite over-trust despite the lack of nuance in performance reporting.

While the spec mentions human oversight for irreversible steps (mitigating **AUTO**) and allows overrides (mitigating some **RELY** concerns), the intentional concealment of AI involvement and the use of coercive UI elements are fundamental design flaws that must be addressed before launch. The assertion of fairness without detailed demographic breakdowns also leaves **BIAS** as a potential risk, though the explicit dark patterns and disclosure failures are the primary blockers.

HARMS: DISC, DARK, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
