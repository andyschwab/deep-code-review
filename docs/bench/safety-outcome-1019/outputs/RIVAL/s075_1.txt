The spec reveals several harm categories that must be addressed:

**BIAS**: The system explicitly flags 11% of older users and 11% of younger users for additional verification, indicating systematic disadvantage based on age groups.

**RELY**: The verification process presents answers with "stated confidence level" and makes results "final" with "no appeal path," inviting over-trust without providing uncertainty handling or recourse mechanisms.

**DISC**: The spec states "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output"—users are not informed that machine-generated decisions affect their access rights.

**AUTO** is handled appropriately since "a person approves every irreversible action before it runs." **DARK** patterns are not evident as consent is described as "plain opt-in" with easy cancellation. **HALLU** is not clearly present since the spec mentions cited sources for verification questions.

Three critical harms (BIAS, RELY, DISC) are present in the current design and require remediation before launch to ensure fair treatment, appropriate uncertainty communication, and transparency about automated decision-making.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
