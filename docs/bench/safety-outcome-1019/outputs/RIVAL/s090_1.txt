Based on the provided specification, the following harm categories are flagged:

**RELY**: The spec explicitly states that for patient-facing output, "the result is final and there is no appeal path." This design prevents users from challenging or correcting the system's output, inviting over-trust by removing any mechanism for override or recourse, despite the high-stakes nature of health information.

**DISC**: While the interface labels the output as "AI-generated" for patients, the summary is also produced "for the care team" to prep for visits. The spec does not state that clinicians are informed the summary is machine-generated. Since clinicians rely on this data for medical preparation without explicit disclosure in the text, this constitutes a disclosure gap for that user group.

The spec indicates that **BIAS** is being monitored with consistent data across age groups, **HALLU** is mitigated by source citations and confidence labels, **DARK** patterns are avoided via opt-in consent and no timers, and **AUTO** risks are managed by requiring human approval for irreversible actions. However, the lack of an appeal path for patients and the potential lack of disclosure for clinicians remain unresolved issues within the described design.

HARMS: RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
