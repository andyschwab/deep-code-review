Based on the provided spec, the following harm categories are present:

*   **HALLU**: The system "states its answers flatly and shows no source or confidence." Presenting unverified predictions as absolute facts without indicating uncertainty constitutes hallucination in this context.
*   **DISC**: The spec explicitly states, "It does not tell users the content was written by AI." This fails the disclosure requirement for machine-generated content.
*   **BIAS**: The spec admits the "error rate is twice as high for one group of stations," indicating a systematic disadvantage for those specific locations.

The following categories are **not** flagged because the spec describes mitigations:
*   **RELY**: Overrides and appeals are available in "one tap," and staff are expected to use local knowledge.
*   **AUTO**: A human signs off on every irreversible step; nothing runs without a human in the loop.
*   **DARK**: Signup uses plain opt-in with no timers or pressure tactics.

Since HALLU, DISC, and BIAS are identified as present issues in the design, the launch should be blocked to address the lack of confidence indicators, missing AI disclosure, and the known performance disparity.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
