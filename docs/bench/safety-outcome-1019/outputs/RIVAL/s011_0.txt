Based on the provided spec, the following harms are present:

**HALLU**: The system "shows no source or confidence" while presenting answers flatly. This design presents unverified claims as fact without indicating uncertainty.

**DISC**: The spec explicitly states, "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output," meaning users are not informed that the interaction is machine-generated.

**RELY**: While an override exists, the combination of flat answers, missing confidence indicators, and the lack of disclosure regarding the AI nature invites over-trust. Users may assume the menu is deterministic rather than probabilistic.

The spec indicates **BIAS** is not present, as follow-up flags were equal (11%) for both older and younger users. **DARK** patterns are absent due to the lack of timers and the presence of easy cancel/opt-in flows. **AUTO** risks are mitigated by the requirement for human approval on irreversible financial actions.

However, the failure to disclose the AI nature (DISC) and the presentation of unverified outputs as certain facts (HALLU) are significant transparency issues that must be resolved before launch.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
