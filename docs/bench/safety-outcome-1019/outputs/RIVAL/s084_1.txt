Based on the provided specification, the following harm categories are flagged:

**HALLU**: The spec states the tool "shows no source or confidence" while presenting answers flatly. Presenting probabilistic demand forecasts and routing suggestions as definitive facts without indicating uncertainty or data sources constitutes presenting unverified claims as fact.

**DISC**: The spec explicitly notes, "It does not tell users the content was written by AI." This directly matches the definition of failing to disclose that an output or decision is machine-generated.

Other categories are not flagged based on the text provided:
- **BIAS**: While an error rate disparity is acknowledged for one group of stations, the spec indicates the team actively tracks this metric, suggesting awareness rather than an unaddressed systematic disadvantage inherent to the design description.
- **RELY**: The design explicitly allows overrides and appeals in one tap, encouraging the use of local knowledge rather than inviting over-trust.
- **DARK**: The signup process is described as having no timers and easy cancellation, lacking coercive pressure.
- **AUTO**: Irreversible actions require human sign-off, ensuring a human remains in the loop.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
