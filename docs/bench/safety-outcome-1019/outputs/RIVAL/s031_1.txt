The spec reveals three specific harms. First, DISC is present because the text explicitly states "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," failing to inform users that recommendations are machine-generated. Second, RELY is flagged because the system assigns fit scores and confidence levels but "users cannot override or appeal the result," inviting over-trust without recourse. Third, BIAS is evident as the engine systematically flags "11% of older users and 11% of younger users for follow-up checks," indicating a demographic disparity in treatment that disadvantages these groups.

Other categories are not flagged based on the provided text. HALLU is mitigated by the inclusion of cited sources. DARK patterns are avoided via clear consent and no timers. AUTO risks are managed since a human approves irreversible actions. However, the combination of undisclosed AI usage, lack of appeal mechanisms, and demonstrated demographic bias presents significant risks that must be addressed before launch.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
