Based on the provided spec, two harm categories are present:

**DARK**: The interface explicitly "pressures users against their own interest" via a "countdown timer" and a "pre-ticked consent box." These patterns prioritize speed over informed, voluntary user action.

**RELY**: While the system offers overrides and confidence levels, the design includes a pre-ticked consent box. This default setting invites over-trust by assuming agreement without active user verification, potentially causing staff to bypass critical review steps.

The spec indicates that **DISC** is handled (visible AI label), **AUTO** is handled (human sign-off on irreversible steps), and **BIAS** is addressed via pilot testing. **HALLU** is mitigated by cited sources. However, the presence of dark patterns and defaults that encourage passive acceptance constitutes a design flaw requiring correction before launch.

HARMS: DARK, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
