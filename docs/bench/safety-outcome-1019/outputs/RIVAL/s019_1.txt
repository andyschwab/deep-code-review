Based on the spec provided, the following harms are present:

**HALLU**: The interface presents every answer as "confirmed fact with no source shown." Despite internal validation, displaying unverified outputs as absolute facts without sourcing constitutes presenting claims as fact without verification context to the user.

**DARK**: The sign-up flow utilizes a "countdown timer and a pre-ticked consent box" to nudge families. These are classic dark patterns that pressure users into making hasty decisions or consenting without active agreement, acting against their interest in informed consent.

The spec explicitly mitigates other categories: **DISC** is handled via visible AI labels; **RELY** is addressed by the one-tap override/appeal; **AUTO** is prevented by the human sign-off requirement for irreversible steps. **BIAS** is not flagged because the spec states error rates were consistent "across every group tested," indicating the design claims to handle this, even if the "presenting as fact" issue remains.

HARMS: HALLU, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
