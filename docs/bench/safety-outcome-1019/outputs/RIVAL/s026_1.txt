Based on the provided spec, the system includes visible AI labels, source citations, confidence indicators, one-tap overrides, opt-in consent, and human sign-off for irreversible actions. These features explicitly address DISC, RELY, DARK, and AUTO concerns.

However, the spec states: "In the pilot's first month, the bot flagged 18% of older users but 9% of younger users." This data indicates the system systematically disadvantages older users compared to younger ones regarding engagement interventions. Since the spec presents this disparity as an observed fact without describing a mitigation strategy, it confirms the presence of bias.
