Based on the provided specification, the following harms are present:

**BIAS**: The spec explicitly states the error rate is "twice as high for one group." While weekly re-training is planned, the system currently operates with a known, systematic disadvantage for a specific demographic, which constitutes a present bias risk at launch.

**DARK**: The interface employs a "ten-minute timer" to force urgency and uses "pre-ticked" consent for email follow-ups. These are classic dark patterns that pressure users into hasty decisions and waive privacy preferences by default, acting against their best interests.

Other categories are adequately addressed in the design: **RELY** is mitigated by the one-tap override/appeal; **AUTO** is prevented by the requirement for human approval on irreversible actions; **DISC** is handled by explicit AI labeling; and **HALLU** is addressed via cited sources and confidence levels. However, the confirmed presence of Bias and Dark patterns requires remediation.

HARMS: BIAS, DARK
