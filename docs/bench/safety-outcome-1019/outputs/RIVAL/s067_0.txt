Based on the provided spec, the following harm categories are flagged:

**HALLU**: The spec explicitly states the engine "shows no source or confidence" and presents answers "flatly" as facts (e.g., "This tutor fits") without reasoning. This design presents unverified claims as absolute fact.

**DISC**: The spec admits the product "does not tell users the content was written by AI," meaning users are not informed that the matching decisions are machine-generated.

The spec indicates other categories are handled: **BIAS** is addressed by pilot testing showing consistent error rates across groups; **RELY** is mitigated by the "one tap" override and appeal mechanism; **DARK** patterns are avoided via plain opt-in consent with no pressure tactics; and **AUTO** risks are managed by requiring human sign-off for irreversible scheduling steps.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
