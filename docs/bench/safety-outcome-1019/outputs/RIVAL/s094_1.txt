The spec explicitly addresses several potential harm categories: it labels outputs as AI-generated (mitigating DISC), shows sources and confidence labels (mitigating HALLU and RELY), allows overrides and appeals (mitigating RELY), and uses opt-in consent without pressure (mitigating DARK). The pilot data suggests consistent performance across groups, implying BIAS is considered, though not definitively proven resolved.

However, the spec states: "the ranked list acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." This describes a high-stakes decision (scholarship matching/ranking affecting financial aid) occurring automatically without human review. This directly fits the AUTO category: a high-stakes action runs without a human in the loop. While the pilot showed low error rates, the lack of human review before the action "acts" remains a defined characteristic of the launched system for the 25 campuses.

HARMS: AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
