While the percentages are identical, the explicit segmentation and flagging based on age groups ("older" vs. "younger") indicates the system is making decisions or highlighting issues systematically based on age. Even if the outcome rate is equal, processing and flagging users based on age categories in a scheduling context fits the definition of BIAS, as it involves group-based differentiation that could lead to disparate treatment or perception of unfairness, regardless of the equal percentage cited. The spec does not describe a mechanism to remove age from this logic, only that the flagging occurred.
