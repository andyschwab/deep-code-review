Based on the provided spec, the design explicitly addresses several potential harm categories. It mitigates **RELY** by allowing one-tap overrides and appeals. It handles **DISC** by labeling all outputs as AI-generated. It avoids **DARK** patterns through opt-in consent, unticked defaults, and the absence of timers. It prevents **AUTO** risks by requiring human sign-off for irreversible actions like fees or data release.

However, the spec indicates a **HALLU** risk. It states Matchly "shows no source or confidence" and presents rankings as flat facts. While internal error rates were low, presenting unverified claims (the specific ranking rationale) as absolute fact without uncertainty indicators aligns with the definition of hallucination-related harm in this context. Additionally, while error rates were consistent across groups in a small trial, the lack of source transparency could mask underlying **BIAS** in how "past-session stats" influence rankings, though the spec does not explicitly describe systematic disadvantage, so BIAS is not flagged based strictly on the text provided. The primary flagged issue is the presentation of unverified algorithmic decisions as certain facts.

HARMS: HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
