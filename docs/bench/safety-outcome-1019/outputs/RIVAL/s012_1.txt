The spec explicitly states the bot "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown." This confirms **HALLU**, as unverified claims are presented as fact without uncertainty or citations.

The design notes that "the result is final and there is no appeal path." Combined with the presentation of answers as absolute facts, this creates **RELY**. Users are invited to over-trust the system because uncertainty is hidden and there is no mechanism to challenge or correct a suggestion, even though the stakes involve financial transactions (upsells).

While the spec mentions an "AI-generated label" (addressing **DISC**) and "plain opt-in consent with no timers" (mitigating **DARK**), the lack of an appeal path for a system acting automatically on financial decisions also touches on **AUTO**. However, the primary harms explicitly described as present features are the fabrication of certainty (HALLU) and the structural inability to override that certainty (RELY). The pilot data regarding error rates across groups suggests **BIAS** is being monitored, not necessarily present as a described harm.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
