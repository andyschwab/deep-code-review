The spec explicitly discloses AI generation ("labels every output as AI-generated"), so DISC is not flagged. It provides cited sources and confidence levels, addressing uncertainty and mitigating RELY. The enrollment flow uses unticked opt-in with easy cancellation, avoiding DARK patterns. The 11% follow-up rate is identical for older and younger users, showing no evidence of BIAS in the described metrics.

However, two critical harms are present. First, the system executes actions ("acts the moment the model decides") regarding medication refills without human review, and users "cannot override or appeal the result." This constitutes AUTO, as it performs high-stakes health interventions without a human in the loop or recourse. Second, while confidence levels are shown, the inability to override or appeal a potentially incorrect automated decision regarding essential medication creates a dangerous reliance on the system's judgment, flagging RELY. The combination of irreversible automated action and lack of appeal mechanism for health-critical decisions presents an unacceptable safety risk.

HARMS: AUTO, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
