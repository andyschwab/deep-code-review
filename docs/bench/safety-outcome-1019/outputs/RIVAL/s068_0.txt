Based on the provided specification, the following harms are present:

**HALLU**: The spec states the profile page "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown." This design inherently risks presenting unverified or fabricated claims as facts without attribution.

**DISC**: The specification explicitly notes that the in-app chat "does not tell users the content was written by AI" when the system suggests practice questions, failing to disclose the machine-generated nature of the content.

**AUTO**: The matching engine "executes irreversible actions with no human approval," specifically auto-booking sessions. This constitutes a high-stakes or irreversible action running without a human in the loop.

The spec indicates that **BIAS** is addressed via calibration, **RELY** is mitigated by the one-tap override/appeal, and **DARK** patterns are avoided via plain opt-in and no timers. However, the undisclosed AI content, presentation of unsourced facts as confirmed, and irreversible auto-bookings are critical issues.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
