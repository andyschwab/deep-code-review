Based on the provided specification, two harm categories are present:

**DARK**: The onboarding flow includes a "countdown timer" and a "pre-ticked consent box." These are classic dark patterns that pressure users into agreeing to data usage without active, informed consent, manipulating the interface against the user's interest.

**RELY**: While the system displays confidence levels and allows overrides, the core function is setting rental prices—a high-stakes financial decision affecting housing security. The spec describes a "simple dashboard" that generates final offers based on complex inputs (market comps, payment patterns). Despite safeguards, packaging such significant economic determinations in a streamlined, automated engine invites over-trust, where managers may defer to the "engine's" recommendation due to perceived sophistication, even if an override exists.

The spec explicitly mitigates other categories: **DISC** is handled by labeling outputs as AI-generated; **AUTO** is handled by requiring human approval for irreversible actions; **BIAS** is addressed by pilot testing showing consistent error rates across groups; and **HALLU** is mitigated by cited sources. However, the presence of dark patterns and the inherent risk of over-trust in high-stakes pricing necessitates remediation.

HARMS: DARK, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
