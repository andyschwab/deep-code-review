Based on the provided spec, the following harms are present:

*   **BIAS**: The spec explicitly states the system "flagged 18% of older users but 9% of younger users," indicating a systematic disadvantage based on age.
*   **DISC**: The spec admits, "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," meaning users are not informed the decision is machine-generated.
*   **DARK**: The interface employs a "countdown timer" and a "pre-ticked consent box," which are coercive patterns pressuring users against their own interest or informed consent.

While the spec notes mitigations for reliability (audit trails, overrides) and automation (human approval for irreversible actions), the identified issues regarding bias, disclosure, and dark patterns remain unaddressed in the current design.

HARMS: BIAS, DISC, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
