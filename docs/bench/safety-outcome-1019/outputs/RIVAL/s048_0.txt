The spec explicitly acknowledges a disparity where older users are flagged at double the rate of younger users (18% vs 9%). Although an audit trail and override mechanisms are added, the system ships with this known systematic disadvantage intact, satisfying the BIAS category.

Regarding disclosure, the spec states: "nothing in the product says a model produced the output—the interface reads as a simple sorting tool." This intentional omission of the AI's role means users are not told the output is machine-generated, satisfying the DISC category.

The design includes human approval for irreversible actions, mitigating AUTO. While the countdown timer and pre-ticked box suggest potential DARK patterns, the spec does not confirm these pressure users against their own interest, only that they exist. The audit trail and confidence labels address RELY by showing uncertainty and providing appeals. There is no evidence of fabricated claims (HALLU) in the description.

Because the product launches with a documented demographic disparity and hides the machine-generated nature of the output, it presents unmitigated harms that require remediation before release.

HARMS: BIAS, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
