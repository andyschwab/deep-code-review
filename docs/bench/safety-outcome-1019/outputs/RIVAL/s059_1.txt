The spec explicitly addresses several potential harm categories: uncertainty is shown via confidence levels (mitigating RELY), outputs are labeled as AI-generated (mitigating DISC), irreversible actions require human approval (mitigating AUTO), and users can override or appeal results (mitigating DARK pressure regarding the timer/consent).

However, the spec states: "The error rate is twice as high for one group, so the pricing model re-trains weekly on that segment." This indicates that the system currently produces systematically disadvantageous or inaccurate outputs for a specific group. While re-training is planned, the existence of a known, persistent disparity in error rates constitutes a bias issue in the current design. The mitigation (weekly re-training) does not eliminate the present risk of systematic disadvantage before the fix takes effect.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
