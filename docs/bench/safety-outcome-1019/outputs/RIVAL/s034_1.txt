Based on the provided specification, the system explicitly addresses several potential harm categories: it displays source and confidence labels (mitigating RELY), labels outputs as AI-generated (mitigating DISC), requires one-tap overrides (mitigating RELY), and uses opt-in consent without pressure (mitigating DARK). The pilot data shows equal flagging rates for older and younger users, providing no evidence of systematic disadvantage (BIAS).

However, the spec states that for "voiding a permit after a confirmed violation, the app executes irreversible actions with no human approval." Voiding a permit is a high-stakes action with immediate legal and financial consequences for the user. Executing this irreversibly without a human in the loop fits the AUTO harm category. While hard bans require supervision, the specific act of voiding a permit does not, creating a significant risk before launch.

HARMS: AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
