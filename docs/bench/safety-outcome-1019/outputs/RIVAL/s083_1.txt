Based on the provided specification, the following harm categories are present:

*   **HALLU**: The spec states the interface "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown." Presenting forecasts as absolute facts without sourcing invites unverified claims to be treated as truth.
*   **RELY**: By presenting answers as "confirmed fact" and hiding sources, the design removes uncertainty cues, inviting over-trust despite the existence of an override mechanism.
*   **AUTO**: The spec explicitly notes, "The system acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." Since this system dispatches crews and adjusts routes (physical resource allocation), this constitutes a high-stakes action running without a human in the loop.

The spec indicates that **DISC** is handled (visible AI-generated label) and **DARK** patterns are avoided (no timers, easy cancel). While **BIAS** is mentioned regarding error rates, the text claims parity ("within one point across every group"), so it is not flagged as a present harm based on this text alone.

However, the combination of presenting outputs as absolute fact, hiding sources, and executing actions without human review creates significant operational risk. The lack of human oversight on physical dispatch decisions (AUTO) coupled with the presentation of probabilistic forecasts as certain facts (HALLU/RELY) requires remediation before launch.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
