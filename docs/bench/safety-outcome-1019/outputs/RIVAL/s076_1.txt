**RELY**: The design explicitly notes "no uncertainty shown, no easy override or appeal path," and denied entries simply loop back to a request form. This invites over-trust by removing mechanisms for users to challenge or understand decisions.

The spec indicates other categories are handled: **DISC** is addressed because outputs are labeled "AI-generated"; **AUTO** is mitigated because a person approves irreversible actions; **BIAS** is acknowledged and being patched before the second site launch; and **DARK** patterns are avoided via opt-in consent and easy cancellation.
