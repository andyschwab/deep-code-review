Based on the provided specification, the following harms are present:

**HALLU**: The spec states the interface "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown." Presenting probabilistic forecasts as absolute facts without sourcing invites the presentation of unverified claims as truth.

**RELY**: By presenting answers as "confirmed fact" and hiding sources, the design removes uncertainty. Although an override exists, hiding the basis for decisions encourages over-trust in the system's absolute accuracy.

**AUTO**: The spec explicitly notes, "The system acts the moment the model decides and nobody reviews it first." Since this system dispatches crews (allocating labor and resources) based on forecasts without human review, it constitutes high-stakes action without a human in the loop.

The spec indicates **DISC** is handled (visible AI label), **DARK** is handled (no timers, easy cancel), and **BIAS** is arguably addressed by the pilot's error rate claims, so these are not flagged.

HARMS: HALLU, RELY, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
