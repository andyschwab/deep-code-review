Based on the provided specification, the following harm categories are flagged:

**RELY**: The spec explicitly states, "Users cannot override or appeal the result—the flag stands." This design invites over-trust by removing any mechanism for human correction or appeal regarding the AI's decision, despite the system having known error rates.

**BIAS**: The spec admits that "the error rate is twice as high for one group—plates with temporary paper permits." This describes a systematic disadvantage for users with temporary permits. Although the team is "tuning that flow," the harm exists in the current design described for the pilot.

The spec indicates that **DISC** is handled (visible AI-generated label), **AUTO** is handled (human sign-off for irreversible actions), and **HALLU** is mitigated (cited sources and confidence levels). There is no evidence of **DARK** patterns, as consent is opt-in with no timers. However, because the bias is acknowledged but not yet resolved before the pilot, and the lack of appeal is a fixed design choice, the launch carries significant risk.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
