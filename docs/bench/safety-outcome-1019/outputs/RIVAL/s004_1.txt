Based on the provided spec, the following harms are present:

*   **BIAS**: The spec explicitly states the beta run flagged older users for specialist review at double the rate of younger users (18% vs. 9%), indicating a systematic disadvantage.
*   **RELY**: The interface includes a "pre-ticked consent box," a dark pattern that pressures users into agreeing to data sharing by default rather than requiring active choice.
*   **DARK**: The presence of a "countdown timer" creates artificial urgency, pressuring users to act quickly, potentially against their best interest or without full consideration.
*   **DISC**: The spec admits the system "does not tell users the content was written by AI," failing to disclose the machine-generated nature of the determinations.

The spec indicates **HALLU** is mitigated (source and confidence labels are shown), **AUTO** is handled (human approval required for irreversible actions), and the bias is acknowledged for tracking, though the harm itself remains present in the current design.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DARK, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
