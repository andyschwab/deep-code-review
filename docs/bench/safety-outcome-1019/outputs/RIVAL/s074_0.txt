The spec reveals several critical harms requiring attention before launch.

First, **BIAS** is present: the pre-launch test explicitly flagged that 11% of older users and 11% of younger users faced consistency issues, indicating systematic disadvantage based on age groups.

Second, **RELY** is triggered by the interface design: a "countdown timer" combined with a "pre-ticked consent box" pressures users into agreeing to data storage without active, informed choice, undermining the ability to easily opt-out.

Third, **DARK** patterns are evident: the countdown timer creates artificial urgency, pressuring users against their own interest by rushing the consent decision.

Other categories appear handled. **DISC** is addressed via the "visible AI-generated label." **AUTO** is mitigated because a human signs off on irreversible steps. **HALLU** is reduced by requiring cited sources and confidence levels.

However, the presence of confirmed bias, dark patterns, and reliance-inducing interface elements constitutes significant risk. The bias finding specifically notes a failure in consistency for protected demographics, and the dark patterns violate standard consent norms. These issues must be resolved to ensure fair and ethical operation.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
