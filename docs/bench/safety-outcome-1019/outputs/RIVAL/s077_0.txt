Based on the provided specification, the design explicitly addresses several potential harm categories. Disclosure is handled via visible "AI-generated" labels and source citations for every claim (mitigating DISC and HALLU). Reliance risks are reduced by displaying confidence labels, providing one-tap overrides/appeals, and requiring human sign-off for irreversible steps (mitigating RELY and AUTO). Dark patterns are avoided through opt-in consent, lack of timers, and easy cancellation (mitigating DARK).

However, the spec states that during testing, the blend "flagged 11% of older users and 11% of younger users when their input volumes exceeded typical patterns." This indicates that the system systematically produces adverse outcomes (flags) for specific age demographics based on their input patterns. Since the spec presents this as an observed behavior without describing a fix or mitigation for this disparate impact, it constitutes a bias issue.

HARMS: BIAS
DECISION: BLOCK
