*   **AUTO**: The spec confirms that irreversible actions (auto-renewal enrollment) execute "with no human approval." While limited to a pilot, the mechanism itself lacks a human in the loop for high-stakes financial commitments.

The spec indicates that **HALLU**, **RELY**, and **DISC** are handled, as the system provides sources, confidence labels, easy overrides, and visible AI-generated labels.
