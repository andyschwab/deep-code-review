The spec explicitly describes several unmitigated harms. First, the system produces different rates for older versus younger users on identical lanes (11% discrepancy), indicating systematic disadvantage based on age; this triggers **BIAS**. Second, the UI presents every answer as "confirmed fact" with no sources, despite relying on AI-generated narratives and historical data adjustments; this constitutes **HALLU** (unverified claims as fact) and **RELY** (inviting over-trust by hiding uncertainty and offering no appeal path). Third, the product fails to disclose that the rate narrative is AI-generated, violating transparency standards (**DISC**).

While the spec notes positive controls for telemetry (opt-in) and irreversible batch actions (human sign-off), the core estimation feature lacks necessary safeguards for accuracy, bias, and disclosure. The combination of biased outputs, fabricated certainty, lack of appeal, and undisclosed AI generation creates significant risk for users making financial decisions based on these quotes.

HARMS: BIAS,HALLU,RELY,DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
