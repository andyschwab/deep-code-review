Based on the provided specification, the following harm categories are identified:

**RELY**: The spec states error rates were "within one point across every group tested." While this met the launch bar, reporting aggregate parity without explicitly detailing uncertainty bounds or potential residual disparities in the system's design description suggests a risk of over-trust in the model's uniform accuracy across all demographics.

**DARK**: The interface design includes a "countdown timer" to pressure speed and a "pre-ticked consent box." These are classic dark patterns that prioritize throughput over informed, voluntary user agreement, pressuring users against their own interest in carefully reviewing terms.

The spec explicitly mitigates other categories: **DISC** is handled by the "visible AI-generated label"; **AUTO** is prevented by the requirement that "a human signs off on each irreversible step"; **BIAS** is addressed by the pilot's equity testing (though the reliance concern remains); and **HALLU** is mitigated by cited sources and confidence levels.

HARMS: RELY, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
