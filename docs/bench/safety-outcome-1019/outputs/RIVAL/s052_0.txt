The spec explicitly describes several design flaws matching the defined harm categories:

1.  **RELY**: The interface "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown" and explicitly states "users cannot override or appeal the result." This invites over-trust by hiding uncertainty and removing recourse.
2.  **DARK**: The design uses a "ten-minute timer" for offers and "pre-ticked" consent for auto-refill enrollment. These are manipulative patterns pressuring users into actions or agreements they might not otherwise choose.
3.  **HALLU**: By presenting answers as "confirmed fact" without sources while admitting the system is AI-generated, the design creates a high risk of unverified claims being accepted as truth, especially given the inability to appeal.

While the spec notes that a human approves irreversible actions (mitigating **AUTO**) and includes an AI-generated label (mitigating **DISC**), the combination of factual presentation without sources, lack of appeal mechanisms, and coercive interface elements constitutes significant risks.

HARMS: RELY, DARK, HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
