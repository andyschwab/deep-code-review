Based on the provided spec, the following harms are present:

**HALLU**: The spec states Beacon "shows no source or confidence" while presenting answers "flatly." Presenting unverified claims as fact without indicating uncertainty or sources fits this category.

**RELY**: The design invites over-trust by omitting uncertainty indicators ("shows no confidence") and explicitly providing "no appeal path after confirmation," preventing users from easily correcting errors.

**BIAS**: The spec admits the "error rate is twice as high for one group of hotel chains." If this disparity systematically disadvantages users who prefer or require those specific chains, it constitutes bias.

The spec indicates **DISC** is handled (visible AI-generated label), **AUTO** is handled (human sign-off on irreversible steps), and **DARK** is not evident (opt-in consent with easy cancellation). However, the combination of high error rates for specific groups, lack of confidence indicators, and no appeal mechanism creates significant risk.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
