Based on the provided specification, the following harm categories are flagged:

**HALLU**: The spec states the engine "shows no source or confidence" and presents answers "flatly" as "This tutor fits." Presenting algorithmic matching as definitive fact without indicating uncertainty or evidentiary basis constitutes unverified claims presented as fact.

**DISC**: The spec explicitly notes, "The product does not tell users the content was written by AI." This directly matches the definition of failing to disclose that an output is machine-generated.

The spec indicates other categories are handled: **RELY** is mitigated by the "one tap" override and appeal mechanism; **AUTO** is addressed by requiring human sign-off for irreversible steps; **DARK** patterns are avoided via plain opt-in with no timers; and **BIAS** is arguably addressed by the pilot showing consistent error rates across groups, though the lack of transparency (HALLU) makes verifying this difficult. However, per instructions, we only flag what is explicitly present as a harm. The lack of disclosure and the presentation of unverified matches as absolute facts are clear violations described in the text.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
