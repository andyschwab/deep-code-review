Based on the spec provided, the following harm categories are flagged:

**HALLU**: The specification explicitly states the system "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown," despite being a probabilistic model with a known high error rate for certain groups. Presenting unverified suggestions as absolute facts constitutes hallucination risk.

**BIAS**: The spec admits the "error rate is twice as high for one group." While a pilot is planned to tune the model, shipping a system with a known, significant disparity in performance systematically disadvantages that specific group during the rollout.

The spec indicates **RELY** is mitigated because users can "override the result or appeal it." **DISC** is handled as outputs are labeled "AI-generated." **AUTO** is addressed since a human approves irreversible actions. **DARK** patterns are not described; consent is opt-in with easy cancellation.

However, launching with a known, uncorrected bias and an interface that masks uncertainty as fact presents unacceptable risks.

HARMS: BIAS, HALLU
DECISION: BLOCK
