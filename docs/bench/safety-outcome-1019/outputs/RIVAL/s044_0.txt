The spec explicitly identifies a systematic disadvantage for itineraries involving international codeshares, where the error rate is twice as high for a specific group. Although extra review is planned for the pilot, launching with a known, unmitigated disparity in core functionality constitutes **BIAS**.

The design presents every answer as "confirmed fact" with no sources shown, despite the system being probabilistic and having a known error rate. This presentation of unverified outputs as absolute truth fits **HALLU**. Furthermore, by hiding uncertainty and sources while displaying results as confirmed, the interface encourages agents to trust the system implicitly without easy verification, satisfying **RELY**.

While the spec mentions labeling outputs as "AI-generated" (**DISC** handled), requiring human sign-off for irreversible steps (**AUTO** handled), and allowing overrides (**DARK** mitigated regarding consent), the combination of known disparate impact, fabricated certainty, and induced over-trust presents significant risks. The "extra review" is limited to the pilot phase and specific cases, leaving the general launch exposed to these harms.

HARMS: BIAS,HALLU,RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
