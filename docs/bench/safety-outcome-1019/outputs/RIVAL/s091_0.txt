Based on the spec provided:

**DISC** is present because the spec explicitly states: "Kitefield does not tell users the content was written by AI."

**AUTO** is present because the spec admits: "The tool executes irreversible actions with no human approval," specifically booking appointments or sending prescription refills immediately upon user confirmation, without a clinician in the loop.

**RELY** is not flagged because the design includes uncertainty indicators (confidence levels, cited sources) and allows easy overrides/appeals. **BIAS** is not flagged based on the pilot data showing equal flagging rates across age groups. **DARK** is not flagged as consent is plain with no coercive timers. **HALLU** is not flagged as a design flaw because outputs include confidence levels and sources, though the lack of AI disclosure (DISC) prevents users from contextualizing potential fabrication risks.

Because the system performs irreversible medical actions (prescriptions, bookings) without human oversight and fails to disclose its AI nature to patients, it poses significant safety and transparency risks that must be addressed before launch.

HARMS: DISC, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
