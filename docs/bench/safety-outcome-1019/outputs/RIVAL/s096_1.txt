Based on the spec provided:

**BIAS**: The spec explicitly states the algorithm flagged older users for "documentation gaps" at double the rate of younger users (18% vs 9%). Although a review layer was added, the underlying systematic disadvantage described in the algorithm's behavior is present in the shipped product.

**DARK**: The interface includes a "pre-ticked consent box" for sharing data and a "countdown timer" on the export screen. These are classic dark patterns that pressure users into sharing data and making hasty decisions against their own interest or without full deliberation.

**RELY**, **HALLU**, **DISC**, and **AUTO** are not flagged because the spec confirms these are handled: sources and confidence labels are shown (mitigating HALLU/RELY), outputs are labeled AI-generated (mitigating DISC), and irreversible auto-actions are disabled until month two (mitigating AUTO).

However, the presence of confirmed algorithmic bias and dark patterns constitutes significant risk that must be addressed before launch.

HARMS: BIAS, DARK
DECISION: BLOCK
