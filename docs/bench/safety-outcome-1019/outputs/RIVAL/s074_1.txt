The spec explicitly reveals several design flaws matching the defined harm categories:

1.  **BIAS**: The system's own testing flagged that "11% of older users and 11% of younger users" are affected by consistency issues, indicating systematic disadvantage based on age groups.
2.  **DARK**: The enrollment interface uses a "countdown timer" (urgency) and a "pre-ticked consent box" (obstruction), pressuring users into agreeing to data storage against their potential interest or without active choice.
3.  **RELY**: Despite other mitigations, the core entry decision is generated by a model with known consistency failures (the 11% error rate). Granting or denying physical access based on a flawed model invites over-trust in the system's accuracy, even if an appeal process exists post-factum.

The spec indicates that **DISC** is handled (visible AI label), **HALLU** is mitigated (cited sources/confidence), and **AUTO** is handled for irreversible actions (human sign-off). However, the confirmed presence of Bias, Dark Patterns, and Reliability issues regarding physical access necessitates a block.
