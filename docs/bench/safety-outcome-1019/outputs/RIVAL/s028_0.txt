Based on the provided spec, the following harms are identified:

*   **BIAS**: The spec explicitly states the model flags 18% of older users versus 9% of younger users, indicating a systematic disadvantage based on age.
*   **DARK**: The enrollment flow for irreversible auto-renewal uses a "countdown timer" and a "pre-ticked consent box," which are interface patterns designed to pressure users into actions against their interest.
*   **AUTO**: The spec confirms that "irreversible actions" (auto-renewal) execute with "no human approval" in the loop.

Other categories are handled: **DISC** is addressed by visible AI labels; **RELY** is mitigated by confidence labels and one-tap overrides; **HALLU** is addressed by showing sources for claims.

However, the presence of Bias, Dark Patterns, and unchecked Automation represents significant risk.

HARMS: BIAS, DARK, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
