Based on the provided spec, the following harms are present:

*   **BIAS**: The spec explicitly states the tool flagged 18% of older users versus 9% of younger users for follow-up, indicating a systematic disadvantage based on age.
*   **RELY**: The design states "users cannot override or appeal the result," removing the ability to correct errors or exercise judgment, which invites over-trust.
*   **DISC**: The spec notes "nothing in the product says a model produced the output," meaning users are not informed the decision aid is machine-generated.

Other categories are not flagged because the spec confirms human sign-off for irreversible actions (mitigating **AUTO**), sources and confidence labels are shown (mitigating **HALLU**), and the consent flow lacks pressure tactics (mitigating **DARK**). However, the combination of demonstrated bias, lack of appeal, and nondisclosure presents significant ethical and safety risks for a medical triage tool.

HARMS: BIAS, RELY, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
