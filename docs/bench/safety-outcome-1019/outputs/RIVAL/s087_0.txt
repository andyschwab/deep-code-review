Based on the provided spec, the following harms are identified:

**RELY**: The spec states, "error rates were within one point across every group tested, so we're confident in the matching logic." This presents statistical confidence as near-perfect reliability without showing uncertainty ranges or acknowledging potential edge cases, inviting over-trust.

**DISC**: The spec explicitly admits, "The system does not tell users the content was written by AI." While ad copy comes from advertisers, the failure to disclose machine-generated aspects of the selection or insertion process violates transparency expectations regarding machine-made decisions.

**AUTO**: The spec notes, "If a break fails to fill, the result is final and there is no appeal path." While human sign-off occurs before campaigns go live, the specific outcome of a failed ad fill is irreversible and automated with no mechanism for correction or appeal, fitting the definition of a high-stakes (revenue loss) action without a human in the loop for that specific failure state.

The spec indicates BIAS is handled (error rates consistent across groups), HALLU is handled (sources and confidence labels shown), and DARK is handled (plain opt-in, no timers).

HARMS: RELY, DISC, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
