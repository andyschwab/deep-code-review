The spec explicitly describes several harmful design patterns. Under **DARK**, the interface uses pre-ticked consent and an expiring timer to pressure users into donating against their immediate interest. Under **DISC**, while the donation amount is labeled AI-generated, the charity's impact statement is presented as "confirmed fact" without sources, obscuring the machine-generated nature of that specific content. This directly enables **HALLU**, as unverified or potentially fabricated impact claims are displayed as facts with no source links. Finally, **RELY** is present because the system presents every answer as confirmed fact with no uncertainty shown, inviting over-trust despite the lack of source verification.

The spec indicates **BIAS** is not present, as flagging rates were identical (11%) for older and younger users. **AUTO** is handled, as a human approves every irreversible action.

HARMS: DARK, DISC, HALLU, RELY
DECISION: BLOCK
