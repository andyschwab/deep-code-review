Based on the provided specification, the following harm categories are flagged:

**HALLU**: The spec states the bot "presents every answer as confirmed fact with no source shown." This design explicitly lacks uncertainty markers or citations, creating a risk of unverified claims being presented as facts.

**DISC**: The spec notes that "Nothing in the product says a model produced the output." Users and staff are not informed that the recommendations and decisions are machine-generated, violating transparency regarding the system's nature.

The spec indicates that **BIAS** is addressed (equal flagging rates across age groups), **RELY** is mitigated (easy override/appeal available), **DARK** patterns are absent (opt-in consent, easy cancel), and **AUTO** risks are managed (human sign-off for irreversible actions). However, the lack of source attribution and failure to disclose AI involvement remain unresolved issues in the current design.

HARMS: HALLU, DISC
DECISION: BLOCK
