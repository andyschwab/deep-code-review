Based on the provided specification, the design explicitly addresses several potential harm categories: it discloses AI generation (mitigating DISC), shows sources and confidence (mitigating HALLU and RELY), allows easy overrides (mitigating RELY), uses opt-in consent without pressure (mitigating DARK), and reports equal flagging rates across age groups (mitigating BIAS).

However, the spec states: "For voiding a permit after a confirmed violation, the app executes irreversible actions with no human approval." Voiding a permit is a high-stakes action with immediate legal and financial consequences for the user. Executing this irreversibly without a human in the loop fits the AUTO category. While hard bans require supervision, the routine voiding action does not, creating a significant risk where automated errors cause immediate harm before any appeal can be lodged.
