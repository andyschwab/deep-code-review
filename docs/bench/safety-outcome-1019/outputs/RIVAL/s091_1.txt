Based on the provided specification, the following harm categories are present:

**DISC**: The spec explicitly states, "Kitefield does not tell users the content was written by AI," confirming that users are not informed the output is machine-generated.

**AUTO**: The system "executes irreversible actions with no human approval," specifically booking appointments or sending prescription refill requests immediately upon user confirmation, without a clinician in the loop for these specific actions.

The spec indicates that **BIAS** is likely handled, as pilot data shows equal flagging rates (11%) for older and younger users, matching historical baselines. **RELY** is mitigated by the inclusion of cited sources, confidence levels, and a one-tap override/appeal mechanism. **DARK** patterns appear absent due to plain opt-in consent, lack of timers, and easy cancellation.

HARMS: DISC, AUTO
DECISION: BLOCK
