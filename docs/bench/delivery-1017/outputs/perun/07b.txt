**Delivery Plan: Emergency Fare Calculation Hotfix**

**Immediate Actions (G0/G1 Intake & Spec)**
*   **Scope:** Apply hotfix from `emergency` branch capping surge multipliers for inter-city bus routes.
*   **Constraints:** Bypass 2-day staging; execute manual sanity suite; deploy within 1 hour.
*   **Risk:** Legacy monolith coupling (train/ferry impact); flaky smoke tests; irreversible DB schema changes.
*   **Verification Target:** Dashboard metrics stabilization; revenue leak cessation.

**Lanes & Serialization**
*   **Lane 1: Code Integrity & Isolation (Serialized First)**
    *   **Actor:** Builder (Independent of original hotfix author if possible).
    *   **Task:** Checkout `emergency` branch. Verify diff contains *only* the multiplier cap logic. Ensure no unintended schema migrations or side-effects on train/ferry modules are included.
    *   **Output:** Confirmed clean commit SHA on `emergency` branch.
*   **Lane 2: Targeted Verification (Parallel to Lane 1 completion, before Deploy)**
    *   **Actor:** QA (Independent).
    *   **Task:** Deploy to a isolated staging clone (not shared prod). Run manual "sanity check suite" explicitly covering:
        1.  Inter-city bus surge calculation (peak hours).
        2.  Train and ferry pricing regression (verify no bleed-over).
        3.  Tax calculation edge cases (known flake area).
    *   **Condition:** Must pass on the exact SHA from Lane 1. Flaky test failures require manual triage, not ignore.
*   **Lane 3: Deployment Preparation (Parallel to Lane 2)**
    *   **Actor:** Release Engineer.
    *   **Task:** Prepare production deployment manifest. Document rollback procedure (full DB restore, 45min ETA). Pre-draft incident communication.

**Gates & Checks**
*   **Gate G4 (Implement):** Builder confirms SHA matches `emergency` branch head. No new dependencies added.
*   **Gate G5 (Verify):** QA signs off on sanity suite results for the specific SHA. **Hard Stop:** If train/ferry regression fails or tax calc is broken, deployment is aborted.
*   **Gate G6 (Review):** Lead Engineer reviews the diff and QA receipts. Security quick-scan for injection risks in the cap logic.
*   **Gate G8 (Release - Human Decision):**
    *   **Approver 1:** Lead Engineer (Real-time approval required).
    *   **Approver 2:** Operations Director (Retroactive approval allowed within 4h per incident policy, but notification must be sent immediately).
    *   **Decision Point:** Go/No-Go based on G5 receipts.

**Landing & Rollback**
*   **Merge/Deploy Strategy:** Direct deploy of the verified SHA to production. Do not merge to `main` until post-deploy stability is confirmed to avoid contaminating the nightly pipeline.
*   **Human Gate:** Lead Engineer must explicitly authorize the push. Operations Director is notified instantly for retroactive sign-off.
*   **Post-Deploy Verification (G9):**
    *   Monitor dashboard metrics for 15 minutes.
    *   Confirm surge multipliers are capped correctly.
    *   Verify no spike in error rates for train/ferry modules.
*   **Rollback Trigger:** If metrics do not stabilize within 15 minutes, or if train/ferry errors spike:
    1.  Halt traffic.
    2.  Execute full DB restore from backup (45min downtime).
    3.  Notify stakeholders of extended outage.

**Execution Rhythm**
1.  **T+0:** Lane 1 validates code; Lane 3 preps deploy/rollback.
2.  **T+15m:** Lane 2 completes sanity checks on isolated env.
3.  **T+25m:** G6 Review complete. Lead Engineer approves.
4.  **T+30m:** Deploy to Production.
5.  **T+45m:** G9 Verification complete. Confirm stabilization or initiate rollback.
6.  **T+60m:** Incident resolved or rollback in progress. Director notified.

**Critical Constraints Enforced:**
*   No deployment without QA sign-off on the exact SHA.
*   No skipping of the manual sanity suite despite time pressure.
*   Rollback plan ready before the first command is run.
*   Finance team updated only after G9 confirmation to avoid premature reporting.
