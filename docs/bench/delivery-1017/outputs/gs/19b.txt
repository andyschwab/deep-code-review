# Delivery Plan: PulseRate Isolation

## Work Lanes & Parallelism

**Lane A: Extraction & Refactoring (Platform Guild)**
*   **Scope:** Copy renewal logic from `claims_processor.py` and relevant schemas into the new `PulseRate` service.
*   **Constraint:** Must avoid editing patient record interfaces. Work on `claims_processor.py` is serialized outside 09:00–17:00 EST.
*   **Parallelism:** Runs parallel to Lane B, but must pause if Clinical hotfixes conflict during locked hours.

**Lane B: Interface Adapter (Platform Guild)**
*   **Scope:** Build the communication layer (API/gRPC) so the monolith calls `PulseRate` instead of executing local logic. Update `/config/global` ensuring backward compatibility for the three legacy tools.
*   **Parallelism:** Can proceed independently of Lane A’s internal logic implementation, provided the interface contract is defined first.

**Lane C: Data Migration Strategy (DBA + Finance)**
*   **Scope:** Design zero-downtime migration for `HealthVault`. Since schema changes are irreversible, this requires a dual-write or shadow-table strategy.
*   **Serialization:** Must complete validation before Lane D begins.

**Lane D: Audit & Compliance (Finance Team)**
*   **Scope:** Verify separation meets audit requirements.
*   **Serialization:** Occurs after Lane A & B are feature-complete in staging.

## Gates & Checks

**Gate 1: Contract Freeze (Day 3)**
*   **Check:** Define the exact input/output signature between Monolith and `PulseRate`.
*   **Revision:** Locked in Git before extraction begins.

**Gate 2: Local Integrity (Pre-Commit)**
*   **Check:** Unit tests for `PulseRate` logic.
*   **Environment:** Local developer machines to avoid CI costs.
*   **Revision:** Feature branch head.

**Gate 3: Staging Verification (CI Pipeline)**
*   **Check:** Full integration test suite.
*   **Constraint:** Trigger only when Lane A, B, and C artifacts are ready.
*   **Mitigation:** Due to 20% flaky network checks, require two consecutive successful runs before approval.
*   **Cost Control:** Limit to final release candidates and critical fixes to manage the $45/run cost.

**Gate 4: Clinical Lock Compliance**
*   **Check:** Automated scan ensuring no touches to patient record interfaces.
*   **Timing:** Enforced on every push to the feature branch.

## Review Strategy

**Primary Review: Platform Guild Lead**
*   **Focus:** Code quality, modularity, and adherence to the "no patient record modification" rule.
*   **Independence:** Must not be the author of the extraction logic.

**Secondary Review: Finance Representative**
*   **Focus:** Accuracy of renewal calculations and audit trail integrity.
*   **Timing:** After Gate 3 passes.

**Safety Review: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**
*   **Focus:** Risk assessment regarding claim adjudication stability.
*   **Timing:** Pre-deployment.

## Landing & Rollback

**Merge Strategy**
*   **Branch:** Merge `pulserate-extraction` to `main` only after dual approval (CMO + VP Engineering).
*   **Timing:** Deployment window scheduled outside 09:00–17:00 EST to avoid Clinical team locks.

**Deployment Phases**
1.  **Shadow Mode:** Deploy `PulseRate` alongside the monolith. Route 1% of traffic to the new service; compare results against the monolith without affecting live claims.
2.  **Dual Write:** Enable writing to new schemas while reading from old.
3.  **Full Cutover:** Switch read traffic to `PulseRate`.

**Human Decisions Required**
*   Approval to switch read traffic from old to new schema (VP Engineering + CMO).
*   Final sign-off on audit compliance (Finance).

**Rollback Plan**
*   **Trigger:** Any discrepancy in billing calculations or claim adjudication failures >0.1%.
*   **Action:** Immediate revert of the feature flag routing traffic to `PulseRate`. The monolith code remains intact as the fallback.
*   **Data:** Disable dual-write; revert to legacy schema as the source of truth. Irreversible schema changes in `HealthVault` necessitate that the new tables are simply abandoned, not dropped, to preserve audit logs.
