### Delivery Plan: Inventory Sync Sequence (PRs #101–#106)

**1. Lanes & Serialization**
*   **Serialized Lane (Critical Path):** All six PRs (#101 through #106) must merge strictly sequentially. No parallel execution is permitted.
    *   **Actor:** Senior Ops Engineer (sole approver/merger).
    *   **Constraint:** PR $N$ merges only after PR $N-1$ is fully integrated, CI-passed, and verified in Staging.
*   **Parallel Lane (Observation):** Logistics Team monitors the dashboard and warehouse sensors.
    *   **Actor:** Logistics Coordinators.
    *   **Timing:** Active during the low-traffic window, verifying data integrity after each individual merge.

**2. Gates & Checks**
*   **Pre-Merge Gate (Every PR):**
    *   **Revision:** Head of the feature branch rebased on the immediate previous merged PR (e.g., #102 on top of merged #101).
    *   **Check:** Full CI pipeline (45 mins).
    *   **Flaky Check Protocol:** If the "network simulation" stage fails on the 3rd merge (likely PR #103 or #104), a Lead Logistics Manager must manually verify the error is false before triggering a retry. Do not retry automatically.
*   **Staging Verification Gate (Every PR):**
    *   **Timing:** Immediately after CI passes and merge to Staging/Main (depending on branch protection).
    *   **Action:** Logistics Team confirms dashboard stock counts match expected values for that specific delta.
    *   **Stop Condition:** Any mismatch halts the sequence immediately.

**3. Review Strategy**
*   **Primary Review:** Completed previously (assumed by "open PR" status).
*   **Independent Verification:**
    *   **Who:** Lead Logistics Manager (independent of the Ops Engineer merging).
    *   **What:** Validates the "network simulation" failure if it occurs; signs off on the Staging data integrity after every single PR.
    *   **Separation:** The Senior Ops Engineer executes the merge; the Logistics Lead validates the business outcome. Neither approves their own work.

**4. Landing & Merge Protocol**
*   **Window:** Tomorrow morning (low-traffic).
*   **Method:** Standard Merge Commit (No Squash). This preserves the individual generation steps for the audit trail.
*   **Human Decision Points:**
    1.  **Go/No-Go:** Logistics Lead confirms the previous PR's data accuracy before authorizing the next merge.
    2.  **Flaky Failure:** Lead Logistics Manager decides if a CI failure is the known false positive.
    3.  **Abort:** Any data mismatch triggers an immediate stop.

**5. Rollback Plan**
*   **Trigger:** Data corruption detected in Production or irreversible documentation published with errors.
*   **Action:**
    1.  Halt all further merges.
    2.  Revert the most recent PR immediately via a new revert commit (preserving history).
    3.  If multiple PRs have landed, revert in reverse order (#106 down to the culprit).
    4.  Restore the master ledger from the pre-window backup if sensor mismatch persists.
*   **Constraint:** Documentation published to the external partner portal cannot be edited; a full revert and republish cycle is required if that specific artifact is corrupted.

**6. Completion Criteria**
*   All six PRs merged individually.
*   CI green on final state.
*   Logistics Team confirms final dashboard accuracy against warehouse sensors.
*   Audit trail verified (6 distinct merge commits present).
