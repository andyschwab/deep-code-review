### Delivery Plan: Inventory Schema Update

**1. Work Lanes & Parallelism**
*   **Lane A (Schema & Provider):** Modify the protobuf definition to add `warehouse_zone` as a required string. Update the provider service to populate this field.
    *   *Constraint:* Must be completed first; blocks Lane B.
*   **Lane B (Consumer Validation):** Once Lane A commits the proto change, vendors update their parsers against the new definition.
    *   *Parallelism:* Vendors work in parallel with Lane C, but cannot start until Lane A pushes the proto update.
*   **Lane C (Coordination):** Contact Jane Smith immediately to lock the cutover time. Notify ShopFast and MegaMart integration teams of the pending proto change and agreed timeline.

**2. Gates, Checks & Revision Control**
*   **Gate 1 (Pre-Build):** Verify proto compatibility locally before pushing. Ensure no redundant CI triggers occur during development.
*   **Gate 2 (CI Execution):** Trigger the single required CI pipeline ($45 cost, 22 min duration) **only on Wednesday morning** to avoid Tuesday staging instability.
    *   *Revision:* Run against the exact commit hash containing both the proto change and the provider implementation.
    *   *Check:* Validate that the new field is present and non-null in the staging payload.
*   **Gate 3 (Partner Sync):** Confirm both vendors have successfully parsed the new schema in their staging environments. No deployment proceeds without written confirmation from both.

**3. Review Process**
*   **Technical Review:** Conducted by a senior engineer independent of the author, focusing on proto backward compatibility and null-safety in the provider logic.
*   **Security & Ops Review:** Required for production access. The Security Officer must verify no data leakage risks in the new field; the Logistics Lead must confirm operational readiness.
*   **Independence:** The code author cannot approve the deployment. The two approvers (Logistics Lead, Security Officer) must review independently of each other.

**4. Landing Strategy**
*   **Merge Condition:** Merge to main only after:
    1.  Wednesday CI passes.
    2.  Jane Smith confirms the exact cutover time.
    3.  Both vendors confirm parser readiness.
*   **Deployment Window:** Execute deployment strictly at the confirmed cutover time to align with partner parser updates.
*   **Human Decision:** The Logistics Lead and Security Officer must manually approve the production promotion. No automated rollout.
*   **Rollback Plan:** Reverting is **impossible** without a 4-hour data migration once the column becomes non-nullable.
    *   *Mitigation:* If the deployment fails post-activation, immediately engage the data engineering team for the migration rollback. Do not attempt a standard git revert.
    *   *Prevention:* If any gate fails, abort the merge entirely. Do not merge partially.

**Critical Path Summary:**
Proto Change → Vendor Notification → Jane Smith Time Lock → Wednesday CI → Partner Staging Validation → Dual Approval → Timed Deployment.

Any deviation from the Wednesday testing window or the synchronized cutover time risks system crashes or missed shipping windows. Precision in timing and validation is mandatory.
