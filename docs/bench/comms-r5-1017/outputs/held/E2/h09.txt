**Decision needed: Choose Option A (On-Prem) or Option B (Cloud) by tomorrow for the Director of IT. Recommendation: Option B, derived from the stated need for scalability and reduced hardware management despite the longer timeline.**

**Situation:** You have a $150K budget and a security/compliance mandate. The current choice is between refreshing on-prem hardware or migrating to the cloud, with a decision required tomorrow.

- **Option A (On-Prem Refresh):**
  - **Cost:** $120K one-time (fits within $150K budget).
  - **Time:** 3 months.
  - **Risk/Constraint:** Requires ongoing hardware management; less scalable.
  - **Upside:** Faster implementation; no recurring subscription costs mentioned.

- **Option B (Cloud Migration):**
  - **Cost:** $100K/year (Year 1 fits budget; Year 2 requires new funding). Break-even is 2 years.
  - **Time:** 6 months.
  - **Risk/Constraint:** Longer timeline; ongoing annual costs.
  - **Upside:** Scalable (can scale down); eliminates hardware management.

**Recommendation:** Option B. Although it takes twice as long (6 months vs. 3) and introduces recurring costs, it directly addresses your stated priorities: scalability and removing the burden of hardware management. The Year 1 cost ($100K) remains under the $150K budget.

**Not known:** Specific compliance controls required to verify if Option B meets the security mandate without extra cost; exact savings from "scaling down"; funding availability for Year 2.

**If approved:** Draft the formal proposal for the Director highlighting the trade-off between the 6-month timeline and the long-term operational benefits.
