**Batching reduces median write latency from 12ms to 3ms; 99th percentile rises to 40ms due to the 500ms flush interval.**

### Motivation
Under load, individual audit-log writes incurred 12ms latency. Buffering writes reduces this overhead by amortizing I/O costs.

### Change
- **New logic:** Writes are buffered and flushed every 500ms or when the buffer reaches 2048 entries, whichever occurs first.
- **Files:** `batch.go` (new, 210 lines), `audit_writer.go` (+142/-38), `audit_writer_test.go` (+96/-12), `go.mod` (+4).
- **Behavior:** Median latency drops to 3ms. The 99th percentile latency increases to 40ms, bounded by the flush interval.

### Testing
- **Status:** 142 tests pass, 3 skipped, 0 failures.
- **Known Gap:** The failure-recovery path (process crash mid-batch requiring WAL replay) is **not tested**.
