**Southern corridor predictive ETAs are 12% more accurate; accuracy for oversized loads remains unverified due to insufficient training data.**

The new model delivers a confirmed 12% accuracy improvement on the southern corridor. However, confidence drops sharply for trains carrying oversized loads (e.g., heavy machinery, large transformers). This gap exists because the current dataset contains only ~200 historical trips for this segment, all originating from a single season, making the data non-diverse and incomplete.

Acquiring additional real-world data from other corridors is underway but proceeding slowly. To address the immediate shortfall, the team plans to augment the dataset with synthetic samples, though this approach carries inherent risks to model reliability. No decision is required at this time; this update serves to flag the data limitation. We will report future accuracy metrics for the oversized load segment as they become available.

*Note: The 12% figure reflects verified gains on the southern corridor only; no aggregate system-wide accuracy is claimed.*
*Note: Synthetic data augmentation is a proposed mitigation, not a verified fix.*
