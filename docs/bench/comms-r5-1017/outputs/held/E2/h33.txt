**Lab tests are green (500+ cycles); field beta shows firmware crashes on 3 of 20 units during sudden stalls, with a tentative fix target by month-end.**

The safety-certified torque sensor is performing flawlessly in the lab, completing over 500 test cycles without issue. However, field beta units are experiencing a firmware crash specifically when the arm encounters a sudden stall. This has occurred on 3 out of 20 deployed beta units.

We are currently analyzing the crash logs and suspect a race condition in the control loop, though the root cause is not yet confirmed. A potential workaround exists that limits the torque spike to prevent the crash, but it reduces system responsiveness by 10%. We are evaluating this trade-off while pursuing a permanent fix.

We aim to deliver a resolution by the end of the month, though this timeline remains tentative pending the root cause analysis. No decision is required from you at this time; the team is actively investigating.
