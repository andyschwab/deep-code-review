**Risk & Operational Impact**

*   **Option A (Single Vendor):** Carries higher risk. Consolidating entirely to LearnFast will result in the loss of custom integrations currently used by SkillBridge and CourseHub. This will negatively impact reporting capabilities for two departments. However, this option offers the highest reduction in administrative overhead (estimated at 40%).
*   **Option B (Two Vendors):** Lower risk. Retaining SkillBridge alongside LearnFast preserves necessary custom integrations and departmental reporting. While annual savings are $50,000 lower than Option A, the implementation is twice as fast and requires half the upfront capital.

**Decision Required**
Approve the migration strategy. The Two-Vendor Option is financially superior in the short term (paying for itself in 9 months vs. 13 months) and mitigates the risk of disrupted reporting. The Single-Vendor Option should only be chosen if the 40% reduction in administrative overhead is deemed critical enough to justify the longer timeline, higher upfront cost, and immediate loss of reporting functionality for two departments.

The study confirms that maintaining the current three-vendor structure is the least efficient use of capital.
