**Status Update: Print Farm Operations**

Print farm uptime stands at 94% over the last two weeks. While the ten printers running standard materials are operating normally, two of the twelve units dedicated to the new ceramic material profile are currently failing calibration.

We suspect a firmware mismatch triggered by the latest slicer update, though we have not confirmed this yet and currently lack a workaround. Consequently, the two affected printers are down for ceramic jobs. We are rerouting these tasks to the remaining operational units, which is introducing additional latency to the workflow.

We are monitoring the situation daily and have opened a support ticket with the slicer vendor, but we do not have an estimated time for resolution. No decision is required from you at this time; this is purely for visibility. We will escalate the issue if it remains unresolved by the end of the week.

Note: Latency may increase if rerouting volume grows before a fix is found.
