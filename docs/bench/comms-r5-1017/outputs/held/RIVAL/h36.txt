**Subject:** Status Update: RTL Simulation Cluster

The new RTL simulation cluster is currently delivering a 3.2x speedup on our standard testbench, which is 30% faster than projected.

This slight overflow is causing some jobs to queue temporarily. To resolve this, we are procuring an additional 50 licenses at a total cost of $500k ($10k per license). The procurement request is underway, and we expect approval by next week.

We also observed that the cluster is consuming 5% more power than initially estimated. This variance is minor and does not impact current operations.

We have a clear plan to address the license concurrency and do not require any immediate decisions from you. This update is for your situational awareness.
