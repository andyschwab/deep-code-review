**Executive Summary: Data-Privacy Audit and Compliance Options**

**To:** Rachel, General Counsel
**From:** Alumni-Donations Office
**Subject:** Compliance Strategy for New State Privacy Law

**Context and Risk**
The recent data-privacy audit identified critical gaps in our handling of donor information. Currently, 15% of donor records lack explicit consent flags required for marketing emails, and our 30-day data retention policy is inconsistently enforced. We face a six-month deadline to achieve compliance with the new state privacy law. Failure to comply exposes the university to fines of up to $50,000 per violation.

**Option A: Manual Process Improvement**
*   **Approach:** Manually update consent records and strengthen internal workflows.
*   **Cost:** $120,000 (primarily for hiring two temporary staff members).
*   **Timeline:** 5 months.
*   **Risks:** Relies heavily on human execution, leaving residual risk of error. Does not provide an automated audit trail.

**Option B: Automated Consent Management Tool**
*   **Approach:** Purchase and implement a dedicated software tool to automate consent tracking and integrate with our existing CRM.
*   **Cost:** $200,000 upfront plus $40,000 annually.
*   **Timeline:** 3 months.
*   **Benefits:** Significantly reduces human error and generates a clear, automated audit trail. No additional staffing required.
*   **Risks:** Potential integration challenges with our legacy CRM system. The vendor does not offer 24/7 support.

**Current Recommendation Lean**
Based on the audit findings, the preference leans toward **Option B**. While the upfront cost is $80,000 higher than Option A, the accelerated three-month implementation provides a larger safety buffer before the six-month deadline. Additionally, the automation of consent tracking directly addresses the root cause of the 15% compliance gap and mitigates the risk of future human error, which is critical given the high per-violation fine structure. The lack of 24/7 vendor support and potential legacy integration issues are noted concerns that would need to be addressed during the procurement phase.

**Decision Required**
Please advise on whether to proceed with the procurement process for the automated tool (Option B) or to initiate the manual remediation plan (Option A).
