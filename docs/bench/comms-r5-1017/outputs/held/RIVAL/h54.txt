**Strategic Options**

**Option A: Full Refactor (6 Months)**
*   **Cost:** $1.43M
*   **Timeline:** 6 months
*   **Constraint:** Requires a complete freeze on new features for the duration.
*   **Benefit:** Fastest resolution of debt; immediate recovery of delivery speed.

**Option B: Incremental Refactor (12 Months)**
*   **Cost:** $1.6M (includes overhead)
*   **Timeline:** 12 months
*   **Constraint:** No feature freeze required; work spreads across two quarters.
*   **Benefit:** Maintains feature velocity; higher total cost due to extended timeline and coordination overhead.

**Key Risks**
Refactoring alone may not yield expected improvements if underlying architectural issues remain unaddressed. Success depends on coupling code cleanup with architectural correction.

**Recommendation Context**
The decision rests on whether the business prioritizes immediate cost efficiency and speed of resolution (Option A) or continuous feature delivery despite higher long-term costs (Option B).
