**Status: Orthorectification Pipeline**

The new pipeline is processing 40% more scenes per day. However, the atmospheric correction step introduces a color-shift artifact on images over snow-covered terrain. This affects approximately 5% of daily volume. Geometric accuracy remains intact; the issue is purely visual.

We have temporary workarounds, such as applying a different correction method for affected scenes, but this increases manual review time. We are currently tracking the volume of impacted imagery.

No decision is required at this time. We will open a ticket to scope and estimate a permanent fix during the next sprint. If the issue escalates, we may consider pausing the atmospheric correction step, though current conditions do not warrant that action.

Noted for tracking; will update once the fix is scoped.
