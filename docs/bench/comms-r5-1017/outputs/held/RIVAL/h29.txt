**Subject:** Update: Unified Patient Portal Rollout and SSO Issue

The unified patient portal is now live in 34 of our 43 clinics (78%). The remaining nine locations are on schedule for deployment next month.

We are currently facing a failure in the single sign-on (SSO) integration with the legacy radiology system. This issue does not block the overall rollout, but it requires clinicians to log in twice when accessing radiology records, which has generated complaints.

We have escalated the matter to the vendor, but they have not yet provided a timeline for a patch. At this stage, no decision is required from you. Our team prefers to wait for the vendor's fix rather than deploying a temporary workaround immediately. However, if the vendor does not provide a solution within two weeks, we will proceed with an interim measure to resolve the double-login issue.

Note: I will send another brief update if the vendor responds or if we reach the two-week mark without a patch.
