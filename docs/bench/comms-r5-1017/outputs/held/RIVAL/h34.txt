**Status Update: Automated Pallet-Mover Fleet**

The new automated fleet is currently exceeding performance targets, handling 42 moves per hour compared to the baseline of 36. This represents a 15% increase in throughput.

We are actively investigating an intermittent issue affecting one unit in the freezer aisle. Approximately every two hours, the unit loses its location beacon for about 30 seconds before recovering automatically. Preliminary checks confirm that beacon signal strength remains within normal parameters. We have not yet identified the specific cause of the interference; potential factors include the metal racking configuration, low temperatures, or an external source.

To maintain operations while we investigate, we have implemented a manual override for the affected aisle. This temporary measure adds approximately five minutes to each shift.

We expect to complete a full analysis and identify the root cause by next Tuesday. No action is required from you at this time; this update is to ensure you are aware of the situation and our current mitigation steps.

Note: The manual override ensures continuity despite the brief beacon losses. We will share the findings from next Tuesday's analysis as soon as they are ready.
