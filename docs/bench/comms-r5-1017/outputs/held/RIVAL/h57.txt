**Executive Summary: Device-Management Platform Evaluation**

**Decision Context**
A choice is required within one month between building a custom device-management platform (Option A) or purchasing a commercial solution (Option B) to support the transition to a subscription model for industrial sensors.

**Option A: Build In-House**
*   **Cost:** $1.8M upfront development; $200K annual maintenance.
*   **Timeline:** 14 months to develop.
*   **Success Probability:** 70%.
*   **Key Advantages:** Enables tight integration with proprietary protocols, potentially reducing data loss by 5% compared to the buy option. Offers a potential competitive advantage, with the possibility of licensing the platform to other manufacturers for an estimated $500K revenue in year two.
*   **Risks:** Lower probability of meeting requirements; longer time to market.

**Option B: Buy Commercial**
*   **Cost:** $1.2M upfront; $350K annual licensing.
*   **Timeline:** 6 months to implement.
*   **Success Probability:** 90%.
*   **Key Advantages:** Faster deployment; includes cloud service and mobile app; higher certainty of meeting baseline requirements.
*   **Risks:** Limited customization; potential vendor lock-in; risk that the vendor may not support specific sensor models long-term.

**Current Stance**
The evaluation team currently leans toward Option A (Build). This preference is driven by the strength of the in-house engineering team and the strategic necessity of integrating proprietary sensor protocols to minimize data loss.

**Financial Comparison (Year 2)**
*   **Build Total Cost:** $2.0M ($1.8M + $200K) minus potential $500K external revenue = Net $1.5M.
*   **Buy Total Cost:** $1.55M ($1.2M + $350K).

The decision rests on balancing the higher upfront cost and execution risk of building against the long-term strategic control and potential revenue upside, versus the speed and lower initial risk of buying with its accompanying limitations and recurring costs.

Note: The 5% data loss reduction and $500K revenue potential are projections included in the source report.
