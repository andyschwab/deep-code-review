**Batching audit-log writes cuts median latency from 12ms to 3ms; Priya must approve the approach and sign off on the untested crash-recovery risk.**

### Motivation
Under load, individual audit writes incurred 12ms latency. Buffering writes reduces this cost by amortizing I/O operations.

### Change
- **Buffering:** Writes are now buffered and flushed every 500ms or when the buffer reaches 2048 entries (whichever occurs first).
- **Files:** `audit_writer.go` (logic update), `batch.go` (new buffer implementation), `audit_writer_test.go` (coverage), `go.mod` (deps).
- **Behavior:** Median latency drops to 3ms. The 99th percentile latency increases to 40ms due to the flush interval wait time.

### Verification
- **Tests:** 142 total, 3 skipped, 0 failures.
- **Gap:** The failure-recovery path (process crash mid-batch requiring WAL replay) is **not tested**. This is a known risk requiring explicit acceptance.

### Request
**Priya:** Please confirm the batching approach is acceptable and sign off on shipping with the untested crash-recovery gap.
