**Mobile key adoption is 60% (1,200/2,000 guests); iPhone 8 users (10% of traffic) cannot unlock doors via Bluetooth, while a web app workaround is under reliability testing with a fix targeted by month-end.**

Adoption at the flagship property reached 1,200 out of 2,000 guests last week. The primary blocker is a Bluetooth handshake failure specific to the iPhone 8, affecting approximately 10% of guests. These users currently cannot unlock doors using the native mobile feature.

A workaround exists via the web app, though the experience is suboptimal. Engineering is currently testing this workaround to verify its reliability for immediate use. A permanent fix for the iPhone 8 Bluetooth issue is targeted for completion by the end of the month.

Failure rates on all other phone models stand at 2%, which remains within acceptable specifications. No decision or action is required from you at this time; the team is proceeding with the testing and fix implementation.

Note: All metrics reflect data from the flagship property for the previous week.
The "end of month" target is the current engineering estimate based on today's status.
