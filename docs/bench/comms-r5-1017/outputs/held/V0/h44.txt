**Fixes aggressive retries causing duplicate charges; Alex must decide whether to handle out-of-order events in this PR or file a follow-up.**

This change replaces the immediate retry loop with exponential backoff and jitter (1s to 5m over 5 attempts) in the payments webhook handler. The update reduces load and prevents duplicate charge attempts triggered by firing too aggressively.

**Known Gaps**

*   **High load:** The backoff behavior under high load has not been tested.
