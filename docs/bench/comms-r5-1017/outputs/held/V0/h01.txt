**Recommendation to VP of Operations: Adopt Option B (Forecasting Tool) to meet the 3% spoilage reduction target with lower upfront cost and zero operational disruption, contingent on a 2-week data quality audit.**

**Decision Context**
The board requires a 3% spoilage reduction this year. Budget allows for one initiative.

**Option Comparison**
*   **Option A (Sensor Upgrade):** $210K cost. Proven technology. Requires 2 weeks of operational disruption per store across 40 locations. Vendor availability: Next month.
*   **Option B (Forecasting Tool):** $180K cost. Software-based rollout (no store disruption). Dependent on historically spotty data quality. Integration timeline: 6 weeks.

**Rationale**
Option B is the preferred path because it preserves store operations during the critical rollout period and costs $30K less than Option A. While Option A offers proven hardware reliability, the cumulative 80 weeks of store disruption (2 weeks × 40 stores) poses a significant risk to sales and staffing that outweighs the benefit of "proven tech" for a spoilage target that software can theoretically address.

The primary risk for Option B is data quality. Since the current data is "spotty," the tool's effectiveness is not guaranteed without remediation. However, this risk is manageable via a targeted audit before full commitment, whereas the disruption from Option A is a guaranteed negative impact.

**Required Next Steps**
1.  **Immediate Action:** Initiate a 2-week data quality audit to validate if current datasets can support the forecasting tool.
2.  **Contingency:** If the audit fails, revert to Option A immediately to utilize the vendor's next-month start date.
3.  **Timeline:** Present final go/no-go decision to the VP by Friday.
