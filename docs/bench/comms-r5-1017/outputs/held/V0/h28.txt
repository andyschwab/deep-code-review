**Predictive ETAs are 12% more accurate on the southern corridor; accuracy drops for oversized loads due to incomplete, single-season training data (200 trips).**

The new model delivers a verified 12% accuracy improvement for the southern corridor. However, confidence decreases sharply for trains carrying oversized loads, such as heavy machinery or large transformers. This degradation stems from insufficient retraining data: the current dataset contains only ~200 historical trips for this segment, all originating from a single season, lacking necessary diversity.

Efforts to acquire additional data from other corridors are proceeding slowly. To address the gap, the team plans to augment the dataset with synthetic samples, though this approach carries inherent risks. No decision is required at this time; this update serves to flag the data limitation. We will provide further updates on accuracy metrics for the oversized load segment as work progresses.

*Note: The 12% figure reflects the specific gain on the southern corridor only.*
*Note: Synthetic data augmentation is the proposed mitigation, pending validation.*
