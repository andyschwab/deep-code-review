You write test scenarios for evaluating an AI assistant that helps people draft messages and short documents that other people will read without the writer present.

Write exactly 10 scenarios, one per seed in the list at the end, in order. Each scenario is a message a working software engineer, product manager or team lead would really send to an AI assistant: first person, plain text, and messy the way real pasted material is (fragments, abbreviations, a stray aside, numbers in the middle of a sentence). The requester pastes the raw material and asks for the finished piece, ready to send or paste.

Length of each scenario depends on the seed tag: [ask] raw notes 130 to 230 words for a message that asks a named senior reader to choose between two or three options, each option with at least one cost, time or risk figure; [status] raw notes 120 to 200 words for a status update to a named reader that asks for no decision; [diff] a diff summary (files changed, line counts, test results, one thing not tested) 160 to 260 words for a named reviewer; [report] a long report pasted in full, then the request for an executive summary for a named executive, 450 to 600 words.

Every scenario: includes at least two real numbers or dates and at least one unknown or open question (something the requester does not know or has not decided); says who will read the result and what that reader must do or decide. Recommendation: an [ask] or [report] seed that says "leans X" has the requester state that lean in the scenario in their own words; an [ask] or [report] seed that says "no lean" has the requester state no view; [status] and [diff] scenarios state no recommendation. Do not hint at what a good answer looks like. Do not mention any skill, framework, style guide, template or tool by name.

Rules: fictional companies, people and products only (for example Acme, Northwind, Jane, Ravi, Priya); each scenario must stand alone; plain text inside JSON strings, no markdown, use \n for line breaks.

Output only a JSON array of 10 objects {"id": "<two-character seed number>", "prompt": "<scenario text>"}. No code fence, no commentary.
