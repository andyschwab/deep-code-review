### Lane A: Unit Economics & Pricing Structure Analysis

**Current Baseline Math**
*   **Total Users:** 50,000
*   **Current Paid Subscribers:** 2,000 (4% of 50k)
*   **Current Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR):** $19,980 (2,000 × $9.99)
*   **Feature Engagement:** 15,000 free users engage weekly (30% of 48k free users).
*   **Current Costs:** $19,000/mo ($4k server + $15k marketing).
*   **Current Margin:** ~$980/mo (before other opex).

**Scenario: Monetizing Advanced Analytics at $2.99**
This is a **packaging decision** (Good-Better-Best vs. Add-on). The arithmetic depends entirely on two unknown variables you must test: **Conversion Rate (CR)** to the $2.99 tier and **Cannibalization Rate** (how many existing $9.99 users downgrade or cancel).

**The Formula for Net Revenue Impact:**
`Net Change = (New $2.99 Subs × $2.99) − (Lost $9.99 Subs × $9.99) − (Downgraded Users × $7.00)`

*Note: "Downgraded Users" are current $9.99 subscribers who drop to $2.99 only.*

**Sensitivity Analysis (Apply your test data here):**
If you target the 15,000 engaged free users:
*   **Optimistic Case:** 2% convert to $2.99 (300 users) = +$897. Zero cannibalization. **Net Gain: +$897.**
*   **Pessimistic Case:** 1% convert (150 users) = +$448. But 20 current subscribers ($199.80 value) cancel entirely due to frustration. **Net Loss: -$151.**
*   **Break-Even Point:** You need roughly **70 new $2.99 subscribers** just to offset the loss of **one** existing $9.99 subscriber.

**Decision Aid**
1.  **Read:** At a 6% monthly churn, losing even a small fraction of high-value users requires significant volume at the lower price point to recover.
2.  **Flip Input:** The critical variable is **Cannibalization**. If >0.5% of current subscribers downgrade or cancel, the strategy fails unless new conversion exceeds 3.5%.
3.  **Threshold Rule of Thumb:** If LTV of a $2.99 user is <30% of a $9.99 user (likely, given lower price), do not proceed if it risks >1% of the core base.
4.  **Next Step:** Run a **A/B test** on 10% of free users only. Do not roll out globally. Track: (a) Conversion to $2.99, (b) Churn of existing $9.99 users in the test group vs. control, (c) Support tickets citing "paywall frustration."

**Metrics to Track During Test**
*   **Incremental ARPU:** Does total revenue per user rise?
*   **Churn Delta:** Compare churn rates between test and control groups.
*   **Feature-Specific Conversion:** % of engaged free users who pay.
*   **Downgrade Rate:** % of existing subscribers who switch plans.

**Note:** Server costs may rise if the feature is compute-intensive; ensure contribution margin remains positive after variable costs.
**Note:** If the feature is essential to the core value proposition, gating it may violate user trust expectations established during the free period.
