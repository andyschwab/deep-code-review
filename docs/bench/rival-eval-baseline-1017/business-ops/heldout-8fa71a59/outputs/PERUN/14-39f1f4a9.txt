**2. Lifetime Value (LTV) – Simplified Model**
To calculate LTV, we must first determine the Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) and apply your churn estimate.
*   **Addressing your uncertainty:** Without individual customer logs, you cannot perfectly separate churn from usage fluctuation yet. For a seed-stage model, it is standard to treat the net revenue retention drop as "effective churn" until you have more data. We will use your estimated **5% monthly churn rate**.
*   **Average Monthly Revenue per Customer (Month 1):**

    *   **ARPU:** **$10.00**
*   **Gross Margin:** 80% (0.80)
*   **Monthly Churn Rate:** 5% (0.05)
*   **Formula:** `LTV = (ARPU × Gross Margin) ÷ Churn Rate`

**3. LTV:CAC Ratio**
Formula: `LTV ÷ CAC`

**4. Payback Period**
Formula: `CAC ÷ (ARPU × Gross Margin)`
