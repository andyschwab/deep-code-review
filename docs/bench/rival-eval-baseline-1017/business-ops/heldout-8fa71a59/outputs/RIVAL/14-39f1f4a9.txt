You observed revenue dropping from Month 1 to Month 2 (e.g., Jan: $2,000 → $1,800).

1.  **Logo Churn:** ~5% of customers cancelled.
2.  **Contraction/Usage Drop:** The remaining 95% of customers reduced their spend (downgrades or lower usage) by roughly another 5%.

**For LTV modeling:** You must use **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)**, not just logo churn. Your data shows an NRR of 90% ($1,800/$2,000). Using only the 5% churn rate would overstate your LTV. We will model based on the actual 10% monthly revenue decay observed.

### 3. Lifetime Value (LTV) Calculation
*Formula: (Average Monthly Revenue per Account × Gross Margin) / Monthly Revenue Churn Rate*

*   **Average Monthly Revenue per Account (ARPA):** $10 (based on Month 1).
*   **Gross Margin:** 80% (0.80).
*   **Monthly Revenue Churn:** 10% (0.10) — *Derived from the 90% retention observed.*

**LTV = ($10 × 0.80) / 0.10 = $8 / 0.10 = $80**

*Note: If you modeled this using only the 5% logo churn and ignored the usage drop, you would incorrectly calculate an LTV of $160, which would be dangerous for fundraising.*

### 5. Payback Period
*Formula: CAC / (Monthly ARPA × Gross Margin)*
*   **Numerator:** $50

2.  **Payback Period (6.25 months):** While under 12 months is generally acceptable, the low LTV ceiling limits your ability to reinvest in growth. You are barely breaking even on a lifetime basis after marketing costs.
