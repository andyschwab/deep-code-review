### Executive Summary: Do Not Sign Without Safeguards

Signing this deal as currently structured is **high-risk and strategically inadvisable**. While the guaranteed $500k in Year 1 covers your annual burn ($480k) and provides stability, the exclusivity clause creates a "ceiling" on your growth in a market projected to be worth significantly more ($1.2M total revenue × 15% = $180k potential *if* opened to multiple partners, but likely much higher if the market grows as implied).

The core trade-off is **certainty of survival vs. potential for scale**. You are trading the upside of a 15%+ growth engine for a fixed fee that merely keeps the lights on. If the distributor underperforms or the market explodes, you are contractually paralyzed.

### Critical Risks
1.  **Opportunity Cost:** If the Vietnam market grows 30% annually, your cap at $500k/$800k means you leave massive revenue on the table.
2.  **Single Point of Failure:** If the distributor fails to execute marketing or sales effectively, you cannot intervene or bring in other partners to fix it.
3.  **Valuation Impact:** Investors typically discount revenue dependent on a single exclusive channel partner compared to diversified, direct, or multi-partner revenue streams.

### Essential Contractual Safeguards
To protect your interests, you must convert this from a "fixed exclusivity" deal to a "performance-based exclusivity" deal. Insist on the following clauses:

**1. Minimum Performance Commitments (MPCs) with Clawbacks**
Do not accept a "guarantee" that only applies at the end of the year. Structure the deal with quarterly minimums.
*   **Clause:** "Exclusivity is contingent upon achieving 25% of the annual target per quarter."
*   **Remedy:** If they miss a quarterly target by >10%, exclusivity automatically converts to non-exclusive for the following quarter, allowing you to onboard other partners immediately until they catch up.

**2. The "Step-Up" or "Buy-Down" Clause**
Protect against the market growing faster than their guarantee.
*   **Clause:** If total transaction volume in Vietnam exceeds the guaranteed amount (e.g., >$500k), the commission rate paid to the distributor decreases on the excess volume, OR they must pay an additional fee to maintain exclusivity on that incremental growth.
*   **Alternative:** Allow SourceLink to sell directly to key enterprise accounts named in a "carve-out list," regardless of the exclusivity agreement.

**3. Short-Term Renewal & Termination for Cause**
Never lock a 2-year deal without an exit ramp.
*   **Clause:** The agreement renews annually, not bi-annually. Continuation into Year 2 is strictly conditional on 100% achievement of Year 1 targets.
*   **Termination:** Include a clause allowing termination with 30 days' notice if they fail to meet two consecutive quarterly targets.

**4. Marketing & Activity Obligations**
Volume doesn't happen by magic; it requires effort.
*   **Clause:** Define specific non-financial KPIs (e.g., number of manufacturer onboardings, marketing events hosted, leads generated). Failure to meet these activity metrics constitutes a breach of the exclusivity terms.

**5. Right of First Refusal (ROFR) vs. Absolute Exclusivity**
Instead of a hard ban on other partners, negotiate a ROFR.
*   **Clause:** SourceLink must offer new partnership opportunities to this distributor first. If they decline or cannot meet the projected volume within 14 days, SourceLink is free to engage other partners.

### Recommendation
Counter-offer with a **1-year exclusive pilot** with quarterly performance reviews and automatic conversion to non-exclusive upon missed targets. This secures your immediate burn coverage while preserving the option to capture the full 15%+ growth potential if the distributor proves incapable of scaling with the market.

*Note: Ensure legal counsel drafts the "conversion to non-exclusive" mechanism to avoid ambiguity. Do not rely on verbal assurances regarding market expansion.*
