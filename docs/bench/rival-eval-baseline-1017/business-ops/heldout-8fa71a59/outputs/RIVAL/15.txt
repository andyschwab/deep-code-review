### GreenGrid Grant Net Value Evaluation

**1. Direct Financial Calculation**
*   **Gross Grant Value:** $60,000
*   **Internal Labor Cost (Opportunity Cost):**
    *   CTO: 15 hrs/week × 26 weeks (6-month pilot) × $200/hr = $78,000
    *   Ops Lead: 10 hrs/week × 26 weeks × $200/hr = $52,000
    *   *Total Internal Labor:* **$130,000**
*   **Estimated Hidden Costs:**
    *   Legal Review (Terms/Clawbacks): ~$3,000–$5,000 (one-time)
    *   Third-party Audit Fees (if not fully covered): ~$2,000
    *   *Total Hidden Costs:* **~$6,000**
*   **Net Financial Impact:** $60,000 (Revenue) - $130,000 (Labor) - $6,000 (Fees) = **-$76,000 Loss**

**2. Strategic Opportunity Cost**
Your team is bootstrapped with only $50,000 in reserves. Dedicating 25 hours/week of your two most critical operators to compliance means:
*   **Product Stagnation:** The CTO cannot build features needed for *paid* commercial clients.
*   **Sales Paralysis:** The Ops Lead cannot onboard paying customers who require less bureaucracy.
*   **Cash Flow Risk:** Grants often reimburse *after* expenses are incurred. You may need to front costs for labor and audits before seeing a dime, threatening your $50k runway.

**3. Decision Framework: The "Distraction Ratio"**
Use this rule for future funding decisions:
*   **Formula:** (Grant Amount) ÷ (Total Hours Required × Fully Loaded Hourly Rate)
*   **Threshold:** If the result is **< 0.8**, the grant destroys value.
*   **Your Ratio:** $60,000 ÷ $130,000 = **0.46**
*   **Verdict:** For every $1.00 of grant money, you are spending $2.17 in high-value engineering time. This is a net destroyer of company value.

**Recommendation: Decline the Grant**
Unless this pilot provides a non-financial asset that guarantees $500k+ in future revenue (e.g., an exclusive case study with a major city that unlocks a specific enterprise market you cannot otherwise enter), **do not accept this grant.**

The compliance burden exceeds the payout, and the opportunity cost threatens your survival. Your limited team bandwidth is your scarcest resource; allocating 25 hours/week to administrative reporting instead of product development or sales is likely fatal for a bootstrapped startup. Focus entirely on securing paid commercial pilots where you control the scope, payment terms, and reporting requirements.

*Note: If you must pursue government work, negotiate a "pass-through" clause where the grant explicitly pays for external compliance staff or legal fees, rather than consuming your core team's time.*
*Only reconsider if the pilot contract includes an automatic conversion to a multi-year, no-bid commercial contract upon successful completion.*
