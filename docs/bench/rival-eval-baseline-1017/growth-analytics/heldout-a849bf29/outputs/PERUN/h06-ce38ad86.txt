### FitPulse Day-One Instrumentation Plan (MVP)

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Users completing a **Completed Workout** (value delivered), not total installs or pageviews.

**Priority 1: Instrument Immediately (Day One)**
These events answer the only questions that matter for retention and activation before your next code deploy. Do not instrument "just in case."

1.  **`workout_started`**
    *   **Question:** Do users initiate the core loop?
    *   **Properties:** `workout_type`, `entry_point` (e.g., home, notification).
2.  **`workout_completed`** (Critical)
    *   **Question:** Do users derive value? (This drives the North Star).
    *   **Properties:** `duration`, `workout_type`, `calories_logged`.
3.  **`progress_viewed`**
    *   **Question:** Does seeing history drive retention?
    *   **Properties:** `time_spent_seconds`, `chart_type_viewed`.
4.  **`app_opened`** (Session start)
    *   **Question:** Are users returning? (Required for retention cohorts).
    *   **Properties:** `user_id` (pseudonymous/stable), `days_since_install`.
5.  **`crash`** (Already tracked, ensure it includes `screen_name`).

**Priority 2: Defer to Post-Launch Release**
Do not block launch for these. If social sharing is a friction point, it will manifest as low retention in the core loop first. Fix the bucket before pouring water (acquisition/social) into it.

*   **Social Share Clicks/Success:** Instrument only after confirming users are retaining without it.
*   **Profile Edits / Settings Changes:** Low impact on immediate value verification.
*   **Tutorial Skips/Completes:** Unless activation is critically low, assume the UI works; verify via retention data first.

**Implementation Constraints & Rules**
*   **Identifier Seam:** Use a stable, pseudonymous `user_id` to track cohorts across sessions. Do not use raw PII here; route identity mapping to privacy compliance protocols.
*   **No Vanity Metrics:** Ignore "total screens viewed" or "buttons clicked" unless tied to the specific questions above.
*   **Baseline Only:** We will judge success against FitPulse's own Week 1 retention, not external industry benchmarks which are unavailable and often misleading.

**This Week's Action Steps**
1.  **Define Schema:** Finalize the property list for the five events above; reject any event that does not answer a named question.
2.  **Verify Pipeline:** Confirm that `workout_completed` flows to your dashboard with <24h latency before April 10.
3.  **Set Guardrail:** Establish a "stop-the-line" threshold if Day-7 retention of `workout_completed` users drops below 20% of Day-1 completers (adjust based on internal goals, not external data).

*Note: If social sharing is hypothesized as a primary growth engine, add a single `share_attempted` event now, but do not prioritize it over core loop completion.*
*Delay full social funnel analysis until the core retention bucket is proven leak-free.*
