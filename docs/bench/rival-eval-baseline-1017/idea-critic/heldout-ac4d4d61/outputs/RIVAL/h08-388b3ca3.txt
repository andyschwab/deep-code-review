*   **Network Topology vs. Raw Speed:** Averages hide spikes. Real-time applications often fail on p99 latency (the worst 1% of requests), not averages. A 15ms average increase could imply 100ms+ spikes during peak traffic, causing timeouts or dropped connections in real-time streams.

*   **Data Gravity:** If your users or dependent services remain close to your current provider's regions, moving compute away introduces physical distance latency that no amount of optimization can fix.
