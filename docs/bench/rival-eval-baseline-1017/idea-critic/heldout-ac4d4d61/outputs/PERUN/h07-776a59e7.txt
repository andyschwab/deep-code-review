**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" Sunset Plan, Not an Immediate Cut.**

Do not execute a hard sunset immediately. Instead, adopt a **Go-If** strategy: Commit to sunsetting the legacy line by year-end *only if* a controlled pilot migration achieves a 45% conversion rate with a Net Promoter Score (NPS) above -10 within the next 45 days. If the pilot fails these metrics, pause the sunset and switch to a "maintenance-only" mode to stabilize revenue while re-engineering the value proposition.

**Strategic Rationale & Stress Test**
Your assumption that 60% of users will migrate is likely overstated and represents the single greatest risk to this plan. Users relying on "critical workflows" exhibit high inertia; even with discounts, friction in migrating critical data often leads to churn rather than conversion. A forced migration of a loyal base risks converting 3,000 advocates into vocal detractors, potentially damaging the new platform's reputation before it gains traction.

The financial urgency is real ($60k quarterly loss), but a failed migration could incur higher hidden costs via brand damage and support overload. The "Do Nothing" option (continuing current operations) is unsustainable, but a "Hard Cut" is too binary. The middle path is a **validated migration**:
1.  **Cost/Risk:** A pilot limits exposure. If conversion is low, you stop the bleed on migration costs and preserve the remaining revenue stream longer.
2.  **Reversibility:** A pilot is a two-way door; a full sunset announcement is a one-way door that triggers immediate churn.
3.  **Evidence Needed:** You currently lack data on *why* users stay. Is it feature dependency or data lock-in? The pilot answers this.

**Decision Framework**
*   **Decision:** Go-If (Pilot Success).
*   **Kill Criteria:** If <45% of the pilot group converts or if negative sentiment spikes >15% in support tickets, halt the sunset plan immediately.
*   **Leading Metric:** Pilot Conversion Rate (target: 45%) and Migration NPS (target: >-10).
*   **Review Date:** August 15th (to allow time for final CEO decision by Sept 1st).

**Action Plan**
Immediately segment 500 users (approx. 17% of the base) representing your most critical workflows. Offer them the discounted transition package with white-glove support. Measure actual conversion and sentiment. Use this data to refine the 60% assumption before committing the entire base.

*Note: This approach converts your unknown churn variable into a measured statistic before the irreversible decision point.*
*Ensure the "discounted package" covers data migration services, as technical friction is the primary blocker for critical workflow users.*
