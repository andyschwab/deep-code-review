**Stress Test of the Two-Week Migration Window:**
The assumption that 12 TB can be migrated in two weeks is highly fragile. At a sustained transfer rate of 50 Mbps (a realistic conservative estimate for wide-area transfers accounting for protocol overhead and latency), moving 12 TB requires approximately 24 days of continuous transmission, exceeding your window before any validation, schema conversion, or cutover logic is applied. If your connection averages 100 Mbps, the transfer takes 12 days, leaving zero margin for error, re-syncs, or testing. Without a dedicated, high-bandwidth direct connect (which incurs additional setup time and cost), the two-week window is mathematically improbable for a zero-downtime cutover.
