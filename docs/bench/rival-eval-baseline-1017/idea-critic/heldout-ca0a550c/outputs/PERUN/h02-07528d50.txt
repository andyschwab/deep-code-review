**Recommendation: Apply the 15% increase only to new customers starting today; maintain current pricing for the 450 existing customers until their March 1st renewal.**

This approach maximizes net revenue over the next twelve months by eliminating the immediate, unquantified risk of mass churn during the renewal cycle. Your assumption that existing customer churn will remain under 5% is currently unverified and represents a critical single point of failure. Without data on price elasticity, applying a 15% hike to 450 enterprise accounts risks triggering a churn rate significantly higher than 5%, which would cause an immediate net revenue loss that new sales cannot offset within a year. Furthermore, enterprise contracts often contain negotiation clauses; pressing existing clients may result not in clean churn, but in prolonged negotiations that delay cash flow and damage brand trust, leading to down-sells rather than renewals.

By restricting the hike to new customers, you secure immediate margin expansion on all new logos without jeopardizing the recurring revenue base. This preserves brand trust with your current installed base, positioning price increases for existing customers as a future, data-driven decision rather than a reactive cost-covering measure. Use the time between now and March 1st to run targeted price sensitivity surveys or A/B tests on a small, non-critical segment of renewals to gather the elasticity data required for a future broad increase.

**Stress Test of Assumption:** The assumption that churn will stay below 5% fails if even 10% of customers (45 accounts) leave or refuse to renew. Given that you do not know the actual price elasticity, treating the 5% figure as fact is a gamble. If the real churn rate is 10-15% due to the hike, the lost annual contract value from existing customers would likely exceed the total additional revenue gained from the 15% increase, resulting in a net negative outcome for the year.

**Decision:** No-go for existing customer hikes at this time.
**Leading Metric:** Monitor renewal confirmation rates weekly; if any segment shows hesitation >2% above baseline, freeze all price change discussions immediately.
