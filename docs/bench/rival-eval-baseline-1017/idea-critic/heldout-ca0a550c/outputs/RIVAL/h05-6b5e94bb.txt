**Recommendation: Delay Launch to October 20th**

**Decision:** Do not launch on September 20th. The risk of permanent brand damage outweighs the $50,000 marketing loss.

**Rationale:**
An 8% crash rate on older devices is catastrophic for a mobile launch. App Store and Google Play algorithms heavily weigh early retention and crash-free session rates. A buggy launch will trigger a flood of 1-star reviews, causing organic visibility to collapse. Once a game is flagged as "unstable" by the algorithm and user base, recovery is statistically improbable, regardless of subsequent patches. You cannot fix a reputation deficit with code updates alone; you would need to re-launch under a new identity or spend significantly more than $50,000 on user acquisition to overcome the negative social proof.

The $50,000 marketing spend is a sunk cost if the product fails. Losing that amount is painful but survivable; losing the game's lifetime value due to a tainted store listing is existential.

**Stress Test: Is One Month Sufficient?**
Your assumption that a one-month delay guarantees a fix is **high-risk and unverified**.

1.  **Root Cause Unknown:** You have identified the symptom (8% crash on older devices) but not the cause. Is it a memory leak, a specific GPU driver incompatibility, or a third-party SDK failure? Without identifying the root cause, you cannot estimate the fix time. It could take 3 days or 3 months.
2.  **Regression Testing:** Fixing crashes on older devices often introduces instability on newer devices. A proper fix requires a full regression cycle across your entire device matrix, not just the failing units.
3.  **The "Fixed" Trap:** Developers often declare a issue "fixed" after verifying it on a few test devices. Real-world variance (OS versions, background processes, low storage) means the 8% could drop to 4%—still double your threshold—after a month of work.

1.  **Commit to Delay:** Frame the $50,000 as "insurance" against total product failure.
2.  **Demand a Diagnostic Sprint:** Before committing to the October date, require the engineering lead to produce a *Root Cause Analysis* within 5 days. If the cause is not found in 5 days, the one-month timeline is invalid.
