**Stress Test: The "5 Other Clients" Assumption**
Your assumption that this feature will sell to five other enterprises within a year is fragile and likely overstated due to three specific risks:

3.  **Maintenance Drag:** Even if generalized, custom features often create a "fork" in your codebase. If the architecture isn't perfectly abstracted, every future core update requires regression testing against this module, silently taxing engineering velocity for years.
