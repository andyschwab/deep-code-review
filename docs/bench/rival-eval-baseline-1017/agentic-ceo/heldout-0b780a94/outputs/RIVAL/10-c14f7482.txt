### Zenith Pricing Launch: Coordinated Work Session Plan

**Objective:** Sequence the 15% price increase launch to ensure support readiness precedes public announcement, mitigating churn risk.

#### 1. Critical Path Sequence
To prevent the irreversible risk of untrained support facing angry customers, the workflow must follow this strict order:
1.  **Experiment Setup (Technical):** Activate the 10% traffic split for the new pricing model. *Constraint: No external communication yet.*
2.  **Internal Enablement (Support):** Train support staff on the 20 updated macros using the live experiment data. Verify competency.
3.  **Public Announcement (Marketing):** Send the company-wide email to the 50,000 users and update public pricing pages.
4.  **Full Rollout:** Expand the experiment from 10% to 100% of new traffic only after steps 1–3 are confirmed complete.

#### 2. Decision Matrix: Sign-off vs. Delegation

| Decision Area | Requires Your Final Sign-off | Delegate To |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Messaging Tone** | **Yes.** Specifically the phrasing regarding "grandfathering" and the justification for the 15% increase. This sets the company voice. | Marketing Lead (Drafting options) |
| **Macro Content** | No. | Support Lead (Finalizing the 20 macros based on your tone guidelines) |
| **Experiment Config** | No. | Tech Lead (Setting the 10% split and monitoring metrics) |
| **Training Schedule** | No. | Support Lead (Organizing sessions to ensure completion before Dec 1) |
| **Send Time** | **Yes.** Final approval on the exact hour the announcement goes out relative to support shift changes. | Marketing Lead (Logistics) |

#### 3. Immediate Next Actions (First 3 Steps)
Execute these immediately to secure the timeline:

1.  **Conduct a "Tone & Policy" Alignment Meeting (30 mins):** Review the draft copy with the Marketing and Support leads. You must explicitly approve the final tone for the price increase justification. Once approved, delegate the final polishing of the 20 support macros to the Support Lead and the final email layout to the Marketing Lead.
2.  **Activate the Silent Experiment:** Instruct the Tech Lead to deploy the 15% price increase to exactly 10% of new traffic *immediately*, with all public-facing announcements paused. This generates real-world data for the support team to practice on without alerting the broader user base.
3.  **Lock the Training Deadline:** Set a hard deadline for Support Lead to certify that 100% of the support team has completed training on the new macros. This deadline must be at least 48 hours before the scheduled December 1st announcement to allow for buffer time.

***

*Note: Do not authorize the "Send" command for the user announcement until the Support Lead provides written confirmation that training is complete.*
*Ensure the Tech Lead has a rollback plan ready should the 10% experiment show unexpected technical errors before full rollout.*
