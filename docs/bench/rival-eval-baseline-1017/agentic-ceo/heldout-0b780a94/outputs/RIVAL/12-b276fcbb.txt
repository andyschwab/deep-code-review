### SkyNet Solutions Cloud Cost Reduction Plan

**Objective:** Reduce monthly cloud spend by $11,250 (25% of $45,000) within 45 days without impacting production latency or breaking integration tests.

#### Phase 1: Resource Auditing & Risk Segmentation (Days 1–10)
1.  **Tagging & Ownership Verification:** Immediately audit the three identified non-production environments. Verify resource tags to distinguish between team-specific assets and shared infrastructure.
2.  **Dependency Mapping:** Map all dependencies between non-production and production environments. Identify any "shared resources" currently running in non-prod that production relies on. These must be migrated to production-only clusters or isolated immediately to prevent accidental deletion.
3.  **Usage Pattern Analysis:** Analyze compute and storage metrics for the non-production environments to identify idle periods (nights, weekends) versus active integration test windows.

#### Phase 2: Negotiation & Shutdown Windows (Days 11–25)
1.  **Team Workshops:** Convene leads from the three distributed teams. Present usage data and propose "Shutdown Windows" (e.g., 7 PM – 7 AM and weekends) rather than permanent termination.
2.  **Test Schedule Alignment:** Negotiate specific time slots for integration tests. If teams require 24/7 access, challenge the necessity with data; if valid, explore spot instances or lower-tier hardware for these specific windows.
3.  **Agreement on Auto-Scaling Policies:** Define policies to automatically scale down non-essential resources outside agreed-upon windows.

#### Phase 3: Implementation & Monitoring (Days 26–45)
1.  **Phased Rollout:** Implement shutdown schedules for one environment at a time, monitoring for test failures for 48 hours before proceeding to the next.
2.  **Hard Limits:** Apply budget caps and auto-termination rules for non-compliant resources in non-production.
3.  **Verification:** Confirm the $11,250 reduction trajectory by day 40 to allow a 5-day buffer for adjustments.

---

### Approval Authority Matrix

**Must Be Approved Personally (High Risk/Irreversible):**
*   **Deletion of Shared Resources:** Any action involving resources tagged as "shared" or lacking clear single-team ownership.
*   **Architecture Changes:** Migrating services or altering network configurations that could inadvertently bridge non-prod and prod.
*   **Permanent Termination:** Shutting down an entire environment permanently rather than scheduling downtime.

**Delegatable to DevOps Lead (Medium/Low Risk):**
*   **Scheduling:** Implementing auto-start/stop scripts for nights and weekends.
*   **Right-sizing:** Downgrading instance types for non-critical dev tools.
*   **Storage Cleanup:** Archiving old logs or snapshots older than 30 days in non-prod.

### First Three Actions for Today

1.  **Freeze Deletions:** Issue an immediate moratorium on deleting *any* resource in the three non-production environments until the dependency map is complete. This prevents the irreversible risk of outage.
2.  **Extract Usage Reports:** Generate and review the last 30 days of CPU, memory, and network utilization reports for the three target environments to quantify potential savings from scheduling versus downsizing.
3.  **Schedule Stakeholder Kickoff:** Send calendar invites to the three team leads and the DevOps lead for a 45-minute session tomorrow to present the cost mandate and gather initial constraints on their testing windows.

*Note: This plan prioritizes safety over speed to ensure the 2 million daily users remain unaffected. Strict adherence to the approval matrix is required to mitigate outage risks.*
