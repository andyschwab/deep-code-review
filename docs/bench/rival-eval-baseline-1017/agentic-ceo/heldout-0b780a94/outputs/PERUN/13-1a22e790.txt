### Vertex Logistics Postmortem Agenda & Action Plan (72-Hour Sprint)

**Objective:** Produce a finalized, blame-free postmortem report with assigned action items within 72 hours.

#### Phase 1: Fact-Finding & Data Collection (Hours 0–12)
*Goal: Establish a single source of truth before discussion.*
1.  **Freeze & Gather:** Collect logs, metrics, and deploy timestamps from Network, Database, and App Engineering into a shared, read-only document. No interpretations yet, only raw data points aligned to the timeline.
2.  **Individual Pre-Reads:** Send a strict template to each team lead requiring:
    *   "What changed in your system 24h prior?"
    *   "What did your monitoring show at T-0?"
    *   "What hypothesis do you hold, and what data supports it?"
    *   *Constraint:* No blaming other teams in this section; focus strictly on own-system behavior.
3.  **Synthesis:** You (the leader) merge these into a unified "Master Timeline" highlighting gaps and contradictions.

#### Phase 2: Blame-Free Investigation Workshop (Hours 12–24)
*Goal: Derive root cause from system interactions, not human error.*
1.  **Review Master Timeline:** Walk through the event sequence chronologically.
2.  **The "Five Whys" Protocol:** For every failure point, ask "Why?" five times to move from "Team X deployed" to "The deployment pipeline lacked a specific integration test."
3.  **Interaction Mapping:** Specifically analyze hand-offs between Network, DB, and App layers. Most outages occur in the gaps between owned systems.
4.  **Output:** A draft Root Cause statement focusing on process/tooling gaps, not individual actions.

#### Phase 3: Remedy Planning & Ownership (Hours 24–36)
*Goal: Assign effective fixes without creating future bottlenecks.*
1.  **Generate Countermeasures:** Brainstorm fixes for the root causes identified.
2.  **Ownership Decision Matrix:**
    *   *Technical Leads Decide:* Specific implementation details, code changes, and config updates.
    *   *You Decide:* Cross-team process changes, budget for new tooling, and any fix where two teams claim "not our scope."
3.  **Deadlock Breaking Rule:** If teams disagree on ownership, assign the fix to the team closest to the *user impact* or the team that controls the *upstream dependency*. Revisit in 30 days if data proves misalignment.

#### Phase 4: Drafting & Alignment (Hours 36–60)
1.  **Draft Report:** Compile findings, root cause, and action items (with owners and due dates).
2.  **Pre-Read Circulation:** Send to all three leads with a hard deadline for written objections. Silence implies consensus.
3.  **Resolution Call:** A 30-minute sync to address any remaining written objections.

#### Phase 5: Finalization (Hours 60–72)
1.  **Final Edit:** Incorporate agreed changes.
2.  **Distribution:** Send to enterprise clients and internal stakeholders.

### Critical Decisions for You to Make
*   **Scope of Blame:** Explicitly state that the postmortem analyzes *systems and processes*, not individuals. Interrupt any personal attribution immediately.
*   **Ownership Disputes:** When Team A and Team B argue over a systemic fix, you must assign it based on who controls the critical path, overriding technical debates to meet the deadline.
*   **Resource Allocation:** Approve immediate time-boxed effort for data gathering, shielding engineers from other tasks during the 12-hour collection window.
