### Session Plan: MapStream Migration (6-Week Deadline)

**Execution Order**
1.  **Week 1: Parallel Prototyping & Feature Gap Analysis.** Spin up two isolated branches. Implement the 4,000 lines of rewritten logic for both vendors simultaneously. Do not sequence them; parallel execution is required to identify the "perfect feature match" gap immediately.
2.  **Weeks 2–3: Comparative Load Testing & Accuracy Validation.** Run both prototypes against historical traffic data from your 1,200 vehicles. Measure latency, cost, and specifically the "few meters" deviation risk. The vendor with >99.9% coordinate accuracy under load proceeds; the other is discarded.
3.  **Week 4: Staged Canary Deployment.** Switch 5% of the fleet (60 vehicles) to the selected provider in production. Monitor routing success rates and driver reports for 72 hours.
4.  **Week 5: Full Cutover.** If the canary holds, migrate the remaining 95%. Keep MapStream credentials active but unused until January 15th as a rollback trigger.
5.  **Week 6: Contingency Buffer.** Reserved strictly for emergency hotfixes if the canary reveals edge cases.

| **Owner Decisions (Strategic/Business)** | **Engineering Delegations (Technical Validation)** |

**First Three Immediate Actions**
1.  **Freeze Non-Essential Work:** Halt all feature development not related to this migration. Reassign 100% of available senior backend engineering capacity to the two prototype branches immediately.
2.  **Secure Historical Data Snapshot:** Extract and anonymize the last 30 days of GPS traces and routing decisions from your 1,200 vehicles. This dataset is the ground truth required for Week 2's accuracy validation; without it, you cannot verify the "few meters" risk.
3.  **Initiate Vendor Proof-of-Concept (PoC) Keys:** Contact both shortlisted vendors today to request enterprise-tier sandbox access with rate limits matching your production volume (1,200 vehicles). Do not wait for sales cycles; escalate to technical account managers to guarantee immediate key delivery.

**Note:** The 4,000-line rewrite is the critical path; if engineering estimates exceed 3 weeks, you must descope non-critical routing features immediately.
**Note:** Do not attempt a "big bang" cutover on January 14th; the canary phase in Week 4 is mandatory to prevent fleet-wide stranding.
