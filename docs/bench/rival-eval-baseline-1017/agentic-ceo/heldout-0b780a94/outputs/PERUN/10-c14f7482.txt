### Zenith Pricing Launch: Critical Path & Immediate Actions

**Sequenced Workflow**
1.  **Experiment Setup & Validation (Days 1–3):** Configure the 10% traffic split for the new pricing tier. Verify that grandfathering logic correctly excludes existing users. *Constraint:* Do not enable the public-facing price display yet; keep the experiment backend-only until support readiness is confirmed.
2.  **Support Macro Update & Training (Days 4–10):** Update the twenty support macros to reflect the new tiers and justification scripts. Conduct mandatory training sessions for the support team. *Gate:* Support Lead must sign off on team competency before proceeding.
3.  **Internal Announcement (Day 11):** Distribute the company-wide announcement to align internal teams. Ensure all non-support staff know the effective date and where to direct customer queries.
4.  **Public Launch & Experiment Go-Live (December 1):** Activate the public price display for the 10% test cohort. Monitor sentiment and churn metrics closely.

**Decision Rights Matrix**
*   **Requires Your Final Sign-off:**
    *   **Messaging Tone:** The specific phrasing used in the public announcement and support macros regarding the 15% increase. This defines brand voice during a sensitive change.
    *   **Experiment Parameters:** Final approval on the 10% traffic allocation and the specific start date/time.
    *   **Go/No-Go Decision:** The final authorization to move from "Support Training" to "Public Launch" based on the Support Lead's readiness report.
*   **Delegated to Leads:**
    *   **Support Lead:** Updating the twenty macros, scheduling training, assessing team readiness, and managing initial customer tickets during the rollout.
    *   **Marketing Lead:** Drafting the company-wide announcement, scheduling its distribution, and setting up the dashboard for monitoring sentiment/churn metrics.

**First Three Immediate Actions**
1.  **Freeze the Copy:** Lock the draft messaging for both support macros and the public announcement. No further edits allowed without your direct approval to prevent scope creep and confusion.
2.  **Audit the Grandfathering Logic:** Have engineering verify specifically that existing users are mathematically excluded from the 15% increase in the current build. This is the highest technical risk.
3.  **Schedule the Support Training Block:** Book the mandatory training sessions for the support team immediately, working backward from December 1st to ensure completion at least 48 hours before the planned launch.

**Note:** The irreversible risk is strictly the timing gap between announcement and support readiness; do not communicate externally until the Support Lead confirms training is complete.
