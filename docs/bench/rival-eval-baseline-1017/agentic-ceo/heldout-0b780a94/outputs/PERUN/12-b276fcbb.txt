### Executive Summary: 45-Day Cloud Cost Reduction Plan

**Objective:** Reduce monthly cloud spend by $11,250 (25% of $45k) within 45 days.
**Constraints:** Zero impact on 2M daily users; zero disruption to integration tests.
**Primary Risk:** Accidental deletion of shared production resources.

#### 1. Auditing and Negotiation Sequence
To ensure safety before speed, execute the following order:
1.  **Resource Tagging & Dependency Mapping (Days 1–5):** Audit the three non-production environments. Explicitly tag every resource as `Prod-Shared`, `NonProd-Isolated`, or `Unknown`. Do not touch `Unknown` items. Verify that no "non-prod" environment holds a hard dependency on a `Prod-Shared` resource (e.g., a specific database subnet or IAM role).
2.  **Team Negotiation & Window Definition (Days 6–10):** Present the dependency map to the three distributed teams. Negotiate specific "shutdown windows" (e.g., nights and weekends) rather than permanent termination. Agree on a "break-glass" procedure for each team to instantly restore resources if tests fail.
3.  **Phased Implementation (Days 11–45):**
    *   *Phase A:* Implement auto-shutdown schedules for clearly isolated resources.
    *   *Phase B:* Right-size remaining always-on non-prod instances (reduce vCPU/RAM) rather than shutting them down completely.
    *   *Phase C:* Archive and delete old snapshots/volumes identified in the audit.

#### 2. Approval Authority Matrix
Clear delegation is required to maintain velocity without accepting irreversible risk.

| Action Type | Risk Profile | Authority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Scheduling Shutdowns** | Reversible (can restart) | **DevOps Lead** (Execute immediately upon team agreement) |
| **Right-sizing Instances** | Reversible (can scale up) | **DevOps Lead** (Within pre-agreed performance bounds) |
| **Deleting Snapshots/Logs** | Irreversible (data loss) | **You (CEO/Requester)** (Must approve specific resource IDs) |
| **Modifying Shared Networks** | Critical (outage risk) | **You (CEO/Requester)** (Strictly forbidden for Non-Prod modification) |
| **Emergency Restoration** | High (time sensitivity) | **DevOps Lead** (Auto-approved to restore service) |

#### 3. Immediate Actions for Today
Start the reduction process immediately with these three low-risk, high-visibility steps:

1.  **Initiate the "Read-Only" Audit:** Instruct the DevOps Lead to generate a cost-allocation report for the last 30 days, filtered strictly for the three identified non-production environments. Output must list every running instance and storage volume with its current tags. **Do not make changes yet.**
2.  **Freeze Non-Essential Provisioning:** Issue an immediate temporary freeze on creating *new* non-production resources until the audit is complete. This stops the bleeding while you plan the cuts.
3.  **Schedule the Alignment Meeting:** Send a calendar invite to the leads of the three distributed teams for a 30-minute sync tomorrow. The agenda is solely: "Reviewing non-prod dependency maps to define safe shutdown windows." Do not discuss cuts yet; discuss safety first.

**Note:** This plan prioritizes reversible actions (scheduling, resizing) to meet the 25% target before considering irreversible deletions.
**Note:** If the audit reveals that production depends on these "non-prod" environments, the strategy must shift to right-sizing rather than shutting down.
