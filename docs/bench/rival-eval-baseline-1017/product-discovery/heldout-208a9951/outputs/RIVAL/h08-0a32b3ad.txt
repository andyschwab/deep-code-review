### Enhanced Demand Test Framework for FitTrack Corporate

**Objective:** Move beyond "interest" (clicks) to validate "purchase intent" and "willingness to pay" before building the prototype.

#### 1. The Experiment Design: "The Commitment Ladder"
Replace the single "Learn More" link with a multi-step landing page flow that requires increasing levels of commitment. This filters out casual curiosity from genuine need.

*   **Step 1 (The Hook):** Email describes the specific corporate problem solved (e.g., "Reduce employee burnout by 20%").
*   **Step 2 (The Value Prop):** Landing page details features and, crucially, displays a **price range** (e.g., "$5–$8 per employee/month") or a "Starting at $X" model. *Do not hide pricing.*
*   **Step 3 (The Micro-Commitment):** Instead of just "Learn More," offer two distinct Call-to-Action (CTA) buttons:
    *   *Option A (Low Intent):* "Download the Corporate Wellness Guide" (Captures email only).
    *   *Option B (High Intent):* "Join the Pilot Waitlist" or "Request a Pilot Quote."
*   **Step 4 (The Validation Gate):** If they click Option B, present a short form asking:
    *   Company size.
    *   "Who is the decision-maker for wellness budgets?" (Tests if the user has influence).
    *   "When are you looking to implement a solution?" (Tests urgency).
    *   *Optional but powerful:* "Would you be willing to sign a Letter of Intent for a pilot at $X/user?" (Checkbox).

#### 2. Specific Metrics for Go/No-Go
Discard the generic 5% click-through rate. Use these tiered metrics to gauge genuine intent:

*   **Metric A: Price-Visible Click-Through Rate.**
    *   *Definition:* % of email recipients who click through *after* seeing the price mention in the email or immediately on the landing page.
    *   *Target:* >2% (Lower than generic clicks, but higher quality).
*   **Metric B: High-Intent Conversion Rate.**
    *   *Definition:* % of landing page visitors who choose Option B (Waitlist/Quote) over Option A (Guide).
    *   *Target:* >10% of visitors. If everyone wants the free guide but no one wants the quote, there is no demand.
*   **Metric C: Qualified Lead Rate.**
    *   *Definition:* % of High-Intent users who complete the form indicating they have budget authority and a timeline <6 months.
    *   *Target:* 15–20 qualified leads from the 500-user list.
*   **Metric D: The "Skin in the Game" Metric.**
    *   *Definition:* Number of users who agree to a follow-up call or sign a non-binding Letter of Intent.
    *   *Target:* 5+ scheduled discovery calls.

#### 3. Follow-Up Actions for Lukewarm Response
If metrics fall below targets (e.g., high clicks but zero form completions):

*   **Action 1: Conduct "The Mom Test" Interviews.**
    *   Reach out to the users who clicked but *didn't* convert. Ask: "I noticed you checked out the corporate page but didn't request a quote. Can you walk me through what was missing?" Focus on past behaviors, not hypothetical futures.
*   **Action 2: Test the Value Proposition, Not the Product.**
    *   The issue may be the messaging or the price anchor. A/B test the email with a different problem statement (e.g., focus on "retention" vs. "health") to see if the pain point resonates.
*   **Action 3: Pivot the Segment.**
    *   Analyze if the individuals on your list actually hold budget authority. If they are individual contributors, your next test must target HR Directors or Benefits Managers directly, not employees.
*   **Action 4: The "Concierge" Test.**
    *   Before building software, offer to manually solve the problem for one company using spreadsheets and existing tools for a fee. If they won't pay for the manual service, they won't pay for the software.

#### Decision Matrix for Executives
*   **GO:** >5 Qualified Leads + >3 Scheduled Calls. Proceed to prototype.
*   **NO-GO:** <2 Qualified Leads + Zero willingness to discuss budget. Kill idea or pivot segment.
*   **ITERATE:** High clicks but low form completion. Refine messaging/pricing and re-test in 2 weeks.

*Note: Ensure your email compliance (GDPR/CAN-SPAM) allows for B2B outreach to existing individual users regarding corporate products.*
*Timebox this entire experiment to 10 days to meet your end-of-month deadline.*
