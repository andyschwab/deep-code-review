### Risk Assessment: DataStream Reporting Feature

**Executive Summary**
The decision to deprecate or improve the reporting feature hinges on a single-point-of-failure risk (MegaCorp) versus a chronic efficiency drain (20 engineering hours/month). Immediate deprecation carries a high probability of losing $100k ARR and damaging enterprise trust. However, the current "keep as-is" strategy incurs a hidden annual cost of ~$24k–$48k in engineering opportunity costs while failing to serve 97% of the user base.

**Risk Analysis by Category**

1.  **Viability Risk (Financial Impact)**
    *   **Threat:** Deprecating the feature risks immediate churn of MegaCorp ($100k/year). If this signals to other enterprise clients that compliance needs are not prioritized, the reputational damage could exceed the single contract value.
    *   **Opportunity Cost:** The feature consumes ~240 engineering hours annually (20 hrs × 12 months). If fully loaded engineering cost is $100/hr, this is a $24k direct burn. If those hours were spent on features for the 97% majority, the potential revenue uplift or retention gain is unknown but currently forfeited.
    *   **Assessment:** The financial risk of losing MegaCorp outweighs the direct engineering cost of maintenance in the short term.

2.  **Feasibility & Technical Debt Risk**
    *   **Current State:** The module is unstable (constant bug fixes), indicating fragile architecture or outdated dependencies.
    *   **Trajectory:** Without investment, bug frequency will likely increase as the codebase ages, escalating the 20-hour monthly burden.
    *   **Assessment:** High risk of escalating maintenance costs. The "do nothing" approach is technically unsustainable.

3.  **Value Risk (Market Fit)**
    *   **Observation:** Only 3% adoption suggests the feature is either too niche, too difficult to use, or poorly marketed.
    *   **Hypothesis:** The low usage may be a usability issue rather than a lack of need. If the feature is critical for compliance, other users might need it but cannot figure out how to activate it.
    *   **Assessment:** Moderate risk. The value exists (proven by MegaCorp), but accessibility is failing.

**Strategic Recommendations**

**Option A: Immediate Deprecation (Not Recommended)**
*   **Outcome:** High probability of losing MegaCorp ($100k loss). Engineering bandwidth freed immediately.
*   **Verdict:** Too risky without a mitigation plan.

**Option B: Targeted Stabilization & Validation (Recommended)**
*   **Action 1 (Client Retention):** Engage MegaCorp immediately. Acknowledge the bugs and propose a "Compliance Stability Sprint." Ask if they would accept a temporary manual workaround or a simplified version if it guarantees uptime, buying time for research.
*   **Action 2 (Root Cause Analysis):** Interview 5–10 users from the 3% who *do* use the feature. Determine if the low adoption is due to discoverability, complexity, or lack of need.
*   **Action 3 (Cost-Benefit Test):** Allocate exactly 40 engineering hours (one sprint) to refactor the most critical bugs. Measure if maintenance drops below 10 hours/month. If it remains high, the technical debt is too deep.
*   **Action 4 (Monetization Check):** Consider moving this feature to an "Enterprise Tier" add-on. If only 3% need it, it may justify a higher price point that covers its own maintenance costs.

**Final Decision Framework for CTO**
Do not deprecate yet. The $100k anchor client makes the feature viable *today*. However, treat it as a legacy module.
1.  **Freeze** all new feature development on this module.
2.  **Allocate** one sprint to stabilize critical bugs specifically for MegaCorp's workflow.
3.  **Validate** if the 3% usage can be converted to a paid enterprise add-on to offset engineering costs.
4.  **Re-evaluate** in 60 days: If maintenance hours do not drop by 50% post-refactor, present MegaCorp with a migration path to a custom solution while officially deprecating the core module.

*Note: This assessment assumes the $100k contract is renewable soon; if the contract is multi-year locked, the urgency to fix bugs decreases slightly.*
*Next step: Schedule a discovery call with MegaCorp's compliance lead before the CTO review.*
