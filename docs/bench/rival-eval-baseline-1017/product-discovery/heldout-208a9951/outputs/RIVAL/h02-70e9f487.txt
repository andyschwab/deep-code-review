# Integration Prioritization Decision Matrix

**Objective:** Select one integration to deliver by end of Q3 based on revenue risk vs. market expansion potential.

| Integration Option | Immediate Revenue Risk | Long-Term Market Expansion | Strategic Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | : |
| **Legacy Tracking API**<br>(3 Clients) | **CRITICAL**<br>• Accounts for 20% of total billings.<br>• Churn risk is unknown but immediate.<br>• Loss would create a significant revenue hole. | **LOW**<br>• Only 3 clients requested this.<br>• Likely niche use case for legacy systems.<br>• Limited appeal to new prospects. | **Defensive Play**<br>Prioritize only if churn confirmation exceeds 15% of billings. High risk of building a "custom" solution with low scalability. |
| **ERP System Connection**<br>(8 Clients) | **MODERATE**<br>• No immediate churn threat cited.<br>• Assumes $50k ARR upside.<br>• Validates core enterprise workflow. | **HIGH**<br>• Highest request volume (8 clients).<br>• ERP connectivity is a standard enterprise requirement.<br>• Unlocks broader market entry and upsell potential. | **Offensive Play (Recommended)**<br>Best balance of demand volume and growth. Addresses the most common enterprise barrier to entry. |
| **HR Software Sync**<br>(5 Clients) | **LOW**<br>• Lowest perceived urgency.<br>• No specific revenue threat identified. | **MEDIUM**<br>• Moderate request volume (5 clients).<br>• Useful feature but likely secondary to core logistics/ERP data. | **Defer**<br>Lacks the urgency of Legacy or the growth ceiling of ERP. |

### Final Recommendation: Prioritize ERP System Connection

**Rationale:**
While the Legacy Tracking request carries the highest *immediate* financial threat, building a custom API for three legacy tools is a low-scalability effort that may not prevent churn if the underlying issue is broader platform fit. Conversely, the ERP integration addresses the largest group of prospects (8 clients) and aligns with the strategic goal of market expansion ($50k ARR potential).

**Critical Pre-Condition:**
Before committing engineering resources to the ERP build, the Product Lead must immediately contact the three Legacy Tracking accounts to quantify churn probability. If the risk of losing 20% of billings is confirmed as >80% without an immediate fix, the decision shifts to a short-term "patch" for Legacy clients while the ERP integration proceeds as the primary Q3 roadmap item. If the churn risk is negotiable, proceed directly with ERP.

---
*Note: This matrix assumes engineering effort is roughly equivalent across options; if Legacy API complexity is 3x higher, the recommendation strongly favors ERP.*
