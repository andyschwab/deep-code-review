# Research Plan: LedgerLite Onboarding Drop-off Investigation

## 1. Recruitment Strategy
**Target Segment:** Prioritize the 15 beta users who **partially completed** the flow over those who dropped off immediately.
*   **Rationale:** Users who stopped immediately may have encountered a technical blocker or lost interest before engaging with the content. Users who started but didn't finish have demonstrated intent and have experienced the specific friction points within the tutorial steps. They can articulate *where* and *why* they stopped.
*   **Sampling:** Recruit 8 users (to ensure 6 completed interviews given no-shows).
    *   **Criteria:** Signed up in beta, started the new onboarding flow, did not complete the final step.
    *   **Exclusion:** Users who never clicked "Start Tutorial."
*   **Outreach Script Focus:** "We noticed you started setting up your LedgerLite account but didn't finish. We aren't selling anything; we want to understand what got in your way so we can fix it for you."

## 2. Interview Questioning Strategy
**Objective:** Uncover specific friction points and validate the "lengthy steps" hypothesis without leading the witness. Follow *The Mom Test* principles: focus on past behavior, not future opinions.

### Opening (2 min)
*   "Thanks for joining. We're looking to improve the LedgerLite setup experience. There are no right or wrong answers; we just want to hear about your actual experience trying to get started."
*   Confirm permission to record.

### Warm-Up: Context (3 min)
*   "Walk me through what was happening in your work/life when you decided to sign up for LedgerLite."
*   "What were you hoping to accomplish specifically that day?"

### Core Exploration: The Onboarding Event (15 min)
*   **Reconstruct the timeline:** "Take me back to the moment you started the new tutorial. What was the very first thing you saw?"
*   **Specific actions:** "Walk me through the steps you actually completed. What did you do next?" (Listen for hesitation or skipping).
*   **The Drop-off Point:** "You mentioned you stopped at [Step X]. What was going through your mind right then?"
    *   *Probe:* "Can you show me (or describe) exactly what was on the screen?"
    *   *Probe:* "What else were you doing at that moment?"
*   **Testing the Hypothesis (Non-leading):**
    *   *Bad:* "Was the tutorial too long?"
    *   *Good:* "How much time did you spend on that step before moving on or stopping?"
    *   *Good:* "What was the hardest part about getting through those steps?"
    *   *Good:* "Did you feel you were making progress? How did you know?"
*   **Value Perception:** "At what point, if any, did you feel the app was useful to you?"
*   **Alternative Actions:** "After you stopped the tutorial, what did you do instead? Did you try to use the app another way, or did you close it?"

### Wrap-Up (5 min)
*   "If you could change one thing about that setup process to make it easier for you, what would it be?"
*   "Is there anything I didn't ask that you think is important?"

## 3. Data Synthesis Plan
*   **Immediate Debrief:** After each interview, the researcher and PM will spend 5 minutes mapping the user's journey against the current flow to tag exact drop-off coordinates.
*   **Pattern Matching:** Look for convergence on specific steps, wording, or time thresholds across the 6 interviews.
*   **Output:** A friction map highlighting the top 3 barriers preventing completion.
