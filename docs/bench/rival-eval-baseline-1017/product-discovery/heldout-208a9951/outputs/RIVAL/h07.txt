# Q3 Discovery Roadmap: Employee Directory Optimization

**Objective:** Validate the severity of support issues to align engineering sprints with actual user impact, moving beyond ticket volume alone.

### Priority 1: Search Functionality (High Frequency, Unknown Severity)
*Hypothesis:* Search failures are preventing users from finding colleagues, causing workflow delays despite low "urgent" tagging.
*   **Discovery Action:** Conduct 5–7 targeted customer interviews with users who submitted search tickets.
*   **Key Questions:**
    *   "Walk me through the last time you tried to find a colleague. What specifically happened?"
    *   "What did you do immediately after the search failed? Did you find an alternative way?"
    *   "How did this impact your ability to complete your task (e.g., sending an invite, checking org chart)?"
*   **Success Metric:** Confirm if search failures result in abandoned tasks or significant time loss (>5 mins).
*   **Engineering Alignment:** Reserve 20% of sprint capacity for search logic refactoring *only if* interviews confirm blocked workflows.

### Priority 2: Profile Photo Uploads (Medium Frequency, High Friction Potential)
*Hypothesis:* Upload failures create emotional friction and identity verification issues, even if ticket volume is lower than search.
*   **Discovery Action:** Analyze the 30 ticket threads for error patterns and attempt to reproduce the top 3 failure scenarios in a staging environment.
*   **Key Questions (for follow-up surveys):**
    *   "How many times did you attempt to upload your photo before giving up or submitting a ticket?"
    *   "Is having a visible photo critical for your daily role (e.g., remote teams, client-facing)?"
*   **Success Metric:** Identify if this is a "one-and-done" annoyance or a recurring barrier for new hires.
*   **Engineering Alignment:** Assign a junior engineer to fix specific error-handling cases if reproduction confirms a bug; otherwise, improve error messaging.

### Priority 3: UI Glitches Cluster (High Volume, Low Cohesion)
*Hypothesis:* The 80 scattered tickets represent diverse, low-impact visual bugs that do not warrant a dedicated feature sprint.
*   **Discovery Action:** Perform a quantitative tag analysis to group the 80 tickets into sub-categories (e.g., mobile responsiveness, alignment, color contrast).
*   **Key Action:** Review usage analytics to see if these glitches correlate with drop-off rates or session abandonment.
*   **Success Metric:** Determine if any single sub-category exceeds 15 tickets. If not, classify as "maintenance debt."
*   **Engineering Alignment:** Do not create a specific project. Instead, add validated high-frequency glitches to the general "Tech Debt" backlog for ad-hoc resolution during slack periods.

### Next Steps for Product Ops
1.  **Week 1:** Complete interviews and reproduction tests.
2.  **Week 2:** Synthesize findings into a "Go/No-Go" recommendation for the Search project.
3.  **Month 1 Goal:** Finalize Q3 scope based on validated behavioral data, not just ticket counts.

*Note: This plan assumes access to the raw ticket logs for user recruitment. If interview recruitment fails, substitute with a targeted in-app survey asking about the specific friction points.*
