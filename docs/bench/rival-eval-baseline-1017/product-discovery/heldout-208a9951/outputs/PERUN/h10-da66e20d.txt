### Prioritization Framework: Riskiest-Assumption Gate

To select the two assumptions for your workshop, do not guess. Use this blank scoring table. Fill in the **Impact** (1–10: how badly failure hurts the product) and **Uncertainty** (1–10: how little hard evidence you currently have) for each of your ten whiteboard items. **Selection Rule:** Pick the top two ranked assumptions. If "Data Accuracy" and "Adoption Frequency" are indeed the top two, proceed. If another assumption scores higher (e.g., "Managers will pay for this" or "We can access the data legally"), test that instead. High impact + high uncertainty = highest risk.

### Stakeholder Roadmap Status
For your presentation on the 20th:
*   **Validated:** Assumptions where the test met the success criteria.
*   **Invalidated:** Assumptions that hit the Kill Threshold (this is valuable learning, not failure).
*   **Unverified:** Assumptions not yet tested due to the 2-experiment limit. Explicitly list these as "High Risk - Pending Test" in your roadmap. Do not claim they are true.

**Note:** Your intuition that "data accuracy" is critical is only correct if the *uncertainty* about it is also high. If you already have internal logs proving accuracy, its risk score drops, and user adoption may become the true priority. Let the math decide.
**Note:** Do not build features to test these assumptions. Use concierge methods (manual simulation) or fake-door tests to gather behavioral evidence within your three-week constraint.
