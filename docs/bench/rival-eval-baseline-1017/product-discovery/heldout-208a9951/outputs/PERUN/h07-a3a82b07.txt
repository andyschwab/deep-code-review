### Q3 Discovery Roadmap Draft: Employee Directory

**Objective:** Validate whether high-frequency support tickets represent critical workflow blockers or minor annoyances before committing engineering resources.

**Priority 1: Search Functionality (40 Tickets)**
*   **Hypothesis:** Search failures are preventing users from completing core tasks (e.g., contacting colleagues, finding org charts), not just causing frustration.
*   **Discovery Action:** Conduct 5 "Switch Interviews" with users who submitted search tickets.
    *   *Ask:* "Walk me through the last time you used search. What specific task were you trying to finish? What did you do when search failed?"
    *   *Metric to Define:* % of search failures that forced a workaround (e.g., calling IT, asking a manager) vs. those where the user gave up on a non-critical task.
*   **Decision Gate:** If >50% of failures blocked a critical task, prioritize fix. If mostly minor annoyance, deprioritize despite volume.

**Priority 2: Profile Photo Uploads (30 Tickets)**
*   **Hypothesis:** Upload failures are causing onboarding delays or compliance issues, or they are purely vanity issues with low business impact.
*   **Discovery Action:** Analyze ticket context for user role and timing.
    *   *Ask:* "Is this preventing you from completing your first-week onboarding checklist? Does your team require visible photos for recognition?"
    *   *Metric to Define:* Correlation between upload tickets and "time-to-productivity" for new hires.
*   **Decision Gate:** If linked to onboarding delays, treat as high severity. If purely aesthetic, bundle into a low-priority UI polish sprint.

**Priority 3: Minor UI Glitches (80 Tickets)**
*   **Hypothesis:** The high volume represents a "death by a thousand cuts" affecting overall trust, or it is noise from a specific browser/device configuration.
*   **Discovery Action:** Cluster the 80 tickets by specific glitch type and user environment.
    *   *Ask:* "Did this glitch prevent you from saving data or navigating away? Or was it visual only?"
    *   *Metric to Define:* Frequency of "blocked workflow" reports within the glitch cluster.
*   **Decision Gate:** If no single glitch blocks workflow, classify as "Technical Debt" to be addressed only after Priority 1 and 2 are validated. Do not build a dedicated fix sprint yet.

**Next Steps for Product Ops:**
1.  Approve the interview guide for the 5 search users by [Date].
2.  Define the "critical task" list for GreenHouse HR users to score interview results against.
3.  Set a kill threshold: If search is not a critical blocker, we will not allocate Q3 engineering headcount to it.

---
*Note: This plan assumes no tickets were marked urgent; validation must come from user behavior, not ticket severity flags. Do not engineer a solution until the "critical task" metric is quantified.*
