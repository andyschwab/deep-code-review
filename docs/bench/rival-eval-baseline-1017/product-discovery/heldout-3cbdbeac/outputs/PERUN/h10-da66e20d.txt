### Prioritized Assumption Tests for BlueSky Analytics

To maximize learning and minimize risk before your stakeholder presentation on the 20th, you must validate the assumption with the highest **Impact × Uncertainty** using the cheapest possible behavioral test. While data accuracy is foundational, it is often an internal engineering constraint. If managers never check the dashboard (adoption), perfect data yields zero value. Therefore, **user adoption frequency** is likely the riskiest assumption because it validates the core business hypothesis: that a problem exists worth solving.

Use the **Riskiest-Assumption Gate** framework below to select your two experiments. Do not guess the scores; fill the blanks with your team's consensus to force a decision.

**Selection Rule:** Pick the two with the highest **Risk Score**. If "Data Accuracy" scores lower than "Adoption," do not test it first; perfect data for a product no one uses is waste.

#### Step 2: Define the Two Experiments

#### Step 3: Non-Goals for This Sprint
To protect your limited resources, explicitly record what you are **not** doing yet:
*   **Not Building Infrastructure:** Do not write code for the predictive model or dashboard until Experiment A passes.
*   **Not Surveying Intent:** Do not ask users "Would you use this?" (hypothetical); only measure what they *do* (behavioral).
*   **Not Validating Low-Risk Items:** Ignore assumptions with low uncertainty (things you already know) or low impact (nice-to-haves).
