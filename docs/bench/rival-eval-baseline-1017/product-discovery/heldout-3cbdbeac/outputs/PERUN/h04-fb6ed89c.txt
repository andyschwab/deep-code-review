### Research Plan: LedgerLite Onboarding Friction Diagnosis

**Objective:** Identify specific friction points causing 0% completion in the new onboarding flow within 10 days of launch.

**1. Recruitment Strategy: Prioritize "Immediate Drop-offs"**
Do not split your sample yet. Prioritize the users who **dropped off immediately** (e.g., before completing step 2 of the tutorial).
*   **Reasoning:** Users who partially completed the flow have already demonstrated some tolerance for the length. The "zero completion" metric suggests a hard barrier at the very start. If the first step is broken or confusing, interviewing those who made it further will yield biased data about "length" rather than the root cause of the total failure.
*   **Action:** Select 6 participants from the "immediate drop-off" segment first. If these interviews reveal no blocking issues at the start, only then recruit from the "partial completion" group to investigate mid-flow fatigue.
*   **Outreach Script Focus:** "We noticed you started setting up LedgerLite but didn't finish. We aren't selling anything; we just want to understand what happened in those first few minutes so we can fix it."

**2. Questioning Strategy: The Mom Test & Switch Interview**
Avoid asking hypothetical questions like "Did you find the tutorial too long?" or "Would you use this if it were shorter?" Users will politely agree to save face. Instead, reconstruct the specific session.

**Ask about past behavior, not future intent:**
*   "Walk me through exactly what you were doing right before you opened LedgerLite last Tuesday." (Context/Push)
*   "Take me to the exact moment you closed the app. What was the last thing you saw on the screen?" (Specific friction point)
*   "What were you trying to accomplish in that session? Did you have a specific transaction or account you were eager to set up?" (Job-to-be-Done)
*   "When you saw the first tutorial step, what did you think you had to do? What did you actually do?" (Mental model mismatch)
*   "Have you tried to set up a finance app before? How did you handle the setup then?" (Habit/Comparison)

**3. Execution Rules for the Researcher**
*   **No Pitching:** Do not explain how the tutorial *should* work or defend its length. If the user says "It was long," ask "What specifically made you feel that way? Was it the number of clicks, the reading, or something else?"
*   **Look for Behavior, Not Opinions:** Ignore "I think..." statements. Focus on "I clicked...", "I closed...", "I got stuck at..."
*   **Stop Condition:** If the first 3 interviews all cite the same specific screen or error message as the drop-off point, you have found your root cause. You may not need all 6 interviews to justify a fix.

**4. Expected Output**
A list of observed behaviors mapped to specific UI elements (e.g., "4/6 users closed the app immediately after the 'Connect Bank' request because they didn't understand why it was needed upfront").

*Note: If users claim they "forgot" or "got busy," dig deeper into whether the value proposition was clear enough to warrant returning. Zero completion often indicates a value gap, not just a time gap.*
*Ensure the researcher records the exact device and browser used, as technical failures can mimic design friction.*
