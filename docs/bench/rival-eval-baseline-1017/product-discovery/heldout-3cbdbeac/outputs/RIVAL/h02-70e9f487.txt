**Objective:** Select one integration to deliver by end of Q3 based on revenue risk vs. market expansion.

### Final Recommendation: Prioritize ERP System Connection

**Rationale:**
While the Legacy Tracking request carries a severe "unknown" risk regarding 20% of billings, building a custom API for three clients contradicts scalable product development. The ERP integration represents the strongest signal for **sustainable growth**: it has the highest request volume (8 clients) and a quantified upside ($50k ARR).

**Immediate Action Required:**
Before finalizing the Q3 roadmap, the Product Lead must contact the three Legacy Tracking clients to quantify the churn risk. If their combined value is significantly less than the projected $50k ERP upside, the decision is clear. If their value exceeds $50k and churn is imminent, a temporary manual workaround should be offered to retain them while engineering builds the scalable ERP solution.
