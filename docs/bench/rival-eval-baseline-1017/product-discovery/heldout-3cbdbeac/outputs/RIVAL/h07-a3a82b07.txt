# Quarterly Discovery Roadmap: Employee Directory Optimization

**Objective:** Validate the severity of support issues to align engineering sprints with actual customer blockers, moving beyond ticket frequency to assess business impact.

### Priority 1: Search Functionality (High Frequency, Unknown Severity)
*Hypothesis:* Search failures are preventing users from finding critical contact information, forcing them to log tickets or seek workarounds.
*   **Discovery Action:** Conduct 5–7 rapid customer interviews with users who submitted search-related tickets.
*   **Key Questions:**
    *   "Walk me through the last time you tried to find a colleague. What specifically happened?"
    *   "What did you do immediately after the search failed? Did you call, email, or use another tool?"
    *   "How did this delay affect your workflow or the person you were trying to reach?"
*   **Success Metric:** Confirm if search failures cause workflow stoppages (critical) or mere inconvenience (minor). If critical, prioritize a full UX audit and backend index review.

### Priority 2: Profile Photo Uploads (Medium Frequency, High Friction Potential)
*Hypothesis:* Upload failures are causing repeated attempts and frustration, potentially linked to file size limits or format errors not clearly communicated.
*   **Discovery Action:** Analyze the specific error logs for the 30 tickets and perform a "dog-fooding" session (internal team attempting uploads with various file types).
*   **Key Questions (for follow-up interviews):**
    *   "Tell me about the last time you tried to update your photo. Where did the process break?"
    *   "How many times did you try before giving up or submitting a ticket?"
*   **Success Metric:** Identify if this is a technical bug or a usability gap. If users abandon the task after one failure, this is a high-friction point requiring an immediate fix or clearer error messaging.

### Priority 3: UI Glitches Cluster (High Volume, Low Cohesion)
*Hypothesis:* The 80 scattered tickets represent a pattern of instability (e.g., specific browser issues, mobile responsiveness) rather than isolated bugs.
*   **Discovery Action:** Tag and cluster the 80 tickets by browser, device, and specific UI element. Look for commonalities (e.g., "all occurring on Safari mobile").
*   **Key Questions (for support team sync):**
    *   "Do these glitches prevent task completion, or are they visual only?"
    *   "Is there a correlation between these glitches and specific user roles or locations?"
*   **Success Metric:** Determine if these scattered issues form a coherent technical debt story (e.g., "Mobile experience is broken") warranting a dedicated refactor sprint, or if they should be handled as routine maintenance.

### Next Steps for Product Ops
1.  **Week 1:** Complete interviews and log analysis.
2.  **Week 2:** Synthesize findings into a "Severity vs. Effort" matrix.
3.  **Week 3:** Finalize Q3 engineering goals based on validated pain points, not just ticket counts.

*Note: Do not commit engineering resources to search fixes until interviews confirm it is a blocker rather than an annoyance.*
