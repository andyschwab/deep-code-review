# Research Plan: LedgerLite Onboarding Drop-off Investigation

## 1. Recruitment Strategy
**Target Segment:** Prioritize the **10 users who dropped off immediately** (within the first 2 steps) over those who partially completed the flow.
*   **Rationale:** With a 0% completion rate, the primary barrier is likely at the very entrance. Users who made it further provide data on secondary friction, but the "immediate drop-off" group holds the key to the critical failure point preventing *any* value realization.
*   **Sampling:** Recruit 8 users from the immediate drop-off group and 2 from the partial completion group to validate if friction shifts after the initial hurdle.
*   **Outreach Script:** "We noticed you started setting up LedgerLite but didn't finish. We aren't selling anything; we simply want to understand what got in your way so we can fix it. The interview takes 20 minutes."

## 2. Interview Approach
**Method:** 1:1 Remote Interviews (30 mins max).
**Principle:** Focus strictly on **past behavior** and specific instances. Do not ask "Would you use this?" or "Is this too long?" (Leading questions).

### Interview Script Outline

**A. Warm-up (Context)**
*   "Walk me through the day you decided to try LedgerLite. What was happening that made you look for a finance app?"
*   "What were you hoping to accomplish in that specific session?"

**B. Core Exploration (The Event)**
*   "Take me back to the moment you opened the new onboarding flow. What was the very first thing you saw?"
*   "Walk me through exactly what you did next. Did you read the text? Click a button? Close the tab?"
*   "At what specific point did you decide to stop? What was on the screen?"
*   "What was going through your mind right before you closed the window?"
*   "Have you tried to set up a finance app before? How did that experience compare to this one?"

**C. Probing for Root Cause**
*   *If they mention length:* "Can you describe the specific step that felt too long? What were you expecting to happen instead?"
*   *If they mention confusion:* "What did you think that screen was asking you to do?"
*   "What have you tried since then to solve your finance tracking need?"

**D. Wrap-up**
*   "Is there anything about that setup process we didn't discuss that frustrated you?"
*   "If you could change one thing about that first screen to make it easier, what would it be?"

## 3. Synthesis & Output
**Goal:** Identify the specific friction point causing the drop-off.
**Deliverable:** A friction map highlighting the exact step, user expectation vs. reality, and the emotional state (confusion, boredom, distrust) at the moment of exit.
**Success Metric:** Consistent patterns emerging across 6+ interviews regarding the same specific barrier.
