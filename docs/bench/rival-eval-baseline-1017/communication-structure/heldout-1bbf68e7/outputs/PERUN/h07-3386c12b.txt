Dear Account Manager,

We are updating the format of the CSV files you download from your dashboard. This change ensures your reports work smoothly with more third-party tools.

**What is changing?**
On Tuesday, the system will automatically update. When you download a report after this date, the column headers (the top row of the file) will have new, standardized names. The data inside the file remains the same; only the labels at the top are different.

**Why does this matter?**
If your company uses automated software or scripts to read these files, those tools might stop working because they expect the old column names. Manual users who open files in Excel or Google Sheets will see the new names but can still read the data without issue.

**What you need to do:**
1.  **Forward this notice** to your IT team or anyone who manages automated data feeds today.
2.  **Ask them to check** if any scripts rely on specific column names in your export files.
3.  **Update those scripts** to match the new headers before Tuesday to avoid interruptions.

If you are unsure whether your team uses automated tools, it is safer to send this note to your IT contact. If you encounter issues after Tuesday, our support team is ready to help you resolve them quickly.

Thank you for helping us improve your reporting experience.
