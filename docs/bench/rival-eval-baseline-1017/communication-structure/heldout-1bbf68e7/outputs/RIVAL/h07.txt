Subject: Action Required: Your downloaded reports will change on Tuesday

Dear Account Manager,

Starting this Tuesday, the format of the CSV reports you download from our system will change slightly. If your company uses automated tools or scripts to process these files, this update may affect them.

**What is changing?**
The column headers at the top of your downloaded files will have new, standardized names. The data inside the reports remains the same, but the labels describing that data will look different. We made this change to ensure our reports work smoothly with a wider range of business tools.

**What you need to do**
Please forward this message to your IT team or anyone who manages your automated reporting tools. They need to verify that any systems pulling data from our platform are updated to recognize the new column names before Tuesday. If these systems are not updated, your automated reports may fail or display errors after the change goes live.

If you only download reports manually to view them in Excel or Google Sheets, you likely do not need to take any action, though you may notice the column names look different than before.

We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your help in ensuring a smooth transition. If your team encounters any issues after Tuesday, please contact our support desk immediately, and we will assist you.

Thank you,

The Product Team
