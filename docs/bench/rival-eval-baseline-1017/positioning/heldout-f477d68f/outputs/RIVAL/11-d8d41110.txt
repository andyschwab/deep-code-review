## Value Proposition

**For** budget-conscious public school administrators in Texas
- **that need** to maximize classroom device density without compromising reliability or draining annual capital budgets
- ReNew Tech
- **is a** certified refurbished laptop marketplace
- **that** delivers enterprise-grade durability at 60% of the cost of new devices, backed by a failure rate under 2% and a comprehensive two-year warranty

## Differentiation Statement

- **Unlike** LeaseCorp's monthly leasing traps or DirectDell's discounted bulk new units
- ReNew Tech
- **provides** immediate, full ownership with superior long-term value and proven reliability, eliminating the "used gear" risk through rigorous certification that outperforms entry-level new hardware

***

*Note: This positioning explicitly targets the "fiscally responsible" angle while using the <2% failure rate to neutralize the stigma of refurbished goods, effectively making "sustainability" a secondary benefit rather than the primary hook.*
