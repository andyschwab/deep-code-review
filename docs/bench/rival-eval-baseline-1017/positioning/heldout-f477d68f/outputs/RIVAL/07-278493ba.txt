**Value Proposition**

**For** owners of legacy farm equipment in the Midwest
- **that need** reliable, long-term fleet tracking without replacing functional machinery or draining mobile devices
- Acme Fields
- **is a** retrofit telematics sensor
- **that** delivers three years of continuous, battery-powered visibility on older tractors at half the cost of factory-installed systems

**Differentiation Statement**

- **Unlike** BigTractor Inc.'s pre-installed modules (which require new machinery) or GreenTrack's smartphone app (which relies on unstable phone batteries)
- Acme Fields
- **provides** a dedicated, industrial-grade tracking solution that extends the life of existing heritage equipment with zero maintenance for three years

***

This positioning rejects the "budget" angle to avoid a race to the bottom, instead framing your product as the only viable option for the specific job of upgrading old assets. It directly addresses investor concerns by defining a defensible niche between the expensive incumbent and the unreliable free alternative.
