### Differentiation Analysis

**Vs. Generic Fitness Trackers (e.g., basic Garmin/Whoop models)**
Generic trackers are reactive; they tell you how you slept or what your heart rate was *yesterday*. They count steps and monitor general readiness but lack predictive specificity. StrideSense is proactive. By leveraging an algorithm with 85% accuracy to forecast injury risk 48 hours in advance, you shift the user from "monitoring health" to "preventing disaster." A generic tracker says, "You are tired." StrideSense says, "If you run today, you have a high probability of a stress fracture in three days."

**Vs. Expensive Lab Testing**
Lab testing offers high accuracy but suffers from low frequency and high friction. An athlete might get tested once a season, leaving them blind for the other 11 months. StrideSense provides lab-grade predictive insights daily, at a one-time hardware cost of $199 versus hundreds per lab visit. You are not just cheaper; you are continuous. You turn a sporadic medical checkup into a daily training companion.
