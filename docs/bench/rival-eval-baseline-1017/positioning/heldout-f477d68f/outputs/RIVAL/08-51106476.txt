**Recommended Primary Message:**
"Focus on your art, not your taxes: The invoicing tool that automatically sets aside 25% for the IRS and gets you paid 40% faster."

**Supporting Sub-points:**
1.  **Effortless Compliance:** Unlike complex enterprise suites or bare-bones apps, we automatically reserve your tax portion from every invoice so April never brings a surprise.
2.  **Designer-First Simplicity:** Built specifically for creatives who hate accounting, delivering the power of financial advice with the ease of a sketchbook.

**Evaluation of Your Options:**
Between your two draft options, "Never miss a tax deadline again" is the stronger differentiator, but it needs refinement. "Get paid faster with smart invoicing" is too generic; InvoiceMaster and SimpleBill both claim speed, and "smart invoicing" is meaningless fluff to a designer. However, the *automatic tax set-aside* is your unique wedge. Neither the complex enterprise tool (InvoiceMaster) nor the cheap app (SimpleBill) offers proactive financial advice or automation for taxes. Your beta data proves this feature drives the outcome (40% reduction in late payments), likely because users feel more financially secure and organized.

The fear of tax season is a massive emotional pain point for freelancers who currently use spreadsheets or ignore the problem. Addressing this directly separates you from "boring accounting firms" by framing the solution as a protective shield that lets them get back to work, rather than a compliance chore.

**Message Hierarchy Rationale:**
The primary message leads with the emotional benefit ("Focus on your art") to immediately resonate with the target identity, then delivers the hard proof ("automatically sets aside 25%" and "40% faster"). This hierarchy works because it first validates their self-image as creators, then solves their biggest fear (taxes), and finally proves operational superiority (speed). It avoids sounding like an accounting firm by focusing on the *outcome* (peace of mind, more time designing) rather than the *process* (ledger balancing, form filing).

Note: This positioning explicitly targets the gap between "too complex" and "too simple" by offering specialized intelligence without the bloat.
Ensure your homepage visuals reinforce the "creative" identity to avoid drifting into sterile fintech aesthetics.
