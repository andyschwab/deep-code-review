**Deliverable: Weekly Metric for Board Presentation**

**Primary Metric:** **Contribution Margin per Square Meter per Week**

**Definition:**
`(Revenue from Harvested Lettuce per m²) − (Variable Energy Cost per m² + Variable Water/Nutrient Cost per m² + Packaging/Labor Cost per m²)`

**Why this replaces "Total Biomass" and "Yield per m²":**
Total biomass and raw yield are vanity metrics in your current context; they rise even if profitability falls due to spiking energy costs. Since your energy costs have risen 12% since March, a high yield could actually mask a loss if the electricity required to grow that extra kilogram costs more than the lettuce sells for. This metric forces the trade-off between biological output (yield) and operational efficiency (energy/water) into a single financial number that directly answers the investor's concern for unit economics.

**How to Interpret Shifts Given Climate Control Challenges:**

1.  **Metric Rises:** You are successfully balancing yield gains against energy costs. This indicates your climate control algorithms or insulation upgrades are offsetting the regional electricity price hikes. **Action:** Proceed with Q3 expansion; the unit economics support scale.
2.  **Metric Flatlines while Yield Rises:** Your energy consumption per kilogram is increasing faster than your output. The "extra" lettuce is being eaten by the 12% energy hike. **Action:** Pause expansion. Investigate HVAC set-points or lighting schedules immediately; do not add more square footage until the marginal cost of production drops.
3.  **Metric Declines:** The Dubai heat load or electricity tariffs have broken your current unit economics. You are paying to grow lettuce that loses money on every square meter. **Action:** Trigger an emergency review of pricing or a temporary reduction in growing cycles during peak heat/price hours. Expansion is financially dangerous until this trend reverses.
