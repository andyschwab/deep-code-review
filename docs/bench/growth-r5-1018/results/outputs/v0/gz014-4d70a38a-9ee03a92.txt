### Cohort Analysis Framework for BoxedDelight Onboarding Redesign

**Objective:** Isolate the impact of the January 20th onboarding flow on Month-2 retention (target: 62% vs. baseline 55%) to inform Q1 budget allocation.

#### 1. Cohort Definition & Grouping Logic
To compare "apples to apples" amidst seasonal sign-up fluctuations, define cohorts by **Sign-Up Week**, not Month. Weekly granularity smooths volume spikes and aligns better with the January 20th launch date.

*   **Control Cohorts:** Users signing up in the 4 weeks prior to Jan 20th.
*   **Treatment Cohorts:** Users signing up in the 4 weeks post-Jan 20th.
*   **Primary Split (The Hypothesis):** Within every weekly cohort, create two sub-groups:
    1.  **Preference Selectors:** Users who completed the preference selection step.
    2.  **Skippers:** Users who bypassed or skipped this step.
*   **Secondary Split (Channel):** Further segment by acquisition channel (e.g., Paid Social, Organic Search, Email) to identify where the "Preference Selector" lift is strongest for budget allocation.

#### 2. Time Windows & Retention Calculation
Define "Month-2 Retention" strictly as a **binary event** occurring within a specific window relative to the sign-up date (Day 0).

*   **Window:** Days 30–59 post-sign-up.
*   **Success Event:** A paid renewal or a specific high-value engagement (e.g., "Box Customization for Month 3") occurring within this window.
*   **Formula:** `(Users in Cohort X with Success Event in Days 30-59) / (Total Users in Cohort X)`.
*   **Seasonality Control:** Compare the Treatment cohorts only against the same calendar weeks from the previous year (if available) or rely on the immediate pre-launch Control cohorts. Do not compare January sign-ups to November sign-ups without adjusting for holiday churn.

#### 3. Critical Drop-off Points to Monitor
Instrument these three specific events to diagnose *why* retention changes. Each event must answer a named question:

1.  **Event:** `onboarding_step_started`
    *   **Question:** Are users entering the new flow?
    *   **Action:** If drop-off is high here, the entry point is confusing; fix UI before analyzing retention.
2.  **Event:** `preference_selection_completed` (Property: `time_spent_seconds`)
    *   **Question:** Does friction in selecting preferences correlate with future churn?
    *   **Action:** If selectors retain better but completion rates drop, the step is valuable but too hard; simplify the interface.
3.  **Event:** `first_box_customization` (Days 0–29)
    *   **Question:** Is early engagement driving the Month-2 retention?
    *   **Action:** If this metric rises alongside retention, the mechanism is working. If retention rises without this, investigate external factors (e.g., a concurrent email campaign).

#### 4. Reading the Results for the Marketing Director
*   **The Decision Rule:** If the "Preference Selector" sub-group in Treatment cohorts shows >62% retention while "Skippers" remain flat or decline, the feature works. Allocate more budget to channels driving users who *complete* this step.
*   **The Guardrail:** Monitor `cost_per_acquired_selector`. If the new flow increases acquisition cost (by dropping users before they convert to paid) more than it improves LTV, the net value is negative.
*   **Verification Note:** Since historical data on "Preference Selectors" is missing, the initial read (Feb 20th) will be `UNVERIFIED` for causal claims until the first full monthly cohort matures. Use the first 2 weeks of post-launch data only to verify instrumentation, not retention outcomes.
