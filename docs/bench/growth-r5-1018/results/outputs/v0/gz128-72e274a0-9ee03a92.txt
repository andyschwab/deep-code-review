### Analysis Design: Net Margin Impact of Loyalty Pastry Reward

**Objective:** Isolate the causal effect of the "free pastry after 5 visits" reward on total basket size and net margin, distinguishing between genuine frequency lifts and cannibalization of paid add-ons.

**1. Cohort Definition (The Comparison)**
*   **Test Group:** The 4,000 enrolled members who have completed ≥5 visits since September 1st (triggering the reward).
*   **Control Group:** A synthetic control of non-members matched by location and historical visit frequency (pre-September), OR enrolled members who have not yet reached 5 visits (if the reward is strictly visit-count triggered). *Note: Do not compare against all non-members, as enrollment bias skews results.*

**2. Required Data Slices (Transaction Level)**
Extract the following fields for both groups for the period September 1–Present:
*   `transaction_id`, `user_id` (masked), `location_id`, `timestamp`.
*   `is_member`: Boolean.
*   `visit_count_cumulative`: Integer (to identify the exact transaction where the threshold was met).
*   `gross_basket_value`: Total CLP before discounts.
*   `paid_addon_revenue`: Revenue specifically from paid pastries/add-ons (exclude the free item).
*   `discount_cost`: The COGS (Cost of Goods Sold) value of the free pastry given (not the retail price).
*   `net_margin_contribution`: `(gross_basket_value - paid_addon_revenue) - (COGS_of_all_items + discount_cost)`.

**3. The Three Critical Metrics to Compute**
Do not report top-line revenue. Report these three derived metrics per user per month:
*   **Metric A (Frequency Lift):** Average visits per user/month.
    *   *Question:* Did visits actually rise by the assumed 1.5x, or is this vanity?
*   **Metric B (Cannibalization Rate):** Average `paid_addon_revenue` per transaction for visits 6+ (post-reward) vs. visits 1–4.
    *   *Question:* Are users stopping the 300 CLP add-on purchase because they expect a free one?
*   **Metric C (Net Margin Delta):** `(Metric A × Net Margin per Visit_Post) - (Baseline Visits × Net Margin per Visit_Pre)`.
    *   *Question:* Does the increased frequency cover the cost of the free pastry and lost add-on sales?

**4. Reading Rules for the CFO**
*   **The Break-Even Threshold:** Calculate the minimum increase in frequency required to offset the loss of the 300 CLP add-on. If the free pastry COGS is 150 CLP, the user must generate enough *additional* visits to cover that 150 CLP loss per reward cycle.
*   **The "Peeking" Warning:** Do not stop the analysis early if Week 1 looks good. Loyalty behavior stabilizes over 30–45 days. Compare September full-month cohorts against October to date.
*   **Segmentation:** Slice results by `location_id`. If the lift is driven only by 5 high-traffic stores, a national rollout may fail in lower-traffic zones.
