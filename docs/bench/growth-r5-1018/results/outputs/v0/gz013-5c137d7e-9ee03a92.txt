### Action Plan: Botnet Isolation & Validation for CTO Elena

**1. Behavioral Signals to Measure (Instrument Immediately)**
To distinguish scripts from real players without relying on inaccurate geo-tags, instrument these three specific events tied to the "Pearl" currency loop. Each event must capture `session_id`, `time_between_actions_ms`, and `action_sequence_hash`.

*   **Signal A: Action Regularity (The "Metronome" Test)**
    *   *Question:* Are actions occurring at human-variable intervals or fixed script intervals?
    *   *Measurement:* Calculate the standard deviation of `time_between_actions_ms` for "Pearl Collection" events.
    *   *Threshold:* Humans exhibit high variance (>15% std dev). Scripts often show near-zero variance or perfect periodicity. Flag sessions where std dev < 2% over 50+ actions.
*   **Signal B: Path Linearity vs. Exploration**
    *   *Question:* Does the user navigate the race track with organic deviation or optimal straight-line efficiency?
    *   *Measurement:* Compare the actual distance traveled vs. the theoretical minimum distance for the course (Efficiency Ratio).
    *   *Threshold:* Real players vary lines (corners, mistakes). Bots farming currency often hit 99-100% efficiency repeatedly. Flag sessions with >98% efficiency sustained over 10 races.
*   **Signal C: Session Duration & Drop-off Cliffs**
    *   *Question:* Do sessions end abruptly after a specific currency threshold?
    *   *Measurement:* Track session length relative to "Pearls Earned."
    *   *Threshold:* Identify clusters where sessions terminate within 2 seconds of reaching a specific Pearl count, repeating this pattern across hundreds of IDs.

**2. Validation Strategy: The "Canary" Cohort**
To ensure legitimate high-frequency gamers (e.g., tournament grinders) are not falsely banned, execute a **shadow filter** before enforcement:

*   **Step 1:** Apply the proposed thresholds to the last 48 hours of data but tag results as `SUSPECT_SHADOW` rather than `BANNED`.
*   **Step 2:** Cross-reference `SUSPECT_SHADOW` IDs against two known legitimate cohorts:
    *   Users with prior purchase history (whales are rarely bots).
    *   Users who completed the tutorial manually (bots often skip or speed-run this differently).
*   **Step 3:** If >5% of known payers fall into `SUSPECT_SHADOW`, tighten the variance thresholds. If 0% of payers are flagged, the signal is safe for enforcement.
