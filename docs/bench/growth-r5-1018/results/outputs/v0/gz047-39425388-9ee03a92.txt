**Recommendation for Board Approval: North Star Metric**

**Primary Metric:** **Active Capital Deployed per Retained Investor**
*(Definition: The sum of capital currently held in active commercial assets by investors who have completed at least one secondary action—such as reinvestment, portfolio rebalancing, or referral—within the last 12 months.)*

**Executive Summary**
For the commercial vertical launch, do not select "Asset Count" or "Total Capital Deployed" as your standalone North Star. Both are vanity metrics in this specific context. "Asset Count" incentivizes fragmentation without value (e.g., splitting $10k across five poor assets), while "Total Capital Deployed" rewards one-time whales who churn immediately after deployment.

Your North Star must measure **sustained value capture**, not just initial transaction volume. Commercial real estate differs from residential in hold times and liquidity; therefore, the metric must prove that investors are not just entering, but staying and compounding.

**Comparison of Candidates**

| Metric | Pros | Cons & Risks for Commercial Launch |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Asset Count** | • Simple to track.<br>• Aligns with your data showing diversification correlates to retention. | • **Vanity Trap:** Users can game this by over-fragmenting capital into sub-optimal assets.<br>• **Value Blind:** Holding 10 failing properties looks "healthier" than holding 1 winning one.<br>• **Commercial Mismatch:** Commercial deals are fewer, larger, and less frequent; counting them ignores the magnitude of capital at risk. |
| **Total Capital Deployed** | • Directly ties to revenue (fees on deployment).<br>• Correlates with referral activity. | • **Churn Blind:** As noted, large one-time deployers never return. This metric rises even if retention collapses.<br>• **False Growth:** A single institutional splash can spike the metric while the retail base erodes.<br>• **No Behavior Signal:** Does not distinguish between a passive holder and an active portfolio builder. |

**Why "Active Capital per Retained Investor" Wins**
This composite metric solves the specific contradictions in your data:
1.  **Filters One-Time Noise:** By requiring a "retained" status (defined by a second action), it excludes the large-sum deployers who vanish, ensuring the metric only rises when users *stay*.
2.  **Values Quality over Quantity:** It measures the *amount* of capital trusted to the platform over time, respecting the high-value nature of commercial assets, rather than just the count of lines in a portfolio.
3.  **Aligns with Long-Term Health:** It directly tracks the "diversified investor" behavior you value (as they naturally deploy more capital across assets to stay retained) without incentivizing useless fragmentation.

**Implementation Guardrails**
*   **Baseline:** Your current 18-month average hold time is a lagging indicator. Do not use it as a target. Instead, track the **month-over-month trend** of this new metric against your own pre-launch baseline.
*   **Verification:** Any claim that this metric is "up" must be verified by cohort analysis (e.g., Q1 launch cohort vs. Q2). Do not compare against external industry benchmarks, as commercial fractional ownership lacks standardized public data.
*   **Input Metrics:** To move this North Star, track: (1) Time-to-Second-Deployment, and (2) Percentage of Portfolio Reinvested within 6 months of an exit.

**Decision for the Board**
Approve **Active Capital Deployed per Retained Investor** as the primary success metric. It is the only option that penalizes churn while rewarding the deep engagement required for commercial real estate success.

*Note: Ensure the definition of "retained" is codified in your event taxonomy before launch to prevent post-hoc redefinition. This metric requires a stable user identifier to track across sessions and asset classes.*
