**To:** CEO, Product Design Lead

**Subject:** Urgent Analysis: Churn Increase Post-'Harvest Prediction' Deployment

**Executive Summary**
Between October 3 and October 17, weekly churn among the 450 farmers with access to the new 'harvest prediction' feature rose from 1.8% to 2.1%. While cumulative, this represents a ~17% relative increase in流失 during a critical pre-planting window. Contrary to our hypothesis that transparency builds trust, early data suggests the feature may be introducing friction or eroding confidence. We must validate the root cause within five days to decide on a rollback before the planting cycle locks in.

**Three Distinct Hypotheses**

1.  **Hypothesis A: Prediction Accuracy Mismatch (The "Broken Promise" Effect)**
    *   **Premise:** The recent rainy season caused actual harvest yields to deviate significantly from the model's predictions. Farmers seeing large discrepancies between the dashboard forecast and field reality lost trust in the platform's core utility.
    *   **Indicator:** High correlation between users who viewed the prediction vs. actuals gap and subsequent churn.
    *   **Implication:** The algorithm requires recalibration for weather variance; the UI is not the primary fault.

2.  **Hypothesis B: Interface Complexity & Cognitive Overload**
    *   **Premise:** The new dashboard introduces unnecessary complexity. Farmers, who need quick answers, are confused by the new data visualization, leading to frustration and abandonment rather than clarity.
    *   **Indicator:** Short session durations on the new page, high click-through rates on help tooltips (if logged), or repeated refreshing of the same view without downstream action.
    *   **Implication:** The feature needs a UX simplification or an "expert mode" toggle; the data logic may be sound.

3.  **Hypothesis C: Premature Exposure to Negative Variance**
    *   **Premise:** Previously, farmers operated on optimism. The dashboard explicitly visualizes risk (e.g., "low yield probability") which, while accurate, causes anxiety and disengagement among risk-averse growers who prefer not to see negative projections before planting.
    *   **Indicator:** Churn concentrated specifically among users whose predicted harvest values were flagged as "below average" or "high risk."
    *   **Implication:** The feature is working technically but fails the "job to be done" emotionally; we may need to reframe how uncertainty is communicated.

**Validation Plan: Next 5 Days**

We will validate **Hypothesis A (Accuracy Mismatch)** first, as it poses the highest reputational risk. If the model is visibly wrong, no amount of UX polish will fix trust.

*   **Data Source:** Existing application logs (user ID, timestamp, page view), prediction database (forecasted yield), and harvest reporting logs (actuals entered by farmers).
*   **Methodology:**
    1.  **Cohort Segmentation:** Split the 450 users into three groups based on the absolute % error between their *predicted* yield (Oct 3-10) and their *reported* actuals (or regional averages if individual actuals are pending).
        *   Group 1: High Accuracy (<10% error)
        *   Group 2: Medium Accuracy (10-25% error)
        *   Group 3: Low Accuracy (>25% error)
    2.  **Churn Comparison:** Calculate the churn rate for Oct 10-17 for each group.
    3.  **Behavioral Trace:** For Group 3 (Low Accuracy), analyze session logs for "rage clicks" or immediate exits after viewing the prediction screen.
*   **Success Metric:** If Group 3's churn rate is statistically significantly higher (>3.0%) than Group 1 (<1.5%), Hypothesis A is confirmed.
*   **Timeline:**
    *   Day 1-2: Data extraction and cleaning.
    *   Day 3: Statistical analysis and segmentation.
    *   Day 4: Draft findings.
    *   Day 5: Go/No-Go decision meeting.

**Recommendation**
Prepare a rollback script immediately. If the analysis on Day 3 confirms that high prediction error drives churn, we must rollback the feature by Day 5 to prevent further erosion of trust before the planting cycle begins. If prediction accuracy is not the driver, we proceed to test Hypothesis B via user session recordings.

**Note:** This analysis relies entirely on internal logs; no external benchmarks were used. If actual harvest data is missing for key users, the accuracy metric will be marked `UNVERIFIED` for those specific accounts.
