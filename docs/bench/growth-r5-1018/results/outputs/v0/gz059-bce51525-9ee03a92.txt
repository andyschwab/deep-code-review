### Governance Health Framework for GlobalDAO

**North Star Metric: Value-Adjusted Participation Rate**
Reject raw participation (12%) as a vanity metric; it rises even when spam increases. Instead, measure the percentage of active token holders voting on **high-signal proposals** (defined below) within a 30-day window. This metric only rises when users engage with decisions that materially impact the treasury, filtering out noise from low-quality spam.

**Input Metrics (The Funnel)**
Track these three inputs to move the North Star, reading from retention of quality engagement upward:

1.  **Proposal Signal-to-Noise Ratio (Activation Quality)**
    *   *Question:* Is the tool attracting better decisions or just more noise?
    *   *Definition:* Ratio of proposals passing a minimum "discussion threshold" (e.g., >5 unique commenters, >24h discussion time) to total submissions.
    *   *Action:* If this ratio drops while submissions rise, tighten submission requirements (e.g., mandatory temperature check) rather than celebrating volume.

2.  **Voter Retention on Core Issues (Retention)**
    *   *Question:* Do users return to vote on critical treasury matters, or do they fatigue and leave after seeing spam?
    *   *Definition:* Percentage of voters in Month N who also voted in Month N+1 on proposals tagged "Treasury" or "Protocol Upgrade."
    *   *Action:* If retention drops despite high total votes, voter fatigue is setting in due to low-quality queue clutter. Prioritize curation tools over acquisition.

3.  **Delegation Stability (Referral/Trust)**
    *   *Question:* Are delegators confident enough to keep their stakes delegated, or are they withdrawing due to governance chaos?
    *   *Definition:* Net change in delegated tokens during periods of high proposal volume.
    *   *Action:* A spike in undelegation during high activity indicates a loss of trust in the decision-making process.

**Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only What Answers a Question)**
Do not instrument "everything." Implement only these events with pseudonymous user IDs to track cohorts without exposing PII:
*   `proposal_submitted`: Properties: `has_discussion_thread`, `category`. (Answers: Are we spamming?)
*   `vote_cast`: Properties: `proposal_id`, `voter_cohort` (delegate vs. direct), `time_since_submission`. (Answers: Is voting impulsive or considered?)
*   `proposal_executed`: Properties: `treasury_impact_estimate`. (Answers: Did we act on high-value items?)

**Reading the Data: Bottom-Up Diagnosis**
Do not pour effort into acquiring more voters if **Retention** (Metric #2) is leaking. If voters participate once then vanish, the queue quality is the blocker, not awareness. Fix the Signal-to-Noise ratio first; only then does increased participation become valuable.

**Experiment Rigor for Governance Changes**
If you test a new spam-filtering rule:
*   **Pre-commit:** Define the success metric (e.g., "Signal-to-Noise ratio increases by 20%") and the guardrail ("Total valid votes do not drop by >5%") before launching.
*   **No Peeking:** Do not declare victory after one high-quality proposal passes. Run the test for a fixed cycle (e.g., 4 weeks) to account for asynchronous global participation.
*   **Verdict:** If the filter reduces spam but causes a guardrail breach (valid votes drop), the friction is too high. Iterate.
