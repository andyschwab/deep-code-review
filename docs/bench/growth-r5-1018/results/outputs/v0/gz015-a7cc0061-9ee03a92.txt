**Diagnostic Plan: 'Industry Insights' Engagement Drop**
**Audience:** European Regional Director | **Decision Date:** Next Tuesday

**1. Immediate Hypothesis & Segmentation Strategy**
The drop in session duration (8m → 5.5m) coupled with low CTR (1.2%) suggests the feed is either irrelevant (users leave immediately) or disruptive (cannibalizing core flows). We will segment the data by three dimensions to isolate the friction:

*   **User Cohort:** Split by "Paris-only" vs. "Rest of Europe" to confirm if this is a content-localization failure or a global UX issue. Further split by "Power Users" (daily messagers) vs. "Casual Browsers" to test the cannibalization theory.
*   **Interaction Depth:** Categorize sessions into:
    *   *Bounce:* Viewed feed, no click, exited < 2 mins. (Indicates irrelevant content/header confusion).
    *   *Dead-End:* Clicked feed item, no subsequent action, exited. (Indicates poor landing page or content mismatch).
    *   *Displaced:* Clicked feed item, then immediately used Messaging. (Indicates cannibalization, not necessarily failure).
*   **Content Type:** Break down the 1.2% CTR by topic (e.g., Tech, Finance, Local Paris News) to identify if specific categories are dragging the average down.

**3. Analysis Logic & Decision Rules**
Run these queries against your analytics platform using the baseline from September 11th:

*   **Test for Layout Friction:** Analyze scroll depth on the feed.
