**Recommended North Star Metric: Weekly Active Learners Achieving a "Verified Mastery Loop"**

**Definition:** The count of unique tutees who, within a 7-day window, complete a tutoring session **and** subsequently trigger a specific high-intent retention event (e.g., booking a follow-up session with the same tutor, or accessing a shared resource link generated during the session) within 48 hours.

**Why this replaces "Completed Sessions":**
Your current metric (sessions/week) is a vanity metric; it rises even when value is low, as evidenced by your short, low-quality interactions. The proposed metric shifts focus from *activity volume* to *validated continuity*.
*   **Filters Noise:** A single 10-minute unproductive session rarely leads to an immediate re-booking or resource engagement. This naturally filters out the "inflated" interactions you identified.
*   **Proxy for Outcome:** Without grade transcripts, the strongest behavioral proxy for "learning happened" is the user's voluntary decision to continue the specific learning path. Repeat booking implies the tutee perceived enough value to return; resource access implies immediate utility.
*   **Aligns Incentives:** This forces tutors to focus on engagement quality that drives return visits, rather than rushing through sessions to boost volume counts.

**Evaluation of Alternatives:**
*   **Session Duration:** **Reject.** Longer sessions do not equal better learning; they can indicate confusion or inefficiency.
*   **Self-Reported Confidence Scores:** **Use as a Guardrail, not North Star.** These are subjective and prone to bias (users rating high to be polite). Track the *average* score to ensure it doesn't drop, but do not let it drive investment decisions.
*   **Repeat Booking Rates:** **Strong Component.** This is the core engine of the recommended metric. However, as a raw rate, it can be skewed by a small power-user base. Counting *unique users* who achieve this loop ensures you are measuring broad value creation.

**Implementation Plan (Given Data Limitations):**
1.  **Instrumentation:** Immediately tag the event `session_completed` with a property `follow_up_booked_within_48h` (boolean) and `resource_link_clicked` (boolean).
2.  **Baseline Calculation:** Run a historical query on your existing 1,200 users to establish the current baseline of this "Mastery Loop." Expect this number to be significantly lower than your total session count; this gap represents your quality leak.
3.  **Dashboard Presentation:** Present the trend of "Weekly Mastery Loops" alongside "Total Sessions." The divergence between these two lines visually demonstrates the shift from vanity to value.

**This Week's Step:**
Define the specific database query for the "Mastery Loop" using your current event logs (looking for Session ID → Follow-up Booking ID within 48h) and calculate the baseline conversion rate. If the rate is below 20%, your immediate product priority is not acquisition, but improving the first-session experience to drive that second booking.

*Note: Since grade transcripts are unavailable, this behavioral proxy is the highest-fidelity measure of value you can construct without fabricating academic benchmarks.*
*Do not set an arbitrary "good" threshold for this metric; judge success solely by its month-over-month growth relative to your own baseline.*
