**North Star Metric for Referrals**
Reject "Total Referrals" (300) as a vanity metric. Adopt **Referral Retention Rate (Week 2)** as the primary indicator of value. Currently, this is **20%** (60 retained out of 300 referred). This metric rises only when a referred student finds value (a successful match), not just when they sign up for credit.

**Leading Indicators (The "Quality" Signals)**
To predict if a referred user will retain before the second session booking, instrument these three specific events. Do not track "page views"; track only actions that answer a named question:

1.  **Event:** `search_attempt_with_grade_filter`
    *   **Question Answered:** Is the user actively looking for their specific grade level?
    *   **Property:** `grade_level_requested`, `subject_requested`.
    *   **Reading Rule:** If this event count is low compared to sign-ups, the issue is **pricing/perception** (they aren't trying to use the service). If high, proceed to the next indicator.

2.  **Event:** `search_result_zero_count`
    *   **Question Answered:** Did the matching algorithm fail to supply a tutor?
    *   **Property:** `grade_level_requested`, `subject_requested`.
    *   **Reading Rule:** A high rate of zero-result searches directly validates your hypothesis about **subject availability**. This is the signal to improve matching logic, not increase bonuses.

3.  **Event:** `tutor_profile_view_to_booking_ratio`
    *   **Question Answered:** Are users seeing profiles but hesitating to book?
    *   **Reading Rule:** If users view profiles but do not book, the friction is likely **pricing perception** or tutor profile quality, not inventory availability.

**Decision Framework for August Investment**
Do not guess; let the data trigger the action. Use the user's own baseline (20% retention) to set the tripwires:

*   **Scenario A: Improve Matching Logic**
    *   **Condition:** `search_attempt_with_grade_filter` is >70% of sign-ups, AND `search_result_zero_count` is >40% of those searches.
    *   **Action:** Invest in matching logic. Doubling the bonus here wastes money on users who literally cannot find a tutor.

*   **Scenario B: Double Referral Bonus / Adjust Pricing**
    *   **Condition:** `search_result_zero_count` is <10%, BUT `tutor_profile_view_to_booking_ratio` is <15%.
    *   **Action:** The inventory exists, but the conversion fails. Test a pricing incentive or increase the bonus to lower the barrier to the first paid session.

*   **Scenario C: Stop & Investigate Onboarding**
    *   **Condition:** `search_attempt_with_grade_filter` is <30% of sign-ups.
    *   **Action:** Referred users are claiming the credit but have no intent to learn. Do not increase bonuses; fix the referral messaging to attract serious students.
