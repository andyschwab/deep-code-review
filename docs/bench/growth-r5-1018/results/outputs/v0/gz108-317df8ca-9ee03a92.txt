### Revised Growth Targets & Measurement Plan for LondonLiving

**1. North Star Metric: Weekly Value-Transacted Volume (WVTV)**
Replace "Total Listings" (a vanity metric that ignores regulatory caps) with **WVTV**: the count of unique tenants completing a lease signing where the total fee complies with the new June 1st caps. This rises only when real, compliant business value is delivered.
*   **Input Metrics:** Premium Service Attachment Rate (landlords adding paid services), Compliance Pass Rate (% of listings auto-flagged as compliant before publishing), and Active Landlord Retention (30-day).

**2. Revised Growth Targets (Fiscal Year)**
*   **Volume Goal:** Accept the modeled **5% QoQ listing growth** as the baseline constraint. Do not target 20%; aiming higher incentivizes non-compliant workarounds or low-quality inventory.
*   **Value Goal:** Target a **15% QoQ increase in Revenue Per Listing (RPL)** driven solely by premium service attachment. This offsets the volume slowdown without violating fee caps.
*   **Transition Milestone:** By end of Q3, premium services must contribute ≥30% of total gross margin to validate the pivot.

**3. Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only What Answers a Question)**
Do not instrument "everything." Implement these four specific events to validate the pivot:
*   `listing_compliance_check`: (Property: `result` [pass/fail], `reason_code`). *Question: Are we blocking too many valid listings or letting risky ones through?*
*   `premium_service_view`: (Property: `service_tier`, `landlord_segment`). *Question: Is there organic interest in premium tiers?*
*   `premium_service_purchase`: (Property: `service_id`, `revenue_amount`). *Question: What is the actual conversion baseline? (Currently UNVERIFIED)*
*   `lease_signed_compliant`: (Property: `total_fee_amount`, `fee_breakdown`). *Question: Is the core action happening under the new rules?*

**4. Measurement Rules & Guardrails**
*   **No Fabricated Benchmarks:** We cannot assert a "good" conversion rate for premium services yet. The baseline is **UNVERIFIED** until the first 100 transactions post-June 1st. Compare all future performance against this internal baseline, not industry averages.
*   **Retention First:** Before spending on acquisition to hit the 5% volume target, verify that landlords who adopt premium services renew at a higher rate than standard users. If premium retention is flat, the pivot fails regardless of attachment rate.
*   **The Identifier Seam:** Ensure a stable, pseudonymous `landlord_id` tracks users across sessions to measure retention cohorts. Do not use high-cardinality log labels (per `observability.md`); route PII handling to privacy compliance protocols.

1.  **Define the "Compliance Pass" Event:** Engineering to instrument `listing_compliance_check` on the upload flow by Friday; Product to define the `reason_code` dictionary for failures.
2.  **Establish the Baseline Window:** Set June 1–June 30 as the "Discovery Phase." No growth targets will be judged against this period; the sole goal is to collect 500 `premium_service_purchase` events to establish a statistical baseline.
3.  **Draft the Guardrail:** Set a hard stop on any marketing campaign that drives >5% non-compliant listing attempts in a week, indicating market confusion or product friction.
