### Success Hierarchy: From Interest to Habitual Saving

**1. North Star Metric (Customer Value)**
**"Challenge Completion Rate"**: The percentage of enrolled users who make the final required deposit to finish a specific challenge.
*Why:* Enrollment is vanity; completion signals the user experienced the full loop of setting a goal and achieving it. This is the earliest proxy for the "aha" moment that predicts long-term retention, replacing raw signups as the primary success indicator.

**2. Input Metrics (The Funnel, Read Bottom-Up)**
*   **Retention of Core Action (Primary Diagnostic):** **Week-2 Continuation Rate**. Of users who made the first deposit, what percentage make a second deposit within 14 days?
    *   *Target Logic:* Since 80% drop out after deposit #1, this metric must move before acquisition scales. If this stays flat, new enrollments are wasted spend.
*   **Activation:** **Time-to-First-Deposit**. The median time between enrolling in a challenge and making the initial deposit.
    *   *Goal:* Reduce friction between intent (enrollment) and action.
*   **Acquisition:** **Organic Challenge Enrollment**. Number of users starting a challenge without paid promotion.
    *   *Constraint:* Only optimize this once Week-2 Continuation stabilizes.

**3. Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only What Answers a Question)**
Do not instrument "everything." Implement these specific events to validate the hypothesis that completion drives 6-month retention:
*   `challenge_enrolled` (Properties: `challenge_type`, `goal_amount`) → *Answers: Which goals attract users?*
*   `deposit_made` (Properties: `challenge_id`, `deposit_sequence_number`, `days_since_previous_deposit`) → *Answers: Where exactly do users drop off in the sequence?*
*   `challenge_completed` (Properties: `challenge_id`, `total_duration_days`) → *Answers: Does finishing correlate with returning next month?*

**4. Immediate Actions (Before March Semester)**
*   **Diagnose the Drop-off:** Query the `deposit_made` event to segment the 80% dropout by `challenge_type`. If the drop is uniform, the friction is in the funding mechanism; if specific to certain goals, the goals are unrealistic for teens.
*   **Redefine "Success" for Compliance:** Shift internal reporting from "Total Enrolled" to "Active Challengers" (users who made ≥2 deposits). This aligns with promoting responsible habits rather than one-time transactions.
*   **Run a Micro-Test:** For users who drop off after deposit #1, test a single nudge (e.g., progress bar visualization vs. peer comparison) to see if it lifts the Week-2 Continuation Rate. Pre-commit to a sample size of 500 users per variant and read results only after 14 days to avoid peeking bias.

**Note:** Long-term retention (6 months) remains `UNVERIFIED` until data accumulates; use Week-2 Continuation as the leading indicator for now. No external benchmarks for "good" teen retention are used; compare only against WonPay's baseline trends post-February 14th.
