### Conversion Definition Framework for LuxResale Board Presentation

**Primary North Star Metric: "Verified High-Value Settlement"**
For LuxResale, a conversion is strictly defined as the **final shipment confirmation of a transaction exceeding €10,000**.

**Rationale:**
In a high-end consignment model, the business only realizes revenue and validates the supply chain once the item physically ships to the buyer and the consignor's payout is triggered.
*   **Why not the deposit?** A deposit indicates intent but carries high friction risk (buyer remorse, authentication failure, or financing issues) before the high-value exchange is complete. Counting deposits inflates success rates without guaranteeing cash flow.
*   **Why not the wishlist?** As your data shows, wishlisting is a low-commitment browsing behavior. With 8,000 visitors and only 45 ultimate buyers, treating wishlists as conversion proxies creates vanity metrics that mask the true leakage in your funnel.
*   **Board Justification:** This definition aligns acquisition spend directly with realized Gross Merchandise Value (GMV). It proves to the board that you are acquiring *buyers*, not just *browsers*.

**Secondary Input Metrics (The Funnel)**
Monitor these specifically to diagnose where the 7,955 non-buyers drop off, reading from retention backward:

1.  **Consignment Acceptance Rate:** % of submitted high-value items accepted for sale. (If low, acquisition is wasted on inventory you cannot sell).
2.  **Authentication-to-Listing Time:** Average hours from item receipt to live listing. (Speed impacts buyer intent for exclusive goods).
3.  **Deposit-to-Ship Completion Rate:** % of deposits that result in final shipment. (This isolates post-commitment friction; if this is low, fix trust/payment flows, not acquisition).
4.  **Repeat Purchase Rate (90-day):** % of the 45 buyers who return. (In luxury, LTV relies on relationship, not one-off transactions).

**Event Taxonomy for Instrumentation**
Do not track "pageviews." Instrument only these specific events to answer named questions:
*   `high_value_item_listed` (Question: Is our inventory growing?)
*   `deposit_initiated` (Question: Is pricing/interest aligning?)
*   `shipment_confirmed` (Question: Did we realize value? -> **The North Star**)
*   `consignor_payout_completed` (Question: Is the supply side healthy?)

**Board Narrative Strategy**
Present the data as: "We acquired 8,000 visitors. Our 'Verified High-Value Settlement' rate is 0.56%. Our focus this quarter is not increasing top-of-funnel traffic, but improving the *Deposit-to-Ship Completion Rate* to maximize the value of existing intent."

**This Week's Actions**
1.  **Reclassify Historical Data:** Query your database to recalculate last season's conversion rate using *only* `shipment_confirmed` events >€10k. Compare this against the "deposit" count to quantify the over-optimism gap for the board.
2.  **Define the Leak Point:** Calculate the exact drop-off percentage between `deposit_initiated` and `shipment_confirmed`. If this gap is >20%, pause acquisition spend and investigate authentication or payment friction before the Milan meeting.

*Note: All metrics must be compared against LuxResale's own prior baseline; no external luxury benchmarks are applied here as they vary wildly by category and region.*
