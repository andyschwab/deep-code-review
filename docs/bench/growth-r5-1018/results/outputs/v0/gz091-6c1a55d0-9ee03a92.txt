**Executive Summary for CEO: Hazard Alert Feature Trial (March 3–17)**

**The Verdict: Do Not Roll Out.**
The 18% rise in Daily Active Users (DAU) is currently a **vanity metric** masking a critical failure in product value. The surge is driven by "thrashing" (repeated triggering due to instability or confusion), not genuine safety utility. With critical crashes rising from 4 to 27 (a 575% increase), the feature is actively degrading trust in our core brand promise: reliability. In safety equipment, instability is not a bug; it is a disqualifier.

**Metric Hierarchy & Interpretation**
To weigh these signals correctly, we must reject the top-line DAU growth and prioritize the following:

1.  **Primary North Star (Unverified): "Successful Hazard Resolutions."**
    *   *Definition:* A user triggers an alert AND confirms the hazard was resolved without app crash or re-trigger within 5 minutes.
    *   *Current Status:* **UNVERIFIED.** We lack the event taxonomy to distinguish a "test trigger" from a "real save." Until we instrument this, the 18% DAU lift is noise.
2.  **Critical Guardrail: Crash-Free Session Rate.**
    *   *Threshold:* Must exceed 99.5% for safety-critical tools.
    *   *Current Reality:* The spike to 27 critical crashes indicates the system is below this floor. One crash per 100 users is unacceptable when the user's physical safety depends on the app.
3.  **Diagnostic Metric: "Repeat Trigger Rate per Session."**
    *   *Hypothesis Validation:* If users trigger the alert >3 times per session without a "success" state, this confirms your assumption of interface confusion or sensitivity testing. This is the specific behavior driving the fake DAU growth.

**The Trade-off Analysis**
You are facing a false trade-off between "engagement" and "stability." There is no value in higher engagement if the engagement is users fighting a broken tool.
*   **Scenario A (Rollout now):** We scale the crash rate. A public failure where a safety app crashes during a real hazard event poses existential reputational risk and liability. The 18% DAU gain vanishes immediately upon the first major incident.
*   **Scenario B (Pause & Fix):** We treat the current DAU spike as a stress test that revealed a flaw. We lose two weeks of "growth" narrative but preserve the brand's integrity.

**This Week's Action Plan**
1.  **Immediate Halt:** Freeze the rollout. Communicate to the CEO that the "engagement" is confirmed as error-state behavior, not adoption.
2.  **Instrument the "Why":** Deploy a patch to log `alert_trigger_reason` (user-initiated vs. auto) and `session_outcome` (crash vs. resolved). Do not guess; measure the ratio of "thrashing" vs. "saving."
3.  **Define the Ship Criteria:** We will only reconsider rollout when:
    *   Critical crashes return to baseline (<5 per two weeks).
    *   "Repeat Trigger Rate" drops below 1.2 per session.
    *   We can prove a positive trend in "Successful Hazard Resolutions" using our own baseline data.

**Note:** The 27 crash reports represent a systemic failure mode, not random noise; fixing the sensitivity logic is a prerequisite to any further measurement.
