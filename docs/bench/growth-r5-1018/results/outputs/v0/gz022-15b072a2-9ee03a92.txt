### Diagnostic Plan: NomadStitch Activation Friction

**Objective:** Isolate the root cause of the 60% drop-off between profile verification and first bid submission to inform next Friday's sprint.

**1. Immediate Data Segmentation (Run Today)**
Do not treat the 850 onboarded artisans as a single block. Slice the existing data to confirm or refute the "mobile/cumbersome" hypothesis before designing new tests.
*   **Device & OS Split:** Calculate the drop-off rate separately for Mobile Web, iOS App, Android App, and Desktop. If the hypothesis is correct, mobile drop-off should significantly exceed desktop (e.g., >75% vs <40%).
*   **Day-of-Week Cohort:** You noted a spike on Tuesdays/Wednesdays. Compare the drop-off rate for users starting verification on Tue/Wed versus Thu–Mon. If the rate is consistent across days, the "Tuesday/Wednesday" volume is likely just higher traffic, not higher friction. If the *rate* spikes on those days, investigate external factors (e.g., network congestion in specific regions, support staff availability).
*   **Time-to-Complete:** Calculate the median time spent on the verification screen for completers vs. abandoners. A sharp cliff (e.g., abandoners leave at 45 seconds, completers take 3 minutes) suggests a specific blocking error; a gradual decay suggests boredom or confusion.

**2. Qualitative Triage (48-Hour Turnaround)**
Since qualitative evidence is missing, deploy a low-friction intercept to the 758 artisans who dropped off last quarter.
*   **Exit Survey Trigger:** If possible, trigger a one-question email/SMS to recent drop-offs: "What stopped you from submitting your first bid? (A) Camera/Upload failed, (B) Form too long, (C) Didn't understand requirements, (D) Other."
*   **Rapid Interviews:** Schedule 5–10 minute calls with 5 artisans who dropped off on mobile and 5 on desktop. Ask them to walk through the flow from memory. Look for specific vocabulary mismatches (e.g., they don't know what "identity verification" entails).

**3. Instrumentation Gaps (What is Currently `UNVERIFIED`)**
Your current data identifies *where* they leave, but not *why*. To make a confident decision, the following events must be instrumented immediately if not already present:
*   `verification_step_started` (property: `device_type`, `os_version`)
*   `verification_document_uploaded` (property: `upload_duration_ms`, `retry_count`)
*   `verification_error_displayed` (property: `error_code`, `field_name`)
*   `verification_abandoned` (property: `step_reached`, `time_on_step_seconds`)

Without `verification_error_displayed` and `retry_count`, you cannot distinguish between a broken camera API (technical bug) and a confusing UI (design flaw).

**4. Decision Matrix for Next Friday's Sprint**
Use the results above to select exactly one sprint focus:
*   **If Mobile Drop-off > 2x Desktop:** Sprint focuses on mobile UX (simplify upload, reduce fields, native camera integration).
*   **If High `retry_count` or Specific `error_code`:** Sprint focuses on engineering fixes (API stability, file size limits).
