**Growth Plan: EduMex FY2025 (July 1 Start)**

**1. North Star Metric: Weekly Active Learners Completing Core Modules**
Reject "Total Enrollments" as your primary target; it is a vanity metric that rises even when quality drops and support fails. Your North Star must be **students completing their weekly core vocational module**. This only rises if students are actually learning and staying engaged. If enrollments rise but this metric flatlines, you are scaling failure, not success.

**2. Operational Guardrail: The 24-Hour Support Ceiling**
Your current 48-hour response time indicates you have already exceeded your sustainable capacity.
*   **Rule:** Do not approve any acquisition campaign that pushes average support response time above **24 hours**.
*   **Action:** Calculate your "Support Capacity Limit" immediately: (Total Support Hours Available / 24 hours) = Max Concurrent Active Students.
*   **Target:** Set your enrollment growth target to match the rate at which you can hire/train support staff to maintain this 24-hour window, not the 20% month-over-month you assumed. If you cannot hire, your growth target is **0%** until response times drop.

**3. Funnel Diagnosis: Retention Before Acquisition**
Stop pouring effort into the 20% acquisition goal until you verify retention.
*   **The Leak:** With stretched support, churn is likely rising among organic users, let alone new partnership students.
*   **The Metric:** Measure **Week-4 Retention** (percentage of students still active 4 weeks after signup) separately for "Organic" vs. "Partnership" cohorts.
*   **Decision Rule:** If Partnership retention is lower than Organic (due to mismatched expectations or support delays), pause the partnership expansion until the gap closes. Acquiring low-retention students burns cash and team morale.

**4. Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only What Answers a Question)**
Do not track everything. Implement only these three events to answer specific questions:
*   `module_completed` (Properties: `student_id`, `module_type`, `days_since_start`): *Question: Are students getting value?*
*   `support_ticket_created` (Properties: `student_id`, `response_time_hours`, `source_channel`): *Question: Is support capacity breaking?*
*   `cohort_churned` (Properties: `student_id`, `acquisition_source`, `lifetime_days`): *Question: Which source brings sticky students?*

**5. Financial Reality: LTV Unknowns**
You stated LTV for partnership students is unknown.
*   **Status:** `UNVERIFIED`. Do not set revenue targets based on assumed LTV.
*   **Interim Proxy:** Use **90-Day Retention Rate** as a proxy for LTV. If partnership students stay 90 days at the same rate as organic, assume similar LTV. If they churn faster, their LTV is lower, and your cost to acquire them must be lower.
