### Activation Criteria Framework for GreenBalance
**Audience:** CEO and Marketing Lead
**Objective:** Distinguish curious visitors from committed buyers to optimize onboarding before the January $15k spend.

#### 1. The North Star & Definition of Activation
*   **North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Buyers (users purchasing ≥1kg of credits within a 7-day window).
*   **Primary Activation Event:** "First Purchase Completed" (Transaction > 0kg).
    *   *Rationale:* Per your core assumption, value is only realized when carbon is offset. Browsing or calculating footprint are leading indicators, not activation itself.
*   **Input Metrics (Leading Indicators):**
    *   `project_detail_view_count`: Views per session.
    *   `footprint_calculator_completed`: Binary (Yes/No).
    *   `time_to_first_purchase`: Duration from signup to transaction.

#### 2. Hypothesis Testing Plan (Pre-January Spend)
Do not guess thresholds; derive them from December data using this rigorous approach:

*   **Cohort Construction:** Group December users by behavior:
    *   Cohort A: Calculated footprint + Viewed Projects → Purchased.
    *   Cohort B: Viewed Projects Only → Purchased.
    *   Cohort C: Calculated Footprint Only → Purchased.
    *   Cohort D: Purchased Immediately (No prior engagement).
*   **Retention Correlation:** Measure Week-2 and Week-4 retention for each cohort.
    *   *Decision Rule:* If Cohort A retains 20% higher than Cohort B, "Footprint Calculation" is a required onboarding step. If no correlation exists, remove friction and push straight to purchase.
*   **Threshold Determination:**
    *   Analyze the distribution of `time_to_first_purchase` for users who retained >30 days.
    *   Set the **Activation Timeframe** at the 75th percentile of this group (e.g., if 75% of retained users bought within 48 hours, your activation window is 48 hours).
    *   Set the **Engagement Threshold** based on the median `project_detail_view_count` of retained users vs. churned users.

#### 3. Recommended Thresholds (Placeholders until Dec 15th Data Read)
*Since specific benchmarks cannot be fabricated, use these provisional rules until December data validates them:*
*   **Activation Window:** 7 Days from Signup. (Users not purchasing by Day 7 are likely "curious," not "committed").
*   **Engagement Signal:** Users viewing ≥3 project details OR completing the calculator have a higher probability of conversion. Treat this as a "Warm Lead" segment for email nurturing, not as "Activated."

#### 4. Immediate Next Steps (This Week)
1.  **Instrument Events:** Ensure `signup`, `footprint_calculator_completed`, `project_detail_view`, and `purchase_completed` are logged with a stable, pseudonymous User ID to track cross-session behavior.
2.  **Define Query:** Prepare the retention cohort query now: "Of users who signed up in Week 1 of December, what % purchased in Week 2, broken down by whether they used the calculator?"
3.  **Review Date:** Schedule a decision meeting for **December 18th**. Use the first 2.5 weeks of data to finalize the definition of "Activated" before unlocking the January budget.

**Note:** Do not optimize acquisition channels until the activation rate of the *current* traffic is stable; pouring budget into a leaky funnel wastes capital.
**Note:** All thresholds above must be recalculated against actual December retention data; do not hardcode these numbers into January campaigns without validation.
