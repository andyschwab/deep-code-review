### Proposal: Redefining Activation for NeoVerse

**Recommended New Activation Definition:**
"User completes a first transaction **AND** sets up a Savings Pocket with an initial deposit (≥€10) within 7 days of signup."

**Rationale & Validation Plan:**
Your current "first transaction" metric is a vanity signal; it rises while retention leaks. The 60% dormancy rate proves spending alone does not correlate with long-term value for Gen Z. The hypothesis is that *financial commitment* (savings) creates the "aha" moment, not social features. Social actions (splitting bills) are engagement drivers but likely lagging indicators of habit formation compared to the deliberate act of saving.

To validate this definition before next Wednesday without long-term data, execute the following immediate analysis on your existing 1,200 early adopters and a matched control group of 1,200 standard users:

1.  **Retrospective Cohort Comparison (The "Stickiness" Test):**
    *   **Query:** Compare Day-30 retention of the 1,200 Savings Pocket users against a control group of users who only transacted (matched by signup week and acquisition channel).
    *   **Success Signal:** If the Savings cohort's Day-30 retention is statistically higher than the control's (even with wide confidence intervals), it confirms the "savings = habit" hypothesis.
    *   **Action:** If retention is >10% higher, adopt the new definition immediately. If inconclusive due to sample size, proceed to step 2.

2.  **Correlation Matrix (Identifying the True Predictor):**
    *   **Query:** Calculate the correlation coefficient between Day-30 retention and three specific events: (A) First Transaction, (B) First Savings Deposit, (C) First Social Split.
    *   **Success Signal:** The event with the highest correlation to retention becomes your primary activation component.
    *   **Constraint:** Do not include "social features" in the core definition unless their correlation to retention exceeds that of savings. Gen Z values financial autonomy; saving is likely the stronger leading indicator.

3.  **Forward-Looking Instrumentation (The "This Week" Step):**
    *   **Event Taxonomy:** Instrument exactly three events to answer specific questions:
        *   `savings_pocket_created` (Properties: `initial_deposit_amount`, `time_since_signup`). *Question: Does deposit size correlate with retention?*
        *   `social_split_initiated` (Properties: `peer_count`, `time_since_signup`). *Question: Is social usage a precursor to saving or a distractor?*
        *   `core_action_completed` (Definition: Transaction + Savings Setup). *Question: Does this composite event predict Day-30 return better than transaction alone?*
    *   **Guardrail:** Monitor `average_time_to_first_deposit`. If the new definition makes activation impossible to reach (e.g., takes >14 days), the definition is too strict for a growth funnel.

**Decision Framework for the CPO:**
*   **Ship the New Definition If:** The retrospective check shows *any* positive lift in Day-30 retention for savers, or if the correlation of "Savings Setup" to retention is >0.3 while "Transaction Only" is <0.1.
*   **Hold/Refine If:** The data shows savers are just naturally higher-intent users (selection bias) and the act of saving doesn't *cause* retention. In this case, run an A/B test forcing a savings prompt during onboarding for 50% of new users to prove causality.
*   **Reject Social Features for Now:** Unless data explicitly shows "Splitting Bills" predicts retention better than "Saving," exclude it. Complexity dilutes the North Star.

**Immediate Next Steps (By Wednesday):**
1.  Run the SQL query for Day-30 retention comparison (Savers vs. Non-Savers) using existing data.
2.  Plot the retention curve for the 1,200 adopters vs. the baseline.
3.  Present the CPO with the single composite metric: "Activated = Transaction + Savings Deposit," backed by the observed retention delta.

*Note: Do not fabricate a "good" retention benchmark; compare only against NeoVerse's current 40% activation/60% churn baseline. If the 1,200 sample yields high variance, state the result as "directional evidence" and commit to a 4-week validation window.*
