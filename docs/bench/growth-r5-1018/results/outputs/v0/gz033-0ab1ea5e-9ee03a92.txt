### Instrumentation Plan for ML Fraud Model Impact

To measure the true cost of fraud versus manual review without generating noise, instrument only the following four events. Each event answers a specific decision question required for your Tuesday presentation. Do not log generic "page views" or intermediate model scores unless they directly trigger a state change.

#### 1. `transaction_decision_made`
*   **Question Answered:** Did the new model change the outcome compared to the baseline, and what was the immediate financial exposure?
*   **Trigger:** The ML model returns a final verdict (Allow, Block, or Review).
*   **Critical Fields:**
    *   `transaction_id` (stable identifier)
    *   `user_id` (pseudonymous, stable across sessions)
    *   `decision_outcome` (Enum: `allowed`, `blocked`, `manual_review`)
    *   `transaction_amount` (currency standardized)
    *   `is_high_value` (Boolean: based on your specific threshold)
    *   `model_confidence_score` (0.0–1.0)
    *   `previous_rule_outcome` (What the old system would have done, for A/B comparison)

#### 2. `manual_review_completed`
*   **Question Answered:** What is the actual operational cost (time/labor) of the "Review" bucket, and how accurate was the model's uncertainty?
*   **Trigger:** A risk analyst closes a ticket marked for review.
*   **Critical Fields:**
    *   `transaction_id`
    *   `reviewer_id`
    *   `time_to_resolve_seconds` (Start: event created, End: event closed)
    *   `final_verdict` (Enum: `legitimate`, `fraud`)
    *   `friction_applied` (Boolean: Did the user have to upload docs/call support?)

#### 3. `false_positive_confirmed`
*   **Question Answered:** Is the 5% false positive rate holding, and how much volume are we losing to friction?
*   **Trigger:** A blocked or heavily frictioned transaction is later confirmed as legitimate (via user appeal or successful retry).
*   **Critical Fields:**
    *   `transaction_id`
    *   `original_decision` (Enum: `blocked`, `manual_review`)
    *   `appeal_method` (Enum: `auto_retry`, `support_ticket`, `chargeback_reversal`)
    *   `days_to_resolution`

#### 4. `fraud_loss_realized`
*   **Question Answered:** What is the true cost of fraud that slipped through the "Allowed" bucket?
*   **Trigger:** A chargeback is filed and upheld, or fraud is confirmed post-transaction.
*   **Critical Fields:**
    *   `transaction_id`
    *   `loss_amount` (Transaction amount + fees)
    *   `days_to_detection`
    *   `model_confidence_score_at_time` (To analyze if the model was overconfident)

### Calculation Logic for Compliance Officer
To present the "True Cost" next Tuesday, aggregate these events into two formulas:

1.  **Cost of Friction (False Positives):**
    `(Count of false_positive_confirmed × Avg Transaction Amount) + (Count of manual_review_completed × Avg Time to Resolve × Analyst Hourly Rate)`
2.  **Cost of Fraud (False Negatives):**
    `Sum of fraud_loss_realized loss_amount`

**North Star Metric:** *Net Validated Volume* = (Total Allowed Volume) − (Fraud Losses) − (Estimated Churn from False Positives).
**Guardrail Metric:** *Manual Review Queue Depth* (Must not exceed current capacity).
