**3. Diagnostic Logic & Decision Rules**
Once the above events are live for 5 business days, apply these reading rules to determine the fix:
*   **Scenario A (Vendor Friction):** If the gap between `order_submitted` and `docs_complete_verified` exceeds 24 hours for >60% of delayed orders, the bottleneck is external.
    *   *Action:* Update vendor portal requirements or automate doc validation checks at submission.
*   **Scenario B (Finance Queue):** If `docs_complete_verified` to `finance_approval_decision` exceeds 48 hours, and `docs_attached_count` was sufficient at submission, the bottleneck is internal capacity.
    *   *Action:* Implement tiered approval limits (raise threshold to $15k) or add temporary approver capacity.
*   **Scenario C (Mixed/Unknown):** If data remains inconclusive due to variance, run a controlled process change on 10% of traffic (e.g., auto-approve trusted vendors under $12k) to measure cycle time impact directly.
