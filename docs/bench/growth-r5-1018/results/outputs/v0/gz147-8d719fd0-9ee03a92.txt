### Pricing Tier Health Evaluation Plan for DesignPro

**North Star Metric:** Net Revenue Per Active User (NRPAU) segmented by acquisition cohort, excluding one-time migration effects.
*Why:* Total revenue is currently distorted by downgrades. NRPAU isolates whether the new tier creates genuine value expansion or merely reshuffles existing spend.

**Primary Funnel Analysis (Retention First)**
Do not analyze acquisition yet. Diagnose the leak in retention and tier stability before assessing if the new plan attracts new users.
1.  **Tier Stability Rate (Weeks 4, 8, 12):** Measure the percentage of users who remain on their *initial* chosen tier after 30, 60, and 90 days.
    *   *Question:* Is the "Pro Plus" plan a stable destination or a waiting room for churn/downgrade?
    *   *Action:* If >15% of Pro Plus users downgrade to Basic within 60 days, the feature set is misaligned with the price point.
2.  **Cannibalization vs. Expansion Ratio:**
    *   *Formula:* (Revenue from new Pro Plus users previously on Basic) / (Revenue lost from Enterprise users downgrading to Pro Plus).
    *   *Threshold:* If this ratio is <1.5 after 90 days, the tier destroys margin.
3.  **Feature Utilization Gap:**
    *   *Instrument:* Track usage of the top 3 "Enterprise-only" features among Pro Plus users.
    *   *Question:* Are Pro Plus users hitting the ceiling of their plan (indicating a need to upgrade) or are they satisfied (indicating Enterprise is overpriced)?

**Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only These)**
*   `plan_changed`: Properties `{user_id, old_tier, new_tier, reason_code}`. Answers: "Where is the flow leaking?"
*   `feature_access_attempt`: Properties `{user_id, tier, feature_name, blocked_boolean}`. Answers: "Is the feature gap driving upgrades or frustration?"
*   `subscription_renewed`: Properties `{user_id, tier, mrr_value}`. Answers: "What is the true retention cost per tier?"

**Experiment Rigor & Reading Rules**
*   **Baseline Comparison:** Compare current churn and NRPAU against the pre-February 20 baseline. Do not use industry benchmarks for "good" churn; use your own historical trend.
*   **Cohort Separation:** Analyze "New Signups (Post-Feb 20)" separately from "Migrated Users." Mixing these conceals whether the tier works for new buyers versus confusing existing ones.
*   **Guardrail Metric:** Enterprise Plan Retention Rate. If this drops >5% relative to baseline, the test risks long-term brand devaluation regardless of short-term volume.

**Decision Framework for the Board**
*   **Keep:** If Tier Stability >85% at Day 90 AND Cannibalization Ratio >1.5.
*   **Adjust:** If Pro Plus users frequently attempt Enterprise features (high `feature_access_attempt` blocks) but do not upgrade. *Action:* Move one high-value feature from Enterprise to Pro Plus or raise Pro Plus price.
*   **Remove:** If Cannibalization Ratio <1.0 after 90 days AND Net Revenue Per Active User remains flat or declines. The 2% lift is likely temporary noise from migration.
