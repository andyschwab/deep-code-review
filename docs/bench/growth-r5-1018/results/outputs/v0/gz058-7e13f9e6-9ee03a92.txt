### Leading Indicators for GreenTech Oslo Pitch Deck

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Buildings (WAB) achieving >5% energy reduction.
*Why:* Cumulative pilot signings are a vanity metric in a long sales cycle. WAB proves the hardware delivers recurring value, which is the prerequisite for renewal and referral.

**Primary Leading Indicators (Fast-Moving Signals)**
Replace "number of buildings signed" with these three metrics in your deck to demonstrate momentum:

1.  **Activation Rate of Building Managers:** The % of signed pilots where the building manager logs into the dashboard and configures at least one automation rule within 14 days of install.
    *   *Signal:* High activation predicts successful long-term retention. If managers don't engage early, the 10% reduction is accidental, not systemic.
    *   *Target Question:* "Do users realize value immediately?"

2.  **Organic Referral Velocity:** The number of new pilot inquiries generated per active building manager via direct introduction or case study sharing.
    *   *Signal:* In a B2B sales cycle, peer validation shortens the 6-month lag. A rising referral rate indicates product-led growth before the sales team closes the next deal.
    *   *Target Question:* "Is the product selling itself?"

3.  **Energy Savings Consistency (Volatility Score):** The % of active buildings maintaining >5% reduction for 4 consecutive weeks.
    *   *Signal:* This separates a one-off weather anomaly from a sticky behavioral or systemic change. Investors need to know the 10% reduction is repeatable, not a fluke of October weather.
    *   *Target Question:* "Is the value durable?"

**Event Taxonomy for Immediate Instrumentation**
Instrument only these events to answer the questions above. Do not track generic pageviews.

| Event Name | Properties | Question Answered |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `manager_dashboard_login` | `building_id`, `days_since_install` | Are managers engaging post-install? |
| `automation_rule_created` | `rule_type`, `building_id` | Are they configuring the system for value? |
| `referral_link_shared` | `source_building_id`, `channel` | Is organic growth happening? |
| `weekly_energy_report_viewed` | `building_id`, `savings_percent` | Are they monitoring the outcome? |

**Reading the Data (Bottom-Up)**
Do not pour marketing spend into acquiring more pilots until **Activation Rate** exceeds 60% and **Consistency** holds for 8 weeks. If activation is low, the 6-month sales cycle will yield churn, not growth. Fix the onboarding experience for the current 50 buildings first; their behavior is your only valid baseline for predicting Series A success.

**This-Week Step**
1.  Query your current pilot data to calculate the **Activation Rate** (logins within 14 days) for the 50 October buildings.
2.  If the rate is <50%, delay the "growth" slide in your deck and instead present a "Product-Market Fit Validation" plan focused on improving manager onboarding.
3.  If the rate is >50%, add a "Referral Velocity" chart to your deck showing the organic pipeline generated by these 50 sites.

*Note: All benchmarks must be derived from your own 50-building baseline; do not cite industry averages for "good" activation rates as they vary wildly by hardware complexity. Policy changes are an external confounder; address them as a scenario risk, not a metric.*
