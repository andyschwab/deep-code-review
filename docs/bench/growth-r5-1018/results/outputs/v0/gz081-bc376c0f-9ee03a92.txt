### Measurement Plan: Neon Squirrels (First 30 Days)

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Users completing Puzzle 5 within 2 minutes.
*Why:* This directly measures the hypothesized "value moment" (speed + completion) rather than vanity metrics like total downloads or raw signups, which do not guarantee engagement or revenue.

**Priority Event Taxonomy**
Do not track every tap; it creates noise and infrastructure cost without answering specific questions. Instrument only these four events:

1.  `tutorial_start`
    *   **Question:** How many users actually begin the core loop?
    *   **Properties:** `user_id` (pseudonymous), `session_id`, `device_type`.
2.  `level_complete`
    *   **Question:** Where is the specific drop-off point in the tutorial?
    *   **Properties:** `level_number` (1–5), `time_spent_seconds`, `attempts_count`.
3.  `tutorial_exit`
    *   **Question:** Do users quit voluntarily before finishing, or does the app crash?
    *   **Properties:** `exit_level_number`, `exit_reason` (if detectable, e.g., "force_close").
4.  `first_purchase`
    *   **Question:** Does the speed/completion hypothesis hold true?
    *   **Properties:** `item_id`, `price_usd`, `time_since_install_minutes`.

**How to Interpret Initial Results**
Read the funnel bottom-up, starting with retention, not acquisition.
*   **The Leak Check:** Compare your new Day-1 return rate against the beta baseline of 42%. If the new rate is significantly lower, stop all marketing spend immediately. Acquisition is wasted on a leaking bucket.
*   **The Drop-Off Point:** Analyze `level_complete` to find the largest percentage drop between consecutive levels (e.g., if 80% pass Level 1 but only 30% pass Level 2, Level 2 is the blocker). Fix the specific level design before optimizing anything else.
*   **The Hypothesis Test:** Correlate `time_spent_seconds` on levels 1–5 with `first_purchase`. If users completing under 2 minutes do *not* show a higher purchase rate than slower users, the assumption is **UNVERIFIED** and false. Do not optimize for speed until this correlation is proven with live data.

**This Week's Steps**
1.  Implement the four events above with pseudonymous `user_id` stitching to track cross-session behavior; ignore all other taps.
2.  Set a dashboard alert to trigger if Day-1 retention falls below 35% (a conservative buffer from your 42% beta baseline).
3.  Run a manual query on day 7 comparing purchase rates of users who finished the tutorial in <2 mins vs. >2 mins to validate or reject the core monetization hypothesis.

*Note: All benchmarks must be derived from your own live data trends; no external industry averages are applied here. Ensure `user_id` handling complies with privacy regulations before ingestion.*
