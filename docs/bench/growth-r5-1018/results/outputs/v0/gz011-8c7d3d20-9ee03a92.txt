### Executive Summary: Video Streaming A/B Test Plan
**Decision Deadline:** October 20, 2024 | **Test Start:** October 3, 2024

**1. Primary Metric & Guardrails**
*   **Primary Metric:** *Bid Frequency per Active User* (Total Bids / Unique Bidders) in the Gobi region.
    *   *Success Threshold:* A statistically significant lift of ≥15% over the control group.
*   **Guardrail Metric:** *Page Load Latency (95th percentile)* and *Session Drop-off Rate*.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If latency increases by >200ms or drop-off rises by >5% in the treatment group, halt the test immediately to prevent infrastructure strain.

**2. Experimental Design Adjustments**
*   **Seasonality Control:** Do not assume stabilization within five days post-Naadam without data. Instead, use a **CUPED (Controlled-Experiment Using Pre-Experiment Data)** approach using the 7 days *prior* to the festival as a covariate to reduce variance, or extend the test duration to 14 days to wash out post-festival noise.
*   **Uneven Sample Sizes:** Due to connectivity issues causing uneven splits, do not use a standard Z-test. Use **Welch's t-test** (which handles unequal variances and sample sizes) or a **Bayesian hierarchical model** to estimate the lift.
*   **Peeking Prevention:** Pre-commit to a sample size of **1,200 users per group** (calculated to detect a 15% lift with 80% power). Do not check significance daily. Analyze results **once** on October 17th. Checking early inflates false positives; a "win" on day 3 is `UNVERIFIED`.

**4. Interpretation & Budget Decision Matrix**
*   **Scenario A (Ship):** Bid Frequency ↑ ≥15% AND Latency stable.
    *   *Action:* Allocate Q4 budget to **Infrastructure Upgrades** (CDN/edge nodes) to support video scale.
*   **Scenario B (Kill):** Bid Frequency ↑ <5% OR Latency ↑ >200ms.
    *   *Action:* Allocate Q4 budget to **Marketing** (static image campaigns); video is not ROI-positive for Gobi connectivity.
*   **Scenario C (Inconclusive):** Confidence interval crosses 0% or sample size <80% of target due to connectivity.
    *   *Action:* Extend test 7 days. If still inconclusive, treat as **Kill** for Q4 planning.
