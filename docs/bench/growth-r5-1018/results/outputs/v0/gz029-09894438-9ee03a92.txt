# SkyDrop Beta Measurement Framework: Technical Performance & Human Experience

**North Star Metric:** "Successful Calm Deliveries" (SCD)
*Definition:* A delivery is counted as an SCD only if: (1) The package lands within the designated zone without technical fault, AND (2) The recipient reports an anxiety level ≤ 3/10 post-flight.
*Why:* Speed alone is vanity if the experience terrifies users. This metric rises only when both safety and comfort are achieved.

## 1. The Funnel (Read Bottom-Up)
Do not optimize acquisition (number of test users) until retention and experience are stable.
*   **Retention/Experience (Primary Focus):** Did the user accept a second delivery? What was their anxiety score?
*   **Activation:** Did the user complete the pre-flight consent and landing zone setup?
*   **Acquisition:** Number of beta sign-ups. (Ignore this if Experience metrics are failing).

## 2. Event Taxonomy (Answering Named Questions)
Instrument only these specific events to answer critical go/no-go questions. Do not track "everything."

| Event Name | Question Answered | Properties to Capture |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `flight_anxiety_peak` | How anxious did the user get during the flight? | `user_id` (pseudonymous), `flight_phase` (approach/hover/landing), `self_reported_score` (1-10), `timestamp` |
| `landing_reaction` | Did the landing mechanism cause alarm? | `user_id`, `reaction_type` (surprised/scared/neutral/pleased), `distance_from_target` (meters) |
| `core_delivery_complete` | Did the technical handoff succeed? | `user_id`, `total_flight_time`, `battery_remaining`, `weather_condition` |
| `repeat_request` | Is the service viable for commercial use? | `user_id`, `days_since_last_delivery`, `referral_sent` (boolean) |

**Identifier Seam:** Use a stable, pseudonymous `user_id` to link flight telemetry with user feedback. Do not store names or addresses in this analytics stream; route PII to secure compliance storage.

## 3. Stage-Specific Instrumentation (MVP Phase)
Since this is an MVP/Beta:
*   **Measure:** Activation (setup completion), Retention (repeat requests), and the core "Anxiety vs. Speed" correlation.
*   **Ignore:** Referral rates or complex revenue modeling until the anxiety baseline is established.

## 4. Experiment Rigor: Testing the "Speed" Assumption
You assume speed is the #1 factor. Do not guess; test it against anxiety.
*   **Hypothesis:** "Users prefer faster deliveries even if anxiety is slightly higher."
*   **Design:** A/B Test (Pre-committed).
    *   **Variant A (Speed Priority):** Drones fly at max safe velocity.
    *   **Variant B (Comfort Priority):** Drones fly slower, with louder approach warnings and slower descent.
*   **Pre-commitment:** Define sample size (e.g., 250 deliveries per variant) and duration (full 30-day window) *before* looking at results.
*   **Primary Metric:** `Successful Calm Deliveries` rate.
*   **Guardrail Metric:** `Technical Fault Rate` (must not increase in Variant B).
*   **Rule:** Do not peek at day 5. If you check early, you risk false positives. Read once at the end of the 500 deliveries.

## 5. Go/No-Go Decision Criteria
Make the cut based on these specific thresholds derived from your 500 tests:
*   **GO:** `Successful Calm Deliveries` rate > 85% AND `repeat_request` rate > 40%.
*   **NO-GO:** Anxiety score average > 6/10 OR `repeat_request` rate < 20%.
*   **PIVOT:** If speed correlates negatively with retention but anxiety is low, shift marketing from "Fastest" to "Smoothest."
