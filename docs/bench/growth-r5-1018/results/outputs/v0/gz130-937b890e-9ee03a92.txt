**Executive Summary: Donate Button A/B Test Recommendation**

**Decision:** Do not declare a winner yet. Extend the test for exactly 7 days (not 14) to reach a pre-committed sample size, then make a final go/no-go decision before the December 31st campaign end.

**Current Data Reliability**
Your current result (45 donations from 2,500 visitors) yields an observed conversion rate of **1.8%**. While this provides a baseline, the sample size is too small to rule out random noise. Declaring a winner now risks a "false positive"—adopting a change that appears better due to chance but fails to deliver value long-term. Conversely, stopping early could miss a genuine lift. The data is **directional but unverified**; it cannot support a causal claim that the new color caused the conversions without a completed, pre-committed test design.

**Recommended Next Steps**
1.  **Pre-commit to a Sample Size:** Calculate the minimum visitors needed to detect a meaningful lift (e.g., a 20% relative increase from your baseline). Assuming a standard baseline conversion of ~1.5% and aiming for 80% statistical power, you likely need ~3,500–4,000 visitors per variant to be confident.
2.  **Extend by 7 Days Only:** Given the tight budget and the December 31st deadline, a 14-day extension is too risky. Run the test for 7 more days. If traffic remains consistent (~357 visitors/day), this will add ~2,500 visitors, bringing the total to ~5,000. This approaches the threshold needed for a reliable read on moderate effects.
3.  **Define the Stop Rule Now:** Before looking at any new data, agree on the rule: "If the new variant shows a statistically significant lift (p < 0.05) in conversion rate after 7 days, we ship it. If not, we revert to the control to protect donation revenue."
4.  **Guardrail Check:** Monitor the **average donation amount** daily. If the new button increases conversion but lowers the average gift size (e.g., attracting smaller donors), the net revenue impact could be negative. A win on conversion is a loss if total revenue drops.

**Required Context for Final Decision**
To confidently declare a winner, you must segment the final data by:
*   **Device Type (Mobile vs. Desktop):** Button visibility issues often differ by screen size. If the lift is only on mobile, the fix is specific; if uniform, the color works broadly.
*   **Traffic Source:** Ensure the split is balanced between paid ads, email, and organic. A skew in high-intent traffic (e.g., email) to one variant will invalidate the result.
*   **Time-of-Day/Day-of-Week:** Verify the 7-day extension covers a full weekly cycle to smooth out weekend/weekday donation patterns.

**Immediate Action:** Lock the test parameters today, run for 7 days, and analyze the aggregate conversion rate and average gift size once the traffic target is hit. Do not peek at the results mid-week to avoid biasing the decision.

*Note: All metrics must be compared to your own historical baseline; no external benchmarks are used. If the 7-day extension does not reach the required sample size due to low traffic, the result remains "Unverified," and the default action is to revert to the control.*
