**Report Structure: Onboarding Experiment Impact & Sustainability Assessment**
**To:** Mrs. Reyes, Board Member
**From:** Growth Lead, BituinLearn
**Date:** [Current Date]
**Subject:** Revenue Impact Analysis of Video Onboarding (Oct 12 – Nov 5)

**1. Executive Summary & The Core Question**
The video onboarding variant increased course completion by 14% among 3,400 new signups. However, completion is a vanity metric if it does not convert to paid enrollment or if it incurs unsustainable support costs. This report isolates the *net revenue impact* to determine if the production cost of additional videos is justified.

**2. Critical Data Points for Revenue Isolation**
To move beyond completion rates, the report must present the following funnel metrics, segmented by variant (Video vs. Text):
*   **Activation to Paid Conversion:** The percentage of users who completed onboarding *and* enrolled in the first paid module within 14 days. This validates the assumption that completion drives revenue.
*   **Revenue Per Visitor (RPV):** Total revenue generated divided by total signups in each variant. This is the single most important financial metric for the board.
*   **Support Cost Ratio:** Number of support tickets generated per 100 users in each variant. With a staff of twelve, a spike in ticket volume could erode the margin gained from higher conversions.
*   **Cohort Retention (Weeks 1–4):** Percentage of enrolled students who remain active after the first month. This addresses the risk of drop-off post-first module.

**3. Presenting Uncertainty & Long-Term Value**
Since the test ended on November 5, we lack full data on long-term retention and lifetime value (LTV). The report will visualize this uncertainty using:
*   **Confidence Intervals on RPV:** Instead of a single "lift" number, we will show the range of probable revenue outcomes. If the intervals overlap significantly, the result is statistically inconclusive despite the 14% completion lift.
*   **The "Break-Even" Support Threshold:** A clear line showing how many additional tickets the video variant can generate before the support costs cancel out the extra revenue. If current ticket trends approach this line, the risk is high.
*   **Status Labeling:** Any metric relying on data beyond November 5 (e.g., 30-day retention) will be explicitly marked as **UNVERIFIED** until the cohort matures. We will not fabricate long-term projections.

**4. Recommendation & Next Steps**
*   **If RPV is positive and support costs are stable:** Proceed with a phased rollout of video content to 50% of traffic while monitoring the support queue daily.
*   **If RPV is flat or support costs spike:** Halt video production immediately. The 14% completion lift is a false positive for business growth.
*   **Immediate Action:** Run the specific query for "Paid Enrollment within 14 Days" and "Support Tickets tagged 'Onboarding'" for both variants by end-of-day tomorrow. Without these two figures, the business case remains unproven.

***

*Note: This analysis relies strictly on BituinLearn's internal data; no external industry benchmarks for edtech retention or conversion were used or assumed.*
*The 14% completion lift is treated as an intermediate signal, not a final win, until correlated with the revenue and cost data above.*
