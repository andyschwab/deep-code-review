**Deliverable: Trial-to-Paid Prediction Framework for ShieldWall**

**1. Redefine the North Star and Input Metrics**
Stop using "Team Invite" as your primary conversion proxy. It is a vanity metric in this context: it rises (30%) while revenue falls (5%), indicating it no longer predicts value.
*   **New North Star:** "Weekly Active Enterprises Generating Actionable Alerts." This measures actual product value (threat detection), not administrative setup.
*   **Primary Input Metric to Instrument:** "Time-to-First-Value (TTFV) Event." For B2B security, this is likely **"First Custom Report Generated"** or **"First API Key Integrated & Ingesting Data."** These actions prove the user has connected their data source and derived insight, which precedes the decision to buy.

**2. Event Taxonomy: Answer Named Questions Only**
Do not instrument "everything." Implement only these three events to answer specific predictive questions:
*   `report_generated` (Properties: `report_type`, `time_since_signup`, `data_source_connected`).
    *   *Question:* Does creating a custom report correlate with paid conversion more strongly than inviting a teammate?
*   `api_integration_complete` (Properties: `integration_type`, `volume_of_logs_ingested`).
    *   *Question:* Is deep technical integration a stronger predictor of enterprise retention than social invites?
*   `team_invite_sent` (Properties: `time_since_signup`, `role_of_invitee`).
    *   *Question:* Does the *timing* of the invite matter? (Hypothesis: Invites after day 7 predict conversion; invites on day 1 do not).

**3. Analysis Method: Retention-First Cohort Correlation**
Do not look at aggregate averages. Perform a **cohort analysis** segmented by the *first* meaningful action taken:
*   **Cohort A:** Users who invited a teammate within 48 hours.
*   **Cohort B:** Users who generated a custom report within 7 days.
*   **Cohort C:** Users who completed API integration within 7 days.

Calculate the **conversion rate to paid** and **90-day retention** for each cohort. Compare these against your historical 8% baseline.
*   *Expected Finding:* Cohort A likely has low conversion (the "false positive" signal you are seeing). Cohort B or C will likely show a conversion rate significantly higher than 8%.
*   *Action:* If Cohort B shows a 20% conversion rate, shift your trial success definition from "Invite Sent" to "Report Generated."
