### Executive Steering Committee Report: KapaMarket Launch Metrics
**Date:** November 12th Preparation
**Objective:** Validate the hypothesis that trust in device condition drives retention more than price during the Johannesburg/Cape Town expansion.

To avoid vanity metrics (e.g., total signups or pageviews) and focus on your core assumption, monitor these three actionable metrics daily. Each directly links to the goal of 500 transactions and long-term retention.

#### 1. Core Action Retention Rate (Week 1 Cohort)
*   **Definition:** The percentage of users who complete a **second** transaction within 30 days of their first successful purchase.
*   **Why it matters:** This is your true North Star input. If your hypothesis is correct, users who trust the device condition will return regardless of minor price fluctuations. High acquisition with low second-transaction rates indicates a "leaky bucket" where trust is broken at the first delivery.
*   **Link to Goal:** Directly predicts whether the 500-transaction target is sustainable or requires constant, expensive re-acquisition.
*   **Data to Capture Immediately:** `user_id` (pseudonymous), `transaction_id`, `transaction_date`, `device_category`, `city`.

#### 2. Trust-Verification Conversion Delta
*   **Definition:** The conversion rate difference between users who view a **Seller Verification Badge** or **Condition Report** versus those who do not, specifically for high-value items (>R2000).
*   **Why it matters:** This isolates the "trust" variable. If the conversion lift for verified sellers is significantly higher than for non-verified sellers, it validates that verification drives the sale more than price alone. If there is no delta, your verification process is invisible or untrusted.
*   **Link to Goal:** Ensures the 15,000 waitlist converts efficiently by highlighting the specific trust signal that unlocks revenue.
*   **Data to Capture Immediately:** `session_id`, `item_viewed_id`, `seller_verification_status` (boolean), `condition_report_viewed` (boolean), `added_to_cart` (boolean), `price_point`.

#### 3. Post-Purchase Dispute Resolution Success Rate
*   **Definition:** The percentage of opened disputes regarding "device condition" that are resolved without a refund chargeback or negative seller rating.
*   **Why it matters:** You asked whether to track dispute volume or resolution. Volume is a lagging indicator of failure; **resolution success** is a leading indicator of retained trust. A high volume of disputes resolved fairly proves your platform protects the user, reinforcing the trust hypothesis. Unresolved disputes kill retention immediately.
*   **Link to Goal:** Protects the reputation required to hit 500 transactions in new cities where word-of-mouth is critical.
*   **Data to Capture Immediately:** `dispute_id`, `dispute_reason` (categorized: condition vs. shipping vs. other), `resolution_type` (refund/partial/refusal), `user_satisfaction_score` (post-resolution), `days_to_resolve`.

### Immediate Instrumentation Requirements
To validate your hypothesis from Day 1, your event taxonomy must capture the following properties on every relevant event. Do not instrument "everything"; capture only what answers the questions above:

1.  **Identity Seam:** A stable, pseudonymous `user_id` is mandatory to link the first purchase to the second (Retention). Do not rely on session cookies alone.
2.  **Trust Exposure:** Every `view_item` event must include properties for `seller_verified_status` and `condition_report_available`. Without this, you cannot calculate the Conversion Delta.
3.  **Outcome Granularity:** Every `purchase_complete` and `dispute_closed` event must tag the `city` (Johannesburg vs. Cape Town) and `device_category` to detect local trust variances.

**Verdict on Your Specific Question:** Prioritize tracking **Dispute Resolution Success Rate** over raw dispute volume or verification check frequency. Frequency of checks is an intermediate behavior that may not correlate to sales; successful resolution is the actual moment trust is preserved or lost.

*Note: All baseline comparisons must be made against your own Day 1–7 performance; no external "industry standard" retention rates exist for this specific market context. If data for any metric is missing at launch, label it `UNVERIFIED` rather than estimating.*
