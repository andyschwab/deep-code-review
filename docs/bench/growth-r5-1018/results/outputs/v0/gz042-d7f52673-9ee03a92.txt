### Prioritized Event Taxonomy for NutriPath Mobile Launch

**1. Core Value Activation: `glucose_log_completed`**
*   **Question Answered:** Does the user perform the primary value action (logging health data) within the critical 48-hour window?
*   **Properties:** `timestamp`, `days_since_signup`, `device_type`, `is_first_log` (boolean).
*   **Why:** Validates your hypothesis that early logging predicts 6-month retention. This is your primary activation metric. Without this, you cannot distinguish curiosity from commitment.

**2. Engagement Depth: `recipe_interaction_depth`**
*   **Question Answered:** Is passive viewing or active utility (exporting) the stronger retention signal for this niche?
*   **Properties:** `recipe_id`, `interaction_type` (enum: `viewed`, `saved`, `grocery_list_exported`), `time_on_page_seconds`.
*   **Why:** Resolves the ambiguity between "recipe views" and "grocery exports." By tagging the interaction type, you can correlate specific actions against future retention without instrumenting two separate heavy events.

**3. Retention Cohort Anchor: `subscription_renewal_confirmed`**
*   **Question Answered:** Are users actually staying subscribed at the 30, 60, and 90-day marks?
*   **Properties:** `subscription_cycle_number`, `plan_type`, `cohort_week` (ISO week of signup).
*   **Why:** Since you lack 6-month historical data, you must rigorously track the 90-day window you have. This event allows you to build weekly retention cohorts immediately. It is the "bottom-up" metric to verify if activation efforts are working.

**4. Friction Point: `onboarding_step_abandoned`**
*   **Question Answered:** Where do users drop off before reaching the "aha" moment of logging glucose?
*   **Properties:** `step_name` (e.g., `profile_creation`, `condition_selection`, `first_log_prompt`), `exit_reason` (if available, else null).
*   **Why:** With only two developers, you cannot fix everything. This pinpoints exactly where the new mobile interface fails, allowing targeted fixes rather than guessing.

**5. Privacy-Safe Identity: `pseudonymous_user_session_start`**
*   **Question Answered:** Can we track a single user across devices without storing PII in analytics?
*   **Properties:** `user_pseudonym_id` (hashed, non-reversible), `session_id`, `app_version`.
*   **Why:** **The Identifier Seam.** To measure retention and activation accurately, you must link events to a stable user ID. This event establishes that link using a pseudonymous ID, adhering to patient privacy norms by ensuring no raw health data or personally identifiable information (PII) enters the analytics pipeline.

***

**Note:** Do not instrument "pageviews" or generic clicks; they are vanity metrics that obscure the signal for your specific hypothesis.
**Note:** All health data (glucose values, specific conditions) must remain in your secure database; only the *event occurrence* and *metadata* listed above should be sent to the analytics pipeline.
