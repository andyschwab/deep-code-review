### Measurement Plan: Q4 Revenue Alignment for SalesForce IL

**1. The North Star & Leading Indicator**
Reject "demo scheduled" as your primary leading indicator; it measures activity, not value. Adopt **"Proposal Sent"** as your single North Star input metric. In a 90-day B2B cycle with a $45k ACV, a demo is merely a handshake; a proposal represents validated intent and economic alignment. Measuring demos risks incentivizing the sales team to book low-quality meetings that drain capacity without moving revenue.

**2. Event Taxonomy (Answering Named Questions)**
Instrument only these three events to bridge the gap between sign-up and close:
*   `demo_completed` (Properties: `time_to_demo`, `attendee_role`): Answers "Does speed-to-demo correlate with proposal generation?"
*   `proposal_sent` (Properties: `deal_size_estimate`, `stage_duration`): Answers "Is this opportunity truly qualified?"
*   `deal_closed_won` (Properties: `revenue`, `cycle_length`): The lagging truth.

**3. Defining a "Qualified Opportunity"**
To align the ten-person sales team, a lead is **Qualified** only when:
1.  A demo has been completed within 7 days of sign-up.
2.  A proposal has been sent with a defined budget holder present.
*Action:* Configure your CRM to withhold commission credit or "qualified" status until `proposal_sent` is logged. This stops the team from chasing vanity metrics (demos) and focuses them on advancing deals to the economic buying stage.

**4. Handling the 90-Day Lag**
Do not wait 90 days to read results. Use a **cohort analysis** based on the `proposal_sent` date.
*   **Leading Signal:** Track the conversion rate from `proposal_sent` → `deal_closed_won` for cohorts aged 30, 60, and 90 days.
*   **Prediction:** If the 30-day cohort shows a 20% close rate, and you need $1M in Q4, you can mathematically derive the required `proposal_sent` volume today without waiting for the cycle to finish.
*   **Verification Note:** Your assumption that demos increase conversion by 30% is currently `UNVERIFIED`. You must compare the `proposal_sent` rate of users who received a demo <24h vs. those who did not, using your existing 200 leads as the baseline.

**5. Reporting Cadence & Audience**
*   **Who:** Sales Director and the 10 Account Executives.
*   **Frequency:** **Weekly**, every Monday morning.
*   **Format:** A single-page dashboard showing:
    1.  Count of `proposal_sent` last week (vs. target).
    2.  Conversion rate of proposals sent 60+ days ago (the "harvest" view).
    3.  Average `time_to_demo` for new leads.
*   **Why Weekly?** Daily reviews create noise in long cycles; monthly reviews are too slow to adjust outreach. Weekly allows you to pivot tactics (e.g., changing demo scripts) while the cohort is still active.

**This Week's Step:**
1.  Query your current 200 leads to calculate the actual conversion rate from `demo_completed` to `proposal_sent` for those contacted within 24 hours versus those contacted later.
2.  If the data shows no statistical lift, halt the "24-hour demo" mandate and retrain the team on qualification criteria before the next proposal is sent.

*Note: Do not invent industry benchmarks for "good" proposal conversion rates; use your own historical data from the August 1st cohort as the sole baseline.*
*Ensure user identifiers in your analytics are pseudonymous to comply with privacy standards when tracking individual sales rep performance.*
