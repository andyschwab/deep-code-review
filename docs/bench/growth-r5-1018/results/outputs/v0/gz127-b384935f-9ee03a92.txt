### Instrumentation Plan: Saborea Review Flow Optimization

**Objective:** Validate whether photo uploads increase review helpfulness and determine the optimal timing for review prompts (immediate post-payment vs. next-day) without degrading checkout completion.

**1. Core North Star & Input Metrics**
*   **North Star:** *Booking Conversion Rate driven by High-Quality Reviews.* (We measure if photo-rich reviews actually lead to more bookings, not just higher "helpfulness" scores).
*   **Input Metrics:** Review Completion Rate, Photo Attachment Rate, Average Helpfulness Score per Review, Checkout Drop-off Rate.

**2. Event Taxonomy (What to Tag)**
Instrument only these specific events to answer the named questions. Do not tag generic clicks.

| Event Name | Trigger Point | Properties to Capture | Question Answered |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | : |
| `checkout_step_completed` | Final payment success screen | `timestamp`, `user_id` (pseudonymous), `restaurant_id` | Baseline for funnel entry; ensures checkout integrity. |
| `review_prompt_shown` | Moment prompt appears | `timing_strategy` ("immediate" vs "delayed"), `session_id` | Does the prompt appear correctly for both cohorts? |
| `review_prompt_dismissed` | User closes prompt without action | `timing_strategy`, `time_since_checkout` | **Friction:** Does immediate prompting cause higher dismissal? |
| `review_submission_started` | User types first character or opens camera | `timing_strategy`, `has_photo_intent` (bool) | Activation: Are users engaging with the new flow? |
| `photo_upload_attempted` | User selects/takes a photo | `source` ("camera" vs "gallery"), `file_size_kb` | **Friction:** Is the upload mechanism failing or too slow? |
| `review_submitted` | Final success confirmation | `has_photo` (bool), `timing_strategy`, `rating_value`, `text_length_chars` | **Quality:** Does the "immediate" cohort attach fewer photos? |
| `review_helpfulness_voted` | Another user clicks "Helpful" | `target_review_id`, `voter_user_id`, `target_has_photo` (bool) | **Hypothesis Check:** Do photo reviews get 2x more votes? |
| `booking_initiated_from_listing` | User clicks "Book" on a restaurant page | `listing_review_mix` (% of visible reviews with photos), `restaurant_id` | **North Star:** Do listings with more photo reviews drive more bookings? |

**3. Experiment Design & Reading Rules**
*   **Design:** A/B Test (50/50 split).
    *   **Control:** Prompt shown 24 hours post-meal (current behavior).
    *   **Variant:** Prompt shown immediately after `checkout_step_completed`.
*   **Guardrail Metric:** `checkout_step_completed` rate. If the variant shows a statistically significant drop (>1%) in successful checkouts due to UI interference, **stop immediately**.
*   **Primary Metric:** `review_submitted` rate segmented by `has_photo`.
*   **Reading Rule:** Do not peek at results before reaching 5,000 completed reviews per cohort (approx. 5 days at 10% baseline). A difference observed on Day 1 is `UNVERIFIED`.

**4. Specific Signals for Photo Influence**
To determine if photos influence future booking decisions, correlate `booking_initiated_from_listing` with the `listing_review_mix`.
*   **Signal:** Compare booking conversion rates for restaurants where >30% of recent reviews have photos vs. those with <10%.
*   **Threshold:** If listings with high photo density show a >2% lift in booking conversion, the hypothesis is validated.
