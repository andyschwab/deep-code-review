### MarketPlace NG: First 30-Day Liquidity Dashboard

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Users Completing a Transaction (Buyers + Sellers).
*Why:* This measures actual value delivery. Cumulative signups or pageviews are vanity; they rise regardless of whether the marketplace works. Only a completed match proves liquidity.

**Core Funnel Metrics (Read Bottom-Up)**
Monitor these daily to diagnose where the funnel leaks. Do not pour marketing spend (Acquisition) until Retention and Activation stabilize.

1.  **Listing Conversion Rate (Activation)**
    *   *Definition:* % of registered sellers who publish at least one live listing within 24 hours of signup.
    *   *Why:* Without supply, buyers bounce. This validates your "cold start" supply assumption.
2.  **Match Rate (Liquidity Health)**
    *   *Definition:* % of live listings that receive an inquiry or purchase within 48 hours.
    *   *Why:* You asked about the listings-to-buyers ratio. There is no universal "good" benchmark for the Nigerian context we can fabricate. Instead, track this rate against your own Day 1 baseline. If this trends down, your ratio is broken, regardless of the absolute number.
3.  **Transaction Count vs. GMV**
    *   *Decision:* Track **Transaction Count** only for the first 30 days.
    *   *Why:* At a target of 100 transactions/week, Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) will be noisy and statistically insignificant. Count proves the mechanism works; value optimizes later.
4.  **Day-7 Return Rate (Retention)**
    *   *Definition:* % of users (buyers or sellers) from Cohort X who perform a core action again in Week 2.
    *   *Why:* Acquisition is wasted if users do not return. This is the first metric to fix if growth stalls.

**Dashboard Layout for Advisory Board**
*   **Top Row (The Verdict):** Weekly Active Transactors (North Star) | Total Transactions (Count) | Day-7 Return Rate.
*   **Middle Row (The Diagnosis):** Listing Conversion Rate | Match Rate (Inquiry/Purchase per Listing) | Time-to-First-Transaction (Median).
*   **Bottom Row (The Supply/Demand Balance):** New Listings Created | New Buyer Signups | Ratio of Active Buyers to Active Sellers (7-day rolling).

**Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only These)**
Do not instrument "everything." Define these specific events to answer named questions:
*   `listing_published` (Properties: `seller_id`, `category`, `time_since_signup`) → *Question: Do sellers activate quickly?*
*   `item_viewed` (Properties: `buyer_id`, `listing_id`) → *Question: Is there demand?*
*   `transaction_completed` (Properties: `buyer_id`, `seller_id`, `amount`) → *Question: Did we deliver value?*
*   `user_returned` (Properties: `user_id`, `days_since_last_action`) → *Question: Is the product sticky?*

**Experiment & Measurement Rules**
*   **No Fabricated Benchmarks:** We will not assert what a "good" match rate is. We will compare Week 2 performance strictly against Week 1. If the trend is positive, the ratio is working.
*   **Identifier Seam:** Ensure every event carries a stable, pseudonymous `user_id` to track cohorts across sessions. This is distinct from system logs and required for product analysis.
*   **Stage Appropriateness:** As an MVP launch, we ignore complex revenue funnels or referral metrics until the core transaction loop (Listing → Match → Sale) is stable.

**This Week's Steps**
1.  **Implement Tracking:** Instrument the four events above with strict `user_id` persistence before launch day.
2.  **Set Baseline:** On Day 7, record the initial Match Rate and Listing Conversion Rate. These become your only valid benchmarks for Month 1.
3.  **Liquidity Trigger:** If Match Rate drops below 50% of the Day 1 baseline for two consecutive days, pause buyer acquisition and focus entirely on seller onboarding incentives.
