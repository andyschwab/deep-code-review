### Prioritized Event Taxonomy for GreenFlow Irrigation Beta

To justify funding and validate value without fabricating benchmarks, instrument only these three events. They shift the North Star from "engagement frequency" (vanity) to "reliable automation" (customer value).

**1. `automation_cycle_completed`**
*   **Definition:** Fired when the valve executes a scheduled watering cycle without user intervention or error.
*   **Properties:** `zone_id`, `scheduled_duration`, `actual_duration`, `weather_override_applied` (boolean), `season_phase`.
*   **Question Answered:** "Is the core promise (set-and-forget) actually working?"
*   **Interpretation:** In this domain, **sparse** user interaction combined with **high** `automation_cycle_completed` counts indicates success, not abandonment. If users rarely adjust schedules but this event fires consistently, the product is delivering value by removing labor. A drop in this metric during peak season signals a critical failure, even if "adjustment" events rise.

**2. `manual_override_triggered`**
*   **Definition:** Fired when a user manually starts, stops, or modifies a running/pending schedule via the app or physical interface.
*   **Properties:** `override_type` (start/stop/duration_change), `time_since_last_auto_cycle`, `reason_code` (if selectable, e.g., "rain", "maintenance"), `device_connectivity_status`.
*   **Question Answered:** "Why is the automation failing to satisfy the farmer's immediate need?"
*   **Interpretation:** This is your primary negative indicator. A high ratio of `manual_override_triggered` to `automation_cycle_completed` suggests the algorithm is misaligned with field conditions. Segment by `season_phase`: frequent overrides during dormancy may be noise; frequent overrides during peak growth indicate a broken core loop. Investigate specific `override_type` clusters to fix the logic.

**3. `system_health_alert_acknowledged`**
*   **Definition:** Fired when a user views and dismisses a critical system alert (e.g., "Low Battery," "Valve Stuck," "Connectivity Lost").
*   **Properties:** `alert_severity`, `alert_category`, `time_to_acknowledge`, `resolution_action_taken`.
*   **Question Answered:** "Are users aware of and responding to failures that stop value delivery?"
*   **Interpretation:** Since farmers may not check the app daily, this measures **responsiveness to critical failure** rather than engagement. If `automation_cycle_completed` drops and `system_health_alert_acknowledged` does not rise, users are unaware the system is broken (churn risk). If alerts are ignored for >48 hours during peak season, the notification channel (push/SMS) is failing.

### Reading Sparse Usage & Seasonality
Do not compare your retention or frequency to generic SaaS benchmarks; they are `UNVERIFIED` and irrelevant to hardware-agriculture.
*   **The Baseline:** Your only valid benchmark is your own pre-season baseline. Establish the "normal" ratio of `automation_cycle_completed` to `manual_override_triggered` during the beta's first month.
*   **Seasonal Adjustment:** Expect `manual_override_triggered` to spike during planting/harvest and drop during dormancy. Value is proven if `automation_cycle_completed` remains stable or grows relative to the crop cycle, regardless of login frequency.
*   **The Verdict:** If `automation_cycle_completed` is high and `manual_override_triggered` is low, you have product-market fit, even if daily active users (DAU) are near zero. Investors must see that "silence" equals "satisfaction."
