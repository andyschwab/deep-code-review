**Behavioral Signal Interpretation for Clinical Advisory Board**

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Users completing a "Resolved Session" (defined below), not raw Daily Active Users (DAU) or total time spent. The rise from 800 to 950 DAU is a vanity metric until paired with resolution outcomes; it confirms reach, not value.

**Differentiating Engagement from Distress**
Do not weigh login frequency against session length in isolation. Instead, map these dimensions to a **Resolution State**. A "Resolved Session" must be instrumented as a specific event where the user explicitly indicates closure (e.g., selects "I feel better," ends with a positive sentiment score, or returns to the home screen after a cooling-down exercise).

*   **Healthy Engagement Pattern:** High frequency of logins paired with **moderate, stable session durations** that consistently terminate in a "Resolved Session" event. The user returns because the tool works, not because they are stuck.
*   **Potential Harm/Distress Pattern:** Increasing session durations **without** a corresponding "Resolved Session" event. If the average session length grows while the rate of explicit resolution drops, users are likely looping in distress or the bot is failing to de-escalate. This is the primary signal of harm.

**Data Patterns Indicating the Module is Harmful**
The clinical board should look for these three specific divergences in the data post-February 20th:

1.  **The "Looping" Cohort:** A segment of users whose session duration exceeds the 90th percentile but shows **zero** resolution events over a 7-day window. If the new empathy module encourages endless venting without guiding users to closure, this cohort will expand. This indicates the bot is reinforcing rumination rather than resolving crisis.
2.  **Retention of the Distressed:** If retention rates rise specifically among users who *never* trigger a resolution event, the product is successfully addicting users to their own distress. Healthy retention requires users to progress from "crisis" to "resolved" states; retaining users solely in the "crisis" state is a failure mode.
3.  **Frequency vs. Duration Inversion:** If login frequency increases while the *percentage* of sessions ending in resolution decreases, the module is driving dependency without efficacy. Users are returning more often because their underlying issues remain unaddressed.

**Immediate Interpretation Guidelines**
*   **Ignore raw "Time Spent" increases.** Without a resolution tag, longer time is an ambiguous signal that could mean deep therapeutic work or unresolved crisis.
*   **Prioritize the "Unresolved Long-Session" rate.** This is the leading indicator of safety risk. If this rate spikes after Feb 20, the module requires immediate rollback or adjustment, regardless of DAU growth.
*   **Contextualize the 150-user DAU lift.** If these new users fall primarily into the "Unresolved Long-Session" category, the growth is a liability, not an asset.
