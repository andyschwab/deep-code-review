### Proposed North Star Metric: "Heritage Connections"

**Definition:** A user completes a **Heritage Connection** when they perform one high-intent action within 48 hours of opening an article:
1.  **Physical Visit Proxy:** Scanning a QR code at the location OR enabling geofenced check-in within 50 meters of the archive site.
2.  **Active Preservation:** Adding the location to "Favorites" OR sharing the story with a specific contact/group.

**Why this replaces "Opened Articles":**
"Opened Articles" is a vanity metric; it rises even when users bounce in five seconds, creating a false narrative of growth for your grant committee. "Time on Page" is equally flawed for your mission, as passive reading does not prove a connection to local heritage. The **Heritage Connections** metric only increments when a user takes an active step to preserve memory or physically engage with the city, directly aligning with your mission statement.

**Input Metrics (The Drivers):**
To move the North Star, track these specific inputs rather than generic traffic:
*   **Activation Rate:** % of downloaders who complete one Heritage Connection in their first 7 days.
*   **Retention of Action:** % of users who complete a second Heritage Connection in Week 2.
*   **Museum Partner Yield:** Connections generated per digitized asset (to prove value to your three museum partners).

**Instrumentation Plan (Answering Named Questions):**
Do not instrument "everything." Implement only these events to answer specific grant questions:
*   `event: connection_made` (Properties: `type` [visit/favorite/share], `asset_id`, `time_since_open`) → *Answers: Are we driving real engagement?*
*   `event: location_proximity_verified` (Properties: `asset_id`, `distance_meters`) → *Answers: Are users visiting physical sites? (Requires explicit user consent for location data).*
*   `event: article_abandoned` (Properties: `duration_seconds`, `scroll_depth`) → *Answers: Is content failing to resonate early?*

**Grant Committee Narrative:**
Present the data as a shift from "Consumption" (downloads/views) to "Connection" (actions). If you have 2,500 downloads but only 150 Heritage Connections, the story is not failure; it is a clear baseline showing that 6% of users are deeply engaged. The funding request is then framed around moving that 6% to 15% by improving the activation path, rather than blindly chasing more downloads.

**Note on Data Gaps:**
You currently lack verification for physical visits. Until geofencing or QR scanning is implemented, any claim about offline visits is `UNVERIFIED`. Do not estimate this number for the committee; state it as the primary objective for the next fiscal cycle's technical roadmap.

2.  **Baseline Audit:** Query your current database for "Favorites" and "Shares" over the last 30 days to establish the immediate baseline for the new metric before new instrumentation lands.
3.  **Privacy Check:** Route the proposed geofencing logic to your compliance review to ensure user consent flows meet GDPR standards before promising "Physical Visit" data to the committee.
