### GreenBite Series A Funnel Definition & Measurement Plan

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Subscribers completing a successful delivery (value realized), not cumulative sign-ups.

**Defined Funnel Structure (Bottom-Up Analysis Order)**
To satisfy investor scrutiny and isolate the trial bottleneck, define your funnel starting from value realization backward to acquisition. Do not start at "Landing Page" for conversion analysis; that is marketing top-of-funnel. For product conversion, the funnel begins at **Quiz Completion** (committed intent).

1.  **Activation (The "Aha" Moment):** Quiz Completed → First Order Confirmed (Cut Type + Frequency Selected).
    *   *Question:* Do users who finish the quiz actually place an order?
    *   *Instrumentation:* Event `quiz_completed` (properties: `dietary_pref`, `time_spent`); Event `first_order_confirmed` (properties: `cut_type`, `frequency`, `trial_accepted`).
2.  **Retention (The Core Value):** First Order Confirmed → Second Recurring Charge Successful.
    *   *Question:* Does the trial convert to recurring revenue? This is your primary bottleneck hypothesis.
    *   *Instrumentation:* Event `subscription_converted` (properties: `trial_length_days`, `churn_reason_if_cancelled`).
3.  **Acquisition:** Landing Page Visit → Quiz Completed.
    *   *Question:* Is the traffic qualified?
    *   *Instrumentation:* Event `landing_page_view`; Event `quiz_started`.

**Critical Conversion Points to Track**
Present these three specific rates to investors. They map directly to your business model's unique steps:
*   **Quiz-to-Order Rate:** (% of `quiz_completed` that trigger `first_order_confirmed`). If low, the cut selection or frequency UI is the friction, not the trial length.
*   **Trial-to-Recurring Rate:** (% of `first_order_confirmed` that trigger `subscription_converted`). This validates or refutes your trial-length hypothesis.
*   **Frequency Selection Mix:** Distribution of `frequency` properties on `first_order_confirmed`. Correlate specific frequencies (e.g., weekly vs. bi-weekly) with higher retention.

**Investigation Tripwires (Drop-off Thresholds)**
Do not use industry benchmarks. Use your own baseline variance. Trigger an immediate onboarding investigation if:
1.  **Quiz-to-Order Drop:** The rate falls >15% below your 30-day rolling average for any specific `cut_type`. This indicates a supply or UI issue with that specific meat cut.
2.  **Trial Conversion Cliff:** Retention drops >20% between Day X and Day X+1 where X equals your current trial end date. If the drop happens *before* the trial ends, the product isn't delivering value; if it happens *at* the end, the price/trial length is the barrier.
3.  **Frequency Abandonment:** >40% of users who select a frequency on the quiz fail to confirm the order. This signals a "sticker shock" moment when the final price is revealed.

**This Week's Steps**
1.  **Instrument:** Implement `quiz_completed` and `first_order_confirmed` events with `cut_type` and `frequency` properties immediately. Without these, your trial hypothesis is `UNVERIFIED`.
2.  **Query:** Run a cohort analysis comparing `subscription_converted` rates for users on 7-day vs. 14-day trials (if variable) or segment by `cut_type` to see if specific products drive higher retention.
3.  **Decide:** If the Quiz-to-Order rate is stable but Trial-to-Recurring is low, proceed with your trial-length experiment. If Quiz-to-Order is low, fix the cut/frequency selection flow before touching trial length.

*Note: All metrics above must be calculated from your internal analytics data; no external benchmarks are applied. Ensure user identifiers in these events are pseudonymous to comply with privacy standards.*
