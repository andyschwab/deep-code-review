### Recommended Behavioral Signals & Validation Plan for SautiCare

To define activation accurately with limited engineering bandwidth, track these three specific events. Each answers a critical question about user value without requiring complex infrastructure.

**1. Event: `sms_checker_completed`**
*   **Properties:** `user_id`, `timestamp`, `symptom_category`.
*   **Question Answered:** Does engaging with the low-friction SMS path predict eventual conversion to paid services?
*   **Action Rule:** If users triggering this event convert to paid consults within 14 days at a rate >5%, the "video-first" activation definition is too narrow. If conversion is near zero, the SMS path is a dead end or needs a stronger nudge to video.

**2. Event: `prescription_viewed_or_saved`**
*   **Properties:** `user_id`, `timestamp`, `delivery_method` (downloaded vs. viewed in-app).
*   **Question Answered:** Is the download step itself a friction point, or is the lack of value earlier in the funnel?
*   **Action Rule:** If 80% of video consult users view the prescription but only 18% download, the barrier is technical (download friction). If few view it, the barrier is the consultation quality or relevance. This distinguishes a product failure from a delivery failure.

**3. Event: `return_visit_booked`**
*   **Properties:** `user_id`, `timestamp`, `days_since_first_action`, `channel` (SMS vs. Video).
*   **Question Answered:** What is the actual time-to-value for retained users?
*   **Action Rule:** Track the median days between signup and this event for all retained users. This data directly validates your 48-hour window hypothesis.

**Validating the 48-Hour Window**
Do not guess; compute the **Median Time-to-Activation** for your retained cohort.
1.  Filter for users who have returned for a second paid service (your true retention signal).
2.  Calculate the time elapsed between their `signup` and their first `video_consultation_completed`.
3.  **Decision Logic:**
    *   If the median time is **< 48 hours**: Your window is correct; the problem is conversion friction, not timing.
    *   If the median time is **> 48 hours** (e.g., 72 hours): Your current metric is penalizing valid users. Extend the activation window to the 75th percentile of this distribution (e.g., 5 days) and re-calculate your activation rate.
