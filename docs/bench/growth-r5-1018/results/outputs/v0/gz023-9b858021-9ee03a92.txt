### Activation Definition and Measurement Plan for KeyHold

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Leases Initiated (signed lease intents or verified move-in agreements).
**Rationale:** Profile creation and message counts are vanity metrics in a two-sided marketplace if they do not lead to housing secured. Revenue depends on successful matches, not conversation volume.

**Revised Activation Definition:**
A tenant is "Activated" only when they **send a first message containing a specific viewing request or lease intent that receives a landlord response within 48 hours.**

*Why this change?* Your current definition (sending *any* message) captures noise—students testing the interface or asking generic questions that landlords ignore. In the London student market, hesitation stems from trust and pricing transparency. A message that triggers a response proves the listing was credible, the price was clear enough to engage, and the interface worked. If 85 users sent messages but few got replies, your bottleneck is not student hesitation; it is landlord responsiveness or listing quality.

**Input Metrics to Move the North Star:**
1.  **Listing Quality Score:** % of profiles with verified photos, explicit pricing, and lease terms (addresses pricing transparency).
2.  **Response Rate:** % of first messages from tenants that receive a landlord reply within 48 hours (addresses trust/engagement).
3.  **View-to-Message Conversion:** % of profile views that result in a qualified viewing request (addresses interface confusion).

**Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only These):**
*   `viewing_request_sent`: Triggered when a message includes keywords like "view," "visit," or "lease." *Question: Are students moving past browsing?*
*   `landlord_response_time`: Timestamp delta between request and first reply. *Question: Is landlord latency killing trust?*
*   `lease_intent_declared`: Explicit flag when a user indicates readiness to sign. *Question: Does this correlate better with revenue than generic messages?*

**Analysis of Current Data:**
With 1,200 profiles and only 85 messages (7% conversion), followed by unknown reply rates, you cannot yet claim product-market fit based on messaging volume. If landlords are not replying to those 85 messages, the "activation" never truly completed. Optimizing for messaging volume now would incentivize spam, worsening landlord trust. You must optimize for **successful handshakes** (message + reply).

**Recommendation for April 20 Investor Review:**
Do not report "85 activated users." Instead, report: "1,200 profiles created; 85 initial contacts made; [X] resulted in landlord responses; [Y] led to viewings."
*   If [X] is low (<40): The barrier is **landlord trust/listing quality**. Action: Implement "Verified Landlord" badges and mandatory pricing fields before launch.
*   If [X] is high but [Y] is low: The barrier is **interface/viewing logistics**. Action: Simplify the scheduling flow.
