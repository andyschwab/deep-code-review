**3. How to Interpret the Data (The Reading Rules)**
Compare Instant vs. Traditional cohorts using your existing baseline. Do not look for industry benchmarks.

*   **Hypothesis A: Distrust of Automation.**
    *   *Signal:* High `listing_viewed` but low `photo_gallery_interaction` and low `seller_inquiry_sent`. Buyers see the "Instant" badge and bounce without inspecting details.
    *   *Action:* Pivot UI to emphasize human oversight or add a "verified by expert" secondary check for high-value cars.
*   **Hypothesis B: Insufficient Photo Quality.**
    *   *Signal:* High `photo_gallery_interaction` (high zooms, long time) but low `seller_inquiry_sent`. Buyers are looking closely but can't find the detail they need.
    *   *Action:* Tighten upload requirements (e.g., force flash, specific angles) before allowing submission.
*   **Hypothesis C: Selection Bias (The "Lemon" Problem).**
    *   *Signal:* Instant listings have significantly lower average price or older vehicle age than traditional ones.
    *   *Action:* The feature attracts lower-quality inventory. Adjust marketing to target premium sellers or re-weight the algorithm.
