### ScootBarcelona Pilot Metric Framework (May 1 Launch)

**1. The North Star: Safe Value Delivered**
Reject "rides per scooter per day" as your sole North Star; it is a vanity metric that rises even when the business fails (via damage costs and city bans).
**Adopt: "Completed Safe Rides per Active Scooter."**
A ride counts only if: (a) the trip finishes in a designated zone, (b) no damage is reported within 24 hours, and (c) no community complaint is linked to that specific vehicle ID. This ensures utilization only counts when it is sustainable.

**2. The Balanced Scorecard (Input Metrics)**
Measure these three inputs to move the North Star. Do not instrument anything else yet.

| Metric | Question It Answers | Reading Rule & Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Geofencing Compliance Rate** (% of rides ending in valid zones) | Are users parking correctly without human intervention? | **Baseline:** Your Gracia beta rate. **Action:** If <95%, pause expansion in that zone and tighten app UI prompts before adding more scooters. |
| **Incident Density** (Reports per 100 rides, split by "Damage" vs. "Obstruction") | Is growth causing physical harm or community friction? | **Baseline:** Gracia's 20% damage rate. **Action:** If "Obstruction" spikes in a new zone, trigger an immediate operational sweep; if "Damage" spikes, ground that batch for inspection. |
| **Fleet Availability** (% of scooters rentable vs. damaged/maintenance) | Is high utilization eating our margin via repair costs? | **Baseline:** Current operational % in Gracia. **Action:** If availability drops below 85%, stop adding demand; the fleet is being overused. |

*Note on Trip Duration:* Do not prioritize average trip duration yet. It is an ambiguous signal (long trips could mean happy tourists or broken batteries preventing return). Only analyze this if "Fleet Availability" drops without a corresponding rise in "Incident Density."

**3. How to Read the Pilot Data**
Read bottom-up from **Community Impact** to **Utilization**.
*   **Step 1 (Retention/Safety):** Check *Incident Density* first. If complaints or damage rates exceed the Gracia baseline (20%), your acquisition is buying liability, not growth. High ride volume with rising incidents is a failure, not success.
*   **Step 2 (Activation):** Check *Geofencing Compliance*. If users cannot find valid spots, they will block sidewalks regardless of intent.
*   **Step 3 (Acquisition/Volume):** Only after Steps 1 and 2 are stable, look at *Rides per Scooter*.

**4. Experiment Rigor for the Rollout**
Do not peek at daily data to declare victory. Pre-commit to a **2-week evaluation window** for each new district.
*   **Primary Metric:** Safe Value Delivered (North Star).
*   **Guardrail Metric:** Incident Density must not exceed 15% (an improvement on Gracia, not an invented market standard).
*   **Decision Rule:** If the Guardrail is breached at any point, halt expansion in that district immediately. If the Primary Metric rises while the Guardrail holds after 14 days, approve the next zone.

**5. This-Week Steps**
1.  **Instrumentation:** Tag every ride event with `end_zone_valid` (boolean) and link `vehicle_id` to the maintenance ticketing system to auto-calculate "Safe Rides."
2.  **Baseline Setup:** Calculate the exact "Incident Density" and "Geofencing Compliance" from the Gracia beta data to serve as your only valid benchmark.
3.  **Dashboard Build:** Create a single view showing "Safe Rides" vs. "Total Rides" per district; hide raw ride counts from the City Council deck to prevent vanity interpretation.

*Note: All thresholds above are derived from your stated Gracia baseline; no external market benchmarks were invented. Present the "Safe Rides" definition clearly to the City Council to demonstrate proactive governance.*
