### Dashboard Specification: Proposal Customization Coach

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Reps completing a **Customized Proposal Save** (value delivery), not total clicks or pageviews.

**Event Taxonomy (Instrument only these four events):**
Every event must include `rep_id`, `deal_id`, `template_version`, and `timestamp`.

1.  `pricing_editor_opened`
    *   **Question:** Are reps attempting customization?
    *   **Action:** If open rate is low, coach on *when* to customize.
2.  `pricing_line_item_modified`
    *   **Question:** Are reps engaging with the core value lever?
    *   **Action:** If opened but zero modifications, the UI may be confusing or the rep lacks pricing authority.
3.  `pricing_editor_abandoned` (Triggered if >3 minutes idle after open/modify without save)
    *   **Question:** Where is the friction causing drop-off?
    *   **Action:** Identify specific deals stuck in draft; intervene immediately.
4.  `pricing_editor_saved`
    *   **Question:** Did the rep complete the high-value action?
    *   **Action:** Correlate with deal velocity and value.

**Dashboard Views for Regional Directors:**
*   **Funnel Leakage:** Count of `opened` → `modified` → `saved`. Highlight the largest drop-off step.
*   **Stuck Deals List:** Real-time list of `deal_id`s where `pricing_editor_abandoned` fired in the last 48 hours.
*   **Adoption Rate:** % of active reps with ≥1 `pricing_editor_saved` this week.

**Reading Rules:**
*   **Retention First:** Do not celebrate high `opened` counts if `saved` is low. Volume without completion is vanity.
*   **Baseline Comparison:** Compare this week's `saved` rate to the pre-launch baseline. Do not assert that "20% faster" is achieved until the data shows a trend over at least 10 closed deals per cohort.
*   **Unverified Claims:** The assumption that customization drives 20% faster closes is currently `UNVERIFIED`. The dashboard tracks behavior; CRM data must be joined later to confirm velocity.

**This Week's Steps:**
1.  **Instrument:** Implement the four events above with pseudonymous `rep_id`s. Do not track individual keystrokes or mouse movements; they create noise without answering a named coaching question.
2.  **Query:** Run a weekly cohort query: "Of reps who `pricing_editor_opened`, what % `pricing_editor_saved`?"
3.  **Intervene:** If `pricing_editor_abandoned` > 15% of opens, schedule a 15-minute review with reps having >2 abandoned deals to identify UI blockers or training gaps.

*Note: Ensure `rep_id` generation complies with privacy standards (pseudonymous); route PII handling to your compliance protocol. Do not set arbitrary "good" thresholds; use your own week-over-week trend to judge progress.*
