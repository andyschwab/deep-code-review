### Three Data Points to Validate the "Real-Time Verification" Assumption

To test whether brewery managers value real-time verification over long-term impact, track these three specific events during your beta sign-up phase. Each metric directly answers a question about user intent and differentiates your industrial algae solution from generic offset providers.

**1. The "Live Data" Click-Through Rate**
*   **Event to Instrument:** `verification_preview_clicked`
*   **Definition:** Count instances where a user clicks a "View Live Sequestration Data" or "Real-Time Verification" link/button on the pricing or sign-up page *before* completing registration.
*   **Question Answered:** Does the promise of immediate, granular data drive initial interest more than static impact claims?
*   **Action Rule:** If this click-through rate is >20% of page visitors, your assumption holds: real-time visibility is a primary hook. If <5%, managers may care more about the final certificate or cost, and you should de-prioritize complex real-time backend streams in favor of robust reporting features.

**2. The "Impact Horizon" Selection Ratio**
*   **Event to Instrument:** `report_frequency_selected`
*   **Definition:** During sign-up, offer a choice: "Get Monthly Impact Certificates" (Long-term) vs. "Get Weekly Sequestration Logs" (Short-term/Real-time). Track which option is selected by default or chosen manually.
*   **Question Answered:** When forced to choose, do users prioritize frequent, operational data or consolidated, long-term proof?
*   **Action Rule:** A majority selection of "Weekly Logs" validates the need for high-frequency data pipelines. A majority of "Monthly Certificates" suggests your real-time hypothesis is a "nice-to-have" rather than a conversion driver, allowing the technical co-founder to simplify the ingestion architecture.

**3. The "Specific Brewery" Drop-off Point**
*   **Event to Instrument:** `onboarding_step_abandoned` (with property `step_name`)
*   **Definition:** Track exactly where users drop off when asked to input their brewery's specific production data (e.g., "Enter monthly CO2 output") versus when asked for billing details.
*   **Question Answered:** Is the friction of providing data for *real-time verification* (which requires accurate input) causing abandonment, or is the value proposition strong enough to overcome it?
*   **Action Rule:** If drop-off spikes at the data-input step, the demand for "real-time" may not outweigh the effort required to enable it. This signals a need to automate data ingestion (API integrations with brewery systems) before launch, rather than relying on manual entry for the real-time dashboard.

**Implementation Note for Technical Co-Founder:**
Instrument only these three events with clear properties (e.g., `user_type`, `brewery_size`). Do not build full dashboards yet; build the *collection* layer for these specific actions to validate the core value hypothesis before March.

**Boundary Check:**
These metrics rely entirely on your beta user behavior; no external benchmarks for "good" conversion rates are applied. If data is missing, the metric is `UNVERIFIED` until the beta launches.
