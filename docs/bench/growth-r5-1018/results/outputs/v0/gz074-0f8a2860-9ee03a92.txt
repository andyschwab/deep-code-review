### Experiment Proposal: Priority Pickup Trade-off Analysis

**Objective**
Validate the revenue potential of 'Priority Pickup' while ensuring standard user reliability in high-density zones (Andheri) remains within acceptable limits before the June monsoon season.

**1. Traffic Split & Segmentation**
Do not run a global 50/50 test immediately. Given the risk of network-wide congestion, use a **geo-fenced, stepped rollout**:
*   **Phase 1 (Canary):** Limit to **5% of total traffic** strictly within **Andheri** during peak rush hours (6 PM–9 PM).
    *   *Treatment Group:* 50% of users in this zone see the Priority option; 50% do not (Control).
    *   *Constraint:* Hard cap Priority bookings at **8% of total local volume** (below your 10% theoretical threshold) to test the safety margin.
*   **Phase 2 (Expansion):** If Phase 1 guardrails hold for 7 days, expand to **15% of traffic** across two additional high-density zones.

**2. Metrics Definition**
*   **Primary Metric (Success):** **Incremental Revenue Per Available Hour (RPAH)**. This isolates the fee revenue minus any cancellation costs attributable to the feature.
*   **Guardrail Metrics (Stop Conditions):**
    *   **Standard User ETA Delta:** The percentage increase in wait time for non-Priority users in the test zone vs. control.
        *   *Tripwire:* If Standard ETA increases by **>45 seconds** (statistically significant over a 24h rolling window), halt the test immediately. This is more actionable than a generic "reliability" score.
    *   **Driver Utilization Variance:** Monitor if drivers cluster excessively in Andheri, creating deserts in adjacent zones (e.g., Santacruz).
    *   **Cancellation Rate (Standard):** A spike here indicates supply starvation for regular users.

**3. Experimental Rigor & Duration**
*   **Design:** Fixed-horizon test. Do not peek at results daily to declare a winner.
*   **Duration:** Run for **14 consecutive days** to capture full weekly cycles (weekday rush vs. weekend nights).
*   **Sample Size:** Calculate the minimum detectable effect (MDE) on RPAH based on current baseline revenue. If the current volume in Andheri cannot reach statistical significance in 14 days without breaching guardrails, the risk is too high for a wider rollout; the feature requires algorithmic refinement first.
*   **Pre-commitment:** Define the primary metric and guardrail tripwires in the tracking plan *before* launch. Any "win" observed before day 14 is `UNVERIFIED`.

**4. Risk Mitigation for Director Review**
*   **Driver Complaints:** Implement a "soft opt-in" for drivers to accept Priority requests initially, rather than auto-assigning, to gauge sentiment without forcing behavior. Track driver app-session length as a proxy for frustration.
*   **Monsoon Contingency:** If heavy rain occurs during the test window, pause the experiment. Weather is a massive confounder that will invalidate the ETA delta measurements. Resume only when conditions normalize.
