# Tender Posting Funnel Measurement Plan

To isolate friction points and secure engineering resources, you must replace your single-stage form tracking with a five-step micro-conversion funnel. This structure specifically targets the document upload hypothesis while capturing the unknown mobile/desktop variance.

### 1. Defined Funnel Stages

Track these specific events with the associated properties to enable deep segmentation:

| Stage | Event Name | Trigger Point | Critical Properties |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | : |
| **1. Initiation** | `tender_start_clicked` | Click on "Post New Tender" CTA | `device_type`, `browser`, `user_segment` |
| **2. Draft Created** | `tender_draft_saved` | Successful save of basic info (Step 1 of form) | `time_on_page`, `fields_completed_count` |
| **3. Upload Started** | `document_upload_started` | Click on "Attach Files" or drag-and-drop zone activation | `file_count_attempted`, `device_type` |
| **4. Upload Completed** | `document_upload_success` | All required files validated and uploaded | `total_file_size_mb`, `upload_duration_sec`, `error_count` |
| **5. Submission** | `tender_submitted` | Final confirmation page load | `total_time_to_complete`, `draft_age_days` |

**Essential Global Properties:** Every event must include `device_type` (mobile/desktop/tablet), `browser`, `os`, and `user_id` to allow cross-device analysis and session reconstruction.

### 2. Data Interpretation Strategy

To present clear evidence to your executive steering committee, analyze the data using these three specific lenses:

**A. The Drop-Off Rate Analysis**
Calculate the conversion rate between each consecutive stage (e.g., Stage 3 → Stage 4).
*   **Validation:** If your hypothesis is correct, the drop-off between *Upload Started* and *Upload Completed* will be significantly higher (e.g., >40%) than the drop-off between *Draft Created* and *Upload Started* (typically <15%).
*   **Executive Signal:** A sharp cliff at Stage 4 directly justifies engineering investment in the upload module.

**B. The Device Disparity Check**
Since mobile visibility is currently zero, segment every stage by `device_type`.
*   **Validation:** Compare the Stage 3 → Stage 4 completion rate for Mobile vs. Desktop.
*   **Executive Signal:** If mobile upload completion is <50% of desktop completion, the friction is likely technical (e.g., file picker compatibility, timeout limits on cellular networks) rather than user intent. This distinguishes a "bug" from a "usability issue."

**C. The Error Correlation**
Analyze the `error_count` and `upload_duration_sec` properties for users who triggered *Upload Started* but never triggered *Upload Completed*.
*   **Validation:** High average duration followed by abandonment suggests performance timeouts; high error counts suggest format validation issues.
*   **Executive Signal:** Quantify the loss: "X% of users failed due to timeout, representing Y potential tender value lost."

### 3. Implementation Priority

Given your April 30 deadline, implement tracking immediately in this order:
1.  **Instrument Stage 3 and 4 first.** These validate your primary hypothesis.
2.  **Add device segmentation** to all existing events.
3.  **Backfill historical data** is impossible; focus on clean data collection starting today to build a statistically significant sample before the board review.

By isolating the upload step and splitting by device, you transform a vague feeling of "low conversion" into a specific, actionable metric: "Mobile users abandon the upload step at 3x the rate of desktop users due to timeout errors."

*Ensure your engineering team validates that the `document_upload_success` event only fires after server-side confirmation, not just client-side completion, to avoid false positives.*
