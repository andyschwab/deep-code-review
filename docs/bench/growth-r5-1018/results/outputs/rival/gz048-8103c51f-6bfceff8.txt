# Funnel Leakage Diagnosis Framework for DevTools Inc

To resolve the conflict between Marketing and Sales and isolate the root cause of the dropping close rate and deal size, you must move from aggregate metrics to segmented cohort analysis. The data suggests a shift in lead quality or sales capacity constraints rather than a simple failure of execution. Use the following data slices to pinpoint the leakage.

### 1. Source and Campaign Cohort Analysis
Do not compare "all leads" vs. "all leads." Isolate the new content marketing campaign against historical baselines.
*   **Slice:** Compare Close Rate and Average Deal Size (ADS) for `Source = Content Campaign` vs. `Source = Organic/Direct` vs. `Source = Paid` over the last 60 days.
*   **Hypothesis:** The content campaign is successfully attracting smaller companies (SMBs) who naturally have lower ADS and different buying committees, diluting the overall close rate and ADS. If the close rate *within* the campaign cohort is consistent with historical SMB benchmarks, Marketing is succeeding at targeting, but the definition of "qualified" has shifted.

### 2. Stage-by-Stage Conversion Velocity
Identify exactly where the drop-off occurs in the sales pipeline.
*   **Slice:** Calculate conversion percentages between each stage: `Demo Requested` → `Discovery Completed` → `Proposal Sent` → `Closed Won`.
*   **Hypothesis A (Top of Funnel Leak):** If the drop happens between `Demo Requested` and `Discovery`, the leads may be unqualified (wrong company size, no budget), indicating a targeting mismatch.
*   **Hypothesis B (Mid-Funnel Leak):** If the drop happens between `Discovery` and `Proposal`, the product fit or pricing may be mismatched for this new segment.
*   **Hypothesis C (Capacity Leak):** If `Discovery` completion rates have dropped while demo requests spiked, the sales team may be overwhelmed, leading to slower follow-up times and lost momentum, particularly with larger enterprise deals that require high-touch engagement.

### 3. Company Size and Tier Segmentation
Validate the assumption that deal size is shrinking due to company profile.
*   **Slice:** Segment all opportunities by `Employee Count` or `Estimated Revenue` (e.g., <50, 50-200, 200+). Compare the distribution mix of the last two months vs. the prior six months.
*   **Hypothesis:** The mix has shifted heavily toward <50 employee companies. If the close rate for 200+ employee companies remains at 22% but their volume has dropped by 40%, the issue is a loss of enterprise traction, not a failure to close small deals.

### 4. Response Time and Activity Correlation
Rule out sales capacity issues without assigning blame.
*   **Slice:** Correlate `Time to First Contact` and `Number of Touchpoints` with `Closed Won` vs. `Closed Lost` for the new campaign leads.
*   **Hypothesis:** If leads contacted after 24 hours have a significantly lower close rate, the 35% volume increase may have degraded response times, disproportionately affecting larger, more competitive deals.

### 5. Disposition Code Analysis
Quantify the "why" from existing CRM data before seeking new feedback.
*   **Slice:** Aggregate `Closed Lost` reasons for the new campaign cohort. Look for clusters in "No Budget," "Too Small," "Competitor," or "No Decision."
*   **Hypothesis:** A spike in "No Budget" or "Too Small" confirms a targeting shift. A spike in "No Decision" or "Ghosted" suggests a lack of urgency or champion identification, potentially due to lower-quality inbound interest.

### Recommended Immediate Hypotheses to Test
1.  **The "Wrong Fit" Hypothesis:** The content campaign attracts SMBs who cannot afford the enterprise solution, lowering ADS and close rates naturally.
2.  **The "Dilution" Hypothesis:** Enterprise lead volume remained flat, but the influx of SMB leads diluted the aggregate close rate percentage.
3.  **The "Capacity" Hypothesis:** Sales response time increased due to volume, causing enterprise prospects (who have multiple options) to disengage before discovery.

**Note:** Do not aggregate data across these segments; the average is currently hiding the specific failure point. Reconcile the total number of closed deals in your slices against the CRM total to ensure no data is silently dropped.
