# Measurement Plan: Q4 Revenue Leading Indicators

### 1. Primary Leading Indicator: "Proposal Sent"
Do not use "Demo Scheduled" as your primary leading indicator for Q4 revenue. With a $45k ACV and 90-day cycle, a scheduled demo is a weak signal; it measures interest, not sales readiness. Many demos occur with unqualified prospects who never reach the negotiation stage.

**"Proposal Sent"** is the correct leading indicator because:
*   It confirms the prospect has budget, authority, and a defined need (qualified opportunity).
*   It historically correlates much higher with closed-won deals than demo attendance.
*   It aligns directly with the sales team's effort to advance deals, not just book meetings.

**Definition of a Qualified Opportunity:**
A lead becomes a "Qualified Opportunity" only when a **Proposal is Sent**. This definition aligns incentives: Account Executives (AEs) are measured on advancing leads to the proposal stage, not just filling calendars with demos.

### 2. Measurement Framework & Lag Management
To manage the 90-day lag between sign-up and revenue, implement a **Cohort-Based Funnel Report** rather than a simple monthly total.

**Tracking Logic:**
*   **Cohort Grouping:** Group leads by "Sign-up Month" (e.g., August Cohort, September Cohort).
*   **Observation Window:** Track each cohort for 90 days post-sign-up.
*   **Metrics per Cohort:**
    1.  % Demo Scheduled (<24h vs >24h) – *Process Metric*
    2.  % Proposal Sent – *Leading Indicator*
    3.  % Closed Won – *Lagging Result*

**Why this works:** You do not need to wait 90 days to see if the *current* strategy is working. By comparing the "Proposal Sent" rate of the August cohort (now mature) against the September cohort (in progress), you can predict Q4 outcomes early. If the September cohort's proposal rate lags by Day 30, you adjust outreach immediately rather than waiting for Q4 to end.

### 3. Reporting Cadence & Audience

**The Weekly Pulse (Operational)**
*   **Audience:** Sales Director + 10 AEs.
*   **Frequency:** Every Monday morning.
*   **Content:** Only two numbers per AE:
    1.  New Qualified Opportunities (Proposals Sent) last week.
    2.  Stalled Opportunities (Demoed >5 days ago, no proposal).
*   **Goal:** Tactical adjustment of outreach for specific stuck deals. Keeps AEs focused without data overload.

**The Monthly Strategy Review (Strategic)**
*   **Audience:** Growth Manager + Sales Director.
*   **Frequency:** First week of the month.
*   **Content:** Cohort analysis comparing "Demo-to-Proposal" conversion rates.
    *   *Key Question:* Did the "24-hour demo" hypothesis hold true for the previous month's cohort?
    *   *Action:* If the 24h group isn't showing a higher proposal rate by Day 30, pivot the SDR script or demo timing for the current active leads.

### 4. Implementation Steps
1.  **Tagging:** Ensure your CRM tags the exact timestamp of "Demo Scheduled" and "Proposal Sent."
2.  **Dashboard:** Build a simple view grouping leads by Sign-up Month, displaying the cumulative % reaching "Proposal Sent" at Day 30, Day 60, and Day 90.
3.  **Incentive Alignment:** Communicate to the team that bonus accelerators or performance reviews will weigh "Proposals Sent" heavier than "Demos Completed."

**Note:** Validate the "30% lift" assumption by running a historical correlation check on the 200 existing leads: compare the close rate of those who received a demo within 24h versus those who didn't.
**Note:** Exclude "Demo Scheduled" from the executive revenue forecast; it introduces noise. Use "Proposal Sent" velocity as the sole predictor for Q4 closings.
