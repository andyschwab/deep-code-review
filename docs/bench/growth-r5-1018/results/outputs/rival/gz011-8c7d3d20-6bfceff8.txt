### Daily Tracking Plan for CloudYak Video Streaming Test

**Primary Metric:**
*   **Bid Frequency per Active User:** Total bids divided by unique active users in each group (Control vs. Treatment) within the Gobi region.
*   **Target:** 15% increase in Treatment vs. Control.

**Secondary & Guardrail Metrics:**
*   **Stream Load Success Rate:** Percentage of users who successfully load the video player (critical due to connectivity issues).
*   **Session Duration:** Average time spent on auction pages.
*   **Latency Complaints:** Count of support tickets or error logs related to streaming lag.
*   **Conversion Rate:** Percentage of viewers who place at least one bid.

**Daily Reporting Structure:**
Create a simple spreadsheet with the following columns for each day starting October 3rd:
1.  Date
2.  Group (Control/Treatment)
3.  Unique Active Users (Gobi only)
4.  Total Bids
5.  Bid Frequency (Bids/Users)
6.  Stream Load Success Rate (Treatment only)
7.  Notes (e.g., "Network outage in Dundgovi")

### Interpreting Results with Uneven Sample Sizes

Since connectivity issues in remote aimags will likely cause uneven group sizes, do not compare raw total bids. Instead:

1.  **Use Rates, Not Totals:** Always compare *Bid Frequency per User*, not total bid counts. This normalizes the data regardless of group size imbalance.
2.  **Segment by Connectivity Quality:** Tag users based on their connection speed (if available) or region reliability. Analyze if the video feature performs differently in high-connectivity vs. low-connectivity zones. If video fails to load, those users effectively become a "broken treatment" group—exclude them from the primary analysis but report them as a implementation failure rate.
3.  **Apply a 5-Day Burn-In Period:** Given your assumption about post-Naadam stabilization, treat October 3rd–7th as a calibration period. Do not make final decisions on data from these days. Begin formal evaluation on October 8th. If historical data becomes available later, validate this assumption; until then, proceed with caution and note this limitation to the CEO.
4.  **Confidence Over Significance:** With potentially small daily samples in specific aimags, statistical significance may be hard to reach quickly. Focus on the *direction* and *magnitude* of the effect. If the treatment group consistently shows a 10–20% higher bid frequency over 7–10 days (Oct 8–17), that is a strong signal even without a perfect p-value.

### Decision Framework for CEO Batbayar (Due Oct 20)

*   **Allocate to Infrastructure:** If Bid Frequency increases by >10% *but* Stream Load Success Rate is below 85%, or if latency complaints spike. This indicates demand exists but technical barriers are limiting growth.
*   **Allocate to Marketing:** If Bid Frequency increases by >15% *and* Stream Load Success Rate is above 90%. The feature works; now scale awareness.
*   **Pause/Reevaluate:** If Bid Frequency shows <5% improvement or negative trends after October 15th. Reinvestigate the hypothesis or user experience before committing Q4 budget.

**Note:** Ensure your tracking script excludes non-Gobi users entirely from this specific dataset to avoid diluting the regional effect. Manually verify group assignment daily for the first week to catch any routing errors caused by connectivity drops.
