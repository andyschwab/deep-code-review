### GreenBite Referral Funnel Definition & Tracking Plan

**Recommended Funnel Start Point:**
Start the core conversion funnel **after the Dietary Preference Quiz**.
*Reasoning:* The quiz is a high-effort micro-commitment that validates intent and collects essential data for fulfillment. Including "Landing Page Visit" in your *core* conversion rate dilutes the metric with cold traffic noise, making it harder to isolate whether the bottleneck is acquisition (marketing) or conversion (product/trial). Track "Landing Page → Quiz Start" as a separate *Acquisition Efficiency* metric, but define your *Referral Conversion Funnel* starting from qualified leads who have expressed specific dietary intent.

**Defined Funnel Stages:**

1.  **Qualified Lead (Stage 0):** User completes Dietary Preference Quiz.
    *   *Context:* Captures intent + basic data.
2.  **Trial Activation (Stage 1):** User selects Cut Types & Delivery Frequency + Enters Payment Info.
    *   *Context:* This is the critical "configuration" step unique to your model. Friction here often masks as "trial length" issues.
3.  **Trial Started (Stage 2):** User confirms order and $0.00 (or discounted) trial charge processes.
    *   *Context:* The official start of the trial period.
4.  **Conversion to Paid (Stage 3):** User converts to recurring monthly plan post-trial.
    *   *Context:* The primary business goal.

**Critical Conversion Points to Track:**

| Metric | Calculation | Benchmark Alert |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Quiz-to-Config Rate** | Stage 1 / Stage 0 | < 60% indicates confusion on value prop or overwhelming options. |
| **Config-to-Trial Rate** | Stage 2 / Stage 1 | < 70% suggests payment friction or "sticker shock" before the trial ends. |
| **Trial-to-Paid Rate** | Stage 3 / Stage 2 | < 25% (Current: 25%) indicates the trial experience or length is failing to prove value. |
| **Time-to-Config** | Avg. time between Stage 0 → 1 | > 5 minutes suggests decision paralysis on cut types/frequency. |

**Current Diagnosis:**
Your overall referral conversion is 25% (300/1,200). If your "Quiz-to-Trial" rate is healthy (>40%), your assumption about trial length is likely correct. However, if users are dropping off before configuring their box (Stage 1), the bottleneck is **choice architecture**, not trial duration. You cannot optimize trial length if users never start the trial.

**Drop-Off Rates Triggering Immediate Investigation:**

1.  **>45% drop-off between Quiz and Cut Selection:** Immediate UX audit required. The jump from "I like plants" to "Choose your brisket cut" is too steep. Simplify options or use defaults.
2.  **>35% drop-off between Frequency Selection and Payment Entry:** Pricing anxiety. Ensure the trial offer ("First box 50% off" vs. "Free") is clearly reiterated at this exact step.
3.  **Trial-to-Paid <20% after 30 days:** If this metric holds steady after segmenting by trial length (e.g., 7-day vs. 14-day cohorts), then trial length is confirmed as the primary lever to test.

**Investor Narrative Strategy:**
Present the funnel as a "Qualified Intent" model. Highlight that you filter for high-intent users early (via the quiz), making your 25% conversion rate a measure of *product-market fit* among qualified leads, not just marketing efficiency. This defends against investor concerns about "wasted" top-of-funnel traffic.

*Note: Ensure your analytics tool tracks "Cut Type" and "Frequency" as event properties on the Trial Started event to segment conversion rates by these variables later.*
*Do not merge "Landing Page" data into this specific funnel report; keep acquisition and activation metrics distinct for clarity.*
