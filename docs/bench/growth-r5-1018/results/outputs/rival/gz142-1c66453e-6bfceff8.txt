# Business Registration Portal: Pilot Measurement Framework

## 1. Define Success Beyond Speed
To justify continued funding, success must be defined as **Efficient Completion**, not just speed. Use this composite metric:

*   **Primary Success Metric:** "First-Touch Completion Rate" (FTCR).
    *   *Definition:* Percentage of users who complete registration without requiring manual intervention from ministry staff or re-submitting documents.
    *   *Target:* >85% (Current baseline estimated at <40% given 60% drop-off).
*   **Secondary Metric:** "Total Effort Time."
    *   *Definition:* Active user time + Wait time for verification.
    *   *Target:* <4 hours total.
*   **Guardrail Metric:** "Data Integrity Error Rate."
    *   *Definition:* Percentage of completed registrations requiring post-hoc correction due to invalid data entry.
    *   *Target:* <2% (Ensures speed doesn't compromise legal validity).

## 2. Diagnostic Metrics (The "Why")
To distinguish between technical errors and user confusion at the document upload stage:

| Metric | Indicates Technical Issue If... | Indicates Confusion If... |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Upload Error Rate** | >5% of attempts fail with system error codes (timeout, format rejection). | <1% system errors, but users abandon after viewing requirements. |
| **Retry Frequency** | Users retry the *exact* same file multiple times immediately. | Users abandon after 1 failed attempt or leave the page entirely. |
| **Support Ticket Ratio** | Tickets cite "broken button," "timeout," or "file too large." | Tickets ask "Which document do I need?" or "Is this scan acceptable?" |
| **Time-on-Step** | Extremely short (<10s) before error/exit. | Extremely long (>5 mins) staring at the upload screen. |

**Action:** Implement event tracking on the upload widget to capture: `file_selected`, `validation_start`, `validation_result (pass/fail/error_code)`, and `step_abandon`.

## 3. Segmentation Strategy
Since you lack clear industry segmentation, derive it from the **Economic Activity Code (EMTAK)** selected during the initial step.

*   **Segment A: Low-Risk Entities** (e.g., Private Limited Companies with single shareholder).
    *   *Hypothesis:* Should have highest speed; drop-off here indicates critical UX failure.
*   **Segment B: Regulated Industries** (e.g., Finance, Healthcare, Transport).
    *   *Hypothesis:* Higher drop-off expected due to complex document requirements.
    *   *Measurement:* Compare FTCR of Segment B vs. Segment A. If the gap is >20%, the complexity is the barrier, not the tool.
*   **Segment C: Foreign vs. Domestic ID.**
    *   *Measurement:* Split by authentication method (e.g., Estonian ID-card vs. Mobile-ID vs. e-Residency card).
    *   *Insight:* If foreign users drop off at 80% while domestic is 20%, the issue is identity provider integration, not the portal UI.

## 4. Data Interpretation for Ministry Review
Structure your quarterly review findings using this decision matrix:

*   **Scenario 1: High Drop-off + High System Errors.**
    *   *Conclusion:* Technical instability.
    *   *Funding Ask:* Infrastructure investment and bug fixes.
*   **Scenario 2: High Drop-off + Low System Errors + High "Which File" Support Tickets.**
    *   *Conclusion:* Content/Clarity failure.
    *   *Funding Ask:* UX writing resources and dynamic document checklists based on company type.
*   **Scenario 3: Low Drop-off + High Data Integrity Errors.**
    *   *Conclusion:* Users are rushing/gaming the system.
    *   *Funding Ask:* Implementation of real-time validation rules before submission.
*   **Scenario 4: High Completion + <4 Hours + Low Error Rate.**
    *   *Conclusion:* Success.
    *   *Funding Ask:* Scale marketing to increase adoption.

**Reporting Cadence:** Weekly dashboards showing FTCR trended against `EMTAK` code groups. Highlight the "Cost of Failure" by estimating the manual labor hours saved per successful digital completion versus the previous 3-day manual process.

*Note: Ensure all tracking complies with GDPR and Estonian data protection standards; do not log personal identity codes in analytics tools.*
*Baseline the "manual process" cost immediately to establish the ROI denominator for the funding review.*
