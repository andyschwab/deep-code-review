### Prioritized Event Tracking Plan for GreenFlow Irrigation

Given your constraint of three events and the need to prove value to investors despite sparse interaction patterns, you must shift your measurement focus from **frequency of interaction** to **success of automation**. In agricultural IoT, "set and forget" is often the ultimate sign of product success, not failure. Investors need to see that the system is reliably managing water without constant human intervention.

Here are the three critical events to instrument, prioritized by their ability to demonstrate value:

#### 1. `automation_cycle_completed`
*   **Definition:** Triggered every time the valve successfully executes a scheduled watering cycle without manual interruption or error.
*   **Properties:** `duration_minutes`, `water_volume_gallons`, `soil_moisture_pre`, `soil_moisture_post`, `weather_override_applied` (boolean).
*   **Why this matters:** This is your primary proxy for value delivery. Unlike SaaS where daily logins equal value, here, silent, successful execution equals value. A high volume of this event with zero user interaction proves the "smart" aspect is working. It directly correlates to water savings and crop health, the core value propositions for your investors.

#### 2. `manual_override_initiated`
*   **Definition:** Triggered when a farmer manually starts, stops, or modifies a scheduled cycle via the app or physical interface.
*   **Properties:** `override_type` (start/stop/skip/extend), `trigger_reason` (if selectable: e.g., "unexpected rain", "system error", "crop stress"), `time_since_last_auto_cycle`.
*   **Why this matters:** This is your critical guardrail metric. You need to distinguish between "happy forgetting" and "abandonment due to distrust."
    *   **Low override rate + High cycle completion:** Validates your hypothesis that infrequent interaction means satisfaction.
    *   **High override rate:** Indicates the automation logic is flawed, causing farmers to lose trust and revert to manual control. This signals product risk to your CTO.

#### 3. `seasonal_profile_configured`
*   **Definition:** Triggered when a user sets or updates a long-term watering strategy (e.g., "Summer Corn Schedule" or "Winter Dormancy").
*   **Properties:** `crop_type`, `profile_name`, `estimated_duration_days`, `is_update` (boolean).
*   **Why this matters:** This captures the "setup" moment which defines the engagement window. Since farming is seasonal, a user configuring a profile indicates active intent and commitment to the platform for the coming months. It allows you to cohort users by season and crop, proving to investors that you have sticky, recurring annual usage patterns even if daily events are silent.

### Interpreting Sparse Usage Patterns

To justify funding, you must reframe the narrative around these metrics:

*   **The "Silent Success" Ratio:** Calculate the ratio of `automation_cycle_completed` to `manual_override_initiated`. A ratio of 50:1 or higher demonstrates that the system is reliable enough to be ignored. Present this to investors as "Autonomy Score." High autonomy reduces support costs and increases customer lifetime value.
*   **Seasonal Cohort Retention:** Do not measure retention by daily active users (DAU). Instead, measure "Profile Active Days." If a user configured a `seasonal_profile_configured` in March and the system is still firing `automation_cycle_completed` events in August without new input, that is 150 days of retained value.
*   **The Absence of Noise:** Explicitly tell investors that a *lack* of `manual_override_initiated` events during critical growth stages is a positive signal. It means the algorithm is correctly interpreting soil and weather data better than the farmer could manually.

**Implementation Note:** Ensure `automation_cycle_completed` fires even if the hub is offline during execution, syncing the data once connectivity is restored to avoid undercounting value.
**Strategic Note:** Frame the "forgetfulness" of users in your pitch deck as "Time Saved per Acre," converting the lack of app opens into a tangible economic benefit for the farmer.
