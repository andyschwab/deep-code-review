# BituinLearn Onboarding Test: Revenue Impact Report Structure

**To:** Mrs. Reyes, Board Member
**From:** Growth Lead
**Date:** November 6
**Subject:** Revenue Sustainability Analysis: Video vs. Text Onboarding (Oct 12 – Nov 5)

## 1. Executive Summary & Recommendation
State the primary finding immediately: While video onboarding increased flow completion by 14%, the report determines if this translates to net-positive revenue after accounting for production costs and potential support strain.
*   **Recommendation:** [Proceed / Pause / Iterate] based on the "Net Revenue per Signup" metric defined below.

## 2. Core Metric: Net Revenue Per Signup (NRPS)
Do not report completion rates as the success metric. The board must evaluate the business based on this calculated field for each variant:
$$NRPS = (\% Enrolled \times Avg. Course Price) - (\text{Support Cost per User} + \text{Video Production Amortization})$$

## 3. Critical Data Points by Funnel Stage
To isolate true revenue impact, the report must present data across three distinct windows, not just the onboarding period.

### A. Acquisition & Onboarding (Oct 12 – Nov 5)
*   **Total Signups:** 3,400 (Split by Variant A: Text, Variant B: Video).
*   **Onboarding Completion Rate:** Confirm the +14% lift.
*   **Cost Basis:** Total production cost of video assets divided by 3,400 signups (amortized cost per user).

### B. Conversion to Paid (The "Leakage" Check)
*   **Paid Module Start Rate:** % of users who completed onboarding *and* started the first paid module within 7 days.
    *   *Hypothesis Risk:* If video users complete onboarding but drop off before payment, the 14% lift is a vanity metric.
*   **Time-to-Purchase:** Average days from signup to payment. If video delays this, cash flow is impacted.

### C. Post-Purchase Sustainability (The "Strain" Check)
*   **Support Ticket Volume:** Tickets per 100 paid users, categorized by "Content Confusion" vs. "Technical Issue."
    *   *Critical Threshold:* If video variant tickets exceed text variant by >20%, calculate the cost of 12 staff members handling the overflow.
*   **Refund/Churn Rate (Day 14):** % of users requesting refunds or cancelling within two weeks of payment. High completion + high refund = misleading success.

## 4. Presenting Uncertainty to the Board
Mrs. Reyes needs to see the range of possible outcomes, not just a single average. Present the data using **Confidence Intervals** rather than point estimates.

*   **Visual Format:** Use a "Range Bar" chart for NRPS.
    *   *Left Edge:* Worst-case scenario (High support costs + Low conversion).
    *   *Right Edge:* Best-case scenario (Low support costs + High conversion).
    *   *Center Mark:* Current observed average.
*   **The "Break-Even" Analysis:** Explicitly state: "For the video strategy to be profitable, the increase in enrollment must exceed X% to cover the additional support hours required."
*   **Cohort Labeling:** Clearly label the current data as "Early Cohort (Days 1–24)." Note that long-term retention (Day 60) is currently **Pending** and excluded from the final ROI calculation to avoid false precision.

## 5. Decision Framework
Conclude with a conditional recommendation:
*   **Green Light:** If NRPS (Video) > NRPS (Text) + 10% margin for error.
*   **Yellow Light:** If completion is higher but paid conversion is flat. *Action:* Run a follow-up test on the pricing page for video graduates.
*   **Red Light:** If support ticket volume threatens SLA compliance for the 12-person team, regardless of revenue lift.

***

*Note: Ensure all revenue figures use the same observation window (e.g., 14 days post-signup) for both variants to prevent skewed comparisons.*
*Do not include raw ticket counts without normalizing them per 100 users, as variant traffic splits may not be perfectly 50/50.*
