# LondonLiving Growth Reset: Targets & Measurement Plan

## Revised Growth Targets (Fiscal Year)

Given the regulatory caps effective June 1st, shift from pure volume growth to **value-density growth**.

| Metric | Previous Goal | Revised Goal | Rationale |
|--------|--------------|--------------|-----------|
| **Total Listings Growth** | +20% QoQ | +5% QoQ | Reflects market contraction under fee caps; maintains market presence without overspending. |
| **Premium Service Adoption** | N/A | **15% of active landlords** by Q3 | Primary offset mechanism; requires aggressive baseline establishment immediately. |
| **Revenue Per Listing (RPL)** | Flat | **+12% YoY** | Compensates for volume slowdown via premium upsells and efficiency. |
| **Landlord Retention Rate** | 85% | **90%** | Critical as acquisition costs rise; focus on keeping existing high-value partners. |

## Leading Indicators to Track Weekly

To validate the pivot early, monitor these leading indicators before revenue lags:

1.  **Premium Inquiry Rate**: % of landlord interactions asking about premium tiers.
    *   *Target*: >8% by end of Month 1.
    *   *Why*: Validates demand before purchase.
2.  **Service Attachment Rate**: % of new/renewed listings including a paid add-on.
    *   *Target*: 10% in Month 1, scaling to 15%.
    *   *Why*: Direct proxy for the new revenue model's viability.
3.  **Landlord Churn Signal**: Increase in support tickets regarding "fees" or "cancellation."
    *   *Target*: <2% increase over baseline.
    *   *Why*: Early warning of regulatory friction causing exit.
4.  **Sales Cycle Length (Premium)**: Days from inquiry to premium contract sign.
    *   *Target*: <14 days.
    *   *Why*: Ensures the new revenue stream converts fast enough to offset volume loss.

## Measurement Framework: Balancing Volume vs. Value

Do not report volume and value in isolation. Use a **Weighted Growth Score** to present a single health metric to the board:

$$ \text{Growth Score} = (0.4 \times \text{Listing Growth \%}) + (0.6 \times \text{Revenue Per Listing Growth \%}) $$

*   **Why this mix**: Heavily weights value (RPL) to align with the strategic pivot, while keeping enough weight on volume (40%) to ensure market share doesn't collapse.
*   **Success Threshold**: A positive Growth Score indicates the pivot is working, even if listing growth is flat.

## Immediate Next Steps (Before Month-End)

1.  **Establish Baseline**: Run a historical query to determine what % of current landlords *would have qualified* for premium services under the new definition. This is your "Day 1" baseline.
2.  **Define "Premium" Events**: Ensure analytics tracks `premium_inquiry_started`, `premium_demo_completed`, and `premium_contract_signed` as distinct conversion events.
3.  **Segment Reporting**: Split all dashboards into "Regulated Standard" vs. "Premium" cohorts immediately. Do not blend these numbers, or you will mask the transition progress.

**Note**: Since premium data is new, treat Month 1 targets as exploratory benchmarks rather than hard failures; adjust Q2 goals based on actual attachment rates observed in Week 2-4.
**Caution**: Avoid dividing full-quarter spend by partial-month premium revenue; always match observation windows when calculating early ROI.
