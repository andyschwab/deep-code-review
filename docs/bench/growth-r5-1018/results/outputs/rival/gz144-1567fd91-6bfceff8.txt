### Recommended Conversion Events & Analysis Plan

**Primary Hypothesis:** The current "team invite" event is a leading indicator of *collaboration*, not necessarily *value realization*. In B2B cybersecurity, purchase intent correlates more strongly with data integration and insight generation than with user count.

**1. Define Candidate "Value" Events**
Replace or supplement "team invite" with these higher-fidelity signals of value:
*   **Data Integration:** `api_key_connected` or `data_source_linked` (Critical: The product is useless without live threat data).
*   **Insight Generation:** `custom_report_generated` or `alert_rule_created` (Signals the user is configuring the tool for their specific environment).
*   **Depth of Usage:** `dashboard_view_count > 5` within the first 3 days (Indicates habitual checking, not just one-off setup).
*   **Admin Action:** `settings_configured` (e.g., adding team domains or SSO setup).

**2. Measurement Framework**
Do not change your tracking definition yet. Instead, run a **retrospective cohort correlation analysis** on the last 6 months of trial data.

*   **Unit of Analysis:** Individual Trial Account.
*   **Variables:**
    *   *Independent Variables (Binary):* Did they invite a teammate? Did they connect an API? Did they generate a report?
    *   *Dependent Variable:* Converted to Paid (Yes/No) within 45 days.
*   **Metric to Calculate:** **Lift Ratio**.
    *   Formula: `(Conversion Rate of users who did Event X) / (Overall Conversion Rate)`
    *   *Example:* If users who connect an API convert at 20%, and your average is 8%, the Lift Ratio is 2.5x.

**3. Execution Steps**
1.  **Extract Data:** Pull a row-level export of all trials from the last 6 months. Columns: `trial_id`, `start_date`, `converted (Y/N)`, `invited_teammate (Y/N)`, `connected_api (Y/N)`, `generated_report (Y/N)`.
2.  **Compute Correlations:** Calculate the conversion rate for each segment.
    *   *Segment A:* Invited teammate ONLY.
    *   *Segment B:* Connected API ONLY.
    *   *Segment C:* Did both.
    *   *Segment D:* Did neither.
3.  **Identify the "Aha" Moment:** The event with the highest Lift Ratio that occurs *before* the typical drop-off point is your new leading indicator.
4.  **Validate Timing:** Ensure the event happens early enough (e.g., within the first 7 days) to be actionable for your sales team to intervene.

**4. Strategic Recommendation for VP of Sales**
*   **If API Connection shows highest correlation:** Shift the Customer Success playbook. The goal of Day 1 is no longer "invite a colleague," but "ingest first data stream." Sales should prioritize outreach to trials that have *not* connected an API by Day 3.
*   **If Report Generation shows highest correlation:** Focus on guiding users to build their first custom view. The "team invite" may still be valuable, but only *after* the primary user has seen value.
*   **Immediate Action:** Do not wait for new data. Run this analysis on existing historical logs this week. If "API Connection" proves to be a 3x better predictor than "Team Invite," update your internal dashboard definitions immediately to reflect "Active Trial" as "API Connected," not "Team Invited."

**Note:** Ensure your observation window for conversion is consistent (e.g., 45 days from trial start) for all cohorts to avoid skewing results. Exclude any trials still currently active from the "failed" bucket; mark them as "pending" until their window closes.
