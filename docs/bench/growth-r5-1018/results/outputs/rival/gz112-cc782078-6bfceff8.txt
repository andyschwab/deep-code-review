### WriteWise Q1 Success Metrics & Interpretation Guide

**Primary North Star Metric**
*   **Metric:** **Team Activation Rate**
*   **Definition:** Percentage of trials where ≥2 distinct users generate ≥1 document within the first 7 days.
*   **Why:** Validates your hypothesis that collaboration drives retention better than individual volume.
*   **Target:** Increase from current baseline to >40% of new trials.

**Leading Indicators (Behavioral)**
Track these weekly to predict the monthly conversion result:

1.  **Invitation Velocity**
    *   *Measure:* Average number of colleague invites sent per trial account by Day 3.
    *   *Interpretation:* If this rises but conversion stays flat, the invite flow works but the collaborator onboarding fails. If both rise, you have found your growth lever.
2.  **Multi-User Document Density**
    *   *Measure:* % of total documents created by accounts with >1 active user.
    *   *Interpretation:* A shift here confirms whether value is derived from *shared* work vs. solo output. If solo density remains high among churned accounts, individual productivity is not the buying trigger.
3.  **The "Aha" Moment Frequency**
    *   *Measure:* Average time (in hours) from signup to first multi-user edit session.
    *   *Interpretation:* Compressing this timeline correlates directly to higher conversion. If it exceeds 48 hours, intervention (email/support) is required.

**Lagging Indicators (Revenue)**
Report these in your investor update to link behavior to money:

1.  **Trial-to-Paid Conversion by Segment**
    *   *Measure:* Separate conversion rates for "Solo Users" vs. "Collaborating Teams" (defined as ≥2 users).
    *   *Interpretation:* You expect the "Collaborating Teams" rate to be 3–5x higher. If the gap is narrow (<2x), the collaboration feature set is not yet delivering distinct value.
2.  **Expansion Potential Score**
    *   *Measure:* Average seat count requested at upgrade vs. initial active users.
    *   *Interpretation:* Indicates if teams are buying for current usage or anticipated growth. Higher deltas suggest strong product-market fit for scaling teams.

**How to Interpret Shifts Next Quarter**

*   **Scenario A (Validation):** Invitation Velocity increases + Multi-User Density increases + Conversion Rate rises.
    *   *Action:* Double down on collaboration features. Pivot marketing messaging from "Write Faster" to "Write Together."
*   **Scenario B (Friction):** Invitation Velocity increases + Conversion Rate stays flat.
    *   *Action:* The invite mechanism works, but the second user experience is broken. Investigate the collaborator's first 24 hours.
*   **Scenario C (False Positive):** Individual document count rises + Conversion Rate drops.
    *   *Action:* Confirms your suspicion. High solo usage without collaboration is a vanity metric for B2B sales. Deprioritize solo-speed features.

**Implementation Note**
Do not build new dashboards. Export event logs (invites, document creates, user IDs) to a spreadsheet and calculate these ratios manually once per week to save engineering resources.

**Investor Narrative Link**
Frame the narrative as a pivot from "Productivity Tool" (low retention, commodity) to "Collaboration Platform" (high retention, network effects), using the divergence in conversion rates between solo and team accounts as the primary evidence.
