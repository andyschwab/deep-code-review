# Diagnostic Tracking Plan: AutoSwap Instant Verification Feature

To diagnose the 15% engagement gap and inform the Romania expansion decision, you must move beyond aggregate conversion rates and track specific behavioral signals that distinguish between **trust deficits** (buyers doubting the verification) and **information deficits** (photos failing to show necessary details).

### 1. Primary Diagnostic Metrics
Track these three specific metrics, segmented by **Listing Type** (Instant vs. Traditional) and **Market** (Poland vs. Hungary):

*   **Verified Badge Interaction Rate:**
    *   *Definition:* Percentage of users who click or hover over the "Instantly Verified" badge on the listing card or detail page.
    *   *Interpretation:* Low interaction suggests the badge lacks credibility or visibility. If clicks are high but bounce rates remain high, users are investigating the verification but remain unconvinced by the underlying data.
*   **Photo Gallery Engagement Depth:**
    *   *Definition:* Average number of photos viewed per session and percentage of users who expand photos to full-screen mode.
    *   *Interpretation:* If users view fewer photos or skip expansion on instant listings compared to traditional ones, the automated photos likely lack critical angles (e.g., interior, engine, flaws), creating an information gap.
*   **Inquiry-to-View Ratio (Quality-Adjusted):**
    *   *Definition:* Number of seller inquiries divided by unique listing views, specifically filtering for users who viewed at least 3 photos.
    *   *Interpretation:* This isolates intent from browsing. A lower ratio here confirms that even engaged users are not compelled to contact the seller, pointing to a trust issue with the verification status rather than just passive browsing behavior.

### 2. Secondary Contextual Metrics
*   **Time-on-Page by Scroll Depth:** Correlate time spent with how far down the page users scroll. Short time + low scroll = immediate rejection. Short time + high scroll = rapid information scanning (potentially negative).
*   **Return Visitor Rate:** Percentage of users who view an instant listing and return to it within 48 hours. Lower returns on instant listings suggest a lack of "saving for later" intent.

### 3. Data Interpretation Framework for Romania Decision
Use the following matrix to interpret the data and recommend a path forward to the Operations Directors:

| Scenario | Badge Interaction | Photo Depth | Inquiry Ratio | Diagnosis | Recommendation for Romania |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A** | Low (<5%) | Low | Low | **Trust/Visibility Failure:** Buyers ignore or distrust the badge. | **Pause Expansion.** Run A/B tests on badge copy/design and educational tooltips before launching. |
| **B** | High (>15%) | Low | Low | **Content Insufficiency:** Buyers check verification but find photos lacking. | **Pivot Feature Logic.** Require minimum photo angles (e.g., force interior shot) before allowing "Instant" status. Launch with stricter photo rules. |
| **C** | High | High | Low | **Price/Condition Mismatch:** Buyers engage but reject the specific cars or pricing logic. | **Proceed with Caution.** The feature works technically, but inventory quality is the blocker. Expand only if inventory controls improve. |
| **D** | Moderate | Moderate | Equal | **Sample Noise:** No significant behavioral difference; the 15% drop may be statistical anomaly or external factor. | **Expand.** The feature is safe; the engagement dip is likely due to the novelty of faster listings attracting lower-intent browsers. |

### Implementation Note
Ensure all events include a `verification_method` property (`instant` vs. `manual`) to allow precise cohort filtering in your analytics tool. Do not rely on aggregate site-wide averages.

**Decision Rule:** If Scenario A or B is confirmed after 2 weeks of data collection, do not expand to Romania until the specific friction point (trust or photo quality) is resolved via a targeted iteration.
