### Executive Presentation Framework: Checkout Interface Test Results

**1. The Core Trade-Off Summary**
Present the conflict immediately as a tension between efficiency and compliance, not just "good vs. bad" results.
*   **Efficiency Gain:** Average session time reduced by **18 seconds**; sales conversion lifted by **4%**.
*   **Compliance Failure:** Visual contrast ratio dropped from **4.8** (Pass) to **2.9** (Fail). This violates the stated assumption of maintaining minimum visual clarity thresholds.
*   **Population Impact:** Explicitly state that while 4,200 monthly users benefit from speed, the specific subset relying on high-contrast modes or screen readers now faces a blocked or degraded path.

**2. Required Data Points for the CEO (Business Impact)**
Focus on the magnitude of the gain versus the risk of exclusion.
*   **Revenue Projection:** Calculate the annualized revenue impact of the 4% lift based on current heated glove sales volume.
*   **Efficiency Value:** Quantify the 18-second reduction in terms of support cost savings or increased throughput capacity during peak seasons.
*   **Risk Exposure:** Estimate the percentage of your 4,200 users who rely on accessibility features. If unknown, flag this as a critical data gap requiring immediate survey or analytics segmentation.
*   **Legal Precedent:** Briefly note that a contrast ratio of 2.9 fails WCAG 2.1 AA standards (minimum 3.0 for large text, 4.5 for normal), exposing the company to potential compliance claims.

**3. Required Data Points for Legal/Compliance (Risk Assessment)**
Focus on the severity of the failure and the intent.
*   **Deviation from Baseline:** Highlight the drop from 4.8 to 2.9. Emphasize that the previous version was comfortably above the threshold, while the new version is below it.
*   **Assumption Breach:** Document that the test proceeded under the explicit assumption that "visual clarity would remain above minimum threshold," which was proven false by the internal audit.
*   **User Exclusion Evidence:** Present any qualitative data (support tickets, user testing notes) showing actual failure points for low-vision users during the two-week window.
*   **Remediation Timeline:** Provide an estimate of how long a "partial fix" (adjusting colors only) would take compared to a full rollback.

**4. Framing the Decision: Rollback vs. Partial Fix**
Structure the open question around three distinct paths, avoiding a recommendation but clarifying the implications of each.

*   **Option A: Immediate Rollback**
    *   *Narrative:* Prioritizes inclusivity and compliance over unverified gains.
    *   *Trade-off:* Sacrifices the 4% sales lift and 18-second speed gain immediately; restores full access for all users.
    *   *Question for Leadership:* Is the risk of excluding any user segment unacceptable regardless of the revenue gain?

*   **Option B: Partial Fix (Color Adjustment Only)**
    *   *Narrative:* Attempts to retain speed gains while restoring compliance.
    *   *Trade-off:* Requires development time (define exact hours/days); risks altering the layout enough to negate the 18-second speed improvement; requires re-testing.
    *   *Question for Leadership:* Can we tolerate a short period of sub-optimal performance to engineer a compliant solution, or must speed be preserved exactly as tested?

*   **Option C: Segmented Rollout (Conditional)**
    *   *Narrative:* Deploys the fast layout only to users not detected as needing high-contrast modes.
    *   *Trade-off:* High technical complexity; potential for misidentification; creates a "two-tier" user experience.
    *   *Question for Leadership:* Is creating differentiated experiences based on ability aligned with our brand values?

**5. Critical Missing Information to Highlight**
Conclude the framework by listing the data needed to make the final call:
*   Exact number/percentage of the 4,200 users affected by the contrast failure.
*   Whether the 4% sales lift included transactions from users with accessibility needs (did they abandon at higher rates?).
*   The specific legal jurisdiction's enforcement posture on WCAG violations for e-commerce.

*Note: Ensure all percentages are backed by the raw conversion data you possess. Do not speculate on the number of affected users; label it "Unknown" if data is missing.*
*This framework separates business gain from compliance risk to force a deliberate choice rather than an emotional reaction.*
