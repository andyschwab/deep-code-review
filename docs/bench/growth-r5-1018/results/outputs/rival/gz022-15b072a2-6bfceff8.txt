# Diagnostic Plan: NomadStitch Verification Friction

**Objective:** Isolate whether mobile identity verification is the primary driver of the 60% drop-off between profile verification and first bid, specifically targeting Tuesday/Wednesday abandonment.

### Phase 1: Quantitative Deep Dive (Immediate – 24 Hours)
*Leverage existing analytics to segment the drop-off without new code.*

1.  **Device & Browser Segmentation**
    *   Filter the funnel drop-off by `device_category` (mobile vs. desktop) and `operating_system`.
    *   *Hypothesis Check:* If mobile accounts for >75% of the Tuesday/Wednesday drop-off compared to its traffic share, the mobile hypothesis gains strength.
    *   *Action:* Compare completion rates for iOS vs. Android specifically during the 9 AM – 5 PM Uruguay window on Tue/Wed.

2.  **Temporal & Latency Analysis**
    *   Correlate drop-off timestamps with server response times or page load duration for the verification step.
    *   *Hypothesis Check:* If drop-off spikes coincide with higher latency or specific carrier networks common in Uruguay during mid-week work hours, friction may be technical rather than UX-based.
    *   *Action:* Identify if users abandon immediately upon loading the page (UX confusion) or after a specific duration (timeout/frustration).

3.  **Error Event Audit**
    *   Review logs for failed API calls or form validation errors during the verification step.
    *   *Hypothesis Check:* A high rate of `verification_failed` events followed by no retry suggests a hard block; high `page_exit` events before submission suggests abandonment due to perceived effort.

### Phase 2: Qualitative Rapid Discovery (48 Hours)
*Gather the missing "why" directly from the users who left.*

1.  **Targeted Exit Survey (Intercept)**
    *   Implement a one-question modal triggered when a user attempts to leave the verification page on mobile.
    *   *Question:* "What's stopping you from completing verification right now?" (Options: Camera not working, Document unclear, Taking too long, Doing this later, Other).
    *   *Deployment:* Activate immediately for mobile traffic on Tue/Wed.

2.  **Session Replay Sampling**
    *   Review 20–30 recorded sessions of mobile users who dropped off on Tue/Wed.
    *   *Look for:* "Rage clicks" on upload buttons, repeated attempts to scan documents, or switching apps (indicating difficulty locating ID).
    *   *Focus:* Specifically observe the time elapsed between page load and exit.

3.  **Micro-Interviews**
    *   Contact 5 artisans who started but didn't finish verification last Tuesday/Wednesday.
    *   *Script:* "We noticed you started your profile but didn't finish. Was there a specific technical issue or something about the process that felt too long?"

### Phase 3: Synthesis & Recommendation (Day 4)
*Consolidate findings for the sprint planning session.*

*   **Confirm/Refute Hypothesis:** Does data show mobile-specific failure rates > desktop? Do session replays show UI struggle?
*   **Identify Secondary Friction:** If mobile is not the sole culprit, identify if the issue is document type requirements, lighting conditions for photos, or lack of clear instructions.
*   **Sprint Definition:** Define the specific fix (e.g., "Simplify camera interface," "Allow manual upload," "Add progress bar") based on the dominant friction point.

### Additional Data Required
To make a confident decision, we currently lack:
1.  **Specific Error Codes:** We need to know if failures are due to image rejection (quality) or system timeouts.
2.  **User Intent Signals:** We do not know if drop-offs are returning later to complete the task (delayed conversion) or churning permanently.

**Note:** Prioritize the session replays and exit survey; they will yield the highest confidence insights within the 4-day window. Ensure all data collection complies with local privacy regulations regarding user recordings.
