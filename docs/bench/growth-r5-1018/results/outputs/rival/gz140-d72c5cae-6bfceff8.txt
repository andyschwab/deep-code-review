# PageTurners Recommendation Engine Health Measurement Plan

## 1. Core Hypotheses to Test
*   **H1 (Consumption Displacement):** Increased time spent consuming recommendations directly reduces available time for review creation.
*   **H2 (Filter Bubble):** Reduced topic diversity in recommendations lowers user motivation to contribute unique perspectives.
*   **H3 (Adjustment Period):** The review drop is a temporary behavioral shift that will stabilize as users adapt to the new interface.

## 2. Primary Metrics Dashboard
Track these metrics daily, segmented by **User Tenure** (New vs. Returning) and **Activity Level** (Heavy vs. Light):

### Consumption Metrics (The "Input")
*   **Recommendation CTR:** Clicks on recommended items / Impressions (Current: +25%)
*   **Session Duration:** Average time spent per session
*   **Content Depth:** Average number of items consumed per session
*   **Topic Diversity Score:** Entropy calculation of genres/authors consumed per user per week

### Contribution Metrics (The "Output")
*   **Review Rate:** Total reviews posted / Total active users (Current: -10%)
*   **Review Latency:** Time between finishing a book/thread and posting a review
*   **Review Length:** Average word count per review (Quality proxy)
*   **Discussion Breadth:** Number of unique threads receiving new comments

### Health & Balance Ratios
*   **Contribution Ratio:** Reviews Posted / Items Consumed (Critical health indicator)
*   **Diversity Delta:** Change in topic diversity score pre- vs. post-algorithm
*   **Retention Cohort:** Day-7 and Day-30 retention for pilot vs. control group

## 3. Segmentation Strategy
Do not analyze aggregates alone. Slice data by:
*   **User Persona:** "Lurkers" (consume only) vs. "Contributors" (historically high review rate). Did your best contributors stop writing?
*   **Content Type:** Fiction vs. Non-Fiction; Popular vs. Niche titles.
*   **Recommendation Source:** "Because you read X" vs. "Trending in your group."

## 4. Qualitative & Leading Indicators
*   **Sentiment Analysis:** Run NLP on recent reviews and forum posts for keywords like "same," "boring," "surprised," or "diverse."
*   **Micro-Conversions:** Track "Save for Later" or "Add to Reading List" actions. A drop here alongside review drops suggests disengagement, not just displacement.
*   **Exit Surveys:** Trigger a one-question poll for users who stop posting reviews: "What stopped you from sharing your thoughts this week?"

## 5. Experimental Tuning Plan
Before the summer launch, run two targeted A/B tests on the pilot traffic:

*   **Test A (Diversity Injection):** Force 20% of recommendations to be outside the user's standard genre profile.
    *   *Success Metric:* Stabilization of Review Rate without significant CTR drop.
*   **Test B (Contribution Nudges):** Add contextual prompts after consumption ("You just finished [Book]. What did you think?") specifically for high-value contributors.
    *   *Success Metric:* Increase in Review Latency conversion.

## 6. Decision Framework
*   **Green Light (Launch):** CTR remains elevated, Review Rate stabilizes within 5% of baseline, and Diversity Score holds steady.
*   **Yellow Light (Tune):** CTR high, but Review Rate drops >15% or Diversity Score plummets. *Action:* Implement Test A immediately.
*   **Red Light (Pause):** Both CTR and Review Rate decline, or retention drops in the contributor segment. *Action:* Revert to previous logic.

## 7. Implementation Timeline
*   **Week 1:** Instrument "Contribution Ratio" and "Topic Diversity" in existing dashboards.
*   **Week 2-3:** Run qualitative sentiment analysis and user surveys.
*   **Week 4:** Launch Test A (Diversity) on 10% of traffic.
*   **Week 6:** Finalize algorithm weights based on Test A results before summer scale-up.

This plan shifts focus from vanity metrics (CTR) to ecosystem health, ensuring the engine drives engagement without silencing the community voice that defines PageTurners.

*Note: Ensure "Topic Diversity" is calculated using Shannon Entropy on genre tags to avoid subjective bias. Baseline all metrics against the 4 weeks prior to March 1st.*
