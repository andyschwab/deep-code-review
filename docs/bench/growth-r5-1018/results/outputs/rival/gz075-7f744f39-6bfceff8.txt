# Churn Driver Analysis Framework for MusicRow Premium

## 1. Define Cohorts and Observation Windows
To isolate the impact of the April 10th schedule change, you must compare behavior before and after this date while respecting observation windows. Do not mix full-period spend with partial-period results.

*   **Pre-Change Cohort:** Users active Jan 1 – Apr 9.
*   **Post-Change Cohort:** Users active Apr 10 – Present.
*   **Churn Definition:** A subscriber is "churned" only after their billing cycle ends and they do not renew. Users currently in an active billing cycle are "pending," not churned. Exclude them from churn rate calculations until their window closes.

## 2. Segmentation Matrix
Break down exiting users by the following dimensions to identify if the issue is frequency-specific or systemic.

### A. Consumption Volume (The Power User Test)
*   **Light Listeners:** < 3 episodes/month.
*   **Moderate Listeners:** 3–9 episodes/month.
*   **Power Users:** 10+ episodes/month.
*   *Hypothesis:* If churn spiked only among Light/Moderate listeners post-April 10, frequency is the culprit. If Power Users are churning at the same rate, the issue is likely content quality, host changes, or audio issues affecting core value.

### B. Show Affinity
*   **Single-Show Listeners:** 100% of listens directed to one podcast.
*   **Multi-Show Listeners:** Consumes content across ≥2 shows.
*   *Action:* Cross-reference churned accounts with specific show release dates. Did churn spike immediately following a specific host change or a noticeable drop in audio fidelity on a flagship show?

### C. Tenure and Acquisition Source
*   **New vs. Legacy:** Did users acquired in the last 90 days churn faster than those subscribed >1 year?
*   **Source:** Organic search vs. Paid ads vs. Partner referrals.
*   *Insight:* New users may have higher frequency expectations set by marketing. Legacy users may be more tolerant of schedule shifts but sensitive to quality changes.

## 3. Data Collection & Event Requirements
Ensure your analytics team tracks these specific events with the noted properties. If missing, implement immediately via your tag manager or app SDK.

| Event Name | Required Properties | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `subscription_cancelled` | `cancel_reason` (self-reported), `days_since_last_listen`, `total_episodes_30d`, `primary_show_id` | Captures explicit intent and immediate behavior context. |
| `episode_played` | `episode_id`, `show_id`, `audio_quality_metric` (bitrate/errors), `host_name` | Correlates listening drops with specific content attributes. |
| `login_failed` | `error_type`, `device_type` | Rules out technical access issues as a churn driver. |

## 4. Analysis Logic & Decision Rules
Compute churn rates for each segment independently. Do not eyebell totals; derive every rate from row-level data.

*   **Frequency Hypothesis Confirmed:** Post-change churn rate for Light/Moderate listeners increases significantly (>20% relative lift) compared to Pre-Change, while Power User churn remains stable.
    *   *Recommendation:* Revert to weekly schedule or introduce bonus mini-episodes to maintain touchpoints.
*   **Quality/Content Hypothesis Confirmed:** Churn increases uniformly across all volume segments, or spikes correlate with specific `host_name` or `audio_quality_metric` anomalies rather than the April 10 date.
    *   *Recommendation:* Invest in production improvements or host training; do not increase frequency until quality stabilizes.
*   **Mixed Signals:** If Power Users are churning but citing "cost" or "no time," while Light Users cite "not enough content," the problem is segmentation mismatch.
    *   *Recommendation:* Consider tiered pricing or content bundles rather than a blanket schedule change.

## 5. Reporting Output
Deliver a single dashboard to the Content Director by month-end showing:
1.  **Churn Rate by Volume Segment** (Pre vs. Post April 10).
2.  **Churn Rate by Primary Show** (Highlighting any outliers).
3.  **Self-Reported Cancel Reasons** aggregated by volume segment.
4.  **Final Recommendation:** Explicitly state whether to revert the schedule or invest in acquisition/production, citing the specific segment data that drives the conclusion.

*Note: Ensure all UTM parameters on acquisition links use lowercase and hyphens to prevent data fragmentation in your source analysis.*
