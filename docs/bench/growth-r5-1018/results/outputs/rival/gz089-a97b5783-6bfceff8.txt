### Frustration Indicators for International Transfer Flow

To diagnose user frustration without capturing screen content or input data, implement the following touch events and navigation patterns within your transfer wizard. These metrics isolate confusion around exchange rates and fees while adhering to SecureBank's security protocols.

**1. Rage-Click Detection (Info Icon & Fee Fields)**
*   **Event:** `icon_rapid_tap`
*   **Trigger:** 3+ taps on the exchange rate info icon or fee breakdown field within 2 seconds.
*   **Properties:** `element_id`, `screen_name`, `tap_count`, `time_delta_ms`.
*   **Insight:** Indicates users are desperately seeking clarity on costs but finding the existing explanation insufficient.

**2. Navigation Reversals (The "Looping" Pattern)**
*   **Event:** `nav_back_forward_cycle`
*   **Trigger:** User navigates back from the "Review" or "Confirm" screen to the "Amount/Currency" screen, then forward again, repeated 2+ times in a single session.
*   **Properties:** `from_screen`, `to_screen`, `cycle_count`, `total_time_spent_ms`.
*   **Insight:** Suggests users are re-entering data hoping the exchange rate or fee calculation will change, or they are trying to re-read terms they missed.

**3. Abandonment Latency (The "Hesitation" Drop-off)**
*   **Event:** `session_abort_after_view`
*   **Trigger:** App force-close or backgrounding occurring within 5 seconds of viewing the "Final Confirmation" screen (where total fees are displayed).
*   **Properties:** `last_screen_viewed`, `time_on_screen_ms`, `transaction_step`.
*   **Insight:** Differentiates between technical crashes and voluntary abandonment due to "sticker shock" or confusion at the final hurdle.

**4. Dwell Time Anomalies**
*   **Event:** `field_dwell_time_exceeded`
*   **Trigger:** Time spent on the "Exchange Rate Display" or "Fee Breakdown" view exceeds the 75th percentile of successful transactions (e.g., >45 seconds) without interaction.
*   **Properties:** `screen_name`, `dwell_duration_ms`, `subsequent_action` (e.g., `cancel`, `back`, `confirm`).
*   **Insight:** Long pauses without taps often indicate cognitive overload or confusion rather than engagement.

**5. Error-Interaction Clustering**
*   **Event:** `error_adjacent_taps`
*   **Trigger:** Any tap event occurring within 1 second of an inline validation error or fee warning display.
*   **Properties:** `error_type`, `element_tapped`, `time_since_error_ms`.
*   **Insight:** Reveals if users are blindly tapping to dismiss errors or trying to interact with non-interactive warning text.

**Implementation Notes for Development:**
*   Ensure all `element_id` properties use static identifiers, not dynamic coordinates, to maintain anonymity.
*   Aggregate these events daily to identify if the "Rage-Click" rate correlates with the 15% support call increase.

**Note for Customer Support:**
*   Prioritize help article updates on "Understanding Exchange Rate Fees" if `icon_rapid_tap` events exceed 10% of total transfer attempts.
