### Bot Detection & Validation Plan for NeonTides

To isolate the botnet targeting your "Pearl" currency and protect server margins before the September 25th tournament, implement the following behavioral signal analysis and validation protocol immediately.

#### 1. Primary Behavioral Signals to Measure
Since geo-tagging is currently unreliable, focus on **in-game telemetry** rather than network metadata. Bots farming currency exhibit distinct non-human patterns:

*   **Action Interval Variance:** Calculate the standard deviation of time between "Pearl collection" events. Human players have variable reaction times (high variance); scripts often execute actions at fixed millisecond intervals or with unnaturally low variance (<50ms).
*   **Session Duration vs. Activity Density:** Plot "Actions Per Minute" against "Total Session Time." Bots often run 24/7 with consistent high density, whereas humans show fatigue curves, breaks, or session limits. Flag sessions with >4 hours of continuous max-effort farming.
*   **Input Telemetry:** If your client captures it, measure mouse/controller jitter and pathing. Scripts move in perfect straight lines or identical loops between resource nodes. Humans have micro-deviations and inefficient paths.
*   **Failure Rate:** Bots optimizing for speed often have a 0% failure rate in skill-based challenges or ignore environmental hazards that slow humans down. A sudden cluster of accounts with perfect success rates in complex zones is a strong indicator.
*   **Economic Velocity:** Track "Pearls Earned Per Minute." Establish a 99th percentile baseline from pre-September 12th data. Any account consistently exceeding this theoretical human maximum is a candidate for filtering.

#### 2. Isolation Strategy (The "Shadow Ban" Approach)
Do not immediately ban IPs, as you risk false positives and tipping off the attacker. Instead, implement a **logic-based filter**:

1.  **Tagging:** Create a dynamic segment `suspected_bot` based on the signals above (e.g., Action Variance < threshold AND Session Duration > 4 hours).
2.  **Shadow Mode:** Route traffic from this segment to a isolated server instance or apply a "null reward" modifier where Pearls are earned visually but not saved to the ledger or counted toward leaderboards.
3.  **Cost Monitoring:** Observe if server costs decouple from the `suspected_bot` session count. If costs drop while total session count remains high, you have confirmed the noise source without disrupting the visible user experience.

#### 3. Validating Against False Positives (Protecting Legitimate Gamers)
To ensure high-frequency legitimate gamers (e.g., tournament grinders) are not caught in the filter:

*   **The "Whale" Control Group:** Manually review the top 50 legitimate players by historical spend and playtime. Run your detection algorithm against their past 30 days of data. If the flag triggers for known humans, tighten the variance thresholds.
*   **Behavioral Clustering:** Legitimate high-frequency players usually vary their activities (customization, social hub visits, different game modes). Bots often loop a single "farm" map. Require a "diversity score" (number of unique maps/modes visited) for the flag to stick.
*   **A/B Test the Filter:** Before full deployment, apply the filter to only 10% of the suspected traffic. Monitor support tickets and community sentiment for complaints about "missing rewards" from this specific subset.

*   **Safety Margin:** Confirmation that 0% of top-tier paying users match the bot profile.
