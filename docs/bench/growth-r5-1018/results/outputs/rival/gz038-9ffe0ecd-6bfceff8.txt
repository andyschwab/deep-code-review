# LexiScan Success Metrics Framework: The "Trust-Efficiency Balance"

To prove value to skeptical General Counsels, you must shift from measuring "speed alone" to measuring "net time saved with verified accuracy." The following framework combines efficiency, accuracy, and trust indicators into a single dashboard.

## 1. Core Quantitative Metrics

Track these three pillars simultaneously. A win in one pillar cannot justify a loss in another.

### A. Efficiency (The Speed Promise)
*   **Net Time Saved Per Document:** `(Manual Review Time Baseline) − (AI Review Time + Human Override Time)`.
    *   *Why:* This accounts for the time lawyers spend fixing AI errors. If the AI is fast but creates many false positives, this number drops.
*   **Final Approval Cycle Time:** Time from document upload to final partner sign-off.
    *   *Why:* Partners care about the total workflow duration, not just the AI processing slice.

### B. Accuracy & Trust (The Safety Guardrails)
*   **Override Rate (%):** `(Number of AI Suggestions Modified or Rejected) / (Total AI Suggestions)`.
    *   *Benchmark:* Target <15%. A rate >30% indicates the AI is creating work, not saving it.
*   **False Positive Ratio:** `(Incorrect Flags Identified by User) / (Total Flags Raised)`.
    *   *Why:* High false positives destroy trust faster than missed items.
*   **Critical Miss Rate:** `(Issues Found Post-Approval by External Audit or Counterparty) / (Total Documents)`.
    *   *Why:* This is the ultimate "trust killer" metric. It must be near zero.

### C. Adoption & Confidence (The Behavioral Signal)
*   **Acceptance Rate by Clause Type:** Track which clauses lawyers accept vs. reject.
    *   *Insight:* If lawyers accept 90% of "Confidentiality" flags but only 20% of "Indemnification" flags, you know where your model is trustworthy and where it isn't.
*   **Re-work Loop Count:** How many times a document returns to the AI after human editing before final approval.
    *   *Goal:* Should be 0 or 1. Higher numbers indicate the AI output is unusable without heavy lifting.

## 2. The "Trust-Efficiency" Matrix

Plot your beta data on a 2x2 matrix to visualize performance for your September release:
*   **X-Axis:** Net Time Saved (High to Low)
*   **Y-Axis:** Override Rate (Low to High)
*   **Target Quadrant:** High Time Saved / Low Override Rate.
*   **Danger Zone:** Low Time Saved / High Override Rate (The AI is creating extra work).

## 3. Qualitative Feedback Loops

Integrate these directly into the workflow to capture context behind the numbers:

*   **One-Click Rejection Reasoning:** When a lawyer overrides an AI suggestion, require a single-tag reason (e.g., "False Positive," "Context Missed," "Too Aggressive," "Formatting Error"). This turns every override into training data.
*   **"Trust Score" Pulse Survey:** After every 10 documents reviewed, prompt the user: *"On a scale of 1-5, how much did LexiScan reduce your cognitive load today?"*
    *   *Why:* Cognitive load is the true measure of assistance vs. distraction.
*   **Partner "Red Line" Review:** Monthly roundtable with General Counsels to review the top 5 "Critical Misses" or "Major False Positives." Qualitative stories from these sessions explain the quantitative outliers.
*   **Escape Clause Logging:** Create a specific log for instances where a lawyer disables the AI for a specific document type. This signals a complete breakdown of trust for that category.

## Implementation for September 20th Release

1.  **Baseline Immediately:** Calculate the average manual review time and error rate from your beta firms to set baselines.
2.  **Dashboard Build:** Create a real-time view showing "Net Time Saved" vs. "Override Rate" broken down by law firm and clause type.
3.  **Success Criteria:** Define launch success as achieving a **Net Time Saved >40%** while maintaining an **Override Rate <20%** across all five beta firms for two consecutive weeks.

This framework proves you are not just selling speed, but selling *reliable* speed—the only metric that matters to risk-averse legal teams.

*Note: Ensure all "time saved" calculations use the same observation window for both numerator and denominator to avoid skewed data.*
*Recommendation: Prioritize reducing False Positives over increasing speed for the initial GA launch; trust is harder to regain than efficiency is to improve.*
