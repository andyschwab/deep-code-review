**Recommended Primary Metric: Weekly Active Learning Pairs (WALP)**

**Definition:** The count of unique tutor-tutee pairs per week who complete at least one session lasting **45+ minutes** AND where the tutee books a **follow-up session** within 14 days.

**Why this metric overcomes your current challenges:**

1.  **Filters Low-Value Activity:** By imposing a 45-minute minimum, you eliminate "inflated" short interactions that likely lack substantive tutoring. This addresses your observation that session count alone is misleading.
2.  **Captures Retention & Value:** Requiring a follow-up booking proves the tutee perceived enough value to return. One-off sessions often indicate mismatched pairs or unresolved needs; repeat engagement signals successful relationship building.
3.  **Proxy for Outcomes without Grades:** Since you lack transcript access, *continued engagement* is the strongest available proxy for learning progress. Students rarely return for tutoring that doesn't help them.
4.  **Aligns Incentives:** This metric rewards tutors for building lasting relationships and delivering effective help (leading to re-booking), rather than rushing through many short, low-quality sessions.
5.  **Investment Justification:** For university partners, "Active Learning Pairs" demonstrates a thriving ecosystem of sustained academic support, which is more compelling than raw activity volume.

**Implementation Strategy for Your Dashboard:**

*   **Primary Number:** Display WALP prominently as your North Star.
*   **Supporting Context (Small Sub-metrics):**
    *   *Average Session Duration:* Ensure it stays above the 45-min threshold.
    *   *Repeat Booking Rate:* Percentage of first-time sessions that lead to a second.
    *   *Qualitative Signal:* Include a small sample of recent self-reported confidence score deltas (pre/post session) if available, to add color to the quantitative data.

**Why not the alternatives?**
*   *Session Duration alone:* Can be gamed (tutors dragging out sessions) and doesn't prove learning occurred.
*   *Repeat Booking Rate alone:* Lacks scale context (high rate on tiny volume is weak).
*   *Confidence Scores:* Too subjective and prone to bias without grade correlation; better as a secondary qualitative indicator.

By shifting focus from "sessions completed" to "sustained learning relationships," WALP provides a defensible, actionable, and outcome-oriented metric that aligns with your mission despite current data limitations.

*Note: Validate the 45-minute threshold against your specific subject matter norms (e.g., math may require longer than language practice) once you gather more user feedback.*
