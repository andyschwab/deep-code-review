**Recommendation: Redefine Conversion to "Qualified Lead" and Implement Two-Stage Measurement**

For SunLease Australia, defining a "conversion" strictly as a completed application form is currently misaligning your marketing spend with revenue outcomes. Your data indicates a significant degradation in funnel efficiency: while top-of-funnel volume increased by 20% post-redesign, the lead-to-site-visit rate dropped from 25% to 15%. This represents a net loss in actual sales opportunities despite higher traffic.

To align with your revenue goals, you must shift your primary conversion definition from **Form Completion** to **Qualified Lead (QL)**. A Qualified Lead is defined as a submitted form that meets your sales team's criteria for a viable installation site visit.

**Strategic Actions:**

1.  **Adopt a Two-Stage Conversion Model:**
    *   **Micro-Conversion (Form Complete):** Continue tracking this to measure UX friction and landing page usability. Use this metric solely for optimizing page layout and copy clarity, not for budget allocation.
    *   **Macro-Conversion (Qualified Lead):** This is your new north star for advertising. Configure your analytics to fire a primary conversion event only when a lead is marked "Qualified" by your sales team or passes an automated pre-qualification threshold.

2.  **Implement Pre-Qualification:**
    Introduce a lightweight pre-qualification step (e.g., 2-3 additional fields regarding roof ownership, electricity bill amount, or location eligibility) before the final submission. While this will likely decrease your total form completion rate, it will filter out low-intent or ineligible users earlier, raising the percentage of submissions that result in site visits.

3.  **Reallocate Budget Based on Cost Per Qualified Lead (CPQL):**
    Stop optimizing ad campaigns for Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) based on form fills. Instead, calculate CPQL. If Channel A generates $50 form fills but only a 10% qualification rate ($500 CPQL), and Channel B generates $80 form fills with a 30% qualification rate ($266 CPQL), Channel B is the superior investment despite higher upfront costs.

**Measurement Framework for the CRO:**

To report on this for the upcoming fiscal year, restructure your dashboards to show:
*   **Volume:** Total Form Submissions (Monitor for drastic drops).
*   **Quality:** Qualification Rate (% of forms becoming site visits).
*   **Efficiency:** Cost Per Qualified Lead (Total Spend / Total Qualified Leads).
*   **Revenue Impact:** Projected Annual Site Visits based on current CPQL vs. historical CPA.

By optimizing for Qualified Leads, you accept lower total volume in exchange for higher sales team efficiency and a direct correlation between ad spend and revenue-generating activities.

*Note: Ensure your sales team provides rapid feedback loops (within 24 hours) on lead status so your marketing data remains current.*
*Implementation requires updating your CRM-to-Analytics integration to pass "Qualified" status back to your ad platforms for bid optimization.*
