### Tracking Plan: Green Route Beta Validation

**Objective:** Validate if the Carbon Efficiency Score (1–100) increases booking retention by ≥8% among environmentally conscious manufacturers.

#### 1. Critical Events to Capture
To minimize engineering load, implement only these four high-signal events. Do not track generic page views for this feature.

| Event Name | Trigger | Essential Properties |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `green_badge_viewed` | Badge renders in viewport (>50% visible for >1s) | `shipment_id`, `carbon_score` (1-100), `price_difference_vs_standard` |
| `green_badge_hovered` | Cursor hovers over badge or tooltip opens | `shipment_id`, `carbon_score`, `time_on_page` |
| `booking_submitted` | User confirms booking | `shipment_id`, `selected_route_type` (green vs. standard), `user_segment` |
| `booking_cancelled` | User cancels within 24h | `shipment_id`, `cancellation_reason`, `selected_route_type` |

**Implementation Note:** Use your existing analytics tag manager to fire `green_badge_viewed` only once per session per shipment to avoid data inflation. Ensure `carbon_score` is passed as an integer, not a string, to enable range filtering.

#### 2. Primary Metrics for Decision Making
Focus your board readout on these three derived metrics. Do not present raw event counts.

*   **Green Attachment Rate:** % of bookings where `selected_route_type` = "green" among users who triggered `green_badge_viewed`.
    *   *Target:* Must show an uplift compared to a control group (users not shown the badge) or historical baseline.
*   **Retention Delta:** Comparison of 30-day repeat booking frequency between Beta users (who saw the badge) and Non-Beta users (matched by industry/size).
    *   *Target:* ≥8% increase in retention for the Beta cohort.
*   **Price Sensitivity Ratio:** Correlation between `price_difference_vs_standard` and `green_badge_hovered` → `booking_submitted`.
    *   *Insight:* Determines if the badge overrides price concerns or only works when prices are comparable.

#### 3. Statistical Readout Structure
For the board meeting, structure the analysis as follows to prove significance without complex modeling:

1.  **Cohort Definition:**
    *   **Test Group:** 450 beta clients who logged in post-launch.
    *   **Control Group:** 450 randomly selected non-beta clients with similar shipping volumes and industries.
2.  **Success Criteria:**
    *   Calculate the difference in retention rates between Test and Control.
    *   Apply a two-proportion z-test (standard in any spreadsheet tool) to determine if the observed lift is statistically significant (p < 0.05).
    *   *Rule:* If the confidence interval of the lift includes 0%, the result is inconclusive. Do not claim success based on raw percentage differences alone.
3.  **Visualization:**
    *   Present a single bar chart comparing "Retention Rate (Beta)" vs. "Retention Rate (Control)" with error bars showing the 95% confidence interval.
    *   Include a secondary table showing "Green Attachment Rate" broken down by `carbon_score` ranges (e.g., 80–100 vs. 40–79) to prove the score magnitude matters.

**Data Quality Check:** Before the meeting, verify that `green_badge_viewed` fires for at least 60% of active beta users. If visibility is <20%, the test failed due to UI placement, not user preference, and you cannot validate the hypothesis yet.

*Ensure your engineering team hardcodes the control group logic now; retroactively creating a control group after the beta ends will invalidate the statistical comparison.*
