# HeritageWalks Engagement Definition: "Heritage Connection"

To satisfy your grant committee and align with your mission of connecting people to local heritage, we recommend replacing "Opened Articles" with a composite metric called **Heritage Connection Events (HCE)**. This metric shifts focus from passive consumption to active engagement and real-world action.

### The Metric Formula
One **Heritage Connection Event** is recorded when a user completes **any one** of the following actions within a single session:

1.  **Deep Read:** Spending **>45 seconds** on an article (filtering out the <5s bounces).
2.  **Active Curation:** Adding a location to "Favorites" or creating a custom walking route.
3.  **Social Propagation:** Sharing a story via native share tools or copying a link.
4.  **Physical Verification (Proxy):** Clicking "Get Directions" or opening the location in a map application.

*Secondary Qualitative Goal:* Track the number of users who submit user-generated content (photos/stories) as a "Super Connector" segment, though this is too rare for a primary daily metric.

### Why This Definition Works for Your Grant
*   **Filters Vanity:** It explicitly excludes the "open-and-close" behavior you identified, ensuring your success rate reflects genuine interest.
*   **Mission Alignment:** It weights "Physical Verification" and "Active Curation" equally with reading, acknowledging that your goal is to get people *to* the locations, not just keep them in the app.
*   **Actionable Insight:** If "Deep Reads" are high but "Physical Verification" is low, you know users enjoy the history but aren't motivated to visit. This tells the museums exactly where to adjust content (e.g., adding stronger calls-to-action like "Visit this site today").

### Implementation Tracking Plan
To measure this immediately without complex new infrastructure, configure your analytics to track these four specific events:

| Event Name | Trigger Condition | Strategic Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `article_deep_read` | Time on page > 45s | Validates content quality |
| `location_favorited` | Click on "Heart/Save" icon | Indicates intent to return |
| `story_shared` | Click on "Share" button | Measures community advocacy |
| `directions_requested` | Click on "Map/Directions" | **Primary proxy for physical visits** |

**Reporting Format for the Committee:**
Instead of reporting "2,500 downloads," report: *"In Q1, HeritageWalks generated **X Heritage Connection Events**, indicating that **Y%** of our users actively engaged with Cork's history beyond a casual glance. Specifically, **Z** users took action to visit a physical site."*

### Addressing the "Physical Visit" Gap
You cannot definitively track if a user physically stood at the location using only the app without invasive background tracking, which may violate privacy norms. However, `directions_requested` is the industry-standard proxy for "intent to visit." For your grant report, frame this as: *"We track verified intent to visit via navigation requests. Future funding will allow us to pilot 'check-in' features or QR codes at museum partners to measure actual footfall."*

***

*Note: Set the "Deep Read" threshold to 45 seconds based on average reading speed for typical story lengths; adjust this slightly after reviewing your specific content word counts.*
*Ensure you exclude internal team traffic and museum staff testing from these counts to maintain data integrity for the committee.*
