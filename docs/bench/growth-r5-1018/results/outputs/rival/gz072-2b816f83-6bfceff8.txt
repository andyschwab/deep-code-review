# Recommended Behavioral Signals for Membership Renewal Prediction

To determine whether to invest Q2 budget in classes or equipment, MotorCity Makers must track specific behaviors that correlate with the $120 renewal decision. Based on your hypothesis and current data gaps, prioritize these five behavioral signals immediately:

### 1. The "Second-Visit" Velocity
*   **Definition:** Days elapsed between a member's first and second workshop attendance.
*   **Why Track It:** Your hypothesis suggests two visits create advocates. This metric validates *how quickly* that threshold must be met. If members who return within 14 days renew at 80% vs. 30% for those returning in 30+ days, budget should shift to **onboarding classes** that accelerate that second visit.
*   **Action:** Tag every check-in with `visit_number` and `days_since_join`.

### 2. Revenue-Per-Active-Hour (RPAH)
*   **Definition:** Total spend on filament and machine rental divided by total hours logged in the workshop.
*   **Why Track It:** Attendance alone is a vanity metric if members only use free resources. This identifies "high-value engagers." If members attending 4+ workshops but spending <$10 on materials have low renewal rates, "attendance" is a false proxy for engagement.
*   **Action:** Link POS transactions (filament/rentals) to member IDs and session timestamps.

### 3. Project Completion Rate
*   **Definition:** Percentage of started prints that are successfully retrieved and finished (vs. abandoned failures).
*   **Why Track It:** Failed prints cause frustration and churn. A low completion rate suggests the issue is **equipment reliability**, not a lack of education. If members with >50% failure rates renew at lower rates, the Q2 budget must go to **maintenance**, not more classes.
*   **Action:** Track `print_started` vs. `print_retrieved` events per member.

### 4. The "Tool Touch" Diversity
*   **Definition:** Number of distinct machine types used by a member per month (e.g., FDM printer, resin printer, laser cutter).
*   **Why Track It:** Members who diversify their tool usage are more embedded in the community ecosystem and less likely to cancel. This signals deep engagement beyond a single hobby project.
*   **Action:** Record `machine_type_id` at every check-in/rental.

### 5. Peer Interaction Frequency
*   **Definition:** Instances where a member books a "community table" or attends an open hack night vs. solo booked slots.
*   **Why Track It:** Community hubs retain members through relationships, not just hardware. If solo users churn faster than social users, budget should support **community events** rather than just individual machine time.
*   **Action:** Categorize booking types as `solo_work` vs. `community_event`.

## Implementation Priority
Start by instrumenting your check-in system to capture **Visit Velocity** and **RPAH**. These two metrics directly answer Sarah's question:
*   If **Velocity** predicts renewal → Invest in **Classes** (to drive the second visit).
*   If **RPAH** or **Completion Rate** predicts renewal → Invest in **Maintenance** (to ensure reliable, affordable printing).

Do not rely on satisfaction surveys; let the ratio of material spend to workshop attendance dictate the Q2 allocation.

*Note: Ensure all tracking links to the specific member ID associated with the $120 renewal contract to calculate accurate cohort retention rates.*
*Avoid tracking "time spent" without context; a member struggling with a broken machine for 3 hours is not "engaged," they are frustrated.*
