# Pilot Success Criteria Proposal: GlobalManufacturing Inc. Contract Management Deployment

**Prepared for:** Jessica Alverez, Account Executive
**Review Date:** Next Tuesday (Meeting with Chief Legal Officer)
**Pilot Period:** April 1 – June 29 (90 Days)

## Executive Summary
This proposal outlines a dual-track success framework designed to validate both efficiency gains and risk mitigation capabilities. Given the uncertainty regarding the Legal team's primary valuation (speed vs. accuracy), this framework establishes baselines during the first 30 days and sets adaptive targets for the remaining 60 days.

## 1. Baseline Establishment Strategy (Days 1–30)
Since historical data is unavailable, the first month of the pilot will serve as the "Measurement Phase" to establish a credible internal baseline for GlobalManufacturing's specific workflow.

*   **Action:** Run the new tool in "Shadow Mode" or parallel processing for a representative sample of contracts (n=50).
*   **Data Collection:**
    *   Record actual time-stamps for each approval stage to calculate *Current Average Approval Time*.
    *   Manually audit tool-extracted clauses against final executed contracts to calculate *Current Error Rate*.
    *   Survey legal counsel on perceived risk levels for automated vs. manual reviews.

## 2. Proposed Success Metrics (Days 31–90)
We propose three tiered metrics to accommodate the potential prioritization of speed or safety.

### Primary Metric: The Efficiency-Risk Balance
Instead of a single target, we will measure the **Net Cycle Time Improvement** adjusted for **Critical Error Incidents**.

*   **Target A (Speed Focus):** Reduce average approval time from the established baseline (estimated ~14 days) to **≤ 8 days** (approx. 43% reduction), provided critical error rate remains < 1%.
*   **Target B (Risk Focus):** Achieve **99.5% accuracy** in clause extraction (zero critical errors in a sample of 100 contracts), provided approval time does not increase by more than 10% over baseline.

### Secondary Metrics (Adoption & Quality)
*   **User Adoption Rate:** ≥ 85% of assigned legal staff actively using the tool for draft reviews by Day 60.
*   **Clause Extraction Confidence Score:** Average confidence score reported by the tool for extracted clauses ≥ 90%.
*   **Reversion Rate:** < 5% of contracts reverted to fully manual processing due to tool failure or user distrust.

### Guardrail Metrics (Non-Negotiables)
*   **Data Security Compliance:** 100% adherence to GlobalManufacturing's data residency and encryption standards.
*   **System Uptime:** ≥ 99.9% availability during business hours.

## 3. Decision Framework for the CLO
To resolve the speed vs. risk ambiguity during next Tuesday's meeting, we recommend presenting the following trade-off matrix to the Chief Legal Officer:

| Scenario | If CLO Prioritizes... | Then Success Is Defined As... | Trade-off Accepted |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Option 1** | **Velocity** | Approval time reduced by 40%+ | Tolerance for minor non-critical flagging errors requiring human review. |
| **Option 2** | **Risk Mitigation** | Zero critical clause extraction errors | Acceptance of a slower rollout (20% time reduction) to ensure perfect accuracy. |
| **Option 3** | **Balanced Approach** | 25% time reduction + <1% error rate | Moderate gains in both areas; safest bet for enterprise stability. |

## 4. Next Steps
1.  **Confirm Baseline Method:** Agree on the sample size for the Days 1–30 measurement phase.
2.  **Select Primary Orientation:** CLO to indicate preference for Option 1, 2, or 3 above.
3.  **Finalize KPI Dashboard:** Configure reporting views to track the selected primary metric daily.

***

*Note: All targets are provisional pending the actual baseline data collected in April. The "Critical Error" definition must be jointly defined by our team and GlobalManufacturing's legal counsel before launch.*
