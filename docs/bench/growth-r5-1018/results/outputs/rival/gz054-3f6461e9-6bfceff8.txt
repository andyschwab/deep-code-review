**Proposed Activation Framework for LagosConnect**

Given the constraints of intermittent connectivity and high data costs, activation should not be defined by a single "magic number" but by a **Composite Activation Score** that balances engagement depth with technical feasibility. Your current metric (3 DMs) fails because it measures *output* without confirming *social reciprocity* or *community embedding*.

Here are the prioritized activation signals, ranked by predictive likelihood for retention in the Lagos market:

**1. Primary Signal: "The Reciprocal Loop" (High Priority)**
*   **Definition:** A user sends a message AND receives a reply within 24 hours, OR reacts to a received message.
*   **Why:** Sending messages into a void is a churn trigger. In high-context cultures like Lagos, activation is confirmed only when social validation (a reply) is received. This proves the network effect is working for that specific user.
*   **Data Requirement:** Match `message_sent` timestamp with `message_received` (by recipient) timestamp.

**2. Secondary Signal: "Community Anchoring" (High Priority)**
*   **Definition:** Joining a specific local group (e.g., "Yaba Tech," "Lekki Parents") AND viewing at least 3 posts within that group.
*   **Why:** Individual DMs are fragile; group membership creates stickiness. Viewing posts is low-data usage compared to uploading media, making it a sustainable early behavior for users on limited data plans.
*   **Context:** This leverages your community leader onboarding strategy. If they joined via a leader, did they enter the leader's specific group?

**3. Tertiary Signal: "Low-Bandwidth Participation" (Medium Priority)**
*   **Definition:** Reacting to content (emoji/like) or commenting on a thread without uploading media.
*   **Why:** Uploading photos/videos is expensive and prone to failure on unstable networks, causing frustration. Text-based interactions or simple reactions are high-success actions that signal intent without the friction of heavy data usage.

**4. Negative Signal (Churn Indicator): "The Ghost Broadcast"**
*   **Definition:** Sending 3+ messages with 0 replies within 48 hours.
*   **Action:** Flag these users immediately for intervention by community leaders. This is likely your current "activated" cohort that is actually churning.

**Implementation Strategy for Your Retention Model**

Since you lack long-term historical data, do not wait for perfect cohorts. Instead, run a **7-Day Leading Indicator Analysis** on your existing 5,000 users:

1.  **Segment:** Divide users into four groups based on the first 7 days of activity:
    *   Group A: Sent 3+ DMs (your current definition).
    *   Group B: Achieved "Reciprocal Loop."
    *   Group C: Joined a Group + Viewed 3 posts.
    *   Group D: Did none of the above.
2.  **Measure:** Calculate Day-7 and Day-14 return rates for each group.
3.  **Hypothesis:** You will likely find Group B and C have 2-3x higher retention than Group A.

**Onboarding Recommendations for Yaba Team**
*   **Shift the Goal:** Instruct community leaders to guide new users to *join a group* and *reply to an existing thread* before encouraging them to start new DM conversations.
*   **Optimize for Data:** Ensure the "Join Group" and "Reply" buttons are prominent and lightweight in the UI. Hide heavy media upload features until the user has completed these lighter activation steps.
*   **Immediate Feedback:** If a user sends a message, use push notifications aggressively to alert the recipient, increasing the chance of a reply and closing the "Reciprocal Loop."

**Note:** Focus your engineering team on logging "reply latency" (time between send and reply) as this is your strongest leading indicator of network health.
