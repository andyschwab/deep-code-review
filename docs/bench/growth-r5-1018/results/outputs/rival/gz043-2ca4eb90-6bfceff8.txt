# CloudScale Discounted Pilot Experiment Design

## Executive Summary
This experiment tests whether a 20% discount on the 60-day pilot program accelerates enterprise deal velocity and improves pilot-to-contract conversion without compromising long-term deal quality or brand perception. Given the nine-month sales cycle, success will be measured through a combination of leading indicators (0–90 days) and lagging revenue outcomes (9+ months).

## Experimental Cohorts

To isolate the impact of the discount while controlling for external variables, we will compare three distinct cohorts over a 90-day enrollment window:

1.  **Control Group (Standard Pricing):** Enterprise leads receiving the standard pilot proposal with no discount. This establishes the baseline conversion rate (historically 30%) and quality metrics.
2.  **Treatment Group A (Discounted Pilot):** Enterprise leads receiving a 20% discount on the pilot fee. This tests the primary hypothesis regarding conversion lift to 45%.
3.  **Treatment Group B (Value-Add Pilot):** Enterprise leads receiving the standard price but with added implementation support or extended success management. This serves as a guardrail to determine if price is the actual barrier versus perceived risk.

**Segmentation Variables:**
Within each cohort, we must track performance by **Industry Vertical** (e.g., FinTech, Healthcare, Manufacturing) to detect price sensitivity variations. Additionally, we will segment by **Deal Size** (ACV) to ensure the discount does not disproportionately attract smaller, lower-quality opportunities.

## Leading Indicators (0–90 Days)

Since waiting nine months for final revenue data is impractical for board reporting, we will monitor these leading indicators to gauge early traction and potential risks:

### Velocity Metrics
*   **Pilot Acceptance Rate:** Percentage of proposals accepted within 14 days. A significant increase here validates the "acceleration" assumption.
*   **Time-to-Signature:** Average days from proposal sent to contract signed. We expect a reduction in this metric for the Discounted cohort.
*   **Sales Cycle Compression:** Reduction in time spent negotiating the pilot phase specifically.

### Engagement & Quality Metrics
*   **Pilot Activation Speed:** Days from contract sign to first successful integration. Slower activation in the discount cohort may indicate lower commitment.
*   **Feature Adoption Depth:** Number of core modules utilized during the 60-day pilot. Lower usage in the discount cohort could signal "bargain hunter" behavior.
*   **Stakeholder Breadth:** Number of distinct user accounts active during the pilot. High-level executive engagement (CIO/CTO logins) is a critical quality signal.

### Sentiment & Perception Guardrails
*   **Negotiation Friction Score:** Qualitative rating from Account Executives on the difficulty of closing the *full* contract post-pilot. If discount pilots require more concessions later, the strategy devalues the product.
*   **Churn Risk Flags:** Early indicators during the pilot (e.g., lack of executive sponsorship, missed milestone reviews) that historically correlate with post-contract churn.

## Lagging Outcomes (9+ Months)

While the board presentation focuses on early signals, the experiment's ultimate success depends on these long-term metrics, which must be tracked in the CRM now:
*   **Pilot-to-Contract Conversion Rate:** Target increase from 30% to 45%.
*   **Net Revenue Retention (NRR):** Comparing NRR of discount-originated deals vs. control after 12 months.
*   **Lifetime Value (LTV) Variance:** Assessing if the initial 20% discount results in permanently lower contract values or upsell resistance.
*   **Payback Period:** Time to recover the cost of acquisition, accounting for the initial discount.

## Implementation Timeline for Board Presentation

*   **Weeks 1–2:** Finalize cohort assignment logic in CRM and train sales team on script variations.
*   **Weeks 3–14:** Run the 90-day enrollment window.
*   **Day 45 (Interim Report):** Present early velocity metrics (Acceptance Rate, Time-to-Signature) to the board.
*   **Day 90 (Full Early Review):** Present engagement depth and initial conversion trends.
*   **Month 9+:** Final revenue impact analysis.

This approach allows the VP of Sales to demonstrate momentum and data-driven decision-making within the board's timeframe while maintaining rigorous controls to protect long-term unit economics.

*Note: Ensure sales compensation plans are adjusted so reps are not penalized for selling discounted pilots if volume targets are met.*
