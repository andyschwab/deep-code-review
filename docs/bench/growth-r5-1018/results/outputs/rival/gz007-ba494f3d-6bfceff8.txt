# MarketPlace NG: Launch Liquidity Dashboard (Days 1–30)

**Primary Goal:** Achieve 100 transactions in Week 1; establish sustainable liquidity.
**Philosophy:** In a cold-start marketplace, **count and speed** matter more than value. Ignore Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) for now; at low volumes, a single high-value item skews data, hiding whether you actually have enough activity to sustain the platform. Focus on successful matches and velocity.

### Core Metrics Table

| Metric | Definition | Why It Matters (Cold Start Context) | Target (Week 1) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Liquidity Rate** | `(Transactions Completed) / (Total Active Listings)` | The true health indicator. It measures the probability that a listed item sells. A low rate means buyers can't find what they want; a high rate means sellers make money and stay. | 2–5% daily |
| **Time-to-First-Sale** | Average hours from `listing_created` to `transaction_completed` | Speed signals demand. If items sit for >48 hours, sellers lose confidence. Fast sales create the "buzz" needed for your waiting list conversion. | < 24 hours |
| **Buyer Conversion Rate** | `(Users who purchase) / (Unique visitors from waitlist)` | Validates your 2,500-person waitlist quality. If traffic is high but purchases are low, your pricing or trust mechanisms (payments/delivery) are broken. | 4–8% |
| **Seller Activation Rate** | `(Sellers with ≥1 sale) / (Total active sellers)` | Retention driver. In P2P, one successful sale convinces a seller to list more. If this is low, you will run out of inventory despite having listings. | 20% of sellers |
| **Search/Listing Match Rate** | `(Searches resulting in a click or sale) / (Total searches)` | Indicates inventory relevance. If users search "bags" and see nothing, your supply mix is wrong for Lagos demand. | > 60% |
| **Daily Transaction Count** | Total completed transactions per 24h | Your primary north star for the advisory board. Simple, undeniable proof of traction toward the 100/week goal. | 14+ daily |

### Dashboard Layout Recommendation

**Row 1: The "Pulse" (Daily Counters)**
*   **Total Transactions Today** (Big bold number)
*   **Liquidity Rate %** (Color-coded: Red <1%, Yellow 1-3%, Green >3%)
*   **Active Buyers vs. Active Sellers** (Side-by-side count)

**Row 2: The "Friction" (Diagnostic Trends)**
*   **Line Chart:** Time-to-First-Sale (Hours) over last 7 days. *Goal: Downward trend.*
*   **Bar Chart:** Transactions by Category (e.g., Fashion, Decor, Art). *Identifies which niche is driving early adoption.*

**Row 3: The "Funnel" (Waitlist Conversion)**
*   **Funnel View:** Waitlist Invite Sent → Site Visit → Item View → Purchase.
*   *Critical for explaining to the board where drop-off occurs.*

### Strategic Notes for the Advisory Board
1.  **Why not GMV?** At 100 transactions, one ₦500k sale could make a week look "successful" while 99 users had a bad experience. Volume proves the *mechanism* works; value comes later.
2.  **The Ratio Benchmark:** Do not fixate on a static "Listings-to-Buyers" ratio. A marketplace with 1,000 listings and 10 buyers fails if the items don't match. Focus on **Liquidity Rate** (sell-through %); if it stays above 2%, your ratio is healthy regardless of absolute numbers.
