**Prioritized Event Instrumentation Plan for NutriPath Mobile Launch**

**1. `clinical_data_entry_completed`**
*   **Properties:** `data_type` (e.g., blood_glucose, ketones), `entry_latency_hours` (time since signup), `device_source` (manual vs. integration).
*   **Rationale:** Validates your core hypothesis regarding early clinical engagement. Tracking `entry_latency` specifically allows you to test if the 48-hour window is the true cliff or if earlier/later entry correlates equally with retention. This is your primary predictor variable.

**2. `meal_plan_activation_started`**
*   **Properties:** `plan_type` (e.g., keto-modified, low-phenylalanine), `customization_count` (number of swaps made before saving), `completion_status` (completed/abandoned).
*   **Rationale:** Distinguishes between passive signups and active utility. For rare metabolic conditions, the ability to successfully customize a plan to strict dietary restrictions is likely a stronger retention driver than generic browsing. High `customization_count` followed by `completion_status: completed` signals high intent.

**3. `grocery_list_exported`**
*   **Properties:** `export_method` (pdf, email, apple_wallet), `item_count`, `time_to_export_hours`.
*   **Rationale:** Hypothesized as a stronger leading indicator than recipe views because it represents a transition from digital planning to real-world action. Users who export lists have likely committed to the regimen for the coming week, reducing churn risk compared to those who only view recipes.

**4. `recipe_view_duration_seconds`**
*   **Properties:** `recipe_id`, `dietary_tag_match` (boolean: does recipe match user's specific condition?), `scroll_depth_percent`.
*   **Rationale:** Replaces simple "view" counts with engagement depth. For this niche, a user scrolling 90% through a compatible recipe indicates validation of content relevance. `dietary_tag_match` is critical to ensure you aren't measuring curiosity about irrelevant food options.

**5. `onboarding_step_skipped`**
*   **Properties:** `step_name` (e.g., "connect_device", "enter_medication", "set_reminders"), `exit_point`.
*   **Rationale:** With only two developers, you cannot fix everything post-launch. This event identifies exactly where users with rare conditions friction out during setup. High skip rates on specific medical data steps may indicate privacy concerns or complexity barriers that directly impact long-term trust and retention.

**Implementation Notes:**
*   **Privacy:** Do not transmit actual glucose values or specific condition names to third-party analytics tools; send only categorical tags (e.g., `data_type: glucose`) and boolean flags to remain HIPAA/GDPR compliant.
*   **Engineering:** Implement these as a single shared `data_layer` schema to minimize developer overhead, ensuring all five events populate the same core user profile table for easy cohort analysis.
