# SkyDrop Beta Measurement Framework: Technical Performance & Human Experience

## Executive Summary
This framework evaluates commercial viability by correlating operational efficiency with recipient trust. While delivery speed is a baseline requirement, **anxiety reduction** and **landing confidence** are the differentiators for mass adoption. The go/no-go decision will rely on a composite "Viability Score" rather than speed alone.

## 1. Quantitative Operational Metrics (The "Hard" Data)
*Collected automatically via drone telemetry and app logs.*

| Metric | Definition | Target Benchmark |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **On-Time Delivery Rate** | % of deliveries within promised window | >98% |
| **Flight Efficiency** | Actual flight time vs. optimal route time | <110% variance |
| **Landing Precision** | Deviation (cm) from intended drop zone | <50cm |
| **Abort/Retry Rate** | % of flights requiring manual intervention or re-attempt | <2% |
| **Noise Complaint Trigger** | Decibel spikes exceeding local limits during hover/landing | 0 incidents |

## 2. Human Experience Metrics (The "Soft" Data)
*Collected via immediate post-delivery micro-surveys (SMS/App) and optional longitudinal tracking.*

### A. Anxiety Index (During Flight)
*Measured via push notification at "Drone Approaching" stage.*
- **Metric:** Self-reported anxiety level (1–5 scale) upon notification of drone arrival.
- **Context:** Tracks if visibility of the drone causes distress before it lands.
- **Target:** Average score <2.0.

### B. Landing Confidence Score (Post-Delivery)
*Measured immediately after package retrieval.*
- **Metric:** "How safe did you feel during the drop?" (1–5 scale).
- **Secondary:** "Did the landing mechanism seem stable?" (Yes/No).
- **Target:** >4.5 average; >95% "Yes."

### C. Net Trust Score (NTS)
- **Question:** "How likely are you to recommend SkyDrop to a neighbor based on *this* delivery?"
- **Differentiation:** Unlike NPS, this specifically isolates trust in the *technology*, not just the service speed.

## 3. Qualitative Feedback Loops
- **Incident Narratives:** Open text field triggered only if any metric scores <3.
- **Visual Verification:** Optional user upload of package condition/landing spot for anomaly analysis.
- **Community Sentiment:** Weekly monitoring of local Seattle community forums/social media for unsolicited mentions of "drone noise" or "safety concerns."

## 4. The Viability Matrix (Decision Framework)
Plot every delivery on a 2x2 matrix: **Operational Efficiency** (X-axis) vs. **Human Trust** (Y-axis).

| Quadrant | Status | Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High Efficiency / High Trust** | **Green** | Proceed to scale. |
| **High Efficiency / Low Trust** | **Yellow (Risk)** | Speed is working, but people are scared. Pause scaling; focus on UX/communication/landing softness. |
| **Low Efficiency / High Trust** | **Yellow (Optimization)** | People love it, but it's too slow/expensive. Optimize routing algorithms before scaling. |
| **Low Efficiency / Low Trust** | **Red** | **No-Go.** Fundamental product-market fit failure. |

## 5. Go/No-Go Decision Criteria
To approve full-scale deployment, the beta must achieve:
1.  **Statistical Significance:** Minimum 400 completed deliveries with full data sets.
2.  **Composite Score:** Weighted average of 70% Operational Efficiency + 30% Human Trust > 4.0/5.0.
3.  **Safety Threshold:** Zero incidents causing property damage or injury.
4.  **Anxiety Trend:** No statistically significant increase in anxiety scores over the 30-day period (indicating habituation, not fatigue).

**Data Collection Cadence:** Real-time telemetry dashboards for ops; daily aggregated sentiment reports for leadership; weekly deep-dive correlation analysis (e.g., "Does louder wind noise correlate with lower landing confidence?").

*Note: Ensure all user data collection complies with Washington state privacy laws and FAA data security guidelines.*
*Investor decks should highlight the "Trust Correlation" graph to demonstrate long-term retention potential beyond initial novelty.*
