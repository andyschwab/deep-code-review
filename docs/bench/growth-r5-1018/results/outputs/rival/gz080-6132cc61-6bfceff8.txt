### WonPay Savings Challenge: Metric Hierarchy & Success Definition

To move beyond vanity metrics like total enrollment, success must be redefined as **sustained behavioral change** rather than one-time participation. Given the 80% dropout rate after the first deposit, the current "enrollment" metric is misleading. The following hierarchy prioritizes retention and habit formation over initial acquisition.

#### Level 1: Activation (The "Real" Start)
*Current enrollment counts users who clicked "Join." This must change.*
*   **Metric:** **Challenge Initiation Rate**
*   **Definition:** % of enrolled users who make a *second* deposit within 7 days of the first.
*   **Why:** This filters out the 80% who drop off immediately. If a user deposits twice, they have engaged with the mechanism, not just the idea.
*   **Target:** Increase from current ~20% to 35% by March.

#### Level 2: Retention (The Habit Loop)
*This measures if the challenge is creating a routine.*
*   **Metric:** **4-Week Continuity Score**
*   **Definition:** % of users who make at least one deposit every week for four consecutive weeks.
*   **Why:** Financial health for teens is about consistency, not lump sums. This identifies users transitioning from "gamified participants" to "savers."
*   **Target:** 15% of initiators achieve 4-week continuity.

#### Level 3: Outcome (Financial Health Proxy)
*Since you lack 6-month data, use balance stability as a leading indicator.*
*   **Metric:** **Net Positive Balance Duration**
*   **Definition:** Average number of days a user maintains a non-zero balance after completing a challenge milestone.
*   **Why:** Completing a challenge shouldn't mean withdrawing everything immediately. This measures if the feature encourages keeping capital in the ecosystem.
*   **Target:** Median duration increases from 3 days to 14 days post-milestone.

#### Level 4: North Star (Long-Term Value)
*The ultimate measure of responsible financial habits.*
*   **Metric:** **6-Month Cohort Retention (Projected)**
*   **Definition:** % of users who initiated a challenge in February and still have an active account with >$0 balance in August.
*   **Why:** This validates your core assumption that challenge completers are stickier long-term users.
*   **Action:** Begin tracking this cohort now; report interim results at 30 and 60 days.

### Implementation Notes for Compliance & Product
1.  **Redefine "Success" Internally:** Immediately stop reporting "Total Enrolled" as a primary KPI to stakeholders. Replace it with "Active Challengers" (Level 1) to align incentives with retention, not just sign-ups.
2.  **Intervention Trigger:** Build an automated alert for users who enroll but fail to make a second deposit within 48 hours; this is the critical churn point where a nudge (push notification or educational tip) is most effective.
