# PixelQuest South Korea: Pre-Launch Success Metric Framework

Given your beta data (18m session length but only 25% D1 retention) and the hypothesis that social guilds drive longevity, do not track "total registrations" or "daily active users" as primary success indicators yet. These are vanity metrics in a soft launch; they mask the leakage in your funnel. Instead, instrument these five actionable metrics to validate product-market fit (PMF) specifically for the Korean market's high-social gaming expectations:

### 1. Guild Formation Rate (Day 1 & Day 7)
**Definition:** Percentage of new users who join or create a guild within 24 hours and 7 days of install.
**Why it matters:** This directly tests your core hypothesis. In Korea, where community (nori) is central to gaming, if users aren't forming guilds immediately, the social hook has failed.
**Actionable Signal:** If <15% of users form/join a guild by Day 1, the onboarding flow for social features is too friction-heavy or not prominent enough.

### 2. Social Retention Delta
**Definition:** The difference in D1/D7 retention between users in a guild vs. solo players.
**Why it matters:** Absolute retention (25%) is low, but the *delta* tells you if your solution works. If guilded users retain at 60% while solo users retain at 10%, your hypothesis is correct, and the problem is purely acquisition/onboarding into guilds.
**Actionable Signal:** A delta of <20% suggests guilds aren't providing enough value or the game loop works equally well alone (invalidating the social premise).

### 3. "First Friend" Time-to-Value
**Definition:** Average time from install to first meaningful social interaction (guild join, friend add, or co-op match).
**Why it matters:** Korean mobile gamers expect instant social gratification. An 18-minute session is strong, but if those minutes are spent solo before churn, the long-term LTV will be low.
**Actionable Signal:** If this exceeds 10 minutes, you are losing users before the social stickiness kicks in.

### 4. Core Loop Completion Rate (Session Quality)
**Definition:** Percentage of sessions where a user completes the primary game loop (e.g., match start → match end → reward claim) at least once.
**Why it matters:** Session length (18m) can be inflated by users being stuck or confused. Completion rate validates that the gameplay itself is functional and rewarding.
**Actionable Signal:** Low completion with high session time indicates frustration; high completion with low retention indicates the core loop is fun but lacks a reason to return (likely the missing social layer).

### 5. Viral Coefficient (K-Factor) by Guild
**Definition:** Average number of new invites sent per existing guild member that result in an install.
**Why it matters:** In a competitive market, organic growth via guild recruitment is essential for sustainable scaling.
**Actionable Signal:** A K-factor >0.5 within guilds indicates strong product-led growth potential specific to the social mechanic.

### Monetization Strategy: Delay Revenue Metrics
Do **not** track monetization (ARPU, LTV, conversion rate) as a success metric for the April 5th soft launch. With D1 retention at 25%, optimizing for revenue is premature and will distort product decisions. Focus entirely on fixing the retention leak via the social guild mechanic. Introduce monetization tracking only once D1 retention stabilizes above 40% and the Guild Formation Rate exceeds 20%.

### Implementation Priority
Instrument events for `guild_created`, `guild_joined`, `friend_invite_sent`, and `co_op_match_started` immediately. Ensure these fire before the user leaves the onboarding flow. Without this data, you cannot distinguish between a game people don't like and a social system people can't find.

*Note: All metrics must be segmented by "Guilded" vs. "Solo" to prove causality. Do not report aggregate averages to the founding team.*
