### Recommended Activation Definition for CloudVault

Based on your data, volume (50GB) and breadth (3 folders) are vanity metrics that do not correlate with retention. Your high-renewal cohort reveals that **reliability** and **workflow integration** drive value, not storage capacity.

**New Activation Criteria:**
A customer is "Activated" when they achieve **Automated Continuity**, defined as:
1.  **Core Job Done:** Automated backups are configured and have successfully run at least **three consecutive cycles** (proving reliability).
2.  **Workflow Integration:** At least **two active seats** have accessed the platform within a 7-day window (proving adoption beyond the admin).

*Discard the 50GB upload and external sharing requirements as primary activation gates. These are expansion behaviors, not foundational value drivers.*

### How to Measure and Read Usage Patterns

To validate this new definition and guide your onboarding redesign, shift your analytics focus from "accumulation" to "habit formation."

**1. Measure Frequency over Volume**
*   **The Metric:** *Weekly Active Seats (WAS)* relative to total licenses purchased.
*   **The Insight:** Your data suggests that a client with 10GB and high frequency outperforms a client with 50GB and low frequency. Track the "Time to First Automated Run" and "Time to Second Active User."
*   **Action:** If a client uploads data but doesn't set up automation within 14 days, they are at high churn risk. Trigger Customer Success intervention here, not when they hit storage limits.

**2. Measure Integration Depth (The "Stickiness" Factor)**
*   **The Metric:** *Integration Events per Week* (e.g., API calls, third-party tool connections, automated script executions).
*   **The Insight:** Clients who embed CloudVault into their existing IT infrastructure (automated backups) face higher switching costs and perceive higher value than those manually dragging and dropping files.
*   **Action:** Redesign in-app guidance to prioritize the "Setup Automation" wizard immediately after account creation, pushing "Share Folder" features to a secondary onboarding track.

**3. Re-evaluating the "Sharing" Hypothesis**
*   **The Finding:** The 40% churn rate among those sharing folders suggests that external sharing without internal automation creates a "fragile" use case. They may be using CloudVault as a temporary transfer tool rather than a storage system.
*   **The Pivot:** Treat "External Sharing" as an *expansion* metric to be unlocked *after* activation, not a prerequisite for it.

### Implementation Plan for Onboarding & Guidance

**For Customer Success:**
*   **New Health Score:** Weight "Automated Backup Status" at 50% and "Active Seat Count" at 30%. Remove "Total GB Stored" from the initial health calculation.
*   **Intervention Trigger:** Alert CSMs when a client hits 20GB storage but has *zero* automated tasks configured. This is the "False Positive" danger zone.

**For Product/Onboarding Emails:**
*   **Email Sequence Change:** Stop sending "Unlock more storage" or "Invite your team" emails as the primary call-to-action in Week 1.
*   **New Flow:**
    *   *Day 1:* "Connect your first data source" (Focus on automation setup).
    *   *Day 3:* "Verify your first successful backup" (Reinforce reliability).
    *   *Day 7:* "Invite a colleague to verify access" (Drive seat activation).
    *   *Day 14:* "Ready to share externally?" (Introduce sharing only after value is proven).

By redefining activation around **automated reliability** and **team habit**, you align your success metrics with the behaviors that actually predict renewal.

*Note: Validate this new definition against a holdout group of recent signups before fully automating health scores. Ensure your event tracking captures "backup cycle completion" distinctly from "data upload."*
