### Top Three Daily Metrics for Executive Steering

**1. Verified Transaction Completion Rate**
*Definition:* The percentage of initiated purchases where the seller passed identity verification AND the buyer confirmed receipt without opening a dispute within 48 hours.
*Link to Goal:* This directly validates your core assumption that trust drives volume. A high rate indicates that verification checks are successfully enabling transactions rather than creating friction. It moves beyond "signups" to measure actual trusted exchanges, critical for hitting the 500-transaction target.

**2. First-Repeat Purchase Ratio (7-Day Window)**
*Definition:* The number of users making a second purchase within 7 days of their first, divided by the total number of first-time buyers in the preceding 7 days.
*Link to Goal:* This measures retention driven by trust in device condition. If your hypothesis holds, users who receive devices matching their description will return quickly. Monitoring this daily allows you to detect early signals of trust breakdown (e.g., misrepresented conditions) before they impact the 30-day goal.

**3. Dispute Resolution Success Rate**
*Definition:* The percentage of opened disputes resolved in favor of the platform's standard policy (either refund issued or release of funds) within 24 hours, where both parties accept the outcome without escalating to chargebacks or external complaints.
*Link to Goal:* This measures the effectiveness of your trust safety net. High success rates indicate that your resolution process reinforces trust even when things go wrong, encouraging future participation. It distinguishes between "disputes happening" (normal) and "trust failing" (unresolved conflict).

### Immediate Data Capture Requirements

To validate your trust hypothesis from Day 1, you must capture these specific data points for every transaction:

*   **Seller Verification Status:** Timestamp and method of verification completion (e.g., ID upload, phone verification) prior to listing activation.
*   **Device Condition Declaration:** The specific condition grade selected by the seller at listing time (e.g., "Like New," "Good," "Fair").
*   **Buyer Condition Confirmation:** A binary flag recorded at delivery confirmation indicating whether the received item matched the declared condition (Yes/No), with a mandatory text field for "No" responses.
*   **Dispute Trigger & Outcome:** The specific reason code for any dispute opened (e.g., "Item not as described," "Non-delivery") and the final resolution status with timestamps for each step.
*   **User Cohort ID:** Tag each user as "Waitlist Convert" or "Organic Launch" to segment behavior between your 15,000 warm leads and new traffic.

These data points allow you to correlate verification rigor with dispute rates and repeat purchases, directly testing whether trust mechanisms drive the retention you need for sustainable expansion.

*Ensure all tracking events include a unique transaction ID to link seller actions, buyer receipts, and dispute outcomes into a single lineage for accurate cohort analysis.*
