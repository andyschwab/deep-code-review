### ScootBarcelona Pilot Metric Framework (May 1 Launch)

To secure City Council approval and ensure sustainable growth, you must abandon "rides per scooter" as your primary success metric. Your Gracia beta proved that maximizing volume directly correlates with increased damage and community friction. Instead, adopt a **Balanced Scorecard** approach where expansion permissions are gated by safety and compliance performance, not just utilization.

Present the following four-pillar framework to your stakeholders:

#### 1. Community & Compliance (The Gatekeeper Metrics)
*These metrics determine if you are allowed to operate. If these thresholds are not met, utilization targets are irrelevant.*
*   **Geofencing Compliance Rate:** % of rides ending in designated zones vs. illegal sidewalk blocking.
    *   *Target:* >95% compliance.
    *   *Action:* If <90%, pause expansion and tighten geofencing penalties.
*   **Incident Density:** Reported obstructions or accidents per 1,000 rides (normalized by volume).
    *   *Target:* <5 reports per 1,000 rides.
    *   *Why:* This normalizes complaints against growth, showing the City you manage risk even as you scale.

#### 2. Safety & Asset Health (The Sustainability Metrics)
*These measure the physical cost of your operations.*
*   **Damage Rate per 100 Rides:** Track mechanical failures and vandalism relative to usage, not total fleet size.
    *   *Target:* <5% (down from your 20% beta).
    *   *Insight:* A spike here indicates reckless riding or poor parking enforcement.
*   **Safety Incident Severity:** Weighted score of incidents (minor scrape vs. pedestrian injury).
    *   *Action:* Any severity level 3+ incident triggers an immediate zone audit.

#### 3. Utilization & Efficiency (The Business Metrics)
*Only optimize these if the Gatekeeper metrics are green.*
*   **Effective Rides per Scooter:** Rides completed *without* a compliance violation.
    *   *Shift:* This replaces "total rides." A scooter doing 6 rides with 2 violations counts as only 4 effective rides.
*   **Average Trip Duration & Distance:** Monitor for "joyriding" vs. utility.
    *   *Insight:* Extremely short trips (<2 mins) often indicate users moving scooters to bypass geofences rather than genuine transport.

#### 4. Community Sentiment (The Qualitative Metric)
*   **Net Community Score:** Ratio of positive app feedback/support tickets vs. external complaints (311 calls, social media mentions).
    *   *Target:* Positive trend line week-over-week.

### How to Read the Pilot Data

Do not report these metrics in isolation. Use a **Traffic Light Dashboard** for your weekly reviews with the City Council:

1.  **The "Stop" Rule:** If *Geofencing Compliance* drops below 90% or *Damage Rate* exceeds 10% for two consecutive weeks, automatic suspension of new zone deployments until corrected. This demonstrates proactive responsibility to the Safety Board.
2.  **The Efficiency Trade-off:** Plot *Effective Rides* against *Incident Density*. If rides increase but incident density rises, you are growing unsustainably. The goal is to flatten incident density while raising effective rides.
3.  **Zone Heatmaps:** Overlay high-usage zones with high-complaint zones. If they overlap, you have a placement problem, not a user behavior problem. Relocate docking stations immediately.

**Recommendation:** Prioritize **Geofencing Compliance Rates** and **Incident Reports per 100 Rides** over trip duration. Duration is a preference metric; compliance is a legal license to operate. By tying your internal bonuses to "Effective Rides" (compliant rides) rather than total volume, you align your team's incentives with the City's safety goals.

Present this framework as a "Safety-First Growth Model," explicitly acknowledging the Gracia lessons learned to build trust with the Council.
