# Diagnostic Plan: Power User Churn Analysis (Jan–Mar)

**Objective:** Isolate the primary driver of the 4% → 9% churn increase among power users (>20 projects/month) by analyzing session logs to identify the precise "moment of decision" to leave.

### Phase 1: Cohort Definition & Data Segmentation
*   **Target Cohort:** Filter logs for users with >20 projects/month in the 30 days prior to churn.
*   **Time Windows:** Split data into two distinct periods: Pre-Change (Oct–Dec) and Post-Change (Jan–Mar).
*   **Churn Definition:** Define "churn event" strictly as subscription cancellation or 30 days of inactivity following a login.
*   **Competitor Flag:** Tag sessions where users visited known competitor URLs (if available in referrer logs) or searched for competitor names within the app help center.

### Phase 2: Behavioral Forensics (The "Moment of Decision")
Analyze the last 5 sessions before churn for the Jan–Mar cohort compared to the baseline. Focus on three specific friction indicators:

1.  **Layering Tool Latency Correlation:**
    *   Isolate events involving >10 layers.
    *   Calculate the delta between `action_initiated` and `action_completed` (e.g., merging, masking).
    *   **Hypothesis Test:** If the slowdown is the driver, churned users will show a statistically significant increase in "abandonment after timeout" or "repeated retry attempts" in the layering module compared to retained power users.

2.  **Workflow Abandonment Points:**
    *   Map the drop-off rate at each step of the complex workflow (Import → Layer → Edit → Export).
    *   Identify the specific step where the exit rate spiked in Q1 vs. Q4. A spike specifically at the "Layer" step confirms the tool change hypothesis.

3.  **Error & Frustration Signals:**
    *   Count `error_throw` events, `force_quit` instances, and rapid clicking (rage clicks) within the canvas area.
    *   Cross-reference these spikes with the timestamp of the layering tool update deployment.

### Phase 3: Competitive & Alternative Driver Analysis
*   **Feature Usage Shift:** Analyze if churned users reduced usage of *other* core features before leaving, suggesting a broader dissatisfaction or a shift to a competitor's all-in-one solution.
*   **Support Ticket Correlation:** Join session logs with support tickets. Filter for keywords like "slow," "lag," "layering," or "competitor name" in the 7 days prior to churn.
*   **Survey Re-evaluation:** Re-code open-ended survey responses from churned users using the specific friction points identified in Phase 2 (e.g., search for mentions of "speed" specifically during "complex edits").

### Phase 4: Synthesis & Recommendation Framework
Deliver a report to Marcus Thorne structured as follows:

1.  **The Smoking Gun:** A single chart showing the correlation between layering latency spikes and session abandonment in Q1.
2.  **Competitor Impact Score:** Qualitative assessment of competitor mentions vs. performance complaints.
3.  **The Decision Moment:** A user journey map highlighting the exact step where 60%+ of churned users exited.
4.  **Strategic Options:**
    *   *If Performance:* Immediate rollback plan or engineering sprint prioritization.
    *   *If Competitor:* Feature gap analysis and rapid prototyping roadmap.
    *   *If Both:* A segmented communication strategy addressing performance fixes while teasing upcoming feature parity.

**Timeline:** Data extraction (2 days), Analysis (3 days), Reporting (1 day).

*Note: Ensure all analysis excludes users who churned due to billing issues to maintain cohort purity. This plan relies solely on existing log data; no new user interviews are required for this initial diagnostic.*
