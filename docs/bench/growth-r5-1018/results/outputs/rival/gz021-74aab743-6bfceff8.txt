### Proposed Dashboard Metrics for Beta Validation

To validate your assumption that brewery managers prioritize real-time verification over long-term impact, and to identify the specific metric driving beta sign-up conversion, track these three distinct data points in your prototype dashboard:

**1. The "Verification Latency" Delta**
*   **Definition:** The time difference (in hours or minutes) between a brewery's physical CO2 capture event and the moment that data appears as "verified" on their dashboard.
*   **Why it matters:** If your assumption is correct, this is the primary value driver. Generic providers often report with a 30–90 day lag. By explicitly tracking how often users check this specific "time-to-verification" figure versus older historical data, you prove whether "real-time" is actually the hook. If users ignore this delta, your core differentiation hypothesis is flawed.

**2. The "Process Interruption" Alert Acknowledgement Rate**
*   **Definition:** The percentage of automated alerts (e.g., "Sequestration efficiency dropped 15%") that are clicked and acknowledged by the brewery manager within 24 hours.
*   **Why it matters:** This distinguishes between passive interest in "green credentials" and active operational reliance. If managers treat your dashboard as an operational tool (checking real-time health) rather than a marketing badge (checking total tons offset), this rate will be high. A low rate suggests they view carbon offsetting as a back-office compliance task, not a real-time operational priority, requiring a pivot in your product narrative.

**3. The "Impact Horizon" Toggle Ratio**
*   **Definition:** The ratio of session time spent viewing the "Live/Today" data view versus the "Cumulative/Long-term" report view during a single login.
*   **Why it matters:** This directly tests your assumption. If users spend 80% of their session on live data, your assumption holds. If they immediately switch to cumulative totals to share with stakeholders or for annual reporting, then "long-term impact" remains the primary conversion driver, and your real-time features are secondary. This metric tells your technical co-founder exactly which backend data pipelines to optimize first: low-latency streaming infrastructure or robust historical aggregation engines.

These metrics focus on user behavior within the dashboard to determine value perception, allowing your team to prioritize instrumentation based on actual engagement rather than founder intuition.

*Note: Ensure these events are tagged with the specific brewery partner ID to allow for segment analysis by brewery size or type.*
*Do not aggregate these numbers across all users initially; individual brewery behavior patterns will reveal more than the average.*
