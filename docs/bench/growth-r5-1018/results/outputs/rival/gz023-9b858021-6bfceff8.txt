### Recommended Activation Metric: "Verified Intent to Rent"

**Definition:** A user is activated when they **send a first message to a landlord AND include a specific proposed move-in date or viewing request within that first interaction.**

Do not count a profile creation or a generic "Hello" as activation. Do not wait for a signed lease to claim activation for product-market fit (PMF) analysis at this stage, as the sales cycle (landlord verification, referencing, contracts) introduces external friction unrelated to your algorithm's matching quality.

#### Why This Metric?
1.  **Filters Vanity:** It eliminates the 1,115 users who browse but hesitate, isolating the 85 who take action, and further filtering those who send low-effort spam vs. genuine inquiries.
2.  **Aligns with Revenue:** In the London student market, a tenant specifying a date (e.g., "Moving in Sept 1st") signals immediate monetization potential. Landlords ignore vague inquiries; they respond to dates and viewing requests.
3.  **Diagnoses the Barrier:**
    *   *High Profile Views + Low Messages:* Interface confusion or trust deficit in the platform itself.
    *   *High Messages + Low "Verified Intent":* Tenants are unsure of their dates or testing the waters (pricing transparency issue).
    *   *High "Verified Intent" + Low Landlord Response:* Your algorithm is matching well, but landlord supply/trust is the bottleneck.

#### Implementation Plan for April 20 Review
To demonstrate PMF to investors, report on the **"Intent Conversion Rate"**:
*(Users with Verified Intent) / (Total Profiles Created)*

Currently, your raw messaging rate is ~7% (85/1,200). If only 40 of those 85 included a date/request, your true activation rate is 3.3%. This lower, sharper number is more honest and actionable for investors than inflating the metric with empty messages.

**Immediate Next Steps:**
1.  **Audit the 85 Messages:** Manually categorize them by "Specific Intent" (date/viewing) vs. "Generic."
2.  **Modify the Interface:** Require a "Preferred Move-in Date" field before the "Send Message" button becomes active. This forces the signal you need.
3.  **Landlord Feedback Loop:** Survey the landlords who received "Verified Intent" messages. Did they reply? If yes, your product works for the demand side; the friction is supply-side trust. If no, the pricing or listing quality is the issue.

This metric shifts the narrative from "we have low engagement" to "we have identified the precise moment value is created, and we are optimizing the funnel from 3.3% upward."

*Note: Ensure your data pipeline captures the content of the first message or the structured form data to automate this classification.*
*For the investor deck, pair this metric with the "Time to First Verified Intent" to show velocity.*
