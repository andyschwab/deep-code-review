# Measurement Strategy: Balancing Parking Efficiency and Commercial Vitality

## Executive Summary
Vienna SmartPark has successfully reduced search time by 41% (12 to 7 minutes). To address concerns that faster turnover reduces visit duration below the critical 45-minute threshold for retail conversion, we propose a privacy-preserving correlation study. This strategy links aggregated parking patterns with economic indicators without tracking individual identities, enabling the committee to decide on network expansion or pricing adjustments.

## Proposed Measurement Framework

### 1. Data Collection (Privacy-First Approach)
**Parking Data (Existing Sensors):**
- Aggregate session durations by hour/day (no license plates stored)
- Calculate percentage of sessions <45 minutes vs. ≥45 minutes
- Track turnover rate per block face

**Economic Indicators (Partner Data):**
- **Anonymous transaction aggregates**: Partner with local business associations to collect daily/weekly sales totals and transaction counts (no individual receipts)
- **Foot traffic proxies**: Use anonymized mobile network data or voluntary Wi-Fi counting to measure pedestrian volume patterns
- **Survey sampling**: Deploy brief, voluntary intercept surveys at parking exits (2-week sample period) asking: visit purpose, duration, spending range, and whether parking availability influenced the visit

### 2. Correlation Methodology
**Temporal Alignment:**
- Compare weekly parking duration distributions with weekly sales aggregates by zone
- Identify correlation between % of short stays (<45 min) and transaction volume/value trends
- Control for external factors: weather, day of week, seasonal tourism patterns, special events

**Segmented Analysis:**
- Separate high-turnover commercial streets from mixed-use residential zones
- Compare pre-sensor baseline (historical estimates) with post-sensor data
- Analyze peak vs. off-peak periods separately

### 3. Privacy Safeguards
- All data aggregated to minimum 10-session thresholds before analysis
- No linkage between specific parking sessions and specific transactions
- Survey data collected anonymously with explicit consent; results reported only in aggregate
- Compliance with GDPR and Austrian data protection standards verified by city legal team

### 4. Timeline for Committee Decision
- **Week 1 (by 30th)**: Present this framework, establish business association partnerships, deploy surveys
- **Weeks 2-5**: Collect minimum 4 weeks of correlated data (captures weekday/weekend variation)
- **Week 6**: Complete analysis, present findings with confidence intervals
- **Decision point**: Expand network, adjust pricing, or maintain current model

## Expected Trade-off Scenarios

| Scenario | Indicator | Recommended Action |
|----------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Efficiency without harm** | Short stays increase, but sales/transaction counts stable or rising | Expand sensor network; current model optimizes both flow and commerce |
| **Genuine conflict** | Short stays correlate with declining sales, especially in retail-heavy zones | Pilot dynamic pricing: lower rates for 45-90 min stays, higher for <30 min |
| **Mixed results** | Some zones show decline, others improvement | Zone-specific policies; targeted merchant support in affected areas |
| **Inconclusive** | No clear correlation after 4 weeks | Extend study period; invest in direct merchant transaction tracking partnerships |

## Key Metrics for Committee
1. **Search time reduction**: 5 minutes saved per driver (quantified benefit)
2. **Visit duration shift**: Change in % of sessions ≥45 minutes
3. **Economic correlation**: Relationship between duration distribution and sales trends
4. **Merchant sentiment**: Survey results on perceived impact

This approach provides evidence-based decision-making while respecting privacy, allowing Vienna to lead in smart city innovation that serves both mobility and local commerce.

*Note: Success requires early buy-in from business associations for data sharing. Survey design should be finalized before the 30th to enable immediate deployment.*
