# SwiftRide Referral Incentive Test: Q3 Performance & LTV Projection Report

## 1. Executive Summary
*   **Test Period:** August 1 – August 28 (4 weeks)
*   **Variant:** Double-sided $10 credit vs. Control $5 credit
*   **Primary Outcome:** Referral volume increased by 25%, but Cost Per Acquired Rider (CPAR) rose 33% ($18 → $24).
*   **Recommendation:** Do not scale globally yet. The hypothesis that volume would lower blended acquisition cost via organic spillover has not materialized within the observation window. Immediate action requires isolating fraud/gaming and projecting 90-day LTV to determine if the higher upfront cost is justified by long-term retention.

## 3. Forward-Looking Metrics & LTV Projection
Since the test window (4 weeks) is too short to observe full payback, the report must shift from *realized* ROI to *projected* LTV. Include these specific cohorts:

### A. Cohort Quality Comparison (Weeks 1-4)
*   **Activation Rate:** % of referred users who complete a first ride within 7 days. (Low activation suggests "gaming" for credits without intent to use).
*   **Repeat Rate (Day 30):** % of users completing a 2nd+ ride.
*   **Average Order Value (AOV):** Do $10-incentivized users book cheaper rides to maximize credit utility?

### B. Projected LTV Model (6-Month Horizon)
Construct a sensitivity analysis showing the required retention lift to justify the $24 CPAR:
*   **Scenario A (Status Quo):** If new user retention matches the control group, the $10 incentive results in a net loss of $[Calculated Amount] over 6 months.
*   **Scenario B (Required Lift):** To break even, the variant cohort must demonstrate a [X]% higher retention rate or [Y]% higher ride frequency than the control.
*   **Fraud Adjustment:** Deduct estimated losses from self-referrals (same device/IP) from the total volume before calculating LTV.

## 4. Strategic Recommendations
1.  **Implement Fraud Guards:** Before scaling, enforce stricter limits (e.g., device fingerprinting, phone number verification) to filter out users gaming the $10 credit. Re-calculate CPAR excluding flagged accounts.
2.  **Extend Observation Window:** Commit to a 90-day LTV review. The "spillover" effect may lag; users acquired in late August may not generate sufficient ride history until November.
3.  **Segmented Rollout:** If LTV projections are positive only for specific segments (e.g., new cities vs. mature markets), restrict the $10 offer to high-churn zones where volume is critical, rather than a global blanket increase.

**Decision Gate:** Approve continued spend only if the Day-45 retention rate of the variant cohort exceeds the control by at least 5%, or if fraud-adjusted CPAR drops below $20.
