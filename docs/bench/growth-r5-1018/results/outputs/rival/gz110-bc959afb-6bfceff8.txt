# Proposal: Redefining Activation for NeoVerse Gen Z Users

## Recommended New Activation Definition
**"Savings Commitment + Social Engagement"**
A user is activated when they complete **both** actions within 7 days of signup:
1. **Fund a savings pocket** (minimum €10)
2. **Complete one social transaction** (split bill, send money, or invite a friend)

This replaces the current "first transaction" metric.

## Rationale

Your current 40% activation rate is a vanity metric—60% dormancy proves it doesn't predict retention. The savings pocket early adopters (n=1,200) showing stickiness suggests **financial commitment** creates stronger habits than passive spending. However, for Gen Z, **social validation** is equally critical for habit formation.

Combining these signals captures users who:
- Have skin in the game (savings = commitment)
- Are embedded in your social ecosystem (splitting bills = network effects)
- Demonstrate intent to use NeoVerse as their primary financial tool

## Validation Data Needed (Before Wednesday)

Since you lack long-term data, gather these **leading indicators** from your existing 1,200 early adopters and a comparable control group:

### 1. Cohort Retention Comparison (Immediate)
- **30-day retention** for: savings-only users vs. social-only users vs. both vs. neither
- **Week 1, 2, 3, 4 activity rates** for each cohort
- This tells you which combination actually predicts stickiness

### 2. Behavioral Correlation Analysis
- Correlation between **days to first savings action** and 30-day retention
- Correlation between **number of social transactions in week 1** and 30-day retention
- Identify the **minimum threshold** where retention jumps (e.g., is €5 enough, or does it need €20?)

### 3. Qualitative Validation (Fast)
- Survey 50 activated users (both actions completed): "What made you keep using NeoVerse?"
- Survey 50 dormant users (first transaction only): "What stopped you from continuing?"
- Look for pattern differences in stated reasons

### 4. Predictive Power Test
- Calculate what your activation rate **would have been** over the past 3 months using the new definition
- Compare that rate's correlation with **Month 2 retention** vs. current definition's correlation
- The definition with higher predictive validity wins

## Implementation Timeline

**Next 30 Days:**
- Implement new tracking for both actions
- A/B test onboarding flows optimized for the new activation definition

**Success Metric:**
The new definition is valid if users meeting it show **<20% dormancy at 30 days** (vs. current 60%) and **higher LTV at 90 days**.

## Why Not Just Savings or Just Social?

- **Savings alone** may attract savers who never engage socially (limited network effects)
- **Social alone** may create activity without financial commitment (easy to churn)
- **Both together** signal a user who sees NeoVerse as their **primary financial hub**, not just a tool

---

*Note: If data shows savings alone has equal predictive power, simplify to just that metric—but test this explicitly before deciding. Do not assume; let the cohort retention data drive the final definition.*
