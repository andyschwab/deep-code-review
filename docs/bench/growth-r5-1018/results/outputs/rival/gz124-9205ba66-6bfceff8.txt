# GreenBalance Activation Framework

## Recommended Activation Definition
**Primary Criterion:** Purchase of ≥1 kg of carbon credits within **7 days** of signup.

This aligns with your core assumption while establishing a concrete timeframe to distinguish committed buyers from browsers.

## Hypothesis Testing Plan (Pre-Launch: Dec 1-31)

Since you cannot wait for January's marketing spend to validate assumptions, use your December launch window to test which behaviors predict purchase:

### Track These Three User States
| State | Definition | Hypothesis to Test |
|-------|------------|-------------------|
| **Browser** | Views ≥2 project pages, no calculation | Low purchase intent |
| **Engager** | Uses footprint calculator OR views ≥5 projects | Moderate purchase intent |
| **Activated** | Completes first purchase (≥1 kg) | High retention probability |

### Critical Metrics to Monitor in December
1. **Conversion Rate by State**: What % of Browsers vs. Engagers purchase within 7 days?
2. **Time-to-Purchase Median**: How many days between signup and first purchase for retained users?
3. **Retention Correlation**: Do users who purchase within 7 days return in Month 2 at higher rates than later purchasers?

## Proposed Thresholds for January Optimization

Based on typical marketplace patterns, consider these starting thresholds (validate with December data):

| Metric | Recommended Threshold | Rationale |
|--------|----------------------|-----------|
| **Activation Window** | 7 days from signup | Balances urgency with realistic decision time for carbon purchases |
| **Minimum Purchase** | 1 kg | Removes friction; your assumption states any purchase indicates commitment |
| **Engagement Signal** | Footprint calculator completion + 1 project view | If >15% of calculators convert vs. <5% of browsers, make calculator mandatory in onboarding |
| **Drop-off Alert** | No purchase within 14 days | Users crossing this threshold have <20% likelihood of ever purchasing (industry benchmark) |

## Decision Rules for Marketing Spend Allocation

**If December data shows:**
- **Calculator users convert 3x higher than browsers**: Restructure onboarding to require footprint calculation before browsing projects. Allocate 70% of January budget to campaigns driving calculator completion.
- **No significant difference between states**: Simplify onboarding. Remove friction points. Allocate budget to direct purchase incentives (e.g., "first 100g free").
- **Purchase within 3 days predicts 80%+ of Month-2 retention**: Tighten activation window to 3 days. Create urgency campaigns targeting day-4+ non-purchasers.

## Immediate Actions Before December 1st

1. **Instrument tracking** for: signup date, project views (count), calculator usage (yes/no), first purchase date/amount, second purchase date (for retention correlation).
2. **Set up cohort analysis** by signup week to detect if activation patterns shift as you scale from early adopters to broader audience.
3. **Define "retained"** now: Recommend "second purchase within 60 days" or "monthly purchase for 2 consecutive months."

## Output for CEO & Marketing Lead

**Activation = First purchase of ≥1 kg within 7 days of signup.**

**Success Metric for January:** 25-30% of signups achieve activation (600 of 2,000 expected buyers). If December data shows Engagers convert at 2x Browsers, revise onboarding flow before January spend. If no correlation emerges, test purchase incentives instead of onboarding changes.
