# EduMex FY Growth Plan: Sustainable Scaling Framework

## Executive Summary
Given your current constraints (5-person team, 48-hour support lag, 800 students), a 20% month-over-month (MoM) growth target is **unsustainable** and risks catastrophic churn and team burnout. At 20% MoM, you would reach 2,400 students in six months—a 3x increase your current team cannot support without external funding or automation. This plan shifts focus from "maximum acquisition" to "capacity-constrained growth" that preserves educational quality.

## Recommended Growth Targets

### Phase 1: Stabilization (Months 1-2)
- **Target:** 5-8% MoM enrollment growth
- **Focus:** Reduce support response time to <24 hours before accelerating acquisition
- **Rationale:** Your 48-hour response time indicates you're already at capacity. Growing faster now will increase churn, destroying the LTV of new students.

### Phase 2: Controlled Growth (Months 3-6)
- **Target:** 10-12% MoM enrollment growth
- **Prerequisite:** Hire 1-2 part-time support staff or implement student self-service tools
- **Maximum sustainable enrollment:** ~1,400 students by December (75% increase, not 300%)

### Phase 3: Partnership Scaling (Months 7-12)
- **Target:** 15% MoM **only** for business partnership channel
- **Condition:** Validate LTV of partnership students exceeds organic before scaling
- **Cap total enrollment** at 2,000 students until team expands to 7-8 FTE

## Operational Guardrails

**Do not exceed these ratios:**
- Students per full-time staff: **200:1** (you're currently at 160:1, approaching the limit)
- Support tickets per student per month: Track baseline; if it rises >15%, pause acquisition
- Churn rate threshold: If monthly churn exceeds 8%, freeze new enrollment until resolved

## Immediate Actions Required

1. **Calculate channel-specific LTV within 30 days**
   - Track 90-day retention and revenue for partnership vs. organic students
   - Do not scale partnerships until you confirm they don't churn faster

2. **Implement a waiting list** for organic signups
   - Cap monthly organic enrollments at 80-100 students until support improves
   - Use waitlist to gauge demand without overcommitting

3. **Hire before growing**
   - Add 1 part-time support role (20 hrs/week) before exceeding 1,000 students
   - Budget: ~$800-1,200 MXN/month; fund from current revenue, not projected growth

4. **Measure leading indicators weekly**
   - Support response time (target: <24 hrs)
   - Student satisfaction/NPS (target: >40)
   - Instructor workload hours (target: <45 hrs/week avg)

## Revised Projections (Conservative vs. Aggressive)

| Month | Conservative (8-10% MoM) | Aggressive (20% MoM) | Risk Level |
|-------|-------------------------|---------------------|------------|
| July | 864 students | 960 students | Low |
| August | 933 students | 1,152 students | Medium |
| September | 1,008 students | 1,382 students | **High** |
| December | 1,260 students | 2,389 students | **Critical** |

**Recommendation:** Adopt the conservative path. The aggressive path requires 3x support capacity you don't have and will likely increase churn to 15%+, negating revenue gains.

## Messaging for Advisory Board

"We are prioritizing **sustainable unit economics** over vanity growth metrics. Our constraint is not demand—it's delivery capacity. By capping growth at 10% MoM until Q3, we protect our NPS, reduce churn risk, and validate that partnership students have equal or higher LTV before scaling that channel. This approach ensures we remain profitable without external funding while building a reputation for quality that drives organic referrals long-term."

*Note: This plan assumes no major product changes or price increases. If you raise prices or improve retention, revisit targets quarterly.*
