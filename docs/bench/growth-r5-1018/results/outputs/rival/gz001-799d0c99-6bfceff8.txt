### Recommended Behavioral Signals for Activation

Given your team size and the specific context of rural telehealth in Kenya, avoid complex event tracking. Focus on these three high-signal, low-engineering-cost events:

1.  **`sms_checker_completed`**: Track when a user successfully receives an SMS diagnosis result after initiating a symptom check.
    *   *Why:* This validates your hypothesis that users skipping video calls are still engaging with core value. It distinguishes between users who abandon the app entirely and those who prefer low-bandwidth interactions.
    *   *Implementation:* Log this server-side when your SMS gateway confirms delivery. No frontend changes needed.

2.  **`prescription_viewed_or_saved`**: Track when a user opens the prescription PDF/image or forwards the prescription SMS.
    *   *Why:* "Downloading" might be too strict a definition if data costs are high or storage is limited. Viewing or saving the file indicates the user has received the tangible value of the consultation, even if they don't complete a technical "download" action.
    *   *Implementation:* Trigger on the server response that serves the prescription file or sends the prescription SMS.

3.  **`return_visit_booked`**: Track when a user schedules a follow-up consultation or purchases a repeat medication pack within 30 days of signup.
    *   *Why:* This is your ultimate validation of "activation." If users who skip the initial video call but use the SMS checker still book return visits, your current activation definition is too narrow.
    *   *Implementation:* Log upon successful payment confirmation or calendar booking creation.

### Validating the 48-Hour Window

Do not build a new dashboard. Perform a **one-time cohort analysis** using your existing database logs:

1.  **Extract Data:** Query all 1,450 users. For each, record the timestamp of signup and the timestamp of their first "value event" (video consult completion, prescription view, or SMS checker completion).
2.  **Calculate Time-to-Value:** Compute the hours elapsed between signup and that first event.
3.  **Plot Cumulative Percentage:** Create a simple spreadsheet column showing what percentage of activated users completed their value event by hour 12, 24, 48, 72, and 168 (1 week).
4.  **Identify the Knee:** If 85%+ of users who eventually activate do so within 48 hours, keep the window. If there is a significant jump (e.g., another 20% of users) between hour 48 and hour 72, your window is too short, likely penalizing users with connectivity issues or those needing time to gather funds.

**Recommendation Strategy:** Present the cumulative curve to your investors. If the curve flattens significantly after 48 hours, defend your current metric. If it rises steeply afterward, propose extending the activation window to 7 days and re-forecasting your LTV based on this broader definition.
