### Recommended Funnel Definition: The "Dual-Entry" Model

For a two-sided marketplace like DesignHub where friction exists on both supply (freelancers) and demand (clients) sides, defining the funnel starting at a single point (either job post or portfolio view) obscures the root cause. Instead, adopt a **Dual-Entry Funnel** that tracks two parallel streams converging at the "Hire" event. This aligns your teams by separating **Demand Activation** from **Supply Response**.

**Stage 1: Demand Activation (Client Side)**
*   **Entry Point:** Client views first portfolio OR searches for a skill.
*   **Conversion Event:** Job Posted.
*   **Metric:** *Job Posting Rate* = (Jobs Posted / Active Browsers).
*   **Insight:** If this rate is low, the bottleneck is trust or clarity in value proposition, not proposal quality.

**Stage 2: Supply Response (Marketplace Side)**
*   **Entry Point:** Job Posted.
*   **Conversion Event:** Job receives ≥3 qualified proposals within 24 hours.
*   **Metric:** *Liquidity Rate* = (Jobs with ≥3 Proposals / Total Jobs Posted).
*   **Insight:** If this rate is low, your assumption about "proposal quality" may be wrong; the issue is likely freelancer visibility or incentive to bid.

**Stage 3: Selection (Decision Side)**
*   **Entry Point:** Job receives ≥3 qualified proposals.
*   **Conversion Event:** Freelancer Hired.
*   **Metric:** *Selection Rate* = (Hires / Jobs with ≥3 Proposals).
*   **Insight:** Only if this rate is low is "proposal quality" or "client indecision" the actual bottleneck.

**Current Data Application:**
Your current 20% overall conversion (800 hired / 4,000 posted) aggregates these distinct problems.
*   If only 2,000 of your 4,000 jobs receive bids, your *Liquidity Rate* is 50%.
*   If 1,600 of those 2,000 result in hires, your *Selection Rate* is 80%.
*   **Conclusion:** The bottleneck is **Supply Response** (freelancers not bidding), not proposal quality. Clients aren't rejecting bad proposals; they aren't receiving any.

### How to Measure Drop-Off Rates

To implement this before your funding round, configure your analytics (GA4/Mixpanel) to track these specific transitions:

1.  **Calculate Cohort-Based Rates:** Do not divide monthly totals. Track jobs posted in Week 1 and measure proposals received within 48 hours of *that specific post*.
2.  **Define "Qualified Proposal":** Filter out spam by requiring a minimum character count or attachment in the event property `proposal_submitted`.
3.  **Visualize the Split:** Create a dashboard with two distinct funnels.
    *   *Funnel A:* View Portfolio → Search → Post Job.
    *   *Funnel B:* Post Job → Receive Bid → Interview → Hire.
4.  **Segment by Job Type:** High-value jobs may have high drop-off at the Supply stage (fewer qualified freelancers), while low-value jobs may drop off at Selection (too many low-quality bids).

**Note:** Ensure your event tracking captures the timestamp difference between `job_posted` and `first_proposal_received` to validate the 24-hour liquidity window.
**Note:** Align Marketing KPIs to Funnel A (driving posts) and Product KPIs to Funnel B (improving matching/bidding mechanics).
