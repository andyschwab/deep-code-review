# GlobalDAO Governance Health Framework

To move beyond raw participation and assess true governance health, GlobalDAO should adopt a balanced scorecard measuring **Engagement Quality**, **Decision Efficiency**, and **Treasury Impact**. This framework distinguishes between "activity" (votes cast) and "health" (sustainable, value-adding decisions).

### 1. Engagement Quality Metrics
*Goal: Ensure increased participation reflects informed consensus, not spam or fatigue.*

*   **Valid Proposal Ratio:** The percentage of submitted proposals that reach a quorum and pass vs. those rejected or withdrawn due to low quality/spam.
    *   *Target:* Stabilize or increase this ratio even as total submissions rise.
*   **Voter Retention Rate:** The percentage of unique token holders who vote in consecutive cycles.
    *   *Why:* A spike in one-off voters followed by a drop indicates fatigue or confusion, not healthy engagement.
*   **Delegation Stability:** The net change in delegated voting power.
    *   *Why:* If active delegators are revoking delegation due to noise/spam, the tool is eroding trust.
*   **Discussion-to-Vote Ratio:** The volume of substantive forum/discord comments per proposal relative to the final vote count.
    *   *Why:* High votes with zero discussion often signal bot activity or blind following; healthy governance requires deliberation.

### 2. Decision Efficiency Metrics
*Goal: Measure how effectively the DAO converts proposals into executed outcomes.*

*   **Proposal Lifecycle Time:** The average duration from submission to final execution (or rejection).
    *   *Why:* If spam clogs the queue, high-quality proposals face delays. A rising average time signals process friction.
*   **Quorum Achievement Rate:** The percentage of proposals that successfully reach the required quorum.
    *   *Why:* If spam dilutes attention, critical proposals may fail to reach quorum, indicating a broken signal-to-noise ratio.
*   **Reversal Rate:** The frequency with which a passed proposal requires a subsequent "fix-it" proposal or emergency patch within 30 days.
    *   *Why:* High reversal rates indicate poor vetting or rushed decision-making despite high participation.

### 3. Treasury & Impact Metrics (Proxy for Quality)
*Goal: Approximate the long-term value of decisions without complex longitudinal studies.*

*   **Treasury Efficiency Ratio:** The amount of treasury funds allocated per *successful* (executed) proposal vs. total funds requested.
    *   *Why:* Tracks if funds are being deployed effectively or locked up in failed/spam initiatives.
*   **Execution Completion Rate:** The percentage of passed proposals that are fully implemented by contributors within the promised timeline.
    *   *Why:* A vote is only as good as its execution. Low completion rates suggest the DAO is over-promising or under-resourcing.
*   **Sentiment Delta:** A qualitative pulse check (via snapshot polls or sentiment analysis on governance channels) before and after major voting cycles.
    *   *Why:* Captures the "vibe" and trust levels of the community, which often precedes quantitative drops in participation.

### Implementation Strategy for Core Contributors
1.  **Establish Baselines:** Calculate current averages for these metrics using the last 3 months of data prior to the tool launch.
2.  **Set Thresholds:** Define "healthy" ranges (e.g., Valid Proposal Ratio > 60%, Execution Completion > 80%).
3.  **Quarterly Reviews:** Report these metrics alongside raw participation numbers to token holders to demonstrate that the tool is driving *quality* governance, not just noise.

By tracking these dimensions, GlobalDAO can detect if the new tool is attracting genuine stakeholders or merely amplifying noise, ensuring the treasury and protocol health remain the primary focus.

*Note: Consider implementing a lightweight "reputation" tag for proposers based on historical execution success to naturally filter low-quality submissions over time.*
