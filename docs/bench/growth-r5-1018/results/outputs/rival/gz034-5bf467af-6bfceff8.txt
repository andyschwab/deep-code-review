# SkillForge Engagement Diagnostic Framework

## 1. Critical Action Sequence to Measure
To pinpoint exact drop-off, track this specific funnel for every learner, segmented by team and role:

1.  **Login Event:** Timestamp and device type.
2.  **Module Selection:** Time elapsed between login and clicking "Start."
3.  **Content Consumption:**
    *   *Video:* Seconds watched vs. total duration (identify exact second of exit).
    *   *Text/Interactive:* Scroll depth (%) and time-on-page.
4.  **Interaction Attempt:** Clicks on quizzes, downloads, or discussion prompts.
5.  **Completion Signal:** "Mark as Complete" click or final quiz submission.
6.  **Post-Module Action:** Immediate logout vs. navigation to next module vs. idle timeout.

**Key Diagnostic Metric:** Calculate the "Time-to-Drop" average. If users leave within 2 minutes of starting, it is likely **usability** or **relevance**. If they leave halfway through, it is likely **content fatigue** or **lack of interaction**.

## 2. Hypotheses for Three-Week Testing Cycle

**Week 1: Relevance & Onboarding**
*   **H1 (Role Mismatch):** Learners drop off because the default learning path does not match their specific job function.
    *   *Test:* Manually segment 5 teams into role-specific paths; compare completion rates against the control group.
*   **H2 (Value Prop Clarity):** The module introduction fails to articulate "What's in it for me?" for remote workers.
    *   *Test:* A/B test module intro screens: one featuring peer success stories vs. one featuring manager endorsement videos.

**Week 2: Interaction & Social Proof**
*   **H3 (Isolation Effect):** Lack of visible peer activity reduces motivation to continue.
    *   *Test:* Enable a "Live Activity" feed showing anonymized peers currently learning; measure impact on session duration.
*   **H4 (Synchronous Deficit):** Asynchronous content feels isolating without scheduled touchpoints.
    *   *Test:* Pilot a "Cohort Start" for 3 teams where everyone begins Module 1 on the same day with a scheduled Slack discussion thread.

**Week 3: Notification & Usability**
*   **H5 (Notification Fatigue):** Users ignore generic "Keep Learning" emails.
    *   *Test:* Switch 5 teams to "Trigger-Based" notifications (e.g., "Your peer X just finished this module") vs. standard weekly digests.
*   **H6 (Friction Point):** A specific UI element (e.g., complex quiz login) causes abandonment.
    *   *Test:* Heatmap analysis on Module 1 exit pages; remove one friction point for a test group.

## 3. Unknown Variables: Manager Involvement
Manager behavior is likely the hidden variable influencing the 34% baseline. Investigate:

*   **The "Assignment" Gap:** Do managers formally assign courses with deadlines, or merely suggest them? (Assigned tasks usually have 2x completion rates).
*   **Review Rituals:** Is course completion discussed in 1:1s? If learners perceive no accountability, priority drops.
*   **Modeling Behavior:** Are the managers themselves completing the training? Remote teams often mirror leader behavior.
*   **Resource Protection:** Do managers block "learning time" on calendars, or expect training to happen during "spare" time?
