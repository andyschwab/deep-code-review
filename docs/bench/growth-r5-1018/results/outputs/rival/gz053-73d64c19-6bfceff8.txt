# StreamFlow Interactive Ad Impact Investigation Plan

## Executive Summary
Session duration dropped 25% (55m → 41m) post-February 10th launch of 15-minute interactive ads. We must distinguish between **content skipping** (users staying but watching less per session) and **churn** (users leaving the platform entirely) to determine if ad revenue lift offsets viewing loss.

## Phase 1: Segment Exit Behavior (Days 1-2)

**Analyze exit points relative to ad breaks:**
- Calculate % of sessions ending within 2 minutes *before* a scheduled 15-minute ad break vs. within 2 minutes *after* an ad displays
- Compare to pre-February 10th baseline for the same time-of-day patterns
- **Key metric:** Ad-triggered exit rate = (exits within 2 min post-ad) / (total ads served)

**Interpretation:**
- High post-ad exit rate → Users are leaving *because* of the ad experience
- Uniform exit distribution → Drop may be due to content choices, not ads

## Phase 2: Distinguish Skipping vs. Churn (Days 2-4)

**Cohort analysis of February 10th+ users:**
1. **Same-day return rate:** Of users who exited after an ad, what % returned within 24 hours on any device?
2. **7-day retention:** Compare pre/post cohorts for Day-7 return rates
3. **Subscription status:** Cross-reference exited users with cancellation data (if available) or payment failures

**Metrics to compute:**
- **Skip behavior:** Users with multiple short sessions (<15 min) in one day are likely skipping content to avoid ads
- **True churn:** Users with zero sessions for 7+ days post-exit
- **Device hopping:** Users exiting on one device but returning on another within 48 hours (indicates frustration, not churn)

## Phase 4: Content Acquisition Impact (Day 5)

**Licensing cost efficiency:**
- Calculate cost-per-view before and after (licensing costs / total viewing minutes)
- If viewing minutes dropped 25% but licensing costs are fixed, your cost-per-view increased 33%
- Present this to content team: are we devaluing our content library by reducing exposure?

## Recommended Immediate Actions

1. **Create a control group:** If technically feasible, show the old ad format to 10% of users to isolate the interactive format's effect
2. **Survey exited users:** Trigger a one-question survey at exit: "What's the main reason you're stopping?" with options including "Too many ads" vs. "Finished watching"
3. **Test frequency:** Run a quick A/B test with ads every 20 minutes vs. 15 minutes to find the inflection point
4. **Monitor subscription cancellations:** Set up daily alerts for cancellation spikes correlated with ad exposure

## Decision Framework for Leadership

**Continue current format if:**
- Revenue per user increased >15% (compensating for engagement loss)
- 7-day retention unchanged
- Cost-per-view remains stable

**Adjust frequency if:**
- Revenue flat or down
- Post-ad exit rate >2x pre-ad exit rate
- Churn cohort shows 20%+ increase in cancellations

**Revert format if:**
- Both revenue and retention declined
- Users are device-hopping to avoid ads (indicates severe frustration)

---
*Analysis requires only your existing ad click, exit point, and session data. Subscription cancellation data would strengthen conclusions but is not essential for initial diagnosis.*
