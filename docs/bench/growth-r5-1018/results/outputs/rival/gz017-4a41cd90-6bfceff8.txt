# PayForward Brazil Beta: Core Metric Framework (Days 1–90)

**Objective:** Validate market fit and achieve 10,000 active users by June 8th.
**Primary Hypothesis to Test:** Local payment integration speed is the primary driver of adoption.

## 1. Acquisition Metrics (Top of Funnel)
*Focus: Efficiency of spend and channel relevance in a new market.*

| KPI | Definition | Why It Matters for Brazil Entry |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Install-to-Open Rate** | % of app installs that result in a first open. | Validates if marketing messaging matches the app store presence; critical for paid user acquisition efficiency. |
| **CAC by Channel** | Total spend / New users per channel (e.g., Meta, Google, Local Influencers). | Identifies which local channels drive volume vs. quality before scaling spend. |
| **Signup Initiation Rate** | % of opens that start the KYC/Onboarding flow. | Measures initial trust and appeal of the value proposition immediately upon landing. |

## 2. Activation Metrics (The "Speed" Hypothesis)
*Focus: Proving or disproving that integration speed drives conversion.*

| KPI | Definition | Why It Matters for Brazil Entry |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Onboarding Completion Rate** | % who start KYC vs. % who fund wallet. | The primary conversion metric. A drop-off here indicates friction in local ID verification or bank linking. |
| **Time-to-First-Transaction (TTF)** | Median time from app open to first successful payment. | **Critical Hypothesis Validator.** If TTF is high, your core assumption about "speed" is failing regardless of feature set. |
| **Funding Success Rate** | % of attempted wallet funding that succeeds. | Isolates technical failures in local payment rails (PIX, boleto) from user hesitation. |
| **Onboarding Drop-off Point** | Specific step where users abandon the flow. | Pinpoints exactly which local compliance or UI step creates friction compared to competitors like Nubank or PicPay. |

## 3. Early Retention Metrics (Stickiness)
*Focus: Determining if users return after the initial novelty.*

| KPI | Definition | Why It Matters for Brazil Entry |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Day-1 & Day-7 Retention** | % of users returning to app 1 and 7 days after signup. | Early signal of product-market fit. Low D1 suggests the first experience failed to deliver value. |
| **Repeat Transaction Rate** | % of users making a 2nd transaction within 14 days. | Confirms the wallet is being used as a habitual tool, not just a one-off experiment. |
| **Active User Ratio** | DAU / MAU. | Measures overall engagement health as you approach the 10k goal. |

## Board Dashboard Summary View
*Report weekly to track progress against the 90-day goal.*

*   **Goal Progress:** Current Active Users vs. 10,000 Target (Trajectory line).
*   **Hypothesis Health:** Median Time-to-First-Transaction (Target: < 3 minutes).
*   **Funnel Health:** Signup-to-Funded Conversion Rate (Benchmark target: Establish baseline Week 1, improve by 20% by Week 6).
*   **Retention Health:** Day-7 Retention Rate (Target: > 25% for fintech beta).

## Implementation Notes
1.  **Event Naming:** Ensure all events (e.g., `kyc_started`, `pix_payment_completed`) include a `region: BR` property to isolate this data from other LATAM markets immediately.
2.  **Cohort Analysis:** Segment all retention metrics by "Signup Week" to see if product iterations are actually improving performance over time.
