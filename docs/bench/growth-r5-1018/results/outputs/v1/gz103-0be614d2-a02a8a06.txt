**Recommendation: Prioritize Monthly Retention as the Primary North Star**

For FreshBox Meals, **Monthly Retention** must be your North Star for Q3, not Referral Conversion. In a subscription model like meal kits, retention is the primary driver of Lifetime Value (LTV). Your current 62% retention rate implies a significant leak; improving this by even 5 percentage points compounds value across the entire existing base, whereas referrals only impact the top of the funnel. Pouring acquisition spend (even via referrals) into a bucket with a 38% monthly churn rate is economically inefficient. The assumption that retention yields higher LTV is mathematically sound for subscriptions, but your specific cost structure requires the modeling below to confirm the magnitude.

**Missing Data & Modeling Requirements**
To provide the definitive financial proof your VP needs, you must compute the following using your internal data. Current gaps prevent a precise "break-even" decision:

1.  **Extended Attribution Window:** Your 7-day window is insufficient for referral tracking. Meal kit decisions often involve household discussion or trial periods extending beyond one week. You need to re-query referral attributions over a **30-day window** to capture the true 8% baseline. The current data likely undercounts referral efficacy, skewing the comparison.
2.  **Cohort-Based LTV vs. CAC:** You lack a direct comparison of *Marginal LTV* gained from a 1% retention lift versus *Marginal LTV* from a 1% referral lift.
    *   *Action:* Calculate the net revenue of a customer retained for an extra month versus the net revenue of a referred customer (accounting for the $20 credit cost).
    *   *Formula:* `(Avg Monthly Revenue × Gross Margin %) × (Retention Lift %)` vs. `(Avg Monthly Revenue × Gross Margin %) - $20 Credit`.
3.  **Churn Reason Taxonomy:** To move retention from 62%, you need to know *why* users leave. Instrument events for "Skip Week," "Pause Subscription," and "Cancel," tagged with exit reasons (price, recipe variety, logistics). Without this, you cannot target the retention fix.
