### WriteWise Q1 Growth Measurement Plan

**1. North Star Metric: Weekly Active Collaborating Teams (WACT)**
*Definition:* The count of unique companies where ≥2 distinct users each generate ≥1 document within a 7-day window.
*Why:* This rejects "total documents" (vanity) and "total signups" (vanity). It directly measures the core value hypothesis: that collaboration drives retention and conversion. If this number rises, revenue potential rises.
*Input Metrics:*
*   **Invites Sent per Active Company:** Measures the viral loop initiation.
*   **Activation Rate of Invited Users:** % of invited colleagues who generate a document within 48 hours.

**2. The Funnel (Read Bottom-Up)**
Focus engineering only on these three conversion points. Do not instrument intermediate clicks.
*   **Retention (The Leak Check):** % of WACT companies still active in Week 4.
    *   *Interpretation:* If this is low, do not spend on acquisition. The product isn't sticking even for collaborators. Fix the core workflow first.
*   **Activation:** % of new trials where a second user joins and creates a document before Day 7.
    *   *Interpretation:* This validates your "team collaboration" hypothesis. If this rate is <20%, the onboarding flow for invites is broken.
*   **Conversion:** % of active trials upgrading to paid.
    *   *Interpretation:* Compare conversion rates between "Solo Active" vs. "Collaborating Active" cohorts. If Collaborating converts >2x higher, your investor narrative shifts entirely to "Network Effects."

**3. Event Taxonomy (Engineer Load: <3 Days)**
Instrument only these four events. Every event must include `company_id` (stable pseudonymous ID) and `user_id`.
*   `document_generated`: Properties: `{document_type, word_count}`. Answers: "Is core value being delivered?"
*   `invite_sent`: Properties: `{channel, role_of_invitee}`. Answers: "Are users trying to collaborate?"
*   `invite_accepted`: Properties: `{time_to_accept}`. Answers: "Is the invitation frictionless?"
*   `trial_upgraded`: Properties: `{plan_tier, payment_method}`. Answers: "Did behavior translate to revenue?"

**4. Interpretation Guide for Investor Update**
*   **Scenario A (Collaboration Wins):** WACT grows 10%, but solo usage flatlines. Conversion for WACT is 15% vs 2% for solo.
    *   *Narrative:* "WriteWise is a team platform, not a single-user tool. We are optimizing for network density."
*   **Scenario B (Productivity Wins):** Total documents rise, but WACT stays flat. Conversion correlates with high word-count per user, not invites.
    *   *Narrative:* "Individual productivity is the primary driver; collaboration is a secondary feature."
*   **Scenario C (Leaky Bucket):** Both metrics rise, but Week 4 retention is <10%.
    *   *Narrative:* "We have validated interest but not long-term value. Next quarter focuses exclusively on retention mechanics before scaling acquisition."

**5. This-Week Steps**
1.  **Engineering:** Implement the four events above with `company_id` linkage. Verify data flow by Wednesday.
2.  **Analysis:** Segment last month's 12 converters by "Solo" vs. "Team" activity. Calculate the exact conversion lift of having a second user.
3.  **Decision:** If "Team" conversion is >2x "Solo," commit Q2 roadmap to collaborative features (comments, shared folders) and pause individual productivity tweaks.

*Note: All benchmarks above are derived from your own baseline trends; no external industry averages are used to avoid false positives. Ensure `company_id` remains pseudonymous to align with privacy compliance standards.*
