### Growth Measurement Plan: EduNaija Offline Homework Feature

**North Star Metric**
**Weekly Active Completers**: The count of unique students who successfully submit at least one assignment per week.
*Why:* Downloads are a vanity metric in an offline-first context (80% download vs. 40% submit in your pilot proves this). Value is only delivered when learning is demonstrated via submission, not when bytes are transferred.

**Input Metrics (The Funnel)**
Track these three specific events to distinguish technical barriers from engagement gaps:
1.  `content_download_attempted` (Properties: `content_id`, `file_size_mb`, `network_state_at_start`)
    *   *Question:* Are students trying to get the work?
2.  `content_download_completed` (Properties: `content_id`, `duration_seconds`, `error_code_if_failed`)
    *   *Question:* Is the offline sync working technically? (Gap between Attempted and Completed = Technical Barrier).
3.  `assignment_submitted` (Properties: `content_id`, `time_since_download_hours`, `device_is_shared_flag`)
    *   *Question:* Did the download lead to learning? (Gap between Completed and Submitted = Engagement/Workflow Barrier).

**Handling Shared Device & Low Frequency Constraints**
Do not force a daily active user (DAU) model on a weekly reality.
*   **Identity:** Use a stable, pseudonymous `student_id` persisted locally on the device, not a device ID. This allows you to track the same student even if they log in on different shared tablets, provided they log in with credentials. If credentials aren't used, track by `school_id` + `class_id` cohorts rather than individuals.
*   **Cadence:** Measure **Weekly Retention Cohorts**. Group students by the week of their first download. Measure if they return to *submit* in Week 2, Week 3, etc. A student accessing once a week is "retained" if they complete a task every 7–10 days, not if they open the app daily.

**Administrator & Investor Reporting View**
*   **Technical Health Ratio:** `(content_download_completed / content_download_attempted)`. If this drops below 90%, the issue is connectivity/sync, not student interest.
*   **Activation Rate:** `(assignment_submitted / content_download_completed)`. Your pilot shows this is currently 50% (40% submit / 80% download). This is your primary lever for product improvement.
*   **Weekly Completion Trend:** A line chart of unique submitters per week, overlaid with school term dates.

**This Week's Steps (Before March 20th Rollout)**
1.  **Instrument the Gap:** Implement the three events above immediately. Specifically, capture `error_code` on failed downloads to quantify exactly how much of the 40% drop-off is due to sync failures versus students simply not opening the file.
2.  **Define the "Shared" Cohort:** Tag any device with >3 distinct `student_id` logins in a week as "Shared." Analyze if the `assignment_submitted` rate differs significantly on Shared vs. Personal devices. If Shared devices have lower completion, the barrier is access time, not content quality.
3.  **Set the Guardrail:** Monitor `app_crash_rate` during the sync process. If the new offline logic increases crashes, pause the rollout regardless of download numbers.

*Note: All metrics must be compared against your pilot baseline (50% activation rate); do not compare to industry benchmarks which do not account for your specific connectivity constraints. Ensure all `student_id` handling complies with local data privacy regulations before logging.*
