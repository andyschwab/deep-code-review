**Deliverable: Conversion Definition & Measurement Framework for LuxResale Board**

**Primary North Star Metric: "Verified High-Value Settlement"**
Define conversion strictly as **Final Shipment Confirmation** for transactions >€10,000.
*   **Rationale:** In a consignment model, the business only realizes revenue and margin once the item ships. An initial deposit is a leading indicator of intent, but it carries cancellation risk and does not represent delivered customer value. Since your goal is to justify acquisition spend based on long-term value, measuring the completed loop (shipment) prevents optimizing for "depositors" who never close. This aligns the metric directly with cash flow and actual marketplace liquidity.

**Secondary Input Metrics (The Funnel)**
Monitor these specific stages to diagnose where value leaks occur, rather than tracking vanity volume:
1.  **Deposit-to-Ship Rate:** The percentage of users who pay the deposit and complete the purchase. A low rate indicates friction in the final payment step or buyer's remorse specific to high-ticket items.
2.  **Wishlist-to-Deposit Ratio:** Measures the quality of "intent." If this is high but Deposit-to-Ship is low, the wishlist feature works, but the checkout/consignment process fails. If this is low, the wishlist is a "window shopping" feature, not a conversion signal.
3.  **High-Value Retention Cohort:** The percentage of users who complete a >€10k purchase and return to buy (or sell) again within 6 months. This validates the "long-term value" hypothesis for the board.

**Board Narrative & Acquisition Justification**
Present the data as: *"We acquired 8,000 visitors to generate 45 Verified Settlements (0.56% conversion). Our acquisition spend is justified not by traffic volume, but by the Lifetime Value (LTV) of these 45 settlers. We will optimize the Deposit-to-Ship rate to increase yield from existing traffic before scaling acquisition."*

**Immediate Next Steps**
1.  **Instrument the "Deposit Abandonment" Event:** Tag every user who pays a deposit but does not ship within 7 days with the specific reason code (e.g., "payment failed," "user cancelled") to quantify the leak.
2.  **Run a Cohort Analysis:** Segment the 45 buyers by acquisition source. If one channel drives 20% of the traffic but 60% of the shipments, reallocate the next quarter's budget there immediately.

*Note: Do not count wishlist additions as conversions; they are engagement signals only. Ensure your analytics tool distinguishes between "deposit paid" and "shipment confirmed" as separate events to calculate the drop-off rate accurately.*
