### Executive Steering Committee Report: KapaMarket Launch Metrics
**Date:** November 12th | **Focus:** Trust Hypothesis Validation & Retention Drivers

To validate your core assumption that trust drives retention more than price, and to avoid vanity metrics like raw waitlist size or pageviews, monitor these three actionable metrics daily. These directly measure the "health" of the marketplace and the specific trust mechanisms you are testing.

#### 1. Core Action Retention Rate (Week 1 Cohort)
*   **Definition:** The percentage of users who complete a **second transaction** within 30 days of their first successful purchase.
*   **Why it matters:** This is your true North Star input. If your trust hypothesis holds, users who have a verified, high-quality first experience will return. Raw transaction volume is vanity; repeat behavior proves value delivery.
*   **Link to Expansion:** Sustainable growth in Johannesburg and Cape Town depends on unit economics where Lifetime Value (LTV) exceeds acquisition cost. High repeat rates lower the burden on your 15,000-user waitlist.
*   **Data to Capture Immediately:** `user_id` (pseudonymous), `transaction_id`, `timestamp`, `is_repeat_buyer` (boolean), `days_since_first_transaction`.

#### 2. Seller Verification Completion Rate (Pre-Transaction)
*   **Definition:** The percentage of listed items that successfully pass your seller verification check *before* being marked "Available for Sale."
*   **Why it matters:** You asked whether to track verification frequency or dispute resolution. **Prevention beats cure.** Tracking dispute resolutions is a lagging indicator of failure; tracking verification completion is a leading indicator of supply quality. If this rate drops, your inventory quality degrades, directly threatening the trust hypothesis before a buyer even sees the item.
*   **Link to Expansion:** As you scale to two cities, maintaining consistent device condition standards is critical. A drop here signals operational friction in onboarding sellers in new regions.
*   **Data to Capture Immediately:** `seller_id`, `listing_id`, `verification_start_time`, `verification_status` (passed/failed/pending), `failure_reason_code`.

#### 3. Trust-Adjusted Conversion Rate
*   **Definition:** The conversion rate (View → Purchase) segmented specifically by listings that display the "Verified Condition" badge versus those that do not (if any exist during rollout) or by seller verification tier.
*   **Why it matters:** This isolates the variable of "trust." If items with verified status convert significantly higher than the baseline, you have quantitative proof that trust drives purchases more than price alone.
*   **Link to Expansion:** This metric justifies the operational cost of your verification team. If the lift is negligible, your assumption is flawed, and you must pivot to price competitiveness immediately.
*   **Data to Capture Immediately:** `session_id`, `listing_viewed_id`, `badge_displayed` (boolean), `purchase_made` (boolean), `listing_price`, `device_category`.

### Immediate Instrumentation Requirements
To validate the hypothesis from Day 1, your event taxonomy must capture the following properties on every relevant event:
1.  **Stable User Identifier:** A pseudonymous ID to link sessions across devices and time (essential for retention cohorts).
2.  **Trust Signal Exposure:** A boolean flag on every product view indicating if the "Verified" badge was visible.
3.  **Verification Latency:** Time elapsed between listing creation and verification completion.

**Recommendation on Your Specific Question:** Do not prioritize "dispute resolution rate" as a primary daily metric yet; it is too rare in the first 30 days (targeting 500 transactions) to yield statistical significance. Prioritize **Seller Verification Completion Rate**. It is a high-frequency signal that directly controls the quality of inventory, which is the prerequisite for the trust you aim to sell. Disputes are a failure state; verification is the control mechanism.

**Next Step:** Ensure your analytics pipeline ingests these three specific events with the listed properties by launch day. Any metric without this underlying instrumentation is `UNVERIFIED` and should not be presented to the committee.
