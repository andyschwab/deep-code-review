**Interpretation of Conflicting Signals**
The 2% lift in checkout completion paired with a 5% drop in product page views indicates a **funnel distortion**, not a uniform confidence boost. Two likely scenarios explain this:
1.  **The "Clutter" Hypothesis:** The badge adds visual noise, causing casual browsers to bounce earlier (fewer page views), but those who remain are higher-intent users who convert at a higher rate. This artificially inflates conversion rate while shrinking total revenue potential.
2.  **The "Segmentation" Hypothesis:** The badge negatively impacts a specific user cohort (e.g., price-sensitive buyers or users from specific EU regions skeptical of data sharing) who stop browsing, while trust-focused buyers convert faster. Aggregated data hides this leakage.

**Required Data Slices & Immediate Actions**
To resolve this before Friday, request the following cuts from your analytics team immediately. Do not invent benchmarks; compare these slices against their own control group performance.

1.  **Segment by User Geography & Device:**
    *   *Query:* Split conversion lift and page view drop by buyer country (e.g., Germany vs. France) and device (Mobile vs. Desktop).
    *   *Decision Rule:* If the page view drop is isolated to Mobile, the issue is UI clutter on small screens. **Action:** Redesign badge placement for mobile only. If the drop is uniform across high-value markets, the trust signal may be triggering privacy concerns.

2.  **Segment by Seller Tier:**
    *   *Query:* Compare metrics for buyers viewing "New/Unverified" sellers vs. "Established" sellers.
    *   *Decision Rule:* If page views drop only for new sellers, the badge might inadvertently highlight their "newness" rather than their "verification," scaring off risk-tolerant bargain hunters. **Action:** Test badge visibility thresholds (e.g., show only after 5 sales).

3.  **Funnel Velocity Analysis:**
    *   *Query:* Measure time-on-page and clicks-per-session for the treatment group.
    *   *Decision Rule:* If time-on-page decreased significantly alongside page views, users are scanning less. If conversion rose *despite* less browsing, the badge is acting as a heuristic shortcut. **Action:** Calculate net revenue impact. If fewer views × higher conversion = lower total orders, the feature reduces top-line growth.
