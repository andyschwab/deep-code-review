### Governance Health Framework for GlobalDAO

**North Star Metric: Value-Adjusted Participation Rate**
Reject raw participation (12%) as a vanity metric; it rises even when spam increases. Instead, measure the percentage of active token holders voting on **high-signal proposals** per quarter. This rises only when genuine engagement increases, not when noise floods the queue.

**Input Metrics (The Funnel)**
Track these three inputs to move the North Star, reading from retention/quality upward:
1.  **Proposal Signal-to-Noise Ratio:** Count of proposals passing a minimum discussion threshold (e.g., >5 unique commenters, >24h debate) divided by total submissions. This diagnoses the spam leak immediately.
2.  **Voter Retention on Core Issues:** Percentage of voters who return to vote on *subsequent* high-signal proposals within 60 days. If this drops while raw votes rise, you have voter fatigue driven by low-quality noise.
3.  **Delegation Stability:** Net change in delegated voting power to active representatives. In asynchronous governance, stable or growing delegation indicates trust; rapid churn suggests confusion or fatigue.

**Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only What Answers a Question)**
Do not instrument every click. Define these specific events with properties:
*   `proposal_submitted`: Properties must include `author_reputation_score` and `initial_discussion_window_open`. *Question: Are spammers targeting low-reputation accounts?*
*   `vote_cast`: Properties must include `proposal_category` (e.g., "treasury", "protocol upgrade", "spam") and `voter_tenure`. *Question: Is participation rising only in low-stakes categories?*
*   `proposal_outcome_executed`: Properties must include `treasury_impact_estimate`. *Question: Did passed proposals actually move treasury value?* (Mark as `UNVERIFIED` until you define a manual review process for impact).

**Stage-Based Instrumentation**
You are in the **MVP** stage for this tool. Stop measuring raw pageviews or total vote counts. Focus strictly on the **Signal-to-Noise Ratio** and **Voter Retention**. If you cannot objectively score proposal quality yet, institute a manual "Core Contributor Review" tag for the first 50 proposals to establish a baseline classification. Do not fabricate a quality score; label unreviewed proposals as `UNVERIFIED` in your dashboard until a human tags them.

**Experiment Rigor: The Spam Filter Test**
Before rolling out global filters, run a controlled test on a subset of proposal categories.
*   **Pre-commitment:** Define a fixed duration (e.g., 30 days) and a primary metric (Signal-to-Noise Ratio). Do not peek at day 5 to declare victory if spam drops; early reads are `UNVERIFIED` due to natural variance in submission timing.
*   **Guardrail:** Monitor `Voter Retention`. If spam drops but legitimate voters also stop returning (false positives blocking good proposals), the test fails regardless of noise reduction.
*   **Decision Rule:** If the Signal-to-Noise Ratio improves by >20% without depressing the Guardrail, ship the filter globally. If the Guardrail dips, extend the test or revert.

3.  **Set the Tripwire:** Establish a rule: If `Voter Retention` drops below the pre-tool baseline (5% active retention) for two consecutive cycles, automatically pause new proposal submissions for 48 hours to reset fatigue, regardless of total vote volume.
