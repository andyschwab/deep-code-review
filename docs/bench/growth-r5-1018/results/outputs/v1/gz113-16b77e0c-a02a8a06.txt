*   **Current Status:** The test is severely underpowered. The wide confidence interval likely includes zero and negative values. Declaring a "win" now is a false positive caused by "peeking" at early variance.

**Critical Missing Data (Guardrail Metric)**
You assumed no negative impact on average donation size (ADS), but this is `UNVERIFIED`. A color change could increase click-through rate while decreasing trust, lowering the average gift.

*   **Risk:** If Green converts at 2.3% but lowers ADS by 5%, the net revenue impact is negative despite the higher conversion rate. RPV is the primary metric for this decision, not conversion rate alone.
