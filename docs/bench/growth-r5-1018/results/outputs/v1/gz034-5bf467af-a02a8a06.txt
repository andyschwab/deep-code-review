### Diagnostic Framework: SkillForge Engagement Drop-Off
**Audience:** Learning & Development Directors
**Workshop Date:** April 10th
**Objective:** Isolate the specific friction point causing the 11% decline in completion rates (45% → 34%) among remote European teams.

#### 1. The Measurement Sequence (Event Taxonomy)
Do not measure "usage." Measure only the events that answer whether a learner receives value. Instrument these four specific checkpoints to locate the leak:

*   **Event A: `content_started`** (Properties: `module_id`, `user_team_id`, `device_type`)
    *   *Question:* Is the drop-off happening before learning begins (acquisition/access issue) or during?
*   **Event B: `first_quile_completed`** (Properties: `time_to_complete`, `module_id`)
    *   *Question:* Do users pass the initial "activation" threshold? If A is high and B is low, the issue is **usability** or **initial content relevance**.
*   **Event C: `peer_interaction_attempted`** (Properties: `interaction_type`, `success_boolean`)
    *   *Question:* Validates your hypothesis. Are users trying to find peers and failing, or are they not trying at all?
*   **Event D: `manager_nudge_received`** (Properties: `source`, `time_since_last_login`)
    *   *Question:* Correlates manager involvement with retention. Did the user return within 48 hours of a nudge?

**Reading Rule:** Analyze retention cohorts bottom-up. If retention at Event B is stable but Event C drops, your "lack of synchronous interaction" hypothesis is confirmed. If Event A is dropping, the issue is **notification fatigue** or access, not content.

#### 2. Hypotheses to Test (3-Week Sprint)
Run these as structured inquiries, not A/B tests yet, to gather baseline data without peeking for false positives.

*   **H1: The "Lonely Learner" Hypothesis (Primary)**
    *   *Premise:* Remote workers drop off after Module 1 due to missing peer accountability.
    *   *Test:* Segment completion rates by teams that have >5 active users vs. isolated users.
    *   *Action:* If isolated users drop 20% faster, prioritize building synchronous breakout rooms over new content.
*   **H2: The "Noise" Hypothesis**
    *   *Premise:* Notification fatigue causes users to ignore start prompts.
    *   *Test:* Compare `content_started` rates for users receiving >3 emails/week vs. <1.
    *   *Action:* If high-frequency users have lower start rates, implement a "digest mode" immediately.
*   **H3: The "Relevance" Hypothesis**
    *   *Premise:* New Q1 modules are misaligned with current team goals.
    *   *Test:* Measure `first_quile_completed` time. A sharp increase in time-to-complete suggests confusion or lack of relevance.
    *   *Action:* If time spikes on new modules, pause new content releases and audit the top 3 drop-off lessons.

#### 3. Unknown Variables: Manager Involvement
You cannot measure what you do not define. Before April 10th, clarify these variables with your data team:
*   **The "Nudge" Definition:** Does "manager involvement" mean the manager assigned the course, or did they send a personal message? These have different impacts.
*   **Visibility Lag:** Is there a delay between a manager assigning work and the user seeing it in the EU time zones?
*   **Incentive Alignment:** Are managers rewarded for their team's completion rates? If not, their "involvement" is likely performative and won't move the metric.
