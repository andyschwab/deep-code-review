### MarketPlace NG: First 30-Day Liquidity Dashboard

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Users Completing a Transaction (Buyers + Sellers).
*Why:* This measures real value exchange, not vanity. Cumulative signups or pageviews rise regardless of success; only completed transactions prove the marketplace is working.

**Core Metric Set (Daily Monitor)**

1.  **Liquidity Rate (Primary Health Indicator)**
    *   **Definition:** (Number of Unique Buyers who purchased) / (Number of Unique Sellers with active listings) over a rolling 7-day window.
    *   **Why it matters:** Replaces the static "listing-to-buyer ratio." A high ratio of listings to buyers means inventory is stagnant; a low ratio means buyers face stockouts. This dynamic rate tells you if supply matches demand *in motion*.
    *   **Target Context:** Do not seek an external "Nigerian benchmark" (none exists for your specific niche). Your baseline is Day 1. If this rate drops for 3 consecutive days, liquidity is failing.

2.  **Activation Rate (Cold-Start Critical)**
    *   **Definition:** % of waitlist users who complete their *first* transaction within 48 hours of account creation.
    *   **Why it matters:** With 2,500 waitlist users, your immediate risk is not acquisition, but activation. If they sign up and don't transact quickly, they churn before habit formation.
    *   **Goal:** Focus on hitting your 100 transaction goal by maximizing this %, not by adding more users.

3.  **Match Failure Rate**
    *   **Definition:** (Searches with zero clicks + Clicks with zero messages) / Total Searches.
    *   **Why it matters:** Identifies *why* liquidity is missing. High "zero click" means poor inventory relevance; high "zero message" means pricing or trust barriers.

4.  **Transaction Count (Volume)**
    *   **Definition:** Total successful payments processed.
    *   **Why it matters:** At this stage, **count matters more than Gross Merchandise Value (GMV).** GMV is noisy with low volume (one expensive item skews data). Count proves the mechanism works. Track GMV only as a secondary sanity check.

**Measurement Rules**
*   **No Benchmarks:** We compare today's performance only against our own yesterday and last week. External "good ratios" are irrelevant until we have our own trend line.
*   **Event Taxonomy:** We will only instrument events that answer specific questions (e.g., "Did the user message a seller?"). We will not track "page views" or "time on site" as they are vanity metrics that distract from liquidity.
*   **Identifier Seam:** We will use a stable, pseudonymous User ID to track individuals across sessions to measure retention, distinct from system logs.

**This Week's Steps**
1.  **Instrument Activation:** Define and track the exact event "First Transaction Completed" for the 2,500 waitlist users upon launch.
2.  **Manual Liquidity Check:** If the Liquidity Rate drops below 0.5 in the first 48 hours, manually contact 20 top sellers from the waitlist to list specific high-demand items.
3.  **Board Reporting:** Present the "Liquidity Rate" trend line, not just total signups, to demonstrate actual market health for the funding tranche.
