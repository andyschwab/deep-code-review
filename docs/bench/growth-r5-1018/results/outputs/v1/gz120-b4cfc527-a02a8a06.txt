### Core Conversion Funnel Definition for DesignHub

**Funnel Start Point:** The funnel must start when a **Client Posts a Job**.
Starting at "portfolio view" captures browsing intent, not transactional commitment. Since your primary conflict is between "low proposal quality" and "insufficient bid volume," the measurement must begin at the point of supply creation (the job post) to diagnose whether the failure is **liquidity** (no bids) or **matching quality** (bad bids). Viewing a portfolio is an acquisition/activation metric, not a conversion funnel step for hired projects.

**Proposed Event Taxonomy & Drop-off Measurement**
Instrument only these four events to answer specific questions. Do not track intermediate clicks unless they directly block these steps.

| Stage | Event Name | Question Answered | Drop-off Calculation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | : |
| **1. Supply Creation** | `job_posted` | How many opportunities enter the market? | Baseline (4,000/mo). |
| **2. Liquidity Check** | `first_bid_received` | Did the job attract *any* interest? | `(job_posted - first_bid_received) / job_posted`. <br> *High drop-off here validates the client claim: "not enough bids."* |
| **3. Consideration** | `proposal_viewed_by_client` | Did the client engage with the bids? | `(first_bid_received - proposal_viewed) / first_bid_received`. <br> *High drop-off here suggests bids are arriving but are irrelevant/spam.* |
| **4. Conversion** | `freelancer_hired` | Did the interaction result in value? | `(proposal_viewed - freelancer_hired) / proposal_viewed`. <br> *High drop-off here validates the team assumption: "proposal quality/pricing mismatch."* |

**Diagnostic Logic for Your Teams**
*   **If Stage 2 drop-off > 60%:** The bottleneck is **Liquidity**. Clients aren't seeing bad proposals; they are seeing none. Marketing must drive freelancer supply to specific job categories, not improve proposal templates.
*   **If Stage 2 drop-off < 20% but Stage 4 drop-off > 80%:** The bottleneck is **Quality/Matching**. Clients have choices but reject them. Product must improve freelancer matching algorithms or proposal guidelines.
*   **Current Baseline:** With 4,000 posts and 800 hires, your overall conversion is 20%. Without the intermediate steps, you cannot know if you lose 1,000 users at Stage 2 and 2,200 at Stage 4, or vice versa. The strategy for each scenario is mutually exclusive.

**This Week's Execution Plan**
1.  **Instrument:** Deploy tracking for `first_bid_received` and `proposal_viewed_by_client` immediately. Tag every job post with a unique ID to link these events.
2.  **Segment:** Run a cohort analysis on last month's 4,000 jobs. Split them by category (e.g., Logo Design vs. UI Design) to see if the bottleneck varies by vertical.
3.  **Decision Rule:** If >50% of jobs receive zero bids within 24 hours, the Product team pauses proposal quality features and shifts roadmap priority to freelancer acquisition alerts. If >50% receive bids but no hires, Marketing pauses supply campaigns and Product A/B tests proposal ranking logic.

**Note:** Do not fabricate industry benchmark retention rates; compare these new stages only against DesignHub's own historical baseline once instrumented. Ensure user IDs are pseudonymous to comply with privacy standards while allowing cross-session tracking.
