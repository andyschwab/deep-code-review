**Recommendation for Board Approval: North Star Metric**

**Selected Metric:** **Active Capital Deployed per Retained Investor**
*(Definition: The sum of capital currently held in active commercial assets by investors who have completed at least one secondary action—such as reinvestment, portfolio rebalancing, or referral—within the last 12 months.)*

**Executive Summary**
For the commercial vertical launch, **Asset Count** and **Total Capital Volume** are both insufficient as standalone North Star metrics. Asset Count risks incentivizing fragmentation without value (vanity), while Total Capital Volume captures "one-and-done" whales who do not contribute to platform liquidity or network effects. The health of a fractional ownership marketplace depends on **recurring engagement and liquidity**, not just static holdings. Therefore, the Board should adopt a composite metric that ties capital to *active* retention.

**Comparison of Candidates**

| Metric | Pros | Cons & Risks for Commercial Vertical |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Asset Count** (Properties held) | • Simple to track.<br>• Aligns with the hypothesis that diversification aids retention. | • **Vanity Risk:** Investors can hold 10 tiny, non-performing assets.<br>• **Commercial Mismatch:** Commercial deals are larger and fewer; forcing "count" may encourage unhealthy over-fragmentation.<br>• **Ignores Value:** Does not distinguish between a $1k stake and a $100k stake. |
| **Total Capital Volume** (Sum deployed) | • Directly correlates to revenue (fees on AUM).<br>• Captures the "whale" impact immediately. | • **Leaky Bucket:** Includes inactive users who deployed once and left (as noted in your data).<br>• **False Health:** A single large deployment spikes the metric while retention rots.<br>• **No Behavior Signal:** Does not measure if the investor is *engaged* with the marketplace. |

**Why "Active Capital per Retained Investor" Wins**
This metric solves the specific contradictions in your data:
1.  **Filters "One-and-Done" Capital:** By requiring a secondary action (reinforcement of the "retained" status), it excludes the large sums deployed by users who never return, ensuring the metric reflects *sustainable* health.
2.  **Validates the Diversification Hypothesis:** If diversified investors truly stay 40% longer, they will naturally accumulate more *active* capital over time through reinvestment. This captures the *result* of diversification (long-term value) rather than the *mechanism* (count), which may vary between residential and commercial asset classes.
3.  **Aligns with Commercial Reality:** Commercial assets have longer hold times (currently 18 months). A metric based on *active* status prevents the dashboard from looking "dead" during long hold periods, provided the investor is engaged in other platform behaviors (e.g., viewing deals, referring others).

**Board Decision Context (Meeting on the 20th)**
Adopting this metric shifts the annual goal from "accumulate assets" to "cultivate active portfolios." It acknowledges that while hold time may fluctuate as you move to commercial assets, the *quality* of the investor relationship is defined by their continued participation, not just their initial check size.

2.  **Define "Active":** Finalize the specific secondary action (e.g., login + view, referral, or reinvestment) that qualifies an investor as "retained" for this formula before the Board meeting.
