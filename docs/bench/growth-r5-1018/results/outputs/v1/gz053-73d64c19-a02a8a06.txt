### Investigation Plan: Interactive Ad Impact Assessment

**Audience:** Advertising Sales Director, Content Acquisition Team
**Objective:** Determine if the 25% drop in session duration (55m → 41m) represents total churn or content avoidance, and calculate the net revenue impact.

#### 1. Diagnose the Drop: Exit vs. Avoidance
We must distinguish between users quitting the app entirely (churn risk) and users manipulating playback to skip ads (behavioral adaptation).

*   **Metric A: Session Termination Rate at Ad Boundaries**
    *   *Query:* Calculate the percentage of sessions ending within 60 seconds of an ad impression start time, compared to the pre-Feb 10 baseline.
    *   *Reading Rule:* If this rate spikes significantly while overall daily active users (DAU) remain stable, users are **exiting the app** due to friction. If DAU also drops, this indicates **subscription churn**.
*   **Metric B: Content Skip Rate Post-Ad**
    *   *Query:* Measure the frequency of "skip forward" or "next episode" actions immediately following an ad completion.
    *   *Reading Rule:* A rise here indicates **avoidance**. Users are staying in the app but consuming less licensed content, directly impacting your licensing cost-per-view efficiency.
*   **Metric C: Cross-Device Return Rate (UNVERIFIED)**
    *   *Gap:* You noted uncertainty about users returning on different devices. Without a stable, pseudonymous user ID linked across devices, this metric is currently `UNVERIFIED`.
    *   *Action:* Instrument a logged-in user ID immediately. Until then, assume session-based data undercounts retention.

#### 2. Financial Reconciliation: Revenue vs. Value Loss
Do not declare victory based on ad click rates alone. We must weigh the new ad revenue against the loss in content consumption value.

*   **Formula:** `Net Impact = (New Ad RPM × Impressions) − (Lost Content Minutes × Licensing Cost Per Minute)`
*   **Break-Even Analysis:**
    *   Calculate the minimum increase in ad yield required to offset the 14-minute average loss per session.
    *   *Tripwire:* If the incremental ad revenue does not cover the licensing cost of the missing 14 minutes of content within 7 days, the format is destroying margin, regardless of click-through rates.

#### 3. Immediate Actions (This Week)
1.  **Segment the Exit:** Run the termination query for Feb 10–Present vs. Jan 10–Feb 9. If >60% of the session drop occurs exactly at the 15-minute mark, the frequency is the primary driver, not the ad creative.
2.  **Guardrail Check:** Isolate subscribers who have canceled since Feb 10. If their last session ended at an ad boundary, the format is driving direct churn. Report this count to the Content Acquisition team immediately.
3.  **Frequency A/B Test Design:** Do not roll back yet. Design a test with three cohorts: Current (15m), Control (No interactive ads), and Reduced Frequency (30m).
    *   *Pre-commitment:* Run for 14 days. Primary metric: Total Content Minutes per User. Guardrail: Ad Revenue per User.
    *   *Decision Rule:* If the 30m cohort retains >90% of content minutes with <10% ad revenue loss, switch to the lower frequency.

**Note:** Do not attribute the drop to "ad fatigue" without verifying if users are returning on other devices; install cross-device tracking to resolve this `UNVERIFIED` status.
**Note:** Licensing costs must be converted to a per-minute rate to make the break-even calculation actionable for the acquisition team.
