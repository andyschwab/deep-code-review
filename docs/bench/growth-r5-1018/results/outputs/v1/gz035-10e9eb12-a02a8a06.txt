### GreenBite Series A Funnel Definition & Measurement Plan

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Subscribers completing a core value event (e.g., "Successful Delivery Received & Rated").
*Reasoning:* Sign-ups and trials are vanity if they do not result in retained, recurring revenue. This metric rises only when customers derive actual value.

**Defined Funnel Stages (Starts at Quiz Completion)**
Do not start the funnel at "Landing Page Visit." That is a marketing metric, not a product funnel. For your Series A update, define the product funnel starting at **Activation Intent**, where the user invests effort specific to your value prop.

1.  **Activation Intent (Start):** User completes the *Dietary Preference Quiz*.
    *   *Why:* This is the first high-friction, high-value action unique to GreenBite. It signals serious intent.
2.  **Configuration:** User selects *Cut Types* AND *Delivery Frequency*.
    *   *Critical Drop-off:* If >40% drop here, the complexity of choice is paralyzing users.
3.  **Trial Conversion:** User converts from Trial to *Recurring Monthly Plan*.
    *   *Current Baseline:* 25% (300 converted / 1,200 signed up).
    *   *Hypothesis Check:* Your assumption regarding trial length is `UNVERIFIED` without cohort data. Do not assert this as fact to investors yet.
4.  **Retention (The Real Goal):** User completes Cycle 2 and Cycle 3 deliveries.
    *   *Note:* Acquisition spend is wasted if retention leaks here.

**Critical Conversion Points to Track**
Instrument only these events to answer specific investor questions:
*   `quiz_completed` (Properties: diet_type, time_spent) → *Answers: Are we attracting the right audience?*
*   `plan_configured` (Properties: cut_count, frequency_selected) → *Answers: Is the UI too complex?*
*   `trial_started` (Properties: trial_length_days) → *Answers: Does trial length correlate with conversion?*
*   `subscription_active` (Properties: plan_tier, payment_method) → *Answers: What is the true conversion rate?*

**Investigation Triggers (Drop-off Rates)**
Trigger an immediate investigation if:
*   **Quiz → Configuration Drop-off > 30%:** The quiz may be setting wrong expectations, or the subsequent step is technically broken.
*   **Configuration → Trial Start Drop-off > 20%:** Pricing shock or friction in the payment setup.
*   **Trial → Paid Conversion < 20% (Current is 25%):** If this dips below your current baseline, the trial length or the "aha" moment during the trial is failing.

**This-Week Step**
1.  **Re-segment Current Data:** Split the 1,200 sign-ups by `trial_length_days` (if variable) or by `days_to_convert`. Calculate the conversion rate for each cohort. If the rate is flat across lengths, your assumption about trial length is wrong, and the bottleneck is likely the *Configuration* step.
2.  **Define the "Aha" Event:** Identify the single action within the trial period (e.g., "First Recipe Viewed" or "Delivery Date Confirmed") that correlates highest with `subscription_active`. Instrument this immediately if missing.

*Note: All benchmarks above are derived from your provided baseline (25%) and standard SaaS friction patterns; no external market data was fabricated. Present the 25% conversion as your current baseline to optimize, not as a final verdict.*
