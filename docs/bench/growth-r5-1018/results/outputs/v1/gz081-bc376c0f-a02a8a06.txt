### Measurement Plan: Neon Squirrels (First 30 Days)

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Users completing Puzzle 5 within 2 minutes.
*Why:* This measures delivered value (core loop mastery), not vanity. Cumulative downloads or raw session counts will rise regardless of player enjoyment and mislead investors.

**Priority Event Taxonomy**
Do not track every tap; it creates noise and infrastructure cost without answering specific questions. Instrument only these four events to validate your "5 puzzles in 2 minutes" hypothesis and find the tutorial leak:

1.  `tutorial_start` (Properties: `user_id`, `session_id`, `build_version`)
    *   *Question:* How many users actually begin the core loop?
2.  `puzzle_complete` (Properties: `user_id`, `level_number`, `duration_seconds`, `attempts`)
    *   *Question:* Where exactly do players drop off? (e.g., Is there a spike in failures at Level 3?)
3.  `activation_milestone` (Properties: `user_id`, `time_to_complete_seconds`)
    *   *Trigger:* Fired only when `level_number` == 5 AND `total_duration` < 120s.
    *   *Question:* Does hitting this specific threshold correlate with retention and purchase?
4.  `iap_attempt` (Properties: `user_id`, `item_id`, `price_point`, `success_boolean`)
    *   *Question:* Are fast completions actually driving revenue, or is the assumption false?

**How to Interpret Initial Results**
Read the funnel bottom-up. Your beta showed 42% Day-1 retention, which is a strong baseline, but without level data, you are flying blind.
*   **If `activation_milestone` is low:** The drop-off is in the tutorial. Look at `puzzle_complete` durations. If Level 4 takes 3x longer than Level 3, that is your friction point. Fix the level design before spending on acquisition.
*   **If `activation_milestone` is high but `iap_attempt` is low:** Your hypothesis that "speed equals spend" is **UNVERIFIED** or false. Do not optimize for speed; investigate price points or value perception.
*   **Vanity Check:** Ignore total signups. If 10,000 users download but only 5% hit the `activation_milestone`, the business is leaking value regardless of top-line growth.

**This Week's Steps**
1.  **Instrument:** Implement the four events above with a stable, pseudonymous `user_id` to track cohorts across sessions.
2.  **Verify:** Run a query on the first 1,000 post-launch users to calculate the correlation coefficient between `activation_milestone` and `iap_attempt`. If the correlation is below 0.3, pause optimization on speed and interview users about purchase barriers.
3.  **Report:** Present investors with a single funnel chart showing conversion from `tutorial_start` to `activation_milestone`, explicitly stating that revenue optimization is pending verification of the speed-spend link.

*Note: Ensure `user_id` is pseudonymous to comply with privacy standards; do not store raw PII in analytics events. Compare all new metrics against your 42% beta baseline, not industry averages.*
