### Activation Criteria & Hypothesis Test Plan
**Audience:** CEO and Marketing Lead, GreenBalance
**Date:** Pre-Launch (Before Dec 1)

**1. Definition of Activated User**
An user is **Activated** only upon **completing the purchase of ≥1 kg of carbon credits**.
*   *Rationale:* Browsing projects and calculating footprints are vanity metrics until correlated with revenue. Per our directive, we do not assume these predict retention without data. They are "input metrics" to be tested, not activation criteria yet.

**2. Hypothesis Test Design (December 1–31)**
To determine if browsing or footprint calculation predicts retention before spending the January budget, we will run an observational cohort analysis on the first 2,000 users.

*   **Hypothesis A:** Users who calculate their footprint *and* view ≥2 projects within 24 hours of signup have a higher Day-7 retention rate than those who only view projects.
*   **Hypothesis B:** Users who purchase within 48 hours of signup have a significantly higher Day-30 retention rate than those who purchase later.

**3. Specific Thresholds & Timeframes**
We will distinguish "curious visitors" from "committed buyers" using these provisional thresholds, to be validated or adjusted after the December data read:

*   **The "Curious" Window:** 0–48 hours post-signup.
    *   *Action:* If a user has not purchased by hour 48, they enter a "nurture" track (email sequence), not the "activated" cohort.
*   **The Activation Threshold:** **1 transaction of any size** within the first **7 days**.
    *   *Reasoning:* In marketplaces, speed to first value correlates strongly with long-term LTV. Waiting longer than 7 days to classify activation delays learning.
*   **The "Committed" Signal:** A second purchase or a referral action within **30 days**.
    *   *Usage:* This is our *Retention* metric, not activation. We will measure what percentage of "Activated" users hit this signal.

**4. Decision Rules for January Budget ($15k)**
We will allocate the marketing budget based on the December read:
*   **If Hypothesis A is true** (Footprint calc + browsing predicts purchase): Optimize onboarding to force these steps before allowing purchase. Direct ad spend to channels driving this specific behavior.
*   **If Hypothesis A is false** (No correlation): Remove friction. Allow immediate purchase without forcing calculations. Direct ad spend to channels driving immediate checkout completion.
*   **Stop Rule:** If Day-7 retention of purchasers is <20% (baseline assumption), pause January spend. Do not pour acquisition into a leaky bucket.

**5. This-Week Steps**
1.  **Instrument Events:** Define `signup`, `footprint_calculated`, `project_viewed`, and `purchase_completed` with a stable pseudonymous user ID. Do not instrument "pageviews" generally.
2.  **Build Cohort Query:** Prepare a query to segment users by "Actions taken in first 24h" vs. "Purchased in first 7 days."
3.  **Set Baseline:** Record the raw conversion rate of the first 100 users as the baseline; do not compare to external industry benchmarks.

*Note: All metrics are UNVERIFIED until December data is ingested. Do not set fixed "good" percentage targets yet; use the internal trend.*
*PII handling for user IDs must route to privacy compliance protocols before implementation.*
