**Recommended North Star Metric: Weekly Active Learners Achieving a "Verified Mastery Loop"**

**Definition:** A user (tutee) is counted in this metric only once per week if they complete a session **and** return to the platform within 7 days to either book a follow-up or engage with a specific post-session resource (e.g., a summary note or practice problem generated during the session). For tutors, the count triggers upon facilitating a session that results in this specific tutee return behavior.

**Why this replaces "Completed Sessions":**
Your current metric (sessions/week) is a vanity metric; it rises even when value is low, as evidenced by your short, low-quality interactions. "Verified Mastery Loop" filters for **retention of the core action**, which is the strongest proxy for learning outcomes when grade transcripts are unavailable.
*   **Session Duration:** Reject as a primary metric. Long sessions can indicate confusion or inefficiency, not learning.
*   **Repeat Booking Rates:** Strong, but too lagging for a weekly dashboard. It is an input metric to watch, not the North Star.
*   **Self-Reported Confidence:** Reject as the North Star. It is subjective, easily gamed, and lacks behavioral verification.

**Input Metrics to Move the North Star:**
1.  **Activation Rate:** % of new users who complete their first "Mastery Loop" within 48 hours of signup.
2.  **Quality Filter:** % of sessions lasting >15 minutes (to exclude the "low-quality" noise you identified).
3.  **Tutor Utilization:** % of tutors facilitating at least one "Mastery Loop" per week.

**Dashboard Narrative for Stakeholders:**
Present the trend of "Weekly Active Learners Achieving a Verified Mastery Loop." Explain that this number only grows when students find enough value to return, directly correlating to the program's goal of sustained academic improvement. Explicitly state that while official grade correlation is pending, this behavioral proxy isolates *engaged* learning from mere activity volume.

**This Week's Steps:**
1.  **Instrument the Event:** Define and log a single new event `mastery_loop_completed` with properties `{user_id, session_id, timestamp, return_action_type}`. Do not instrument everything; only track this specific sequence.

Label this figure `UNVERIFIED` until the new event taxonomy is live, but use the proxy of "users with >1 session in a 7-day window" from your raw logs to establish an immediate trend line for the meeting.
3.  **Stakeholder Caveat:** Add a footnote to the dashboard: "Metric excludes single-session interactions to filter low-quality volume; correlates to retention as a leading indicator of grade improvement pending transcript access."
