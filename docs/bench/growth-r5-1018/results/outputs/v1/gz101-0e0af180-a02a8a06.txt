**Recommendation for Director: Redefining Activation to Predict Renewal**

**Executive Summary**
The current definition of activation ("resolving five tickets") is likely too late and too high for smaller teams, contributing to the 30% early churn rate. We recommend shifting our single North Star metric for activation to **"First Value Realized,"** defined specifically as: *An account where at least one agent resolves a ticket AND the account has configured at least one custom workflow rule.*

This composite event captures both immediate utility (solving a problem) and stickiness (investing effort into customization), which historically correlates stronger with renewal than volume alone.

**Immediate Actions (Before Next Tuesday)**
Since we cannot build new dashboards immediately, we will validate this hypothesis using existing raw data exports:

1.  **Retrospective Cohort Query:** Export the last 6 months of account activity. Segment accounts into three groups based on their first-week behavior:
    *   Group A: Resolved 5+ tickets (Current Definition).
    *   Group B: Resolved 1+ ticket AND configured 1 workflow.
    *   Group C: Only invited agents (No resolution).
2.  **Correlation Check:** Map these groups against 90-day retention/renewal rates. We expect Group B to show a higher retention correlation than Group A, particularly for teams with fewer than 10 seats.
3.  **Threshold Calibration:** For accounts that churned after Day 3, identify the maximum number of tickets they resolved. If the median is below 3, the "5-ticket" bar is definitively blocking successful activation signals for small teams.

**Validation Plan**
Do not wait for a perfect dashboard. Use a simple spreadsheet pivot of the exported logs to answer: "Of the accounts that renewed, what was the *first* meaningful action they completed?" If "workflow configuration" appears frequently before the 5th ticket, it is a leading indicator we are currently ignoring.

**Decision Rule**
*   **If** Group B's retention rate exceeds Group A's by >10%, **then** we officially update the activation metric to "First Ticket + Workflow Config" and trigger an automated onboarding email when this event occurs.
*   **If** the data shows small teams rarely configure workflows but still renew upon resolving just 1 ticket, **then** we lower the threshold to "First Ticket Resolved" and remove the workflow requirement for teams under 10 users.

**Next Step**
Run the retrospective query on Monday morning. Bring the retention comparison chart to Tuesday's meeting. The goal is to stop measuring "effort" (5 tickets) and start measuring "value adoption" (solving + customizing).
