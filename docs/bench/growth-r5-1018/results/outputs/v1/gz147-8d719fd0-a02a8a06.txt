### Pricing Tier Health Evaluation Plan for DesignPro

**North Star Metric:** Net Revenue Per Active User (NRPAU) segmented by acquisition cohort, excluding one-time migration effects.
*Why:* Total revenue is currently distorted by downgrades. NRPAU isolates whether the new tier creates genuine value expansion or merely reshuffles existing spend.

**Primary Input Metrics & Event Taxonomy**
Instrument only these events to answer specific questions. Do not track "everything."

1.  **Event:** `subscription_change`
    *   **Properties:** `from_tier`, `to_tier`, `reason_code` (price vs. feature), `user_tenure`.
    *   **Question:** Is the flow primarily Basic→Pro Plus (expansion) or Enterprise→Pro Plus (cannibalization)?
2.  **Event:** `feature_access_session`
    *   **Properties:** `feature_id`, `tier_at_access`.
    *   **Question:** Are Pro Plus users utilizing the "gap" features enough to justify the $29 price, or are they accessing Enterprise-level tools that should remain gated?
3.  **Event:** `churn_cancellation`
    *   **Properties:** `exit_tier`, `stated_reason`, `days_since_last_upgrade`.
    *   **Question:** Does the new tier increase retention for Basic users, or does it accelerate churn among downgraded Enterprise users?

**How to Read the Data (The Method)**

**1. Diagnose Retention Before Acquisition**
Do not celebrate the 25% upgrade rate from Basic yet. If those users churn in Month 2 because Pro Plus doesn't solve their core problem, the upgrade was vanity.
*   **Action:** Compare Month-1 and Month-3 retention curves for: (A) Original Basic, (B) Basic→Pro Plus upgraders, and (C) Enterprise→Pro Plus downgraders.
*   **Decision Rule:** If Group C churns >15% faster than historical Enterprise averages, the feature gap is too small, and cannibalization is permanent.

**2. Analyze Feature Utilization to Define the "Gap"**
The 2% revenue lift suggests the price delta ($21) isn't matched by perceived value delta.
*   **Action:** Calculate the % of Pro Plus users who trigger >3 sessions/week on features *exclusive* to Pro Plus vs. those available in Basic.
*   **Decision Rule:** If <40% of Pro Plus users touch exclusive features weekly, the tier is perceived as "Basic with a higher price," driving the Enterprise downgrade. You must either remove features from Pro Plus or add significant value to Enterprise.

**3. Project Lifetime Value (LTV) with Guardrails**
*   **Action:** Compute LTV for the three cohorts above using current churn rates. Apply a guardrail: The blended LTV of the new mix must exceed the pre-test LTV by at least 5% to account for implementation costs.
*   **Decision Rule:** If the blended LTV is flat or negative after 90 days, the tier destroys long-term value despite the short-term 2% lift.

**Experiment Rigor Note**
Your current read is observational, not causal. You cannot claim the 2% lift is solely due to the tier without controlling for seasonality or concurrent marketing changes. Treat the current data as `UNVERIFIED` for long-term projections until you have 90 days of retention data. Do not peek at daily revenue to make final decisions; wait for the pre-committed 90-day cohort maturity.

**This Week's Steps**

2.  **Audit:** List the top 5 features accessed by Pro Plus users; if any are Enterprise-only, flag them for immediate gating review.
3.  **Decide:** If downgrader churn is >10% higher than baseline by next Friday, draft a proposal to the CFO to either (a) increase Enterprise value immediately or (b) sunset Pro Plus for existing Enterprise customers.
