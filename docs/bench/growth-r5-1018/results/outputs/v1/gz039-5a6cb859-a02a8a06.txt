### Diagnostic Plan: Power User Churn Analysis
**Audience:** Engineering Lead | **Deadline:** August 8th

**1. Define the North Star & Segment**
*   **North Star:** Weekly Active Power Users completing a "Complex Render" (not just logins).
*   **Cohort:** Users with >50 dashboards created prior to the interface update.
*   **Baseline:** Compare the current 15% churn against the same cohort's churn rate in the quarter *before* the UI change. Do not compare to casual users yet; the baseline must be historical power-user behavior.

**2. Event Taxonomy: Instrument Only What Answers a Question**
Do not "track everything." Implement these specific events to locate the breaking point:
*   `feature_access_attempt`: Logs when a power user clicks a menu item that was moved or hidden in the new UI.
    *   *Properties:* `feature_name`, `previous_location`, `new_location`, `success_boolean`.
    *   *Question:* Are they failing to find tools they previously used?
*   `workflow_abandonment`: Triggered when a user opens a complex dashboard (>10 widgets) but closes it without interaction within 30 seconds.
    *   *Question:* Does the new layout cause immediate friction on heavy loads?
*   `error_rate_by_user_tier`: Filter system errors (from `observability.md`) specifically for the >50 dashboard cohort.
    *   *Question:* Is the simplification causing performance timeouts on large datasets?

**3. The Analysis Method: Retention First**
Analyze the funnel bottom-up. Before looking at acquisition or activation, map the **Retention Curve** of the power user cohort week-by-week post-update.
*   Identify the "Cliff": The specific week or day where usage drops sharply before cancellation.
*   Correlate the Cliff with the `feature_access_attempt` failures. If 60% of churning users failed to access "Advanced Filtering" three days before canceling, you have your causal link.

**4. Decision Matrix (Performance vs. Capability)**
Present the findings using this logic to drive the roadmap decision:
*   **If** `error_rate` spikes for power users but not casuals → **Performance Issue**. Action: Optimize rendering engine; do not revert UI.
*   **If** `feature_access_attempt` failure rates are high and `workflow_abandonment` correlates with specific hidden features → **Capability Issue**. Action: Re-introduce "Pro Mode" toggle or revert specific navigation paths.
*   **If** both metrics are stable → **Value/Price Mismatch**. Action: Initiate user interviews (qualitative); do not engineer a fix yet.

**5. Unknown Factors & Competitor Moves**
*   **Constraint:** I cannot provide specific Swiss competitor data or benchmarks as I have no access to external market reports. Fabricating a "market average" churn rate is invalid.
*   **Actionable Investigation:** Instead of guessing competitor moves, analyze your own cancellation survey text for named competitors. If >20% of churning power users mention a specific rival tool, *that* is the signal. If no data exists, the answer is `UNVERIFIED`—do not assume a competitor caused this without evidence.
