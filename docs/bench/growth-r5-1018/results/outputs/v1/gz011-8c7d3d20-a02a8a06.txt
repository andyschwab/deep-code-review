### Growth Analytics Plan: Video Streaming Feature Test

**1. North Star & Input Metrics**
*   **North Star:** Weekly Active Bidders completing a bid via the video stream (measures real value, not just views).
*   **Primary Input:** Bid Frequency per Active User (targeting the hypothesized 15% lift).
*   **Guardrail Metric:** Page Load Latency & Drop-off Rate during stream initialization (critical given remote connectivity concerns).
*   **Vanity Rejection:** Do not track "Video Views" or "Total Sessions" as primary success criteria; these rise regardless of value delivery.

**2. Experiment Design & Seasonality Handling**
*   **Start Date Adjustment:** Do not start October 3rd. Your assumption of 5-day stabilization is `UNVERIFIED` without the archived historical data. Starting immediately post-Naadam risks confounding the "festival hangover" with your treatment effect.
    *   *Action:* Delay launch to **October 10th** to allow a 7-day buffer, or run a "pre-period" analysis on non-video features from Oct 3–9 to establish a fresh baseline trend before flipping the switch.
*   **Sample Size & Power:** With 8,500 MAUs, splitting 50/50 yields ~4,250 per group. If daily active usage is ~20% (~1,700 users/day), you have ~850 users/group/day.
    *   To detect a 15% lift with 80% power (assuming a baseline bid rate of ~10%), you likely need ~10–14 days of clean data.
    *   *Pre-commitment:* Define the end date as **October 24th** (14 days post-adjusted start) to ensure sufficient N before the CEO's Oct 20th decision deadline. You must present preliminary trends on Oct 17th, but the final "Ship/No-Ship" call requires the full duration to avoid peeking errors.

**3. Handling Uneven Samples (Connectivity Issues)**
Connectivity in remote aimags will cause attrition, creating uneven group sizes.
*   **Analysis Method:** Do not compare raw totals. Analyze **rates per user** (Bids / Active Users) within each group.
*   **Segmentation:** Split results into "High Connectivity" vs. "Low Connectivity" cohorts post-hoc.
    *   *Interpretation Rule:* If the lift exists only in "High Connectivity," the feature is viable but requires infrastructure investment (supporting the Q4 infrastructure budget). If the lift is zero in "Low Connectivity" due to drop-offs, the feature fails for the Gobi region without significant tech upgrades.
*   **Statistical Note:** If variance is high due to connection drops, use the pre-period data (Oct 3–9) as a covariate (CUPED approach) to reduce noise, provided you log this method in the analysis plan now.
