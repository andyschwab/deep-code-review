# Measurement Strategy: Balancing Parking Efficiency and Commercial Vitality
**To:** City Council Transportation Committee
**From:** Data Lead, Vienna SmartPark
**Date:** [Current Date]
**Subject:** Proposal to Correlate Parking Turnover with Local Economic Health (Innere Stadt Pilot)

## 1. North Star Metric: "Value-Adjusted Turnover"
We reject "total parking sessions" or "average duration" as standalone success metrics, as they are vanity measures that can rise while local commerce suffers. Instead, we define our North Star as **Value-Adjusted Turnover**: the rate of parking space utilization weighted by the likelihood of a commercial transaction occurring.
*   **Input Metrics:** (a) Percentage of stays >45 mins (our proxy for "shopping-ready" duration); (b) Hourly turnover rate; (c) Zone-specific foot traffic density.

## 2. Event Taxonomy & Privacy-Safe Correlation
We will instrument only events that answer specific questions, adhering to strict pseudonymization (no direct PII linkage).
*   **Event A: `parking_session_end`**
    *   *Properties:* `duration_bucket` (<15m, 15-44m, 45m+), `time_of_day`, `zone_id`, `day_of_week`.
    *   *Question:* "Did the efficiency gain shift the distribution of stay durations below the 45-minute commerce threshold?"
*   **Event B: `zone_footfall_index`** (Aggregated from anonymized mobile signal or street counters, not individual tracking)
    *   *Properties:* `hour`, `zone_id`, `weather_condition`.
    *   *Question:* "Did faster turnover increase total unique visitors, even if individual stays shortened?"
*   **The Identifier Seam:** We will use a **daily aggregated zone-level key** to join parking data with economic indicators. We will *not* track individual users across sessions to preserve privacy. Individual vehicle IDs will be hashed and discarded after session calculation.

## 3. Correlation Strategy (No Direct Transaction Linking)
Since we cannot link specific parking sessions to retail receipts without violating privacy, we will use **time-series correlation at the zone level**:
1.  **Baseline Establishment:** Compare pre-March 20th vs. post-March 20th trends in the Innere Stadt for:
    *   Ratio of short-stay (<45m) vs. long-stay (>45m) sessions.
    *   Aggregate retail sales tax data (provided by the finance department, lagged by one month) for the same zones.
2.  **Control Group Analysis:** Compare Innere Stadt trends against a similar district *without* sensors (e.g., Leopoldstadt) during the same period to isolate the sensor effect from seasonality or broader economic shifts.
3.  **Survey Sampling:** Deploy a voluntary, anonymized QR-code survey at exit points asking: "Did you make a purchase?" and "How long did you stay?" (Target N=500/week). This provides the missing conversion rate parameter without systemic tracking.

## 4. Decision Framework for the Committee
We propose a **4-week diagnostic sprint** ending April 24th, with a final recommendation on May 1st.
*   **If** the ratio of <45m stays increased by >20% *and* aggregate zone retail revenue (from tax data) shows a negative trend relative to the control district:
    *   *Action:* **Adjust Pricing.** Implement a "grace period" (first 45 mins free/cheap) to encourage longer stays while maintaining turnover for non-shoppers.
*   **If** short stays increased but total footfall and estimated retail volume (footfall × survey conversion rate) remained stable or grew:
    *   *Action:* **Expand Network.** The efficiency gain is attracting more potential customers, offsetting shorter durations.
*   **If** data is inconclusive due to noise:
    *   *Action:* **Extend Pilot** for 8 more weeks with refined survey targeting; do not expand yet.

## 5. This Week's Steps
1.  **Define Buckets:** Configure the analytics pipeline to aggregate `parking_session_end` events into the three duration buckets by zone, starting tomorrow.
2.  **Secure Data Share:** Formalize the data-sharing agreement with the Finance Department to receive weekly aggregated sales tax data by district.
3.  **Launch Survey:** Deploy the QR-code intercept survey at 5 high-traffic exits in the Innere Stadt to establish the baseline purchase conversion rate.

*Note: All individual vehicle data remains pseudonymous; only aggregated zone-level trends will be presented to the committee. No benchmark "good" retention rate is asserted; success is defined solely by the trend relative to our own March 20th baseline and the control district.*
