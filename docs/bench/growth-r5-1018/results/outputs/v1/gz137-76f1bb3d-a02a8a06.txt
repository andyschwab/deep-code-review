### Urgent Analysis Plan: Order Confirmation Leakage

**North Star Focus:** Daily Active Traders executing >10 trades (Core Value Action).
**Immediate Hypothesis Test:** UI Friction vs. Fee Transparency vs. Market Context.

Execute the following three data slices immediately to isolate the root cause before the risk committee meeting. Do not rely on aggregate averages; the drop is specific to high-frequency users.

#### 1. The "UI Tweak" Cohort Split (Primary Suspect)
Segment users by **app version** or **feature flag exposure** regarding the confirmation button update.
*   **Query:** Compare conversion rate (Preview → Confirm) for:
    *   Group A: Users on the old UI (control).
    *   Group B: Users on the new UI (treatment).
*   **Filter:** Restrict to users with >10 trades/day history.
*   **Decision Rule:** If Group B's conversion is >5% lower than Group A's, the UI tweak is the primary driver.
*   **Action:** If confirmed, recommend an immediate rollback or A/B test revert for high-volume traders only.

#### 2. The "Fee Disclosure" Interaction Heatmap
Analyze interaction specifically with the new fee transparency element.
*   **Query:** Measure the % of users who **scroll to** or **click/hover over** the fee disclosure line on the preview screen before dropping off.
*   **Segment:** Compare this behavior between the pre-drop baseline (2 weeks ago) and current data.
*   **Decision Rule:** If drop-offs correlate strongly with the *presence* of the disclosure but not the *interaction* (i.e., they see it and leave without clicking), the disclosure itself creates friction. If they click it and then leave, the *content* of the fee change is the blocker.
*   **Action:** If the disclosure is the blocker, draft a copy adjustment to clarify that fees haven't changed for their tier, or move the disclosure to a post-trade receipt for high-frequency users (if compliant).

#### 3. The "Volatility vs. Asset" Matrix (Context Check)
Validate the assumption that market volatility is the cause, despite stable latency.
*   **Query:** Break down the drop-off rate by **asset class** (e.g., Forex vs. Crypto vs. Equities) and **spread width** at the moment of the drop-off.
*   **Logic:** High-frequency traders are spread-sensitive. If the drop is isolated to assets with widened spreads (even if latency is low), the "preview" price may look unfavorable compared to the "confirmation" price due to slippage, causing hesitation.
*   **Decision Rule:** If the drop is uniform across all assets regardless of volatility, reject the market volatility hypothesis. If concentrated in high-spread assets, the issue is pricing logic, not UI.
*   **Action:** If pricing logic, engage engineering to review slippage tolerance settings on the confirmation screen.
