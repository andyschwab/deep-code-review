**Deliverable: Weekly Executive Metric for VerdantSky Board**

**Primary Metric:** **Contribution Margin per Square Meter per Week**
*Formula:* `(Weekly Revenue from Harvested Lettuce − Variable Costs) / Total Cultivated Square Meters`
*Variable Costs must include:* Energy (electricity for climate control), water, nutrients, packaging, and direct labor for that specific week.

**Why this replaces "Total Biomass" and "Yield per m²":**
Your current focus on yield per square meter is a vanity metric in this context because it ignores the 12% rise in energy costs. In Dubai's vertical farming environment, higher yields often require aggressive cooling and lighting, which can erode profits if electricity prices spike. This new metric forces a balance: it only rises if you increase output *or* reduce variable costs without sacrificing quality. It directly answers your investors' demand for unit economics over raw volume.

**Interpreting Shifts for Q3 Strategy:**

1.  **Metric Rises:** You have successfully decoupled growth from energy intensity. This signals readiness for Q3 expansion. The unit economics are positive enough to scale square footage without burning cash.
2.  **Metric Flatlines while Yield Rises:** Your energy costs are consuming your growth gains. The 12% cost increase is outpacing production efficiency. **Action:** Pause physical expansion. Direct engineering teams to optimize HVAC setpoints or lighting schedules before adding more grow beds.
3.  **Metric Declines:** Your current operational model is unsustainable at current energy prices. **Action:** Do not expand. Conduct an immediate variance analysis to isolate whether the drop is driven by the regional electricity price hike or internal inefficiency (e.g., equipment failure causing over-cooling).

**Weekly Reading Rule:**
Compare this week's figure strictly against your own baseline from January 2024 and the previous week. Do not compare against external industry benchmarks, as Dubai's specific energy mix and climate control loads are unique. If the metric drops for two consecutive weeks, trigger a "Cost-Per-Kg" deep dive before any capital expenditure approval.

**Note:** Ensure your data pipeline separates fixed costs (rent, salaries) from variable costs (energy, water) to keep this metric pure.
This metric serves as the single source of truth for the Tuesday board meeting; all other operational stats are secondary inputs.
