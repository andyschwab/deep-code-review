**Subject:** Q2 Budget Decision Framework – Engagement Metrics & Tracking Plan

**North Star Metric:** Annual Membership Renewal Rate.
*Reasoning:* Weekly check-ins (200 → 350) are a vanity metric; they rise regardless of financial health. Renewals directly dictate sustainability. We must measure behaviors that predict this specific outcome, not just foot traffic.

**Recommended Behavioral Signals to Track Immediately**
We will instrument only events that answer the question: "Does this action correlate with renewal?" Do not track generic attendance.

1.  **Event: `workshop_completed`** (Properties: `user_id`, `workshop_type`, `project_finished_boolean`)
    *   *Question Answered:* Is "project completion" a stronger predictor than raw "attendance"?
    *   *Hypothesis Test:* Compare renewal rates of users with `project_finished_boolean=true` vs. those who attended but did not finish.
2.  **Event: `resource_consumed`** (Properties: `user_id`, `resource_type` [filament/machine], `cost_amount`)
    *   *Question Answered:* Do members who spend money early (filament/rentals) retain better than free-tier users?
    *   *Action:* If spenders renew at 2x the rate, prioritize equipment maintenance (Q2) to reduce friction for this high-value cohort.
3.  **Event: `milestone_reached`** (Properties: `user_id`, `count_within_30_days`)
    *   *Question Answered:* Is the "2 workshops in 30 days" hypothesis valid?
    *   *Action:* If users hitting this count renew >50% more often, allocate Q2 budget to classes that accelerate reaching this count. If no correlation exists, shift budget to maintenance.

**Reading Rules for Q2 Decision**
*   **Retention First:** Do not analyze new signups until we confirm if current members are renewing. A leaky bucket makes new classes wasteful.
*   **Cohort Comparison:** Segment users by "Jan 5–Feb 5 Joiners." Compare renewal intent (or early renewal) between those who triggered `milestone_reached` and those who did not.
*   **Vanity Check:** If check-ins rise but `resource_consumed` stays flat, the new location is driving tourism, not sustainability.

**This Week's Steps**
1.  **Define Schema:** Finalize the three event names above with properties in our database today.
2.  **Backfill/Start:** Begin logging `project_finished_boolean` for all current workshops immediately.
3.  **Baseline Query:** Run a one-time manual check: Of the 350 weekly check-ins, how many unique users have visited twice in 30 days? Report this count to Sarah by Friday to set the baseline before full automation.

*Note: All metrics compare MotorCity Makers' own historical baselines; no external industry benchmarks are used. If data is missing for "project completion," it is marked UNVERIFIED until the manual log begins.*
