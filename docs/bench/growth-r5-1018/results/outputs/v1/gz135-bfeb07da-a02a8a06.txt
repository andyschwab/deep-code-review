### Recommended Analytics Event Shortlist for NeueHeimat Hub

To demonstrate efficacy to your board and funding partners while respecting server constraints, replace the "downloads" proxy with three high-signal events that track **value delivery** rather than file transfer. These events answer specific questions about user success without requiring heavy instrumentation.

**1. Event: `core_action_completed`**
*   **Definition:** Triggered when a user clicks an external link to a verified housing listing, submits a contact form for legal aid, or copies a language school registration code.
*   **Why measure it:** Downloads are a vanity metric if the guide is never used. This event captures the moment a user transitions from *browsing* to *acting* on a resource. It directly correlates with your mission outcomes (securing housing, enrolling in class) better than a file download.
*   **Data to capture:** `resource_type` (housing/legal/language), `action_type` (click/submit/copy), and `session_id`.
*   **Decision Rule:** If `core_action_completed` rises while downloads stay flat, your anecdotal feedback is confirmed: browsing *is* valuable. Shift reporting focus from "guides distributed" to "actions initiated."

**2. Event: `returning_user_value`**
*   **Definition:** Triggered when a known user (via pseudonymous cookie or login) performs a `core_action_completed` in a second or third session within 30 days.
*   **Why measure it:** Retention is the truest indicator of product-market fit for a resource center. A user who returns to take a second action is successfully integrating. This filters out "one-time lookers" from those relying on your hub for ongoing support.
*   **Data to capture:** `days_since_first_visit`, `resource_type_sequence`.
*   **Decision Rule:** If this rate is low, users are not finding sustained value. Prioritize improving the depth of content for the most common first-action type before spending on acquisition.

**3. Event: `helpfulness_signal`**
*   **Definition:** A lightweight, single-click interaction on a resource page (e.g., "Was this useful? Yes/No" or a simple "thumbs up" that stores a boolean flag).
*   **Why measure it:** Since you cannot track real-world outcomes (like signed leases) directly due to privacy and technical limits, this explicit signal bridges the gap between digital action and real-world success. It validates whether the *action* taken actually helped.
*   **Data to capture:** `rating` (boolean), `resource_id`.
*   **Decision Rule:** Correlate low ratings with specific `resource_type` categories. If "legal aid" pages have high clicks but low helpfulness scores, the content is misleading or the partners are unresponsive—trigger a content audit for that specific category.

**Implementation Note for Annual Report:**
Report the **conversion rate** from Visitor → `core_action_completed` → `returning_user_value`. This narrative proves you are driving tangible steps toward integration, not just hosting files.

**Constraint Check:**
These events require storing only small text strings and counters, minimizing database load compared to full session recordings or heavy file-tracking logs. Ensure all user identifiers are pseudonymous and aggregated before presentation to comply with privacy standards for vulnerable populations.
