### Recommended Behavioral Signals & Validation Plan for SautiCare

**1. Three High-Value Behavioral Signals (Low Engineering Cost)**
To refine activation beyond the current rigid definition, instrument these three specific events. Each answers a distinct question about value delivery without requiring complex infrastructure.

*   **Event A: `sms_checker_completed`**
    *   **Properties:** `symptom_category`, `advice_given_type` (e.g., "home care" vs. "see doctor").
    *   **Question Answered:** Does the SMS symptom checker provide enough immediate value that users return later, even without a video call? This validates if "activation" can occur via low-bandwidth channels first.
*   **Event B: `prescription_viewed_or_saved`**
    *   **Properties:** `time_since_consultation`, `device_type`.
    *   **Question Answered:** Is the download step a technical friction point or a lack of intent? If users view the prescription in-app but don't download, the "download" metric is a false negative for activation.
*   **Event C: `repeat_visit_within_14_days`**
    *   **Properties:** `initial_activation_method` (video vs. SMS), `paid_service_used` (boolean).
    *   **Question Answered:** This is your true retention proxy. It decouples "activation" from the specific 48-hour window and measures whether the user finds enough value to return, regardless of how they started.

**2. Validating the 48-Hour Window**
Do not guess; run a **cohort lag analysis** using your existing data from March 12 to present.
*   **The Query:** Group users by their sign-up week. For each group, calculate the cumulative percentage who completed a video consultation + prescription download at 24h, 48h, 72h, and 7 days.
*   **The Decision Rule:** If the curve flattens significantly after 48 hours (e.g., only 2% more users activate between 48h and 7 days), keep the 48-hour window. If the curve rises steeply between 48h and 72h (e.g., gaining an extra 10% of activations), extend the definition to **72 hours**. Given rural connectivity issues and asynchronous behavior, a 72-hour window is likely more realistic, but the data must confirm the marginal gain justifies the looser metric.
