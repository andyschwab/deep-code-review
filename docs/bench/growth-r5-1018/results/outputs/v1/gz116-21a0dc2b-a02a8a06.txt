### Critical Event Taxonomy & Readout Plan for 'Green Route' Beta

To validate your hypothesis without overwhelming your pipeline, instrument only the following four events. Each answers a specific decision question for the board.

**1. `green_badge_impression`**
*   **Question:** Do users actually see the score?
*   **Properties:** `user_id` (pseudonymous), `session_id`, `route_quote_id`, `badge_score` (1-100), `device_type`.
*   **Why:** If impressions are low relative to page views, the feature is invisible, not ineffective.

**2. `green_badge_interaction`**
*   **Question:** Does the score trigger curiosity or trust?
*   **Properties:** `user_id`, `session_id`, `route_quote_id`, `interaction_type` (e.g., "hover", "click_details", "sort_by_green").
*   **Why:** Distinguishes between passive visibility and active engagement.

**3. `booking_attempt`**
*   **Question:** Is the user moving toward conversion?
*   **Properties:** `user_id`, `session_id`, `route_quote_id`, `is_green_route` (boolean), `price_rank` (e.g., cheapest, 2nd cheapest).
*   **Why:** Captures intent before payment completion; essential for funnel drop-off analysis.

**4. `booking_completed` (North Star Input)**
*   **Question:** Did the green score drive the final value?
*   **Properties:** `user_id`, `session_id`, `route_quote_id`, `is_green_route`, `total_value`, `customer_segment` (e.g., "manufacturing").
*   **Why:** This is the core action. Retention is measured by the repeat rate of this event among those who saw the badge.

**Primary Metric & Guardrail**
*   **Primary Metric:** **Retention of Core Action.** Compare the % of beta users who complete a second `booking_completed` within 30 days against a matched control group (non-beta users with similar historical volume). Do not use raw signups or total pageviews; they are vanity metrics.
*   **Guardrail Metric:** **Average Revenue Per Booking.** Ensure the "Green Route" selection does not cannibalize higher-margin routes or shift users to significantly lower-value bookings.

**Statistical Readout Structure**
Do not peek at results daily. To present a valid finding to the board:
1.  **Pre-commit Sample Size:** Calculate the required number of `booking_attempt` events needed to detect an 8% lift with 80% power (typically ~2,000+ conversions per variant depending on baseline variance).
2.  **Fixed Horizon:** Set a hard end date (e.g., 4 weeks) based on when you expect to hit that sample size given your 450-user pilot volume.
3.  **Single Read:** Analyze the data **once** at the end date. A result that looks significant on day 3 but fades by day 28 is noise (false positive).
4.  **Segmentation:** If the aggregate result is flat, slice by `customer_segment`. The hypothesis specifically targets "environmentally conscious manufacturers"; a win there matters even if the aggregate is neutral.

**Board Decision Matrix**
*   **Ship:** Retention lift ≥ 8% AND Guardrail (Revenue) is flat or positive.
*   **Iterate:** Retention lift < 8% BUT `green_badge_interaction` is high (users care, but the score isn't driving the final click).
*   **Stop:** `green_badge_impression` is low OR Retention is flat AND interactions are near zero (users ignore it).
