### Diagnostic Metrics & Decision Framework for AutoSwap Instant Verification

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Completed Transactions originating from Instant-Verified Listings.
*Reject vanity metrics like "total photo uploads" or "badges issued." Value is only realized when a car sells.*

**1. Event Taxonomy: Instrument Only What Answers a Named Question**
Do not track everything. Implement these three specific events to isolate the friction point (Trust vs. Quality):

| Event Name | Properties | Question Answered |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `listing_view_detail` | `verification_type` (instant/traditional), `has_verified_badge` | Does the badge attract initial clicks? (Acquisition/Activation) |
| `listing_engagement_depth` | `time_on_page`, `scroll_depth_pct`, `photo_gallery_expand_count` | Are buyers inspecting photos closely due to distrust, or skipping due to poor quality? |
| `seller_inquiry_sent` | `listing_id`, `verification_type`, `inquiry_type` (price/info/condition) | Does the verification reduce friction to contact, or shift inquiry volume to "condition" questions? |

**2. How to Interpret the Data (The Diagnosis)**
Compare the **Instant** cohort against the **Traditional** baseline (your own data, not industry benchmarks).

*   **Scenario A: Low Click-Through, High Scroll Depth.**
    *   *Signal:* Buyers click less, but those who do stay long and zoom photos.
    *   *Diagnosis:* **Trust Deficit.** The badge may signal "low effort" or "spam." Buyers are manually verifying what the algorithm missed.
    *   *Action:* Pivot UI. Add human-verified spot checks or a "satisfaction guarantee" badge alongside the automated one.
*   **Scenario B: High Click-Through, Low Inquiry Rate.**
    *   *Signal:* Buyers click, but bounce quickly without contacting.
    *   *Diagnosis:* **Quality Deficit.** The photos are technically "verified" but visually insufficient (blurry, bad angles) to drive desire.
    *   *Action:* Pivot Onboarding. Reject uploads that fail quality thresholds (lighting/angle) before allowing "instant" status.
*   **Scenario C: High Inquiry Rate, but "Condition" Questions Dominate.**
    *   *Signal:* Engagement is high, but buyers ask "Is this scratched?" repeatedly.
    *   *Diagnosis:* **Information Gap.** The verification confirms existence, not condition.
    *   *Action:* Feature Tweak. Force sellers to tag specific defects during the photo upload flow.

**3. Experiment Rigor & The Romania Decision**
You observed a 5% lift vs. a 20% target. Before deciding, verify the read:
*   **Check for Peeking:** If this data is from March 1 to today without a pre-committed sample size, the result is `UNVERIFIED`. Do not treat the 15% engagement drop as final until the test runs for a full business cycle (e.g., 2 weeks) or reaches a pre-calculated user count.
*   **Guardrail Metric:** Monitor **Dispute/Return Rate**. If instant listings have higher returns, the 5% volume gain is toxic growth.

**Decision Matrix for Directors (Romania Expansion):**
*   **Expand:** If `listing_view_detail` CTR is neutral/positive AND `seller_inquiry_sent` rate matches traditional within 5%.
*   **Pause & Fix:** If `listing_view_detail` CTR is down >10% (Trust issue) OR photo quality scores are low. Fix the upload flow before Romania.
*   **Kill:** If `Dispute/Return Rate` on instant listings is >2x traditional listings.
