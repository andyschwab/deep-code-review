# PayForward Brazil Beta: Core Metric Framework for Board Dashboard

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Users Completing a Successful Local Payment (Value Delivered).
*Rejects vanity metrics like cumulative signups or app downloads, which rise regardless of product value.*

### 1. Acquisition (Top of Funnel)
*Focus: Quality of traffic, not just volume.*
- **Metric:** Cost Per Qualified Lead (CPQL).
- **Definition:** Marketing spend divided by users who complete identity verification (KYC).
- **Why:** In Brazil, KYC friction is high. Tracking cost per *verified* user, not just install, prevents wasting budget on users who cannot transact.
- **Board View:** Trend line of CPQL vs. total verified users.

### 2. Activation (The "Aha" Moment)
*Focus: Speed to first value, validating the "integration speed" hypothesis.*
- **Metric:** Time-to-First-Payment (TTFP).
- **Definition:** Median time elapsed from account creation to first successful local payment.
- **Why:** Directly tests the assumption that speed drives adoption. If TTFP > 24 hours, the hypothesis fails regardless of signup volume.
- **Secondary Metric:** Onboarding Drop-off Rate by Step (specifically at PIX integration or bank linking).
- **Board View:** Histogram of TTFP; Bar chart of drop-off percentages per onboarding step.

### 3. Early Retention (The Truth Test)
*Focus: Is the product sticky enough to justify acquisition spend?*
- **Metric:** Day-7 Repeat Payment Rate.
- **Definition:** Percentage of users who make a second successful payment within 7 days of their first.
- **Why:** Single transactions may be one-offs. Repeat usage confirms product-market fit in the local context.
- **Cohort View:** Group users by signup week (Week 1, Week 2, etc.) to see if product iterations improve retention.
- **Board View:** Line chart showing D7 retention for each weekly cohort.

### Implementation Rules for the Board
1.  **No External Benchmarks:** The dashboard will display **PayForward's own baseline and trend only**. Claims like "Industry standard is X%" are excluded as they are unverifiable and often irrelevant to our specific niche. Success is defined by week-over-week improvement in our own cohorts.
2.  **Event Taxonomy:** We will instrument only three core events: `user_kyc_completed`, `payment_initiated`, and `payment_success`. Each event includes properties for `payment_method` (e.g., PIX, boleto) and `latency_ms`. No other events will be tracked until these answer our primary questions.
3.  **Data Integrity:** All metrics rely on a stable, pseudonymous User ID to track individuals across sessions. Raw counts without user deduplication are excluded to prevent inflation.
