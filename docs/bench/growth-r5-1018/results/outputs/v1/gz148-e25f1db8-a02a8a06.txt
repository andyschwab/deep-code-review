### Investigation Plan: Balancing Checkout Speed and Revenue

**Primary Directive:** Do not revert the update yet. The 13-point gain in completion rate (35% → 48%) likely outweighs the 20% drop in Average Order Value (AOV), but this must be verified by **Revenue Per Visitor (RPV)**, not isolated metrics.

**1. The North Star Metric: Revenue Per Visitor (RPV)**
Stop optimizing for "completion rate" or "AOV" individually. Calculate RPV for the pre-launch baseline vs. post-launch current state:
*   **Baseline RPV:** 35% completion × ₹650 AOV = **₹227.50 per visitor**.
*   **Current RPV:** 48% completion × ₹520 AOV = **₹249.60 per visitor**.
*   **Verdict:** If these numbers hold, the update is already generating ~9.7% more revenue per user despite the lower basket size. The decision to revert is likely incorrect unless the AOV drop worsens or the completion rate was inflated by a specific segment that doesn't repeat.

**2. Funnel Diagnostics: Where is the Value Leaking?**
To confirm *why* AOV dropped and ensure it isn't a retention time-bomb, instrument these three specific events immediately. Do not track generic "scroll depth"; track actions tied to revenue decisions.

*   **Event A: `addon_view_rate` vs. `addon_click_rate`**
    *   *Question:* Are users not seeing the suggestions, or seeing them and ignoring them?
    *   *Measurement:* Percentage of users who render the add-on module vs. percentage who click an add-on.
    *   *Reading Rule:* If `view_rate` is stable but `click_rate` dropped, the friction is in the *offer* (price/relevance), not the UI speed. If `view_rate` dropped, the new 4-click flow is hiding the module too aggressively.

*   **Event B: `cart_review_duration` (Time between Cart Open and Checkout Click)**
    *   *Question:* Is "rushing" actually happening?
    *   *Measurement:* Median time spent on the cart/summary screen.
    *   *Reading Rule:* Compare the median time pre- and post-launch. If time decreased by >50% and correlates directly with lower AOV, users are skipping deliberation. If time is unchanged, the AOV drop is due to layout visibility, not speed.

*   **Event C: `coupon_abandonment_rate`**
    *   *Question:* Did removing friction remove the incentive to hunt for deals (which often increases basket size to meet thresholds)?
    *   *Measurement:* % of sessions where a coupon field is expanded but no code is applied, leading to checkout.
    *   *Reading Rule:* A spike here suggests users want to save money but can't find codes quickly, causing them to checkout with smaller baskets rather than building the basket to meet a threshold.

**3. Decision Matrix for Operations & Marketing**
Present this table to stakeholders. The action depends entirely on the RPV calculation and the `addon_view_rate`.

| Scenario | Metric Signal | Financial Impact | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | : |
| **Win-Win** | RPV > ₹227.50 AND `addon_view_rate` stable | Net revenue up; efficiency up. | **Ship.** Optimize add-on relevance later. |
