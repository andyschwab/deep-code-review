**Evaluation Strategy: Distinguishing Spam Reduction from Engagement Loss**
**Audience:** Sophie Laurent, Community Manager
**Deadline:** Report for Moderation Team Lead (Tomorrow)

**1. Immediate Diagnostic: The "Signal-to-Noise" Ratio**
Do not rely on the aggregate drop in threads. Calculate the ratio of **Approved Threads to Reported Threads** for the period before (Jan 20 – Feb 19) and after (Feb 20 – Present) the change.
*   **If the ratio improved significantly:** The drop in total threads is likely healthy spam reduction. The guidelines are working as intended.
*   **If the ratio remained flat or worsened:** Legitimate users are being silenced alongside spammers. The visibility change is causing false positives or intimidation.

**2. Cohort Analysis: New vs. Established Users**
Segment the 15% decline by user tenure to isolate the "intimidation" effect.
*   **Query:** Compare thread creation rates for **First-Time Posters** (accounts < 30 days) vs. **Established Users** (accounts > 90 days).
*   **Interpretation:**
    *   Drop concentrated in *First-Time Posters*: Supports the "intimidation" hypothesis. New contributors are hesitant.
    *   Drop concentrated in *Established Users* or evenly distributed: Suggests a broader friction point or that spam bots (often using aged accounts) are being blocked.

**3. Content Quality Audit (Sample-Based)**
Since automated classification may not be ready, perform a manual review of a random sample of 50 threads from the pre-change period and 50 from the post-change period that were *not* reported.
*   **Action:** Tag them as "High Value," "Low Effort," or "Spam-like."
*   **Goal:** Determine if the *average quality* of remaining threads has risen. If total volume is down but average quality is up, the trade-off is positive. If volume is down and quality is unchanged, you have lost genuine engagement.

**4. This-Week Action Plan**
*   **Run the Ratio Query Today:** Pull the Approved/Reported ratios for the two periods. This is the primary metric for tomorrow's meeting.
*   **Segment the Decline:** Generate the New vs. Established user breakdown.
*   **Decision Rule for Sophie:**
    *   *If New User drop > 25%:* Propose a temporary A/B test tomorrow. Show prominent guidelines to 50% of new users and standard guidelines to the other 50% to measure the specific friction cost.
    *   *If Ratio Improved & New User drop < 10%:* Maintain current visibility. The 15% total drop is an acceptable cost for a cleaner forum.
    *   *If Ratio Unchanged:* The guidelines are not filtering spam effectively but are hurting engagement. Recommend reverting the prominent display and exploring automated pre-post filters instead.

**Note:** Do not characterize the 30% drop in reports as a standalone success; it is only positive if the denominator (total threads) didn't shrink proportionally due to fear.
**Note:** All figures must be pulled from your existing analytics dashboard; no external benchmarks are required or applicable.
