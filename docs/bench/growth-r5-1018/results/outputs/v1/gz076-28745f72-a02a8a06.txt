### Referral Quality Metrics & Decision Framework for Maria

**North Star Metric:** **Week-4 Retention of Referred Users** (Percentage of referred students who complete ≥2 sessions within 28 days of sign-up).
*Why:* Raw referral count (300) is vanity; only users returning for a second session validate the match and the unit economics. Your current baseline is **20%** (60/300).

**Leading Indicators (Predictors of Retention)**
Track these three inputs to predict if a referred user will hit the North Star before the 28-day window closes:

1.  **Time-to-First-Match (TTFM):** Duration between sign-up and the first successful tutor booking.
    *   *Hypothesis:* If TTFM > 48 hours, retention drops sharply due to lost intent.
2.  **Subject-Grade Fit Rate:** Percentage of referred users whose requested subject/grade has ≥3 available tutors in the database.
    *   *Hypothesis:* Low fit confirms your suspicion about inventory gaps rather than pricing.
3.  **Activation Depth:** Binary flag for whether the first session lasted >30 minutes and included a "book next session" action during the call.

**Diagnostic Segmentation (To Isolate the Root Cause)**
Split the 60 retained vs. 240 churned users by these cuts to decide between **Matching Logic** vs. **Pricing/Bonus**:

| Segment | Metric to Check | If Result Is... | Action Trigger |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | : |
| **Inventory Constrained** | Users where requested grade/subject had <3 tutors | >70% of churned users fall here | **Fix Matching Logic:** Prioritize inventory recruitment for high-demand grades before August. Do not increase bonus. |
| **Friction High** | Median TTFM for churned users | >48 hours vs. <24 hours for retained | **Optimize Onboarding:** Auto-assign top-rated tutors for referrals to reduce wait time. |
| **Value Mismatch** | Retention rate of users who *did* find a match quickly | <15% (similar to overall) | **Test Pricing/Bonus:** The match works, but the $5 credit isn't enough to sustain habit. Run an A/B test on bonus size. |

**Experiment Design: Bonus vs. Logic**
Do not double the bonus blindly. Run a controlled test starting this week:
*   **Control (50% of new referrals):** Current $5 credit + Standard Matching.
*   **Variant A (25%):** $10 credit + Standard Matching (Tests Price Sensitivity).
*   **Variant B (25%):** $5 credit + "Priority Matching" (Guaranteed match <24h or free session) (Tests Inventory/Logic).
*   **Primary Metric:** Week-4 Retention.
*   **Guardrail Metric:** Cost Per Retained User (CPRU). If Variant A doubles retention but triples CPRU beyond LTV, it fails.
*   **Readout Rule:** Pre-commit to a sample size of 100 referrals per variant. Read results **once** after 30 days. Do not peek at day 7.
