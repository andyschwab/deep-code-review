### Behavioral Signal Plan: Technical Friction vs. Content Disinterest

To distinguish between network-induced failure and content rejection for the CTO and Regional Expansion Team, instrument only these four events. Each answers a specific binary question required for Friday's budget decision.

**1. `video_start_attempt`**
*   **Properties:** `user_id` (pseudonymous), `region` (rural/urban), `device_type`, `estimated_bandwidth` (if available from client handshake).
*   **Question:** Are users even trying to play, or dropping off at the UI layer?
*   **Reading Rule:** If `start_attempt` is low relative to `episode_page_view`, the friction is UI/discovery, not playback.

**2. `buffering_state_change`**
*   **Properties:** `state` (start/stall/resume), `duration_ms`, `episode_id`, `bitrate_current`.
*   **Question:** Is the stream technically failing to sustain playback?
*   **Reading Rule:** High frequency of `state=stall` with `duration_ms > 2000` preceding abandonment confirms technical friction. If this metric is low, the network is not the primary blocker.

**3. `playback_completion`**
*   **Properties:** `episode_id`, `completion_percentage` (0-100), `exit_reason` (user_click, network_error, end_of_file).
*   **Question:** Did they finish, and why did they stop?
*   **Reading Rule:** Compare completion rates between users with `buffering_state_change` (high stall time) vs. those without.
    *   *Scenario A (Technical):* Low completion correlates strongly with high stall time. Rural users drop at the exact moment of stalls.
    *   *Scenario B (Content):* Completion is low even for users with zero stall events, or drop-off happens at specific narrative points regardless of region.

**4. `low_bandwidth_mode_toggle` (Prototype Event)**
*   **Properties:** `action` (enabled/disabled), `trigger_context` (auto/manual).
*   **Question:** If given the option, do users self-select lower quality to finish content?
*   **Reading Rule:** High adoption + increased completion among togglers validates the low-bandwidth mode investment.
