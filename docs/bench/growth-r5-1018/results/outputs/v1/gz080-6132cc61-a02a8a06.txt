### Success Hierarchy: From Interest to Habitual Saving

**North Star Metric: "Active Saver Retention"**
*Definition:* The percentage of challenge enrollees who make a **second voluntary deposit** (outside the initial challenge requirement) within 30 days of enrollment.
*Why:* Enrollment is vanity; the first deposit is often a one-time test. A second voluntary deposit proves the user derived value and is forming a habit, directly correlating to your assumption of long-term retention without needing six months of data.

**Input Metrics (The Funnel)**
1.  **Activation Rate (The "Aha" Moment):** % of enrollees who complete the *first* required deposit.
    *   *Current Signal:* Your 20% completion rate (80% drop-off) indicates a broken activation loop. This is the immediate fix.
2.  **Challenge Completion Velocity:** Median days taken to finish the first challenge.
    *   *Target:* Faster completion should correlate with higher activation. If it takes too long, interest decays.
3.  **Habit Formation Ratio:** % of users who initiate a *new* challenge or set a recurring rule within 14 days of finishing the first one.
    *   *Purpose:* Bridges the gap between a single event and a recurring behavior.

**Guardrail Metrics (Compliance & Responsibility)**
*   **Over-Extension Rate:** % of users who attempt a deposit but fail due to insufficient funds >2 times in a week.
    *   *Action:* If this rises, the challenge difficulty is misaligned with teen cash flow, risking financial stress.
*   **Drop-off Point Frequency:** The specific step where the 80% abandon the process (e.g., after reading rules, at funding source selection, or post-first-deposit).

**Immediate Actions for March Launch**
1.  **Diagnose the 80% Leak:** Segment the drop-off by device (mobile web vs. app) and funding source. If the drop is uniform, the challenge barrier is too high; if specific to a bank, it is a technical friction. *Decision Rule:* If drop-off >70% at the funding step, simplify the initial deposit amount to a micro-transaction (e.g., ₩1,000) to lower the psychological barrier.
2.  **Redefine "Success" for Compliance:** Shift the team's target from "5,000 enrollments" to "1,000 Active Savers" (users hitting the North Star). This aligns incentives with responsible habit formation rather than gamified signups.
3.  **Instrument the "Second Deposit" Event:** Ensure your analytics track `deposit_made` with a property `is_voluntary` (boolean) and `days_since_enrollment`. Without this specific tag, you cannot calculate the North Star.

**Verification Note:** All thresholds above are derived from your stated 80% drop-off and the need for a leading indicator of 6-month retention; no external benchmarks were used.
