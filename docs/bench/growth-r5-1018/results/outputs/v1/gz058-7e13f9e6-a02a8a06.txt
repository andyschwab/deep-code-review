### Leading Indicators for GreenTech Oslo Pitch Deck

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Building Managers completing an energy-optimization action (e.g., approving an automated load-shift or adjusting a setpoint).
*Why:* This measures realized value (energy saved) rather than intent (signing a pilot). It rises only when the product works, unlike cumulative pilot counts which are vanity metrics in a long sales cycle.

**Primary Leading Indicators (Move Faster Than Sales):**

1.  **Activation Rate of Pilot Sites:** Percentage of the 50 pilot complexes where the dashboard was logged into and at least one optimization rule was active within 14 days of installation.
    *   *Signal:* High activation predicts retention and case study viability. If this is low, the 10% reduction is likely manual or temporary, not product-led.
2.  **Organic Referral Velocity:** Number of new pilot inquiries generated directly by introductions from the existing 50 building managers (tracked via unique referral codes or "referred by" fields).
    *   *Signal:* Validates product-led growth and manager satisfaction independent of your sales team's six-month grind.
3.  **Feature Stickiness (Weekly):** Ratio of Weekly Active Users (WAU) to Monthly Active Users (MAU) among building managers specifically performing *value-driving* actions (not just viewing dashboards).
    *   *Signal:* Indicates the solution is becoming part of their operational routine, a prerequisite for long-term contracts.

**Event Taxonomy for Immediate Instrumentation:**
Instrument only these events to answer specific questions for your deck:
*   `optimization_rule_activated` (Properties: `building_id`, `rule_type`, `estimated_savings_kwh`) → *Does the core value happen?*
*   `dashboard_login_successful` (Properties: `user_role`, `session_duration`) → *Are they returning?*
*   `referral_link_shared` (Properties: `source_building_id`, `channel`) → *Is the network effect starting?*

**Reading the Data for Your Audience:**
*   **For VCs:** Focus on *Referral Velocity* and *Activation Rate*. These prove that once the foot is in the door, the product sells itself or sticks, de-risking the long sales cycle.
*   **For Grant Committees:** Focus on *Actual Energy Saved per Active Site* (derived from `optimization_rule_activated` events) rather than total pilot count. This demonstrates efficient use of public funds toward real carbon reduction.
