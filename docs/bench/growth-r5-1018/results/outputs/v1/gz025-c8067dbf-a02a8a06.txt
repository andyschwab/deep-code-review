### Tracking Schema Audit and Consolidation Plan

**Objective:** Reduce event volume by 30% and storage costs while ensuring 100% compliance with UK healthcare retention laws (7-year rule) by December 1.

**Phase 1: Legal & Compliance Baseline (Days 1–5)**
*Action:* Before touching technical logs, define the "Must-Keep" list.
*   **Task:** Convene a session with Legal and the Chief Compliance Officer to explicitly list every data point legally required for 7-year retention under UK healthcare regulations.
*   **Deliverable:** A "Protected Schema" document. Any event containing these specific data fields is automatically flagged as **RETAIN** and excluded from deletion candidates.
*   **Outcome:** Clear boundary between legal necessity and operational noise.

**Phase 2: Usage Forensics & Question Mapping (Days 6–15)**
*Action:* Identify which of the remaining 40% of events actually answer a business question.
*   **Task:** Survey clinical and compliance team leads. For every reported dashboard or alert they use, map it back to the specific event names powering it.
*   **Rule:** If an event cannot be linked to a specific, named question (e.g., "Did the nurse view the patient's allergy list?"), mark it as **CANDIDATE FOR ARCHIVE**.
*   **Task:** Analyze query logs from your analytics platform. Identify events with zero queries in the last 90 days.
*   **Deliverable:** A "Usage Matrix" categorizing events into: *Active (Queried)*, *Passive (Defined but unused)*, and *Orphan (No definition/owner)*.

**Phase 3: Definition Harmonization (Days 16–25)**
*Action:* Resolve conflicting definitions for core actions like 'patient login' and 'record view'.
*   **Task:** Select the most accurate definition from either legacy system as the "Source of Truth."
*   **Action:** Map legacy event names to new, standardized names (e.g., `legacy_login_v1` → `core_patient_login`).
*   **Deliverable:** A "Translation Layer" specification for the engineering team to implement, ensuring historical data remains queryable under new names.

**Phase 4: The Cut & Archive Strategy (Days 26–30)**
*Action:* Execute the reduction safely.
*   **Task:** Move all **CANDIDATE FOR ARCHIVE** events to cold storage (cheaper tier) rather than immediate deletion. This satisfies the "reduce storage costs" goal immediately while preserving data in case of audit surprises.
*   **Task:** Implement a "New Event Governance" rule: No new event name can be added to the production schema without a documented owner and a specific question it answers.
*   **Deliverable:** Final report for the IT Director showing the 30% cost reduction projection and the compliance sign-off.

**Immediate Next Steps for This Week:**
1.  Schedule the Legal/Compliance workshop to draft the "Protected Schema" list.
2.  Export the last 90 days of query logs to identify the top 20% of events driving 80% of insights.

*Note: Do not delete any data until the "Protected Schema" is signed off by Legal; archive to cold storage instead to mitigate risk.*
*This plan prioritizes compliance verification before technical consolidation to prevent regulatory breaches.*
