**Recommendation for Chief Revenue Officer: Redefining Conversion and Aligning Spend**

**1. Redefine "Conversion" Immediately**
Stop treating "Form Completion" as your primary conversion metric. It is currently a vanity metric driving volume while degrading value. For SunLease, a true **North Star Metric** must be **"Qualified Site Visits Booked"** (or further down the funnel, "Contracts Signed" if data latency allows). Form completion is merely an *input* event, not a value event. Optimizing ads for form fills incentivizes low-intent traffic; optimizing for qualified visits aligns marketing spend directly with sales capacity and revenue potential.

**2. The Measurement Framework: Quality-Adjusted Funnel**
Shift your analytics focus from the top of the funnel (Acquisition) to the leak in the middle (Activation/Retention of lead quality).
*   **Primary Metric:** Cost Per Qualified Visit (CPQV). Calculate this as `Total Ad Spend / Number of Qualified Site Visits`.
*   **Secondary Metric (Guardrail):** Form Submission Volume. Monitor this to ensure the pre-qualification step does not starve the pipeline entirely, but do not optimize for it.
*   **Event Taxonomy Update:** Instrument two distinct events:
    *   `form_submitted`: Properties must include `traffic_source`, `campaign_id`, and `device_type`.
    *   `lead_qualified`: Triggered only when sales confirms a site visit is viable. Properties must include `time_to_qualify` and `disqualification_reason`.
    *   *Note:* Ensure a stable, pseudonymous user ID links these events to track the drop-off rate per cohort.

**3. Strategic Decision: Implement Pre-Qualification**
The data (20% traffic up, qualification rate dropping from 25% to 15%) indicates the streamlined form is reducing friction for *unqualified* users. You are paying for noise.
*   **Action:** Introduce a high-friction pre-qualification step (e.g., asking for electricity bill amount or roof ownership status before the full form).
*   **Hypothesis:** This will decrease total `form_submitted` events by ~30% but increase the `lead_qualified` conversion rate back to >25%, lowering your overall CPQV.
*   **Validation Method:** Do not guess. Run an A/B test where 50% of traffic sees the current short form and 50% sees the new pre-qualified flow.
    *   **Pre-commitment:** Define the sample size based on detecting a 20% lift in CPQV, not just form fills.
    *   **Duration:** Run for a full sales cycle (e.g., 14–21 days) to capture the `lead_qualified` event. Do not peek at results after 48 hours; early reads on lead quality are statistically unreliable due to sales team processing lag.
    *   **Stop Rule:** If the pre-qual flow increases CPQV by >10% without dropping total qualified volume below the break-even point required to meet quarterly targets, ship to 100%.

**4. Budget Allocation Implication**
Reallocate the upcoming fiscal year budget away from channels with high form-fill volume but low `lead_qualified` rates. Shift spend toward channels that historically drive higher intent, even if their cost-per-form is higher. The goal is to maximize *revenue-generating actions*, not database size.
