**Deliverable: HeritageWalks Engagement Definition for Grant Committee**

**North Star Metric: "Heritage Connections"**
We propose replacing "Opened Articles" with **Heritage Connections**, defined as a user completing a **Meaningful Interaction** within a session. A Meaningful Interaction occurs when a user performs at least one of the following high-intent actions:
1.  **Favorites:** Adds a location or story to their personal list.
2.  **Share:** Shares a story externally or internally.
3.  **Deep Read:** Spends >45 seconds on an article *and* scrolls past the 50% mark (filtering out passive background tabs).
4.  **Visit Intent:** Clicks "Get Directions" or views the map pin for a specific location.

**Why This Matters**
Current data shows 2,500 downloads, but "Opened Articles" is a vanity metric; it rises even when users bounce in five seconds, masking a lack of real value. Our mission is connecting people to heritage, not maximizing screen time. Passive reading is ambiguous, but favoriting, sharing, or navigating to a location proves the content resonated enough to trigger action. This metric aligns directly with the grant's goal of preserving and activating local history.

**Measurement Plan & Instrumentation**
To track this immediately without fabricating benchmarks, we will instrument three specific events to answer named questions:
*   `story_favorited`: *Does the user want to return to this history?*
*   `story_shared`: *Is the user advocating for this heritage?*
*   `directions_requested`: *Is the digital story driving physical foot traffic?*

We will treat "Deep Read" as a derived metric from existing scroll and timer events, validated against a 45-second threshold to ensure intent. We cannot currently measure actual physical visits without new sensor data or surveys; therefore, "Directions Requested" serves as the verified proxy for offline activation. Any claim of actual footfall without this instrumentation is `UNVERIFIED`.

**Grant Justification Narrative**
"In the next fiscal cycle, HeritageWalks will shift success measurement from passive consumption to active connection. Our North Star, 'Heritage Connections,' tracks users who favorite, share, or navigate to historical sites. This ensures funding supports features that drive real-world engagement with Cork's history, rather than merely inflating pageview counts."

**Next Steps (This Week)**
1.  **Instrument:** Add tracking properties for `scroll_depth` and `time_on_page` to the existing article view event to calculate the "Deep Read" component.
2.  **Define Baseline:** Run a query on the last 30 days of data using the new definition to establish the current "Heritage Connections" rate before the grant submission.
3.  **Validate:** Confirm with the museum partners that "Directions Requested" is an acceptable proxy for physical visits in the grant report, noting the limitation.

*Note: We are comparing your metrics only to your own historical baseline; no external industry benchmarks for "good" engagement rates are used as they vary wildly by niche.*
*Note: Actual physical visitation remains `UNVERIFIED` until a post-visit survey or geofence trigger is implemented.*
