**Behavioral Signal Framework for Clinical Advisory Board**
**Subject:** Differentiating Engagement from Distress Post-Empathy Module Launch
**Date:** [Current Date] | **Context:** Feb 20 launch; DAU 800 → 950

**1. Core Measurement Strategy: The Value Matrix**
Do not weigh login frequency against session length in isolation; their ratio defines the signal. Map users into a 2x2 matrix to distinguish healthy usage from crisis:
*   **High Frequency / Moderate Duration:** Indicates **Healthy Habituation**. Users return often for brief, resolved interactions (the "aha" moment is recurring).
*   **Low Frequency / Extreme Duration:** Indicates **Unresolved Distress**. Users rarely return, but when they do, they remain stuck in long loops. This is the primary harm signal.
*   **High Frequency / Extreme Duration:** Indicates **Dependency or Crisis Escalation**. Users are living in the bot. While possibly engaged, this risks replacing human intervention.

**2. Specific Data Patterns Indicating Harm**
The clinical board should flag the new module as potentially harmful if the following patterns emerge in the cohort post-Feb 20:
*   **The "Looping" Pattern:** Session duration increases, but the *completion rate* of specific conversation flows decreases. If users spend 40% more time but complete 20% fewer distinct topics, the bot is likely failing to resolve queries, causing circular dialogue.
*   **The "Late-Night Spike":** A disproportionate increase in session length occurring between 00:00–04:00 local time without a corresponding rise in successful "calming" or "resource referral" events. Extended night sessions often correlate with acute crisis rather than casual engagement.
*   **Retention Decay:** If the 7-day retention rate for users with >15 minute sessions drops below the baseline of users with <5 minute sessions. Healthy engagement predicts return; unresolved distress often leads to abandonment after a failed long attempt.
*   **Exit Sentiment Divergence:** If average session length rises while the frequency of positive closure keywords (or explicit "feel better" self-reports) declines. Rising time + falling positive closure = friction, not empathy.

**3. Immediate Interpretation Rules for the Board**
*   **Reject Vanity Metrics:** The rise from 800 to 950 DAU is unverified value. If the additional 150 users fall into the "Low Frequency / Extreme Duration" quadrant, growth is actually a safety incident.
*   **The Baseline Rule:** Do not compare current duration to industry benchmarks. Compare the post-Feb 20 cohort strictly to the pre-Feb 20 CedarMind baseline. A 20% increase in average session length is only "good" if resolution rates also rose; otherwise, it is a regression.
*   **The Crisis Threshold:** Define a "Crisis Session" not by a fixed minute count, but by deviation from the user's personal median. If a user's session exceeds their 90th percentile historical duration *and* contains repeated re-prompts of the same issue, this requires human review, regardless of the global average.
