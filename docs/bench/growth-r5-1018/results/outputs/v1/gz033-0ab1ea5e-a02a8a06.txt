### Instrumentation Plan for Fraud Model Impact (PayGuard)

**Objective:** Measure the net economic impact of the new ML model by balancing fraud loss against manual review costs and friction-induced volume loss.

**Core North Star:** *Net Verified Value* = (Total Successful Transaction Volume × Margin) − (Confirmed Fraud Loss) − (Cost of Manual Reviews).
*Reject vanity metrics like "Total Transactions Flagged" which rise even if the business loses money.*

#### 1. Event Taxonomy: Instrument Only What Answers a Question
Do not log every internal score. Log only these three events with the specified properties to answer specific business questions.

**Event A: `transaction_decision`**
*Question:* Did the model allow value to flow, and what was the risk context?
*Trigger:* Every transaction attempt.
*Properties:*
- `transaction_id` (stable ID for cohorting)
- `timestamp`
- `amount` & `currency`
- `is_high_value` (boolean, based on your threshold)
- `model_score` (0–100, for later threshold tuning)
- `decision`: `allowed` | `blocked_auto` | `sent_to_review`
- `user_segment`: (e.g., "new_merchant", "legacy_enterprise")

**Event B: `review_outcome`**
*Question:* What is the true cost of our manual team, and how accurate was the "sent_to_review" decision?
*Trigger:* When a human analyst closes a ticket.
*Properties:*
- `transaction_id` (joins to Event A)
- `review_duration_seconds` (calculates labor cost)
- `final_verdict`: `fraud_confirmed` | `false_positive` | `true_positive_allowed`
- `action_taken`: `refunded` | `completed_late` | `blocked_final`

**Event C: `friction_drop_off`**
*Question:* Are we losing legitimate volume due to added friction (your 15% assumption)?
*Trigger:* User abandons flow after a delay or extra step.
*Properties:*
- `transaction_id`
- `stage_failed`: `review_wait_timeout` | `user_abandoned_challenge`
- `time_in_review_queue_seconds`

#### 2. Critical Data Fields for Cost Evaluation
To present to the Risk Compliance Officer, you must join these events to calculate:
1.  **Fraud Cost:** Sum of `amount` where `final_verdict` = `fraud_confirmed`.
2.  **Review Cost:** Count of `review_outcome` events × Average Cost Per Minute × `review_duration_seconds`.
3.  **Friction Cost:** Sum of `amount` for `friction_drop_off` events (legitimate money lost).

*Note on Identity:* Ensure `transaction_id` is stable across sessions to track users through the review queue, but keep it pseudonymous. Route any PII handling to your privacy compliance protocol.

#### 3. Analysis Method: Retention & Baseline First
Do not compare your results to industry benchmarks. Compare March 1st+ data against your own February baseline.
- **Baseline:** Current 200 flags/day @ 5% false positive rate.
- **Hypothesis Check:** If `friction_drop_off` rises significantly without a matching drop in `fraud_confirmed`, the 15% volume gain assumption is failing.
- **Funnel Order:** Analyze **Retention** of legitimate high-value users first. If they abandon due to review delays, acquisition of new merchants is wasted spend.
