### ScootBarcelona Pilot Metric Framework (May 1 Launch)

**1. The North Star: Safe Completed Trips per Scooter per Day**
Reject "rides per scooter" as your sole North Star; it is a vanity metric that rises even when damage rates spike and community sentiment sours. Your new North Star is **Safe Completed Trips per Scooter per Day**. This metric only increases when a user successfully completes a ride *without* triggering a damage event or a compliance violation. It aligns utilization directly with sustainability.

**2. The Balanced Scorecard (Input Metrics)**
To move the North Star, track these three specific inputs. Do not instrument anything else until these questions are answered.

*   **Utilization & Value:** *Average Trip Duration* and *Completion Rate*.
    *   *Question:* Are users getting where they need to go, or abandoning rides early due to poor parking availability?
    *   *Reading Rule:* If duration drops while ride count rises, users are taking short, reckless hops. If completion rate falls, friction (like broken scooters or bad parking) is blocking value.
*   **Safety & Asset Health:** *Incident Reports per 100 Rides* (Primary Guardrail).
    *   *Question:* Is higher utilization causing physical harm or asset destruction?
    *   *Reading Rule:* Compare this strictly to your Gracia baseline (20% damage rate). Any pilot zone exceeding a 5% incident rate triggers an immediate pause in that zone. Do not compare to industry averages; compare to your own break-even point where repair costs erase ride revenue.
*   **Community Impact:** *Geofencing Compliance Rate at Stop*.
    *   *Question:* Are riders parking in designated zones or blocking sidewalks?
    *   *Reading Rule:* Measure the percentage of rides ending in a valid geofence. A rate below 90% indicates the "reckless parking" hypothesis is true. This is the metric the City Council cares about most.

**3. How to Read the Pilot Data**
Read the funnel bottom-up, starting with **Community Impact**, then **Safety**, then **Utilization**.
*   **Step 1 (Retention/Compliance):** If Geofencing Compliance is <90%, acquisition is wasted. No amount of ride volume justifies sidewalk blockage. Fix parking incentives before scaling.
*   **Step 2 (Safety):** If Incident Reports >5%, the product is unsafe. High ride frequency with high damage is a failure, not growth.
*   **Step 3 (Utilization):** Only if Compliance and Safety are green, optimize for *Safe Completed Trips*.

**4. Experiment Rigor for the Pilot**
Do not peek at data daily to declare a "win." Pre-commit to a **2-week evaluation window** for each zone. Define your success threshold now: "We expand only if Zone X achieves >4 Safe Completed Trips/scooter/day AND <5% Incident Rate AND >90% Compliance." Any result read before the 2-week mark is `UNVERIFIED`.

**5. This-Week Steps**
1.  **Instrumentation:** Define three events: `ride_completed` (with duration), `incident_reported` (with type), and `parking_validated` (boolean). Ensure every event carries a pseudonymous user ID to track repeat offenders.
2.  **Dashboard Setup:** Build a single view showing the three input metrics segmented by zone. Hard-code the red thresholds (5% incident, 90% compliance) so violations are visually obvious.
3.  **Council Presentation Prep:** Draft the narrative that you are measuring "Safe Completed Trips," explicitly rejecting raw volume to prioritize city safety. Present the Gracia 20% damage rate as the baseline you are committed to reducing, not hiding.

*Note: All benchmarks cited are derived from your stated Gracia data and logical break-even points; no external market averages were invented. Ensure user IDs are pseudonymized to comply with privacy regulations before ingestion.*
