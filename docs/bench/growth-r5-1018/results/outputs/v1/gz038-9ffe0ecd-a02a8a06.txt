### Success Metrics Framework for LexiScan General Release

**North Star Metric: Verified Time-to-Final-Approval**
Reject "Time Saved" as a standalone North Star; it incentivizes speed over correctness and ignores the cost of fixing AI errors. Instead, track **Verified Time-to-Final-Approval**: the elapsed time from document upload to the partner's final sign-off, *only* for documents where the AI's suggestions required zero critical overrides. This rises only when the tool delivers both speed and accuracy. If users accept suggestions quickly but spend hours correcting false positives later, this metric stays flat or worsens, correctly signaling a lack of value.

**Input Metrics (The Efficiency/Accuracy Balance)**
To move the North Star, track these three specific inputs:
1.  **Acceptance Rate of High-Confidence Flags:** The percentage of AI-flagged issues (marked >90% confidence) that users accept without editing. A drop here indicates rising false positives.
2.  **Override Density:** The number of user edits or rejections per 1,000 words processed. Segment this by "False Positive" (user rejects flag) vs. "Correction" (user edits AI suggestion). High False Positive density proves the AI is creating work.
3.  **Review Loop Count:** The average number of times a document returns to the queue after an AI pass. If the AI creates confusion, documents bounce back for human re-review, spiking this metric.

**The "Trustworthy Automation" Score**
Combine the above into a single composite index for your General Counsel dashboard:
*Formula:* `(Acceptance Rate of High-Confidence Flags) × (1 / (1 + Override Density))`
This score penalizes speed if it comes at the cost of accuracy. A high score means lawyers are trusting the automation; a low score means they are double-checking everything.

**Qualitative Feedback Loops**
Integrate these directly into the workflow to explain the quantitative spikes:
*   **One-Click Rejection Reason:** When a lawyer overrides an AI flag, require a single-tap reason (e.g., "Not an issue," "Wrong clause type," "Context missed"). Aggregate these weekly to identify specific model blind spots.
*   **The "False Alarm" Cohort:** Monthly, interview users who had an Override Density in the top 10%. Ask specifically: "Did this error make you distrust subsequent flags?" This links metric spikes to sentiment.
*   **Partner Sign-off Commentary:** Add an optional field at final approval: "Did the AI reduce your review time today? (Yes/No/Neutral)." Correlate these binary responses with the Verified Time-to-Final-Approval for that user.

**Reading the Data: What Each Result Means**
*   **If Processing Speed is up but Override Density is up:** The AI is generating noise. *Action:* Pause rollout to high-stakes teams; retrain the model on the specific rejection reasons before September 20.
*   **If Acceptance Rate is high but Review Loop Count is high:** The AI misses downstream impacts, forcing re-work. *Action:* Instrument deeper context windows; do not market "time saved" yet.
*   **If Verified Time-to-Final-Approval drops and Trust Score rises:** You have product-market fit. *Action:* Scale acquisition.
