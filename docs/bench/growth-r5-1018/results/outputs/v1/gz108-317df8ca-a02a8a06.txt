### Revised Growth Targets & Measurement Plan for LondonLiving

**1. North Star Metric: Weekly Value-Adjusted Transactions (VAT)**
Replace "Total Listings" (vanity) with **Weekly Value-Adjusted Transactions**. This counts only listings that result in a signed lease, weighted by the revenue mix (standard vs. premium service).
*   *Why:* Under fee caps, raw listing volume no longer correlates linearly to revenue. This metric rises only when users complete the core value exchange (leasing) and captures the shift to premium services.
*   *Input Metrics:* Premium Service Attachment Rate (% of leases including premium add-ons), Landlord Retention Rate (cohorts returning to list within 6 months), and Time-to-Lease.

**2. Realistic Growth Targets (Fiscal Year)**
Do not fabricate a "good" conversion rate for premium services without data. Instead, set targets based on your stated baselines and the "break-even" logic required to offset the volume drop.
*   **Volume Goal:** Accept the modeled **5% QoQ listing growth** as the baseline constraint. Do not target 20%; chasing this under new caps risks compliance or unsustainable subsidies.
*   **Revenue Offset Goal:** Calculate the specific **Premium Attachment Rate** needed to maintain total revenue flat if volume grows at only 5%.
    *   *Action:* If historical average revenue per lease was $X and the new cap reduces it to $Y, and premium service adds $Z, your target is: `(Old Revenue - New Volume * Y) / (New Volume * Z) = Required Attachment %`.
    *   *Target Setting:* Set Q1 target at **50% of that calculated break-even attachment rate** (since you have no baseline), scaling to 100% by Q4. This makes the goal measurable against your own math, not an invented industry benchmark.

**3. Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only What Answers a Question)**
Stop tracking generic pageviews. Instrument these three specific events to validate the pivot:
*   `premium_service_viewed`: (Property ID, Landlord ID). *Question:* Is there demand interest?
*   `premium_service_added_to_lease`: (Property ID, Landlord ID, Service Tier). *Question:* Are landlords willing to pay before the lease signs?
*   `lease_signed_with_premium`: (Property ID, Landlord ID, Total Value). *Question:* Does this actually drive the North Star?
*   *Identifier Seam:* Ensure `Landlord ID` is stable and pseudonymous across sessions to track retention cohorts, distinct from ops logs.

**4. Leading Indicators for Early Validation**
Since you lack baseline data for premium services, monitor these leading indicators weekly to detect failure early:
*   **Activation Leak:** % of landlords who view premium options but drop off before adding to lease. If >80%, the value proposition is unclear.
*   **Retention Cohort Slope:** Track landlords who listed in Month 1. Did they return in Month 2? If retention drops below your historical baseline immediately, the fee caps are driving churn faster than premium services can compensate.
*   **Mix Shift Velocity:** The week-over-week change in the % of total revenue coming from premium services. A flat line here after 4 weeks indicates the pivot is not gaining traction.

**5. This-Week Steps**
1.  **Compute the Break-Even Attachment Rate:** Use your finance team's numbers for the fee cap impact ($X vs $Y) and the price of the premium service ($Z) to define the exact % of landlords who must buy premium to keep revenue flat at 5% volume growth. Set this as your Q4 target.
2.  **Instrument the "Add to Lease" Event:** Deploy tracking for `premium_service_added_to_lease` immediately. Do not wait for the fiscal year start; you need 4 weeks of data to establish a baseline before the board meeting.
3.  **Run a "No-Peeking" Cohort Check:** Pull the retention curve for landlords who listed in the last 90 days. Compare it to the same period last year. If the slope is already flattening due to regulatory uncertainty, your Q1 target must be adjusted downward to reflect reality, not hope.

*Note: All targets above are derived from your internal break-even math, not external benchmarks which are unavailable for this specific regulatory shift. Any metric without the corresponding event instrumentation is currently `UNVERIFIED`.*
