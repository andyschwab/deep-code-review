### Revised Activation Definition & Measurement Plan

**New Activation Hypothesis:**
A customer is "activated" only when they establish **operational dependency**, defined as: *Completing one automated backup workflow OR integrating with one external tool (e.g., Slack, Jira, CI/CD) within the first 14 days.*

Volume (50GB) and manual sharing are vanity metrics in this context; they measure capacity usage, not value realization. Your data proves that high volume without automation yields low retention (40%), while low volume with automation yields high retention (90%). The core value of enterprise storage is not "holding data" but "securing workflows."

**What to Measure (Event Taxonomy):**
Instrument only these three events to answer specific questions:
1.  `automation_configured` (Properties: `source_type`, `frequency_schedule`)
    *   *Question:* Did the user set up a "set-and-forget" safety net?
2.  `integration_connected` (Properties: `integration_name`, `scope`)
    *   *Question:* Is CloudVault embedded in their daily toolchain?
3.  `active_seat_ratio` (Properties: `total_seats`, `seats_with_action_last_7d`)
    *   *Question:* Is adoption broad or siloed?

**How to Read the Data:**
*   **Retention First:** Do not analyze acquisition until you confirm retention of the *new* activation definition. Cohort users by whether they hit the new definition in Week 1. Compare the renewal rates of "Automated/Integrated" vs. "Manual/High-Volume."
*   **The Input Metrics:** Track the % of new signups who complete an automation or integration within 14 days. This is your primary input metric to move the North Star (Annual Recurring Revenue from retained clients).
*   **Reject Vanity:** Ignore total GB uploaded as a success metric unless correlated with an automation event. A 1TB manual upload is a ticking churn bomb; a 10GB automated stream is a sticky asset.

**Experiment Design for Onboarding Redesign:**
To validate this before full rollout, run an A/B test on the upcoming onboarding emails:
*   **Control:** Current guidance (pushing for 50GB upload and folder sharing).
*   **Variant:** New guidance (step-by-step wizard to set up *one* automated backup or *one* integration).
*   **Primary Metric:** % of users completing the target action within 14 days.
*   **Guardrail Metric:** Total support tickets per user (ensure the new flow isn't confusing).
*   **Rule:** Pre-commit to a sample size of 300 new clients (150 per arm) or a 4-week duration. Read the result **once** at the end. Do not peek at day 2; early differences in setup speed do not predict year-long retention.

**This Week's Steps:**

3.  **Draft:** Write the Variant onboarding email sequence focusing solely on the "First Automation" milestone, removing references to storage volume targets.

*Note: Your baseline (90% renewal with automation) is your benchmark; do not seek external industry averages for "good" activation rates.*
