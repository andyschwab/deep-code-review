# SkyDrop Beta Measurement Framework: Technical Performance & Human Experience

**North Star Metric:** "Successful Calm Deliveries" (SCD) per week.
*Definition:* A delivery is counted as an SCD only if: (1) The package lands within the target zone (<2m error), (2) Total flight time is within the promised window, AND (3) The recipient reports a "Calm/Confident" sentiment (score ≥4/5) in the post-flight survey.
*Why:* Speed alone is vanity if it terrifies users. This metric rises only when the service is both fast and psychologically acceptable.

## 1. Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only What Answers a Named Question)
Do not instrument everything. Capture these specific events to answer the viability questions:

| Event Name | Trigger | Properties Captured | Question Answered |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | : |
| `flight_initiated` | Drone leaves hub | `distance_km`, `weather_condition`, `time_of_day` | Does context affect success rates? |
| `landing_attempt` | Drone begins descent | `landing_zone_type` (yard, driveway, patio) | Which surfaces cause anxiety or errors? |
| `landing_completed` | Rotors stop, package released | `landing_error_meters`, `noise_level_db`, `duration_seconds` | Is the technical landing precise and quiet? |
| `user_anxiety_peak` | (Proxy) User opens app/tracks drone | `time_to_first_check`, `tracking_refresh_rate` | Does excessive tracking indicate high anxiety? |
| `post_flight_survey` | User submits feedback | `sentiment_score` (1-5), `noise_complaint` (Y/N), `would_recommend` (Y/N) | Did the human experience match the technical performance? |
| `support_ticket_created` | User contacts support | `category` (noise, safety, delay, damage) | What specific failures drive operational cost? |

**Identifier Seam:** Use a stable, pseudonymous `recipient_id` to link flight data with survey responses across sessions. Do not store PII in analytics; route consent management to privacy compliance protocols.

## 2. The Funnel: Read Retention/Sentiment First
Do not pour marketing into acquisition until the "Calm" retention is proven. Analyze in this order:
1.  **Sentiment Retention:** Of users who received a delivery, what % report "Calm/Confident" on their *second* delivery? If this drops, speed is irrelevant; the product fails on anxiety.
2.  **Activation (First Successful Calm Delivery):** Did the first experience meet the SCD definition? If not, onboardings are broken.
3.  **Acquisition:** Only analyze volume once steps 1 and 2 are stable.

## 3. Go/No-Go Decision Criteria (Pre-Committed)
Before November 10, agree on these thresholds based on your 500 test deliveries. No peeking at interim data to move goalposts.

*   **GO:** ≥85% of deliveries meet "Successful Calm Delivery" definition AND <5% of users file a noise/safety ticket.
*   **NO-GO:** SCD rate <70% OR >15% of users report "Anxious/Scared" sentiment.
*   **EXTEND:** SCD between 70-84%. Run 200 more tests focusing on the specific `landing_zone_type` causing failures.

## 4. This-Week Steps
1.  **Define the "Anxiety" Proxy:** Finalize the 3-question micro-survey (Sentiment, Noise, Safety) to be sent via SMS/App immediately after `landing_completed`. Ensure it takes <30 seconds.
2.  **Instrument the Seam:** Engineering to implement `recipient_id` linking between the flight telemetry database and the survey tool. Verify one end-to-end trace before Oct 10.
3.  **Pre-Commit the Dashboard:** Build a single view showing "Successful Calm Deliveries" trended daily, segmented by `landing_zone_type`. Hide raw speed metrics from the primary view to prevent vanity distraction.

*Note: All benchmarks above are derived from your pre-committed decision rules, not external market data. Real figures must be pulled from your analytics tool once the beta begins.*
*If the "Anxiety" survey response rate is <40%, the SCD metric is UNVERIFIED; prioritize survey delivery mechanics immediately.*
