### PixelQuest Pre-Launch Measurement Framework
**Audience:** Founding Team & Local Publishing Partner (South Korea)
**Context:** Soft Launch April 5th | Beta Baseline: 18m session, 25% D1 Retention

#### 1. The North Star Metric
**Weekly Active Users Completing a Core Guild Action.**
*Definition:* Count of unique users who join or create a guild AND complete one cooperative game session within a 7-day window.
*Why:* Session length (18m) is a vanity metric if users leave forever after day one. This metric ties your hypothesis (social guilds drive value) to actual retention behavior. It rises only when users get real social value.

#### 2. The Top Five Metrics (AARRR Funnel)
1.  **Retention (Priority #1):** D7 Retention of users who joined a guild vs. those who did not.
    *   *Actionable Signal:* If guild members retain 2x higher than solo players, the hypothesis is valid. If not, the guild mechanic is broken or irrelevant.
2.  **Activation:** % of new users who join/create a guild within their first 3 sessions.
    *   *Actionable Signal:* Measures if the "aha" moment is reachable. If <20%, the onboarding flow hides the social feature.
3.  **Engagement (Core Action):** Average cooperative sessions per weekly active user.
    *   *Actionable Signal:* Distinct from total session time; measures depth of the specific value proposition.
4.  **Monetization (Guardrail):** ARPDAU (Average Revenue Per Daily Active User) segmented by guild status.
    *   *Decision:* Track from Day 1 but do not optimize for it yet. In Korea's competitive market, early monetization friction can kill retention. Use this only to ensure new features don't crash revenue.
5.  **Referral:** Invites sent per active guild.
    *   *Actionable Signal:* Validates organic growth potential in a high-cost acquisition market.

#### 3. Instrumentation Plan (Immediate Actions)
Stop tracking "everything." Implement only these events to answer specific questions before April 5th:
*   `guild_created` (Properties: `user_id`, `timestamp`, `game_mode`) → *Answers: Are users building communities?*
*   `guild_joined` (Properties: `user_id`, `invite_source`, `timestamp`) → *Answers: Is the viral loop working?*
*   `co_op_session_start` (Properties: `user_id`, `guild_id`, `duration`) → *Answers: Is the core loop actually social?*
*   `purchase_attempt` (Properties: `user_id`, `item_id`, `success_bool`) → *Answers: Is monetization blocking flow?*

**Identifier Seam:** Ensure a stable, pseudonymous `user_id` persists across app reinstalls to accurately measure D7/D30 retention cohorts. Do not use device IDs alone.

#### 4. Vanity vs. Actionable Signals
*   **Vanity:** "Total Downloads" or "Average Session Length." These rise even if the product fails. A user playing 60 minutes once and never returning is a failure, not a success.
*   **Actionable:** "D1 Retention of Guild Members." This tells you if the specific mechanic drives stickiness.
*   **Cultural Context:** In South Korea, social proof and community are paramount. If the "Guild" metric lags, local marketing spend will burn. Prioritize fixing the social onboarding over adding content.

#### 5. Monetization Strategy
**Track but do not optimize.** Record all transaction events immediately to establish a baseline and detect regressions (guardrail). However, your primary optimization target for the first 4 weeks must be **Retention** and **Guild Activation**. Monetization optimization before establishing a retained user base is premature and risks churning the 2,000 beta users' worth of learnings.

#### This Week's Steps
1.  **Define the Event Schema:** Finalize the JSON properties for `guild_joined` and `co_op_session_start` by Wednesday; ensure engineering implements them with the stable `user_id`.
2.  **Build the Cohort Query:** Create a dashboard view comparing D7 retention for "Guild Users" vs. "Solo Users" using beta data proxies; if the gap is <15%, delay the social-heavy marketing push.
3.  **Set the Guardrail:** Configure an alert that triggers if ARPDAU drops >10% week-over-week during the soft launch, indicating a monetization friction point.

*Note: All benchmarks must be derived from your April 5th soft-launch baseline, not external industry averages. No external "good" retention rate exists for your specific context.*
