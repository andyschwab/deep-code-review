**To:** CEO, Product Design Lead
**From:** Director of Analytics
**Date:** October 18
**Subject:** Urgent Analysis: Churn Increase Post-'Harvest Prediction' Deployment

**Executive Summary**
Between October 3 and October 17, weekly churn among the 450 farmers with access to the new 'harvest prediction' feature rose from 1.8% to 2.1%. While cumulative signups remain stable, this retention leak indicates the feature may be eroding core value rather than enhancing it. Per our growth analytics protocol, we prioritize retention diagnostics over acquisition metrics; pouring traffic into a leaking bucket wastes resources. The following hypotheses address why transparency might be driving frustration.

**Three Distinct Hypotheses**

1.  **The "Accuracy Mismatch" Hypothesis (Data Integrity):**
    The recent rainy season caused actual harvest yields to deviate significantly from the model's predictions. If the dashboard displayed confident but incorrect forecasts, farmers likely lost trust in the platform's core utility.
    *   *Reading Rule:* If churn is highest among users whose *actual* logged harvests differed by >15% from the *predicted* values, the model accuracy is the root cause.
    *   *Action:* If confirmed, immediately flag predictions as "Beta/Unverified" or disable the feature until the model is retrained on wet-season data.

2.  **The "Cognitive Overload" Hypothesis (UX/Activation):**
    The new interface introduces complexity that obscures the "core action" (logging daily inputs). Farmers may be confused by the new layout, failing to complete their primary workflow, leading to abandonment.
    *   *Reading Rule:* If the drop-off occurs specifically at the "View Prediction" step, and subsequent "Log Harvest" events decrease by >10% compared to the control group (farmers without access), the UI is the blocker.
    *   *Action:* If confirmed, simplify the dashboard to a single "Insight" card or revert to the previous layout for the planting cycle.

3.  **The "Premature Exposure" Hypothesis (Segmentation):**
    The feature was deployed to all 450 users regardless of readiness. Early adopters might tolerate bugs, but mainstream farmers may find the incomplete feature disruptive.
    *   *Reading Rule:* If churn is uniform across all farm sizes but correlates strictly with "Time on Page" < 30 seconds (bounce), users are rejecting the feature immediately upon sight.
    *   *Action:* If confirmed, restrict access to a voluntary opt-in cohort of 20 friendly farms for iterative testing.

**Validation Plan (Next 5 Days)**

We will validate the **Accuracy Mismatch Hypothesis** first, as incorrect data poses the highest reputational risk before the planting cycle.

*   **Data Source:** Existing application logs and harvest entry records (no new instrumentation required).
*   **Method:**
    1.  **Cohort Split:** Isolate the 450 users with access.
    2.  **Event Query:** Calculate the `Prediction_Error_Rate` for each user: `(ABS(Predicted_Yield - Actual_Logged_Yield) / Predicted_Yield)`.
    3.  **Correlation Check:** Compare the `Prediction_Error_Rate` against the `Churn_Event` (account deletion or 14-day inactivity) for the Oct 3–17 window.
*   **Success Metric:** A statistically significant correlation where users with >15% error rates show churn >2.5%.
*   **Decision Tripwire:**
    *   **If Correlation Exists:** Rollback the feature by October 23. Communicate to users that we are "refining accuracy for the rainy season."
    *   **If No Correlation:** Proceed to test the UX Hypothesis via a hotfix A/B test on button placement, but keep the feature live for observation.

**Recommendation**
Do not expand access. Prepare a rollback script now. We will run the accuracy query tomorrow morning and present the final go/no-go decision by EOD October 20.

***

*Note: All metrics above are derived from TerraForm's internal baseline; no external industry benchmarks were applied. The "churn" definition assumes account deletion or 14-day inactivity as per standard SaaS retention cohorts.*
