1.  **Primary Guardrail (Critical):** Crash Rate per Active Session.

2.  **North Star Candidate:** Successful Hazard Confirmations (not just "alerts triggered").
    *   *Gap:* We are measuring "alerts triggered" (vanity/activity), which includes errors and tests. We must distinguish between a worker seeing a real hazard vs. a worker spamming a button because the app froze.
    *   *Action:* We cannot calculate the true North Star yet because the crash data contaminates the session logs.

**Interpretation of Conflicting Signals**
Your assumption that workers are "testing sensitivity" due to instability is the most probable cause. In safety contexts, high frequency without confirmed resolution usually signals anxiety or confusion, not utility. If the interface were clear and the sensor stable, users would trigger an alert once per hazard. Repeated triggers suggest the feedback loop is broken (e.g., user triggers -> app lags/crashes -> user tries again). Pouring more users into this funnel (acquisition) would amplify the reputational risk, not the value.

**Decision Framework**
*   **If we ship now:** We risk a public failure where the safety tool fails during an actual emergency. The cost of one missed hazard due to a crash outweighs the benefit of 18% more "alerts."
*   **If we delay:** We lose two weeks of potential data but preserve the brand's "safety first" promise.
