### Essential Event Taxonomy: FitPulse Unboxing & Setup

To isolate whether goal setting *causes* adherence or merely correlates with it, and to enable targeted onboarding, instrument only these configuration and sync events. Each event answers a specific question about the user's journey and the hypothesis.

**1. `device_first_sync`**
*   **Properties:** `device_id` (pseudonymous), `firmware_version`, `sync_timestamp`.
*   **Question Answered:** Did the user successfully pair and sync the device after unboxing? (Activation baseline).
*   **Action:** Triggers the "Welcome" email sequence. If this event is missing 24h after purchase scan, the unboxing experience is broken.

**2. `setup_step_viewed`**
*   **Properties:** `step_name` (e.g., "profile_creation", "goal_setting", "notifications"), `session_id`.
*   **Question Answered:** Where do users drop off during the initial wizard?
*   **Action:** Identifies friction points in the flow. High drop-off at "goal_setting" suggests the UI is confusing, not that users lack motivation.

**3. `goal_setting_attempted`**
*   **Properties:** `attempt_id`, `has_custom_goal` (boolean), `timestamp`.
*   **Question Answered:** Did the user engage with the goal interface, regardless of completion?
*   **Action:** Differentiates between users who skipped the step entirely vs. those who struggled to set a value.

**4. `goal_configured`**
*   **Properties:** `goal_type` (e.g., "weekly_steps"), `goal_value` (integer), `is_default` (boolean), `session_id`.
*   **Question Answered:** Did the user successfully commit to a specific target?
*   **Action:** This is the primary treatment indicator. Users triggering this enter the "Goal Set" cohort for your A/B analysis against those who did not. Enables the "You're all set" email.

**5. `setup_completed`**
*   **Properties:** `total_duration_seconds`, `steps_skipped` (list), `timestamp`.
*   **Question Answered:** Did the user finish the entire onboarding flow?
*   **Action:** Final trigger for the marketing director's "Ready to Move" campaign.

**6. `wear_time_sync_daily`**
*   **Properties:** `date`, `total_wear_hours` (derived from last sync vs current sync, capped at 24), `device_id`.
*   **Question Answered:** Is the user wearing the device ≥18 hours/day? (The outcome metric).
*   **Action:** The dependent variable for your hypothesis. Compare the 60-day retention of the `goal_configured` cohort vs. the `setup_completed` (but no goal) cohort.

**Analysis Logic for the Hypothesis**
Do not compare "Goal Set" users to all users. Compare **`goal_configured`** users against users who triggered **`setup_step_viewed` (goal_setting)** but *did not* trigger **`goal_configured`**. Both groups showed intent by entering the screen; the difference isolates the impact of completing the action versus just being motivated enough to look.

**Privacy & Implementation Note**
No continuous heart rate or location data is included. All `device_id` values must be hashed before leaving the device to comply with GDPR. The `wear_time_sync_daily` metric is calculated server-side from sync intervals, not streamed continuously.
