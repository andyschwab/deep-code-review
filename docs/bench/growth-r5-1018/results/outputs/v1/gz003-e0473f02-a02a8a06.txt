**Report Structure: Onboarding Video Impact & Revenue Sustainability**
**Audience:** Mrs. Reyes, Board Member
**Objective:** Determine if video production costs are justified by net revenue lift, isolating completion gains from downstream drop-off and support costs.

**1. Executive Summary: The "Real" Lift**
State the primary finding immediately: Does the 14% completion lift translate to a statistically significant increase in **Net Revenue Per Signup (NRPS)**?
*   **Formula:** `(Total Revenue from Cohort - Attributable Support Costs) / Total Signups in Cohort`.
*   **The Verdict:** Present the NRPS for Video vs. Text. If Video NRPS is lower despite higher completion, the hypothesis is flawed.

**2. Critical Data Points (The Isolation Layer)**
To answer Mrs. Reyes' concern about post-onboarding drop-off and support strain, the report must include these four specific metrics, segmented by variant (Video vs. Text):

*   **A. Paid Module Conversion Rate:** The percentage of users who completed onboarding *and* purchased the first paid module. This isolates whether video creates "happy completers" or "paying customers."
*   **B. Week-4 Retention Rate:** The percentage of users still active 28 days after signup. This addresses the "sustainability" question; if video users churn faster after the first module, the initial lift is vanity.
*   **C. Support Ticket Volume per 100 Users:** Total tickets generated by the cohort divided by cohort size, multiplied by 100. Multiply this rate by your average cost-per-ticket (staff time) to quantify the "strain" in pesos.
*   **D. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Payback Period:** How many days it takes for the margin from a video-user to cover the incremental video production cost.

**3. Presenting Uncertainty: The "Break-Even" Analysis**
Do not present a single predicted number. Instead, present a **Sensitivity Table** showing the conditions under which the video investment fails. This clarifies the risk for the board:

| Scenario | Assumed Drop-off After Module 1 | Assumed Support Cost Increase | Net Revenue Impact | Decision |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- :--- |
| **Base Case** | Equal to Text | +10% | **Positive** | Scale Video |
| **Risk Case A** | +15% higher than Text | Equal to Text | **Neutral** | Pause & Investigate |
| **Risk Case B** | Equal to Text | +25% higher than Text | **Negative** | Revert to Text |

The Base Case assumes no difference in long-term retention; if video users drop off 15% faster after payment, the production cost is not recovered."

**4. Recommendation & Next Step**
*   **If NRPS is positive:** Approve production for the next quarter, contingent on monitoring the support ticket ratio weekly.
*   **If NRPS is negative or UNVERIFIED:** Do not scale. Extend the measurement window to capture full funnel data (payment to week-4) before committing budget.
