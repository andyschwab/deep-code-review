### Activation Signal Prioritization for LagosConnect

**North Star Candidate:** Weekly Active Users completing a "Local Connection Loop" (defined below), rather than raw signups or total messages sent.

**Recommended Activation Definition:**
Discard "3 DMs sent" as your primary activation signal; the data confirms it is a vanity metric that does not correlate with retention. Instead, define activation as a user completing a **Local Connection Loop** within 7 days of signup. This loop must include three specific, low-bandwidth events that prove value delivery in the Lagos context:

1.  **Event: `group_joined_verified`**
    *   **Properties:** `group_type` (e.g., market, church, estate), `join_method` (invite_link vs. search).
    *   **Rationale:** In community-centric markets, value lies in the group, not the individual profile. Joining a verified local group anchors the user to a recurring content stream without requiring active data consumption immediately.
2.  **Event: `content_consumed_offline_cached`**
    *   **Properties:** `content_type`, `cache_success` (boolean), `data_size_kb`.
    *   **Rationale:** Given high data costs and intermittent connectivity, a user who successfully caches content for later reading demonstrates an intent to return when connectivity is poor. This is a stronger signal than a fleeting view.
3.  **Event: `low_friction_interaction_completed`**
    *   **Properties:** `interaction_type` (e.g., voice_note_sent, emoji_reaction, poll_vote), `data_cost_estimate`.
    *   **Rationale:** Replace heavy text/DM requirements with low-data interactions. A voice note or reaction is often more culturally relevant and data-efficient than typing three distinct messages. If they interact with the group they joined, the loop is closed.

**Instrumentation Plan (MVP Stage):**
Do not instrument everything. Add only these three events to your taxonomy immediately. Each must carry a `user_cohort_id` (linked to the specific community leader who onboarded them) to allow segmentation by source quality later. Ensure the user identifier is pseudonymous and stable across sessions to track this loop even if the app closes due to connection loss.

**Immediate Next Steps for Yaba Team:**
1.  **Query Current Logs:** Run a query counting users who performed all three events above within 7 days of signup since Jan 20th. Compare the return rate of this specific cohort against the "3 DMs" cohort. If the "Loop" cohort shows higher Day-7 return, adopt this as your official activation definition.
2.  **Onboarding A/B Test:** Design a test where the control group is prompted to "Send 3 Messages" and the variant is guided to "Join a Group and Listen to a Voice Note." Measure which group completes the "Local Connection Loop" faster. Pre-commit to a sample size of 1,000 users per variant and read the result only after the full cycle completes to avoid peeking errors.

**Note:** Long-term retention cohorts cannot be calculated yet; focus strictly on Day-7 return as the leading indicator. All metrics must be compared against your own baseline trends, not external benchmarks.
