### Measurement Plan: Q4 Revenue Alignment for SalesForce IL

**1. The North Star & Leading Indicator**
Reject "demo scheduled" as your primary leading indicator; it measures activity, not value. Adopt **"Proposal Sent within 14 Days of Sign-up"** as your single North Star input metric.
*   **Reasoning:** In a 90-day B2B cycle, a demo is a step, but a proposal signals validated fit and buying intent. If your hypothesis holds (demo drives +30% conversion), the signal will appear first in the *rate* of proposals sent, not just demo bookings.
*   **Definition of Qualified Opportunity:** A lead is "Qualified" only when: (1) Demo completed within 48 hours of sign-up, AND (2) Specific pain-point budget confirmed by the prospect during the demo.

**2. Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only These)**
Do not track every click. Instrument these three events to answer specific questions:
*   `signup_created`: (Property: `source`, `company_size`). *Question: Where is volume coming from?*
*   `demo_completed`: (Property: `time_to_schedule`, `attendee_role`). *Question: Are we meeting the 24h SLA?*
*   `proposal_sent`: (Property: `days_since_signup`, `demo_attended_bool`). *Question: Does the demo actually accelerate the proposal?*

**3. Handling the 90-Day Lag (Cohort Analysis)**
Do not wait 90 days to read results. Analyze by **Signup Week Cohort**.
*   **Week 2 Read:** Measure % of cohort that received a proposal. This is your early warning system. If Week 2 proposal rates drop, Q4 revenue will miss in 90 days.
*   **Week 6 Read:** Measure % of proposals converted to "Negotiation" stage.
*   **Baseline Comparison:** Compare the "Demo <24h" group against the "Demo >24h or No Demo" group within the same cohort. Do not invent a "30%" benchmark; calculate the actual lift from your 200 leads.

**4. Reporting Cadence & Audience**
*   **Who receives this:** Sales Director and the 10 Account Executives (AEs).
*   **Frequency:** **Bi-weekly** (every two weeks).
    *   *Why:* Weekly is too noisy for a 90-day cycle; monthly is too slow to adjust outreach.
*   **Format:** A single-page dashboard showing:
    1.  Current Cohort's "Proposal Sent" rate vs. Previous Cohort.
    2.  % of Leads meeting the "Qualified Opportunity" definition.
    3.  Average time from Signup to Proposal.

**5. Immediate Action (This Week)**
1.  **Audit the 200 leads:** Manually tag them now into "Demo <24h" and "Demo >24h/None". Calculate the current proposal rate difference. If the difference is <10%, the 24h SLA may not be the lever; if >20%, enforce the SLA strictly.
2.  **Align Incentives:** Change AE commission triggers to reward "Qualified Opportunities" (as defined above) generated, not just demos booked. This prevents gaming the system with low-quality demos.
3.  **Stop Tracking:** Remove any metrics tracking "page views" or "email opens" from the executive report. They are vanity metrics that distract from the 90-day revenue reality.

*Note: If your current CRM cannot filter by "days since signup" automatically, export the raw data to a spreadsheet for the first two bi-weekly reviews before engineering a dashboard.*
*Ensure the "Proposal Sent" event includes a property for "Deal Value" to verify if faster proposals correlate with higher contract values or just smaller, easier deals.*
