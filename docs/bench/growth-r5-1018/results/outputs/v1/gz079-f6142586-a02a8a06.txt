**Executive Summary: Do Not Roll Out Globally Next Week**

The reported 25% lift is **UNVERIFIED** as a causal effect of the feature. Because the test window (April 1–14) coincides exactly with a market shock driving organic remote adoption, the treatment and control groups were likely exposed to the same external confounder. Without isolating the feature's impact from the market trend, rolling out globally risks attributing situational growth to product value, potentially scaling a feature that adds no marginal benefit.

**Recommended Approach to Isolate Impact**

To provide the CEO with a realistic assessment, we must re-analyze the data using one of the following methods before any deployment decision:

1.  **Difference-in-Differences (DiD) Analysis**:
    Compare the *change* in Daily Active Users (DAU) for the treatment group against the *change* in a synthetic control or a holdout segment that was not exposed to the feature but was exposed to the market shock. If a pure control group exists within the app (users not eligible for the test), calculate: `(Treatment_Post - Treatment_Pre) - (Control_Post - Control_Pre)`. This subtracts the market shock effect common to both groups, leaving the estimated feature lift.

2.  **Pre-Trend Baseline Extension**:
    Analyze the DAU trend for 30 days prior to April 1st. Project this baseline forward to April 14th assuming no intervention. Compare the actual treatment group performance against this projected baseline. While less robust than DiD (as it assumes the market shock affects the projection equally), it helps visualize the deviation caused by the shock versus the feature.

3.  **Cohort Retention Check (The "Leaky Bucket" Test)**:
    Immediate DAU spikes often reflect acquisition surges, not value. Segment the April 1–14 users by cohort. If the feature provides real value, the *retention* of the treatment cohort in Week 2 (post-shock stabilization) should exceed the control cohort's retention. If retention curves converge once the shock stabilizes, the 25% lift was purely situational.

**Decision Framework for the CEO**

*   **If DiD shows <5% lift**: The feature is neutral or negligible. **Action**: Do not roll out. Re-prioritize engineering resources.
*   **If DiD shows >10% lift with stable retention**: The feature has standalone value. **Action**: Proceed with rollout, but monitor Week-2 retention closely as a guardrail metric.
*   **If data cannot be re-segmented (no clean control)**: The result remains **UNVERIFIED**. **Action**: Extend the test for 14 more days (April 15–28) assuming market volatility stabilizes, or run a "geo-split" test in a region less affected by the specific shock.
