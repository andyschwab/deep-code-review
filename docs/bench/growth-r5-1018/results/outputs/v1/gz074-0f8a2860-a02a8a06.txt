**Experiment Proposal: Priority Pickup Trade-off Analysis**
**Audience:** Raj, Operations Director
**Objective:** Validate revenue lift against platform reliability risks before June monsoon season.

**1. Traffic Split & Segmentation**
Do not run a global 50/50 test immediately. Given the risk of degrading service for standard users in high-density zones (Andheri), use a **geo-segmented, stepped rollout**:
*   **Phase 1 (Canary):** Limit to 5% of total traffic in **one** high-density zone (e.g., Andheri West only) during peak rush hours (6 PM–9 PM).
*   **Phase 2 (Expansion):** If Phase 1 guardrails hold, expand to 10% of traffic across two zones.
*   **Control Group:** Users in the same zone/time who do not see the priority option. This isolates the impact of priority pickups on standard wait times.

**2. Metrics Definition**
*   **Primary Metric (Success):** *Net Revenue Per Available Hour.* Calculate as: `(Priority Fee Revenue + Standard Ride Revenue) / Driver Hours`. This captures whether the 20% utilization gain translates to actual profit after accounting for any lost standard rides.
*   **Guardrail Metric 1 (Critical Stop):** *Standard User ETA Delta.* Measure the percentage increase in wait time for non-priority users in the test zone vs. control.
    *   **Tripwire:** If standard ETAs increase by **>8%** (leaving a 2% buffer below your 10% theoretical threshold) for more than 30 minutes, **auto-halt** the experiment.
*   **Guardrail Metric 2 (Driver Sentiment):** *Driver Cancellation Rate & Support Tickets.* Track cancellations specifically tagged as "unfair dispatch" or "too far."
    *   **Tripwire:** If cancellation rates rise >5% above baseline or support tickets spike, pause immediately.

**3. Statistical Rigor & Duration**
*   **Design:** Pre-commit to a **fixed horizon** of 14 days (covering two full weekly cycles) to avoid "peeking" errors. Do not stop early if results look good; early significance is often noise.
*   **Sample Size:** Calculate the minimum detectable effect (MDE) based on current rush-hour volume. If Andheri rush hour generates 1,000 rides/hour, a 5% split yields 50 priority rides/hour. Ensure this volume is sufficient to detect the 8% ETA degradation with 80% power. If not, extend duration rather than increasing traffic %.
*   **Analysis Rule:** Read results **once** at the end of the 14 days. A result observed on Day 3 is `UNVERIFIED`.

**4. Risk Mitigation for Monsoon Launch**
*   **Hard Cap:** Implement a hard system limit capping priority bookings at 8% of total requests in any 15-minute window, automatically reverting excess demand to the standard queue.
*   **Communication Plan:** Prepare a driver-facing FAQ explaining that priority fees subsidize base rates, addressing the "unfairness" concern proactively.
