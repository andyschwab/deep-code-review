### Early Growth Measurement Framework: GoShare New City Launches

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Renters (users completing a first vehicle unlock within 7 days of install).
*Reject vanity metrics like total app downloads or map views; they rise regardless of business value.*

**Input Metrics (The Funnel):**
1.  **Install → Verification Start:** % of users who open the ID upload screen.
2.  **Verification Start → Approval:** % of users approved instantly vs. delayed.
3.  **Approval → First Unlock:** % of approved users who book within 48 hours.
4.  **Map View → No Booking:** % of users viewing the map but not starting verification (identifies interest loss).

**Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only These):**
Every event below must include `city`, `user_id` (pseudonymous), and `timestamp`.
*   `app_installed`: Answers "How many entered the funnel?"
*   `verification_started`: Answers "Did they attempt the core requirement?"
*   `verification_approved`: Answers "Was the delay caused by us or the user?" (Property: `approval_latency_minutes`).
*   `map_viewed`: Answers "Did they browse without intent?"
*   `vehicle_unlocked`: Answers "Did they derive value?"

**Stage Strategy (MVP/Growth Hybrid):**
Since this is a new market launch (MVP behavior in new regions), focus strictly on **Activation** and **Retention** of the core action (first unlock). Do not instrument referral or revenue nuances yet.
*   **Cohort Analysis:** Segment strictly by `city` (Denver, Portland, Seattle) and `approval_latency` (Instant vs. >1 hour).
*   **The Diagnostic Question:** "Is the drop-off between *Verification Start* and *First Unlock* higher in cities with slower approval times compared to the home base baseline?"

**Experiment & Decision Rigor:**
*   **Baseline Comparison:** Compare new city conversion rates against the home base's 60% instant-verification benchmark. Do not invent a "good" rate for new cities; use the home base as the only reference.
*   **The Go/No-Go Trigger (June 15):**
    *   **GO:** If `Approval → First Unlock` conversion exceeds 45% (allowing for new market friction) AND `verification_approved` volume meets minimum traffic thresholds.
    *   **NO-GO:** If conversion is <30% AND the primary drop-off is linked to `approval_latency` > 2 hours, indicating a process failure rather than market fit.
    *   **EXTEND:** If data is insufficient (sample size < 200 verified users per city) or results are ambiguous due to external factors (e.g., weather events affecting all cities equally).

**This Week's Steps:**
1.  **Instrument:** Deploy the five events listed above with `city` and `approval_latency` properties by May 19th. Verify that `user_id` persists across sessions to track the 48-hour window accurately.
2.  **Query:** Build a single dashboard view showing the funnel conversion rates broken down by city and approval speed (Instant vs. Delayed).
3.  **Action:** If `verification_started` is high but `verification_approved` is low in a specific city, pause marketing spend in that city immediately and alert operations to check document processing logs.
