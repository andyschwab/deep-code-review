**Deliverable: Real-Time Coaching Dashboard Specification for Sales Managers**

**North Star Metric:** "Customization Completion Rate" (Percentage of opened proposals where the pricing table is saved). This measures value delivery; raw clicks are vanity.

**Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only These)**
Do not track every click. Instrument only events that answer specific coaching questions:

1.  **`proposal_opened`**
    *   *Properties:* `proposal_id`, `rep_id`, `template_type` (default vs. custom start).
    *   *Question:* Did the rep engage with the tool?
2.  **`pricing_editor_entered`**
    *   *Properties:* `proposal_id`, `rep_id`, `time_since_open`.
    *   *Question:* Did they attempt to customize?
3.  **`pricing_edit_action`** (Aggregate counter, not individual logs)
    *   *Properties:* `proposal_id`, `edit_type` (line_item_add, discount_apply, margin_override).
    *   *Question:* Are they actively building value or staring at a blank screen? *Note: Log the count per session, not every keystroke, to avoid noise.*
4.  **`pricing_editor_abandoned`** (Triggered after 2 mins of inactivity inside editor)
    *   *Properties:* `proposal_id`, `rep_id`, `last_action_type`, `steps_completed`.
    *   *Question:* **Where do they hesitate?** This is your primary intervention signal.
5.  **`pricing_table_saved`**
    *   *Properties:* `proposal_id`, `rep_id`, `total_time_in_editor`, `final_discount_pct`.
    *   *Question:* Did they complete the core action?

**Dashboard Views for Weekly Pipeline Reviews**
Configure the dashboard to surface these three specific cohorts for director intervention:

*   **View A: The "Hesitation" List (High Priority)**
    *   *Filter:* Reps who triggered `pricing_editor_entered` but triggered `pricing_editor_abandoned` in the last 7 days.
    *   *Action:* Director coaches on specific `last_action_type` (e.g., if they abandon after "margin_override," they lack pricing authority guidance).
*   **View B: The "Velocity" Correlation**
    *   *Metric:* Average days from `pricing_table_saved` to `deal_closed` grouped by `edit_type`.
    *   *Validation:* Verify your 20% faster assumption using only `saved` events. Do not correlate incomplete sessions.
*   **View C: Adoption Funnel**
    *   *Flow:* `proposal_opened` → `pricing_editor_entered` → `pricing_table_saved`.
    *   *Goal:* Identify if the drop-off is at entry (don't know how to start) or mid-process (tool is too hard).

**Reading Rules & Guardrails**
*   **No Benchmarks:** Do not compare your completion rate to industry averages. Compare this week's rate to your pre-April 5th baseline.
*   **Privacy Seam:** Ensure `rep_id` is pseudonymous in the analytics tool. Route any PII (client names in proposal titles) to your privacy compliance review; do not display client specifics in a broad behavioral dashboard.
*   **Vanity Check:** Ignore total "clicks." A high click count with low save rates indicates confusion, not engagement.

**This Week's Steps**
1.  **Instrument:** Implement the five events above with the specified properties by April 4th. Reject requests to log individual keystrokes.
2.  **Baseline:** Run a query on historical data (if available) or wait 7 days post-launch to establish the current `pricing_editor_abandoned` rate.
3.  **Coach:** In next week's review, directors must pull the "Hesitation List" and contact exactly three reps who abandoned the editor to ask *why* they stopped.

*Note: Your $15k vs $8k deal value observation is a correlation, not causation, until you control for deal size in the experiment design. Do not claim the tool causes higher value yet.*
