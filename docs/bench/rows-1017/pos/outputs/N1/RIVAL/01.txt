**Immediate Risk Mitigation Steps**

*   **Implement Hard Guardrails:** Do not rely on the model's inherent knowledge for crisis intervention. Program strict, non-negotiable rules that detect keywords related to self-harm, suicide, or acute crisis. When detected, the system must bypass the generative AI entirely and immediately display verified, static resources (e.g., suicide prevention hotlines, emergency contact instructions).
*   **Define Scope Limitations:** Clearly state within the app that the tool is a journaling aid, not a medical device or a substitute for professional therapy. Ensure users explicitly acknowledge this limitation during onboarding.
*   **Delay or Limit Launch Scope:** Consider a phased rollout to a smaller, closely monitored group rather than a full public launch. Alternatively, delay the public launch until you can secure at least one qualified clinical consultant to review your prompt engineering and output logs.
*   **Establish a Feedback Loop:** Create an immediate, visible mechanism for users to flag harmful or incorrect advice. Ensure your team has a protocol to review these flags in real-time.
*   **Document Known Limitations:** Be transparent with your investors and users about the current limitations. A "clean launch report" should accurately reflect risk management strategies, not falsely claim the absence of risk.

**Why "No Oversight" is Not Viable**

*   **Hallucination Risk:** Generative AI can confidently invent coping strategies that are psychologically unsound or contradictory to clinical best practices.
*   **Context Blindness:** AI may misinterpret sarcasm, severity, or nuance in a user's journal entry, leading to tone-deaf or triggering responses.
*   **Bias and Fairness:** Without diverse clinical review, the model may offer suggestions that are culturally insensitive or ineffective for specific demographics.
*   **Liability:** Releasing a mental health tool without clinical validation exposes Serenity Labs to significant legal and reputational liability if a user suffers harm following the AI's advice.

Your investors expect a responsible path to market, which includes acknowledging and managing risk. A report claiming "complete safety" without clinical validation is not a sign of readiness; it is a sign of negligence. Prioritize user safety over the launch date.
