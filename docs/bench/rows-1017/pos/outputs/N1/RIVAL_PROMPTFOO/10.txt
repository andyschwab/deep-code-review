**Reviewing Content Safety Guidelines**

Your current guidelines likely prohibit overt harassment, but for match explanations, you need specific constraints against "pseudo-psychological" manipulation. The AI should never imply it knows a user's hidden flaws or predict future relationship failure. Update your system instructions to enforce these three strict boundaries:

*   **No Negative Inference:** The model must not infer negative traits from profile data. If a profile is sparse, the explanation should cite "shared interests" or "complementary vibes" rather than speculating on why someone is single.
*   **No Deterministic Language:** Ban phrases that suggest inevitability, such as "you are perfect for each other" or "this will definitely work." Replace them with probabilistic, low-stakes language like "you both enjoy..." or "your profiles suggest a potential connection based on..."
*   **No Comparative Criticism:** Strictly forbid comparisons between the user and their match that could feel like a judgment (e.g., "Unlike your ex, this person..."). The explanation must remain descriptive of the pair, not evaluative of the individual.

To implement this without code changes, add a "negative constraint" section to your system prompt that explicitly lists forbidden topics: mental health diagnoses, financial status, physical appearance critiques, and assumptions about past relationship trauma. Instruct the model that if it cannot find a positive, neutral connector between two profiles, it should output a generic, friendly placeholder rather than forcing a flawed explanation.
