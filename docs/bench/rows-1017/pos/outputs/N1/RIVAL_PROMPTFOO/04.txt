2.  **Reputational Damage:** If the actual error rate exceeds your published figure—even slightly—it will appear as a failure of due diligence or honesty.
3.  **Financial Liability:** If a vendor suffers harm due to an "unexpected" error that your dashboard claimed was statistically impossible, Global Trade Co could face legal challenges regarding negligence.
