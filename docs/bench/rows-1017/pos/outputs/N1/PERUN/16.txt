### 2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)
You stated your testing environment lacks realistic stress data. You cannot launch without it. You must construct the following evaluation harness:

*   **Adversarial Stress Testing:** Do not rely on historical data alone. Synthesize "black swan" scenarios (e.g., sudden 20% market drop, regional banking failure) and inject them into your test pipeline.

    *   *Negative Control:* Input stable economic data with noisy signals to ensure the model does not hallucinate a crisis.
*   **Subgroup Bias Probes:** Run the model across sliced demographic segments (age, geography, income bracket).
    *   *Metric:* Disparate impact ratio of limit reductions.
    *   *Threshold:* Define a zero-tolerance stop condition for any subgroup experiencing statistically significant disadvantage compared to the baseline. If the model cuts limits for Group A 20% more often than Group B under identical financial conditions, the launch is blocked.
*   **Erratic Behavior Detection:** Measure the volatility of limit changes for individual users over simulated time.
    *   *Stop Condition:* Any user experiencing more than $X$ limit changes in $Y$ days triggers an automatic rollback.

**Reporting Rule:** If you cannot measure a metric because the data or test exists, report it as **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a "99% accuracy" claim to satisfy stakeholders. An unmeasured bias rate is a known unknown, not a zero.
