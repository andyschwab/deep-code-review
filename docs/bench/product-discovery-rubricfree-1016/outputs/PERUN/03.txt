**Subject: Pricing page: what we do next, and what we don't do yet**

**Recommendation:** Don't start the 4-week redesign yet. Run about 2 weeks of cheap checks first. We have a real signal but can't yet say whether the page is costing us signups or whether the Team/Studio tiers are the problem. A redesign only fixes the first.

**What we know**
- 12 prospects on sales calls called the plans confusing (two months). A few customers asked how Team and Studio differ.
- The pricing page gets ~1,800 visitors/month and converts to trial at 3.1% (~56 trials/month).
- We have no benchmark for 3.1%, and I won't borrow one I can't source.

**What we don't know**
1. How many sales calls those 12 came from. If it's 12 of 15, that's a pattern. If it's 12 of 80, it's a minority.
2. Whether the confusion is the page (layout, wording) or the tiers (the offer itself is unclear).
3. Whether confusion actually reduces trial signups. Complaints are stated opinion, not behavior.

**Riskiest assumption:** "Fixing page confusion will lift trial signups enough to pay for 4 weeks of work." The checks below target it.

**Checks (about 2 weeks)**
- **Sales (owner: head of sales):** Count calls in the last two months where pricing came up vs. total calls. Pull the exact words used. Do they say "I can't find X" (page) or "why would I pick Studio?" (tier design)?
- **Support:** Same count for the customer emails, and what each person ended up choosing.
- **Jane, 5 comprehension tests (about 1 hour each):** Show the current page to 5 people from small agencies and give them a scenario: "You're a 6-person agency. Which plan, and why?" Record whether they pick correctly without help. If most can't explain Team vs. Studio, the tiers are the likely cause.
- **Cheap page fix instead of a redesign:** Add a "who it's for" line and a short Team vs. Studio comparison to the existing page. Compare trial conversion before and after.

**Kill threshold (we set these before looking at results)**
- Minimum conversion lift that would justify the 4-week redesign: ____ (derive it: the extra trials/month needed to cover the cost of 4 weeks of design and engineering, using our own trial-to-paid rate and revenue per account)
- Sales calls where pricing confusion came up that would make this a priority: ____ of ____
- Comprehension test: fewer than ____ of 5 choose the right plan → treat as a tier problem, not a page problem
- Decision date: ____

**Caution on the page test:** At ~56 trials/month, a small lift won't be statistically visible in a few weeks. Treat the before/after as directional, and lean more on the comprehension tests and call tallies.

**How this decides the redesign**
- Confusion is common and the page is the problem → proceed with the redesign, scoped to the fix.
- Confusion is about the tiers → fix the packaging first, since a prettier page won't solve it.
- Confusion is rare → keep the page and spend the 4 weeks elsewhere.

**Not doing yet (non-goals):** Full redesign, new tier structure, and any new pricing experiments until the checks come back. Reopen this if the results surprise us.
