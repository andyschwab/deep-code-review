# Larkspur Q1 discovery plan (10 weeks, 1 researcher + Tomas part-time)

**Core question:** Where do customers struggle or leave, and which problem is worth building for first? Tickets show where customers *complain*. They are silent on quiet churners, so the plan tests that gap before trusting the ticket ranking.

**Riskiest assumption:** "Ticket volume ≈ the problems that cost us customers." The cheapest test is to compare churned accounts against ticket filers (Workstream 1b). Run it first.

## Workstreams, in order

**1. Weeks 1–2: Cheap checks on data you already hold**
- **1a. Scanning triage.** Tomas and the researcher read a random sample of the ~280 scanning tickets and code each as bug, setup mistake, hardware/device, or other. *Learn:* whether scanning is an engineering problem or an onboarding problem. Sample size: ____ (the user sets this; larger if the first batch is mixed).
- **1b. Churn vs tickets.** Take the accounts that churned or downgraded in the six months and check how many filed any ticket, and in which cluster. *Learn:* whether tickets predict churn. Needs billing/CRM access; confirm who owns it. If no churn list exists, that is itself a finding.
- **Start recruiting for Workstream 2 in week 1.** Recruitment is the usual schedule risk.

**2. Weeks 2–7: Interviews on real past behavior**
- **2a. Churned/downgraded customers** (the blind spot). Ask about the last time they considered leaving and what they did instead. No pitching. Target: ____ interviews.
- **2b. Transfers.** Walk active multi-location customers through their last real transfer, ideally screen-shared. *Learn:* whether the confusion is UI, concepts, or process.
- **2c. Supplier ordering.** Treat the 150 requests as a stated solution, not a verified need. Ask how they reorder today, what it costs them, and what they already pay for or use. *Learn:* whether the job is painful enough to justify building.

**3. Weeks 6–8: One behavioral test, chosen by what 1–2 show**
If supplier ordering looks promising, run a fake-door or concierge test. Define the metric and a kill threshold before launch (below).

**4. Weeks 9–10: Synthesis and ranking**

## Kill thresholds (set before each test runs)
- **1b:** If fewer than ___% of churned accounts filed tickets, tickets are a weak guide and interview findings outrank ticket counts. You decide the cutoff, using the smallest gap that would change your priorities.
- **3:** Drop supplier ordering if fewer than ___ of ___ exposed users take the action by ___ date.
