# Recommendation: Built-in Scheduling at Northwind Clinics Software

**Recommendation: Don't commit the 8-week build yet. Spend 2–3 weeks on targeted validation, then decide.** Five calls show a real signal but can't support the commitment.

## What we know
- 5 calls with clinic office managers; 3 raised scheduling unprompted, 2 only after being asked ("nice to have").
- 2 of the 5 pay about $40/month for a separate booking tool and would happily drop it.
- Cost: about 8 weeks for 2 developers.
- Base: 220 paying accounts.

## Why five calls isn't enough
1. **Biased sample.** All five had recently contacted support. They may be more frustrated or more engaged than typical accounts, so we can't assume they represent the 220.
2. **Stated vs. actual behavior.** "Would happily drop it" is an opinion. The $40/month spend is real, but nobody has yet been asked to switch.
3. **Switching barrier untested.** Current tools hold patient reminders. If we can't match reminders, clinics may not leave, which would undercut the value.
4. **Mixed demand.** Only 3 of 5 raised it unprompted, and 2 of those 3 pay for a tool today. The rest see it as a nice-to-have.
5. **Revenue is modest.** If 2 of 5 is a rough guide, that is a hypothesis, not a forecast. At $40/month per adopting clinic, the upside depends on adoption we haven't measured.

## What to find out before deciding
| Question | How | Pass signal |
|---|---|---|
| How common is the need across all accounts? | Short survey or in-app question to all 220 accounts: do you use a separate booking tool, which one, and what do you pay? | A meaningful share (e.g., 25%+) already pay for one. Set your own threshold with leadership beforehand. |
| Is our sample skewed? | 8–10 more calls with clinics who have *not* contacted support recently, chosen by size and specialty | Scheduling comes up unprompted at similar rates |
| Will they switch? | Ask about the last time they changed a tool; ask what they'd need to keep (especially reminders); test a waitlist or pre-commitment | Several clinics sign up for early access or a pilot |
| Is reminder parity required? | Ask what reminders they rely on, and have engineering scope it | Scope and timeline for reminders are known |
| Is the build estimate solid? | Engineering reviews whether the 8 weeks includes reminders and integrations | Estimate covers the minimum a clinic needs to switch |

## Suggested decision rule for Thursday
- **Build** if the survey and new calls show broad, paid demand, and pilot clinics confirm they'd switch with a defined reminder feature.
- **Narrow or defer** if demand is concentrated in a few accounts or reminders make the real scope much bigger.
- **Drop** if unprompted demand is rare outside the support-contact group.

## Ask for Thursday
Approve 2–3 weeks of validation (survey plus ~10 calls plus a reminder scoping session) and revisit the build decision afterward.

*Note: the figures above come only from your five calls, so I haven't estimated revenue or adoption. Set the pass thresholds with leadership before running the research, so the results aren't read to fit the answer.*
