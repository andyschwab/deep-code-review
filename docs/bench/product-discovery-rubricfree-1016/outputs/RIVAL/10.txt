# Quillstone: Assumption Testing Order (4-week plan)

## Key judgment
The assumption that would sink the idea is **"the problem is worth paying for"**. You're unsure whether the pain is *time* or *missed sessions*. That decides your pitch, your features, and your price. Test it first, using the same interviews that cover several other assumptions.

## Priority order

| # | Assumption | Why this position | Test | Pass signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pain is time vs. missed sessions** (and tutors spend 3+ hrs/wk scheduling) | Defines the problem. If wrong, everything downstream shifts. | 12-15 tutor interviews about their *last* week: walk through each booking, reschedule, no-show, and chase-up. Ask them to estimate hours and count lost sessions/income. | ≥8 of 15 report 3+ hrs/wk, *or* ≥8 report losing paid sessions monthly. Note which dominates. |
| 2 | **Tutors use a messy mix of texts/spreadsheets** (and would switch from free tools) | Switching from free tools is the likeliest killer. Same interviews. | Ask to see their actual setup (screen share). Ask what they've tried and abandoned, and why. | ≥8 of 15 show a patched-together system; ≥5 have tried and dropped a free tool, which means they are open to switching. |
| 3 | **Tutors would pay $15/mo** | Viability. Cheap to test early with real money signals. | Fake-door landing page with a price and a "start trial / join waitlist with card or deposit" step. In interviews, ask what they pay for now. Try $10, $15, $25 variants. | ≥5% of visitors reach the paid-intent step; ≥5 tutors commit to a paid pilot or pre-order. |
| 4 | **Reach tutors cheaply via online communities** | Needed for the landing page test anyway, so run it in parallel. | Post in 3-4 tutor communities/groups (and one small paid test, about $100-150). Track cost per interview booked and per signup. | Cost per qualified signup of roughly under $15-20 (about one month's revenue). Set your own threshold on Monday. |
| 5 | **Parents will book through a link** | Tutors control adoption, but parents can block it. | Concierge test: 5-8 interviewed tutors send their real clients a booking link (a Calendly-style form is enough). Track completion. | ≥70% of parents book without hand-holding. |
| 6 | **Payment collection, reminders, calendar sync, group sessions** | Feature-level. Matters only if 1-5 pass. | Rank in the interviews: "Which would you miss most?" Use the concierge pilot to see whether reminders reduce no-shows. | Use the results to pick the MVP's top 2 features; defer the rest. |

## Suggested schedule
- **Week 1:** Write the interview script, recruit through communities, launch the landing page, start interviews.
- **Week 2:** Finish interviews (#1, #2). Read the results on Wednesday to decide whether the problem is time or missed sessions, then adjust the landing page copy.
- **Week 3:** Price variants and paid-intent data (#3, #4). Start the concierge booking pilot (#5).
- **Week 4:** Finish the pilot, rank features (#6), and hold the go/no-go meeting.

## Go/no-go rules to agree on Monday
- **Commit** if #1-#3 pass and cost per signup is viable.
- **Pivot the framing** if missed sessions are the pain: lead with reminders and payments rather than scheduling time.
- **Stop or rethink** if tutors are happy with free tools or won't commit money.

*Notes: The thresholds above are starting points, so agree on your own before collecting data. With 12-15 interviews the results are directional, not statistical.*
