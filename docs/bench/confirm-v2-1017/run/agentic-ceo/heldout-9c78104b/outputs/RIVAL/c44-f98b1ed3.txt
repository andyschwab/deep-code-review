### Emergency Work Session Structure: Tunnel Safety Monitoring Replacement

**Immediate Actions (Initiate Within 5 Minutes)**
1.  **Freeze All Calibration Attempts:** Issue a standing order to all eight technicians that NO calibration sequences are to be triggered on any node until the Physical Wiring Verification (PWV) phase is signed off. This prevents irreversible locking of units.
2.  **Deploy Verification Teams:** Split the eight technicians into four pairs. Assign each pair a specific quadrant of Sector Four. Their sole objective is physical wiring inspection against the schematic. They must report "Clear" or "Fault" per node, nothing else.
3.  **Establish Central Command Log:** Designate one stationary lead (or yourself) to maintain a real-time master checklist of the 200 nodes. This log tracks the status of every unit: *Installed → Wiring Verified → Ready for Calibration → Calibrated*. No node moves to "Ready for Calibration" without a verbal confirmation from a verification pair.

**Order of Operations**
1.  **Physical Wiring Verification (Hours 0–2.5):** Technicians inspect all 200 nodes. Defects are repaired immediately. Only when a node is physically perfect is it marked "Verified" in the Command Log.
2.  **Director's Gate Review (Hour 2.5):** You personally review the Command Log. If 200/200 nodes are "Verified," you authorize the next phase. If not, repair teams focus solely on remaining faults while verified nodes wait.
3.  **Staged Calibration (Hours 2.5–5.0):** Upon your explicit "Go" command, technicians trigger calibration. Do this in batches of 50 to manage network load and confirm success rates. If a batch fails, halt immediately before proceeding.
4.  **System Integration Test (Hour 5.0–5.45):** Validate real-time data flow for air quality and structural stability from all nodes to the surface control room.
5.  **Final Safety Sign-Off (Hour 5.45–6.00):** You confirm system integrity. The mine is cleared for the night shift.

**Decision Matrix**

**Decisions Reserved for You (Safety Director):**
*   **The "Go/No-Go" for Calibration:** Only you can authorize the transition from Wiring Verification to Calibration based on the Command Log.
*   **Resource Reallocation:** If a specific quadrant is failing, you decide whether to pull technicians from completed zones to assist or accept a delay.
*   **Shift Change Call:** The final determination of whether the system is safe enough to allow the night shift entry at 06:00.

**Instructions to Delegate to Field Leads:**
*   **Technician Pairing & Zoning:** Assign specific technicians to specific quadrants.
*   **Repair Protocols:** Field leads determine the specific method to fix wiring faults (e.g., re-soldering, cable replacement) as long as it meets the schematic.
*   **Batch Execution:** Once you give the "Go" for calibration, field leads manage the timing and execution of the 50-node batches.
*   **Fault Reporting:** Field leads must immediately escalate any wiring defect that cannot be fixed within 15 minutes.

**Note:** Strict adherence to the "Verify Before Calibrate" rule is the only way to prevent permanent unit lockout.
Do not allow any technician to multitask between verification and calibration; these roles must remain separate until the gate review.
