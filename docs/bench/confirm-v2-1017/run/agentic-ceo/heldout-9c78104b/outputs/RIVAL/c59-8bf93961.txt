**Work Session Agenda: Snowplow Routing Optimizer Deployment**

**Phase 1: Simulation & Scenario Modeling (Days 1–4)**
*   **Lead:** Senior Data Scientist.
*   **Activity:** Run high-fidelity simulations using historical blizzard data and predicted storm patterns. Test the new algorithm against "worst-case" scenarios (e.g., whiteout conditions, equipment failure) without touching live vehicles.
*   **Goal:** Validate that the new routing logic reduces clearance time by at least 20% compared to the legacy system under extreme stress.

**Phase 2: Controlled Pilot Testing (Days 5–7)**
*   **Lead:** Lead Fleet Manager.
*   **Activity:** Deploy the algorithm to a single, isolated depot or a small subset of three non-critical trucks during dry conditions or light flurries (if occurring).
*   **Goal:** Verify hardware compatibility, driver interface usability, and real-time data transmission. Establish a "hard stop" protocol where drivers can manually override the system instantly.

**Phase 3: Full Rollout Preparation (Days 8–9)**
*   **Lead:** City Official (You) & Lead Fleet Manager.
*   **Activity:** Finalize the deployment script for the entire fleet. Conduct a tabletop exercise with all seven team members to rehearse the switchover procedure.
*   **Goal:** Ensure every truck is pre-loaded with the software update, ready for immediate activation upon your command.

**Phase 4: Activation Window (Day 10 – Pre-Warning)**
*   **Action:** Deploy to full fleet *before* the official blizzard warning is issued.
*   **Constraint:** No changes allowed once the warning is active.

**Specialist Assignment for Scenario Modeling**
The **Senior Data Scientist** must lead the scenario modeling. They possess the statistical expertise to interpret the 15-day forecast, adjust the algorithm's parameters for predicted snowfall intensity, and quantify the risk of failure. The fleet managers should provide operational constraints (e.g., fuel limits, shift lengths) to the data scientist to ensure the simulations reflect reality.

**Emergency Protocols Requiring Authorization**
As the city official, you must authorize the following protocols before Day 5:
1.  **The "Golden Switch" Protocol:** A definitive, pre-storm deadline (e.g., 6 hours before the first snowflake) after which no software updates or configuration changes are permitted. Missing this window mandates sticking to the legacy system.
2.  **Manual Override Authority:** Explicit authorization for drivers to disregard the optimizer's route if they perceive immediate danger or inefficiency, with a guarantee of no disciplinary action.
3.  **Rollback Contingency (Pre-Storm):** A procedure to remotely wipe the new software and revert to the old system *only* during the dry pre-storm window if the pilot test reveals critical flaws. Once the storm starts, the system is locked.

**First Concrete Task**
**Conduct a Data Integrity Audit.** Before writing a single line of new code or running a simulation, the data science team must verify that the current map data, road hierarchy classifications, and real-time traffic feeds are 100% accurate and compatible with the new algorithm. Garbage in will result in stranded trucks regardless of how sophisticated the routing logic is. This audit must be completed within 48 hours.

*Note: The irreversible nature of the live deployment dictates that the "Golden Switch" deadline is the most critical governance tool you possess.*
