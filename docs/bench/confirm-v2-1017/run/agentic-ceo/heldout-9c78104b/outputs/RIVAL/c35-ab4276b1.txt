### Emergency Work Session Structure: SecureTrack Protocol Rollout

**1. Session Agenda & Order of Operations (Midnight – 04:00)**
*   **00:00 – 00:45: Backend Integration & Dry Run.** Before touching hardware, the six-person team must verify the CargoVision backend can ingest SecureTrack data. Run a simulation using historical data from 500 containers to confirm protocol compatibility. If the backend fails, halt; do not risk the scanners.
*   **00:45 – 01:30: Pilot Firmware Update (Group A).** Select 10 non-critical scanners from a single dock zone. Execute the firmware update. Verify successful boot and SecureTrack handshake. Do not proceed if any unit bricks.
*   **01:30 – 02:30: Phased Rollout (Group B).** If Group A succeeds, update 20% of the fleet (approx. 2,400 units equivalent logic, though physically limited by time, focus on critical path scanners first). Prioritize scanners used for inbound customs clearance.
*   **02:30 – 03:30: Validation & Stress Test.** Have dockworkers perform live scans on moving containers. Monitor latency and data integrity in real-time.
*   **03:30 – 04:00: Contingency Review & Go/No-Go Decision.** Confirm system stability for the 06:00 deadline. If unstable, execute rollback immediately.

**2. Required Contingency Plans**
*   **The "Brick" Protocol:** If an update fails mid-process, the device is unusable. You must have a stockpile of pre-configured spare scanners (updated *before* the maintenance window starts) ready to swap instantly. Calculate a 5% failure rate; prepare at least 600 spare units if updating the whole fleet, or proportionate spares for the phased approach.
*   **Manual Fallback:** Establish a temporary manual logging procedure using paper manifests and radio confirmation for any lane where scanners fail. This prevents total loading halts while individual units are replaced.
*   **Rollback Strategy:** Preserve the previous CargoVision firmware version on a local server. If SecureTrack integration causes data corruption, the team must be able to flash the old firmware within 15 minutes to resume basic operations, even if customs compliance is temporarily delayed (accepting the financial risk to prevent total operational paralysis).

**3. Authority Required to Proceed**
To execute the scanner updates, you must explicitly grant the following authorities to the IT Team Lead:
*   **Operational Halt Authority:** The power to stop all loading operations in specific zones if a firmware anomaly is detected, overriding shift supervisors who may pressure them to "keep moving."
*   **Hardware Disposal/Replacement Authority:** Immediate authorization to declare bricked units as failed and deploy spares without seeking secondary approval during the 4-hour window.
*   **Data Integrity Override:** Permission to pause non-essential CargoVision reporting functions to dedicate all bandwidth and processing power to the SecureTrack handshake verification during the rollout.

Without these specific grants, the IT team will hesitate at the first sign of trouble, wasting precious minutes in the maintenance window and increasing the risk of missing the 06:00 deadline.

**Note:** This plan assumes the SecureTrack firmware binary is already downloaded and verified on the local update server.
**Note:** Ensure the 50% battery minimum requirement is met on all handhelds before the window opens to prevent power-failure bricking.
