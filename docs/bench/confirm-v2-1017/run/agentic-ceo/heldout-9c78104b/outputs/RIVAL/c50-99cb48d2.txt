### Strategic Plan for Offshore Wind Farm Grid Integration

**Phase Sequence**
1.  **Capacity Gap Analysis & Architecture Review:** Quantify the specific hardware and software modifications required to triple capacity from 5MW to 15MW response time.
2.  **Sensor Recalibration Protocol Development:** Design the standardized procedure for updating sensors across all forty substations, ensuring data consistency.
3.  **Staged Simulation & Dry-Runs:** Execute offline simulations of the 15MW fluctuation scenarios, followed by isolated live tests on non-critical substation clusters.
4.  **Authorized Protocol Validation:** Submit final interface protocol changes to the two authorized specialists for rigorous review and formal approval.
5.  **Final Synchronization Rehearsal:** Conduct a full-scale, timed dry-run of the switch-over sequence without engaging the irreversible synchronization mechanism.
6.  **Execution & Monitoring:** Perform the final synchronization test during a pre-defined low-demand window with full specialist standby.

**Required Technical Validations (Pre-Signoff)**
Before signing off on hardware upgrades, you must confirm completion of the following:
*   **Load Stress Verification:** Proof that the upgraded system can sustain a 15MW fluctuation input for a duration exceeding maximum expected grid instability periods without thermal or logical failure.
*   **Latency Confirmation:** Verified data showing sensor-to-actuator response times remain within the "seconds" threshold under full 15MW load across the most distant substation in the network.
*   **Fail-Safe Integrity Test:** Successful demonstration that the system defaults to a safe, non-discharging state if communication with any of the forty substations is lost during a fluctuation event.
*   **Protocol Compatibility Certificate:** A signed document from both authorized specialists confirming the new hardware firmware is fully compatible with existing grid interface protocols and introduces no security vulnerabilities.

**Immediate Task for the Lead Engineer**
The lead engineer must immediately draft and distribute the **Sensor Recalibration Specification Document**. This document must define the exact calibration parameters, communication handshake sequences, and rollback procedures for the forty substations. This is the critical path item; without a standardized calibration method, the hardware upgrades cannot be tested effectively, and the timeline for the six-month deadline is at risk. This draft will serve as the basis for the upcoming strategy session discussion.

*Note: Ensure the two authorized specialists are briefed on the draft protocol changes prior to the strategy session to expedite the approval phase.*
*Priority must be given to defining the rollback procedure to mitigate the risk of cascade failure during testing.*
