### ValleyAid Deployment Session Structure

**Phase 1: Preparation & Data Validation (Days 1–10)**
*   **Objective:** Ensure map data aligns perfectly with new road bridges before any algorithmic logic is applied.
*   **Team-Initiated Protocols:**
    *   Conduct offline data synchronization drills for the 12-member team across three time zones to simulate limited satellite connectivity.
    *   Aggregate current bridge coordinates and cross-reference with existing static maps.
    *   Establish a "manual override" communication channel (e.g., encrypted SMS or radio frequency) independent of the platform.
*   **Coordinator Sign-Off Required:**
    *   **Map Data Integrity:** You must explicitly sign off on the final verified dataset of new road bridges. This is the single point of failure for the routing algorithm.
    *   **Connectivity Baseline:** Approval of the minimum bandwidth threshold required to trigger automated vs. manual modes.

**Phase 2: Controlled Testing & Simulation (Days 11–17)**
*   **Objective:** Validate the routing logic in a sandbox environment without touching live dispatch.
*   **Team-Initiated Protocols:**
    *   Run "shadow mode" simulations where the algorithm suggests routes but does not execute them. Compare these against manual dispatch decisions for the 200 shelters.
    *   Stress-test the system with simulated data outages to ensure graceful degradation to manual protocols.
*   **Coordinator Sign-Off Required:**
    *   **Simulation Success Criteria:** You must approve the variance tolerance between algorithmic suggestions and known safe routes. Any deviation regarding bridge locations results in an immediate halt.

**Phase 3: Staged Launch & Activation (Days 18–21)**
*   **Objective:** Go live with safety nets in place before the monsoon hits.
*   **Team-Initiated Protocols:**
    *   Deploy the platform to a pilot group of 10 shelters first, monitoring for 24 hours.
    *   Finalize the "Kill Switch" procedure to instantly revert to manual dispatch if anomalies are detected.
*   **Coordinator Sign-Off Required (The Irreversible Step):**
    *   **Algorithm Activation:** Only you can authorize turning on the automated routing algorithm. This requires a dual-verification signature confirming that Phase 1 data is locked and Phase 2 simulations passed 100% of bridge-alignment checks.

**Critical Risks Requiring Your Explicit Sign-Off**
1.  **Data-Route Mismatch:** The risk of trucks being routed to washed-out paths due to outdated map data.
2.  **Loss of Manual Control:** The risk that the automated override prevents human intervention during unforeseen edge cases.
3.  **Connectivity Blackout:** The risk that the platform becomes unusable during peak storm conditions, requiring a pre-approved switch to non-digital protocols.

**First Concrete Actions for Today**
1.  **Freeze Map Data Collection:** Halt all automatic map updates. Mandate a manual, human-led verification of every new bridge coordinate against recent satellite imagery or ground reports.
2.  **Define the "Kill Switch" Protocol:** Draft and circulate the exact steps to disable the automated routing algorithm instantly. Ensure all 12 team members acknowledge this procedure.
3.  **Schedule the Data Sign-Off Meeting:** Set a hard deadline (no later than Day 9) for your personal review and signature on the final bridge dataset. Do not proceed to testing without this meeting on the calendar.

Note: The three-week timeline allows no room for iterative fixes after activation; all validation must occur pre-launch.
Prioritize the "Kill Switch" drill over feature completeness to ensure team safety.
