### Grid Load Balancer Cutover Plan: Assessment to Activation

**Phase 1: Immediate Assessment & Freeze (Now – Friday)**
*   **Objective:** Validate the new vendor's configuration against current load models and freeze all non-essential changes.
*   **Team Tasks:** Engineers run simulation models using last summer's peak data (40°C scenarios) against the new vendor's specs. They draft the physical rerouting procedure step-by-step and identify single points of failure.
*   **Your Decisions:** Approve the simulation parameters and authorize the "code freeze" on the existing system. You must decide if the risk profile from the simulation is acceptable or if a fallback manual override mechanism is required before proceeding.

**Phase 2: Rehearsal & Role Assignment (Saturday)**
*   **Objective:** Dry-run the procedure without touching live hardware.
*   **Team Tasks:** Conduct a table-top exercise walking through the six-hour window minute-by-minute. Assign specific roles: one engineer for physical rerouting, one for monitoring telemetry, one for communication with dispatch, and one as the "stop authority." Prepare rollback scripts (if any partial digital rollback is possible) and emergency communication templates for stakeholders.
*   **Your Decisions:** Finalize the "Go/No-Go" criteria. You alone define the specific telemetry thresholds (e.g., latency spikes, voltage fluctuations) that will trigger an immediate abort during the window.

**Phase 3: Execution Window (Sunday, 6 Hours)**
*   **Objective:** Physical rerouting and validation.
*   **Team Tasks:** Execute the physical signal reroute. Monitor real-time load distribution across the two million households. Validate signal integrity with the new vendor's backend.
*   **Your Decisions:** Give the final "Execute" command at the start of the window. Make the critical call to abort if early indicators deviate from the plan before the point of no return (revoking old vendor access).

**Phase 4: Activation & Stabilization (Post-Reroute)**
*   **Objective:** Confirm stability and revoke old access.
*   **Team Tasks:** Gradually increase load on the new system while monitoring for thermal stress. Once stable for 30 minutes, formally revoke old vendor credentials.
*   **Your Decisions:** Authorize the permanent revocation of the old vendor's access. This is the irreversible step.

### Decision Matrix: Leader vs. Team

| Decision Area | Your Responsibility (Team Lead) | Team's Independent Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Risk Tolerance** | Define acceptable failure modes and abort thresholds. | Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies. |
| **Procedure Approval** | Sign off on the final rerouting procedure and rollback plan. | Draft the technical steps and conduct the dry-run simulations. |
| **Go/No-Go Authority** | Give the final command to start and the command to revoke old access. | Provide real-time data recommendations to support your decision. |
| **Stakeholder Comms** | Manage upward communication and public impact statements. | Draft technical status updates and internal coordination logs. |

### Immediate First Step

**Conduct a "Pre-Mortem" Simulation immediately.** Gather your ten engineers for a 90-minute session to assume the cutover has already failed catastrophically on Tuesday during the heatwave. Have them work backward to determine exactly what technical or procedural cause led to the blackout. This identifies hidden vulnerabilities in the new vendor's configuration or the physical rerouting plan before you commit to the Sunday window. Do not write a single line of code or touch a cable until this simulation is complete and the top three identified risks have mitigation plans attached.

*Note: Ensure the "stop authority" engineer has no other duties during the Sunday window to maintain unbiased monitoring.*
*Time is critical; prioritize the simulation over documentation perfection.*
