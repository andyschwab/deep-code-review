### Replatforming Sequence and Action Plan

**Phase 1: Immediate Triage and Scope Freeze (Days 1–3)**
*   **Action:** Halt all non-critical feature development on the new system. Focus exclusively on the data migration path and core publishing workflow.
*   **Your Approval:** You must personally approve the final list of features cut from the initial launch to guarantee the October 30th freeze.
*   **Delegated:** The engineering lead determines which specific maintenance tasks on the live site can be paused or handled by junior staff.

**Phase 2: Migration Dry-Run and Risk Mitigation (Days 4–20)**
*   **Action:** Execute a full-scale mock migration of the 20-year archive in a staging environment. Measure data integrity and time-to-complete.
*   **Your Approval:** You must sign off on the "Rollback Threshold"—the specific number of corrupted articles or downtime minutes that would trigger an abort of the migration.
*   **Delegated:** Database specialists design the extraction scripts and the validation logic to compare old vs. new data.

**Phase 3: Load Testing and Staff Training (Days 21–35)**
*   **Action:** Simulate 500,000 concurrent users against the new backend. Simultaneously, train editors on the new interface.
*   **Your Approval:** You must approve the final "Go/No-Go" criteria based on load test results and editor feedback.
*   **Delegated:** Backend engineers optimize database queries and caching layers to meet concurrency targets.

**Phase 4: Code Freeze and Final Validation (Oct 24–30)**
*   **Action:** No new code enters the system. Perform final security audits and content sanity checks.
*   **Your Approval:** You must give the final authorization to proceed with the production cutover.
*   **Delegated:** The engineering lead manages the deployment pipeline and monitors the staging environment for anomalies.

**Phase 5: Cutover and Election Night Monitoring (Nov 1–5)**
*   **Action:** Execute the live migration during a low-traffic window. Monitor system health continuously through election night.
*   **Your Approval:** You hold the sole authority to order a rollback to the legacy system if critical failures occur during the live window.
*   **Delegated:** The technical team executes the migration scripts and manages real-time incident response.

**Decision Matrix: You vs. Engineering Lead**
*   **You Must Approve:** Scope cuts, risk tolerance thresholds (data loss/downtime), final go/no-go decisions, and the rollback command.
*   **Engineering Lead Delegates:** Technical implementation details, staff scheduling, script creation, testing execution, and routine incident management.

**First Concrete Action**
Conduct a 60-minute "Risk & Scope Audit" immediately. In this session, list every remaining feature requested for the new system and ruthlessly cut anything not required for basic article publishing and archival access. Confirm the exact date and time for the first full data migration dry-run.

Note: The irreversibility of the database decommissioning makes the dry-run in Phase 2 your single most critical safety net. Do not proceed to Phase 3 without a successful, verified mock migration.
