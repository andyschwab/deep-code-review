### Kickoff Meeting Structure: Veterinary Records Migration

**1. Data Quality Audit Lead**
The **Senior Data Analyst** with the strongest background in healthcare data interoperability must lead the initial audit. While clinic managers understand the context, only a data analyst can technically assess the integrity of the legacy schema, identify corruption patterns in the unsupported vendor format, and quantify the risk level of the two million records. This individual will produce the baseline report required before any mapping logic is written.

**2. Critical Policy Choices Requiring Your Decision**
You must establish written protocols for the following three conflict scenarios before migration begins:
*   **Conflicting Allergy Data:** If the legacy system lists an allergy that contradicts a more recent manual entry (or vice versa), which source serves as the "single source of truth"? Given the life-safety risk, you must decide if the protocol defaults to the most recent entry, the most conservative (assuming an allergy exists), or requires manual clinic verification.
*   **Incomplete Histories:** Define the threshold for "acceptable incompleteness." Can a record with missing vaccination dates be migrated with a "null" flag, or must it be halted for manual review? With 2 million records, a zero-tolerance policy may stall the project, while a lenient one risks insurance claim rejections.
*   **Duplicate Patient Profiles:** When merging records from twelve clinics, determine how to handle animals with identical names and birthdates but different microchip numbers. You must decide if the system automatically merges them based on name matching or strictly requires unique identifier verification.

**3. First Actionable Step: Secure the Legacy Database**
Immediately execute a **full, read-only snapshot backup** of the entire legacy database to a secure, isolated storage environment that is physically and logically disconnected from the migration pipeline.
*   Do not attempt to extract or transform data yet.
*   Verify the integrity of this backup by calculating checksums.
*   Restrict write-access to the live legacy system to "Emergency Only" status to prevent new data from entering the system while the snapshot is being validated.
This creates an immutable restore point, ensuring that if the mapping process corrupts data or the extraction fails, the original two million records remain untouched and recoverable.

***

**Note:** Given the November 1st deadline, the data mapping phase must begin immediately after the audit, leaving minimal room for iterative testing.
**Note:** Ensure the three clinic managers are assigned to validate the policy decisions above, as their operational knowledge is critical for defining the "correct" business logic.
