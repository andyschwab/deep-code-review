### Greenfield Waste Management Expansion Strategy Plan

**Phase 1: Data Synthesis and Baseline Modeling (Weeks 1-2)**
*   Aggregate the decade of historical pickup data to establish volume baselines per street segment.
*   Map the exact geographic boundaries of the three new suburbs against the existing 40-vehicle capacity.
*   Simulate current static routes on the new combined map to quantify the projected fuel cost increase (validating the "doubling" risk).

**Phase 2: Parameter Definition and Zone Locking (Weeks 3-4)**
*   **Planner Approval Required:** You must personally approve the **Zone Boundary Definitions** and the **Maximum Route Duration/Distance** parameters. These are irreversible once drivers are assigned; changing them mid-cycle causes the missed pickups and public outrage you identified.
*   **Team Decision Authority:** The six logistics coordinators will determine the specific **Stop Sequence Optimization**, **Truck-to-Zone Assignments** (within your capacity limits), and **Start Time Staggering** to fit the overnight billing window.
*   Finalize the zone definitions. Once signed off, these are locked to prevent rollback issues.

**Phase 3: Algorithm Calibration and Billing Integration (Weeks 5-7)**
*   Configure the routing algorithm using the approved parameters.
*   Run parallel simulations: Ensure the generated route manifests can be successfully ingested by the billing software during its overnight processing window.
*   Validate that the 40-vehicle fleet can cover the expanded geography without exceeding the approved duration limits.

**Phase 4: Dry Run and Contingency Planning (Weeks 8-9)**
*   Execute a "paper rollout" where coordinators manually trace the new routes against historical data to spot logical errors.
*   Develop a manual dispatching fallback protocol specifically for the first week of live operation in case of critical integration failure.

**Phase 5: Live Rollout (April 24 – May 1)**
*   **April 24:** Load final zone definitions into the system.
*   **April 25-30:** Run the new algorithm in "shadow mode" (generating routes but not dispatching) to verify overnight billing integration daily.
*   **May 1:** Go live with new routes for the incorporated suburbs.

***

**Immediate First Step:**
Convene the team immediately to aggregate and clean the historical pickup data specifically for the three new suburb geographies. Without this validated dataset, you cannot model the capacity constraints of your 40-vehicle fleet or prove the fuel cost risks, making any subsequent algorithm configuration guesswork that jeopardizes the May 1st deadline.
