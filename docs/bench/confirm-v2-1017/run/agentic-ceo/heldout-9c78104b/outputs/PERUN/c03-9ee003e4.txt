**Phase 2: Technical Preparation & Dry Run Design (Hours 2–6)**

*   **Contingency Plan B (Hard Failure):** If the new region becomes inconsistent, immediately route all traffic back to the local data center. This requires the legacy system to remain the "source of truth" until final verification.

**Phase 3: Validation Protocol Definition (Hours 6–8)**
*   Define "success" not just as data transfer, but as checksum verification of every patient record.
