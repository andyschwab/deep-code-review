**Decision:** Proceed with a phased "Validate-Then-Scale" strategy, deferring full 15MW deployment until sensor recalibration is verified on a non-critical cluster. The plan rests on one number: **40 substations** must be recalibrated and validated in batches before the 6-month deadline, requiring a throughput of ~2 sites per week to allow for regression testing.

**Figures:** 40 substations ÷ 24 weeks (6 months minus 4 weeks final buffer) = 1.67 sites/week. Rounding up to 2 sites/week provides a 4-week safety margin. Current capacity (7 specialists) supports this if focused, but the bottleneck is the 2 authorized approvers. If approvers spend >20% of their time on non-approval tasks, the schedule slips by 3 weeks.

**Strategic Phases:**

1.  **Phase 1: Controlled Batch Recalibration (Weeks 1–12)**
    *   Recalibrate sensors in batches of 4 substations (10 batches total).
    *   Validate each batch against the 5MW prototype limit first, then incrementally test up to 10MW.
    *   **Gate:** No batch moves to Phase 2 without signed validation from both authorized approvers.

2.  **Phase 2: Intermediate Stress Testing (Weeks 13–20)**
    *   Aggregate validated batches to simulate 15MW fluctuations in a sandboxed grid segment.
    *   Verify latency and synchronization logic under load.
    *   **Gate:** Successful 15MW simulation without latency spikes >50ms.

3.  **Phase 3: Pre-Synchronization Dry Runs (Weeks 21–24)**
    *   Execute full switch-over simulations using digital twins.
    *   Finalize the "kill switch" protocol for cascade failure prevention.
    *   **Gate:** Zero critical errors in 3 consecutive dry runs.

4.  **Phase 4: Final Synchronization (Week 25)**
    *   Execute the irreversible switch-over during the lowest demand window.
    *   Monitor real-time with all 7 specialists deployed.

**Technical Validations Required Before Hardware Sign-off:**
*   **Sensor Linearity Check:** Confirmation that recalibrated sensors report accurately between 5MW and 15MW loads (data logs required).
*   **Protocol Handshake Verification:** Proof that the new balancing system communicates correctly with the legacy grid interface without packet loss.
*   **Fail-Safe Trigger Test:** Demonstrated ability of the system to isolate itself within 200ms if a cascade condition is detected.

**First Immediate Task for the Lead Engineer (Today):**
Draft the **Batching Matrix** that groups the 40 substations by geographic proximity and electrical load similarity to minimize travel time and risk variance during Phase 1. This matrix must be ready for review by the two authorized approvers within 48 hours to unlock the first calibration batch.

**Halt Trigger:** If any single substation batch fails validation twice consecutively, halt all recalibration and convene a root-cause analysis before proceeding.
**Fallback Trigger:** If by Week 10 fewer than 15 substations are fully validated, defer the 15MW target to 10MW for the initial grid connection to meet the 6-month deadline safely.
