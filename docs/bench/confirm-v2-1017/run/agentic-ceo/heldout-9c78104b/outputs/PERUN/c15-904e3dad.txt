**Decision:** Proceed with a phased canary deployment gated by a hard "kill-switch" trigger, rather than a full cutover. The single number driving this is the **November 20th regulatory audit deadline**, which leaves only four days for contingency before the Black Friday surge.

**Figures:**
*   Timeline: Nov 20 (Audit) to Nov 24 (Surge) = 4 days buffer.
*   Performance Gap: Current 60% → Target 95% detection; False Positive cap < 2%.
*   Risk: 100% immediate cutover risks irreversible revenue loss if false positives spike.

**Workflow Sequence & Decision Rights:**

1.  **Shadow Mode Validation (Immediate – Nov 18):** Run the new model alongside the legacy system without affecting live decisions. Compare outputs to ensure the 95% detection/2% false positive ratio holds on live traffic.
    *   *Technical Lead Authority:* Authorize shadow infrastructure and data piping.
    *   *Product Lead Decision:* Define the specific "failure" metrics that halt progression to the next stage.

2.  **Regulatory Audit Gate (Due Nov 20):** Submit shadow mode results and logic documentation for compliance sign-off.
    *   *Product Lead Decision:* **Go/No-Go** on deployment based strictly on audit clearance. If audit fails, the rollout stops; no technical workaround is permitted.
    *   *Technical Lead Authority:* Prepare audit artifacts and remediate non-critical findings.

3.  **Canary Deployment (Nov 21 – Nov 23):** Route 1% of live traffic to the new model, scaling to 10% only if stability holds for 24 hours.
    *   *Technical Lead Authority:* Execute the traffic split and monitor system latency/error rates.
    *   *Product Lead Decision:* Set the **Risk Threshold**. You must define the exact false positive rate (e.g., >2.5% for 1 hour) that triggers an automatic revert. The Technical Lead cannot adjust this threshold without your explicit written approval.

4.  **Full Deployment (Nov 24):** Switch primary logic to the new model for 100% of traffic.
    *   *Joint Authorization:* Both must sign off confirming the Canary phase met all success criteria.

**Immediate First Action:**
In the strategy session, immediately **define and document the "Halt Trigger" values.** Before discussing infrastructure or timelines, agree on the specific false positive percentage and latency increase that will force an automatic reversion to the legacy 60% system. This ensures that if the model behaves unpredictably during the surge, the team reacts to pre-agreed data rather than debating under pressure.

**Note:** Do not attempt to optimize the model further after November 20th; any changes post-audit invalidate compliance.
**Note:** The "irreversible" nature of the step is mitigated only by the speed of the automated revert mechanism, not by planning perfection.
