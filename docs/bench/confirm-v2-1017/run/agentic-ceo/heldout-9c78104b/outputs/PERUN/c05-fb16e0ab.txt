**Decision:** Proceed with a "Freeze-Audit-Map" kickoff today; the single number driving this plan is the **10-day deadline**, which forces a parallel track of data auditing and rule definition rather than a sequential waterfall.

**Figures:** 4 TB data / 6 specialists = ~667 GB per person; 10 days minus 2 days for testing/cutover leaves 8 days for mapping; 12% duplication rate implies ~480 million duplicate records if not deduplicated pre-migration (assuming 4B total records based on 8 years history).

### Kickoff Session Agenda (4 Hours)

1.  **Risk Alignment (30 min):** Confirm the "Point of No Return." Explicitly acknowledge that unmapped fields = permanent loss.
2.  **Data Triage & Audit Strategy (60 min):** Define the scope of the 4 TB. Do not attempt to map everything. Identify the "Golden 20%" of fields used daily by the 50 AEs.
3.  **Decision Matrix Workshop (90 min):** You (the owner) must decide on the following mapping rules immediately:
    *   **Conflict Resolution:** When System A and System B disagree on a customer email, which system wins? (Rule: "Newest timestamp" vs. "System A priority").
    *   **Field Dropping:** List the specific legacy fields we agree to *discard* to meet the deadline. If you cannot decide today, they are automatically dropped.
    *   **Duplication Logic:** Define the exact matching criteria (e.g., Email + Phone vs. Email + Company Name) to merge the 12% duplicates.
4.  **Team Assignment & Parallel Tracks (60 min):** Split the 6 specialists into two squads:
    *   *Squad A (3 people):* Focus solely on the "Golden 20%" critical fields for Day 1 sales readiness.
    *   *Squad B (3 people):* Handle historical data archiving and non-critical field mapping.

### Preparatory Work for Integration Team (Start Immediately)

*   **Schema Inventory:** Generate a unified dictionary of all fields from the three legacy systems. Highlight conflicts in naming conventions and data types.
*   **Sample Extraction:** Pull a 1 GB representative sample of the data (including known dirty data) for immediate testing of mapping scripts. Do not touch the full 4 TB yet.
*   **Tooling Setup:** Configure the staging environment to accept writes. Verify that the rollback mechanism works on this staging environment before touching production.

### Initial Concrete Action to Prevent Data Corruption

**Execute a Read-Only Freeze and Create a Immutable Backup.**
Before any mapping script runs or any cutover planning proceeds, you must issue a command to lock all three legacy databases to "Read-Only" mode for a test window and trigger a full, verified backup to cold storage. This is your only safety net. If the migration fails or data corrupts during the cutover weekend, this backup is the only way to restore the pre-migration state before the schemas are erased. Do not rely on the "undo" function of the migration tool; rely on this external backup.

**Note:** The 10-day timeline is extremely aggressive for 4 TB; if the "Golden 20%" mapping is not 100% complete by Day 7, you must defer the historical data migration to post-quarter to ensure sales continuity.
**Note:** Your direct involvement in the "Decision Matrix" is the critical path; if those rules are delayed, the team cannot proceed without risking data loss.
