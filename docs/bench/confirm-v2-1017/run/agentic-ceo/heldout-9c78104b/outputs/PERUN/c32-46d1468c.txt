**Decision:** Do not attempt the full 300,000-account migration today. The risk of missing the December 31st deadline due to an unrecoverable error during a "big bang" cutover exceeds the risk of a phased approach. We will execute a **Pilot Migration** today to validate the pipeline, aiming for full production cutover only after successful pilot reconciliation.

**Figures:** 300,000 accounts total. Pilot scope: 300 accounts (0.1%). If the pilot takes 4 hours including validation, the full migration at linear scale would require ~4,000 hours (166 days), which is impossible before December 31st. This confirms the necessity of parallel processing validation or a staged rollout rather than a single sequential pass.
