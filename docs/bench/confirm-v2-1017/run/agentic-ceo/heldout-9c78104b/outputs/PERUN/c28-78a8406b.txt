**Decision:** Start immediately with a **stress test using archived 4K footage**, not shadow traffic or regional rollout. You cannot validate capacity or buffering thresholds on live user traffic until the pipeline is proven to handle the peak load in isolation; shadowing adds latency risk without proving stability, and a regional rollout assumes the system works before you have verified it can sustain 400 hours/day at 4K.

**Sequence of Tasks (3-Week Countdown to Aug 20):**

1.  **Days 1–4: Isolated Stress & Baseline.** Ingest 48 hours of archived 4K content at 2x peak velocity (800 hours/day equivalent) into the new pipeline. Measure transcoding latency, buffer depth, and error rates. *Goal: Prove the core engine holds 4K without buffering under overload.*
2.  **Days 5–10: Shadow Traffic Verification.** Route a copy of *live* ingest traffic to the new pipeline (no output to users). Compare output quality and timing against the legacy system. *Goal: Validate data integrity and synchronization without user impact.*
3.  **Days 11–14: Regional Canary (5%).** Switch the load balancer for 5% of geographic regions to serve output from the new nodes. Monitor real-user buffering metrics closely. *Goal: Validate the load balancer switch logic and CDN integration.*
4.  **Days 15–17: Full Dry Run & Rollback Drill.** Simulate the full cutover during a low-traffic window, then immediately execute the rollback plan. Time the failure detection and reversion. *Goal: Ensure the "irreversible" step is actually reversible within the churn tolerance window.*
5.  **Days 18–19: Buffer & Freeze.** Halt all non-essential changes. Pre-warm caches. Finalize the "Go/No-Go" checklist.
6.  **Day 20 (Premiere): Execute Cutover.** Switch global load balancer 4 hours before premiere.

**Critical Decisions You Must Make Now:**
*   **Failure Threshold:** Define the exact buffering duration (e.g., >2 seconds) or error rate that triggers an automatic abort during the 5% canary.
*   **Rollback Authority:** Name the single individual authorized to call the rollback during the premiere window; this cannot be a committee decision.
*   **Data Fidelity:** Acceptable tolerance for visual artifacts between legacy and new outputs (must be zero for 4K flagship).

**Immediate Actions for Engineering Team:**
*   **Backend (5 engineers):** Isolate 72 hours of diverse 4K archived footage today. Script the ingestion harness to pump this at 2x peak speed by tomorrow morning. Hard-code the circuit breaker to kill the job if latency exceeds 150% of SLA.
*   **QA (3 testers):** Draft the "Go/No-Go" checklist based on the stress test metrics. Do not wait for the test to finish; define the pass/fail criteria now. Prepare the load balancer configuration snippets for the 5% split.

**Halt Trigger:** If the Day 4 stress test shows >0.5% frame drops or buffering at 1.5x load, stop all rollout planning and fix the core pipeline.
**Fallback Trigger:** If the 5% canary (Day 14) shows churn risk >0.1%, revert to 0% immediately and extend the timeline; do not proceed to global switch.

*Note: This plan prioritizes verifying capacity before exposing users. Do not attempt regional rollout until the isolated stress test passes.*
