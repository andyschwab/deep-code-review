**To:** Product Steering Committee
**From:** Product Management
**Date:** October 14
**Subject:** Prioritization Proposal: Native Slack Integration vs. Analytics Dashboard

**Executive Summary**
We propose pausing development on the planned Analytics Dashboard to immediately prioritize a native Slack integration. This decision is driven by urgent retention signals from our largest accounts, though it requires validation to confirm widespread demand before full commitment.

**The Problem & Opportunity**
Between October 10–12, four distinct support tickets were filed by our largest remote teams. Team leads report manually copying status updates into Slack channels, consuming ~30 minutes daily per lead.
*   **Current State:** High-friction manual workflow causing operational drag for power users.
*   **Hypothesis:** Automating this flow will increase Daily Active Usage (DAU) by ≥15% within 30 days of release.
*   **Risk:** These four tickets may represent edge-case workflows rather than a systemic issue across our 500-company base. If unique to these clients, the ROI does not justify diverting engineering resources.

**Comparative Analysis**

| Feature | Slack Integration | Analytics Dashboard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Value** | **Retention & Efficiency:** Removes daily friction for high-value accounts; directly addresses churn risk. | **Insight:** Provides visibility into usage trends; currently a "nice-to-have" for admins. |
| **Urgency** | **High:** Active pain point occurring daily; competitors offer similar integrations. | **Medium:** Strategic goal, but no immediate customer complaints blocking adoption. |
| **Engineering Effort** | **Medium:** Requires Slack API authentication, webhook management, and secure data mapping. Estimated 3–4 sprints. | **High:** Requires complex data aggregation, visualization libraries, and performance optimization. Estimated 5–6 sprints. |
| **Impact Scope** | Unknown (Needs Validation). Potentially high if workflow is standard. | Known (Broad). Useful to all 500 companies, but low frequency of use. |

**Recommendation & Next Steps**
We recommend a **"Validate then Build"** approach rather than an immediate full-scale pivot.

1.  **Immediate Action (Week 1):** Conduct rapid discovery with the 4 requesting clients and a control group of 10 similar-sized accounts to verify if this manual process is standard.
2.  **Decision Gate:**
    *   *If >40% of surveyed accounts confirm the pain point:* Prioritize Slack Integration immediately. The retention upside and reduced support load outweigh the dashboard delay.
    *   *If <40% confirm:* Deprioritize integration; proceed with Analytics Dashboard and offer custom API support to the requesting leads.
3.  **Technical Note for CTO:** Preliminary assessment suggests the Slack integration is less architecturally complex than the Dashboard but introduces ongoing maintenance liabilities (API versioning, uptime dependencies). A lightweight MVP using existing webhooks is feasible within 2 sprints for testing.

**Conclusion**
Do not commit to full development yet. Validate the prevalence of this workflow immediately. If validated, the Slack integration offers a higher immediate impact on retention and DAU than the Analytics Dashboard and should jump the queue.

*Validation interviews are scheduled for Oct 16–17; a final go/no-go recommendation will follow by Oct 18.*
*Engineering spike requested to refine effort estimates for the MVP path.*
