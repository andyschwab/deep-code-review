### Executive Summary: Checkout Simplification Proposal

**Current State:** 60% abandonment at identity verification (approx. 1,200 lost transactions/week).
**Proposed Solution:** Reduce form fields from 12 to 4.
**Critical Gap:** Root cause of drop-off is unconfirmed (Form Length vs. Mobile Latency).

#### Evidence Assessment
We currently **do not have sufficient evidence** to proceed directly to an A/B test of the simplified form. The hypothesis assumes form length is the primary friction point, but analytics indicate a strong alternative variable: slow loading times on rural mobile networks.

If we test the form reduction without addressing latency, we risk a "false negative" result where the new design fails simply because the page loads too slowly, leading us to discard a potentially valid UX improvement. Conversely, if latency is the sole culprit, simplifying the form adds no value while potentially increasing fraud risk.

#### Recommended Immediate Actions (Pre-Sprint)
Before committing engineering resources to an A/B test, execute these low-effort diagnostics:
1.  **Segment Abandonment by Device/Network:** Correlate drop-off rates with connection speed (3G/4G/Wi-Fi) and device type. If abandonment spikes specifically on slow connections regardless of time-on-page, latency is the primary driver.
2.  **Analyze Time-on-Step:** Compare the average time spent on the verification step for completions vs. abandonments. If abandoners leave almost immediately (<5 seconds), it suggests a loading error or immediate friction (latency). If they leave after 30+ seconds, form fatigue is more likely.
3.  **Compliance Pre-Check:** Engage Legal/Compliance immediately to confirm which of the 8 removed fields are legally mandatory for Brazilian banking regulations (e.g., CPF validation, address proofing). Removing mandatory fields is not an option for testing.

#### Potential Outcomes & Risks

| Scenario | Outcome | Risk Level | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | : |
| **Latency is the root cause** | Form simplification yields <2% conversion lift. | **High** (Wasted Dev Cycle) | Prioritize performance optimization (lazy loading, asset compression) before UX changes. |
| **Form length is the root cause** | Conversion increases significantly; fraud rates remain stable. | **Low** | Proceed with full rollout upon compliance sign-off. |
| **Simplification compromises fraud** | Conversion increases, but chargebacks/fraud spike >15%. | **Critical** | Implement backend verification APIs to replace manual fields; do not remove data collection, just hide it from UI. |
| **Regulatory Violation** | Product launch blocked or fined by local authorities. | **Critical** | Secure written approval from Compliance Officer before any code is merged. |

#### Recommendation for CTO & Compliance Officer
**Hold the A/B test.** Allocate 2–3 days for the diagnostic segmentation analysis. If data confirms form fatigue is the driver, proceed with the test *only* after Compliance validates that the 4 remaining fields satisfy all regulatory requirements. If latency is the driver, pivot the sprint to performance engineering.
