### Recommendation: Prioritize Performance Optimization

**Decision:** Allocate the Q3 budget and engineering resources exclusively to **performance optimization** to reduce app load time. Defer the dark theme initiative.

**Rationale:**
For a mobile wallet in a market with inconsistent connectivity like Nairobi, **functional reliability is the primary driver of retention**, not aesthetic preference. Your data indicates a critical failure point: a 15% drop-off rate during peak latency. This represents approximately 30,000 users monthly who are attempting to transact but failing. In the fintech sector, a failed transaction is not merely a frustration; it is a breakdown of trust that often leads to permanent churn.

While 30% of users requested a dark theme, survey votes measure *preference*, not *behavioral impact*. There is no evidence linking visual themes to transaction completion rates. Conversely, the link between latency and drop-off is already proven by your current analytics. Reducing load time by two seconds directly addresses the root cause of your biggest leakage point.

**Impact Analysis:**
*   **Performance Optimization:** If successful, recovering even half of the 15% drop-off translates to ~15,000 additional successful transactions per month. This directly increases revenue, user trust, and lifetime value. Speed is a feature that works for 100% of your user base, especially those on older devices or slower networks.
*   **Dark Theme:** The potential upside is limited to perceived battery savings and satisfaction for a subset of users. It does not solve the friction preventing transactions. Launching a new visual feature while the core utility (sending money) is fragile during peak hours risks appearing tone-deaf to user needs.

**Strategic Approach:**
Treat the dark theme as a low-risk follow-up initiative. Once the core transaction flow is stabilized and the drop-off rate is minimized, you can revisit the theme request with a clearer baseline. For now, the "must-have" (reliability) must supersede the "nice-to-have" (aesthetics).

**Next Steps for Engineering & Marketing:**
1.  **Engineering:** Define specific latency targets for peak hours and instrument detailed logging to measure the reduction in drop-off rates post-optimization.
2.  **Marketing:** Frame the Q3 campaign around "Reliability" and "Speed," highlighting SavannaPay's commitment to working when users need it most, rather than promoting visual updates.

***

*Note: This recommendation assumes the 15% drop-off is primarily caused by timeout errors rather than unrelated UI confusion.*
*Re-evaluate the dark theme only after performance metrics stabilize below a 5% drop-off rate.*
