# Decision Brief: Dark Mode Feature Launch Strategy

**To:** Head of Design, Engineering Lead
**From:** Product Manager, EuroDesign Hub
**Date:** March 15
**Subject:** Recommendation on Dark Mode Beta Launch vs. Further Research

### Executive Summary
Conflicting data exists between high user demand (60% rating dark mode as "critical") and observed usability degradation (20% increase in navigation errors in prototypes). While user preference is strong, the navigation errors present a significant risk to immediate support volume and user trust. **Recommendation: Delay the broad beta launch by two weeks to conduct targeted contrast ratio analysis and A/B testing.** Proceeding immediately with the current prototype risks validating a flawed implementation rather than the feature itself.

### Data Synthesis & Conflict Analysis
*   **Demand Signal:** The March 1st survey indicates a clear market expectation. For a design tool, aesthetic customization is often tied to perceived professional utility. Ignoring this risks churn among the 15 agency partners.
*   **Usability Signal:** The 20% error rate in navigation is a leading indicator of future support tickets. If 60% of users adopt this feature and encounter a 20% error rate, we anticipate a substantial spike in "broken UI" complaints, potentially overshadowing the satisfaction gain.
*   **Root Cause Hypothesis:** The assumption that errors are merely due to an "adaptation period" is unproven. It is equally probable that the current palette fails WCAG contrast standards or that the theme-switching logic disrupts DOM rendering, causing fundamental interaction failures.

### Trade-off Assessment

| Option | Pros | Cons | Risk Profile |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Launch Beta Next Month** | • Validates demand quickly<br>• Delivers on explicit user requests<br>• Generates real-world usage data | • High probability of increased support tickets<br>• Potential erosion of trust if the feature feels "broken"<br>• Technical debt if architecture needs rework | **High:** Risks confusing "bad implementation" with "bad feature." |
| **Delay for Research (2 Weeks)** | • Isolates root cause (contrast vs. logic)<br>• Allows optimization before user exposure<br>• Reduces predicted support volume | • Delays value delivery to eager users<br>• Requires engineering resources for investigation | **Low:** Mitigates immediate operational risk while preserving the roadmap item. |

### Recommended Action Plan
1.  **Immediate Pause on Broad Beta:** Do not release to the full network next month.
2.  **Targeted Diagnostic Sprint (2 Weeks):**
    *   **Engineering:** Audit contrast ratios against WCAG AA/AAA standards specifically for the dark palette. Review theme-switching state management for latency or rendering glitches.
    *   **Design:** Create two variant prototypes: one with adjusted contrast only, one with simplified navigation structures.
3.  **Micro-Validation:** Run rapid usability tests (n=5 per agency) on the adjusted prototypes. If navigation errors drop below 5%, proceed to a limited "Friends & Family" beta with 3 agencies.
4.  **Communication:** Inform stakeholders that we are optimizing the feature to ensure it meets EuroDesign Hub's quality standards, framing the delay as a commitment to excellence rather than a blocker.

### Conclusion
The demand for dark mode is valid, but the execution currently carries unacceptable usability risks. A short delay to distinguish between poor contrast and fundamental architectural flaws will prevent a costly support crisis and ensure the feature drives satisfaction rather than frustration.

***

*Note: This brief assumes the 20% error rate was statistically significant within the test group size. If the sample size was fewer than 10 users, the diagnostic sprint should include expanding the test pool.*
