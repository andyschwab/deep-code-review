### Critical Experiments to Validate Social Feed Demand

Given the constraint of zero user-generated content (UGC) in text forums and the high risk of spending 50% of your runway on a hypothesis that may be flawed, do not build the full social feed yet. The lack of posts likely indicates a **motivation/culture issue** or a **lack of worthy content**, not just a UI problem. Artisans may view their work as transactional (sell and leave) rather than social, or they may fear criticism.

Run these three low-cost experiments to distinguish between a product failure and a community culture issue before writing code:

#### 1. The "Concierge" Content Experiment (Tests: Do they have content?)
**Goal:** Determine if users actually have visual content worth sharing but lack the channel, or if they simply don't create content.
**Method:** Manually recruit 20 active artisans via WhatsApp or direct message. Offer to create a "Featured Artisan" post for them on a temporary landing page or even a dedicated Instagram account managed by you. Ask them to send you 3 photos and a short story about their latest piece. You format and post it.
**Success Metric:** If >40% refuse to send photos or struggle to find recent work, the problem is **content availability**, not the interface. If they send content eagerly but never visit the page, the problem is **distribution/consumption**.
**Cost:** <$500 (staff time + small incentives).

#### 2. The "Fake Door" Intent Test (Tests: Is there motivation?)
**Goal:** Measure intent to post without building the feed.
**Method:** Add a "Share Your Work" button in the current app navigation that leads to a simple form asking for a photo and caption. Upon submission, show a message: *"Thanks! We are building a new community gallery to showcase this. Would you like to be notified when it launches?"* Include an optional field: *"What stops you from sharing your work publicly today?"*
**Success Metric:** Track the click-through rate (CTR) on the button. If CTR is <2% of daily active users, the desire to share is low. Analyze the qualitative feedback in the optional field to identify cultural barriers (e.g., "I don't want competitors copying me," "My photos aren't good enough").
**Cost:** ~$1,000 (minor frontend tweak).

#### 3. The "Seeded Community" Pilot (Tests: Will they engage with others?)
**Goal:** Validate if a visual feed drives engagement or if the culture is fundamentally passive.
**Method:** Create a private WhatsApp Group or Telegram channel with 50 hand-picked artisans. Seed it daily with high-quality photos of their work (with permission) and prompt discussions ("How did you achieve this texture?"). Act as the community manager for 2 weeks.
**Success Metric:** Observe if members start posting unprompted. If even in a low-friction, high-trust environment like WhatsApp they remain silent, a dedicated app feature will not solve the **cultural/motivation** barrier.
**Cost:** <$200 (community manager time).

### Recommendation for Stakeholders
Present these findings to your co-founder and investor. If these experiments fail to generate organic content or reveal deep-seated cultural hesitations, proceeding with a $25,000 build is highly risky. The data will likely show that the issue is not the *format* (text vs. visual) but the *incentive structure*. You may need to pivot to incentivizing content (e.g., "Post to get featured in our newsletter") or accepting that your users prefer a pure marketplace utility over a social network.

*Note: These experiments can be executed within 2 weeks for under $2,000, preserving 90% of your allocated budget.*
*Focus on the "Why" behind the silence before solving the "How" of posting.*
