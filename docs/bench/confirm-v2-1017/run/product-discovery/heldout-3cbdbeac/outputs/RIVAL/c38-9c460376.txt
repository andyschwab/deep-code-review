**To:** CTO, SeoulStream
**From:** Product Manager
**Subject:** Risk Assessment: Proposed Public Template Gallery Investment

**Executive Summary**
Proceeding with the $8,000 infrastructure investment for a public template gallery carries a critical risk of resource waste. Our data indicates a fundamental disconnect between our hypothesis (users want to create/share layouts) and reality (zero creations in six weeks among 4,000 DAU). While pre-built templates receive 600+ clicks hourly, user-generated content (UGC) is non-existent. Building a gallery to host content that users have demonstrated no desire to create results in an empty storefront. We are solving a distribution problem for a product feature that has failed initial value validation.

**The Core Assumption Flaw**
The engineering team's proposal assumes the barrier to entry is *discovery* (users need a place to browse). However, the data suggests the barrier is *motivation* or *capability* (users do not want to build, or cannot build, templates).
*   **Current State:** 0 saved custom templates / 6 weeks / 4,000 DAU.
*   **Proposed Solution:** A gallery for browsing/sharing.
*   **The Gap:** A gallery requires supply (creators) before it can offer value to demand (browsers). Without evidence that users want to *create*, building the infrastructure to *share* creates zero utility.

**Recommendation**
Pause the $8,000 infrastructure build. Redirect immediate effort toward qualitative discovery with our top 10 power users to validate if the desire to create templates exists at all. If the desire is absent, the gallery is unnecessary, and we should double down on expanding the pre-built library which already shows strong traction.

***

**Discovery Questions for Top 10 Power Users**
*Ask these in a 1:1 interview context. Focus on past behavior, not future intent.*

1.  "Walk me through the last time you felt frustrated with the current layout options. What specifically were you trying to achieve that you couldn't?"
2.  "We noticed many of you use the pre-built templates frequently. Can you describe a specific instance where you considered modifying a template or building one from scratch? What stopped you?"
3.  "Have you ever used a 'create your own template' feature on any other platform? If yes, what was the outcome? If no, why not?"
4.  "If you had a perfect layout today, would you prefer to build it yourself over 30 minutes, or have us add it to the pre-built library? Why?"
5.  "What would need to happen for you to feel confident sharing a layout you created with the rest of the SeoulStream community?"

**Next Steps**
Upon completing these interviews, we will categorize findings into "Usability Issue" (tool is too hard), "Value Issue" (users don't care), or "Distribution Issue" (users create but don't know how to share). Only if evidence points to a Distribution Issue should we reconsider the gallery build.

*Note: Do not mention the planned gallery during these interviews to avoid biasing responses.*
*Budget hold recommended until findings are reviewed.*
