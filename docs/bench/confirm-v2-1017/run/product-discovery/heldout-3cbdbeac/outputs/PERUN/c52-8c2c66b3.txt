### Decision: Do Not Build Yet (Riskiest Assumption Gate Failed)

Your hypothesis that "a visual interface will spark engagement" is untested. Building a $25,000 feature on top of a community with **zero** existing text contributions is a high-risk bet that confuses *format* with *motivation*. If users lack the desire or safety to share publicly, a prettier feed will not solve it.

You must validate the root cause (Content Gap vs. Motivation Gap) before writing code. Below are the three cheapest experiments to run immediately.

#### Experiment 1: The "Concierge" Feed (Test Motivation)
**Goal:** Determine if users have content they are willing to share if friction is removed.
**Method:** Do not build a feed. Manually curate one.
1.  DM 50 active buyers/sellers asking for one photo of their latest work or workspace.
2.  Ask permission to post it to a temporary, manually updated "Community Spotlight" page (or even a simple PDF/Notion link sent via email).
3.  Track: How many say yes? How many provide high-quality photos without a dedicated UI?
**Metric:** Conversion rate of DM requests to received photos.
**Kill Threshold:** If <15% of contacted users provide a photo when asked personally, a social feed will likely remain empty. The issue is motivation/trust, not UI.

#### Experiment 2: The "Fake Door" Intent Test (Test Demand)
**Goal:** Measure intent to post vs. passive consumption.
**Method:** Add a "Share Your Work" button to the current app header.
1.  When clicked, show a modal: "We're building a visual feed! Would you post your latest project here?" with options: "Yes, definitely," "Maybe," and "No, I prefer browsing."
2.  Optionally, ask a follow-up open text: "What would make you comfortable sharing?"
**Metric:** Percentage of clicks that result in a "Yes, definitely."
**Kill Threshold:** If <5% of daily active users click the button, or if <2% of those clickers commit to "Yes," the demand is insufficient to justify $25k.

#### Experiment 3: The "Anxiety" Interview (Root Cause Analysis)
**Goal:** Uncover the specific barrier (Fear? Lack of time? No photos?).
**Method:** Conduct 5–7 "Mom Test" interviews with registered users who haven't posted.
**Ask:** "Tell me about the last time you took a photo of your craft." (Past behavior). "What happened to that photo?" "Who did you show it to?" "What worries you about posting it in a public forum?"
**Output:** A list of specific anxieties (e.g., "I don't want competitors copying my style" or "My workshop is messy").
**Decision Rule:** If the barrier is *privacy/competition*, a feed requires robust privacy controls (complex build). If the barrier is *no photos exist*, a feed is useless.

### Revised Financial Plan
*   **Current Plan:** Spend $25,000 (50% runway) on a feature with a high probability of zero adoption.
*   **Recommended Plan:** Spend <$500 and 1 week running the experiments above.
    *   **If Experiments Pass:** Proceed to a minimal "Photo-Only" MVP with a strict kill metric (e.g., "If <50 posts in month 1, sunset the feature").
    *   **If Experiments Fail:** Pivot resources to solving the specific motivation barrier (e.g., education, trust features, or direct messaging) or double down on core marketplace liquidity.

**Verdict for Investor/Co-founder:** We are pausing the social feed build. We will allocate 1 week to validate whether the silence is due to a lack of content or a lack of trust. We will not spend runway on a solution until we confirm the problem.
