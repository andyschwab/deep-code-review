The sales lead must ask the prospect directly: "If we deliver these three features in January instead of December, does that disqualify us from the current procurement cycle?" If the answer is "yes," the revenue risk equals the compliance risk. If "no," the features are safely deferrable.

You are comparing a **known, hard deadline** with **potential existential consequences** against a **revenue opportunity** dependent on an **unverified assumption**.

*   **Scenario A (Build Features):** You miss the Dec 31 deadline or trigger an audit flag. Result: Potential loss of 30% of clients and legal exposure. The new features become irrelevant if the platform cannot legally operate.
