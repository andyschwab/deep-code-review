**Strategic Memo: AI Pivot Validation Status**
**Date:** [Current Date]
**To:** Investors
**From:** Founder, LagosAgri Tech
**Subject:** Validation Gaps Before AI Roadmap Commitment

**Current Evidence Strength: Weak / Anecdotal**
We hold two pilot signups expressing interest in predictive yield algorithms. While promising, this constitutes **stated intent**, not **behavioral validation**. These two cooperatives are early adopters and likely unrepresentative of the broader market, which may prefer manual tools. We cannot yet confirm if the "30% premium" assumption holds for the mass market or if the AI features solve a critical enough pain point to drive retention post-harvest.

**Decision:** We are pausing the full engineering pivot until we disconfirm the riskiest assumption: *That the broader market values AI predictions enough to pay a premium over simple manual tracking.*

**Immediate Action Plan (Before October Harvest):**
We will not build the full AI suite yet. Instead, we will run the following cheap tests to validate demand before committing capital:

1.  **The "Fake Door" Test on Pilots:**
    *   **Action:** Present the two pilot farms with a mock-up or manual simulation of the AI insight (Wizard-of-Oz). Do not build the algorithm yet.
    *   **Metric:** Will they change their farming behavior based on this manual insight?
    *   **Kill Threshold:** If fewer than 1 of the 2 pilots acts on the insight or asks for it again without prompting, we pause the AI build.

2.  **Market Representativeness Check:**
    *   **Action:** Conduct 5 "Mom Test" interviews with non-pilot farmers focusing on their *current* yield prediction methods and what they currently pay for, not their interest in our future AI.
    *   **Metric:** Evidence of existing spend on complex analytics vs. preference for simplicity.
    *   **Kill Threshold:** If 4 out of 5 farmers express anxiety about complexity or cite $0 current spend on analytics, the "30% premium" assumption is invalid.

3.  **Willingness-to-Pay Verification:**
    *   **Action:** Attempt to collect a deposit or signed letter of intent for the *premium* tier specifically before the feature exists.
    *   **Metric:** Cash or binding commitment.
    *   **Kill Threshold:** If pilots refuse to commit financially to the premium tier despite verbal enthusiasm, the pricing model is unproven.

**Risks of Pivoting Now:**
*   **Building the Wrong Thing:** Engineering a complex AI stack for a market that needs reliability and simplicity.
*   **Cash Burn:** Diverting resources from core manual tracking stability (which the pilots actually signed up for) to unproven features.
*   **False Positive Bias:** Mistaking the enthusiasm of two innovative cooperatives for mass-market demand.

**Next Update:**
We will report results from the above tests by [Date 2 weeks from now]. If thresholds are met, we proceed to MVP build. If not, we double down on optimizing manual tracking for retention.
