# Decision Memo: Visual Drag-and-Drop Builder Investment

**To:** VP of Product, Engineering Director
**From:** Product Manager, Mexico City Makers
**Date:** [Current Date]
**Subject:** Go/No-Go Recommendation for No-Code Store Builder

### Executive Summary (BLUF)
**Recommendation: Proceed to Targeted Technical Validation, then Build.**
The user evidence strongly supports the *problem* (high friction causing churn), but the *solution* carries a critical technical risk that must be resolved before committing three engineers for a full quarter. We have sufficient evidence to justify a one-week "feasibility spike," but not yet a full build.

### Evidence Summary
*   **Problem Validation:** 12 of 20 interviewed non-technical merchants (60%) explicitly stated they cannot edit storefronts without hiring developers. This aligns with our quantitative data: 40% of new sign-ups churn within month one, citing complexity.
*   **Hypothesis:** A no-code builder will reduce early churn by 50% (from 40% to 20%) and increase satisfaction.
*   **Critical Unknown:** Can our current rendering engine support real-time preview on older mobile devices (30% of our user base) without performance degradation?

### Riskiest Assumption Gate
The decision to build hinges on a single **feasibility assumption**: *The current engine can render real-time previews on low-end mobile devices with acceptable latency.*

If this assumption is false, the product fails for 30% of our users, rendering the investment wasted. We cannot validate this via interview; it requires a code-based test.

**Required Action Before Full Build:**
Execute a **one-week technical spike** (1 engineer) to prototype the rendering loop on target devices.
*   **Metric:** Frame rate and load time on defined "older mobile" specs.
*   **Kill Threshold:** If real-time preview causes >2s lag or crashes on >10% of test devices, the current architecture cannot support this feature. We must either pivot to a "save-and-preview" model or reject the build until infrastructure is upgraded.

### Financial & Resource Justification
*   **Request:** 3 Engineers for 1 Quarter.
*   **Conditionality:**
    1.  **Week 1:** 1 Engineer runs the feasibility spike.
    2.  **Weeks 2–12:** Full team (3 Engineers) commences build *only if* the spike meets the performance threshold.
*   **Impact:** Addressing the 40% churn rate directly impacts LTV. Reducing churn by half effectively doubles the lifespan of a significant cohort, likely outweighing the development cost within two quarters.

### Non-Goals (Scope Defense)
To ensure delivery within the quarter, the following are explicitly **out of scope** for this initial release:
*   **Desktop-only optimization:** The solution must work on mobile first; desktop parity is a subsequent phase.
*   **Advanced custom code injection:** This tool is for *non-technical* users; developer hooks are a non-goal for V1.
*   **Template marketplace:** We are building the *editor*, not the content library.

### Decision Required
1.  **Approve** the one-week feasibility spike immediately.
2.  **Conditional Approval** for the full quarter allocation, contingent on the spike passing the performance kill threshold.
3.  **Reject** the project if the spike confirms the engine cannot handle the load, triggering a search for alternative solutions (e.g., third-party integration or infrastructure overhaul).
