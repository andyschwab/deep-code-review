### Structured Assessment: Signup Simplification Trade-offs

**Current State Evidence**
*   **Baseline Conversion:** 14% (Landing Page → Funded Account).
*   **Primary Friction Point:** 60% of total drop-off occurs at the document upload stage.
*   **Volume Context:** 8,000 applications processed in Q2.
*   **Risk Constraint:** Maximum acceptable fraud rate is 1.5%.
*   **Financial Exposure:** Breaching the fraud threshold risks >$50,000 in losses.

**The Unverified Assumption**
*   **Claim:** Removing three verification steps will lift conversion to ≥22%.
*   **Basis:** Competitor benchmarks from last year.
*   **Status:** **UNVERIFIED.** This is an external market claim, not internal behavioral data. Local user behavior in Nairobi may differ from the competitor's context or timeframe.

**The Riskiest Assumption Gate**
Before a full rollout before the 15th, the critical unknown is not whether conversion will rise (which is likely given the friction removal), but whether **fraud will remain ≤1.5%**.
*   **The Test:** A limited "concierge" or "fake-door" experiment is required. Release the simplified flow to a small, controlled segment (e.g., 5% of traffic or a specific geographic sub-region) rather than the entire marketing push.
*   **Kill Threshold:** If the fraud rate in the test segment exceeds **1.5%** (or a statistically significant leading indicator of it), the simplified flow must be halted or reverted immediately, regardless of conversion gains.
*   **Decision Rule:** Do not proceed to full scale until the test segment provides real fraud data, not projected benchmarks.

**Post-Launch Monitoring Metrics (If Proceeding)**
If the decision is made to proceed, the following metrics must be tracked daily with explicit denominators:

1.  **Fraud Rate by Cohort:** (Confirmed Fraud Cases / Total Funded Accounts) specifically for the *simplified flow* vs. the *legacy flow*.
    *   *Alert Trigger:* Simplified flow rate > 1.5%.
2.  **Conversion Lift Validation:** (Funded Accounts / Landing Page Visits) for the simplified cohort.
    *   *Goal Check:* Does it approach the hypothesized 22%, or is the lift marginal?
3.  **Cost-of-Risk per New Customer:** (Total Fraud Losses in Cohort / New Customers Acquired).
    *   *Viability Check:* Does the margin from the extra volume cover the increased loss rate?
4.  **Time-to-Fund:** Average time from signup to disbursement.
    *   *Secondary Benefit:* Verify if the removal of steps actually speeds up capital access, a key value prop for small businesses.
5.  **False Negative Rate:** (Legitimate users rejected by downstream automated checks after passing simplified signup).
    *   *Risk:* Shifting friction later in the funnel may frustrate users who already invested time.

**Non-Goals for This Decision**
*   **Permanent Policy Change:** This assessment covers the immediate marketing push window. A permanent removal of verification requires a longer-term fraud pattern analysis.
*   **Total Market Sizing:** Estimating the total addressable market gain is out of scope; focus is strictly on the marginal impact of the flow change on the existing 8,000-application volume baseline.

**Required Inputs for Final Go/No-Go**
To finalize the decision before the 15th, you must supply:
1.  The specific definition of "fraud" used for the 1.5% threshold (e.g., first-payment default, identity theft, synthetic identity).
2.  The minimum sample size required to detect a 0.5% increase in fraud with statistical significance given your current volume.
