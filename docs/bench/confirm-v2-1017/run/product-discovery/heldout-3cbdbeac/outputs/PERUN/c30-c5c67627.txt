**Recommendation: Execute Deep Performance Optimization**

**Decision Rationale**
Based on the evidence provided, the initiative to reduce app load time by two seconds is the only viable path for Q3. The choice is between addressing a confirmed, revenue-blocking friction point (15% drop-off during peak latency) versus implementing a requested aesthetic feature (dark theme) with no established link to your core retention metrics.

**Evidence Analysis**
1.  **Confirmed Loss vs. Hypothetical Gain:** You have hard data showing that 15% of your 200,000 monthly active users (30,000 users) abandon transactions during peak hours due to performance issues. This is a direct leak in your core loop: transaction completion. In contrast, the dark theme request relies on an *assumption* that it will improve battery life perception and session duration. You explicitly stated there is "no concrete evidence" linking visual theme to retention.
2.  **The Riskiest Assumption Gate:** Your current riskiest assumption is that "aesthetic improvements can outweigh functional friction." Without a prior experiment proving that dark mode increases transaction completion or retention in low-connectivity markets, building it is a gamble. The performance optimization, however, targets a known failure mode. Reducing load time directly addresses the cause of the 15% drop-off.
3.  **Stage Awareness:** With 200k MAU, SavannaPay is in a growth/stabilization phase where retention and reliability dominate feature breadth. A 2-second improvement in a high-friction environment (Nairobi's inconsistent connectivity) is a structural fix that enables all other features to work. A dark theme is a "nice-to-have" that does not fix the broken pipe.

**Projected Impact**
*   **Performance Path:** If the 2-second reduction recovers even half of the dropped sessions (7.5% of total traffic), you retain ~15,000 additional monthly transacting users. This directly impacts revenue and trust.
*   **Dark Theme Path:** Even if 30% of users prefer it, if it does not solve the latency crash, those users will still encounter the 15% drop-off. Satisfaction with the look of an app does not compensate for the inability to complete a payment.

**Action Plan**
1.  **Commit to Performance:** Allocate the full Q3 budget and developer time to the 2-second load time reduction.
2.  **Define Success Metric:** The primary metric is the reduction of the peak-hour drop-off rate from 15% to <5%. Secondary metric: Increase in total transaction volume during peak hours.
3.  **Defer Dark Theme:** Record "Dark Theme" as a strategic non-goal for Q3. Revisit only after the performance baseline is stabilized and you have run a low-cost test (e.g., a fake-door or A/B test on a small segment) that proves its impact on retention, not just preference.

**Note:** Do not split resources; doing both partially will likely fail to move the needle on either drop-off rates or user satisfaction.
**Note:** Communicate to the 30% who voted for dark mode that reliability improvements are the prerequisite for a better overall experience.
