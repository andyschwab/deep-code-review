Your assumption is that filters are the solution, but you have zero evidence that this specific group can or wants to use them. Before writing code, you must test the single riskiest assumption: *"Our older demographic needs and can successfully use faceted filters to find variants."*

**Run this cheap test immediately (Cost: <4 hours):**
*   **Method:** Concierge/Wizard-of-Oz Test.

*   **Task:** Ask them to find a specific variant.
*   **Variable A (Sorting):** Manually reorder the list for them based on their verbal cues ("I want the red one") to simulate "smart sorting."

*   **Metric:** Time to success and observed frustration (hesitation, misclicks).
