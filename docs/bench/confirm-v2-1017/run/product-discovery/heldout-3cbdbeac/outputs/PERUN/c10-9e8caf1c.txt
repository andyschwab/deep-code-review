1.  **The "Fake Door" Test (Desirability)**

    *   **Metric:** Click-through rate and completion of a manual onboarding call (concierge test).
    *   **Kill Threshold:** If fewer than 5% of visitors click the button, or if fewer than 10 teams express interest in a manual setup over two weeks, **drop the freemium idea**. The demand does not exist.

2.  **The "Security Bypass" Interview (Viability)**

    *   **Question:** Ask about the last time they successfully introduced a new tool to their enterprise without a formal security review. Do not ask "Would you use this?"; ask "Show me the last tool you snuck in."

    *   **Question:** "If this tool became widely available for free to junior staff, how would that impact your confidence in recommending it for critical data?"
