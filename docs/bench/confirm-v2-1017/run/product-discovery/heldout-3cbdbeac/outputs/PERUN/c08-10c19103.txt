**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Co-founders, Seed Investors
**FROM:** Founder, Thames Learn
**DATE:** October 26, 2023
**SUBJECT:** Decision Framework: Social Sharing Feature vs. Mobile App Release

**Current Evidence Status: UNVERIFIED**
We currently possess zero behavioral evidence supporting the hypothesis that "incentives are the missing link." The data point of **0 organic invites from 1,200 users** over six months is a strong negative signal. Before committing resources to gamified rewards, we must distinguish between a *motivation* problem (users want to share but need a push) and a *value* problem (users do not find the certificate worth sharing).

**The Riskiest Assumption Gate**
Our critical assumption is: *Users desire to showcase Thames Learn achievements, but lack sufficient incentive.*
If this assumption is false, building gamification will not trigger viral growth; it will only add complexity to a product users already ignore socially.

**Required Validation Tests (Must Run Before Build)**
We cannot proceed to development until we disconfirm the risk that the value proposition itself is too niche. We propose two cheap, behavioral tests:

1.  **The "Fake Door" Intent Test:**
    *   **Action:** Add a button next to the certificate: "Share to LinkedIn for a chance to win a [High-Value Prize/Course Upgrade]."
    *   **Metric:** Click-through rate (CTR).
    *   **Kill Threshold:** If CTR is <5% even with an external reward, the desire to share is effectively zero. **Decision:** Drop the social feature entirely.
    *   **Cost:** 2 days engineering time.

2.  **Mom-Test Interviews (Qualitative):**
    *   **Action:** Interview 10 users who completed a course but did not share.
    *   **Question Focus:** Ask about the last time they shared *any* educational achievement and what prompted it. Do not ask "Would you share if we gave you points?"
    *   **Goal:** Uncover if the certificate lacks social currency regardless of rewards.

**Opportunity Cost Analysis**
*   **Scenario A (Build Social Feature Now):**
    *   **Resource Drain:** Diverts engineering focus from the Q1 Mobile App.
    *   **Risk:** High probability of building a "ghost town" feature if the underlying desire to share is absent.
    *   **Delay:** Pushes Mobile App launch by estimated 4–6 weeks.
*   **Scenario B (Validate First, Then Decide):**
    *   **Resource Drain:** Minimal (48 hours for test implementation + interview time).
    *   **Risk:** Low. We either kill a bad idea quickly or proceed with high confidence.
    *   **Impact:** Protects the Mobile App timeline.

**Recommendation**
**Do not build the gamified social feature yet.** The absence of organic sharing despite visible buttons suggests the issue is likely the *value* of the share, not the *incentive*.

Proceed immediately with the **Fake Door Test** and **User Interviews**.
*   **If tests pass:** Re-evaluate scope for a lightweight integration that does not block the Mobile App.
*   **If tests fail:** Formally declare "Social Sharing" a **Non-Goal** for the current stage. Reallocate 100% of resources to the Mobile App and core curriculum improvements, which have demonstrated user retention.

**Next Steps**
1.  Approve the 2-day sprint for the Fake Door test.
2.  Schedule 5 user interviews for next Tuesday.
3.  Review results next Friday to make the final Go/No-Go decision.
