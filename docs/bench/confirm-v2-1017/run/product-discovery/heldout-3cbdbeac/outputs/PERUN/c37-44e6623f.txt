*   **Financial Signal:** $12,000 spent on agency outreach yielded 0 conversions.
*   **Verdict:** The market has rejected the "team collaboration" hypothesis in favor of a "solo productivity" reality.

If you build collaborative features next sprint despite this evidence, you face three critical risks:
1.  **Capital Destruction on Low-Probability Bets:** You are allocating engineering resources to a feature set that only 3 existing customers have demonstrated interest in, while ignoring the 85% who are actively using the tool. This is building for a hypothetical future rather than the verified present.
2.  **Product-Market Fit Erosion:** By prioritizing team features, you risk degrading the experience for your actual power users (freelancers) or diluting your roadmap focus. If the tool becomes "okay for teams but mediocre for solos," you may lose your only traction without gaining the team segment.
3.  **Cash Flow Crisis:** The $12k sunk cost in failed sales outreach indicates a distribution problem for the team use case. Doubling down on product for a segment you cannot sell to increases your burn rate without a clear path to revenue.

**Recommendation: Pivot Messaging and Delay Team Features**
Do not build collaborative whiteboarding features next sprint. The evidence does not support the investment.

*   **Immediate Action (Delay):** Pause all development on team-collaboration infrastructure. Treat the "team of five" persona as an unproven hypothesis that has currently failed validation.

*   **Validation Gate:** Before any team feature is built, you must pass a **Riskiest-Assumption Test**.
    *   *Test:* Secure 10 paying team contracts (not just sign-ups) via manual concierge sales (no new code).
    *   *Kill Threshold:* If you cannot close 10 teams in 4 weeks without building the feature, the market signal is definitive: do not build it.
