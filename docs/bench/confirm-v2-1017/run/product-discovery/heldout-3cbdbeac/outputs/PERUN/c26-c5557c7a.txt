**Decision: Do not commit to full localization yet. The riskiest assumption is untested.**

**1. Riskiest-Assumption Gate**
*   **Assumption:** Language barriers are the primary friction point preventing upgrades (desirability/viability).
*   **Current Evidence:** 40% sign-up spike in PL/VN/BR; 800 active users; 2% premium conversion.
*   **Missing Evidence:** Zero data confirming users *want* to upgrade but cannot due to language. They may be using free features successfully or abandoning for price/feature fit.
*   **Cheapest Test (Run before coding):** "Concierge" Localization + Behavioral Survey.
    *   **Action:** Manually translate the *upgrade* page and key friction points into Polish, Vietnamese, and Portuguese for 50 random users in each region (use browser extensions or a freelancer, not engineering). Simultaneously, send a 3-question survey to the 800 users asking: "What stopped you from upgrading?" with "Language barrier" as one option among others (Price, Missing Feature, Not needed).
    *   **Metric:** Conversion lift in the concierge group vs. control; % of survey respondents citing language.
    *   **Kill Threshold:** If conversion does not increase by at least 1.5x in the test group OR if fewer than 20% of non-upgraders cite language as the blocker, **drop the full localization project** for now.
    *   **Timeline:** 5 days to set up, 7 days to run.

**2. Discovery Interview Guide (Mom Test Style)**
Do not ask "Would you use this if it were in your language?" (Hypothetical). Ask about past behavior:
*   "Walk me through the last time you tried to upgrade or looked at the premium features. What specifically did you do?"
*   "When you hit the English text, what was the very next thing you did? Did you use a translator, ask a colleague, or close the tab?"
*   "What tools are you currently using to overcome the language gap, if any? How much time does that cost you?"
*   "Have you paid for other logistics software in your local language? Which ones?"

**3. Cheap Experiment Design**
*   **Fake Door Test:** Add a "Change Language" button in the settings for these regions. When clicked, show a modal: "Coming Soon: [Language]. Join the waitlist."
*   **Metric:** Click-through rate on the button.
*   **Threshold:** If <5% of active users in these regions click the button, language is likely not the top priority.

**4. Honest State Read**
*   **PMF Status:** `UNVERIFIED`. A 2% conversion rate is low, but without cohort retention data or survey results, we cannot attribute this to language.
*   **Prioritization (ICE Framework - Inputs Required):**
    *   *Impact:* Unknown (Need test results).
    *   *Confidence:* Low (Currently an assumption).
    *   *Ease:* Low (6-week engineering timeline).
    *   *Result:* Until the "Impact" and "Confidence" scores are populated with real data from the tests above, this initiative ranks lower than experiments that validate the problem.

**Recommendation to VP & Engineering Lead:**
Pause the 6-week localization sprint. Allocate 3 days of product/design time to run the Concierge Test and Survey defined above. If the kill threshold is met, proceed to localization. If not, investigate other retention levers (e.g., pricing, feature gaps) specific to these markets. Building a full localization engine on an unvalidated assumption is a high-risk waste of resources.

**Non-Goals for This Sprint:**
*   Full database schema changes for multi-language support.
*   Translating marketing materials or help docs.
*   Any engineering work on the core dashboard UI.
