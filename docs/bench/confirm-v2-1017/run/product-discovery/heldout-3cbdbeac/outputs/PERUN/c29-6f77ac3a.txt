# Risk Analysis: Legacy Compliance Module Sunset

**Verdict: UNVERIFIED. Do not proceed with the December 31st sunset date.**

Current evidence is insufficient to declare the sunset safe. The absence of complaints and feature requests is **negative evidence**, not validation of low dependency. In regulated banking environments, silence often indicates hard-coded reliance or "silent failure" risks rather than satisfaction. Your assumption that users are "captive" is a **riskiest assumption** that currently lacks behavioral proof; if false, a forced shutdown could trigger immediate contract breaches or regulatory incidents for your twelve key accounts.

### Critical Knowledge Gaps (Must Fill Before Decision)
You cannot decide based on internal metrics (cost/usage) alone. You must gather outside evidence on the following unknowns:

1.  **Regulatory Lock-in:** Which specific 2018-era regulations does this module satisfy that your new platform does not yet certify for?
2.  **Workflow Integration:** Is the module a primary workflow or a dormant "insurance policy" only accessed during annual audits?
3.  **Workaround Status:** Have banks built internal bridges that make your module invisible to you but critical to their data integrity?

### Required Immediate Actions (The Cheap Test)
Before setting a date, execute this **discovery gate**:

*   **Action:** Conduct "Switch Interviews" with technical stakeholders at all 12 banks.
*   **Question Pattern:** Do not ask "Can we shut this down?" (They will say yes to be polite). Ask: "Walk me through the last time you generated a compliance report using this module. What specific regulation was it for? What would happen to your audit trail if that specific file format disappeared tomorrow?"
*   **Kill Threshold:** If **any** of the 12 banks identify a hard regulatory dependency or a manual workaround that breaks upon shutdown, the "Safe Sunset" hypothesis is **false**. The project must pivot to a migration support plan before any date is announced.

### Recommended Decision Framework
Do not use a fixed calendar date yet. Use a **conditional milestone**:
*   **Condition A (Safe to Sunset):** 12/12 banks confirm zero active regulatory dependency AND provide written sign-off that their internal workarounds are complete.
*   **Condition B (Risk Detected):** 1+ banks cite active dependency. **Action:** Pause sunset. Initiate a "Concierge Test" where you manually support the specific edge cases for those banks while accelerating the new platform's compliance certification.

### Non-Goals for This Phase
*   **Do not** draft client communication emails yet. Sending a sunset notice before validating dependencies is a reputational risk.
*   **Do not** assume zero usage equals zero value. In compliance, low-frequency/high-stakes usage is common.
*   **Do not** reallocate the 30% maintenance budget until the migration path is proven safe.

### Next Steps for CRO & Account Management
1.  **Halt** the December 31st target publicly; internally reclassify as "Target Pending Validation."
2.  **Schedule** the 12 discovery calls within the next 10 days.
3.  **Prepare** a "Migration Support" fallback offer in case dependencies are found.

**Summary:** The financial case for sunset is strong, but the risk case is currently undefined. One catastrophic failure for a major bank outweighs the server cost savings. Validate the dependency map first.

*Note: This analysis structures your unknowns; it does not fabricate user responses. The "UNVERIFIED" status remains until real user evidence is supplied.*
