**Recommendation: Pause Development. Do not build yet.**

The request to build a $40,000, four-month chatbot fails the **Riskiest-Assumption Gate**. You are currently assuming the problem is "volume of simple queries" and that the solution is "automation," but your own hypothesis suggests the opposite: that these are complex, empathy-driven issues. Building now risks automating frustration, increasing churn, and wasting capital on a feature that may actively harm your core value proposition.

You have **zero behavioral evidence** that a bot can resolve these specific tier-one inquiries or that clients will pay for it. Stated interest (300 tickets asking "do you have this?") is not validation of value; it is often curiosity or a request for a feature they think they *should* have, not one they need.

**Immediate Action Plan: The Cheap Test**
Before writing code, you must run a **Concierge/Wizard-of-Oz test** to disconfirm your fear that these queries require human empathy.

1.  **The Experiment:** Select 50 recent "tier-one" tickets. Have your support team draft responses *as if* they were generated by a bot (strictly factual, no empathy, immediate).
2.  **The Metric:** Measure the **Re-open Rate** and **Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)** on these specific tickets compared to your baseline human-handled tickets.
3.  **The Kill Threshold:** If the re-open rate exceeds **15%** or CSAT drops below **4.0/5.0**, the assumption that "these can be automated" is false. **Drop the idea** or pivot to an internal agent-assist tool rather than a customer-facing bot.
4.  **Willingness-to-Pay Test:** Before building, present a mock-up of the "AI Premium Tier" to 5 of your largest BPO partners. Ask for a signed Letter of Intent (LOI) stating they will pay an extra **$X per seat** for this feature.
    *   **Kill Threshold:** If fewer than **2 out of 5** partners sign an LOI, the viability assumption is false. Do not build.

Your board cannot make a decision on the current data. You must return with the following filled slots, not estimates:

Until the concierge test proves these queries *can* be handled without empathy, and the sales test proves partners *will* pay for it, the project is **UNVERIFIED**. The cost of being wrong ($40k + 4 months + potential churn) vastly outweighs the cost of running these two-week tests.
