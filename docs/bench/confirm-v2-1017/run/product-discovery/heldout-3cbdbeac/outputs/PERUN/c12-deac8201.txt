# Content Strategy Evaluation: Nairobi GrowTech

**Status:** **UNVERIFIED / DO NOT BUILD YET**
**Reason:** The proposed solution (shorter videos) addresses a symptom (low completion) without testing the riskiest assumption: whether the content is relevant to the farmer's immediate needs. Building a new library of 30-second clips or switching to text/audio before validating the *value* of the advice is a high-cost experiment with a high probability of failure.

### 1. The Riskiest Assumption Gate
*   **Current Assumption:** "Farmers aren't watching because videos are too long."
*   **Riskiest Reality:** "Farmers aren't watching because the advice does not match their current planting stage or specific local conditions."
*   **The Cheap Test (Required Before Production):**
    *   **Action:** Do not produce new content yet. Select 20 farmers from the pilot who dropped off. Conduct 15-minute "Mom Test" interviews focusing on their *current* field activities.
    *   **Question to Ask:** "Walk me through the last time you tried to solve a pest problem or prepare soil this week. What did you do? Who did you ask?" (Do not mention your platform yet).
    *   **Metric:** Count how many mention a problem your January module actually solved.
    *   **Kill Threshold:** If fewer than **5 out of 20** farmers describe a problem that matches your content topics, **drop the video revision plan entirely**. The issue is relevance, not format. Pivot to discovering the real problems first.

### 3. Strategic Recommendation & Next Steps
**Do not allocate budget to production yet.** The 12% completion rate is a signal that the *offer* is mismatched, not necessarily the *package*.

1.  **Run the Relevance Test (1 Week):** Execute the 20 interviews defined above.
2.  **Decision Rule:**
    *   **If Relevance is Low (<25% match):** Halt all production. Redesign the content roadmap based on the problems farmers *actually* voiced. Format is irrelevant if the advice is wrong.
    *   **If Relevance is High (>25% match) but Format is the blocker:** Only then run a micro-test. Create **three** 30-second clips and **three** audio summaries of the *same* high-value topic. Send to 50 farmers. Measure completion and action taken.
3.  **Pilot Ordering:** The first "pilot" is the interview process itself. The second is the micro-test. Do not scale to a full series until the micro-test shows a completion rate >60%.

**Verdict for Investors:** We are pausing the $X production budget to validate problem-solution fit. This reduces the risk of building a comprehensive library that no one uses. We will report back in 10 days with the "Relevance Match" metric to authorize the next phase.
