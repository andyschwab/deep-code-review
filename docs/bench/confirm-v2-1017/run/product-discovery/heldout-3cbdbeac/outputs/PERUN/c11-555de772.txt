**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Product Steering Committee
**FROM:** Product Management
**DATE:** October 13
**SUBJECT:** Prioritization Decision: Native Slack Integration vs. Analytics Dashboard

**Decision Status: UNVERIFIED / DEFERRED**

We cannot currently approve the Slack Integration as a priority jump ahead of the Analytics Dashboard. While the pain point for four specific teams is real, we lack the evidence to confirm this represents a widespread retention risk or a 15% usage lift across our 500-company base. Proceeding now risks building a feature for an outlier workflow rather than a universal need.

**1. Evidence Gap & Riskiest Assumption**
*   **Observation:** Four tickets (Oct 10–12) from largest accounts indicate team leads spend ~30 mins/day manually copying status updates to Slack.
*   **Assumption to Test:** "This manual workflow is a universal blocker driving churn or limiting adoption across the broader base, not just a niche requirement for our largest clients."
*   **Current Data:** Insufficient. Four tickets represent <1% of our customer base. We do not know if the other 496 companies use Slack, use different channels, or have automated this already.
*   **Risk:** If we build this and only 5% of customers use it, we have diverted engineering resources from the Analytics Dashboard, which may solve a broader visibility issue for all users.

**2. Required Validation Steps (Before Build)**
Before committing engineering cycles, we must run the following cheap tests to validate the assumption:
*   **Targeted Survey (48 Hours):** Send a one-question survey to a stratified sample of 50 customers (mix of enterprise and SMB).
    *   *Question:* "How do you currently share daily status updates with your team? If manual, how many minutes per day does this take?"
    *   *Kill Threshold:* If fewer than 20% of respondents report a manual process taking >15 mins/day, we drop this initiative for now.
*   **Concierge Test:** Manually integrate one of the requesting teams using a simple webhook or Zapier script (no native code).
    *   *Metric:* Does their daily active usage actually increase? Does the 30-minute saving materialize?
    *   *Goal:* Verify the claimed 15% usage lift before automating.

**4. Engineering Complexity Estimate (Preliminary)**
*Note: Final estimate requires Tech Lead review.*
*   **Scope:** OAuth handling, real-time message formatting, error logging, permission mapping.
*   **Complexity Driver:** Maintaining sync reliability and handling Slack API rate limits vs. internal data consistency.
*   **Opportunity Cost:** Every sprint spent here delays the Analytics Dashboard, which may be required for *all* customers to prove value.

**Recommendation**
Do not start development. Assign one designer/PM to run the survey and concierge test immediately. Re-evaluate in one week with hard data. If the survey confirms >20% prevalence and the concierge test shows usage lift, we revisit prioritization. Otherwise, proceed with the Analytics Dashboard.

***

**Non-Goal for this Sprint:** Building a polished, native, bi-directional Slack integration. We are only validating the *need* and the *behavioral change*, not the implementation.
