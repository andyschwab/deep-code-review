### Stress Test: Weakest Point
Your current draft's fatal flaw is **prioritizing efficiency (speed) over the primary barrier to entry: trust.** In cross-border real estate, a fast "no" is worthless; investors need confidence before they commit capital. By leading with "ten days vs. six weeks," you appeal to logic but ignore the emotional anxiety of buying unseen property. Speed is a nice-to-have benefit; trust is the must-have prerequisite. If you do not solve the "trust deficit" first, the speed of the transaction is irrelevant because the customer will never reach the decision stage.

### Revised Positioning Statement

**Value Proposition**
**For** overseas real estate investors in the UK and Russia
**that need** to validate high-value UAE property purchases without the risk of flying blind or relying on biased local agents
**SkyView VR**
**is a** remote due diligence platform
**that** replaces uncertain static photos and informal WhatsApp videos with immersive, self-guided virtual tours that replicate the physical viewing experience, granting buyers the confidence to commit in ten days rather than six weeks.

**Differentiation Statement**
**Unlike** hiring a local agent to send curated video walkthroughs or relying on static listings on PropertyFinder
**SkyView VR**
**provides** an unbiased, autonomous inspection experience that eliminates the "human filter," allowing investors to verify every detail personally and bridge the trust gap inherent in cross-border transactions.

***

*Note: This revision shifts the primary benefit from "speed" to "confidence via autonomy," positioning speed as the outcome of trust rather than the core feature.*
*The "Unlike" section now explicitly names the biased human element of agents as the competitor, not just the medium of communication.*
