## Value Proposition

**For** traditional family-owned soy farms in the Pampas region managing over 500 hectares
- **that need** harvest forecasts accurate enough to secure financing and plan logistics, but distrust "black box" satellite data that misses local micro-climates
- AgriVision Argentina
- **is a** satellite-native yield intelligence platform
- **that** delivers harvest predictions within 5% of actual weight by analyzing hyper-local canopy health across your entire farm daily, eliminating the blind spots of sporadic human field walks.

## Differentiation Statement

- **Unlike** hiring local agronomists for manual scouting or relying on global weather platforms like ClimateFieldView
- AgriVision Argentina
- **provides** continuous, whole-farm visibility that captures micro-climate nuances through proprietary Pampas-specific imagery models, offering lab-grade accuracy at a fraction of the cost of manual labor while proving reliability through two seasons of verified data on 50,000 hectares.

***

**Strategic Recommendation:** Position as a **supplementary dashboard** that *replaces the frequency* of human scouts, not the agronomist entirely. Your low conversion (20%) stems from skepticism about "space-only" data; framing this as a tool that empowers the farm manager with daily data (which humans can't provide) rather than firing their trusted advisors reduces friction. Emphasize **scale of coverage** over cost savings; traditional families value risk mitigation and total farm visibility more than cutting staff costs, and your 5% accuracy proof point validates the technology better than a price argument ever could.
