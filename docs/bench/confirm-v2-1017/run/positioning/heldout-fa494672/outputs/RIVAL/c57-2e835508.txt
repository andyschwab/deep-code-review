### Positioning Statement

**For** owners and operators of mid-sized textile factories in Vietnam
**that need** to stop energy waste and prevent machine failures before they impact production schedules
**EcoFactory**
**is a** diagnostic energy intelligence platform
**that** identifies specific sources of waste in real-time to cut energy costs by over 20% and reduce downtime by 15%
**Unlike** standard meters that only track total consumption or slow manual audits from firms like EnergyCheck
**EcoFactory** provides immediate, machine-level diagnostics that pinpoint inefficiencies the moment they occur, turning raw data into actionable prevention.

***

### Stress Test: Tracking Usage vs. Identifying Waste

To test your pitch against a skeptical factory owner, stop talking about "data visibility" and start talking about "money leaking right now."

**The Skeptic's Objection:** "I already have meters. I get a bill every month. I know how much I spend. Why pay you more?"

**The Weak Pitch (Tracking Focus):** "Our device gives you real-time dashboards so you can see exactly when and where you use electricity. It's more detailed than your monthly bill."
*Why this fails:* The owner hears "more charts." They don't see the link between a chart and profit. They assume their current manual process (waiting for the bill, then calling an auditor) is "good enough" because it's free or low-cost until the next cycle.

**The Strong Pitch (Waste Identification Focus):** "Your current meter tells you *how much* you lost last month; EcoFactory tells you *which machine* is wasting money right now so you can fix it before it breaks. While you wait for EnergyCheck's monthly report, our system already found the inefficient motor causing a 15% downtime risk and saved you 22% on this week's bill. We don't just track usage; we hunt down the specific profit leaks your current meters miss."

**The Litmus Test:** If the factory owner asks, "Which machine is broken?" immediately after your pitch, you have successfully shifted the conversation from "tracking" to "identifying waste." If they ask, "Can I export the data to Excel?", you are still stuck in the tracking trap.

*Note: This positioning deliberately targets the "owner/operator" hybrid role common in Vietnam's mid-market to unify the financial (CFO) and operational (Plant Manager) incentives. Avoid splitting your message; the cost savings fund the reliability, and the reliability ensures the savings.*
