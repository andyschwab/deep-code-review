## Value Proposition

**For** mission-driven e-commerce lifestyle brands in Sweden
- **that need** durable, weather-resistant shipping solutions that align with their sustainability values without compromising product safety
- GreenPack Sweden
- **is a** home-compostable mailer manufacturer
- **that** eliminates plastic waste and future carbon tax liabilities while ensuring packages survive wet Nordic weather

## Differentiation Statement

- **Unlike** low-cost Chinese virgin plastic importers and fragile local paper bag suppliers
- GreenPack Sweden
- **provides** the only mailer that combines full home-compostability and carbon neutrality with the durability required for reliable last-mile delivery

***

*Note: This positioning targets brands where reputation risk from damaged goods or greenwashing accusations outweighs the 20% unit cost premium. It frames the price difference as an investment in brand integrity and regulatory future-proofing rather than just a packaging expense.*
