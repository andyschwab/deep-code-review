**Value Proposition**

**For** executive directors at non-profits
**that need** to protect sensitive donor relationships and prove program impact without risking data loss or staff burnout
**DataForGood**
**is a** secure donor management platform
**that** eliminates the fragility of spreadsheets to save ten hours weekly on reporting while guaranteeing zero data entry errors.

**Differentiation Statement**

**Unlike** familiar but fragile Google Sheets or Excel files
**DataForGood**
**provides** an automated, error-proof environment that secures historical data against accidental deletion and frees staff to focus on fundraising rather than manual data cleanup.

***

**Strategic Recommendation:** Position primarily as a **Security Upgrade that Enables Efficiency**. Your audience's primary barrier is fear (losing historical data), not just desire for speed. Framing the product as an "efficiency engine" alone invites comparison on price against "free" spreadsheets. However, framing it as a "security upgrade" changes the conversation from cost to risk mitigation. The efficiency gain (10 hours/week) becomes the powerful secondary benefit that justifies the $49/month investment once the fear of data loss is resolved. You are not selling a faster spreadsheet; you are selling insurance for their most valuable asset (donor data) that happens to make their jobs easier.

**Risk Comparison for Stakeholders:**
*   **Spreadsheets (Current Alternative):** High risk of accidental formula breaks, version control conflicts, and irreversible data deletion; no audit trail for sensitive donor info; requires manual validation leading to 90% higher error rates.
*   **DataForGood (Proposed Solution):** Automated validation prevents entry errors; structured database architecture prevents accidental data loss; centralized history ensures data integrity for compliance and reporting.
