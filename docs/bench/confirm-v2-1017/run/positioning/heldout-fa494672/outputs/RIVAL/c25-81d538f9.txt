### Strategic Recommendation: Prioritize the Luxury B2B Market

Given your current resource constraints and the stark contrast in unit economics, you should **double down on the luxury B2B market**.

Your data reveals a classic "chasm" problem. You are currently straddling two distinct markets with conflicting needs: high-value enterprise clients who value speed and bulk licensing, and price-sensitive individual renters who cannot afford your current pricing. Attempting to serve both simultaneously with limited resources will dilute your product roadmap and burn cash.

The B2B segment offers a clear path to sustainability. With an average contract value (ACV) of $12,000, you only need ~83 clients to reach $1M in ARR. Your current traction with major developers like Emaar validates that your core differentiator—generating high-quality 3D renderings in under two minutes versus the competitors' hour+—is a critical workflow accelerator for professionals. In contrast, pivoting to a freemium B2C model requires massive scale (hundreds of thousands of users) to match the revenue of a few dozen enterprise deals, demanding significant marketing spend and infrastructure changes you may not yet afford.

Positioning as an "enterprise efficiency tool" leverages your existing strengths and paying customer base. Positioning as a "democratic design partner" forces you to compete on price against free tiers, a battle you are currently losing.

***

### LuxSpace Positioning Statement

**Value Proposition**
**For** high-end property developers and luxury real estate agencies
**that need** to accelerate sales cycles by visualizing furnished interiors instantly without compromising on photorealistic quality
**LuxSpace**
**is a** generative AI interior design platform
**that** delivers bulk, showroom-quality 3D room renderings in under two minutes, enabling teams to present multiple design options during live client meetings.

**Differentiation Statement**
**Unlike** consumer-focused design tools like Homestyler and Planner 5D
**LuxSpace**
**provides** an enterprise-grade bulk licensing model and a specialized rendering engine that reduces production time from over an hour to under two minutes, specifically optimized for high-volume commercial workflows rather than single-user hobbyist projects.

***

*Note: This positioning explicitly excludes the budget renter segment to focus your engineering and sales efforts on the high-ACV B2B channel where you already have product-market fit.*
*To execute this, consider pausing B2C feature development and creating a dedicated "Enterprise API" or bulk-upload feature to further lock in developer contracts.*
