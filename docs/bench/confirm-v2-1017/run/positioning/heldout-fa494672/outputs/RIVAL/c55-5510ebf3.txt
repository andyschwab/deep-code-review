## Value Proposition

**For** state-level political campaign managers in the US
- **that need** to automate social media scheduling without risking legal violations or expensive compliance errors
- VoteSync
- **is a** regulated political marketing platform
- **that** reduces legal review time by 30% while guaranteeing every post meets complex election laws

## Differentiation Statement

- **Unlike** generic schedulers like Buffer or manual spreadsheet workflows
- VoteSync
- **provides** built-in legal guardrails that automatically enforce election regulations, eliminating the need for costly pre-post legal reviews

***

**Strategic Recommendation:** Position strictly as the "compliance safety net." Your data (30% reduction in legal review) and current traction (42 state candidates) validate this niche. The "efficiency engine" angle is a commodity battle you cannot win against well-funded generic tools. Regarding your fear of scaring small campaigns: In politics, fear of legal liability outweighs fear of complexity. Small campaigns are often *more* vulnerable to fines because they lack in-house counsel; framing compliance as "insurance" rather than "bureaucracy" makes the product essential, not heavy. Ignore federal campaigns for now; their zero adoption suggests a different regulatory hurdle or sales cycle that distracts from your proven state-level fit.
