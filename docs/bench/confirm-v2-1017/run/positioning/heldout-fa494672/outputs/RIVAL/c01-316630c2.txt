**Recommended Choice: Lead with Speed (Time-to-Hire)**

For cash-strapped, early-stage startups in a hyper-competitive market like the Bay Area, the immediate existential threat is not long-term turnover; it is the inability to ship product *now*. A 40-day hiring cycle can kill a startup's momentum or cause them to miss a critical market window. While quality is vital, speed is the urgent "bleeding neck" problem that forces a purchasing decision today. Greenhouse and Lever solve for process and compliance (quality/risk), which matters more to scaled companies. Your wedge is velocity. Once you get them in the door with speed, you can prove quality through retention data later. Positioning around "12 days vs. 40 days" creates a stark, quantifiable contrast that resonates with a founder's panic mode far more effectively than a promise of future retention.
