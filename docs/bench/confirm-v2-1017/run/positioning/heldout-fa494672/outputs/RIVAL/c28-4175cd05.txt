### Positioning Statement for SafeStep

**Value Proposition**
**For** anxious adult children of elderly parents living alone in Tokyo
**that need** immediate reassurance that their parents are safe after a fall, without the burden of constant checking or expensive medical equipment
**SafeStep**
**is a** family safety monitoring service
**that** delivers instant fall alerts directly to family via LINE, enabling rapid response and providing daily peace of mind.

**Differentiation Statement**
**Unlike** medical-grade monitors (e.g., Omron) that prioritize clinical data precision for doctors but lack immediate family connectivity
**SafeStep**
**provides** a dedicated family-first emergency response loop that replaces outdated emergency call buttons, sacrificing clinical granularity for speed, simplicity, and the specific communication channels families actually use.

***

**Strategic Note:** Your 40% churn stems from targeting the wrong buyer; health-conscious seniors and doctors expect clinical utility you cannot provide without complex sensors. Pivot entirely to "anxious adult children" selling peace of mind, not medical data, and stop trying to serve the medical community.
