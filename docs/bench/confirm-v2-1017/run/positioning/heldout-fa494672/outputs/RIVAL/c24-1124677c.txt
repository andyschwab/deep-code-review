## Value Proposition

**For** marina owners in the Netherlands managing crowded infrastructure
- **that need** to increase berth occupancy and generate new revenue streams without upfront capital expenditure
- ShoreCharge
- **is a** fast-charging network infrastructure partner
- **that** drives a projected 20% increase in marina occupancy by eliminating range anxiety for the growing fleet of 300 electric boats

## Differentiation Statement

- **Unlike** DockPower's slower, low-cost chargers that fail to meet the demands of modern electric fleets, and CaptainKit's expensive €1,200 portable batteries that place the burden of infrastructure on individual captains
- ShoreCharge
- **provides** a zero-capex, fast-charging network that transforms charging availability into a competitive advantage for tenant acquisition, directly boosting the marina's bottom line through shared revenue rather than shifting costs to the customer.

***

**Strategic Choice:** You must position for the **B2B marina owner** using the occupancy statistic. Your business model relies on their signature and capital (via installation responsibility); the captain is the end-user, but the owner is the buyer. Solving the owner's skepticism about ROI is the only path to deploying the network that subsequently solves the captain's range anxiety.
