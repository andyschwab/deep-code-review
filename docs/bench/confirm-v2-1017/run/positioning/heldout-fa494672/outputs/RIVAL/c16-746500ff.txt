### Positioning Statement

**For** street food vendors in Mexico City
**that need** to secure daily earnings and prevent lost sales during frequent cellular outages in crowded markets
**VentaRápido**
**is a** mobile point-of-sale system
**that** guarantees transaction continuity offline while eliminating the theft risks of cash handling

**Unlike** carrying untracked cash or using connected-only apps like Square that fail without internet
**VentaRápido**
**provides** an offline-first reliability engine that ensures every sale is captured and secured, regardless of network status.

### Message Hierarchy Recommendation

Based on your data, lead with **Reliability (Preventing Lost Sales)** as the primary hook, supported by Security as the trust builder.

**1. Primary Hook: "Never Miss a Sale" (Efficiency/Reliability)**
*   **Rationale:** Your 15% churn rate when vendors *don't* experience connectivity issues suggests the value proposition isn't felt until the pain point (network drop) occurs. However, in crowded markets, the fear of missing peak-hour sales due to Square failing is a more immediate, active anxiety than the passive fear of theft. Vendors understand "lost opportunity" instantly.
*   **Radio Ad Angle:** "When the market crowds and the signal dies, Square stops working. You stop earning. With VentaRápido, the network can drop, but your sales never do. Keep selling when others can't."

**2. Secondary Support: "Safe Earnings" (Security)**
*   **Rationale:** Security is the baseline requirement to switch from cash, but it is not the differentiator against Square. Use this to close the deal against cash holders.
*   **Flyer Angle:** "Stop carrying dangerous amounts of cash. Track every peso digitally, even without internet. Safer than cash, more reliable than Square."

**3. Proof Point: The Offline Guarantee**
*   **Tactic:** Since churn is high if users don't hit a connectivity wall early, your onboarding must simulate or highlight this immediately. Consider a "Network Stress Test" mode in the app demo that shows a sale going through while Wi-Fi/Data is toggled off, proving the value before they rely on it in the wild.

**Strategic Decision:** Market as an **Efficiency Booster** first. Security gets them to consider digital; Reliability gets them to choose *you* over Square. If you lead with security, you compete with safes and banks. If you lead with reliability, you compete with lost income, which is a stronger motivator for daily wage earners.

*Note: Your churn data indicates a "time-to-value" problem; ensure new users experience an offline simulation within day one to anchor the reliability benefit immediately.*
*Focus radio scripts on the specific scenario of "peak hour network failure" to trigger immediate recognition among your target audience.*
