**For** managing partners at small Toronto law firms

**ClauseWise**
**is a** specialized AI lease review assistant
**that** delivers 95% of the accuracy of a junior associate at one-tenth the cost, flagging niche Ontario-specific risks in minutes rather than days.

**Differentiation Statement**
**Unlike** relying on expensive junior associates or generic tools like Microsoft Word with manual checklists

**Refined Message Hierarchy & Stress Test**

Your current draft claiming to "replace junior associates entirely" is too aggressive and likely triggers the exact liability fears you identified. Small firm partners do not buy "replacement"; they buy "leverage" and "risk mitigation." If you claim total replacement, you assume 100% of the liability if the AI misses a clause. Instead, position ClauseWise as the ultimate force multiplier that handles the grunt work, leaving the high-judgment final approval to the partner.

**Level 1: The Hook (Speed & Cost)**
"Cut your commercial lease review costs by 90% and turn around times from days to minutes."
*Why it works:* It immediately addresses the economic pain (junior associate billing rates) without threatening the partner's role.

**Level 2: The Trust Builder (Specificity & Safety)**
"Trained specifically on Ontario commercial tenancy acts, ClauseWise catches niche provincial clauses that generic checklists miss, acting as a tireless first pass for your team."
*Why it works:* It differentiates from Microsoft Word (generic) and acknowledges the "human eye" is still needed for the final pass, reducing the fear of algorithmic error.

**Level 3: The Close (The New Workflow)**
"Stop burning billable hours on routine reviews. Use ClauseWise to handle the heavy lifting, so your junior associates can focus on high-value strategy while partners retain confident final control."
*Why it works:* It reframes the narrative from "firing humans" to "elevating humans." It solves the partner's dilemma: they get the speed/cost benefits of AI but keep the liability shield of human review.

**Strategic Note:** Do not sell "replacement." Sell "augmentation." Your 40 beta firms are likely using you as a filter already; lean into that. Your strongest case study isn't "we fired a junior," it's "we reviewed 50 leases in the time it used to take to review five, with zero missed clauses."
