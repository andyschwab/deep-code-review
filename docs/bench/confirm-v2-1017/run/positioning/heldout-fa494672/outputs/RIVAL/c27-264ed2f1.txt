### Revised Positioning Statement

**For** real estate brokerages in São Paulo with 50+ agents
**that need** to close property deals rapidly without risking legal invalidity due to non-compliant digital signatures
**SignaBrasil**
**is a** specialized real estate transaction platform
**that** guarantees court-admissible contracts with integrated CPF verification, reducing deal closure time from 3 days to 2 hours
**Unlike** global tools like DocuSign or generic PDF editors like Adobe Acrobat
**SignaBrasil**
**provides** ironclad compliance with specific Brazilian notary requirements, eliminating the fear of challenged deeds while accelerating sales cycles.

***

**Analysis & Recommendations:**

Your draft claim of being the "only legally bulletproof option" is too aggressive and likely triggers defensiveness rather than trust; instead, position as the "compliance-guaranteed partner" which frames you as an ally against legal risk rather than making an absolute legal promise you cannot personally uphold. Target large brokerages (50+ agents) rather than freelancers; they have higher volume to justify the switch, face greater liability risks if deeds are challenged, and their adoption creates a network effect that forces freelancers to eventually comply with the brokerage's chosen standard.
