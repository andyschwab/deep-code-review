### Recommendation: Target Aggressive Fintech Lenders

Do not target risk-averse loan officers first. Their primary fear is job displacement, and no amount of data will overcome the psychological barrier of an algorithm threatening their livelihood. Trying to sell "safety" to people who view your product as an existential threat is a losing battle.

Instead, target **aggressive fintech lenders** (or SACCOs with growth mandates). Their primary pain point is not job security; it is **scalability**. They cannot scale fast enough using manual assessments, and traditional credit scores (CreditInfo) exclude the very micro-borrowers they want to serve. For this segment, your AI is not a replacement for human judgment but a **force multiplier** that allows them to lend safely to the "unscorable" majority. They care about speed and volume, and your 40% reduction in defaults is the key that unlocks their growth without increasing risk.

### Positioning Statement

**For** aggressive fintech lenders and growth-focused SACCOs in Kenya
**that need** to scale micro-loan portfolios beyond the limits of manual assessment and traditional credit bureaus
**KijaniCheck**
**is a** mobile-money-based fraud detection engine
**that** enables safe lending to the unscorable majority by reducing default rates by 40% compared to human-only reviews
**Unlike** relying on slow manual loan officer assessments or the limited coverage of the CreditInfo Kenya database
**KijaniCheck** provides instant, transparent risk scoring based on actual cash-flow behavior, allowing lenders to expand their book 10x faster without increasing exposure to fraud.
