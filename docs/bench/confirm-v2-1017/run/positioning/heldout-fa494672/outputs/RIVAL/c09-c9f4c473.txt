Your draft positioning, "The fastest way to file taxes without the human error," fails to address your core challenge. By leading with speed and the absence of humans, you inadvertently validate the very fear causing prospects to choose Cabinet Dupont or the Freelancer Guild: that ComptaFast is a cold, impersonal algorithm that cannot offer the reassurance of a trusted advisor. In a market where "trust" is the primary currency, framing your service as "without human error" sounds like "without human understanding."

To compete with established human networks, you must reframe your value proposition. Do not position yourself as a replacement for human advice, but as a tool that *guarantees* the accuracy humans strive for, freeing them to focus on strategy rather than data entry. Your differentiation is not that you are faster; it is that you provide the reliability of a large firm with the accessibility freelancers need, removing the anxiety of mistakes that even good clerks can make under pressure.

Here is a revised Geoffrey Moore-style positioning statement to stress-test against your current draft:

**Value Proposition**
*   **For** freelance graphic designers and consultants in Lyon and Marseille
*   **that need** to ensure monthly filings are flawless without sacrificing the confidence of local expertise
*   **ComptaFast**
*   **is a** automated bookkeeping service
*   **that** delivers 90% fewer errors and cuts preparation time from six hours to 45 minutes, providing the reliability of a top-tier firm with immediate digital access.

**Differentiation Statement**
*   **Unlike** independent clerks from the Yellow Pages or traditional firms like Cabinet Dupont
*   **ComptaFast**
*   **provides** algorithmic precision that eliminates manual data-entry risks, allowing freelancers to trust their numbers instantly rather than waiting for human review cycles.

**Strategic Feedback on the Reframe:**
1.  **Shift from "No Humans" to "Better Outcomes":** The new phrasing acknowledges the desire for confidence ("confidence of local expertise") but argues that true confidence comes from *results* (90% fewer errors), not just the presence of a person.
2.  **Address the Alternative Directly:** Instead of ignoring Cabinet Dupont, the differentiation explicitly contrasts "manual data-entry risks" (the weakness of humans) against "algorithmic precision" (your strength). This turns the "impersonal" critique into a "super-human accuracy" benefit.
3.  **Redefine Trust:** Trust is no longer about having a coffee with an accountant; it is about knowing your filing is correct. The new position argues that ComptaFast is the *most* trustworthy option because it removes the variable of human fatigue.

**Recommendation:** Do not lead with "fastest." Lead with "flawless." Speed is a bonus; accuracy is the requirement for trust.

*Note: Test this messaging by asking current users if they feel more "confident" or just "saved time."*
*If they say "confident," you have solved the trust gap.*
