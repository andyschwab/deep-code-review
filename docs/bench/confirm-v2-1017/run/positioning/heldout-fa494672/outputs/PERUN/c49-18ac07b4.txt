**Decision:** Position GradPath as a **career-launching essential for first-time job seekers**.

**Why:** Your 3,200 users are engineering students uploading *drafts*, indicating they are starting from scratch rather than refining existing professional documents. The "technical upgrade" segment assumes users already have strong content that just needs formatting; your users likely lack both structure and ATS optimization. Furthermore, a 4% conversion rate suggests the value proposition isn't urgent enough for those who already have internships. First-time seekers face the highest anxiety and the greatest risk of automatic rejection, making them more willing to pay for a tool that guarantees their application is actually read. They are not comparing you to a "better tool"; they are comparing you to the fear of being ignored.

For **final-year engineering students in India** who **need to secure their first full-time role without being filtered out by software**, GradPath is a **career-launching resume builder** that **transforms rough drafts into ATS-verified applications**; unlike **generic design tools like Canva or broad job portals like Naukri.com**, it **delivers a 40% higher pass-rate by combining role-specific content guidance with automated technical optimization**.

**Headline:** Stop getting rejected by bots. Start your career with a resume built to pass.

*   **Vs. Naukri.com:** Naukri provides a generic text box meant to serve millions of profiles. GradPath provides a guided, step-by-step build specifically for engineering graduates, trading "broad reach" for "targeted success."

To test this, run an A/B test on your landing page: one headline focusing on "Beautiful Designs" (current status) vs. one focusing on "Beat the ATS Filter."
