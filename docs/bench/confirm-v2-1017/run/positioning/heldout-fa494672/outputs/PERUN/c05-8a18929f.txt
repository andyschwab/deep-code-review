### Message Hierarchy: PawsPremium

**Core Message (The Hook)**
Focus on **immediate, visible vitality** (coat shine and energy) rather than long-term longevity. Longevity is an abstract promise that feels distant; a shinier coat and higher energy within two weeks provide the tangible proof new customers need to justify the price jump today.

**Headline**
"See the difference in two weeks: Human-grade nutrition that powers their play, not just fills their bowl."

**Supporting Pillar 1: The Ingredient Reality**
*   **Claim:** We use 100% human-grade meat; mass-market kibble relies on fillers and by-products.
*   **Proof Point:** [Insert specific list of top 3 meat sources vs. generic filler ingredients found in competitor labels].
*   **Differentiation:** You aren't paying for bulk; you are paying for biology their bodies actually recognize.

**Supporting Pillar 2: The Value of Health (Addressing the Price Objection)**
*   **Claim:** The 2,400 yen monthly difference is an investment in preventing digestive distress, not just buying food.
*   **Proof Point:** [Insert aggregated data from your 2,500 subscribers regarding reduced digestive issues]. *Note: Frame the "40% reduction in vet visits" as a observed trend among current members rather than a guaranteed medical outcome to avoid over-promising causality.*
*   **Differentiation:** Cheap food costs more when you add up the treats, supplements, and worry about stomach upsets.

**Supporting Pillar 3: Respectful Positioning (Avoiding Snobbery)**
*   **Claim:** We respect that every owner loves their dog; we simply offer a different tool for those ready to prioritize ingredient transparency.
*   **Proof Point:** Our subscription model eliminates waste and ensures freshness that static supermarket shelves cannot match.
*   **Differentiation:** This isn't about judging budget choices; it's about offering an upgrade for owners who want to see immediate changes in their dog's zest for life.

**Call to Action**
"Try a half-month trial. If you don't see a brighter coat and more energy in 14 days, we'll refund the difference."

***

**Strategic Note:** The "immediate improvement" hook works best because it lowers the risk barrier for skeptical customers comparing you to convenience store options.
**Verification Required:** You must validate the "40% reduction" claim with a formal case study or third-party verification before using it as a definitive statistic in paid advertising.
