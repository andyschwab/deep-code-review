**Decision:** Position VoteSync as the **compliance safety net for risk-averse campaign managers**.

**Why:** In political campaigns, the cost of a compliance error (fines, disqualification, scandal) vastly outweighs the value of minor efficiency gains. Your 30% reduction in legal review time is a powerful *proof point* for the safety argument, not a standalone efficiency play. The zero adoption among federal campaigns suggests your current fit is specifically with state-level races where resources are tight but regulatory stakes remain high. Small, informal campaigns often underestimate risk until it hits; selling "safety" to them is harder than selling it to managers already terrified of making a mistake.

**Positioning Statement (Hypothesis to Validate):**
For **state-level campaign managers** who **fear costly regulatory violations**, VoteSync is a **compliant social media scheduler** that **guarantees every post meets election laws**; unlike **manual spreadsheets or generic tools like Buffer**, it **automates legal guardrails to cut review time by 30% while eliminating the risk of human error**.

**Headline:** Stop Guessing. Start Complying. The Only Scheduler Built for Election Law.

**Addressing Your Doubt:**
You worry that emphasizing "strict regulation" scares small campaigns. The rebuttal is that small campaigns are actually *more* vulnerable to errors because they lack dedicated legal counsel. They do not need "less formal" tools; they need affordable protection. If the messaging feels too heavy, shift the tone from "bureaucratic compliance" to "peace of mind," but do not remove the core value of safety. A small team punching above its weight cannot afford a lawsuit that ends their race.

**Alternative Analysis:**
*   **Vs. Manual Spreadsheets:** Buyers give up the false security of "we checked it ourselves." Your winning line: "Spreadsheets don't update when laws change; VoteSync does."
*   **Vs. Generic Tools (Buffer):** Buyers give up ease-of-use for safety. Your winning line: "Buffer schedules posts; VoteSync protects your campaign."

**Riskiest Assumption:** That state-level managers perceive the risk of non-compliance as high enough to pay for a specialized tool over a free spreadsheet.
**Test:** Run a landing page test targeting state campaign managers with two headlines: one focusing on "Save 10 hours a week" (Efficiency) and one on "Zero Risk of Election Violations" (Safety). Measure click-through and sign-up intent.
