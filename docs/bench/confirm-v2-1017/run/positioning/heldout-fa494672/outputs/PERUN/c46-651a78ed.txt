**Decision:** Position AgriVision as a **supplementary dashboard for existing farm managers**, not a replacement for human scouts.

**Reasoning:** Your primary barrier is trust in "micro-climate nuances," not the cost of labor. Attempting to replace the agronomist forces you to defend the one area where skepticism is highest (satellite nuance) against the one asset farmers trust most (human intuition). By positioning as a supplement, you reframe the satellite data as a "wide-angle lens" that tells the agronomist exactly where to walk, turning your perceived weakness (lack of ground sensors) into a strength (scalable targeting that reduces wasted field walks).

**Positioning Statement (Hypothesis to Validate):**
"For **family-owned soy farm managers in the Pampas** who **struggle to verify micro-climate risks across vast hectares**, AgriVision is a **satellite-based yield intelligence dashboard** that **pinpoints high-risk zones with 95% accuracy**; unlike **relying solely on periodic human field walks or generic global weather platforms**, it **provides daily, whole-farm coverage that directs agronomists to specific problem areas, reducing scouting time while capturing yield threats invisible to the naked eye**."

**Key Differentiator & Proof:**
*   **Differentiator:** Targeted efficiency vs. blind coverage.
*   **Proof Point:** [USER TO SUPPLY: Specific case study where AgriVision identified a yield threat on a specific date that a field walk missed until later, or data showing reduction in scouting hours per hectare].
*   **Addressing Skepticism:** The message admits satellites cannot replace the "boots on the ground" for nuance but argues they are superior for *finding* where that nuance matters.

**Recommended Headline for Trials:**
"Don't Replace Your Agronomist. Give Them X-Ray Vision."

**Why this over cost savings:**
Traditional family farms view agronomists as partners, not just costs. A "cheaper replacement" sounds risky and low-quality. A "force multiplier" for their trusted advisor sounds like an upgrade. Emphasizing scale (50,000+ hectares supported) proves the system handles the Pampas' size, which individual scouts cannot do daily.

**Riskiest Assumption to Test:**
That farm managers believe a satellite alert is actionable enough to change their scouting route.
*   **Test:** Run a trial where you provide the "hotspot map" to 10 current trial users and ask their agronomist to visit only those spots. Measure if the agronomist confirms the issue and if the time saved is valued higher than the subscription cost.

**Note:** The 5% accuracy stat is strong but must be framed as "predictive reliability" rather than "perfect replacement." Do not claim legal clearance on the name "AgriVision" without a trademark search.
