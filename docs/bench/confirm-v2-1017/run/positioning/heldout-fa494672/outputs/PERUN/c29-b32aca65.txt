### Message Hierarchy: Permanence Over Promise

**Core Message**
Reforestation is a promise that trees will grow and stay; Direct Air Capture is a guarantee that carbon is removed today and never returns. For airlines facing 2050 net-zero mandates, only permanent removal eliminates the risk of future reversal.

**Supporting Pillar 1: The Reversal Risk**
*   **Claim:** Tree-based offsets carry a high risk of reversal due to fire, disease, or logging, potentially returning captured carbon to the atmosphere within decades.
*   **Proof Point:** SkyOffset facilitates only verified permanent removal technologies (e.g., Direct Air Capture), ensuring every ton offset is permanently sequestered, protecting airlines from future liability and reputational damage associated with reversed credits.

**Supporting Pillar 2: Regulatory Future-Proofing**
*   **Claim:** Upcoming EU regulations are shifting from "avoidance/reduction" toward "permanent removal" as the gold standard for credible net-zero claims.
*   **Proof Point:** By securing permanent removal credits now, airlines like KLM and easyJet future-proof their compliance strategies against tightening definitions of "carbon neutral," avoiding costly retrofits or credit replacements later.

**Supporting Pillar 3: Premium Brand Alignment**
*   **Claim:** Luxury and legacy carriers cannot afford the reputational risk of "greenwashing" accusations linked to low-cost, non-permanent offsets.
*   **Proof Point:** SkyOffset's model allows airlines to market a verifiable, science-backed climate action story that resonates with high-value corporate travelers who demand transparency, justifying the premium through brand integrity rather than just volume.
