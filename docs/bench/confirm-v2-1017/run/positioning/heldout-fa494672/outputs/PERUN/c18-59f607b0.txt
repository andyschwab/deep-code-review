**Recommendation:** Position TurnoverShield to **people-focused HR VPs** as a retention tool that saves top talent, not primarily as a cost-saving line item for CFOs. While the ROI is financial, the buyer with the urgency and authority to act on predictive data is the HR leader accountable for turnover. CFOs validate the budget, but HR VPs feel the pain of losing high performers and lack the tools to stop it.

**Positioning Statement (Hypothesis to Validate):**
For **HR VPs at US retail chains** who **need to reduce voluntary turnover among high performers before it happens**, TurnoverShield is a **predictive retention platform** that **identifies flight risks up to three weeks in advance with 82% accuracy**; unlike **reactive monitoring or historical engagement surveys (e.g., SurveyMonkey)**, it **enables managers to intervene and save specific employees before they resign**.

**Message Hierarchy**

**Core Message:** Stop guessing who will quit; know who is leaving three weeks early so you can save them.

**Pillar 1: Predictive Precision vs. Historical Lag**
*   **Claim:** Move from wondering why people left to knowing who is leaving next.
*   **Proof:** Our model predicts resignations with 82% accuracy up to three weeks in advance, based on pilot data from 12 regional chains (4,500 employees).
*   **Contrast:** Traditional surveys only tell you how people felt last month; we tell you who is walking out next week.

**Pillar 2: Targeted Intervention vs. Broad Brush**
*   **Claim:** Save your best people, not just "reduce headcount churn."
*   **Proof:** Alerts pinpoint specific at-risk employees, allowing managers to have timely, personalized retention conversations.
*   **Contrast:** Doing nothing means reacting only after exit interviews; generic surveys offer no actionable list of who to save.

**Pillar 3: Economic Impact via Retention**
*   **Claim:** Retaining one employee pays for the entire platform.
*   **Proof:** At $1,200/month, the subscription costs less than 25% of the average $5,000 expense to recruit and train a single replacement.
*   **Contrast:** The status quo of high churn silently drains budgets; our tool stops the bleed before the cost is incurred.

**Headline Option:** "Don't wait for the exit interview. Know who's leaving three weeks early."

**Handling Alternatives:**
*   **Against "Doing Nothing":** The cost of inaction is explicit: every unprevented resignation costs ~$5,000. Waiting for someone to quit is a choice to pay that fee.
*   **Against "Survey Tools":** Surveys measure sentiment history; TurnoverShield predicts future behavior. Knowing 82% of resignations three weeks early renders historical data obsolete for retention purposes.

**Riskiest Assumption & Test:**
The riskiest assumption is that HR VPs trust an algorithmic "flight risk" score enough to intervene managerially. **Test:** Run a 30-day A/B test with two pilot chains: give one chain the raw predictive list and the other only aggregate trends. Measure whether the group with specific names conducts more retention conversations and achieves lower actual turnover.

*Note: This positioning assumes the 82% accuracy holds across diverse retail sub-segments; validate this before scaling claims. Ensure legal review before labeling individuals as "flight risks" in internal communications.*
