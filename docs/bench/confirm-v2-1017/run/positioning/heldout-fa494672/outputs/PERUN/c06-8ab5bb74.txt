**Recommendation:** Position DataForGood as an **efficiency engine that frees up staff time for fundraising**, framed through the lens of risk mitigation. Executive directors care less about "database security" in the abstract and more about the tangible loss of donor relationships and staff burnout. The "security upgrade" angle often triggers fear of complex migration without offering a clear ROI, whereas "time for fundraising" directly addresses their primary survival metric: revenue.

**Positioning Statement (Hypothesis to Validate):**
"For **non-profit executive directors** who **need to maximize fundraising capacity without losing historical donor data**, DataForGood is a **donor relationship platform** that **reclaims ten hours a week for program staff**; unlike **fragile, manual spreadsheets** where version errors and accidental deletions threaten data integrity, it **guarantees 90% fewer data entry errors and secure, automatic migration of your existing sheets**."

**Headline:** Stop Managing Spreadsheets. Start Funding Your Mission.

**Comparison of Risks: Spreadsheets vs. DataForGood**

| Risk Area | The Spreadsheet Alternative (Status Quo) | DataForGood (Proposed Solution) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Staff Capacity** | **High Cost:** Program directors spend 10 hours/week manually compiling reports and fixing errors, time stolen directly from donor cultivation and grant writing. | **Recovered:** Automated reporting frees those 10 hours weekly, directly translating to increased fundraising potential and program impact. |
