**Recommendation: Lead with the Marketing Director (Sales Growth)**

While the operations angle offers clear cost savings, the primary friction in denim e-commerce is the shopper's hesitation to buy due to fit uncertainty. Positioning DenimFit as a "logistics saver" treats the symptom (returns) after the sale. Positioning it as a "confidence booster" treats the cause (hesitation) before the sale, directly unlocking the $12 increase in average order value and higher conversion rates you have already measured. In a growth phase, revenue expansion via conversion optimization typically outweighs margin protection via return reduction. Furthermore, a tool that drives sales is easier to sell to a CEO or CMO than a tool that merely cuts costs; the former is an investment, the latter an expense.

**Definitive Positioning Statement**

For **marketing directors at mid-sized denim brands** who **struggle with low conversion rates caused by shopper size anxiety**, **DenimFit** is a **virtual fitting room technology** that **eliminates fit uncertainty to boost sales and average order value**. Unlike **static size charts and manual guessing**, which **leave shoppers hesitant and prone to error**, DenimFit **leverages licensed data from 15 major brands to guarantee fit confidence, proven to increase average order value by $12 per transaction and reduce returns by 35%**.

**Choice Analysis: Sales Growth vs. Margin Protection**

| Feature | **Angle A: Marketing Director (Sales Growth)** | **Angle B: Operations Lead (Margin Protection)** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Goal** | Increase top-line revenue and conversion. | Protect bottom-line margin by cutting costs. |
| **Core Pain Solved** | Shoppers abandon carts due to fear of wrong size. | Retailers lose money on reverse shipping and restocking. |
| **Value Metric** | +$12 Average Order Value (AOV); Higher Conversion %. | -35% Return Rate; Lower logistics spend. |
| **Emotional Hook** | "Stop losing sales to uncertainty." | "Stop bleeding money on returns." |
| **Strategic Fit** | **High.** Addresses the root cause: shopper behavior. | **Medium.** Addresses the downstream consequence. |
| **Risk** | Requires proving lift in conversion (which you have). | May be viewed as a commodity cost-cutting measure. |

**Why this wins:**
By leading with sales growth, you align DenimFit with the retailer's most aggressive goals. The 35% return reduction becomes a powerful *supporting proof point* ("It pays for itself by slashing returns") rather than the *primary headline*. This framing makes the purchase decision easier: the tool generates new money first, then saves existing money second.

*Note: This positioning assumes your current pilot data (35% return reduction, $12 AOV lift) is statistically significant across the 15 licensed partners. Ensure legal clearance on using specific brand names like Levi's or Gap in public marketing materials.*
