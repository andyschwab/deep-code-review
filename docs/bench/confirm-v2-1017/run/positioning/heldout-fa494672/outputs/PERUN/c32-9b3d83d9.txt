**Recommended Strategy: The "Enhancement" Pivot**
Do not position as a total replacement for maintenance contracts yet. Your identified barrier—trust in tangible human inspection for safety compliance—is the critical friction point. Claiming to "eliminate downtime completely" validates your fear: it sounds impossible and triggers skepticism, causing buyers to retreat to the "tangible" safety of human inspectors.

Instead, position ElevateGuard as the **intelligence layer** that makes existing physical maintenance smarter. Your differentiator is not replacing the mechanic, but telling the mechanic exactly when and where to look, shifting the narrative from "software vs. humans" to "data-guided humans vs. blind scheduling." This lowers the risk for the buyer; they keep their trusted safety inspectors but upgrade their efficiency.
