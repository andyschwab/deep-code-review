### Message Hierarchy for LunetteNumérique

**Core Message (The Hypothesis to Validate)**
"For independent luxury eyewear brands struggling to balance digital reach with high-end aesthetics, LunetteNumérique is a high-fidelity virtual try-on engine that elevates brand storytelling while reducing returns; unlike generic social AR filters or static galleries, it delivers photorealistic rendering that respects luxury craftsmanship, turning hesitation into confident purchases."

    *   *Argument:* Generic filters distort light and texture, cheapening the product. Our engine preserves the specific material qualities (acetate grain, metal finish) essential to luxury perception.

2.  **Pillar: Confident Conversion (The Engagement Engine)**
    *   *Argument:* Shoppers aren't just playing; they are spending 3 extra minutes engaging deeply with the collection, signaling intent rather than boredom.
    *   *Proof Point:* Current pilot data shows a +3 minute increase in time-on-site per session across 15 boutique brands.
    *   *Status:* Verified by user input.

3.  **Pillar: Operational Efficiency (The Utility Outcome)**
    *   *Argument:* Reducing uncertainty at the top of the funnel directly lowers the cost of reverse logistics, protecting margins without discounting.
    *   *Proof Point:* Pilot partners report a 25% reduction in return rates.
    *   *Status:* Verified by user input.

**Differentiation Statement**
"Unlike the cheap, distorted AR filters on Instagram that treat eyewear as a toy, or static photos that leave fit to imagination, LunetteNumérique integrates 400+ distinct frame models with lighting physics that match your boutique's ambiance, ensuring the digital experience feels as premium as the physical product."

**Recommendation on Positioning**
Lead with **Enhanced Customer Experience (Brand Storytelling)**, supported by the financial metrics as validation, not the headline.
*Why:* Your buyers are skeptical of "gimmicks" and care deeply about aesthetics. Leading with "logistics savings" frames your tool as a back-office utility, which implies the front-end experience might be compromised. Leading with "high-fidelity storytelling" directly addresses their fear of brand dilution. The 25% return reduction and 3-minute engagement lift are the *evidence* that the premium experience works, not the primary hook.

**Headline for Skeptical Owners**
"Stop choosing between digital reach and luxury standards: High-fidelity try-on that protects your brand image and cuts returns by 25%."

**Riskiest Assumption & Test**
*Assumption:* Brand owners believe "high-fidelity" is technically achievable without slowing down site performance.
*Test:* Present a live demo on a mid-tier luxury brand site to 5 skeptical owners; if they ask about load times before commenting on visuals, the "aesthetic first" message fails.

*Note: This hierarchy is a hypothesis to be validated in sales conversations; do not claim legal clearance on "high-fidelity" claims without technical benchmarks.*
