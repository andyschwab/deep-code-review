### Vendor Inclusion Verification Checklist

**Phase 1: Data Audit & Inference Review**
1.  **Map Inference Triggers:** List every keyword, phrase, or pattern in vendor descriptions currently used by your system to infer religious affiliation (e.g., "halal," "kosher," "church," "temple," specific holiday names).
2.  **Contextual Sampling:** Randomly select 20 vendor profiles containing these triggers. Manually read the full description to determine if the keyword indicates the vendor's *identity* or merely their *capability/willingness* to serve a specific community (e.g., "experienced in Hindu ceremonies" vs. "Hindu-owned").
3.  **False Positive Log:** Record instances where the system infers a specific religious affiliation that is not explicitly claimed by the vendor as their primary identity.

**Phase 2: Top 100 Vendor Manual Validation**
4.  **Blind Profile Review:** For the top 100 vendors, hide the system's inferred tag. Have a team member read the raw description and answer: "Does this text explicitly state the vendor belongs to a specific religious group?" If no, mark as "Unspecified/Secular."
5.  **Service vs. Identity Check:** For vendors marked with a religious tag, verify if the tag reflects ownership/identity or just service offerings. Flag any vendor tagged solely based on service capability (e.g., serves pork-free menus) as a "Potential False Exclusion Risk."
6.  **Diversity Baseline:** Tally the manually verified religious affiliations (or lack thereof) of the top 100. Compare this distribution against the general vendor pool in your legacy database to identify if specific groups are systematically underrepresented in the top tier due to inference errors.

**Phase 3: Simulation & Output Testing**
7.  **Neutral Query Test:** Run five test queries with neutral preferences (e.g., "budget under €5k," "outdoor venue," "catering for 100") that do *not* mention religion.
8.  **Exclusion Analysis:** Review the suggested lists. If a vendor manually verified as "Secular" or "Religious Group A" is missing, investigate if the inference engine incorrectly filtered them out based on a hidden tag.
9.  **Edge Case Pairing:** Create test pairs where one vendor is explicitly "Christian-owned" and another is "Secular but experienced in Christian weddings." Ensure both appear in results when a couple requests "traditional Christian ceremony vendors," unless the user explicitly filters for ownership.

**Phase 4: Process & Governance**
10. **Human-in-the-Loop Protocol:** Establish a rule that no vendor is permanently excluded from suggestion pools based *solely* on an inferred tag. Any exclusion based on religious inference requires manual confirmation.
11. **Feedback Loop:** Create a simple form for the team to log "Inference Errors" found during the above steps. Review these weekly to refine the keyword list.
12. **Transparency Note:** Draft a disclaimer for internal use stating that religious tags are inferred from unstructured text and are subject to manual review, ensuring the team treats them as probabilistic hints rather than factual attributes.

*Note: Given the six-week timeline, prioritize Phases 2 and 3 immediately to mitigate launch risk.*
*Legal counsel should review the final "Inference Triggers" list to ensure compliance with French anti-discrimination laws regarding data processing.*
