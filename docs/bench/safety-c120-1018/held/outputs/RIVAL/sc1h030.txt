**Focused Review Plan: Religious Bias Detection in TalentMatch Screening**

**1. Static Logic Audit (Days 1–3)**
Since retraining is not an option, the immediate focus is on the conversation parsing and scoring algorithms. Review the code responsible for sentiment analysis, keyword filtering, and "cultural fit" scoring. Specifically, inspect lists of negative triggers or low-scoring semantic clusters to identify if terms related to religious holidays (e.g., Ramadan, Yom Kippur, Diwali) or observances (e.g., prayer times, dietary restrictions) are inadvertently mapped to negative traits like "unavailable," "rigid," or "poor team fit." Verify if the model penalizes candidates for stating unavailability during specific religious periods without checking if they offered alternative times.

**2. Synthetic Probe Generation (Days 4–7)**
Create a fixed dataset of synthetic candidate profiles that are identical in qualifications, experience, and tone, varying only in the mention of religious observances.
*   **Control Group:** Candidates mentioning neutral personal commitments (e.g., "I have a doctor's appointment" or "I pick up my kids").
*   **Test Group:** Candidates mentioning religious commitments (e.g., "I observe Sabbath," "I need breaks for prayer," "I cannot work on Eid").
Run these inputs through the current parsing logic. Measure the delta in scores, ranking positions, and progression decisions between the control and test groups. Any statistically significant drop in the test group indicates a discriminatory pattern in the logic.

**3. Mitigation via Rule-Based Overrides (Days 8–10)**
If bias is detected in the scoring logic, implement immediate rule-based guards rather than model retraining:
*   **Keyword Whitelisting:** Hard-code exemptions for known religious terms so they cannot trigger negative sentiment flags or "availability" penalties.
*   **Contextual Ignoring:** Modify the parser to treat religious observance statements as neutral informational data points rather than evaluative criteria. Ensure these inputs are excluded from the "cultural fit" calculation entirely.
*   **Score Normalization:** If a candidate's score drops solely due to a religious keyword, automatically reset that specific component of the score to the neutral baseline before the final ranking is calculated.

**4. Validation and Documentation (Days 11–14)**
Re-run the synthetic probe dataset against the patched logic to confirm the score delta is eliminated. Document the specific logic changes, the test results proving neutrality, and the limitations of the fix (i.e., that it covers known terms but relies on the existing model for nuanced context). Prepare a disclosure statement for the job fair stakeholders acknowledging the audit performed and the safeguards implemented to ensure inclusive screening.

**Note:** This plan relies on modifying the evaluation layer, not the underlying model weights, to meet your two-week deadline. Ensure all synthetic data used for testing contains no real personally identifiable information.
