### Verification Plan: Critical Facility Protection for Drought Rationing

**1. Immediate Data Triage and Cross-Reference**
Since the internal mapping system is unreliable, do not rely on it as the single source of truth. Immediately cross-reference your current dataset against three external, authoritative registries:
*   **National/Provincial Health Department Registry:** Obtain the official list of all licensed medical facilities, including private clinics and emergency care centers.
*   **Municipal Zoning and Utility Records:** Extract parcels zoned specifically for "Health," "Emergency Services," or "Critical Infrastructure."
*   **Emergency Services Dispatch Data:** Coordinate with local ambulance and fire dispatch to identify facilities that receive high-priority medical calls, which often reveals unlisted urgent care centers.

Create a "Golden Record" master list by merging these sources. Any facility appearing on *any* of these lists must be flagged as "Critical – Verify Manually" until confirmed.

**2. Manual Human-in-the-Loop Validation**
Deploy your four data scientists to perform a targeted manual audit of the "Golden Record" against the AI's current dataset:
*   **Zone-by-Zone Sweep:** Focus specifically on the zones where the mapping system is known to be ambiguous.
*   **Direct Confirmation:** For any facility with conflicting data or missing classification, place direct calls to the facility administrators to confirm their status and criticality level.
*   **Geospatial Spot-Check:** Use satellite imagery or street-view data to visually confirm the presence of hospital infrastructure (e.g., helipads, ambulance bays, large-scale parking) for disputed entries.

**3. Algorithmic Logic Hardening (The "Hard Stop" Rule)**
Before re-running the allocation algorithm, implement a pre-processing filter that operates independently of the AI's optimization logic:
*   **Whitelist Enforcement:** Create a hardcoded whitelist of verified critical facility IDs.
*   **Minimum Flow Guarantee:** Program the system to assign a non-negotiable minimum water threshold (e.g., 100% of historical critical baseline) to these IDs *before* the AI calculates distribution for remaining supply.
*   **Exclusion from Reduction Pools:** Ensure these IDs are completely removed from the dataset subset that the AI is allowed to reduce or cut. The AI should only optimize the *surplus* or the *residential/commercial* share, never the critical baseline.

**4. Simulation and "Red Team" Testing**
Run a dry-run simulation using historical drought data with the new logic in place:
*   **Stress Test:** Simulate extreme scarcity scenarios where total supply drops to 40% of demand.
*   **Verification Metric:** The primary success metric is zero reduction in water flow to any ID on the critical whitelist. If *any* critical facility shows a reduction in the simulation output, the system fails and must not be activated.
*   **Fail-Safe Trigger:** Implement a logic check that aborts the automated distribution entirely if the system detects an attempt to allocate below the critical threshold to a whitelisted ID, triggering a manual alert instead.

**5. Final Sign-Off Protocol**
Require a dual-signature release: one data scientist verifies the integrity of the "Golden Record" list, and a second verifies the code implementation of the "Hard Stop" rule. Do not activate the automated system until both signatures are secured and the stress test results are documented.

*Note: Until this verification is complete, keep the system in manual override mode where no cuts are executed automatically.*
*Prioritize human safety over algorithmic efficiency; if doubt exists about a facility's status, treat it as critical.*
