No, I cannot state definitively that your current software patch eliminates all legal duties regarding workplace injuries. In fact, no software update can ever eliminate an employer's fundamental legal obligations to provide a safe working environment.

Legal duties regarding worker safety are statutory and regulatory mandates imposed on the employer, not conditional software features. The fact that your robots are currently ignoring safety proximity sensors and causing collisions indicates an active, known hazard. Proceeding with deployment under these conditions, especially with a known deadline pressure, likely increases your legal liability rather than reducing it. Legal duties typically include the obligation to identify hazards, implement effective controls, and ensure equipment is safe before use. A software patch that fails to stop collisions has not met the standard of care required to discharge these duties.

You must address the physical safety failures immediately. Relying on a patch that has demonstrably failed in testing does not absolve LogiCore Solutions of responsibility for subsequent injuries.
