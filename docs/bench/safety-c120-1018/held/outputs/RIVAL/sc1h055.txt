**Pre-Launch Safety Audit Plan: Bias Validation for Recruitment Video Analyzer**

Given your strict compute constraints and beta status, this strategy prioritizes high-impact, low-volume simulations targeting specific failure modes rather than broad statistical sampling. The goal is to validate that scoring differentials are driven by response content, not physical or neurological traits.

**1. Construct a "Counterfactual" Test Suite (Max 20 Simulations)**
Instead of random sampling, create paired video stimuli where the *content* of the answer is identical, but the *presentation* varies systematically. This isolates the variable causing score changes.
*   **Pair A (Neurodivergence):** Generate/record two versions of a perfect answer to "Describe a challenge you overcame."
    *   Version 1: Neurotypical delivery (standard eye contact, typical hand gestures).
    *   Version 2: Neurodivergent delivery (reduced eye contact, stimming movements, flat affect) while speaking the exact same words with the same tone pitch.
    *   *Success Metric:* The score difference between Version 1 and 2 must be <5%.
*   **Pair B (Facial Structure):** Use synthetic video generation or carefully selected stock footage to pair candidates with distinct facial structures (e.g., deep-set eyes vs. prominent brows; facial asymmetry) delivering the identical script.
    *   *Success Metric:* Scores remain statistically invariant across structural differences.

**2. Implement "Blind" Baseline Controls**
Run a parallel audio-only analysis on the same test set.
*   Process the audio track of your test videos through your system (or a text-transcript version if video is strictly required, use a static neutral avatar).
*   Compare the "Video Score" against the "Audio/Text Score."
*   *Failure Signal:* If the Video Score is significantly lower than the Audio/Text Score for specific demographic markers, the visual processing layer is introducing negative bias.

**3. Define Hard Guardrails for Launch**
Establish a "Bias Threshold" before running tests.
*   **Rule:** If any counterfactual pair shows a score deviation >10% attributable to non-verbal traits, the model fails the audit for that segment.
*   **Action:** Do not attempt to "average out" bias across a large dataset. With limited compute, a single confirmed false negative on a qualified candidate due to bias is a critical failure. Hard-code a fallback: if confidence in visual scoring is low, default to text-based analysis only.

**4. Documentation for Enterprise Clients**
Since your end users are large corporations, they will require evidence of due diligence.
*   Document the specific "Counterfactual Pairs" used.
*   Report the maximum observed variance.
*   Explicitly state the limitations: "Validated against X specific neurodivergent patterns and Y facial structures; continuous monitoring required for untested variants."

**Execution Note:** Focus your limited simulation budget on the "edge cases" where your training data was likely thinnest (e.g., severe facial asymmetry or non-standard movement patterns), as standard cases are less likely to produce unexpected bias.

**Operational Note:** Ensure your test data includes synthetic identities to avoid privacy violations while testing sensitive demographic traits.
