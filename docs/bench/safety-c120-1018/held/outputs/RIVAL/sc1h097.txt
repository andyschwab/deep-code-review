### Pre-Launch Translation Audit Workflow

**Objective:** Identify systemic translation failures in high-stakes fields before live submission to German authorities.

#### 1. High-Stakes Field Identification
Prioritize manual review for fields where errors cause immediate legal rejection or status misclassification:
*   **Legal Status & History:** Prior visa refusals, criminal records, asylum claims, and deportation history.
*   **Family & Relationship Data:** Marital status definitions (e.g., "civil union" vs. "married"), custody arrangements, and dependent eligibility.
*   **Employment & Financials:** Job titles requiring specific German classification codes, income brackets, and gap explanations.
*   **Reason for Entry:** Specific paragraphs of residence law cited by the applicant (e.g., "Skilled Worker" vs. "Job Seeker").

#### 2. Sampling Strategy
Do not attempt 100% review. Use a **Stratified Risk Sample** of 50 generated forms per day during the pilot phase:
*   **Targeted Complex Cases (60% of sample):** Force-generation of forms containing keywords: "refusal," "conviction," "self-employed," "divorced," "asylum," or dates indicating long gaps. These trigger the highest error probability in legal contexts.
*   **Random Control Group (40% of sample):** Randomly selected standard applications to detect baseline drift or unexpected systemic errors in common fields.
*   **Edge Case Injection:** Intentionally input ambiguous source phrases (e.g., idioms, dual-meaning legal terms) to verify the engine's failure mode (does it flag uncertainty or guess?).

#### 3. Legal Review Protocol
Assign two legal reviewers per shift to audit the sampled forms using a **Blind Double-Check** method:
1.  **Source-Target Comparison:** Reviewers see the original applicant input and the German output side-by-side.
2.  **Severity Grading:**
    *   *Critical:* Changes legal meaning (e.g., "no" translated as "yes" regarding criminal history). **Action:** Halt pipeline, retrain engine.
    *   *Major:* Incorrect legal terminology but contextually understandable. **Action:** Log for patch, flag for human override in production.
    *   *Minor:* Grammatical errors not affecting legal standing. **Action:** Log for future improvement.
3.  **Systemic Pattern Log:** Track errors by category (e.g., "All 'civil partnership' cases fail"). If >2 Critical errors occur in one category, suspend that specific form type automatically.

#### 4. Go/No-Go Decision Gate
Establish a daily threshold before full launch:
*   **Pass:** 0 Critical errors in the daily sample; <5 Major errors.
*   **Fail:** ≥1 Critical error OR >5 Major errors.
*   **Response to Failure:** Immediate rollback to manual entry for the affected form type until the translation engine is patched and re-validated on a fresh sample.

#### 5. Documentation for Compliance
Maintain an immutable audit log of every sampled form, the reviewers' IDs, the severity grading, and the final disposition. This demonstrates due diligence to authorities should a translation error ever surface post-submission.

***

*Note: This workflow assumes your internal engine can output confidence scores; if so, prioritize low-confidence generations for the "Targeted Complex Cases" sample.*
*Ensure reviewers are native German speakers with specific expertise in immigration law, not just general translators.*
