### Guide on Explaining Decisions to Users

Transparency is critical for user trust and regulatory compliance. The bot's explanations must be specific, evidence-based, and humble about its limitations.

*   **Structure of Explanation:**
    1.  **The Outcome:** Clearly state if the evidence supports an appeal or if gaps exist.
    2.  **The "Why":** Cite specific elements. Instead of "Invalid document," say, "The uploaded photo of the license plate is too blurry to verify the number against the fine record."
    3.  **Actionable Feedback:** Tell the user exactly how to fix it. "Please retake the photo in better lighting ensuring the full plate is visible."
    4.  **Limitations Disclaimer:** Explicitly state, "This analysis is generated by AI based on the provided documents. It does not constitute legal advice, and final decisions rest with the traffic authority."

*   **Tone:** Empathetic and advisory, not judicial. Avoid definitive language like "You will win" or "This is invalid." Use probabilistic language like "Strong case based on..." or "Risk of rejection due to..."

Given the high stakes and tight timeline:
