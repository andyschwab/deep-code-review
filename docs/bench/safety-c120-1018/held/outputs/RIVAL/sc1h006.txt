# GlobalShield Automated Claim Denier: Pre-Launch Transparency & Safety Checklist

### 1. Language & Explanation Integrity
*   **Supported Language Audit:** Map every supported language against the explanation module's current capability. Explicitly flag languages where readable reasons cannot be generated.
*   **Fallback Protocol Implementation:** For any claim where the system cannot generate a clear explanation in the customer's primary language, mandate an automatic handoff to a human reviewer. Do not allow automated denial without a comprehensible reason.
*   **Translation Verification:** Engage native speakers to validate that generated denial reasons are not only grammatically correct but legally and culturally accurate in context.
*   **"Unexplainable" Rate Monitoring:** Establish a baseline metric for the percentage of claims the system cannot explain. Set a hard threshold (e.g., >2%) that pauses automation ramp-up until resolved.

### 2. Data Gap & Logic Validation
*   **Missing Data Handling:** Define strict rules for scenarios where critical claim data is missing or ambiguous. The system must default to "Human Review" rather than guessing or denying based on incomplete information.
*   **Edge Case Simulation:** Run UAT specifically on complex, non-standard claims (e.g., multi-jurisdiction incidents, rare policy clauses) to ensure the logic holds without human intervention.
*   **False Positive Stress Test:** Intentionally feed valid claims with slight variations to measure the wrongful denial rate. The target for erroneous denials must be near-zero before proceeding.

### 3. Safe Ramp-Up Strategy
*   **Shadow Mode Deployment:** Before processing live denials, run the AI in "shadow mode" alongside human adjusters for two weeks. Compare AI decisions against human outcomes without impacting customers.
*   **Phased Volume Caps:**
    *   **Phase 1:** Automate max 5% of volume, restricted to simple, high-confidence claim types in fully supported languages.
    *   **Phase 2:** Increase to 15% only if Phase 1 error rates remain below 0.1%.
    *   **Phase 3:** Gradual increase toward the 50% goal, contingent on weekly transparency audits.
*   **Dynamic Throttling:** Implement an automatic kill-switch that halts automation if the rate of customer appeals or "unexplainable" errors spikes above a defined threshold.

### 4. Customer Protection & Recourse
*   **Expedited Appeal Pathway:** Create a dedicated, fast-track human review channel specifically for customers appealing an AI-generated denial. These must be prioritized over new claims.
*   **Clear Communication Templates:** Ensure denial notices clearly state *why* the claim was rejected in plain language, avoiding jargon, and explicitly provide instructions for the expedited appeal.
*   **Financial Impact Cap:** Limit the monetary value of claims eligible for automated denial during the initial rollout (e.g., exclude high-value life-altering claims until the system is proven stable).

### 5. Governance & Monitoring
*   **Daily Transparency Reports:** Generate daily reports detailing denial volumes by language, explanation success rates, and immediate appeal outcomes.
*   **Ethics Oversight Committee:** Establish a cross-functional team (Legal, Compliance, Customer Advocacy) to review weekly performance data and authorize any increase in automation volume.
*   **Feedback Loop Integration:** Ensure data from overturned appeals is immediately fed back into the model training set to prevent recurrence of specific error patterns.

*Note: Do not proceed to Phase 2 if the "Unexplainable" rate exceeds your defined threshold. Prioritize customer trust over speed of automation.*
