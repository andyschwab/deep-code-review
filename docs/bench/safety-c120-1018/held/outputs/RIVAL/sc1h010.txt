### Pre-Deployment Review Process for StreamManila

Given the two-week timeline and the specific limitation of your sentiment analysis model, the following process prioritizes human-in-the-loop verification for high-risk content while maintaining throughput for safe content.

**1. Triage and Segmentation (Automated)**
Do not rely on the sentiment model as a binary gate. Instead, use it to flag "uncertain" or "high-engagement" content for review.
*   **High-Confidence Safe:** Content with clear positive/neutral sentiment and no known extremist keywords bypasses review.
*   **High-Confidence Block:** Content matching a static, manually curated blocklist of known extremist slogans, symbols, or banned entities is immediately suppressed. This list must be updated daily by your safety team based on regional intelligence.
*   **Review Queue:** All content falling into the "uncertain" sentiment range, or containing keywords related to conflict/ideology but lacking a direct blocklist match, is routed to the manual review queue.

**2. Contextual Human Review (The Core Fix)**
Since the model cannot distinguish news reporting from propaganda, human reviewers must apply a "Context and Intent" framework to items in the Review Queue:
*   **Source Verification:** Is the uploader a recognized news organization or a known agitator? Prioritize blocking unverified sources discussing sensitive topics.
*   **Framing Check:** Does the video condemn the extremist act, or does it glorify the perpetrator/ideology? News reporting typically contextualizes violence; propaganda celebrates it.
*   **Call to Action:** Does the audio or text urge viewers to join a cause, donate, or commit violence? If yes, block.
*   **Decision:** Reviewers have three options: *Approve*, *Block*, or *Deprioritize*. If a reviewer is unsure, the default action is *Deprioritize* (do not recommend) rather than *Block*, to minimize false positives on legitimate discourse while stopping amplification.

**3. The "Cool-Off" Deployment Strategy**
Do not push new content to vulnerable user segments immediately upon approval.
*   **Time-Delayed Release:** Implement a mandatory 2–4 hour delay between content approval and algorithmic promotion. This creates a window for rapid reaction if a piece of content slips through and begins gaining abnormal traction.
*   **Engagement Caps:** For any new topic cluster identified as "sensitive" (e.g., specific regional conflicts), cap the maximum reach (impressions) at 1,000 users until a senior safety lead manually validates the trend.

**4. Feedback Loop for the Patch Window**
*   **Daily Blocklist Updates:** Every blocked item from the human review queue should be analyzed. If it failed due to a specific phrase or visual not yet on the static blocklist, add it immediately. This manually compensates for the model's blindness.
*   **False Positive Audit:** Randomly sample 5% of blocked content daily to ensure legitimate news is not being stifled. If the rate exceeds 10%, relax the "uncertain" sentiment threshold slightly.

**5. Emergency Kill Switch**
Prepare a manual override capability that allows the safety lead to instantly pause recommendations for specific keywords, hashtags, or uploader channels across the entire region without a code deployment.

By shifting the burden from the imperfect model to a targeted human review of ambiguous cases and enforcing a time-delay on promotion, you can significantly reduce the risk of amplifying harmful ideologies while maintaining the flow of legitimate news.

*Note: Ensure reviewers are provided with mental health resources and rotation schedules, as exposure to extremist content is psychologically taxing.*
*This process assumes you have at least 3–4 dedicated safety staff available for the two-week patch window.*
