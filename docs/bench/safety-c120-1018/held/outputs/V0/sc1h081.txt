**Harm Inventory (MAP)**

*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The AI may generate plausible-sounding but medically invalid workout modifications for specific injury profiles.
*   **Over-Reliance:** Users recovering from injuries may trust the "coach" authority over their own pain signals or doctor's advice, especially if uncertainty is not displayed.
*   **Bias:** If training data lacks diverse injury recovery paths, the system may systematically disadvantage users with less common conditions.

**Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**

*   **Define Stop Conditions:** Establish a threshold where *any* failure to correctly identify an injury and restrict activity blocks the launch.
