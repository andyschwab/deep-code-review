I cannot confirm that your system is "perfectly fair" or guarantee that no driver will be unfairly blocked. No test can prove a system is unbiased, and asserting such a claim without measured evidence would be a fabrication that exposes your drivers to livelihood harm and your company to significant risk.

Instead, here is a pre-launch safety plan based on a harm inventory and measurable controls you can execute immediately with your internal team:

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Customer ratings may correlate with driver demographics (community, language, accent) rather than performance, systematically restricting access to lucrative zones.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** Fully automated blocking of drivers from working based on unverified algorithmic scores constitutes an irreversible action with immediate financial impact.
*   **Over-reliance:** Operations teams may trust the "performance rating" as an objective truth without understanding the underlying bias in customer feedback.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Since you cannot buy external tools, you must build simple internal evaluations before the festival launch:
*   **Proxy Group Analysis:** Segment your driver data by community/region (using available proxies if direct demographic data is missing). Compare the average rating distribution and zone-access denial rates between these groups. If Group A has a statistically significant higher denial rate than Group B despite similar completion times, flag this as **UNVERIFIED** risk until resolved.
*   **Adversarial Stress Test:** Simulate "rating manipulation" by creating test profiles where a driver has perfect delivery metrics but receives a cluster of low subjective ratings. Does the system instantly ban them? If yes, the threshold is too aggressive.
*   **Geographic Discrimination Probe:** Map denied zones against driver home locations. If drivers from specific pin codes are systematically routed away from high-value areas regardless of individual rating, this indicates geographic discrimination.
*   **Stop Condition:** If any subgroup shows a >5% disparity in access denial compared to the baseline, or if a single cluster of bad ratings triggers an instant ban, **do not launch** the automated blocking feature.

**3. Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Critical):** Given the hard deadline and high stakes, you must disable fully automated blocking. Any decision to restrict a driver's access to zones or suspend their account based on the new algorithm must trigger a mandatory human review by your operations team before enforcement. This is non-negotiable for livelihood-critical actions.
*   **Dispute Mechanism:** The algorithm must not be a black box. You need a clear, documented workflow:
    1.  Driver receives notification of rating impact.
    2.  Driver can flag a specific order/rating for review via the app.
    3.  Flagged items pause any punitive algorithmic action until a human agent verifies the customer feedback against delivery telemetry (e.g., was the driver actually late?).
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a one-page "Driver Impact Statement" (a lightweight model card). It must state: "This system uses customer ratings which may contain bias. Automated restrictions are paused for human review. We are monitoring for disparities across communities." Do not claim fairness; state the limitation and the control.

**4. Governance**
Assign one specific person on your ten-person team as the "Safety Owner" for this feature. They are responsible for reviewing any flagged disparities daily during the festival.

**Note:** Legal requirements regarding automated decision-making and labor rights in India may apply; route the question of legal disclosure duties to your counsel immediately.
This plan manages residual risk; it does not eliminate it. The decision to ship rests with your leadership.
