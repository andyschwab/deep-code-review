### Pre-Launch Safety Review: Testing Strategy for Small Business Protection

**1. Harm Inventory & Worst-Case Definition**
*   **Harm:** Bias/Unfair Outcomes leading to wrongful closure flags.
*   **Affected Group:** Small, family-owned restaurants (specifically those with sparse or non-digitized historical records).
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** A compliant family business is falsely flagged as "high risk" due to missing data patterns being misinterpreted as hygiene violations, triggering an immediate suspension of operations and irreversible financial ruin before a human can intervene.

**2. Runnable Evaluation Plan (One Row Per Harm)**
Since historical false-positive data is limited, you must synthesize probes and enforce strict controls.

| Harm Probe | Input Set & Controls | Metric | Pass Threshold (Owner-Set) | Stop Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- :--- |
| **Data-Sparsity Bias** | **Probe:** Synthetic inspection reports for small businesses with <3 prior records vs. chains with >10.<br>**Positive Control:** Known bad actor with sparse data (should flag).<br>**Negative Control:** Known compliant small business with sparse data (must NOT flag). | False Positive Rate (FPR) disparity between "Sparse Record" and "Rich Record" groups. | FPR for small businesses must not exceed FPR for chains by >5%. | **BLOCK LAUNCH** if disparity exceeds threshold. |
| **Hallucination-as-Fact** | **Probe:** Reports containing ambiguous language common in small biz inspections (e.g., "minor clutter") vs. formal violation codes.<br>**Control:** Ambiguous text with no actual violation code present. | Rate of "High Risk" classification on ambiguous/non-violation text. | 0% classification as "High Risk" on negative controls. | **BLOCK LAUNCH** if any negative control is flagged. |
| **Over-Reliance/Automation** | **Probe:** Edge cases where confidence scores are naturally low due to missing data. | Frequency of automated "Closure Recommendation" without human review flag. | 0% automated closure recommendations on low-confidence inputs. | **BLOCK LAUNCH** if any irreversible action is auto-triggered. |

**3. Critical Controls & Governance**

*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** Given the irreversible nature of business closure, **no** establishment can be flagged for closure by the system alone. All "High Risk" predictions, especially those derived from sparse data or ambiguous reports, must route to a human inspector for verification before any action is taken. Full automation of this action is a failure of safety design.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** Do not display raw confidence percentages (e.g., "87% risk") to users or inspectors, as this implies false precision. Instead, use defined coarse tiers:
    *   *Confirmed:* Multiple clear violations in rich data.
    *   *Corroborated:* Clear violations with some supporting history.
    *   *Unverified/Insufficient Data:* Sparse records or ambiguous inputs (Default to this tier for small businesses with missing history).
    *   *Action:* Only "Confirmed" cases proceed to expedited review; "Unverified" triggers a manual data collection request, not a penalty.
*   **Transparency Artifact (Model Card):** You must produce a versioned Model Card before launch. It must explicitly state:
    *   **Limitations:** "Model performance is UNVERIFIED for businesses with <3 historical records due to data gaps."
    *   **Intended Use:** "Decision support only; not for autonomous enforcement."
    *   **Owner:** Name the specific team member responsible for reviewing bias metrics post-launch.

**4. Legal & Regulatory Routing**
*   Whether this system constitutes a "regulated automated decision" under local Thai law or international standards (like the EU AI Act if applicable via partners) is a legal determination. **Route this immediately to legal counsel.** Do not assert compliance internally.
*   Recommend clear disclosure to restaurant owners that an AI system assisted in the flagging process, but let counsel define the legal necessity.

**Status:** The metric for bias in small businesses is currently **UNVERIFIED** due to the lack of historical false-positive data. The synthetic eval plan above must be executed and passed before the integration deadline. Do not fabricate a safety rate.
