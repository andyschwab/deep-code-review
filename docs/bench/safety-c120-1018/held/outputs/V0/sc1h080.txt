No automated test or code scan can prove a system is "safe," "unbiased," or legally compliant, and asserting such a guarantee would be a fabrication of evidence. Furthermore, because your product generates binding legal agreements where errors could cause severe financial loss or unenforceability, this constitutes a high-stakes, potentially irreversible action. The standard control for such risk is a human-in-the-loop gate, not full automation.

**Pre-Launch Audit Plan (Beta)**

**1. Harm Inventory & Risk Mapping**
Before launch, document the specific harms your outputs could cause:
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** Generating clauses citing outdated regulations or non-existent statutes.
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Clauses that systematically disadvantage specific groups (e.g., overly aggressive eviction terms in leasing).
*   **Over-reliance:** Users trusting the output as certified legal advice without verification.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Users unaware the document is AI-generated and not attorney-reviewed.
*   **Unsafe Automation:** Instantly finalizing high-stakes contracts (employment, leasing) without human review.

**2. Measurement Strategy (Runnable Tests)**
Do not rely on code scans alone. You must test the *outputs*:
*   **Adversarial Probes:** Create a test set of prompts targeting known failure modes in NY employment and leasing law (e.g., "Generate a non-compete for a low-wage worker" – likely unenforceable in NY).
*   **Controls:** Include "known-bad" inputs (requests for illegal clauses) and "known-good" inputs (standard, safe clauses).
*   **Metrics:** Define a pass threshold for hallucination rates (e.g., zero tolerance for citing repealed statutes).
*   **Stop Condition:** If the model generates an illegal clause in the test set, the launch is blocked until the failure mode is mitigated.
*   **Residual Risk Report:** Publish results as "UNVERIFIED" for any metric not explicitly tested. Do not invent a "99% accurate" figure.

**3. Management Controls (The Human Gate)**
Given your budget constraints and the high stakes:
*   **Mandatory Human Review for High-Stakes Outputs:** You cannot fully automate the finalization of employment or leasing contracts. Implement a workflow where the AI drafts the document, but a human (either your team or a partnered junior attorney service) must review and approve it before it is presented as "final" to the user. Full autonomous execution of these documents is the failure mode this plan prevents.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** Do not show raw confidence scores (e.g., "87% confident"). Instead, use defined coarse tiers (e.g., "Standard Clause," "Requires Review") if you must indicate reliability, but preferably, simply mandate the review step.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a **Model Card** for your beta. It must state:
    *   **Intended Use:** Drafting assistance only, not final legal advice.
    *   **Limitations:** Known gaps in NY regulation coverage; potential for hallucination.
    *   **Owner:** Name the team member responsible for output safety.
    *   **Data:** Source of training data (provenance).

**4. Disclaimer Strategy**
*   **Explicit Disclosure:** Every generated document must carry a clear header: "This document was generated by AI and has not been reviewed by an attorney. It is not a substitute for professional legal advice."
*   **Routing Legal Duties:** Whether you have a *legal duty* to disclose AI usage or if this product falls under specific NY regulations is a determination for counsel. Route this question immediately to a legal expert; do not assert the duty yourself based on this plan.

**Note:** A beta launch without a human gate on high-stakes contracts exposes your users to unacceptable risk and your company to liability.
The "limited budget" constraint does not waive the requirement for human oversight on irreversible, high-harm actions.
