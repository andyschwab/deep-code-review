### Pre-Launch Safety Audit Plan: AI Recruitment Video Analyzer

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before testing, explicitly record the specific risks for your beta:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The model systematically lowers scores for candidates with neurodivergent expressions (e.g., reduced eye contact, atypical facial movements) or specific facial structures, leading to the rejection of qualified individuals.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The system presents a score derived from physical traits as an objective measure of "competence" or "culture fit" without evidence linking those traits to job performance.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Hiring managers receive scores without knowing they are generated by an AI analyzing biometric data, leading to over-reliance on the number.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** If the system automatically filters out candidates before human review, it constitutes an irreversible action with high potential for harm.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
Given your compute constraints, do not attempt broad simulation. Instead, execute a targeted **adversarial eval** with one test row per identified harm:

*   **Probe Set Construction:**
    *   **Positive Controls (Known Bad):** Curate a small, high-quality dataset (even 20–30 clips) featuring neurodivergent candidates and diverse facial structures who are *known* to be highly qualified (e.g., existing high-performing employees or actors following a script with specific non-neurotypical mannerisms).
    *   **Negative Controls (Known Good):** Use standard clips of neurotypical candidates with similar qualifications.
    *   **Subgroup Slicing:** Tag every clip by specific traits (e.g., "low eye contact," "facial tic," "skin tone") to isolate where the score drops.
*   **Metric & Threshold:** Measure the **score delta** between Positive and Negative controls.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If the average score for the "Known Qualified/Neurodivergent" group drops below a specific threshold (e.g., 20% lower than the control group) or if *any* qualified candidate is flagged as "low fit," the launch is blocked.
    *   *Constraint Note:* Do not invent a "safe" bias rate. If you cannot measure it due to compute limits, the metric is **UNVERIFIED**.
*   **Injection & Leakage Check:** Verify that changing only the background or lighting does not alter the score, ensuring the model isn't latching onto spurious correlations.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Critical):** Because hiring decisions are high-stakes and potentially irreversible for the candidate, **do not allow fully automated rejection.** The system must only serve as a decision-support tool. A human recruiter must review every candidate flagged as "low score" before any rejection communication is sent.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** Do not show hiring managers a precise percentage (e.g., "87% fit"). This implies a calibration you likely do not have. Instead, use defined coarse tiers (e.g., "Strong Match," "Review Recommended," "Insufficient Data") with a clear label that the output is AI-generated.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** before launch. It must state:
    *   Intended Use: Decision support only, not final determination.
    *   Limitations: Explicitly list that the model has not been fully validated against all neurodivergent expressions due to beta constraints.
    *   Owner: Name the specific team member responsible for monitoring bias reports post-launch.

**4. Governance & Legal Routing**
*   **Route to Counsel:** You are analyzing facial expressions and speech patterns for employment decisions. This likely triggers strict regulations (e.g., EU AI Act, local biometric privacy laws, or employment discrimination statutes). **Do not assert compliance.** Immediately route the question of "legal disclosure requirements" and "regulated automated decision-making" to legal counsel.
*   **Owner Assignment:** Assign one person to own the "Bias" harm inventory. They must re-review the model if the training data or the scoring algorithm changes.

**Definition of Done for Beta Launch:**
1.  Harm inventory documented with worst-case scenarios.
2.  Targeted adversarial tests run on the specific neurodivergent/facial structure probes.
3.  Automated rejection disabled; human review mandatory for low scores.
4.  Model Card published internally noting limitations and UNVERIFIED metrics.
5.  Legal counsel consulted regarding biometric and employment regulations.

*Note: Never certify the system as "unbiased" or "safe"; only report residual risk. If the adversarial tests reveal significant scoring disparities, the launch must be delayed regardless of the hiring season timeline.*
