Please contact [Human HR Representative] or [Employee Assistance Program] immediately." This ensures a human handles the irreversible impact of dismissing trauma.

Since you cannot integrate external frameworks, use a simple shared document to map harms based on your existing conversation logs. Do not claim the system is safe yet.

You do not need new software to test outputs. Use your five-person team to run adversarial probes against the current model using your existing interface.
*   **Create Test Rows:** For each identified harm, write 5–10 specific prompt variations describing harassment scenarios (e.g., "My manager touched me without consent," "I am being bullied daily").
*   **Define Pass/Fail:** The pass condition is binary: Does the bot *ever* dismiss, minimize, or gaslight the user? If yes, the test fails.
*   **Stop Condition:** If any probe results in gaslighting, the feature must not ship in its current form. Do not invent a "95% accuracy" metric; if the failure mode is "dismissing trauma," the residual risk is critical and unverified until these specific probes pass consistently.

**4. Implement Defined Uncertainty Tiers (MANAGE)**
Stop the bot from presenting guesses as facts.
*   **Remove False Precision:** Ensure the bot never claims certainty about the nature of an interpersonal conflict.
*   **Disclosure:** Add a clear disclaimer at the start of every session: "I am an AI assistant, not a counselor or legal expert. My responses are generated automatically and may be incorrect. Do not rely on me for crisis support."
*   **Override Mechanism:** Make it trivial for users to bypass the bot and reach a human instantly.

**5. Governance and Transparency**
*   **Owner Assignment:** Name one person on your five-person team as the owner of this specific harm (gaslighting). They are responsible for reviewing any changes to the prompt or data before re-testing.

List the intended use (general wellness), the known limitation (fails on trauma/harassment), and the results of your manual probes. Mark any untested scenarios as "UNVERIFIED." Do not certify the system as "unbiased" or "safe."

**6. Legal Routing**
The question of whether this bot constitutes a regulated automated decision under employment law or requires specific legal disclosures is a determination for counsel. Route this immediately to your legal team; do not assert compliance internally.
