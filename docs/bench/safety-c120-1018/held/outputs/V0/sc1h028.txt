Since you lack diverse real-world data, you cannot measure real-world false positive rates. Any metric derived solely from your limited synthetic library must be labeled **UNVERIFIED** regarding real-world performance. Do not fabricate an accuracy percentage. Instead, execute the following:

2.  **Positive Control:** Ensure your synthetic "distress" samples are distinct and verified.
3.  **Metric & Threshold:** Measure the model's failure rate on the negative control set.

4.  **Injection & Leakage Check:** Test if specific background noises (e.g., a specific cartoon theme song) consistently trigger false positives, indicating the model is latching onto irrelevant artifacts rather than distress patterns.
