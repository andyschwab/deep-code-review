### Pre-Deployment Review Process: StreamManila Content Recommendation

Given the urgent two-week timeline and the known limitation of your sentiment analysis tool (inability to distinguish news reporting from propaganda), you cannot rely on automated certification of safety. Instead, implement a **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) gate** specifically for high-risk content clusters.

**1. Immediate Harm Inventory & Triage**
Before deploying the patch, document the specific failure mode: "System amplifies extremist ideology to vulnerable users due to inability to differentiate news reporting from propaganda." Define the worst-case impact as accelerated radicalization in the region. Do not claim the fix eliminates bias; explicitly state that residual risk remains until the model distinction improves.

**2. The "Gray Zone" Human Gate**
Since your tool cannot distinguish between news and propaganda, treat any content flagged by the sentiment model as "potentially extremist" as a **high-stakes automated action**.
*   **Action:** Disable fully autonomous pushing for any video hitting the "extremist" sentiment threshold.
*   **Process:** Route these items to a manual review queue staffed by regional experts who understand the local context of legitimate discourse vs. radicalization.
*   **Rule:** No content in this "gray zone" is recommended to users without explicit human approval. This prevents both under-blocking (pushing harmful content) and over-blocking (stifling legitimate news), as humans can make the nuance distinction the model cannot.

**3. Runnable Measurement Plan (Pre-Launch)**
Do not assert the system is "safe." Instead, run a targeted evaluation before the two-week deadline:
*   **Probe Set:** Curate a dataset of 50 known extremist videos and 50 known news reports covering similar events.
*   **Test:** Run these through your current pipeline.
*   **Success Metric:** The system must correctly identify *all* 50 extremist videos for human review (0% false negatives on the block list). It is acceptable if it also flags the 50 news reports for review (false positives), as the human gate will clear them.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the system fails to flag even one known extremist video for human review, **do not deploy**. The human gate is broken if dangerous content bypasses it.

**4. Transparency & Uncertainty Disclosure**
*   **Internal Card:** Create a one-page "Model Limitation Card" for your team stating: "Sentiment model cannot distinguish news from propaganda. Reliance on HITL gate is mandatory for flagged content."
*   **User Interface:** If a user encounters content that was previously flagged but cleared by humans, consider adding a subtle context label (e.g., "Verified by editorial team") to rebuild trust, though legal counsel should review specific wording.

**5. Governance & Ownership**
Assign a specific product owner responsible for reviewing the human queue metrics daily. If the queue grows too large for timely review, the system must default to **not recommending** the content rather than auto-releasing it. Safety defaults to silence, not potential harm.

**Next Steps:**
Execute the probe test immediately; if the stop condition is triggered, extend the timeline rather than deploying a leaky filter. Route any questions regarding legal liability for radicalization impacts to counsel immediately, as this involves high-stakes regional safety.
