### Product Output Safety Framework: VitalCheck Symptom Checker

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before launch, explicitly document the following risks for the "young female" segment:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The model systematically downgrades atypical stroke symptoms (e.g., fatigue, nausea) common in young women compared to classic male-presenting symptoms.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The system invents medical authority for unverified claims or suggests definitive diagnoses where only probability exists.
*   **Unsafe Automation:** The system fails to trigger a human review or emergency warning for high-stakes, irreversible outcomes (missing a stroke).
*   **Over-reliance:** Users trust the "low risk" output without seeking human care due to confident phrasing.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Do not assert safety; measure residual risk. Execute one test row per harm:
*   **Probe Set:** Construct adversarial inputs describing subtle/atypical stroke symptoms specifically reported by young females (e.g., "sudden extreme fatigue," "hiccups," "confusion without weakness").
    *   *Positive Control:* Inputs known to be severe strokes that must trigger emergency escalation.
    *   *Negative Control:* Inputs known to be benign that should not trigger escalation.
*   **Metric:** False Negative Rate (FNR) for severe conditions within the young female subgroup.
*   **Threshold & Stop Condition:** Set owner-defined tolerance (recommend near-zero for missed strokes). If the FNR exceeds this threshold, **block launch** regardless of the campaign date.
*   **Injection & Leakage Checks:** Test if user-supplied text can manipulate the triage logic (prompt injection) or if the system leaks private health data in its reasoning.
*   **Reporting:** Report the measured FNR. If unmeasured, label the risk **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a "99% accurate" claim.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** Any output suggesting "low risk" for symptoms matching high-severity patterns must be gated behind human clinical review before final delivery, or the system must default to "Seek Emergency Care" when uncertainty is detected. Fully autonomous dismissal of potential stroke symptoms in this demographic is a failure condition.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** Do not display raw model confidence scores (e.g., "87% sure"). Instead, use defined coarse tiers (e.g., **"Confirmed Pattern," "Corroborated," "Single Source," "Unverified – Seek Human Review"**). Publish the definition of each tier.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Ship a **Model Card** and **Data Card**. These must state:
    *   Intended use and explicit out-of-scope uses.
    *   Measured performance **by subgroup** (specifically young females), not just aggregate.
    *   Known limitations regarding atypical symptom presentation.
    *   Data provenance and consent status.
    *   *Finding:* Shipping without these cards is a transparency failure.

**4. Governance & Legal Routing (GOVERN)**
*   **Ownership:** Name a specific owner responsible for re-reviewing the model if the data or underlying algorithm changes.
*   **Legal Determination:** Whether this tool constitutes a regulated medical device or requires specific legal disclosures under regimes like the EU AI Act is a legal determination. **Route this immediately to counsel.** Do not assert compliance here; counsel must name the binding regime and deadlines.

**Definition of Done:**
The feature ships only when the harm inventory is complete, the high-stakes "missed stroke" scenario has a human gate or failsafe, the subgroup evals are run with reported residual risk (no fabricated metrics), and the Model/Data cards are published. If the fixed launch date prevents meeting these controls, the launch must be delayed or the feature scope reduced to non-high-stakes guidance.

*Note: This framework identifies risks and controls; it does not certify the system as "safe" or "unbiased." The final ship decision rests with the product owner.*
