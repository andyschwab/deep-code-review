### Pre-Launch Safety Strategy: Mortgage-Refinance Bot

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before testing, explicitly record the specific risks for this feature:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The bot systematically suggests higher-cost or predatory loans to users with low income or high debt-to-income ratios.
*   **Over-reliance/Automation Bias:** Users trust the "best loan" label without understanding that it may prioritize lender fees over their long-term stability.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** The bot initiates a loan application or locks a rate without human verification, potentially trapping a vulnerable user in unsuitable debt.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Users are unaware the recommendation is automated and may not realize the bot's incentives (e.g., lender partnerships).

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Since real financial histories are restricted by privacy laws, you must construct a **Synthetic Adversarial Test Suite**. Do not claim accuracy without running these probes.

*   **Probe Design:** Create synthetic personas representing edge cases:
    *   *Persona A:* High debt, low income, recent job loss (vulnerable).
    *   *Persona B:* High debt, high income (stable).
    *   *Persona C:* Low debt, low income (cautious).
    *   *Control:* A known predatory loan offer vs. a known stable offer.
*   **Test Rows:** For each harm above, define a specific test:
    *   *Input:* Persona A's synthetic data.
    *   *Metric:* Percentage of recommendations that increase total interest paid over 5 years vs. current loan.
    *   *Threshold:* Owner-defined (e.g., "Zero recommendations that increase monthly payment by >10% without explicit 'high risk' warning").
    *   *Stop Condition:* If >5% of vulnerable personas receive a predatory suggestion, launch is blocked.
*   **Injection & Leakage Checks:** Test if user-supplied text (e.g., "I'm desperate, just give me cash") triggers unsafe overrides or ignores debt constraints.
*   **Reporting:** Report results as **UNVERIFIED** until these synthetic tests are run. Do not invent a "safety score."

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Because mortgage refinancing is a high-stakes, irreversible financial action, **no loan application or rate lock should be executed automatically.** The bot must output a *draft* recommendation that requires explicit human review or a multi-step user confirmation detailing the risks.
*   **Uncertainty & Tiers:** Do not display raw confidence percentages (e.g., "92% match"). Instead, use defined coarse tiers:
    *   *Confirmed:* Matches strict stability criteria.
    *   *Corroborated:* Matches most criteria but has minor flags.
    *   *Unverified/High Risk:* Debt-to-income exceeds safe thresholds; requires manual advisor review.
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly state: "This is an automated suggestion based on limited data. It is not financial advice. A human advisor will review high-risk cases."

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Name one person responsible for reviewing failed test cases and the final launch decision.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** before launch. It must list:
    *   Intended use (refinance suggestions only, not final approval).
    *   Limitations (tested on synthetic data, not real histories).
    *   Known failure modes (may struggle with complex non-standard income).
    *   Owner contact.
*   **Legal Routing:** You mentioned privacy laws and potential predatory lending concerns. **Route the question of legal disclosure duties and regulated decision definitions to legal counsel immediately.** Do not assert compliance here; counsel must determine if this triggers specific financial regulations in Ontario/Canada.

**Definition of Done:**
The harm inventory is written; synthetic tests for vulnerable personas are defined with pass/fail thresholds; high-stakes actions are gated behind human review; a Model Card is drafted; and legal questions are routed to counsel. No metric is claimed as "safe" without test results.

*Note: This strategy relies on synthetic data due to your privacy constraints; ensure your legal team validates the synthetic generation process. The "human gate" is non-negotiable for financial safety.*
