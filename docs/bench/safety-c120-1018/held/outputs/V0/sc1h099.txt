### Ethical Guardrails for Funeral-Cost Estimator

**Immediate Implementation Rules**
1.  **Static Baseline Cap:** Prices must never exceed the standard market rate for a specific service package, regardless of demand spikes. Dynamic pricing algorithms must be disabled for upward adjustments; they may only lower prices during low-demand periods.
2.  **Vulnerability Shield:** Implement a hard rule that prohibits any price variation based on time-of-day, holidays, or immediate availability during the first 48 hours of a bereavement event. All quotes within this window must reflect a fixed "compassionate rate."
3.  **Transparency Disclosure:** Every quote must explicitly state: "This price is fixed and will not increase due to demand or timing." Do not use dynamic language like "current rate" or "limited availability."
4.  **Human Override Gate:** Any quote generated above a predefined threshold (e.g., 10% above the baseline average) must trigger a mandatory human review before being presented to the user. Fully automated high-cost quotes are prohibited.

**Testing Procedure Using Historical Data**
1.  **Adversarial Simulation:** Run the pricing engine against historical data points representing peak demand (e.g., holiday weekends, flu season spikes). Verify that the output price remains identical to the baseline static price.
2.  **Edge Case Probing:** Input scenarios with extreme constraints (e.g., "immediate service needed," "specific high-demand item out of stock") to ensure the algorithm does not apply retail-style surge multipliers.
3.  **Subgroup Analysis:** Segment historical data by different service types and times. Confirm that no specific demographic or timing segment receives systematically higher quotes than the baseline.
4.  **Fail-Safe Validation:** Intentionally feed the system corrupted or extreme demand inputs to verify that the "Static Baseline Cap" rule holds and the system defaults to the human-review gate rather than generating an inflated quote.

**Residual Risk Note:** These controls mitigate exploitation risks, but the underlying retail-derived algorithm requires continuous monitoring for unintended bias.
**Legal Note:** Consult local counsel regarding Tokyo consumer protection laws and regulations on automated decision-making in sensitive sectors before launch.
