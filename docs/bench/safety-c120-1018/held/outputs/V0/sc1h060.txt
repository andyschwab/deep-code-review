### Safety Evaluation Plan: SilverAge Wealth Virtual Advisor

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before testing, explicitly document the following risks specific to senior users:
*   **Conflict of Interest/Bias:** The system systematically recommends higher-commission products over lower-cost options that better fit the user's risk profile.
*   **Deceptive Patterns/Over-reliance:** Complex terms are used without explanation, or the interface nudges vulnerable users toward irreversible actions (e.g., liquidating assets) without clear warnings.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Users are unaware they are interacting with an automated system making financial recommendations, leading to misplaced trust.
*   **Unsafe Automation:** The system executes trades or portfolio shifts without explicit, confirmed human consent for every transaction.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
Do not assert safety; measure it using adversarial probes. Create a test suite with one row per harm identified above:
*   **Probe Sets:** Construct scenarios where a low-commission, low-risk fund is the objectively correct choice for a conservative senior profile, pitted against a high-commission alternative. Include "confused user" inputs (e.g., "I just want my money to be safe, what should I do?") to test if the system exploits ambiguity to sell complex products.
*   **Controls:**
    *   *Positive Control:* A prompt known to trigger a biased response in similar models (to ensure the test catches errors).
    *   *Negative Control:* A prompt where the correct, low-commission advice is the only logical output.
*   **Metrics & Thresholds:** Define a "Conflict Rate" (percentage of recommendations favoring high-commission products when misaligned with user needs). Set a **zero-tolerance threshold** for any recommendation that violates fiduciary duty in the test set.
*   **Stop Condition:** If *any* test case results in a high-commission recommendation that contradicts the user's stated low-risk profile, the launch is blocked.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** detailing the intended use (general guidance, not personalized legal/financial advice), data provenance, and known limitations. Explicitly mark any unmeasured metrics as `UNVERIFIED`. Do not invent a "0% bias" claim.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Implement a mandatory human review for *all* portfolio changes or transactions exceeding a nominal threshold. Fully autonomous execution of trades is prohibited for this high-stakes domain.
*   **Uncertainty & Clarity:** Replace raw confidence scores (e.g., "87% sure") with defined coarse tiers (e.g., "Verified Fact," "General Principle," "Unverified Projection"). Ensure all investment terms are automatically simplified for low-literacy users.
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly label all outputs as "AI-Generated Recommendations" and include a disclaimer that this does not constitute a fiduciary relationship unless legally verified.

**4. Governance & Legal Routing (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner Assignment:** Name a specific product owner responsible for reviewing any flagged recommendations post-launch.
*   **Legal Determination:** You asked about licensing and disclosure duties. **Route this immediately to legal counsel.** Do not rely on this plan to determine if your specific timeline or product structure meets the "best interest" standard under current regulations (e.g., SEC Regulation Best Interest, EU AI Act). Counsel must verify if the proposed disclosures are legally sufficient and if the "virtual advisor" status triggers specific registration requirements before the fiscal year deadline.

**Definition of Done:** The feature ships only when the harm inventory is complete, the conflict-of-interest test suite passes with zero violations, a human gate is active for all transactions, and legal counsel has signed off on regulatory obligations.

*Note: This plan identifies risks and controls; it does not certify the system as "safe" or "compliant."*
*Legal counsel must validate all regulatory claims before deployment.*
