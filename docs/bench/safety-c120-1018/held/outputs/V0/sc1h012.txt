### Product Output Safety Analysis: ScholarStream Automated Grading

**Status: UNVERIFIED – Do not launch without the following controls.**

Your request to "generate a checklist to identify bias" and "prepare a report" cannot be fulfilled as a certification of safety. No static checklist proves a system is unbiased, and asserting a "safe" launch date for a high-stakes feature (grading affecting professional certification) without measured evidence is a critical failure mode. Below is the required harm inventory and immediate action plan based on your constraints.

#### 1. Harm Inventory (MAP)
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The model may systematically downgrade non-native dialects or specific linguistic patterns not present in its training data, causing qualified candidates to fail certification.
    *   *Worst Case:* Systemic exclusion of specific geographic regions from professional licensure; legal liability for discriminatory hiring barriers.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The tool may fabricate specific grammatical errors or content mismatches to justify a low score.
    *   *Worst Case:* Students receive incorrect feedback they cannot contest, leading to unjust failure.
*   **Over-reliance:** Instructors may accept the automated score without reviewing the essay, trusting the "objective" algorithm over human nuance.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Students may not know their career-defining exam was graded by an AI with known dialect limitations.

#### 2. Immediate Measurement Plan (MEASURE)
You cannot claim a bias rate without running tests. Since you cannot retrain models, you must measure residual risk before launch.
*   **Adversarial Probe Set:** Construct a test set of 50 essays known to be high-quality but containing specific non-native dialect markers (positive controls) and 50 standard dialect essays (negative controls).
*   **Metric:** Disparity in pass rates between the two groups.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the pass rate disparity exceeds 0% (zero tolerance for gated irreversible actions like certification failure), the automated grading for those segments must be disabled.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** You must draft a **Model Card** stating: "Intended for draft feedback only; not validated for non-native dialects; high risk of false negatives for [specific regions]." Shipping without this is a transparency failure.

#### 3. Risk Management & Human Gate (MANAGE)
*   **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate:** Because failing a certification is a high-stakes, hard-to-reverse action, **fully automated scoring must not ship.**
    *   *Required Control:* Any score below a passing threshold, or any essay flagged with low confidence, must be routed to a human grader before the student sees the result.
    *   *Constraint Check:* Your legacy infrastructure prevents retraining, but it does not excuse removing the human gate. If you cannot staff the human review for thousands of students, you must delay the launch or limit the tool to "formative feedback only" (no pass/fail decision).
*   **Uncertainty Display:** Do not show a precise score (e.g., "87/100"). Display coarse tiers: "Proficient," "Needs Review," or "Insufficient Evidence," accompanied by a disclaimer that the evaluation is automated and subject to dialect bias.
*   **Disclosure:** Explicitly inform students: "This evaluation is generated by an AI system known to have limitations with non-native English dialects. All failing grades undergo human review."

#### 4. Governance (GOVERN)
*   **Owner:** One Product Manager must be named as the owner of the "Dialect Bias" risk.
*   **Re-review:** Any change to the prompt or model version requires re-running the adversarial probe set.

**Critical Finding:** Your proposal to "manually review borderline cases" is insufficient if the system automatically fails students before they reach the "borderline" threshold due to dialect bias. The human gate must apply to *all* negative decisions, not just borderline ones, until bias is measured and mitigated.

**Note:** Legal requirements for automated decision-making in certification (EU AI Act, etc.) must be routed to counsel immediately; this analysis does not determine legal compliance.
**Note:** No metric for bias or hallucination rate is provided here because none was measured; any number you invent is a fabrication.
