**Risk Assessment Framework & Monitoring Strategy for Credit-Limit Adjustment Engine**

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before launch, explicitly record these specific risks for the elderly segment:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Spending patterns (e.g., reduced mobility leading to fewer transactions) may be misinterpreted as financial distress, systematically disadvantaging seniors despite solvency.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** Silently reducing limits on accounts used for essentials (medicine, food) constitutes an irreversible, high-stakes action with potential for immediate human harm.
*   **Missing Disclosure/Deceptive Pattern:** The "silent" nature of the decision prevents users from understanding why their purchasing power vanished, creating a deceptive user experience and eroding trust.
*   **Over-reliance:** The system assumes internal transaction history is a sufficient proxy for creditworthiness without demographic context, inviting automation bias where the algorithm's output is treated as absolute truth.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Do not assert safety; measure residual risk using the following evals on your 5% soft launch cohort:
*   **Probe Set:** Isolate the "elderly" segment (proxy via account age/spending categories if demographics are missing). Run adversarial tests: simulate stable-income seniors with changing spending habits (e.g., seasonal changes, health events).
*   **Controls:**
    *   *Positive Control:* Known vulnerable accounts that should *not* have limits cut (manual review baseline).
    *   *Negative Control:* Accounts with genuine risk signals that *should* trigger adjustment.
*   **Metrics & Thresholds:**
    *   *False Positive Rate (FPR) on Essentials:* Measure % of cuts affecting merchants categorized as "Pharmacy," "Grocery," or "Utilities." **Stop Condition:** If FPR > 0% for this category in the soft launch, halt expansion immediately.
    *   *Distress Signal:* Monitor customer support tickets and call volume from the 5% cohort within 48 hours of a limit change. **Stop Condition:** Spike in "unable to purchase" complaints triggers immediate rollback.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** documenting the lack of demographic data as a known limitation, the specific logic used for "spending patterns," and the intended use (risk mitigation, not punitive action). Mark any bias metrics as **UNVERIFIED** due to data gaps.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate:** **Immediate Requirement:** Remove "silent" automation for any limit reduction exceeding a specific threshold (e.g., >20%) or affecting accounts with recent essential-spending activity. These cases must route to a human agent for review before execution. Full autonomous execution of high-stakes financial cuts is the failure mode to prevent.
*   **Uncertainty & Override:** Do not present the decision as a precise score. If communicating with the user, use defined tiers (e.g., "Review Required" vs. "Auto-Adjusted") rather than implying false precision. Provide an immediate, easy-to-access channel for users to request a manual review and temporary limit restoration.
*   **Disclosure:** Even if not legally asserted here, implement a proactive notification system. Users must be informed *before* or *immediately upon* a limit change, explaining the general reason (e.g., "unusual spending activity") and how to appeal.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific product owner responsible for reviewing the daily "distress signal" metrics during the soft launch.
*   **Re-review Trigger:** Any change in the transaction data schema or the adjustment algorithm requires a re-run of the harm inventory and evals.
*   **Legal Routing:** Because this involves automated consequential decisions affecting credit and vulnerable populations, route the question of regulatory compliance (e.g., adverse action notice requirements) to legal counsel immediately. Do not rely on internal assumptions about "silent" adjustments.

**Note:** The lack of demographic data makes bias measurement inherently difficult; treat any claim of "fairness" as unverified until proxy methods are validated. The "silent" nature of the current design is a critical vulnerability that must be addressed via notification and HITL gates before any launch.
