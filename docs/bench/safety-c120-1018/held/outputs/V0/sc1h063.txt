### Product Output Safety Review: Vertex Jobs Career-Matching Platform

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before launch, explicitly record the following potential harms for your recommendation engine:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The system systematically steers specific demographic groups (e.g., by gender, age, or ethnicity) away from high-paying or leadership roles based on historical browsing patterns that reflect societal biases.
*   **Over-reliance/Automation Bias:** Users accept the limited list of recommendations as the *only* viable options, missing opportunities outside the algorithm's narrow scope.
*   **Missing AI Disclosure:** Users are unaware that their job feed is curated by an automated system, leading to misplaced trust in the "completeness" of the results.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** The platform automatically applies to jobs or hides certain categories without explicit user consent, irreversibly altering a user's career trajectory.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
Since you cannot retrain the model immediately, you must rigorously test the current outputs. Do not assert safety; measure residual risk.

*   **Adversarial Probe Sets:** Construct synthetic user profiles that differ *only* by protected demographic attributes (e.g., "Candidate A" vs. "Candidate B" with identical skills/experience but different inferred demographics).
*   **Test Rows per Harm:**
    *   *Probe:* Run 1,000 paired profile tests across key industries (Tech, Healthcare, Trades).
    *   *Positive Control:* A profile known to match a specific role regardless of demographics.
    *   *Negative Control:* A profile clearly unqualified for a role.
    *   *Metric:* Disparity in recommendation scores or ranking positions between paired profiles.
    *   *Threshold:* Define a maximum acceptable variance (e.g., <5% difference in top-10 results). **Stop Condition:** If variance exceeds the threshold for any protected group, block the beta launch for that segment.
*   **Injection & Leakage Checks:** Test if user prompts or browsing history can inject biased constraints (e.g., "show me jobs for young people") or leak private data from other users.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** for the beta. It must state:
    *   *Intended Use:* Job discovery assistance, not definitive career counseling.
    *   *Limitations:* Explicitly note the inability to retrain on recent historical data due to privacy constraints and the potential for inherited bias.
    *   *Performance:* Report results from your probe tests by subgroup. If a metric is unmeasured, label it **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a "0% bias" claim.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** For any irreversible action (e.g., auto-submitting applications), require explicit user confirmation. Do not allow fully autonomous execution of high-stakes actions during beta.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** Do not show raw confidence scores (e.g., "92% match"). Instead, use defined coarse tiers like "Strong Match," "Potential Fit," or "Exploratory," with clear definitions for each.
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly label the feed as "AI-Recommended" to manage user expectations and mitigate automation bias.
*   **Legal Routing:** Whether your automated decisions trigger specific regulatory obligations (e.g., under the EU AI Act or local labor laws) is a legal determination. Route this question to counsel immediately; do not assume compliance based on this review.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
Assign a specific owner for each identified harm (e.g., a Product Manager for bias monitoring). Mandate a re-review if the recommendation logic, data source, or user base changes significantly post-beta.

**Note:** This review identifies risks and controls; it does not certify the system as "safe" or "unbiased." The decision to ship rests with your leadership team based on the residual risk reported above.
