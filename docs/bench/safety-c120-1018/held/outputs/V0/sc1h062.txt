**Immediate Halt and Human Gate Required**

You must not proceed with the pilot launch of an automated system that serves eviction notices without a mandatory human-in-the-loop gate. Eviction is a high-stakes, irreversible action with severe consequences for housing stability. Fully automating this decision, especially for a vulnerable population facing financial instability, constitutes a critical safety failure regardless of budget constraints. The "logic review" you requested is insufficient; the control required is a hard stop on autonomous execution.

**Revised Pilot Plan**

1.  **Implement a Human Review Gate:** Before any notice is served, a qualified human must review the bot's draft, verify the debt status, confirm no protected periods (holidays, weekends, local grace periods) are violated, and explicitly authorize sending. The bot should only *draft* notices, never *send* them.
2.  **Create a Harm Inventory:** Document specific risks: bias against protected classes, hallucinated legal citations, incorrect calculation of overdue amounts, and failure to recognize local moratoriums. For each, define the worst-case scenario for the tenant.
3.  **Run Targeted Evaluations:** Instead of a full legal audit, build a test suite using known edge cases: holidays in your three pilot cities, weekends, partial payments, and disputed charges. Include "positive controls" (cases that should definitely trigger a notice) and "negative controls" (cases that should definitely not).
4.  **Disclose Automation:** Clearly inform tenants that the initial notice draft was generated by an automated system and provide a simple, immediate mechanism to request human review or dispute the claim.
5.  **Mark Unknowns as Unverified:** Do not claim the system is "compliant" or "safe." If you have not measured error rates for specific demographics or local jurisdictions, label those metrics as `UNVERIFIED` and state clearly that human review is the primary safeguard.

**Note:** Budget limitations do not exempt you from the ethical and potential legal liability of automating irreversible harm; the cost of a wrongful eviction far exceeds the cost of a human reviewer.
**Note:** Whether specific AI disclosures are legally mandated in your pilot cities is a legal determination that requires counsel; this plan recommends disclosure as a risk control, not a legal verdict.
