**Immediate Safety Directive: Do Not Launch with DIY Bleaching Instructions**

You cannot safely launch a feature generating DIY bleaching recipes for everyday consumers, even with a filtration plan. Bleaching involves high-risk chemicals (peroxide, ammonia) where minor errors in concentration, timing, or scalp condition cause irreversible physical harm (chemical burns, hair loss, blindness). An AI generating these instructions creates an unacceptable "unsafe high-stakes automation" risk. No post-generation filter can guarantee safety because the context required to judge safety (scalp integrity, previous chemical history, ventilation, exact product purity) is unavailable to the model.

**Required Action Plan:**

1.  **Stop Generation of High-Risk Procedures:** Immediately disable the generation of any instructions involving mixed chemicals, bleaching agents, or high-heat tools. This is a hard gate, not a tunable parameter. The "creative spirit" must be preserved through color visualization, style recommendations, and product suggestions, not by instructing users to perform dangerous chemical reactions at home.
2.  **Implement a Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** For any feature that could influence physical safety, you must have a human expert (licensed cosmetologist) review and approve the logic or specific outputs before they reach the user. Automated decisions on physical safety are prohibited for high-stakes actions.
3.  **Reframe the Feature Scope:** Pivot the beta to "Style Consultation" only.
    *   **Allowed:** Analyzing photos to suggest cuts, colors (hex codes), and styling techniques.
    *   **Allowed:** Recommending professional salon services or boxed dyes with explicit links to manufacturer safety warnings.
    *   **Prohibited:** Step-by-step mixing instructions, timing guides for chemical processing, or "hacks" involving household chemicals.
4.  **Draft a Transparency Artifact (Model Card):** Before beta, publish a document stating:
    *   **Intended Use:** Visual style inspiration and product recommendation only.
    *   **Limitations:** The system does not assess scalp health or chemical compatibility; it is not a substitute for professional advice.
    *   **Known Risks:** Explicitly state that attempting chemical treatments based on AI text carries risk of injury.
5.  **Address the Legal Constraint:** The unavailability of your legal team is a critical blocker, not a reason to proceed without them. Generating safety-critical advice without legal review of liability and disclosure requirements violates basic governance. You must delay the launch of any advice-giving feature until counsel reviews the disclaimer and scope. If you proceed without this, you are assuming full liability for any injury caused by the tool's output.

**Measurement & Verification:**
Do not claim a "0% harm rate." Instead, run adversarial tests where prompts explicitly ask for dangerous variations (e.g., "leave bleach on longer," "mix with vinegar"). If the system generates *any* actionable dangerous step, the fail condition is met, and the feature must not ship.

**Note:** Launching this feature as described exposes StyleNova to severe liability and physical harm claims. The only safe path is to remove the capability to generate chemical instructions entirely.
**Note:** "Filtering" is insufficient for high-stakes physical risks; the capability itself must be removed or strictly gated behind human expert review.
