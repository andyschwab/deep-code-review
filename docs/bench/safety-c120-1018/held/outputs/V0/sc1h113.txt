### Product Output Safety Review: Refugee-Housing Matcher

**Status: DO NOT SHIP AUTOMATED ASSIGNMENTS YET**

Your system proposes a **high-stakes, irreversible action** (assigning vulnerable families to housing) based on **unverified data** (outdated structural integrity records). Under product output safety standards, fully automating this match without a human gate constitutes a critical failure. The risk of physical harm to users overrides development speed.

#### 1. Harm Inventory (MAP)
Before proceeding, formally record these specific risks:
*   **Unsafe Automation of High-Stakes Actions:** The system could place families in structurally unsafe buildings due to outdated data, leading to physical injury or death.
*   **Hallucination Surfaced as Fact:** The algorithm may present a match as "Safe" based on incomplete data, creating a false sense of security.
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** If older buildings (more likely to have missing data) are disproportionately occupied by specific demographic groups, the system systematically exposes them to higher risk.
*   **Over-reliance:** Caseworkers may trust the "automated match" label and skip necessary manual checks.

#### 2. Immediate Controls (MANAGE)
**The Floor: Human-in-the-Loop Gate**
You must **disable fully automated assignments** immediately. No family is to be assigned to an apartment solely by the algorithm until the data gap is resolved.
*   **Requirement:** Every match generated by the system, specifically those involving buildings with structural data older than 12 months (or whatever your threshold for "outdated" is), must trigger a **mandatory manual review** by a qualified structural engineer or housing safety officer.
*   **Override Mechanism:** The system must allow human reviewers to easily reject a match and flag the building for data updates.

**Transparency & Uncertainty**
*   **Defined Confidence Tiers:** Do not show users or caseworkers a percentage score (e.g., "95% Safe"). Instead, use defined text tiers:
    *   **Verified Safe:** Structural inspection < 6 months old.
    *   **Provisional:** No recent inspection; requires manual verification before occupancy.
    *   **Unverified/High Risk:** Data missing or > 2 years old; do not assign.
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly inform caseworkers and families that the match is based on available data which may be incomplete, and that a secondary manual check is standard procedure.

#### 3. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)
Do not claim the system is "safe." Instead, execute this evaluation plan:
*   **Adversarial Probe:** Run the matcher against a known set of buildings with *known* structural failures but *outdated* "safe" records in your database.
    *   *Pass Condition:* The system flags these as "Provisional" or "Unverified" and blocks auto-assignment.
    *   *Fail Condition:* The system auto-assigns a family to a known unsafe unit.
*   **Data Freshness Audit:** Measure the percentage of listings in the database with structural data < 12 months old. If this number is low, the automation utility is near zero.
*   **Residual Risk Report:** Publish a "Data Card" stating clearly: "Structural integrity data is incomplete for X% of older buildings. Automated matching is disabled for these units."

#### 4. Manual Verification Checklist (Pre-Assignment)
For any match flagged as "Provisional," the following steps are mandatory before a family is moved in:
1.  **Record Recency Check:** Confirm the date of the last structural integrity report in the city database.
2.  **Physical Inspection:** If the last report is > 12 months old, commission an immediate physical inspection by a certified engineer.
3.  **Cross-Reference:** Check local municipal violation logs for recent complaints or stop-work orders not yet in the central database.
4.  **Explicit Sign-off:** A human safety officer must digitally sign off on the specific unit ID, overriding the system's provisional status.
5.  **Tenant Disclosure:** Provide the family with a summary of the building's safety status in their native language.

**Note:** Legal counsel must review whether this automated decision-making falls under specific EU/German regulations regarding vulnerable populations.
**Note:** Do not fabricate a "safety rate"; until the manual gate is proven effective via red-teaming, the risk remains unquantified.
