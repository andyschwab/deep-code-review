### Safety Assessment Plan: Sizing Recommender (Beta)

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before launch, explicitly record the following specific risks for your beta:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcome:** Systematic recommendation of tight/unsafe fits for plus-size users due to data scarcity.
*   **Physical Harm/Discomfort:** Suggestions ignoring fabric stretch limits or body shape constraints, leading to restricted movement, skin irritation, or circulation issues.
*   **Over-reliance:** Users trusting the "recommended size" without manual verification, assuming the AI understands comfort.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Users unaware the model is less reliable for plus-size bodies due to limited training data.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
Do not assert safety; test specifically for the gaps in your dataset.
*   **Adversarial Probe Set:** Create a test suite of 50+ synthetic or held-out plus-size profiles varying in shape (apple, pear, hourglass) and specific measurements where fabric tension is critical (chest, hips, thighs).
*   **Positive Control:** Inputs where the correct output is clearly "Size Up" or "Do Not Recommend" based on known fabric limits.
*   **Negative Control:** Standard sizes with ample data where the model should perform normally.
*   **Metric & Threshold:** Measure the "Unsafe Recommendation Rate" (instances suggesting a fit tighter than the fabric's known stretch limit).
    *   *Stop Condition:* If the Unsafe Recommendation Rate for plus-size segments exceeds **0%**, block the automated suggestion for that segment and force a human review or a generic "consult size chart" fallback.
*   **Fabric Logic Check:** Manually verify that the system ingests fabric composition (e.g., 100% cotton vs. elastane blend). If the model cannot distinguish stretch capacity, it must not output a specific size fitment for high-risk categories.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** For any user identified as falling into the under-represented plus-size segments, disable fully automated "Buy This Size" actions. Instead, route the recommendation to a human stylist review or present a range (e.g., "Likely L or XL") with a mandatory disclaimer.
*   **Defined Uncertainty Tiers:** Do not show a confidence percentage (e.g., "92% match"). Use coarse, defined text tiers:
    *   *Verified Fit:* Supported by ample data.
    *   *Estimated Fit:* Limited data; check fabric details.
    *   *Unverified:* Insufficient data for this body shape; consult our team.
*   **Explicit Disclosure:** Clearly state on the UI: "Our beta model has limited data for plus-size body types. Recommendations are estimates; please verify against our detailed size guide."

**4. Governance & Artifacts**
*   **Owner:** Assign one team member as the "Safety Owner" responsible for reviewing every failure in the probe set before launch.
*   **Transparency Artifact (Model Card):** Publish a brief internal/external note stating: "Intended Use: Fashion sizing. Limitations: Known data gap for plus-size body types; performance not verified for this segment. Mitigation: Human review gate active for affected users."
*   **Legal Routing:** Consult counsel regarding whether this constitutes a regulated automated decision or requires specific consumer protection disclosures in the EU/Italy given the physical harm potential.

**Status:** The system is **UNVERIFIED** for plus-size safety until the probe set runs and the stop condition is met. Do not fabricate a safety rate.

*Note: This plan addresses product behavior; it does not certify the code is bug-free or legally compliant.*
*Re-evaluate this inventory immediately if the dataset or model architecture changes before Fashion Week.*
