### Wedding Planner AI: Vendor Diversity & Inclusion Validation Checklist

**Objective:** Validate that vendor suggestions remain diverse and inclusive despite relying on inferred religious affiliations from unstructured text, ensuring compliance with anti-discrimination norms before the six-week launch.

**Phase 1: Harm Inventory & Inference Audit (Days 1–3)**
*   [ ] **Define "Exclusion" Scenarios:** Explicitly list how a couple's preference (e.g., "secular ceremony") could incorrectly filter out a vendor who is actually inclusive but described with ambiguous terms (e.g., "traditional," "family-oriented").
*   [ ] **Sample the Inference Logic:** Manually review the raw text descriptions for 20 random vendors across different categories (florists, venues, caterers). Compare the AI's inferred tag against the actual text.
*   [ ] **Identify High-Risk Keywords:** Create a "watch list" of ambiguous terms in the legacy database that frequently trigger false religious inferences (e.g., specific holiday names used decoratively vs. religiously).

**Phase 2: Top 100 Vendor Manual Verification (Days 4–10)**
*   [ ] **Blind Ground-Truthing:** Assign two team members to independently review the top 100 vendors' public websites and social media (ignoring the AI's inference). Label them as "Confirmed Religious," "Confirmed Secular," or "Ambiguous/Neutral."
*   [ ] **Discrepancy Resolution:** Compare the human labels against the AI's inferred tags. Flag any vendor where the AI assigned a specific religious affiliation that the human reviewers marked as "Ambiguous" or "Secular."
*   [ ] **Diversity Stress Test:** Simulate 10 distinct user personas with varying cultural and religious preferences (including "no preference"). Run these against the top 100 list.
    *   *Pass Condition:* No single demographic group sees a suggestion list where >80% of vendors come from a single inferred background unless explicitly requested.
*   [ ] **False Negative Check:** Specifically search for qualified vendors who were *excluded* from suggestion lists due to inferred religious mismatches but are actually inclusive upon manual review.

**Phase 3: Mitigation & Human-in-the-Loop Gates (Days 11–14)**
*   [ ] **Implement "Uncertain" Tier:** For any vendor where the text description is ambiguous, force the system to label the religious attribute as "Unverified" rather than inferring a specific tag.
*   [ ] **Mandatory Disclosure:** Add a visible note to user interfaces stating: *"Vendor details are based on self-descriptions and may not reflect full inclusivity. Please confirm directly with the vendor."*
*   [ ] **High-Stakes Override:** If a user filter results in fewer than 5 viable vendors in a category, automatically disable the religious/cultural inference filter and show all available options with a warning.
*   [ ] **Final Sign-off:** Document the residual risk of the remaining unstructured data. Do not claim the system is "bias-free"; instead, record the error rate found in the Top 100 audit.

**Phase 4: Ongoing Monitoring (Pre-Launch to Summer)**
*   [ ] **Feedback Loop:** Create a simple mechanism for users and vendors to report incorrect categorization.
*   [ ] **Weekly Spot Checks:** Randomly audit 10 new vendors added to the database weekly using the Blind Ground-Truthing method.
