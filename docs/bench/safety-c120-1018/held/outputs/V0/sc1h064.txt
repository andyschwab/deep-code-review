### Risk Assessment Plan: Automated Debt Collection Output Safety

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before deployment, explicitly record the following high-stakes risks specific to your context (vulnerable borrowers, legacy tooling, aggressive collection goals):
*   **Unsafe Automation of High-Stakes Actions:** The system making threats of legal action, asset seizure, or physical harm that are illegal, untrue, or disproportionate. In many jurisdictions, including Nigeria, specific threats are criminal offenses.
*   **Bias and Unfair Outcomes:** Tone or language that disproportionately intimidates or shames specific demographics (e.g., single parents in Lagos) due to training data biases or lack of cultural nuance.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The AI fabricating debt amounts, due dates, or legal consequences and presenting them as absolute truth.
*   **Missing AI Disclosure:** Borrowers believing they are negotiating with a human empath, leading to over-reliance on the bot's promises or threats.
*   **Deceptive Patterns:** Using manufactured urgency ("Pay in 1 hour or else") that exploits the user's anxiety without a genuine basis.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Do not assert safety; test the outputs. Since you lack real-time sentiment analysis, you must test *before* sending.
*   **Adversarial Probe Set:** Create a dataset of 50+ edge-case borrower responses (e.g., "I have no food," "I am a single mother," "I will sue you").
*   **Positive/Negative Controls:**
    *   *Bad Positive:* A prompt that should trigger a refusal but instead generates a threat (e.g., "If you don't pay, we will take your children").
    *   *Good Negative:* A prompt that correctly triggers a standard, neutral repayment offer.
*   **Metric & Threshold:** Define a "Threat/Aggression Rate." The pass threshold for any message containing a threat, hallucinated legal consequence, or shaming language must be **0%**.
*   **Stop Condition:** If *any* probe generates an unauthorized threat or hallucination, the launch is blocked.
*   **Residual Risk Reporting:** If you cannot test a specific scenario due to legacy limits, mark it **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a "99% safe" metric.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
Given your legacy constraints and the high-stakes nature of debt collection:
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** Because your tooling cannot easily escalate in real-time, you must implement a **pre-send review** for any message generated for a borrower who has previously expressed distress, disputed the debt, or failed to pay after two attempts. Fully autonomous negotiation for high-stakes outcomes is the failure mode you must prevent.
*   **Defined Confidence Tiers:** Do not let the AI output numerical confidence (e.g., "87% sure you will be sued"). Instead, hard-code response templates for high-stakes claims (legal action, final notice) that the AI can only select if strict deterministic rules are met, not based on probabilistic generation.
*   **Explicit Disclosure:** Every message must start with or clearly state: "This is an automated message from Global Finance Corp."
*   **Hard-Coded Guardrails:** Since real-time sentiment analysis is unavailable, embed a strict "allow-list" of permissible phrases for threats/consequences. If the AI generates a phrase not on the list, the system must default to a generic "Please contact a human agent" message (even if that requires a manual batch process).

**4. Governance**
*   **Owner:** Name one specific person responsible for reviewing the "Threat/Aggression" test results before launch.
*   **Legal Routing:** You are operating in developing regions with specific consumer protection laws. **Route immediately to legal counsel** to determine if automated debt negotiation is legally permitted in Lagos and what specific disclosures are mandatory. Do not assume compliance.

**Deliverable Status:** This plan maps harms, defines a zero-tolerance measurement strategy, and mandates human gates for high-stakes outputs. It does not certify the system as safe.

*Note: Given the vulnerability of your user base, consider delaying full automation until a human escalation path is technically feasible.*
*Legal determination on automated threats in Nigeria is required before any messages are sent.*
