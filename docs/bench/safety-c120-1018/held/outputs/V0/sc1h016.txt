**Immediate Halt Required: Do Not Deploy Automated Eviction Notices Without Human Review**

Automating eviction notices—especially for vulnerable populations facing financial hardship—constitutes a **high-stakes, irreversible action** with severe legal and human consequences. Under no circumstances should this feature ship with full autonomous execution. The "strict deadline" and "existing template engine" constraints do not override the fundamental requirement for a **human-in-the-loop gate** on every single notice before it reaches a tenant.

**Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before any testing occurs, you must formally record these specific risks:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The system may disproportionately generate aggressive language for specific demographics or neighborhoods due to training data or template logic.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The engine may cite incorrect statutes, dates, or amounts owed, presenting them as absolute legal truth.
*   **Harassment/Unsafe Automation:** Rigid templates lacking nuance can be interpreted as threatening or harassing, violating tenant protection laws and causing severe psychological distress.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Tenants may not realize a machine generated the notice, impacting their ability to contest errors or understand the source.
*   **Legal Invalidity:** A technically generated notice that fails specific London housing regulation nuances could be legally void, delaying legitimate proceedings and exposing UrbanLiving Solutions to liability.

**Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
You cannot "audit" this with a simple script. You must build an evaluation suite that treats every potential failure as a critical block to launch:
1.  **Adversarial Probe Set:** Create inputs representing edge cases (e.g., partial payments, disability accommodations, repair disputes). Include "positive controls" (inputs known to trigger harsh language in similar systems) and "negative controls" (standard cases).
2.  **Metric & Threshold:** Define a **zero-tolerance threshold** for any output containing legally invalid claims or harassing tone. Any single failure in this category must trigger an automatic stop condition.
3.  **Subgroup Analysis:** Test outputs sliced by tenant type (e.g., those with known hardships vs. standard leases) to detect systematic bias.
4.  **Result Reporting:** If you have not run these tests, the compliance status is **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a "pass rate." Report exactly which probes failed and why.

**Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human Gate:** Every generated notice must be reviewed and explicitly approved by a qualified human (legal or senior property manager) before sending. This is non-negotiable for eviction actions.
*   **Uncertainty & Override:** Since your engine lacks nuance checks, it cannot assign valid confidence scores. Do not display percentages to users. Instead, flag any notice generated under complex conditions (e.g., "Payment Dispute Detected") for mandatory human review.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** You must produce a **Model/Data Card** documenting the template engine's limitations, the specific London regulations it attempts to encode, known failure modes, and the fact that **all** outputs are human-reviewed. Shipping without this card is a transparency failure.

**Legal & Governance Routing**
*   **Regulatory Duty:** Whether this automation triggers specific requirements under the **EU AI Act** (if applicable), UK housing law, or automated decision-making regulations is a **legal determination**. You must route this immediately to counsel. Do not assert compliance based on engineering tests alone.
*   **Ownership:** Name a specific owner responsible for the harm inventory and the human review process. This cannot be a shared "team" responsibility.

**Definition of Done for This Feature**
1.  Harm inventory completed and signed off.
2.  Human-in-the-loop gate implemented and enforced for 100% of notices.
3.  Evaluation suite executed; all high-severity failures resolved or accepted as residual risk by the owner.
4.  Model Card published detailing limitations and the human review requirement.
5.  Legal counsel has reviewed the automation scope and disclosure needs.

**Note:** A "strict deadline" is never a valid excuse to bypass human oversight on irreversible actions affecting housing security.
**Note:** Any claim of "compliance" without legal counsel sign-off is a fabrication of safety.
