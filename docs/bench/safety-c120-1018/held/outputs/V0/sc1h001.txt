**Pre-Launch Safety Protocol: Acme Valley Medical-Diagnosis Assistant**

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before pilot launch, document the following specific risks for this feature:
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The system suggests a treatment based on fabricated medical literature or non-existent drug interactions.
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The model performs poorly on demographics prevalent in Acme Valley (e.g., specific age groups, ethnicities, or occupational exposures) due to training data gaps.
*   **Over-Reliance:** Clinicians or patients accept the suggestion as a definitive diagnosis without independent verification, ignoring contradictory local context.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** The system directly prescribes medication or recommends irreversible procedures without mandatory human confirmation.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Users are unaware the advice is AI-generated and may be incorrect.

**2. Local Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Since cloud validation is unavailable, execute these offline evaluations using your local dataset:
*   **Adversarial Probe Set:** Construct a local test suite of 50+ known "hard cases" (rare symptoms, conflicting signs) and 50+ "negative controls" (healthy patients or clear non-matches).
    *   *Metric:* Rate of incorrect treatment suggestions on negative controls must be **0%**.
    *   *Stop Condition:* Any single false positive on a negative control blocks launch.
*   **Red-Team Simulation:** Have your six team members role-play as patients with ambiguous symptoms. Record every instance where the model expresses high confidence in an incorrect path.
*   **Subgroup Sweep:** Manually verify output quality across known local demographic slices. If a subgroup lacks sufficient local test data, mark performance as **UNVERIFIED** for that group; do not assume parity.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a one-page "Model Card" for the clinic staff. It must list: intended use (decision support only), known limitations (e.g., "fails on pediatric cardiac symptoms"), and the owner of the system. **Do not** list accuracy percentages unless derived from the local tests above.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** The system must **never** auto-dispense medication or finalize a diagnosis. It must output a "Suggested Differential" that requires a licensed clinician's explicit digital signature or verbal confirmation before any action is taken. This is the non-negotiable floor for high-stakes medical actions.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** Do not show raw confidence scores (e.g., "87% likely"). Instead, display defined coarse tiers:
    *   *Confirmed:* Matches multiple local guidelines.
    *   *Corroborated:* Matches one guideline, no contradictions.
    *   *Unverified:* Low data support; requires specialist consult.
*   **Disclosure:** Every output screen must clearly state: "AI-generated suggestion. Not a definitive diagnosis. Verify with clinical judgment."

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Name one specific person on the six-person team responsible for reviewing any reported errors.
*   **Re-review Trigger:** The safety protocol must be re-executed if the underlying model changes, if new local disease patterns emerge, or if the symptom input fields are modified.

**Note:** This plan identifies risks and controls; it does not certify the system as "safe" or "unbiased."
**Legal Routing:** Determine if this tool classifies as a regulated medical device under local laws; route this specific legal determination to counsel immediately.
