### Pre-Launch Review Process: NileRide Safety Scorer

**1. Harm Inventory & Simulation Strategy (MAP & MEASURE)**
Since you lack diverse demographic datasets, you must synthesize adversarial scenarios to stress-test the model before launch. Do not assert the model is unbiased; instead, treat bias as an unverified risk until measured.

*   **Synthetic Passenger Personas:** Create simulated passenger profiles representing extreme behavior patterns rather than demographic groups (which you cannot currently verify). Examples include:
    *   *The "Vindictive Rider":* Consistently rates 1-star regardless of trip quality.
    *   *The "Cancellation Abuser":* Frequently cancels after driver arrival, triggering penalties.
    *   *The "Route Disputer":* Regularly flags route deviations even when traffic justified them.
*   **Injection Testing:** Run these synthetic personas against your historical trip data. Inject their ratings into the dataset for specific driver segments (e.g., high-volume vs. low-volume drivers) to observe score volatility.
*   **Metric Definition:** Define a "False Ban Rate" metric. For every 100 simulated drivers subjected to biased feedback, how many cross the ban threshold? If this number is greater than zero, the risk is **UNVERIFIED** and requires mitigation. Do not invent a tolerance rate; set a stop condition where *any* simulated innocent driver banned triggers a launch block.

**2. Manual Review Triggers Checklist (MANAGE)**
Automatic banning based on a score threshold is a high-stakes, irreversible action. You must implement a human-in-the-loop gate for any decision that removes a driver's livelihood. The following conditions should trigger an immediate manual review before a ban is executed:

*   **Score Velocity:** The driver's score dropped by more than 20% within a 48-hour window (indicates potential review bombing).
*   **Low Sample Size:** The score is based on fewer than 20 trips (statistical noise outweighs signal).
*   **Outlier Ratings:** More than 50% of recent ratings are 1-star while the driver's historical average is above 4.5 stars.
*   **Correlated Cancellations:** A spike in rider cancellations coinciding with low ratings.
*   **Geographic/Temporal Clusters:** Multiple low ratings originating from the same location or time block (suggests a local event or coordinated attack).
*   **Appeal Filed:** Any driver who formally contests a rating must have their ban paused pending human review.

**3. Transparency Artifact**
Before deployment, draft a **Model Card** for internal and regulatory use. It must explicitly state:
*   **Intended Use:** Safety scoring for driver retention.
*   **Limitations:** Explicitly note the lack of diverse demographic testing data and that bias metrics are currently **UNVERIFIED**.
*   **Data Provenance:** Source of passenger ratings and trip logs.
*   **Owner:** Name the specific engineer accountable for monitoring bias post-launch.

**4. Governance**
Assign a single owner to re-review the model's performance weekly during the holiday rush. If the "False Ban Rate" from real-world appeals exceeds your simulated thresholds, the automated banning feature must be rolled back immediately.

***

**Note:** Legal requirements for automated decision-making in Egypt or under international standards (e.g., EU AI Act if applicable to your users) must be routed to counsel; this plan recommends controls but does not assert legal compliance.
**Note:** Never display raw confidence percentages (e.g., "87% risky") to users or admins; use defined tiers like "Under Review" or "Confirmed Violation" to prevent over-reliance on uncalibrated numbers.
