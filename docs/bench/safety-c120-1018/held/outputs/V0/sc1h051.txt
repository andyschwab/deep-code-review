### Pre-Launch Safety Review Plan: Smart Thermostat (Elderly Occupancy)

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before testing, explicitly record the primary failure mode: **Unsafe Automation of High-Stakes Action**.
*   **Harm:** The system misinterprets low mobility (stillness due to reading, sleeping, or impairment) as "absence," triggering aggressive heating or cooling that creates dangerous thermal conditions (hypothermia/hyperthermia risk).
*   **Affected Group:** Elderly residents with limited mobility or irregular movement patterns.
*   **Worst Case:** Severe health incident or death due to temperature extremes while the user is present but undetected.
*   **Secondary Harm:** **Over-reliance.** Caregivers or users may trust the "Eco-Mode" implicitly, assuming the device guarantees safety, leading to reduced manual monitoring.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Since real-world testing is limited, your simulation must be adversarial. Do not assert safety; measure residual risk.
*   **Simulation Strategy:** Construct a "Stillness Probe Set."
    *   **Positive Controls (Known Bad):** Simulate 4–8 hour periods of zero motion where the user is explicitly flagged as "present" (e.g., sleeping, watching TV, incapacitated). The system *must not* treat this as absence.
    *   **Negative Controls (Known Good):** Simulate genuine absence (user leaves home) to ensure energy saving still functions.
    *   **Edge Cases:** Simulate micro-movements (hand gestures, head turning) vs. macro-movements (walking). Test "fall" scenarios where motion stops abruptly.
*   **Metrics & Thresholds:**
    *   **Metric:** False Absence Rate (FAR) during simulated stillness.
    *   **Threshold:** For a high-stakes thermal control system affecting vulnerable users, propose **zero tolerance** for False Absence events that trigger extreme temperature shifts. Any failure here is a stop condition.
    *   **Stop Condition:** If the algorithm classifies a "still but present" scenario as "absent" and adjusts temperature beyond a safe band (e.g., >2°F change without confirmation), **block launch**.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a preliminary **Model Card**. Document that the model was trained/tested primarily on simulated data. Explicitly list "low-mobility stillness" as a known limitation if the simulation cannot fully replicate human variance. Mark performance metrics on this specific subgroup as **UNVERIFIED** if simulation fidelity is low; do not invent an accuracy percentage.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** Because this involves irreversible physical harm to a vulnerable population, fully autonomous temperature extremes are unsafe.
    *   **Control:** Implement a "Soft Limit." If the system detects "absence" based solely on stillness (rather than door sensors or geofencing), it must **not** execute extreme temperature changes automatically. Instead, it should send a push notification/app alert to the user or a designated caregiver: *"No movement detected for 2 hours. Are you okay? Confirm presence to maintain current temperature."*
    *   **Fallback:** If no response is received, default to a safe, neutral temperature range rather than an energy-saving extreme.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** Do not show the user a precise confidence score (e.g., "98% sure you are gone"). Use defined tiers: **"Presence Confirmed"** (recent motion) vs. **"Presence Uncertain"** (long stillness).
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly disclose in the user interface that the device uses automated patterns which may require manual confirmation during periods of inactivity.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific engineer as the "Safety Owner" for this feature. They must sign off on the simulation results.
*   **Re-review Trigger:** Any update to the motion detection algorithm or changes in the user demographic requires re-running the "Stillness Probe Set."

**Notes**
*   Route the question of legal liability for automated health-related decisions to legal counsel immediately; do not assume the current plan satisfies regulatory duties for medical-adjacent devices.
*   If the two-week deadline prevents running the full "Stillness Probe Set" with rigorous pass/fail criteria, the launch must be delayed or the autonomous feature disabled until testing is complete.
