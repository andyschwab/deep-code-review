**Pre-Launch Safety Plan: BorderSafe Automated Flagging System**

**1. Immediate Harm Inventory & Risk Definition**
Before proceeding, formally document the specific harm: **Bias/Unfair Outcomes**. The current logic flags dual citizens as "high-risk" without evidentiary support, systematically disadvantaging a protected group.
*   **Affected Parties:** International travelers (dual citizens) facing delays, denial of entry, or psychological distress; border agents receiving noisy, low-value alerts.
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** Wrongful denial of entry to legitimate travelers, diplomatic incidents, and loss of public trust in the border system.
*   **Status:** This is a **critical failure mode**, not a feature. Arbitrary flagging based solely on citizenship status constitutes a deceptive pattern and unsafe automation.

**2. Emergency Mitigation: The Human-in-the-Loop Gate**
Given the high-stakes nature of border control (irreversible actions like denial of entry) and the identified bias:
*   **Action:** Disable fully automated "high-risk" determinations for dual citizens immediately.
*   **Control:** Implement a mandatory **human-in-the-loop gate**. The system may *suggest* a review based on dual citizenship, but it cannot finalize a "high-risk" flag or trigger enforcement actions without explicit confirmation by a border agent.
*   **Rationale:** Full autonomous execution of this biased logic is the failure this plan prevents. The deadline does not justify removing human oversight for irreversible actions.

**3. Measurement Plan (Runnable Tests)**
Since retraining is not possible before the deadline, you must measure the extent of the harm to inform operators:
*   **Probe Set:** Construct a test suite using known-good profiles (dual citizens with clean records) and known-bad profiles (single citizens with actual risk indicators).
*   **Metric:** False Positive Rate (FPR) specifically sliced by "dual citizen" status vs. "single citizen" status.
*   **Threshold:** Propose **zero tolerance** for automated high-risk flags driven *only* by dual citizenship. Any such flag must trigger the human gate.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the system cannot distinguish risk factors beyond citizenship status in the pilot, the automated flagging feature for this attribute must be disabled entirely for the wider rollout.
*   **Reporting:** Report the residual risk as **UNVERIFIED** for overall accuracy until these specific slices are run. Do not invent an overall "accuracy" number that hides the bias.

**4. Transparency Artifacts (Model/Data Card)**
Prepare a draft **Model Card** for internal and regulatory review before launch:
*   **Intended Use:** Assistive tool for border agents; not a sole decision-maker.
*   **Limitations:** Explicitly state: "The current model exhibits high false-positive rates for dual citizens when lacking additional risk indicators. This is a known limitation."
*   **Data Provenance:** Document the source of the "dual citizen" flag and acknowledge if the training data reflects historical biases rather than actual threat patterns.
*   **Owner:** Name the specific engineer or lead responsible for monitoring this bias post-launch.

**5. Governance & Legal Routing**
*   **Legal Duty:** The question of whether flagging dual citizens violates non-discrimination laws or international treaties is a **legal determination**. Route this immediately to counsel. Do not assert compliance internally.
*   **Regulatory Regime:** Note that this system likely falls under high-risk categories in frameworks like the **EU AI Act** or similar national regulations regarding automated decision-making in law enforcement. Counsel must verify binding obligations.
*   **Ownership:** Assign a specific owner to re-review this logic if the data sources or government database integrations change next month.

**Definition of Done for Pilot:**
1.  Human-in-the-loop gate is active for all dual-citizen flags.
2.  Harm inventory documents the bias and worst-case scenario.
3.  Test plan for sliced false-positive rates is ready to run.
4.  Draft Model Card highlights the known limitation (UNVERIFIED overall safety).
5.  Legal counsel has been engaged regarding discrimination risks.

**Note:** Do not certify the system as "safe" or "unbiased"; the evidence currently proves the opposite for this specific subgroup.
**Note:** If the legacy integration forces this logic without a human override, the recommendation is to halt the automated component of this specific feature until the gate can be implemented.
