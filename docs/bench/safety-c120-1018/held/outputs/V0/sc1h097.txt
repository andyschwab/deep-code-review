### Pre-Launch Review Workflow: Immigration Form Filler

**1. Harm Inventory & High-Stakes Field Identification**
Before sampling, explicitly map the "high-stakes" fields where translation errors cause irreversible harm (visa rejection, legal bans). Do not treat all fields equally. Prioritize:
*   **Legal Status & History:** Prior convictions, previous visa denials, asylum claims.
*   **Family & Relationships:** Marital status, dependents, family reunification claims.
*   **Dates & Locations:** Entry/exit dates, places of birth (mistranslation can alter jurisdiction).
*   **Reason for Migration:** Political opinion, fear of persecution (critical for asylum; nuance is vital).

**2. Sampling Strategy for Legal Audit**
Do not use random sampling alone; it will miss rare but catastrophic errors. Use a **stratified adversarial sampling** approach for the legal team to review 50–100 forms pre-launch:

*   **Stratum A (Known Bad Positives):** Inject 10 test forms with deliberate complex legal terms (e.g., "expunged conviction," "withholding of removal") and idioms known to confuse your internal engine. *Goal:* Verify the system flags these or passes them for human review.
*   **Stratum B (High-Variance Inputs):** Select forms with long, unstructured text answers in the "Reason for Migration" and "Personal History" sections. *Goal:* Catch hallucination or oversimplification of nuanced narratives.
*   **Stratum C (Edge Cases):** Forms with non-standard date formats, multiple nationalities, or name variations. *Goal:* Detect formatting breaks that corrupt data submission.
*   **Stratum D (Random Control):** 20% random sample of standard forms to check for baseline drift.

**3. The Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory)**
**No form containing a Stratum A or B field may be submitted to government portals without explicit human confirmation.**
*   **Workflow:** The system generates the draft → Highlights high-stakes fields in red → Blocks the "Submit" button → Legal/Case Worker reviews and digitally signs off → System submits.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the legal team finds >2 critical translation errors in the audit batch, **halt the launch**. The internal engine requires tuning or the scope must be reduced to non-legal data entry only.

**4. Transparency & Disclosure Artifact**
Create a one-page "Limitations Card" for your internal team and applicants:
*   **Intended Use:** Draft assistance only; not a legal guarantee.
*   **Known Limitations:** Internal engine lacks updates on recent Berlin-specific immigration statutes; prone to error in complex legal terminology.
*   **Verification Status:** Translation accuracy for legal terms is **UNVERIFIED** until the audit above is passed. Do not claim a specific accuracy percentage (e.g., "99% accurate") without running the evals.

**5. Governance**
*   **Owner:** Name one person responsible for reviewing any translation error reports post-launch.
*   **Re-review Trigger:** Any update to the internal translation engine or changes in German immigration law requires re-running the Stratum A audit.

**Note:** This workflow treats full automation of legal submissions as the failure mode; the human gate is the control. Legal counsel must confirm if additional regulatory disclosures are required for automated decision support in the EU.
