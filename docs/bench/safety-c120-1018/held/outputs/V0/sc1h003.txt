### Product Output Safety Plan: AI Legal Advisor

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before pilot launch, explicitly document these specific risks for the cease-and-desist generator:
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The system cites non-existent statutes, misstates case law, or fabricates evidence of infringement, exposing the client to defamation or malicious prosecution suits.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** The system drafts threats of illegal actions (e.g., physical harm, unauthorized data seizure) or demands exceeding legal limits, creating immediate liability.
*   **Over-Reliance:** Users treat the draft as a final legal opinion without attorney review, assuming the AI has verified the specific facts of their case.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Recipients or users are unaware the letter was machine-generated, potentially misleading them about the level of legal scrutiny applied.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
Do not assert the system is "safe." Instead, execute these runnable tests:
*   **Adversarial Probe Set:** Create 50+ hypothetical scenarios ranging from clear infringement to fair use and non-infringement.
    *   *Positive Controls:* Inputs known to trigger hallucinations (e.g., obscure statutes, conflicting facts).
    *   *Negative Controls:* Inputs known to be safe/simple.
*   **Metric & Threshold:** Measure the rate of "Critical Legal Errors" (false statutes, illegal threats).
    *   *Stop Condition:* **Zero tolerance.** If *any* draft contains an illegal threat or a fabricated statute, the feature cannot ship.
    *   *Owner-Set Threshold:* Define "Professional Tone" via a rubric scored by your internal lawyers; set a pass rate (e.g., 95%) but acknowledge residual risk.
*   **Injection & Leakage Checks:** Test if user-provided facts can inject prompts that override safety constraints or leak data from other cases.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Publish a **Model Card** for the pilot. It must state: intended use (drafting aid only), limitations (not a substitute for counsel), known failure modes (hallucination risk), and the results of your internal testing. Mark any unmeasured metric as `UNVERIFIED`.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** Because sending a cease-and-desist letter is a high-stakes, irreversible action with legal consequences, **full autonomous execution is prohibited.**
    *   *Control:* Every generated draft must be reviewed and explicitly approved by a qualified human (one of your four lawyers) before it is sent. The AI is a drafting assistant, not the decision-maker.
*   **Uncertainty Rendering:** If the model is unsure about a statute or fact, it must surface this as a defined tier (e.g., "Unverified Claim – Requires Attorney Confirmation") rather than a false precision number (e.g., "87% confident").
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly label all outputs as "AI-Generated Draft – Requires Legal Review" within the document header.

**4. Feedback Loop & Governance**
*   **Flagging Mechanism:** Embed a "Report Issue" button in the UI for users to flag hallucinations, tone issues, or legal errors.
*   **Rapid Triage:** Assign one engineer and one lawyer to review flagged items weekly. Do not retrain immediately on raw flags; curate a verified dataset of failures first.
*   **Ownership:** Name a specific product owner responsible for re-evaluating the harm inventory if the model, data, or legal scope changes.

**Note:** Whether specific AI disclosures are legally required for your pilot jurisdiction is a determination for counsel; this plan recommends disclosure as a safety control but does not assert legal duty. Never fabricate a "0% error rate"; report residual risk honestly.
