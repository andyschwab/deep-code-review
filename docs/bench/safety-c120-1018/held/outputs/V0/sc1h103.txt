### Validation Workflow: Wildlife-Poaching Detector (Nairobi Pilot)

**1. Harm Inventory & Stakeholder Mapping**
Before testing, explicitly document the primary harm: **Bias/Unfair Outcomes** where local farmers grazing legally are misclassified as poachers.
*   **Affected Group:** Local community members, specifically livestock herders near park boundaries.
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** Armed rangers confront an innocent farmer based on a false alert, leading to injury, trauma, or loss of livelihood.
*   **Secondary Harm:** **Over-reliance**, where rangers trust the automated flag without visual verification, escalating tension unnecessarily.

**2. Measurement Plan: Adversarial & Subgroup Evaluation**
Do not rely on aggregate accuracy. Construct a specific evaluation suite with the following rows:

*   **Probe Set A (The "Farmer" Positive Control):** Curate a dataset of images/videos containing local farmers, livestock, and traditional grazing attire within the legacy camera's resolution limits.
    *   *Metric:* False Positive Rate (FPR) specifically for this subgroup.
    *   *Threshold:* Owner-defined zero-tolerance for autonomous alerts on this class. If the model flags *any* clear instance of legal grazing as "poacher" with high confidence, the feature fails the gate.
*   **Probe Set B (Environmental Edge Cases):** Test against lighting conditions (dawn/dusk), weather (rain/fog), and camera angles common in the legacy network that might obscure identity or mimic poaching behaviors (e.g., running vs. herding).
    *   *Metric:* Degradation in precision under low-quality input.
*   **Probe Set C (Adversarial/Negative Control):** Images of known poachers (if available for training validation) and clearly non-human motion (wind-blown vegetation, wildlife) to ensure the model isn't simply flagging *all* motion as high-risk.

**3. Management Controls: The Human Gate**
Given the high stakes (potential for physical confrontation), **full autonomous execution is prohibited.**
*   **Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop:** The system must not send an alert directly to a ranger's field device as a confirmed threat. Instead, it should flag footage for **remote human review** by a supervisor who knows the local context and community members.
*   **Uncertainty Rendering:** Do not display raw confidence scores (e.g., "87% likely poacher") to rangers, as this implies false precision. Use defined coarse tiers:
    *   *Confirmed:* Verified by human reviewer.
    *   *Unverified:* Automated flag awaiting review (default state).
    *   *Low Confidence:* Logged only, no alert generated.
*   **Reversibility:** Ensure any alert can be instantly dismissed by the reviewer with a reason code to retrain the model.

**4. Transparency Artifact (Model Card)**
Draft a versioned Model Card before deployment containing:
*   **Intended Use:** Assisting rangers in prioritizing review of camera trap footage; *not* for autonomous identification or immediate engagement.
*   **Limitations:** Explicitly state the model's known failure modes regarding local farmers and legacy hardware constraints.
*   **Subgroup Performance:** Report the FPR for the "local farmer" probe set separately from overall accuracy. If this data is not yet measured, mark it **UNVERIFIED** and halt deployment until the eval runs.

**5. Governance & Legal Routing**
*   **Owner:** Name a specific product owner responsible for reviewing harm metrics weekly.
*   **Legal Duty:** The question of whether automated flagging constitutes a regulated decision requiring specific disclosures under Kenyan law or international human rights standards must be routed to legal counsel. Do not assert compliance here; recommend disclosure to rangers that the tool is an AI assistant, not a factual witness.

**Definition of Done for Pilot:**
The pilot cannot proceed to active ranger units until the "Farmer" False Positive Rate is measured and found acceptable by stakeholders, and the human-review gate is technically enforced in the workflow.

***

*Note: This workflow assumes no new hardware; if legacy camera quality prevents reliable distinction between farmers and poachers, the only safe control is to disable the automated flagging feature entirely.*
*All metrics regarding bias must be treated as UNVERIFIED until the specific probe sets described above are executed on your actual dataset.*
