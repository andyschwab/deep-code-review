### Pre-Launch Safety Plan: AI Legal Assistant for Immigration

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before beta launch, explicitly document the following high-risk failure modes for your specific context:
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The bot invents legal precedents, misstates eligibility criteria, or fabricates regulatory deadlines, presenting them as absolute truth.
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The model systematically misinterprets inputs from specific dialects, cultural contexts, or trauma narratives, leading to incomplete or contradictory applications for protected groups.
*   **Unsafe Automation (Irreversible Harm):** The system submits a form containing errors that trigger immediate rejection, bans, or deportation proceedings without human review.
*   **Over-Reliance:** Users trust the bot's output as equivalent to licensed legal counsel, failing to disclose critical nuances because the interface implies certainty.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Users are unaware that a machine, not an attorney, is generating their legal strategy and filing documents.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
Do not assert safety; measure residual risk through targeted evaluation:
*   **Adversarial Red-Teaming:** Construct a probe set of "known-bad" inputs (ambiguous trauma stories, conflicting dates, edge-case visa categories) and "known-good" controls (standard cases with verified outcomes).
*   **Regulatory Stress Tests:** Since you cannot hire external consultants, manually curate a dataset of recent regulatory updates and ambiguous guidance. Run the bot against these specific scenarios to identify where it defaults to outdated or overly confident logic.
*   **Metric Definition:** Define a pass threshold of **zero tolerance** for any error that leads to an irreversible filing action. If the bot generates a form ready for submission, it must be 100% accurate on material facts.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a **Model Card** for your beta users and internal team. This document must state the intended use (assistance, not representation), list known limitations (e.g., "cannot handle complex criminal history"), and report results from your red-team exercises. Mark any untested scenario as `UNVERIFIED`.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate:** Given the risk of deportation, **fully autonomous submission is prohibited.** The system must generate a draft, but a qualified human (internal team member or partner) must review and explicitly authorize every single submission to immigration authorities. The "Submit" button should never be automated.
*   **Uncertainty Rendering:** Do not display raw confidence scores (e.g., "87% sure"). Instead, use defined coarse tiers for internal reviewers:
    *   *Confirmed:* Matches verified statutory text.
    *   *Corroborated:* Supported by multiple reliable sources in the context.
    *   *Unverified:* Based on ambiguous input or novel legal interpretation; **requires mandatory human review.**
*   **Explicit Disclosure:** Every interaction must clearly state: "This is an AI assistant, not an attorney. Outputs are drafts requiring human review. No attorney-client privilege is established."

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Ownership:** Assign a specific team member as the "Safety Owner" responsible for reviewing any flagged `Unverified` outputs and updating the harm inventory when immigration laws change.
*   **Re-evaluation Trigger:** Any update to the underlying model, the regulatory landscape, or the form structures triggers an immediate re-run of the adversarial probe set before further deployments.

**Note:** This plan identifies risks and controls but does not certify the system as "safe" or "unbiased"; the decision to ship rests with your leadership. Legal duties regarding unauthorized practice of law must be routed to qualified counsel immediately, even on a limited budget.
