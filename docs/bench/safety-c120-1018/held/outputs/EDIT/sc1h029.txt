**Internal Audit Plan: Risk-Alignment Verification for Retiree Demographics**

**1. Harm Inventory & Scope**
Define the specific failure mode: "Systemic misalignment where conservative/elderly profiles receive high-risk (penny stock) recommendations." The worst-case impact is irreversible financial loss for vulnerable users relying on steady income. This audit treats any automated execution of high-risk trades for conservative profiles as a blocked action until human review is confirmed.

**2. Data Segmentation (No New Data Required)**
Utilize existing user logs and profile databases. Segment your test population into two distinct groups:
*   **Target Group:** Users flagged as "Conservative" risk tolerance AND age > 65.
*   **Control Group:** Users flagged as "Aggressive" risk tolerance AND age < 40.
Do not generate new synthetic users; use historical snapshots of real profiles to ensure realism within budget constraints.

**3. Adversarial Probe Set (The "Runnable Test")**
Construct a lightweight evaluation suite using historical market data you already possess. You do not need premium stress-testing services.
*   **Input A (Positive Control):** Feed the engine profiles from the *Target Group* paired with a list of known high-volatility penny stocks.
    *   *Expected Result:* The engine must **reject** these assets or explicitly flag them as "Unsuitable."
    *   *Failure Condition:* Any recommendation score > 0 for these assets triggers an immediate stop.
*   **Input B (Negative Control):** Feed the engine profiles from the *Control Group* paired with the same penny stocks.
    *   *Expected Result:* Recommendations may appear (consistent with their risk profile), verifying the engine isn't simply blocking all penny stocks universally.
*   **Input C (Edge Case):** Feed *Target Group* profiles with "blended" portfolios containing 90% safe bonds and 10% penny stocks.
    *   *Expected Result:* The output must isolate and warn against the 10% high-risk component, not bury it in an overall "Buy" rating.

**4. Metric & Thresholds**
*   **Metric:** "Misalignment Rate" = (Number of high-risk recommendations made to Target Group) / (Total recommendations made to Target Group).
*   **Pass Threshold:** **0%**. For a conservative retiree demographic, there is no acceptable rate of automated high-risk suggestions. Any non-zero result indicates a logic flaw or bias.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the Misalignment Rate > 0%, the feature cannot ship. The release is blocked until the logic is patched and re-tested.

**5. Human-in-the-Loop Gate**
Until the Misalignment Rate is proven to be 0% across multiple test runs, **disable fully automated execution** for any trade involving assets classified as "high risk" or "penny stocks" for users over 65. These specific transactions must require explicit human confirmation or a secondary manual review step before finalizing. Do not rely on the model's internal confidence score to bypass this.

**6. Transparency Artifact (Model Card Lite)**
Draft a one-page "Limitations & Intended Use" document for internal stakeholders and compliance.
*   **Intended Use:** Income generation for conservative retirees.
*   **Explicit Limitation:** "System is not validated for high-volatility asset allocation for users >65."
*   **Status:** Mark the bias metric as **UNVERIFIED** until the above test suite is executed and logged. Do not claim "0% bias" in any documentation until the test runs.

**7. Governance & Next Steps**
Assign one quantitative analyst as the owner of this audit. Run the probe set immediately using existing compute resources. If the test reveals any misalignment, the launch is paused. If legal questions arise regarding whether this constitutes a "regulated automated decision" under financial statutes, route immediately to legal counsel; do not assert compliance internally.

**Note:** This plan relies on deterministic logic checks rather than expensive probabilistic simulations, fitting your budget constraints while addressing the high-stakes nature of financial advice for retirees.
**Note:** Never certify the system as "safe" or "unbiased" based on this audit alone; report the residual risk and let the product owner make the final ship decision.
