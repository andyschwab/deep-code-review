**Pre-Launch Safety Review Plan: Acme Climate Smart Thermostat**

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Primary Failure Mode:** "Stillness Misclassification." The algorithm interprets low mobility (due to age, illness, or sleep) as "absence," triggering energy-saving heating reductions that create unsafe cold conditions for vulnerable residents.
*   **Affected Group:** Elderly residents with limited mobility, chronic conditions, or irregular sleep patterns.
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** Hypothermia or exacerbation of respiratory/cardiac conditions due to prolonged exposure to unintended low temperatures during winter.
*   **Secondary Risk:** Over-reliance. Caregivers may assume the system is monitoring safety rather than just optimizing energy, delaying manual checks.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
*   **Simulation Protocol:** Construct adversarial test rows within your existing simulation budget. Do not rely on aggregate accuracy; test specific subgroups.
    *   *Row A (Positive Control):* Simulate an elderly user sleeping 12 hours or sitting still for 4+ hours. **Metric:** System must *not* classify as "absent." **Threshold:** 0 false absences allowed in this segment.
    *   *Row B (Negative Control):* Simulate a truly empty house with no motion for 4+ hours. **Metric:** System *must* classify as "absent" to validate energy savings logic.
    *   *Row C (Edge Case):* Simulate irregular movement patterns (e.g., bathroom visits every 2 hours, slow shuffling). **Metric:** Continuous "occupied" status.
*   **Stop Condition:** If Row A yields *any* false "absent" classification, the launch is blocked. This is a non-negotiable gate for irreversible physical harm.
*   **Reporting:** Report residual risk as "UNVERIFIED" for any scenario not covered by your simulation parameters. Do not invent a "99% safe" metric without empirical evidence from these specific probes.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (The Floor):** Because lowering heat in winter for an immobile person is a high-stakes, potentially irreversible action, full automation is unsafe.
    *   *Implementation:* If the system detects "absence" based *only* on motionlessness (rather than explicit user override or geofencing), it must enter a "Caution Mode." In this mode, the temperature drops only to a safe minimum floor (e.g., 60°F/15°C), never to an eco-low setting, until human confirmation is received via a caregiver app or a secondary sensor check.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** Do not show a precise confidence percentage (e.g., "87% occupied") to users or caregivers. Use defined coarse tiers: **"Confirmed Occupancy"** (recent motion), **"Likely Occupancy"** (low motion but historical pattern matches), or **"Unverified/Check Needed"** (no motion, pattern mismatch).
*   **Fallback Rule:** Hard-code a rule that prevents temperature drops below a safety threshold if the external temperature is below freezing, regardless of the algorithm's "absence" prediction.

**4. Governance & Transparency (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific engineer as the owner of the "Stillness Misclassification" risk. They must sign off on the simulation results before launch.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a one-page "Model Card" for internal support teams and caregivers. It must state: "Intended Use: Energy optimization. Limitation: May misinterpret stillness as absence. Safety Measure: Temperature never drops below X degrees without secondary confirmation."
*   **Legal Routing:** Since this involves vulnerable populations and potential physical harm, route the question of regulatory disclosure (e.g., EU AI Act high-risk classification) to legal counsel immediately. Do not assert compliance internally.

**Note:** This plan prioritizes a hard safety floor over energy optimization efficiency to meet your two-week deadline without requiring new hardware.
**Note:** If simulations cannot replicate the specific mobility edge cases, the feature must default to the safe temperature floor until real-world data is gathered.
