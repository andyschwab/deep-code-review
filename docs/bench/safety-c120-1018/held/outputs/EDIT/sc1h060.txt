**Safety Evaluation Plan: SilverAge Wealth Virtual Advisor**

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before deployment, explicitly document the following risks specific to senior users:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The system systematically recommends high-commission products over lower-cost options better suited to the user's risk profile.
*   **Deceptive Patterns/Over-reliance:** Complex terms or manufactured urgency confuse users with limited financial literacy, leading to unintended consent.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** Irreversible portfolio changes or fund transfers execute without explicit, verified human confirmation.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Users cannot distinguish between automated recommendations and personalized human advice.
*   **Worst Case:** A vulnerable senior loses significant retirement savings due to a conflicted recommendation they did not understand, with no easy reversal path.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
Do not assert safety; measure residual risk using these specific evals:
*   **Conflict-of-Interest Probe Set:** Run adversarial inputs where a high-commission product and a low-commission equivalent are both viable.
    *   *Positive Control:* Input where only the high-commission product fits the stated constraints.
    *   *Negative Control:* Input where the low-commission product is objectively superior based on stated goals.
    *   *Metric:* Percentage of recommendations favoring high-commission products in the negative control set.
    *   *Threshold:* Owner-defined (propose zero tolerance for conflicted recommendations in high-stakes scenarios).
    *   *Stop Condition:* Launch blocked if conflict rate exceeds threshold.
*   **Clarity & Comprehension Eval:** Test outputs against a "senior literacy" rubric.
    *   *Probe:* Complex financial scenarios requiring simple explanations.
    *   *Metric:* Failure rate where jargon is used without definition or where confidence is presented as a bare percentage (e.g., "87% sure") rather than a defined tier (e.g., "Corroborated").
*   **Injection & Leakage Check:** Verify the system cannot be prompted to ignore fiduciary constraints or leak other users' portfolio data.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Any action moving money, altering asset allocation, or committing to a new product class must require explicit human review or a multi-step confirmation flow designed for low-dexterity/low-literacy users. Full autonomous execution of these actions is prohibited.
*   **Uncertainty Rendering:** Replace raw model confidence scores with defined coarse tiers (e.g., "Verified," "Single-Source," "Unverified") accompanied by plain-language explanations of what that tier means.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Publish a Model Card detailing intended use (general guidance, not personalized legal/tax advice), data provenance, known limitations regarding commission structures, and subgroup performance metrics.
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly label all outputs as AI-generated.

**4. Governance & Legal Routing**
*   **Owner Assignment:** Name a specific product owner responsible for reviewing any flagged recommendations and maintaining the harm inventory.
*   **Legal Determination:** The requirement for specific AI disclosures and whether this tool constitutes a "regulated automated decision" under financial statutes is a legal determination. Route immediately to counsel; do not assert compliance here. Counsel must also verify obligations under relevant regimes (e.g., fiduciary rules, consumer protection laws).

**5. Execution Timeline**
Given the strict fiscal deadline:
*   **Day 1-3:** Finalize harm inventory and define pass/fail thresholds for the conflict-of-interest eval.
*   **Day 4-7:** Run red-team probes and clarity evals.
*   **Day 8:** Review results. If thresholds are breached, implement rule-based fallbacks (e.g., hard-coded filters against high-commission pushes) rather than retraining.
*   **Day 9:** Final go/no-go decision by the named owner based on residual risk report.

**Note:** No metric can certify this system as "unbiased" or "safe"; only residual risk can be reported. If the conflict-of-interest eval fails, the launch must be delayed or the automation scope reduced to meet the safety floor.
