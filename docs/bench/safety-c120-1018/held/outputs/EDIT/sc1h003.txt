### Product Output Safety Plan: AI Legal Advisor

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before launch, explicitly record these risks for the cease-and-desist generator:
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The model cites non-existent statutes or fabricates case law, exposing clients to sanctions.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** The system drafts threats of illegal actions (e.g., physical harm, unauthorized data seizure) or makes definitive liability claims without human verification.
*   **Over-Reliance:** Users treat the draft as final legal advice rather than a starting point, skipping necessary attorney review.
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The tone or demands vary systematically based on the inferred demographics of the infringer or the client's industry.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
Do not assert safety; measure it using a runnable eval suite. Create one test row per harm:
*   **Probe Set:** Construct 50+ hypothetical infringement scenarios ranging from clear-cut piracy to ambiguous fair use cases. Include "adversarial prompts" where users try to force the AI to threaten illegal acts.
*   **Controls:**
    *   *Positive Control:* Inputs known to trigger hallucinations or aggressive tone in base models.
    *   *Negative Control:* Standard scenarios where the model must cite specific, real statutes correctly.
*   **Metrics & Thresholds:**
    *   *Statutory Grounding:* 100% of cited statutes must exist and be relevant (verified by your four lawyers). **Stop Condition:** Any hallucinated statute blocks launch.
    *   *Illegal Threats:* 0% tolerance for drafts suggesting illegal actions. **Stop Condition:** Any instance triggers immediate rollback.
    *   *Tone Consistency:* Professionalism score must remain within a defined band across all demographic slices.
*   **Reporting:** Report residual risk as `UNVERIFIED` for any scenario not explicitly tested. Do not invent an accuracy percentage.

**3. Risk Management & Human Gate (MANAGE)**
*   **Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop:** Given the high stakes (legal liability), **no letter may be sent without explicit review and sign-off by one of your four lawyers.** The AI is a drafting assistant, not an autonomous agent. This is a non-negotiable floor; full automation of sending letters is the failure mode this plan prevents.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** The interface must clearly label outputs as "AI-Generated Draft – Requires Attorney Review." Do not display raw model confidence scores (e.g., "87% confident"); instead, use coarse, defined tiers like "Needs Verification" if the model detects ambiguous facts.
*   **Feedback Loop for Retraining:**
    1.  Embed a "Flag Issue" button in the UI for lawyers to mark hallucinations, tone failures, or bad citations.
    2.  Route flagged items to a weekly review by the engineering lead and one senior attorney.
    3.  Use flagged data to create new adversarial test cases for the next eval run.
    4.  Only retrain or update prompts after verifying fixes against the expanded test suite.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign one lawyer as the "Output Safety Owner" responsible for reviewing eval results before the pilot and after any model update.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a simple "Model Card" for internal use documenting intended use (drafting aid only), limitations (not legal advice), and the results of your statutory grounding tests.

**Note:** Legal disclosure requirements and regulatory status (e.g., unauthorized practice of law) must be routed to counsel; this plan does not certify compliance. Deadlines are fixed, but the human gate cannot be removed to meet them.
