### Wedding Planner AI: Vendor Diversity & Inclusion Validation Checklist

**Objective:** Validate that vendor suggestions remain diverse and inclusive despite inferring religious affiliation from unstructured text, ensuring compliance with anti-discrimination norms before the six-week launch.

**Phase 1: Harm Inventory & Risk Mapping (Days 1–3)**
*   [ ] **Define Protected Groups:** Explicitly list the religious and cultural groups relevant to the Paris market that must not be systematically excluded.
*   [ ] **Identify Inference Triggers:** Manually review 50 random vendor descriptions to list keywords or phrases the AI currently uses to infer religious affiliation. Flag ambiguous terms (e.g., "traditional," "family-oriented") that may cause false positives.
*   [ ] **Worst-Case Scenario Statement:** Document the specific harm: "Qualified vendors are excluded from suggestion lists due to incorrect AI inference of religious affiliation, leading to reduced choice for couples and potential legal liability."

**Phase 2: Manual Verification of Top 100 Vendors (Days 4–10)**
*   [ ] **Ground Truth Establishment:** For the top 100 vendors, manually verify their actual religious affiliation (or lack thereof) via direct contact, official websites, or public records. Do not rely on the legacy database.
*   [ ] **Inference Audit:** Run the current inference mechanism against these 100 verified profiles.
*   [ ] **Discrepancy Log:** Create a table comparing "Actual Affiliation" vs. "AI Inferred Affiliation." Highlight any mismatches where a vendor is incorrectly tagged or excluded.
*   [ ] **Diversity Slice Test:** Simulate three distinct user personas (e.g., secular, specific religious requirement, no preference) and record the top 10 suggestions for each. Verify that qualified vendors from all groups appear in relevant searches where appropriate.

**Phase 3: Mitigation & Human-in-the-Loop Gates (Days 11–20)**
*   [ ] **Confidence Tiering:** Replace any numerical confidence score (e.g., "85% likely Catholic") with defined coarse tiers: **Confirmed** (explicitly stated by vendor), **Corroborated** (multiple independent sources), **Single-Source** (one unverified mention), and **Unverified** (inferred only from ambiguous text).
*   [ ] **Exclusion Gate:** Implement a hard rule: No vendor tagged as **Unverified** or **Single-Source** for religious affiliation may be automatically excluded from a search result. They must remain visible unless explicitly deselected by the user.
*   [ ] **Disclosure Draft:** Prepare a user-facing note: "Vendor categories are assisted by AI analysis of public descriptions. If a specific cultural or religious requirement is critical, please confirm directly with the vendor."
*   [ ] **Override Mechanism:** Ensure customer support can manually override any AI exclusion within 24 hours if a couple reports a missing vendor.

**Phase 4: Final Review & Launch Readiness (Days 21–42)**
*   [ ] **Residual Risk Report:** Summarize the error rate found in Phase 2. If the error rate for exclusion is >0% for any protected group, document this as **UNVERIFIED** safety for full automation; maintain the human gate.
*   [ ] **Transparency Artifact:** Create a one-page "Data Card" for internal use detailing that religious data is inferred from unstructured text, listing known limitations, and naming the product owner responsible.
*   [ ] **Legal Routing:** Submit the inference logic and the "Exclusion Gate" policy to legal counsel to confirm alignment with French anti-discrimination laws. Do not assert compliance internally; await counsel's written confirmation.
*   [ ] **Rollback Plan:** Define the trigger for disabling the inference feature entirely (e.g., if >5% of verified vendors are misclassified during a post-launch spot check).
