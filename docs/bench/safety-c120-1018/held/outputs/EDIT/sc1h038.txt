**Immediate Safety Review & Pre-Launch Plan for EduFuture Scholarship Platform**

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Identified Harm:** Bias/Unfair Outcomes. The system systematically disadvantages applicants from non-traditional school backgrounds.
*   **Affected Group:** Students with non-traditional education (e.g., homeschooling, vocational paths, gap years).
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** Deserving students are permanently denied funding due to algorithmic exclusion, reinforcing systemic inequality and exposing EduFuture to reputational and legal risk.
*   **Status:** **CONFIRMED** based on your observation of disproportionate rejection rates.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Since new data collection is impossible before the deadline, we must evaluate the *current* model behavior using existing assets.
*   **Probe Set:** Construct a "counterfactual" test set from your existing rejected pool. Pair rejected non-traditional essays with accepted traditional essays that have similar semantic content/quality scores but different school backgrounds.
*   **Controls:**
    *   *Positive Control:* A known qualified non-traditional applicant (manually verified) that the model currently rejects.
    *   *Negative Control:* A known unqualified applicant (any background) that the model correctly rejects.
*   **Metric:** Disparate Impact Ratio (rejection rate of non-traditional vs. traditional groups).
*   **Pass Threshold:** **Owner-defined.** Given the high stakes (financial aid), propose near-zero tolerance for unexplained disparity. If the ratio remains below 0.8 (the common legal heuristic) without justification, the feature fails the gate.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the disparate impact ratio exceeds the threshold, **block launch** or restrict the automated decision to a "recommendation only" mode with mandatory human review for all non-traditional flags.
*   **Reporting:** Report residual risk as **UNVERIFIED** for any subgroup where sample sizes are too small for statistical significance. Do not invent a "fairness score."

**3. Risk Management & Immediate Adjustments (MANAGE)**
You cannot retrain the model, so you must constrain its agency and adjust the decision logic.
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** Because this is a high-stakes, irreversible financial decision with known bias, **full automation is unsafe.**
    *   *Action:* Configure the system to **auto-refer** any applicant flagged as "non-traditional school" or any applicant falling within the bottom 20% of scores solely due to school-background features to a human committee member.
    *   *Constraint:* The algorithm may only "auto-approve" clear matches; it must never "auto-reject" a non-traditional candidate without human confirmation.
*   **Software Adjustment (Rule-Based Fallback):** Implement a pre-processing rule that neutralizes or down-weights the "school type" feature for the scoring calculation, forcing the model to rely on essay content and extracurricular metrics instead. If the model architecture prevents feature masking, use the model only to rank, not to binary classify, for the at-risk group.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** immediately. It must state:
    *   *Intended Use:* Initial screening only, not final determination.
    *   *Limitations:* Explicitly disclose the identified bias against non-traditional schools and the fact that mitigation is via human gating, not retraining.
    *   *Performance:* Report the disparity metrics found in your evaluation above. Do not claim the system is "unbiased."

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign one data scientist as the explicit owner of this bias risk. They must sign off on the human-review workflow before launch.
*   **Re-review:** Trigger an immediate re-evaluation if the definition of "non-traditional" changes or if the volume of referred applications overwhelms the human reviewers.

**Note:** Legal counsel must determine if this automated decision triggers specific regulatory disclosures (e.g., adverse action notices) under local education or lending laws; this plan assumes technical mitigation only.
Do not publish any aggregate "accuracy" or "fairness" percentage without the specific eval results; label unmeasured claims as UNVERIFIED.
