### Manual Review Strategy: Age-Bias Evaluation for Hiring System

**1. Construct a Synthetic Test Corpus (The "Probe Set")**
Since automated alerts are offline, you must manually generate a controlled dataset before processing real applications. Create 20–30 paired synthetic resumes that are identical in skills, education quality, and role relevance but differ strictly in age signals:
*   **Pair A:** Candidate X (Graduated 2023, 2 years experience) vs. Candidate Y (Graduated 1995, 30 years experience).
*   **Pair B:** Candidate X (No graduation date listed, recent certifications) vs. Candidate Y (Graduation date 1990, same certifications).
*   **Control:** Include a "negative control" pair where both candidates have identical age signals to verify the system returns equal scores when no variable changes.

**2. Execute the Manual Scoring Run**
Run these synthetic profiles through your current filtering logic. Record the raw score, ranking position, and any automated rejection flags for each profile. Do not rely on aggregate pass rates; inspect individual pair outcomes.

**3. Analyze for Systematic Disadvantage**
Compare the scores within each pair.
*   **Finding:** If the older candidate consistently scores lower than their identical younger counterpart, your logic contains age bias (likely weighting "years since graduation" negatively or capping "years of experience").
*   **Threshold:** Given the high stakes of employment discrimination, treat *any* consistent score delta favoring the younger candidate in identical skill-matches as a **stop condition**. Do not launch if the system systematically downgrades based on graduation era.

**4. Implement a Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Immediate Mitigation)**
Until the bias detection module is restored and this manual review shows zero systematic disadvantage:
*   **Freeze Automated Rejections:** Configure the system to flag any application from a candidate with >15 years of experience or a graduation date prior to 2005 for **mandatory human review** before a rejection is issued.
*   **Override Mechanism:** Ensure recruiters can easily override a low algorithmic score if the manual review of the resume confirms skill fit.

**5. Document Residual Risk (Transparency Artifact)**
Create a temporary "System Limitation Note" for your internal team and clients:
*   **Status:** Bias detection module offline; manual audit in progress.
*   **Known Limitation:** Potential for unverified age-related scoring variance.
*   **Action:** High-experience candidates are routed to human review.
*   **Owner:** Name the specific engineer/recruiter responsible for this audit.
*   **Metric Status:** Mark the bias rate as **UNVERIFIED** until the synthetic test corpus yields stable, non-discriminatory results. Do not invent a "0% bias" claim.

**6. Next Steps**
*   Run the synthetic corpus immediately.
*   If bias is detected, retrain or adjust the weighting logic to ignore graduation dates entirely, focusing only on skill recency and relevance.
*   Re-test before processing the first real batch of applications.

***

**Note:** This manual strategy substitutes for your offline tool but does not certify the system as "unbiased"; it only identifies obvious failures in your specific test cases.
**Note:** Employment screening is a highly regulated domain; route the final decision on legal compliance and disclosure duties to legal counsel before activating the system for clients.
