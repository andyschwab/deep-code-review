**Risk Assessment Plan: Automated Debt Collection Language & Tone**

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before deployment, we must explicitly record the following risks specific to contacting vulnerable borrowers in regions like Lagos:
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** The system issuing threats of legal action, asset seizure, or police involvement that are factually incorrect, legally unauthorized, or disproportionate to the debt.
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Tone or phrasing that systematically disadvantages specific demographics (e.g., single parents) or cultural groups, leading to aggressive escalation where a human would de-escalate.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The AI fabricating specific debt amounts, due dates, or legal statutes and presenting them with absolute authority.
*   **Missing AI Disclosure:** Borrowers believing they are negotiating with a human empath, leading to over-reliance on the bot's promises or threats.
*   **Deceptive Patterns:** Using manufactured urgency ("Pay in 1 hour or else") or hidden defaults that trap users into unaffordable plans.
*   **Over-Reliance/Automation Bias:** The system continuing to press for payment despite clear signals of distress or inability to pay, because it lacks sentiment analysis.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
We cannot certify the system is "safe." Instead, we will run the following evaluations:
*   **Probe Set A (Aggression Escalation):** Input scenarios where a user expresses extreme distress, unemployment, or single-parent status.
    *   *Positive Control:* A prompt known to trigger aggression in similar models.
    *   *Negative Control:* A prompt where a polite refusal is the correct response.
    *   *Metric:* Count of messages containing threatening language, false legal claims, or failure to acknowledge distress.
    *   *Threshold:* **Zero tolerance** for false legal threats or harassment. Any failure blocks launch.
*   **Probe Set B (Hallucination & Accuracy):** Input varied debt amounts and local Lagos/Nigerian legal contexts.
    *   *Metric:* Percentage of outputs citing non-existent laws or incorrect balances.
    *   *Status:* If not measured against a verified legal database, this metric is **UNVERIFIED**.
*   **Probe Set C (Injection & Leakage):** Test if a borrower can trick the bot into revealing other users' data or ignoring safety rules via prompt injection.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
Given the legacy platform constraints and high stakes:
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory Floor):** Because the tool cannot easily escalate in real-time, **no fully autonomous action** is permitted for high-stakes outcomes (e.g., finalizing a repayment plan, threatening legal action, or contacting third parties). The system must draft messages for human review before sending if the conversation exceeds three turns or detects keywords related to "hardship," "court," or "police."
*   **Defined Confidence Tiers:** Do not display raw model confidence percentages to users. If the system is unsure of a legal fact, it must output a defined tier status (e.g., "Unverified – Requires Human Review") rather than guessing.
*   **Explicit Disclosure:** Every message must clearly state it is an automated message from Global Finance Corp.
*   **Kill Switch:** A manual override must exist to halt all outbound communications instantly if a pattern of harmful output is detected.

**4. Governance & Next Steps**
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific individual responsible for reviewing failed evals and approving the "go/no-go" decision.
*   **Legal Routing:** Determining whether these automated decisions constitute a regulated activity under local Nigerian law or international standards (e.g., EU AI Act implications if data crosses borders) is a legal determination. **Route immediately to counsel** to define permissible language and required disclosures; do not assert legal compliance here.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a one-page "Model Card" documenting intended use, known limitations (no sentiment analysis), and the results of the aggression probes.

**Note:** Do not invent a "0% bias" or "99% safe" metric; report residual risk as "UNVERIFIED" until the probes above are run. The inability to perform real-time sentiment analysis on the legacy platform increases the necessity of the human review gate for any complex negotiation.
