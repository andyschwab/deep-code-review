**Testing Strategy: Conflict Zone & Civil Unrest Avoidance**

**1. Data Source Validation (The "Ground Truth" Audit)**
Before testing the algorithm, validate the inputs. Create a "Golden Set" of 50 known historical and simulated scenarios containing active conflict zones, civil unrest, and safe corridors.
*   **Positive Controls:** Inputs where threat data explicitly marks a route as dangerous. The system must flag these.
*   **Negative Controls:** Inputs with normal traffic congestion but no safety threats. The system must not treat these as conflict zones.
*   **Latency Probe:** Inject delayed threat data (e.g., an incident reported 2 hours ago) to verify the system rejects stale data rather than routing based on it. If the data source cannot prove freshness within a defined window (e.g., 15 minutes), the system must default to "Unverified" and avoid the route.

**2. Decision Logic Red-Teaming**
Run adversarial simulations against your routing engine using the Golden Set.
*   **The "Desperation" Test:** Simulate a scenario where the *only* available route passes near a risk zone. Verify the system does not silently route through it; it must either stop navigation or demand explicit human confirmation (see Gate below).
*   **The "Hallucination" Check:** Feed the system contradictory reports (one source says "safe," another says "unrest"). The logic must prioritize the safety warning (precautionary principle) rather than averaging the risk or guessing.
*   **Injection Resistance:** Attempt to inject false "all-clear" signals into the data stream. The system must have a whitelist of trusted providers; unverified overrides must be rejected.

**3. The Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Non-Negotiable)**
For any route classified as having "potential risk" or where data is "Unverified":
*   **Hard Stop:** The system must not auto-execute the turn or route change.
*   **Explicit Confirmation:** The interface must present a clear, non-technical warning: *"Route passes near reported civil unrest. Data confidence: Low. Do you accept this risk?"*
*   **Default Behavior:** If the driver does not respond within a short window, the system must default to the safest known alternative or pull over safely, not proceed into the unknown.

**4. Transparency Artifact (Model/Data Card)**
Draft a one-page "Safety Limitations Card" for the vehicle manufacturer and end-users.
*   **Intended Use:** Real-time avoidance of known conflict zones based on [List Providers].
*   **Known Limitations:** "Cannot detect spontaneous unrest occurring faster than data refresh rates; may route near risks if no alternative exists."
*   **Unverified Metrics:** Do not claim "100% safety" or "Zero risk." State clearly: "Residual risk exists where data is unavailable or delayed."

**5. Execution Plan for Tight Deadline**
*   **Day 1:** Finalize the 50-scenario Golden Set with your data analysts.
*   **Day 2:** Run automated regression tests against these scenarios. Any failure on a Positive Control is a launch block.
*   **Day 3:** Manual review of all "Edge Case" decisions by the lead engineer. Sign-off required on the "Human-in-the-Loop" trigger logic.
*   **Launch Condition:** If any high-stakes route (one passing near a known threat) executes without the human gate, the integration is blocked.

**Note:** This plan measures residual risk; it does not certify the system as "safe." Legal counsel must review whether specific regions trigger mandatory regulatory disclosures under local laws.
