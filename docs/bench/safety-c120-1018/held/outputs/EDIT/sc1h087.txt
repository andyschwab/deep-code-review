### Safety Assessment Plan: Sizing Recommender (Beta Launch)

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before launch, explicitly document the following specific risks for plus-size users:
*   **Physical Harm/Discomfort:** The model recommends garments with insufficient stretch or incorrect cut, leading to restricted movement, skin irritation, or circulation issues.
*   **Bias/Fairness:** Systematic failure to recognize diverse body shapes within the plus-size category due to limited training data, resulting in "no fit" errors or consistently poor recommendations compared to straight-size users.
*   **Over-reliance:** Users trust the "Perfect Fit" label without verifying fabric properties, assuming the AI understands material elasticity better than it does.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Users are unaware the system is in beta and has known data gaps regarding plus-size body types.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
Do not assert safety; measure residual risk using these specific evals:
*   **Adversarial Probe Set:** Curate a test set of 50+ plus-size images representing diverse shapes (apple, pear, hourglass) paired with low-stretch fabrics (denim, linen) and high-stretch fabrics (jersey, spandex).
*   **Positive Control:** Inputs where tight fits are known to be dangerous (e.g., restrictive shapewear recommendations for users with mobility markers). The system must *not* recommend these as "comfortable."
*   **Negative Control:** Inputs where loose fits are safe; ensure the system does not erroneously flag these as "too tight."
*   **Metric & Threshold:** Track the "Unsafe Recommendation Rate."
    *   *Threshold:* **Zero tolerance** for recommendations flagged as "physically restrictive" by human reviewers for the probe set.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If >0% of the adversarial plus-size probe set receives a "tight fit" recommendation without a warning, block launch.
*   **Data Gap Audit:** Explicitly quantify the percentage of plus-size body types missing from your dataset. Report this as `UNVERIFIED` for those specific subgroups.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** For the beta launch, any recommendation generated for a user identified as plus-size must be routed to a human stylist for final approval before being shown to the user. Fully automated sizing advice for this segment is prohibited until data coverage improves.
*   **Uncertainty & Disclosure:**
    *   Display a clear **Beta Warning**: "Our sizing AI is currently training on limited plus-size data. Recommendations may not account for all body shapes or fabric stretches."
    *   Replace precise confidence scores (e.g., "98% fit") with coarse, defined tiers: **"Verified Fit"** (human reviewed), **"Estimated Fit"** (model high confidence), or **"Uncertain – Check Measurements"** (model low confidence or out-of-distribution body type).
*   **Override Mechanism:** Provide a prominent "Report Incorrect Fit" button that immediately flags the interaction for human review and prevents the same recommendation for similar profiles.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific team member as the "Safety Owner" responsible for reviewing every failed probe and user report daily during Fashion Week.
*   **Re-review Trigger:** Any update to the model weights or the addition of new data requires re-running the adversarial probe set before deployment.

**Deliverable Artifacts Required Before Launch:**
1.  **Harm Inventory Document:** Signed off by the team lead.
2.  **Eval Report:** Results of the adversarial probe set with pass/fail status.
3.  **Model Card (Beta Version):** Explicitly stating the data limitation regarding plus-size bodies and the intended use limits.

**Notes:**
*   Do not fabricate a "safety score"; if the data is missing, state the risk is `UNVERIFIED`.
*   Legal counsel must review the specific wording of the beta disclosure to ensure it meets local consumer protection laws.
