**Validation Protocol: Predictive Policing Allocation Audit**

**1. Harm Inventory & Risk Statement**
Before proceeding, we must record the specific risks inherent to this feature:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The algorithm may systematically direct patrols to specific residential areas based on historical arrest data rather than actual crime incidence, reinforcing over-policing in vulnerable communities.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** Heatmaps presented as "objective risk scores" may be interpreted as verified future crime predictions, despite being extrapolations of biased historical records.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Officers may not realize the data lacks current demographic cross-references, leading to false confidence in the neutrality of the output.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** Fully autonomous deployment of resources based on these scores without human contextual review constitutes an irreversible action with potential civil rights implications.

**2. Measurement Plan (Runnable Tests)**
Since updated demographic data is unavailable, we cannot calculate disparate impact metrics directly. Instead, we must run the following adversarial probes and controls. Each test requires a defined stop condition that blocks the council presentation if failed.

*   **Test A: Historical Feedback Loop Probe**
    *   *Input:* Historical data from a known period of documented over-policing in a specific neighborhood vs. a demographically similar neighborhood with lower arrest rates.
    *   *Positive Control:* The model should *not* assign significantly higher risk scores to the over-policed area if the input normalizes for reporting bias (if possible) or should flag the discrepancy.
    *   *Metric:* Disparity ratio in allocated patrol hours between the two areas.
    *   *Threshold:* Owner-defined (e.g., <1.2x disparity).
    *   *Stop Condition:* If the disparity exceeds the threshold, the heatmap generation is blocked.

*   **Test B: Synthetic Demographic Stress Test**
    *   *Input:* Inject synthetic demographic markers into the training proxy data (where legally permissible) or use adjacent public census tracts as a proxy layer.
    *   *Probe:* Run the allocation algorithm against these proxies.
    *   *Metric:* Correlation coefficient between predicted risk and proxy demographic density.
    *   *Threshold:* Zero tolerance for strong positive correlation (>0.5) without explicit justification.
    *   *Stop Condition:* High correlation triggers an immediate "UNVERIFIED" status on the output.

*   **Test C: "Blind" Spot Analysis**
    *   *Input:* Remove all historical crime data for a specific high-income vs. low-income zone; replace with nulls.
    *   *Probe:* Observe if the model imputes high risk based solely on geographic adjacency to historically policed zones.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If risk is imputed purely by proximity to policed areas without incident data, the smoothing algorithm is flawed.

**3. Management Controls & Disclosure**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** No patrol allocation derived solely from this dashboard is to be executed automatically. Command staff must explicitly confirm deployments, documenting the non-algorithmic rationale for any high-intensity allocation.
*   **Uncertainty Rendering:** Do not display precise risk percentages (e.g., "87% risk"). Use defined coarse tiers: **"Corroborated"** (multiple data sources), **"Single-Source"** (historical arrests only), and **"Unverified"** (no demographic cross-check). Color-code these tiers clearly.
*   **Transparency Artifact (Model Card):** You must produce a one-page Model Card for the council. It must state:
    *   *Intended Use:* Resource planning aid only, not probable cause.
    *   *Limitations:* Explicitly state: "Demographic bias testing is UNVERIFIED due to data access restrictions."
    *   *Owner:* Name the specific analyst accountable for the output.

**4. Legal & Governance Routing**
*   **Regulatory Duty:** Whether this system constitutes a "high-risk" AI system under emerging regulations (e.g., EU AI Act principles or local civil rights ordinances) requiring mandatory demographic impact assessments is a legal determination. **Route immediately to legal counsel.** Do not assert compliance.
*   **Decision Authority:** The decision to present this tool to the city council while demographic validation is "UNVERIFIED" rests solely with the department head, not the technical team.

**Note:** If the stop conditions in Step 2 are triggered, the only valid output to the council is a report on the failure, not a diluted version of the heatmap.
**Note:** Never fabricate a "bias score" to satisfy the deadline; mark the metric as UNVERIFIED.
