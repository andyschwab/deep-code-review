**Immediate Risk Assessment & Action Plan**

**Critical Finding:** Deploying an **automated eviction notice generator** without a mandatory human-in-the-loop gate for every single output constitutes an unsafe, high-stakes automation. Eviction is a legally irreversible action with severe consequences for housing security. Full automation of this process, especially using a template engine lacking nuance checks, violates the fundamental safety floor for high-stakes decisions. **You cannot ship this feature as fully automated.**

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before testing, you must record these specific risks:
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** The system executes a legally consequential action (eviction initiation) without human verification of context (e.g., pending hardship applications, payment plans).
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The engine may insert incorrect dates, statute references, or financial figures presented as absolute legal truth.
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Rigid templates may disproportionately harm vulnerable groups (e.g., those with limited English proficiency or cognitive disabilities) by failing to accommodate protected characteristics or reasonable adjustments.
*   **Deceptive Patterns/Harassment:** Aggressive or legally invalid language generated by rigid logic could be interpreted as harassment, violating tenant rights and housing regulations.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Do not assert compliance; measure it. Build a test suite with one row per harm:
*   **Probe Set:** Create inputs representing edge cases: tenants with active hardship claims, partial payments made, known disabilities, and non-standard lease terms. Include "positive controls" (inputs known to trigger invalid notices) and "negative controls" (inputs that should result in no notice or a standard warning).
*   **Metric:** Percentage of outputs flagged by a qualified housing lawyer as "legally invalid" or "potentially harassing."
*   **Threshold:** **Zero tolerance.** Any notice deemed invalid or harassing by counsel must block launch.
*   **Stop Condition:** If *any* test case produces an invalid notice, the automated generation feature is blocked from shipping until the defect is resolved and re-verified.
*   **Injection/Leakage Check:** Verify that user-supplied data (tenant names, amounts) does not leak into unrelated fields or cause template breaking.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** Every single generated notice must be reviewed and explicitly approved by a human property manager or legal officer before being sent to a tenant. The system should *draft* the notice, not *send* it. This is non-negotiable for irreversible actions.
*   **Uncertainty & Override:** Since your engine lacks nuance, it cannot assign confidence scores. Instead, flag any notice generated from non-standard inputs for **mandatory** senior review. Do not display raw confidence percentages to users.
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly label the document as a "Draft Notice Generated by AI" requiring human verification. Whether a specific legal disclosure is *required* under the new housing regulations is a determination for your legal counsel, not an engineering assumption.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific individual (e.g., Head of Legal or Product Lead) who owns the residual risk of every notice sent.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a **Model/Data Card** for this feature documenting: intended use (drafting only), limitations (no nuance check, high risk of error on edge cases), and the mandatory human review process. If this card does not exist, the feature is not ready for transparency.

**Constraint Response:** Your 14-day deadline and existing template engine do not override the requirement for a human gate. If you cannot implement a mandatory human review workflow within 14 days, you must delay the launch of the *automated* aspect. Shipping a fully automated eviction tool without this gate is the failure mode this audit identifies.

**Note:** Route all questions regarding specific legal duties under the new housing regulations to qualified counsel immediately; do not rely on engineering assumptions for regulatory compliance.
No harm metric (e.g., "0% harassment rate") should be claimed without running the above evals; until then, all metrics are UNVERIFIED.
