**Risk Mitigation Strategy: Telehealth Skin Analysis Pilot**

**1. High-Risk Deferral List (Automatic Human Review)**
The system must automatically route the following to a human dermatologist before returning any result to the user:
*   Lesions with irregular borders, multiple colors, or rapid growth characteristics (potential melanoma).
*   Images classified as "low confidence" by the model due to poor lighting, blur, or occlusion.
*   Any upload from a user self-reporting pain, bleeding, or rapid change.
*   Pediatric cases (under 18) and immunocompromised user profiles.

**2. Uncertainty & Escalation Protocol**
*   **Defined Confidence Tiers:** Do not display raw model percentages (e.g., "87% confident"). Instead, use defined text tiers:
    *   *Corroborated:* High agreement with training data; provide preliminary triage advice.
    *   *Single-Source/Uncertain:* Model detects features but lacks high confidence; display: "Analysis inconclusive. Please consult a doctor within 24 hours."
    *   *Unverified:* Image quality insufficient or features ambiguous; display: "Unable to analyze. Immediate human review required."
*   **Escalation Path:** Any "Uncertain" or "Unverified" output triggers an immediate ticket to the on-call medical team. The user receives a notification that a human is reviewing their case, with a guaranteed response window (e.g., 2 hours).

**3. Mandatory Disclaimers & Disclosure**
*   **AI Disclosure:** Every screen must state: "This analysis is generated by an AI assistant, not a doctor. It is for informational triage only and does not constitute a medical diagnosis."
