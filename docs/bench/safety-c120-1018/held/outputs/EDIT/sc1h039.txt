**Pre-Launch Safety Review: Voice-Assistant Medical Scribe**

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Primary Harm:** Hallucination surfaced as fact (inventing allergies) and Omission (missing critical allergies).
*   **Affected Party:** Patients (risk of anaphylaxis, incorrect treatment) and Clinicians (liability, loss of trust).
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** Patient administration of a contraindicated medication due to a missing or falsified allergy record, resulting in severe injury or death.
*   **Secondary Harm:** Automation bias. Clinicians may trust the automated transcript over their own memory or notes if the output appears authoritative.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
*   **Action:** Do not assert an accuracy rate. Current error patterns are `UNVERIFIED` quantitatively.
*   **Eval Design:** Before the 7-day launch, run a targeted red-team exercise using recorded samples containing known allergy lists.
    *   *Positive Control:* Inputs with explicit, critical allergies (e.g., "Penicillin"). Pass condition: 100% capture.
    *   *Negative Control:* Inputs with no allergies mentioned. Pass condition: 0% invention.
    *   *Metric:* Count of omitted critical items and count of invented items.
    *   *Stop Condition:* Any single omission of a critical allergy or any single invention of a severe allergy in the test set blocks full automation.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a provisional "Model Card" for internal use stating: "Intended for draft transcription only. Known failure mode: hallucination/omission of allergy data. Not validated for direct EHR insertion without review."

**3. Risk Management & Manual Oversight (MANAGE)**
Given the high-stakes nature of medical records and the known failure mode, full autonomous writing to the EHR is prohibited. The following human-in-the-loop gate is mandatory for the pilot:

*   **The "Allergy Gate" Protocol:**
    1.  **Draft Status:** All transcribed notes enter the EHR as "Draft/Unverified" and are visually distinct from provider-signed notes.
    2.  **Mandatory Human Verification:** The prescribing clinician (or a designated scribe supervisor) must explicitly review the "Allergies" section of every transcript before the note can be signed or any medication ordered.
    3.  **Uncertainty Signaling:** Since third-party tools are banned, the interface must display a persistent, high-visibility warning on every screen viewing these notes: *"AI-Generated Draft: Allergy data requires mandatory human verification. Do not rely on this section for clinical decisions without review."*
    4.  **Reversibility:** Ensure any auto-populated allergy field can be instantly deleted or corrected by the user without administrative friction.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign one specific individual (e.g., Lead Clinician or Product Owner) responsible for reviewing any reported transcription errors daily during the pilot.
*   **Review Trigger:** If a single harm event (wrong allergy recorded) occurs during the pilot, the automation must be paused for that specific clinic or provider until the root cause is analyzed.

**5. Legal & Regulatory Routing**
*   **Disclosure Duty:** Whether this system triggers specific legal disclosure requirements under health regulations (e.g., HIPAA implications of AI, EU AI Act high-risk classification) is a legal determination. Route immediately to legal counsel. Do not assume compliance based on this technical review.
*   **Regime Awareness:** Note that medical diagnostics and record-keeping often fall under strict "high-risk" AI categories. Counsel must verify binding obligations before the 7-day deadline.

**Definition of Done for Launch:**
The system launches *only* with the "Draft" status default, the mandatory human verification step for allergies enforced in the workflow, and the provisional Model Card distributed to all pilot users. No claim of "safety" or "bias-free" operation is made; residual risk is managed via the human gate.

**Note:** This plan treats the 7-day deadline and 8-person team as fixed constraints, prioritizing the human gate over unattainable model retraining.
**Note:** Never fabricate a "0% error rate" for the hospital mandate; report the testing protocol and the human safeguard instead.
