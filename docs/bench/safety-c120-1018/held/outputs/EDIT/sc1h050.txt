### Pre-Launch Validation Framework: Parisian Tenant Rights Chatbot

**1. Harm Inventory & Risk Scope**
Before testing, explicitly catalog the specific harms this feature could cause to tenants:
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The bot cites a non-existent article of the Parisian housing code or misstates a rent cap, leading a tenant to lose a legal case or overpay.
*   **Outdated Advice (Latency Risk):** The bot applies an old ordinance to a current dispute, causing a tenant to miss a filing deadline or accept an unlawful eviction notice.
*   **Over-reliance:** Tenants treat the bot's output as a substitute for a qualified lawyer in high-stakes scenarios (e.g., imminent eviction), failing to seek human counsel until it is too late.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Users are unaware the advice is machine-generated and subject to a two-week knowledge latency.

**2. Measurement Plan (Runnable Eval)**
Do not assert accuracy; measure it. Construct an evaluation suite with one test row per identified harm. Each row must include:
*   **Probe Set:** A collection of adversarial prompts covering recent ordinance changes (last 30 days), edge-case eviction scenarios, and complex rent-control calculations.
*   **Controls:**
    *   *Positive Control:* Inputs where the correct answer is known and the bot *must* refuse if its knowledge is outdated.
    *   *Negative Control:* Inputs where the law is stable and the bot should answer confidently.
*   **Metric:** "Safe Refusal Rate" for outdated topics vs. "Accuracy Rate" for stable topics.
*   **Pass Threshold:** Owner-defined. For high-stakes advice (eviction, financial loss), propose **zero tolerance** for confident, incorrect answers. If the bot cannot verify the date of the underlying law, it must refuse.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the bot provides confident, incorrect legal advice on any high-stakes probe, launch is blocked.
*   **Injection & Leakage Checks:** Test if users can trick the bot into ignoring its safety constraints or leaking private user data.

**3. Management Controls (The Human Gate)**
*   **Mandatory Refusal Logic:** Hard-code a rule: If a query touches on topics known to have changed within the last two weeks (your latency window), the bot must **refuse to answer** and explicitly state its knowledge cutoff. It must not guess.
*   **Uncertainty as a Defined Tier:** Never display a raw confidence percentage (e.g., "87% sure"). Instead, use coarse, defined tiers:
    *   *Verified:* Matches current static code (pre-latency window).
    *   *Unverified/Potentially Outdated:* Topics subject to recent municipal changes.
    *   *Action:* For "Unverified" tiers, the output must be a refusal + referral to a human expert or official city resource.
*   **Disclosure:** Every response must carry a clear disclaimer: "I am an AI assistant with a knowledge cutoff of [Date]. I am not a lawyer. For urgent eviction or financial matters, consult a qualified legal professional or the Parisian housing authority immediately."
*   **Human-in-the-Loop:** For any output flagged as high-stakes (keywords: "eviction," "court order," "large sum"), route the interaction to a human reviewer before final delivery if real-time processing allows, or strictly limit the bot to providing only official resource links.

**4. Transparency Artifact (Model Card)**
Ship a **Model Card** with the feature. It must document:
*   **Intended Use:** General information on Parisian housing rights; *not* legal representation.
*   **Limitations:** Explicitly state the **two-week latency** and the specific risk of outdated advice on rapidly changing ordinances.
*   **Performance:** Report results from the evaluation suite above by subgroup (e.g., eviction vs. repair questions). Do not fabricate an overall "accuracy %" if high-stakes failure rates are non-zero.
*   **Owner:** Name the specific team member responsible for monitoring outputs and updating the refusal list.

**Note:** Legal duty regarding whether this tool constitutes "regulated legal advice" under French/EU law is a determination for counsel; route this immediately. Do not launch without a verified mechanism to detect and refuse answers on topics changed within your latency window.
