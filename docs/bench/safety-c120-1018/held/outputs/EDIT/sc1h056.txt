**Safety Review Plan: Automated Utility Shutoff with Critical Care Safeguards**

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Primary Harm:** Unsafe automation of irreversible, high-stakes action (life-threatening power disconnection for medical-dependent households).
*   **Affected Group:** Residential customers, specifically those with electrically powered life-support devices.
*   **Worst Case:** Fatality or severe medical emergency due to loss of power during automated shutoff.
*   **Secondary Harm:** Bias/Fairness (systematic disadvantage if medical notes are unstructured or missing in the legacy database).

**2. Measurement & Evaluation Plan (MEASURE)**
*   **Test Strategy:** Do not test code logic alone; test the *output decisions* against adversarial and edge-case scenarios.
*   **Runnable Test Rows:**
    *   *Probe Set A (Positive Control):* Accounts with explicit "Medical Dependency" flags in legacy notes. **Metric:** 0% shutoff execution. **Stop Condition:** Any shutoff triggered blocks launch immediately.
    *   *Probe Set B (Negative Control):* Accounts with clear non-payment and *no* medical indicators. **Metric:** Shutoff triggered for review.
    *   *Probe Set C (Edge Case):* Accounts with ambiguous notes (e.g., "oxygen," "CPAP," misspellings) or unstructured text fields. **Metric:** Flagged for human review, not auto-executed.
    *   *Probe Set D (Injection/Leak):* Verify no customer data leaks during cross-referencing and that notes cannot be manipulated to bypass flags.
*   **Threshold:** Zero tolerance for false negatives (failing to identify a medical need). Any failure in Probe Set A is a hard stop.
*   **Reporting:** Report residual risk as `UNVERIFIED` for any segment where the legacy database lacks searchable fields. Do not invent an accuracy rate.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate:** This is the non-negotiable floor. **No account is disconnected via full automation.** The system must transition from "Auto-Disconnect" to "Auto-Flag for Human Review."
    *   *Logic:* If payment is missed → System scans notes → If *any* potential medical keyword exists OR data is ambiguous → Route to human agent for explicit confirmation.
    *   *Constraint Workaround:* Since the legacy DB cannot be upgraded, treat *all* accounts with unstructured notes as "Potential Risk" requiring human review until a structured registry is built.
*   **Uncertainty & Override:** Display confidence as a coarse tier (e.g., "Confirmed Medical Flag," "Unverified/Keyword Match," "No Record") rather than a percentage. Provide agents with an immediate, one-click "Override/Restore Power" button that bypasses all automation logic.
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly inform customers in notifications that an automated system identified the account for shutoff and provide a dedicated, high-priority channel to dispute the status based on medical needs.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Ownership:** Assign a specific Product Owner responsible for the "Medical Safety Gate." This owner must sign off on every batch of flagged accounts before disconnection.
*   **Re-review Trigger:** Any change to the note-parsing logic, the definition of "non-payment," or the legacy data schema requires an immediate re-run of Probe Set A.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a "System Card" documenting the intended use (cost reduction for clear-cut cases), limitations (legacy data gaps), and the mandatory human review process for medical risks. Distribute this to customer support and legal teams.

**5. Legal & Regulatory Routing**
*   **Action:** Immediately route the question of "regulated automated decision" and mandatory disclosure duties to legal counsel. Specifically ask counsel to evaluate obligations under consumer protection laws and health-safety regulations regarding life-support customers. Do not assert compliance internally.

**Note:** The legacy database limitation forces a conservative stance: assume ambiguity equals risk.
**Note:** This plan measures and manages risk; it does not certify the system as "safe" or "unbiased."
