### Pre-Deployment Review Process: StreamManila Content Engine

**1. Immediate Harm Inventory & Scope Definition**
Before deploying any fix, explicitly document the specific failure mode: the system currently amplifies extremist content to vulnerable users due to an inability to distinguish news reporting from propaganda. Define the "worst realistic case" as the radicalization of a user segment within the two-week window. Acknowledge that the current sentiment model is `UNVERIFIED` for this specific distinction; do not assert it is "safe" or "accurate."

**2. The Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate (Non-Negotiable)**
Because the stakes involve potential real-world harm (radicalization) and the automated tool is known to be imperfect, **fully automated promotion of borderline content must stop immediately.**
*   **Action:** Implement a mandatory human review queue for any content flagged by the sentiment model as "potentially extremist" or falling within a defined confidence threshold where the model historically fails.
*   **Constraint:** Do not auto-publish high-risk content. If the team cannot staff enough reviewers to clear the queue within the engagement window, the system must default to *not* recommending the content rather than risking amplification. The failure mode is "over-blocking legitimate news," not "under-blocking extremism."

**3. Measurement Plan: Adversarial Probes, Not Aggregate Metrics**
Do not rely on overall accuracy metrics. Construct a specific evaluation set for the two-week patch:
*   **Positive Controls:** A curated list of known extremist propaganda pieces previously misclassified as news.
*   **Negative Controls:** A curated list of legitimate news reports on extremism previously misclassified as propaganda.
*   **Probe Strategy:** Run the current model against these specific sets. If the model cannot distinguish them with a margin of safety defined by your human reviewers, the automated component must be disabled for those content clusters entirely.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the human review queue grows beyond the team's capacity to review within 24 hours, or if a single piece of known extremist content slips through the gate during testing, the deployment halts.

**4. Transparency and Uncertainty Disclosure**
*   **Internal:** Clearly label the output of the sentiment model as "Unverified/Experimental" in all internal dashboards. Do not present model confidence scores (e.g., "87% safe") as facts; use coarse tiers like "Requires Review" or "Likely Safe."
*   **External:** If the recommendation logic changes significantly (e.g., reduced volume of certain topics), consider a transparent communication strategy about prioritizing safety over pure engagement, without revealing exploit details.

**5. Governance and Ownership**
*   **Owner:** Assign a single product owner responsible for the daily sign-off of the human review guidelines.
*   **Legal Route:** Immediately route the question of "regulated decision" status and potential liability for radicalization impacts to legal counsel. Do not assume the current patch satisfies regulatory duties (e.g., EU AI Act implications for high-risk systems); let counsel define the binding requirements.
*   **Artifact:** Create a temporary "Model Card" for this patch version documenting the known limitation (news vs. propaganda), the HITL workaround, and the specific evaluation results from the probe set.

**6. Deployment Strategy**
Execute a staged rollout. Enable the new review process for a small user segment (e.g., 5%) first. Monitor for any escalation in reported harmful content. Only expand exposure if the human gate proves effective and sustainable. If the tooling constraint remains unresolvable by the deadline, the only safe path is to disable the automated recommendation for the affected content categories entirely until a robust model is available.

**Note:** This plan prioritizes preventing harm over maintaining full automation velocity. The "imperfect tool" cannot be the sole decision-maker for high-stakes content.
**Note:** Legal counsel must validate if this specific risk profile triggers mandatory reporting or disclosure obligations in your operating regions.
