**Immediate Action Plan: Internal Bias Detection & Risk Mitigation**

Given your hard constraint (no data export) and the high-stakes nature of automated hiring decisions, you cannot "verify" the system is unbiased before launch. No test proves fairness. Instead, you must measure residual risk, implement a human gate, and disclose limitations. Execute this workflow within your sandbox immediately:

**1. Map the Harm Inventory (Do this today)**
Formally record the specific risk: **Bias/Unfair Outcomes**.
*   **Affected Group:** Applicants from specific neighborhoods or demographic segments.
*   **Worst Case:** Systematic exclusion of qualified workers from protected groups, leading to legal liability and reputational damage.
*   **Status:** Until measured, this risk is **UNVERIFIED**. Do not assert a "0% bias" rate.

**2. Measure via Synthetic Probes (The Sandbox Strategy)**
Since you cannot export real data, you must inject **synthetic test cases** into your sandbox that mirror real applicant diversity.
*   **Construct Paired Profiles:** Create identical applicant profiles varying *only* one protected attribute at a time (e.g., same experience/skills, but different zip codes associated with specific demographics).
*   **Run Positive/Negative Controls:**
    *   *Positive Control:* A profile explicitly designed to pass (high score) across all groups.
    *   *Negative Control:* A profile explicitly designed to fail (low score) across all groups.
*   **Metric & Threshold:** Calculate the **disparate impact ratio** (selection rate of protected group / selection rate of reference group).
    *   *Stop Condition:* If the ratio falls below 0.8 (the "four-fifths rule" heuristic) or any owner-defined threshold, **block launch** for that segment.
*   **Owner:** Assign one engineer to own this eval suite. They must review every failure manually.

**3. Manage Residual Risk (The Human Gate)**
Because this is a high-stakes, irreversible decision (denying work opportunities) and your testing is limited to synthetic sandbox data:
*   **Implement a Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate:** You cannot fully automate rejections at launch. Any application flagged as "borderline" or falling from a protected neighborhood must trigger a **manual review** by a human recruiter before a final "no interview" decision is sent.
*   **Surface Uncertainty:** If the system's confidence in a rejection is not absolute, display this internally to the reviewer. Do not show applicants a raw confidence percentage (e.g., "87% match"); use defined tiers like "High Match," "Review Needed," or "Low Match."

**4. Transparency Artifact (Model Card)**
Before deployment, draft a **Model Card** for internal and regulatory use. It must state:
*   **Intended Use:** Filtering gig-worker applications.
*   **Limitations:** Explicitly note that testing was performed on synthetic data within a sandbox due to technical constraints; real-world performance is **UNVERIFIED**.
*   **Known Gaps:** List the demographic slices tested and those where data was insufficient.

**5. Legal Routing**
Automated hiring decisions are heavily regulated (e.g., EU AI Act, local labor laws).
*   **Action:** Immediately route the question of legal disclosure duties and regulated decision status to **legal counsel**. Do not assume compliance based on internal testing.
*   **Regime Awareness:** Note that regimes like the EU AI Act may classify this as "high-risk," requiring specific conformity assessments you cannot self-certify.

**Summary of Deliverables for Launch:**
1.  Completed Harm Inventory document.
2.  Sandbox eval report with disparate impact ratios (marked UNVERIFIED if real-data validation is missing).
3.  Active HITL workflow for borderline/protected-group rejections.
4.  Draft Model Card acknowledging testing limitations.
5.  Ticket assigned to Legal for regulatory determination.

**Note:** Do not remove the human gate to meet the holiday rush deadline; doing so converts a manageable risk into a guaranteed failure mode.
**Note:** If legal counsel determines this is a regulated automated decision, you may be legally prohibited from launching without external audit, regardless of internal sandbox results.
