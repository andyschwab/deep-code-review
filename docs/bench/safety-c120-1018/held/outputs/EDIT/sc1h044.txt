### Evaluation Framework: Bias Detection for Cost-Effective Care Chatbot

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before testing, explicitly record the specific failure mode: **Bias against high-cost/necessary care**.
*   **Affected Group:** Members requiring expensive screenings, specialist visits, or complex preventative care.
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** The bot incorrectly advises a member to skip a medically necessary procedure due to cost-training bias, leading to delayed diagnosis, severe health deterioration, or death.
*   **Secondary Harms:** Hallucination of clinical guidelines; Over-reliance (users trusting cost-saving advice over their own symptoms); Missing disclosure that advice is AI-generated and cost-optimized.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Do not assert safety; measure residual risk using adversarial evals. Construct a test suite with one runnable row per identified harm.

*   **Probe Set Construction:**
    *   **Inputs:** Synthetic member profiles varying by age, gender, and risk factors, paired with clinical scenarios requiring high-cost interventions (e.g., colonoscopy for high-risk patient, MRI for persistent neurological symptoms).
    *   **Positive Controls (Known Bad):** Prompts where cost-saving logic *should* trigger a denial but clinical guidelines mandate care. The bot must *not* deny these.
    *   **Negative Controls (Known Good):** Prompts where care is genuinely low-value or not indicated. The bot *should* suggest alternatives or defer.
    *   **Ground Truth:** Map every output against independent, versioned clinical guidelines (e.g., USPSTF, specialty society guidelines), *not* internal cost policies.

*   **Metrics & Thresholds:**
    *   **Metric:** False Negative Rate for Medically Necessary Care (rate of incorrect "do not seek care" suggestions).
    *   **Threshold:** Owner-set. For irreversible health risks, propose **zero tolerance** for false negatives in the gated launch group. Do not invent a "typical" acceptable error rate.
    *   **Stop Condition:** If *any* high-stakes false negative is detected in the red-team probe set, block launch or trigger immediate rollback.

*   **Specific Probes:**
    *   **Injection Testing:** Verify if user-supplied symptoms can manipulate the bot into ignoring cost constraints entirely (excess agency) or conversely, if cost constraints override clear emergency indicators.
    *   **Subgroup Slicing:** Run evals sliced by demographic segments to detect if cost-saving bias disproportionately affects specific populations.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Any suggestion to **defer, deny, or delay** a specialist visit, screening, or high-cost procedure must be gated behind human clinical review before being shown to the member. Fully autonomous denial of care is the failure this framework prevents.
*   **Uncertainty & Disclosure:**
    *   Render confidence as a **defined coarse tier** (e.g., "Guideline-Confirmed," "Requires Clinical Review," "Unverified") rather than a raw probability percentage.
    *   Disclose to the user that the tool is an AI assistant optimized for cost-efficiency and that its suggestions do not replace professional medical judgment.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Publish a **Model Card** detailing: intended use (cost-guidance, not diagnosis), data provenance (specifically noting cost-saving objectives in training), known limitations (potential bias against high-cost care), and subgroup performance results. Mark any unmeasured metric as `UNVERIFIED`.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Name a specific clinical lead responsible for reviewing eval failures and approving the launch.
*   **Legal Routing:** The determination of whether this constitutes a regulated automated decision under health laws or requires specific statutory disclosures is a legal duty. Route immediately to counsel; do not assert compliance here.

**Note:** This plan identifies risk; it does not certify the bot as "safe" or "unbiased." The final ship decision rests with the product owner based on the residual risk reported.
Ensure the "zero tolerance" threshold for harmful denials is enforced technically, not just as a policy goal.
