### Immediate Action Plan: Harm Inventory, Measurement, and Governance

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before launch, explicitly record these high-stakes risks for your emerging democracy context:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Systematic suppression of minority party platforms or local dialects due to training data gaps, silencing vital civic discourse.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** Generating or prioritizing fabricated election details presented with authority.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Users unaware that feed prioritization is automated, leading to misplaced trust in the curation logic.
*   **Unsafe Automation:** Irreversible impact on voter perception without a human gate for high-visibility topics.
*   **Worst Case:** The platform inadvertently alters election outcomes by hiding critical information from specific demographic groups.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Do not assert the algorithm is "fair." Instead, execute these specific evaluations:
*   **Probe Set A (Representation):** Inject known posts from minority parties and various dialects. Measure ranking position against majority content.
    *   *Metric:* Relative Rank Difference.
    *   *Threshold:* Owner-defined (e.g., no >20% deviation).
    *   *Stop Condition:* If minority content consistently ranks below a visibility floor, block launch.
*   **Probe Set B (Integrity):** Inject known false election claims (positive control) and verified facts (negative control).
    *   *Metric:* False Positive Rate (hallucination/promotion of falsehoods).
    *   *Threshold:* Zero tolerance for promoting known falsehoods as top-tier news.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Publish a **Model Card** detailing the known data gaps (dialects, minority parties), intended use, and these measured limitations. Mark unmeasured metrics as `UNVERIFIED`.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Given the three-week deadline and high stakes, fully autonomous ranking for "election-critical" tags is unsafe. Implement a mandatory human review queue for any content flagged as election-related until post-launch stability is proven.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** Do not show raw confidence scores (e.g., "92% relevant"). Use defined coarse tiers (e.g., "Verified Source," "Single Report," "Unverified") with clear definitions to prevent over-reliance.
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly label the feed as "Automated Curation" to users. Route the specific legal requirement for AI disclosure in this jurisdiction to counsel immediately; do not assume compliance based on engineering judgment.

**4. Monitoring Dashboard Design**
Build the dashboard to track these real-time signals, not just system uptime:
*   **Distribution Watch:** Real-time histogram of source diversity and dialect representation. Alert if any group drops below a baseline percentage.
*   **Anomaly Detection:** Spike detection for specific political entities suddenly disappearing from the feed.
*   **Human Override Rate:** Track how often human reviewers reject the algorithm's top picks. A high rate indicates a broken model.
*   **Kill Switch:** A manual override to revert to a chronological, un-ranked feed if bias metrics breach thresholds.

**Governance:** Assign a specific owner (not the whole team) responsible for reviewing these metrics daily. If the data gap regarding dialects cannot be fixed before the election, the residual risk must be explicitly accepted by leadership with a mitigation plan (e.g., manual injection of missing content), not ignored.
