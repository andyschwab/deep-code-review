### Product Output Safety Review: Refugee-Housing Matcher

**Immediate Directive:** Do not enable fully automated assignments for this feature. Given the high-stakes nature of housing vulnerable families and the known gap in structural integrity data, any match involving buildings with unverified or outdated safety records must trigger a mandatory human-in-the-loop gate. Full automation of this action constitutes a critical safety failure.

#### 1. Harm Inventory (MAP)
Before proceeding, record these specific risks for your feature:
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** The system assigns a family to a structurally unsafe building due to outdated data, causing physical harm.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The system presents a match as "Safety Verified" when the underlying data is merely "Last Updated 2019" without current validation.
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Older buildings (more likely to have data gaps) are disproportionately assigned to specific demographic groups if the algorithm optimizes for speed over verification status.
*   **Over-Reliance:** Caseworkers trust the "Match Score" without noticing the missing structural data flag.

#### 2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)
You cannot certify the system as "safe." Instead, execute these evaluations before launch:
*   **Adversarial Probe:** Run a test set of known unsafe buildings (positive controls) and known safe buildings (negative controls) through the pipeline.
*   **Metric:** The system must flag 100% of positive controls as "Requires Manual Review." Any automated assignment of a positive control is a hard stop.
*   **Data Gap Sweep:** Specifically test inputs where structural records are >2 years old. The output must never be a confident "Safe" recommendation; it must default to "Unverified/Review Needed."
*   **Reporting:** Publish a **Model Card** and **Data Card**. Explicitly state in the Data Card that structural integrity data is incomplete for older buildings. Mark any safety metric derived from this incomplete data as `UNVERIFIED`. Do not invent a "99% safe" statistic.

#### 3. Risk Management & Manual Verification Checklist (MANAGE)
Implement a **Human-in-the-Loop Gate** for all matches where structural data is not current (e.g., updated within the last 12 months). The system should generate a "High-Risk Match Report" for human review, not an automatic assignment.

**Manual Verification Checklist (Required before assignment):**
1.  **Source Recency Check:** Confirm the date of the last structural integrity inspection in the city database. If >12 months, proceed to step 2.
2.  **Physical/Direct Verification:** Contact the building manager or city inspector for a current status report. Do not rely on the database alone.
3.  **Visual/Documentary Evidence:** Require upload of a recent safety certificate or photos of critical structural elements (foundation, roof, load-bearing walls) if available.
4.  **Uncertainty Disclosure:** When presenting the match to the caseworker, display a clear warning: *"Structural data outdated. Safety not algorithmically verified. Manual confirmation required."* Use a coarse tier label like **"Unverified"** rather than a numerical confidence score.
5.  **Explicit Confirmation:** The caseworker must manually check a box stating "I have verified current structural safety" before the assignment is finalized.

#### 4. Governance (GOVERN)
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific individual (e.g., "Head of Housing Safety") as the owner of this risk.
*   **Re-review Trigger:** This process must be re-evaluated immediately if the city database updates its frequency or if any incident occurs.
*   **Legal Routing:** Consult counsel regarding disclosure duties to refugee families about how matches are generated and the limitations of the data. Do not assert legal compliance internally; route this determination.

**Note:** A shipped AI feature without a Model/Data card detailing these data gaps is a transparency failure. Never fabricate a safety metric to satisfy stakeholder pressure; report residual risk as `UNVERIFIED` where data is missing.
