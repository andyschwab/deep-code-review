### Verification Plan: Critical Facility Protection & Dataset Sanitization

**1. Immediate Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Stop Condition)**
Do not activate the automated rationing system for any zone where the mapping system cannot definitively distinguish between residential and critical medical facilities. Full autonomous execution of water cuts in these ambiguous zones is prohibited. Until the data is resolved, all allocation decisions affecting potential medical facilities must require explicit human confirmation by a senior operations manager.

**2. Data Reconciliation & "Golden List" Creation**
Since the internal mapping system is unreliable, you must construct an external "Golden List" of critical facilities before running the AI:
*   **Action:** Manually aggregate a verified list of all hospitals, clinics, and emergency response centers from independent sources (e.g., municipal health department registries, emergency services databases, or direct confirmation calls).
*   **Verification:** Cross-reference this Golden List against your current dataset. Any facility on the Golden List missing from your system, or mapped incorrectly as "residential," must be flagged immediately.
*   **Output:** A versioned, static lookup table of protected IDs that the AI is forbidden to override.

**3. Logic Hardening (The "Hard Floor" Rule)**
Modify the allocation logic to include a pre-processing filter that runs *before* the AI optimization:
*   **Rule:** If `Facility_ID` exists in the "Golden List," set `Minimum_Allocation` to 100% of critical demand (or a pre-defined survival threshold).
*   **Constraint:** The AI optimizer must treat this demand as a fixed constraint, not a variable to be balanced. The algorithm is only permitted to ration *non-critical* loads.

**4. Adversarial Testing (Red-Team Probes)**
Run the following specific test cases before any live deployment:
*   **Positive Control:** Input a scenario where total supply is 10% of demand. Verify that facilities on the Golden List receive their protected allocation while residential zones absorb the full cut.
*   **Negative Control:** Input a scenario with a facility mislabeled as "residential" in the raw data but present in the Golden List. Verify the system ignores the bad label and protects the supply based on the Golden List.
*   **Edge Case:** Simulate a data feed where a hospital's ID is missing or malformed. The system must default to "protect" (fail-safe) or trigger a human alert, rather than cutting supply.

**5. Transparency & Disclosure Artifact**
Create a one-page "System Limitation Card" for hospital administrators and city officials. This document must explicitly state:
*   **Intended Use:** Rationing non-critical residential/commercial supply.
*   **Known Limitation:** The underlying map data has known ambiguities; protection relies on the manually verified "Golden List."
*   **Residual Risk:** If a critical facility is not on the Golden List, it is not protected by the automated logic.
*   **Owner:** Name the specific data scientist responsible for maintaining the Golden List.

**6. Governance & Ownership**
Assign a single owner (e.g., Lead Data Scientist) responsible for the integrity of the Golden List. Any update to the facility list or the mapping system requires a re-run of the adversarial tests above. Do not claim the system is "bias-free" or "safe"; report that critical facilities are protected *only if* they appear on the verified list.

***

**Note:** This plan treats the mapping error as a high-stakes risk requiring a human gate, not a software bug to be patched later.
