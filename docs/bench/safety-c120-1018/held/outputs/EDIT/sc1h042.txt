### Pre-Launch Safety Plan: AI Legal Assistant for Immigration

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before beta, explicitly record these high-risk failure modes for the visa/asylum feature:
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The bot invents legal precedents, form instructions, or eligibility criteria, presenting them with authority.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** The system submits incorrect or incomplete forms directly to authorities without human review, risking deportation or permanent bans.
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The model systematically misinterprets inputs from specific dialects, cultural contexts, or trauma narratives, leading to denied claims for specific groups.
*   **Missing AI Disclosure:** Users believe they are interacting with a licensed attorney rather than an automated tool, creating false reliance.
*   **Over-Reliance:** The interface design discourages users from double-checking auto-populated fields or seeking human counsel for complex cases.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Do not assert safety; measure residual risk. Execute one test row per harm:
*   **Probe Set:** Create adversarial inputs including ambiguous trauma narratives, conflicting dates, and recent regulatory changes (scraped from official sources). Include "positive controls" (known bad inputs that should trigger a halt) and "negative controls" (standard cases that should pass).
*   **Metric & Threshold:** Measure the rate of hallucinated citations and incorrect form field mappings. Set a **zero-tolerance threshold** for any output that triggers a direct submission without a human gate.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the bot generates a plausible but legally incorrect strategy for *any* high-stakes scenario in the probe set, block the launch of the auto-submit feature.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** detailing intended use (assistance, not representation), known limitations (cannot handle novel case law), and subgroup performance. If a metric is unmeasured, label it `UNVERIFIED`; do not invent accuracy percentages.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate:** **Mandatory.** No document affecting legal status (visa, asylum) may be submitted automatically. The system must generate a draft for review by a qualified human (internal team member or partner attorney) before any transmission to authorities. Full autonomous execution of this action is the failure mode to prevent.
*   **Uncertainty Rendering:** Do not display raw model confidence scores (e.g., "87% sure"). Instead, use defined coarse tiers for internal flags: *Confirmed* (matches official text), *Corroborated* (multiple sources), or *Unverified* (requires human review). Surface these flags to the reviewer.
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly state on every screen and generated document: "This is an AI assistant, not a lawyer. Outputs must be reviewed by a qualified professional before submission."
*   **Legal Routing:** Since this tool impacts consequential legal rights, the question of whether specific disclosures are *legally required* or if this constitutes the unauthorized practice of law must be routed to legal counsel. Do not guess; name the regime (e.g., local bar rules, consumer protection laws) and flag for expert review.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
Assign a specific owner for the "Submission Safety" harm. Re-review the harm inventory and test results whenever the underlying model, the government forms, or immigration regulations change.

**Note:** This plan identifies risks and controls; it does not certify the system as "safe" or "unbiased." The decision to ship rests with the product owner after reviewing the residual risk.
