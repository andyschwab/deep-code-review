### Pre-Launch Safety Strategy: Mortgage-Refinance Bot

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before testing, explicitly record the specific risks for this feature:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The bot systematically recommends higher-cost or predatory loans to users with low income/high debt compared to wealthy users with similar credit profiles.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** The bot pushes a user toward refinancing that increases their long-term debt burden or risks foreclosure without human verification.
*   **Over-reliance:** Users trust the "suggested loan" as the only viable option, missing better alternatives not in the model's training data.
*   **Missing Disclosure:** Users do not realize the suggestion is an automated estimation, not personalized financial advice from a licensed advisor.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Since privacy laws prevent using real financial histories, you must construct **Synthetic Adversarial Personas**. Create one test row per harm:

*   **Probe Set:** Generate 50+ synthetic user profiles representing edge cases (e.g., "High Debt/Low Income," "Near-Retirement," "Variable Income Gig Worker"). Include "Positive Controls" (profiles known to trigger bad offers in similar systems) and "Negative Controls" (profiles known to be safe).
*   **Metric:** Calculate the "Stability Score" for each recommendation. Does the suggested loan lower monthly payments without extending the term excessively? Does it increase total interest paid by >15%?
*   **Threshold:** Set a **zero-tolerance** threshold for any recommendation that increases the user's risk of default based on the synthetic profile's cash flow.
*   **Stop Condition:** If >5% of vulnerable personas receive a "predatory" classification (higher cost/risk than current loan), **block launch**.
*   **Injection & Leakage Checks:** Test if malicious prompts can force the bot to ignore risk parameters (prompt injection) or if it accidentally reveals data from other synthetic users.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Do not allow the bot to auto-submit applications or finalize recommendations for users flagged as "high debt/low income." These cases must route to a human loan officer for review before any offer is presented.
*   **Uncertainty & Disclosure:** Never display a raw confidence percentage (e.g., "92% match"). Instead, use defined tiers: **"Verified Fit"** (meets all stability criteria), **"Potential Fit"** (requires human review), or **"Unverified"** (insufficient data).
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** for internal and external use. It must state: intended use (pre-screening only), limitations (not financial advice), known failure modes (struggles with irregular income), and the fact that testing used synthetic data due to privacy constraints. Mark any unmeasured metrics as `UNVERIFIED`.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific Product Manager as the owner of the "Predatory Recommendation" risk.
*   **Re-review Trigger:** Mandate a re-evaluation if the underlying loan products change, the model is updated, or if real-world user complaints regarding suitability exceed a set limit.

**Legal Routing Note:**
Whether this bot constitutes "regulated financial advice" requiring specific legal disclosures under Ontario/Canadian law is a determination for **legal counsel**, not this review. Route this question immediately to your legal team alongside the draft Model Card. Do not assert compliance; only recommend disclosure as a safety control.
