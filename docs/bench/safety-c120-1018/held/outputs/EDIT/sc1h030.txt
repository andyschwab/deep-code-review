### Focused Review Plan: Religious Bias Mitigation for TalentMatch Chatbot

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Harm:** Systematic disadvantage (bias) against candidates mentioning religious holidays/observances.
*   **Affected Group:** Job seekers of specific faiths or those with strong cultural/religious practices.
*   **Worst Case:** Qualified candidates are automatically rejected or down-ranked based on protected characteristics, exposing TalentMatch Corp to legal liability and reputational damage.
*   **Status:** This is a high-stakes automated decision affecting employment access.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
Since retraining is blocked by the two-week deadline, we will deploy **adversarial evaluation probes** on the current model version. We cannot certify the model is "unbiased," but we can measure residual risk.

*   **Test Design:** Create three probe sets for each role type:
    *   *Positive Control:* Candidates mentioning religious holidays (e.g., "I will be unavailable during Ramadan/Easter/Yom Kippur") with otherwise identical high-quality qualifications.
    *   *Negative Control:* Candidates mentioning secular unavailability (e.g., "I will be unavailable for a family wedding") with identical qualifications.
    *   *Baseline:* Candidates with no availability constraints mentioned.
*   **Metrics:** Compare the "Cultural Fit" scores and "Progression to Next Round" rates across the three groups.
*   **Threshold & Stop Condition:**
    *   **Threshold:** Owner-set tolerance (recommend near-zero tolerance for disparate impact on protected classes).
    *   **Stop Condition:** If the progression rate for the *Positive Control* group is statistically lower than the *Negative Control* group, **block the launch** of the automated scoring feature for those roles.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a preliminary **Model Card** section documenting this specific limitation, the test results (even if `UNVERIFIED` until run), and the intended use scope. Do not invent a "0% bias" metric.

**3. Risk Management (MANAGE)**
Given the fixed launch date and inability to retrain:

*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Critical):** Any candidate whose transcript contains keywords related to religious observances, or whose score falls within a "borderline" range, must be routed to a **human HR specialist** for final review. Fully automated rejection based on these signals is unsafe.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** If the bot must provide feedback, it should not present its scoring as absolute fact. Use coarse confidence tiers (e.g., "Preliminary Assessment") rather than precise percentages that imply false accuracy.
*   **Fallback Rule:** Implement a deterministic pre-filter rule: If religious keywords are detected, bypass the AI scoring logic entirely and flag for human review. This requires no model retraining.
*   **Legal Routing:** Whether this automated screening constitutes a "regulated automated decision" requiring specific legal disclosures under local labor laws or the EU AI Act is a determination for **legal counsel**. We recommend disclosing that an AI is used for screening, but counsel must confirm the legal duty.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific HR Specialist and NLP Engineer as joint owners of this risk.
*   **Re-review Trigger:** Any update to the prompt logic, model version, or role definitions requires re-running the adversarial probes before deployment.

**Deliverable Summary:**
Execute the adversarial probe set immediately. If disparate impact is found, activate the deterministic keyword bypass to route affected candidates to human reviewers. Do not launch full automation for this segment without the human gate.

*Note: This plan identifies risk and proposes controls; it does not certify the system as safe or compliant. Legal counsel must verify regulatory obligations.*
