**Recommendation: Do not proceed with the current proposal.**

The financial justification provided is fundamentally flawed. You are calculating a "saving" of €300k based on avoiding a carbon tax on 12 tonnes of CO₂ at €25/tonne. However, 12 tonnes × €25 equals only **€300**, not €300,000. To reach a €300k saving at €25/tonne, you would need to offset 12,000 tonnes, not 12.

Even if the EU carbon price were to rise dramatically to €250/tonne (a 10x increase), the total value of the offset would only be €3,000 per year. The subscription costs **€30,000 per year** (€2,500 × 12). You would be spending €30,000 to avoid a theoretical liability of €300–€3,000. This is a net loss of €27,000+ annually, regardless of future tax rates.

Furthermore, the offset volume (12 tCO₂) covers less than 27% of your annual emissions (45 tCO₂). Even if fully utilized, it leaves 33 tonnes unaddressed, making it impossible to hit your board-approved 30% reduction target through this instrument alone.
