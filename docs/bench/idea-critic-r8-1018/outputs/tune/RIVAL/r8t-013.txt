**To:** Lila Mwangi, CEO
**From:** VP of Operations
**Subject:** Recommendation on Nimbus Wings Retrofit

**Recommendation: Proceed with a limited 3-aircraft pilot only.**
Do not authorize the full fleet retrofit yet. While the vendor's math suggests a 2.8-year payback, their data is irrelevant to our operating environment (temperate Europe vs. Kenyan heat and dust). We must validate the failure rate and actual fuel savings on a small scale before committing $576,000 for the full fleet. Note that the vendor's cost spreadsheet contains a $240,000 error double-counting the first three aircraft; ensure this is corrected in any final contract.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**
1.  **Environmental Degradation:** The wing extensions may delaminate or lose aerodynamic efficiency due to high temperatures and abrasive dust on unpaved runways, voiding the 7.5% savings claim.
2.  **Maintenance Spike:** If the retrofit increases drag in dirty conditions or traps debris, it could increase engine wear or require frequent cleaning, eroding fuel savings with higher maintenance labor and parts costs.
3.  **Resale Value Impact:** Modifying the airframe with unproven technology in this region could complicate future aircraft sales or insurance renewals if the parts are deemed non-standard or high-risk.

**Kill Switch Metric**
If the average fuel savings across the three pilot aircraft fall below **4%** after the first 12 months of operation, we will terminate the project and remove the retrofits. This threshold accounts for minor variances while ensuring we do not sustain a costly modification for marginal gain.

**Hidden Risk Identified**
**Warranty Voidance in "Out-of-Spec" Conditions:** AeroTech's warranty likely relies on their European operational data. There is a significant risk that they will deny warranty claims for failures in Kenya by classifying our high-temperature, dusty environment as "out of specification" or "misuse," leaving us liable for the full cost of removal and repair. We must negotiate a specific clause guaranteeing coverage in East African conditions before signing.
