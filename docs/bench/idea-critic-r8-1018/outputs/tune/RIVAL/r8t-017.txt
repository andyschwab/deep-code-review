**Recommendation:** Proceed with the Stone Loom proposal only after securing a fixed-price transport contract or a municipal subsidy guarantee; do not commit to full production until the highway toll schedule is published and locked in.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Transport Cost Blowout:** If the new highway tolls exceed $4,500 per unit (approx. 5% of the $92k price), the margin collapses from 10% to near zero, rendering the business model unviable given the high volume of shipments required.
2.  **Zoning Delay Cascade:** The 90-day approval assumption is aggressive; a delay beyond six months burns through 25% of your seed funding before revenue starts, forcing a down-round or scope cut before the first unit ships.
3.  **On-Site Finish Bottleneck:** Fabricating 12 units/month is useless if on-site interior crews cannot match that pace; a backlog of unfinished shells incurs storage costs and delays final payment milestones, strangling cash flow.

**Cancellation/Redesign Trigger:** Cancel or redesign the plan if the confirmed transport cost (fuel + tolls + logistics) exceeds **6% of the unit price ($5,520 per unit)**. At this threshold, the cumulative impact on the 12-units-per-month volume destroys the projected $30M sales target and extends the break-even point beyond 30 months, violating the seed funding runway constraints.
