**Recommendation: Do not approve the pilot in its current form.**

The proposal contains a critical financial impossibility and unquantified operational risks that make immediate approval irresponsible. While the potential efficiency gains are attractive, the current plan lacks the necessary guardrails to protect the department from significant budget overruns and operational disruption.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**

1.  **Financial Mismatch:** The pilot requires €14.4 million (12 units × €1.2 million) for hardware alone, yet your total available budget for 2025 is only €8 million. The proposal is mathematically impossible without securing an additional €6.4 million or reducing the fleet size to six units. Furthermore, the model ignores insurance premiums for autonomous vehicles, which are typically 2–3× higher than standard commercial auto policies, and omits integration engineering costs.
2.  **Technical Dependency Failure:** The entire operation hinges on the city's street-level 3D map being completed by Q2 2025. If this map is delayed even by two weeks, the €1.2 million units become inert assets. Additionally, the "zero additional licensing fees" claim for software integration is highly suspicious; legacy routing systems rarely support autonomous handoffs without costly middleware or API upgrades.
3.  **Regulatory and Liability Gap:** Lisbon may not yet have specific ordinances permitting fully autonomous waste collection trucks on mixed-traffic residential streets. A single accident involving a pedestrian or property damage could result in immediate impoundment of the fleet and massive liability exposure if insurance clauses exclude autonomous mode operations.

**Cancellation Trigger:**
The program must be terminated immediately if **total operational costs (including lease amortization, energy, maintenance, and insurance) exceed €180,000 per unit per month** over any rolling 30-day period during the trial. This threshold accounts for the projected savings while providing a hard cap against runaway downtime costs.

**Glaring Oversights:**
*   **Budget Arithmetic:** As noted, you cannot buy 12 units for €14.4M with an €8M budget. You must reduce the pilot to 6 units (€7.2M) to leave €800k for insurance, integration, and contingency.
*   **Maintenance Baseline Error:** You currently spend €2.25M annually (45 trucks × €50k) on maintenance. The vendor promises a 15% labor cut, but autonomous trucks often have *higher* specialized sensor maintenance costs initially. The net maintenance impact is unknown.
*   **Disposal/Resale Risk:** There is no exit strategy for the hardware. If the pilot fails, selling 12 specialized autonomous trucks will yield pennies on the euro, turning the capital expenditure into a total loss.

**Next Step:** Renegotiate for a 6-unit pilot, demand a written guarantee on the 3D map delivery date, and require the vendor to provide a binding insurance quote before signing.
