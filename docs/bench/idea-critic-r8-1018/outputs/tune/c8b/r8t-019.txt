**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" Pilot**
Move forward with the June 1 launch, but strictly condition full-year funding on hitting specific unit-economics targets during the three-month pilot. Do not commit the full $30,000 marketing budget or the 1,500-subscriber goal until the pilot validates your cost assumptions and churn rate.

**Primary Failure Modes & Hidden Risks**
1.  **Cannibalization Underestimation:** Your assumption that only 30% of wholesale customers will switch is a critical vulnerability. If 50% switch, you lose $24,000 in wholesale revenue instead of $12,000, effectively doubling your revenue gap. Wholesale contracts often have penalties or volume discounts that vanish if numbers drop, creating hidden costs beyond simple lost sales.
2.  **Variable Cost Volatility:** You have fixed shipping at $5 and duties at 7% of a $1 estimate. International shipping rates fluctuate, and customs valuations can change. A $1 increase in shipping or duty per box wipes out ~12% of your margin. At 1,500 subscribers, a $1 cost creep equals $18,000 in annual unexpected loss.
3.  **Acquisition Cost Reality:** Acquiring 1,500 subscribers with $30,000 implies a Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) of $20. If your actual CAC is $25 (a common variance in DTC), you need $37,500 to hit your goal, leaving the project underfunded by 25% before the first box ships.
4.  **Churn Cliff:** The "unknown" churn rate is your biggest killer. If churn exceeds 8% monthly after the pilot, you will burn through your 1,500-subscriber base in less than a year without constant, expensive reinvestment.

**Kill Criteria & Pivot Triggers**
Establish these tripwires to be measured at the end of the three-month pilot (August 31, 2024):

*   **Kill the Project If:**
    *   **Net Margin per Box < $15:** If (Price $35) minus (Packaging $2 + Shipping $5 + Duty + Tea Cost) minus (Actual CAC based on pilot spend) is less than $15, the unit economics do not support scaling.
    *   **Monthly Churn > 10%:** If more than 10% of subscribers cancel in any single pilot month after the first, the retention model is broken.
    *   **Wholesale Cannibalization > 40%:** If more than 40% of wholesale clients migrate to the lower-margin subscription channel, the net revenue impact is negative.

*   **Pivot If:**
    *   **CAC > $25:** Pause mass marketing. Pivot to a referral-only model or higher-priced tier to improve margins before spending more.
    *   **Duty/Shipping Variance > 15%:** Pivot to a regional-only launch (domestic only) to stabilize logistics costs before attempting international scale.

**Leading Metric to Watch:** Contribution Margin per Active Subscriber (CMAS) weekly during the pilot. This combines revenue, variable costs, and amortized acquisition cost into a single truth-telling number.
