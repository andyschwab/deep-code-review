**Recommendation:** Proceed with the Stone Loom proposal only under a **"Go-If" condition**: secure a written binding estimate or cap on the highway toll rates from the agency before committing to the full 12-unit/month production schedule. Without this, the project's margin and break-even timeline are too exposed to a single unverified variable.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Transport Cost Overrun:** If the unpublished toll rates exceed **4.5% of the unit price** (approx. $4,140 per unit), the 10% margin evaporates, pushing the break-even point beyond 24 months and jeopardizing the $5.2M seed runway.
2.  **Zoning Delay:** If the mixed-use zoning approval slips beyond the assumed **90-day window**, permitting delays will halt on-site finishing, creating a bottleneck that prevents the 12-units/month velocity required to hit the $30M sales target.
3.  **Inflation Underestimation:** The current **3% annual inflation buffer** is insufficient for volatile construction materials; a spike to 6-7% would increase the $92k fabrication cost, further eroding margins before the first unit is sold.

**Cancel/Redesign Trigger:**
Cancel or redesign the logistics plan immediately if the final toll fee schedule results in a transport cost greater than **$4,500 per unit** (approx. 4.9% of unit price). At this threshold, the projected $26.5M revenue no longer covers the combined fabrication, transport, and operational costs within the 18-month break-even window, rendering the current business model insolvent under the existing seed funding.
