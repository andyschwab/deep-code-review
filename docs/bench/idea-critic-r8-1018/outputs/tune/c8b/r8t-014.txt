**Recommendation: NO-GO for a full migration before Q4 launch.**

Proceeding based on the current data exposes Pixel Rift to unacceptable financial and technical risks. The cost model is fundamentally incomplete, having excluded network egress which alone adds ~$600k/month, and relies on average load comparisons rather than peak spikes, likely understating total serverless costs by a significant margin. Furthermore, the technical validation is insufficient; a pilot of 150 concurrent sessions does not prove stability for the forecasted 500k users, especially given the unknown churn dynamics of the premium tier. A full migration is a one-way door that cannot be reversed quickly if costs spiral or latency degrades under peak load. Instead, execute a targeted "peak-load stress test" immediately and cap the serverless deployment to non-critical services until a complete total-cost-of-ownership (including egress and peaks) is verified against the on-prem baseline.

**Top Three Failure Modes:**
1.  **Financial:** Monthly costs exceed $4M due to unmodeled network egress ($600k+) and compute spikes during peak concurrency, rendering the unit economics negative compared to on-prem.
2.  **Technical:** Latency sustains >25ms during peak traffic (beyond the 150-session pilot), violating the ≤20ms player satisfaction threshold and causing churn.
3.  **Market:** The assumed 12% premium conversion rate fails to materialize due to unknown churn factors, leaving revenue insufficient to cover the variable serverless cost structure.

**Kill Criteria (Stop/Pivot Triggers):**
*   **Latency:** Sustained average latency >25ms over any 1-hour peak window.
*   **Cost:** Total monthly infrastructure cost (compute + storage + egress) >$4M.
*   **Conversion:** Premium conversion rate <8% after the first 30 days of full availability.
