**Recommendation:** Proceed with the Stone Loom proposal only under a **"Go-If" condition**: secure a binding cap on highway toll rates or a fixed-cost transport contract before committing to the full 12-unit/month production schedule. Without this, the project's margin is too exposed to an unverified variable to guarantee the 18-month break-even.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Transport Cost Overrun:** The unreleased highway tolls push logistics costs above the 3% inflation buffer, eroding the 10% margin and delaying break-even beyond the 18-month runway.
2.  **Zoning Timeline Slip:** If the city takes longer than 90 days to approve the mixed-use zoning, the production start date shifts, burning seed capital without revenue and extending the path to profitability past the 12-month funding coverage.
3.  **On-Site Finish Bottleneck:** Relying on on-site interior completion after shipping creates a dependency on local labor availability; any shortage there stalls unit turnover, capping sales volume below the projected 12 units/month.

**Cancel/Redesign Trigger:**
Cancel or redesign the plan if the final highway toll rate causes total transport costs to exceed **4.5% of the unit price** (approx. $4,140 per unit). At this threshold, the combined impact of tolls and the existing 3% inflation buffer eliminates the projected 10% margin, making the current financial model unsustainable.

*Note: The current sales calculation ($26.5M) appears to exclude the margin rather than add it; verify if the $30M target requires higher volume or pricing. The $5.2M seed covers 12 months, but the break-even is at 18 months; ensure bridging financing is identified for months 13–18.*
