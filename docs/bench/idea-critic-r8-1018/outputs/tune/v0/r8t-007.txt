2.  **Invalid Baseline Comparison:** The US study (1,500 beds) likely had different baseline protocols; if our 2.8% error rate stems from systemic understaffing rather than dispensing mechanics, the robot cannot fix the root cause.
3.  **Hidden Training Debt:** The proposal explicitly excludes staff training time; if training requires 20+ hours per nurse, the labor cost will immediately eclipse the $45k annual penalty savings.

If a robot dispenses incorrectly, the vendor contract likely limits their liability to the cost of the drug, leaving your organization fully exposed to the regulatory penalties and reputational damage of the error, potentially increasing your insurance premiums beyond the $45k savings.
