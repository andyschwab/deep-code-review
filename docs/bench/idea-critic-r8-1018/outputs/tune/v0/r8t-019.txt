**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" conditional launch.**
Launch the three-month pilot on June 1, 2024, but treat the full $30,000 marketing spend and 1,500-subscriber target as contingent on hitting specific unit-economic guardrails during the pilot. Do not commit to the full-year scale until the pilot proves the churn rate and customs cost assumptions.

**Primary Failure Modes & Hidden Risks:**
1.  **Customs Cost Volatility:** Your model assumes a fixed $1/box duty. Customs duties on agricultural imports often fluctuate based on trade agreements, origin certification, and weight brackets. A 2–3% increase in duty rates or a reclassification of the tea leaf could erase your thin margin per unit.
2.  **Wholesale Cannibalization Underestimation:** Assuming only 30% of wholesale customers switch is optimistic if the subscription offers a perceived higher value (freshness, exclusivity). If 50% switch, your "limited" $12,000 wholesale loss doubles to $24,000, drastically altering the net revenue picture.
3.  **The "Churn Cliff":** You identified churn as the unknown, but the specific risk is a "cliff" effect where users cancel immediately after the 3-month pilot ends because the introductory novelty wears off or the $35 price point feels high for a recurring expense without ongoing perks.
4.  **Marketing Efficiency:** Acquiring 1,500 subscribers with $30,000 implies a Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) of $20. If your pilot CAC exceeds $25, you will burn through the budget before reaching the target, leaving you with insufficient volume to cover fixed logistics costs.

**Kill Criteria & Pivot Triggers:**
*   **Kill Trigger:** If the pilot reveals a **monthly churn rate >8%** after month 3, or if the **actual landed cost per box (including duty) exceeds $9.50**, terminate the full launch. At these levels, the lifetime value (LTV) cannot support the $20 CAC.
*   **Pivot Trigger:** If wholesale cannibalization exceeds **40%**, pivot the strategy to a "Wholesale-First" hybrid model where existing wholesale clients get a discounted subscription tier to retain them, rather than treating them as a lost revenue block.
*   **Review Date:** Re-evaluate all metrics on **September 1, 2024** (end of pilot) before releasing any further marketing funds.

**Note:** Your current margin per box is approximately $26 ($35 - $2 packaging - $5 shipping - $1 tea - $1 duty), which looks healthy but leaves little room for error if churn spikes.
