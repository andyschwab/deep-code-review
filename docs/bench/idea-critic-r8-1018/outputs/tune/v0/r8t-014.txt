**Recommendation: NO-GO on full migration before Q4 launch.**

Proceed with a hybrid approach: keep the core game logic on your current on-prem (or provisioned) infrastructure to guarantee peak stability, and migrate only non-critical, bursty workloads (like matchmaking lobbies or asset processing) to serverless. A full shift based on average load metrics creates an unacceptable risk of latency spikes and cost overruns during the Q4 launch surge.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**

1.  **Technical (Cold Starts & Peak Spikes):** Your pilot tested 150 concurrent sessions, but 500k users will create massive, unpredictable spikes. Serverless functions suffer "cold start" latency when scaling up instantly. If real-world peaks trigger thousands of simultaneous cold starts, average latency could jump from 15ms to >200ms, breaking the <20ms player satisfaction threshold and causing mass disconnects.
2.  **Financial (The Egress & Peak Trap):** You calculated costs using average load and excluded network egress in the pilot. With 5 PB of traffic, the $600k egress estimate is likely a floor, not a ceiling, as serverless architectures often shuffle more data between functions. Furthermore, pricing based on average load ignores the cost multiplier of peak concurrency. If actual peaks are 5x your average (common in gaming), your compute bill could easily exceed the $4M monthly danger zone, burning runway before premium conversions materialize.
3.  **Market (Churn & Conversion Unknowns):** You are betting on a 12% premium conversion rate with zero data on churn. If the serverless migration introduces even minor instability (latency jitter), early adopters will leave before converting. Without a stable baseline, you cannot distinguish if poor conversion is due to product-market fit or infrastructure failure, making the "unknown" churn variable a critical blind spot for revenue forecasting.

**Kill Criteria (Stop/Pivot Triggers):**

*   **Latency:** Sustained p95 latency >25ms over any 1-hour window during beta loading.
*   **Cost:** Total monthly infrastructure cost (compute + storage + egress) exceeds $3.2M (80% of your $4M hard cap) in the first full month of operation.
*   **Stability:** More than 0.5% of sessions fail to initialize due to timeout or provisioning errors.

*Note: Re-run cost modeling using peak concurrency multipliers, not averages, before revisiting migration. The 20ms target is an assumption; validate if 25ms actually impacts retention before setting it as a hard failure line.*
