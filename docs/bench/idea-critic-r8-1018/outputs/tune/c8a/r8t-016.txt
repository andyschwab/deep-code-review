The proposal contains a fatal financial contradiction and unverified operational assumptions that make immediate approval irresponsible. Additionally, there is no mention of regulatory approval from Portuguese transport authorities for Level 4 autonomy on public streets; a single denial or restriction could halt the pilot entirely after capital is committed.
