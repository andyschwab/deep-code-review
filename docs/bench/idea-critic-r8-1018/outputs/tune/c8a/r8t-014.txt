Proceeding with a complete shift to a serverless stack based on the current data presents an unacceptable financial and technical risk. The exclusion of network egress from the primary cost model already reveals a $600k monthly blind spot; applying this same oversight to peak traffic modeling could push total monthly costs well beyond the $4M threshold before you even reach the 500k user forecast.
