**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" pilot, not a full launch.**
Approve the three-month pilot starting June 1, 2024, but condition the full-scale rollout on validating that the blended Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) remains under $20 and monthly churn stays below 4%. Do not commit the full $30,000 budget or the 1,500-subscriber target until these two metrics are proven in the pilot.

**Primary Failure Modes & Hidden Risks:**
1.  **Cannibalization Underestimation:** Your assumption that only 30% of wholesale customers will switch is a critical vulnerability. If even 45% defect, the $12,000 estimated loss doubles to ~$28,000, eroding nearly the entire marketing budget. Wholesale relationships often have hidden contractual penalties for direct poaching that are not accounted for.
2.  **Unit Economics Fragility:** At $35 revenue, your variable costs are $8.07 ($2 packaging + $5 shipping + $1 tea + 7% duty on $1 is negligible, but if duty applies to the $35 value or higher tea cost, it spikes). This leaves $26.93 for marketing and overhead. To acquire 1,500 users with $30,000, your CAC must be exactly $20. If pilot data shows a CAC of $25, you will burn $7,500 extra just to hit your target, excluding the wholesale loss.
3.  **Customs & Logistics Volatility:** You estimated duty on a $1 tea cost. If customs assesses duty on the retail value ($35) or if tea leaf prices fluctuate, your margin collapses. Furthermore, shipping individual boxes internationally often incurs unexpected brokerage fees not present in bulk wholesale shipping.

**Kill Criteria & Pivot Triggers:**
*   **Kill Switch:** Terminate the project immediately if the pilot reveals a monthly churn rate exceeding **5%** after month 3, or if the verified CAC exceeds **$22**. At these levels, the Lifetime Value (LTV) cannot support the acquisition cost given your thin margins.
*   **Pivot Trigger:** If wholesale cannibalization exceeds **35%**, pivot the strategy to exclude existing wholesale accounts from the DTC marketing funnel entirely, even if it lowers the total subscriber count.
*   **Review Date:** Re-evaluate on **September 1, 2024** (end of pilot). If the "Go-If" conditions are not met by this date, do not release further funds.

*Note: Your calculation of the 7% duty on the $1 tea cost ($0.07) seems unusually low; verify if duties apply to the declared retail value or landed cost in your target regions. Ensure your wholesale contracts do not have non-compete clauses that this DTC move might violate.*
