**Recommendation:** Proceed with the Stone Loom proposal only under a **"Go-If" condition**: secure a binding cap on highway toll rates or a fixed-cost transport contract before committing to the full 12-unit/month production schedule. Without this, the current financial model is too fragile to support the $5.2M seed deployment.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Transport Cost Overrun:** The undefined highway tolls could push logistics costs beyond the 10% margin buffer. If transport exceeds **12% of the unit price** ($11,040 per unit), the project breaks even only after month 24+, exhausting the 12-month operating runway included in your seed funding.
2.  **Zoning Timeline Slip:** The model assumes city approval within 90 days. A delay to 180 days halts on-site finishing, creating a cash-flow gap where fabricated units sit idle while burning storage and capital costs, likely requiring an emergency capital raise at unfavorable terms.
3.  **Production Ramp Friction:** Achieving 12 units/month immediately assumes zero fabrication learning curve. If actual output averages 8 units/month for the first six months due to supply chain or labor constraints, revenue delays will deplete the seed fund before the projected 18-month break-even point.

**Cancel/Redesign Trigger:**
Cancel or redesign the plan if the finalized highway toll schedule indicates a transport cost exceeding **$11,500 per unit** (approx. 12.5% of the $92k price). At this threshold, the projected $30M sales volume no longer covers the combined inflation and logistics variance, rendering the 4-year ROI negative.
