**Recommendation: Re-work**

Do not proceed to installation yet. The current proposal relies on unverified assumptions regarding price elasticity and material performance that pose unacceptable financial risk at a $1.2M capital outlay. You must first execute a paid market pilot to validate the 8% sales lift and confirm the 5% price premium acceptance across a statistically significant sample, rather than a small focus group. Additionally, secure a verified recycling rate certification for the new material to ensure the core value proposition holds. Once these data points are confirmed, recalculate the breakeven analysis; only then should you seek final approval.

**Top Three Failure Modes:**
1. **Price Elasticity Mismatch:** The broader market rejects the 5% premium, causing volume to drop rather than rise, which would increase the effective cost per unit and extend breakeven beyond 36 months.
2. **Operational Friction:** The new recyclable material jams existing high-speed filling lines or has different shelf-life properties, leading to production downtime or increased spoilage that erodes margins.
3. **Greenwashing Backlash:** If the "recyclable" claim fails due to lack of local infrastructure (the material is technically recyclable but practically landfilled), the brand faces reputational damage without achieving the environmental benefit.

**Kill Condition:**
Terminate the project immediately if the 90-day pilot demonstrates a net revenue lift of less than 4% (half the forecast) OR if the actual production waste rate exceeds 3% due to material handling issues.
