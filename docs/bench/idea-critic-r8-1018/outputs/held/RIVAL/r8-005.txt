**Recommendation: GO, with a strict adoption checkpoint.**

Stage Ember is financially sound even with modest uptake. Your hard savings ($10,000) plus the 2% fee on current volume ($8,400) nearly cover the annual fixed costs ($10,500). The 5% sales lift ($90,000) is pure upside. The risk is not financial ruin, but operational chaos if patrons reject the digital shift.

**Three Main Failure Modes:**

1.  **Patron Friction (The "Lobby Logjam"):** Older demographics struggle with app-based entry, causing long queues and missed curtain times. This damages the brand more than paper tickets ever did.
2.  **Integration Blind Spots:** The portal fails to sync real-time seat availability with your existing house management, leading to double-bookings or broken accessibility seating workflows.
3.  **Support Overload:** Your front-of-house staff becomes unpaid IT support, diverting attention from guest experience during the critical pre-show window.

**Kill Trigger:**

**Adoption below 40% by July 1, 2025.**

If fewer than 40% of ticket holders are using digital entry two weeks into the season, the friction cost outweighs the efficiency gain. At that point, revert to hybrid/paper for the remainder of the run to protect the guest experience.

*Note: Ensure the contract includes a clause allowing a revert to paper scanning without penalty if this trigger is hit.*
