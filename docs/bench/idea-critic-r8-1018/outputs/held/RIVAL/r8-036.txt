**Recommendation: Proceed with a Conditional Pilot**
Approve the €120,000 budget but restrict the initial launch to a controlled cohort of 500 customers (10% of base) rather than the full 5,000. The unit economics are viable only if the 30% booking value lift holds; however, the unquantified regulatory risk regarding Greek data privacy and the untested willingness-to-pay premium create a "binary failure" scenario that demands a smaller blast radius. Use the Q3 timeline to run a 6-week legal feasibility study in parallel with a technical proof-of-concept. Do not commit to the full rollout until the legal opinion confirms the personalization engine complies with local data sovereignty laws.

**Three Most Likely Failure Modes**
1.  **Regulatory Blockade:** Greek or EU data authorities classify the depth of personalization required for the €1,560 booking value as excessive profiling, forcing a reduction in AI capabilities that renders the premium offering indistinguishable from standard packages.
2.  **Unit Economics Collapse:** The opt-in rate hits the projected 70%, but the average booking value only increases by 10% (to €1,320) rather than 30%. When combined with the €0.12 per-recommendation processing fees, the margin expansion fails to cover the €15,000 licensing fee and integration costs, resulting in a net loss per user.
3.  **Operational Friction:** The exclusion of staff training costs proves critical; frontline agents, unfamiliar with the AI logic, inadvertently upsell traditional packages or fail to trust the AI itineraries during customer queries, suppressing adoption regardless of customer interest.

**Specific Pivot/Stop Trigger**
Stop or pivot the initiative if, after the first 30 days of the limited cohort launch, the **paid conversion rate for AI-curated packages is less than 15%** (against the 70% opt-in assumption) OR if the **average booking value for AI users does not exceed €1,350**. Either metric indicates the value proposition is insufficient to justify the variable processing costs and fixed licensing fees.
