**Recommendation: Proceed with Conditional Approval**
Approve the rollout only if a $1.5M contingency reserve is secured and a winterized integration pilot is completed by 15 January 2025. The core economics are sound, but the current plan treats unproven technical assumptions as facts.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**
1.  **The "Seamless" Integration Fallacy:** The plan assumes zero friction integrating AquaSense devices with your legacy SCADA system, specifically under sub-zero conditions. If the proprietary protocol requires middleware translation or hardware gateways to function in winter, you face unplanned software licensing fees and latency issues that could blind operators during critical freeze events. This is your highest probability technical risk.
2.  **Labor Escalation via Retrofit Complexity:** The $1.2M labor budget assumes a standard swap. However, older residential fittings often require pipe modification or valve replacement that isn't visible until installation begins. If 20% of homes require retrofitting (a common industry occurrence), labor costs could spike by 30–40%, eroding your ROI timeline by two years.
3.  **Data Integrity & Cyber Liability:** Smart meters expand your attack surface. A breach affecting 12,000 endpoints could allow mass water shutoffs or billing manipulation. The current plan budgets for hardware and labor but lacks line items for endpoint security hardening, continuous penetration testing, or cyber-liability insurance specific to IoT infrastructure.

**Specific Kill-Switch Triggers**
Stop or fundamentally rethink the project immediately if either of these occurs during the pre-deployment phase:
*   **Cost Trigger:** The verified integration pilot reveals middleware or gateway costs exceeding **$450,000** (roughly 8% of total hardware capex), indicating the "seamless" assumption is false.
*   **Security Trigger:** An independent third-party audit identifies a **critical severity vulnerability** (CVSS score > 9.0) in the AquaSense firmware that cannot be patched over-the-air without physical access.

*Note: Your 5% savings assumption is aggressive given the pilot's 4.8% result over only three months; consider stress-testing the financial model at 3.5% sustained savings to ensure the project remains viable if conservation behavior normalizes over time.*
