In railway contexts, these are historically the highest-failure domains. Proceeding without de-risking these turns the 120 million ruble pilot into a high-probability sunk cost.

**Three Main Failure Modes**

1.  **Data Ingestion Collapse:** The assumption that AI can ingest legacy timetable data without manual cleaning is likely false. If the data requires significant normalization (expected), the project shifts from an AI deployment to a multi-year data engineering remediation, blowing the six-month timeline.
2.  **Integration Cost Overrun:** The vendor's 5–10% integration cost estimate (6–12 million rubles) is suspiciously low for signaling systems. If true integration costs mirror industry standards for safety-critical rail infrastructure (often 20–30% of budget), you will exhaust the pilot budget before achieving operational status.
3.  **Safety Certification Block:** Even if the software works, railway signaling changes require rigorous safety certification. If the AI's decision logic cannot be deterministically audited by regulators, the system cannot legally go live, rendering the efficiency gains theoretical.

**Hidden Risks & Arithmetic Slips**

*   **The "Simple API" Fallacy:** There is no such thing as a "simple" API hookup to existing signaling systems. These are typically proprietary, hardened protocols (e.g., fail-safe relays or specific telegrams), not RESTful endpoints. Treating this as a standard IT integration is a critical strategic error.
*   **Currency & Budget Mismatch:** You cite costs in **rubles** (120M pilot, 500M operating base) but savings in **USD** ($3M). With volatile exchange rates, a 20% ruble devaluation against the dollar could artificially inflate the perceived USD savings while your actual ruble-denominated costs skyrocket. Standardize all projections to rubles for internal approval.
*   **Timeline Compression:** A six-month window (Oct–Apr) includes winter operations, which often introduce non-standard scheduling variables. If the AI trains on standard data but deploys during winter disruptions, its performance may degrade precisely when you need it most, leading to a false negative on its capabilities.
