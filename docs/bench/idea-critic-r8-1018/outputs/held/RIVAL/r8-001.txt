A €1.05M investment cannot generate €1.05M in *net profit* (full recoupment) plus a 15% return in just 12 months from a 1.2MW asset without violating basic physics and market rates. You must correct the unit economics before seeking approval.

**Three Biggest Failure Points:**

1.  **Mathematical Impossibility:** To recoup €1.05M in one year, the system must generate roughly €2,877 *daily* in net profit. At €0.12/kWh, this requires selling ~24,000 kWh daily. A 1.2MW system typically stores/discharges 2–4 hours of capacity (2.4–4.8 MWh total) per day, not the 24 MWh required. The revenue model is off by a factor of 5x–10x.
2.  **Regulatory Cliff:** The model assumes the €0.12 feed-in tariff holds for three years, yet explicitly admits uncertainty post-2025. If the tariff drops or the subsidy structure changes after year one, the ROI collapses instantly, turning a projected profit into a long-term liability.
3.  **Adoption Dependency:** The plan relies on the municipality adopting 20% of capacity within year one. If municipal bureaucracy delays this offtake agreement, the projected cash flow for those months vanishes, extending the payback period well beyond the promised 12 months.
