**Recommendation: Proceed with a conditional "Pilot-First" launch, but immediately restructure the financial model to cap variable data costs and lower the adoption dependency.** Do not commit to the full 2,500-farmer scale until a 200-farmer, 4-week sprint validates actual data consumption and connectivity reliability in the specific Rift Valley target zones.

**Primary Failure Modes & Hidden Risks:**

1.  **The Data Subsidy Black Hole:** Your budget allocates $20,000 for data subsidies over six months. At 150 MB/farmer/month, this covers ~2,222 farmers. If actual usage hits 250 MB (common with map updates or video content) or if rural data prices spike by 20%, you will exhaust this fund by Month 4, forcing you to either cut off access (killing adoption) or divert funds from training.
2.  **Adoption vs. Yield Lag Mismatch:** You assume 80% adoption and monetize based on a 12% yield increase after *three* cropping cycles. However, your $10/user/year revenue model expects immediate cash flow. If farmers do not see visible yield gains in the *first* cycle, renewal rates for Year 2 will collapse, making the $45/ha monetization theoretical rather than realized. The hidden risk is that "adoption" (downloading the app) does not equal "retention" (using it enough to generate yield data).
3.  **The Maintenance Underestimation:** You identified $12,000/year for server maintenance but excluded it from the initial 6-month runway. This creates an immediate $6,000 hidden liability for the pilot period. Combined with potential data overruns, your $200,000 total pilot budget is effectively already oversubscribed before a single seed is planted.
4.  **Infrastructure Fragility:** The "industry average" of 150 MB assumes consistent connectivity. In rural Rift Valley, intermittent signal often causes apps to retry data transmissions, inflating consumption by 30-50% without delivering better user experience.

**Kill/Pivot Triggers:**

*   **KILL THE PROJECT IF:**
    *   **Adoption < 45%** after Month 2 (indicates the value proposition or UX fails before yield results are visible).
    *   **Data Cost Per Farmer > $1.80/month** (this breaches your subsidy cap before the pilot ends).
    *   **Connectivity Downtime > 30%** of farming hours (renders the app unusable regardless of content quality).

*   **PIVOT THE STRATEGY IF:**
    *   **Yield Increase < 5%** after Cycle 1 (suggests the agronomic advice is mismatched to local soil conditions; pivot to a "human-first" agent model rather than pure app reliance).
    *   **Server Latency > 3 seconds** on 3G networks (pivot to an SMS/USSD hybrid interface to reduce data load).

**Note:** Your current model bets heavily on perfect execution of variable costs; shift risk to fixed-cost contracts with local telcos immediately.
The $10/year price point is likely too low to sustain server costs if adoption scales; consider a B2B2C model where the cooperative pays the license fee.
