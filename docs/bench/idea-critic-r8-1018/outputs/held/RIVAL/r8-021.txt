**Recommendation:** Proceed with the investment, but restructure the 36 million SEK commitment into tranches tied to regulatory milestones and field-trial consistency, rather than funding the full three years upfront.

**Three Biggest Risks:**
1.  **Regulatory Mismatch:** Assuming EU wheat approval will mirror the 18-month corn timeline is dangerous; cereal crops often face stricter scrutiny and longer review periods, potentially delaying revenue by years.
2.  **Environmental Variance:** The jump from 9% yield increase in two sites to a guaranteed 15% average across 12 diverse southern Swedish locations is statistically weak; poor performance in remaining trials could invalidate the core value proposition.
3.  **Competitive Underestimation:** GreenField Agro's 10% gain via conventional breeding sets a low bar for entry but a high bar for premium pricing; if they improve to 12% without GMO baggage, our 15% gain may not justify the regulatory risk to farmers.

**Stop/Pivot Trigger:** Halt the project immediately if the average yield increase across all 12 field trials falls below 12% or if the EU regulatory body issues a "major objection" notice before month 20.

*Note: The current plan over-relies on optimistic regulatory timing and insufficient field data. Tranching the funding protects capital while validating these specific uncertainties.*
