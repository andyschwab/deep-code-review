**Recommendation:** Do not move forward with the issuance in its current form. The proposed 40-basis-point green premium (4.2% vs. 3.8%) is unjustified without a quantified investor tax offset or a confirmed liquidity premium analysis. Issuing now risks labeling the bond as "expensive greenwashing," potentially leading to an undersubscribed offering that damages SolarCrest's reputation with the Capital Market Authority and future investors. You must first model the specific tax benefits and validate the liquidity spread before setting the coupon.

**Three Main Failure Modes:**
1.  **Regulatory Delay:** The CMA approval takes longer than the assumed 30 days, causing a mismatch with the targeted solar project funding windows and forcing the fund to bridge capital at higher short-term rates.
2.  **Pricing Misalignment:** Without the tax offset quantification, institutional investors reject the 4.2% coupon as insufficient compensation for perceived liquidity constraints compared to the 3.5% sovereign yield, resulting in a failed book build.
3.  **Asset Performance Gap:** The underlying solar projects fail to meet the projected 15% return by 2025 due to operational delays or grid connection issues, triggering covenant breaches or reputational damage regarding the "green" label's financial viability.

**Kill Trigger:** Less than 60% investor commitment in the first subscription window. Given the unquantified tax variables and liquidity risks, a threshold lower than 60% indicates a fundamental market rejection of the pricing structure, making a rescue attempt financially imprudent.
