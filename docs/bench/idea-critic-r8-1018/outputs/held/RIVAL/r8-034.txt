**Recommendation:** Proceed with the pilot launch on 1 September 2025, but condition the full €2.2 million capex commitment on securing a fixed lead-time guarantee for printheads and validating that at least 40% of target accounts commit to trial orders during the six-month pilot.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Adoption Miscalculation:** The plan assumes 70% of accounts will adapt to smaller, custom orders. If B2B clients resist shifting from bulk (300 sqm) to custom runs (150 sqm) due to their own inventory constraints, revenue will fall short of the 18% lift, extending the break-even period beyond 18 months.
2.  **Supply Chain Delay:** Unconfirmed printhead lead times could push full operation to Q3 2026. A delay of this magnitude risks missing the 2027 revenue target entirely and exposes the project to potential technology obsolescence before full deployment.
3.  **Hidden Operational Costs:** Benchmarking against a competitor's €1.8 million capex without factoring their higher maintenance contracts creates a false cost baseline. If energy savings are offset by steep maintenance fees and the complexity of small-batch logistics, the projected margins will erode, jeopardizing ROI.

**Halt Trigger:** Terminate the project if, at the end of the six-month pilot (February 2026), less than 25% of existing accounts have placed a repeat custom order, or if the confirmed equipment delivery date slips beyond December 2025.

*Note: The 25% repeat order threshold is a more conservative and realistic proxy for sustainable demand than the initial 70% projection. Ensure the maintenance contract terms are fully negotiated before the pilot concludes.*
