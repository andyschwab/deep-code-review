**Recommendation: NO-GO (Conditional Pause)**

Do not authorize the full $3.1M upfront investment yet. While the unit economics ($35 saving/session) are theoretically sound, the plan relies on two unverified critical path assumptions: an 80% provider adoption rate within two months and an undefined regulatory approval timeline. Proceeding now exposes SaúdeViva to significant sunk costs before confirming market fit or legal viability. Instead, authorize a **$400k Pilot Phase** limited to 5,000 members and 20 providers for Q3 2025. This caps exposure while validating the adoption curve and securing regulatory clarity before committing to the full network rollout.

**Top Three Failure Modes**

1.  **Provider Adoption Collapse:** The financial model breaks if provider adoption stays below 60%. If clinicians reject the platform due to workflow friction or reimbursement delays, utilization will stagnate near the current 12%, turning the $1M annual maintenance fee into a pure loss rather than a leveraged investment.
2.  **Regulatory Stoppage:** Launching without a confirmed regulatory approval timeline risks an abrupt shutdown after integration costs are sunk. If Brazilian health authorities (ANS) delay licensure or data sovereignty rulings beyond Q4 2025, the platform becomes a stranded asset with no revenue stream to offset the $2.5M integration spend.
3.  **Patient Trust Deficit:** Even with available providers, utilization may fail to reach the 20% target if members reject virtual psychiatric care due to privacy concerns or cultural preference for in-person interaction. Without early patient acceptance data, the projected $4.2M savings are speculative.
