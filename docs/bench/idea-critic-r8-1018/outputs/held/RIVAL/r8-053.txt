**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Pilot-First" Strategy, Not Full Launch.**
Do not commit the full €250k budget or hire dedicated staff yet. Instead, allocate €40k to train two existing senior consultants as CinderGuard specialists and secure *one* anchor client for a pilot audit starting July 1. The math on your projected revenue is dangerously optimistic regarding cash flow and utilization. Selling three audits per quarter (12 annually) at six weeks each requires 72 weeks of audit capacity per year. Since there are only 52 weeks in a year, you cannot deliver this volume with a single team; you would need three parallel teams immediately, which contradicts a cautious market entry. Furthermore, offering a 15% discount on a complex, high-liability service like zero-trust audits erodes your margin buffer needed for the steep learning curve of a new framework.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Utilization Bottleneck:** Your plan assumes 138% team utilization (72 weeks of work in 52 weeks). You will either miss delivery deadlines, burning client trust, or be forced to subcontract at high costs, destroying the projected margin.
2.  **Regulatory Whiplash:** If the EU data-localisation rules (early 2025) mandate specific data residency that CinderGuard's current architecture doesn't support, you could be mid-audit when the scope changes, forcing expensive re-work or contract renegotiation.
3.  **Sales Cycle Mismatch:** Enterprise zero-trust deals rarely close in time for a July 1 start. If the first three clients don't sign by June, your specialized staff sit idle while burning the €250k budget.

**Kill/Pivot Trigger:**
**Trigger:** Failure to sign a paid Letter of Intent (LOI) for the pilot audit by **June 1, 2024**, OR if the pilot audit reveals that EU data-localisation compliance requires custom engineering exceeding €30k per client.
**Action:** If triggered, **kill** the dedicated service line. **Pivot** to a "Referral Partnership" model where you resell CinderGuard audits performed by their certified partners for a 10% commission, eliminating your delivery risk and capital exposure until regulations stabilize.

*Note: Your renewal rate assumption (85%) is irrelevant for Year 1 cash flow survival; focus entirely on the initial sale and delivery cost.*
