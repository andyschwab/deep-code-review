**Recommendation: Proceed with a Staged Pilot, Not a Full Launch**

Do not commit the full €250,000 budget immediately. Instead, allocate €40,000 to a 4-week "Integration Spike" and limited user test. The assumption that Dutch AR adoption mirrors the U.S. market is a critical unverified variable; cultural and hardware penetration differences could invalidate the 15% sales lift projection. By staging the rollout, we cap our initial exposure while validating the single biggest unknown: the integration cost with GreenLoop's legacy e-commerce platform. If the spike confirms the integration is feasible and user engagement matches 60% of the U.S. benchmark, we release the remaining funds for the full 12-week build.

**The Three Biggest Failure Modes**

1.  **Integration Cost Overrun:** The hidden complexity of connecting the AR layer to GreenLoop's specific inventory and checkout API could consume 40–50% of the budget before a single user sees the filter, destroying the ROI model.
2.  **Market Mismatch (The "US Bias"):** Dutch consumers may prioritize utility over novelty. If the "try-on" feature is perceived as a gimmick rather than a decision aid, adoption will stall regardless of marketing spend, leaving us with high development costs and no sales lift.
3.  **Friction-Induced Drop-off:** AR experiences often suffer from high bounce rates if loading times exceed 3 seconds or if browser permissions feel intrusive. If the user journey adds friction to the checkout flow, conversion rates could drop below the current 20% baseline, actively hurting revenue.

**Pre-Agreed Kill Trigger**

**The "40% Validation Gate":** At the end of Week 4 (the Integration Spike), we kill the project immediately if:
*   The confirmed integration cost exceeds **€65,000** (adjusting the total projected spend to >€315k), OR
*   User testing with 500 Dutch participants yields an engagement rate below **40% of the U.S. benchmark** (i.e., fewer than 2,000 unique users projected at scale).

If either condition is met, we terminate development, salvage the code for future use, and reallocate the remaining budget to proven performance marketing channels.

*Note: This approach protects the downside while keeping the upside intact. Ensure the legal team reviews data privacy implications for AR camera access in the EU before the pilot starts.*
