**Recommendation: Proceed with a Single-Project Pilot Only**

Do not adopt DesertBrick Constructs for all three projects immediately. Instead, authorize the modular kit **only for the 5,000 sqm Office project** (deadline 15 Dec 2025). Treat the Hotel and Residential Tower as traditional builds until the Office project proves the labor savings and warranty stability.

While the 30% time reduction is attractive, the unquantified warranty risk and the confirmed 10% labor rate increase create a fragile cost model. By limiting exposure to one project, you secure the potential schedule win for the earliest deadline while capping the financial risk at $210,000 plus training costs, rather than exposing $630,000 in kit costs and three distinct warranty liabilities to an unproven vendor.

**Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **Warranty Cost Explosion:** The vague "low-risk" statement masks potential structural or sealing failures common in modular systems. If claims exceed $50,000 per project in year two, the initial construction savings are erased.
2.  **Labor Efficiency Gap:** The promised 20% labor reduction may not materialize if the specialized training proves insufficient or if site conditions require rework, compounding the already higher hourly rates.
3.  **Supply Chain Rigidity:** Unlike traditional builds where materials can be sourced locally if delayed, a 6-week shipping window for kits offers no flexibility. A single logistics failure delays the entire critical path.

**Kill/Pivot Triggers**
Execute the following "Stop-Loss" clauses during the Office project:

*   **Trigger 1 (Schedule):** If kit installation or integration delays the project timeline by more than **3 weeks** against the baseline schedule, **pivot immediately** to traditional construction methods for the remaining Hotel and Residential projects.
