**Recommendation: Do not proceed with the full 12-unit rollout yet.** Instead, execute a **3-unit limited pilot** for 6 months. **Three Biggest Ways This Could Fail:**

1.  **The Downtime Domino Effect:** If software updates cause 3 days of downtime monthly (the upper bound), availability drops by 10%. To maintain the same freight volume, you'd need to lease extra diesel trucks or pay overtime, instantly erasing the projected $3.5M fuel savings.
2.  **The Insurance & Maintenance Double-Tax:** If premiums rise 8% and autonomous sensor maintenance exceeds the $45k diesel baseline (likely given the tech complexity), your operational expenditure increases even if fuel costs drop, turning the "savings" into a net loss.
3.  **Regulatory Slip:** If approval slips past 30 Sep 2025, the $500k training budget burns while trucks sit idle, and the 3-year ROI window shrinks, potentially making the payback period exceed the hardware lifespan.

**Kill/Pause Trigger Metric:**

**"Net Operational Variance > $150,000 per month."**

Pause the project immediately if, in any rolling 30-day period, the sum of *(Fuel Savings) – (Increased Insurance + Increased Maintenance + Downtime Opportunity Cost)* is negative by more than $150k. This specifically accounts for downtime averaging **more than 1.8 days per month** or insurance hikes exceeding **5%**, whichever hits first.

*Note: This metric forces you to measure actual cash flow impact rather than just tracking uptime or fuel in isolation.*
*The 3-unit pilot allows you to hit this trigger safely without endangering the company's annual budget.*
