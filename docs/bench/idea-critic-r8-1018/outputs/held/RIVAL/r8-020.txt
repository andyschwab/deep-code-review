**Three Most Likely Failure Points:**

1.  **CAC vs. LTV Mismatch:** Your acquisition cost ($42+) vastly exceeds the first-year lifetime value of a customer (~$78 gross profit before churn), making the unit economics fundamentally negative.
2.  **Churn Underestimation:** An 8% monthly churn (roughly 60% annually) is aggressive for coffee; if actual churn hits 10-12%, the required acquisition volume becomes impossible to sustain with the allocated budget.
3.  **Channel Cannibalization:** If more than 30% of your existing wholesale/retail base migrates to the lower-margin or same-margin subscription channel, you will erode your stable cash flow without gaining enough new net revenue to offset the operational complexity.
