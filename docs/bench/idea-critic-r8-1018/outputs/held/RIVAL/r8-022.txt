**Three Likely Failure Modes**

1.  **The "One-and-Done" Churn Trap:** The 9% lift assumes sustained engagement. If users try the virtual tasting once and never return (churn >85% after month 1), the "repeat purchase" revenue stream vanishes. If you need to hire a production crew or dedicate 10+ hours/week of staff time to manage the platform, your effective margin drops below the 34% model, turning the projected profit into a loss.
3.  **Cannibalization of High-Margin Direct Sales:** While you assume zero cannibalization of cellar-door visits, there is a risk of cannibalizing *high-margin direct online sales*. If existing loyal customers migrate from buying cases directly (full margin) to buying single bottles via the platform (potentially lower margin or discounted), you may see revenue growth but profit contraction.
