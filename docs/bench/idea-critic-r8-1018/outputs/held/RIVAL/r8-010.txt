**Recommendation: PAUSE**

Do not proceed to investment yet. While the unit economics look promising on paper, the regulatory uncertainty (EU REACH) and the unverified "made-to-order" price elasticity create a binary risk profile that could wipe out the projected €208,000 saving. You must validate the polymer certification timeline and cost *before* committing the €195,000 CAPEX/OPEX.

**Three Main Failure Modes**

1.  **Regulatory (Critical):** The new polymer fails REACH compliance or requires costly reformulation. If certification takes >6 months, you miss the 2025 production window entirely, turning the €195k investment into sunk costs with zero return.
2.  **Market:** Customers reject the "made-to-order" wait time or perceive 3D-printed soles as lower quality than leather-cork. If volume drops even 15% below the forecasted 260k units due to churn, the contribution margin evaporates, and you cannot cover the fixed investment.
3.  **Technical/Operational:** The pilot's €4.20 cost was subsidized by ideal conditions. At scale, printer maintenance, material waste, and energy spikes could push the real unit cost above €5.00, eliminating the saving vs. the current leather-cork supply chain.

**Kill Triggers (Pre-agreed)**

*   **Regulatory:** Certification is not granted within **6 months** from application date.

**Arithmetic & Hidden Cost Flags**

*   **Volume Math Error:** Your current volume is ~200k (12k pilot / month × 12 is not the baseline, but 260k is a 30% increase implies a baseline of 200k). Ensure the 260k forecast is backed by soft commitments, not just hope.
*   **Hidden CAPEX:** The €150k printer cost likely excludes installation, facility upgrades (power/ventilation), and spare parts inventory. Budget an extra 15–20% contingency.
*   **Training Underestimation:** €45k for training seems low for industrial 3D printing operations. Does this cover ongoing staff turnover? Specialized operators command higher wages.
*   **Disposal/Recycling:** REACH may require specific disposal protocols for failed prints or support materials, adding a hidden waste management cost not in the €4.20 pilot figure.
*   **Price Elasticity Unproven:** You assume the €120 price holds. Have you tested if customers will actually wait longer for a "custom" sole, or will they switch to competitors with immediate stock?
