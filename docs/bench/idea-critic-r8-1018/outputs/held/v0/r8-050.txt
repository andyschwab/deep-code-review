**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" conditional launch.**
Do not commit the full $200,000 budget immediately. Instead, authorize a **$45,000 Phase 1 pilot** (covering development MVP, 500 farmers, and 2 months of operations) contingent on validating two critical assumptions before scaling to the full 2,500 farmers. The current plan relies on unverified data consumption rates and an overly optimistic adoption curve; scaling prematurely risks burning 60% of your budget on a model that fails unit economics.

**Primary Failure Modes & Hidden Risks:**
1.  **Data Cost Volatility:** The 150 MB/month industry average may not reflect rural Rift Valley usage patterns or local carrier pricing spikes. If actual data costs exceed $0.80 per farmer/month, your $20,000 subsidy will deplete in 4 months rather than 6, leaving farmers disconnected.
2.  **Adoption vs. Activation Gap:** An 80% "adoption" (sign-up) rate does not guarantee active usage. If farmers register but do not complete three cropping cycles due to app complexity or lack of immediate visible value, the projected 12% yield increase—and your $45/ha monetization—will not materialize.
3.  **Hidden Operational Drag:** The $12,000 annual server estimate excludes local technical support and troubleshooting. In low-connectivity zones, app failures often require manual intervention, creating an unbudgeted labor cost that could erode margins.
4.  **Revenue Timing Mismatch:** Monetizing at $45/ha assumes immediate contract enforcement. Smallholders often pay post-harvest; if cash flow is delayed, you may face a liquidity crunch while still paying for data subsidies and staff.

**Kill Criteria & Pivot Triggers:**
Execute the following tripwires at the end of Month 3 (August 2024):
*   **KILL the project if:** Active daily usage drops below **45%** of registered users, OR if average data consumption per farmer exceeds **210 MB/month** (40% over estimate), causing the subsidy burn rate to threaten the 6-month timeline.
*   **PIVOT the strategy if:** Adoption exceeds 60% but yield increases are less than **5%** after the first cycle. This indicates the app is popular but ineffective; shift focus from user acquisition to content/agronomy redesign before spending further on expansion.
*   **PROCEED to full scale only if:** Data costs remain under **$0.70/farmer/month** AND active usage sustains above **70%** with verified yield improvements trending toward 8%+.

*Note: Secure a flexible data agreement with a local carrier now to cap volatility. Do not hire full training staff until the Phase 1 validation confirms the training model works.*
