**Recommendation: Re-work**

Do not proceed to installation yet. The current proposal relies on unverified assumptions that could turn a projected profit into a significant loss. You must validate the 8% sales lift and the 5% price premium with a larger, statistically significant market test before committing $1.2 million in capital. The current focus group of 150 is insufficient to predict behavior at the scale of 2.5 million units.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**
1. **Price Elasticity Mismatch:** The broader market rejects the 5% price premium, causing volume to drop rather than rise, which increases the effective cost per unit sold.
2. **Operational Cost Creep:** The estimated $0.48 per-unit cost rises due to supply chain volatility or lower-than-expected recycling efficiency, eroding margins before the 18-month breakeven.
3. **Greenwashing Backlash:** If the actual recycling rate of the new material is low due to local infrastructure limits, the brand suffers reputational damage without achieving the environmental goal, negating the sales lift.

**Kill Criterion**
Terminate the project immediately if, after a three-month pilot in two distinct geographic regions, the actual sell-through volume is less than 70% of the forecasted target *or* if verified customer willingness to pay falls below a 3% premium.

*Note: Recalculate the breakeven analysis using a "do nothing" baseline where plastic costs rise by 10% annually due to potential regulations, as this may alter the urgency.*
*Ensure the pilot measures the actual recycling disposition of the packages, not just the purchase intent.*
