**Recommendation: GO-IF**

Proceed with the LumenCare Brasil launch, conditional on securing a binding regulatory timeline within 30 days and capping the initial clinician onboarding wave at 40% (rather than the planned 80%) until patient acceptance rates are verified after Month 1.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Regulatory Paralysis:** Launching integration spend ($2.5M) without a confirmed approval date could trap capital in a non-revenue-generating state, missing the Q1 2026 coverage target entirely.
2.  **Adoption Mismatch:** The assumption that 80% of providers will adopt the platform in two months is aggressive; if clinician uptake lags while patient demand spikes due to lower costs, service quality will collapse, damaging the brand.
3.  **Net-Negative Economics:** While per-session savings are $35, the $1.2M in added annual fixed costs (maintenance + legal) requires a minimum volume of ~34,300 sessions just to break even against the *current* baseline. If utilization only reaches the 20% target (30,000 sessions), the project will actually lose ~$150k annually compared to the status quo, rather than saving millions.

**Kill/Pivot Trigger:**
Terminate or pivot to a pilot-only model if: (a) regulatory approval is not granted by December 31, 2025; or (b) net utilization increase is less than 5% after six months of full operation; or (c) any confirmed data-privacy breach occurs.

*Note: The current financial model appears to double-count savings by comparing total projected virtual volume against total in-person costs without subtracting the new $1.2M fixed overhead from the gross savings.*
*Ensure the "binding regulatory timeline" is a written commitment from counsel, not a verbal estimate, before releasing integration funds.*
