**Recommendation: GO-IF**
Proceed with installation only if the collective formally reserves $2,700 (covering the $4,000 gap plus the maximum $900 annual maintenance for three years) before signing contracts. Do not deplete the emergency reserve below $2,300.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**
1.  **Climate Variance:** Rainfall drops below the 2022-23 average (e.g., to 80mm), reducing capture by ~27% and failing to meet the 80% irrigation target, forcing expensive municipal water top-ups.
2.  **Maintenance Cost Shock:** The annual fee stabilizes at the high end ($900) while volunteer labor evaporates, creating a recurring deficit that drains the collective's operating funds within two years.
3.  **Infrastructure Mismatch:** The 1,600L total storage (12 tanks × 200L) is insufficient to handle heavy storm events common in the region, causing overflow waste exactly when capture potential is highest, while failing to sustain plants during dry spells.

**Stop/Redesign Trigger**
Halt expansion or redesign the network if actual captured volume averages **less than 9,000L per month** (60% of projection) for **two consecutive months** during the first rainy season post-commissioning.
