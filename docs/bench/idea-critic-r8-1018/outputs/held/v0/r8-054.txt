Do not authorize the full $5.4M spend or the 120-unit target yet. The current plan assumes a 15% adoption rate without evidence of customer willingness to pay a premium for retrofitted autonomy versus new factory models, and it treats a 9–12 month regulatory delay as a timeline nuisance rather than a binary blocker that could strand inventory.

**Three Most Likely Failure Modes:**
1.  **Regulatory Stranding:** If federal certification exceeds 12 months, you risk holding $2M+ in installed hardware that cannot be legally operated commercially, turning active assets into stranded inventory while incurring storage and depreciation costs.
2.  **The "Dust Degradation" Gap:** The business case assumes $8,000 savings based on full autonomy. If lidar reliability in heavy dust drops below 95% uptime, human intervention will be required frequently, eroding labor savings to near zero while adding high-cost sensor cleaning/maintenance cycles you explicitly assumed away.
3.  **Adoption Reversal:** Customers may reject retrofitted units due to liability concerns or warranty fragmentation (OEM warranty vs. FieldMason kit). If adoption falls to 5% instead of 15%, annual returns drop to $320,000, extending the payback period from ~6 years to over 16 years, destroying the investment thesis.
