**Recommendation: MODIFY (Pause Capital Commitment Pending Regulatory & Budget Validation)**

Do not approve the full $14.5M spend or the Q4 2025 launch date yet. Proceed only under a "Go-If" condition: secure a written preliminary indication of Ministry of Health (MoH) clearance and finalize the $1.2M post-market surveillance budget before committing to the manufacturing line. The current plan contains a critical financial gap and an unverified regulatory timeline that threatens the entire venture.

**Three Main Failure Modes:**

1.  **Regulatory Delay Cascades:** The plan assumes MoH clearance by Q3 2025 with "unknown" timing. If clearance slips by six months, the Q4 2025 rollout fails, leaving the $12M manufacturing line idle while burn rate continues. Without a confirmed regulatory path, the production capacity is a stranded asset.
2.  **Structural Undercapitalization:** The proposal explicitly excludes the $1.2M cost for post-market surveillance and software updates. In medtech, these are mandatory, not optional. Excluding them artificially lowers the break-even point and misrepresents the cash runway required to reach profitability.
3.  **Reimbursement Dependency Risk:** The $350 price point relies on 80% insurance coverage. If the MoH or private insurers negotiate a lower reimbursement rate or delay coverage inclusion, volume will collapse. Selling at full out-of-pocket price ($350) is likely unsustainable for mass adoption, invalidating the 20,000-unit sales projection.

**Kill Criteria & Pivot Triggers:**

*   **Kill Trigger:** If MoH clearance is not granted (or a definitive pathway confirmed) by **June 30, 2025**, terminate the project to salvage R&D assets.
*   **Pivot Trigger:** If initial reimbursement negotiations cover less than **60%** of the device cost, immediately pivot from a mass-market B2C model to a specialized B2B hospital-only deployment to reduce volume dependency.

**Board Focus Areas:**
The break-even math is currently flawed because it ignores the $1.2M surveillance liability; recalculating with this cost raises the required unit sales significantly above the stated 150,000 units. Furthermore, presenting a launch plan with "unknown" regulatory timing exposes the board to unacceptable execution risk; the timeline must be converted from an assumption to a verified milestone before funding.
