**Recommendation: Proceed with Pilot, Conditional on Logistics Verification**

Proceed with the Q3 2025 pilot only if you validate the reverse-logistics cost assumption before finalizing the budget. The current model relies on a €25 flat shipping rate for 50‑kg sofas, which is likely severely underestimated for heavy freight and reverse flows in Italy. If actual shipping costs exceed €45 per unit, your gross margin will collapse below the 45% target, rendering the unit economics negative despite strong top-line revenue.

**Three Main Failure Modes**

1.  **Logistics Cost Blowout:** The €25 shipping assumption is based on small-package benchmarks, not heavy furniture freight. If reverse-logistics costs double to €50 due to weight, volume, and handling fees, the €30 refurbishing cost plus shipping will erode the entire margin per returned item, turning the circular model into a loss leader.
2.  **Churn Cascade:** The model assumes 8% quarterly churn. If churn mimics CasaNova's adjusted rate (approaching 12–15% due to lower price sensitivity or quality issues), the pool of available inventory will shrink faster than the 80% refurbishment rate can replenish it, forcing expensive new manufacturing to meet subscription demand and breaking the cash-flow cycle.
3.  **Refurbishment Bottleneck:** The plan assumes 80% of returns are refurbished within two weeks. If complex damage assessment or parts scarcity extends this cycle to four weeks, inventory availability will drop by half, causing fulfillment delays that drive further churn and increase holding costs, creating a negative feedback loop.

**Kill Criteria**

Terminate the initiative immediately if, by the end of Q2 2026, the verified average reverse-logistics cost per unit exceeds €40 **OR** if the net churn rate (after refurbishment re-entry) sustains above 12% for two consecutive quarters.

*Note: Recalculate the €2.2M revenue projection using net-active subscriptions (accounting for churn) rather than gross capacity to avoid overstating Year 1 cash flow. The €150k marketing budget assumes a Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) that must be validated against the actual conversion rate of the circular pitch versus traditional sales.*
