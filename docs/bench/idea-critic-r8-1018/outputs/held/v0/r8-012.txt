**Recommendation:** Proceed with a limited "Founders Circle" pilot of 500 units before the June 1 launch, rather than a full-scale rollout. This validates the critical assumption of consumer trust in AI-generated scents while capping exposure to inventory and reputation risk.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1. **Trust Rejection:** Customers perceive the "AI blend" as impersonal or gimmicky, resulting in a conversion rate significantly below the projected 30%, leaving expensive inventory unsold.
2. **Margin Erosion:** Unforeseen costs in data licensing, regulatory compliance for new chemical combinations, or higher-than-expected inference costs at scale reduce the €720,000 gross profit projection.
3. **Brand Dilution:** A high return rate or negative reviews regarding scent quality damages Maison Lune's artisanal reputation, cannibalizing sales of the established "Midnight Rose" line.

**Halting Trigger:** Halt the project immediately if the pilot return rate exceeds 15% or if the net promoter score (NPS) from the first 500 customers falls below +20.

*Note: The €0.12 inference cost is negligible compared to the €45,000 sunk cost and potential inventory loss; focus risk management on brand perception. Ensure the pilot includes a blind comparison against human perfumer blends to isolate the "AI" variable.*
