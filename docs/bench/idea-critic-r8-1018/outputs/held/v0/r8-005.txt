1.  **Adoption Stagnation:** Patrons refuse to switch from paper, keeping digital usage below 5%. This nullifies the projected revenue lift while you still incur the $8,000 annual maintenance fee, resulting in a net loss compared to the status quo.

3.  **Fee Structure Creep:** The 2% transaction fee combined with lower-than-expected ticket volumes causes the effective cost per ticket to exceed the current $10,000 aggregate spend on prints and third-party fees, making the new system more expensive than the old one despite the efficiency promises.
