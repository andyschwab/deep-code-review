**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" conditional pilot, not a full launch.**
Approve the $200,000 initial spend only if a binding kill criterion is set for data costs and adoption at the 6-week mark. Do not commit to the full six-month timeline or the $45/ha monetization assumption until these variables are verified in the field.

**Primary Failure Modes & Hidden Risks:**
1.  **Data Subsidy Insolvency:** The budget allocates $20,000 for data subsidies for 2,500 farmers over 6 months ($1.33/farmer/month). At 150 MB/month, this implies a data cost of ~$0.008/MB. If rural pricing exceeds $0.01/MB or usage spikes to 200 MB (common with media-rich training), the subsidy fund depletes by Month 4, halting access.
2.  **Adoption vs. Yield Lag:** The business case assumes 80% adoption and 12% yield increase after *three* cropping cycles. However, the pilot is only six months (June–November). In many Rift Valley contexts, this covers only one, perhaps two cycles. You will not see the monetized yield increase within the pilot window, making the $45/ha revenue projection impossible to validate before funds run out.
3.  **Hidden OPEX Gap:** The $12,000 annual server maintenance cost is excluded from the pilot budget. If the pilot extends or scales, this unaccounted liability erodes margins immediately.
4.  **Revenue Model Mismatch:** Charging $10/user/year to smallholders who have not yet realized the 12% yield gain (due to the cycle lag) creates a payment barrier that directly contradicts the 80% adoption target.

**Kill Criteria (Tripwires):**
Execute a formal review at **Week 6 (mid-July 2024)**. Terminate or pivot the project immediately if:
*   **Adoption < 45%:** Indicates friction in onboarding or value perception before the first harvest.
*   **Average Data Cost > $1.50/farmer/month:** Projects a total subsidy need of >$22,500 for the remaining period, exceeding the remaining budget.
*   **Active Usage < 2 days/week:** Suggests the training content is not engaging or accessible, invalidating the yield improvement hypothesis.

**Leading Metric to Watch:**
*   **Cost-Per-Active-Farmer-Week:** Track total spend (subsidies + staff) divided by unique active users weekly. If this exceeds $0.40/week, the unit economics cannot support the $10/year price point.
