**Recommendation:** Proceed with a limited "Founders' Circle" pilot of 500 units in June instead of the full 12,000-unit launch. This approach validates the critical assumption of consumer trust in AI-generated scents while capping initial exposure and preserving capital.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Trust Rejection:** Customers perceive the AI blend as impersonal or low-quality compared to a human perfumer, resulting in a conversion rate significantly below the projected 30%, rendering the marketing spend inefficient.
2.  **Unit Economics Collapse:** If the actual cost per acquired customer or return rate exceeds estimates due to scent dissatisfaction, the €0.12 inference cost becomes negligible compared to the loss on unsold or returned inventory, eroding the projected €720,000 gross profit.
3.  **Brand Dilution:** A flawed initial batch of AI-generated scents damages Maison Lune's reputation for artisanal quality, negatively impacting sales of the core "Midnight Rose" line and future product launches.

**Halt Trigger:** Stop the project immediately if the pilot conversion rate falls below 15% or if the return/complaint rate exceeds 10% within the first 60 days.

*Note: The current projection assumes zero data-licensing fees; verify this contractually before scaling. The €45,000 sunk cost should not influence the go/no-go decision for the pilot.*
