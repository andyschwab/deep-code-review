**Recommendation:** Do not proceed to the board with the current financial model. Revise the proposal to calculate the $12,000 projected gain using **contribution margin** (profit per pour) rather than total revenue, and treat the 3% transaction fee as a negligible offset ($0.45 per pour) that does not cover the $1,500 monthly maintenance. The current math overstates profitability and understates the break-even adoption rate.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Margin Illusion:** Basing the $12,000 benefit on total revenue instead of contribution margin creates a false positive; if your beer margin is 70%, the actual lift is ~$8,400, potentially leaving the project net-negative after maintenance.
2.  **Adoption Friction:** Achieving a 40% download rate (720 users) from a tap-room audience is exceptionally high for a voluntary app; if adoption lands at a more standard 15%, the transaction fees and lift will not cover the $1,500/month burn.
3.  **Integration Stall:** The "unknown" effort to integrate with Square could delay the June 15 launch, turning a fixed-cost pilot into a sunk-cost drain before a single data point is collected.

**Kill Criteria:**
Terminate the pilot immediately if, after 60 days: (1) active user adoption is below 20% of monthly visitors, or (2) the net operational profit (lift + fees minus maintenance) is negative for two consecutive months.
