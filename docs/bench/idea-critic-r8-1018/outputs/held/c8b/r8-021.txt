**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **conditional one-year pilot (Go-If)** rather than committing to the full three-year, 36M SEK program immediately. Authorize the first 12M SEK only if a binding regulatory pre-check confirms the corn precedent applies to wheat within 90 days, and if field trials at four additional diverse sites demonstrate a statistically significant yield increase (>12%) with acceptable variance by month 18.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Regulatory Mismatch:** The EU authority rejects the 18-month corn timeline for wheat, extending approval beyond 24 months and destroying the five-year revenue window.
2.  **Environmental Variance:** The 9% yield lift from the initial two sites proves anomalous; broader testing across the remaining 10 sites reveals high variance or lower averages (<10%), failing to outperform GreenField Agro's conventional 10% lift at a higher cost.
3.  **Market Mispricing:** Capturing 5% of the market requires displacing established conventional seeds; if farmers prioritize the lower cost/known safety of GreenField's solution over our unproven CRISPR trait, the 200M SEK revenue pool remains inaccessible.

**Stop/Pivot Trigger:** Halt the project immediately if the EU regulatory body issues any formal objection regarding the wheat-specific timeline before month 12, or if the aggregate yield data from the first six field sites averages below 11% after two growing seasons.

*Note: This approach treats the regulatory timeline and yield consistency as unverified assumptions rather than facts, preventing sunk costs in a high-variance environment.*
