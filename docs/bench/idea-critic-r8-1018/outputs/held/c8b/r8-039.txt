**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" conditional approval, not a full green light.**
Do not commit the full $12M or finalize the tower count until the spectrum licensing fee is confirmed in writing and a 30-day power reliability audit is completed for the specific tower sites. The current plan relies on two unverified variables (spectrum cost and 95% grid reliability) that could individually destroy the project's unit economics.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Capital Explosion from Hidden Spectrum Costs:** If the "low-six-figure" fee per MHz applies to the total bandwidth required for 250k households rather than a flat regional rate, licensing could consume $3–5M instead of the implied ~$500k, blowing a 25–40% hole in the budget before construction begins.
2.  **OpEx Collapse due to Power Instability:** The 95% grid reliability assumption is based on a 2022 report; if recent outages have degraded reliability to 80%, the cost of diesel generators and fuel for 45 remote towers will skyrocket, likely exceeding the $5 ARPU gain and turning every new subscriber into a net loss.
3.  **Revenue Shortfall from Overestimated ARPU:** The $5 monthly increase assumes immediate uptake and usage; if rural adoption is slower or usage lower due to device affordability, achieving only a $2 ARPU lift would extend the payback period from ~2 years to over 5 years, making the $12M investment financially unviable against the 3% churn rate.

**Kill Criteria (Halt/Re-scope Triggers):**
*   **Spectrum Cost:** Halt immediately if the formal licensing quote exceeds **$750,000 total** (approx. 50% above the optimistic "low-six-figure" aggregate assumption).
*   **Power Reliability:** Re-scope the tower count or halt if the 30-day site audit shows grid availability below **90%**, requiring a redesign for solar/hybrid power that increases CAPEX by >15%.
*   **ARPU Performance:** Halt expansion after the pilot (July 2024–Jan 2025) if the realized ARPU increase is below **$3.00** after six months of operation, as this indicates the $9M Year-2 revenue target is unachievable.

*Note: The $2M staff training budget appears disproportionately high relative to the $9M total tower CAPEX; verify if this includes community hiring programs or if it can be optimized. Ensure the "low-six-figure" spectrum hint is clarified as per-MHz or per-license before any financial modeling is finalized.*
