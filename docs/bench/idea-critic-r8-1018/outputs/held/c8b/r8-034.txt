**Recommendation:** Proceed with the pilot only if the supplier provides a binding delivery guarantee for printheads by 30 June 2025; otherwise, defer the capex decision to Q1 2026 to avoid stranded setup costs.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Adoption Overestimation:** The plan assumes 70% of B2B accounts will adopt custom orders despite a 50% reduction in average order size (300 to 150 sqm). If operational friction causes uptake to stay below 40%, the 18% revenue lift vanishes while fixed costs remain.
2.  **Hidden Operational Costs:** Benchmarking against a competitor's lower energy draw while ignoring their higher maintenance contract likely understates total cost of ownership. If maintenance fees exceed the energy savings, the 18-month break-even target will slip significantly.
3.  **Supply Chain Delay:** The unspecified lead time for printheads poses a critical path risk. A delay pushing rollout to Q3 2026 extends the pilot start date, deferring revenue and potentially misaligning with the 2027 projection window.

**Kill Criterion:** Halt the project immediately if, after the six-month pilot, less than 25% of existing accounts have placed a repeat custom order or if the average order size remains below 100 sqm.
