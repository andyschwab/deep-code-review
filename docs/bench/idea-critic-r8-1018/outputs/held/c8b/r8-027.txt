**Recommendation: GO-IF**

Proceed with the LumenCare Brasil launch, conditional on securing a provisional regulatory pathway confirmation within 45 days and locking the platform maintenance fee at a fixed rate for 24 months. The projected net savings of $2.6M annually ($5.2M gross savings minus $1.2M ongoing costs) against a $3.1M upfront investment yields a payback period of approximately 14 months, which is acceptable given the strategic necessity of increasing utilization from 12% to 20%. However, the "go" decision is strictly contingent on de-risking the regulatory timeline and capping variable infrastructure costs before committing the full integration budget.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**

1.  **Regulatory Paralysis:** The assumption of a Q1 2026 full coverage relies on an undefined approval timeline. If Brazilian tele-health regulations tighten or approval delays extend beyond Q4 2025, the projected session volume will not materialize, turning the $3.1M upfront spend into a sunk cost with no revenue offset.
2.  **Provider Adoption Friction:** The plan assumes 80% provider adoption within two months. If clinicians resist the new workflow due to inadequate training or technical friction, session capacity will remain bottlenecked regardless of patient demand, preventing the move from 18,000 to 30,000 sessions.
3.  **Patient Acceptance Drop-off:** While initial curiosity may drive early numbers, if the patient acceptance rate falls below 50% after the first month due to cultural preferences for in-person care or poor user experience, the utilization target of 20% will be mathematically unreachable without unsustainable marketing spend.

**Kill/Pivot Trigger:**

Terminate or pivot the project if **utilization increases by less than 5% (approx. 900 additional sessions) six months post-launch** OR if **any confirmed data-privacy breach occurs**. The utilization metric validates the core volume hypothesis; failing to hit this minimal threshold indicates the model cannot scale to the 20% target. A privacy breach is a non-negotiable stop condition given the sensitivity of psychiatric data and the regulatory environment.
