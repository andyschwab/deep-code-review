**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" Condition**
Approve the full rollout only if a targeted 30-day winter integration pilot confirms SCADA stability below -10°C and validates the 5% loss-reduction rate. Without this, the risk of systemic integration failure outweighs the projected savings.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**
1.  **Environmental Integration Collapse:** The assumption of "seamless" SCADA integration is untested in sub-zero temperatures. If the AquaSense devices fail to transmit or corrupt data during winter freezes, the utility loses visibility into 12,000 endpoints, potentially masking massive leaks rather than detecting them.
2.  **Hardware Cost Underestimation:** The total hardware budget of $5.4M relies entirely on the $375/unit quote. Supply chain volatility or hidden import tariffs could easily push unit costs up by 15–20%, creating an immediate $800k–$1M funding gap not currently priced into the $6.6M total project cost.
3.  **Savings Realization Lag:** The pilot's 4.8% drop was observed over only three months. If the savings degrade over time due to sensor drift or if the 5% annual reduction assumes a constant leak rate that doesn't persist, the ROI timeline could stretch from the projected ~5.5 years to over 8 years, failing to meet internal hurdle rates.
