Do not commit the full $5.4M or the full 120-unit scope yet. Instead, execute a hardened 20-unit Phase 0 immediately within your existing $2M budget to validate the dust reliability and maintenance assumptions before scaling to 60 or 120 units. The current math relies on unverified "zero maintenance" and "flat insurance" assumptions that, if wrong, destroy the ROI.

**Three Most Likely Failure Modes:**
1.  **The "Zero Maintenance" Fallacy:** Retrofitted sensors on older chassis (2022–2024) face higher vibration and dust ingress than factory-integrated systems. If lidar cleaning or recalibration adds just $1,500 per tractor annually, your net savings drop by ~19%, extending payback from 5.6 years to over 7 years, likely killing adoption.
2.  **Insurance & Liability Spike:** Insurers often treat retrofits as high-risk modifications. A premium increase of only $2,000/year per unit wipes out 25% of your projected savings, turning the $8,000 benefit into $6,000 and making the $45,000 capex unjustifiable for customers.
3.  **Regulatory Limbo:** If federal certification takes 18 months instead of 9–12, your Q1 2026 completion target fails. You risk having 60 idle, capitalized assets generating zero revenue while incurring storage and depreciation costs, burning cash without data.
