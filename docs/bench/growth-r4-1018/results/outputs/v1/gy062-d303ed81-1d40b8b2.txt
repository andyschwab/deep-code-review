### Proposed Granular Funnel: Supply Visibility to Demand Conversion

To isolate the disconnect between supply availability and demand discovery, replace the current broad funnel with the following five-step instrumentation plan. Each step maps to a specific hypothesis regarding visibility, pricing, or trust.

**1. Event: `menu_viewed`**
*   **Definition:** An office manager loads a chef's public menu page.
*   **Question Answered:** Is the friction **visibility** (offices aren't seeing the menu) or **interest** (they see it but don't engage)?
*   **Hypothesis:** If `menu_viewed` counts are low relative to `menu_published`, the issue is discovery algorithm or listing placement. If counts are high but drop-off occurs later, visibility is not the root cause.

**2. Event: `menu_scroll_depth` (Properties: `percentage_reached`, `time_on_page`)**
*   **Definition:** User scrolls past 50% of the menu or spends >15 seconds on the page.
*   **Question Answered:** Is the content (photos, descriptions) retaining attention?
*   **Hypothesis:** Low scroll depth supports your "poor photo quality" theory; users bounce immediately upon seeing the visual presentation. High scroll depth with no action suggests the content is engaging, but the offer (price/terms) is the blocker.

**3. Event: `pricing_details_expanded`**
*   **Definition:** User clicks to view full pricing breakdown, serving sizes, or dietary details.
*   **Question Answered:** Is the friction **pricing** or **clarity**?
*   **Hypothesis:** If users scroll deeply but never expand pricing, the cost structure may be unclear or perceived as too high without needing explicit confirmation. This isolates price sensitivity before the inquiry attempt.

**4. Event: `inquiry_initiated`**
*   **Definition:** User clicks the "Contact Chef" or "Request Quote" button.
*   **Question Answered:** Is the friction in the **trust signal** or the **form complexity**?
*   **Hypothesis:** A drop-off here (after viewing price) indicates a lack of trust signals (reviews, chef credentials) or anxiety about the commitment level required to send a message.

**5. Event: `inquiry_submitted`**
*   **Definition:** User successfully sends the inquiry form.
*   **Question Answered:** Is the form itself broken or too demanding?
*   **Hypothesis:** If `inquiry_initiated` is high but `inquiry_submitted` is low, the form fields are likely too intrusive or technically failing.

### Implementation Priorities for Engineering & Design

*   **Instrumentation Scope:** Implement these five events immediately for the MVP stage. Do not track "time on page" globally; only track it where it answers the specific question of content engagement.
*   **Identifier Seam:** Ensure a stable, pseudonymous user ID links these events across sessions to distinguish between a single office manager browsing multiple chefs versus unique traffic.
*   **Data Governance:** Route any PII collected in the inquiry form to privacy compliance protocols; do not store raw form data in the analytics event properties.
