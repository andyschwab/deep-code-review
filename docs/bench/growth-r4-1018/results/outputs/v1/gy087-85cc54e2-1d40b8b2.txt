### Funnel Tracking Framework: Wanderlust Bookings Relaunch

**Audience:** Marketing Team & Product Operations
**Effective Date:** April 5 (Launch Day)

#### 1. The North Star Metric
**Metric:** Weekly Confirmed Bookings (Value Delivered).
**Rejection of Vanity:** Do not optimize for "Landing Page Views" or "Initiated Checkouts." These rise regardless of business health. Focus strictly on users completing the core value exchange.

#### 2. Defined Funnel Stages & Event Taxonomy
Instrument only these five events. Each must answer a specific question about friction. Do not instrument "just in case."

| Stage | Event Name | Question Answered | Critical Properties |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :|
| **Activation** | `checkout_started` | Did the user commit to buying? | `destination_id`, `device_type`, `traffic_source` |
| **Friction 1** | `checkout_step_1_complete` | Is the traveler info form blocking us? | `step_number` (1-5), `time_on_step_sec` |
| **Friction 2** | `checkout_step_2_complete` | Is the date selection causing drop-off? | `step_number`, `errors_count` |
| **Friction 3** | `checkout_step_3_complete` | Are add-ons confusing the user? | `step_number`, `addons_selected` |
| **Friction 4** | `checkout_step_4_complete` | Is the payment method selection failing? | `step_number`, `payment_method_type` |
| **Conversion** | `booking_confirmed` | Did the transaction succeed? | `total_value`, `currency`, `booking_id` |

**Success Criteria:**
*   **Baseline Status:** `UNVERIFIED`. The assumption that drop-off will match 2019 (65%) is invalid due to post-pandemic shifts and broken tags. You must establish a new baseline from Day 1 data.
*   **Target:** Identify the single step with the highest relative drop-off (>20% loss between steps) within the first 7 days.

#### 3. Handling Exits and Returns (The Identifier Seam)
To track users exiting and returning after the 30-minute session timeout:
*   **Requirement:** Implement a stable, pseudonymous `user_id` persisted in local storage or cookies, distinct from the session ID.
*   **Logic:** Attribute `booking_confirmed` to the original `checkout_started` event if the `user_id` matches, even if the timestamp exceeds the 30-minute window.
*   **Constraint:** Do not store PII (names, emails) in analytics events. Route identity management to privacy compliance protocols. Without this `user_id`, cross-session conversion is `UNVERIFIED`.

#### 4. External Correlation Factors
Do not build complex real-time feeds into the core funnel dashboard yet. Instead, tag events with these static properties at the time of occurrence to enable later segmentation:
*   `destination_risk_level`: (Low/Med/High) based on manual weekly updates regarding travel restrictions.
*   `currency_volatility_flag`: (True/False) triggered if FX fluctuation exceeds 5% against the user's home currency on that day.
*   **Action:** Compare conversion rates of users tagged with `destination_risk_level: High` vs. `Low` to isolate policy friction.

#### 5. This-Week Steps
1.  **Engineering:** Deploy the five events listed above with the `user_id` property by April 4. Verify data flow in the staging environment.
2.  **Analytics:** Create a "Step-to-Step Drop-off" report grouped by `device_type`. If mobile drop-off is >15% higher than desktop on Step 1, flag UI layout as the primary hypothesis for Week 2 testing.
3.  **Operations:** Manually log the `destination_risk_level` for top 10 destinations into a lookup table for join-analysis on April 12.

*Note: No benchmark thresholds are asserted here; all "good" or "bad" readings must be compared against your own Day 1–7 baseline. Any metric lacking the specified instrumentation is considered `UNVERIFIED`.*
