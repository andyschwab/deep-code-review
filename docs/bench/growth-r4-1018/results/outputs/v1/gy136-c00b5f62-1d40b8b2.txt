**Recommendation: Adopt "Weekly Active Users Sending ≥3 Direct Messages" as the North Star Metric.**

For your Series B pitch and the next twelve months of growth, **individual user utility (direct messages)** is the superior North Star over network density. While density (3.5 connections/user) suggests potential, it is a **vanity metric** in isolation; a user can have many connections without deriving value or returning. Direct messages represent the **core action** where actual value exchange occurs. If users are not messaging, the network is dormant regardless of connection count.

**Evaluation of Downstream Effects**

*   **Choosing Density (Connections):** Optimizing for connection count risks inflating the graph with low-quality ties. Users may accept invites passively but never engage. This creates a "hollow network" where acquisition looks healthy (high signups/connections) but **retention leaks** because the core value proposition (communication) is unused. You risk burning capital acquiring users who churn immediately after onboarding.
*   **Choosing Utility (Messages):** Optimizing for messages forces the product to solve the "cold start" problem for every individual. It ensures that every new user finds immediate value. Crucially, **retention drives acquisition** in networking tools; a user who messages weekly is statistically more likely to invite colleagues organically than a user who simply has a high connection count. This aligns with the **AARRR framework's** directive to fix retention before pouring fuel on acquisition.

**Strategic Rationale for Investors**
Venture partners prioritize **sustainable growth**, which is a function of retention, not just top-line signup velocity. A North Star based on *active utility* proves product-market fit. A North Star based on *density* proves only that the onboarding flow works. By selecting "Weekly Active Users Sending ≥3 Direct Messages," you signal that ConnectHub optimizes for **daily value delivery**, which is the only reliable predictor of long-term LTV and organic virality.

**Input Metrics to Move the North Star**
To drive this North Star, track these specific inputs:
1.  **Activation Rate:** % of new users sending their first message within 24 hours.
2.  **Connection-to-Message Conversion:** % of new connections that result in a message within 7 days.
3.  **Weekly Retention Cohorts:** % of users who sent ≥3 messages in Week 1 and return to do so in Week 2.

1.  **Instrumentation:** Verify your event taxonomy includes a `message_sent` event with a `user_id` property to calculate the weekly active messenger count. If this data is missing, label it `UNVERIFIED` and prioritize this instrumentation immediately.
