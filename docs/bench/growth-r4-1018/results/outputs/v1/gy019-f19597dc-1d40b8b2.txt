### Q3 Measurement Plan: Frequency vs. Reach for NicheGear

**North Star Metric:** Quote Requests per Unique Professional (not total leads or raw impressions).
*Reject vanity metrics:* Total impressions and raw reach are irrelevant in a 5,000-person market; they rise even if trust falls. Focus strictly on the conversion of known individuals.

**Event Taxonomy & Instrumentation**
Instrument only these three events to answer specific questions. Do not track "views" generally; track them against the user identity.

1.  **Event:** `ad_impression_delivered`
    *   **Properties:** `professional_id` (pseudonymous, stable across sessions), `campaign_id`, `creative_variant`, `timestamp`.
    *   **Question Answered:** "How many times has this specific professional seen our message?"
    *   **Note:** Requires a stable identifier to link impressions to the same person across devices/sessions. Without this, frequency analysis is impossible.

2.  **Event:** `quote_request_initiated`
    *   **Properties:** `professional_id`, `source_campaign`, `cumulative_impressions_at_conversion` (calculated at event time).
    *   **Question Answered:** "At what impression count did this professional finally act?"

3.  **Event:** `ad_negative_signal` (Proxy for fatigue)
    *   **Properties:** `professional_id`, `signal_type` (e.g., "hide_ad", "bounce_rate_>90%", "unsubscribe").
    *   **Question Answered:** "At what point does repetition cause active disengagement?"

**Analysis Method: The Frequency Curve**
Do not guess the "optimal" number. Construct a cohort analysis post-campaign using your own data:
*   **Bucket A:** Professionals with 1–2 impressions.
*   **Bucket B:** Professionals with 3–5 impressions.
*   **Bucket C:** Professionals with 6–10 impressions.
*   **Bucket D:** Professionals with 11+ impressions.

Calculate the **Conversion Rate** (Quote Requests / Unique Professionals) for each bucket.
*   **The Sweet Spot:** The bucket with the highest conversion rate.
*   **The Fatigue Threshold:** The point where the conversion rate drops or negative signals spike despite higher exposure.

**Experiment Design (Pre-committed)**
Since you cannot know the threshold beforehand, run a **Frequency Capped A/B Test** for the first 30 days of Q3.
*   **Control Group:** Standard algorithmic delivery (no hard cap).
*   **Test Group A:** Hard cap at 3 impressions per user per week.
*   **Test Group B:** Hard cap at 7 impressions per user per week.
*   **Primary Metric:** Quote Requests per Unique Professional.
*   **Guardrail Metric:** Cost Per Quote Request (ensure higher frequency doesn't just burn budget without lift).
*   **Rule:** Do not stop early based on day-to-day fluctuations. Run for the full 30 days to account for the long purchase cycle, then compare the aggregate conversion rates of the three groups.

2.  **Define the Cap Buckets:** Instruct the agency to set up the three frequency cap segments (No Cap, 3x, 7x) with equal budget allocation.
3.  **Baseline Check:** Pull February's data to calculate the current average impressions per converter (if identity data exists); if not, mark the baseline as `UNVERIFIED` and rely entirely on the Q3 test results.

*Note: All benchmarks (e.g., "good conversion rate") must be derived from NicheGear's historical data, not industry averages, which do not exist for this niche.*
*Ensure PII handling for `professional_id` complies with privacy regulations before ingestion.*
