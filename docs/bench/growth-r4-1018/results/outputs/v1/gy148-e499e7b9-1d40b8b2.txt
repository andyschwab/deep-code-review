**Recommendation: North Star Metric – "Quarterly Active Enterprises with Verified Repeat Purchases"**

For GreenLedger's Series B narrative and internal alignment, the recommended North Star is the count of **unique enterprise clients who have purchased verified carbon offsets in at least two distinct quarters within the last 12 months**.

This metric shifts the focus from cumulative volume (which can be inflated by one-off deals) to sustained client value, directly addressing your concern about quality and permanence without waiting for 14-month contract renewals.

**Evaluation of Candidate Metrics**

*   **Total Tons Offset (Current):**
    *   *Pros:* Simple to calculate; appeals to immediate environmental impact claims.
    *   *Cons:* Encourages sales to chase large, one-time transactions regardless of project quality or client fit. It masks churn; you could lose 20% of your clients while tons rise due to a single big deal. It does not incentivize engineering to build retention features.
*   **Number of Active Corporate Buyers:**
    *   *Pros:* Tracks market penetration; easy for sales to understand.
    *   *Cons:* "Active" is often defined too loosely (e.g., logged in or signed a contract). A client who buys once and never returns counts the same as a loyal partner. This risks encouraging sales to close low-quality leads just to boost the headcount.
*   **Diversity of Offset Projects:**
    *   *Pros:* Aligns with portfolio risk management and quality narratives.
    *   *Cons:* Dangerous as a primary North Star. It may incentivize product teams to fragment the catalog unnecessarily or sales to push obscure projects clients don't need, potentially hurting conversion rates and overall volume.
*   **Repeat Purchase Rate per Quarter (Recommended Core):**
    *   *Pros:* Directly measures whether clients derive ongoing value. It forces Sales to qualify leads that fit long-term needs and Engineering to streamline the re-purchase experience. It solves the "14-month lag" by measuring behavior quarterly rather than waiting for contract renewal.
    *   *Cons:* Requires robust event tracking to distinguish distinct purchase events across quarters.

**Behavioral Influence**

Adopting "Quarterly Active Enterprises with Verified Repeat Purchases" aligns teams effectively:
*   **Sales:** Will prioritize closing clients with genuine recurring needs rather than one-off compliance spikes, as a single sale does not move the North Star.
*   **Product/Engineering:** Will focus on reducing friction in the re-buy flow, improving reporting dashboards that prove value to the client before the next quarter, and highlighting high-quality projects that encourage repeat business.
*   **Leadership:** Provides a leading indicator of health. If this number grows, revenue stability follows; if it stalls, you know immediately despite high total tonnage.

**Implementation Note**
To track this, you must instrument a `purchase_completed` event with a stable `enterprise_id` and `timestamp`. Do not rely on contract start/end dates alone; the metric requires evidence of discrete transactional intent in multiple quarters.
