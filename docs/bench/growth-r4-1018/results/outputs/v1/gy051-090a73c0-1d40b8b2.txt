**Recommendation: Do not roll out globally. Limit the simplified form to first-time bookers only and retain the detailed form for returning logistics managers.**

The data indicates a segmentation failure, not a universal win. While the variant lifted new user conversion to 18.4%, it halved the efficiency for your core power users (9.1%). Returning managers likely rely on the "extra fields" you removed to configure complex multi-stop routing or specific temperature protocols in a single session. By simplifying the interface, you inadvertently increased the number of steps or clicks required for these complex bookings, causing friction. Rolling this out globally risks alienating your most valuable, repeat customers to chase marginal gains in new user acquisition.

**Metrics to Track Next**
To validate this hypothesis and refine the experience, instrument the following specific events immediately. Do not track generic pageviews; track actions that answer whether the form supports complex workflows.

1.  **Event: `booking_flow_abandonment_stage`**
    *   **Question Answered:** At exactly which step do returning users drop off in the simplified flow compared to the original?
    *   **Properties:** `user_type` (new vs. returning), `form_variant` (original vs. simplified), `step_name` (e.g., "route_details", "temperature_config"), `booking_complexity_score` (number of stops selected).
    *   **Decision Rule:** If returning users drop off specifically at the "add stop" or "advanced config" steps in the simplified variant, the hypothesis is confirmed: they need the detailed fields upfront.

2.  **Event: `post_booking_support_ticket`**
    *   **Question Answered:** Does the simplified form increase operational load by forcing users to call/email to complete complex bookings they couldn't finish online?
    *   **Properties:** `user_id`, `days_since_last_booking`, `ticket_category` (e.g., "routing_error", "missing_field"), `variant_seen`.
    *   **Decision Rule:** If support tickets regarding "missing options" or "routing errors" rise among returning users exposed to the variant, the cost of the "simplification" exceeds the conversion gain.

3.  **Event: `booking_completion_time_by_complexity`**
    *   **Question Answered:** Does the simplified form actually save time for complex bookings, or does it force users to backtrack?
    *   **Properties:** `booking_type` (single-stop vs. multi-stop), `variant`, `duration_seconds`.
    *   **Decision Rule:** If multi-stop bookings take longer on the simplified variant, the UI is hiding necessary complexity rather than removing it.
