**Decision Recommendation: Segment by Device (Mobile Only) with Immediate Desktop Investigation**

Do not execute a global rollout. The net impact of a global deployment is mathematically ambiguous without traffic weighting, but the risk of alienating high-value desktop users violates your core constraint. Proceed with a mobile-only release to capture the 15% gain while holding desktop on the control variant. Simultaneously, launch a targeted investigation into the desktop regression before the June 1 deadline.

**Risk Analysis by Path**

*   **Global Rollout:** High Risk. If desktop traffic constitutes more than ~65% of your volume (assuming equal average order value), the 8% loss outweighs the 15% mobile gain, resulting in net revenue decline. Even if mobile volume is higher, the "high-value" nature of desktop users suggests the revenue loss per user is likely disproportionate, increasing the break-even threshold for mobile volume. Furthermore, if the desktop drop stems from perceived security issues, this damages trust beyond immediate conversion metrics.
*   **Segment by Device (Recommended):** Moderate Risk. This captures verified mobile upside immediately. The primary risk is engineering complexity in maintaining two checkout flows and potential data fragmentation in future analysis. However, this aligns with the hypothesis that device context drives behavior (typing friction vs. security perception).
*   **Investigate Further (No Action):** High Opportunity Cost. Delaying the mobile win until the desktop issue is fully resolved likely means missing the peak summer season start. Given the clear mobile signal, inaction leaves money on the table.

**Data Required to Resolve Uncertainty**

To finalize the long-term desktop strategy, gather the following immediately:

1.  **Quantitative Guardrails (Revenue-Weighted):** Recalculate the net impact using *Revenue Per Visitor* (RPV) rather than raw conversion rate. If desktop users have a 2x higher Average Order Value (AOV), an 8% conversion drop is effectively a 16% revenue hit relative to mobile. You need the exact traffic split and AOV by device to compute the true net revenue delta.
2.  **Funnel Drop-off Location:** Instrument events to identify *where* desktop users abandon the simplified form. If they drop at the payment entry step, it supports the "security perception" hypothesis. If they drop earlier, the issue may be layout or trust signals unrelated to field count.
3.  **Qualitative Validation:** Deploy an exit-intent survey specifically for desktop users on the variant who abandoned checkout. Ask: "What prevented you from completing your booking?" Look for keywords related to "trust," "security," "too simple," or "suspicious."
