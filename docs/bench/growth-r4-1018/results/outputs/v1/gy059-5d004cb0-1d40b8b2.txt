### Re-engagement Diagnostic Framework: FitTrack Dormant User Campaign

**Objective:** Isolate the friction point between email open and app return for the 30,000-user cohort (dormant >60 days) to inform the next wave launching in two weeks.

**1. Funnel Segmentation & Hypothesis Testing**
Do not analyze the 30,000 users as a single block. Slice the data by these specific dimensions to identify if the 1.5% CTR is universal or isolated:
*   **Dormancy Duration:** Split into 60–90 days, 90–180 days, and 180+ days. *Hypothesis:* Users dormant >180 days may have uninstalled the app or forgotten their credentials, creating a technical or psychological barrier unrelated to offer relevance.
*   **Last Known Activity:** Segment by their last core action (e.g., "completed a workout" vs. "only viewed dashboard"). *Hypothesis:* Users who previously derived value (completed workouts) should respond better to a "we miss you" message than passive viewers.
*   **Device/OS:** Split by iOS vs. Android and app version at time of dormancy. *Hypothesis:* If one OS shows significantly lower CTR, a broken deep link or OS-specific notification permission change may be blocking the path.
*   **Acquisition Source:** Compare organic vs. paid acquisition cohorts. *Hypothesis:* Paid users may have lower intrinsic motivation to return without a stronger monetary incentive.

**2. The "Return Path" Audit (Technical & UX)**
Since opens are healthy (22%) but clicks are low, the friction likely exists between the decision to click and the successful app launch.
*   **Deep Link Validation:** Verify that the email links direct users to the specific "Free Week" redemption screen inside the app, not just the home screen or a generic login page. If the user must hunt for the offer, drop-off increases.
*   **Login Friction:** Analyze if the click leads to a login wall. If users forgot passwords, a "reset password" step before seeing the offer kills conversion.
*   **Load Time & Crashes:** Check crash logs specifically for this cohort's device types during the campaign window (Feb 10–present). An old app version crashing on launch would suppress CTR.

**3. Message-Offer Resonance Check**
*   **A/B Test Content (Next Wave):** For the upcoming campaign, test two variables against a holdout group:
    *   *Variable A (Offer):* "Free Week Premium" vs. "Personalized Progress Report" (curiosity vs. utility).
    *   *Variable B (Timing):* Send time optimization based on the user's historical active hours rather than a blanket send time.
*   **Survey Micro-intercept:** Add a one-click survey in the email footer for non-clickers: "Why aren't you returning?" with options like "Too busy," "Forgot password," or "Not interested."

**4. Immediate Actions for Next Wave (Launch in 2 Weeks)**
*   **Clean the List:** Exclude users dormant >180 days from the general "Free Week" blast; route them to a "Re-onboarding" flow addressing account recovery first.
*   **Fix the Deep Link:** Ensure the CTA button lands users directly on the premium activation screen post-login.
*   **Define Success Metric:** Shift focus from CTR to "Day-1 Retention after Re-activation." A click is vanity if they don't complete a workout within 24 hours of returning.

**Note:** All benchmarks must be calculated against FitTrack's own historical segments; do not compare to industry averages. Ensure all user identifiers used in this analysis are pseudonymized per privacy compliance standards.
