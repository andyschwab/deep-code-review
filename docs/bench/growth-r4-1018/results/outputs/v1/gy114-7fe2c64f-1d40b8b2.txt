### Event Tracking Priority Plan for LearnHub Pilot
**Audience:** CTO | **Context:** Aug 20 Soft Launch (50 Schools) | **Goal:** Validate conversion drivers without engineering bloat.

#### Non-Negotiable Day One Events (Launch: Aug 20)
Instrument only these three to answer the core business question: *Does completing the core workflow predict value?*

1.  **`course_activated`**
    *   **Question Answered:** Did the user move beyond signup to start the core value loop? (Activation)
    *   **Properties:** `user_id` (pseudonymous), `school_id`, `course_id`, `timestamp`.
    *   **Why:** Signups are vanity; activation is the first signal of intent. Without this, we cannot measure the funnel entry.

2.  **`lesson_completed`**
    *   **Question Answered:** Is the user retaining and finishing the core unit of value? (Retention/Core Action)
    *   **Properties:** `user_id`, `lesson_id`, `course_id`, `completion_status` (pass/fail if applicable), `time_to_complete`.
    *   **Why:** You hypothesize this drives conversion. We must measure the *outcome* (completion), not just the effort. This is your primary North Star input for the pilot.

3.  **`payment_initiated`** (or `subscription_started` if free trial)
    *   **Question Answered:** Did the user attempt to convert? (Revenue)
    *   **Properties:** `user_id`, `plan_type`, `amount`, `currency`.
    *   **Why:** To correlate `lesson_completed` with revenue, you must capture the conversion event. Even in a pilot, tracking the *attempt* validates the monetization path.

#### Deferred Events (Target: September)
Delay these until the core funnel is stable and you have baseline data.

1.  **`video_watch_time_detailed`**
    *   **Reason to Wait:** Your content team argues this indicates learning, but without historical data, it is an unverified hypothesis. Tracking every second creates high-volume noise.
    *   **Pivot Strategy:** For the pilot, rely on `lesson_completed` as the proxy for learning. If completion rates are high but churn is also high in September, *then* instrument detailed video telemetry to see if "passive watching" vs. "active engagement" is the differentiator. Do not build this before validating the core completion metric.

2.  **`social_share_clicked` / `referral_sent`**
    *   **Reason to Wait:** Referral is the top of the AARRR funnel but the last priority for a new product with leaky retention. Optimizing acquisition before proving retention (via `lesson_completed`) wastes engineering cycles.
    *   **Pivot Strategy:** Add this only after you confirm that users who complete lessons actually stay and pay.

#### Implementation Rules for the Sprint
*   **Identifier Seam:** Ensure `user_id` is stable across sessions to track cohorts, but keep it pseudonymous. Route any PII handling to privacy compliance protocols; do not store raw student names in analytics events.
*   **No Benchmarks:** Do not expect "good" completion rates from external sources. Your baseline is the Week 1 performance of these 50 schools. Compare Week 2 to Week 1, not to an industry average.
*   **Vanity Check:** Reject tracking "pageviews" or "video_loads." If an event does not directly map to activation, completion, or revenue, it is noise for this sprint.

2.  Commit to a fixed analysis date (e.g., Sept 20) to review the correlation between `lesson_completed` and `payment_initiated` before adding new events.
