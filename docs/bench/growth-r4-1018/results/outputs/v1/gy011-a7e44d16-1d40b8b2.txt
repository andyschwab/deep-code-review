### Growth Analysis: Motorcycle Taxi Viability (Lagos Pilot)

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Riders completing ≥2 Motorcycle Trips.
*Reject cumulative signups or total trip volume as primary success indicators; they are vanity metrics that rise regardless of product value.*

**Priority Metrics & Segmentation Strategy**
To determine viability for a city-wide launch, analyze the existing pilot data (March 12–26) using this bottom-up funnel approach:

1.  **Retention of Core Action (Primary):** Calculate the percentage of users from the first week who returned for a second trip within 7 days, segmented by district (Ikeja vs. Yaba). If retention is low, acquisition spending for a city-wide launch is wasted.
2.  **Cancellation Root-Cause Segmentation:** Isolate the 18% cancellation rate by cross-referencing three specific dimensions:
    *   **Weather vs. Time:** Compare cancellation rates during rainy afternoons (12:00–18:00) against dry afternoons in the same zones.
    *   **Driver Supply Gap:** Correlate cancellations with "time-to-match" latency. If cancellations spike when wait times exceed 5 minutes during rain, the issue is **driver availability/safety hesitation**, not pricing.
    *   **User vs. Driver Cancellations:** Split the 18% by who canceled. High *driver* cancellation during rain indicates safety/pricing incentives for supply; high *rider* cancellation indicates reliability failure.
3.  **Unit Economics Guardrail:** Compute Contribution Margin per Trip = (Avg Fare: ₦850) – (Variable Costs + Incentives). Ensure the lower fare does not result in negative margin even at 100% utilization.

**Decision Framework for Board & Operations**
Present the findings using this "If/Then" logic to guide Ravi's hiring decision:

*   **Scenario A (Hire):** If rider retention >25% AND driver cancellations constitute >60% of the total cancellation volume during rain.
    *   *Action:* The demand exists; the bottleneck is supply safety/incentives. Hire more drivers and implement a "Rain Surge" pricing model to offset driver risk.
*   **Scenario B (Pause):** If rider retention <10% OR rider cancellations constitute >60% of the total volume due to long wait times.
    *   *Action:* The product fails the reliability test in Lagos traffic conditions. Do not expand; return to discovery to solve the matching algorithm or safety perception before hiring.
*   **Scenario C (Iterate):** If retention is strong but unit economics are negative at ₦850.
    *   *Action:* Test a higher base fare (e.g., ₦1,000) in a controlled A/B test before city-wide rollout.
