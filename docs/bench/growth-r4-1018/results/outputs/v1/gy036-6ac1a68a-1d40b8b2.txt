### Analysis Plan: Diagnosing the Conversion/Retention Divergence

**Objective:** Determine if the simplified onboarding (Sept 1) increased conversion by removing necessary friction (attracting low-intent users) or by hiding critical complexity that causes post-payment shock.

**1. Cohort Definitions**
Compare these three specific cohorts to isolate the variable:
*   **Control Cohort:** Users who started trials between Aug 1–Aug 31 (Pre-simplification).
*   **Treatment Cohort:** Users who started trials between Sept 1–Sept 30 (Post-simplification).
*   **Split Treatment Cohort:** Within the Treatment group, split by "Converted" vs. "Churned during trial" to see if the flow also increased early drop-off of low-fit users (which it seemingly did not, given the conversion rise).

**2. Behavioral Patterns & Events to Investigate**
Instrument or query the following events during the **Trial Period** only. The goal is to find the "Silent Failure"—actions users *didn't* take because the flow was too easy, or actions they took that signaled false confidence.

*   **The "Complexity Encounter" Rate:**
    *   *Event:* `attempted_advanced_feature` or `viewed_pricing_tier_upgrade` during trial.
    *   *Hypothesis:* Pre-simplification users encountered complexity early and self-selected out (lower conversion, higher retention). Post-simplification users skipped this, converting without knowing the product's true nature.
    *   *Metric:* % of trials completing this event before conversion. Expect this to be significantly lower in the Treatment cohort.

*   **The "Setup Completeness" Score:**
    *   *Event:* `completed_core_configuration_step` (e.g., data import, team invite, API key generation).
    *   *Hypothesis:* Simplified flow may have deferred critical setup. Users who convert with 0% configuration are high-risk.
    *   *Metric:* Average configuration completion % at the moment of `payment_success`. Compare correlation with `day_30_retention` across both cohorts.

*   **Time-to-Value Compression:**
    *   *Metric:* Time elapsed between `trial_start` and `first_core_value_action` (the "Aha" moment).
    *   *Hypothesis:* If the Treatment cohort reaches this too fast (e.g., <2 mins) without depth, they may lack the context to sustain usage.
    *   *Action:* Plot retention curves for "Fast Aha" (<10 mins) vs. "Deep Aha" (>1 hour) within the Treatment cohort.

*   **Support/Help Seeking Latency:**
    *   *Event:* `clicked_help_doc` or `submitted_support_ticket`.
    *   *Hypothesis:* Pre-simplification users asked questions *during* trial (filtering themselves). Post-simplification users may be converting first and asking questions *after* payment, leading to frustration.
    *   *Metric:* Ratio of pre-conversion vs. post-conversion support interactions.

**3. Decision Matrix for Head of Growth**
Based on the data above, execute one of the following:

*   **Scenario A (Hidden Complexity Confirmed):** Treatment converts with near-zero configuration and low "complexity encounter" rates, yet churns immediately after Day 1 of billing.
    *   *Diagnosis:* The flow hid the product's learning curve.
    *   *Action:* **Tweak, don't revert.** Re-introduce a mandatory "sandbox" step or a checklist requiring one advanced setup task *before* the paywall. Qualify via effort, not just clicks.

*   **Scenario B (Low-Quality Lead Influx):** Treatment shows similar setup depth to Control, but the demographic/source mix shifted (e.g., higher % from broad awareness campaigns triggered by the "easy" messaging).
    *   *Diagnosis:* The flow itself isn't the bug; the traffic quality is.
    *   *Action:* **Adjust Qualification.** Add a friction point unrelated to product complexity (e.g., a specific use-case selection or company size field) to filter non-ICP leads before the trial starts.

*   **Scenario C (False Positive):** Retention drop is uniform across all behavioral segments in the Treatment cohort, including those who did deep setup.
    *   *Diagnosis:* External factor (pricing shock, market seasonality, or a bug introduced Sept 1 unrelated to flow).
    *   *Action:* **Investigate Billing/Post-Sale.** The onboarding flow is likely not the root cause; examine the first paid invoice experience or recent product changes affecting power users.

**This Week's Step:** Run the "Setup Completeness" vs. "Day 30 Retention" correlation query for the Sept 1–30 cohort. If users with <20% setup completion have <40% retention, implement a mandatory configuration gate before the checkout page by next Friday.
