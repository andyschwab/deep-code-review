### Recommended KPIs for Clinical Advisory Board

To satisfy the clinical advisory board and bridge the gap between engagement and medical value, replace the vanity metric of "daily app opens" with the following three outcome-oriented indicators. These metrics move from raw sensor data to actionable health insights, specifically tailored for the elderly demographic and the June launch timeline.

**1. Clinical Activation Rate (The "Aha" Moment)**
*   **Definition:** The percentage of users who generate a valid HRV trend report AND share it with a healthcare provider or caregiver within the first 14 days of device setup.
*   **Why this matters:** This directly tests your core hypothesis regarding doctor-patient collaboration. High app usage without report sharing indicates the device is a toy, not a medical tool. For the clinical board, this proves the workflow integrates into actual care pathways.
*   **Measurement Logic:** Track the event `report_generated` (requiring ≥7 days of valid data) followed by the event `report_shared` (via email, PDF export, or direct API link) within a 14-day window.
*   **Target Baseline:** Establish the current % from your 500 beta testers. If this is low, the barrier is not sensor accuracy but workflow friction.

**2. Physiological Stability Index (PSI)**
*   **Definition:** The percentage of users showing a statistically significant reduction in HRV *variability* (increased stability) or a sustained improvement in resting heart rate trends over a rolling 30-day period, excluding days with <18 hours of wear time.
*   **Why this matters:** You noted uncertainty about whether "decreased resting heart rate" is sensitive enough. For elderly patients, *stability* is often a stronger predictor of health than absolute rate. This metric shifts the claim from "we measure heart rate" to "we detect physiological improvement." It filters out noise from non-compliant users.
*   **Measurement Logic:** Calculate the coefficient of variation for daily resting HRV per user over 30 days. Classify as "Improved" if the trend line slope is positive (stability) or resting HR decreases by >5% with high data completeness.
*   **Target Baseline:** Compute this retrospectively on the 500 beta testers. Present the distribution: "X% of compliant users showed improved stability."

**3. Actionable Alert Conversion**
*   **Definition:** The ratio of system-generated health anomalies (e.g., "Irregular HRV detected") that result in a user-initiated action (viewing details, sharing report, or logging a symptom) within 24 hours.
*   **Why this matters:** This validates that the insights are understandable and urgent enough to drive behavior. High alert volume with low conversion suggests false positives or confusing UI, which erodes clinical trust.
*   **Measurement Logic:** Numerator: Count of `alert_viewed` + `report_shared` + `symptom_logged` within 24h of `anomaly_detected`. Denominator: Total `anomaly_detected` events for users with >20 days of data.
*   **Target Baseline:** Analyze the beta cohort to determine if alerts are driving engagement or being ignored.

**Immediate Next Steps for the Board Review**
1.  **Recompute the Beta Data:** Run the PSI calculation on the existing 500 users immediately. If the signal is weak, do not claim "heart rate reduction"; pivot the clinical narrative to "HRV stability monitoring" before June.
2.  **Instrument the Gap:** If `report_shared` is not currently tracked, add this event capture to the app build this week. Without this, the "Clinical Activation Rate" remains `UNVERIFIED`, and you cannot prove the care-loop hypothesis to investors.
3.  **Define the Threshold:** Do not assert a universal "good" PSI score. Define success as "Users in the top quartile of PSI show X% fewer emergency incidents" (if historical data exists) or simply establish the baseline distribution now to measure against post-launch.

*Note: All metrics must be reported with confidence intervals given the sample size of 500. Do not compare these rates to external industry benchmarks; compare them strictly to your own January 10 baseline to demonstrate trajectory.*
