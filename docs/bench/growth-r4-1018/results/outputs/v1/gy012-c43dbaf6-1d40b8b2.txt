**Decision Framework: Mandatory Account Creation Reversion**

**1. Diagnostic Read (Existing Data Only)**
Isolate the friction by segmenting the drop-off between "Shipping Info" and "Payment Page" for two cohorts: **New Guests** vs. **Returning Logged-in Users**.
*   **Hypothesis Confirmation:** If the drop-off doubled *only* for New Guests while Remaining flat for Returning Users, the mandatory step is the primary cause. If both rose, investigate external factors (e.g., payment gateway issues) concurrently.
*   **Session Recording Audit:** Review 50 random session recordings of New Guests who abandoned at this specific step. Tag instances where the user hesitates at the "Create Account" field, attempts to bypass it, or exits immediately upon seeing the requirement. Look for "rage clicks" or rapid back-button usage.
*   **Revenue Impact Calculation:** Multiply the *excess* abandonment rate (13% increase) by the average order value (AOV) and daily traffic volume since Feb 10th. This yields the **daily revenue bleed** attributable to the change.

**2. Risk Assessment (Fraud & Data)**
*   **Fraud Exposure:** Compare the fraud rate (chargebacks/flagged orders) of historical "Guest Checkout" orders (pre-Feb 10) against current "Forced Account" orders.
    *   *Decision Rule:* If historical Guest fraud was <2% of revenue, the risk of reverting is negligible compared to the confirmed 13% sales loss. If historical data is missing, label fraud risk as `UNVERIFIED` and recommend a backend-only fraud scan on current abandoned carts (without forcing login) to estimate intent.
*   **Data/Retention Trade-off:** Quantify the "Long-Term Loss." Calculate the Lifetime Value (LTV) delta between a captured email via forced account vs. a captured email via optional post-purchase signup.
    *   *Reality Check:* A lost sale has an LTV of $0. Capturing 20% of abandoning users via an optional post-purchase prompt is mathematically superior to capturing 100% of data from 0% of converting users.

**3. Recommended Action: Silent Canary Reversion**
Do not run a public A/B test that exposes VIPs to a broken flow. Instead, execute a **backend configuration toggle** for a small, non-VIP segment (e.g., 5% of new traffic from non-loyalty email domains).
*   **Variant A (Control):** Keep mandatory account creation.
*   **Variant B (Treatment):** Revert to optional account creation (Guest Checkout enabled).
*   **Guardrail Metric:** Monitor fraud flags per 1,000 orders in Variant B. If fraud spikes >3x baseline, auto-revert.
*   **Primary Metric:** Conversion rate from Shipping to Payment.
*   **Duration:** Run until statistical significance is reached based on current traffic (likely 3–5 days given the high abandonment volume).

**4. Final Decision Matrix**
*   **Revert Immediately If:** Variant B shows >5% relative improvement in conversion with no spike in fraud flags. The revenue gain outweighs the marginal data loss.
*   **Hybrid Approach If:** Fraud risk is uncertain. Implement "Guest Checkout" but require email verification *after* payment success to create the account automatically. This preserves speed while securing the data.
*   **Maintain Status Quo Only If:** Data proves that forced accounts significantly reduce fraud costs by more than the lost revenue (highly unlikely given the 13% drop).
