# BuzzHub Infinite Scroll: Engagement vs. Cost Trade-off Analysis

**North Star Metric:** **Profit Per Active User (PPAU)**
Reject "Daily Active Users" or "Time Spent" as primary success metrics; these are vanity indicators in this context because they are rising while margins collapse. PPAU is defined as *(Total Ad Revenue – Total Infrastructure Cost) / Daily Active Users*. This metric only rises if the incremental revenue from the infinite scroll exceeds its incremental infrastructure cost.

**Input Metrics to Track:**
1.  **Incremental Cost Per Session:** Isolate the server cost specifically attributed to infinite scroll API calls and data transfer, distinct from baseline app usage.
2.  **Ad Fill Rate & CPM on Scroll Events:** Measure revenue generated specifically from ads served during the new scroll behavior, not total app revenue.
3.  **Retention Cohort (Week 1 & Week 2):** Determine if the 50% spike in DAU includes users returning after day 1, or if this is a one-time novelty spike. High churn with high costs confirms unsustainability.

**AARRR Diagnosis (Bottom-Up):**
Do not pour more acquisition budget into this feature yet. The leak is in **Retention** and **Revenue**, not Acquisition.
*   **Retention Check:** If users spike on Day 1 but drop off by Day 3, the "engagement" is a hollow loop consuming resources without building value.
*   **Revenue Check:** If time-spent increases by 50% but ad revenue increases by less than 200% (the cost increase), the unit economics are broken. The current trajectory burns cash for every additional minute of engagement.

**Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only What Answers a Question):**
Do not instrument "everything." Implement only these three events to answer specific financial questions:
1.  `scroll_depth_threshold_reached` (Properties: `depth_pixels`, `ads_served_count`): *Question: At what scroll depth does the cost of serving content exceed the revenue of ads shown?*
2.  `session_cost_calculation` (Properties: `mb_transferred`, `compute_duration_ms`): *Question: What is the exact variable cost of a single infinite scroll session?*
3.  `return_user_scroll_action` (Properties: `days_since_last_visit`): *Question: Are these engaged users returning, or is this a churned cohort?*

**Experiment Rigor & Decision Framework:**
Treat the current rollout as an uncommitted experiment. Do not declare the 50% spike a "win" based on engagement alone; without a pre-committed profitability threshold, this is **peeking** at vanity metrics.
*   **Primary Metric:** PPAU (must remain positive or improve vs. baseline).
*   **Guardrail Metric:** Total Monthly Infrastructure Spend (must not exceed budget cap).
*   **Analysis Rule:** Calculate the **Break-Even Ad Revenue Rate**. If `(Ad Revenue per Scroll Hour) < (Infrastructure Cost per Scroll Hour)`, the feature is financially unsustainable at current scale.

**Recommendation for CFO & VP Engineering:**
1.  **Immediate Action:** Cap the infinite scroll depth or preload limits for 50% of users (A/B test) to reduce the 200% cost spike while measuring if engagement drops proportionally.
2.  **Verification:** Recompute the unit economics using the capped group. If costs drop 50% but engagement drops only 10%, the original implementation was inefficient.
3.  **Decision Trigger:** If the break-even analysis shows that ad revenue cannot mathematically cover the variable cost of the scroll even at 100% fill rate, **revert the change entirely**. Growth that increases losses is not growth; it is liquidation.

Query the last 7 days of logs to calculate the exact `cost_per_scroll_session` and compare it to `revenue_per_scroll_session`. If cost > revenue, implement a hard limit on scroll depth by Friday to stop the budget bleed while the optimization test runs.
