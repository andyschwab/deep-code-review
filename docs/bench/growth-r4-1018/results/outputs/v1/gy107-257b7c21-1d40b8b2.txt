### Technical Specification: Network Density Metric

**Objective:** Quantify local network density to validate the hypothesis that 5+ mutual connections within a city drives a 40% engagement lift.

**1. Data Requirements & Schema**
To calculate this, the data pipeline must ingest the following events with these specific properties:
*   `user_id` (Pseudonymous stable ID): Unique per user.
*   `city_code` (String): Derived from user profile or IP geolocation at signup.
*   `connection_created` (Event): Properties must include `initiator_id`, `receiver_id`, and `timestamp`.
*   `mutual_connection_count` (Derived Attribute): Computed nightly or via stream processing.

**2. The Formula**
Do not track "total connections." Track **Local Density Score (LDS)** per user:
$$LDS_{u} = \text{Count}(\{c \in \text{Connections}_u \mid \text{City}_c == \text{City}_u \land \text{Mutual}_c \geq 5\})$$
*Where `Mutual_c` is the count of shared connections between user $u$ and connection $c$ within the same city.*

**3. Instrumentation Rules**
*   **Question Answered:** "Does reaching 5 mutual local connections correlate with higher retention/referral?"
*   **Constraint:** Do not instrument "all graph traversals." Compute this aggregate only for the **Activation** funnel stage.
*   **Status:** Until the `mutual_connection_count` property is emitted by the backend, this metric is **UNVERIFIED**. Do not estimate the 40% lift; compare users who *have* hit the threshold against those who haven't using your own historical baseline.

**4. Visualization for Underserved Regions**
*   **Axis X:** City (Kharkiv, Lviv, Kyiv, etc.).
*   **Axis Y:** % of Active Users with $LDS_u \geq 1$.
*   **Heatmap Overlay:** Average time-to-first-5-mutuals by city.
*   **Actionable Signal:** Cities with high signup volume but low % of users hitting $LDS_u \geq 1$ are "underserved" and require community seeding, not just ad spend.

**The Strategy: Retention Before Acquisition**

3.  **Allocation Rule:** Allocate marketing spend only to cities where the "Time-to-5-Mutuals" is under 7 days. If a city takes 30 days to reach density, organic growth will churn before value is realized.
