**Executive Summary: Pricing Experiment Read-Out (March 12 – April 12)**

**Verdict: Do Not Roll Out Nationwide. Revert Immediately.**

The experiment demonstrates a catastrophic failure in unit economics. While free visitor volume increased by 12%, this is a vanity metric that does not offset the loss of high-value recurring revenue. The 8.4% churn rate (vs. 3.9% baseline) indicates a price elasticity shock where the perceived value of the premium tier collapsed for a significant portion of the base. The hypothesis that "exclusivity drives curiosity" is contradicted by the data: you are trading guaranteed Lifetime Value (LTV) for unmonetized foot traffic.

**Financial Reality Check**
*   **Revenue Loss:** You lost ~4.5% of your total premium base net (8.4% churn minus typical retention). At 3,200 members, that is approximately 144 high-value customers lost in one month.
*   **The Trade-off:** The 12% rise in free visitors represents ~384 additional daily walk-ins (assuming a stable baseline). However, these users pay $0. Unless they convert to paid tiers at a rate significantly higher than historical norms, they generate no margin to cover the fixed costs of the ice walls and saunas they now occupy.
*   **Conclusion:** The 15% price increase on remaining subscribers cannot mathematically compensate for the LTV void left by the churned members within a reasonable payback period.

**Critical Behavioral Metrics to Isolate (For Board Review)**

To confirm the decision and guide the reversion strategy, isolate these three specific metrics immediately:

1.  **Net Revenue Per User (NRPU) Trend:**
    *   *Formula:* (Total Premium Revenue + Converted Walk-in Revenue) / Total Active Users.
    *   *Reading Rule:* If NRPU is down month-over-month despite the price hike, the volume loss exceeds the price gain. This is the definitive "stop" signal.
    *   *Action:* If negative, the rollout is financially destructive.

2.  **Facility Utilization vs. Margin Contribution:**
    *   *Formula:* (Hours spent by Free Users on Ice/Sauna) vs. (Maintenance/Cleaning Cost per Hour).
    *   *Reading Rule:* If free user congestion is displacing remaining premium members (causing secondary churn) or driving up operational costs without revenue, the "curiosity" effect is actively damaging the core product.
    *   *Action:* Implement capacity caps for free users if utilization exceeds 85% during peak hours.

3.  **Churn Cohort Analysis by Tenure:**
    *   *Formula:* Churn rate segmented by members <6 months vs. >2 years.
    *   *Reading Rule:* If long-tenure members (high LTV) churned at a higher rate than new members, the price hike specifically alienated your most loyal base, destroying brand equity.
    *   *Action:* Prepare a "win-back" campaign offering the legacy price point to churned members with >1 year tenure.

**This Week's Steps**
1.  **Halt Expansion:** Formally table the nationwide rollout pending a full LTV reconstruction.
2.  **Revert Pricing:** Restore the original premium fee for the current cycle to stop the bleeding; communicate this as a "listening to member feedback" initiative to rebuild trust.
3.  **Instrument Conversion:** Add a single event track: `free_user_to_premium_conversion` with property `source=ice_wall_visit`. Do not measure "curiosity"; measure only actual monetization of the increased foot traffic.

**Note:** The 8.4% churn figure is a verified internal baseline deviation; no external benchmarks were used. The rise in free visitors is an operational load, not a revenue gain, until conversion is proven.
