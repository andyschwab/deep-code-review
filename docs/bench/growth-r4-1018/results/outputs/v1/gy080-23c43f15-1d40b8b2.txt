**Comparative Analysis: Start Frequency vs. Listening Duration for Sonido**

**Option A: Optimize for Episode Start Frequency**
*Pros:* Directly measures the engine's ability to surface relevant content quickly, reducing search friction. In a discovery app, a high start rate confirms the "hook" is working. Your beta data (40% lift) proves this metric responds rapidly to algorithmic changes like promoting short-form clips.
*Cons:* Highly susceptible to "clickbait" behavior. As your beta showed, a 40% rise in starts coupled with a 10-minute drop in duration suggests users are sampling but not satisfying their intent. In the Latin American market, where data costs can be a constraint, incentivizing starts without completion may frustrate users who burn data on unfulfilling content, harming long-term retention. This is a vanity metric if not paired with completion rates.

**Option B: Optimize for Total Listening Duration**
*Pros:* Stronger proxy for actual value delivery and content affinity. Users rarely listen for long periods unless they are engaged. This aligns better with subscription retention, as "time well spent" correlates with habit formation. It discourages the promotion of low-quality trending clips that fail to hold attention.
*Cons:* Can inadvertently bias the engine toward long-form content, potentially suppressing excellent short episodes or new creators who haven't built long libraries. It may slow down the feedback loop for engineering, as duration takes longer to accumulate than a simple "start" event.

**Recommendation: Reject Both as the Sole North Star**
Neither metric alone captures the *value* Sonido delivers. Start frequency rewards shallow browsing; duration rewards passive background noise. Optimizing for either in isolation risks distorting the product: one becomes a click-farm, the other a radio station.

**Proposed North Star: "Weekly Active Listeners Completing 3+ Episodes"**
This metric combines discovery (starting) with satisfaction (completing) and frequency (weekly). It rises only when users find multiple pieces of content valuable enough to finish.
*   **Why this works for Argentina/LATAM:** It respects data constraints by valuing completed listens over wasted bandwidth on dropped starts.
*   **Input Metrics to Track:**
    1.  *Start-to-Completion Rate* (Quality of discovery).
    2.  *Average Session Duration* (Depth of engagement).
    3.  *Week-2 Retention Cohort* (The ultimate validator).
