# Fjord Green Beta Success Metrics: Investor Committee Brief

**Context:** Oslo/Bergen Beta (5,000 users); Deadline: June 1.
**Principle:** Measure verified user value and environmental impact, not vanity volume.

### 1. North Star Metric: Weekly Active Reducers (WAR)
*   **Definition:** The count of unique users who log **three or more distinct** sustainable actions within a rolling 7-day window.
*   **Why:** This directly validates your core hypothesis that frequency drives retention. Unlike "Total Logins" or "Cumulative Downloads" (which rise regardless of value), WAR only increases when a user demonstrates the specific behavior linked to long-term retention. It balances engagement (logging) with intent (distinct actions).
*   **Norwegian Context:** Aligns with the cultural preference for consistent, practical action (*dugnad* spirit) over performative one-off gestures.

### 2. Primary Impact Metric: Verified Carbon Avoidance per Active User
*   **Definition:** The sum of backend-calculated carbon reduction (kg CO2e) divided by the number of Weekly Active Reducers.
*   **Why:** This isolates actual environmental impact from mere app usage. Measuring total carbon alone is a vanity metric if driven by a few power users; measuring per *active* user ensures the core cohort is delivering real value. It answers: "When a user engages, do they actually reduce emissions?"
*   **Note:** Do not report "Total Tonnes Saved" until the user base is stable; early totals are noisy and easily manipulated by outliers.

### 3. Retention Cohort: Week-4 Repeat Rate
*   **Definition:** The percentage of users who achieve the "3 actions/week" threshold in Week 1 and repeat it in Week 4.
*   **Why:** Retention is the leading indicator of business viability. If users do not return to the core action after the novelty fades, acquisition spend is wasted. This metric reads the funnel bottom-up, prioritizing the durability of the habit over the speed of signups.
*   **Target Logic:** Compare this cohort against your own Week-2 baseline. Do not benchmark against global averages; your baseline is the only valid truth for this specific Norwegian beta.

### 4. Activation Efficiency: Time-to-Third-Action
*   **Definition:** The median time (in days) from install to the third distinct logged action.
*   **Why:** This measures the friction in your onboarding. If the hypothesis holds, users who hit three actions quickly should retain longer. A rising median time signals a leak in the activation bucket that will depress future retention.

### 5. Guardrail Metric: Data Integrity Rate
*   **Definition:** The percentage of logged actions that pass backend validation checks (e.g., plausible values, non-duplicate entries).
*   **Why:** As you incentivize logging, users may game the system. If this rate drops, your North Star and Impact metrics become `UNVERIFIED`. This ensures the "environmental impact" reported to investors is grounded in reality, not inflated input.

---

**Measurement Rules for the Committee:**
*   **No Fabricated Benchmarks:** Success is defined by positive trends against Fjord Green's own Week-1 baseline, not external industry averages.
*   **Event Taxonomy:** Every logged event must answer a named question (e.g., "Did the user complete the core loop?"). Do not instrument "just in case."
*   **Verification:** Any metric lacking a defined instrumentation plan is marked `UNVERIFIED` and excluded from the final report.

**Immediate Next Steps (Pre-June 1):**
1.  **Define the "Distinct Action" List:** Finalize the taxonomy of what counts as a distinct action to ensure the North Star is calculated consistently before beta launch.
2.  **Establish Baseline Queries:** Build the dashboards for WAR and Week-4 Repeat Rate now, using dummy data, to ensure the logic captures the "3 actions/week" rule correctly upon real user arrival.
