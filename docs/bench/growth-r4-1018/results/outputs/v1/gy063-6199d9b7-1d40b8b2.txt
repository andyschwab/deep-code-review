**Primary North Star Metric: Weighted Quarterly Value Units (WQVU)**

Replace "Monthly Active Users" with **Weighted Quarterly Value Units**, aggregated on a rolling 90-day basis to smooth seasonal spikes. This metric quantifies actual customer value delivery rather than presence.

*   **Definition:** One WQVU = One completed performance review cycle containing ≥3 distinct feedback entries per employee.
*   **Seasonality Normalization:** By measuring over a rolling quarter, the September spike and December trough average out, presenting a stable trend line to the board.
*   **Weighting Logic:** To address off-peak penalization, apply a **Contract-Stage Weight** rather than a time-based weight.
    *   *Implementation:* Multiply raw WQVUs by a factor derived from the customer's contract renewal window. Activity in "quiet" months (e.g., December) counts 1.5x toward the health score if it occurs within 60 days of a renewal date, acknowledging that maintenance activity during off-peaks is a stronger signal of retention intent than peak-volume activity.
*   **Why this works for the CFO:** It decouples "busyness" (logins) from "value" (completed reviews). If WQVU grows year-over-year even while MAU fluctuates, it proves the product is becoming more essential, not less stable.

**Supporting Guardrail Metrics**

1.  **Off-Peak Engagement Depth Ratio**
    *   **Definition:** The average number of feedback items submitted per active user in non-peak months (Jan–Aug) divided by the same average in peak months (Sep–Dec).
    *   **Purpose:** Prevents the team from ignoring the product during quiet periods. A rising ratio indicates successful adoption of asynchronous HR workflows, proving the tool is valuable beyond mandatory review cycles. If this drops, it signals over-reliance on seasonal mandates.

2.  **Renewal-Window Activity Coverage**
    *   **Definition:** The percentage of customers up for renewal in the next 90 days who have generated at least one WQVU in the preceding 60 days.
    *   **Purpose:** Directly ties activity to revenue risk without needing historical correlation data. It answers the specific question: "Are our at-risk customers showing value signals right now?" This protects against the false negative of low raw volume during off-peak renewal periods.

**Reading the Data**
Do not compare current months to previous months (MoM); compare current rolling quarters to previous rolling quarters (YoY Q-over-Q). A flat or rising YoY Q-over-Q trend in WQVU, even with a 60% drop in December MAU, validates a stable growth narrative. Until the correlation between "feedback depth" and renewal is verified with your internal data, treat the specific weighting factors as **UNVERIFIED** hypotheses to be A/B tested against actual renewal outcomes in the next cycle.
