# Measurement Plan: Starlight Cinemas Holiday Promo
**To:** Carlos, Marketing Director
**From:** Growth Analytics
**Subject:** Tracking Incremental Revenue and Margin During Dec 1–24 Promo

### 1. North Star Metric
**Net Incremental Contribution Margin**
*Definition:* (Revenue from Promo Tickets + Associated Concession Sales) minus (Full-Price Revenue Cannibalized + Cost of Goods Sold).
*Why:* Total redemption is a vanity metric; it rises even if we lose money by discounting users who would have paid full price. This metric isolates genuine growth from profit erosion.

### 2. Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only These)
Every event below answers a specific decision question for next year's budget.

| Event Name | Properties | Question Answered |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `promo_offer_sent` | `channel` (email/push/SMS), `day_type` (weekend/weekday), `user_segment` | Which channel/timing yields the highest reach without fatigue? |
| `promo_ticket_redeemed` | `channel_source`, `ticket_type`, `user_id`, `was_full_price_eligible` | Did this user redeem because of the offer, or would they have bought anyway? |
| `concession_purchase` | `user_id`, `transaction_id`, `amount`, `linked_ticket_id` | Does the 50% discount correlate with lower ancillary spend (the 40% margin risk)? |
| `full_price_foregone` | `user_id`, `estimated_full_price`, `channel_source` | **Critical:** Estimate of revenue lost when a full-price buyer uses the code. |

### 3. Analysis Method: Reading Bottom-Up
Do not judge success by total tickets sold. Diagnose in this order:
1.  **Retention/Behavior:** Did redemption users buy concessions at the same rate as historical full-price buyers? If concession attachment drops below the 40% margin baseline, the campaign is destroying value regardless of volume.
2.  **Activation:** Compare `promo_ticket_redeemed` against a control group (5–10% of members who do *not* receive the offer). The difference is your **Incremental Lift**. If redemption in the test group equals the control group's organic purchase rate, you are cannibalizing 100% of those sales.
3.  **Acquisition:** Only after confirming positive incremental margin, analyze which channel (`email` vs. `push` vs. `SMS`) drove the cheapest *incremental* dollar.

### 4. Experiment Design: Channel & Timing
To validate your weekend hypothesis and channel efficiency without peeking errors:
*   **Design:** A/B/n test with pre-committed sample sizes. Split the 200k members into mutually exclusive groups per channel.
*   **Guardrail Metric:** **Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) including concessions.** If ARPU drops significantly in a variant, stop that channel immediately, even if redemption spikes.
*   **Timing Test:** Randomize send times (Weekend vs. Weekday) within each channel to isolate the "weekend assumption."
*   **Reading Rule:** Do not declare a winner mid-campaign based on daily spikes. Calculate the total **Net Incremental Contribution Margin** only after Dec 24. Early reads are `UNVERIFIED` due to holiday shopping volatility.

### 5. This-Week Steps
1.  **Implement Identifier Seam:** Ensure `user_id` persists from the notification click through ticket redemption and concession POS transaction to link ancillary spend to the source channel.
2.  **Define Control Group:** Withhold the offer from 10,000 random members immediately to establish the organic baseline for cannibalization calculations.
3.  **Set Tripwire:** If concession attachment rate for promo users falls below 30% (vs. historical norm) by Dec 10, pause the lowest-margin channel.

*Note: All benchmark comparisons must use Starlight's own historical data (e.g., last year's $1.2M spike adjusted for current membership size); no external industry retention rates apply here.*
*Unknowns such as specific cannibalization rates are currently `UNVERIFIED` and will resolve only after the control group data is collected.*
