### Growth Analysis: FrostBite Boxes Pilot (March 12 – April 30)

**Executive Recommendation for Board:** **Pause expansion to Aarhus.** The data indicates a leakage in **value retention** (shrinking basket size) rather than pure churn. Expanding delivery logistics while the core unit economics are degrading will amplify losses. The immediate action is to diagnose the driver of the Average Order Value (AOV) drop before committing capital to new geography.

#### 1. Diagnosis: Separating Churn from Downsizing
Your 68% retention rate against a 55% baseline is a **verified win** for product-market fit regarding *presence*; customers are not leaving. However, the 25% drop in AOV (420 DKK → 315 DKK) signals a failure in *value capture*. In a cold-chain model, fixed logistics costs per drop are high; a smaller box likely erodes margin faster than revenue.

To distinguish "skipping weeks" (temporary pause) from "downsizing" (permanent value reduction), you must isolate these three specific metrics immediately:

*   **Metric A: Active Subscriber Box Mix.** Segment your retained 68% by SKU size week-over-week.
    *   *Reading Rule:* If the % of subscribers on the "Small Box" plan increased while "Large Box" subscribers migrated down or cancelled, this is **downsizing**. If the mix is stable but total order count dropped, this is **skipping**.
*   **Metric B: Purchase Frequency vs. Spend Per Active User.**
    *   *Reading Rule:* Calculate `Total Revenue / Active Subscribers` for Week 1 vs. Week 6. If the denominator (actives) is stable but the numerator (revenue) falls, the behavior is **downsizing**. If both fall proportionally, it is **skipping**.
*   **Metric C: The "Skip" Rate.** Measure the percentage of active subscribers who explicitly used a "skip delivery" feature vs. those who simply didn't order (if applicable).
    *   *Reading Rule:* A high skip rate in May/June suggests **seasonal adjustment** (valid). A low skip rate with low AOV suggests **product dissatisfaction** or price sensitivity (critical risk).

**Current Verdict:** Given the sharp AOV decline while retention holds, the signal points to **downsizing**. Customers like the service enough to stay but are reducing spend, possibly due to summer meal planning or price friction. This is a margin leak, not just a seasonal pause.
