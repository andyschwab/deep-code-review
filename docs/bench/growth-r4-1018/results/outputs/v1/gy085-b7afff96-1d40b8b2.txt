# Event Unification Strategy: 'Luz' to 'Lumina' Transition

**Audience:** Data Science Team, Regional Marketing Lead (Lisbon)
**Objective:** Unify fragmented event streams for accurate Q4 reporting and YoY growth analysis without losing historical context.

### Step 1: Define the Canonical Taxonomy (Immediate)
Stop treating `luz_` and `lumina_` as separate metrics in dashboards. Define a single **canonical event name** for each action (e.g., `add_to_cart`).
*   **Action:** Create a transformation layer in your data warehouse (or via your analytics tool's stream processing) that maps both `luz_add_to_cart` and `lumina_add_to_cart` to the canonical `add_to_cart` event.
*   **Property Injection:** Add a property `client_version_brand` with values `'legacy_luz'` or `'current_lumina'` to every unified event. This preserves the ability to segment by app version while keeping the core metric whole.

### Step 2: Validate Data Integrity via Cohort Overlap
Before aliasing globally, verify that the behavior of the two streams is comparable to ensure the rebrand didn't alter the user journey logic.
*   **Action:** Run a parallel comparison for the period **Sept 1–Sept 9** (pre-rebrand) vs. **Sept 11–Sept 17** (post-rebrand).
*   **Check:** Compare the conversion rate of `luz_add_to_cart` → `luz_purchase` against `lumina_add_to_cart` → `lumina_purchase`.
*   **Outcome:** If rates diverge by >5%, the rebrand may have introduced a UI/UX bug, not just a naming change. Investigate before unifying. If stable, proceed with aliasing.

### Step 3: Establish the "Cutoff Date" Logic
Do not set a fixed calendar date for dropping support; use a dynamic adoption threshold to protect the 12% revenue stream from Portugal.
*   **Metric:** Track the daily ratio of `lumina_` events to total (`lumina_` + `luz_`) events for the Portugal region.
*   **Threshold:** When the `lumina_` share exceeds **90%** for **7 consecutive days**, declare the **Deprecation Cutoff Date**.
*   **Post-Cutoff Action:**
    1.  Stop mapping `luz_` events to the canonical stream in *real-time* dashboards to reduce noise.
    2.  Move raw `luz_` data to a "cold storage" historical table for long-term YoY queries only.
    3.  Document the cutoff date in the Q4 report methodology section.

### Step 4: Handling Year-Over-Year (YoY) Comparisons
For Q4 reports comparing 2023 vs. 2024:
*   **2023 Data:** Consists entirely of `luz_` events.
*   **2024 Data:** Will be a mix until the Cutoff Date.
*   **Strategy:** Use the **unified canonical stream** (from Step 1) for all 2024 reporting. Since the canonical stream includes the aliased legacy data, your YoY comparison remains apples-to-apples. The `client_version_brand` property allows you to isolate if any drop in performance is due to legacy users failing to update.

### Step 5: Communication & Governance
*   **For Marketing:** Confirm that Q4 targets are measured against the unified `add_to_cart` metric, ensuring the rebrand fragmentation does not artificially suppress reported growth.
*   **For Data Science:** Implement an alert if the `luz_` event volume spikes unexpectedly after the Cutoff Date, indicating a potential rogue legacy client or integration error.
