# SwiftRide Referral Program: Measurement & Attribution Plan

**Objective:** Define the business logic to measure ROI, attribute value, and detect fraud for the driver referral program launching December 1st.

### 1. North Star Metric
**Net Incremental Completed Trips by Referred Drivers.**
We reject "Invites Sent" or "Signups" as vanity metrics; they rise regardless of value. We only count value when a referred driver completes a verified trip. This metric rises only when the program delivers real marketplace liquidity.

### 2. Attribution Logic: The "First Earned Dollar" Rule
To resolve the open question on timing, we attribute the referral success at the moment the referred driver completes their **first paid trip**, not upon signup or reaching an earnings threshold.
*   **Reasoning:** Signup is cheap to fake; a completed trip requires real effort and validates the "trust endorsement" hypothesis. Waiting for an earnings threshold delays feedback and obscures activation leaks.
*   **The Loop:** Inviter gets credit only when the Invitee's status moves from "Registered" to "First Trip Completed."

### 3. Measuring Incremental Value (The Counterfactual)
To prove referred drivers have higher Lifetime Value (LTV) than organic ones, we must compare them against a like-for-like baseline, not a generic average.
*   **Cohort Definition:** Compare "Referred Drivers (Dec 1–31)" against "Organic Drivers (Dec 1–31)" who joined in the same city and vehicle class.
*   **The Metric:** **90-Day Retained Trip Volume**.
    *   *Formula:* (Total trips by referred cohort in 90 days) minus (Total trips by matched organic cohort in 90 days).
    *   *Verdict:* If the referred cohort's retention curve stays above the organic curve after week 4, the "trust endorsement" holds. If the curves converge immediately, the assumption is false, and the bonus is subsidizing drivers who would have joined anyway.

### 4. Fraud Detection & Guardrails
Fraud in two-sided markets usually manifests as self-referral or rapid churn after bonus payout. We will track these guardrails weekly:
*   **The "Same Device" Flag:** Any invite pair sharing a device ID, IP subnet, or bank account is automatically flagged as `UNVERIFIED` and excluded from bonus payouts until manual review.
*   **The "Churn-after-Payout" Rate:** The percentage of referred drivers who complete the minimum trips for the bonus and then go 14 days without driving.
    *   *Threshold:* If this rate exceeds 20% of the referred cohort, the program is paying for fake activity. Pause bonuses immediately.
*   **Circular Riding:** Detect patterns where the inviter and invitee are the only two parties in a series of short, high-cost trips.

### 5. Financial ROI Model for Approval
The Finance Director requires a break-even calculation based on unit economics, not total volume.
*   **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA):** Total Bonuses Paid / Number of Referred Drivers with 1+ Trip.
*   **Payback Period:** The number of days it takes for the *incremental* margin from a referred driver to exceed the CPA.
*   **Decision Rule:**
    *   **Ship/Scale:** If Payback Period < 60 days AND Fraud Rate < 5%.
    *   **Stop:** If Payback Period > 90 days OR Fraud Rate > 10%.
    *   **Investigate:** If activation is high but 90-day retention matches organic exactly (no incremental value).
