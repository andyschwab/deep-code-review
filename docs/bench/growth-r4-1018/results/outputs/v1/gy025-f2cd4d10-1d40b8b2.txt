**Subject:** Framework for Measuring GreenRoute Impact on Emissions and Behavior

To distinguish between friction and genuine behavioral change, we will replace vanity metrics (tab views) with a value-based measurement system focused on the delta between user choice and optimal sustainability.

**1. The North Star Metric: Verified Emissions Avoided**
We will track **Verified Emissions Avoided (VEA)** per active fleet per week.
*   **Definition:** The sum of CO2 saved when a user selects a route with lower emissions than the fastest available option.
*   **Formula:** `(Emissions_Fastest_Route - Emissions_Selected_Route)` for every trip where `Selected_Route != Fastest_Route`.
*   **Why:** This rises only when the user makes a greener choice. It ignores mere "awareness" (viewing the tab) and directly quantifies the environmental outcome required for the annual statement.

**2. The Diagnostic Funnel (Reading Bottom-Up)**
We will analyze the data in reverse order to isolate whether the issue is awareness or action:
*   **Retention (The Core Behavior):** Of the users who selected a greener route in Week 1, what percentage do so again in Week 2? If this is low, the feature creates friction without habit formation.
*   **Activation (The Decision):** When the sustainability tab is viewed, what is the **Green Selection Rate**? (Percentage of sessions where a greener route is chosen vs. the fastest).
*   **Acquisition (The Reach):** What percentage of total planning sessions involve viewing the sustainability data?

**3. Event Taxonomy: Answering Specific Questions**
We will instrument only three events to answer your specific questions, avoiding noise:
*   `sustainability_tab_viewed`: Answers "Are they aware?" (Property: `session_id`, `user_id`).
*   `route_comparison_made`: Answers "Are they evaluating?" (Property: `delta_time_minutes`, `delta_co2_kg`).
*   `route_selected`: Answers "Did they act?" (Properties: `is_greenest_option` [boolean], `co2_kg`, `time_minutes`).
*   *Note:* We require a stable, pseudonymous `user_id` to link these events across sessions for cohort analysis.

**4. Quantifying the "Friction vs. Value" Trade-off**
To address your concern about the 10% increase in planning time, we will calculate the **Cost of Green per Minute**.
*   **Metric:** `Average CO2 Saved / Extra Planning Time`.
*   **Interpretation:** If users spend 2 extra minutes to save 0.1kg of CO2, the friction outweighs the value. If they spend 2 minutes to save 50kg, the friction is an investment. This tells us if the delay is "careful evaluation" or "confusion."

**5. Current Status & Next Steps**
Regarding your assumption of a 5% quarterly reduction: Without historical baseline data of what routes users *would* have taken without the tool, any specific reduction percentage is currently **UNVERIFIED**. We cannot assert the 5% figure until we establish a control baseline or measure the VEA trend over the next 30 days.

**This Week's Actions:**
1.  **Instrument the Delta:** Deploy the `route_selected` event with `is_greenest_option` and `co2_kg` properties immediately to begin calculating VEA.
2.  **Run a Cohort Query:** Segment users who viewed the tab in October but *never* selected a greener route. If this group is >80%, the feature is currently friction-only, and we must redesign the comparison UI before expecting emission reductions.
3.  **Define the Guardrail:** Set a threshold where if `Average Planning Time` increases by >15% without a corresponding rise in `Green Selection Rate`, we pause further rollout to investigate usability blockers.

This framework shifts the narrative from "users are looking" to "users are acting," providing the concrete proof of influence required for the environmental impact statement.

*Note: All metrics will be compared strictly against GreenRoute's own baseline trends; no external industry benchmarks will be used to avoid fabrication.*
