**Decision: Do not ship to all 300 clients this Friday.**

The divergence between a 14% lift in booking clicks and a 450% spike in support tickets (2 to 11/week) indicates a critical failure in the "Activation" phase of your funnel. While the tooltip successfully drives the primary action, it is doing so by creating confusion that your 12-person team cannot sustain. With only 300 total clients, an increase of 9 tickets per week represents a massive noise-to-signal ratio that will drown your small support capacity, likely degrading the experience for existing users and damaging long-term retention.

In a Product-Led Growth motion, "conversion" achieved through confusion is vanity; true activation requires the user to succeed *without* needing human intervention. If 11 users needed help in a 9-day window among a small test cohort, rolling this out to 300 clients projects roughly 30–40 extra tickets weekly. For a team of 12, this is not a "slight cost"; it is an operational bottleneck that threatens your service reliability.

**Recommended Action Plan:**

1.  **Immediate Halt:** Keep the tooltip disabled for the remaining 90% of users. Do not ship the current version.
2.  **Qualitative Triage (48 Hours):** Have the designers or founders personally review the 11 new support tickets. Categorize the specific confusion points. Is the tooltip obscuring data? Is the language unclear? Is it triggering too early?
3.  **Iterate and Re-test:** Modify the tooltip based on the ticket analysis (e.g., simplify copy, delay trigger, or make it dismissible). Run a new A/B test with a **pre-committed duration** of 14 days.
    *   **Primary Metric:** Booking button click-through rate.
    *   **Guardrail Metric:** Support tickets related to "onboarding" or "confusion."
    *   **Success Criteria:** The new version must maintain the click-through lift *without* exceeding the baseline support volume (approx. 2 tickets/week). If the guardrail breaks, the test fails regardless of conversion gains.

**This Week's Steps:**
*   **Today:** Draft a response to the 11 affected users thanking them for the feedback and confirming the feature is being refined.
*   **Tomorrow:** Hold a 1-hour session with the design lead and one founder to tag the 11 tickets and propose one specific UI copy or timing change.
*   **Friday:** Deploy the *revised* tooltip to only 10% of traffic (30 users) for a quiet observation period, rather than a full rollout.

The goal is not just higher clicks, but higher *successful* bookings with zero incremental support load. Fix the leak before pouring more users into the bucket.
