### Cohort Analysis Plan: Discount-Driven vs. Organic LTV

**Objective:** Determine if the Lifetime Value (LTV) of users acquired via the 20% discount exceeds their negative initial margin ($-2) within a defined payback period, compared to organic acquisitions.

**1. Cohort Definitions**
*   **Treatment Cohort:** Lapsed users who redeemed the 20% discount code in the test month.
*   **Control Cohort A (Organic Returners):** Lapsed users who returned in the same period *without* a discount code.
*   **Control Cohort B (Historical Baseline):** Lapsed users who returned in the same month last year (adjusts for seasonality if current organic volume is low).

**2. Core Metrics & Questions**
Track these metrics weekly for 12 weeks, then monthly up to 52 weeks:
*   **Repeat Purchase Rate (RPR):** % of cohort making a ≥2nd purchase at full price.
    *   *Question:* Do discount users return at the same rate as organic users?
*   **Time-to-Second-Purchase:** Days between first and second order.
    *   *Question:* Do discount users take longer to convert again, delaying cash flow?
*   **Average Order Value (AOV) on Return:** Value of the 2nd, 3rd, and 4th orders.
    *   *Question:* Do discount users condition themselves to buy only on promotion, suppressing future AOV?
*   **Cumulative Contribution Margin:** (Revenue - COGS - Shipping - Marketing) per user over time.
    *   *Question:* When does the cumulative margin cross $0 (Payback Period)?

**3. The Sustainability Formula**
Calculate the **Break-Even Repeat Rate** to present to the CEO:
*   *Initial Loss:* $2 per user.
*   *Target Margin on Full Price Order:* Assume $15 (based on pre-discount baseline).
*   *Required Return Rate:* $2 / $15 = **13.3%**.
*   *Verdict Rule:* If the Treatment Cohort's repeat rate at 90 days is <13.3%, the strategy destroys value immediately. If it is >13.3%, calculate how many months until the extra volume justifies the working capital strain.

**4. Execution Steps (This Week)**
1.  **Tagging:** Ensure all orders from the test month are tagged in your database with `acquisition_source: discount_lapsed_test` vs. `organic_lapsed`.
2.  **Query:** Run a cohort retention query pulling `user_id`, `order_date`, `order_value`, and `margin` for both groups.
3.  **Visualization:** Plot two lines on a single chart: "Cumulative Margin per User" over 52 weeks. The point where the Discount line crosses the Organic line is your "Value Crossover Date."

**5. Decision Framework for CEO**
*   **Ship (Scale Promo):** If Repeat Rate > 20% (providing a safety buffer) AND Payback Period < 3 months.
*   **Extend Test:** If Repeat Rate is between 13.3% and 20%. Extend tracking to 6 months to confirm long-term retention before holiday scaling.
*   **Stop:** If Repeat Rate < 13.3% OR if the second-order AOV drops significantly below the organic average (indicating price conditioning).

**Note:** Do not assume future behavior; the 13.3% threshold is the mathematical floor based on your stated unit economics. If data tagging was not implemented during the test, the specific LTV comparison is currently `UNVERIFIED` and requires a new, smaller-scale test to establish the baseline.
