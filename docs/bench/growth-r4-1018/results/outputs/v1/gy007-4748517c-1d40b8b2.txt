### SoundWave Beta Event Taxonomy & Hierarchy

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Users completing a "Value Session" (defined below).
*Reject "Total Songs Played" as a primary metric; it is a vanity metric that rises with passive background noise and does not prove value.*

**Hierarchy of Events (Instrument in this order):**

1.  **`session_start`** (Properties: `user_id`, `device_type`, `entry_point`)
    *   **Question Answered:** Can we acquire and launch a session reliably?
    *   **Why First:** Baseline for all subsequent rates. Without this, engagement rates are uncalculable.

2.  **`core_action_complete`** (Properties: `user_id`, `session_id`, `content_id`, `play_duration_seconds`, `completion_percentage`)
    *   **Question Answered:** Did the user listen to enough of a track to signal intent?
    *   **Definition of Value:** A "Value Session" = ≥3 tracks played with >60 seconds duration each within 7 days.
    *   **Why Critical:** In a saturated market, passive listening (background radio) is common. Deep engagement (listening past the intro) is the only leading indicator of retention. This replaces your assumption of "songs per session" with "qualified listening time."

3.  **`active_preference_signal`** (Properties: `user_id`, `session_id`, `action_type` [like, skip, playlist_add, share], `content_id`, `timestamp_offset_seconds`)
    *   **Question Answered:** Is the user actively curating their experience or just tolerating recommendations?
    *   **Why Secondary:** Skip rates and playlist creations are high-signal *inputs* that move the North Star, but they are sparse. A user can love the service without making a playlist (low frequency), but they cannot love it without listening (high frequency). Use this to tune the recommendation algorithm, not to judge overall product health yet.

4.  **`browse_interaction`** (Properties: `user_id`, `genre_category`, `search_query`, `result_count`, `time_spent_ms`)
    *   **Question Answered:** Is our content library discoverable, or are users stuck in a loop?
    *   **Why Last:** High time spent browsing often signals *failure* (cannot find content), not value. Instrument this to diagnose friction only after confirming core listening behavior.

**Implementation Rules for Engineering:**
*   **Identifier Seam:** Ensure `user_id` is a stable, pseudonymous ID persisted across sessions to enable cohort retention analysis. Do not use transient session IDs for long-term tracking.
*   **No "Just in Case" Data:** Do not instrument mouse hovers, scroll depth, or background tab visibility yet. These add cost without answering a named question for the beta phase.
*   **Baseline Only:** Do not hardcode "good" thresholds (e.g., "skip rate < 20%") into the pipeline. The system must output raw counts/rates so we can compare against our own Week 1 baseline.

**Reading Plan (Retention First):**
Do not look at acquisition volume (5,000 users) until you verify retention.
1.  Calculate % of users returning for a second "Value Session" within 7 days.
2.  If retention is low, analyze `active_preference_signal` to see if skips correlate with churn.
3.  Only then optimize acquisition.

**This Week's Step:**
Implement the `core_action_complete` event with the `play_duration_seconds` property immediately. Run a query on Day 3 of beta: "What % of users completed 3+ Value Sessions?" If the number is <15%, pause acquisition spend and interview those users; do not add more tracking until this baseline is understood.

*Note: All metric values are currently UNVERIFIED until the beta launches and data flows. No industry benchmarks for "good" audio retention are asserted here.*
