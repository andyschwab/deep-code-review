### Proposed Activation Criteria for QuillStream

**Option 1: The "Manifest Export" (Current Hypothesis)**
*Definition:* User generates, edits, and exports a shipping manifest.
*Pros:* Directly validates the core value proposition for maritime coordinators; highest correlation to paid tier conversion; filters out casual browsers.
*Cons:* Likely too high a barrier for Week 1, explaining the 18% return rate; maritime manifests are complex, and users may need multiple sessions to complete one; with only 120 beta users, the absolute number of events will be too low for statistically significant A/B testing in the short term.

**Option 2: The "Draft Save" (Lower Friction)**
*Definition:* User generates content and saves at least one draft to their workspace.
*Pros:* Captures intent earlier in the workflow; accommodates the multi-session nature of complex logistics planning; increases the sample size of "activated" users, allowing for faster iteration on onboarding flows.
*Cons:* Risk of vanity metric inflation (saving a blank or irrelevant draft); weaker immediate signal of monetary value compared to an export; may include users who never intend to finalize a document.

**Option 3: The "Two-Step Completion" (Balanced Approach)**
*Definition:* User generates a document AND performs one high-intent edit (e.g., adding a specific cargo code or port field) without requiring final export.
*Pros:* Verifies engagement with the specific domain logic of maritime logistics (distinguishing from generic writing); lower friction than a full export but higher fidelity than a simple save; better suited for the 120-user cohort to yield readable trends within Q4.
*Cons:* Requires precise event instrumentation to define what constitutes a "high-intent" edit; still subject to some noise if users edit randomly.

**Recommendation for Elena:**
Given the specialized niche and small cohort (120 users), **Option 3** is the strongest candidate for the Q4 dashboard. It respects the complexity of maritime documentation while providing a measurable signal before the lengthy export process is complete. Adopt Option 1 as the long-term "North Star" for revenue correlation, but use Option 3 to optimize immediate retention.

**Note:** Do not compare these rates to industry benchmarks; measure only against your own October 14th baseline. Ensure the "high-intent edit" event captures specific maritime properties (e.g., IMO numbers) to maintain data relevance.
