**Executive Summary for the Editorial Board**

The current data presents a classic tension between engagement depth and inventory volume. While the 12% increase in time-on-site suggests successful immersion, the 5% drop in pages-per-session directly threatens the ad-revenue model dependent on page views. Without long-term retention data, we cannot yet validate the "brand loyalty" hypothesis. Therefore, the immediate decision must rely on **revenue-per-user efficiency** rather than raw engagement metrics.

**Critical Segmentation to Request Immediately**
To determine if this trade-off is acceptable, you must isolate the behavior of **Returning Visitors vs. New Visitors**.
*   **Returning Visitors:** If the drop in pages-per-session is concentrated here, loyal readers are consuming fewer ad impressions per visit, signaling an immediate revenue leak. If their time-on-site increases significantly, they may be watching video ads (if monetized), which could offset the page-view loss.
*   **New Visitors:** If the pattern holds here, the new layout may be failing to drive the "deep dive" behavior necessary to convert casual traffic into habitual readers.

**Additional Context Required**
1.  **Video Ad Completion & Yield:** Does the increased time-on-site correlate with completed video ad impressions? Calculate if the higher CPM of video ads offsets the loss of display ad inventory from fewer page views.
2.  **Bounce Rate by Entry Point:** Are users landing on video stories bouncing faster than those landing on text? This determines if the layout hurts acquisition funnel efficiency.
3.  **Device Breakdown:** Video consumption often differs drastically between mobile and desktop. Ensure the 5% drop isn't isolated to a platform where video auto-play is restricted or data-heavy.

**Decision Framework**
Do not frame this as "Engagement vs. Revenue." Frame it as **"Short-term Inventory Loss vs. Long-term Value Hypothesis."**
*   **Keep the Layout IF:** The revenue per daily active user (RPU) remains flat or grows due to higher-value video ad fill rates, OR if returning visitor frequency (visits per week) shows an early uptick.
*   **Revert the Layout IF:** RPU drops proportionally to the page-view decline and there is no compensating increase in visit frequency or video ad yield.

**This Week's Action Plan**
1.  **Query:** Run a cohort analysis comparing `pages_per_session` and `time_on_site` for `user_type = returning` vs. `user_type = new` from Sept 20–27 against the prior 30-day baseline.
2.  **Calculate:** Derive `estimated_revenue_per_session` using current ad yield rates for video vs. display to see if the 12% time gain monetizes better than the lost 5% page volume.
3.  **Define Tripwire:** Set a hard guardrail: If `revenue_per_daily_active_user` drops by more than 3% by next Friday, initiate a partial revert (A/B test) to restore text prominence for returning users.

*Note: Long-term retention remains `UNVERIFIED` until a 4-week cohort can be tracked; do not cite loyalty benefits as fact yet.*
