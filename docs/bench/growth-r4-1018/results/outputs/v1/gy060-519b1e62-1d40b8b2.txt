# GreenChain MVP Metrics Dashboard Specification
**Target Audience:** Pilot Partners & Product Steering Committee
**Launch Date:** September 1
**Status:** Finalized for MVP

## 1. North Star Metric: Verified Carbon Reduction Rate
**Definition:** The percentage of a client's total logistics network where carbon emissions are both *measured* and *reduced* compared to their baseline, excluding unverified data.
**Why this over "Total Tons Saved":** "Total tons" is a vanity metric that incentivizes chasing large, easy wins while ignoring data quality. It fails when suppliers don't report. The **Rate** forces accountability for data completeness and actual reduction, directly reflecting the product's core value: trustworthy sustainability intelligence.

## 2. Input Metrics (The Drivers)
These three metrics move the North Star and answer specific MVP questions:

*   **Data Completeness Ratio:** `(Verified Supplier Reports / Total Expected Reports) * 100`
    *   *Question Answered:* Are we getting enough data to make a claim?
    *   *Action:* If <80%, trigger manual outreach workflows for missing suppliers.
*   **Normalization Factor (Industry-Adjusted):** `CO2 Saved per Unit of Activity` (e.g., tons saved per 1,000 units shipped for retail; tons saved per $1M production output for manufacturing).
    *   *Question Answered:* How do we compare performance across verticals?
    *   *Action:* Display separate cohort views for Manufacturing vs. Retail; do not aggregate raw tons across verticals.
*   **Verification Confidence Score:** `(1 - (Estimated Data / Total Data)) * 100`
    *   *Question Answered:* How much of this total is hard data vs. model estimation?
    *   *Action:* Any client report with a score <70% is flagged as "Preliminary" and excluded from marketing claims.

## 3. Handling Data Gaps & Normalization
*   **The Gap Protocol:** Never fill missing supplier data with averages in the primary view. Instead, display two numbers: **Verified Savings** (hard data only) and **Potential Savings** (verified + estimated). The North Star uses only **Verified**.
*   **Vertical Normalization:** Do not normalize by company size. Normalize by **activity intensity** relevant to the vertical:
    *   *Retail:* CO2 per pallet-mile or per SKU shipped.
    *   *Manufacturing:* CO2 per unit of finished good or per energy unit consumed.
    *   *Dashboard View:* Clients see their trend line against their own historical baseline, not an arbitrary industry benchmark.

## 4. Experiment & Validation Plan (MVP Phase)
*   **Primary Hypothesis:** Clients who achieve a Data Completeness Ratio >85% will renew their pilot.
*   **Guardrail Metric:** Time-to-First-Report (days from onboarding to first verified data upload). If this exceeds 14 days, the onboarding flow is broken.
*   **Reading Rule:** Review retention of the "core action" (uploading verified data) weekly. If retention drops, pause acquisition of new pilot features and fix the data ingestion pipeline.

## 5. This-Week Steps
1.  **Instrument Events:** Define `supplier_report_submitted` (properties: `industry_vertical`, `data_source_type`, `carbon_value`, `verification_status`) and `dashboard_viewed_by_client`.
2.  **Build the "Gap" Visual:** Create a dashboard component that explicitly shows the % of data missing per supplier, rather than hiding it.
3.  **Baseline Setup:** Ingest the last 12 months of historical data for the three pilot companies to establish their individual baselines before September 1.

***

*Note: No external industry benchmarks are used; all success criteria are based on improvement against each client's own historical baseline. Any metric relying on unverified supplier inputs is labeled `UNVERIFIED` in the dashboard.*
