### Success Metrics Plan: StreamMax Mexico Launch

**North Star Metric: Weekly Active Viewers Completing Episode 2+**
Reject "Total Viewing Hours" as the primary indicator; it is a vanity metric that rises with passive background play or single-episode sampling without proving value. Instead, track the count of unique users who watch beyond the first episode within 7 days of sign-up or content release. This measures actual content resonance and the "aha" moment where a user commits to the series. Pair this with **Input Metrics**: New Sign-ups (Acquisition), Episode 1 Start Rate (Activation), and Episode 2 Completion Rate (Retention).

**Funnel Diagnosis (Bottom-Up Approach)**
Do not prioritize acquisition volume until retention is verified. Diagnose in this order:
1.  **Retention:** Of the users who started Episode 1, what percentage returned to finish Episode 2 within 48 hours? If this is low, the content or localization fails regardless of sign-up volume.
2.  **Activation:** Of new sign-ups, what percentage actually start Episode 1?
3.  **Acquisition:** Are we hitting the 200,000 sign-up target? (Only optimize this if steps 1 and 2 are healthy).

**Event Taxonomy (Instrumenting Specific Questions)**
Instrument only events that answer named strategic questions. Do not track "everything."
*   `content_play_start` (Properties: `content_id`, `episode_number`, `user_type` [new/existing], `language_pref`): Answers "Are users starting the content?"
*   `content_play_complete` (Properties: `content_id`, `episode_number`, `completion_percentage`): Answers "Are users finishing episodes?"
*   `session_end` (Properties: `duration_seconds`, `last_action`): Answers "How long do engaged sessions last?"
*   `share_action_clicked` (Properties: `platform`, `content_id`): A proxy for word-of-mouth; tracks observable intent to share rather than vague "resonance."
*   `search_query_submitted` (Properties: `query_text`, `result_count`): Measures active intent to find local content.

**Segmentation for Strategic Questions**
*   **Cultural Resonance & Word-of-Mouth:** Proxy this via `share_action_clicked` rates per 1,000 viewers and `search_query_submitted` volume for specific local talent names or show titles. High search volume for cast members indicates deep cultural interest.
*   **Existing vs. New Subscriber Impact:** Tag every event with `user_tenure_bucket` (e.g., "Pre-launch Subscriber" vs. "Jan Launch Sign-up"). Compare the `content_play_complete` rate between these two cohorts. If existing subscribers show higher completion rates, the content drives retention; if only new users engage, it is purely an acquisition driver.

**Measurement Cadence & Governance**
*   **Launch Week:** Monitor Episode 1→2 drop-off daily. If drop-off exceeds 60% (baseline to be set from pilot data or historical similar launches), trigger a creative or UX review immediately.
*   **Month 1:** Shift to weekly cohort retention curves. Compare the Week 1, Week 2, and Week 3 retention of the "Mexico Launch Cohort" against the global average.
*   **Guardrail Metric:** Track `cancel_subscription_event` for the Mexico region. Ensure the influx of new users does not correlate with a spike in churn among existing local subscribers due to content displacement or price sensitivity.

**This Week's Steps**
1.  **Define the "Core Action" Threshold:** Confirm with the Content Lead that "Episode 2 Completion" is the agreed-upon proxy for value before engineering instruments the events.
2.  **Verify Identifier Seam:** Ensure the analytics pipeline captures a stable, pseudonymous `user_id` that persists across devices and distinguishes `user_tenure_bucket` without exposing PII, complying with privacy standards.
3.  **Baseline Check:** Pull the current `content_play_complete` rate for non-Spanish content in Mexico to establish a pre-launch baseline; without this, post-launch lift is `UNVERIFIED`.

*Note: All benchmark comparisons must use StreamMax's own historical data for the Mexico region; no external industry retention rates will be used as they are unverifiable and often misleading.*
*Any metric lacking a corresponding instrumentation ticket in the backlog is considered `UNVERIFIED` and should not be reported to the board.*
