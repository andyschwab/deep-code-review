**Subject:** Mitigation Strategy: Optimizing Delivery Estimates Amid Driver Shortages

**Executive Summary**
To address the driver shortage and rising cancellation rates (4% → 11%) without eroding long-term revenue, we will test a hypothesis: **Displaying accurate, extended delivery estimates upfront reduces cancellations and protects brand trust, even if initial order volume dips.** We will validate this via a controlled A/B test starting immediately, with results ready for Thursday's board meeting.

**1. Primary Metric (North Star for this Test)**
**Adjusted Gross Merchandise Value (AGMV) per Session**
*Definition:* Total value of successfully completed orders divided by total app sessions.
*Rationale:* Raw GMV may drop if users are deterred by longer times, but "Completed Orders" is the true revenue driver. If showing a 45-minute estimate prevents a user from ordering only to cancel later (wasting driver dispatch costs and frustrating the customer), AGMV per session should rise or stabilize, even if raw conversion drops. This metric captures the trade-off between volume and fulfillment reliability.

**2. Guardrail Metrics (Must not degrade beyond thresholds)**
We will monitor these daily. If any breach their threshold, the test variant is paused immediately.
*   **App Abandonment Rate (Pre-Order):** The percentage of users who close the app after seeing the delivery estimate but before adding items to the cart.
    *   *Threshold:* Must not exceed current baseline by >15%. (This answers your concern about the "abandon entirely" point).
*   **Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) / NPS:** Post-delivery survey scores.
    *   *Threshold:* Must remain stable or improve. A drop indicates that even with accurate times, the service level is unacceptable.
*   **Driver Utilization Rate:** Orders completed per driver hour.
    *   *Threshold:* Must not decrease. We need to ensure the new logic isn't creating inefficient routing or idle time.

**3. Experimental Design & Reading Rules**
*   **Method:** A/B Test (50/50 split).
    *   *Control:* Current algorithm (optimistic estimates).
    *   *Variant:* Real-time adjusted estimates (+buffer for current shortage).
*   **Duration:** 7 Days (Fixed Horizon). We will **not** peek at results before Day 7 to avoid false positives from daily volatility.
*   **Decision Rule:**
    *   *Ship:* If AGMV per Session increases OR stays flat while Cancellations drop >30%.
    *   *Stop:* If App Abandonment spikes >15% OR AGMV drops >5%.
    *   *Extend:* If results are inconclusive but trends favor the variant, extend for 3 more days.

**4. Immediate Next Steps (This Week)**
1.  **Instrumentation Check (Today):** Verify that the "Estimate Displayed" event logs the specific time shown (e.g., "45 mins") to analyze the specific drop-off cliff later.
2.  **Launch Test (Tomorrow):** Deploy variant to 10% of traffic initially, scaling to 50% by Wednesday to ensure data stability for Thursday.
3.  **Board Prep (Thursday):** Present the Day 3-4 interim *trends* (clearly labeled as non-final) focusing on the correlation between estimate length and cancellation reduction.

**Note:** We are comparing against our own pre-shortage baseline; no external industry benchmarks are used as they do not reflect current Milan-specific constraints.
**Note:** If "App Abandonment" spikes, we will segment by time-of-day to see if specific peak hours are driving the rejection.
