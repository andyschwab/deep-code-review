### Barn Buddy Beta: Event Taxonomy & Measurement Plan (March 15 – April 2)

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Collaborators (users who both view a peer's case AND leave a verified comment within 24 hours).
*Rationale:* This matches your definition of "successful interaction." Vanity metrics like total pageviews or raw signups are excluded as they do not prove value delivery.

**1. Core Event Definitions**
Instrument only these three events to answer specific questions about collaboration quality.

| Event Name | Trigger Condition | Properties to Capture | Question Answered |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | : |
| `case_viewed` | User opens a case file not authored by them. | `case_id`, `animal_category` (exotic vs. common), `viewer_id`, `timestamp` | Is the content discoverable and interesting enough to click? |
| `comment_submitted` | User posts a comment that passes automated verification. | `case_id`, `comment_id`, `time_since_view_ms`, `viewer_id` | Does viewing lead to contribution? (Filters spam/unverified input). |
| `collaboration_completed` | **Derived Metric:** `comment_submitted` occurs ≤ 24h after `case_viewed` for the same `case_id` and `user_id`. | `animal_category`, `time_to_comment_hours` | Did a genuine knowledge exchange occur? (This is your North Star input). |

**2. Decision Logic: Bookmarks & Weighting**
*   **Bookmarking:** Do **not** instrument `bookmark_clicked` as a core success metric for this beta.
    *   *Reasoning:* You stated a concern about noise. Without a proven correlation between bookmarking and future retention in your specific data, this is a vanity signal. Treat it as a secondary "guardrail" only if users explicitly request it during beta interviews; do not let it inflate the dashboard.
*   **Exotic vs. Common Weighting:** Do **not** apply artificial weights (e.g., counting an exotic case as 1.5x).
    *   *Reasoning:* Weighting fabricates value. Instead, **segment** your retention cohorts by `animal_category`.
    *   *Action:* Compare the `collaboration_completed` rate for the 60% (common) vs. 40% (exotic) groups. If exotic cases have lower completion rates, the insight is a product problem (harder to find peers?), not a math problem to solve with weighting.

**3. Retention Measurement Strategy (Bottom-Up)**
Since you are measuring retention improvements for investors:
1.  **Define Cohorts:** Group users by their first `collaboration_completed` date (Week 1, Week 2, etc.).
2.  **Measure Return:** Track if these users return to perform *another* `collaboration_completed` in subsequent weeks.
3.  **Ignore Acquisition Noise:** Do not report total beta signups (500) as success. Report the % of those 500 who formed a habit (returned >1 time).

**4. This-Week Steps (Before March 15 Launch)**
1.  **Implement Identifier Seam:** Ensure `viewer_id` is a stable, pseudonymous ID persisting across sessions to link views and comments accurately.
2.  **Hard-Code the 24h Logic:** Build the `collaboration_completed` derivation in your analytics tool now, using the 24-hour window rule. Do not wait for launch to define this.
3.  **Set the Baseline:** Record the current retention rate of users *without* Barn Buddy. Any lift claimed in April must be compared to this specific baseline, not an industry average.
