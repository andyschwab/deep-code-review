### Methodology to Define Activation for DevForge

**1. Retrospective Cohort Analysis (The "Aha" Moment Search)**
Do not guess the threshold; calculate it using last quarter's data.
*   **Segment:** Isolate users who signed up 6–12 months ago (allowing time for the 9-month sales cycle to mature into a "Won" or "Lost" state).
*   **Correlate:** Map specific early behaviors (Days 1–14) against final contract status. Test these hypotheses:
    *   *Volume:* Did users making >X calls in week 1 convert at a higher rate than those making <X?
    *   *Breadth:* Did users integrating >1 framework convert better than single-framework users?
    *   *Persistence:* Did users returning on Day 4+ (addressing your 40% drop-off) have a higher close rate?
*   **Identify the Threshold:** Find the specific behavior where the conversion probability curve inflection point is highest. If users making 50 calls convert at 20% but those making 51 calls convert at 45%, your activation event is "50 API calls."

**2. Instrumentation Plan (Next Sprint)**
Instrument only the events that answer the questions above. Avoid "tracking everything."
*   **Event: `api_key_created`**
    *   *Properties:* `key_type`, `environment` (prod/sandbox), `framework_detected` (via User-Agent).
    *   *Question:* Does production key creation predict conversion better than sandbox?
*   **Event: `api_call_made`** (Aggregate or sampled to manage volume)
    *   *Properties:* `status_code`, `latency_bucket`, `endpoint_category`.
    *   *Question:* Is successful traffic (2xx) the predictor, or just volume?
*   **Event: `integration_completed`**
    *   *Properties:* `framework_name`, `time_to_first_success_call`.
    *   *Question:* Does speed of integration correlate with deal size?
*   **Identifier Seam:** Ensure a stable, pseudonymous `user_id` persists across sessions to track the "return after day three" metric accurately. Do not log PII or payload contents (respecting firewall/privacy constraints); log only metadata available at the gateway.

**3. The New Definition of Activation**
Until the analysis completes, treat "Activation" as a composite leading indicator, not a single click:
*   *Provisional Definition:* A user is "Activated" when they create a key **AND** make >X successful calls **AND** return on Day 3+.
*   *Validation:* Once the retrospective analysis identifies the strongest predictor (e.g., "3 successful calls from a production IP"), update the definition immediately.

**4. Operationalizing for Sales & CS**
*   **High Priority Outreach:** Users who hit the new activation threshold but have not engaged with sales within 48 hours.
*   **Rescue Workflow:** Users who created a key but failed to return by Day 3 (your 40% leak). Trigger an automated check-in or CS alert specifically for this cohort.
*   **Ignore:** Users who create keys but never hit the volume threshold (casual explorers); do not waste sales cycles on them unless they re-engage.
