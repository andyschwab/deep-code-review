**Subject:** Pricing Experiment Read-out: Anchor Shift (Jan 5 – Feb 20)

**Executive Summary**
The shift of the premium anchor from 4,500 CAD to 5,200 CAD successfully captured immediate value, driving a **14% increase in Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)**. However, this gain is coupled with an **8% contraction in Standard Tier conversion**, indicating the higher anchor may have depressed volume among core institutional buyers rather than solely filtering for high-value accounts.

**Interpretation of Findings**
The data suggests a classic trade-off: we are extracting more margin per unit but losing transaction volume. The assumption that institutional buyers are entirely price-insensitive appears partially flawed; the 8% drop in standard conversions implies that the new price architecture altered the perceived value of the entry-level kit or that the "sticker shock" of the premium tier negatively impacted the entire pricing page's conversion flow.

We cannot yet determine if this is sustainable value capture or alienation. The 14% ARPU lift is a short-term accounting win; if the 8% volume drop represents lost long-term contracts or eroded trust with core research stations, the Lifetime Value (LTV) may decline despite the immediate revenue bump. The risk of future churn remains **UNVERIFIED** as the experiment duration (6 weeks) is insufficient to observe renewal behavior in a seasonal, high-stakes market like Arctic logistics.

**Risks & Opportunities**
*   **Risk (Volume Erosion):** If the 8% drop persists globally, we risk ceding market share to competitors in Nunavut who offer lower-cost entry points. Institutional budgets are often fixed; a perceived price hike across the board may force buyers to seek alternatives.
*   **Risk (Churn Lag):** Higher initial prices can lead to higher scrutiny during renewal. Without six months of data, we cannot rule out a churn spike that cancels the 14% ARPU gain.
*   **Opportunity (Segmentation):** The ARPU lift proves there is willingness to pay among a subset of buyers. The opportunity lies in isolating *who* converted at 5,200 CAD versus who dropped off.

**Decision & Next Steps**
Do not roll out globally yet. The current read is **inconclusive on long-term viability** due to the volume trade-off and unknown churn impact.

**This-Week Actions:**
1.  **Segment the Drop:** Query the analytics to isolate the 8% conversion drop by buyer type (Institutional vs. Individual). If the drop is entirely individual researchers, the institutional assumption holds, and a targeted global rollout to institutions is viable. If institutions also dropped, the pricing strategy requires revision.
2.  **Define the Guardrail:** Establish a churn tripwire before any broader launch. If renewal rates for the new price cohort fall below the historical baseline by >5% in the next quarter, the price must revert.
3.  **Extend Observation:** Treat the current result as a "mid-test" read. Commit to a fixed observation period of 6 months to measure actual renewal/churn before declaring the 14% lift a permanent win.
