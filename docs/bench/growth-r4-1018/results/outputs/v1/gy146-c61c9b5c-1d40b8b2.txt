**To:** VP of Customer Success, PeopleFirst
**Subject:** Feature Adoption Measurement & Intervention Plan for AI Performance Review Module

**1. The North Star: Reviews Completed per Active Employee**
Reject "administrators enabled" (80%) and "time spent" as primary metrics. The former is a vanity metric reflecting admin setup, not user value; the latter is noise that does not correlate with outcome. "Managers initiating" is a necessary input but not the value itself.
**Adopt this North Star:** *Weekly Active Employees completing a review step.* This rises only when the core value (performance feedback) is delivered. Pair it with one input metric: *% of Managers initiating a review cycle.*

**2. Diagnosis: The Activation Leak**
Your data shows a classic retention leak, not a training deficit yet. Pouring training into a bucket where 88% of users never enter is wasted spend.
*   **The Gap:** 80% Admin Enablement vs. 12% Employee Interaction.
*   **The Hypothesis to Test:** The barrier is likely **visibility** or **trigger timing**, not utility. If pilot feedback was positive, the product works once found. The failure is in the "Activation" step of the funnel, not "Retention."
*   **Action:** Do not measure "time spent." Measure the **drop-off rate** between "Manager Initiates" and "Employee Opens." If managers initiate but employees don't open, the notification channel or UI placement is the blocker.

**3. Event Taxonomy: Instrument Only What Answers a Question**
Stop instrumenting "just in case." Define these three events immediately to answer specific questions for your intervention plan:
*   `review_initiated` (Property: `manager_id`, `team_size`): *Question:* Are managers starting the process?
*   `review_step_completed` (Property: `employee_id`, `step_type`): *Question:* Are employees actually doing the work? (This drives the North Star).
*   `help_article_viewed` (Property: `user_role`): *Question:* Is lack of knowledge the blocker? (Only if this correlates with low completion do you blame training).

**4. Intervention Plan Logic (Read Bottom-Up)**
Segment your top 50 clients by the **Manager Initiation Rate**.
*   **Scenario A (Low Initiation):** Managers aren't starting. *Intervention:* Executive alignment workshops. The metric to watch is `review_initiated`.
*   **Scenario B (High Initiation, Low Completion):** Managers start, employees ignore. *Intervention:* In-app nudges, email trigger optimization, or UI changes. The metric to watch is the conversion rate from `review_initiated` to `review_step_completed`.
*   **Scenario C (High Completion, Low Depth):** Employees start but don't finish. *Intervention:* Targeted training or UX simplification.

**5. This-Week Steps**
1.  **Query your analytics** for the conversion rate between `review_initiated` and `review_step_completed` for the bottom 20 clients. If the drop is >90%, the issue is visibility/trigger, not training.
2.  **Define the "Activated" threshold:** Identify the minimum number of `review_step_completed` events in week 1 that correlates with a client renewing or expanding (use your historical CRM data). Any client below this threshold in week 2 triggers an automatic CS outreach.
3.  **Pause broad training rollout** until you confirm via the `help_article_viewed` event that users are actually looking for help. If they aren't looking, training won't fix the visibility problem.

*Note: All benchmarks above are derived from your own 80%/12% baseline; no external industry standards were applied. Ensure user IDs in these events are pseudonymous to comply with privacy standards.*
