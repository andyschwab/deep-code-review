### Conversion Funnel Definition: CasaFind Spain (Mobile)

**Objective:** Distinguish between UX friction (drop-off) and offline conversion (bypass) to prioritize Q3 roadmap items.

**North Star Metric:** *Verified Property Connections per Active User* (replaces raw "leads" to account for verification success).

**Revised Funnel Structure & Instrumentation**
Instrument only these steps to answer specific diagnostic questions. All events must include `device_type`, `property_id`, and a stable pseudonymous `user_id`.

1.  **Property Viewed** (Baseline)
    *   *Question:* Is inventory being consumed?
    *   *Current Status:* 15k/day.
    *   *Action:* No change.

2.  **Contact Intent Initiated** (New Event)
    *   *Definition:* User clicks "Contact Agent" or "View Phone Number."
    *   *Question:* Is the drop-off happening before or after the intent to connect?
    *   *Diagnostic:* If *Views* → *Intent* drops >20% on mobile vs. desktop, the property page layout is the blocker.

3.  **Verification Step Started** (New Event)
    *   *Definition:* User enters phone number but has not yet submitted the code.
    *   *Question:* Is the friction point the input field or the SMS delivery?
    *   *Diagnostic:* High drop-off here confirms your hypothesis about mobile friction.

4.  **Verification Step Completed** (Existing "Lead" precursor)
    *   *Definition:* Valid code entered; contact details revealed or form submitted.
    *   *Question:* What is the true conversion rate once friction is overcome?

5.  **Offline Bypass Detected** (Proxy Event - Critical)
    *   *Definition:* User clicks "Reveal Number" (if applicable) OR spends >45 seconds on the agent contact card *without* completing verification, followed by immediate app backgrounding or session end.
    *   *Question:* Are users getting the info they need and leaving to call manually?
    *   *Diagnostic:* If *Intent* is high but *Verification* is low, yet *Bypass Proxy* is high on mobile, users are successfully converting offline. The UX is not "broken"; the tracking is incomplete.

**Analysis Plan for Product Director**
Compare the conversion rates of these steps between **Desktop** and **Mobile** for the period **Aug 20–Present**.

*   **Scenario A (Broken UX):** Mobile drop-off is severe at *Verification Started* → *Completed*, and *Offline Bypass* is low.
    *   *Roadmap Action:* Prioritize removing/optimizing phone verification on mobile (e.g., magic links, social auth).
*   **Scenario B (Offline Success):** Mobile drop-off at *Verification* is high, but *Offline Bypass* correlates with high *Intent*.
    *   *Roadmap Action:* Do not fix verification yet. Prioritize "Click-to-Call" integration or server-side call tracking to capture this value.
*   **Scenario C (Content Mismatch):** Drop-off occurs at *Property Viewed* → *Contact Intent*.
    *   *Roadmap Action:* Improve property data quality or image loading speeds; the friction is not in the form.

**This Week's Steps**
1.  **Instrument:** Deploy the three new events (*Intent Initiated*, *Verification Started*, *Offline Bypass Proxy*) with the specified properties.
2.  **Query:** Run a cohort analysis comparing Mobile vs. Desktop conversion rates for the post-Aug 20th period.
3.  **Decide:** If *Offline Bypass* > 15% of *Intent* on mobile, shift Q3 priority from "Fix Verification" to "Capture Offline Value."

*Note: Do not assert a "good" conversion rate; compare mobile performance strictly against your own desktop baseline. Ensure the `user_id` logic complies with GDPR/pseudonymity standards before implementation.*
