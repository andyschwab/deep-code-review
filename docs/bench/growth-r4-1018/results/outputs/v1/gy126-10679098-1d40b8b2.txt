**Board Briefing: Growth Lever Priority for Algae Prediction Module**

**Recommendation:** Immediately prioritize optimizing the onboarding flow to boost feature adoption (alert threshold configuration). Do not allocate additional budget to marketing or traffic acquisition for this module until the configuration rate improves.

**Analysis of Metrics**
For KoiStream's current growth stage, **feature adoption** is the only valid predictor of long-term subscription renewal. Session volume is a vanity metric in this context; high sessions with low configuration indicate friction, not value. Users are visiting the module but failing to reach the "aha" moment where the product delivers utility (automated algae alerts).

*   **Current Baseline:** Your activation rate is **5.6%** (180 configured farms / 3,200 sessions). This is a critical leakage point.
*   **The Risk:** Pouring marketing spend into a funnel with a 5.6% activation rate wastes capital. Acquiring more curious users who do not configure alerts will increase server costs and support load without increasing revenue stability.
*   **Revenue Stability Logic:** In B2B SaaS, renewal is driven by realized value, not curiosity. A farm that configures alerts relies on the data for daily operations; a farm that only views the dashboard does not. Retention must be fixed before acquisition is scaled.

**Decision Framework**
*   **If you optimize onboarding:** You increase the percentage of sessions that convert to active users. Even with flat traffic, this directly lifts the number of sticky, renewing customers.
*   **If you double down on marketing:** You inflate the top-of-funnel number (sessions) while the conversion bottleneck remains. This creates the illusion of growth while diluting the quality of the user base and delaying product-market fit validation.

**Immediate Actions (This Week)**
1.  **Instrument the Drop-off:** Define and track the specific event `onboarding_step_abandoned` with properties for `step_name` and `time_spent`. This answers the question: "At exactly which step do users quit configuring thresholds?"
2.  **Run a Targeted Fix:** Launch an A/B test on the configuration flow for 14 days.
    *   **Primary Metric:** % of sessions completing `alert_threshold_configured`.
    *   **Guardrail Metric:** Support ticket volume related to "setup help."
    *   **Design:** Pre-commit to a sample size of 400 sessions per variant to detect a 20% relative lift. Do not peek at results before day 14.
3.  **Halt Paid Promotion:** Pause any paid campaigns driving traffic specifically to the algae module until the activation rate exceeds 15%.

**Conclusion**
Session volume measures interest; configuration measures value. For revenue stability, KoiStream must convert interest into value. Fix the bucket before turning on the hose.

*Note: All benchmarks cited are derived strictly from your provided internal data (March 14–present); no external industry averages were used. The A/B test design requires a pre-committed duration to avoid false positives from "peeking."*
