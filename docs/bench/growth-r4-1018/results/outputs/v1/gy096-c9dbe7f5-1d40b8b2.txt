**Subject:** Virality Measurement Plan & Budget Decision Framework

**Current Status:** The reported viral coefficient of 0.8 is `UNVERIFIED` because it relies on incomplete attribution (missing indirect views and untracked DM sources). We cannot determine if organic mechanics sustain growth until we fix the event taxonomy.

**1. Revised Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only What Answers a Question)**
Stop tracking "shares" as a vanity metric. Implement these three specific events to answer attribution questions:
*   `invite_sent`: Properties: `channel` (dm, instagram_story, whatsapp), `recipient_count`. *Question: Which channel drives the highest volume of attempts?*
*   `install_attributed`: Properties: `source_type` (direct_link, deep_link_context, organic_search_after_view). *Question: Did the user come from a tracked link or an untracked view?*
*   `collab_session_started`: Properties: `initiator_id`, `joiner_id`, `time_to_join`. *Question: Does the "collab" action actually happen, or do they drop off after install?*

**2. Solving the Indirect Attribution Gap**
You cannot track a user who sees a photo on Instagram without clicking a link using standard event logging alone. To capture this "passive virality":
*   **Immediate Tactic:** Embed a unique, pseudonymous `creator_id` watermark or visible handle on all exported images. When a new user signs up, ask "Who inspired you?" with a search bar populated by recent active creators. Match self-reported names to `creator_id` to estimate the indirect conversion rate.
*   **Measurement:** Calculate a "View-to-Install" proxy by correlating spikes in organic search/install volume in regions where specific influencers or power users posted, minus the volume from direct links. Label this metric `estimated_indirect_lift` and treat it as a range, not an exact figure.

**3. Reading the Funnel Bottom-Up**
Do not pour budget into influencers (Acquisition) until we verify Retention of the core action.
*   **Step A:** Measure retention of users who joined via `collab` vs. organic search. If `collab` users do not return to edit a second image within 7 days, the feature is a leaky bucket. Increasing influencer spend now will waste capital.
*   **Step B:** Compare conversion rates of `invite_sent` (channel: dm) vs. `invite_sent` (channel: public). If your assumption holds (DM > Public), the product mechanic should nudge users toward private sharing, not public posting.

**4. Decision Framework for March 20 Deadline**
We will not fabricate a "good" viral coefficient benchmark. We will compare against our own baseline once the new events are live (estimated 3 days to instrument, 7 days for data).

*   **Scenario A (Ship Influencer Budget):** If `collab` user 7-day retention > organic user retention AND `estimated_indirect_lift` shows a positive trend. *Action: Increase influencer budget; the mechanic sustains growth.*
*   **Scenario B (Hold Budget, Fix Product):** If `collab` retention < organic retention. *Action: Pause influencer spend. The "collab" feature attracts users but fails to retain them. Fix the onboarding flow for joiners first.*
*   **Scenario C (Shift Channel Strategy):** If DM conversion is 2x Public sharing (statistically significant). *Action: Reallocate marketing creative to encourage "Send to a friend" over "Post to Story," regardless of total share volume.*
