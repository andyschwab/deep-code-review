**Subject:** Q3 Retention Paradox – Investigation Plan & Decision Framework

**Executive Summary**
The 40% return rate is a vanity metric in this context because it decouples from the North Star: **Weekly Completed Workouts**. A drop from 3.5 to 0.8 sessions indicates that while users are returning to the app, they are not receiving core value. Retention without core action completion is unsustainable; it signals "zombie retention" where users open the app but fail to activate. Do not pivot the roadmap to social features yet. The data suggests a friction problem in the activation path or a displacement effect, not a successful product evolution.

**Immediate Investigation: Three Behavioral Patterns**
To resolve the paradox before committing roadmap resources, instrument and analyze these three specific segments. Each event must answer a named question.

1.  **The "Social-Only" Cohort (Displacement Hypothesis)**
    *   **Question:** Are returning users consuming the social feed *instead* of tracking workouts?
    *   **Events to Query:** `social_feed_view` (property: duration_seconds) AND `workout_start_attempt` (property: success_boolean).
    *   **Analysis:** Segment the 40% returners into those who viewed the feed >2 mins vs. those who did not. Compare the `workout_start_attempt` rate between these two sub-groups.
    *   **Decision Rule:** If feed viewers have a significantly lower workout start rate than non-viewers, the social feature is cannibalizing core value. **Action:** Deprioritize social expansion; introduce friction to feed access or redesign the home screen to force workout initiation first.

2.  **The "Friction" Cohort (Onboarding Hypothesis)**
    *   **Question:** Are users forgetting how to start a workout due to UI changes or lack of cues?
    *   **Events to Query:** `app_open` → `time_to_first_click` → `workout_start_success`.
    *   **Analysis:** Measure the time delta between `app_open` and `workout_start_attempt` for January signups vs. a baseline cohort (e.g., pre-social feature). Look for increased hesitation or "rage clicks" on non-interactive elements.
    *   **Decision Rule:** If time-to-start has increased by >20% or failure rates on `workout_start_attempt` are high, the issue is usability, not interest. **Action:** Double down on onboarding improvements and UI simplification; revert recent navigation changes.

3.  **The "Passive Value" Validation (Sustainability Check)**
    *   **Question:** Is passive consumption actually driving long-term retention if we ignore workouts?
    *   **Events to Query:** `social_feed_view` → `retention_day_30`.
    *   **Analysis:** Correlate pure social consumption with Day-30 retention *independent* of workout counts.
    *   **Decision Rule:** If high social consumption does *not* correlate with sustained Day-30 retention beyond week 4, the behavior is a novelty spike, not a new North Star. **Action:** Treat social as a secondary engagement loop, not a primary pivot.
