### Proposed Activation Definition for SmartFilter

**Activation Event:** A user is considered "Activated" when they complete the **"Value Realization Sequence"** within 14 days of their first upload. This sequence requires three distinct actions that prove reliance on the model's output rather than passive exposure:

1.  **Exposure:** The user views the auto-generated tags for at least one document.
2.  **Verification (The "Intent" Signal):** The user explicitly accepts, edits, or adds to the auto-generated tags (distinguishing intentional interaction from accidental clicks).
3.  **Utility (The "Reliance" Signal):** The user performs a **search or filter action** using those specific tags to locate or organize files.

**Threshold Criteria:**
*   **Frequency:** The sequence must be completed for **at least 2 distinct documents**. This filters out one-off curiosity and establishes a pattern of reliance.
*   **Timeframe:** Actions must occur within 14 days of the first upload to align with the "first week" upload baseline while allowing time for the second interaction.

**Dashboard Metrics to Track:**
*   **Activation Rate:** % of users who uploaded in Week 1 and completed the Value Realization Sequence by Day 14.
*   **Drop-off Points:** % of users who stop after Exposure vs. % who stop after Verification.
*   **Core Action Retention:** % of activated users who repeat the "Utility" step in Week 3 and Week 4.

**Implementation Notes for Teams:**
*   **Customer Success:** Trigger "success" outreach only when the *Utility* step is recorded. If a user stops at *Verification*, trigger an educational email demonstrating search use-cases.
*   **Product Marketing:** Measure the December 10th launch campaign success by the lift in the **Activation Rate**, not just the upload volume.

**Event Taxonomy for Instrumentation:**
To track this, instrument exactly these three events with the following properties:
1.  `tags_viewed` (Properties: `document_id`, `tag_count`)
2.  `tags_modified` (Properties: `document_id`, `action_type` [accept/edit/add], `tag_count`)
3.  `search_via_tag` (Properties: `tag_used`, `results_found_count`)

Do not instrument "clicks" generally; only these three answer the question "Did they rely on the model?" Any metric value (e.g., "good activation rate") must be calculated against your own historical baseline once data is collected; no external benchmark is applied here.
