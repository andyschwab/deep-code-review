**To:** CFO, Sales Director
**From:** Growth Lead
**Date:** February 21
**Subject:** Pricing Experiment Read-out: Premium Anchor Shift (Jan 5 – Feb 20)

**Executive Summary**
The shift of the premium bundle anchor from 4,500 CAD to 5,200 CAD successfully captured immediate value, driving a **14% increase in Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)**. However, this gain came with an **8% conversion drop in the Standard tier**, indicating the higher anchor may have depressed overall demand or caused hesitation among volume-driven institutional buyers.

**Interpretation of Findings**
The data suggests a classic "trade-up" friction. While the higher anchor likely made the Standard tier appear relatively cheaper to some, the aggregate volume loss implies that for our Arctic research market, the absolute price point or the perceived value gap between tiers has shifted unfavorably. The assumption that institutional buyers are entirely price-insensitive appears partially flawed; while they may absorb higher premium costs, the market elasticity at the Standard level is tighter than anticipated.

**Risk Assessment**
*   **Volume Erosion:** The 8% drop in Standard tier conversions threatens our market share in volume-dependent contracts. If this trend holds globally, total unit volume could decline faster than ARPU gains can compensate.
*   **Unverified Churn Risk:** The six-month churn impact is currently **UNVERIFIED**. Higher prices often correlate with higher expectations; if the product experience does not match the new premium perception, renewal rates for institutional contracts may suffer.
*   **Sustainability Unknown:** A 45-day window in peak winter (Jan–Feb) may reflect urgent, inelastic demand. This lift may not sustain during shoulder seasons when procurement cycles are more deliberate.

**Opportunity**
*   **Margin Expansion:** The 14% ARPU lift proves there is willingness to pay among a segment of buyers, likely those with immediate, critical thermal needs or less constrained budgets.
*   **Segmentation Leverage:** The divergence between premium uptake and standard drop-off signals an opportunity to explicitly segment offers by buyer type (Institutional vs. Individual) rather than relying on a unified pricing page.

**Recommendation & Next Steps**
Do not roll out globally yet. The current result is a net positive on revenue but a negative on volume health, with unknown long-term retention implications.

1.  **Extend Measurement Window:** Continue the current pricing configuration for 30 more days to capture early spring procurement behavior, monitoring if the Standard tier drop accelerates as urgency fades.
2.  **Cohort Analysis:** Immediately segment the Jan–Feb data by buyer type (Institutional ID vs. Individual). If the 8% drop is isolated to individuals, the global rollout can proceed with institutional safeguards. If institutions are dropping, the strategy must be revised.
3.  **Define Guardrails:** Establish a "break-even volume" threshold. If Standard tier conversions drop beyond 12%, the ARPU gain is mathematically negated by lost volume; this is the tripwire to revert the anchor price.

**Decision:** Extend test to validate seasonality and segment performance before global commitment.

***

*Note: Churn projections remain UNVERIFIED until renewal data is available; no benchmark was applied. The "price sensitivity" assumption requires validation against the segmented cohort data requested above.*
