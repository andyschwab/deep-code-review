**Subject:** Recommendation on Push Notification Timing A/B Test (Jakarta vs. Surabaya)

**Decision: Do not roll out globally. Restrict the 11:00 AM timing to Jakarta only and halt the change for Surabaya immediately.**

The aggregate "4% lift" is a misleading vanity metric driven entirely by Jakarta's volume, masking a severe 12% revenue leak in Surabaya. Rolling this out globally would actively degrade performance in a key secondary market based on an unverified assumption about cultural lunch habits.

**Analysis of Conflicting Results**
The divergence indicates that the intervention (timing change) interacts negatively with user behavior in Surabaya. While the hypothesis cites "cultural lunch timing," this remains **UNVERIFIED** as no local meal habit data was instrumented or cited. The 12% drop suggests the 11:00 AM reminder is either arriving before the decision window opens or is perceived as noise, increasing opt-out risk. In contrast, Jakarta users likely operate on an earlier cycle where 11:00 AM aligns with their activation trigger. Treating these distinct cohorts as a single population violates the principle of reading retention and conversion by segment first.

**Financial & Risk Implication**
A global rollout applies a -12% penalty to all Surabaya orders. Unless Jakarta's volume is disproportionately massive compared to Surabaya, the net global gain will be significantly lower than 4%, or potentially negative if Surabaya's baseline volume is high. The cost of "annoying" users extends beyond immediate lost orders to long-term retention erosion (push disablement), which is harder to recover than a single missed conversion.

**Required Next Steps (This Week)**
1.  **Segmented Deployment:** Configure the push notification engine to apply the 11:00 AM rule *only* to the Jakarta geo-cohort. Revert Surabaya to the 12:30 PM baseline effective immediately.
2.  **Instrument the "Why":** Define and instrument one specific event to validate the lunch-timing hypothesis: `push_interaction_by_local_time_bucket`. This tracks clicks relative to local time, not server time, to identify the actual "activation window" for each city without guessing.
3.  **Surabaya-Specific Test:** Design a follow-up A/B test strictly for Surabaya comparing 12:30 PM (control) against 1:00 PM or 1:30 PM (treatment), pre-committing to a sample size that detects a 5% lift with 95% confidence. Do not peek at results before the committed duration ends.

**Reading Rule for Future Decisions**
Any future aggregate metric showing growth must be validated against the weakest performing major segment. If any segment drops by >5%, the aggregate win is declared **UNVERIFIED** until the segment leak is plugged or isolated.
