**Subject:** Virality Measurement Strategy & Budget Decision Framework

**Current Status:** The reported viral coefficient of 0.8 is `UNVERIFIED` for decision-making because it relies on incomplete attribution. It captures direct link clicks but ignores "passive virality" (users downloading after seeing edited photos on Instagram without clicking a link). Consequently, we cannot yet determine if organic mechanics can replace paid influencer spend.

**1. Redefine the North Star for Virality**
Stop using "sessions" or raw "invites sent." Our North Star for this initiative is **Weekly Active Collab Pairs**. This metric only rises when two distinct users derive value together, filtering out vanity noise from single-user edits or failed invites.

**2. Fix the Attribution Gap (The "Passive" Path)**
To capture the indirect path (seeing a photo on Instagram → searching/downloading app), we must instrument a **Cohort-Based Lift Model** rather than relying solely on click-through attribution:
*   **Event Taxonomy:** Instrument `collab_invite_sent` (properties: `channel` [DM vs. Public], `recipient_status` [new/existing]) and `app_install`.
*   **The Measurement Method:** Identify users who installed the app within 24 hours of a collab partner sharing an image to a public feed. Compare their activation rate and retention against a control group of users who installed organically (no collab partner activity) during the same week.
*   **The Metric:** Calculate **Incremental Installs per Public Share**. If this lift is significant, our current coefficient of 0.8 is a severe undercount.

**3. Test the Channel Hypothesis**
Your assumption that DMs convert higher than public feeds is unproven without data. We will run a segmented analysis on the `collab_invite_sent` event:
*   **Segment A:** Invites via Direct Message.
*   **Segment B:** Invites via Public Social Feeds.
*   **Action:** Compare the `install_to_activation` rate for recipients in both segments. If Segment A shows >20% higher conversion, we prioritize features that facilitate DM sharing. If Segment B drives higher volume despite lower conversion, we optimize the public image watermark to drive search intent.

**4. Decision Framework for March 20**
We will not guess the budget allocation. We will use the following tripwires based on the recomputed data:
*   **Scenario 1 (Organic Sustains Growth):** If the *Incremental Installs per Public Share* combined with *DM Conversion* yields a corrected viral coefficient > 1.0 over a 14-day cohort.
    *   *Decision:* Hold influencer budget flat; reinvest savings into collab feature polish.
*   **Scenario 2 (Hybrid Required):** If the corrected coefficient is between 0.6 and 0.9.
    *   *Decision:* Allocate 50% of budget to influencers to top-of-funnel, relying on product for retention.
*   **Scenario 3 (Paid Dependency):** If the corrected coefficient remains < 0.6 after accounting for passive lift.
    *   *Decision:* Increase influencer budget immediately; product mechanics are insufficient for standalone growth.
