### Proposed Activation Criteria for QuillStream

**Option 1: The "Value Realized" Threshold (Current Hypothesis)**
*Definition:* User generates, edits, and exports a shipping manifest.
*Pros:* Directly correlates to your paid tier and proves the tool solved the specific maritime logistics problem (exportable documentation). It filters out curiosity seekers from serious coordinators.
*Cons:* With only 120 beta users and an 18% 3-day return rate, this bar may be too high to detect early friction. If users drop off before exporting due to minor UI issues rather than lack of value, this metric will remain artificially low, hiding successful engagement steps.

**Option 2: The "Commitment" Threshold**
*Definition:* User generates a document and saves it as a draft or performs an edit.
*Pros:* Captures the "Aha!" moment where the AI output is deemed worthy of keeping. For maritime coordinators, saving a draft often implies intent to finalize later due to shift work or verification workflows common in Hamburg ports. It provides a larger sample size for statistical significance given your small user base.
*Cons:* Risks inflating activation numbers with users who save drafts but never complete the workflow. It measures intent, not necessarily the delivery of full business value.

**Option 3: The "Hybrid Cohort" Threshold**
*Definition:* Primary metric is Option 1 (Export); Input metric is Option 2 (Save/Edit) within the first session.
*Pros:* Allows you to track the funnel leakage specifically between "saving" and "exporting." If many users save but few export, the friction lies in the finalization/export features, not the AI generation. This is critical for a niche audience where the document format must be exact.
*Cons:* Requires tracking two events instead of one, though this is negligible engineering cost. It complicates the dashboard slightly by requiring a conversion rate view rather than a single percentage.

**Recommendation for Q4 Dashboard:**
Adopt **Option 3**. Given the small sample size (120 users) and specialized audience, treating "Export" as the sole North Star risks having no signal to optimize. Define "Activated" as **Export** for revenue modeling, but instrument **Save/Edit** as the primary diagnostic metric for this week. If the gap between Save and Export is wide, the issue is likely trust in the manifest's accuracy or export formatting, not the AI's writing capability.

**Note:** Do not compare these rates to industry benchmarks; they are unavailable for this niche. Compare week-over-week trends of your own 120 users only.
**Next Step:** Instrument the "Save Draft" and "Export Manifest" events immediately to calculate the conversion rate between them by Friday.
