### Proposed Activation Definition for SmartFilter

**Activation Event:** A user is considered "Activated" when they complete the **"Value Realization Sequence"** within 14 days of their first upload.

**The Sequence (must occur in order):**
1.  **Upload:** User uploads at least one document (Current baseline: 60%).
2.  **Exposure:** System displays auto-generated tags.
3.  **Intentional Interaction:** User performs **one** of the following high-intent actions:
    *   *Search via Tag:* Clicks a generated tag to filter/search their document library.
    *   *Organize via Tag:* Moves a document into a folder/category suggested by a tag.
    *   *Correction:* Edits or deletes a generated tag (signals active management of the taxonomy).
    *   *Note:* Simple clicks on a tag without a subsequent filter or organization action are classified as "Exploration," not activation, to filter out accidental clicks.

**Frequency Threshold:**
*   **Single Session Sufficiency:** One complete sequence is sufficient to mark the user as "Activated" for cohort reporting. This captures the "Aha!" moment where the user realizes the model saves them time.
*   **Reliance Signal (Secondary Metric):** To distinguish "power users" for the Customer Success team, track users who repeat the **Intentional Interaction** step **3+ times** across **2 separate sessions** within the first 30 days. This indicates the tool has become part of their workflow, not just a one-time experiment.

**Dashboard Implementation Criteria:**
*   **Metric Name:** `SmartFilter_Activation_Rate`
*   **Numerator:** Count of unique users completing the *Value Realization Sequence*.
*   **Denominator:** Count of unique users who uploaded at least one document.
*   **Current Estimated Status:** `UNVERIFIED`. You have exposure data (15% interact), but without distinguishing between "clicks" and "search/organize" actions, the true activation rate cannot be calculated yet.

**Immediate Instrumentation Requirements:**
To enable this definition, you must instrument the following specific events with these properties:
1.  `tag_clicked` (Property: `click_source` = 'search_bar' vs 'document_card')
2.  `filter_applied` (Property: `filter_type` = 'auto_tag')
3.  `document_moved` (Property: `suggested_by` = 'auto_tag')
4.  `tag_edited` (Property: `action` = 'rename' | 'delete')

**Reading Rule for Launch (Dec 10):**
Do not compare your 15% interaction rate to industry benchmarks. Compare your post-launch `SmartFilter_Activation_Rate` against your own pre-launch baseline of intentional interactions. If the rate rises but `tag_edited` (correction) spikes disproportionately, the model accuracy may be driving friction rather than value.
