### Event Tracking Priority Plan for LearnHub Pilot
**Audience:** CTO | **Context:** Aug 20 Soft Launch (50 Schools) | **Goal:** Validate value drivers for Series A without instrumentation bloat.

#### Non-Negotiable Day One Events (Instrument Immediately)
These three events answer the single most critical question for an edtech pilot: *Do users return to complete the core value loop?* Without these, you cannot distinguish between a marketing win and a product win.

1.  **`course_started`**
    *   **Question Answered:** Did the acquisition channel (school rollout) successfully drive the user to the first moment of value?
    *   **Properties:** `course_id`, `user_id` (pseudonymous), `school_id`, `device_type`.
    *   **Why:** This is your Activation baseline. If this is low, the problem is onboarding or school coordination, not content quality.

2.  **`lesson_completed`**
    *   **Question Answered:** Is the core product delivering value? (This validates your hypothesis that completion predicts conversion).
    *   **Properties:** `lesson_id`, `course_id`, `time_to_complete_seconds`, `attempt_number`.
    *   **Why:** This is your primary North Star input. "Time spent" is a vanity metric if the user is idle; "Completion" proves outcome. You cannot test your conversion hypothesis without this binary signal.

3.  **`user_returned_week_2`**
    *   **Question Answered:** Is there retention? (The bottom of the AARRR funnel).
    *   **Properties:** `user_id`, `days_since_first_start`, `course_id`.
    *   **Why:** Acquisition is wasted if users do not return. This event must be calculated via a cohort query on `course_started`, but explicitly defining it ensures the data pipeline supports retention analysis from Day 1. If this is zero, do not spend on acquisition.

#### Deferrable Events (Postpone until September)
These address secondary hypotheses or optimization. Instrumenting them now risks delaying the launch or cluttering the initial data set with noise before the baseline is established.

1.  **`video_pause` / `video_seek` / `video_watch_duration`**
    *   **Reason to Delay:** Your content team believes time spent indicates learning, but this is unverified. Tracking granular video events creates high-volume noise. First, prove that `lesson_completed` correlates with conversion. If completion rates are high but conversion is low, *then* instrument video engagement in September to diagnose *why* completion isn't driving value. Do not optimize the video player before proving the course sells.

2.  **`quiz_answer_selected`**
    *   **Reason to Delay:** While useful for adaptive learning later, the binary outcome (`quiz_passed` or `lesson_completed`) is sufficient for the pilot's business metrics. Detailed answer tracking is an engineering cost that does not impact the Series A narrative of "Do users finish and pay?" until the core funnel is proven stable.

#### Implementation Notes for Engineering
*   **Identifier Seam:** Ensure `user_id` is stable across sessions to track retention, but keep it pseudonymous. Route any PII (names, emails) strictly to your privacy compliance layer; do not log PII in analytics events.
*   **No Benchmarks:** Do not look for "industry standard" completion rates. Your only benchmark is the trend of these 50 schools over the first 4 weeks.
