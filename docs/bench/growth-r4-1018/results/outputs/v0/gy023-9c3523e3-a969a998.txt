**Subject:** Q3 Retention Paradox – Investigation Plan & Decision Framework

**Executive Summary**
The 40% return rate is a vanity metric in this context because it decouples from the North Star: **Weekly Completed Workouts**. A drop from 3.5 to 0.8 sessions indicates that while users are returning to the app, they are not receiving core value. Retention without the core action is unsustainable; it signals a "zombie cohort" that will churn once novelty fades or ad costs rise. Do not pivot the roadmap to social features yet. The data suggests a broken activation loop, not a successful product evolution.

**Behavioral Patterns to Investigate (Event Segmentation)**
You must instrument and segment the following three specific patterns to answer the "passive vs. forgotten" question. Do not track generic pageviews; track these named events:

1.  **The "Feed-Only" Loop:**
    *   **Event:** `session_start` → `social_feed_scroll` (≥3 times) → `session_end` WITHOUT `workout_start`.
    *   **Question Answered:** Are users deriving value from passive consumption?
    *   **Decision Rule:** If >60% of returning users fit this pattern, social features are cannibalizing core utility. **Action:** Insert a "Start Workout" prompt after 2 scrolls; do not pivot roadmap to social.

2.  **The "Friction" Drop-off:**
    *   **Event:** `workout_start_intent` (e.g., clicking "New Workout") → `error_exit` OR `session_timeout` → `no_workout_complete`.
    *   **Question Answered:** Are users forgetting how to start, or is the UI broken?
    *   **Decision Rule:** If high intent exists but completion fails, the issue is onboarding/usability, not interest. **Action:** Double down on onboarding improvements and simplify the start flow.

3.  **The "Ghost" Return:**
    *   **Event:** `app_open` → `idle_time` (>30s) → `session_end`.
    *   **Question Answered:** Are users opening the app out of habit but lacking direction?
    *   **Decision Rule:** Indicates a lack of clear Call-to-Action (CTA) on the home screen. **Action:** Redesign home screen CTA to prioritize the core action over social content.

**Recommendation for Roadmap Decision**
Do not treat the 40% return rate as success. It is a warning signal that the product is becoming a content viewer rather than a fitness tool.
*   **If Pattern 1 dominates:** Social features are a distraction. Deprioritize social roadmap items; force core action visibility.
*   **If Pattern 2 dominates:** The friction is technical or UX-based. Invest heavily in onboarding.
*   **Sustainability Verdict:** Retention without core action completion is **not sustainable**. Revenue (if subscription-based) or long-term engagement relies on the habit of working out, not scrolling.

**This Week's Steps**
1.  **Query:** Run a cohort analysis on January signups filtering for `session_start` + `social_feed_scroll` AND `NOT workout_complete`. Calculate the percentage of total returning users this represents.
2.  **Instrument:** Add a specific event property `entry_point` (e.g., "push_notification", "direct_open", "social_tab") to the next data dump to see what drives the empty returns.
3.  **Decision Gate:** If >50% of returning users complete zero workouts for two consecutive weeks, halt social feature development and trigger a usability audit of the "Start Workout" flow.

*Note: No industry benchmarks were used; all thresholds above are derived from the necessity of the core action driving business value. Ensure user IDs in these queries are pseudonymous per privacy compliance.*
