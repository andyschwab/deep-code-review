**To:** Priya, Chief Sustainability Officer
**From:** Product Lead, GreenRoute
**Subject:** Framework for Measuring GreenRoute Impact on Emissions and Behavior

To distinguish between friction and genuine behavioral change, we will replace vanity metrics (tab views) with a value-based measurement system focused on the delta between user choice and optimal sustainability.

**1. The North Star Metric: Verified Emissions Avoided**
We will track **Verified Emissions Avoided (VEA)** per active fleet per week.
*   *Definition:* The sum of CO2 saved when a user selects a route with lower emissions than the fastest available option.
*   *Formula:* `(Emissions_Fastest_Route - Emissions_Selected_Route)` for every trip where `Selected_Route != Fastest_Route`.
*   *Why:* This rises only when the user makes a greener choice. It ignores mere "views" and directly quantifies the environmental outcome required for the annual statement.

**2. The Diagnostic Funnel (Reading Bottom-Up)**
We will analyze the data in reverse order to isolate where value is lost:
*   **Retention (The Core Behavior):** Of the users who selected a greener route in Week 1, what percentage do so again in Week 4? If this drops, the feature creates friction without habit formation.
*   **Activation (The Decision):** When the sustainability tab is viewed, what is the **Green Selection Rate**? (Count of greener routes selected / Count of sustainability tab views). A low rate here confirms your hypothesis: users see the data but prioritize speed.
*   **Acquisition:** Only after fixing the above do we measure how many fleets are enabling the feature.

**3. Event Taxonomy: Answering Named Questions**
We will instrument only three specific events to answer your core questions, avoiding "track everything" noise:
*   `sustainability_tab_viewed`: Answers "Are users aware?" (Properties: `session_id`, `route_count_available`).
*   `route_comparison_rendered`: Answers "Did they see the trade-off?" (Properties: `delta_time_minutes`, `delta_co2_kg`).
*   `route_selected`: Answers "Did they act?" (Properties: `is_greenest_option` [boolean], `co2_kg`, `time_minutes`).
*   *Identifier Seam:* These events will link via a pseudonymous `fleet_id` to track behavior over time without exposing personal driver data. PII handling follows existing privacy compliance protocols.

**4. Quantifying the "Friction vs. Change" Hypothesis**
To address your uncertainty about the 10% increase in planning time, we will calculate the **Efficiency of Influence**:
*   *Metric:* `Average CO2 Saved per Minute of Extra Planning Time`.
*   *Interpretation:* If users spend 10 extra minutes but save 0kg of CO2, the feature is pure friction. If they spend 10 minutes and save 50kg, the friction is an investment in value.

**5. Current Status & Next Steps**
*   **Status:** The assumption of a "5% emission reduction" is currently **UNVERIFIED**. We cannot calculate this delta until the `route_selected` event captures the `co2_kg` of the *chosen* route versus the *fastest* option.
*   **This Week's Actions:**
    1.  **Instrument:** Add `co2_kg` and `is_greenest_option` properties to the `route_selected` event immediately.
    2.  **Query:** Run a cohort analysis on fleets active since Oct 1 to calculate the baseline "Green Selection Rate" (currently unknown).
    3.  **Decide:** If the Green Selection Rate is <5% despite high tab views, we will pause acquisition efforts and redesign the comparison UI to reduce the cognitive load of choosing the greener path.

This framework shifts the narrative from "users are looking at data" to "users are saving X tons of CO2," providing the concrete proof needed for the annual statement.

*Note: All benchmarks will be derived strictly from GreenRoute's internal baseline trends; no external industry retention rates will be cited.*
