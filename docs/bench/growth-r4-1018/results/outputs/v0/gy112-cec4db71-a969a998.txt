**Recommendation: Adopt "Verified Yield per Active Cycle" as the North Star Metric**

For GreenStack's Q3 roadmap, the primary KPI must be **Verified Yield per Active Cycle** (the weight of saleable harvest delivered against the projected yield for that specific crop type). Do not select System Uptime.

**The Decision Logic**
Your February churn data provides the definitive evidence: three major clients left despite 99.8% uptime because the *output* (harvest quality/quantity) failed them. Uptime is a binary operational state; it confirms the lights turned on, but not that the plants thrived. In vertical farming, a system can be "up" while delivering incorrect humidity, poor light spectra, or nutrient imbalances that destroy yield. Focusing on Uptime creates a vanity metric where engineering succeeds (green dashboards) while the business fails (churning customers).

**Trade-offs and Seasonal Context**
*   **Focusing on Yield (Recommended):** This aligns engineering and agronomy toward the only outcome that pays bills: saleable crops. It forces engineering to build sensors that detect *agronomic drift* (e.g., subtle temperature spikes affecting fruit set) rather than just "server online" status.
    *   *Seasonal Risk:* Yield naturally fluctuates by crop type and season. To prevent this from becoming noise, the metric must be **normalized against the expected baseline** for that specific crop and growth stage. A 10% drop in lettuce yield in winter might be normal; a 10% drop in tomatoes is a crisis. The metric answers: "Did we deliver the value promised for *this* cycle?"
*   **Focusing on Uptime (Rejected):** This optimizes for infrastructure stability, not customer success. It encourages the engineering team to prioritize server redundancy over sensor calibration accuracy. While high uptime is a necessary *guardrail* (a minimum requirement), it is insufficient as a North Star because it decouples effort from revenue. As your data shows, you can have perfect uptime and zero retention.

**Operational Efficiency Guardrail**
Adopting Yield as the North Star does not ignore efficiency. You must pair it with a **Guardrail Metric**: *Energy Cost per Kilogram of Yield*. This ensures the team does not chase maximum yield by cranking energy usage to unsustainable levels. If Yield goes up but Energy Cost/kg spikes, the win is invalid. This keeps the "vertical" in vertical farming economically viable.
