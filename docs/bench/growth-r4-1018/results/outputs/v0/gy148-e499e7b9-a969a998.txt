**Recommendation: North Star Metric for GreenLedger**

**Selected North Star:** **Quarterly Active Enterprises Completing a Verified Offset Purchase.**

This metric counts the number of unique corporate clients who successfully purchase at least one carbon offset project verified for permanence and quality within a rolling 90-day window. It replaces "Total Tons" as the primary goal.

**Evaluation of Candidate Metrics**

| Candidate Metric | Pros | Cons & Behavioral Risks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Tons Offset** (Current) | Simple to calculate; scales linearly with revenue. | **Vanity Metric:** Incentivizes sales to dump low-quality, cheap volume to hit targets. Engineering may optimize for bulk upload speed over verification rigor. Ignores client satisfaction. |
| **Client Retention Rate** | Strong proxy for long-term value and product stickiness. | **Lagging Indicator:** With 14-month contracts, you cannot detect product failures until clients have already churned. Too slow for quarterly engineering sprints or Series B narrative momentum. |
| **Diversity of Projects** | Encourages a robust marketplace supply side. | **Misaligned Incentive:** Sales may push obscure projects to check boxes rather than solving client needs. Does not measure if clients actually *value* the diversity or if it drives revenue. |
| **Repeat Purchase Rate** | Measures ongoing engagement better than one-off sales. | **Frequency Mismatch:** Enterprise procurement cycles are often annual or biannual. A quarterly "repeat" target may be structurally impossible, demoralizing teams when the metric dips due to contract timing, not product failure. |
| **Quarterly Active Enterprises (Recommended)** | **Leading & Actionable:** Detects friction in the buying flow immediately. Aligns Sales (acquire/expand) and Engineering (reduce friction, ensure verification speed). Directly correlates to recurring revenue without the lag of annual renewal. | Requires robust identity tracking to distinguish unique enterprises across multiple buyers within the same org. |

**Impact on Team Behavior**

*   **Engineering:** Instead of optimizing for raw throughput (tons uploaded), teams will focus on reducing time-to-verification and ensuring the purchase flow is frictionless for *new* and *returning* enterprises. If the metric drops, they investigate onboarding bugs or verification delays, not just server load.
*   **Sales:** Shifts focus from "landing one huge low-quality deal" to "activating and re-activating enterprise accounts." Sales will prioritize clients who see value in high-quality offsets, as only *verified* purchases count toward the goal.
*   **Product:** Prioritizes features that help enterprises track their portfolio quality, as this drives the "verified purchase" action.

**Implementation Note:**
Define "Verified" strictly in your event taxonomy (e.g., `purchase_completed` + `verification_status=gold`). Do not count pending or low-tier offsets toward this North Star.

**This Week's Step:**
1.  Instrument the `enterprise_verified_purchase` event, tagging it with client ID and verification tier, to establish a baseline for Q2.
2.  Run a cohort query on Q1 data: "What % of Q1 buyers made a second verified purchase within 90 days?" to validate if this metric predicts your 14-month retention better than total volume.
