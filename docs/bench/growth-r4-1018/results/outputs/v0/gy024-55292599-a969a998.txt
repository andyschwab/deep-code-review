**Subject:** Leading Indicators for Developer Experience ROI (Nexus APIs)

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Integration Teams (WAIT) completing a **Value-Verified Transaction**.
*Definition:* A team is "active" only when they successfully execute a non-trivial API call (e.g., write operation or complex query, not just `GET /health`) in a production-like environment within a 7-day window. This rejects vanity metrics like total pageviews or raw signups, focusing strictly on realized value.

**Leading Indicators & Event Taxonomy**
To measure adoption depth and justify the $200k investment, instrument only these events that answer specific questions:

1.  **Event:** `integration_guide_completed`
    *   **Question:** Does reading the 'Quick Start' correlate with faster time-to-first-successful-call?
    *   **Properties:** `guide_version`, `time_to_first_200_ok` (calculated), `endpoint_complexity_score`.
    *   **Action:** If correlation is low, the guide is not the bottleneck; investigate SDKs or auth flow.

2.  **Event:** `client_error_pattern_detected` (The "Silent Failure" Proxy)
    *   **Question:** Are developers hitting logical walls (4xx) that look like success (200 OK) to our uptime monitors?
    *   **Properties:** `error_code` (e.g., 400, 401, 403, 429), `endpoint_category`, `is_retry_followed_by_success`.
    *   **Action:** A spike in 400s followed by *no* subsequent success within 24 hours indicates a "silent failure" where the developer gave up. This is your true churn signal.

3.  **Event:** `production_promotion_signal`
    *   **Question:** Is the integration moving from sandbox to live?
    *   **Properties:** `api_key_environment_change` (sandbox → production), `traffic_volume_shift`.
    *   **Action:** This is the ultimate activation metric. If docs improved but promotion signals didn't rise, the friction lies in production onboarding, not documentation.

**Measurement Strategy: Retention First**
Do not pour more traffic into the portal until we verify retention. Analyze the cohort of teams who onboarded post-February 10:
*   **Week 1 Retention:** Did they make a second Value-Verified Transaction 7 days later?
*   **Week 4 Retention:** Are they still integrating?
If retention is flat despite the 15% drop in support tickets, the docs may be solving easy questions while failing to enable deep adoption. The 15% ticket reduction is a lagging efficiency gain; retention is the leading revenue indicator.
