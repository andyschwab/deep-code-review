# Fjord Green Beta Success Metrics: Oslo & Bergen Launch

**1. North Star Metric: Weekly Active Households Completing the "Core Value Loop"**
*   **Definition:** The count of unique households per week that log at least three distinct sustainable actions *and* view their resulting carbon impact summary.
*   **Why:** This rejects vanity metrics (total downloads or raw log counts) by requiring both the behavioral input (logging) and the value realization (seeing the impact). It directly tests your hypothesis that engagement drives retention without conflating frequency with actual environmental outcome. In the Norwegian context, where "dugnad" (community effort) and tangible results are valued, this measures active participation rather than passive sign-ups.

**2. Primary Input Metric: Action-to-Impact Conversion Rate**
*   **Definition:** The percentage of logged actions that result in a user viewing the calculated carbon reduction screen within the same session.
*   **Why:** This validates whether the backend's estimated carbon reduction is successfully communicating value. If users log actions but never view the impact, the "environmental impact" angle is failing to resonate, regardless of logging frequency. This distinguishes between mere data entry and genuine climate awareness.

**3. Retention Cohort: Week-4 Repeat Action Rate**
*   **Definition:** The percentage of users from a specific acquisition week who return in Week 4 to log at least one new action.
*   **Why:** Per the AARRR framework, retention must be diagnosed before acquisition. Measuring return behavior specifically in Week 4 (rather than Day 1 or Day 30) aligns with your hypothesis of building a weekly habit. This metric isolates whether the product creates a lasting routine, which is the leading indicator for your six-month retention goal.

**4. Impact Integrity Metric: Verified vs. Estimated Reduction Ratio**
*   **Definition:** The ratio of carbon reduction derived from high-confidence user inputs (e.g., specific transit tickets, energy bill uploads) versus generic estimated inputs.
*   **Why:** For an investment committee, the credibility of the "environmental impact" claim is paramount. This metric ensures the reported tonnage of CO2 saved is grounded in verified user behavior rather than loose estimations, protecting the brand's integrity in a market skeptical of greenwashing.

**5. Guardrail Metric: Churn by "Zero-Impact" Users**
*   **Definition:** The churn rate of users who have logged actions but have accumulated less than a defined threshold of total carbon reduction (e.g., <10kg CO2) after 30 days.
*   **Why:** This identifies if the "three actions" hypothesis is flawed because the actions chosen are too trivial to motivate users. If users churn despite logging frequently but seeing low impact, the issue is the perceived magnitude of their contribution, not the frequency of logging.

### Implementation Notes for Investor Presentation
*   **Baseline Dependency:** All targets for June 1 must be framed as comparisons against your own Week 1 baseline; do not assert external industry benchmarks for "good" retention as they are unavailable and often misleading.
*   **Data Privacy:** Ensure the unique household identifier used for these cohorts is pseudonymous; route any personally identifiable information (PII) handling to your privacy compliance protocol, not this metrics list.
