**Decision Framework: Mandatory Account Creation Reversion**

**1. Diagnostic Read (Existing Data Only)**
Isolate the cohort of users who entered the checkout flow between Feb 10 and present. Segment this data by device type and traffic source to confirm the drop-off spike is universal and not isolated to a specific broken integration.
*   **Action:** Query session recordings specifically for the transition from "Shipping Info" to "Account Creation."
*   **Reading Rule:** If >40% of sessions show "rage clicks," rapid back-button usage, or immediate tab closure upon seeing the registration form, the friction is confirmed as the primary cause of the 13% abandonment increase. Do not attribute this to price sensitivity or shipping costs without this visual evidence.
*   **Fraud Proxy:** Since we cannot run a live test, analyze the *quality* of accounts created since Feb 10. If the conversion rate to "Second Purchase" for these new accounts is significantly lower than the historical average for guest-turned-registered users, the forced step is attracting low-intent or bot traffic rather than loyal customers.

**2. The Trade-off Evaluation (No New Benchmarks)**
We will not invent a "safe" fraud rate. Instead, we compare the current state against your own pre-Feb 10 baseline.
*   **Revenue Impact:** Calculate the recovered revenue if abandonment returns to 65%. Apply your average order value (AOV) to the difference in completed checkouts.
*   **Data/Fraud Impact:** Estimate the loss of email capture by assuming a conservative guest-to-email capture rate (e.g., via post-purchase email receipt request) versus the current 100% capture attempt.
*   **The Call:** If the recovered immediate revenue (calculated above) exceeds the projected lifetime value of the *incremental* emails lost (based on your historical retention curves), the mandatory step is a net negative. Regarding fraud: if the "Account Creation" page sees a high volume of completions using disposable email domains or suspicious IP patterns in your logs, the step is failing its security purpose while killing sales.

**3. Recommended Action: Staged Reversion with Guardrails**
Do not revert globally immediately if VIP alienation is a concern. Use a "silent" rollout strategy that functions as a quasi-experiment without public announcement.
*   **Step A (Internal Canary):** Revert the mandatory step for 5% of traffic, specifically excluding known VIP segments initially. Monitor fraud flags on these transactions manually for 48 hours.
*   **Step B (The "Soft" Ask):** Replace the hard wall with a pre-checked optional box: "Create an account to track your order?" placed *after* the payment success message or as an optional step before payment.
*   **Guardrail Metric:** Define a "Fraud Tripwire." If the chargeback rate or flagged transaction volume on the reverted cohort exceeds the pre-Feb 10 baseline by >0.5%, halt the reversion immediately.
*   **Primary Metric:** Checkout completion rate (target: return to ≤65% abandonment).
