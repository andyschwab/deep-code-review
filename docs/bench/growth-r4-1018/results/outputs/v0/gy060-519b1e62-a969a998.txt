### GreenChain MVP Metrics Dashboard Specification (Launch: Sept 1)

**1. North Star Metric: Verified Carbon Reduction Rate**
*   **Definition:** The percentage of total estimated supply chain emissions that are successfully verified and reduced, rather than raw "tons saved."
*   **Formula:** `(Verified Tons CO2 Reduced / Total Estimated Baseline Tons) × 100`
*   **Rationale:** Raw "tons saved" is a vanity metric that scales with client size and fails to normalize across verticals (manufacturing vs. retail). A *rate* normalizes performance by company scale. Crucially, it penalizes data gaps: unreported supplier inputs cannot be counted as "verified," preventing the skewing of totals by incomplete data. This forces the behavior of improving data completeness to improve the score.

**2. Input Metrics (The Drivers)**
*   **Data Completeness Ratio:** `(Suppliers with Verified Reports / Total Active Suppliers)`.
    *   *Action:* If this drops, the North Star is unreliable. Trigger: Alert account managers to engage specific non-reporting suppliers.
*   **Baseline Accuracy Score:** A confidence interval (High/Medium/Low) assigned to the client's initial baseline estimate based on the granularity of provided data.
    *   *Action:* Clients with "Low" accuracy must prioritize baseline refinement before claiming reduction wins.

**3. Handling Data Gaps & Normalization**
*   **Normalization Strategy:** Do not compare absolute tons between a manufacturer and a retailer. Compare their **Reduction Rate** against their own **Prior Month Baseline**. The dashboard will display trend lines per client, not a unified leaderboard of raw tons.
*   **The "Unverified" Bucket:** Explicitly visualize "Unreported/Estimated Emissions" as a distinct, non-reducible category in the stack.
    *   *Rule:* Any emission source without a valid, timestamped supplier input is excluded from the "Reduced" numerator but remains in the "Baseline" denominator. This ensures that missing data mathematically lowers the success rate, making data hygiene a core value driver.

**4. MVP Event Taxonomy (Instrumentation)**
*   `baseline_submitted`: Records the initial carbon estimate per supplier. *Question answered: Do we have a starting point?*
*   `report_uploaded`: Records a new monthly input from a supplier. *Question answered: Is data flowing?*
*   `verification_passed`: Records a successful validation of a report against logic checks. *Question answered: Is the data trustworthy?*
*   `reduction_calculated`: Records the delta between baseline and verified report. *Question answered: Did we actually save carbon?*

**5. This-Week Steps**
1.  **Define the Baseline Logic:** Finalize the algorithm that assigns a "Low/Medium/High" accuracy score to baselines based on data granularity; document this for the steering committee.
2.  **Build the "Unverified" Visual:** Mock up the dashboard chart showing "Unreported Emissions" as a visible drag on the success rate to demonstrate to pilots why their supplier compliance matters.
3.  **Draft the Data Contract:** Create the one-page specification for pilot partners detailing exactly what fields are required for a `verification_passed` event, so they know why their data might be excluded.

**Note:** No industry benchmarks for "good" reduction rates are asserted here; success is defined strictly by each pilot's improvement against their own baseline. Raw user IDs for supplier tracking must be pseudonymized before storage; route PII handling to your privacy compliance protocol.
