### Executive Summary: Motorcycle Taxi Viability Assessment

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Riders completing a rain-or-shine trip (completed trips by unique users per week).
*Reject cumulative signups or raw pageviews; they do not prove value delivery.*

**Priority Metrics for Board Decision**
To determine viability for a city-wide launch, analyze these three inputs in order:
1.  **Retention of Core Action:** The percentage of users from the March 12–26 window who booked a *second* motorcycle trip within 14 days. If this is low, acquisition is wasted regardless of trip volume.
2.  **Rain-Adjusted Completion Rate:** Compare completion rates during rainy afternoons (18% cancellation observed) against dry afternoons in the same zones.
3.  **Unit Economics per Completed Trip:** Calculate margin after factoring in cancellation costs (driver time/fuel) specifically for the motorcycle segment.

**Data Segmentation Strategy**
To isolate weather impacts from driver availability, segment the existing dataset as follows:
*   **Segment A (Weather Control):** Rainy afternoons (12:00–18:00) vs. Dry afternoons (12:00–18:00) within Ikeja and Yaba.
    *   *Hypothesis:* If cancellations spike only in rain, it is a safety/weather friction issue.
*   **Segment B (Supply Control):** Rainy afternoons with High Driver Density vs. Rainy afternoons with Low Driver Density.
    *   *Hypothesis:* If cancellations remain high even when drivers are nearby, the issue is rider hesitation (safety), not supply scarcity.
*   **Segment C (Time Control):** Rush hour (07:00–09:00, 17:00–19:00) vs. Off-peak.
    *   *Hypothesis:* Validates if traffic congestion is the primary adoption driver as assumed.

**Decision Logic for Ravi (Operations)**
*   **Hire More Drivers IF:** Cancellations in Segment B drop significantly when driver density is high (indicating a supply gap).
*   **Pause Expansion IF:** Cancellations remain >15% in Segment A (Rainy) regardless of driver density, indicating a fundamental safety/product-market fit barrier in Lagos weather conditions.

**Experiment Design for City-Wide Launch**
Do not launch city-wide immediately. Run a controlled A/B test in two new districts:
*   **Primary Metric:** Completion Rate.
*   **Guardrail Metric:** Safety Incident Reports (must remain flat).
*   **Rule:** Pre-commit to a sample size of 1,000 requested trips per district. Do not peek at results daily; read once at completion to avoid false positives.
