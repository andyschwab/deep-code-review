**Subject:** Operational Efficiency Measurement Framework (Beta Phase)

**North Star Metric: Confirmed Deliveries Completed**
Reject "registered volunteers," "pageviews," or "messages sent" as primary success indicators. These are vanity metrics in a crisis; they rise regardless of whether aid reaches victims. Your single North Star is the count of **Confirmed Deliveries** (a donor-recipient match resulting in a verified handover). This metric rises only when real value (survival support) is delivered.

**Input Metrics (The Drivers)**
To move the North Star, track only these three inputs that directly influence completion:
1.  **Match Acceptance Rate:** Percentage of proposed matches accepted by recipients within 2 hours.
2.  **Sync Success Rate:** Percentage of critical events (match accepted, delivery confirmed) successfully synced from rural zones within 24 hours.
3.  **Time-to-Confirmation:** Median time from "match proposed" to "delivery photo uploaded."

**Event Taxonomy: Instrument Only What Answers a Question**
Do not instrument "everything." Given sporadic connectivity, every byte counts. Implement only these events, each tied to a specific operational question:

| Event Name | Question Answered | Critical Properties |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `delivery_confirmed` | Did the aid actually arrive? | `delivery_id`, `timestamp_local`, `photo_uploaded` (bool), `sync_status` (immediate/queued) |
| `match_accepted` | Is the matching logic working for users? | `match_id`, `time_since_proposal`, `user_role` (donor/recipient) |
| `sync_retry_triggered` | Is connectivity blocking our data? | `event_type_failed`, `retry_count`, `network_state` (wifi/cell/offline) |

**Reading the Funnel: Retention of Action First**
Do not focus on acquiring more volunteers until the current 1,200 are effectively coordinating. Read your funnel bottom-up:
1.  **Verification:** Of the matches made, how many result in `delivery_confirmed`? If this is low, faster matching is irrelevant; the process is breaking at the handover.
2.  **Activation:** Of the registered volunteers, how many complete one `delivery_confirmed`?
3.  **Acquisition:** Only after the above are stable should you measure new signups.

**Connectivity & Data Integrity**
You cannot assume data completeness. The `sync_retry_triggered` event is your guardrail. If the ratio of queued vs. immediate syncs exceeds 20% in a region, your "Time-to-Confirmation" metric is `UNVERIFIED` for that zone. Do not fabricate a benchmark for "good" sync rates; compare today's rate against tomorrow's to detect degradation as the network load increases.
