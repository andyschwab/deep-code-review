### Success Metrics Plan: StreamMax Mexico Launch

**North Star Metric: Weekly Active Viewers Completing Episode 2+**
Reject "Total Viewing Hours" as the primary indicator; it is a vanity metric that rises with passive background play or single-episode sampling without proving value delivery. Instead, track the count of unique users who watch beyond the first episode within 7 days of sign-up or content release. This measures actual content resonance and the "aha" moment where a user commits to the series. Pair this with **Input Metrics**: New Sign-ups (Acquisition), Episode 1 Start Rate (Activation), and Episode 2 Completion Rate (Retention).

**Funnel Diagnosis (Bottom-Up Approach)**
Do not prioritize acquisition volume until retention is verified. Diagnose in this order:
1.  **Retention:** Of the users who started Episode 1, what percentage returned to finish Episode 2 within 48 hours? If this leaks, acquisition spend is wasted.
2.  **Activation:** What percentage of new sign-ups actually start Episode 1?
3.  **Acquisition:** Total sign-ups (your 200k target).

**Event Taxonomy & Instrumentation**
Instrument only events that answer specific strategic questions for the board. Do not track "everything."

| Event Name | Question Answered | Properties Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `content_play_started` | Did the user attempt the core action? | `content_id`, `episode_number`, `user_type` (new vs. existing), `device_type` |
| `content_play_completed` | Did the user consume the full unit? | `content_id`, `episode_number`, `completion_percentage` |
| `session_ended` | Did the user leave after one episode? | `session_duration`, `episodes_watched_in_session` |
| `share_action_clicked` | Is there observable word-of-mouth intent? | `content_id`, `share_channel` (WhatsApp, SMS, etc.) |
| `search_performed` | Are users actively seeking more local content? | `search_query`, `result_clicked` |

**Addressing Specific Strategic Questions**
*   **Cultural Resonance / Word-of-Mouth:** Do not guess. Measure the `share_action_clicked` event (specifically tracking shares to local channels like WhatsApp) and `search_performed` events for related local titles. High share rates relative to views indicate resonance; high search volume indicates active interest.
*   **New vs. Existing Retention:** The `user_type` property on every event is mandatory. Segment your retention curve by "New Subscribers (Jan)" vs. "Existing Subscribers." If existing subscribers show a lift in weekly active days during the launch window compared to their prior 4-week baseline, the content is driving retention. If only new users engage, it is purely an acquisition driver.

**Measurement Stage & Governance**
Since this is an **MVP/Launch** phase, focus strictly on Activation (Ep 1 start) and Retention (Ep 2 completion). Ignore referral revenue or deep funnel monetization for now. Ensure the `user_id` used for cohorting is stable across devices but pseudonymized; route any PII handling to your privacy compliance protocol.

**This Week's Steps**
1.  **Define the Query:** Build the dashboard widget for "Episode 2 Completion Rate" segmented by `user_type` (New vs. Existing) before Jan 1.
2.  **Instrument Shares:** Verify the `share_action_clicked` event is firing with the correct `share_channel` property to capture local word-of-mouth behavior.
3.  **Set the Baseline:** Export the last 4 weeks of existing subscriber activity to establish the pre-launch retention baseline; without this, you cannot prove lift.

*Note: All benchmark comparisons must be made against StreamMax's own historical baselines, not external industry averages. Any metric without the defined instrumentation in place by launch day is UNVERIFIED.*
