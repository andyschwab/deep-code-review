**Board Briefing: Growth Lever Recommendation for Q2**

**Executive Summary**
Do not double down on marketing. Prioritize optimizing the onboarding flow to increase alert threshold configurations. For KoiStream's current stage, **feature adoption (configuration rate)** is the only valid predictor of long-term subscription renewal; **session volume** is a vanity metric that currently masks a critical retention leak.

**The Diagnosis: A Leaky Bucket**
Your data reveals a classic funnel failure. You have 3,200 sessions but only 180 configured farms, resulting in a **5.6% activation rate**.
*   **Session Volume (Vanity):** High sessions indicate curiosity or confusion, not value. In B2B aquaculture, a farmer visiting the tool repeatedly without setting alerts derives zero predictive value. If they do not receive an alert, they cannot act, and if they cannot act, they will not renew.
*   **Configuration (Value):** The "North Star" for this module is not traffic; it is **farms with active alerts**. This is the moment the user receives value (the "aha" moment). Until a farm crosses this threshold, acquisition spend is wasted.

**The Recommendation: Fix Retention Before Acquisition**
Pouring marketing budget into the top of this funnel now would be financially irresponsible. You would be paying to drive more users into a broken experience where 94.4% fail to activate.
1.  **Immediate Action:** Halt aggressive top-of-funnel marketing for this module. Reallocate those resources to product design and customer success.
2.  **Primary Metric:** Shift the board's focus from "Total Sessions" to **"Weekly Active Farms with Configured Alerts."** This is your leading indicator of revenue stability.
3.  **Hypothesis Testing:** Your assumption that setup is "too complex" must be validated immediately. Treat the 5.6% conversion as `UNVERIFIED` regarding *cause* until you observe user behavior.

**This Week's Steps**
1.  **Instrument the Drop-off:** Define and track one specific event: `alert_setup_abandoned`. Record exactly which step in the configuration wizard users exit. This answers the question: "Is the friction technical or conceptual?"
2.  **Cohort Analysis:** Segment the 180 configured farms vs. the non-configured. Compare their Day-7 and Day-30 retention rates. If the configured cohort shows even marginally higher retention, you have empirical proof that configuration drives renewal.
3.  **Qualitative Loop:** Have your customer success team call 5 farms that visited but did not configure. Ask one question: "What stopped you from turning on the alert?" Do not guess; get the direct answer.

**Financial Impact**
Revenue stability depends on farmers trusting your predictions. Trust is built only when the system is active. Improving the configuration rate from 5.6% to 15% will yield more recurring revenue than doubling your session count while keeping conversion broken. Fix the bucket before turning up the hose.
