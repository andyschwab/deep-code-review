**Referral Quality Analysis & Decision Framework for PayPeer**

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Transactors (users completing ≥1 successful P2P transfer in the last 7 days).
*Rejects vanity metrics like "total signups" or "bonus claims." This rises only when real value (money movement) occurs.*

**Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only These)**
Every event below answers a specific question to validate the $50k budget decision:
1.  `referral_invite_sent` (Properties: channel, region) → *Question: Is the friction in sharing or accepting?*
2.  `referral_link_clicked` → *Question: Are invites reaching humans or bots?*
3.  `referee_signup_complete` → *Question: Is the signup flow blocking conversion?*
4.  `first_successful_transfer` (Properties: sender_id, receiver_id, amount, time_since_signup) → *Question: Is the "30% higher LTV" assumption true, or are these dormant bonus hunters?*
5.  `bonus_payout_triggered` → *Question: Are we paying out before value is delivered?*

**Diagnostic Funnel (Read Bottom-Up)**
Do not analyze the 25,000 new users (Acquisition) yet. Start with Retention and Value:
1.  **Retention/Value Check:** Of the 25,000 referred signups, what % completed a `first_successful_transfer` within 14 days?
    *   *If <20%:* The referral channel is generating dormant accounts. Scaling the budget to $50k will burn cash on non-transactors.
    *   *If >40%:* The channel is high-quality; proceed to friction analysis.
2.  **Activation Check:** For those who signed up but didn't transfer, where did they drop? (Click → Signup → Transfer).
3.  **Acquisition Check:** Only after validating value, analyze the cost per *transacting* user (not cost per signup).

**Verification of Assumptions**
*   **LTV Assumption:** The claim that referred users have "30% higher LTV" is currently **UNVERIFIED**. You cannot calculate this without linking the `referrer_id` to the `receiver_id` in the transaction logs and comparing their 30-day transfer volume against ad-acquired cohorts.
*   **Dormant Account Risk:** If the time lag between `referee_signup_complete` and `first_successful_transfer` exceeds 7 days for >50% of users, the current bonus structure likely rewards signups, not usage.

**Experiment Design for Budget Scaling**
Before committing the full $50,000, run a controlled test:
*   **Primary Metric:** Cost Per First Successful Transfer (CPFST).
*   **Guardrail Metric:** Fraud rate (accounts with signup but zero inbound/outbound flow after bonus).
*   **Design:** Do not peek at results daily. Pre-commit to a 14-day readout or use a sequential testing bound. Compare a "Bonus on Signup" cohort vs. a "Bonus on First Transfer" cohort.
*   **Decision Rule:** If the "Bonus on First Transfer" cohort shows a <10% drop in signup volume but a >20% increase in CPFST efficiency, shift the entire $50k budget to this model.
