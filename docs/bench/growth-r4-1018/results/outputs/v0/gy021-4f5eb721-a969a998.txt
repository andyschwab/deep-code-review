**From:** Product Team, Lumina Retail
**Subject:** AR Pilot Event Taxonomy – Westfield Mall (Launch: Nov 15)

Instrument the following five events to validate the hypothesis that AR visualization drives a 20% revenue lift. Each event answers a specific question regarding the user journey from activation to purchase. Do not instrument duration or raw interaction counts unless they map to these defined outcomes.

1.  **`ar_session_started`**
    *   **Question Answered:** What is our true acquisition/activation rate at the kiosk?
    *   **Properties:** `kiosk_id`, `timestamp`, `anonymous_user_token` (stable pseudonymous ID for cohorting), `mall_zone`.
    *   **Why:** Establishes the denominator for all conversion rates. Distinguishes between passersby and engaged users.

2.  **`item_placed_in_scene`**
    *   **Question Answered:** Did the user reach the "aha" moment of visualizing a product in their space?
    *   **Properties:** `item_sku`, `category`, `session_id`, `placement_success_boolean`.
    *   **Why:** This is the core value action. If users start sessions but never place an item, the friction is in the onboarding, not the purchase intent.

3.  **`ar_session_completed`**
    *   **Question Answered:** What is our mid-session abandonment rate?
    *   **Properties:** `session_id`, `items_placed_count`, `duration_seconds`, `exit_trigger` (e.g., "timeout", "manual_stop", "error").
    *   **Why:** Directly addresses the unknown abandonment metric. A high drop-off between `item_placed` and this event indicates the experience fails to sustain engagement long enough to drive intent.

4.  **`qr_code_scanned`**
    *   **Question Answered:** Did the user intend to take the experience home or share it (high-intent signal)?
    *   **Properties:** `session_id`, `items_in_scan`, `scan_location` (kiosk vs. mobile handoff).
    *   **Why:** This is the strongest leading indicator of purchase intent before the actual transaction. It bridges the in-mall experience to the post-visit funnel.

5.  **`purchase_attributed_to_ar`**
    *   **Question Answered:** Did the AR interaction result in incremental revenue?
    *   **Properties:** `order_id`, `total_value`, `items_purchased` (list of SKUs), `time_to_purchase` (delta from `ar_session_started`), `channel` (in-kiosk vs. later online via QR).
    *   **Why:** The primary outcome metric. Allows calculation of Average Order Value (AOV) for AR users vs. baseline mall shoppers to test the 20% lift hypothesis.

**Implementation Notes:**
*   **Identifier Seam:** Use a stable `anonymous_user_token` generated at `ar_session_started` to link these events across sessions without storing PII. Route any raw camera data or personal identifiers to the privacy compliance protocol; do not log them here.
*   **Vanity Check:** Do not log "seconds spent looking at menu" or "button hovers." Only log events that mark a state change in the funnel.
