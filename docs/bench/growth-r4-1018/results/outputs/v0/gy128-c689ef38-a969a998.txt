# BuzzHub Infinite Scroll: Engagement vs. Cost Trade-off Analysis

**Executive Summary**
The 50% spike in Daily Active Users (DAU) and time spent is a **vanity metric** in isolation because it lacks a verified link to revenue or retention. The 200% infrastructure cost increase indicates a broken unit economics model where marginal cost exceeds marginal revenue. Do not optimize, limit, or revert based on current data; the financial impact is currently **UNVERIFIED** because ad revenue correlation has not been measured.

**1. The North Star & Input Metrics**
*   **North Star:** *Revenue Per Active User (RPAU)* adjusted for infrastructure cost. Engagement (time spent) is only valuable if it converts to ad impressions that pay for the server load.
*   **Input Metrics to Instrument Immediately:**
    *   **Ad Fill Rate & CPM per Session:** Does infinite scroll degrade ad quality or fill rates?
    *   **Cost Per Session:** Calculate infrastructure cost divided by total sessions.
    *   **Retention Cohort (Day 1, Day 7):** Is the "spike" new users bouncing quickly due to performance lag, or existing users sticking?

**2. Diagnostic Funnel (Bottom-Up)**
Ignore the acquisition spike until retention is proven.
*   **Retention Check:** Compare Week 1 retention of users exposed to infinite scroll vs. the pre-launch baseline. If retention drops, the feature creates "junk traffic" that inflates costs without long-term value.
*   **Monetization Check:** Plot *Ad Revenue* vs. *Server Cost* hourly. If the slope of cost is steeper than revenue, every additional user deepens the loss.

**3. Event Taxonomy (Answer Named Questions Only)**
Instrument only these three events to answer specific questions:
*   `scroll_depth_reached` (Question: At what depth do ad impressions drop or latency spike?)
*   `ad_impression_served` + `ad_revenue_recorded` (Question: Does revenue scale linearly with scroll depth?)
*   `session_infrastructure_cost` (Derived: Question: What is the exact cost delta per minute of infinite scroll?)

**4. Experiment Rigor & Decision Framework**
Do not treat last week's data as a finalized win. It is an uncontrolled observation with confounding variables (novelty effect).
*   **Action:** Run an A/B test immediately with a **pre-committed sample size** based on detecting a 5% change in *profit margin*, not engagement.
*   **Guardrail Metric:** *Infrastructure Cost per User*. If this rises >10% without a matching rise in RPAU, the test fails automatically.
*   **Peeking Warning:** Do not stop the test early if costs look high on Day 2. Read the result only at the pre-defined end date to avoid false positives from daily variance.

**5. This-Week Steps**
1.  **Query Data:** Calculate the *Cost Per Active User* for last week vs. the prior month baseline. If the ratio has worsened by >50%, flag as critical.
2.  **Instrument Gap:** Verify if `ad_revenue` is tagged per session. If not, mark revenue correlation as **UNVERIFIED** and deploy tracking before making rollout decisions.
3.  **Decision Trigger:** If *Marginal Revenue* < *Marginal Infrastructure Cost* for two consecutive days in the A/B test, **revert** the feature to the previous pagination model immediately to protect the budget.

**Note:** Do not cite industry benchmarks for "good" retention or ad rates; use only BuzzHub's pre-launch baseline. Ensure user IDs used for cohort analysis are pseudonymous and routed to privacy compliance protocols.
