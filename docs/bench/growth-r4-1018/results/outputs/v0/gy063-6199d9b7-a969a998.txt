### Recommended Metric Framework for PeoplePulse

**Primary North Star: "Weighted Core Value Units" (WCVU)**
Replace "Monthly Active Users" with a metric that measures value delivery rather than presence. Define one WCVU as a specific, high-intent action that correlates with retention (e.g., "a completed feedback cycle with >3 participants" or "a manager reviewing submitted feedback").
*   **Seasonality Normalization:** Do not compare September to December directly. Report WCVU as a **percentage of the prior year's same-month baseline** (Year-Over-Year growth) or as a **trailing 3-month average** smoothed against the known seasonal curve. This demonstrates growth in *value density* even when raw volume drops in December.
*   **Rationale:** This shifts the narrative from "users are leaving in December" to "we are delivering X% more core value per client than last December," stabilizing the growth story for the board.

**Guardrail Metric 1: "Off-Peak Engagement Depth Ratio"**
To address the concern about penalizing the product team during low-volume months, track the ratio of "Deep Actions" (e.g., writing feedback, setting goals) to "Shallow Actions" (e.g., logins, viewing dashboards) specifically during off-peak months (Nov–Feb).
*   **Decision Rule:** If total volume drops but this ratio holds steady or increases, the product health is stable; the drop is purely seasonal. If the ratio drops, it signals a genuine retention risk requiring intervention. This protects the team from being judged on natural volume fluctuations while still monitoring genuine disengagement.

**Guardrail Metric 2: "Contract Renewal Leading Indicator"**
Since you lack historical correlation between "feedback depth" and renewals, you must instrument this immediately. Define a binary event: "Account hits minimum viable depth threshold" (e.g., 80% of seats submitted feedback) within the 60 days preceding renewal.
*   **Status:** Currently `UNVERIFIED`. You cannot weight this metric until you correlate it with actual renewal data.
*   **Action:** Tag all renewals in your CRM with the "depth status" of the account 30/60/90 days prior. In Q1, report the *count* of accounts hitting this threshold, not a weighted score. Once you have one full cycle of data, calculate the conversion rate of "High Depth" vs. "Low Depth" accounts to establish the weighting factor.
