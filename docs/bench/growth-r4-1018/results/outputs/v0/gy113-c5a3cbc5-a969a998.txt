### NairaWallet Measurement Framework: June 1 – July 5

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Users completing a **First Successful Transfer**.
*Rationale:* Signups and profile views are vanity; value is only delivered when money moves. This metric aligns directly with unit economics and long-term retention.

**Prioritized Event Taxonomy (Top 5)**
These five events answer the single critical question: *"Do users who start onboarding successfully complete a transfer within 3 days, and where do they drop off?"*

1.  `signup_started` (Properties: `channel`, `device_type`, `network_type`)
    *   **Question:** Are we attracting the right traffic, and does network condition correlate with early drop-off?
2.  `verification_submitted` (Properties: `doc_type`, `submission_latency_ms`)
    *   **Question:** Is the verification step a bottleneck? (Prioritized over "completion" to measure intent and friction before the binary pass/fail).
3.  `deposit_initiated` (Properties: `amount_currency`, `payment_method`)
    *   **Question:** Do users fund accounts after verification? This is a stronger leading indicator of retention than verification alone because it signals financial commitment.
4.  `transfer_completed` (Properties: `time_since_signup_days`, `recipient_type`)
    *   **Question:** Did the user achieve the "Aha!" moment (core value) within the 3-day window? This is the primary success event.
5.  `onboarding_error` (Properties: `step`, `error_code`, `network_status`)
    *   **Question:** Given volatile networks, are failures due to product bugs or connectivity? This distinguishes fixable product leaks from external noise.

**Rationale for Exclusions**
*   **Profile Completion:** Excluded. A user can have a 100% complete profile and zero value. It is a vanity metric that distracts from the money-flow funnel.
*   **Social Sharing:** Excluded. At seed stage, referral is a lagging indicator. Optimizing for sharing before validating core retention (transfer) is premature optimization.
*   **Verification Completion (as a primary event):** We track `verification_submitted` instead. "Completion" is often a system-side state; tracking the *submission* captures the user action and allows us to measure latency and failure rates explicitly, which is critical for your network constraints.

**Investor Readout Plan (July 5 Check-in)**
Do not report raw totals. Report **conversion rates** between these five steps and the **Day-3 Retention Cohort** (users who signed up June 1–7 and transferred by June 10).
*   *Success Signal:* >40% of `verification_submitted` users reach `transfer_completed` within 72 hours.
*   *Failure Signal:* High `onboarding_error` rate at `deposit_initiated` indicates network/payment gateway friction, not product-market fit issues.

**Implementation Note for Engineer**
To mitigate data loss from volatile networks, implement local event queuing with retry logic for these five events only. Do not attempt to track every click; if these five events are lost, the business logic is unreadable. Use a stable, pseudonymous `user_id` generated at `signup_started` to stitch sessions across network failures, ensuring cohort accuracy without storing PII in analytics logs.

**This Week's Step:** Define the exact schema for `onboarding_error` to ensure network timeouts are distinguishable from backend rejections, then hard-code the retry queue for these five events before integrating any other tracking.
