**Recommendation: Feature Net Revenue Retention (NRR) as the primary metric.**

For a B2B enterprise platform like FrostByte Logistics, NRR is the superior indicator of health because it captures both the value of your expansion (€68k) and the impact of churn (€45k) in a single figure. Your current trajectory shows strong product-market fit despite the losses.

**The Calculation (Based on Provided Data):**
*   **Starting MRR Base:** Not explicitly stated, but derived from the context of 340 firms.
*   **Churned MRR:** €45,000
*   **Expansion MRR:** €68,000
*   **Net Impact:** +€23,000 (Expansion exceeds Churn)

Because your expansion revenue (€68k) exceeds your churned revenue (€45k), your Net Revenue Retention is **above 100%** (assuming the starting base is consistent with the 340 firms). This tells investors that even if you acquired *zero* new customers next quarter, your revenue would still grow. Gross Retention would only highlight the €45k loss, obscuring the fact that your core enterprise clients are increasing their spend, which is the specific signal logistics investors look for to validate stickiness.

**Critical Data Gaps to Resolve Before the Slide:**

1.  **The Starting MRR Denominator:** You provided the delta (€45k out, €68k in) but not the total Starting MRR for the period. NRR = ((Starting MRR - Churn + Expansion) / Starting MRR) * 100. Without the exact Starting MRR figure, you cannot calculate the precise percentage to put on the slide. You must extract the total MRR from six months ago to finalize the number.

If the €68k expansion is heavily weighted toward just one or two of these firms, your "stable" assumption is a single point of failure. You need the specific MRR contribution of those five accounts to validate the stability claim.
