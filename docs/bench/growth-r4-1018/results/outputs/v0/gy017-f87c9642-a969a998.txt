### Measurement Plan: StockPop Pilot Early Warning System

**Audience:** Regional Directors and Supply Chain Logistics
**Objective:** Detect inventory misalignment within 48 hours of launch to enable rapid reallocation before the Dec 15–17 peak.

#### 1. The North Star: Peak Weekend Stockout Avoidance
Reject "Total Revenue" and raw "Inventory Turnover" as primary success metrics for this pilot. Revenue is a lagging result of availability; turnover speed varies wildly based on unknown foot traffic. The single metric that rises only when the business succeeds is **% of SKUs Available During Peak Hours (Dec 15–17)**. If an item is out of stock during the peak window, value is lost forever in a three-week operation.

#### 2. The 48-Hour Leading Indicators (The "Canary" Metrics)
To diagnose failure within two days without knowing absolute foot traffic, measure **rates relative to the store's own opening baseline**, not external benchmarks.

*   **Metric A: The "Zero-Sale" SKU Rate (Hourly)**
    *   *Definition:* Percentage of active SKUs with zero transactions in the last 4 hours during operating hours.
    *   *Why:* In a high-traffic pop-up, a healthy assortment should see movement across categories. A spike in zero-sale SKUs indicates either a mismatch in product mix or a hidden stockout (system says "in stock," shelf is empty).
    *   *Threshold for Action:* If >15% of core SKUs show zero sales for 4 consecutive hours on Day 1 or 2, flag for immediate physical audit.

*   **Metric B: Conversion-to-Stock Ratio (Daily Cohort)**
    *   *Definition:* (Total Transactions / Total Units Received at Opening).
    *   *Why:* This normalizes for unknown foot traffic. If Store A receives 1,000 units and does 50 transactions (5%), and Store B receives 1,000 units and does 300 (30%), Store A is failing regardless of how many people walked in. It isolates inventory alignment from traffic volume.
    *   *Threshold for Action:* If a location's ratio is <50% of the pilot median by end of Day 2, trigger a supply chain review.

*   **Metric C: The "Rain Check" Frequency**
    *   *Definition:* Count of customer requests for out-of-stock items logged by staff (or voided transactions due to missing items).
    *   *Why:* This is the most direct signal of demand exceeding supply. Unlike sales data, this captures lost demand immediately.
    *   *Threshold for Action:* Any location logging >5 rain checks per 100 transactions on Day 1 is critically under-stocked for the peak.

#### 3. Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only These)
Do not instrument "everything." Track only these events to answer specific questions:
1.  `sale_completed` (Properties: `sku_id`, `timestamp`, `location_id`) → *Answers: Is inventory moving?*
2.  `stock_audit_scan` (Properties: `sku_id`, `quantity_on_hand`, `timestamp`) → *Answers: Is system data matching physical reality?*
3.  `demand_lost` (Properties: `sku_id`, `reason: out_of_stock`, `timestamp`) → *Answers: Are we turning away customers?*

#### 4. Decision Rules for Logistics
*   **If Metric A spikes (Zero-Sale Rate high):** Dispatch manager to verify shelf placement vs. system stock. If physical stock exists but isn't selling, the mix is wrong for that demographic.
*   **If Metric B is low (Conversion-to-Stock low) AND Metric C is low:** The location has low traffic *and* aligned stock. Do not reallocate away yet; wait for weekend data.
*   **If Metric B is low AND Metric C is high:** Critical failure. You have traffic, but the wrong goods. **Action:** Immediately transfer high-demand SKUs from low-traffic locations (low Metric C) to this site.

#### This Week's Steps
1.  **Define the "Core SKU" list** today; apply the "Zero-Sale" alert only to these 20% of items that drive 80% of value to reduce noise.
2.  **Pre-commit the reallocation rule:** "If a site's `demand_lost` rate exceeds 5% by Dec 5th, 20% of its slow-moving inventory is automatically routed to the top-performing neighbor."
3.  **Instrument `demand_lost`** in the POS interface immediately; if staff cannot log this in <10 seconds, the data will be incomplete and the metric `UNVERIFIED`.

*Note: Do not compare absolute sales numbers between locations; foot traffic is an unknown confounder. Compare only the rates defined above.*
*Note: Ensure `location_id` is stable across sessions for cohort tracking, but keep customer data pseudonymous per privacy standards.*
