**Evaluation Framework: Quality-Adjusted Conversion**
To evaluate this for Legal and Customer Success, stop measuring "completion rate" in isolation. Instead, calculate **Quality-Adjusted Conversion Rate (QACR)**:
*   **Formula:** `(Completed Checkouts) − (Cost of Support Tickets × Weight) − (Estimated Cost of Data Errors × Weight)`
*   **Action:** Assign a monetary value to one support ticket (agent time) and one data error (compliance risk/cleanup). If the revenue gain from the extra 22% conversions is less than the cost of the 10% ticket surge and potential data fixes, the variant destroys value despite the higher completion rate.
