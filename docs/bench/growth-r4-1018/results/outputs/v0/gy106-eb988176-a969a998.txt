**Interpretation of Data Reliability**
The reported 4% lift for the "green button + short FAQ" variant is **UNVERIFIED** as a causal win for those specific elements because the test design violates experimental rigor. You tested three variables simultaneously (button color, FAQ length, coverage summary placement) without a stated pre-committed analysis plan for interaction effects. In multivariate testing, observing a "win" in one combination while another variable appears "inconclusive" often indicates **confounding**, not a masked dominant effect. Without orthogonal allocation (ensuring every combination of variables appears equally) and a pre-specified statistical model for interactions, you cannot statistically disentangle whether the lift came from the button, the FAQ, the summary, or a random noise spike exacerbated by "peeking" at multiple combinations. The "inconclusive" result for the coverage summary suggests the sample size per specific interaction cell was likely underpowered to detect anything but massive effects, rendering the isolation of the button's impact impossible from this dataset alone. Rolling out changes based on this read risks implementing a losing combination globally.
