### Re-engagement Diagnostic Framework: FitTrack Dormant User Campaign

**Objective:** Isolate the friction point between email open and app return for the 30,000-user dormant cohort to inform the next wave launching in two weeks.

**1. Funnel Decomposition (The "Where")**
Stop treating "click-to-app" as a single step. Break the 1.5% CTR into three distinct verification points to locate the drop:
*   **Intent Verification:** Of the 22% who opened, how many clicked *any* link (including "unsubscribe" or "view in browser")? If total clicks are high but app clicks are low, the offer (free premium) is irrelevant or the CTA is unclear.
*   **Technical Handoff:** Of those who clicked the app link, what percentage actually triggered a session start in the analytics? A gap here indicates deep-linking failures, broken URLs, or app crash-on-launch for older OS versions common in dormant accounts.
*   **Authentication Friction:** For those who launched the app, how many hit a login wall vs. seamless entry? Dormant users often forget passwords; if the "free week" requires immediate re-authentication without a magic link, friction spikes.

**2. Segment Slicing (The "Who")**
Do not analyze the 30,000 users as a monolith. Slice the data by these specific dimensions to find the signal:
*   **Dormancy Depth:** Split into 60–90 days vs. 90+ days. Users absent >90 days may have deleted the app or lost context entirely; the "free week" offer may be too weak for this group, requiring a stronger "we miss you" narrative or survey instead of a feature trial.
*   **Last Known Value:** Segment by their last completed core action (e.g., "completed 5 workouts" vs. "signed up but never logged a workout"). High-value past users ignoring the offer suggests a messaging mismatch; low-value users ignoring it suggests the product never found PMF for them initially.
*   **Device/OS Cohort:** Isolate iOS vs. Android and OS version. If the drop is concentrated on older OS versions, the issue is likely technical incompatibility rather than offer relevance.

**3. Hypothesis Testing Plan (Next 2 Weeks)**
*   **Hypothesis A (Offer Mismatch):** The "free premium" feature is not the barrier. *Test:* Send a variant to 10% of the next wave offering a "concierge onboarding call" or a simple "what stopped you?" survey instead of features.
*   **Hypothesis B (Technical Decay):** Accounts or deep links are broken. *Test:* Manually verify the user journey for 50 accounts from the 90+ day segment on varied devices.
*   **Hypothesis C (Timing/Context):** February 10 timing conflicted with user routines. *Test:* Shift send times for the next wave based on the *last known active time* of each user, not a uniform batch send.

**Immediate Action Items:**
1.  **Query:** Run a cohort analysis comparing "Email Open → Link Click → App Session Start" for the Feb 10 batch vs. the historical baseline to quantify the exact drop-off stage.
2.  **Audit:** Verify deep-link integrity for users on iOS/Android versions from 6+ months ago.
3.  **Refine:** For the next wave, suppress users who have uninstalled the app (if detectable via push token status) to protect sender reputation and focus spend on reachable users.

*Note: All metrics must be compared strictly against FitTrack's own historical baselines for similar dormant cohorts; do not apply industry average benchmarks. Ensure user identifiers used for this segmentation remain pseudonymous and comply with existing privacy consent records.*
