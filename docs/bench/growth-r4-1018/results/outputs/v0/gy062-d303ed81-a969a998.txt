### Proposed Granular Funnel & Instrumentation Plan for DeskBites

**Objective:** Isolate the friction point between "Menu Published" and "First Inquiry" to determine if the drop-off is caused by visibility (discovery), engagement (content quality), or trust (pricing/signals).

**New Intermediate Event Taxonomy**
Instrument the following events immediately. Each event answers a specific diagnostic question. Do not instrument "just in case."

| Event Name | Question Answered | Key Properties |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `menu_viewed` | **Visibility:** Are offices finding the menus? | `viewer_role`, `search_query_used`, `listing_position` |
| `menu_photo_expanded` | **Engagement:** Is the visual content compelling enough to inspect? | `photo_count`, `time_on_page` |
| `pricing_details_clicked` | **Interest:** Is there intent to evaluate cost before committing? | `currency_displayed`, `discount_visible` |
| `chef_profile_hovered` | **Trust:** Are users investigating the chef's credentials? | `badge_visible`, `review_count` |
| `inquiry_modal_opened` | **Intent:** Did the user attempt to contact but abort? | `abort_reason` (if captured), `step_abandoned` |
| `inquiry_submitted` | **Conversion:** The current "First Inquiry" metric. | `chef_id`, `office_id` |

**Hypotheses & Decision Rules**
For each potential bottleneck, here is the reading rule and the resulting action for Engineering/Design:

1.  **Hypothesis A: The Visibility Gap**
    *   *Signal:* Low `menu_viewed` count relative to `menu_published`.
    *   *Diagnosis:* Offices cannot find the chefs; the issue is search ranking or category filtering, not photo quality.
    *   *Action:* Prioritize search algorithm tweaks and featured listing placements. Do not invest in photo tools yet.

2.  **Hypothesis B: The Engagement/Quality Gap**
    *   *Signal:* High `menu_viewed` but low `menu_photo_expanded` or low `time_on_page`.
    *   *Diagnosis:* Users see the listing but lose interest immediately, supporting your "poor photo quality" assumption.
    *   *Action:* Build mandatory photo quality checks or provide a templated photography service for chefs.

3.  **Hypothesis C: The Trust/Price Gap**
    *   *Signal:* High `menu_photo_expanded` and `pricing_details_clicked`, but low `inquiry_modal_opened`.
    *   *Diagnosis:* Users are interested but stalled by price sensitivity or lack of trust signals (reviews/badges).
    *   *Action:* A/B test displaying "verified corporate client" badges or introduce a "request sample" micro-conversion before the full inquiry.

4.  **Hypothesis D: The Friction Gap**
    *   *Signal:* High `inquiry_modal_opened` but low `inquiry_submitted`.
    *   *Diagnosis:* The form itself is too long or confusing.
    *   *Action:* Simplify the inquiry form fields; reduce friction in the UI.

**Implementation Priority (MVP Stage)**
Focus strictly on `menu_viewed`, `pricing_details_clicked`, and `inquiry_modal_opened` first. These three events distinguish between "nobody saw it," "nobody liked it," and "nobody trusted it." Add `menu_photo_expanded` only if the first three indicate an engagement issue.

**This Week's Steps**
1.  **Engineering:** Implement the three priority events above with the specified properties by Wednesday. Ensure a stable, pseudonymous `office_id` is attached to all events to enable cohort tracking without exposing PII.
2.  **Design:** Prepare two variants of the chef card (one with prominent "Verified" badges, one with larger default photos) to deploy once data confirms whether the drop-off is Trust or Engagement related.
3.  **Analytics:** Run a baseline query on Friday comparing `menu_published` to `menu_viewed` to immediately validate or reject the Visibility hypothesis.

*Note: All metrics must be compared against your own weekly baseline trends; do not seek external benchmarks for "good" view rates. If `menu_viewed` data is missing by Friday, the root cause remains UNVERIFIED.*
