**1. Profit Per Visitor (PPV) Analysis**
We compare the net profit generated per 1,000 visitors for the Control (800 NOK threshold) versus the Variant (1,250 NOK threshold).

*Assumptions:*
*   **Control AOV:** Baseline $X$.
*   **Variant AOV:** $1.22X$.

*   **Hardware Margin:** 18% of AOV.
*   **Control Shipping Cost:** 95 NOK/order.
*   **Variant Shipping Cost:** Unknown, but strictly >95 NOK due to heavier bundles.

*Control Group Profit (per 1,000 visitors):*
$$ \text{Profit}_{ctrl} = (1000 \times C) \times [(0.18 \times X) - 95] $$

*Variant Group Profit (per 1,000 visitors):*
$$ \text{Profit}_{var} = (1000 \times 0.86C) \times [(0.18 \times 1.22X) - \text{Cost}_{var}] $$
$$ \text{Profit}_{var} = 860C \times [0.2196X - \text{Cost}_{var}] $$

For the Variant to match the Control's profit, the new shipping cost ($\text{Cost}_{var}$) must remain impossibly low. Even if shipping costs remained flat at 95 NOK (ignoring the heavier bundle risk), the Variant requires the hardware margin gain to offset the volume loss.
*   Control Margin Contribution per order: $0.18X - 95$
*   Variant Margin Contribution per order (at flat shipping): $0.2196X - 95$
*   Volume adjustment: The Variant sells only 86% as many units.
