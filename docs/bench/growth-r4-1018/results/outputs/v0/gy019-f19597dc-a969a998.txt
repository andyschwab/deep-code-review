### Q3 Measurement Design: Frequency vs. Reach for NicheGear

**North Star Metric:** Quote Requests per Unique Professional (not total leads or raw impressions).
*Input Metrics:* Impressions per User, Click-Through Rate (CTR), Cost Per Quote Request.

**Event Taxonomy & Instrumentation**
Instrument only events that answer specific questions about the purchase funnel. Do not track "views" generically.
1.  **Event:** `ad_impression_delivered`
    *   **Properties:** `user_pseudonymous_id` (stable cookie/device ID), `campaign_id`, `creative_variant`, `timestamp`.
    *   **Question Answered:** "How many times has this specific professional seen our ad?"
2.  **Event:** `quote_request_initiated` (Primary Conversion)
    *   **Properties:** `user_pseudonymous_id`, `source_campaign`, `total_prior_impressions` (computed at time of event).
    *   **Question Answered:** "At what exposure count did the user convert?"
3.  **Event:** `ad_negative_signal` (Guardrail)
    *   **Properties:** `user_pseudonymous_id`, `signal_type` (e.g., "hide ad", "bounce within 2s", "unsubscribe").
    *   **Question Answered:** "When does frequency cause active annoyance?"

**Analysis Method: Cohort-Based Frequency Curve**
Since you cannot A/B test frequency easily on a 5,000-person market without exhausting the audience, use **observational cohort analysis** on the campaign data:
1.  **Group users** by total impressions received in Q3 (e.g., 1–2, 3–5, 6–8, 9+).
2.  **Calculate Conversion Rate** (Quote Requests / Unique Users) for each bucket.
3.  **Identify the Inflection Point:** Plot Conversion Rate vs. Impressions. The "sweet spot" is the bucket with the highest marginal gain. The "fatigue threshold" is where the curve flattens or negative signals spike.
4.  **Baseline Comparison:** Compare Q3 conversion rates per frequency bucket against the February campaign's baseline. If February had low frequency and low conversion, and Q3 shows rising conversion up to 5 impressions then dropping, you have your answer.

**Experiment Rigor & Guardrails**
*   **Pre-commitment:** Define the primary metric (Quote Requests) and the guardrail (Negative Signal Rate) before launching. Do not stop the campaign early because "3 impressions look good"; run the full quarter to capture the long sales cycle.
*   **No Fabricated Benchmarks:** There is no industry standard for "optimal underwater welding ad frequency." Do not assume 7x is the rule. The data must reveal if your specific audience converts at 3x or 12x.
*   **Identifier Seam:** Use a stable pseudonymous ID to track users across sessions. Do not use raw emails or names in analytics logs; route PII mapping to your secure CRM only.

**This Week's Steps**
1.  **Configure Tracking:** Ensure your ad platform and website analytics pass a stable `user_pseudonymous_id` with every impression and conversion event.
2.  **Define Buckets:** Pre-define your frequency buckets (1–2, 3–5, 6–8, 9+) in your dashboard query to avoid cherry-picking ranges later.
3.  **Set the Stop Rule:** If the `ad_negative_signal` rate exceeds 2% in any frequency bucket, cap delivery to that bucket immediately for the remainder of Q3.
