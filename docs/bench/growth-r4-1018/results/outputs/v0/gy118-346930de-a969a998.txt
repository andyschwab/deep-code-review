### Lean Growth Metrics Plan for TaskFlow
**Audience:** Founder & Co-founder | **Context:** Quarterly Planning (March 5) | **Constraint:** 20 dev-hours/week, no analytics team.

#### 1. The North Star: Weekly Active Creators (WAC)
Stop tracking "Active Users" or "Logins." These are vanity metrics that rise even when value isn't delivered.
*   **Definition:** The count of unique users who complete the **core value action** (e.g., creating a task, finishing a workflow, or sending a report) at least once per week.
*   **Why:** Revenue follows value. If WAC rises, retention and revenue usually follow. If WAC flatlines while signups rise, you are building a leaky bucket.
*   **Input Metrics:** Track only two drivers: (1) New users who hit the core action within 24 hours (Activation), and (2) The percentage of last week's creators who create again this week (Retention).

#### 2. The Funnel: Read Bottom-Up (Retention First)
Do not optimize acquisition until retention is stable. Pouring traffic into a product users don't return to wastes your limited dev time.
*   **Step 1 (Retention):** Are users who tried the core action coming back in Week 2? If no, fix the product experience before building new features.
*   **Step 2 (Activation):** Of the new signups, what % hit the core action? If low, simplify onboarding.
*   **Step 3 (Acquisition):** Only after Steps 1 and 2 are healthy, look at where new users come from.

#### 3. Event Taxonomy: Three Events Only
Instrument only what answers a specific question. Do not track "clicks" or "pageviews." Implement these three events via simple server logs or a lightweight client snippet:

| Event Name | Question It Answers | Properties to Log |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `user_signed_up` | How many new potentials entered? | `source` (organic/referral), `date` |
| `core_action_completed` | Did the user get value? | `feature_used` (e.g., "kanban", "list"), `user_id` (pseudonymous) |
| `subscription_renewed` | Did the value drive revenue? | `plan_type`, `amount`, `user_id` (pseudonymous) |

**Implementation Note:** Use a stable, pseudonymous `user_id` to link these events across sessions. Do not log PII (emails/names) in analytics; route identity management to your auth system.

#### 4. Feature Adoption & Revenue Link
To decide what to build next, correlate `core_action_completed` with `subscription_renewed`.
*   **The Query:** "What % of renewing users completed the core action using Feature X in the last 30 days?"
*   **The Decision Rule:** If a feature has high usage but zero correlation with renewal, it is a "toy"—stop developing it. If a feature has low usage but high correlation with renewal, it is your "killer feature"—double down on onboarding for it.

#### 5. This Week's Steps
1.  **Define the Core Action:** Explicitly agree on the one action that equals "value" (e.g., "Task Created") by tomorrow.
2.  **Instrument the Trio:** Spend <4 dev-hours adding logs for `user_signed_up`, `core_action_completed`, and `subscription_renewed` with pseudonymous IDs.
3.  **Build the "One-Page" Dashboard:** Create a simple view showing WAC trend, Activation Rate, and Retention Rate. Ignore everything else until March 5.

*Note: All benchmarks must be compared to your own prior month's data; do not seek industry averages. If data is missing after implementation, label it UNVERIFIED rather than estimating.*
