### Community Health Dashboard Framework for AyudaTech

**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Users (WAU) completing a "Resolved Value Loop" (a user posts a question and marks an answer as accepted, or a power user provides an answer that receives a positive validation signal). This rejects raw thread counts, which rise regardless of value and currently mask your noise problem.

**The Measurement System (Quantitative + Automated Qualitative)**

To detect decay before churn occurs, instrument these three layers. Every event below must answer a specific named question; do not instrument "just in case."

1.  **Core Health Ratio (The "Answered" Proxy, Refined)**
    *   **Metric:** *Validated Resolution Rate* = (Questions with an "Accepted" answer OR an answer with >2 upvotes) / (Total Questions Created).
    *   **Why:** Your assumption (answered/total) is flawed because a thread can be "answered" with incorrect or toxic information. This metric requires a positive signal from the community or the asker.
    *   **Decay Signal:** If thread volume rises but this rate drops, you are accumulating noise, not value.

2.  **Automated Quality & Toxicity Signals (The "Manual Read" Replacement)**
    *   **Event:** `post_flagged` or `post_hidden`.
    *   **Question Answered:** "Is the community self-policing effectively?"
    *   **Metric:** *Flags per 1,000 Posts*. Track the ratio of flags to new content. A spike here precedes user churn.
    *   **Event:** `negative_reaction` (downvotes) vs. `positive_reaction` (upvotes).
    *   **Metric:** *Sentiment Velocity* = (Downvotes - Upvotes) / Total Reactions on new threads.
    *   **Decay Signal:** If new threads from new users receive immediate negative velocity without a "Resolved" state, toxicity is driving value away.

3.  **Retention Cohorts (The Bottom-Up Read)**
    *   **Metric:** *Week-2 Return Rate* for users who posted a question.
    *   **Question Answered:** "Did solving (or attempting to solve) a problem bring them back?"
    *   **Decay Signal:** If new thread creators do not return in Week 2, the "noise" is breaking the activation loop. Do not look at acquisition (new signups) until this stabilizes.

**Dashboard View for the CEO (End of Quarter)**

Present a single view with the North Star trend line and two input guards:
*   **Primary:** Trend of "Resolved Value Loops" per week.
*   **Guardrail 1:** Validated Resolution Rate (Target: Maintain or increase despite volume growth).
*   **Guardrail 2:** Flags per 1,000 Posts (Target: Flat or decreasing).

**Decision Logic for Moderator Headcount**
*   **Hire (At Risk):** If *Flags per 1,000* rises >20% MoM AND *Validated Resolution Rate* drops >10% MoM. This proves volume is drowning quality and organic policing is failing.
*   **Hold (Healthy):** If thread volume rises but *Validated Resolution Rate* holds steady and *Flags* remain flat. The community is scaling organically; hiring now creates bloat.
*   **Investigate (Unknown):** If *Resolved Value Loops* drop but flags are low. This indicates answers are unhelpful but not toxic (a content quality issue, not a safety issue).

**This Week's Steps**
1.  **Define the "Validated" Event:** Confirm with engineering if you can track "Accepted Answer" or "Upvote on Answer" as a distinct event. If not, this is `UNVERIFIED` and must be instrumented immediately.
2.  **Run a Baseline Cohort Query:** Calculate the Week-2 return rate for users who posted in the last 30 days versus the prior 30 days. If the recent cohort is lower, the "noise" is already causing churn.
3.  **Set the Tripwire:** Establish the current *Flags per 1,000 Posts* baseline. Any 2-sigma deviation from this number triggers an immediate manual review of the top 10 flagged threads.

*Note: Do not assert a "good" retention or flag rate benchmark; compare only against AyudaTech's own baseline established in Step 3. Ensure user IDs used for cohort tracking are pseudonymous; route any PII handling to your privacy compliance protocol.*
