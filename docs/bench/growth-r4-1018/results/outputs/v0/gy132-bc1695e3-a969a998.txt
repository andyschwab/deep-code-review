### Proposed Retention Definitions for StreamFiesta

Given your Friday release cadence and the observed "binge-then-disappear" behavior, standard Daily or Weekly Active Users (DAU/WAU) will indeed obscure true value. Below are three retention definitions tailored to your episodic model, ranked by their ability to distinguish between genuine engagement and vanity metrics for your Q3 review.

#### Option 1: "Episode-Cycle Retention" (Recommended)
**Definition:** A user is "retained" in a given cycle if they consume **any** content within the 7-day window starting from the Friday release date (Friday 00:00 to Thursday 23:59).
*   **Time Window:** Rolling 7-day cycles anchored to the release day (Friday).
*   **Action:** Stream ≥1 episode.
*   **Pros:** Directly aligns with your product value proposition (new episodes). It naturally accommodates your "weekend-only" users without penalizing them for Monday–Thursday inactivity. It filters out the "binge-then-disappear" cohort if they fail to return for the *next* Friday cycle.
*   **Cons:** Requires shifting your reporting calendar from standard calendar weeks to "release weeks," which may need explanation to finance teams accustomed to monthly closes.

#### Option 2: "Core-Action Recurrence" (Strict Value)
**Definition:** A user is "retained" only if they return to watch content in **two consecutive** release cycles.
*   **Time Window:** Comparison of Cycle N vs. Cycle N+1.
*   **Action:** Stream ≥1 episode in both windows.
*   **Pros:** This is your truest North Star input. It immediately disqualifies the 25% who binge once and vanish for three weeks, as they will fail the "consecutive" test. It proves habit formation rather than one-off curiosity.
*   **Cons:** The metric will lag by two weeks (you need two cycles to measure one retention point). It may look lower initially, requiring careful narrative framing to directors to avoid panic.

#### Option 3: "Weekend-Weighted WAU" (Compromise)
**Definition:** A user is "retained" if they are active on any day, but the metric is calculated using a **3-day rolling average** centered on the weekend (Saturday), or strictly counting activity only between Friday and Sunday.
*   **Time Window:** Friday–Sunday only.
*   **Action:** Stream ≥1 episode.
*   **Pros:** Smooths out the "noisy" mid-week zeros while capturing the bulk of your traffic. Easier to explain as "Weekend Engagement."
*   **Cons:** Risks masking a decline in mid-week discovery or catalog browsing. If users start drifting away from the Friday drop specifically, this metric might remain stable too long, delaying necessary product interventions.

### Recommendation for Q3 Directors
Present **Option 1 (Episode-Cycle Retention)** as your primary metric for month-over-month growth. It directly answers whether the Friday release is driving repeat value. Use **Option 2** as a guardrail metric to prove to directors that growth is sustainable and not just a result of one-time bingers.

**Note:** Do not benchmark these rates against global streaming averages; compare them strictly to your own baseline from the last 4–6 release cycles to establish trend direction. Ensure your event taxonomy tags every "play" event with the specific `episode_release_date` to enable these precise cohort queries.
