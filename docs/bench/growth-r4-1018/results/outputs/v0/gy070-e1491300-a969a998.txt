**Recommended Cohort Analysis Framework**

To answer the CEO's question on sustainability, we must isolate the behavior of "Discount-Acquired" users versus "Organic" users over time. We will track two distinct cohorts starting from their first purchase date (Month 0):

1.  **Cohort A (Treatment):** Users whose first order used the 20% lapsed-user code.
2.  **Cohort B (Control):** Users whose first order was organic (no discount) or used a standard <10% promo during the same period.

**Core Metrics to Instrument (The "Questions"):**
*   **Question 1:** Do discount users return?
    *   *Metric:* **Month 1, 2, 3 Retention Rate.** (% of Cohort A vs. Cohort B making a second purchase).
    *   *Decision Rule:* If Cohort A retention is <80% of Cohort B retention, the "loss leader" strategy fails; the discount attracts one-time bargain hunters, not loyal customers.
*   **Question 2:** Does the discount cannibalize future willingness to pay?
    *   *Metric:* **Average Order Value (AOV) on 2nd+ Purchase.** Compare full-price AOV of Cohort A vs. Cohort B.
    *   *Decision Rule:* If Cohort A's subsequent AOV is significantly lower, the discount has anchored price expectations downward.
*   **Question 3:** When do we break even?
    *   *Metric:* **Cumulative Contribution Margin per User.** Track (Revenue - COGS - Shipping - Marketing) over 6 months.
    *   *Decision Rule:* Identify the "Payback Period." If Cohort A does not turn positive cumulative margin by Month 4, the strategy destroys value.
