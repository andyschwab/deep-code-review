**Executive Summary: A/B Test Analysis & Recommendation**

**Decision:** Do not roll out the simplified form globally. Limit deployment to **first-time bookers only** immediately.

**Analysis of Divergence**
The 9.3 percentage point gap (18.4% new vs. 9.1% returning) indicates the simplified form removes critical context required for complex logistics, not that returning users struggle with simplicity. New users likely book standard, single-leg routes where fewer fields reduce friction. Returning managers likely require the "extra fields" you removed to specify multi-stop routing, temperature zones, or specific equipment constraints. For them, the simplified form forces a workflow mismatch, causing drop-off or abandonment because they cannot complete their specific job.

**Recommended Next Metrics**
To validate this hypothesis before full segmentation engineering, track these three specific events for returning users only:
1.  **"Form Abandonment by Step":** Identify exactly which missing field causes the drop-off. If users leave when asked to add a second stop later in the flow, the simplification broke their workflow.
2.  **"Support Ticket Volume (Booking Related)":** Measure if returning users are contacting support to ask how to input complex routing details on the new form. A spike here confirms the UI is blocking necessary data entry.
3.  **"Average Order Value (AOV) per Booking":** Ensure returning users who *do* convert on the simplified form aren't booking simpler, lower-margin shipments by default because the complex options are hidden.

**Action Plan**
1.  **Segment the Rollout:** Configure the portal to serve the original form to users with >1 prior booking and the simplified form to new accounts.
2.  **Instrument the "Complexity" Flag:** Add a hidden tag to bookings indicating if the route involves >1 stop. Correlate this with form version to prove returning users need the detailed view for complex jobs.
3.  **Re-run Test for Returning Users:** Design a new A/B test specifically for tenured users comparing "Original Form" vs. "Progressive Disclosure" (show simple fields first, expand for complexity) rather than permanently hiding fields.

**Note:** The current result is `UNVERIFIED` as a global win because the returning user segment regressed significantly; rolling out globally risks alienating your most valuable recurring revenue source. Ensure user identifiers used for segmentation are pseudonymous and comply with your privacy governance.
