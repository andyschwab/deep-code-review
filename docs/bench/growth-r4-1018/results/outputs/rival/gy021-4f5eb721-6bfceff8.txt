# Lumina Retail AR Pilot: Top 5 Event Tracking Plan

**Target:** Marco (Lead Data Engineer)
**Goal:** Validate hypothesis that AR visualization drives ≥20% higher spend.
**Constraint:** Focus on revenue correlation; minimize noise.

Implement the following five events. Each must include a unique `session_id`, `user_id` (if logged in), `kiosk_id`, and `timestamp`.

### 1. `ar_session_started`
*   **Trigger:** User scans the mall QR code or taps "Start Experience" on the kiosk home screen.
*   **Properties:** `traffic_source` (mall signage, staff referral), `device_type`.
*   **Why:** Establishes the denominator for conversion rates and abandonment metrics. Without this, we cannot calculate session drop-off.

### 2. `item_placed_in_room`
*   **Trigger:** User successfully renders a specific SKU into their virtual space.
*   **Properties:** `product_id`, `category` (sofa, table, lighting), `placement_count` (cumulative count for this session).
*   **Why:** Directly measures engagement depth. This validates if "visualizing" is actually happening. High placement counts should correlate with higher basket size if the hypothesis holds.

### 3. `ar_session_completed`
*   **Trigger:** User clicks "Finish" or "View Summary" after arranging items, explicitly ending the AR mode.
*   **Properties:** `total_items_placed`, `session_duration_seconds`, `final_room_snapshot_id`.
*   **Why:** Distinguishes between active users and those who abandoned mid-session. This is the critical filter for comparing "AR Completers" vs. "Non-AR Shoppers."

### 4. `product_list_viewed_post_ar`
*   **Trigger:** User views the generated list of items they placed, immediately after `ar_session_completed`.
*   **Properties:** `items_in_list_count`, `estimated_total_value`.
*   **Why:** Captures the transition from visualization to purchase intent. It confirms the user is reviewing the specific items they visualized, linking the AR activity to potential cart additions.

### 5. `purchase_completed`
*   **Trigger:** Transaction is finalized at the register or online checkout linked to the session.
*   **Properties:** `total_transaction_value`, `items_purchased` (array of IDs), `ar_attribution_flag` (true if any purchased item was placed in AR during this session).
*   **Why:** The primary outcome metric. The `ar_attribution_flag` allows us to segment revenue: compare Average Order Value (AOV) of transactions with `true` vs. `false` to directly test the 20% lift hypothesis.

***

**Note:** Do not track granular movements (e.g., "item moved left 5cm") or duration timers every second; aggregate duration at the session end to prevent data noise. Ensure `session_id` persists across the kiosk and mobile handoff if the user scans a QR code to take the room home.
