**Recommendation: Prioritize Feature Adoption Over Session Volume**

For KoiStream's current growth stage and business model, **feature adoption (specifically alert threshold configuration)** is the superior predictor of long-term subscription renewal and revenue stability. You should immediately pause efforts to drive additional traffic and instead dedicate all product and engineering resources to optimizing the onboarding flow to increase the configuration rate.

**Analysis of Current Data**
Your current data reveals a critical friction point: while 3,200 sessions indicate strong market curiosity, only 5.6% (180 farms) complete the essential setup. In B2B SaaS, particularly for specialized tools like aquaculture monitoring, "curiosity" does not pay bills; "utility" does. A user who visits the module but fails to configure alerts derives zero tangible value from the product. They are observing a dashboard, not solving a problem.

High session volume with low adoption is a dangerous vanity metric in this context. It suggests you are successfully marketing a promise you are not delivering on due to UX friction. If you double down on marketing now, you will simply inflate your top-of-funnel numbers while your churn risk compounds. You will be paying to acquire users who are statistically likely to cancel because they never experienced the core value proposition: automated algae prediction.

**Why Adoption Predicts Renewal**
Subscription renewal in niche vertical SaaS is driven by habit formation and integrated workflows. Configuring an alert threshold is an "activation event." It signifies that the farm manager has trusted your system enough to set parameters for their specific operation. Once configured, the tool begins working passively for them, creating stickiness. A farm with active alerts is far less likely to churn than a farm that merely logs in to look at graphs.

Therefore, the metric that correlates with renewal is not "how many times they visited," but "did they set it up?" Increasing the configuration rate from 5.6% to even 15% will have a more immediate and profound impact on your revenue stability than doubling your session count while keeping conversion flat.

**Strategic Action Plan**
1.  **Halt Traffic Spend:** Stop any paid campaigns driving traffic specifically to this module until the conversion rate improves.
2.  **Diagnose Friction:** Treat the 94.4% drop-off as a critical product bug. Conduct rapid user interviews with the 180 converters and, crucially, the users who visited but did not convert.
3.  **Simplify Onboarding:** Redesign the setup flow. Can you offer default templates based on farm size? Can you reduce the number of clicks required? Can you trigger a guided tour upon the first visit?
4.  **Measure Success:** Define success not by total sessions, but by the "% of Unique Farms with Configured Alerts."

**Conclusion**
For your board meeting, frame this clearly: Traffic proves interest; adoption proves value. At 47 employees, KoiStream cannot afford to burn cash acquiring users who do not stick. Fixing the leaky bucket of your onboarding flow is the only path to sustainable revenue stability.
