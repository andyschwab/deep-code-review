### Executive Summary: KoiNest Algorithm Experiment Analysis

**The Core Conflict**
The 18-day experiment reveals a critical misalignment between technical sensitivity and user trust. While the new model successfully increased anomaly detection by **22%** (validating the technical hypothesis of earlier detection), it incurred an **8% false positive rate** on healthy tanks. In the context of aquaculture, where farmer livelihoods depend on precise resource allocation and stress management, an 8% error rate is not a statistical nuisance; it is a direct threat to product viability.

**Practical Implications of the Metrics**

1.  **Trust Erosion vs. Detection Gain**
    The 22% increase in detected anomalies provides marginal utility if the delivery mechanism destroys credibility. An 8% false alarm rate means that for every 100 days a healthy tank is monitored, farmers receive nearly a week of unnecessary panic alerts. In high-stakes agriculture, "cry wolf" scenarios lead to alert fatigue, where users begin ignoring *all* notifications, including the genuine 22% of extra anomalies you successfully caught. The three large cooperatives threatening cancellation indicate that your key revenue anchors value reliability over marginal gains in detection speed.

2.  **Revenue Stability Risk**
    Losing three large cooperative farms represents a disproportionate revenue risk compared to the potential upside of the new model. Mid-sized B2B agritech firms rely heavily on cohort stability; public churn from influential cooperatives can trigger a wider market confidence crisis in Vietnam's tight-knit aquaculture community. The immediate financial impact of cancellations likely outweighs the long-term theoretical value of the 22% detection lift.

3.  **Ethical Alignment**
    Your ethical commitment to farmer livelihoods is currently compromised. Causing unnecessary panic leads to real-world costs for farmers: wasted labor investigating non-issues, unnecessary water treatments, and elevated stress levels for both the farmer and the stock. The data suggests the current model prioritizes algorithmic recall (catching everything) over precision (being right), which conflicts with the user's need for actionable certainty.

**Strategic Options & Trade-offs**

| Option | Immediate Action | Trade-off: Revenue | Trade-off: User Trust | Trade-off: Product Vision |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- :--- |
| **Full Rollback** | Revert to previous model immediately. | **Stabilizes:** Halts immediate churn threat from cooperatives. | **Restores:** Re-establishes baseline reliability instantly. | **Delays:** Postpones the benefit of earlier detection; admits the current approach failed. |
| **Segmented Rollout** | Restrict new model to non-critical accounts or internal testing; keep cooperatives on old model. | **Protects:** Shields core revenue while retaining data access. | **Mixed:** Protects key partners but creates a "two-tier" reliability perception if discovered. | **Iterative:** Allows continued tuning without burning bridges, but extends the time to full value realization. |
| **Threshold Adjustment** | Tune the algorithm to reduce false positives below 2%, accepting lower than 22% detection lift. | **Balanced:** Seeks a middle ground but risks satisfying no one fully. | **Uncertain:** Requires re-validation; farmers may remain skeptical until a long error-free period is proven. | **Compromised:** Accepts a lower ceiling on detection capabilities to meet usability standards. |

**Recommendation for Board Discussion**
The data indicates that the cost of the 8% false positive rate exceeds the value of the 22% detection increase in the current market context. The threat of churn from major cooperatives suggests the market is not yet ready for this level of sensitivity. The decision rests on whether KoiNest prioritizes immediate revenue retention and trust repair (favoring a rollback or strict segmentation) or believes it can rapidly re-engineer the precision threshold before the next billing cycle. Given the explicit threats, the practical implication is that the current configuration is commercially unsafe for your largest customers.
