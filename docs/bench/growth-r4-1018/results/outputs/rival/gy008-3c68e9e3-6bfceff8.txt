# Sahara Date Co. Drone Pilot: Critical Success Metrics & Tracking Plan

To prove operational stability and secure regulatory approval, you must track the following daily data points. These metrics distinguish a viable logistics model from a novelty by focusing on reliability, safety, and unit economics rather than just speed.

### 1. Operational Reliability (The "Stability" Proof)
*Regulators need to see that failures are rare and predictable, not random.*

| Metric | Definition | Target Threshold | Why It Matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **On-Time Delivery Rate** | % of deliveries arriving within the 45-min window. | >95% | Proves consistency despite weather/traffic variables. |
| **Mission Abort Rate** | % of flights aborted pre-delivery due to tech/weather. | <2% | Indicates system robustness and safety protocols. |
| **Battery Degradation Impact** | Delivery time vs. battery cycle count per drone. | <5% variance | Proves battery health won't cause mid-pilot collapse. |
| **Weather Disruption Frequency** | # of days/hours operations halted due to wind/heat. | <10% of total time | Demonstrates Oman-specific environmental viability. |

### 2. Safety & Compliance (The "Airspace" Proof)
*Municipal councils require zero ambiguity on risk.*

| Metric | Definition | Target Threshold | Why It Matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Safety Incidents** | Near-misses, forced landings, or property damage. | **0** | Non-negotiable for permanent clearance. |
| **Payload Integrity Rate** | % of deliveries with zero spoilage/damage. | >98% | Validates the "freshness" value proposition. |
| **Noise Complaint Count** | Formal complaints from communities under flight paths. | 0 | Social license to operate in mixed zones. |

### 3. Unit Economics (The "Viability" Proof)
*Investors need to see that scaling reduces cost, not increases it.*

| Metric | Definition | Target Threshold | Why It Matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cost Per Drop (CPD)** | (Total Daily OpEx + Drone Depreciation) / Successful Drops. | < Truck CPD | Must beat current trucking costs at scale. |
| **Utilization Rate** | Actual flights per drone vs. max theoretical capacity. | >70% | Proves the fleet of 12 is sized correctly. |
| **Maintenance Downtime** | Hours drones are grounded for repair/charging. | <4 hrs/day/fleet | Predicts scalability bottlenecks. |

### Daily Reporting Format
Submit a daily log to the Operations Director containing:
1.  **Total Flights Attempted vs. Completed**
2.  **Average Transit Time** (segmented by time of day to catch heat impacts)
3.  **Total Cost Incurred** (energy + labor + maintenance)
4.  **Incident Report** (even near-misses)
5.  **Spoilage Rate** (kg of dates damaged vs. total kg delivered)

### Projection Model for Month-End Report
To satisfy the municipal council by month-end, aggregate the daily data into these three projections:
*   **Failure Rate Curve:** Plot aborts/incidents against battery cycles to predict when maintenance costs will spike.
*   **Weather Sensitivity Index:** Correlate wind speed/temperature with delay minutes to define "no-fly" boundaries for regulations.
*   **Break-Even Volume:** Calculate the number of daily drops required where Drone CPD equals Truck CPD, accounting for the 12-drone fleet cap.

**Note:** Ensure battery data includes ambient temperature readings, as Oman's heat significantly impacts lithium-ion performance and safety margins.
**Note:** Separate "novelty" metrics (like customer excitement) from "stability" metrics; regulators only care about the latter for airspace clearance.
