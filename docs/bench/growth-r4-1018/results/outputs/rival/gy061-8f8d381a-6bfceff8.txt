### Proposed Activation Metrics for QuillStream

Given your niche audience of maritime logistics coordinators and the small beta sample (120 users), the activation metric must balance the high stakes of shipping documentation with the need for early engagement signals. Below are three distinct criteria to test.

**1. The "Manifest Completion" Criterion (Current Hypothesis)**
*Definition:* User generates, edits, and exports a shipping manifest within 7 days of signup.
*   **Pros:** Directly validates your core value proposition and correlates strongly with paid tier usage. For maritime coordinators, an exported manifest is a tangible work product, ensuring the metric reflects genuine utility rather than curiosity.
*   **Cons:** With only 18% returning within three days, this bar may be too high for a Week 1 metric. Maritime manifests often require specific data inputs that users might not have ready immediately, causing false negatives where interested users churn before completing the full loop.

**2. The "Draft Preservation" Criterion (Intermediate Signal)**
*Definition:* User generates a document and explicitly saves it as a draft or returns to edit a saved draft within 3 days.
*   **Pros:** Lowers the friction threshold while still requiring intent. In logistics, workflows are often interrupted; saving a draft indicates the user intends to return to complete the task once they gather necessary cargo details. This captures the "commitment to workflow" without demanding final export immediately.
*   **Cons:** May overestimate activation if users save drafts but never finalize them. It measures intent to work, not necessarily successful work, which could inflate your dashboard numbers without guaranteeing future retention.

**3. The "Multi-Field Engagement" Criterion (Behavioral Depth)**
*Definition:* User interacts with at least three specialized maritime fields (e.g., IMO number, Port of Loading, Cargo HS Code) within a single session.
*   **Pros:** Highly specific to your niche. General writing tools don't have these fields; interacting with them proves the user is engaging with QuillStream's specialized value, not just testing generic text generation. It is a leading indicator that can be measured in the first session, solving the low return-rate issue.
*   **Cons:** Requires robust event tracking implementation. It assumes that field interaction equals value, which might not hold if users are just clicking around to understand the interface without actual intent to create a document.

**Recommendation for Elena's Dashboard:**
Adopt a **tiered approach** for Q4. Use "Multi-Field Engagement" as your primary *Week 1* activation metric to maximize signal from your small sample size, while tracking "Manifest Completion" as the *North Star* metric for long-term retention. This allows you to optimize early onboarding flows without losing sight of the ultimate business goal.

*Note: Ensure your tracking distinguishes between generic text edits and specialized field interactions to validate the third criterion.*
*Given the 47-person team size, prioritize the metric that requires the least engineering overhead to implement immediately.*
