# StudyNest 'Barn Buddy' Beta Tracking Plan

**Objective:** Measure genuine collaboration and retention impact for the beta launch (500 users, March 15) without inflating metrics with passive actions.

### 1. Core Event Hierarchy
We will track three tiers of events. Only Tier 2 counts toward your defined "Successful Interaction." Tier 3 is excluded from core success metrics to prevent noise.

| Tier | Event Name | Definition & Properties | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | : |
| **1. Exposure** | `case_file_viewed` | **Trigger:** User opens a case file.<br>**Props:** `case_id`, `animal_type` (exotic/common), `viewer_id`, `author_id`. | Measures reach and content consumption. |
| **2. Conversion** | `collaboration_completed` | **Trigger:** User leaves a **verified** comment on a case they viewed within the last 24 hours.<br>**Props:** `case_id`, `animal_type`, `comment_length`, `time_since_view_seconds`. | **Primary Success Metric.** Validates your hypothesis of meaningful engagement. |
| **3. Signal** | `case_bookmarked` | **Trigger:** User clicks bookmark.<br>**Props:** `case_id`, `animal_type`. | **Secondary/Exploratory.** Do not count as "engagement" in primary reports yet. Use to correlate with future retention. |

### 2. Addressing Your Specific Concerns

**Should bookmarking count as meaningful engagement?**
**No.** Including bookmarks in your core success metric will inflate numbers and obscure whether users are actually collaborating. Treat `case_bookmarked` as a "intent signal." Analyze it separately to see if users who bookmark are *more likely* to return next week, but do not mix it into the `collaboration_completed` rate.

**How to weight Exotic Livestock vs. Common Pets?**
Do not apply arbitrary mathematical weights (e.g., counting an exotic case as 1.5x). This distorts raw data and confuses investors. Instead:
1.  **Track `animal_type` as a strict property** on every event.
2.  **Segment your reports:** Present two distinct lines in your dashboard: "Exotic Livestock Collaboration Rate" and "Common Pet Collaboration Rate."
3.  **Analysis:** If the 40% of users interested in exotics drive 80% of collaborations, that is a strategic insight to present, not a calculation to hide behind weighted averages.

### 3. Investor Reporting Framework (April 2 Review)

To demonstrate the path to retention for the Chiang Mai Agriculture Fund, structure your April 2 report around these three derived metrics:

*   **Collaboration Density:** `(Total collaboration_completed) / (Total Active Users)`
    *   *Goal:* Prove the feature drives activity, not just passive viewing.
*   **Segmented Retention Lift:** Compare Week-4 retention of users who performed `collaboration_completed` vs. those who only `case_file_viewed`.
    *   *Split by:* `animal_type` to show which demographic drives stickiness.
*   **Beta Conversion Funnel:**
    1.  Viewed Case
    2.  Bookmarked (Optional Step)
    3.  Commented Verified (<24h) → **Success**

### Implementation Notes
*   **Verification Logic:** Ensure your engineering team defines "verified comment" technically (e.g., length > 10 chars, not emoji-only) before March 15 to avoid gaming.
*   **Time Window:** The 24-hour window must be calculated server-side using the timestamp of `case_file_viewed` and `comment_submitted`.

This plan isolates genuine value creation from vanity metrics, providing the clean data needed for your investor review.
