# Evaluation Plan: Mandatory Account Creation Impact

**Objective:** Determine if reverting the mandatory account creation step (implemented Feb 10) will recover lost revenue without exposing LuxeMode to unacceptable fraud risk or long-term retention loss, using existing data only.

## 1. Quantify the Revenue Leak (Drop-off Analysis)
Isolate the specific impact of the new step by comparing pre- and post-February 10 data.
*   **Funnel Segmentation:** Calculate the conversion rate from *Shipping Info* → *Payment Page* for two periods: Jan 1–Feb 9 vs. Feb 10–Present.
*   **Segment Breakdown:** Split this data by user type (Guest vs. Logged-in) and device. If the drop-off spike is isolated to users who would have been "Guests," the friction hypothesis is confirmed.
*   **Revenue Attributions:** Multiply the lost conversion volume by your Average Order Value (AOV) for high-intent carts (> $500) to present the CMO with the exact weekly revenue bleed.

## 2. Qualitative Friction Audit (Session Recordings)
Review 50–100 session recordings of users who abandoned the cart specifically between the *Shipping Info* and *Payment* pages post-Feb 10.
*   **Rage Clicks/Thrashing:** Look for repeated clicking on the "Create Account" fields or the "Back" button, indicating confusion or refusal.
*   **Form Abandonment:** Note how far users get into the registration form before leaving. Do they stop at the password field? This confirms the "speed" friction point.
*   **Exit Velocity:** Measure the time spent on the registration step before exit. High-net-worth clients exhibiting <10 seconds before leaving suggests an immediate rejection of the friction.

## 3. Fraud & Retention Risk Assessment
Address the Head of Fraud Prevention's concerns without a public test.
*   **Historical Guest Fraud Rate:** Retrieve the fraud chargeback rate for *guest* transactions from the period prior to Feb 10. If guest fraud was historically <0.5% (industry standard for luxury), the risk of reverting is likely negligible compared to the 13% increase in abandonment.
*   **Data Redundancy Check:** Audit what data is actually lost without an account. If you already capture email, phone, and shipping address for fulfillment, the "lost data" is merely a password. Evaluate if post-purchase email flows (e.g., "Set your password to track your order") can recover account creation rates without blocking the sale.

## Recommendation Framework
Present the findings using this decision matrix:

| Metric | Threshold for Reverting | Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Drop-off Increase** | >20% increase since Feb 10 | **Revert Immediately** |
| **Guest Fraud Rate** | <1.0% historically | **Risk Acceptable** |
| **Data Loss** | Only password/login credentials | **Mitigatable via Email** |

**Final Deliverable:** If the drop-off correlates strictly with the guest flow and historical guest fraud is low, recommend an immediate revert to "Guest Checkout Optional." Frame this not as losing data, but as removing a barrier to capturing the customer's email via a successful transaction, which allows for post-purchase account conversion.

*Note: Ensure all session recording reviews comply with privacy masking protocols for high-net-worth individuals.*
*Do not implement changes until the fraud team signs off on the historical guest chargeback data.*
