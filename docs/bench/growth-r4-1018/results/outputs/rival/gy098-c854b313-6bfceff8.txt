# AyudaTech Community Health Dashboard Framework

To justify moderator headcount and detect early decay, replace the single "answered ratio" metric with a **Community Health Score (CHS)** composed of three weighted pillars. This approach automates quality detection without manual reading of every post.

### Pillar 1: Resolution Efficiency (40% Weight)
*Replaces simple answer ratios with speed and closure data.*
*   **Time-to-First-Helpful-Answer (TTFHA):** Measure the median time from thread creation to the first reply marked "helpful" or "accepted." An increasing trend indicates noise is drowning out signal, even if total reply counts rise.
*   **Unresolved Thread Velocity:** Track the week-over-week percentage change in threads older than 7 days with no accepted solution. A spike here is a leading indicator of user churn before cancellations occur.
*   **Self-Answer Rate:** High rates of users answering their own questions often indicate they gave up waiting for help, a critical decay signal.

### Pillar 2: Engagement Quality (35% Weight)
*Quantifies "noise" vs. "value" using interaction depth.*
*   **Signal-to-Noise Ratio:** Calculate `(Replies from Unique Users) / (Total Replies)`. If total replies rise but unique repliers stay flat, a few users (or bots) are driving the "noise" complaints.
*   **Depth-of-Thread Distribution:** Segment threads by reply count. A healthy community has a bell curve. A shift toward many 1-2 reply threads (drive-by posting) or massive 50+ reply threads (flame wars) signals degradation.
*   **Power User Retention Rate:** Specifically track the monthly active status of your top 10% of contributors by post volume. If this cohort shrinks by >5% MoM, the community is losing its foundation.

### Pillar 3: Sentiment & Safety Automation (25% Weight)
*Automates qualitative assessment using keyword flagging.*
*   **Toxicity Flag Rate:** Implement a basic keyword filter for aggression, profanity, or dismissive language (e.g., "RTFM," "stupid question"). Report the % of new threads containing flags.
*   **Negative Sentiment Delta:** Use a lightweight NLP tool (or even simple positive/negative word lists) to score thread titles and first posts. Track the average sentiment score weekly. A drop of >10% triggers an alert.
*   **Report-to-Post Ratio:** Automate the tracking of user reports per 1,000 posts. A sudden increase suggests community norms are breaking down faster than moderators can react.

### The Executive Dashboard View
Present these metrics in a single "Traffic Light" view for the CEO:
*   **Green:** All pillars within 10% of baseline. *Action: Maintain current staff.*
*   **Yellow:** One pillar degraded >15% or Power User Retention dropping. *Action: Hire 3-5 contractors immediately.*
*   **Red:** Two+ pillars degraded or Toxicity Flag Rate spikes >20%. *Action: Approve full 10-headcount plan to prevent mass exodus.*

**Leading Indicators for Early Decay:**
Monitor **Time-to-First-Helpful-Answer** and **Power User Retention** weekly. These metrics shift 2–3 weeks before overall user registration or total thread counts begin to fall, giving you the lead time needed to justify proactive hiring.

*Note: Implement basic keyword flagging immediately using existing forum software plugins to populate Pillar 3 without custom development. Ensure all metrics exclude internal team posts to reflect true community health.*
