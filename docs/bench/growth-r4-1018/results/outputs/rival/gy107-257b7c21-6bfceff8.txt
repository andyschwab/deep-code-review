# Technical Specification: Network Density Metric

**Objective:** Quantify local network density to predict engagement and guide marketing spend.

### 1. Data Points Required
To calculate this metric, the data engineering team must aggregate the following real-time data streams:
*   **User Profile:** `user_id`, `registration_date`, `primary_city` (e.g., Kharkiv, Lviv, Kyiv).
*   **Connection Graph:** Directed edges (`user_id_A`, `user_id_B`, `timestamp`) representing established connections.
*   **Engagement Events:** `session_start`, `message_sent`, `post_view` (to correlate density with activity).

### 2. The Formula: "Local Mutual Density Score" (LMDS)
Instead of a global graph density (which is too sparse for early-stage networks), calculate a user-level score based on the hypothesis that **5 mutual connections** is the tipping point.

For each user $U$ in City $C$:
1.  Identify $N_c$: The set of User $U$'s direct connections who also list City $C$ as their primary location.
2.  Calculate $M_u$: The average number of mutual connections between User $U$ and every other user in set $N_c$.
    *   *Simplified Proxy:* Count how many pairs within $N_c$ are also connected to each other.
3.  **Binary Threshold Flag:** `is_dense` = TRUE if User $U$ has $\ge$ 5 mutual connections with at least one other user in the same city.

**Aggregate City Metric:**
$$ \text{City Density \%} = \frac{\text{Count of Users with } (\text{is\_dense} = \text{TRUE})}{\text{Total Active Users in City}} \times 100 $$

### 3. Implementation & Visualization
*   **Storage:** Maintain a materialized view updated hourly. Given 150 connections/hour, a full recalculation is feasible every 60 minutes.
*   **Visualization Dashboard:**
    *   **Heat Map:** Ukraine map colored by "City Density %". Darker shades indicate higher density.
    *   **Scatter Plot:** X-axis = City Density %, Y-axis = Avg Sessions/User. This will visually confirm the 40% engagement lift hypothesis.
    *   **Cohort Tracker:** Line chart showing the % of new users hitting the "5 mutual connection" threshold within 7 days of signup.

### 4. Threshold Identification Strategy
To find the exact kick-in point:
1.  Bin users by their mutual connection count (0-2, 3-4, 5-6, 7+).
2.  Calculate average retention and session time for each bin.
3.  Identify the inflection point where the slope of engagement increases sharply. If the data confirms the 40% lift at 5 connections, set this as the "Activation Threshold."

# Strategic Brief: Targeting Kharkiv and Lviv

**To:** CEO, ConnectPro
**From:** Community Lead
**Subject:** Optimizing Marketing Spend via Network Density

**The Opportunity**
Our growth to 25,000 users is strong, but raw registration numbers hide uneven value. Our hypothesis suggests that users only become highly engaged (and likely to recommend ConnectPro) once they have **5 mutual connections** in their city. Below this threshold, churn risk is high; above it, engagement jumps by ~40%.

**The Strategy: Density-First Acquisition**
We will shift marketing spend in Kharkiv and Lviv from "maximizing signups" to "maximizing local clustering."

1.  **Targeted Clustering:** Instead of broad geo-targeting, we will run ads targeting specific tech hubs, universities, or companies within Kharkiv and Lviv simultaneously. The goal is to acquire small groups of users who are likely to know each other offline, accelerating their path to the "5 mutual connections" threshold.
2.  **The "Connector" Program:** Identify users in these cities who already have high density. Incentivize them to invite former colleagues specifically, rather than random networks.
3.  **Success Metric:** We will stop reporting solely on "New Users per City." The primary KPI becomes **"Activated Density Rate"**: the percentage of new users in Kharkiv/Lviv who reach 5 mutual connections within their first 14 days.

**Expected Outcome**
By focusing spend on creating dense micro-clusters rather than scattered acquisitions, we anticipate a 40% increase in session time and a significant rise in organic referrals in these two key regions within Q4.
