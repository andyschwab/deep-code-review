### Executive Recommendation: Segment by Device with Immediate Investigation

Do not execute a global rollout yet. The conflicting segment performance creates a net risk to total revenue given the high value of desktop users. The recommended path is to **deploy the variant to mobile users only** while maintaining the control experience for desktop users. This captures the 15% mobile gain immediately without jeopardizing desktop revenue.

Simultaneously, launch a targeted investigation to understand the desktop decline before the June 1 deadline. If the root cause is identified and fixed, re-test a "hybrid" version on desktop. If the cause is fundamental to the simplified design's perception on larger screens, abandon the desktop change entirely.

### Risk Analysis of Potential Paths

**1. Global Rollout (Deploy to All)**
*   **Risk:** High. If desktop traffic constitutes more than ~35% of your total volume (assuming equal average order value), the 8% loss on desktop could mathematically outweigh the 15% mobile gain, resulting in a net revenue decrease. Even if mobile volume is higher, alienating high-value desktop users damages long-term LTV and trust.
*   **Outcome:** Potential net negative revenue and brand erosion among premium users.

**2. Segment by Device (Mobile Only)**
*   **Risk:** Low. This isolates the win. The primary risk is technical complexity in maintaining two checkout flows, but modern feature-flagging tools make this manageable.
*   **Outcome:** Immediate realization of mobile growth; desktop revenue remains stable.

**3. Investigate Further (Delay Decision)**
*   **Risk:** Medium. Delaying the mobile rollout means leaving a 15% conversion lift on the table during the critical pre-summer ramp-up. If the investigation takes too long, you miss the peak season opportunity.
*   **Outcome:** Opportunity cost on mobile revenue; potential clarity on desktop issues.

### Data Required to Resolve Uncertainty

To finalize the desktop strategy by June 1, gather the following immediately:

**Quantitative Data:**
*   **Revenue-Weighted Impact:** Calculate the net revenue impact using actual traffic splits and Average Order Value (AOV) per device. Desktop users often have higher AOV; an 8% drop there may cost more than a 15% mobile gain earns.
*   **Funnel Drop-off Points:** Identify exactly *where* desktop users abandon the simplified form. Is it at the start (trust issue) or mid-form (confusion)?
*   **Statistical Significance by Segment:** Confirm the desktop decrease is statistically significant and not noise. Check confidence intervals to ensure the 8% drop is real.

**Qualitative Data:**
*   **Session Recordings (Desktop):** Watch recordings of desktop users on the variant. Look for "rage clicks," hesitation on security badges, or form validation errors that might not appear on mobile.
*   **Post-Test Survey:** Trigger a one-question exit survey for desktop abandoners: "What prevented you from completing your booking today?" with options related to trust, clarity, or technical issues.
*   **Customer Support Logs:** Check for recent tickets mentioning "checkout," "security," or "form errors" correlated with the test dates.

1.  **Approve Mobile-Only Rollout:** Authorize engineering to flip the flag for mobile devices immediately.
2.  **Authorize Rapid Diagnostic Sprint:** Allocate 5 days for product/design to analyze session recordings and survey data regarding the desktop drop.
3.  **Decision Gate (May 29):** Based on diagnostic findings, decide whether to (a) revert desktop to control permanently, (b) design a "trust-enhanced" simplified form for a quick re-test, or (c) accept the trade-off if mobile volume overwhelmingly dominates.

This approach secures the known win while rigorously protecting your high-value desktop segment before peak season.
