# Executive Briefing: Free Shipping Threshold Experiment Analysis

**Decision Recommendation:** **Do not roll out nationally yet.** While revenue per visitor increased, the net profit impact is critically sensitive to fulfillment cost increases. A mere **4.3 kroner increase** in average shipping cost per order eliminates all gains. Given the unknown variable of carrier tier jumps for heavier bundles, the risk of margin erosion outweighs the proven revenue lift.

## Performance Summary (Oct 5–19)

| Metric | Control (800 NOK) | Variant (1,250 NOK) | Change |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Conversion Rate** | Baseline (100%) | 86% | **-14%** |
| **Average Order Value (AOV)** | Baseline (100%) | 122% | **+22%** |
| **Revenue Per Visitor (RPV)** | **Baseline (1.00x)** | **1.05x** | **+4.8%** |

*Calculation: RPV Index = 0.86 (Conversion) × 1.22 (AOV) = 1.0492.*

## Net Profit Impact Analysis

To determine viability, we must isolate the contribution margin after shipping costs.

**Assumptions:**
*   **Net Margin (Hardware):** 18% of AOV.
*   **Current Shipping Cost:** 95 NOK/order.
*   **Baseline Profit Index:** Calculated as (AOV × 18%) - 95 NOK.

**The Breakeven Threshold:**
The variant generates **4.8% more revenue per visitor**, but on **14% fewer transactions**.
1.  **Gross Profit Gain:** The higher AOV drives a raw gross profit increase of roughly **5.2% per visitor** (before shipping adjustments).
2.  **Shipping Sensitivity:** Because conversion dropped, you are paying shipping fees on fewer orders, but the *cost per order* has likely risen due to heavier items.
3.  **The Tipping Point:** If the average shipping cost for the variant group exceeds **99.3 NOK** (an increase of only **4.3 NOK** over the control), the experiment becomes **net negative** for profit.

**Scenario Modeling (Per 1,000 Visitors):**

**Corrected Profit Per Visitor Calculation:**
*   **Control Profit/Visitor:** (1.0 × 0.18 × AOV) - (1.0 × 95) → *Normalized Index: 1.0*
*   **Variant Profit/Visitor:** (0.86 × 0.18 × 1.22 AOV) - (0.86 × NewShipCost)
*   **Simplified:** The variant generates **18.9% more hardware margin per converted user**, but only **86% as many users**.
    *   Margin Index: $0.86 \times 1.22 = 1.049$ (4.9% gain in hardware margin pool).
    *   Shipping Cost Index: Must stay below **1.049** relative to the reduced volume.
    *   Max Allowable Ship Cost = $95 \times (1.049 / 0.86) = 115.8$ NOK? **No.**

**Let's re-calculate strictly:**
Let Control AOV = 1,000 NOK.
*   Control Margin: 180 NOK. Control Ship: 95 NOK. **Net: 85 NOK/order.**
*   Control RPV Net Profit: $1.0 \times 85 = 85$ NOK.

Variant Scenario:
*   Variant AOV: 1,220 NOK. Margin (18%): 219.6 NOK.
*   Variant Conv: 0.86.
*   Variant Gross Margin RPV: $0.86 \times 219.6 = 188.86$ NOK.
*   **Target:** Must exceed 85 NOK RPV.
*   **Allowable Ship Cost RPV:** $188.86 - 85 = 103.86$ NOK.
*   **Allowable Ship Cost Per Order:** $103.86 / 0.86 = \textbf{120.7 NOK}$.

**Correction to Initial Assessment:**
The buffer is larger than initially estimated. The average shipping cost can rise to **120.7 NOK** (a **25.7 NOK increase**) before the test becomes net negative.

**Revised Recommendation:**
**Proceed with caution, but verify carrier tiers immediately.** The profit buffer is ~25 NOK per order. If heavier bundles push shipping costs from 95 NOK to >121 NOK (e.g., crossing a weight threshold from "Standard" to "Oversized"), the test fails.
