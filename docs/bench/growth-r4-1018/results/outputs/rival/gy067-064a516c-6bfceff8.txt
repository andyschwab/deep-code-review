### Proposed Experiment Framework: Delivery Estimate Optimization

**Primary Metric to Optimize: Net Revenue Per Session (NRPS)**
Do not optimize for order volume or cancellation rate alone. Optimizing for volume may show shorter estimates that drive cancellations later; optimizing for cancellations may show such long estimates that users never order.

**NRPS** captures the net outcome of your hypothesis. It is calculated as:
*(Total Completed Order Value – Refunds/Compensations) / Total App Sessions*

This metric directly answers the country manager's question: "At what point does honesty hurt revenue?" If displaying a 50-minute estimate reduces orders by 20% but eliminates 90% of cancellations (and their associated operational costs and lost food), NRPS will rise. If the estimate is so long that users abandon the app entirely, NRPS will fall. This aligns the test directly with the board's financial concerns while validating the operational assumption.

**Guardrail Metrics**
Monitor these daily to ensure the strategy does not cause irreversible brand damage or operational collapse:

1.  **Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) / NPS:** Specifically tracking post-delivery surveys.
    *   *Threshold:* Alert if scores drop >5% vs. baseline. Accurate estimates should improve this; if they don't, the delay itself is unacceptable regardless of accuracy.
2.  **Driver Utilization Rate:** Orders delivered per driver hour.
    *   *Threshold:* Must remain stable or improve. If utilization drops while times are extended, it indicates a demand collapse rather than efficient matching.
3.  **App Bounce Rate (Pre-Restaurant Selection):** The percentage of users who open the app and leave before selecting a restaurant.
    *   *Threshold:* Alert if increase >10%. This detects the "abandon entirely" risk where extended estimates displayed on listing pages cause immediate exit.
4.  **Repeat Order Rate (7-Day Cohort):** Percentage of users from the test group who order again within a week.
    *   *Threshold:* Must not decrease. This ensures that while you might lose immediate transactions, you are not churning long-term customer lifetime value.

**Implementation Note for the Board**
Present this as a "Revenue Protection Strategy" rather than a "Cancellation Reduction" tactic. The narrative must shift from "we can't find drivers" to "we are optimizing market efficiency to maximize profitable transactions." The test should run for 14 days to capture full weekly cycles (weekday lunches vs. weekend dinners) before the next board review.

**Data Integrity Warning**
Ensure the "Session" denominator in NRPS includes all unique device opens, not just logged-in users, to accurately measure total market abandonment. Do not calculate NRPS using only completed orders, as this hides the abandonment effect you are trying to measure.
