# CasaFind Spain: Enhanced Conversion Funnel Definition

To distinguish between UX friction and offline leakage, we must expand tracking from a binary "View → Submit" model to a multi-branch funnel that captures intent signals before the final conversion. This structure allows you to isolate where mobile users disengage versus where they succeed outside your platform.

## 1. The Expanded Funnel Stages

Define these five distinct stages to map the user journey:

**Stage 1: Property Discovery (Intent Initiated)**
*   **Definition:** User views a property detail page (PDP).
*   **Current Status:** Tracked (15k/day).
*   **New Requirement:** Segment by device (Mobile vs. Desktop) and interface version (Legacy vs. New Mobile Interface launched Aug 20).

**Stage 2: Engagement Signal (Intent Validated)**
*   **Definition:** User performs a low-friction action indicating serious interest *before* the contact form.
*   **Events to Track:**
    *   `click_to_reveal_phone`: User clicks to show the agent's number.
    *   `click_to_copy_phone`: User copies the number to clipboard.
    *   `click_to_email`: User clicks the email link (if separate).
    *   `save_listing`: User favorites the property.
*   **Why this matters:** If Stage 1 is high but Stage 2 is low on mobile, the new interface may be hiding contact options. If Stage 2 is high but Stage 3 is low, the friction is specifically in the submission/verification process.

**Stage 3: Conversion Attempt (Friction Point)**
*   **Definition:** User initiates the formal lead capture flow.
*   **Events to Track:**
    *   `contact_form_started`: Form modal opens or user scrolls to form.
    *   `phone_verification_sent`: OTP/SMS triggered.
    *   `phone_verification_failed`: Invalid entry or timeout.
    *   `contact_form_abandoned`: User leaves after starting but before submitting.
*   **Why this matters:** This isolates your hypothesis. A spike in `phone_verification_failed` or `contact_form_abandoned` on mobile confirms the verification step is the blocker.

**Stage 4: Digital Conversion (Success)**
*   **Definition:** Form successfully submitted.
*   **Events to Track:** `contact_form_submitted` (Current metric).
*   **Analysis:** Compare Stage 3 → Stage 4 conversion rates by device.

**Stage 5: Offline Conversion (Leakage Detection)**
*   **Definition:** User bypasses the form to call directly.
*   **Events to Track:**
    *   `tel_link_clicked`: Specific click on the `tel:` protocol link.
    *   `whatsapp_link_clicked`: Click on WhatsApp deep link (highly relevant in Spain).
*   **Why this matters:** If Stage 2 and Stage 5 are high, but Stage 4 is flat, your users are converting successfully via phone, rendering the form friction irrelevant for those specific users. The "problem" is actually a measurement gap, not a UX failure.

## 2. Implementation Priorities for Q3 Roadmap

Present these three specific workstreams to the Product Director:

**A. Instrument "Pre-Form" Intent (Engineering - Low Effort)**
Implement event listeners for `click_to_reveal_phone`, `tel_link_clicked`, and `whatsapp_link_clicked` immediately. This requires no UI change, only code instrumentation.
*   *Success Metric:* Ratio of `tel_link_clicked` to `contact_form_submitted`. If this ratio is >2:1 on mobile, users prefer calling.

**B. Audit Verification Friction (Product/UX - Medium Effort)**
Add granular tracking to the verification step: `verification_input_started`, `verification_code_requested`, `verification_code_error`, `verification_timeout`.
*   *Success Metric:* Drop-off rate between `verification_code_requested` and `contact_form_submitted`. If >40% on mobile, the hypothesis is confirmed.

**C. Post-Conversion Validation (Ops/Engineering - High Effort)**
Implement a "Did you call?" micro-survey or match inbound call logs (if agents log calls) against digital sessions.
*   *Success Metric:* % of "digital leaks" recovered or attributed.

## 3. Expected Outcome

By splitting the funnel at **Engagement Signal** and **Conversion Attempt**, you will generate one of two clear narratives for the roadmap:
1.  **UX Breakage:** High drop-off at verification → Prioritize simplifying mobile auth (e.g., remove OTP, use biometric).
2.  **Channel Preference:** High click-to-call, low form start → Prioritize "Click-to-Call" UI enhancements and attribute those calls as wins, rather than forcing form usage.

This approach stops guessing and provides the data needed to decide whether to fix the form or embrace the phone call.

*Note: Ensure all phone number clicks are tagged with `event_category: outbound_contact` to separate them from internal navigation in GA4.*
*Recommendation: Run a quick heuristic review of the mobile verification flow on a real device before engineering sprints begin to confirm the suspected friction.*
