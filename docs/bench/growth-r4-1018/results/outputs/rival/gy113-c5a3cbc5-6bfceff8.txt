# NairaWallet Pre-Launch Tracking Plan (June 1 – July 5)

**Primary North Star:** % of new signups completing a first transfer within 72 hours.
**Data Integrity Strategy:** Implement local event queuing on the mobile client to store events during network outages and batch-upload when connectivity is restored. This mitigates data loss from volatile mobile networks without requiring complex backend changes.

### The 5 Critical Events

| Priority | Event Name | Key Properties | Rationale for Unit Economics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | `signup_completed` | `method` (phone/email), `timestamp` | **Denominator for all rates.** Defines the cohort start time ($t=0$) for your 3-day activation window. Without this, you cannot calculate activation rate or CAC payback. |
| **2** | `kyc_verification_completed` | `document_type`, `attempt_count`, `duration_sec` | **Funnel Health.** In Nigerian fintech, KYC is the primary drop-off point. Tracking attempts vs. completions identifies friction costing you users before they can transact. |
| **3** | `first_deposit_initiated` | `amount`, `channel` (card/bank), `currency` | **Intent Signal.** While not the final success metric, a deposit indicates high intent. If users deposit but don't transfer, your product has a liquidity or UX blockage, not an acquisition problem. |
| **4** | `transfer_completed` | `amount`, `recipient_type`, `fee_charged` | **Activation & Revenue.** This is your core "aha" moment and primary revenue driver. It validates the unit economics hypothesis directly. Used as the numerator for your 3-day activation rate. |
| **5** | `app_session_started` | `network_type` (4G/3G/WiFi), `session_id` | **Retention & Context.** Essential for calculating DAU/MAU and distinguishing between churn (user left) and technical failure (user couldn't connect). Critical for debugging network-related drop-offs. |

### Strategic Exclusions

We explicitly excluded **Profile Completion** and **Social Sharing** for this phase. Profile completion is a vanity metric; a user with a 100% complete profile who never transfers has $0 LTV. Social sharing is a growth lever, not a validation metric; optimizing for virality before validating core retention (transfer completion) is premature. We must prove users *want* to transfer money before asking them to invite friends.

### Investor Reporting Framework (July 5 Check-in)

To demonstrate traction to your lead investor, report these three derived metrics computed strictly from the events above:

1.  **Activation Rate:** `(Users with transfer_completed within 72h of signup_completed) / (Total signup_completed)`
    *   *Target:* Establish a baseline (e.g., >15%). This proves product-market fit.
2.  **KYC Friction Rate:** `(kyc_verification_completed) / (signup_completed)`
    *   *Target:* Identify drop-off. If <60%, the verification flow is killing your unit economics.
3.  **Monetization Readiness:** `Sum(fee_charged)` from `transfer_completed` events.
    *   *Target:* Early revenue validation, even if nominal.

**Implementation Note:** Instruct your engineer to use a local queue for events 1–4 to ensure no data is lost during network interruptions, syncing when the connection stabilizes. Do not build custom dashboards yet; export raw event logs to a spreadsheet for the July 5 review to ensure data transparency.
