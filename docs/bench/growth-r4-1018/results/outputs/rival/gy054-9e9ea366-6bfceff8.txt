**Recommendation: Adopt "Weekly Active Translators" (WAT) as your North Star Metric**

Do not prioritize "pages translated per session" (depth) or raw "daily active users" (frequency) in isolation. Your data explicitly indicates that high-depth users are not your most valuable segment; rather, users with lower depth but high return frequency drive long-term value. Therefore, your North Star must be **Weekly Active Translators (WAT)**: the count of unique users who perform at least one translation action within a 7-day rolling window.

**Supporting Logic for Strategy Review**

1.  **Alignment with Retention Data:** Your observation that "users who translate fewer pages but return daily are more valuable" invalidates depth-based metrics as a primary growth lever. Optimizing for "pages per session" risks incentivizing features that encourage bulk translation in single sittings, potentially attracting power users who churn quickly once a project ends. WAT directly measures the habit formation you identified as the precursor to retention. It captures the "daily return" behavior you value while smoothing out the weekday/weekend volatility seen in your Daily Active User (DAU) counts.

2.  **Revenue Correlation:** For a browser plugin, revenue (Premium tier) is driven by utility integration into a user's daily workflow, not sporadic heavy usage. A user translating one page every day for a week is more likely to convert to Premium than a user translating 20 pages in one day and never returning. WAT isolates the users who have integrated LinguaBridge into their routine. This metric serves as a leading indicator for Lifetime Value (LTV); if WAT grows while acquisition spend remains constant, your LTV:CAC ratio improves.

3.  **Actionability for Acquisition:** Shifting focus to WAT changes how you evaluate marketing channels. Instead of optimizing ads for maximum installs or immediate heavy usage, you will optimize for campaigns that drive consistent, repeat visitors. This aligns your acquisition spending with the specific user behavior (frequency) that actually predicts revenue, preventing budget waste on users who install, use heavily once, and abandon the plugin.

**Implementation Note:** Track WAT alongside a secondary "Depth Guardrail" (e.g., average pages per active user) to ensure that while we prioritize frequency, we do not degrade the core utility quality.
