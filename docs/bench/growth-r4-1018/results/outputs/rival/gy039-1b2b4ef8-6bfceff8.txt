# StreamMax Mexico Launch: Success Metrics Plan

## Primary Success Indicators (North Star Metrics)
These metrics directly measure the core business objective of sustainable engagement.

1.  **Qualified Viewing Hours:** Total hours watched by users who complete at least two episodes of any original title. This filters out "one-and-done" traffic and validates your concern about masked low engagement.
2.  **Retention Lift:** The difference in 30-day churn rate between existing subscribers who watched the new content versus those who did not. This isolates the content's value in keeping current users.
3.  **New Subscriber Conversion Rate:** Percentage of free trial users or non-subscribers who sign up specifically after landing on a Spanish-original content page.

## Breadth of Reach (Acquisition & Awareness)
Measures how widely the content penetrates the target market.

4.  **Title Penetration Rate:** Percentage of active Mexican users who start at least one episode of the new slate within the first 14 days.
5.  **Organic Traffic Share:** Percentage of new sign-ups arriving via direct navigation or branded search (e.g., "StreamMax [Show Name]") rather than paid ads, indicating organic pull.
6.  **Trailer Completion Rate:** Percentage of users who watch the full trailer for a new title, serving as a leading indicator of intent before the launch.

## Depth of Engagement (Quality & Resonance)
Measures how deeply users connect with the content, addressing the "cultural resonance" requirement through behavior.

7.  **Series Completion Rate:** Percentage of users who finish the entire season of a new original within 30 days of starting. High completion signals strong narrative hold.
8.  **Binge Velocity:** Average time taken to consume the first three episodes. Faster consumption often correlates with higher cultural buzz and immediate relevance.
9.  **Return Viewer Rate:** Percentage of users who return to the platform within 48 hours specifically to watch another episode of the new slate.
10. **Cross-Title Discovery:** Percentage of viewers who watch more than one distinct title from the new Spanish-language slate, indicating broad appeal beyond a single hit.

## Word-of-Mouth & Social Proof (Observable Actions)
Replaces vague "brand love" concepts with trackable user behaviors that signal advocacy.

11. **Social Share Ratio:** Number of times a "Share" button is clicked per 1,000 streams, segmented by platform (WhatsApp, Twitter, Facebook).
12. **Profile Creation Rate:** Number of new user profiles created within existing household accounts specifically after launching a new title (indicates family/household expansion).
13. **Search Volume Spike:** Internal platform search queries for specific character names or plot terms relative to total active users.

## Reporting Cadence
*   **Daily (Launch Week):** Track Qualified Viewing Hours and Series Completion Rate to identify immediate drop-off points.
*   **Weekly (Month 1):** Monitor Retention Lift and Cross-Title Discovery to assess sustained value.
*   **End of Month:** Calculate total New Subscriber Conversion Rate and Organic Traffic Share for the board report.

This framework ensures the board sees not just how many people clicked play, but how many stayed, returned, and brought others into the ecosystem.

*Note: Ensure tracking events distinguish between "New" vs. "Existing" subscriber IDs at the time of playback to accurately calculate Retention Lift.*
*Note: Define "Qualified Viewing Hours" in your data dashboard now to prevent post-launch data cleaning delays.*
