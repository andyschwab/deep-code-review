# Starlight Cinemas Holiday Promo Measurement Plan

## 1. Tracking Infrastructure & Event Schema
Implement the following event structure across Email, Push, and SMS platforms to ensure source attribution persists through to purchase.

**UTM Strategy:**
*   **Source:** `email`, `push`, `sms`
*   **Medium:** `holiday-promo-2024`
*   **Campaign:** `50-off-members`
*   **Content:** `weekend-blast-{date}` (e.g., `weekend-blast-dec07`)

**Required Events:**
| Event Name | Properties | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `promo_offer_viewed` | `channel`, `user_id`, `offer_code` | Establishes exposure baseline. |
| `ticket_purchase_started` | `user_id`, `offer_code`, `full_price`, `discounted_price` | Tracks intent and potential cannibalization. |
| `ticket_purchase_completed` | `transaction_id`, `channel`, `ticket_count`, `revenue_net` | Primary conversion; calculates net revenue. |
| `concession_purchase_completed` | `transaction_id`, `item_count`, `revenue_gross`, `margin_estimate` | Tracks ancillary spend (40% margin driver). |
| `full_price_ticket_foregone` | `user_id`, `historical_avg_spend` | **Critical:** Flag users who bought discounted tickets but historically buy full-price within 48 hours of this window. |

## 2. Incrementality & Cannibalization Logic
To distinguish genuine growth from discount-driven volume, apply these three analytical filters:

**A. The "Holdout" Test (Incrementality)**
*   **Action:** Randomly exclude 5% of the 200,000 members (10,000 users) from receiving *any* promotional notification.
*   **Measurement:** Compare the purchase rate of the Holdout Group vs. the Exposed Group.
*   **Calculation:** `(Revenue_Exposed - Revenue_Holdout) / Total_Campaign_Cost`. If the lift is <20%, the channel is primarily cannibalizing existing demand.

**B. The "Baseline Shift" Analysis (Cannibalization)**
*   **Action:** Compare the Average Order Value (AOV) and Ticket Margin of purchasers during the promo window against their personal 90-day historical average.
*   **Flag:** Users whose `discounted_ticket_revenue` + `concession_margin` < `historical_avg_full_price_margin` are identified as "Value Destroyers."
*   **Metric:** Report "% of Redemptions that are Net-Negative."

**C. The Concession Attachment Rate**
*   **Action:** Track `concession_purchase_completed` linked to `ticket_purchase_completed` by channel.
*   **Hypothesis Test:** Determine if SMS drives higher urgency (ticket only) vs. Email driving higher planning (ticket + food).
*   **Metric:** `Concession_Attachment_Rate = Concession_Transactions / Ticket_Transactions` (segmented by channel).

## 3. Reporting Framework for Budget Allocation
Deliver a weekly dashboard to Carlos with these four specific views:

1.  **Channel Efficiency Matrix:**
    *   X-Axis: Incremental Revenue (vs. Holdout).
    *   Y-Axis: Concession Attachment Rate.
    *   *Goal:* Identify channels in the top-right quadrant (High Incrementality, High Ancillary Spend).

2.  **Cannibalization Ratio:**
    *   Formula: `(Discounted Tickets Sold to High-Value Historical Users) / (Total Discounted Tickets)`.
    *   *Threshold:* If >30%, pause weekend blasts for that specific segment.

3.  **Net Margin per Message:**
    *   Formula: `(Total Ticket Margin + Total Concession Margin - Channel Cost) / Messages Sent`.
    *   *Decision Driver:* Shift budget to the channel with the highest *net* margin, not highest redemption volume.

4.  **Weekend vs. Weekday Delta:**
    *   Compare redemption rates and attachment rates for weekend blasts vs. weekday controls to validate the "weekend hypothesis."
