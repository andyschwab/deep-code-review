# Q3 Campaign Measurement Plan: Frequency vs. Reach for NicheGear

## Core Tracking Structure

Since your TAM is only 5,000 professionals, you must track **individual-level exposure** rather than aggregate averages. Averages hide the fact that some prospects saw your ad once while others saw it 50 times.

### 1. Data Layer Requirements
Implement a user-level data structure that captures:
- **Prospect ID**: Hashed email or cookie ID matched to your CRM
- **Impression Count**: Cumulative ads served per prospect per week
- **Channel Mix**: Which platforms delivered each impression (LinkedIn, Google, industry pubs)
- **Time Decay**: Days since first exposure
- **Conversion Event**: Quote request, demo booking, or whitepaper download

### 2. Cohort Analysis Framework
Create weekly cohorts based on **first exposure date**, then segment by total impressions received within 30 days:

| Impression Range | Cohort Size | Quote Requests | Conversion Rate | Cost per Lead |
|-----------------|-------------|----------------|-----------------|---------------|
| 1-2 impressions | [count] | [count] | [rate] | [cost] |
| 3-5 impressions | [count] | [count] | [rate] | [cost] |
| 6-8 impressions | [count] | [count] | [rate] | [cost] |
| 9-12 impressions | [count] | [count] | [rate] | [cost] |
| 13+ impressions | [count] | [count] | [rate] | [cost] |

### 3. Diminishing Returns Calculation
Track the **marginal conversion rate** per additional impression:
- Calculate conversion rate lift from 1→2 impressions, 2→3 impressions, etc.
- Identify the inflection point where marginal lift turns negative or flat
- Monitor **ad fatigue signals**: declining click-through rates, increasing negative feedback, rising CPMs

### 4. Implementation Steps

**Week 1-2: Foundation**
- Ensure your CRM captures source/medium and first-touch attribution
- Set up UTM parameters with consistent naming (lowercase, hyphens)
- Configure Google Analytics 4 or your analytics platform to track user-level paths
- Work with your media agency to export impression-level data daily

**Week 3-8: Data Collection**
- Run campaigns with varied frequency caps across segments (test 3, 5, 7, 10 caps)
- Export weekly reports matching impression data to conversion events
- Do not optimize mid-campaign; let each frequency segment accumulate sufficient data

**Week 9-10: Analysis**
- Plot conversion rate against impression count to visualize the curve
- Calculate the optimal frequency where marginal cost per lead is lowest
- Identify fatigue threshold where additional impressions increase CPL without improving conversion

### 5. Key Metrics to Report to CEO

| Metric | Purpose | Target Insight |
|--------|---------|----------------|
| **Optimal Frequency** | Impressions yielding lowest CPL | "5-7 impressions maximizes conversions" |
| **Fatigue Threshold** | Point where CPL rises sharply | "Beyond 10 impressions, costs increase 40%" |
| **Reach Efficiency** | % of TAM reached at optimal frequency | "We can reach 65% of TAM 5+ times within budget" |
| **Time-to-Conversion** | Days from first exposure to quote request | "Average 23 days, requiring sustained presence" |

### 6. Privacy-Compliant Approach
Given the small audience and B2B context:
- Use hashed identifiers, not raw emails
- Aggregate reporting for segments under 100 users
- Ensure GDPR/CCPA compliance for EU/California professionals
- Consider LinkedIn's matched audiences for privacy-safe targeting

## Expected Outcome
By Q3 end, you will have empirical data showing exactly how many impressions drive quote requests before diminishing returns set in. This replaces assumptions with evidence, allowing you to justify frequency caps to your CEO and optimize media spend with confidence.

*Note: With only 5,000 prospects, statistical significance requires patience—expect 8-10 weeks minimum for reliable patterns. Consider supplementing with post-conversion surveys asking "How many times did you see our ads before contacting us?"*
