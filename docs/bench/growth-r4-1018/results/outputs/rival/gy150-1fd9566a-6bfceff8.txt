### Recommended Retention Framework for LinguaGo

Do not use a standard 30-day rolling retention metric. For a travel-specific utility like LinguaGo, a fixed time window artificially inflates churn by counting natural trip endings as product failure. Instead, adopt a **Trip-Cohort Retention Model** that aligns the observation window with the user's actual travel dates.

**1. Define "Active Trip" Cohorts**
Segment users by their inferred trip start date (first open in Thailand) and end date (last open before leaving). Since you lack explicit departure data, approximate the trip end as the last session before a >7-day silence during the peak season.
*   **Metric:** *Trip Completion Rate*. The percentage of users who open the app daily for at least 5 of the first 7 days of their trip.
*   **Success Criteria:** A user is "retained" if they remain active throughout their inferred stay, regardless of whether that stay is 5 days or 20 days.

**2. Measure "Post-Trip" Value Separately**
Once the trip ends, shift the metric from daily retention to **Reactivation Potential**.
*   **Metric:** *Return Visitor Rate*. The percentage of users who reopen the app within 6–12 months (aligning with annual vacation planning cycles) or engage with content about new language pairs.
*   **Actionable Insight:** If 60% of users were daily active during their trip but 0% return after 30 days, your *product retention* is actually 100% for the use case. The "drop-off" is a feature, not a bug.

**3. Re-engagement Justification for Marketing**
To justify the email campaign budget, do not argue based on improving daily active users (DAU) in the short term. Instead, frame the campaign around **Lifetime Value (LTV) extension**:
*   **Hypothesis:** Travelers who had a successful trip (high in-trip engagement) are 3x more likely to book another international trip within 12 months than the average user.
*   **Campaign Goal:** Shift the user's mental association from "Thailand Tool" to "Global Travel Companion."
*   **Measurement:** Track conversion from the email campaign to *new trip initiation* (e.g., downloading a French pack or opening the app in a new geolocation) rather than generic opens.

**Implementation Note:**
Configure your analytics to tag events with `trip_id` or `inferred_travel_window` properties. This allows you to calculate retention rates relative to `trip_start_date` rather than `calendar_date`, ensuring your Head of Marketing sees that the product works perfectly for its intended duration.

**Note:** Ensure your re-engagement emails trigger based on the *end* of the inferred trip window, not a fixed calendar date, to avoid messaging users while they are still traveling.
