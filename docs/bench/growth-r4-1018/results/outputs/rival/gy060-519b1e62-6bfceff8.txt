# GreenChain MVP Metrics Dashboard Specification

**Target Launch:** September 1 | **Audience:** Pilot Partners & Steering Committee

## 1. Core Value Metrics (The "North Star")
To address the normalization challenge across manufacturing and retail, we will not rely solely on absolute totals. Instead, we will track a tiered set of metrics:

*   **Primary Metric: Total CO2e Avoided/Reduced (tons)**
    *   *Definition:* The sum of verified emission reductions attributed to GreenChain interventions.
    *   *Usage:* High-level executive reporting.
*   **Normalized Metric: Carbon Intensity Delta (kg CO2e per unit)**
    *   *Definition:* Emissions per standardized unit relevant to the vertical (e.g., *per pallet shipped* for retail, *per kg of raw material processed* for manufacturing).
    *   *Usage:* Enables apples-to-apples comparison across industry verticals and company sizes. This isolates efficiency gains from volume fluctuations.
*   **Efficiency Metric: Reduction Rate (%)**
    *   *Definition:* `(Baseline Intensity - Current Intensity) / Baseline Intensity`.
    *   *Usage:* Demonstrates percentage improvement regardless of the starting baseline, crucial for diverse pilot partners.

## 2. Data Integrity & Robustness Indicators
To account for supplier reporting gaps without skewing totals, the dashboard will explicitly separate "Verified" data from "Estimated" data.

*   **Data Coverage Ratio (%)**
    *   *Formula:* `(Suppliers with Complete Data / Total Active Suppliers) * 100`
    *   *Threshold:* Flag accounts where coverage drops below 80%.
*   **Imputation Flag Count**
    *   *Definition:* The number of data points where values were estimated using industry averages due to missing supplier inputs.
    *   *Display:* Shown as a distinct layer on charts (e.g., stacked bar: 80% Verified, 20% Estimated). This ensures transparency; clients see exactly how much of their "savings" is hard data vs. modeling.
*   **Confidence Score (0-100)**
    *   *Logic:* A calculated score per account based on data recency, source verification level, and coverage ratio.
    *   *Usage:* If the score falls below 70, the "Total Tons Saved" metric is displayed with a caution icon, preventing over-promising on shaky data.

## 3. Dashboard View Structure

### Executive Summary View (For C-Suite)
*   **Headline:** Total CO2e Reduced (tons) [with Confidence Score indicator]
*   **Trend:** Month-over-Month % Change in Carbon Intensity.
*   **Key Insight:** "Top 3 initiatives driving reduction."

### Operational View (For Supply Chain Managers)
*   **Breakdown:** Emissions by Logistics Leg (Freight, Last Mile, Warehousing).
*   **Gap Analysis:** List of non-reporting suppliers causing data gaps.
*   **Normalization Toggle:** Switch view between "Absolute Tons" and "Intensity per Unit" to validate performance across different volumes.

## 4. Handling Data Gaps (Methodology)
*   **Conservative Estimation:** When supplier data is missing, we will apply a "penalty factor" using industry average emissions for that specific route/material, rather than a best-case scenario. This prevents inflating savings.
*   **Exclusion Protocol:** Metrics for specific sub-flows with <50% data coverage will be excluded from the "Verified Savings" total and moved to a "Pending Verification" bucket until data quality improves.
