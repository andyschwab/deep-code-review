# GearUp Discount Cohit Analysis Plan

## Executive Summary
To validate the sustainability of the 20% discount strategy, we must shift from measuring immediate revenue to measuring **Incremental Lifetime Value (LTV)**. The current assumption that discounted users will return at full price is unproven and risky. This plan isolates the behavior of discount-acquired users against a control group to determine if the initial $17 loss per order ($15 profit to -$2 loss) is recovered within 6–12 months.

## 1. Cohort Definition
We will establish two distinct cohorts based on acquisition source during the test period:
*   **Treatment Cohort:** Lapsed users who redeemed the 20% discount code.
*   **Control Cohort:** Lapsed users who received the email but did *not* redeem the code (or a holdout group if available). *Note: If no holdout exists, compare against organic reactivations from the prior 3 months, adjusting for seasonality.*

## 2. Key Metrics & Observation Windows
Track these metrics weekly for 26 weeks (6 months) and monthly up to 52 weeks.

| Metric | Definition | Why It Matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Repeat Purchase Rate (RPR)** | % of cohort making a 2nd purchase within 90/180 days. | Tests the "they will return" hypothesis directly. |
| **Time to Second Purchase** | Average days between Order 1 and Order 2. | Determines cash flow recovery speed. |
| **Full-Price Conversion** | % of 2nd purchases made *without* a discount >10%. | Validates if users accept full price or train themselves to wait for deals. |
| **Cumulative Margin** | (Revenue - COGS - Shipping - Discount) per user over time. | Identifies the exact break-even month. |
| **Return Rate** | % of items returned by cohort. | Discount seekers often have higher return rates, eroding margin further. |

## 3. Analysis Framework: The Break-Even Curve
Plot the **Cumulative Margin per User** over time for both cohorts on a single line chart.

*   **X-Axis:** Weeks since first purchase.
*   **Y-Axis:** Cumulative Profit per User ($).
*   **Starting Point:** Treatment starts at -$2; Control starts at +$15.
*   **The Crossover Point:** Identify if/when the Treatment line crosses above the Control line.
    *   *Scenario A:* Lines never cross. The discount strategy destroys value permanently.
    *   *Scenario B:* Lines cross at Week 20. The strategy is viable only if customer lifespan > 20 weeks.
    *   *Scenario C:* Treatment stays lower but narrows the gap. Calculate the "Discount Cost" vs. "Acquisition Cost" of other channels.

## 4. Implementation Steps
1.  **Data Extraction:** Query transaction logs for users tagged with the specific discount code vs. those who opened the email but didn't convert.
2.  **Attribution Tagging:** Ensure the `acquisition_source` property persists in the user profile for all future events.
3.  **Dashboard Creation:** Build a view filtering by these cohorts to track the metrics above automatically.
4.  **Statistical Check:** Run a significance test on the Repeat Purchase Rate difference between cohorts to ensure observed differences aren't random noise.

## 5. Decision Criteria for Holiday Planning
Present the findings to the CEO using these thresholds:
*   **Green Light:** Discount cohort achieves 80% of the Control cohort's full-price conversion rate AND breaks even on cumulative margin within 180 days.
*   **Yellow Light:** Break-even occurs >180 days. Recommendation: Reduce discount depth (e.g., to 10%) or restrict to high-margin categories only.
*   **Red Light:** No statistical difference in retention, or break-even >12 months. Recommendation: Halt site-wide discounts; shift budget to retention flows or non-monetary reactivation offers.

This analysis moves the conversation from "We made $120k revenue" to "We bought $120k revenue at a cost of X, with a payback period of Y."

*Ensure shipping costs are allocated per unit, not averaged, as discount users may order lower-value items with disproportionate shipping impacts.*
*Exclude users who purchased multiple times within the initial test week to avoid skewing the "repeat" metric.*
