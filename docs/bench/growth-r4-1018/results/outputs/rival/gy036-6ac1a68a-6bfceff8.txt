# Diagnostic Analysis Plan: Onboarding Simplification Impact

## Executive Summary
The divergence between rising conversion (8% → 11%) and falling retention (75% → 60%) suggests the simplified onboarding is successfully reducing friction for sign-ups but failing to set appropriate expectations or guide users to core value. The hypothesis that "lower-quality leads" are the sole cause is unproven; it is equally likely that *all* users are now missing critical setup steps previously enforced by the complex flow.

## 1. Cohort Definitions
To isolate the variable, construct these three specific cohorts from the 5,000 trials processed since September 1st:

*   **Cohort A (Pre-Change Baseline):** Users who started trials August 1–31. (Expected: Lower conversion, higher retention).
*   **Cohort B (Post-Change Churners):** Users who started trials Sept 1–30, converted to paid, but churned within 30 days.
*   **Cohort C (Post-Change Retainers):** Users who started trials Sept 1–30, converted to paid, and remain active at day 30.

*Control:* Exclude any users who entered via paid campaigns with different messaging during this period to ensure traffic source consistency.

## 2. Behavioral Patterns to Investigate
Compare the trial-period behavior of **Cohort B** vs. **Cohort C**. The gap reveals what the simplified flow is failing to trigger.

### A. The "Empty Success" Pattern
Check if users in Cohort B completed the new simplified onboarding but skipped specific "value-realization" actions that were previously mandatory steps.
*   **Metric:** Completion rate of "Core Value Event" (e.g., first data import, first report generated, first team invite) *after* payment vs. *during* trial.
*   **Hypothesis:** The old flow forced this setup pre-payment; the new flow allows users to pay before experiencing value, leading to immediate post-payment disillusionment.

### B. Time-to-First-Value (TTFV) Delta
Measure the time elapsed between "Trial Start" and "First Core Value Event."
*   **Analysis:** If Cohort B has a significantly longer TTFV than Cohort C, the simplification removed necessary guidance, leaving users confused about how to start.
*   **Threshold:** Identify if a TTFV > 48 hours correlates strongly with the churn spike.

### C. Feature Adoption Breadth
Analyze the number of distinct features used during the 14-day trial window.
*   **Pattern:** Look for "Single-Feature Users" in Cohort B who converted based on one surface-level feature but never engaged with the deeper utility required for long-term retention.
*   **Insight:** This confirms if the simplified flow attracts users with mismatched intent (looking for a quick fix vs. a platform).

### D. Support & Friction Signals
Review support ticket volume and "help center" searches during the trial period for Cohort B vs. C.
*   **Signal:** A spike in "how to [basic task]" queries among converters who later churned indicates the simplified UI hid complexity rather than removing it.

## 3. Decision Framework for Head of Growth

| Finding | Interpretation | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cohort B skipped core setup steps** that Cohort A was forced to do. | The flow is too permissive; users pay before realizing value. | **Tweak:** Re-introduce a mandatory "value checkpoint" before the upgrade button unlocks, without restoring full complexity. |
| **Cohort B has low feature breadth** compared to C. | Traffic quality or qualification is the issue; users don't need the full product. | **Tweak:** Add qualification questions or change CTA copy to better filter for power users. |
| **No behavioral difference** between B and C, yet B churns. | The product itself may have a bug or the market fit is weaker for this segment. | **Investigate:** Product stability or pricing alignment; do not revert onboarding yet. |
| **Cohort B shows high confusion signals** (support tickets). | The simplification removed necessary education. | **Revert/Rollback:** Restore specific educational steps from the old flow immediately. |

## Next Steps
1.  Query event logs to segment the 5,000 trials into Cohorts A, B, and C.
2.  Run a regression analysis on trial behaviors against the "Churned within 30 days" label.
3.  Present the top 3 behavioral predictors to the Head of Growth with a recommendation to either add a "gate" before payment or refine traffic sourcing.

*Note: Ensure your analytics window matches the 30-day retention period exactly; do not include incomplete cohorts in the rate calculation.*
*Data must be validated against bot traffic or internal test accounts before finalizing the diagnosis.*
