### Top Three Metrics for EcoStat Post-Update Health

To satisfy your board's need for concrete evidence of stickiness and reliability, and to validate your hypothesis regarding the scheduling feature, track these three specific metrics. These distinguish between passive ownership and active engagement.

**1. Weekly Scheduling Interaction Rate (Stickiness Proxy)**
*   **Definition:** The percentage of active devices where a user manually adjusts, creates, or modifies a temperature schedule at least once per week.
*   **Why it matters:** This directly tests your assumption. A user who sets a static temperature once and never returns is passive; a user who tweaks the schedule is engaging with the core "smart" value proposition. High interaction correlates with higher perceived value and lower return rates because the user has invested time in customization.
*   **Target for Series A:** Aim for >25% of active users interacting weekly. If this is low, the "smart" features are invisible, and the product is merely a remote-controlled thermostat.

**2. Cloud Sync Success Ratio (Reliability Proxy)**
*   **Definition:** The ratio of successful cloud handshakes (data uploaded/downloaded) versus total connection attempts over a 24-hour period, segmented by "active interaction" vs. "background idle."
*   **Why it matters:** This separates hardware connectivity issues from user behavior. If the ratio is high during idle times but drops during active scheduling changes, you have a latency issue that frustrates power users. If the ratio is low overall, your hardware reliability is at risk. Board members need to see that the "smart" cloud dependency is robust, not a point of failure.
*   **Target for Series A:** >98% success rate for active interactions; >95% for background heartbeats.

**3. Feature Depth Score (Engagement Quality)**
*   **Definition:** A composite score (0–3) assigned daily per device: +1 for setting a schedule, +1 for using geofencing/auto-away, +1 for viewing energy reports. Track the % of users hitting a score of 2+ per week.
*   **Why it matters:** This distinguishes the "lover" from the "background runner." A user with a score of 0 is likely indifferent (churn risk). A user with a score of 2+ is deriving multi-faceted value, making them highly unlikely to return the unit. This moves the conversation from "are they using it?" to "how deeply are they integrated?"
*   **Target for Series A:** >15% of users achieving a "Deep Engagement" score (2+) weekly within 30 days of the update.

**Implementation Note:** Ensure your firmware update logs these events locally if the cloud is unreachable, then syncs when connectivity is restored, to avoid skewing the Cloud Sync Ratio during outages.

**Board Presentation Tip:** Present these as cohort trends (e.g., "Users who hit a Feature Depth Score of 2+ in Month 1 have a 0% return rate vs. 12% for Score 0 users") to directly link engagement to financial risk reduction.
