### Recommended Adoption Measurement Framework for PeopleFirst AI Review Module

To distinguish between administrative setup and genuine engagement, you must shift your primary metric from "enablement" to "value realization." Currently, you are measuring distribution (80% enabled) rather than adoption. For an AI-driven performance review module, the following hierarchy of metrics will best predict long-term retention and guide your VP of Customer Success's intervention plan.

#### 1. The North Star Metric: "AI-Assisted Reviews Completed"
**Definition:** The count of performance reviews where the AI module generated insights that were subsequently accepted or incorporated by the manager into the final review document.
**Why it predicts retention:** This measures the core value proposition. If the AI helps complete the work, the feature becomes indispensable. Mere time spent or page views do not confirm utility; only the completion of the workflow using the AI does.
**Actionable Threshold:** Track the ratio of *Reviews Initiated* to *Reviews Completed with AI Input*. A drop-off here indicates where the friction lies (e.g., managers distrust the AI output).

#### 2. Primary Leading Indicator: "Manager Initiation Rate"
**Definition:** The percentage of managers who click "Start Review" or "Generate AI Draft" within 48 hours of the review cycle opening.
**Why it matters:** In enterprise HR, employees rarely self-serve performance reviews; they wait for managers. Therefore, employee interaction (your current 12%) is a *lagging* indicator dependent on manager behavior. Manager initiation is the true leading indicator. If managers don't start the process, employees physically cannot engage.
**Intervention Trigger:** If a client has >80% admin enablement but <20% manager initiation after two weeks, the issue is not training on *how* to use it, but communication on *why* to use it.

#### 3. Secondary Leading Indicator: "AI Acceptance Rate"
**Definition:** The percentage of AI-generated suggestions (e.g., goal drafts, competency ratings, feedback summaries) that a manager accepts without significant editing or deletion.
**Why it matters:** This validates your assumption about utility vs. training.
*   **Low Acceptance + High Usage:** The feature is broken or the AI quality is poor (Utility Issue).
*   **Low Acceptance + Low Usage:** Managers don't trust the tool or don't know how to interpret it (Training/Trust Issue).

#### Metrics to Avoid (Vanity Metrics)
*   **Time Spent in Module:** In a productivity tool, *less* time often means *more* efficiency. High time spent could indicate confusion or difficulty finding the "submit" button.
*   **Total Page Views:** This measures curiosity, not adoption. A manager can view the dashboard 50 times and never run a review.
*   **Admin Enablement Status:** As you have discovered, this is a binary switch, not a measure of human behavior.

#### Intervention Plan Logic for Customer Success
Use the data above to segment your top 50 clients into three intervention buckets:

1.  **The "Unaware" Segment (High Enablement, Low Initiation):**
    *   *Diagnosis:* Managers don't know the cycle has started or don't see the value.
    *   *Action:* Deploy executive sponsorship emails and "one-click" launch guides for leadership. Do not train on features; train on urgency and business value.

2.  **The "Skeptical" Segment (High Initiation, Low AI Acceptance):**
    *   *Diagnosis:* Managers are trying the tool but rejecting the AI output.
    *   *Action:* Targeted training on "How to edit/refine AI drafts" and case studies showing time saved. Address trust gaps specifically.

3.  **The "Adopters" (High Initiation, High Completion):**
    *   *Diagnosis:* Success.
    *   *Action:* Request case studies and referrals. Analyze their configuration to replicate settings in other clients.

**Summary Recommendation:** Stop measuring employee interaction as the primary success metric immediately. It is downstream of manager behavior. Focus your dashboard entirely on **Manager Initiation Rate** and **AI-Assisted Completion Rate**. These two metrics will tell you if the problem is workflow adoption (managers not starting) or product utility (AI not helping), allowing your CS team to apply the correct remedy.

*Note: Ensure your tracking plan distinguishes between "AI Generated" events and "User Submitted" events to calculate the acceptance rate accurately.*
