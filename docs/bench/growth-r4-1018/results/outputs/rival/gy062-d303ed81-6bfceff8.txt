# DeskBites Granular Funnel & Hypothesis Plan

## Proposed Intermediate Funnel Structure
To isolate the disconnect between supply availability and demand discovery, replace the current binary step ("menu published" → "first inquiry") with the following five intermediate events. These must be tracked with properties for `chef_id`, `office_manager_id`, `menu_id`, and `timestamp`.

| Step | Event Name | Key Properties to Capture | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | : |
| **1. Discovery** | `menu_impression` | `location_type` (search vs. featured), `position_rank` | Measures if menus are actually being seen by office managers. |
| **2. Interest** | `menu_card_clicked` | `chef_name`, `cuisine_type`, `price_range` | Validates if the listing preview (photo/title) entices a click. |
| **3. Evaluation** | `menu_detail_viewed` | `scroll_depth_pct`, `time_on_page`, `photos_viewed_count` | Determines if users engage with the full content or bounce immediately. |
| **4. Intent** | `inquiry_button_clicked` | `button_location`, `menu_price_visible` | Isolates friction specifically at the conversion action vs. content evaluation. |
| **5. Conversion** | `inquiry_submitted` | `message_length`, `attachments_included` | Confirms completed demand signal. |

## Diagnostic Hypotheses
Based on the potential drop-off points identified above, here are the primary hypotheses to test regarding the 40% friction:

**H1: The Visibility Gap (Drop-off at Step 1)**
*   **Observation:** Low `menu_impression` counts relative to active office managers.
*   **Hypothesis:** Offices are not seeing the menus due to poor search ranking, lack of notification triggers when new chefs join, or filters excluding these chefs (e.g., dietary restrictions, distance).
*   **Implication:** The issue is algorithmic or distribution-based, not photo quality.

**H2: The Trust/Preview Friction (Drop-off at Step 2)**
*   **Observation:** High impressions but low `menu_card_clicked`.
*   **Hypothesis:** The thumbnail photo quality, chef title, or price preview is unappealing or lacks trust signals (e.g., no ratings visible), causing users to skip the detail view.
*   **Implication:** Your assumption about photo quality is correct, but specifically regarding the *thumbnail*, not the gallery.

**H3: The Content Disconnect (Drop-off at Step 3)**
*   **Observation:** High clicks but low `scroll_depth_pct` or high bounce rate on `menu_detail_viewed`.
*   **Hypothesis:** Users click but leave because the detailed menu lacks critical info (ingredients, sourcing, clear pricing) or the full-size photos do not match the thumbnail promise.
*   **Implication:** The issue is content depth or mismatched expectations, requiring better chef onboarding guidelines.

**H4: The Action Barrier (Drop-off at Step 4)**
*   **Observation:** High engagement (`time_on_page`, `scroll_depth`) but low `inquiry_button_clicked`.
*   **Hypothesis:** Users are interested but hesitate due to missing trust signals at the point of action (e.g., no "verified chef" badge, unclear cancellation policy, or friction in the inquiry form itself).
*   **Implication:** The friction is psychological or UX-based at the conversion point, not related to discovery or interest.

## Implementation Priority
1.  **Immediate:** Instrument `menu_impression` and `menu_card_clicked` to validate if the problem is visibility (H1) or appeal (H2).
2.  **Secondary:** Add `scroll_depth_pct` to `menu_detail_viewed` to assess content engagement (H3).
3.  **Tertiary:** Track `inquiry_button_clicked` separately from submission to isolate form friction (H4).

This structure allows your engineering team to deploy tracking in phases, providing data-backed answers for your Series A deck within two weeks.
