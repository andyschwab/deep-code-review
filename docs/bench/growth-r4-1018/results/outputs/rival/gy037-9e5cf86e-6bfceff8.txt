# SwiftRide Referral Program: Measurement & Attribution Plan

## 1. Core Definitions & Attribution Logic

To ensure fair bonus distribution and accurate ROI calculation, we must define the "Successful Referral" event precisely. Given the two-sided nature of the marketplace (drivers needing riders), attribution should not occur merely upon signup, but upon verified economic activity.

**Attribution Trigger:**
A referral is considered "complete" and eligible for the referrer's bonus only when the **Referred Driver** meets all three criteria:
1.  **Identity Verified:** Background check and document validation passed (mitigates immediate fake account fraud).
2.  **Activity Threshold:** Completed **10 paid trips** within the first 30 days of activation.
    *   *Logic:* A single trip can be self-generated (fraud). Ten trips across different times/locations indicate genuine market participation.
3.  **Earnings Floor:** Generated at least $200 in gross driver earnings.
    *   *Logic:* Ensures the driver is economically viable and not just churning through low-value trips to hit a count.

**Attribution Window:**
The referrer is credited only if the referred driver meets the above criteria within **45 days** of the invite acceptance. This prevents indefinite liability on the budget.

## 2. Primary Success Metrics

These metrics will determine program viability and bonus budget release.

| Metric | Definition | Business Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Invitation Conversion Rate** | (Drivers who accept invite / Total invites sent) | Measures the appeal of the offer to existing drivers. |
| **Activation Rate** | (Referred drivers hitting "Attribution Trigger" / Total referred signups) | Measures the quality of recruits and effectiveness of onboarding. |
| **Cost Per Active Driver (CPAD)** | (Total Bonuses Paid + Program Ops Cost) / (Number of Referred Drivers hitting Trigger) | The true cost of acquisition compared to organic channels. |
| **Referrer Retention Lift** | (Retention rate of Referrers who invited friends vs. those who didn't) | Validates the hypothesis that referrers stay longer due to social investment. |

## 3. Measuring Incremental Value (The "Trust Endorsement")

To prove to Finance that referred drivers are more valuable than organic ones, we will compare cohorts over a 90-day window using **Matched Pair Analysis**.

**Methodology:**
1.  Select a sample of 1,000 Referred Drivers (who hit the trigger).
2.  Select a control group of 1,000 Organic Drivers who signed up in the same geographic zones and weeks.
3.  Match them based on initial vehicle type and city tier.

**Comparison Metrics:**
*   **90-Day Gross Earnings:** Total value generated for the platform.
*   **Trip Completion Rate:** % of accepted trips that are completed (proxy for reliability).
*   **Customer Rating:** Average rider rating received (proxy for the "trust endorsement").
*   **Churn Probability:** % of drivers still active at Day 90.

**Incremental Value Formula:**
*(Avg. Lifetime Value of Referred Cohort) – (Avg. Lifetime Value of Organic Cohort) = Incremental Value per Driver.*

If the Incremental Value exceeds the Bonus Cost, the program is ROI positive.

## 4. Fraud Detection & Anomaly Identification

To protect the budget, the following rules will flag accounts for manual review before bonus payout:

*   **The "Loop" Flag:** Referrer and Referred Driver share the same device ID, IP address, or bank account details.
*   **The "Ghost Ride" Flag:** Referred Driver completes trips only with one specific Rider ID (likely the referrer) or trips occur at impossible intervals (e.g., 2-minute trips back-to-back).
*   **The "Churn" Flag:** Referred Driver hits the 10-trip threshold within 48 hours and then goes inactive for 14+ days.
*   **Geographic Clustering:** >5 referrals originating from a single residential address or IP subnet.

## 5. Budget Approval Logic

The Finance Director should approve the bonus budget based on the **Break-Even Activation Rate**.

*   *Formula:* If the projected Incremental Value per driver is $300, and the bonus cost is $100, the program breaks even even if only 33% of referred drivers succeed (assuming organic acquisition cost is similar).
*   *Approval Condition:* Release funds in tranches. Initial tranche covers 10,000 invites. Subsequent tranches released only if the *Activation Rate* exceeds 15% and *Fraud Rate* remains below 2%.

This structure ensures we pay only for verified, incremental supply-side growth while minimizing exposure to fabrication.

*Note: All "Lifetime Value" calculations for the 90-day analysis must use realized revenue, not projected future earnings, to satisfy audit requirements.*
*Fraud flags should result in a temporary hold on bonuses pending manual verification, not an automatic rejection, to avoid false positives affecting legitimate drivers.*
