### Methodology to Define Activation

To identify the strongest predictor of conversion, execute a **Cohort-Based Correlation Analysis** using your existing historical data. Do not rely on assumptions like "first key creation." Instead, follow this four-step process:

1.  **Define the Cohort:** Isolate all self-serve signups from the last 12 months. Split them into two groups: those who converted to an enterprise contract within 90 days (Converters) and those who did not (Non-Converters).
2.  **Map Behavioral Events:** List every trackable action a user can take in the first 14 days (e.g., *key_created*, *docs_viewed*, *sdk_downloaded*, *calls_made*, *error_logs_viewed*, *team_member_invited*).
3.  **Calculate Conversion Lift:** For each action, calculate the conversion rate of users who performed it versus those who didn't. Crucially, segment by volume thresholds (e.g., users with 1–10 calls vs. 11–50 calls vs. 50+ calls).
4.  **Identify the "Aha" Threshold:** Look for the specific action and threshold where the conversion rate spikes significantly above the baseline. If users who make >50 API calls convert at 25% while those with <50 calls convert at 2%, then "50 API calls" is your activation event, not just "creating a key."

**Recommended Activation Definition Framework:**
Based on your data showing 40% churn after key creation, your new definition should likely be a compound metric:
*   **Primary Signal:** Volume-based usage (e.g., "100 successful API calls").
*   **Secondary Signal:** Integration depth (e.g., "Calls originating from a production IP range" or "Usage of two distinct endpoints").
*   **Timeframe:** Must occur within Days 1–7 (since Day 3 shows high drop-off).

### Instrumentation Strategy for Next Sprint

Given limited visibility behind customer firewalls, you must rely on **client-side SDK telemetry** and **aggregated metadata** rather than deep payload inspection. Instrument the following events immediately:

1.  **`api_call_executed`**: Capture count, endpoint category, and response status (200 vs. 4xx/5xx). Do not log payload content.
2.  **`integration_context`**: Capture the User-Agent string or SDK version to infer the framework (e.g., Python vs. Node.js) and environment type (if distinguishable via header patterns).
3.  **`error_resolution`**: Track if a user hits an error and then successfully retries within 1 hour. This signals active debugging and high intent.
4.  **`team_collaboration`**: Track invites sent or keys shared, as enterprise deals often involve multiple stakeholders early.

**Implementation Note:** Ensure all events include a `user_id` and `org_id` to link self-serve activity to eventual sales records. Use a customer data platform (like Segment) to route this data to both your product analytics tool (for behavior) and your CRM (for sales alerts).

**Operational Note:** Configure automated alerts for the Customer Success team when an account hits your new "Activation Threshold," triggering immediate personalized outreach before the 14-day window closes.
