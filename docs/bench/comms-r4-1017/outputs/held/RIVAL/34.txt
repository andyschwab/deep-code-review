We have two remediation paths for your approval:

**Option 1: Critical Fixes Only ($120k, 6 weeks)**
This approach addresses the highest-impact operational failures:
*   **IT Operations:** Automate database failover to meet the 4-hour target ($80k).
*   **Customer Support:** Enable 100% remote capability for the call center ($25k).

**Option 2: Full Remediation ($186k, 6 weeks)**

**Decision Required**
Please indicate whether to proceed with Option 1 or Option 2, and authorize the corresponding budget. We also need direction on whether to initiate a feasibility study for a third site.
