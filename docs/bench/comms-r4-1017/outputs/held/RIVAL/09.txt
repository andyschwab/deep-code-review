Subject: Phishing Simulation Results and Next Steps for Sales Team

The latest phishing simulation covered 2,000 emails across five departments, resulting in an overall click rate of 12%, down from 18% last quarter. Departmental breakdowns are: Engineering (5%), HR (8%), Marketing (10%), Finance (15%), and Sales (18%).

This marks the third consecutive simulation where Sales recorded the highest click rate. Under current policy, teams failing more than twice require mandatory retraining. While we have not enforced this previously, Sales has now triggered the threshold.

Three employees reported the phishing email, though we did not track their identities. The drop in the overall click rate may reflect improved awareness or the use of a less convincing template; we cannot yet distinguish between these factors.

Given the policy trigger and repeated performance issues, I recommend proceeding with mandatory retraining for the Sales team immediately. Regarding simulation cadence, we should maintain the current schedule for now but switch to more convincing templates for Sales in the next round to ensure accurate testing. We will also implement tracking for employees who report emails to recognize positive behavior.

Please confirm if you approve the retraining mandate for Sales.
