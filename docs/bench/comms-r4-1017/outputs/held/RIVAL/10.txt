**Backup-and-Restore Test Update**

Restores for the CRM and Billing systems completed successfully in under two hours, with all data integrity checks passing. However, the database restore for the Analytics system failed at 45% due to a checksum mismatch. We have not yet determined whether this stems from a corrupted backup or a storage issue.

A root-cause analysis is required but has not started. This finding impacts the decision to go live with the new backup solution next week, which rests with the IT Manager and Compliance Officer. With the test window closing on February 16, we have three days to resolve the issue.

We also observed that the Analytics system backup size is 30% larger than expected. This variance may be normal, but it warrants review. No further action has been taken yet.

Note: The go/no-go decision depends on the pending root-cause analysis.
