**To:** Marcus, CISO
**Subject:** Remediation Approval and DataSync Decision

We assessed eight vendors across security controls, data handling, incident response, and compliance. Seven vendors have submitted remediation plans; one (DataSync) has not.

**Recommended Actions**
Approve the combined remediation budget of **$182,000** ($143k vendor-side, $39k internal testing). This covers critical and high-severity fixes for the following vendors:

1.  Send a final notice requiring a remediation plan by **January 10, 2025**.
2.  If no plan is received by that date, initiate contract termination proceedings.
3.  Withhold any budget allocation for DataSync until a plan is submitted and reviewed.
