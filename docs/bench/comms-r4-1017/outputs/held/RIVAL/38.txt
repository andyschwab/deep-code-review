**Subject:** Decision Required: Market Expansion Strategy  

We need your direction on entering a new market to meet our 2027 goal of doubling revenue to $100M. The team analyzed Europe and Asia as primary candidates.

**Recommendation:** Enter the **European market** first, funded by **reallocating capital from current projects**.

**Rationale:**
*   **Feasibility:** Europe requires an $8M investment with a 2-year break-even, fitting within our $12M cash reserves. Asia demands $15M upfront and a 3-year horizon, exceeding our liquid assets and requiring external funding.
*   **Risk Profile:** While Asia offers higher growth (20% vs. 10%), it carries high competition and regulatory complexity. Europe presents moderate challenges in both areas, aligning better with our limited international experience (only one leader has foreign market exposure).
*   **Execution:** We can begin hiring and legal setup for Europe by Q2 2025 without delaying our three new product launches (Analytics, Mobile CRM, AI Support).

**Trade-offs:**
Choosing Europe means slower top-line growth compared to a successful Asia entry but preserves our balance sheet and avoids dilution from external investors. Pursuing both markets simultaneously is not viable given the $25M total resource requirement against our $12M reserves.

**Next Steps:**
Upon your approval, we will commission detailed customer preference research for Europe and initiate legal entity formation in Q2 2025. If you prefer the high-risk/high-reward Asia path or external funding, we will pivot the plan accordingly.

Please confirm your choice of market and funding approach by December 20 so we can finalize the 2025 operating plan.
