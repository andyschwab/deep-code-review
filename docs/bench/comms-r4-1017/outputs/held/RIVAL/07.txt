Subject: Project Phoenix – Amortization Schedule and Impairment Review

We capitalized $1.2M in development costs for Project Phoenix in Q1. The project is currently behind schedule; we missed the January 31 beta milestone and are now at 80% feature completion as of February 5. The remaining 20% consists of a payment module that is proving more complex than anticipated.

Regarding the amortization schedule and potential impairment: GAAP requires us to assess whether the asset is recoverable if there are indicators of impairment. While the delay is a potential indicator, we currently lack sufficient data to perform a reliable recoverability test. Specifically, we have not yet determined the discount rate to use, and the financial projections for the delayed launch need updating.

Since this delay is isolated to Phoenix and our three other projects remain on track, this appears to be a specific execution issue rather than a broader portfolio problem. Given that the audit schedule is not due until April, I recommend we wait until the next quarter to conduct the formal impairment review. This will allow us to finalize the payment module scope, update our cash flow forecasts, and agree on a discount rate.

In the meantime, we will pause any adjustments to the amortization period. If the payment module delays extend further into Q2, we will trigger the review immediately. Please let me know if you prefer to initiate a preliminary assessment now despite the data gaps.

Note: We need to schedule a brief meeting to agree on the discount rate methodology before Q2 closes.
