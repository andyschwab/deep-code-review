**Recommendation: Wait until Q1 close to trigger the impairment review for Project Phoenix; no amortization adjustment is needed yet.**

**Current Status:** We capitalized $1.2M in Q1. The beta milestone slipped from Jan 31 to Feb 5 due to complexity in the payment module (the remaining 20% of features). Three other projects remain on track.

**The Decision:**
1.  **Wait for Q1 Close:** GAAP requires assessing recoverability only when triggering events indicate the asset's carrying value may not be recoverable. A 5-day slip on one module, with 80% completion, does not yet prove the $1.2M is unrecoverable. Waiting provides the data needed for the discount rate and cash-flow projections.
2.  **No Amortization Adjustment:** Amortization begins when the asset is ready for its intended use. Since the payment module is incomplete, the asset is not yet in service; the schedule remains unchanged until launch.

**Open Item:** The discount rate for the future recoverability test is undetermined. We will draft this assumption alongside Q1 actuals.

**Timeline:** The audit schedule is due in April. Conducting the review at quarter-end ensures we have complete financial data without risking premature impairment charges.
