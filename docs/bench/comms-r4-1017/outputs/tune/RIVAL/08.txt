**Executive Summary: Q3 2024 Software License Audit**

The Q3 audit of 12 software products across four business units reveals a total spend of $4.2M, with significant discrepancies in license allocation. We currently hold 340 unused licenses valued at $180K, primarily in the CRM suite (CRM-1), while simultaneously operating with 85 unlicensed seats valued at $95K. This under-licensing creates immediate compliance exposure: Acme Software (CRM-1) and Northwind Analytics (DAP-2) retain rights to audit and levy penalties of 1.5x and 2x the license fee respectively, representing a potential liability of $202.5K. Additionally, the collaboration tool contract (COL-3) expires on December 1, 2024; failure to act before this date triggers an automatic renewal with a 10% price increase, adding $60K to annual costs. A data gap remains for 15 licenses in Business Unit C due to unavailable Q1 procurement records.

Three decisions are required by November 15. First, determine the remediation strategy for the 85 under-licensed seats: purchase them immediately for $95K, renegotiate contracts to include quarterly true-up clauses, or pursue a hybrid approach. Second, authorize the COL-3 renewal terms to avoid the scheduled price hike. Third, approve the reduction of 340 over-licensed seats to realize $180K in annual savings. Addressing the under-licensing and over-licensing simultaneously would result in a net savings of $85K, reducing the projected 2025 software budget from the current $4.5M allocation to approximately $4.115M. Inaction on the COL-3 renewal alone would increase the baseline spend to $4.26M.

Note: The $25K audit fee has already been paid. The potential penalty calculations assume an immediate vendor audit upon discovery.
