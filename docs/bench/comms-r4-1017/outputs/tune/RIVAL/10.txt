Q3 last-mile delivery costs totaled $3.8M across 2.1M stops, averaging $2.10 per stop. Significant regional variance exists: the West Coast (WC) is the most expensive at $2.65/stop due to low route density and high fuel consumption, while the Midwest is the most efficient at $1.70/stop. The Southwest (SW) faces elevated maintenance costs from an aging fleet, and the Southeast (SE) incurs higher labor costs driven by overtime. Overall on-time delivery stands at 94.5%, though WC (91.2%) and SE (92.8%) lag behind other regions. Without intervention, Q4 costs are projected to rise 5% to $4.0M due to holiday volume.

Four options are available for your decision by November 15. Option A (WC re-routing) costs $150K to implement and may lower stop costs by 8–12%, with a minor initial risk to on-time rates. Option B (SW vehicle replacement) requires a $400K investment for a 17-quarter payback period. Option C (SE driver incentives) costs $10K quarterly to reduce labor expenses by $36K quarterly. Implementing all three options projects a Q4 cost of $3.7M, a 3% decrease from Q3. Data on driver turnover and regional overhead breakdowns remains unavailable.
