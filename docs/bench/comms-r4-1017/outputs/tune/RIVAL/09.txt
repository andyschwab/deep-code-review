**Executive Summary: Customer Concentration Decision**

**Decision Required:** Choose between diversifying the customer base or doubling down on top accounts. Board decision due October 20.

**Current Exposure**
Top 5 customers drive 68% of revenue ($34M of $50M total).
*   **Customer A (25%):** Contract ends June 2025. Stable, but a 10% spend cut equals a $1.25M loss.
*   **Customer B (16%):** Contract ends March 2025. Currently negotiating with a competitor. 30% risk of non-renewal ($8M loss).
*   **Customer C (13%):** Contract ends December 2024. Includes a 30-day termination clause. Spend already dropped from $8M to $6.5M.
*   **Customers D & E (14% combined):** Contracts expire early 2025.

**Strategic Options**
Both options require a $2M investment. Current cash position is $15M.

*   **Option 1: Diversify**
    *   **Action:** Acquire 10 new mid-sized customers ($500K–$1M each).
    *   **Impact:** Reduces top-5 concentration from 68% to 55%.
    *   **Timeline:** 12–18 months.
    *   **Projected Return:** $5M–$10M new revenue.
    *   **Risk:** Execution failure; inability to secure new accounts.

*   **Option 2: Double Down**
    *   **Action:** Enhanced account management, discounts, and extended contracts for top 5.
    *   **Impact:** Increases retention probability by 10–15% per account.
    *   **Timeline:** 6–9 months.
    *   **Projected Return:** Saves $4M–$8M in at-risk revenue.
    *   **Risk:** Customers may still depart despite incentives.

**Data Gaps**
Customer satisfaction scores for the top 5 accounts are unavailable. We do not know if current service levels are driving the reduced spend at Customer C or the competitor talks at Customer B.

**Decision Point**
Approve $2M for diversification (long-term risk reduction) or $2M for retention (short-term revenue protection).

Note: Customer C's contract expires in two months; immediate action is required regardless of the strategic choice.
