**The Call**
Do not put a number in your deck. There is no single "average" runway figure for seed-stage legal-tech companies in Singapore at the time of Series A because runway is a function of your specific burn rate and cash balance, not a market statistic. Using a fabricated benchmark will undermine your credibility with sophisticated investors who expect you to know your own unit economics.

**The Numbers**
Runway is calculated as:
$$ \text{Runway (months)} = \frac{\text{Current Cash Balance}}{\text{Monthly Net Burn Rate}} $$

*   **Current Cash Balance:** [Your actual bank balance]
*   **Monthly Net Burn Rate:** [Your actual monthly operating expenses minus revenue]

Since you requested an outside-world figure ("average") rather than providing your own data, I cannot compute a result. Market benchmarks for "months of runway at Series A" vary wildly based on whether a company raised a large seed round with low growth (long runway) or a small seed round with aggressive hiring (short runway). In the current market, investors often look for **12–18 months** of post-raise runway to reach the next milestone, but the *pre-raise* runway at the moment of signing term sheets is often much lower (sometimes <6 months), which creates the urgency to fundraise. However, citing a specific "Singapore legal-tech average" without a verified source from a reputable firm (e.g., SGInnovate, BCG, or a specific year's state of the ecosystem report) constitutes fabricating data.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The input that changes your narrative is not a market average, but your **Series A readiness milestone**.
*   **Trigger:** If your current runway is <6 months, your deck must highlight imminent traction or signed LOIs that de-risk the round immediately. If >12 months, you must explain why you are raising now rather than waiting for better terms.
*   **Decision Rule:** Replace the "average" slide with a "Use of Funds" slide showing exactly how many months *your* raised capital will buy you to hit specific revenue or product milestones.
*   **This Week's Step:** If you lack a verified benchmark, consider removing the comparative stat entirely and focusing on your specific path to profitability or next valuation step.
