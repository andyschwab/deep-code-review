$$ \text{Gross Margin \%} = \frac{\text{Revenue} - \text{Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)}}{\text{Revenue}} \times 100 $$

*   Therapist payout per session (if contractors) or direct labor costs.
*   Payment processing fees.
*   Direct platform hosting costs per user (video infrastructure).
