**The numbers.** Net Revenue Retention is calculated as:
$$ \text{NRR} = \frac{\text{Starting MRR} + \text{Expansion MRR} - \text{Churned MRR} - \text{Contraction MRR}}{\text{Starting MRR}} \times 100 $$
To derive the median you requested, three specific inputs are required, all of which are currently unavailable:
1.  **Future Performance Data (2026):** Revenue figures for a future year cannot be known.
