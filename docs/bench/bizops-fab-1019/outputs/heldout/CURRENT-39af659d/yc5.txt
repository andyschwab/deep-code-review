$$ \Delta LTV = \frac{(ARPU_{bundle} - ARPU_{single}) \times GrossMargin}{Churn_{single} - Churn_{bundle}} $$

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that flips this call is **fulfillment cost complexity**. If the marginal cost to pack, ship, and support the bundle exceeds the incremental ARPU, the contribution margin collapses, and LTV decreases despite higher retention.
*   **Trigger:** If your fully loaded fulfillment cost per bundle unit exceeds 45% of the incremental price premium, the bundle destroys value.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Bundles only work if the perceived value to the customer exceeds the sum of parts by >20%, while your cost to deliver rises by <10%.
