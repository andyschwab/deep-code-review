**What flips it, and the trigger.**
The single input that changes the "wait vs. raise" decision is your **fundraising cycle length**. The standard rule of thumb is to start raising when you have 6–9 months of runway remaining, ensuring you close before hitting zero.
*   **Trigger Date:** If you maintain current burn, you hit the "start raising" threshold (9 months remaining) in **5 months** (around month 5 of your current timeline).
*   **Decision Rule:** If your typical time from first investor meeting to cash in bank is >4 months, you are already late. If it is <3 months, you can wait 2 more months to gather more traction.
*   **Step this week:** If your current pipeline has zero warm introductions to leads, consider opening conversations now to test market appetite without formally launching the round.
