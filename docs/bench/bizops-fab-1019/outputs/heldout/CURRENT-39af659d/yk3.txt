**The call.** You need 500 customers to break even; therefore, 400 customers by June is insufficient and results in a monthly loss.

**The numbers.**
Formula: $\text{Break-Even Units} = \frac{\text{Fixed Costs}}{\text{Price} - \text{Variable Cost}}$

*   Fixed Costs: $18,000
*   Price: $50
*   Variable Cost: $14
*   Contribution Margin: $36 ($50 - $14)

**Calculation:**
$$ \frac{18,000}{36} = 500 \text{ customers} $$

**Scenario Analysis (400 customers):**
*   Revenue: $20,000 (400 × $50)
*   Variable Costs: $5,600 (400 × $14)
*   Contribution: $14,400
*   Fixed Costs: $18,000
*   **Net Result:** -$3,600 (Loss)

At 400 customers, you cover only 80% of your fixed costs. You are burning $3,600/month at that volume.

**What flips it, and the trigger.**
The single input that changes the call is **volume**. The trigger is reaching **500 customers**.
*   *Rule of thumb:* If you cannot reach 500 customers within your cash runway divided by $3,600 (your burn rate at 400 units), the current cost structure is unsustainable.
*   *Action this week:* If your sales pipeline suggests <500 signups by June, consider raising the price to $56 (which lowers break-even to ~429 units) or cutting fixed costs by $3,600 immediately.
