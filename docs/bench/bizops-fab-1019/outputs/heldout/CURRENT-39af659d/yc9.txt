**Context on Pricing Models (Tier 2 Explanation)**
In B2B software for professional services (like architecture), the general rule is that pricing should align with the customer's value metric.
- *Per-Seat:* Works when collaboration is constant and headcount is stable.
- *Per-Project/Usage:* Often preferred in architecture where revenue is project-based, not headcount-based.
If your Korean prospects are churning, it is likely because their cost scales with hires (seats) while their revenue scales with projects. However, asserting this as a universal fact for "small firms in Korea" without your own data is an unverified assumption.
