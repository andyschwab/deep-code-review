**The numbers:**
Gross Margin % = `(Revenue per Box - COGS per Box) / Revenue per Box` × 100.
*   **Revenue:** Your subscription price (e.g., €X).
*   **COGS:** Sum of oil cost, bottle/packaging, fulfillment labor, and shipping to the Croatian customer.
*   **Missing Input:** Since no public dataset exists for this specific niche/market/year, you must insert your supplier quotes and logistics rates.

The call flips based on your **shipping cost per unit** relative to your price point.
