$$ \text{Payback} = \frac{\text{CAC}}{\text{Monthly Gross Margin per Customer}} $$
Where:
*   **CAC** = Total Sales & Marketing Spend / New Customers Acquired
*   **Monthly Gross Margin** = (Monthly Revenue per Customer × Gross Margin %)
