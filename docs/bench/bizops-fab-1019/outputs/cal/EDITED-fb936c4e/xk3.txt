**The call.** Your unit economics are currently unsustainable; at a 6-month payback on revenue alone, you are likely burning cash on every customer once hosting and support costs are included. Do not scale ad spend until you either lower acquisition costs or increase the initial price.

**The numbers.**
*   **CAC Formula:** Total Ad Spend / New Customers Acquired
    *   $9,000 / 30 = **$300 CAC**
*   **Payback Period Formula (on Revenue):** CAC / Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) per User
    *   $300 / $50 = **6.0 months**

*Critical Note:* This 6-month figure is based on *top-line revenue*, not profit. If your cost to serve (hosting, support, payment processing) is $10/month, your contribution margin is $40. The actual cash payback period is $300 / $40 = **7.5 months**. If margins are thinner, the payback extends further.

**What flips it, and the trigger.**
The single input that changes this verdict is **CAC**. Your current $300 acquisition cost is too high for a $50/month product unless your churn is exceptionally low (<2% monthly) and lifetime value (LTV) exceeds $900 (a 3:1 LTV:CAC ratio).
*   **Trigger:** If you cannot reduce CAC below **$150** (achieving a 3-month revenue payback) within the next **30 days**, pause this specific ad campaign.
*   **Step:** If your next $1,000 ad spend yields a CAC higher than $250, consider shifting budget to organic channels or raising prices to $75/month immediately.
