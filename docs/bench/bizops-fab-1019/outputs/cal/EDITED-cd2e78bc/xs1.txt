**The Numbers**
Since the external input is unavailable, you must compute your own margin using this formula:
$$ \text{Gross Margin \%} = \frac{\text{Subscription Price} - (\text{COGS} + \text{Packaging} + \text{Inbound Freight})}{\text{Subscription Price}} \times 100 $$

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The one input that changes your credibility is **COGS accuracy**. If your lender discovers your oil cost or freight assumptions are unrealistic for the Adriatic region, the entire model fails.
*   **Trigger:** If your landed cost per unit exceeds 55% of your sale price, your margin drops below the viable threshold for subscription retention (usually <45%).
*   **Action:** Before 9:00 AM, email your supplier for a written quote including freight to your fulfillment center. If you cannot get this today, tell the lender: "We are finalizing vendor contracts this week; our pro forma assumes 40% COGS based on preliminary quotes."
