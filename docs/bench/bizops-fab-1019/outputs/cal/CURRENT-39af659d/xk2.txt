**The call.** You have 14 months of runway; you must initiate your fundraising process immediately (this month) to avoid a cash shortfall, as your runway barely covers the 6-month raise window with no safety margin.

**The numbers.**
*Formula:* $\text{Runway} = \frac{\text{Cash Balance}}{\text{Net Monthly Burn}}$
*Inputs:* Cash = $210,000; Burn = $15,000/mo.
*Calculation:* $210,000 / 15,000 = 14$ months.

*Fundraising Deadline Logic:*
*Formula:* $\text{Start Date} = \text{Runway} - \text{Raise Duration} - \text{Safety Buffer}$
*Inputs:* Runway = 14 mo; Raise Duration = 6 mo.
*Base Case (0 mo buffer):* Start in 8 months. (Risk: Zero cash left upon close).
*Recommended Case (3 mo buffer):* Start in $14 - 6 - 3 = 5$ months ago.
*Conservative Case (6 mo buffer):* Start in $14 - 6 - 6 = 2$ months ago.

Since the recommended start date is in the past, the immediate action is required.

*Rule of Thumb:* Always maintain a 6-month cash buffer post-raise to absorb market delays or missed milestones.
