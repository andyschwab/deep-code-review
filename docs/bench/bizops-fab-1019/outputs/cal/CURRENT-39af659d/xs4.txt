**Context on the metric.**
"Usage-based pricing" in marine insurance (often tied to telematics, voyage data, or dynamic hull monitoring) is an emerging innovation rather than a standardized reporting category. Regulators like the **Finanstilsynet** (Financial Supervisory Authority of Norway) track solvency and conduct, not granular pricing model adoption rates by broker. Industry bodies like **Norske Forsikringsmegleres Forening** (Norwegian Association of Insurance Brokers) may have internal surveys, but these are rarely published with the statistical precision required for an "exact figure" on a slide.

Fabricating a number (e.g., "12%") creates immediate reputational risk if challenged by an investor or client with industry experience. It is safer to state the trend direction ("rapidly increasing") and qualify the data source ("based on industry interviews") than to present a false precision.
