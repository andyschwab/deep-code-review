**The numbers.**
*   **CAC Formula:** $\text{Total Ad Spend} / \text{New Customers}$
    *   $9,000 / 30 = \mathbf{\$300}$ per customer.
*   **Payback Period (on Revenue) Formula:** $\text{CAC} / \text{Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) per User}$
    *   $\$300 / \$50 = \mathbf{6.0}$ months.

*Assumptions & Sensitivity:*
*   **COGS Unknown:** These calculations use top-line revenue. If your cost to serve (hosting, support, payment fees) is 20% ($10/user), your *contribution margin* is $40. The true cash payback is $\$300 / \$40 = \mathbf{7.5}$ months.
