**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is **Provider Time**. Current CPT codes for psychotherapy (90832–90838) and interactive complexity require face-to-face or real-time audio/video interaction with a qualified health professional.
*   **Trigger Value:** If you can structurally guarantee a licensed therapist provides at least minimal real-time interaction or immediate availability defined by specific payer policies (often 5+ minutes), you may access existing codes.
*   **Stop/Pivot Date:** If you cannot secure a pilot agreement with a payer for "autonomous" reimbursement by **Month 6** (consuming $240k of your runway), you must pivot to a B2B2C model where clinics pay the $100/session fee directly as an operational expense, rather than relying on patient insurance reimbursement.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Insurance pays for *provider services*, not *software access*, unless the software itself is an FDA-cleared medical device with a specific benefit category.
*   **This Week's Step:** If your current contracts require therapist presence, consider drafting a "remote monitoring" addendum to test if 5 minutes of therapist review per session qualifies for billing under current Massachusetts payer policies.
