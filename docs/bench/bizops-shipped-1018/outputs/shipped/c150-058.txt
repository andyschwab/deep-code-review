**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The deal flips from "viable pilot" to "legal liability" the moment a municipality auditor or state regulator defines "10% of water savings" as a direct assignment of public utility revenue to a private entity.
*   **Trigger Value:** Any contract clause tying compensation directly to a percentage of *collected* utility revenue rather than a fee for *services rendered*.
*   **Stop/Pivot Date:** Before the next council presentation (immediate).
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If the payment source is the utility's top-line revenue stream, it looks like privatization; if the payment source is the city's operational budget line for "maintenance services," it looks like a vendor contract.
*   **This Week's Step:** If your current draft ties payment to "revenue share," consider rewriting it as a "variable service fee" capped at a dollar amount equivalent to the projected savings, payable by the city upon third-party verification of performance.
