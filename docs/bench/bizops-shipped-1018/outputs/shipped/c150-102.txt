**The Call**
Your unit economics are viable on a subscription basis ($299/mo), but your current model likely overstates early cash availability; treat the 24-month hold as a zero-revenue period for the *funeral home's* trust funds, not your SaaS fees, unless your contract explicitly ties your revenue to the release of those funds.
