**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is a **statutory exemption for "small scale" or "behind-the-meter" generation** in Colorado law.
*   *Trigger:* If Colorado statutes or Public Utilities Commission (PUC) rules explicitly exempt systems under a specific capacity (e.g., <50kW) or limit sales to the host property only, you may proceed.
*   *Rule of Thumb:* If the contract transfers *title* of the electricity to the homeowner before it crosses a meter, it is often a lease/service; if you sell kWh measured at the meter, it often looks like utility service.
