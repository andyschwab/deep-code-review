**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is the **legal characterization of the token** by the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA). If the token grants immediate utility (access to registry services) upon purchase, it is likely revenue. If it represents a future right or debt-like instrument, it may be capital.
*   **Trigger:** The moment you draft the token sale agreement.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Funds exchanged for immediate deliverable value are income; funds exchanged for ownership or future claims are capital.
*   **Step this week:** If you intend to defer tax, consider pausing the utility token sale and modeling a SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity) or convertible note structure instead, then immediately engage counsel to validate if Kenyan law recognizes these for crypto-assets.
