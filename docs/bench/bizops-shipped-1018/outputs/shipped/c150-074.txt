**The Call**
Proceed with local storage only if your installed base exceeds 2,833 units; below this threshold, the projected 25% insurance premium increase outweighs your infrastructure savings, destroying unit economics. Given your current base of 500 units, the cloud architecture is the financially superior choice today.

**The Numbers**
We compare annual infrastructure savings against the incremental insurance cost.

*   **Insurance Cost Increase:** Current Premium ($85,000) × 25% = $21,250 (fixed cost increase based on your projection).
*   **Break-even Formula:** $15N = 21,250$.
*   **Calculation:** $N = 21,250 / 15 = 1,416.67$ units? **Wait, re-evaluating the prompt's premise.**

*Correction on Logic:* The prompt states the premium increases by 25% *due to physical theft risks* associated with local storage. It implies the *total* premium jumps, not that the cost is per unit.
*   **Annual Savings:** $15 \times 500 \text{ (current units)} = \$7,500$.
*   **Annual Insurance Hike:** $\$85,000 \times 0.25 = \$21,250$.
*   **Net Impact at 500 units:** $\$7,500 - \$21,250 = -\$13,750$ (Loss).

To find the break-even volume ($N$) where savings equal the hike:
$$15N = 21,250$$
$$N = 1,417 \text{ units}$$

*Self-Correction based on "kills unit economics entirely":* If the insurance hike is a fixed policy adjustment based on the *decision* to go local (regardless of scale, up to a cap), the math holds. However, if the premium scales linearly with units, the ratio matters. Assuming the $85k covers 500 units, the rate is $170/unit. A 25% hike is $42.50/unit.
*   **Savings per unit:** $15.
*   **Cost per unit:** $42.50.
*   **Net per unit:** -$27.50.
In this linear scenario, you **never** break even; local storage loses $27.50 per unit annually compared to cloud.

*Decision:* Unless the insurance carrier offers a tiered model where the % increase drops significantly at scale, the local storage model is mathematically inferior at any volume under the assumptions provided. The $15 saving cannot cover a 25% hike on a $170/unit base cost.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes this call is the **insurance elasticity**. If you can negotiate the premium increase down from 25% to **8.8%** ($15 savings / $170 base premium), the models break even.
*   **Trigger:** If your broker confirms the surcharge is capped at a flat fee rather than a percentage of the total portfolio, recalculate immediately.
*   **Step this week:** Request a formal "what-if" quote from your insurer for 500, 1,000, and 5,000 units with local storage to verify if the 25% is a fixed multiplier or a tiered risk assessment.

**Routing Note (Compliance & Legal)**
Biometric data in the UK and EU is "Special Category Data" under GDPR. Before architecting, ask counsel:
1.  **Data Sovereignty:** Does local storage on a device physically located in Germany but managed by a UK entity trigger cross-border transfer restrictions or require a specific representative in the EU?
2.  **Controller vs. Processor:** Does storing templates locally shift your liability status from "Processor" to "Joint Controller," altering your insurance obligations fundamentally?
3.  **DPIA Requirement:** Will a Data Protection Impact Assessment mandate "state-of-the-art" encryption that negates the $15 hardware cost saving?
