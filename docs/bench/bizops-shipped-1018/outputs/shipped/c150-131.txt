**Distinction & Liability:**
*   **Internal Records:** You can legally issue these today. Employers use them to prove they trained staff, but OSHA can still cite the employer if the training was inadequate. Your liability is low if you clearly state this limitation.
*   **Federally Mandated Certifications:** These are specific to certain standards (e.g., HAZWOPER 29 CFR 1910.120). OSHA does not "certify" individuals directly; employers certify their employees based on training. However, if you claim your course *satisfies* HAZWOPER requirements without being an authorized provider, you risk being flagged for misrepresentation.
*   **Liability Exposure:** High if a worker fails an inspection and sues, claiming your course promised compliance. Your current model exposes you to this if your marketing implies federal validity.
