**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is the **presence of interpretive judgment**.
*   **The Line:** Providing factual data (e.g., "Transaction X occurred on Date Y") is generally safe software functionality. Applying law to facts to suggest a course of action (e.g., "Because of Regulation Z, you should file Form A") constitutes legal advice.
*   **The Trigger:** If your output uses words like "should," "recommend," "strategy," or "interpretation," you have crossed the line.
*   **Decision Threshold:** If the product cannot be engineered to output *only* verifiable data points mapped to specific statute text without explanatory synthesis, do not build it.
