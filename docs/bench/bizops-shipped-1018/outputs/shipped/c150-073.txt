**The Call**
Do not bundle your commission into the per-page rate in New York; structure your $25 connectivity fee and 15% commission as a distinct "platform service charge" billed separately from the statutory transcript fee to avoid violating the letter of NY rate caps. In Florida, your current model is viable, but you must explicitly separate line items on invoices to prevent market backlash over effective rates.

**The Numbers**
*Current Weekly Volume:* 8,000 pages.
*NY Max Statutory Rate:* $4.25/page.
*FL Market Rate:* ~$3.50/page.
*Platform Commission:* 15%.
*Connectivity Fee:* $25/transaction.

**Scenario A: Bundled Pricing (High Risk in NY)**
If you require the law firm to pay a single "all-in" per-page rate that includes your commission:
*   *Reporter Net (NY):* If total price = $4.25, Reporter receives $4.25 / 1.15 = **$3.70/page**.
    *   *Result:* Reporter earns below market potential; likely churn.
*   *Reporter Net (FL):* If total price = $3.50, Reporter receives $3.50 / 1.15 = **$3.04/page**.
    *   *Result:* Reporter earns significantly below market expectation; high churn risk.
*   *Violation Risk:* If you set the reporter's rate at $4.25 and add 15% on top for a total of $4.89 billed to the firm, you risk violating NY regulations if the law interprets "maximum rate" as the total cost to the consumer for the certified transcript service.

**Scenario B: Unbundled Pricing (Recommended)**
Bill the transcript at the statutory/market rate and itemize the platform fee separately.
*   *NY Invoice:* Transcript ($4.25) + Platform Fee (15% of $4.25 = $0.64) + Connectivity ($25).
    *   *Total to Firm:* $4.89 + $25.
    *   *Reporter Earns:* $4.25 (Compliant with cap).
*   *FL Invoice:* Transcript ($3.50) + Platform Fee (15% of $3.50 = $0.53) + Connectivity ($25).
    *   *Total to Firm:* $4.03 + $25.
    *   *Reporter Earns:* $3.50 (Matches market).

**Assumptions:**
1.  **ASSUMED:** NY regulation defines the "rate" as the fee paid directly to the reporter for the certification work, not ancillary administrative fees charged by a third-party platform.
2.  **ASSUMED:** The $25 connectivity fee is fixed per transaction, not per page.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that changes this call is a **NY State Board ruling or Attorney General opinion** defining "charges for a transcript" to include mandatory third-party platform fees.
*   *Trigger:* If a competitor is fined or a bar association issues guidance stating that "total cost to the firm" cannot exceed $4.25/page including all mandatory fees.
*   *Rule of Thumb:* If your platform fee is mandatory to complete the transaction, regulators may view it as part of the transcript cost.
*   *Step this week:* If your terms of service currently state the platform fee is "mandatory," consider drafting an alternative where the law firm can theoretically hire the reporter directly (bypassing the fee) to prove the fee is for optional value-add services, then consult counsel.
