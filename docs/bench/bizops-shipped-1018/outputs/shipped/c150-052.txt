**The Call**
Model two parallel tracks: a "Cash Liquidity" view for runway survival and a "Recognized Revenue" view for unit economics. Do not blend them. Your CAC payback must be calculated on *recognized gross profit*, not cash collected, or you will falsely signal an 18-month payback when the economic reality is likely under 6 months. Investors will discount a model that ignores the 24-month statutory hold; explicitly separating the "Trust Lag" from "Unit Performance" demonstrates sophisticated financial control.

**The Numbers**
*Current Snapshot:*
*   **Monthly Pre-Need Sales (Cash In):** 45 clients × $15,000 = $675,000.
*   **Trust Hold (70%):** $472,500 (Locked for 24 months).
*   **Recognizable Cash (30%):** $202,500 (Available immediately).
*   **Operating Burn:** $40,000/month.
*   **Operating Cash on Hand:** $300,000.
*   **Cash Runway:** $300k / $40k = **7.5 months** (Healthy liquidity despite revenue lag).

*CAC Payback Formula (Adjusted for Trust):*
$$ \text{Payback Months} = \frac{\text{CAC}}{\text{Monthly Recognized Gross Margin}} $$

*Assumptions (Marked ASSUMED as you did not provide CAC or Margin %):*
*   **CAC:** ASSUMED $2,000 per funeral home client (illustrative).
*   **Gross Margin on Recognized Revenue:** ASSUMED 80% (SaaS typical).
*   **Monthly Recognized Revenue per Client:** $15,000 × 30% = $4,500.
*   **Monthly Recognized Gross Profit:** $4,500 × 80% = $3,600.

*Results:*
*   **Distorted View (Using Cash In):** If you incorrectly use the full $15k or even the $4.5k cash flow without accounting for the delay, you might calculate payback based on immediate cash availability.
*   **Corrected View (Economic Reality):**
    *   Numerator: $2,000 (CAC).
    *   Denominator: $3,600 (Monthly Recognized GP).
    *   **Calculated Payback:** 0.56 months (~17 days) *once revenue recognition begins*.
    *   **Effective Payback (including lag):** ~24.6 months from sale date due to the statutory hold.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the investor narrative is **Churn**. At 4% monthly churn, your cohort loses nearly 60% of its value before the 24-month hold expires.
*   **Trigger Value:** If churn exceeds **3.5% monthly**, the Net Revenue Retention (NRR) on recognized revenue may turn negative before the trust releases funds, making the business unit-economically unviable regardless of cash flow.
*   **Stop/Pivot Date:** Calculate the cohort value at Month 24 using $V_{24} = V_0 \times (1 - 0.04)^{24}$. If this value is less than your total CAC + Service Cost, stop selling to low-quality leads immediately.
*   **Actionable Step:** If churn remains >4% next month, consider shifting pricing to a hybrid model (small upfront setup fee recognized immediately + lower monthly pre-need) to improve early cash recognition, pending legal review.
