**The Call**
Do not reject local storage based solely on the insurance hike; the $84/unit cost reduces your net margin from 8% to roughly 4.5%, which is painful but survivable. The decision hinges entirely on whether the engineering cost to build and maintain the secure cloud alternative exceeds $25,200 annually. If your cloud infrastructure and security compliance costs are higher than that annual premium increase, local storage remains the mathematically superior option despite the insurance penalty.

**The Numbers**
*Current Unit Economics (Base Case):*
*   Selling Price: $2,400
*   COGS (Manufacturing): $850
*   Gross Margin: $1,550 (64.6%)
*   CAC: $400
*   Contribution Margin (pre-overhead): $1,150

*Scenario A: Local Storage (with Insurance Hike)*
*   Additional Cost per Unit: $84 ($7,200 total annual increase / 300 units)
*   Adjusted COGS: $934 ($850 + $84)
*   Adjusted Gross Margin: $1,466 (61.1%)
*   Adjusted Contribution Margin: $1,066 ($1,466 - $400 CAC)
*   **Result:** Net margin compression from ~8% to ~4.5% (assuming fixed overheads remain constant).

*Scenario B: Cloud Storage (Break-Even Analysis)*
*   Avoided Insurance Cost: $7,200/year
*   **ASSUMED (Low):** Cloud infra + security engineering = $5,000/year → **Choose Cloud.**
*   **ASSUMED (Base):** Cloud infra + security engineering = $15,000/year → **Choose Cloud.**
*   **ASSUMED (High):** Cloud infra + security engineering = $30,000/year → **Choose Local.**

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is the **fully loaded annual cost of the cloud alternative** (infrastructure, encryption key management, SOC2/compliance audits, and devops labor).
*   **Trigger Value:** $25,200/year (or $84/unit).
*   **Decision Rule:** If (Cloud Engineering Cost) > $25,200, accept the insurance hike and store locally. If (Cloud Engineering Cost) < $25,200, build the cloud solution.
*   **Actionable Step:** This week, obtain a hard quote from your DevOps lead for the incremental annual cost of securing the cloud architecture; if the number is under $2,100/month, pivot to cloud immediately.

**Routing Note**
While the math above handles the unit economics, the choice between local and cloud storage for biometric data in Virginia may trigger specific statutory obligations under the **Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA)** or sector-specific federal regulations regarding biometric identifiers. You must route the final architectural decision to legal counsel to confirm whether "local storage" legally satisfies your duty of care for high-security data centers, as a breach could void the very insurance policy you are trying to optimize.
