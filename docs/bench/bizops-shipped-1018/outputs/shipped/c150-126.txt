**The Call:** Reject the flat $3,500 fee for all volumes; adopt a tiered pricing model based on record count with a mandatory risk surcharge for volumes exceeding 2,000 records, and do not bundle this into the annual subscription.

**The Numbers:**
Your current unit economics for a 5,000-record migration are fragile.
*   **Revenue:** $3,500
*   **Variable Cost:** $1,200 (labor/infra)
*   **Contribution Margin:** $2,300
*   **Risk Exposure:** $50,000+ (potential penalty)

At a 2% estimated probability of a compliance incident (industry baseline for complex migrations), the **Expected Loss** is $1,000 per migration ($50,000 × 0.02).
*   **Risk-Adjusted Margin:** $2,300 - $1,000 = $1,300.

While positive, this leaves no buffer for legal defense costs or reputation damage. If the breach probability rises to 5%, the expected loss ($2,500) nearly erases your margin. Small practices with $10k IT budgets cannot absorb a price hike to $6,000, but they also cannot absorb a breach.

**Proposed Tiered Structure (ASSUMED volume brackets):**
1.  **Tier 1 (0–2,000 records):** $2,200 flat.
    *   *Logic:* Lower complexity reduces breach probability and labor. Covers costs with a safer margin.
2.  **Tier 2 (2,001–5,000 records):** $4,500 flat + Mandatory Cyber Liability Endorsement ($500).
    *   *Logic:* The higher fee covers the increased labor and the higher statistical risk. The endorsement shifts the catastrophic tail risk to an insurer.
3.  **Bundling Decision:** Do not bundle. Separating the fee clarifies the value of the "safe handover" and prevents churn if a clinic decides to delay migration.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes this call is the **actual incident rate** of your migration protocol.
*   **Trigger:** If your pilot of 15 clinics results in *any* reportable privacy incident, the current cost structure is insolvent.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If the fully loaded cost (labor + insurance + expected penalty) exceeds 40% of the fee, the model breaks.
*   **Action Step:** Before signing the remaining pilot clinics, obtain a quote for "technology errors and omissions" insurance specific to data migration; if the premium exceeds $400 per job, consider Y (partnering with a specialized third-party migration vendor instead of building in-house).
