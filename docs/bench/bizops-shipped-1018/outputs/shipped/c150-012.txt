Total cost per carat (Day Shift) = $1,080 (energy) + $180 (other) = $1,260.

To maintain a 25% gross margin on day-shift stones:
Target Price = Total Cost / (1 - Margin Target)
Target Price = $1,260 / 0.75 = **$1,680 per carat**.
