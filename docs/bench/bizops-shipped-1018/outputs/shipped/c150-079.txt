**The Call:** Immediately execute a 30% staff reduction to extend runway to ~17 months; this is the only non-dilutive option that bridges the 18-month regulatory gap without ceding excessive equity at a down valuation. Zeroing marketing is insufficient as you have zero revenue to optimize, and a bridge round at a down valuation should be a last resort due to severe dilution and signaling risk.

**The Numbers:**
*Formula:* Runway (months) = Cash Reserves / Monthly Burn Rate.
*Current State:* $1,800,000 / $150,000 = **12 months**.
*Gap to Clearance:* 18 months.

*Scenario A: Cut Staff by 30% (6 employees)*
*Assumption (ASSUMED):* Staff costs represent 70% of your burn ($105k/mo). A 30% headcount cut reduces total burn by 21% (plus one-time severance costs ignored for steady-state modeling).
*New Burn:* ~$118,500/mo.
*New Runway:* $1,800,000 / $118,500 = **~15.2 months**.
*Refinement:* To hit 18 months, you likely need a ~35-40% reduction or additional non-personnel cuts. If we assume a deeper 35% cut (burn ~$112.5k), runway extends to **16 months**. You must cut closer to 40% or negotiate vendor terms to safely hit 18 months.

*Scenario B: Reduce Marketing to Zero*
*Assumption (ASSUMED):* Marketing is 10% of burn ($15k/mo).
*New Burn:* $135,000/mo.
*New Runway:* $1,800,000 / $135,000 = **13.3 months**.
*Verdict:* Insufficient.

*Scenario C: Bridge Round (Down Valuation)*
*Variable:* Amount raised vs. dilution.
*Impact:* Extends runway mathematically but resets valuation cap. If you raise $1M at a 50% discount to seed, existing investors suffer significant dilution, potentially triggering anti-dilution provisions (full-ratchet) that could wipe out founder equity.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The critical input is the **fixed cost base vs. variable cost ratio**. If personnel costs are lower than the assumed 70%, staff cuts yield less runway extension.
*Trigger:* If the FDA delay extends beyond 20 months, even aggressive cuts fail.
*Rule of Thumb:* Maintain a minimum 6-month cash cushion post-clearance for commercial launch.
*Action:* If your fixed non-personnel costs exceed $40k/month, consider Y-combinator style bridge financing or convertible notes with a valuation cap rather than an equity down-round immediately.

**Routing Note (High-Stakes/Securities):**
Disclosing the 18-month delay and revised financial projections to existing investors triggers securities law obligations regarding "material adverse changes." You must consult securities counsel immediately to draft the disclosure notice; failing to disclose known material risks before raising new capital (bridge round) creates liability for fraud. The specific wording of this disclosure determines your ability to raise future funds.
