**The Call**
Pause capitalization of new costs immediately and commission a formal impairment test; presenting untested inventory at $320k while knowingly facing >12-month regulatory delays risks a qualified audit opinion and destroys debt financing eligibility.

**The Numbers & Accounting Principles**
Under US GAAP (ASC 330 and ASC 450), inventory must be carried at the lower of cost or net realizable value (NRV). NRV is the estimated selling price minus costs to complete and sell.
*   **Current Status:** Book Value = $320,000.
*   **The Trigger:** Regulatory delay >12 months often indicates that the "costs to complete" (holding costs, additional testing, potential reformulation) may exceed the eventual recovery value, or that the asset's utility is diminished.
*   **The Calculation Gap:** You cannot determine the new book value without an **ASSUMED** recovery rate.
    *   *Scenario A (Full Recovery):* If approval is certain despite delay, NRV ≈ $320k. (Unlikely given "struggling" context).
    *   *Scenario B (Partial Impairment):* If delays introduce obsolescence or extra costs of $50k, NRV = $270k. You must write down $50k immediately.
    *   *Scenario C (Total Write-off):* If approval is uncertain, NRV = $0. You must expense the full $320k.

**Capitalization vs. Expensing**
*   **General Rule:** You may capitalize direct costs (materials, labor) only if future economic benefit is *probable*.
*   **The Flip Factor:** "Probable" is the legal/accounting threshold. If the FDA delay makes approval less than probable, you must expense costs as incurred going forward. Continuing to capitalize costs when approval is doubtful is a compliance failure.
*   **Debt Financing Impact:** Lenders typically advance 0% against R&D inventory with >12-month uncertainty. They require "quickly convertible" collateral. An un-audited or potentially impaired inventory block reduces your borrowing base to zero for this asset class.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is the **probability of approval within 18 months**.
*   **Trigger:** If your regulatory counsel cannot provide a "probable" (>70% likelihood) opinion of approval within the next 90 days, you must switch to expensing immediately.
*   **Action:** If the probability is <70%, consider halting production spend on this batch and pivoting resources to a candidate with a clearer path.

**Routing Note (Tier 3)**
This is a binding accounting determination and securities disclosure issue. You must engage your CPA firm to perform the formal impairment test under ASC 330 and draft the footnote disclosure regarding the "uncertainty of realization." Do not finalize the balance sheet or pitch deck without their sign-off, as misstating inventory value can constitute fraud in fundraising.

**Investor Presentation Strategy**
Do not hide the delay. Frame it as a conservative, compliant adjustment: "We are proactively testing inventory valuation against new FDA timelines to ensure our balance sheet reflects only realizable assets, preserving our integrity for future debt partners." This signals governance strength, not distress.
