**The Call:** Reserve **15% of monthly revenue ($6,000/month)** immediately into a restricted legal defense fund until it reaches **$150,000**, then reduce the contribution to **5% ($2,000/month)** for maintenance. Do not pay dividends or accelerate growth hiring until the initial $150,000 cap is hit.

**The Numbers:**
*   **Formula:** Target Reserve = (Estimated Cost Per Case × Simultaneous Case Buffer) + Safety Margin.
*   **Inputs:** $50,000 (consultant estimate per case) × 2 (simultaneous suits) = $100,000 base need.
*   **Assumption (ASSUMED):** Litigation costs may rise 50% due to case complexity or duration (labeled "Complexity Buffer").
*   **Calculation:** $100,000 base + $50,000 buffer = **$150,000 Target Cap**.
*   **Runway Impact:** At $6,000/month (15% of $40k revenue), you reach the cap in **25 months**.
*   **Current Position:** You have $200,000 total. After setting aside the target $150,000, **$50,000** remains for operations/growth.
*   **Burn Rate Check:** Monthly burn is $25,000. Revenue is $40,000. Net positive cash flow is $15,000.
    *   *Scenario A (No Lawsuit):* You grow cash by $9,000/month ($15k net - $6k reserve).
    *   *Scenario B (One Lawsuit):* You draw $50k from reserve. Net cash flow drops to -$35k for that month relative to the reserve, but operations remain funded by the remaining $10k net income.
    *   *Scenario C (Two Lawsuits):* You draw $100k. Reserve drops to $50k. Operations remain safe, but growth halts until reserve is replenished.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes this call is the **actual cost of defense**. If a single case exceeds **$75,000**, your current target cap becomes insufficient for two simultaneous suits.
*   **Trigger Rule:** If legal counsel quotes an estimated defense cost exceeding $60,000 for a potential claim, immediately pause all growth hiring and increase the reserve contribution to **25% of revenue** until the new target is met.
*   **Actionable Step:** If you receive a formal threat letter or a subpoena this week, consider engaging a specialized media liability attorney on a **retainer basis** immediately rather than hourly, to cap initial intake costs.
