**The Call:** Immediately implement automatic redaction of Social Security Numbers (SSNs) before any AI processing begins; do not reject files or rely on tenant consent, as both options create unacceptable operational friction and legal risk. Treat SSNs as "toxic data" that must never enter your active processing pipeline.

**The Numbers:**
*   **Current Unit Economics:**
    *   Revenue: 50 firms × $5,000/yr = $250,000/yr (~$20,833/mo).
    *   Cost of Goods Sold (COGS): 50,000 docs × $2/doc = $100,000/quarter ($33,333/mo).
    *   **Gross Margin:** $20,833 (Rev) - $33,333 (COGS) = **-$12,500/mo** (Negative).
    *   **Burn Rate:** You stated $40,000/mo. This implies ~$27,500/mo in operating expenses (OpEx) beyond direct processing costs.
    *   **Runway:** 9 months (as stated).
*   **Compliance Cost Impact (ASSUMED):**
    *   Adding a pre-processing redaction layer (e.g., specialized OCR/regex filter) typically increases compute cost by 10–20%.
    *   *Low Impact:* +$0.20/doc → New COGS $2.20/doc → Quarterly cost $110,000.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that changes this recommendation is the **false positive rate** of your redaction tool. If the tool accidentally redacts critical financial terms (e.g., rent amounts) alongside SSNs, your product value proposition ("accuracy for deal closures") collapses.
*   **Trigger Threshold:** If redaction errors exceed 0.5% of documents, pause automation and switch to a "human-in-the-loop" review for flagged files only.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** In privacy compliance, the cost of a single breach (statutory damages under NY SHIELD Act or FTC enforcement) vastly exceeds the lifetime value of your current client base.
*   **This Week's Step:** If your engineering team cannot validate a redaction library with <0.1% error rate by Friday, consider engaging a third-party compliance vendor for the filtering layer rather than building in-house, to shift liability.
