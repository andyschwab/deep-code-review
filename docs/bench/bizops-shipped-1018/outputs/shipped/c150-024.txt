**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The decision flips only if you secure the $500,000 working capital *before* changing the model.
*   **Trigger Value:** Secure $500k non-dilutive debt or equity.
*   **Stop/Pivot Date:** If you cannot commit the $500k by **30 days from now**, abort the lease plan.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Never reduce cash runway below 12 months within 6 months of a targeted raise unless the LTV increase is >3x (yours is likely <1.5x given the 3-year term).
*   **This Week's Step:** If you cannot source the $500k immediately, consider a "Rent-to-Own" hybrid at $150/month with a $500 upfront fee to preserve some cash flow while testing lease appetite.

**Routing Note:**
Yes, you need a tax advisor and securities counsel before drafting lease agreements.
*   **Tax:** Lease structures trigger different revenue recognition (ASC 606) and sales tax nexus implications in Texas compared to outright sales; a CPA must model the deferred revenue impact.
*   **Securities/Legal:** If the "lease" is economically a financing arrangement, it may have different UCC filing requirements. Counsel must draft the master lease agreement to ensure it doesn't inadvertently create a security interest that complicates your Series B IP due diligence.
