### Recommendation: Target Parents First

You should sell directly to **parents** as an empowerment tool.

While school licenses offer the allure of bulk contracts, the operational reality you described—overcrowded classrooms with 12:1 ratios—makes schools a poor initial beachhead. In such environments, teachers lack the bandwidth to adopt new digital tools, leading to the "slow adoption" you feared. Schools will view your platform as a "nice-to-have" add-on rather than a critical solution, stalling your growth.

Conversely, parents are experiencing acute pain: isolation, lack of expertise, and prohibitive therapy costs. Your value proposition (50% cost reduction and home-based support) solves an immediate, emotional, and financial crisis for them. Although the sales cycle is longer per unit, the lifetime value is higher because parents are deeply invested in their child's development over many years. By empowering parents first, you build a grassroots movement of 300+ advocates who can later pressure schools to adopt your platform, flipping the sales dynamic from "selling to schools" to "schools buying because parents demand it."
