**Positioning Statement**

**For** Operations Directors at mid-to-large EU electronics retailers
**That need** to maximize asset recovery value from returns while navigating complex EU waste regulations
**ReLoop**
**Is a** reverse logistics network
**That** converts returned inventory into recovered cash flow within 3 to 12 days, depending on your strategic priority
**Unlike** standard waste disposal services or costly internal return centers
**ReLoop** provides a dual-track infrastructure that lets you instantly choose between speed (3-day express resale for liquidity) or sustainability (12-day carbon-neutral refurbishment for compliance), ensuring you never have to sacrifice profit for planet or vice versa.

***

**Recommended Strategy:** Target the **Operations Director**. While Sustainability Officers influence policy, Operations Directors control the budget and feel the immediate pain of inventory sitting idle. By positioning ReLoop as the solution that solves *both* cash flow and compliance through flexible routing, you stop forcing prospects to choose one identity and instead sell the unique advantage of having both options under one roof.
