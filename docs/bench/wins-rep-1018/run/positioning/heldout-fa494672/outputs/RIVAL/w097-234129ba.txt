### Strategic Recommendation: Prioritize Exporters

You should prioritize **seafood exporters** for your next marketing push.

**Reasoning:** In a two-sided marketplace, the side with the higher financial stake in reliability and the greater pain from operational friction usually drives adoption. While farmers are motivated by cost (18% savings), their behavior is anchored in deep social trust with local brokers—a barrier difficult to break with an app alone. Exporters, however, face high penalties for delayed shipments and spoilage. Their current "old habit" is relying on established firms like Mekong Logistics or Saigon Transport, which guarantees safety but suffers from slow booking times (3 days). Your 4-hour booking window directly solves a critical revenue-risk problem for them. Once you secure demand from exporters, farmers will follow the orders, effectively bypassing the need to initially convince them to abandon their brokers voluntarily.
