**Positioning Statement**

**For** operations directors at mid-sized logistics firms managing distribution centers built before 1990,
**that need** to achieve autonomous efficiency without the prohibitive capital expenditure and structural disruption of new construction,
**RetroBotix**
**is a** robotics retrofit platform
**that** delivers 22% labor reduction per shift by upgrading existing manual forklifts to autonomous units.

**Unlike** AutoStore Systems and KivaDirect, which require multimillion-dollar investments in new facilities or total warehouse rebuilds,
**RetroBotix**
**provides** a permanent, high-efficiency automation layer specifically engineered for the structural limitations and floor plans of legacy infrastructure.

***

**Stress Test & Messaging Adjustment**

Your current claim—"same efficiency as new builds at half the price"—is vulnerable. Operations directors know that pre-1990 facilities have narrow aisles, uneven floors, and poor Wi-Fi, which often degrade the performance of standard autonomous solutions. Claiming "same efficiency" invites skepticism about whether you understand their specific infrastructure constraints. Furthermore, framing yourself as a "budget bridge" suggests your solution is temporary, encouraging buyers to wait for a future capital project rather than buying now.

**Adjust your messaging strategy:**
1.  **Shift from "Cheap Alternative" to "Specialized Solution":** Stop competing on price alone. Compete on *fit*. Your data shows 70% of leads are from pre-1990 buildings; this is not a limitation, it is your niche. Position new construction competitors as "ill-suited for legacy sites" rather than just "expensive."
2.  **Validate the "Permanent" Status:** Explicitly state that retrofitting is a permanent operational strategy, not a stopgap. Use the 22% labor reduction metric to prove that older buildings do not require new walls to be profitable.
3.  **Refine the Efficiency Claim:** Change "same efficiency" to "optimized efficiency for legacy layouts." Acknowledge that while a brand-new build offers theoretical maximums, RetroBotix offers the *actual* maximum achievable in an existing 1980s facility without shutting down operations for construction.

**Revised Core Message:** "Don't rebuild your warehouse to automate it. RetroBotix turns your pre-1990 facility's structural limitations into a competitive advantage, delivering proven 22% labor savings without the millions in construction costs or operational downtime required by green-field automation."
