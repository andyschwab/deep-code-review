**Core Value Proposition**
LupusLink enables healthcare providers to deliver specialist rheumatology care to rural Honduras without the prohibitive costs of patient travel or the risks of unguided self-medication, turning a 14-week wait into a 3-day consultation.

**Supporting Proof Points**
1.  **Drastic Access Improvement:** Reduced average consultation wait times from 14 weeks to just 3 days for 120 pilot patients in Olancho and Francisco Morazán.
2.  **Clinical Outcomes:** Achieved a 40% increase in medication adherence by replacing dangerous over-the-counter steroid use with guided specialist care.
3.  **Resource Efficiency:** Eliminates the need for patients to travel six hours to Tegucigalpa for ten-minute visits, maximizing the impact of the region's only 8 specialists.
