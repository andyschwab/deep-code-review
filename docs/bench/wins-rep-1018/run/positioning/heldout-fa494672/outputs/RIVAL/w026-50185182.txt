### Value Proposition

**For** risk-averse HR Directors at major Colombian enterprises (e.g., Bancolombia, Grupo Éxito)
- **that need** to reduce absenteeism and retain high-performing talent without triggering reputational stigma or signaling a "drug problem" within their workforce
- NuevaVida
- **is a** confidential workforce resilience and recovery benefit
- **that** restores employee productivity and loyalty through proven peer-support networks while maintaining absolute organizational anonymity

### Differentiation Statement

- **Unlike** generic Employee Assistance Programs (EAPs) like Sura or internal counseling that offer short-term advice but fail to sustain long-term recovery
- NuevaVida
- **provides** a specialized, outcomes-driven peer ecosystem with a verified track record of 850 successful recoveries, framing addiction support as a strategic retention asset rather than a public liability
