### Message Hierarchy for ExportaFacil

**1. Primary Positioning Statement (The Core)**
"For **individual artisan exporters and small agricultural co-ops in Brazil** making their first international shipments, who **need to navigate complex customs regulations without risking costly delays or lost documents**, ExportaFacil is a **digital customs brokerage platform** that **reduces paperwork processing time from 14 days to 2 days**. Unlike **legacy firms like GlobalTrade Brasil and manual local despachantes**, we **provide a guaranteed error-free process at one-third the cost (R$ 400 vs. R$ 1,200) through a streamlined digital-only workflow.**"

**2. Target Audience Clarification**
*   **Who it is for:** First-time exporters who are price-sensitive and process-averse. This includes both individual artisans and small co-ops.
*   **Strategic Note:** Do not split your message yet. Both segments share the exact same primary pain: fear of bureaucracy and high costs associated with "legacy" players. Your messaging should focus on the *status* (first-time exporter) rather than the *industry* (agriculture vs. artisan) until you have data showing divergent needs.

**3. The Old Method vs. The New Way**
*   **The Old Way (Competitors):** Relying on GlobalTrade Brasil or local despachantes means paying premium fees (R$ 1,200), waiting two weeks for paperwork, and accepting the risk of human error (lost documents) with no transparency. It requires physical meetings and opaque processes.
*   **The ExportaFacil Way:** A fully digital onboarding that cuts processing time by 85% (down to 2 days), eliminates document loss through automated validation, and lowers costs by 66%. It offers total visibility without the need for physical office visits.

**4. Addressing the "Digital-Only" Trust Barrier**
Your concern about trust is valid for first-time exporters, but your metrics are your solution.
*   **The Counter-Narrative:** "Physical offices do not prevent lost documents; modern software does."
*   **Proof Points to Lead With:**
    *   **Track Record:** Highlight the "45 first-time exporters onboarded last quarter with zero errors." This specific statistic proves reliability better than a physical address ever could.
    *   **Speed as Trust:** Emphasize that the 14-day delay common with incumbents is often where documents get lost. Your 2-day turnaround *is* the security feature.
    *   **Risk Reversal:** Since you charge significantly less (R$ 400), the financial risk for a skeptical customer to try you is low compared to the incumbent. Frame the low price not as "cheap" but as "efficient pricing made possible by removing overhead."

**5. Tactical Messaging Pillars**
*   **Headline:** "Export Your First Shipment in 2 Days, Not 2 Weeks."
*   **Sub-head:** "The digital brokerage for Brazilian artisans and co-ops. Zero lost documents. R$ 400 flat fee."
*   **Trust Signal:** "Join 45 first-time exporters who cleared customs last quarter with 100% accuracy."
*   **Call to Action:** "Start your digital clearance now—no office visit required."

By anchoring your message in the specific outcome (2 days, zero errors, low cost) rather than the mechanism (digital platform), you turn your lack of a physical office from a liability into proof of your efficiency.

*Note: Consider adding a "live chat with a specialist" feature to mimic the human reassurance of a physical office without the overhead.*
*Focus your next marketing test on the "first-time" angle rather than splitting between artisans and co-ops.*
