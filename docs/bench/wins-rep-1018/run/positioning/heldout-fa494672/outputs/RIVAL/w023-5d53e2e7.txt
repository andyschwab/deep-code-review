### Strategic Recommendation: Double Down on the School Sector

Before presenting the positioning statement, a strategic choice must be made. Given your current traction (200 school licenses) and the specific, measurable outcome (50% reduction in incidents), the **school sector** is your strongest wedge. While home users have higher willingness to pay, the "free YouTube" alternative makes consumer acquisition costly and churn high. In contrast, schools face a tangible operational crisis (classroom disruption) that free apps cannot solve. Positioning as a "clinical adjunct" is too narrow; instead, position as an **operational necessity for classroom management**. This allows you to leverage existing teacher advocacy to drive volume, while parents may eventually adopt it via school recommendation rather than direct cold marketing.

### Geoffrey Moore Positioning Statement

**For** special needs educators and school administrators in inclusive classrooms
**that need** to manage sensory overload during high-stress transition periods without relying on scarce 1:1 occupational therapy support
**SensoryCalm**
**is a** tablet-based behavioral regulation platform
**that** reduces classroom disruption incidents by 50% through evidence-based, immediate sensory interventions
**Unlike** generic calming apps or passive video content used at home
**SensoryCalm** provides educator-controlled, curriculum-aligned protocols proven to stabilize groups during critical daily transitions.

***

**Note:** This positioning explicitly targets the "operational pain" of schools rather than the emotional desire of parents, making budget approval easier despite tighter funds.
