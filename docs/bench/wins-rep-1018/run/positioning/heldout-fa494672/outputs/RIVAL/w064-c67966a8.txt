### Comparative Analysis & Recommendation

**Segment A: Military Contractors**
*   **Pros:** High willingness to pay; acute, recognized stressors; existing contracts often include mental health stipends; culturally accustomed to confidential, specialized support systems.
*   **Cons:** High churn due to rotation schedules (short-term residency); strict external compliance requirements that may conflict with local data laws; perception of "outsider" services may limit trust building.

**Segment B: Local Government Employees**
*   **Pros:** Stable, long-term residency aligns with your 85% retention metric; deep community ties mean successful treatment has a multiplier effect; high need for discretion due to the "everyone knows everyone" dynamic.
*   **Cons:** Slower sales cycles; budget constraints compared to private contractors; higher sensitivity to local gossip if privacy is breached even once.

**Recommendation: Focus Exclusively on Local Government Employees**
While military contractors offer immediate revenue, their transient nature conflicts with the therapeutic continuity required for deep psychological work. More critically, the core barrier you identified is **confidentiality within a close-knit community**. Local government employees live permanently in this environment; their fear of exposure is highest, but so is their relief when a truly private solution is found. By solving the privacy paradox for this group, you build an unassailable moat. Military contractors can be served later as a secondary stream, but dominating the local institutional market first secures your reputation as the "safe harbor" for the islands' leadership.
