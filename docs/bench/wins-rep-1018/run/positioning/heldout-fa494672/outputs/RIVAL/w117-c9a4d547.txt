## Value Proposition

**For** logistics directors at European cargo airlines
- **that need** to secure sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) supply without exposing their bottom line to volatile spot prices or unverified carbon claims
- SkyGreen
- **is a** guaranteed price-cap booking platform
- **that** stabilizes fuel costs within a 5% premium cap while providing audit-ready compliance data for EU regulations

## Differentiation Statement

- **Unlike** direct bilateral contracts with fuel suppliers or general carbon offset marketplaces like CarbonClear
- SkyGreen
- **provides** immediate financial certainty through a hard price ceiling, eliminating the risk of paying excessive premiums for unverified environmental attributes

***

**Strategic Recommendation:** Position SkyGreen primarily as a **cost-stabilization tool**. Since 60% of your buyers prioritize cost, leading with the "5% price premium cap" addresses their immediate pain (volatility) and builds the trust required to sell the compliance benefit. Framing it as a "compliance necessity" first may trigger price resistance before you can demonstrate value. Use the compliance angle as the validation that the fuel is real, but make the price cap the reason they sign today.
