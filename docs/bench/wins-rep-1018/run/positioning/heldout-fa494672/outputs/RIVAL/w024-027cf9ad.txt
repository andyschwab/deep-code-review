**Positioning Statement for RecoverPT**

**Value Proposition**
**For** hospital IT directors and medical administrators at mid-to-large urban hospitals in Lisbon
**that need** to modernize stroke rehabilitation without disrupting legacy electronic health records or compromising data security
**RecoverPT**
**is a** tele-rehabilitation integration platform
**that** delivers a 20% faster patient recovery time through guided home exercises while ensuring seamless, non-invasive compatibility with existing hospital data silos.

**Differentiation Statement**
**Unlike** standalone patient apps or rigid legacy modules that require costly infrastructure overhauls and create isolated data pockets
**RecoverPT**
**provides** a pre-validated, lightweight integration layer that unifies clinical outcomes with current hospital records, satisfying IT security protocols while immediately improving medical efficacy.

***

**Message Hierarchy for Hospital Administration**

To win over the administration, you must address the IT director's fear first to unlock the door, then pivot immediately to the Medical Director's desire for better outcomes. Do not choose one audience; sequence the argument.

1.  **The Hook (For IT Directors): "Zero-Friction Integration"**
    *   *Argument:* "We do not replace your legacy systems; we sit lightly on top of them."
    *   *Proof:* Highlight the pilot success in the 3 adopted hospitals where no data silos were created. Emphasize that the 12 hesitant hospitals are the only ones missing out on efficiency, not the other way around.
    *   *Goal:* Remove the "No" based on technical fear.

2.  **The Value (For Medical Directors): "Proven Clinical Acceleration"**
    *   *Argument:* "Once integrated, our platform is the only tool proven to accelerate stroke recovery by 20% compared to standard care."
    *   *Proof:* Cite the specific pilot data regarding recovery times and patient adherence to home exercises.
    *   *Goal:* Create the "Yes" based on patient outcomes and hospital reputation.

3.  **The Close (For the CEO/Admin): "Operational Efficiency"**
    *   *Argument:* "By solving the IT compatibility issue, we unlock immediate clinical gains without capital expenditure on new infrastructure."
    *   *Goal:* Align both departments toward a single decision: adoption reduces risk and improves metrics simultaneously.

**Note:** Positioning as purely "technical" ignores the revenue driver (patient outcomes); positioning as purely "clinical" ignores the gatekeeper (IT). The winning narrative is "Clinical excellence enabled by invisible technology."
