**Positioning Statement for GlycoLink**

**Value Proposition**
**For** tech-savvy Type 1 diabetics aged 18–35
**that need** granular visibility into glucose trends without the friction of manual logging or expensive clinic visits
**GlycoLink**
**is a** smart insulin delivery ecosystem
**that** automatically syncs dose data to the cloud, enabling precise lifestyle optimization and real-time trend analysis.

**Differentiation Statement**
**Unlike** traditional reusable pens (NovoPen) and bulky digital add-ons (Capsule)
**GlycoLink**
**provides** a seamless, all-in-one hardware solution that eliminates manual entry errors and offers superior data continuity for independent self-management, rather than just dose tracking or parental monitoring.

***

**Strategic Recommendation:** You must target **independent young adults (18–35)** exclusively. Your current traction (450 users in this demographic) validates product-market fit here, whereas pivoting to "anxious parents" contradicts your existing user base, requires a completely different sales cycle (clinician/parent-led vs. direct-to-consumer), and ignores your core strength as a "lifestyle optimization" tool. In France, where insurance coverage for students is often limited, marketing to young professionals as a tool for *autonomy and career performance* (via better glucose control) is a more defensible niche against low-cost legacy pens than competing on "safety" against established medical device giants.
