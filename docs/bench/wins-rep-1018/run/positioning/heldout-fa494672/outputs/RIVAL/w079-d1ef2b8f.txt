### Recommendation: Target Private Clinics First

Based on your current traction and the need for sustainable revenue, **SolHydra should target private clinics first.**

While the Ministry of Health represents a massive scale opportunity, public sector sales cycles in emerging markets are notoriously long, bureaucratic, and budget-constrained. Relying on them as your primary buyer risks burning through your runway before a contract is signed.

Private clinics, conversely, feel the pain immediately and financially. When patients skip dialysis due to heat exhaustion, clinics lose direct revenue and face higher long-term churn. Your data showing a **15% reduction in emergency room visits** translates directly to **retained revenue** for these clinics. They have the autonomy to make purchasing decisions quickly and a clear incentive to pay for a tool that keeps their patients compliant and healthy. Once you have a profitable, scalable model with private clinics, you can use that case study to negotiate a broader public health contract with the Ministry.
