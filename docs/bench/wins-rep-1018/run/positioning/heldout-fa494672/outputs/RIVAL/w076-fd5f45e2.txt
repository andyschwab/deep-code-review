### Segment Comparison: Ministry of Health vs. Anxious Parents

**1. Ministry of Health (Cost-Savings Tool)**
*   **Primary Motivation:** Reducing systemic burden and long-term complication costs (the 25% regional complication rate) while optimizing limited public health budgets.
*   **Biggest Barrier to Sale:** Extremely long sales cycles involving bureaucracy, budget approval periods, and pilot program requirements. As a self-funded entity, you cannot survive the 6–12 month procurement timeline typical of government contracts.
*   **Revenue Speed:** Slow. Payment often occurs months after service delivery or contract signing.

**2. Anxious Parents (Peace-of-Mind Guarantee)**
*   **Primary Motivation:** Immediate emotional relief from fear of complications and the practical elimination of the "brutal choice" between expensive transport ($150/year savings) and skipping life-saving care.
*   **Biggest Barrier to Sale:** Trust in a new tele-health provider and upfront cash flow constraints for the poorest families. However, the proven $150 annual travel savings directly offsets the service cost, making the value proposition immediately tangible.
*   **Revenue Speed:** Fast. Transactions can happen at the point of care or via monthly subscriptions, generating immediate cash flow.

### Recommendation

**Target Anxious Parents immediately.**

With only six months of runway, your survival depends on speed to revenue. Selling to the Ministry of Health is a strategic long-term goal, but it is a fatal distraction right now; the sales cycle will almost certainly exceed your remaining cash reserves.

Positioning as a "peace-of-mind guarantee" for parents allows you to monetize the immediate $150 travel savings you already prove. Parents are currently paying *something* (either in transport costs or health risks); you are simply offering a cheaper, safer alternative they can access today. This direct-to-consumer approach bypasses bureaucratic gatekeepers, enabling you to collect revenue within weeks rather than quarters. Once you have secured a larger base of paying families and extended your runway, you can then approach the Ministry of Health with leverage and proven outcomes to negotiate a broader contract.
