**For** logistics directors at premium perishable goods fleets (seafood, dairy, pharma)
**that need** to eliminate spoilage losses and guarantee shelf-life compliance across variable Iberian weather conditions
**FreshRoute Spain**
**is a** temperature-aware route planning platform
**that** dynamically prioritizes cargo freshness over pure speed to reduce waste by up to 20%

**Unlike** standard GPS apps (Waze) that optimize only for arrival time, or generic telematics (Verizon Connect) that track rather than prevent spoilage

***

**Strategic Note:** Your draft ("prioritizes shelf life over speed") risks alienating managers by implying a trade-off they don't want; reframe it as "optimizing for *saleable* arrival," which inherently includes speed but prevents costly waste. Targeting premium distributors is the stronger play because fuel savings (15%) are easily replicated by competitors, whereas guaranteeing the integrity of high-margin, time-sensitive cargo creates a defensible moat that generic navigation tools cannot address.
