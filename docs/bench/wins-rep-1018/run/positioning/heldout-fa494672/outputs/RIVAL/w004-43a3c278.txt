**Value Proposition**

**For** middle-class Indian families managing a loved one's heart failure
- **that need** to avoid the catastrophic financial shock of emergency hospitalization (avg. 25,000 INR) caused by missed early warning signs at standard clinics
- HeartGuard
- **is a** preventive health insurance layer
- **that** converts unpredictable, high-cost medical crises into a manageable, fixed monthly investment while reducing emergency admissions by 40%

**Differentiation Statement**

- **Unlike** reactive local clinic visits that appear cheap (300 INR) but frequently miss fluid buildup until expensive emergency care is required
- HeartGuard
- **provides** continuous, data-driven early detection that guarantees financial predictability and prevents the 80x cost spike of a single cardiac crisis
