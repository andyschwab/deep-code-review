**Value Proposition**

**For** Chronic Fatigue Syndrome (CFS) patients in Liechtenstein and their supporting clinicians
**that need** to manage limited energy reserves without triggering post-exertional malaise caused by "push-through" fitness advice
**PaceRight**
**is a** specialized pacing and symptom intelligence platform
**that** replaces harmful activity goals with adaptive algorithms that balance daily exertion against real-time symptom data to prevent crashes and reveal personal energy patterns.

**Differentiation Statement**

**Unlike** generic fitness trackers (e.g., Fitbit) that encourage increasing step counts or paper diaries that offer no analytical insight
**PaceRight**
**provides** a protective, evidence-based pacing engine that actively discourages overexertion while generating clinically relevant trend reports for doctor-patient review, all without requiring formal medical device certification.
