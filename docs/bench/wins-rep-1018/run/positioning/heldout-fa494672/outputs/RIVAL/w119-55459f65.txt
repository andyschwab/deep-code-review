**Positioning Statement**

**For** safety and logistics managers at mining transport firms
**that need** to guarantee oversized load compliance on complex Australian routes to avoid contract-losing fines and delays
**OutbackTrack**
**is a** heavy haulage compliance platform
**that** eliminates the risk of permit violations and axle weight breaches by enforcing route-specific restrictions in real-time

**Unlike** generic GPS fleet solutions like GeoFleet or manual check-in processes
**OutbackTrack**
**provides** specialized regulatory intelligence that actively prevents costly compliance failures rather than merely tracking vehicle location after the fact.

***

**Strategic Recommendation:**
You must position OutbackTrack as a **compliance shield (risk avoidance)**, not an operational efficiency tool. Your prospect's recent loss of a contract due to a bridge weight limit proves that the pain point is existential, not just inconvenient. Generic tools fail because they treat all trucks the same; they lack the specific data on permit routes and axle restrictions that define your market. When selling to safety managers, "efficiency" sounds like a nice-to-have that can be cut during budget tightening, whereas "preventing contract loss and massive fines" is a mandatory insurance policy. To justify the 40% price premium, your message must explicitly frame the cost of your competitor not as their subscription fee, but as the price of one single failed inspection or lost bid. Stop competing on "tracking"; compete on "certainty."
