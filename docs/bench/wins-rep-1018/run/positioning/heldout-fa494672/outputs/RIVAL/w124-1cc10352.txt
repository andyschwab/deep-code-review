## Value Proposition

**For** small Western European exporters lacking dedicated logistics teams
- **that need** to eliminate cross-border compliance risks and replace unreliable manual email chains with guaranteed visibility
- EastWest Haul
- **is a** digital cross-border freight marketplace
- **that** secures compliant Eastern European capacity instantly, reducing administrative overhead by 90% while providing real-time shipment tracking from pickup to delivery

## Differentiation Statement

- **Unlike** traditional freight forwarders (e.g., Kuehne+Nagel) and local broker networks reliant on disjointed email threads
- EastWest Haul
- **provides** a unified digital command center that automates customs documentation and offers live fleet visibility, ensuring border crossings are managed proactively rather than reactively

***

**Strategic Choice Note:** We targeted small exporters because they feel the pain of "compliance headaches" and "manual emails" most acutely; large retailers already have internal teams to manage these complexities.
**Positioning Angle:** We positioned as a "reliability engine" rather than just a cost-cutter, as trust in compliance and visibility is the primary barrier to switching from legacy brokers.
