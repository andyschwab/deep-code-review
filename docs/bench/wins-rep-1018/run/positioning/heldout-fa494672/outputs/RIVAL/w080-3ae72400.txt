### Value Proposition: Operational Efficiency Focus
**For** humanitarian campaign managers leading mass vaccination drives in low-resource settings
**That need** to detect and contain adverse events instantly to prevent campaign stoppages and public panic
**VaxGuard Haiti**
**Is a** rapid-response tele-immunology monitoring platform
**That** reduces adverse event detection time from 48 hours to under 2 hours using basic feature phones, ensuring campaigns continue safely without waiting for hospital data.

### Differentiation Statement
**Unlike** paper-based logbooks or complex hospital systems like DHIS2 that rely on stable internet and delayed reporting cycles
**VaxGuard Haiti**
**Provides** immediate, offline-capable visibility into safety signals via simple SMS, allowing managers to make real-time containment decisions before rumors spread or regulators intervene.

***

**Stress Test Results:** Your claim of "real-time safety" resonates significantly stronger with the **desire for faster campaign completion** than fear of liability. NGOs funders prioritize *continuity of operations*; a halted campaign due to unverified rumors is their primary nightmare. Positioning as a "safety compliance tool" invites scrutiny and comparisons to rigid regulatory standards you may not yet meet. Positioning as an "operational efficiency engine" frames your speed as the mechanism that *enables* safety, directly addressing the funder's goal of maximizing doses administered per day without interruption.

**Revised Message Hierarchy for Grant Proposal:**
1.  **Primary Hook:** Uninterrupted Campaign Velocity (We keep the lines moving).
2.  **Supporting Proof:** 2-hour detection vs. 48-hour national average (Speed prevents panic).
3.  **Enabler:** Zero-infrastructure deployment via feature phones (Works where others fail).
4.  **Outcome:** Higher total vaccination coverage through reduced downtime.
