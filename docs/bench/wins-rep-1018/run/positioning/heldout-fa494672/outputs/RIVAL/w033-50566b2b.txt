### Strategic Recommendation: Target the "Immediate Access" Segment

Do not position SafeHaven Connect as a "charity alternative" or a "stopgap." Framing your service as a last resort for the uninsured undermines clinical trust and limits your market ceiling. Instead, position yourself as the **superior, faster alternative for anyone requiring immediate, culturally competent mental health support.**

Your differentiation is not *who* you serve (refugees vs. residents), but *how* you serve them (speed, language access, and consistency). By narrowing your focus to "uninsured refugees," you commoditize your service as welfare. By expanding to "any resident needing fast, multi-language care," you capture refugees (who need speed and language) alongside Turkish residents and expats (who need speed and specific linguistic matches). Your 48-hour wait time and 5-language capability are premium features, not charity concessions.

**Target Decision:** Expand to include any Turkish resident needing fast, multi-language care.
**Core Positioning:** Speed and Cultural Competence, not Charity.
