**Recommended Positioning: The Efficiency Engine**

You should position SomnusFlow strictly as an **efficiency engine that reduces administrative overhead**, not as a compliance tool.

**The Rationale:**
Your data indicates that clinic owners are currently losing 15 minutes per booking due to workarounds in MedBook CY and Excel. For a clinic managing over 3,200 appointments monthly, this inefficiency represents a massive, tangible operational cost. Clinic directors are skeptical of migration costs; a "compliance" pitch adds perceived risk (fear of fines) without offering immediate financial relief. An "efficiency" pitch, however, offers a direct ROI: the time saved by eliminating workarounds pays for the migration almost immediately. Compliance is a hygiene factor they expect; efficiency is the economic driver that justifies the switch.

**Top Three Value Propositions for Clinic Directors:**

1.  **Recover 800+ Hours of Staff Time Monthly**
    "Unlike MedBook CY, which forces staff to waste 15 minutes per appointment on manual workarounds for sleep study cycles, SomnusFlow automates these specific workflows. For a clinic handling 3,200 appointments, this instantly recovers over 800 hours of administrative labor every month, allowing your team to focus on patient care rather than data entry."

2.  **Eliminate the "Hidden Tax" of General Practice Systems**
    "Stop paying for a general practice system (MedBook CY) that requires expensive custom Excel sheets to track CPAP machines and sleep cycles. SomnusFlow is built specifically for sleep disorder workflows, removing the need for fragile spreadsheets and reducing booking errors caused by fragmented data entry."

3.  **Migration That Pays for Itself in Week One**
    "We understand the fear of switching costs. Our specialized onboarding ensures that the 15 minutes you currently lose on every single booking is reclaimed immediately upon go-live. The operational savings from your first week of appointments cover the transition effort, making the switch a net-positive financial decision from day one."

*Note: Lead with the "800 hours" metric in all initial conversations; it converts the abstract problem of "workarounds" into a concrete financial loss they can feel.*
*Note: Mention compliance only as a secondary benefit of having accurate, specialized data, not as the primary hook.*
