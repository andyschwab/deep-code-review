**Value Proposition**

**For** family caregivers in Barbados managing a relative's chronic liver disease
**that need** to strictly adhere to complex sodium and protein restrictions without the stress of sourcing safe, locally available ingredients
**GreenLeaf Barbados**
**is a** medically compliant meal logistics platform
**that** guarantees dietary safety by automating meal planning with ingredients verified for immediate local delivery.

**Differentiation Statement**

**Unlike** generic global nutrition apps like NutriWorld (which ignore local inventory) or static clinic pamphlets (which lack execution support)
**GreenLeaf Barbados**
**provides** a closed-loop system that bridges the gap between medical prescription and grocery reality, ensuring 100% of planned meals are actually cookable in Barbados today.

***

**Strategic Resolution:** You must position as a **lifestyle convenience app for caregivers**, not a tool for hepatologists. Doctors prescribe the *diet*, but they do not manage the daily *logistics*. Your 80% retention proves your value lies in solving the "what's for dinner?" anxiety for the person doing the shopping and cooking, not in providing medical advice. The medical specificity is your *credibility*, but the local grocery integration is your *differentiation*.
