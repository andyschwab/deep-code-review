**Selected Positioning Angle: Clinical Safety Net**

While saving time and money is a powerful secondary benefit, positioning GrowWell primarily as a "logistics solution" commoditizes your service and invites competition from general telehealth platforms or travel subsidies. Your defensible moat is the **90% adherence rate** and the mitigation of clinical risk. In growth hormone therapy, consistency is efficacy; missed appointments directly threaten the child's long-term health outcomes. Therefore, you must position as the **clinical safety net** that guarantees treatment success, where reduced travel is the *mechanism* that enables the *outcome* of adherence, not the primary product itself.

***

### GrowWell French Guiana Positioning Statement

**For** families in French Guiana with children undergoing growth hormone therapy
**that need** to maintain strict monitoring schedules to ensure treatment efficacy without enduring prohibitive travel burdens
**GrowWell**
**is a** specialized tele-endocrinology platform
**that** replaces the risky choice between costly monthly trips to Cayenne and skipped appointments with a remote monitoring protocol that sustains 90% adherence rates
**Unlike** the status quo of infrequent, high-burden hospital visits that force families to compromise on care frequency
**GrowWell provides** a clinically validated safety net that brings specialist oversight to the home, ensuring therapy success while eliminating the need for 40% of physical travel.

***

*Note: This statement anchors your value in medical outcomes (adherence/efficacy) rather than convenience, making it harder for competitors to replicate and more compelling for insurance reimbursement.*
