**For** adults with drug-resistant epilepsy seeking regained independence
**that need** reliable advance warning to prevent injury and reduce anxiety
**SeizureGuard**
**is a** predictive seizure management wearable
**that** provides a critical 15-minute warning window to secure safety before an event occurs
**Unlike** reactive detection devices (like Empatica) that only alert after a seizure has started
**SeizureGuard** enables proactive intervention, accepting a managed rate of false alerts to deliver life-saving lead time that reactive systems physically cannot provide.

***

**Recommended Initial Buyer:** Pilot exclusively with **neurologists at Helsinki University Hospital** rather than direct-to-consumer. Patients currently lack the clinical context to tolerate 3 false alarms weekly; they interpret them as "broken tech." Neurologists, however, can frame these alerts as "high-sensitivity screening" and guide patients on how to use the 15-minute window effectively. This B2B2C approach validates the medical utility and builds the necessary trust framework before scaling to independent patients.
