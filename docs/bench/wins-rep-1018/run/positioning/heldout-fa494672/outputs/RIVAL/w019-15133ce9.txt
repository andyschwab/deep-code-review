## Value Proposition

**For** cost-conscious patients in Buenos Aires and Córdoba seeking routine vision correction
- **that need** affordable, immediate access to prescription updates without the hassle and high cost of in-person chain visits
- VistaClara
- **is a** remote telemedicine vision service
- **that** delivers certified prescriptions at 40% lower cost with a 65% consultation-to-prescription conversion rate, eliminating unnecessary travel and wait times for stable vision needs

## Differentiation Statement

- **Unlike** traditional optical chains like Ópticas Modernas and Visión Total that require expensive, time-consuming in-person visits for all services
- VistaClara
- **provides** a proven, low-cost remote alternative specifically for routine checkups, allowing patients to bypass the clinic entirely for standard prescriptions while reserving physical exams only for complex medical cases

***

**Strategic Note:** Do not position as a "triage step" for competitors; this admits their superiority and reduces your valuation. The data (65% conversion, 3,200 patients) proves you are a **replacement** for routine care, not a feeder. Your "enemy" is the inefficiency and high cost of the physical chain model for simple prescriptions.
