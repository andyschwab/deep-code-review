### Comparative Analysis: Speed vs. Cost Efficiency

**Option A: Near-shoring Speed (Fashion Angle)**
*   **Strengths:** Directly addresses the pain point of QuickNorth (high cost) while matching their speed. Fashion brands have high inventory carrying costs and short product lifecycles, making a 2-day window critical for reducing markdowns.
*   **Weaknesses:** Limits your total addressable market to time-sensitive verticals. If fashion trends shift or brands reduce cross-border volume, revenue becomes volatile.
*   **Verdict:** High margin, high churn risk.

**Option B: Domestic Cost Efficiency (Home Goods Angle)**
*   **Strengths:** Appeals to high-volume, low-margin categories where every cent counts. Home goods have stable demand.
*   **Weaknesses:** You cannot beat CentroDist on pure real estate cost if they are deep inland. Your differentiator is actually *reliability*, not just cost. Selling on "cost" when your true advantage over the low-cost leader is "no customs delays" creates a confusing message.
*   **Verdict:** Commoditized positioning; hard to defend against pure low-cost players.

**Recommendation: The "Reliable Speed" Lane (Hybrid Dominance)**
Do not choose between "fast/expensive" and "slow/cheap." Your unique structural advantage is the **2-day delivery at a mid-market cost point**. You are not "near-shoring" (Tijuana) nor "deep domestic" (Central Mexico); you are the **optimized cross-border bridge**.

Trying to claim both "lowest cost" and "fastest speed" simultaneously will sound unfocused. However, you *can* dominate the middle ground: **"Predictable Cross-Border Velocity."** This replaces the *trade-off* itself. Your target is not just fashion or home goods; it is **brands for whom customs unpredictability is the primary growth blocker**. This includes fashion (needs speed) and home goods (needs volume without customs gridlock).

**Definitive Positioning Statement**

**For** US-based e-commerce brands scaling into Latin America
**that need** to eliminate the trade-off between delivery speed and customs reliability
**GoBorder Mexico**
**is a** cross-border fulfillment partner
**that** guarantees 2-day delivery to Mexico City by integrating customs clearance directly into the transit window
**Unlike** near-shoring warehouses like QuickNorth that charge premium real estate rates for speed, or domestic centers like CentroDist that suffer 5-day customs delays
**GoBorder Mexico**
**provides** a unified logistics corridor that delivers near-shore velocity at domestic cost structures, ensuring inventory moves freely without border friction.
