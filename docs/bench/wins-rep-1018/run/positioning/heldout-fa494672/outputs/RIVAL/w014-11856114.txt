### Top Three Landing Page Messages for OrthoGuard

**1. "Your Recovery Data Stays on Your Phone—Never in the Cloud."**
*Why it works:* This directly addresses the primary friction point (GDPR fears) by contrasting OrthoGuard with cloud-dependent wearables like MoveSense. It validates the user's privacy concern immediately without using confusing legal jargon. By specifying "on your phone," it makes the abstract concept of data sovereignty tangible and reassuring, signaling that the patient retains full ownership and control, which is critical for the Dutch market.

**2. "Clinically Validated Accuracy Without the Wearable Intrusion."**
*Why it works:* This message bridges the gap between the low-tech paper diary (trusted but inaccurate) and high-tech wearables (accurate but invasive). It reassures hesitant patients that choosing privacy doesn't mean sacrificing medical quality. By highlighting "clinically validated," it leverages your existing hospital partnerships to prove credibility, while "without the wearable intrusion" subtly critiques the competitor's hardware requirement, positioning your camera-based solution as both superior and less invasive.

**3. "Trusted by Dutch Orthopedic Surgeons to Replace Error-Prone Paper Diaries."**
*Why it works:* This creates a clear "before and after" narrative. It acknowledges the current standard (paper diaries) but frames it as flawed ("error-prone"), creating a need for a better solution. Crucially, it anchors trust in a familiar authority figure—their surgeon—rather than in technology alone. This alleviates the fear that a "local-only" app is isolated from medical oversight; instead, it positions OrthoGuard as the modern, preferred tool *of* the medical establishment, making the switch feel safe and recommended.

*Note: These messages prioritize privacy as the differentiator while using clinical validation to prevent the product from seeming "unmonitored." Test these headlines against your 15% drop-off rate to measure conversion improvements.*
