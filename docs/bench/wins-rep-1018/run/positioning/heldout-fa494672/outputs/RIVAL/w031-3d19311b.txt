### Strategic Recommendation: The Convenience Play

Stop targeting "budget-conscious families." Your data indicates that trust in physical stores is your primary barrier, not price sensitivity. Families dealing with immediate mobility crises for aging parents value speed and reliability over a 30% discount. A 2-day wait from MedCare Hellas is unacceptable when a parent needs to leave the hospital or navigate a home immediately.

Your 4-hour delivery window is your defensible moat; price is easily copied by deep-pocketed incumbents. Position Helios Move as the **urgent care solution for mobility**, serving urban children in Piraeus and Athens who need to solve a problem *now* without the friction of visiting a store. You are replacing the habit of "driving to a medical supply store and waiting for inventory checks" with "tap-and-deliver."

### Positioning Statement

**For** adult children in Athens and Piraeus managing immediate mobility needs for aging parents,
**that need** to secure reliable scooters or walkers within hours rather than days,
**Helios Move**
**is a** hyper-local rental marketplace
**that** delivers verified mobility equipment to your door in under 4 hours.
**Unlike** traditional medical supply stores like MedCare Hellas and OrthoCenter,
**Helios Move**
**provides** same-day access without requiring families to visit physical locations or navigate high-deposit bureaucracy.

***

*This positioning shifts your narrative from "cheaper" to "faster and easier," directly attacking the incumbents' weakness (speed) rather than their strength (trust).*
*Focus your marketing on the emotional relief of solving a crisis instantly, not the financial savings.*
