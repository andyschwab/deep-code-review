**For** EU fashion brand CEOs managing cross-border returns
**that need** to unlock trapped inventory capital and reduce working capital drag
**ReturnHub**
**is a** rapid-return processing center
**that** gets returned garments back on sale in 48 hours, accelerating cash flow by 7x compared to internal logistics.

**Unlike** slow internal warehouse loops that tie up capital for 14+ days or liquidation partners like BulkDispose that sacrifice 85% of item value,
**ReturnHub**
**provides** the optimal balance of high-value recovery (65%) and immediate liquidity, turning a cost center into a rapid revenue engine.

***

**Strategic Rationale:**
While a 65% recovery rate is strong, the 3x speed advantage (48 hours vs. 14 days) is your defensible "chasm-crossing" wedge; CEOs prioritize cash flow velocity and inventory agility in volatile fashion markets over marginal gains in recovery percentage.
Positioning on "Recovery" invites direct comparison with liquidators on price alone, whereas positioning on "Speed" reframes the conversation around working capital efficiency, where your 48-hour turnaround is unmatched by both slow internal teams and fast-fire liquidators.
