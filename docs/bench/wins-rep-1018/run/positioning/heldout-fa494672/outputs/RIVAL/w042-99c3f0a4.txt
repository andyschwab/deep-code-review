**Recommendation: Position as a Premium Companion for Existing Wearable Owners**

Given your current constraints, high churn, and the specific economic realities of the Moroccan market, you should immediately pivot to positioning **MigraineFree Morocco** exclusively as a premium analytics layer for existing wearable owners (Garmin, Apple Watch, Fitbit users). Do not bundle a low-cost sensor.

**Strategic Rationale & LTV Analysis**

The "Bundled Sensor" strategy presents a fatal flaw in your unit economics. Introducing hardware requires significant upfront capital expenditure (CAPEX) for inventory, logistics, and warranty support. In a price-sensitive market like Morocco, even a "low-cost" sensor creates a high-friction entry barrier compared to free manual apps. Furthermore, hardware margins are typically thin; you would likely subsidize the device to gain users, dragging down your Lifetime Value (LTV) while increasing churn risk if the hardware fails or feels cheap. Your suspicion is correct: the market is not ready for a new hardware dependency when users already own capable devices.

Conversely, the "Premium Companion" strategy leverages assets your 650 subscribers already possess.
*   **Acquisition Cost:** Near zero for hardware. You are selling software only.
*   **LTV Potential:** Higher. Users who own $300+ Garmins are already qualified as high-intent spenders. They have demonstrated a willingness to invest in their health data. By solving their specific pain point (manual entry fatigue) without asking for new hardware, you reduce friction.
*   **Churn Mitigation:** Your current 15% churn is likely driven by the "barrier to entry" you identified. By removing the requirement to buy *your* hardware and instead supporting the devices they *already* trust, you align with their existing habits. The value proposition shifts from "buy our tool" to "unlock the hidden potential of your current watch."

**Winning Messaging Framework**

To convert this higher-value segment, use the **Geoffrey Moore Positioning Statement** to sharpen your differentiation against manual apps and generic tracker dashboards.

*   **For** chronic migraine sufferers in Morocco who already own advanced wearables (Garmin/Apple Watch),
*   **That need** to understand the biological triggers of their attacks without the fatigue of manual logging,
*   **MigraineFree Morocco** is a specialized correlation engine,
*   **That** automatically synthesizes sleep and HRV data to predict and explain migraine episodes,
*   **Unlike** standalone apps like Headache Log (which require tedious manual entry) or native wearable dashboards (which lack migraine-specific context),
*   **Our product** provides automated, clinical-grade trigger insights by leveraging the hardware you already wear.

**Implementation Note**
Focus your product roadmap on expanding compatibility with the top 3 wearables used in Casablanca and Rabat rather than developing hardware. Use your current subscriber data to identify which specific device models correlate with the lowest churn and target your marketing specifically to those user bases.

**Final Note**
This approach validates your business model with software margins before ever considering hardware risks.
