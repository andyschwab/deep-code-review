## Value Proposition

**For** US-based Etsy sellers moving over 200 orders monthly who struggle to manage complex shipping rules across multiple carriers
- **that need** to automate label generation and consolidate discounts without manual data entry or carrier-hopping
- ShipSwift
- **is a** multi-carrier shipping automation platform
- **that** reduces fulfillment time by 70% and guarantees the lowest net cost per shipment through intelligent carrier selection

## Differentiation Statement

- **Unlike** Pirate Ship (which requires manual label creation) and the native Etsy shipping calculator (which limits carrier choice and lacks advanced rule logic)
- ShipSwift
- **provides** fully automated, rule-based batch processing that selects the optimal carrier for every individual order, justifying the $29 fee by saving 10+ hours of manual labor and unlocking deeper volume discounts unavailable to casual users.

***

**Note:** This positioning explicitly targets the high-volume seller; the "casual crafter" is not your viable market given the $29 price point against free alternatives. Your differentiation is not "discounts" (which carriers match) but "automation at scale."
