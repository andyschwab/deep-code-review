### Message Hierarchy to Address the "Empathy vs. Algorithm" Doubt

To convince parents to trust MilkMate over waiting, your messaging must reframe the product not as a *replacement* for human care, but as the *preserver* of the opportunity to receive it.

**1. Primary Hook: Time is Tissue (The Cost of Waiting)**
*   **Argument:** "Pain unmanaged for 72 hours often leads to stopped breastfeeding. You don't need to replace the human expert; you need to survive the wait until you see them."
*   **Tactic:** Visualize the wait time. Contrast "3 days of suffering" vs. "3 seconds of relief." Position the algorithm not as a doctor, but as an emergency splint.

**2. Secondary Proof: Specificity Over Sentiment**
*   **Argument:** "Empathy comforts you, but correct positioning stops the pain. Our AI analyzes your specific latch mechanics instantly, providing the technical adjustment a human would eventually give, just without the delay."
*   **Tactic:** Acknowledge the emotional gap ("We know we can't hold your hand") but pivot immediately to the functional win ("But we can fix the latch right now so you can rest").

**3. Closing Reassurance: The Handoff Promise**
*   **Argument:** "MilkMate doesn't close the door on human care; it keeps it open. We stabilize the situation so that when your appointment arrives in three days, you are still breastfeeding and ready for advanced support."
*   **Tactic:** Frame MilkMate as the ally of the national health system, not its competitor. You are the "ER Triage" that ensures the patient survives until the "Specialist" is available.

**Summary Tagline for Campaigns:**
*"Don't wait three days in pain. Get the latch right now, so you're still breastfeeding when the expert calls."*

*Note: This positioning works because it validates the user's doubt (AI lacks empathy) while making that doubt irrelevant to the immediate emergency (pain requires speed, not just comfort).*
*Ensure all AI advice includes a disclaimer to seek human help for complex medical issues to maintain trust and safety compliance.*
