**Recommendation:** Target the **frustrated mid-market forwarders** first.

Selling directly to shipping lines (SeaGuard/OceanLink) is a long-cycle enterprise sale where you must convince incumbents to cannibalize their own proprietary lock-in. They have little incentive to integrate an open API that reduces their control. Conversely, mid-market forwarders are currently bleeding money due to dwell times and lack the leverage to demand better tools from shipping lines. They feel the pain of "manual spreadsheets" and "basic portals" daily. Your 18% dwell time reduction is an immediate ROI they can calculate, making the switching risk acceptable compared to the certainty of current losses. This segment offers a faster path to revenue because the buyer (Operations Director) has the budget autonomy to solve their own operational inefficiencies without waiting for shipping line approval.
