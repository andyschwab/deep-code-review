### Value Proposition

**For** hospital administrators at King Edward VII Memorial Hospital and private cardiac clinics in Bermuda
- **that need** to reduce costly post-surgical readmissions without overwhelming nursing staff with false alarms or adding technical burdens to elderly patients
- HeartHome
- **is a** remote patient monitoring service
- **that** cuts readmission rates by 30% through continuous, automated vital sign tracking that filters noise to alert doctors only when clinically necessary

### Differentiation Statement

- **Unlike** weekly manual check-ins or standalone pharmacy cuffs (e.g., from Watsons) that leave data siloed and families guessing
- HeartHome
- **provides** a closed-loop clinical system that integrates directly with hospital records and uses smart-alarm technology to eliminate user anxiety over false alerts, ensuring peace of mind for families and actionable data for doctors.

***

**Strategic Note:** Your primary buyer is the **hospital administration**. While families feel the anxiety, they rarely hold the budget or authority to integrate medical devices into clinical workflows; they are the *influencers*, but the hospital holds the *purse strings* to solve the readmission cost problem. To overcome the family's fear of complexity, your messaging must emphasize "silent monitoring" where the technology works invisibly in the background, requiring zero interaction from the elderly patient unless a doctor calls.
