**Positioning Statement Recommendation**

**For** small tourist resort owners in Palawan
**that need** to ship fresh supplies reliably without the chaos of manual phone coordination
**SailFast Logistics**
**is a** digital inter-island ferry cargo booking platform
**that** guarantees 20% faster booking times and consistent delivery schedules for time-sensitive tourism inventory

**Unlike** traditional manual agents like Tondo Freight or informal captain direct-deals (Barko Connect)
**SailFast Logistics**
**provides** a dedicated, premium digital infrastructure that eliminates communication breakdowns and ensures supply chain certainty for high-value tourism operations.

***

**Strategic Decision:** You should position strictly as a **premium service for the tourism supply chain**.

**Reasoning:** Your current traction validates this path. You already have 45 active users in this specific niche who value reliability over rock-bottom pricing. The data shows adoption among general merchants is flat because they are price-sensitive and currently locked into the informal "Barko Connect" network, where you cannot win on cost. Trying to compete with informal captain direct-deals on price is a race to the bottom that will burn your cash reserves. Instead, double down on the segment that already loves your ease of use and has a critical business need (fresh supplies for tourists) that justifies a premium for reliability.

**Note:** Do not pivot to general merchants until you have dominated the tourism niche and can leverage that scale to lower costs. Your differentiation is certainty, not cheapness.
