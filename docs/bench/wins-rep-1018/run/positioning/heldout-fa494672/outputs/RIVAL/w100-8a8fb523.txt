**Positioning Statement for SoyVision**

**Value Proposition**
**For** yard managers at Brazilian soy export cooperatives
**that need** to visualize container stacking in real-time without complex training or disconnecting from the dusty yard floor
**SoyVision**
**is a** visual yard management system
**that** replaces physical whiteboards with a zero-training digital interface, reducing container re-handling by 30% and increasing loading speed by 15% during peak harvest.

**Differentiation Statement**
**Unlike** generic warehouse tools like StockPro or LogiTrack that require extensive setup and fail to handle seasonal volatility
**SoyVision**
**provides** an immediate, intuitive visual replacement for manual tracking that delivers measurable efficiency gains from day one without changing existing workflows.

**Stress Test & Strategic Advice**

Your draft phrase, "The digital whiteboard that thinks," is risky. While "digital whiteboard" correctly anchors the product in the user's current mental model (solving the "too complex" fear), the suffix "that thinks" may trigger skepticism. To a manager who distrusts technology, "thinking" implies black-box algorithms, loss of control, or hidden complexity. It sounds like marketing fluff rather than a practical tool.

**Recommendation on Framing the Transition:**
Do not position SoyVision as a "high-tech optimization engine" versus a "simple replacement." This is a false dichotomy that confuses the buyer. Instead, frame the transition as **"The Whiteboard, Upgraded."**

1.  **Anchor in Familiarity:** Explicitly state that the interface mimics the physical whiteboard they already trust. The transition is not about learning new software; it is about gaining "superpowers" (real-time data, historical tracking, collision alerts) on a canvas they already understand.
2.  **Highlight the "Zero-Training" Promise:** Your strongest asset is the claim that it requires no training. Make this the primary differentiator against StockPro and LogiTrack. Those competitors sell "features"; you sell "continuity with benefits."
3.  **Reframe the Benefit:** Instead of saying the tool "thinks," say it "prevents mistakes before they happen." This shifts the narrative from abstract AI to concrete risk reduction, which resonates deeply with managers responsible for costly re-handling moves.

**Revised Tagline Suggestion:** "SoyVision: The familiar whiteboard, now with 30% fewer moves." This keeps the comfort of the old method while promising the specific outcome you have proven with your 12 cooperatives.
