### Strategic Recommendation: Lead with Comfort, Validate with Cost

To secure the insurer partnership by Friday and overcome hospital administrator hesitation, you must **position SenNoce as a comfort-focused solution for anxious patients**, using cost savings as the secondary validation for the payer.

**Why this angle drives faster adoption:**
Hospital administrators fear losing authority and revenue. If you lead with "cost-saving," you confirm their fear: you are a cheaper substitute threatening their budget and relevance. They will block you to protect their labs (like Centrum Snú).

However, if you lead with **patient comfort and completion rates**, you become an ally. You solve a problem they cannot fix: the 60% no-show rate. By framing SenNoce as the tool that ensures *their* referred patients actually get diagnosed (92% completion vs. 40% completion at labs), you enhance their clinical outcomes without directly attacking their revenue line initially. The insurer gets the cost benefit automatically, but the hospital gets to save face and improve care metrics.

### Message Hierarchy for Your Pitch Deck

**1. Primary Hook (The "Why Now"): The Diagnosis Gap**
*   **Message:** "60% of patients referred for sleep apnea never get diagnosed due to hospital anxiety and logistical barriers. SenNoce closes this gap."
*   **Proof:** 92% completion rate vs. industry standard 40-60%.
*   **Audience:** Hospital Directors & Insurers.

**2. Core Value Proposition (The "What"): Comfort-First Diagnostics**
*   **Message:** "SenNoce is a home-testing solution that allows anxious patients to complete accurate apnea screening in their own beds, removing the intimidation of clinical sleep labs."
*   **Benefit:** Higher adherence, faster time-to-diagnosis, reduced patient stress.
*   **Audience:** Patients (direct) & Hospital Directors (as a patient-care enhancer).

**3. Secondary Validation (The "Bonus"): System Efficiency**
*   **Message:** "By shifting routine screenings home, we reduce the burden on hospital labs like Szpital Kliniczny, reserving expensive in-lab slots for complex cases while saving the insurance system ~950 PLN per test."
*   **Proof:** 250 PLN (SenNoce) vs. 1,200 PLN (Hospital average).
*   **Audience:** Insurers (primary) & Hospital Directors (framed as resource optimization, not cost-cutting).

### Specific Pitch Angles

**For Hospital Directors (The Partnership Pitch):**
"Do not view SenNoce as a competitor to Centrum Snú. View us as your triage filter. Currently, you lose 6 out of 10 referred patients before they are even tested. We ensure 9 out of 10 complete the test. Let us handle the routine, anxiety-inducing screenings at home so your lab staff can focus on complex, high-reimbursement cases that truly require your facility. We make your referral network more effective."

**For Individual Patients (The Empathy Pitch):**
"Skip the hospital wait and the unfamiliar environment. Get tested in the comfort of your own bed with the same clinical accuracy. Join the 1,500 patients in Warsaw who have already completed their test without the stress of a clinic visit."

**For the Insurer (The ROI Pitch):**
"We deliver a 92% diagnostic completion rate at 20% of the current cost. By partnering with SenNoce, you stop paying for 'no-shows' and start paying for completed diagnoses, saving nearly 1,000 PLN per patient while accelerating treatment initiation."

**Final Note:** Do not lead with the price difference to hospitals; it triggers defensiveness. Lead with the completion rate as a quality metric they are currently failing to meet.
