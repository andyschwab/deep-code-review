**Positioning Statement for CoolTrack**

**Value Proposition**
**For** mid-sized pharmaceutical exporters in Southeast Asia
**that need** to prevent catastrophic revenue loss from heat-spoiled vaccines and insulin during transit
**CoolTrack**
**is a** real-time cold-chain intervention system
**that** stops spoilage before it happens by alerting drivers instantly, protecting millions in goods rather than just recording failure after the fact.

**Differentiation Statement**
**Unlike** manual logs and basic data loggers (e.g., FreezeCheck, LogItEasy) that only document temperature breaches for post-shipment analysis
**CoolTrack**
**provides** live SMS alerts that enable immediate corrective action, turning a potential 15% total loss into a saved shipment.

***

**Strategic Recommendation:** Target **mid-sized firms worried about product loss** first. Large enterprises are often bogged down by long procurement cycles focused on "compliance checkboxes," where the cheapest compliant option wins. Mid-sized firms feel the pain of a single spoiled shipment immediately; losing $50,000 in insulin hurts them far more than a large conglomerate. Your $200 sensor is an easy sell if you frame it as "insurance that pays for itself after saving just one partial pallet," whereas compliance is viewed as a cost center.

**Message Hierarchy for the Skeptical Procurement Manager:**
1.  **Reframe the Cost:** Do not compare $200 vs. $20. Compare $200 vs. $50,000 (the value of a spoiled shipment). The competitor's $20 device is actually expensive because it guarantees 15% loss; your device is cheap because it prevents loss.
2.  **Shift from "Recording" to "Saving":** Explicitly state: "FreezeCheck tells you your money rotted last week. CoolTrack tells your driver to fix the AC *today* so the money arrives safely."
3.  **The Math of Intervention:** Show a simple calculation: If they ship 10 times a year with a 15% spoilage rate, they lose 1.5 shipments annually. If one shipment is worth $40,000, they lose $60,000/year. Your solution costs $200/unit. Even with 10 units, the cost is $2,000. The ROI is immediate and undeniable.
