## Value Proposition

**For** adult children in rural Serbia who worry about their aging parents' isolation and safety
- **that need** a reliable way to ensure their parents receive timely hearing care without forcing them into exhausting travel or admitting dependency
- HearWell
- **is a** remote audiology support service
- **that** restores family peace of mind by delivering professional hearing aid adjustments within 24 hours via the basic mobile phones seniors already own

## Differentiation Statement

- **Unlike** the traditional requirement to travel two hours to Belgrade for clinic visits
- HearWell
- **provides** immediate, dignity-preserving care at home that eliminates physical strain and reduces monthly family care costs by 400 dinars

***

**Note:** This positioning targets the *payer* (adult children) while selling the *outcome* (dignity/safety) that resolves the senior's resistance. It frames the product not as "medical tech" but as a solution to the family's guilt and logistical burden.
