## Value Proposition

**For** low-income expectant and new mothers in rural Kenya with no internet access
- **that need** reliable, immediate guidance on prenatal care and infant nutrition but cannot afford the travel costs or time required for distant clinic visits
- MamaLink
- **is a** mobile health triage and education companion
- **that** delivers medically sound, life-saving advice via basic SMS to keep mothers and infants healthy between infrequent clinical visits

## Differentiation Statement

- **Unlike** relying on traditional community elder advice (which is accessible but often medically unsafe) or traveling to distant public clinics (which are accurate but frequently inaccessible due to cost and distance)
- MamaLink
- **provides** the critical intersection of medical accuracy and immediate accessibility, acting as a safe, always-available bridge that prevents minor issues from becoming emergencies without claiming to replace professional emergency intervention.

***

*Note: This positioning intentionally frames MamaLink as a "bridge" and "triage" tool rather than a "substitute" to mitigate legal risk while still highlighting its superiority over unsafe traditional advice. The term "triage" implies knowing when to seek further help, which protects against overpromising offline capabilities.*
