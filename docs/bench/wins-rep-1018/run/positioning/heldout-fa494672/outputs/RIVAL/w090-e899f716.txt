**For** CFOs of Australian mining operations managing over $2M in annual freight spend
- **that need** to convert hidden logistics overcharges into immediate, verified cash flow recovery rather than absorbing them as operational losses
- AuditFlow
- **is a** specialized freight audit recovery platform
- **that** guarantees the identification and reclamation of at least $50,000 per site annually, transforming freight auditing from a cost center into a proven profit generator with a 6x ROI in year one.

**Differentiation Statement**

- **Unlike** manual audits by firms like Mineral Finance Group or internal accounting teams that offer sporadic, sample-based reviews with high labor costs and delayed findings
- AuditFlow
- **provides** continuous, automated scrutiny of 100% of transactions that isolates specific recoverable funds upfront, shifting the conversation from "software subscription expense" to "uncollected revenue asset."
