### Positioning Statement for MindSpace

**Value Proposition**
**For** teenagers in the UK struggling with anxiety who cannot access timely school counseling due to overwhelming caseloads,
**that need** immediate, private, and unlimited daily emotional support to manage their feelings between rare professional appointments,
**MindSpace**
**is a** guided journaling and mood-tracking companion,
**that** provides 24/7 self-regulation tools and continuous tracking, ensuring no student goes unheard while waiting for their monthly 15-minute counselor slot.

**Differentiation Statement**
**Unlike** school counselors who are limited to brief, scheduled monthly sessions for only the most critical cases,
**MindSpace**
**provides** unlimited, on-demand daily guidance for just £3 a month, acting as an essential bridge that extends care to the 90% of students who currently fall through the cracks of the traditional system.

***

**Strategic Recommendation:** Position MindSpace strictly as a **supplement** that extends counselor reach, not a private alternative. Framing it as an "alternative" validates parental fears of replacing human connection. Instead, communicate that you replace **the silence and isolation** students experience during the weeks between counselor visits. You are not competing with the 200 counselors; you are the infrastructure that allows their limited time to be more effective by keeping students stable in the interim.
