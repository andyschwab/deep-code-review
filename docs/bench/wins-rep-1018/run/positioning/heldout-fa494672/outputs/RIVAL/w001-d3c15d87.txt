## Value Proposition

**For** seniors living in remote Brazilian villages who rely on basic feature phones
- **that need** immediate medical reassurance without enduring long waits or costly travel
- VidaRural
- **is a** priority telehealth subscription service
- **that** guarantees a doctor consultation and follow-up within two hours for a flat $4 monthly fee

## Differentiation Statement

- **Unlike** the government's free consultation line which often requires a 48-hour wait, or the alternative of spending half a day and $15 on bus fare to reach a city clinic
- VidaRural
- **provides** instant access to care that transforms a $4 monthly cost into an investment against uncertainty, ensuring that when health concerns arise, help is available immediately rather than tomorrow.

***

This framing shifts the narrative from "paying for a call" to "buying time and certainty," positioning the fee as insurance against the high hidden costs of waiting. By explicitly naming the 48-hour gap and the $15 travel cost, you validate the free option while making the paid upgrade feel like a logical, low-risk upgrade for peace of mind.
