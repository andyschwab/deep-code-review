## Value Proposition

**For** terminal operators at high-volume port complexes (e.g., Los Angeles/Long Beach)
- **that need** to increase gate throughput and utilization without sacrificing driver compliance or causing operational friction
- PortFlow
- **is a** dynamic appointment orchestration platform
- **that** synchronizes trucker arrivals with terminal capacity to boost gate utilization from 85% to over 95% while eliminating congestion

## Differentiation Statement

- **Unlike** static legacy scheduling systems or manual phone/email coordination that prioritize terminal internal metrics at the expense of flow
- PortFlow
- **provides** a bi-directional efficiency model that proves reducing driver wait times (from 90 to 22 minutes) directly increases terminal throughput by smoothing arrival peaks and eliminating idle gate capacity

***

**Strategic Note:** This positioning resolves your tension by targeting terminal operators directly; trucking companies are your leverage (proof of demand), but the terminal is the buyer because they control the integration and care most about the "95% utilization" outcome. The "manual process" you are replacing is the disjointed, static scheduling method (phone/email/legacy software) that creates bottlenecks rather than flow.
