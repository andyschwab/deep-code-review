**Recommendation: Position as the Premium Companion for Existing Wearable Owners.**

Do not bundle a low-cost sensor at this stage. Your current churn (15% monthly) indicates a retention problem, not a distribution gap. Introducing hardware procurement, inventory risk, and support overhead would dilute your focus and burn capital before you have stabilized the core software value. The "fragility" against free apps is solved by proving the *automated correlation* insight, not by owning the sensor.

**Target Segment Hypothesis**
*For chronic migraine sufferers in Morocco who already own advanced wearables (Garmin/Apple Watch) and are frustrated by manual tracking, MigraineFree Morocco is the automated insight engine that correlates vitals to attacks; unlike standalone manual apps, it delivers passive, 90% automated data correlation without extra hardware costs.*

**Primary Message Headline**
"Stop Logging. Start Correlating. Turn Your Existing Watch into a Migraine Predictor."

**Why This Wins Over the Bundled Option**
1.  **LTV & Unit Economics:** The wearable-owner segment has already demonstrated purchasing power (they bought the $300+ watch). Acquiring them removes the Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) for hardware. Your LTV increases because every dirham of subscription revenue is gross margin, whereas the bundled model forces you to subsidize hardware to overcome price sensitivity, extending your payback period dangerously.
2.  **Barrier Removal:** You identified the wearable requirement as a barrier. However, in the bundled model, the barrier shifts from "ownership" to "affordability of the bundle." In a price-sensitive market, asking users to commit to a higher upfront bundle price (subscription + hardware) is a higher friction point than asking existing owners to simply connect a device they already possess.
3.  **Competitive Defense:** Free apps cannot compete with *automated* data integration. Your differentiator is the algorithm linking HRV/sleep to migraines, not the plastic on the wrist. Lean into the "smart" aspect. If you bundle a cheap sensor, you risk cheapening the brand and competing on hardware specs rather than medical insight.

**Messaging Framework: The "Alternative Cost" Structure**
To convert this higher-value group, use a message house that explicitly attacks the *cost of the alternative* (manual entry):

*   **Core Promise:** Automated correlation reveals triggers manual logging misses.
*   **Pillar 1 (Accuracy):** "Manual logs rely on memory; our integration captures real-time physiological precursors." (Proof: Your 90% automation stat).
*   **Pillar 2 (Effort):** "Zero daily input required. Your watch works while you suffer." (Proof: Integration capabilities).
*   **Pillar 3 (Outcome):** "Identify personal triggers faster to reduce attack frequency." (Proof: Pending user validation).

**Riskiest Assumption & Test**
*Assumption:* The pool of chronic migraine sufferers in Casablanca/Rabat who own advanced wearables is large enough to sustain growth despite the 15% churn.
*Test:* Run a targeted campaign to your current 650 subscribers offering a "Referral Bonus" specifically for users who connect a wearable. If conversion from "app-only" to "wearable-connected" users drops churn significantly, the hypothesis holds. If the addressable market proves too small, *then* reconsider hardware, but only after fixing retention.

**Note:** This positioning treats your current user base as the validation pool; do not expand to hardware until monthly churn is below 5%.
**Note:** Verify that your integration supports the specific wearable models most common in Morocco before finalizing marketing copy.
