**Decision:** Position BuildRight Tokyo as a **risk-mitigation insurance policy for project timelines**, not merely an efficiency play.

**Why:** In central Tokyo's zero-storage environment, the cost of a single work stoppage (thousands per hour) vastly outweighs the marginal gains of "efficiency." Contractors are not buying speed; they are buying certainty to prevent catastrophic crew idle time. Framing this as "efficiency" invites comparison on price against cheap taxi-trucks. Framing it as "risk mitigation" justifies a premium by directly addressing the buyer's deepest fear: halting the project.
