**Recommendation: Pursue the Wellness Category with a "Clinically-Informed" Positioning**

Commit to staying in the wellness category for the next 12 months to secure revenue within your 18-month runway. Do not initiate the medical device regulatory process now.

**Positioning Statement (Hypothesis to Validate)**
For **perimenopausal women in Denmark** who **need to track severe symptoms to prepare for healthcare visits**, **LunaCycle** is a **wellness analytics tool** that **transforms daily logs into doctor-ready trend reports**; unlike **general period trackers (e.g., Flo, Clue)** that **focus on fertility and cycle prediction**, LunaCycle **specializes exclusively in menopause symptom correlation and clinical communication prep**.

**Why This Path Wins on Revenue and Runway**
1.  **Cash Flow vs. Cash Burn:** The medical device route requires €300,000 upfront and 12–18 months for approval in the EU. This exhausts your runway before you generate a single euro of new B2B revenue. The wellness path allows you to monetize your existing 4,200 users immediately via a premium "Doctor Report" feature.
2.  **Differentiation without Regulation:** You cannot claim to "diagnose" or "treat" without regulation. However, you *can* position your detailed trend analysis as the bridge between patient experience and clinical consultation. Your differentiator is not medical validity (which you lack) but **data portability and visualization** for the patient-doctor conversation.
3.  **Competitive Moat:** Flo and Clue are broad. By narrowing your focus strictly to menopause (not fertility), you avoid a direct marketing budget war. You are not selling "cycle tracking"; you are selling "advocacy preparation."

**Addressing the Alternatives**
*   **Against the Medical Device Route:** Choosing this now means halting product growth for compliance. The risk is total runway depletion. *Hypothesis:* Users will pay for better data visualization today rather than waiting two years for a "medically certified" stamp that offers no immediate utility.
*   **Against General Wellness Competitors:** Users leave Flo/Clue because those apps deprioritize menopause data in favor of fertility. *Winning Line:* "Don't just track your cycle; prepare your consultation."

**Immediate Next Steps for Validation**
1.  **Feature Test:** Build a PDF export feature formatted specifically for Danish GPs. Offer it as a paid upgrade to 10% of your user base. If conversion exceeds 5%, the "clinically-informed wellness" hypothesis holds.
2.  **Pilot Partnership:** Approach Rigshospitalet not as a regulated device vendor, but as a data partner for a *observational study*. Ask if they would accept patient-generated data from LunaCycle for review during consultations. This builds clinical trust without triggering full regulatory costs.

**Riskiest Assumption:** That Danish GPs will accept and value patient-generated data from a non-regulated app during a standard 15-minute consultation.
**Test:** Interview 10 local GPs with the generated reports from your beta users. If 7 out of 10 say the report saves them time or clarifies the diagnosis, the path is clear. If they reject the data due to liability concerns, you must pivot or pause.
