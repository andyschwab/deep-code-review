**Recommended Positioning: The "System Efficiency" Angle**

To secure a major insurer partnership by Friday, you must position SenNoce as a **cost-saving alternative for the insurance system**, not primarily as a comfort solution for patients. While patient comfort is your delivery mechanism, system efficiency is your buyer's trigger. Insurers and hospital directors in Poland are currently pressured by waiting lists and budget constraints; a 79% cost reduction (250 PLN vs. 1,200 PLN) combined with a 92% completion rate (vs. 60% no-shows) solves their operational crisis, not just patient anxiety. Framing this as "comfort" risks sounding like a luxury add-on; framing it as "eliminating wasted spend on no-shows" makes you a fiscal necessity.

**Message Hierarchy**

**1. Core Message (The Headline)**
"SenNoce eliminates the 40% waste in sleep apnea diagnostics by delivering hospital-grade results at home for 79% less cost."

**2. Supporting Pillar A: Financial Impact (For Insurers & Directors)**
*   **Claim:** We reduce the cost per *completed* diagnosis by over 75%.
*   **Proof:** Our tests cost 250 PLN versus the hospital average of 1,200 PLN. With a 92% completion rate compared to the 60% no-show rate at facilities like Szpital Kliniczny, you stop paying for empty appointments.
*   **Benefit:** Immediate budget relief and higher throughput without new infrastructure.

**3. Supporting Pillar B: Reliability & Access (For Hospital Administrators)**
*   **Claim:** We extend your diagnostic capacity without undermining your authority.
*   **Proof:** 1,500 units successfully distributed via GP referrals with verified high completion rates.
*   **Benefit:** We handle the logistical burden of low-complexity cases, freeing your labs (like Centrum Snú) to focus on complex, high-revenue procedures that actually require in-person care. We are a triage filter, not a competitor.

**4. Supporting Pillar C: Patient Experience (The Enabler)**
*   **Claim:** Patients complete the test because they can do it in the safety of their own home.
*   **Proof:** 92% completion rate driven by eliminating hospital anxiety and travel barriers.
*   **Benefit:** Faster time-to-treatment for patients, leading to better long-term health outcomes and lower downstream claims for the insurer.

**Pitch Deck Narrative Flow**
Start with the **waste**: "The current system loses 40% of referred patients to no-shows." Move to the **solution**: "SenNoce captures these patients at 1/5th the cost." End with the **partnership model**: "Let us manage the high-volume, low-complexity screenings so your hospital can maximize revenue on complex cases."

**Addressing the Fear of Undermining Authority**
Explicitly state: "We do not replace the hospital; we fill its waiting room with qualified, tested patients who need your specialized intervention, while we handle the routine screening that clogs your schedule."

**Note:** This approach directly answers the insurer's need for ROI and the hospital's need for efficiency, turning your "threat" into a "tool." Ensure all specific hospital names in your final deck are used with permission or generalized to "partner hospitals" to avoid friction before the deal is signed.
