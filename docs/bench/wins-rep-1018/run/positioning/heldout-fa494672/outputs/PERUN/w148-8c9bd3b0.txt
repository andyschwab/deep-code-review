**Recommendation: Prioritize the "Resident Convenience" Segment (Apartment Blocks)**

Commit to deploying the next 200 units inside large apartment blocks. While retail spots offer visibility, your data shows apartment units generate 50% higher utilization (45 vs. 30 pickups/day). In a capital-constrained expansion, unit economics and asset efficiency must drive location strategy over brand awareness. High traffic at retail does not translate to high conversion if the specific job-to-be-done is "seamless receipt of goods," which is best solved at the home. Visibility is a marketing problem; low utilization is a fundamental business model risk.

**Positioning Statement (Hypothesis to Validate)**

*For residents in high-density Warsaw and Krakow apartment blocks who need reliable package receipt without coordinating with couriers, LockNet Poland is a last-mile locker network that guarantees immediate access at their doorstep; unlike home delivery from InPost Direct or pickup at convenience stores like Żabka, it eliminates missed deliveries and detours by placing secure lockers directly in the customer's entryway.*

**Why This Wins Against Alternatives**

*   **Vs. Home Delivery (InPost Direct):** The buyer gives up the illusion of "doorstep" service which often fails due to absence.
    *   *Winning Line:* "Never miss a delivery again; your package waits in your hallway, not at a depot."
*   **Vs. Convenience Store Pickup (Żabka):** The buyer gives up the time and effort of a dedicated errand or combining it with grocery shopping.
    *   *Winning Line:* "Your packages come to your building, so you never have to carry them home from the store."

**Riskiest Assumption & Test**
*Assumption:* Residents value the marginal convenience of "in-building" over the extended hours of retail locations enough to sustain the 45-pickup volume as density increases.
*Test:* Run a 2-week A/B message test on landing pages targeting specific zip codes: one ad promising "24/7 access at local stores" vs. "Pickup in your own lobby." Measure click-through and pre-registration rates.

Note: This positioning treats the 45 vs. 30 pickup metric as the primary validator for segment choice; if retail hours are the critical differentiator for a specific sub-segment (e.g., night workers), re-evaluate the mix.
