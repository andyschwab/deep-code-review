**Recommended Positioning: Clinical Decision Support for Time-Strapped Gastroenterologists**

Commit to positioning GutLink as a **clinical decision support tool** that aggregates lab data to reduce review time, rather than a patient empowerment app. While patients value control, your adoption bottleneck is physician resistance regarding "extra time" and "data privacy." A patient-led narrative requires doctors to opt-in to support patient desires, adding friction. A clinician-led narrative solves the doctor's immediate pain point (time spent synthesizing scattered data) and directly addresses the privacy concern by framing the integration as a secure, professional workflow extension. The 25% faster response statistic is a clinical outcome that validates efficiency, not just patient satisfaction.
