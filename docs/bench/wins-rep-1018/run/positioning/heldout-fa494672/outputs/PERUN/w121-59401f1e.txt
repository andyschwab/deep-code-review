**Positioning Hypothesis to Validate**

For the US-based Etsy jewelry and vintage seller processing 50–200 orders monthly who is losing margin to carrier rate confusion, ShipSwift is the multi-carrier automation layer that guarantees the lowest compliant rate without manual comparison. Unlike Pirate Ship (which requires manual carrier selection per order) or the native Etsy calculator (which limits carrier choice and rule complexity), ShipSwift automatically applies your specific packaging rules and business logic to every label, saving 45 minutes of admin work weekly.

**Recommended Segment Decision**
Commit to the **growing professional (50–200 orders/month)**, not the casual crafter or the enterprise volume seller. The casual crafter will always churn to free tools because their time cost is low; the enterprise seller likely needs custom API integrations you may not yet offer. The "messy middle" seller values their time at more than $29/month but lacks the volume to negotiate custom carrier contracts directly. They are the only segment where your automation directly offsets the subscription fee.
