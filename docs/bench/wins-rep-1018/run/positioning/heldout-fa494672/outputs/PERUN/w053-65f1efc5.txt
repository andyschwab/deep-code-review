**Recommended Segment: Agency Owners (Compliance Shield)**

While nurses drive daily usage, agency owners hold the budget and the legal liability. The "clinical superpower" angle appeals to nurse pride, but it does not solve the immediate, existential threat of privacy fines and data breaches. With 15% of current workflows violating privacy rules, the risk of regulatory penalty is a tangible, urgent pain point that free messaging apps cannot address. Owners are motivated by risk mitigation and operational standardization; therefore, positioning WoundSnap as a mandatory compliance shield is the most effective path to securing enterprise mandates.
