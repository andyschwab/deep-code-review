**Why This Over "Premium Concierge":**
Positioning as a "concierge for the wealthy" limits your market to a tiny fraction of India and frames the 800 INR as a luxury tax. Positioning as a "financial shield for the middle class" frames the 800 INR as a prudent *savings* mechanism. The buyer isn't buying "extra care"; they are buying insurance against a 25,000 INR shock they cannot afford. The alternative you are beating is not just the "300 INR clinic," but the *risk* of the "25,000 INR disaster" that the clinic fails to prevent.

*Assumption:* Families will commit to a recurring 800 INR payment based on the *promise* of avoiding a future cost, rather than waiting until a crisis happens.
