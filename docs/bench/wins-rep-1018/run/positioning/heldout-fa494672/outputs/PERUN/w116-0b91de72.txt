Drop the "quick fix for immediate visibility" angle. Your prospect's hesitation regarding ERP disruption and the CFO's focus on compliance indicate that "speed" is perceived as a threat to stability. Position MapleSight as the **strategic safety layer**: the tool that allows them to keep their "old" ERP (satisfying the fear of change) while gaining the modern insights they need to satisfy auditors and reduce reliance on external consultants.
