**Decision:** Position SailFast strictly as the **premium service for the tourism supply chain** (Palawan resort owners). Do not pivot to general local merchants at this stage.

**Reasoning:** Your current traction validates the tourism segment (45 active users, positive feedback on ease of use), while the merchant segment shows clear rejection due to price sensitivity. Competitors like "Barko Connect" dominate the budget sector through informal, low-cost networks that a digital platform cannot easily undercut without destroying your unit economics. Attempting to win a price war against informal captain direct-deals is a likely path to failure. Instead, double down on the segment that already values reliability and speed over the lowest possible price: resort owners who risk spoilage and guest dissatisfaction if supplies arrive late.

**Positioning Statement (Hypothesis to Validate):**
"For **Palawan resort owners** who **need guaranteed, timely delivery of fresh supplies to prevent spoilage and guest dissatisfaction**, SailFast is a **digital cargo booking platform** that **replaces chaotic phone-call coordination with confirmed, trackable shipments**; unlike **informal captain direct-deals (Barko Connect) or manual agents (Tondo Freight)** which rely on uncertain availability and opaque pricing, SailFast **delivers bookings 20% faster with guaranteed slot confirmation**."

**Core Message Headline:**
"Stop Gambling with Your Fresh Supplies: Guaranteed Booking for Palawan Resorts."

**Why This Wins Against Alternatives:**
*   **Vs. Barko Connect (Informal Network):** Buyers give up the illusion of "lowest possible price" to gain **certainty**. The winning line is: "Can your captain guarantee a slot tomorrow, or just hope? We guarantee it."
*   **Vs. Tondo Freight (Manual Agents):** Buyers give up the familiarity of phone calls to gain **speed and transparency**. The winning line: "Replace 10 phone calls with one click and know your cargo is moving."

**Riskiest Assumption & Test:**
*Assumption:* Resort owners are willing to pay a premium specifically for the *guarantee* of delivery, not just the app interface.
*Test:* Run a sales campaign targeting 20 new Palawan resorts highlighting only "Guaranteed Slot or Money Back" (emphasizing the risk of spoilage) rather than "Easy App." Measure conversion rates compared to features-focused messaging. If they do not convert on the guarantee, the value proposition is weak.

**Note:** This positioning treats your current market facts (45 users, 20% speed gain) as evidence, while the claim of "guaranteed slot" requires operational verification to ensure it holds true in practice.
**Note:** Trademark clearance for "SailFast" and domain availability must be verified by a legal professional; this draft assumes these are clear.
