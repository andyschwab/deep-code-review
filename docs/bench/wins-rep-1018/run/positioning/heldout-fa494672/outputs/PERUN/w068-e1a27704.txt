### Recommended Path: Target the Individual Sufferer First

**Strategic Recommendation**
Prioritize direct-to-patient messaging. While insurance providers care about cost, they move slowly and require large-scale data you do not yet have. Your strongest differentiation lies in the immediate, tangible relief you offer patients compared to the specific pain of their current alternatives: the hours lost driving to Kingston Public Hospital or the ineffectiveness of over-the-counter remedies. Validating this value proposition with your existing 120 patients is the fastest route to proving product-market fit before scaling to B2B sales.

**Core Message Hierarchy**

**1. Primary Audience: The Migraine Sufferer**
*   **The Hook:** "Stop driving through pain to the ER. Get your preventive adjustment from home in minutes."
*   **The Problem:** When a migraine strikes, the current habit is either suffering through ineffective pharmacy advice or enduring a exhausting trip to the Kingston Public Hospital ER, where wait times are long and stress worsens the attack.
*   **The Solution:** IslandNeuro provides immediate video access to neurology specialists who adjust your preventive care plan instantly, keeping you safe at home.
*   **The Proof:** Patients using our service reduced emergency room visits by 35% last quarter, reclaiming hours of their lives and avoiding the trauma of the ER waiting room.

**2. Secondary Audience: Insurance Providers (Future State)**
*   **The Hook:** "Reduce unnecessary ER utilization for migraine management by 35%."
*   **The Problem:** Insurers currently pay high costs for ER visits that could be managed via tele-health, driven by patients lacking immediate access to specialist care.
*   **The Solution:** A dedicated tele-neurology layer that intercepts patients before they seek emergency care, optimizing medication prophylaxis remotely.
*   **The Proof:** Early cohort data shows a 35% reduction in ER visits among enrolled patients, directly lowering claim costs for acute migraine events.

**Why This Differentiation Wins**
Targeting the patient attacks the *habit* directly. The alternative (driving to the ER) is a high-friction, high-pain experience. Your service removes that friction immediately. Pitching to insurance first asks them to bet on a hypothesis; pitching to patients lets you gather the undeniable evidence needed to force the insurance conversation later.

***

*Note: The 35% reduction figure is based on your internal data; ensure this is statistically significant before using it in broad marketing claims. This positioning is a hypothesis to be validated by testing these messages with a segment of your current waitlist or churned users.*
