**Recommendation: Target Independent Designers**

Commit to **independent designers and small creative studios** as your primary segment. While mass manufacturers send higher volumes, their purchasing decisions are driven by unit-cost minimization and consolidated logistics, making your $45 price point (vs. their $20 average) a structural barrier rather than a value add. For independent designers, speed is a revenue driver: a 48-hour turnaround accelerates their creative iteration and time-to-market, directly impacting their ability to secure orders or funding. They view the $25 premium not as a cost, but as an insurance policy against missed deadlines and creative stagnation. Mass manufacturers will always churn for a cheaper rate; designers will pay for the capability to move faster than their competitors.
