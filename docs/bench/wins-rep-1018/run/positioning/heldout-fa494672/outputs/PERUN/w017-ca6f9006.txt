**Positioning Hypothesis to Validate**

**For** rural, low-income expectant and new mothers in Kenya who face prohibitive travel costs and unreliable local advice, **MamaLink** is a **critical health bridge** that delivers medically sound prenatal and infant nutrition guidance via basic SMS. **Unlike** distant public clinics that are accurate but inaccessible, or community elders whose advice is free but potentially dangerous, **MamaLink** provides immediate, offline-accessible triage and education that keeps mothers healthy between rare clinic visits.

**Recommended Lead Message:**
"Your reliable health partner when the clinic is too far and traditional advice isn't enough—medically sound care for you and your baby, delivered instantly to your basic phone."

**Strategic Commitment:**
Position MamaLink strictly as a **bridge**, not a substitute. Claiming to replace clinical care in emergencies creates fatal legal liability and erodes trust when offline limitations prevent real-time crisis intervention. The unique value is not "saving lives in emergencies" (which requires internet/doctors) but "preventing emergencies" through consistent, accurate daily guidance that elders cannot provide and clinics cannot reach. This framing maximizes impact by owning the gap between visits without overpromising on crisis response.

**Addressing Alternatives:**
*   **Vs. Distant Clinics:** Buyers give up travel costs and lost work days; you win by offering immediate access to baseline medical standards without leaving the village.
*   **Vs. Community Elders:** Buyers give up free but risky misinformation; you win by providing verified, life-preserving protocols at zero marginal cost.

**Riskiest Assumption & Test:**
*Assumption:* Mothers trust SMS advice enough to act on it over elder recommendations in non-emergency scenarios.
*Test:* Run a controlled message trial with 200 users presenting a specific nutrition scenario; measure the adoption rate of the SMS advice versus their reported historical reliance on elder counsel.

**Notes:**
1. This statement is a hypothesis requiring validation through direct user feedback and pilot testing; it is not a confirmed market fact.
2. Any claims regarding medical outcomes or legal safety must be reviewed by local healthcare regulators and legal counsel before public deployment.
