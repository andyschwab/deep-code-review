### Message Hierarchy for ExportaFacil

**Core Hypothesis to Validate:**
For **individual artisan exporters** (specifically those shipping their first 1–3 shipments) who are blocked by high costs and complex paperwork, **ExportaFacil** is the **digital customs brokerage** that clears goods in 2 days for 400 BRL; unlike **legacy firms like GlobalTrade Brasil or local despachantes**, it **eliminates document loss and cuts costs by 66% through a fully digital workflow**.

*(Note: This statement assumes individual artisans are more price-sensitive and volume-constrained than co-ops. The riskiest assumption is that "digital-only" is a barrier rather than a trust signal for this specific segment.)*

---

#### 1. Primary Message (The Headline)
**"Clear your first export in 2 days for 400 BRL—guaranteed error-free."**
*Why this works:* It leads with the specific outcome (speed), the concrete cost advantage (400 vs. 1,200 BRL), and the reliability proof (zero errors) that directly attacks the incumbent weakness (lost documents).

#### 2. Supporting Pillars & Proof Points

**Pillar A: Unbeatable Economics for Low Volume**
*   **Claim:** We make small shipments profitable where legacy brokers make them impossible.
*   **Proof Point:** Our flat fee of 400 BRL is 66% lower than the 1,200 BRL average charged by firms like GlobalTrade Brasil, preserving margin for artisans who cannot absorb high fixed costs.
*   **Contrast:** Legacy firms prioritize high-volume clients, leaving small exporters overpaying for unnecessary service layers.

**Pillar B: Speed and Certainty**
*   **Claim:** Go from paperwork to shipment in 48 hours, not two weeks.
*   **Proof Point:** We reduced processing time from 14 days to 2 days for our last 45 first-time exporters.
*   **Contrast:** Manual processes with local *despachantes* rely on physical document handoffs, creating delays and high risks of lost paperwork.

**Pillar C: Digital-First Reliability**
*   **Claim:** No physical office needed because your documents never leave the secure cloud.
*   **Proof Point:** Zero errors recorded across 45 recent shipments due to automated validation checks that replace manual data entry.
*   **Contrast:** Physical offices often imply manual handling, which is the primary cause of the document loss exporters fear.

#### 3. Addressing the "No Physical Office" Trust Barrier
**The Reframe:** Do not apologize for lacking an office; position the lack of physical overhead as the *reason* for your speed and low cost.
*   **Message:** "Your customs clearance doesn't need a brick-and-mortar office; it needs error-free data. Our digital-only model removes the human error of manual handling and the cost of maintaining physical branches, passing those savings directly to you."
*   **Trust Signal:** Highlight the "Zero Errors" stat prominently. For a first-time exporter, the fear of a lost document is greater than the comfort of a physical lobby. Proof of perfect execution builds more trust than a physical address.

#### 4. Why We Chose "Individual Artisan Exporters" Over Co-ops
*   **Decision:** Target individual artisans first.
*   **Reasoning:** Co-ops often have established relationships and may require complex, negotiated contracts that legacy firms are built to handle. Individual artisans are currently underserved, priced out by the 1,200 BRL minimums, and likely more willing to try a digital solution to solve their immediate "first shipment" anxiety.
*   **What they give up:** By staying with a legacy broker, the artisan pays 800 BRL extra per shipment and waits 12 extra days, risking their thin margins on small batches.
