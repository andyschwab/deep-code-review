**Positioning Choice: The Fast Pre-Screening Triage**

You must position VistaClara as a **fast pre-screening tool that feeds into traditional clinics**, not as a full replacement. Attempting to replace the physical exam now directly attacks your users' stated trust in "in-person tools," creating unnecessary friction. By positioning as a triage layer, you partner with the very chains you fear (Ópticas Modernas, Visión Total) rather than fighting them for a capability you cannot yet prove remotely. You win by selling efficiency to the clinics (filtering out non-cases) and convenience to the patient (instant access), while leaving the high-trust physical verification to the established players.

**Positioning Statement (Hypothesis to Validate)**
"For **urban patients in Argentina** who **need immediate vision assessment but hesitate to visit a clinic without a prior indication**, VistaClara is a **remote triage service** that **delivers a preliminary prescription in minutes**; unlike **waiting for an in-person appointment at chains like Ópticas Modernas**, it **filters unnecessary visits and accelerates care for those who truly need physical exams**."

**Headline for Sales:** "Don't replace the exam; optimize the queue."

**Why This Wins Against Alternatives**
*   **Against "Full Replacement":** If you claim to replace the physical exam, you force the user to choose between your unproven remote trust and their established trust in physical tools. You lose. By admitting the physical exam's value, you remove the threat and position your tool as the smart first step.
*   **Against "Doing Nothing/Waiting":** Your differentiator is speed and cost. The buyer gives up weeks of waiting and higher fees at the big chains by using you first.
*   **Against "Local Optometrists":** You are not stealing their final sale; you are sending them higher-quality leads who already have a preliminary data point, making their in-person time more profitable.

**Riskiest Assumption & Test**
*   **Assumption:** Traditional clinics (Ópticas Modernas, Visión Total) will accept referrals from a competitor rather than blocking them.
*   **Test:** Pitch a pilot partnership to one regional manager next week: "We will send you 50 pre-screened patients who need physical verification in exchange for a preferred referral rate." If they reject the lead flow, the "partner" strategy fails, and you must reconsider the "replacement" angle only after gathering more data on remote accuracy.

**Note:** This statement is a hypothesis based on your provided metrics (3,200 patients, 65% conversion) and user feedback; it requires validation through actual partnership discussions. Do not claim trademark clearance or market size without external legal and research verification.
