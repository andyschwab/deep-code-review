**Positioning Statement (Hypothesis to Validate)**

For **private school boards in Costa Rica** who must guarantee student safety during medical emergencies, **SeizureSafe** is the **emergency response coordination system** that reduces medication administration time from minutes to under 90 seconds; unlike **filing cabinets of paper plans and frantic parent phone calls**, it provides **instant, digital access to individualized care protocols** that eliminate decision delays without requiring legal admissions of liability.

**Primary Buyer Recommendation:** Sell to **School Boards**.
*Reasoning:* Parents seek peace of mind, but School Boards hold the budget and the legal mandate to manage on-campus medical risk. The 85% improvement in reporting accuracy and the reduction from 4 minutes to 90 seconds are operational metrics that directly address a board's duty of care and liability exposure, whereas a "parent monitoring dashboard" is a feature, not a primary value proposition for the institution paying the bill.

**Differentiation vs. Alternatives:**
*   **Vs. Paper Logs/Filing Cabinets:** Paper requires physical retrieval during a crisis, causing the 4-minute delay. SeizureSafe delivers the plan instantly to the nurse's hand.
*   **Vs. Stopwatch/Timer:** A stopwatch measures time but does not guide the clinical action or ensure the correct protocol is followed. SeizureSafe orchestrates the response, ensuring the right steps are taken immediately.

**Headline for Board Slide:**
"From 4 Minutes to 90 Seconds: Instant Access to Life-Saving Care Plans."

**Riskiest Assumption & Test:**
*Assumption:* School boards view "speed of administration" as a higher priority than "cost of software" or "parent communication features."
*Test:* Present the single-slide statement to 5 school board members who are not yet customers. Ask them: "Would this specific reduction in response time justify a line-item budget approval next quarter?" If they hesitate or pivot to asking about parent apps, the positioning must shift to emphasize parent satisfaction as the driver for board approval.
