**Recommendation:** Position AutoTrack as a **decision-support tool for private specialist clinics**, targeting rheumatologists who manage high-frequency flare patients, rather than attempting to replace the national blood testing infrastructure.

**Positioning Statement (Hypothesis):**
For **private rheumatologists in Iceland** who **struggle to intervene before severe autoimmune flares escalate**, AutoTrack is a **clinical decision-support platform** that **predicts flare events 48 hours in advance**; unlike **reactive monthly blood tests or emergency appointments**, it **enables proactive medication adjustments using continuous wearable and symptom data**.

**Core Message Headline:**
"Stop waiting for the flare: Predict autoimmune crises 48 hours before they happen."

**Why this segment over the national insurer:**
Targeting the national health insurer with a claim to replace blood tests introduces immediate regulatory barriers and requires proving equivalence to established lab standards—a high-friction sales cycle that stalls early-stage adoption. By contrast, private specialists face the direct pain of emergency visits and patient deterioration between monthly labs. They have the autonomy to adopt new tools that improve patient outcomes without waiting for national policy shifts. Your 200 patients and 5,000 logged episodes provide the specific proof point this segment needs: real-world evidence that continuous monitoring catches what monthly snapshots miss.

**Addressing the Alternatives:**
*   **Vs. Monthly Blood Tests:** The buyer gives up the false security of a "normal" result from 29 days ago. Your differentiator is the **48-hour prediction window**, turning a reactive monthly check into a continuous safety net. *Proof needed:* Data showing how many of your 5,000 episodes would have been missed or delayed by a standard monthly schedule.
*   **Vs. Emergency Appointments:** The buyer gives up the high cost and patient trauma of crisis care. Your differentiator is **early intervention capability**. *Proof needed:* A metric comparing the severity of flares caught by AutoTrack versus those resulting in emergency visits for Dr. Jonsdottir's patients.

**Riskiest Assumption & Test:**
The riskiest assumption is that specialists will trust algorithmic predictions enough to change treatment plans without a confirmatory blood test.
*   **Test:** Run a pilot with 5 private clinics where doctors receive AutoTrack alerts but are instructed to order confirmatory labs only when the system predicts a flare. Measure the correlation between the prediction and the lab result, and track whether early intervention reduced symptom severity compared to their historical average.

**Note:** This positioning treats regulatory clearance as a future hurdle to be managed, not a current blocker, by framing the product as an adjunct tool rather than a diagnostic replacement.
**Note:** Ensure all claims regarding "48-hour prediction" are backed by the specific sensitivity/specificity rates from your 5,000 episode dataset before presenting to clinics.
