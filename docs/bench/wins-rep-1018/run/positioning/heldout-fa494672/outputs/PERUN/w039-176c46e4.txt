### Message Hierarchy: Direct-to-Parent Approach

**Primary Headline**
"Stop waiting for the crisis: Get a 40% earlier warning for your child's sickle cell pain using Lagos weather data."

**Supporting Evidence (The Pillar)**
Lead exclusively with your internal beta metric: "In our recent beta with Lagos families, SickleGuard provided actionable alerts 40% faster than traditional pain diaries or waiting for emergency symptoms."
*Constraint:* Do not frame this as a clinically proven statistic. Frame it as an observed operational result from your user base. Explicitly state: "Based on internal beta data from 1,200 Lagos users."

**Specific Reassurance (Overcoming the Validation Gap)**
Address the lack of hospital endorsement by reframing the product's role from "medical diagnosis" to "proactive preparedness."
*The Message:* "SickleGuard is not a replacement for your doctor at Lagoon Hospitals or the University of Ibadan Teaching Hospital. It is a daily safety net that helps you prepare *before* you need them. While we complete our formal clinical validation with teaching hospitals, our tool empowers you to manage hydration and weather triggers today, turning reactive emergency visits into proactive care."

**Why this works:** It commits to the safety benefit parents need now without fabricating medical authority. It acknowledges the "unproven" status honestly but renders it less critical by positioning the app as a preparatory aid rather than a diagnostic device.

***

**Note:** This positioning treats the 40% figure as an internal observation, not a clinical claim, to mitigate legal risk while maximizing value.
**Note:** Ensure your terms of service explicitly state the tool is for informational support and not medical diagnosis until university validation is secured.
