### Risk Analysis: Cold Storage Booking Feature vs. Fleet Expansion

**1. Value & Usability Risks (The "Internet Gap")**
*   **Assumption:** Cooperatives have reliable daily internet access to use a web-based booking tool two weeks in advance.
*   **Risk Level:** **Critical**. If connectivity is intermittent, a web-only interface creates a barrier to entry exactly when speed is needed. Farmers may revert to phone calls, rendering the software useless while adding development debt.
*   **Alternative:** Partnering with existing phone-based networks (USSD or voice agents) aligns with current behaviors but reduces real-time visibility and automation potential.

**2. Feasibility & Technical Risks**
*   **Assumption:** A simple booking algorithm can accurately match supply (farmers) with demand (storage slots) without overbooking or underutilization.
*   **Risk Level:** **High**. Peak harvest times create volatile demand. If the system fails to optimize slot allocation, you risk alienating customers who reserved space but find it unavailable upon arrival due to synchronization errors.
*   **Alternative:** Manual coordination via phone networks is flexible but does not scale. It relies on human memory and spreadsheets, increasing the risk of double-booking as volume grows.

**3. Viability & Go-to-Market Risks**
*   **Assumption:** Farmers are willing to commit to a specific slot two weeks in advance.
*   **Risk Level:** **Medium**. Harvest dates often shift due to weather. A rigid digital booking system might lack the flexibility farmers need, causing them to abandon the tool for more adaptable (though inefficient) informal arrangements.
*   **Financial Impact:** Allocating the Q3 budget to software development delays fleet expansion. If the software adoption is low due to connectivity issues, the company loses the immediate revenue capacity that additional trucks would provide.

**4. Strategic Trade-off: Build vs. Expand Fleet**
*   **Building the Module:** High upfront cost, high long-term scalability *if* adoption succeeds. Failure mode: You build a tool no one can use, and spoilage continues because physical capacity (trucks/storage) wasn't increased.
*   **Expanding Fleet:** Immediate capacity increase, reduces spoilage directly by moving goods faster or accessing distant storage. Lower technical risk, but operational complexity increases (maintenance, drivers). This solves the symptom (lack of space/movement) without fixing the coordination bottleneck.

**Recommendation for Friday's Decision:**
Do not allocate the full Q3 budget to the web-based module yet. The risk of low adoption due to connectivity is too high. Instead, run a "concierge test": manually manage bookings via phone/WhatsApp for two weeks to validate if farmers will commit slots in advance. Simultaneously, allocate 50% of the budget to fleet expansion to immediately address the 15% spoilage rate. Use the manual data to refine the software requirements before full development.

**Note:** This analysis prioritizes validating the connectivity assumption before heavy engineering investment. Immediate fleet expansion offers a tangible reduction in crop loss while the digital solution is de-risked.
