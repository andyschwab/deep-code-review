### Critical Data Points for Go/No-Go Decision

To resolve the conflict between user demand (68% desire speed) and legal risk (privacy liability), you must gather evidence on three specific fronts before November 15. Do not rely on opinions; collect behavioral data and legal precedents.

**1. Behavioral Friction Threshold (Usability & Value Risk)**
You assume manual consent prompts will be tolerated. You need to prove this.
*   **Data Point Needed:** Abandonment rate at the consent step in a high-fidelity prototype test.
*   **How to get it:** Run a moderated usability test with 8–10 GPs using a clickable prototype that forces the "strict manual consent" flow for every share. Measure exactly how many abort the task when faced with the friction.
*   **Decision Metric:** If >20% of doctors abandon the workflow or express intent to revert to fax/phone due to time pressure, the feature fails the Value test regardless of legal safety.

**2. Legal Precedent for "Manual Mitigation" (Viability & Ethics Risk)**
You assume manual prompts satisfy local regulations. You need written confirmation, not verbal assurance.
*   **Data Point Needed:** A formal legal opinion citing specific German healthcare regulations (e.g., GDPR Art. 9, Telematikinfrastruktur rules) confirming that *per-share manual consent* constitutes sufficient lawful basis for data transfer in your specific architecture.
*   **How to get it:** Request a written memo from legal counsel detailing any known cases where similar manual workflows were deemed insufficient or resulted in fines. Ask specifically: "Does manual consent override the need for automated encryption standards required by German law?"
*   **Decision Metric:** If legal cannot provide a written "safe harbor" opinion or cites ambiguous precedents, the Viability risk is too high to proceed.

**3. Operational Sustainability & Error Rate (Feasibility Risk)**
You assume the technical overhead and human error rates are sustainable.
*   **Data Point Needed:** Estimated time-per-share addition and projected error rate (wrong patient/wrong specialist) under pressure.
*   **How to get it:** During the usability test, measure the time delta between a "frictionless" share and the "manual consent" share. Multiply this by the average daily share volume per GP. Simulate a "rush hour" scenario to observe if doctors begin clicking "consent" without reading (habituation), effectively nullifying the legal protection.
*   **Decision Metric:** If the time cost exceeds 45 seconds per share or if users demonstrate "click-through" behavior during testing, the solution creates a false sense of security and increases liability rather than reducing it.
