**To:** Chief Operating Officer, Belize Bay Resorts
**From:** Product Management
**Date:** October 26, 2023
**Subject:** Recommendation: Luggage Tracking Solution (Build vs. Buy)

**Executive Summary**
Based on current evidence, I recommend **buying** a third-party logistics solution rather than building a custom internal tool. While the pain point is real and urgent, the assumption that a custom build is justified given our 60% seasonal volume fluctuation is high-risk and likely financially unsound before the holiday rush.

**Problem Validation**
The operational failure is confirmed. Over the last 45 days, four resort managers reported critical failures in luggage transfers, resulting in an average resolution time of six hours per incident. This directly impacts guest satisfaction and creates significant reputational risk ahead of the peak season.

**Analysis of the "Build" Assumption**
Our hypothesis suggests a custom tool with barcode scanning and driver handoff confirmation would reduce resolution time to under 30 minutes. While technically feasible, the **Viability** and **Strategy** risks outweigh the benefits:

1.  **Seasonal Utilization Risk:** With tourist volume dropping 60% in the off-season, a custom-built system would sit underutilized for nearly half the year. The fixed costs of development, server maintenance, and software updates remain constant regardless of volume, destroying unit economics during low seasons.
2.  **Time-to-Value:** Developing, testing, and deploying a custom mobile application for drivers and a backend dashboard before the upcoming holiday rush is aggressive. Any delay in launch leaves us exposed during our highest revenue period.
3.  **Distraction from Core Competency:** Our team's strength is hospitality management, not software logistics. Maintaining a custom codebase diverts engineering resources from guest-facing digital experiences.

**The "Buy" Alternative**
Third-party vendors specialize in last-mile logistics and already possess the barcode scanning, GPS tracking, and handoff confirmation features we require.

*   **Speed:** Implementation can occur in days, not months, securing us before the holiday rush.
*   **Cost Structure:** Most vendors operate on a per-scan or monthly subscription model that scales with volume. This aligns costs with our seasonal fluctuations, avoiding fixed overhead during the off-season.
*   **Proven Reliability:** These tools are used by major airlines and hotel chains globally, meaning the "usability" and "feasibility" risks are already mitigated by the vendor.

**Decision Framework**
We evaluated this against four key risk categories:
*   **Value:** Both options solve the 6-hour delay.
*   **Feasibility:** Buying is immediate; building is time-constrained.
*   **Viability:** Buying offers variable costs matching our seasonality; building imposes fixed costs.
*   **Strategy:** Buying allows us to focus on hospitality; building forces us into software maintenance.

**Recommendation**
Do not build the custom tool at this time. The evidence does not support the fixed investment required given the severe seasonal drop-off. Instead, immediately procure a third-party luggage tracking SaaS platform. This approach resolves the immediate logistical headache before the holiday rush, converts fixed costs to variable costs to protect off-season margins, and eliminates the execution risk of a rushed internal development cycle.

We should revisit the "build" option only if luggage volume becomes consistent year-round or if third-party vendors fail to meet specific integration needs after a 12-month trial.
