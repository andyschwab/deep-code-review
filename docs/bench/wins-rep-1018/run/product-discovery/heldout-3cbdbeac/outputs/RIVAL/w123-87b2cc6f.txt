### Executive Summary: Harvest Timing vs. Route Planning Priority

**Recommendation:** Do not prioritize the harvest timing optimization tool based solely on the five complaint emails. The current evidence indicates a critical pain point (spoilage due to timing mismatches), but it fails to validate the proposed solution (predictive analytics requiring private data). The root cause may be a communication gap rather than a prediction failure.

**Evidence Assessment:**
The five emails confirm high emotional intensity and financial loss, satisfying the "strong emotion" criterion for a real problem. However, they represent **outcome complaints**, not **solution validation**. The assumption that growers will share private planting data is a high-risk blocker that remains untested. If growers refuse data sharing, the predictive model cannot function, rendering the $30,000 pilot investment futile.

**Critical Unknowns:**
1.  **Data Willingness:** Will growers trade privacy for potential spoilage reduction?
2.  **Root Cause:** Is the mismatch due to inaccurate weather/crop models (prediction failure) or lack of real-time status updates between driver and farmer (communication failure)?
3.  **Alternative Solutions:** Could a simple manual check-in protocol solve the issue without complex AI?

**Immediate Action Plan (Before Friday's Decision):**
Conduct three rapid "Mom Test" interviews with the complaining growers within 48 hours. Do not pitch the predictive tool. Instead, ask:
*   "Walk me through the last time a truck arrived at the wrong time. What information did you have at that moment?"
*   "What have you tried to prevent this in the past?"
*   "How do you currently decide when to harvest? Who do you share that schedule with?"
*   "If we asked for your detailed planting logs to build a forecast, what would be your biggest concern?"

**Decision Framework for the Board:**
*   **Fund Harvest Timing Tool ONLY IF:** Growers explicitly express willingness to share data and confirm that better forecasting (not just better communication) is their primary unmet need.
*   **Fund Route Planning/Communication Upgrade IF:** Growers indicate they already know their harvest times but lack a reliable channel to update drivers, or if they refuse to share private data.

**Resource Allocation:**
Reserve $5,000 of the $30,000 pilot budget for immediate discovery interviews and a low-fidelity prototype (e.g., a manual SMS update system) to test the communication hypothesis. Allocate the remaining $25,000 only after validating the data-sharing assumption.
