**Strategic Assessment: Billing Automation Investment**

**Recommendation:** Proceed with vendor selection but **delay contract signing** pending a two-week data integrity sprint. Do not sign by November 1st based solely on current internal complaints.

**Executive Summary**
While the operational bottleneck is critical, the assumption that automation will immediately improve cash flow is high-risk without validating data quality. The four formal complaints confirm a severe usability and capacity issue (Value Risk), but they do not validate the feasibility of an automated solution given the known data concerns. Implementing automation on dirty data will likely accelerate dispute generation rather than resolution, negatively impacting the six-month ROI target.

**Risk Analysis of Current Assumptions**
1.  **The "90% Error Reduction" Assumption (Feasibility Risk):** This assumes errors stem purely from manual entry fatigue. If 40% of errors originate from upstream data ingestion (incorrect customer IDs, legacy formatting), automation will simply process these errors faster, increasing the volume of disputed invoices.
2.  **The "Immediate Cash Flow" Assumption (Viability Risk):** Cash flow improves only if invoices are *paid*, not just *generated*. If automation triggers a spike in disputes due to data mismatches, Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) will worsen before it improves, missing the CFO's six-month window.
3.  **The Complaint Volume as Proof (Strategy Risk):** Four complaints indicate a breaking point for the team, validating the *need* for change. However, this is anecdotal evidence of pain, not quantitative proof that a specific automated vendor will solve the root cause without exacerbating data issues.

**Strategic Action Plan**
To secure CFO approval and protect ROI, execute the following before signing:

*   **Conduct a Data Sanity Check (Week 1):** Extract the last 200 disputed invoices. Manually categorize errors into "Process/Manual" vs. "Data/Input." If >20% are data-driven, automation cannot proceed without a parallel data cleansing workstream.
*   **Run a Shadow Pilot (Week 2):** Feed a sample batch of current data into the vendor's sandbox environment. Measure the error rate of *their* system against your *current* data. This tests the "clean enough" assumption with actual behavior, not opinions.
*   **Revised ROI Projection:** Present the CFO with two scenarios:
    *   *Base Case:* Automation + Data Clean-up (ROI realized in Month 5).
    *   *Risk Case:* Automation only (ROI negative in Month 3 due to dispute backlog).

**Conclusion**
The business case for solving the backlog is strong; the business case for *this specific vendor contract by November 1st* is weak without data validation. Delay signing to run the shadow pilot. This minimizes the risk of automating chaos and ensures the six-month ROI target is based on validated feasibility rather than optimistic assumptions.

*Note: The four complaints justify the budget but do not justify the timeline without data verification.*
