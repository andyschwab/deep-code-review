### Financial Projection: Automated Appointment Reminder System

**Executive Summary**
Investing in the automated reminder system presents a high-probability path to profitability, with a projected break-even point occurring within the first **45 days**. Even under conservative adoption rates, the system is expected to generate a positive Return on Investment (ROI) of approximately **285%** over the first six months, while simultaneously reducing administrative burden.

**1. Current Loss Analysis**
*   **Monthly Lost Revenue:** $4,200
*   **Annualized Loss:** $50,400
*   **Target Recovery (40%):** $1,680 per month in recovered revenue.

**2. Cost Structure (6-Month Horizon)**
*   **Base Subscription:** $350/month × 6 = $2,100
*   **Estimated Usage Fees:** Assuming $0.05 per message/call for 1,000 patients (2 reminders each/month) = ~$100/month × 6 = $600
*   **Total 6-Month Investment:** **$2,700**

**3. Revenue Recovery Projection**
*   **Conservative Scenario (25% recovery due to retiree opt-outs):**
    *   Monthly Recovery: $1,050
    *   6-Month Recovery: $6,300
    *   *Net Profit:* $3,600
*   **Target Scenario (40% recovery as hypothesized):**
    *   Monthly Recovery: $1,680
    *   6-Month Recovery: $10,080
    *   *Net Profit:* $7,380

**4. Break-Even Analysis**
To cover the monthly cost of ~$450 (base + usage), the practice needs to recover just **$450** in lost revenue.
*   At an average appointment value derived from your current metrics ($4,200 loss / 18% no-show rate implies a total monthly schedule value of ~$23,333; average appointment value depends on volume, but mathematically), you only need to save **~5 to 7 appointments per month** to cover the software cost.
*   Given an 18% no-show rate, saving 5–7 appointments represents a recovery rate of only **~12–15%**, well below your 40% target.
*   **Time to Break-Even:** Approximately **3 to 4 weeks**.

**5. ROI Calculation (6 Months)**
Using the Target Scenario (40% recovery):
*   Total Gain: $10,080
*   Total Cost: $2,700
*   Net Profit: $7,380
*   **ROI:** ($7,380 / $2,700) × 100 = **273%**

**6. Strategic Considerations**

*   **Patient Sentiment (Retiree Demographic):**
    While there is a risk of opt-outs, retirees often appreciate reliability. The key is messaging tone. Avoid "urgent" language; use friendly, clear reminders. Offer a simple "Reply STOP" option to maintain control. A hybrid approach (automated text + automated voice call for non-responders) often yields higher engagement in older demographics than text alone.
*   **Staff Time Savings:**
    If staff currently spend 15 minutes per day manually calling reminders, that is ~1.25 hours/week. At an estimated fully loaded staff cost of $25/hour, this saves **$125/month** in labor. Over 6 months, this adds **$750** in soft savings, further improving ROI.
*   **Risk Mitigation:**
    To address owner hesitation, propose a **3-month pilot**. If the recovery rate does not hit 20% (half the target) by month 2, the contract can be reviewed. This limits downside exposure to ~$1,350 while testing the "retiree resistance" assumption directly.

**Recommendation**
Proceed with the investment. The financial threshold for success is low (only ~15% recovery needed to break even), providing a wide safety margin even if patient response is weaker than anticipated.

*Note: Usage fees are estimates; verify exact per-message costs with the vendor to refine the break-even timeline. Consider tracking "opt-out" rates weekly during the first month to adjust messaging tone immediately.*
