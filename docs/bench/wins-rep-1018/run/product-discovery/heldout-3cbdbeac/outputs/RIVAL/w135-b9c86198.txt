**Strategic Assessment: Heavy Lift Booking Platform Initiative**
**To:** Lead Investors, Kourou Heavy Lift
**From:** Product Strategy Team
**Date:** October 24, 2023
**Subject:** Go/No-Go Recommendation for Dynamic Booking Platform

**Executive Summary**
We recommend a conditional **GO** on the development of the dynamic booking platform, with an immediate pivot in scope to decouple customer-facing scheduling from backend regulatory automation. The current bottleneck is costing $600,000 in aggregate client storage fees over two months, creating an imminent churn risk that outweighs the regulatory uncertainty surrounding automated cargo manifesting.

**Customer Evidence vs. Regulatory Reality**
The market signal is unambiguous: three major satellite manufacturers have explicitly linked launch window delays to our inefficient booking process. The financial bleed ($200k per client) validates the "Value" and "Viability" of a solution. However, the assumption that a *fully automated* system including real-time regulatory manifesting is required to solve this is flawed.

Regulatory approval for automated cargo manifesting is a high-risk, long-lead variable unlikely to resolve before the November peak season. Waiting for full regulatory automation constitutes a "High Impact, High Risk" delay that cedes market share to competitors entering next quarter. Conversely, the core customer pain point is *visibility and scheduling certainty*, not necessarily the backend regulatory filing speed itself.

**Strategic Recommendation: The Hybrid Approach**
Proceed with building the **Dynamic Scheduling Interface** immediately, but isolate the **Automated Manifesting** module.

1.  **Phase 1 (Immediate - Launch by November):** Deploy a real-time booking dashboard that allows clients to visualize payload integration slots and reserve windows. This solves the communication blackout causing storage fees. Backend manifest approval remains a manual handoff between our operations team and regulators, invisible to the client. This captures the value of "certainty" without triggering the regulatory timeline risk.
2.  **Phase 2 (Q1 Next Year):** Integrate automated cargo manifesting once regulatory frameworks clarify. This future-proofs the platform against competitor automation claims without blocking immediate revenue retention.

**Investor Assurance**
This approach secures current contracts by eliminating the visible bottleneck (scheduling opacity) while mitigating the risk of regulatory non-compliance. It demonstrates to the market that Kourou Heavy Lift is scaling its operational interface now, effectively neutralizing the competitor's entry narrative regarding "modernization." We are not betting the fiscal year on regulatory approval; we are betting on immediate customer workflow optimization.

**Decision:** **GO** on Phase 1 development effective immediately.

*Note: Success metrics must shift from "full automation" to "reduction in client storage fees" and "booking confirmation time."*
*Legal counsel should review Phase 1 UI disclaimers regarding manual manifest verification.*
