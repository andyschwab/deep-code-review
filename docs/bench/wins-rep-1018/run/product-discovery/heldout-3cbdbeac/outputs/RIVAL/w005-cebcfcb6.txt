### Investment Justification Analysis: Data Sync Feature

**Executive Summary**
The proposal to hire two senior engineers (increasing burn by 15%) to capture €75k in immediate revenue relies on a high-risk growth hypothesis. Based on current data, the direct ROI is negative without the realization of the secondary market expansion. The justification for this headcount increase is currently weak due to unvalidated technical feasibility and unproven market contagion.

**Financial Risk Assessment**
*   **Immediate Revenue vs. Cost:** €75k in one-time or annual contract value must be weighed against the permanent increase in monthly burn. If the €75k is annual, it yields ~€6.25k/month. If the hiring cost exceeds this amount monthly, the feature operates at an immediate loss.
*   **Break-Even Dependency:** The business case depends entirely on the assumption that 5 additional clinics will sign within six months. Without these follow-on deals, the 15% burn increase may not be recoverable within a reasonable timeframe, jeopardizing runway.

**Critical Evidence Gaps**
To validate this investment for investors, the following specific evidence is currently missing:

1.  **API Stability & Technical Feasibility (High Risk)**
    *   **Missing Data:** There is no audit of the third-party fitness platforms' API changelogs, deprecation policies, or uptime SLAs over the last 12 months.
    *   **Invalidation Scenario:** If the APIs are unstable or require frequent maintenance updates, the two new engineers will be consumed by upkeep rather than new feature development. This turns a revenue-generating hire into a cost-center for technical debt, potentially breaking the sync shortly after launch and triggering contract penalties or churn from the initial two clients.

2.  **Competitor Moves & Market Contagion (High Risk)**
    *   **Missing Data:** There is no verification of whether competitors already offer this integration or if the "market pressure" cited by the Berlin clients is genuine or a negotiation tactic.
    *   **Invalidation Scenario:** If competitors launch a native integration before October, or if the five projected clinics do not view this feature as a mandatory requirement, the projected revenue vanishes. The assumption that "market pressure" will drive adoption is an opinion, not a fact. Without signed Letters of Intent (LOIs) from the five projected clinics, this revenue is speculative.

**Recommendation**
Do not approve the permanent headcount increase based solely on the two verbal indications. The risk of API instability and the lack of committed contracts from the secondary market make the 15% burn increase unjustifiable at this stage.

**Immediate Next Steps for Investor Review:**
1.  **Technical Spike:** Assign an existing engineer to prototype the API integration within one week to assess stability and effort before hiring.
2.  **Commitment Validation:** Convert the two verbal indications into signed LOIs contingent on the October launch.
3.  **Market Verification:** Interview the five projected clinics to confirm their intent to purchase *before* incurring hiring costs.

*Note: Proceeding with hiring before validating API stability risks allocating scarce engineering resources to a potentially unmaintainable feature.*
*Note: Investor confidence will rely on converting "assumed" follow-on sales into documented customer intent.*
