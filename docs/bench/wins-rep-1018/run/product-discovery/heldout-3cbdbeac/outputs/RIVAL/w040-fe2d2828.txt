# Roadmap Recommendation: Prioritize Device Calibration Tool

**Executive Summary**
For the next six-week development cycle, the team must prioritize the **Device Calibration Tool**. While the data visualization dashboard represents a significant revenue opportunity with five major hospital chains, the current calibration issues pose an immediate threat to product viability, data integrity, and operational sustainability.

**Rationale**

1.  **Foundational Data Integrity vs. Feature Enhancement**
    The core value proposition of a medical monitoring device is accurate data. With 30% of user errors stemming from incorrect setup, the current data reliability is compromised. Launching advanced visualizations on top of potentially flawed data creates a "garbage in, garbage out" scenario. If the underlying measurements are unreliable due to calibration errors, sophisticated dashboards lose all clinical utility and could introduce liability risks. Stability must precede enhancement.

2.  **Immediate Operational Bleed**
    The assumption that fixing calibration will reduce support costs by half addresses an active resource drain. High ticket volumes distract engineering and support teams from strategic work. By resolving the root cause of 30% of errors, we immediately free up capacity to tackle the visualization dashboard in the subsequent cycle. Ignoring this now compounds technical debt and operational fatigue.

3.  **Risk Mitigation Strategy for Hospital Chains**
    While the five hospital chains requested visual analytics, there is no confirmation yet that this is a strict "deal-breaker" for renewal.
    *   **Actionable Step:** Before committing engineering resources, Sales must validate the severity of this requirement. Can we secure renewals with a commitment to deliver the dashboard in Q3?
    *   **Counter-Risk:** If we prioritize the dashboard but fail to fix calibration, we risk losing *all* customers (including these five chains) due to unreliable devices. Losing contracts due to broken core functionality is a higher probability risk than losing them due to a missing feature.

**Proposed 6-Week Plan**

*   **Weeks 1-4:** Develop and deploy the Device Calibration Tool. Focus on reducing setup errors and validating data reliability.
*   **Weeks 5-6:** Begin discovery and prototyping for the Data Visualization Dashboard based on specific feedback from the five hospital chains, ensuring the foundation is ready for integration.
*   **Concurrent Action:** Sales to engage the five hospital chains immediately, presenting the roadmap that prioritizes data accuracy first, with a firm delivery date for the dashboard following the stabilization sprint.

**Conclusion**
Prioritizing the calibration tool secures the product's fundamental promise: accurate health data. This decision minimizes immediate operational costs, mitigates liability, and builds a trustworthy foundation upon which the requested visual analytics can be successfully built and sold.

***

*Note: Validate the "deal-breaker" status with the hospital chains immediately to refine the Q3 delivery commitment. Ensure the calibration fix includes automated testing to prevent regression before starting dashboard work.*
