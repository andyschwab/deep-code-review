# Risk-Adjusted Recommendation: Drying Status Tracker Implementation

**Executive Summary**
The $45,000 loss from moisture-related rejections represents a critical viability threat that justifies immediate intervention. However, building a full-scale IoT solution with continuous real-time transmission is **not recommended** at this stage due to high infrastructure risk in rural Guatemala. Instead, we recommend a phased "Offline-First" pilot for the upcoming February season.

**Risk Analysis**

1.  **Value Risk (High Confidence):** The pain point is validated. Inconsistent moisture levels directly caused $45,000 in losses. Farmers and buyers agree that "good" means consistent drying. The willingness to pay is implicit in the avoidance of total loss.
2.  **Feasibility Risk (High Uncertainty):** Your hypothesis relies on continuous data transmission every ten minutes. Rural Guatemalan cooperatives often suffer from intermittent connectivity. Assuming standard 4G/Wi-Fi availability is a dangerous assumption that could render the device useless during critical drying windows.
3.  **Viability Risk (Medium):** If expensive infrastructure upgrades (satellite links or tower construction) are required to support your current hypothesis, the cost may exceed the $45,000 annual loss, making the project financially unviable.

**Strategic Recommendation: The "Store-and-Forward" Pilot**

Do not build the real-time alerting system yet. The risk of infrastructure failure is too high. Instead, pivot the technical approach for the February season:

*   **Modify the Solution:** Develop sensors that **locally store** temperature and humidity data every ten minutes but only attempt to transmit when a connection is available (store-and-forward architecture).
*   **Change the Alert Mechanism:** Instead of real-time dashboard alerts (which require constant uptime), configure the device to send a **single SMS summary** or sync data once daily when connectivity is detected. This reduces dependency on continuous infrastructure.
*   **Pilot Scope:** Deploy 10 units across the most connectivity-challenged cooperatives.

**Decision Matrix for Leadership**

| Scenario | Action | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Build Real-Time Now** | **Reject** | High probability of failure due to connectivity gaps; high upfront infrastructure cost. |
| **Do Nothing** | **Reject** | Guarantees repetition of $45k loss; reputational damage with international buyers. |
| **Pilot Offline-First** | **Proceed** | Validates sensor accuracy (Value) and tests data recovery in low-signal areas (Feasibility) with minimal infrastructure spend. |

**Next Steps for February Season**
1.  **Engineers:** Prototype the local storage module; disable real-time transmission requirements.
2.  **Product/Operations:** Define the minimum data frequency needed to prevent rejection (is 10 mins necessary, or is hourly sufficient?).
3.  **Leadership:** Approve budget for the pilot hardware only, withholding infrastructure upgrade funds until connectivity patterns are mapped during the pilot.

By decoupling data collection from data transmission, you validate the core value proposition (quality control) without betting the project on unreliable rural internet.

*Note: This recommendation assumes the $45k loss is recurring annually; if this was a one-time anomaly, the urgency decreases.*
*Ensure legal compliance regarding data sovereignty when storing agricultural data locally on devices.*
