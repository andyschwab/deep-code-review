# Priority Assessment: Secure Photo Tracker Feature

**Recommendation:** Proceed with immediate discovery to validate the "High Impact, High Risk" assumption regarding storage costs and workflow efficiency before committing engineering resources. Do not build the full feature yet.

### 1. The Core Problem & Opportunity
*   **Signal Strength:** 90% of interviewed nurses (18/20) identified a critical pain point: privacy risks and workflow friction using personal devices. This indicates a high-value opportunity to improve compliance and nurse satisfaction.
*   **Proposed Solution:** A dedicated, secure photo tracker within the existing app.
*   **Potential Value:** Assumed reduction of 10 minutes per visit via future automated analysis; immediate value in risk mitigation (HIPAA/compliance) and workflow standardization.

### 2. Critical Assumptions & Risks
We have identified two primary assumptions that must be validated before development:

*   **Assumption A (Viability/Feasibility):** Storing high-resolution images for 500 active patients will not double current cloud storage costs.
    *   *Risk Level:* **High**. If costs double, the unit economics of the service may break.
    *   *Unknown:* Current baseline costs vs. projected image storage volume.
*   **Assumption B (Value):** Automated image analysis will realistically save 10 minutes per visit.
    *   *Risk Level:* **Medium**. This is a future-state benefit. The immediate benefit is security/compliance, which is already validated by nurse feedback.

### 3. Impact vs. Risk Analysis
*   **Impact:** **High**. Addresses a compliance liability (personal devices) and affects 100% of the nursing staff daily.
*   **Risk:** **High**. Requires 300 engineering hours and delays the billing module (a revenue-critical feature) by four weeks. The financial risk of unforeseen storage costs is significant.
*   **Classification:** **High Impact / High Risk**. This quadrant requires a targeted experiment, not immediate full-scale build.

### 4. Recommended Next Steps (Discovery Sprint)
Instead of committing 300 hours immediately, execute the following low-effort tests over the next 5 days:

1.  **Cloud Cost Simulation (Engineering):** Calculate the exact storage cost for 500 patients uploading one image daily for a month using current provider rates. *Success Metric:* Confirmed cost increase is <20% of current budget.
2.  **Workflow Prototype (Design/PM):** Create a clickable prototype of the photo upload flow. Test with 3 nurses to verify it actually saves time compared to their current "hack" and doesn't introduce new friction. *Success Metric:* Nurses can complete the flow in under 30 seconds without errors.
3.  **Billing Module Impact Check (Product):** Confirm with the Head of Product if a 4-week delay on billing introduces regulatory or cash-flow risks that outweigh the nursing compliance risk.

### 5. Decision Framework for Sprint Planning
*   **If** storage costs are projected to increase by >25% **OR** the billing delay causes revenue leakage: **Defer** the feature. Explore lightweight alternatives (e.g., approved enterprise camera apps with MDM) while re-architecting storage.
*   **If** costs are manageable AND billing delay is acceptable: **Allocate 40 hours** to build a "Concierge MVP" (manual backend or limited rollout to 10 nurses) to validate the 10-minute time-saving hypothesis before full build.

***

*Note: This assessment prioritizes validating the financial viability (storage costs) before consuming engineering capacity. The compliance risk of personal devices is urgent, but the solution must be economically sustainable.*
