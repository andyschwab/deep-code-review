### Evaluation of Evidentiary Gaps for CalmVR Clinical Adoption

**Current Status:**
Your current evidence package consists of a small-scale pilot (n=20) relying exclusively on subjective self-reported data. While a 40% reduction in perceived pain is a promising signal, it is insufficient on its own to secure broad clinical adoption or Series A funding in the current medical technology landscape.

**Critical Gaps:**
1.  **Lack of Objective Corroboration:** The absence of physiological markers (heart rate variability, cortisol levels, galvanic skin response) leaves the results vulnerable to the "placebo effect" critique. In pain management, especially with novel interventions like VR, distinguishing between psychological distraction and physiological pain modulation is critical for clinical credibility.
2.  **Sample Size and Statistical Power:** Twenty patients provide anecdotal validity but lack the statistical power to generalize findings across diverse demographics, burn severities, or hospital protocols. Administrators require data that predicts outcomes for their specific patient populations, which a sample of 20 cannot reliably provide.
3.  **Comparator Limitations:** Without a control group (e.g., standard care vs. standard care + non-immersive distraction vs. CalmVR), it is difficult to isolate the specific efficacy of the VR immersion versus the novelty of any new intervention.

**Recommendation:**
**Do not proceed with the full expansion to five hospitals yet.** Doing so risks reputational damage if early adopters encounter skepticism they cannot overcome with data, potentially stalling future sales cycles.

**Strategic Path Forward:**
Instead of a full commercial rollout, structure the next phase as a **multi-center feasibility study** with the following components:
*   **Integrate Objective Metrics:** Partner with the five target hospitals to run a structured study collecting both self-reported pain scores (VAS) and physiological data during dressing changes.
*   **Expand Sample Size:** Aim for a cohort of at least 60–80 patients to achieve preliminary statistical significance.
*   **Control for Variables:** Ensure the protocol accounts for pain medication dosage and burn severity to demonstrate that CalmVR adds value beyond standard pharmacological interventions.

**Investor & Advisory Board Message:**
Frame this not as a delay, but as a strategic de-risking step. Investors in MedTech prioritize regulatory readiness and clinical validation over rapid, unproven scaling. Presenting a plan to gather objective data demonstrates scientific rigor and reduces the risk of market rejection. The narrative should shift from "We have great pilot results" to "We have strong preliminary signals and a funded plan to validate them with objective clinical data, positioning us for definitive market entry."

**Conclusion:**
Your assumption that self-reported data alone is sufficient is high-risk. The gap between "promising pilot" and "clinical standard" requires objective validation. Delaying the full commercial expansion to execute a rigorous, data-rich study will significantly increase your valuation and adoption probability.
