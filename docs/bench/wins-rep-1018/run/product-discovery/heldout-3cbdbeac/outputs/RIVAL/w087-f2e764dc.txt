### Critical Information Gaps for Go/No-Go Decision

**1. Regulatory & Compliance Unknowns (High Risk)**
*   **Data Certification Requirements:** Does PSA Terminal or the Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore (MPA) mandate specific data integrity certifications (e.g., ISO 27001, specific port-community system standards) before third parties can display terminal status?
*   **Security Clearance Levels:** What specific security clearance or vetting process is required for PortLink employees to access and re-transmit official terminal data? Is there a formal application window that exceeds the quarter-end deadline?
*   **Liability & SLA Constraints:** If the GPS data proves inaccurate, what are the contractual liabilities? Does the port authority require a guaranteed uptime or accuracy SLA (e.g., 99.9% accuracy) that current trucking partner data cannot legally support?

**2. Data Feasibility & Technical Assumptions (High Risk)**
*   **GPS Accuracy Validation:** Has the existing trucking partner GPS data been audited against actual terminal gate-in/gate-out timestamps? The assumption that it is "accurate enough" is unverified; a 30% delay in updates suggests a potential systemic gap in current data ingestion, not just a display issue.
*   **Last-Mile Visibility:** Does the GPS data cover the specific "blind spots" inside the terminal yard where the 30% of delays occur, or does it only track trucks on public roads? If the delay happens inside the gate, external GPS may be useless regardless of certification.

**3. Market Demand Verification (Medium Risk)**
*   **Willingness to Pay vs. Willingness to Integrate:** While officials acknowledged the pain point, have they explicitly committed to integrating PortLink's solution into their workflow? Acknowledging a problem is not validation of your specific solution.
*   **Competitive/Alternative Solutions:** Are there existing mandated systems or competitors already approved to solve this? The urgency of the calls might stem from an impending regulatory change that favors a different provider.

**4. Strategic & Operational Risks**
*   **Implementation Timeline:** Given the need for potential security clearance and data certification, is it technically possible to launch a compliant MVP before the board's budget approval deadline impacts the project timeline?
*   **Partner Cooperation:** Will trucking partners agree to the increased data granularity or frequency required to meet port standards without renegotiating contracts?

### Recommended Immediate Actions
1.  **Request a Formal Compliance Checklist:** Ask the PSA contacts specifically for the "Third-Party Data Integration Requirements" document rather than discussing features.
2.  **Conduct a Data Audit:** Run a retrospective analysis of the last 30 days of GPS data against known port congestion events to quantify the actual accuracy gap.
3.  **Secure a Pilot Letter of Intent:** Before seeking full budget approval, ask the port authority for a non-binding letter stating they would pilot the feature *if* compliance hurdles are cleared.

*Note: Do not allocate development budget until the data accuracy audit and compliance checklist are received. The risk of building a feature that cannot be legally displayed or technically supported is critical.*
