### Decision Framework: Real-Time Container Tracking Integration

**Recommendation:** Do not commit full resources based solely on six emails. The evidence indicates a strong signal but lacks the breadth to confirm a "universal standard" or validate the $2M renewal assumption. Proceed with a **4-week validation sprint** before locking Q1 strategy.

#### 1. Evidence Gap Analysis
*   **Sample Size Risk:** Six shipping lines represent a significant voice, but without knowing your total partner count, this may be a vocal minority rather than a market standard. If you have 50+ partners, 12% adoption demand is notable but not definitive.
*   **Solution Ambiguity:** The requests may stem from different root causes (e.g., one carrier needs API data, another needs a UI dashboard, a third needs automated alerts). Building a single "beta" risks solving none of them perfectly.
*   **Assumption Validity:** The link between "tracking feature" and "$2M contract renewal" is unproven. Contracts often hinge on SLA adherence, safety, or cost, not just visibility features. The 12-hour delay claim is a strong pain point, but you must verify if *your* specific integration is the only way to solve it.

#### 2. Immediate Validation Steps (Weeks 1-4)
Before the technical team starts coding, execute these three low-effort checks:

*   **Conduct "The Mom Test" Interviews:** Contact the six requesters immediately. Do not pitch the beta. Ask:
    *   "Walk me through the last time a 12-hour delay occurred. What specifically was missing?"
    *   "What workarounds are you currently using to get this data?"
    *   "Have you budgeted time or money to solve this internally if we don't?"
    *   *Goal:* Determine if the workflows are identical or custom.
*   **Survey the Silent Majority:** Send a brief survey to all other shipping line partners. Ask if they face similar visibility gaps and how they currently resolve them. This confirms if the need is universal.
*   **Quantify the Renewal Risk:** Speak with your Account Managers. Ask: "Is there explicit language in these contracts tying renewal to this specific feature? Or is this a 'nice-to-have' that has become loud?"

#### 3. Go/No-Go Decision Matrix for CEO
Use this criteria to finalize the Q1 commitment:

| Scenario | Action | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Universal Need + High Renewal Risk** | **Green Light** | If >50% of partners confirm the pain and contracts explicitly risk churn, commit the 8-week build. |
| **Divergent Needs + High Pain** | **Pivot to Discovery** | If workflows differ, do not build a generic beta. Design a modular solution or prioritize the highest-value carrier for a pilot. |
| **Low Renewal Risk + High Pain** | **Defer or Partner** | If the feature solves a pain point but doesn't drive revenue/retention, explore integrating a third-party tracking tool instead of building custom. |
| **Workarounds Exist + Low Urgency** | **No Go** | If carriers are already solving this via email/phone effectively, the 12-hour delay may be an accepted cost of doing business. |

#### 4. Strategic Recommendation for Q1
Propose a **"Phased Commitment"** to the CEO:
1.  **Allocate 20% of engineering capacity** for 4 weeks to prototype a mock-up or conduct technical spikes (not full build).
2.  **Condition the remaining 80%** on validating that at least 3 of the 6 carriers agree to a pilot program with defined success metrics (e.g., reduction in gate time by 4 hours).
3.  **Re-evaluate in Month 2** with data from the pilot to secure the full Q1 roadmap slot.

This approach protects the $2M revenue assumption from being a guess while demonstrating responsiveness to key partners.

*Note: Ensure the technical spike includes a feasibility check on data latency; real-time tracking often fails due to upstream carrier data delays, not your system's speed.*
*If the validation reveals custom workflow needs, consider positioning this as a premium "Enterprise Integration" tier rather than a standard feature.*
