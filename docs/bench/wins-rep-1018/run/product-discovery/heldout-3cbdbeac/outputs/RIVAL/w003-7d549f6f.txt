### Immediate Action Plan: Validate Before Redesign

Do not proceed with a complete redesign yet. The data suggests a potential usability crisis, but the assumption that "five reviews represent the silent majority" is unverified. A full overhaul is a high-cost solution; you must first validate the scope and root cause of the friction.

#### 1. Quantitative Metrics to Gather (Internal Data)
Before talking to users, analyze existing telemetry to determine if the complaint is widespread among the 8,000 active users:
*   **Funnel Drop-off Rates:** Measure the specific drop-off rate at each step of the "View Lab Results" journey. Compare this to the same period last year.
*   **Time-on-Task & Tap Count:** Calculate the average number of taps and time spent to reach lab results. Segment this by device type and OS version to rule out technical bugs vs. design flaws.
*   **Support Ticket Ratio:** Calculate the percentage of users who opened a support ticket regarding navigation (15 tickets) against the total active user base (8,000). If this is <0.2%, the issue may be isolated; if higher, it indicates a systemic block.
*   **Retention Cohort Analysis:** Isolate the 3% retention drop. Did it occur specifically among users who attempted to view lab results but failed? Correlate the churn event with the specific feature usage.

#### 2. Qualitative Research Plan (User Interviews)
Conduct 5–7 rapid interviews with Brazilian users to test the "silent majority" hypothesis. Do not ask if they like the design; observe their behavior.
*   **Recruitment Criteria:**
    *   3 users who recently viewed lab results successfully.
    *   2 users who attempted to view results but dropped off (if identifiable via analytics).
    *   2 users who contacted support recently (regardless of topic).
*   **Key Questions (Past Behavior Focus):**
    *   "Walk me through the last time you tried to find your lab results on the app. What exactly did you do?"
    *   "Tell me about a time you felt frustrated or confused while using the patient portal. What were you trying to accomplish?"
    *   "What tools or workarounds did you use when you couldn't find what you needed?"
*   **Accessibility Check:** Explicitly observe if any participants use screen readers or have vision impairments to determine if the five reviews stem from a specific accessibility gap rather than general navigation.

#### 3. Decision Matrix
*   **Proceed with Redesign If:** Funnel drop-off is >20% higher than baseline, OR interview participants consistently fail to complete the task without assistance, OR the retention drop correlates directly with the lab results feature.
*   **Defer Redesign If:** The quantitative data shows normal completion rates, and interviews reveal the five reviewers have unique accessibility needs not met by the current standard design (requiring a targeted accessibility patch, not a full overhaul).

**Note:** A full redesign based solely on five reviews risks solving a non-existent problem for the 99% while ignoring the actual retention driver.
**Next Step:** Run the quantitative analysis immediately; schedule interviews for early next week.
