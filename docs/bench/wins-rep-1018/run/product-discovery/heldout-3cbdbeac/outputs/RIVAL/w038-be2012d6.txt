### Evidence Strength Evaluation: Low to Moderate

**Current Evidence:**
*   **Source:** Three specific clinics (Dr. Evans + 2 partners).
*   **Urgency:** High (threat of churn by March 1).
*   **Assumption:** A simple text-based logger solves 80% of needs; these clinics represent a broader market.
*   **Gap:** No data confirms if this need exists outside these three accounts or if their workflows match your existing fitness enthusiast base.

**Verdict:** The evidence confirms a **retention risk** with three specific clients but fails to validate a **market opportunity**. Allocating the entire Q2 budget based solely on this is high-risk. These clinics may be outliers requiring custom enterprise features that do not scale to your core user base. The threat of churn indicates value for *them*, but not necessarily viability for the *product strategy*.

### Rapid Validation Plan (1-Week Sprint)

To justify a pivot (or rejection) to investors by next Tuesday, execute the following steps immediately:

**1. Qualitative "Mom Test" Interviews (Days 1-2)**
Interview the three requesting clinics and five existing fitness users.
*   **Ask the Clinics:** "Walk me through the last time you needed a patient to log diet. What specific data fields did you need? What happened when they used your current tool?" (Focus on specific past behaviors, not future promises).
*   **Ask Existing Users:** "Have you ever tried to log diet for a coach or doctor? If not, why? What tools do you currently use for that?"
*   **Goal:** Determine if the clinics' needs (e.g., HIPAA compliance, clinician dashboards, specific macro splits) are fundamentally different from fitness enthusiasts.

**2. Quantitative Signal Check (Days 3-4)**
*   **Survey Existing Base:** Send a one-question survey to 500 active users: "Would you use a dietary logging feature if it allowed you to share reports directly with a nutritionist? Yes/No/Maybe."
*   **Analyze Support Tickets:** Search historical data for keywords like "diet," "nutritionist," "log," or "export." Zero organic demand suggests these clinics are outliers.

**3. The "Fake Door" Test (Days 5-6)**
*   Add a "Dietary Logging" button to the app for a small segment (excluding the three clinics).
*   When clicked, show a message: "Coming Soon: Share diet logs with your coach. Join the waitlist."
*   **Metric:** If click-through rate is <2%, broader demand is likely weak.

**4. Financial Stress Test (Day 7)**
*   Calculate the Lifetime Value (LTV) of the three clinics vs. the cost of building/maintaining the feature.
*   If the feature costs $100k to build but only saves $15k in annual recurring revenue (ARR) from these three clients, it is a custom service, not a product pivot.

### Recommendation for Investor Update

**Do not commit the full Q2 budget yet.** Instead, propose a **"Targeted Retention Sprint"**:
*   Allocate 20% of Q2 resources to build a *minimal* text-logger specifically for the three clinics to prevent churn.
*   Use the remaining 80% for the core roadmap while running the validation tests above.
*   **Pivot Condition:** Only shift full budget if the survey shows >15% interest from the core base AND the fake door test shows significant engagement.

This approach mitigates immediate churn risk while protecting the company from building a niche feature that alienates the broader market.

*Note: Ensure the "simple text-based" assumption is challenged during interviews; clinics often require structured data (grams/micros) rather than free text.*
