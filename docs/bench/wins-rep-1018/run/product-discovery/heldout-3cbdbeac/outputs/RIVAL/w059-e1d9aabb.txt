### Strategic Decision Framework: HeartMonitor X vs. Patient Feedback System

To finalize the Q3 budget, the executive team requires clarity on four critical data gaps before choosing between immediate revenue and long-term strategic positioning.

**1. Validation of the "Moat" Assumption**
*   **Missing Data:** Competitive landscape analysis regarding HeartMonitor X.
*   **Key Question:** How easily can competitors replicate this integration? If competitors can build the same connector in four weeks, the perceived "moat" is illusory.
*   **Action Needed:** Rapid technical assessment of the HeartMonitor X API openness and a scan of competitor roadmaps.

**2. Quantification of the Feedback System's Revenue Impact**
*   **Missing Data:** The specific correlation coefficient between patient satisfaction scores (driven by feedback tools) and hospital reimbursement rates in your target regions.
*   **Key Question:** What is the potential revenue uplift for a hospital client using your feedback system? If a 5% increase in satisfaction yields $200k in extra funding per hospital, the six-week investment may outperform the $1.2M one-time contract over 12 months.
*   **Action Needed:** Consult healthcare finance experts or review public CMS (or local equivalent) reimbursement formulas to model potential ROI.

**3. Contract Certainty vs. Opportunity Cost**
*   **Missing Data:** The binding nature of the $1.2M request.
*   **Key Question:** Are the hospital chains willing to sign a Letter of Intent (LOI) or pre-pay if development starts now? Conversely, what is the churn risk if the feedback system is *not* built?
*   **Action Needed:** Immediate sales discovery to convert "requests" into committed pipeline value. Determine if the $1.2M is lost forever if delayed to Q4, or if it can be secured with a later delivery date.

**4. Resource Capacity and Strategic Focus**
*   **Missing Data:** The opportunity cost of splitting the team.
*   **Key Question:** Can the team deliver *both* by staggering starts, or does context switching threaten both timelines?
*   **Action Needed:** Review engineering velocity data. If the team must choose one, calculate the "Cost of Delay" for each. Does delaying the feedback system risk losing future contracts tied to satisfaction metrics?

**Recommended Next Steps Before Friday:**
1.  **Sales:** Secure a conditional commitment from the two hospital chains contingent on a start date.
2.  **Product/Research:** Conduct a 48-hour analysis of reimbursement formulas to estimate the feedback system's monetary value.
3.  **Engineering:** Confirm if the HeartMonitor X integration creates a defensible technical barrier or merely a temporary feature lead.

Without these data points, the decision rests on unverified assumptions: that hardware creates a moat and that software feedback lacks direct revenue impact.

*Note: Prioritize gathering the reimbursement correlation data first, as it directly challenges the assumption that the feedback system is purely a "nice-to-have."*
*Ensure the sales team clarifies if the $1.2M contract requires exclusivity, which would further impact the strategic moat calculation.*
