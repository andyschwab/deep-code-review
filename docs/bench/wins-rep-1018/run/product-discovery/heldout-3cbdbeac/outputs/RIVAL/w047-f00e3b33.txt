### Executive Recommendation: Prioritize Real-Time Bed Availability Tracker

Based on the current evidence, the **Real-Time Bed Availability Tracker** demonstrates stronger urgency and clearer customer impact than the Staff Credential Verification system.

**Evidence Comparison**

1.  **Quantifiable Operational Loss (Bed Tracker)**
    *   **Current State:** The organization loses **20 hours per week** directly due to bed coordination errors.
    *   **Projected Impact:** Your assumption of a 45-minute reduction in average wait times addresses a critical bottleneck (ER boarding) that affects patient flow across all 12 hospitals immediately upon deployment.
    *   **Consensus:** Supported by 50% of operations directors, this feature targets a continuous, high-frequency pain point occurring daily.

2.  **Ambiguous Financial Impact (Credential Verification)**
    *   **Current State:** Credentialing delays caused **15 shift cancellations** last month.
    *   **Unknown Variable:** There is no data confirming whether these cancellations resulted in lost revenue, overtime costs, or merely administrative reshuffling. Without knowing the financial cost per cancellation, the total impact remains speculative.
    *   **Frequency:** This is an intermittent issue (15 instances/month) compared to the continuous weekly loss of 20 hours seen in bed coordination.

**Strategic Rationale**
The Bed Tracker addresses a known, quantified inefficiency with immediate implications for patient outcomes and operational throughput. The Credential Verification system, while important, relies on an unvalidated assumption regarding revenue loss. In a resource-constrained environment (one major module), prioritizing the feature with **confirmed high-frequency friction** over the feature with **unclear financial consequences** minimizes risk.

**Immediate Next Steps for Approval**
To secure executive approval by tomorrow:
*   Frame the Bed Tracker as a direct recovery of **80+ operational hours per month**.
*   Flag the Credential Verification system for a rapid discovery sprint next quarter to quantify the actual revenue impact of the 15 monthly cancellations before committing build resources.

***

*Note: This recommendation assumes the 20-hour weekly loss figure is accurate and consistently tracked across all 12 regions.*
