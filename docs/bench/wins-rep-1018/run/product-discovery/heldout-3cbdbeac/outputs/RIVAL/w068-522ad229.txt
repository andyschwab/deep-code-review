**Decision Memo: Patient Communication Upgrade Strategy**

**To:** VP of Engineering, Chief Compliance Officer
**From:** Product Leadership
**Date:** October 26, 2023
**Subject:** Recommendation for Secure Patient Messaging Solution

**Executive Summary**
Following two minor compliance incidents involving standard email, immediate action is required to secure patient-provider communications. We evaluated two paths: building a custom patient portal ($180k, 6 months) or integrating a third-party secure email gateway ($40k/year, 8 weeks). **We recommend proceeding immediately with the Third-Party Secure Email Gateway.**

**Comparative Analysis**

*1. Compliance & Risk Mitigation (Immediate Safety)*
The primary driver for this initiative is risk reduction. Standard email has already failed our compliance standards twice this quarter.
*   **Custom Portal:** Leaves the organization exposed for six months during development. Any delay in the Identity Provider (IdP) integration extends this risk window further.
*   **Secure Gateway:** Can be deployed in eight weeks, drastically reducing the window of non-compliance. It leverages established, certified security protocols immediately upon launch.

*2. Financial & Maintenance Burden (Long-Term View)*
*   **Custom Portal:** Requires a significant upfront CAPEX of $180,000. However, the hidden long-term cost is the ongoing maintenance burden. Engineering will own security patches, uptime monitoring, feature updates, and IdP synchronization indefinitely. This diverts engineering resources from core product innovation.
*   **Secure Gateway:** Operates on an OPEX model ($40,000 annually). While cumulative costs may exceed the build cost after 4.5 years, this fee includes vendor-managed security updates, compliance certifications, and infrastructure scaling. The maintenance burden shifts from our internal team to the vendor.

*3. User Adoption & Demographics*
A critical assumption is that 70% of our elderly patient demographic prefers email interfaces over new login portals.
*   **Custom Portal:** Requires patients to learn a new interface and manage new credentials (unless SSO is seamless). If the assumption holds true, adoption rates may suffer, leading to continued use of insecure channels.
*   **Secure Gateway:** Meets patients in their existing workflow (email) while encrypting the transport layer. This aligns with the presumed preference of our demographic and reduces friction, likely increasing immediate compliance adherence.

**Addressing the Unknown Variable**
The uncertainty regarding our existing Identity Provider's ability to support Single Sign-On (SSO) for a custom portal poses a schedule risk. If the IdP cannot support the requirements without significant refactoring, the six-month timeline for the custom portal will slip, extending our compliance exposure. The secure gateway solution minimizes this dependency, requiring only standard directory synchronization rather than complex SSO flows for external users.

**Recommendation**
Approve the **$40,000 annual budget for the Third-Party Secure Email Gateway**.

This path prioritizes immediate compliance safety, aligns with patient behavior patterns, and eliminates the long-term engineering maintenance burden of hosting a secure messaging infrastructure. We should revisit the custom portal concept only if future strategic goals require deep integration with clinical records that a gateway cannot support.

**Next Steps**
1. Procurement to initiate vendor selection for the secure gateway by Monday.
2. Engineering to begin technical discovery for the 8-week integration timeline.
3. Compliance to draft updated communication policies reflecting the new secure channel.

***

*Note: This recommendation assumes the secure gateway vendor meets our specific HIPAA/BAA requirements, which must be verified during vendor selection.*
*The custom portal option should be archived as a future strategic initiative should patient demographics shift toward app-based interactions.*
