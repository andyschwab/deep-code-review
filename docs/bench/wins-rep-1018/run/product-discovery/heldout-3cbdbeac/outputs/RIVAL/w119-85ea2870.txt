### Verdict: Do Not Build Yet – Conduct Targeted Interviews First

The volume of complaints (10 managers) and the resulting churn (3 clients, €75k loss) validate that a **problem exists**, but they do **not** validate your specific **solution** (a digital booking platform) or the **business model** (premium pricing for guaranteed space).

Building the platform now carries high risk for two reasons:
1.  **Root Cause Ambiguity:** You explicitly noted the possibility that these were "specific issues with our communication." If the core issue was poor communication rather than actual capacity shortages, a software platform will not fix the problem and may add unnecessary friction.
2.  **Unverified Willingness to Pay:** Your assumption that resorts will pay a *premium* for guaranteed space is untested. They may expect guaranteed space as a baseline service included in their existing equipment supply contract. Charging extra could accelerate churn rather than prevent it.

**Recommended Immediate Action:**
Pause development and execute **5–7 structured customer interviews** with the 10 complaining managers (including the 3 who left) before October.

**Interview Objectives:**
*   **Verify the Pain:** Confirm if the issue was truly lack of space or a failure in communication/visibility.
*   **Test the Solution:** Ask how they currently manage off-season storage and what they tried to solve it.
*   **Validate Pricing:** Determine if they have ever paid for guaranteed storage elsewhere or if they view it as a mandatory service level.

**Decision Threshold:**
*   **Proceed to Build:** If at least 4 out of 7 interviewed managers confirm that lack of guaranteed space was the primary driver for leaving AND express willingness to pay a separate fee for a solution.
*   **Pivot:** If the issue was communication, invest in a simpler notification system or process change instead of a full booking platform.
*   **Abort:** If managers view guaranteed storage as a basic expectation that should be free with their equipment supply.

The €75k loss is significant enough to warrant investigation, but not large enough to justify building a complex platform without confirming the root cause and pricing model. Use the next 3 weeks to interview, then decide.

*Note: Focus interviews on past behaviors (e.g., "What did you do last summer when you ran out of space?") rather than hypothetical future usage.*
