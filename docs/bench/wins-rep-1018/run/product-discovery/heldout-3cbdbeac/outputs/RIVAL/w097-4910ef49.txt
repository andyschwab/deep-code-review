### Executive Summary: Cold Chain Monitoring Viability Assessment

**Core Recommendation:** Do not proceed with building a proprietary monitoring dashboard or installing custom hardware at this stage. The root cause of the rejections remains unverified, and the logistical friction with current partners presents a critical "Go-to-Market" and "Feasibility" blocker that outweighs the potential value.

**1. Root Cause Ambiguity (Value Risk)**
Your assumption that sensors will reduce spoilage by 15% relies on the premise that *undetected* temperature excursions are the primary failure point. However, your data only confirms transit time (18 days), not specific failure moments. If the excursions occurred due to pre-cooling failures before the container left Can Tho, or during port handling in Ho Chi Minh City where sensors might be ignored, real-time data alone will not prevent loss. Without knowing *where* and *why* the temperature broke, a dashboard only provides a post-mortem report, not a preventative solution.

**2. Logistics Partner Constraints (Feasibility & Strategy Risk)**
The hesitation of your Ho Chi Minh City logistics partners to allow third-party hardware installation is a critical blocker. Renegotiating service contracts introduces significant delay and cost. If the partner refuses integration, your proprietary hardware becomes useless "stranded assets." Furthermore, if the partner controls the physical handling, they can bypass or damage external sensors, rendering the data unreliable. This suggests a **Team/Partnership risk** that is higher than the technical risk.

**3. Build vs. Buy Analysis (Viability Risk)**
Building a proprietary dashboard is capital-intensive and diverts focus from your core competency: seafood export.
*   **Cost:** Developing a secure, real-time dashboard requires ongoing engineering maintenance, cloud infrastructure, and 24/7 support.
*   **Alternative:** Established cold-chain solutions (e.g., Sensitech, Controlant) already offer certified data logs accepted by US buyers. They often have pre-negotiated standing with major logistics firms.
*   **Verdict:** Building proprietary tech adds unnecessary complexity when the market offers validated, accepted alternatives. The board's concern about addressing symptoms rather than root causes is valid; a custom dashboard risks being an expensive symptom tracker.

**4. Strategic Pivot: Verification Before Investment**
Before approving any capital expenditure, execute the following low-cost validation steps:
*   **Step A (Data Acquisition):** Rent or borrow standard, non-integrated data loggers for the next 3 shipments. These require no contract renegotiation as they are passive. This will definitively identify *when* and *where* the temperature excursions occur (e.g., during loading vs. mid-ocean).
*   **Step B (Partner Dialogue):** Present the passive logger data to your logistics partner. Ask specifically: "At which point in this graph did the breach occur, and what is your protocol to prevent it?" Their response will reveal if the issue is equipment failure, human error, or contractual misalignment.
*   **Step C (Buyer Alignment):** Confirm with the Seattle and Miami buyers if a specific *certified* data provider is required for reinstatement. Proprietary data may not satisfy their compliance teams.

**Conclusion for the Board**
The proposed solution (proprietary dashboard + custom sensors) addresses a hypothetical problem with a high-friction implementation. The immediate priority is **diagnostic clarity**, not technical construction. Approve a minimal budget for passive data logging on the next three shipments. If the data confirms that real-time intervention could have saved the cargo *and* the logistics partner agrees to act on such data, then evaluate existing vendor solutions. Do not build custom software until the physical process failure is isolated and the partner ecosystem is aligned.

*Note: This analysis prioritizes risk mitigation over feature development, aligning with continuous discovery principles to validate root causes before scaling solutions.*
