### Operational Trade-Off Evaluation: Serialized Bottle Tracking

**Executive Summary**
Implementing serialized bottle tracking addresses the critical $120,000 loss from broken/mislabeled shipments by establishing immutable chain-of-custody proof. However, the primary operational risk is a potential breach of the 10% throughput tolerance during the peak harvest season (starting March). The trade-off centers on **accuracy vs. velocity**.

**Key Operational Trade-Offs**

1.  **Throughput Velocity vs. Error Reduction**
    *   **Trade-off:** Scanning every bottle individually inevitably increases touch-time per unit compared to batch scanning or visual checks.
    *   **Risk:** If the scanning process adds >6 seconds per bottle, the 10% slowdown threshold will be exceeded, creating bottlenecks at the packing station.
    *   **Mitigation Requirement:** Hardware latency must be near-zero, and UI design must require minimal interaction (e.g., auto-scan on trigger pull) to maintain pace.

2.  **Staff Cognitive Load vs. Process Compliance**
    *   **Trade-off:** Warehouse staff must shift from speed-focused packing to verification-focused packing.
    *   **Risk:** Under pressure to meet shipping windows, staff may develop "workarounds" (e.g., scanning one bottle to represent a case) if the system feels too burdensome, rendering the data unreliable.
    *   **Mitigation Requirement:** Extensive training and a simplified interface are required to ensure the new process becomes muscle memory before March.

3.  **Upfront Implementation Cost vs. Downstream Liability**
    *   **Trade-off:** Investing in scanners, software integration, and training now versus absorbing potential future rejection costs.
    *   **Risk:** A failed rollout during the high-volume harvest season could cause greater delays than the current error rate, damaging client relationships further.
    *   **Mitigation Requirement:** A phased pilot with one client or one SKU line is essential before full deployment.

**Critical Assumptions Requiring Immediate Validation**
Before board approval, the following assumptions must be tested to ensure feasibility:
*   **Feasibility:** Current warehouse Wi-Fi infrastructure can handle hundreds of concurrent scan requests without latency.
*   **Usability:** Staff can maintain >90% of their current packing speed after a 2-week adaptation period.
*   **Value:** European clients explicitly require *serialized* data to resolve disputes, not just batch-level tracking.

**Recommendation**
Proceed with a **two-week pilot program** immediately using a single packing lane. Measure the exact time-per-bottle increase and error rate reduction. If the slowdown exceeds 8% during the pilot, the solution requires process re-engineering before the March harvest rush. Do not roll out fleet-wide without this data.

*Note: This evaluation assumes the $120k loss was due to internal handling errors; if damage occurs primarily during third-party transit, serialization alone may not solve the root cause.*
