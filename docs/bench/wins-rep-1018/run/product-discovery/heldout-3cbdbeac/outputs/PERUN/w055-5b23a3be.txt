### Decision Framework: Photo-First vs. Video-Mandate

**Option A: Prioritize Photo Uploads (User Volume Focus)**
*   **Hypothesis:** Retaining the 68% majority preference drives long-term organic growth and retention, outweighing the loss of a single partner.
*   **Known Evidence:** 2,856 users (68% of 4,200) explicitly prefer photos; only ~500 users (12%) requested video.
*   **Immediate Risk:** High probability of losing Northside Health contract by March 15.
*   **Unknown Variable (Requires Validation):** Will smaller clinics cancel without video, or do they align with the 68% patient preference?
*   **Revenue Impact Structure:**
    *   *Loss:* [Insert Northside Contract Value]
    *   *Gain/Retention:* [Insert Projected LTV of 2,856 photo-preferring users] + [Insert Value of Smaller Clinics if they stay]
    *   *Net:* (Gain/Retention) – (Loss)

**Option B: Pivot to Video Consultations (Contract Stability Focus)**
*   **Hypothesis:** Securing the enterprise contract provides immediate cash flow and market validation, even if it mismatches current individual user preferences.
*   **Known Evidence:** Northside Health has issued a hard deadline (March 15) for renewal contingent on video.
*   **Immediate Risk:** Alienating the 68% majority who prefer photos; potential drop in individual user engagement if the UI shifts focus to video.
*   **Unknown Variable (Requires Validation):** Can the video feature be built and tested reliably by March 15 without breaking the existing photo workflow?
*   **Revenue Impact Structure:**
    *   *Gain:* [Insert Northside Contract Value] + [Insert Value of Smaller Clinics if they follow suit]
    *   *Loss/Risk:* [Insert Churn Cost if photo-preferring users disengage] + [Insert Development Opportunity Cost]
    *   *Net:* (Gain) – (Loss/Risk)

**Critical Unknowns & Required Tests (Before Final Decision)**
1.  **Smaller Clinic Intent:** Do not assume smaller clinics follow Northside.
    *   *Test:* Contact top 5 smaller clinic partners immediately. Ask: "If we lack video by March 15, does that impact your renewal?" (Yes/No/Maybe).
2.  **Northside Flexibility:** Is the March 15 deadline absolute or negotiable?

3.  **Feasibility Check:** Can the engineering team deliver a stable video MVP by March 15?
    *   *Test:* Engineering lead to provide a "Riskiest Assumption" estimate: Hours required vs. Hours available. If hours required > hours available, Option B carries a delivery failure risk.
