**To:** CTO, FieldCare Solutions
**From:** Product Discovery
**Subject:** Decision Brief: Full Offline Mode vs. Compliance Delay

**Current Evidence State: UNVERIFIED**
The available data does not yet support a decision to build the full offline mode, nor does it confirm the assumption that delaying compliance is too costly. We are currently making a high-stakes architectural decision based on an untested hypothesis regarding user environment constraints.

**1. The Riskiest Assumption Gate**
*   **Assumption:** A critical mass of our 400 users operate in "zero-connectivity" zones where simple caching fails, and that preventing data loss in these specific scenarios is more valuable than the compliance update delay.
*   **Current Data Gap:** We know 50 tickets were logged for data loss, but only 5 explicitly mentioned "total lack of internet." We do not know the denominator: how many total visits occur in zero-connectivity vs. spotty signal zones.
*   **Proposed Cheap Test (Before Build):** Do not start the 3-month build. Instead, run a "Wizard-of-Oz" or manual concierge test for 2 weeks.
    *   *Action:* Identify the 5 nurses who reported total loss. Equip them with a manual "save locally, email export when back online" workflow (or a lightweight script) that mimics offline capability without building the full sync engine.
    *   *Metric:* Adoption rate and successful recovery count.
    *   *Kill Threshold:* If fewer than 15% of nurses in known poor-signal zones actively use the workaround, or if the volume of recoverable data represents <5% of total critical entries, **drop the full offline build** for now. The problem is likely "spotty signal" (solvable with better caching/retry logic) rather than "zero connectivity" (requiring full local DB).

**2. Quantitative Reality Check**
*   **Incident Rate:** 50 tickets/month out of 400 users is a 12.5% incident rate. This is significant.
*   **Severity Split:** Only 10% (5/50) are confirmed "zero connectivity."
*   **Hypothesis:** If the other 45 tickets are due to timeouts on spotty networks, a robust retry/caching layer (estimated <2 weeks) might solve 90% of the pain without the 3-month conflict-resolution overhaul. Maria's estimate of "90% reduction" applies to *all* data loss, but the proposed solution (full offline) only addresses the 10% slice (zero connectivity) effectively.

**3. Recommendation: Do Not Greenlight Full Build Yet**
The cost of a 3-month delay on compliance is a known, certain negative. The benefit of the full offline mode is currently hypothetical for 90% of the reported incidents.

**Immediate Next Steps:**
1.  **Segment the 50 tickets:** Categorize by "timeout/spotty" vs. "no signal."
2.  **Survey the 400 users:** One question: "In your last 10 visits, how many had absolutely zero cellular signal?" (Do not ask if they *want* offline mode; ask about past behavior).
3.  **Decision Rule:**
    *   If >20% of users report frequent *total* blackouts → Proceed to scoping the 3-month build (accepting the compliance delay risk).
    *   If <20% report total blackouts → Reject the full offline mode. Task engineering with an enhanced "retry-and-cache" strategy (2-week effort) to handle spotty signals, preserving the compliance timeline.

**Non-Goal for This Stage:**
Building complex conflict-resolution logic for a problem that may primarily be network latency, not absence.
