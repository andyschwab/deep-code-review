### Roadmap Recommendation: Prioritize Device Calibration Tool (Weeks 1–6)

**Decision Rationale**
Based on the evidence provided, the **Device Calibration Tool** is the immediate priority. The decision rests on the distinction between a verified operational failure and an unverified revenue risk.

1.  **Verified Problem vs. Assumed Need:**

2.  **Impact of Delay:**
    *   Delaying the calibration tool continues to incur known support costs and data reliability issues today.
    *   Delaying the dashboard carries a *potential* revenue risk that has not yet been quantified. If the dashboard is not a strict contract requirement, building it now diverts resources from fixing a broken core loop.
