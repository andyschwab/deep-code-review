### Decision Framework: Record Sharing Feature (Go/No-Go)

**Current Status:** `UNVERIFIED`. You have stated intent (68% want speed) but no behavioral evidence regarding your proposed solution (manual consent). You cannot proceed to build based on the assumption that "strict manual consent" is a viable mitigation without testing the friction cost.

#### 1. The Riskiest Assumption Gate
**Assumption:** Doctors will tolerate the friction of manual consent prompts for every share, and this workflow is legally sufficient under German regulations.
**Impact:** High (Legal liability vs. Workflow abandonment).
**Uncertainty:** High (No data on actual drop-off rates or legal sufficiency).

**Required Test Before Build:**
Do not engineer the full feature. Run a **Wizard-of-Oz** or **Fake-Door** test within your existing prototype or a mockup.
*   **Metric:** Completion Rate of the sharing workflow.
*   **Kill Threshold:** If fewer than **X%** of doctors complete the sharing flow after encountering the consent prompt, the feature fails.
    *   *Derivation:* Calculate X based on the minimum adoption rate required to justify the engineering cost and legal risk. (e.g., "If <40% complete the flow, the friction kills the value prop").
*   **Sample Size:** Minimum 15–20 doctors performing the task in a observed session.
*   **Deadline:** Run this test by **October 25** to allow time for analysis before your November 15 board presentation.

#### 2. Data Points to Gather Next
You need three specific inputs to fill the decision matrix. Do not guess these numbers.

| Data Point | How to Obtain | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Behavioral Drop-off Rate** | Observe doctors attempting to share a record with the manual consent prompt in a usability test. Count how many abort the process. | Product / Research |
| **Legal Sufficiency Confirmation** | Present the *exact* wording and flow of the proposed consent prompt to legal counsel. Ask: "Does this specific interaction meet GDPR/local health data requirements for explicit consent?" | Legal Counsel |
| **Operational Time Cost** | Measure the seconds added per share during the test. Multiply by estimated daily volume to determine if the manual overhead is sustainable for clinic throughput. | Product / Ops |

#### 3. Immediate Non-Goals (Scope Defense)
*   **Automated Consent Logic:** Do not build complex rule engines for consent yet. Test the manual extreme first.
*   **Full Integration:** Do not integrate with external specialist systems until the local workflow completion rate is proven.
*   **Polished UI:** The test interface can be low-fidelity; you are measuring friction, not aesthetics.

#### 4. Recommendation Path
*   **GO:** Only if (1) Legal signs off on the *specific* prompt design, AND (2) Behavioral testing shows >[Kill Threshold]% completion despite friction.
*   **NO-GO:** If legal cannot validate the manual prompt OR if doctors abandon the workflow due to friction.
*   **PIVOT:** If doctors want the speed but hate the friction, explore alternative consent models (e.g., batch consent, pre-authorized networks) *after* validating the failure of the manual model.

**Note:** Do not present the "68% want this" stat to the board as validation; it measures desire, not tolerance for your specific solution.
**Note:** If legal cannot answer the sufficiency question without a built prototype, engage an external healthcare privacy consultant immediately; do not rely on internal assumptions.
