### Strategic Evaluation: SecureLife Integration vs. Small Practices Aggregation

**Status:** Decision cannot be made on current evidence. The core assumption regarding distraction and the open question regarding maintenance costs are untested hypotheses. Building either path now violates the riskiest-assumption gate.

#### 1. The Riskiest Assumption Gate
**Assumption:** "Building the custom integration for SecureLife will not distract us so much that we lose the small practices."
**Status:** **UNTESTED.**
**Required Test Before Build:** Run a "concierge" simulation or time-boxed prototype of the SecureLife integration requirements using current support staff.
**Kill Threshold:** If the simulation consumes >20% of current support capacity or delays small-practice requests by >48 hours, the assumption is false. Do not sign the contract until this is measured.

**Open Question:** "Will SecureLife require ongoing custom maintenance that drains support?"
**Required Test:** Demand a fixed-scope pilot clause in the contract with a capped maintenance window (e.g., 10 hours/month) before full delivery.
**Decision Rule:** If SecureLife refuses a capped maintenance clause, the risk of infinite customization is confirmed. Treat the $200k as potentially negative value if support costs exceed $15k/month.
