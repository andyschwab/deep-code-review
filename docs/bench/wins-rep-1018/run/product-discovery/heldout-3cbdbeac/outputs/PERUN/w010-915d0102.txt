**Decision: Do not allocate the entire engineering sprint yet. Treat this as a targeted discovery sprint first.**

The evidence provided (one client failure) supports a **high-severity incident response**, but it does not yet validate a **broad product investment**. Building a full automated generator for the entire user base based on a single data point violates the riskiest-assumption gate. The critical unknown is whether this is a systemic market need or an isolated vertical requirement. If you build the full feature now and only 5% of your 200 clients need it, you have misallocated a month of engineering capacity that could have addressed broader retention or growth drivers.

**Immediate Action Plan (Next 5 Days):**

1.  **Run the "Riskiest Assumption" Test:**
    *   **Assumption:** "At least 20% of our enterprise portfolio faces identical regulatory logging requirements that manual export cannot satisfy."
    *   **Cheapest Test:** Conduct 15 rapid discovery interviews with Customer Success Managers (CSMs) owning the top 20% of revenue accounts. Do not ask "Would you use this?" Ask: "Have you received audit requests in the last 12 months? What format was required? How many hours did your team spend manually compiling logs?"
    *   **Kill Threshold:** If fewer than 3 distinct clients (outside the original offender) confirm a similar unmet need with financial or contractual risk, **do not build the full product feature**. Instead, proceed with a custom services solution for the at-risk client.

2.  **Interim Remediation (Concierge/Wizard-of-Oz):**
    *   For the specific client at risk of losing $1.2M: Do not wait for a built feature. Assign a senior engineer to manually script and generate the required logs for them *this week*. This solves the immediate 30-day audit window without committing to a permanent codebase change. This is a "concierge" test: if the manual fix satisfies the auditor, you have bought time to validate the broader market need properly.

3.  **Define Non-Goals for This Sprint:**
    *   **Not Building:** A self-service UI, multi-format configuration, or scheduling engine. These are scale features, not validation features.
    *   **Not Assuming:** That "compliance" means the same thing for all 200 clients. Regulations vary by industry (finance vs. health vs. retail).

**Deliverable for VP of Product:**
Present a "Go/No-Go" decision matrix based on the interview results above.
*   **GO (Build Product):** If >15% of interviewed accounts confirm urgent, unpaid compliance gaps.
*   **NO-GO (Custom Services):** If the need is isolated to <5% of accounts. Package the manual script as a billable professional service for that specific vertical.

**Note:** The $1.2M contract risk requires immediate *action* (the manual script), but not immediate *product construction*. Separating the incident response from the product roadmap prevents over-engineering a niche requirement.
