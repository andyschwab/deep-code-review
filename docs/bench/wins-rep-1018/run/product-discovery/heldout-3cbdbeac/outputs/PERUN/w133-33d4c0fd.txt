**Executive Summary: Recommendation to Proceed with Secure Convoy Tracking Module**

**Verdict:** Proceed immediately with development. Do not wait for future satellite options.

**Evidence Basis**
The decision rests on verified historical loss data, not hypothetical market research. In the last six months, two specific incidents (March 12 and August 4) resulted in the theft of over 4 kilograms of raw gold each. Assuming a conservative market value of $65,000 per kilogram, a single incident represents a direct loss of approximately $260,000. The total verified loss over this period exceeds $520,000.

**Financial Logic**
The "Riskiest Assumption" here is not whether the technology works, but whether the cost of implementation exceeds the cost of one prevented theft.
*   **Cost of Failure:** $260,000+ per event (based on actuals).
*   **Cost of Solution:** Even high-end cellular GPS hardware and data subscriptions typically range from $500 to $2,000 per vehicle annually, plus development costs. Unless the total build and operational cost for the entire fleet exceeds $260,000 in the first year, the solution pays for itself by preventing just *one* recurrence.
*   **Time Value:** Waiting for "cheaper satellite options next year" exposes the company to another 12 months of unmitigated risk. A single theft during this waiting period would cost more than the entire projected savings of waiting.

**Strategic Recommendation**
1.  **Build Now:** Develop the secure convoy tracking module using existing cellular/GPS technology. The requirement is real-time tracking with geofenced alerts for route deviation.
2.  **Define Success Metric:** The module is successful if it reduces unauthorized route deviations to zero and provides immediate alerts (<2 minute latency) to headquarters.
3.  **Non-Goal:** Do not wait for perfect coverage or next-generation satellite hardware. The current gap in security is costing more than the inefficiency of current hardware.

**Board Directive**
The evidence shows that the cost of inaction ($520k+ in six months) drastically outweighs the cost of action. The current radio check-in process has been proven insufficient by two distinct failures. Implementing real-time tracking is a defensive necessity with a clear, immediate ROI based on preventing a single future incident. Delaying development constitutes a conscious acceptance of further six-figure losses.

***

*Note: This recommendation assumes the value of gold remains stable or increases; any rise in gold prices further strengthens the case for immediate action. Exact hardware quotes should be obtained to finalize the budget, but the strategic direction is clear based on the loss data provided.*
