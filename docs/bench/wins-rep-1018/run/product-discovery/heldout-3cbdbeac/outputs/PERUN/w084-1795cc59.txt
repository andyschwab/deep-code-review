### Decision Framework: Real-Time Container Tracking Integration

**Verdict: Insufficient Evidence to Commit Full Resources.**
The current evidence (six emails) validates a *problem* but fails to validate the *solution scope* or the *business outcome* (contract renewals). Proceeding to an 8-week beta build without further validation risks building six custom workflows instead of one universal standard, or solving a visibility problem that does not actually drive renewals.

#### 1. The Riskiest Assumption Gate
Before authorizing the 8-week build, you must test the single riskiest assumption: **"All six partners require the same standard integration, and this specific feature is the primary driver for contract renewal."**

*   **Current State:** You are assuming universality and causality based on stated intent (emails).
*   **Required Test:** Conduct "Mom Test" interviews with the six requesters before writing code.
*   **Kill Threshold:** If more than 2 of the 6 partners describe fundamentally different data requirements or workflows, **do not build the beta**. The cost of customization exceeds the value of a single standard feature.
*   **Decision Rule:** If fewer than 4 partners confirm they would delay or cancel renewal *specifically* due to the lack of this integration (not just "it would be nice"), the $2M renewal assumption is unproven.

#### 2. Evidence Gathering Plan (Next 5 Days)
Do not rely on the emails alone. Execute the following to fill the evidence gaps:

*   **Validate the Problem (Behavioral):** Ask the six contacts: "Walk me through the last time a 12-hour delay occurred. What specific data were you missing, and what manual workaround did you use?"
    *   *Goal:* Confirm the 12-hour delay is real and caused by *visibility*, not staffing or hardware.
*   **Validate the Solution (Standardization):** Ask: "Can you share the API spec or data format your other terminal operators use for this? Or are you expecting us to design the workflow?"
    *   *Goal:* Determine if this is a commodity feature (universal standard) or a custom service request.
*   **Validate the Value (Renewal Link):** Ask: "When your renewal comes up, what are the top three criteria? Has a competitor ever lost a contract specifically because they lacked real-time tracking?"
    *   *Goal:* Decouple "nice to have" from "contract breaker."

#### 4. Recommended Immediate Actions
1.  **Pause the 8-week build commitment.** Commit only to a 1-week discovery sprint.
2.  **Interview the 6 partners.** Use the questions above to distinguish between a universal standard and custom requests.
3.  **Define the "Non-Goal" for Q1.** Explicitly state: "We will not build custom carrier-specific workflows in Q1." This protects the team from scope creep if the interviews reveal divergent needs.
4.  **Re-evaluate in 7 days.** If the interviews confirm a universal standard and a direct link to renewals, greenlight the beta. If not, pivot to a lower-cost concierge test (e.g., manual data pushes) to prove value before coding.

**Note:** Email requests represent stated intent, which is notoriously unreliable for predicting actual behavior or willingness to pay.
