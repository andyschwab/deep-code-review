**Recommendation: Do Not Build Yet. Run the Riskiest-Assumption Gate First.**

Ten explicit requests do not validate a $25,000 investment. This is **stated intent**, not **behavioral evidence**. The drivers' claim that an app will "reduce missed calls by 30%" is a hypothesis you have not tested. Building now risks solving a problem that doesn't exist at scale or building a solution drivers cannot use due to smartphone literacy gaps.

**The Riskiest Assumption**
*Assumption:* A significant majority of your active driver pool prefers a dedicated app over WhatsApp *and* possesses the smartphone literacy to use it effectively, resulting in measurable operational gains (faster confirmation, fewer missed calls).
*Impact:* High (core workflow change).
*Uncertainty:* High (sample size of 10 vs. total fleet; unverified literacy levels).

**The Cheapest Disconfirming Test (Run Before Coding)**
Do not write code. Run a **Concierge/Wizard-of-Oz test** using your existing tools to simulate the app experience and measure actual behavior change.

*   **Test Design:**
    1.  **Segment:** Select 20 drivers (the 10 vocal + 10 random/silent).
    2.  **Simulation:** For 2 weeks, send load details via a structured format (e.g., a dedicated Telegram bot, a Google Form link, or a strictly formatted WhatsApp template that mimics an app interface) requiring a specific button click or form submission to "confirm."
    3.  **Constraint:** Do not accept confirmations via free-text reply or voice note for this group during the test window. Force the "app-like" interaction.
    4.  **Metric:** Track **Completion Rate** (did they confirm via the new method?) and **Time-to-Confirm** compared to the control group (standard WhatsApp).

*   **Numeric Kill Threshold (Decision Rule):**
    *   **GO:** If >80% of the test group successfully completes the confirmation via the new method within 15 minutes, AND time-to-confirm drops by >20% compared to baseline.
    *   **NO-GO / PIVOT:** If <50% complete the action, or if error/support requests increase. This indicates literacy barriers or low actual demand.
    *   **Sample Size:** 20 drivers over 14 days (approx. 200 load interactions).
    *   **Deadline:** 3 weeks total (1 week setup/comms, 2 weeks running).

**Non-Goals for This Stage**
*   **Native App Development:** Spending $25k on iOS/Android builds is a non-goal until the behavioral test passes.
*   **Polished UI/UX:** Visual fidelity is irrelevant if the core interaction fails.
*   **Scale:** Do not roll out to the whole fleet yet.

**Board Presentation Summary**
"We have identified a potential workflow improvement requested by 5% of our sampled drivers. However, stated preference often diverges from actual behavior, especially regarding technology adoption in varying literacy contexts. Before committing $25,000 and two months of development time, we will run a $0 behavioral test over 3 weeks. We will only proceed if drivers *demonstrate* the new behavior at a rate that guarantees operational ROI. If the test fails, we save the capital and explore alternative solutions (e.g., voice-based confirmation)."

**Next Step:** Define your total active driver count to calculate if 10 requests represent a statistically significant segment, then launch the concierge test tomorrow.
