**Current Evidence Status:** **INSUFFICIENT FOR BUILD DECISION.**
You have clear problem evidence (3 warnings, 60% coverage gap, 2 temperature failures), but **zero validation** for your proposed solution's viability or the critical regulatory requirement. Building an automated system now carries a high risk of solving the wrong problem or building an unacceptable format.

#### 1. The Riskiest Assumption Gate
**Assumption:** "Certifiers will accept our proposed digital ledger format."
*   **Impact:** Critical. If false, the entire build is wasted capital.
*   **Uncertainty:** Maximum. You explicitly stated this is an "open question."
*   **Verdict:** **DO NOT BUILD YET.** You cannot justify the cost of development until this assumption is disconfirmed or confirmed.

**Required Cheap Test (Before Coding):**
*   **Action:** Present a low-fidelity mockup (PDF or spreadsheet sample) of your proposed digital ledger format to the lead certifier *before* Tuesday's meeting or as the sole agenda item of that meeting.

*   **Kill Threshold:** If the certifier says "No," "Maybe," or "It depends on a third-party vendor we haven't selected," **stop development**. Pivot to identifying the required third-party audit trail first.

| Dimension | **Option A: Build Automated Tracker Now** | **Option B: Wait / Gather Data First** |

#### 3. Immediate Next Steps (Before Development)
1.  **Validate the Format (The Gatekeeper):** Use the Tuesday meeting solely to answer: "Will this specific ledger format pass audit?" Do not pitch the software; pitch the data structure.

**Conclusion:** The warnings strongly support **immediate action on the compliance risk**, but they do **not** support immediate **software development**.
