### Viability Risk Analysis: FitPulse Hospital Integration

**Executive Summary**
The project faces a **critical stopper** unrelated to user demand: an unverified technical assumption regarding hospital API compatibility. While user consent (40% of 12,000) provides a viable top-of-funnel, the "six-week build" estimate is invalid until the middleware assumption is tested. If hospitals require custom middleware, the timeline shifts from weeks to months, potentially violating the March 15 regulatory deadline for compliant data transfer.

**1. The Riskiest Assumption Gate**
*   **Assumption:** "Hospitals will accept our proposed API standard without requiring custom middleware."
*   **Impact:** High. If false, engineering effort increases 5–10x, and the compliance deadline is missed.
*   **Uncertainty:** Total. No evidence exists yet.
*   **Cheapest Disconfirming Test:** Do not build the integration. Instead, send the proposed API schema to the CTOs/IT Directors of **three** target hospital systems immediately with a specific question: "Does your current ingestion layer support this schema natively, or does it require a middleware translation layer?"
*   **Kill Threshold:** If **any** of the three pilot hospitals require custom middleware, **pause the build**. The cost/complexity invalidates the six-week timeline. Pivot to a middleware-first strategy or abandon the direct integration model.

**2. Critical Unknowns & Required Evidence**
Before committing resources, you must resolve these two unknowns. Do not rely on internal estimates.

| Unknown | Why It Matters | Specific Evidence Needed (Actionable) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Technical Compatibility** | Determines if the build is 6 weeks or 6 months. | **Written confirmation** from 3 hospital IT leads stating: (a) They accept the specific API standard, OR (b) They explicitly list the required middleware specs. |
| **Compliance Timeline** | Determines if the March 15 deadline is achievable. | **Formal estimate** from your legal/compliance auditor on the review cycle *specifically for third-party hospital data transfers*. Is it 2 weeks or 4 months? |
| **Consent Flow Validity** | Ensures the 40% opt-in converts to legal consent. | **Legal sign-off** that the proposed "explicit consent" UI meets the March 15 regulation's specific wording requirements. (Survey intent ≠ legal consent). |

**3. Recommended Next Steps (Pre-Build)**
1.  **Run the Technical Test (Days 1–3):** Contact 3 hospital partners. Present the API spec. Record their binary response (Native vs. Middleware).
2.  **Audit the Clock (Days 1–3):** Submit the data flow diagram to legal/compliance for a timeline estimate.
3.  **Decision Rule:**
    *   If **Middleware Required** OR **Audit > 4 Weeks**: **Do not build.** Re-scope or delay launch.
    *   If **Native Support** AND **Audit < 4 Weeks**: Proceed to build the consent flow first, then the API.

**4. Non-Goals for This Stage**
*   **Building the full API integration:** Do not write code until the technical test passes.
*   **Polishing the user consent UI:** Do not design the final flow until legal confirms the specific wording requirements.
*   **Scaling marketing:** Do not promote the feature until the compliance audit is complete.

***

**Note:** The 40% user opt-in is a positive signal but irrelevant if the technical or legal gates fail; prioritize the hospital IT feedback immediately.
**Note:** Do not accept verbal assurances from hospital partners; require email documentation of their API capabilities to satisfy the board.
