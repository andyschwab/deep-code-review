### Decision Framework: Build vs. Validate for TrialMatch Recruitment Module

**Verdict: DO NOT BUILD YET.**
The current evidence (verbal requests) is insufficient to justify the $280,000 cost and four-month engineering opportunity cost. You are attempting to validate **viability** (will they sign?) with **desirability** feedback (they want the feature). You must pass the **Riskiest-Assumption Gate** before writing code.

#### 1. The Riskiest Assumption
*   **Assumption:** Hospitals will sign multi-year contracts worth ~$80k/year specifically because of this automated recruitment feature, even without other compliance tools.
*   **Impact:** High ($4M pipeline).
*   **Uncertainty:** Critical (No signed Letters of Intent; unknown dependency on other tools).
*   **Current Status:** Untested. Building now is a gamble, not a strategy.

#### 2. The Cheapest Disconfirming Test
Do not build the software. Instead, sell the outcome manually to prove the contract value.

*   **Test Design:** "Concierge" Pilot.
    *   Offer the three hospitals a "Guaranteed Recruitment Acceleration" pilot for the next study.
    *   **Condition:** They sign a Letter of Intent (LOI) stating that *if* you reduce their recruitment time by 60% on this specific study, they will convert to a multi-year contract including this module.
    *   **Execution:** Your team manually performs the recruitment work using the current 20-hour process but optimizes it heavily behind the scenes (human-in-the-loop) to try to hit the 60% reduction target. Do not write new code; use existing tools + extra human effort.
*   **Metric:** Signed LOIs contingent on manual success.
*   **Numeric Kill Threshold:**
    *   **GO:** 2 out of 3 hospitals sign the LOI.
    *   **NO-GO (Kill/Pivot):** 0 or 1 hospital signs.
    *   **Rationale:** If they won't sign an LOI for a manual guarantee, they will not pay for the automated software. The cost of the manual pilot is negligible compared to $280k.

#### 3. Evidence Required Before Build
You cannot proceed to development until you have filled these slots with real data:

| Evidence Slot | How to Obtain | Current Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Signed LOI** | Present the Concierge Pilot offer to the hospital decision-makers. | **EMPTY** (Verbal request only) |
| **Dependency Map** | Ask: "What other compliance tools must be present before this recruitment feature becomes usable for you?" | **EMPTY** (Unknown) |
| **Willingness to Pay** | Confirm the specific contract value tied *only* to this feature in writing. | **EMPTY** (Estimated only) |

#### 4. Strategic Recommendation for CEO & Sales
*   **Immediate Action:** Pause engineering planning. Sales must return to the three hospitals with the Concierge Pilot proposal.
*   **Script Pivot:** "We are ready to dedicate resources to reduce your recruitment time by 60% on your next study. To prioritize this over other roadmap items, we need a signed intent that confirms this result leads to a multi-year agreement. Can we structure this pilot for Study X?"
*   **If they refuse the LOI:** The feature is not worth $280k to them, or the "recruitment" problem is not the primary blocker. Do not build.
*   **If they sign:** Run the manual pilot. If you achieve the 60% reduction manually, *then* authorize the $280k build to automate what you just proved valuable.

**Non-Goal for This Stage:**
Building the full automated module, UI polish, or scaling infrastructure. These are non-goals until the LOI is signed and the manual process proves the 60% efficiency gain.
