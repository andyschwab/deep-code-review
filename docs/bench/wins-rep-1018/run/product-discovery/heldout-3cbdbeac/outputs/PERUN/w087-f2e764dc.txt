# Decision Readiness Report: Real-Time Container Visibility Feature

**Status:** UNVERIFIED. Do not approve budget yet.
**Reason:** Critical regulatory and data-validity assumptions remain untested. Building now risks building a feature that cannot be legally displayed or technically trusted.

## 1. The Riskiest Assumption Gate
You are assuming **feasibility and viability** (data accuracy and regulatory clearance) before validating **desirability** with a cheap test. The single riskiest assumption is: *"PSA Terminal will allow us to display their data once we integrate our GPS feed."*

If this is false, the feature has zero value regardless of market demand. You must run the cheapest possible test to disconfirm this before writing code.

### Required Test: The "Permission Probe"
*   **Action:** Return to the PSA officials (or their legal/compliance counterparts) with a specific question, not a pitch.
*   **Script:** "We are preparing a prototype to visualize the 30% of movements lacking updates using our trucking GPS data. Before we proceed, does displaying this derived data on a customer dashboard require a specific security clearance or data certification from PSA? If so, what is the exact standard?"
*   **Metric:** A binary "Yes/No" on the requirement, plus a named document/standard if "Yes."
*   **Kill Threshold:** If the answer is "Yes, and the certification takes >3 months" or "No, you cannot display terminal-adjacent data without a formal partnership agreement we don't have," **drop the idea** or pivot to an internal-only tool.
*   **Deadline:** 5 business days.

## 3. Recommended Immediate Actions (Next 7 Days)
1.  **Stop Development:** Do not allocate engineering sprint time to this feature yet.
2.  **Run the Permission Probe:** Get the regulatory answer in writing (email is sufficient for now).
3.  **Audit Historical Data:** Verify your "accurate enough" assumption against real past delays. If your GPS data misses >10% of actual delays, the feature creates false confidence and increases liability.
4.  **Re-convene Board:** Present the *evidence* (Regulatory status + Data audit results), not the *idea*.

## 4. Non-Goals for This Stage
*   **Building the Dashboard UI:** This is gold-plating before the data pipe is legal and verified.
*   **Scaling to other ports:** Focus strictly on PSA rules; do not generalize requirements yet.
*   **Marketing the feature:** Do not promise visibility to customers until the kill thresholds are passed.

**Verdict:** The market signal (30% opacity) is strong, but the path to value is blocked by unknown regulatory and data-quality gates. **No-Go** on budget approval until the "Permission Probe" and "Data Audit" are complete.
