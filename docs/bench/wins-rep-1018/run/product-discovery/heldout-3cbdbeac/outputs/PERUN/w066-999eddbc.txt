### Comparative Impact Analysis: Staff Fatigue vs. Meal Preference

**Decision Recommendation:** Prioritize the **Patient Meal Preference Tracker** for the immediate sprint. Defer the Staff Fatigue Monitoring System until the union privacy constraint is resolved via a cheap discovery test.

#### 1. Riskiest Assumption Gate
*   **Fatigue System:** The critical blocker is not technical feasibility but **viability/acceptance**.
    *   *Assumption:* Union representatives will accept passive monitoring of staff hours.
    *   *Status:* **Untested/High Risk.** If this fails, the project value drops to zero regardless of error reduction potential.
    *   *Required Test:* Before writing code, conduct a "concierge test" or formal inquiry with union leadership to gauge acceptance of passive monitoring.
    *   *Kill Threshold:* If union leadership indicates >20% likelihood of formal grievance or rejection, the project must pivot or stop.
*   **Meal Tracker:** The assumption is that preference tracking directly correlates to the 15-point satisfaction drop. This is lower risk; the worst case is a neutral impact on scores, not an organizational conflict.

#### 3. Ethical Risks & Non-Goals
*   **Staff Fatigue System:**
    *   *Ethical Risk:* Implementing passive monitoring without explicit buy-in violates trust and may increase burnout (the very problem it aims to solve).
    *   *Non-Goal for this Sprint:* Building the alerting engine or dashboard. Building this now is "gold-plating" a solution that may be legally or culturally blocked.
*   **Meal Tracker:**
    *   *Ethical Risk:* Low. Data sensitivity is minimal compared to biometric/hourly surveillance.
    *   *Non-Goal:* Solving the kitchen supply chain or menu variety. This sprint is strictly about *tracking preferences*, not changing the menu.

#### 4. Strategic Path Forward
1.  **Immediate Action (Sprint N):** Build the **Meal Preference Tracker**. The path to value is clear, the risk is low, and it addresses a documented 15-point drop in patient experience.
2.  **Parallel Discovery (Sprint N):** The Product Director must run the **Union Privacy Test** for the Fatigue System.
    *   *Action:* Schedule a meeting with union reps to present the *concept* (not the code) of fatigue monitoring.
    *   *Metric:* Binary outcome (Go/No-Go) or specific privacy constraints required.
3.  **Future Decision:** Only if the Union Test returns a "Go" should the Fatigue System enter the backlog. At that point, re-run the ICE score with updated Confidence numbers.

**Summary for Leadership:**
We cannot bet engineering resources on the Fatigue System while a single unknown (union approval) can invalidate the entire investment. The Meal Tracker offers guaranteed, measurable progress on patient experience this quarter. We will treat the Fatigue System as a "discovery track" item until the privacy risk is retired.
