**Decision Brief: Human Oversight Mandate for Public Launch**

**Verdict:** **Delay public launch.** The current beta data does not provide sufficient justification to proceed with *only* automated safeguards. While the volume of flagged incidents (12/500) appears low, the presence of unmeasured false negatives and confirmed failures in automated responses (2 instances) creates an unacceptable liability and safety risk for a mental health product.

**Evidence Summary**
*   **Confirmed Failures:** 2 out of 12 high-risk flags resulted in inadequate automated responses. This is a 16.7% failure rate on *detected* crises, indicating the current automation logic is not robust enough for unsupervised deployment.
*   **The Unknown Variable (False Negatives):** You explicitly stated the volume of missed crisis signals is unknown. In mental health, a false negative (missing a cry for help) carries significantly higher legal and ethical weight than a false positive (flagging a safe conversation). Proceeding without estimating this rate is negligent.
*   **Unit Economics vs. Liability:** The assumption that human oversight will "not destroy unit economics" is untested. However, the cost of a single unchecked suicide or severe harm event linked to the bot likely exceeds the operational cost of a limited human review team for the initial launch phase.

**Riskiest Assumption & Required Test**
*   **Assumption:** "Automated detection catches a sufficient percentage of crises to make human review of *only* flagged items safe."
*   **Status:** **Untested and currently disproven** by the 2 inadequate response incidents.
*   **Immediate Action Required:** Before any public launch, you must run a **retrospective manual audit** on a random sample (e.g., 10%) of the *non-flagged* conversations from the beta.
    *   **Goal:** Estimate the false negative rate.
    *   **Kill Threshold:** If the audit reveals *any* missed high-risk signals in the sample, a 24/7 human layer (or a strict "human-in-the-loop" for all ambiguous cases) becomes mandatory for launch. You cannot scale a system where you cannot quantify the miss rate.

**Recommendation for Legal & Steering Committee**
1.  **Do not launch to the public in 20 days** with the current configuration. The marketing deadline is secondary to the fiduciary and ethical duty of care.
2.  **Implement a "Concierge" MVP for Launch:** Instead of a full 24/7 team immediately, launch with a restricted user base where all conversations (or all flagged + random sample) are reviewed by humans within a defined SLA (e.g., 1 hour). This validates the safety protocol and provides real data on the volume of human effort required.
3.  **Reframe the Economics:** Calculate the cost of the human team not as an operational expense, but as the **cost of goods sold (COGS) for safety**. If the unit economics do not work with this included, the business model is currently invalid for this domain.

**Conclusion**
The beta feedback confirms the automation is imperfect and hides the true risk profile. You cannot ethically or legally scale a mental health intervention with an unknown false negative rate. Delay the launch, audit the non-flagged data immediately, and design the public rollout around a verified human-safety net.
