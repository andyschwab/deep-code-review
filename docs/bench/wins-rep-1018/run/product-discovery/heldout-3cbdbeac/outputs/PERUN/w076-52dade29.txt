### Critical Questions for Validation (Empty-Slot Template)

Do not proceed with building integration connectors until real evidence fills these slots. The following are not examples; they are the specific data points you must gather from your pilot and research before July 1.

#### 1. Parental Consent & Privacy (The "Kill" Risk)
*Assumption: Parents will consent to automatic data sharing despite breach news.*
*   **Question to ask parents (in the pilot):** "Thinking about the recent news regarding school data breaches, would you currently sign a form allowing MindTrack to automatically send your child's mood logs to the school counselor, or would you prefer to review and approve each report manually?"
    *   *Slot to fill:* [ % of parents who say "Yes" to automatic vs. manual ]
    *   *How to obtain:* Survey the parents of the current 5,000 users or the specific pilot cohort. Do not guess.
*   **Question to ask parents (behavioral):** "Have you ever opted out of a school data-sharing program in the past? If yes, what specifically triggered that decision?"
    *   *Slot to fill:* [ List of specific triggers cited by parents who opted out previously ]
    *   *How to obtain:* Interview 10–15 parents who have historically been privacy-conscious.
*   **Decision Rule:** If fewer than [ X ]% of parents consent to *automatic* sharing (where X is the % needed to make the engineering cost viable), the automatic integration feature is a **NO-GO**.

#### 2. Administrator/Counselor Value (The Desirability Risk)
*Assumption: Counselors need automatic trends to intervene; manual reports are insufficient.*
*   **Question to ask counselors (past behavior):** "In the last month, how many students did you identify as at-risk based *only* on manually submitted reports versus those you identified through direct observation or other means?"
    *   *Slot to fill:* [ Number of interventions triggered solely by manual reports ]
    *   *How to obtain:* Interview the counselors in the 5 pilot districts. Ask for specific instances, not opinions.
*   **Question to ask administrators (policy):** "What is your district's specific policy timeline for approving new third-party data integrations following a breach in the news? Is there a formal moratorium?"
    *   *Slot to fill:* [ Yes/No on moratorium; specific approval timeline in weeks/months ]
    *   *How to obtain:* Request the written data governance policy from the pilot district IT/legal leads. Do not rely on verbal assurances.
*   **Decision Rule:** If counselors report that manual reports already cover [ Y ]% of their critical cases, or if the approval timeline exceeds your runway, the automatic integration is a **NO-GO** for now.

#### 3. The "Cheap Test" Before Building Connectors
*Assumption: Building full EHR connectors is the only way to test value.*
*   **Test Design:** Before writing integration code, run a "Wizard-of-Oz" test in the pilot.
    *   *Action:* Have a staff member manually export mood trends for consenting users and email them to counselors weekly.
    *   *Metric:* [ % of counselors who open the email and reply with an action taken ]
    *   *Kill Threshold:* If counselors do not act on the manually delivered data within 2 weeks, building the automatic connector will not solve the problem.
    *   *Slot to fill:* [ Actual response rate from the manual test ]

#### 4. Privacy Officer Requirements
*   **Question to legal/professional:** "Given the specific state laws of our 5 pilot districts, does 'automatic' sharing reclassify MindTrack as a covered entity under HIPAA/FERPA in a way that 'manual' sharing does not?"
    *   *Slot to fill:* [ Legal determination: Yes/No/Maybe ]
    *   *How to obtain:* **Route to qualified legal counsel.** Do not ask the model or guess based on general knowledge.
