### Decision Review: Assembly Video Initiative

**Current Status:** **UNVERIFIED.** The proposal rests on a high-impact assumption (videos reduce calls by 60%) and an untested user constraint (older demographic bandwidth/preference). Building the full suite of 20 videos now violates the riskiest-assumption gate.

#### 1. The Riskiest Assumption Gate
*   **Assumption:** Customers prefer video over text and have the bandwidth to use it, leading to a 60% reduction in assembly calls.
*   **Impact:** High. (10 calls/month × 18 EUR = 180 EUR/month direct labor + return shipping costs).
*   **Uncertainty:** High. No evidence exists yet regarding your specific demographic's consumption habits or technical constraints.
*   **Cheapest Disconfirming Test:** Do not produce 20 videos. Produce **one** high-quality video for the single wardrobe model responsible for the majority of the 10 recent calls.
    *   **Method:** Send the video link *only* to the next 5 customers calling about that specific model, instead of talking them through it.
    *   **Metric:** Resolution rate without further calls + explicit feedback on "was this easier than the manual?"
    *   **Kill Threshold:** If fewer than 3 of 5 users successfully assemble the unit using *only* the video (or if 2+ complain about loading/access), the assumption that "video solves this for our demographic" is false. Pivot to enlarged print diagrams or QR codes linking to static images.

#### 2. Economic Sanity Check (Blank Inputs Required)
To justify the production budget to finance, you must fill these slots with real estimates. Do not guess; get quotes from your video vendor.

| Metric | Your Input Needed | Calculation Logic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cost per Video** | __________ EUR | Quote from vendor × 20 units = Total Investment |
| **Monthly Avoidable Cost** | __________ EUR | (Current monthly assembly calls × 18 EUR) + (Avg return shipping × return count) |
| **Break-even Months** | __________ Months | Total Investment ÷ (Monthly Avoidable Cost × 0.60) |
| **Bandwidth Risk** | __________ % | % of target demographic who failed to load test video in pilot |

*Decision Rule:* If Break-even Months > 12, or if Bandwidth Risk > 20%, the initiative is not viable in its current form.

#### 3. Required Evidence Before Full Build
You cannot approve the budget based on the assumption alone. You need the following data points from the **Cheapest Test** above:
1.  **Preference Data:** Did the test users explicitly state the video was clearer than the manual? (Yes/No count).
2.  **Technical Data:** Did any user fail to load the video due to connection speed? (Count).
3.  **Behavioral Data:** Did the support call end immediately after sending the link, or did they call back still confused?

#### 4. Non-Goals for This Stage
*   **Producing all 20 videos:** This is a non-goal until the single-video pilot proves the format works for your demographic.
*   **Hosting on a high-bandwidth platform:** If the pilot reveals connectivity issues, a lightweight alternative (compressed files, SMS delivery) becomes the goal; high-def streaming becomes a non-goal.

**Recommendation:** Run the single-video pilot within 7 days. If the kill threshold is not triggered, proceed to produce the top 5 videos. If triggered, investigate alternative instruction formats (e.g., annotated photos) before spending further budget.

*Note: The 60% reduction target is a hypothesis, not a forecast; treat it as the variable to be tested, not the expected outcome. Ensure the "Kill Threshold" is agreed upon by Finance before the pilot starts.*
