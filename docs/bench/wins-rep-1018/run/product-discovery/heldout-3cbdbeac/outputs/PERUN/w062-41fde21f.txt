### Decision Framework: Automated Reminder System Investment

**Status:** **UNVERIFIED.** The financial projection and ROI cannot be calculated because the core assumption (40% recovery rate) and the risk factor (patient opt-out sentiment) are untested hypotheses. Building or buying the system now violates the **Riskiest-Assumption Gate**.

#### 1. The Riskiest Assumption & Required Test
*   **Assumption:** Retiree patients will accept automated texts/calls and not opt out, resulting in a 40% recovery of missed appointments.
*   **Risk:** High. If the demographic rejects automation, recovery may be near 0%, and you incur costs plus reputational damage.
*   **Cheapest Test (Run Before Buying):** A manual "Wizard-of-Oz" test.
    *   **Action:** For two weeks, have staff manually send the *exact* script proposed for the bot to a small segment (e.g., 50 patients with upcoming appointments). Track responses and opt-outs.
    *   **Metric:** Response rate and negative sentiment count.
    *   **Kill Threshold:** If fewer than **15%** of contacted patients engage positively, or if **>5%** explicitly complain/opt-out, **do not buy the system**. The demographic fit is invalid.
