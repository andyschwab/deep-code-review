### Decision Readiness Assessment: In-House QR Tracking System

**Status:** **DO NOT BUILD YET.**
**Reason:** The riskiest assumption (label durability in >75% humidity) is untested. Building a full system before validating this creates a high probability of immediate failure and wasted capital.

#### 1. The Riskiest-Assumption Gate
Your primary blocker is not software complexity; it is physical environmental fit.
*   **Assumption:** Standard or even "industrial" QR labels will remain scannable for the required duration (e.g., 4 weeks) in >75% humidity without degrading, peeling, or becoming unreadable.
*   **Impact:** High. If labels fail, the system adds scanning steps without solving the identification error, increasing staff frustration and delaying production further.
*   **Uncertainty:** Critical. You have no data on label performance in your specific warehouse conditions.
*   **Required Test (The "Cheap" Test):** Before writing code or buying printers, run a **physical stress test**.
    *   **Action:** Print 50 rolls of your proposed label type. Attach them to fabric rolls currently in the high-humidity zone.
    *   **Metric:** Scan success rate over time.
    *   **Kill Threshold:** If scan success drops below 95% within 7 days, **abort the in-house QR build** immediately. Do not proceed to software development until a label vendor provides a certified solution that passes this 7-day test.
    *   **Sample Size:** 50 rolls across different fabric types.
    *   **Deadline:** 10 days.

#### 2. Evidence Gaps & Non-Goals
*   **Missing Evidence (Root Cause):** You assume "misidentification" is the sole cause of the 6 complaints. You have not confirmed if the errors happen at *loading*, *unloading*, or *storage*. If the error occurs because two rolls look identical to a human *before* scanning, a QR code helps. If the error is due to a roll being placed on the wrong truck *after* scanning (process failure), the QR code solves nothing.
    *   *Action Required:* Conduct 3 "Switch Interviews" with the staff who made the errors. Ask specifically: "What was happening right before you realized the roll was wrong?" Do not ask "Will QR codes help?"
*   **Non-Goal for This Stage:** Building a full dashboard, integrating with factory ERPs, or automating reporting. These are "gold-plating" features. The only goal right now is **reliable identification**. If the label falls off, the dashboard is useless.

#### 3. Alternative Process Changes (To Explore Before Building)
Before committing to a custom build, consider these lower-tech validations:
*   **Color-Coding + Zone Segregation:** Can high-value rolls be segregated physically or marked with large, humidity-resistant color bands to reduce the search space before implementing digital tracking?
*   **Concierge Test:** Have one staff member manually log every move of 100 rolls using a ruggedized handheld scanner (rented) and existing generic labels. Does the *process* of scanning slow down the loading dock too much? This tests the workflow friction without building software.

#### 4. Decision Matrix for Co-Founders
| Option | Condition to Proceed | Estimated Waste if Wrong |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Build In-House** | Only if physical label test passes 7-day humidity check AND root cause is confirmed as identification error. | High (Dev costs + Hardware + Failed rollout) |
| **Buy Off-the-Shelf** | If label vendors guarantee humidity resistance but custom dev is too slow. | Medium (Subscription costs) |
| **Process Change Only** | If root cause is process (e.g., loading verification) rather than ID. | Low (Training time) |

**Next Immediate Step:** Purchase three varieties of high-durability, water-resistant industrial labels. Stick them in the warehouse today. Scan them daily. **Do not write a single line of code until the labels survive.**

---
*Note: The 90% error reduction target is a hypothesis; your baseline data (6 complaints/8 weeks) is too small to statistically guarantee this outcome without a longer pilot. The "20% staff time" metric should be re-measured during the concierge test to establish a true before/after comparison.*
