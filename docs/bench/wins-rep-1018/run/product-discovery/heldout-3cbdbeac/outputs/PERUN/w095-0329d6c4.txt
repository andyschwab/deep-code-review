**Decision Brief: Operational Trade-offs of Serialized Bottle Tracking**

**Current State & Risk**
Three major clients rejected a $120,000 shipment due to breakage and mislabeling. The proposed solution is a serialized tracking layer to establish chain-of-custody proof. The critical constraint is maintaining packing throughput; a slowdown exceeding 10% is unacceptable.

**The Unverified Assumption**
The proposal rests on an untested assumption: *Warehouse staff can perform additional scanning without degrading packing speed by more than 10%.*
Currently, there is **no evidence** to support this. Proceeding to build or buy the tracking software before validating this operational capacity creates a high risk of solving the quality dispute while creating a fulfillment bottleneck.

**Required Action: The Riskiest-Assumption Gate**
Do not invest in the full technology stack yet. You must first run the cheapest possible test to disconfirm the speed assumption.

**Proposed Test: The "Paper & Pen" Concierge Pilot**
*   **Method:** For the next 50 outbound cases (or one full pallet), require staff to manually log a unique ID for every bottle on a clipboard before packing, simulating the scan time.
*   **Metric:** Compare the average "time-per-case" of this pilot group against the baseline average from the previous week.
*   **Kill Threshold:** If the manual logging increases packing time by **>10%**, the current process cannot support serialization without re-engineering the workflow or adding headcount.
*   **Decision Rule:**
    *   *Result < 10% slowdown:* Proceed to software procurement/development.
    *   *Result > 10% slowdown:* Halt software investment. Redesign the packing line layout or staffing model first.

**Data Gaps Preventing Final Verdict**
The following inputs are missing and must be supplied by the Head of Quality Assurance to finalize the business case:
1.  **Baseline Throughput:** Current average cases packed per hour per staff member.
2.  **Estimated Scan Time:** Vendor specification for scanning one bottle (or estimated time for manual entry in the pilot).
3.  **Error Attribution:** Evidence that serialization specifically prevents *breakage* (vs. just mislabeling). If breakage is caused by rough handling rather than identity loss, tracking alone may not solve the $120k loss.

**Recommendation**
Run the 50-case manual pilot immediately. Do not write code or sign vendor contracts until the pilot data confirms the <10% impact threshold. If the pilot fails the threshold, the investment shifts from "software" to "process redesign," fundamentally changing the budget request for the March harvest.
