**2. The Cheap Test (Concierge/Wizard-of-Oz)**

*   **Action:** Select a small batch (e.g., 500kg) for the next shipment. Instead of relying on color charts, have an expert manually cut and test a statistically significant sample from different parts of the batch to map internal maturity against external appearance. *   **Metric:** Compare the rejection rate/waste of this hand-sorted batch vs. your standard batch.
*   **Kill Threshold:** If the hand-sorted batch still faces rejection (due to transit conditions unrelated to harvest maturity) OR if the labor cost of manual sorting exceeds the potential savings from the machine, **do not build**. The problem may be transit temperature or handling, not harvest timing.

**3. The Economic Viability Test**
*   **Action:** Calculate the "Break-Even Waste Reduction."
    *   *Formula:* (€45,000 Capex + Annual Maintenance) / (Profit Margin per Ton) = Tons of waste you must save annually to pay for the machine.

*   **Kill Threshold:** If the tons of waste you need to save exceed your total historical waste volume, or if the machine cannot generate positive cash flow in your lowest 3 months, **do not build**.
