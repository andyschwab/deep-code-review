**Assessment: Do Not Build Full Integration Yet (Riskiest Assumption Gate Failed)**

The current evidence does **not** support building the full $40,000 counselor integration before November 1st. You have identified a real problem (10/50 beta users confused), but your proposed solution rests on untested assumptions regarding *format* (chat vs. video), *willingness to pay*, and *impact volume*. Building the full system now violates the riskiest-assumption gate: you are committing to high-cost infrastructure before validating the cheapest disconfirming test.

**Riskiest Assumption & Required Test**
*   **Assumption:** "Patients need/want live counselor access, and this specific modality will reduce support tickets by 50%."
*   **Cheapest Test:** A "Concierge" or "Fake-Door" experiment using existing tools (email/phone) before writing integration code.
*   **Proposed Kill Threshold:** If fewer than **15%** of confused beta users (approx. 2 out of 10, or a proportional sample) opt-in to a *paid* or *scheduled* counselor session when offered, the assumption that this drives value is false.
*   **Action:** Run this test immediately with the 10 confused users and a small holdout group. Do not write code until this behavioral signal is measured.

**Summary for Clinical Advisory Board**

| Category | Current Evidence | Risk/Benefit Analysis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Problem Validity** | 20% of beta users (10/50) expressed confusion without context. | **Risk:** High liability if confusion leads to patient harm. <br> **Benefit:** Clear signal that raw data is insufficient for laypeople. |
| **Solution Fit** | **Unknown.** Preference (async vs. video) and willingness to pay are unverified. | **Risk:** Building the wrong modality wastes $40k and 6 weeks; low adoption leaves liability unmitigated. <br> **Benefit:** *If* validated, direct guidance reduces misinterpretation risk significantly. |
| **Operational Impact** | Current volume: 30 inquiries/week. Target: 50% reduction. | **Risk:** If uptake is low, ticket volume remains high, and fixed costs of the new system increase burn. <br> **Benefit:** Successful deflection frees team for complex cases; structured advice creates an audit trail (liability shield). |
| **Financial Viability** | **Unverified.** No data on fee acceptance. | **Risk:** $40k sunk cost with no revenue offset if users refuse to pay. <br> **Benefit:** Potential new revenue stream if users value immediate access. |

**Recommendation**
Delay the full build. Execute a **manual pilot** immediately:
1.  Contact the 10 confused users. Offer a scheduled 15-min call with a counselor for a specific fee (or free, to test demand only).
2.  Measure: How many accept? Do they prefer rescheduling (async) or immediate (video)?
3.  **Decision Rule:** If acceptance is low (<15%), pivot to better UI explanations or static FAQs before attempting live integration. If high, proceed to build the specific modality requested.

This approach mitigates liability by addressing the known confusion now (via manual intervention) while preventing the waste of resources on an unproven feature.

*Note: The "50% ticket reduction" target must be validated against the actual uptake rate from the pilot; do not bake this metric into success criteria until the baseline demand is proven.*
*Liability is best reduced by documented guidance; ensure any pilot interaction generates a transcript stored in the patient record.*
