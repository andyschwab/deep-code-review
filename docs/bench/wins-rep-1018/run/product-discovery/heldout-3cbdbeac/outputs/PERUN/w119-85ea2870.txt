**Verdict: UNVERIFIED. Do not build yet.**

The current evidence is insufficient to justify immediate development. You have validated a *service failure* (lost trust due to lack of secure storage), but you have not validated the *solution hypothesis* (that a digital booking platform is the required fix) or the *willingness to pay* for guaranteed space versus improved communication of existing capacity.

Building the platform now risks solving the wrong problem: the resorts may simply need a reliable phone call confirming space, not a self-service portal. If the root cause is operational reliability rather than booking mechanics, software will not stop the churn.

**Immediate Action Plan: The Riskiest-Assumption Gate**

Your riskiest assumption is: *"Resorts will pay a premium for guaranteed digital booking, and the lack of a platform (rather than operational inconsistency) was the primary driver of churn."*

**Required Test: The "Fake-Door" Commitment**
Before writing code, run this cheap behavioral test with the three lost accounts and the seven at-risk managers within the next 14 days.

1.  **The Offer:** Contact the 10 managers personally. Offer a "Priority Guaranteed Storage Contract" for the upcoming season at a 15% premium.
2.  **The Mechanism:** Do not show them a platform. Tell them: *"We are launching a guaranteed reservation system. If you sign this intent-to-buy today, we reserve your specific square footage now. If we don't reach 80% commitment by [Date], we revert to standard terms."*
3.  **The Metric:** Signed Letters of Intent (LOI) or verbal commitments backed by a credit card hold.
4.  **Numeric Kill Threshold:**
    *   **GO:** 4 out of 10 managers commit to the premium/guarantee. (This validates willingness to pay and confirms the pain is about *guarantee*, not just *storage*).
    *   **NARROW/PIVOT:** 1–3 commit. (Pain exists, but willingness to pay is low. Pivot to a free, manual reservation system operated by your team to prove reliability first).
    *   **DROP:** 0 commit. (The churn was likely due to broken trust or price, not the lack of a digital tool. A platform will not solve this).

**Interview Guide (Mom Test)**
When speaking to them, do not pitch the platform. Ask:
*   "Walk me through exactly how you decided to switch competitors last year. What was the specific moment you lost confidence?"
*   "How are you currently securing your off-season gear with the new vendor? Are you paying extra for that guarantee?"
*   "Tell me about the last time you needed to store equipment unexpectedly. What did you do?"

**Decision Rule**
If you cannot secure 4 committed "yes" responses to the *value* (guaranteed space) without showing the *feature* (the platform), you must not build. The solution may be a process change (e.g., a dedicated account manager with a spreadsheet) rather than a software product.

Re-evaluate only after this test data is collected.

*Note: This plan isolates the variable of "digital booking" from "operational reliability."*
