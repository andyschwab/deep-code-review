### Decision Framework: CareFlow Nursing Dashboard Pivot

**Verdict:** Do not strip features yet. The risk of contract violation outweighs unverified efficiency gains. Proceed only after passing the **Riskiest-Assumption Gate** below.

#### 1. The Riskiest Assumption & Required Test
*   **Assumption:** "Hospital administrators will accept a simplified UI without the specific deep-dive reports currently mandated in contracts, provided shift nurses save time."
*   **Impact:** High (Loss of top 3 contracts).
*   **Uncertainty:** Critical (No administrative feedback collected yet).
*   **Cheapest Disconfirming Test:** A "Concierge" review simulation with one key stakeholder from each of the top three hospitals.
    *   **Action:** Present a mock quarterly review packet generated *only* from the proposed simplified data. Ask the Director of Nursing: "Can you sign off on this for the board?"
    *   **Metric:** Binary acceptance/rejection.
    *   **Kill Threshold:** If **any** of the three stakeholders say "No" or "This is insufficient," the pivot fails immediately. Do not build the simplified UI until the reporting gap is solved.
    *   **Deadline:** Complete before next Tuesday's meeting.

#### 2. Evidence Gap Analysis (Rewards vs. Risks)

| Dimension | Current Evidence | Missing Evidence (Must Obtain) | Decision Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | : |
| **Efficiency Gain** | Hypothesis: 4 mins/patient saved. | **Behavioral Data:** Time-on-task measurement of nurses using a prototype vs. current tool. | If observed savings < 2 mins/patient, the ROI does not justify the development cost or risk. |
| **Contract Risk** | Fact: Contracts require deep-dive reports. | **Administrative Intent:** Do admins actually *read* these reports, or just archive them? | If admins admit they rarely use them but *require* them for compliance, you must automate the report, not remove the data. |
| **Retention** | Fact: 92% retention rate. | **Churn Driver:** Is retention high because of the analytics, or despite them? | If analytics are not a primary retention driver, they can be moved to a "backend only" mode. |

#### 3. Recommended Path Forward
1.  **Stop Development:** Halt work on stripping features until the administrative test is run.
2.  **Run the Test:** Conduct the three stakeholder interviews/simulations within 48 hours.
3.  **Pivot Strategy (If Test Passes):** If admins agree the detailed view is unnecessary for *daily* use but still needed for *quarterly* reviews, do not delete the feature. Instead, hide it behind an "Admin Export" button. This achieves the "one-tap" simplicity for nurses while maintaining contract compliance.
4.  **Non-Goal for This Stage:** Do not attempt to renegotiate contracts or build complex new reporting engines until the core workflow simplification is validated with real users.

**Summary for CEO:** The 68% ignore rate suggests a usability failure, not necessarily a feature failure. Removing the feature entirely risks 100% of contract revenue based on a hypothesis of 200 hours saved. Validate the administrative requirement first; if the requirement is real but the usage is low, the solution is **hiding complexity**, not **deleting capability**.

*Note: The "4-minute savings" figure is currently an assumption; treat it as zero in financial modeling until measured.*
*Note: Do not present a final go/no-go to the board without the results of the three stakeholder tests.*
