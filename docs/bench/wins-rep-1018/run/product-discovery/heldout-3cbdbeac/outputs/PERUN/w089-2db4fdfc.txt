# Decision Framework: Cash-on-Delivery vs. Driver Safety

**Status:** UNVERIFIED. Do not build the digital payment feature yet. The riskiest assumption is that customers in high-risk zones can/will switch to digital payments immediately. Building now risks revenue collapse without validating demand.

### 1. The Riskiest-Assumption Gate
Before coding, you must test the single point of failure: **Customer Payment Flexibility**.
*   **Assumption:** "At least X% of customers in high-risk zones possess the means (bank account/smartphone) and willingness to switch to digital payments if offered."
*   **Cheapest Test:** A manual "Concierge" test or survey in the top 3 highest-turnover zones. Do not build the app feature yet. Have drivers or support staff ask the next 50 COD customers in these zones: "If we offered a 5% discount for paying via transfer today, could you do it?"
*   **Proposed Kill Threshold:** If **<40%** of asked customers can pay digitally *today*, the assumption fails.
    *   *Basis:* You need a critical mass to make the safety investment viable. If the majority cannot switch, forcing digital payments equals losing the majority of revenue in those zones.
    *   *Decision Rule:*
        *   **>60% Yes:** Proceed to MVP build.
        *   **40-60% Yes:** Run a hybrid pilot (digital optional) with strict cash limits.
        *   **<40% Yes:** **Drop** the immediate digitization plan. Pivot to safety protocols for cash (e.g., armored pickup points, lower cash caps) instead of removing cash.
