### Decision Framework: Build vs. Buy for Customs Automation

**Status:** **UNVERIFIED.** You cannot yet decide to build or buy because the core assumption—that this feature alone unlocks revenue—has not been stress-tested against the "cheapest possible test."

#### 1. The Riskiest Assumption Gate
**Assumption:** "Automating customs documentation is the *single* deciding factor for closing Q4 deals, and the lack of it was the true blocker (not price, integration depth, or timing)."
**Risk:** High. If you build this and they still don't buy due to other hidden friction, you have wasted your only engineering sprint before December.

**Required Test Before Build:**
Do not write code yet. Run a **Concierge Test** on the next prospect or a re-engagement with one of the failed pilots.
*   **The Test:** Offer to manually generate the customs documents for them using your team's internal tools (or even spreadsheets) within 1 hour of their shipment data upload, positioning it as a "beta concierge service."
*   **Metric:** Will they commit to a paid pilot *contingent* on this manual service working?
*   **Kill Threshold:** If they hesitate to sign even with the problem "solved" manually, the issue is not the feature; it is the product fit or sales cycle. **Do not build.**
