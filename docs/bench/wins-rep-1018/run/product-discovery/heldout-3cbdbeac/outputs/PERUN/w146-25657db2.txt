**Decision: Do not approve the build yet. The riskiest assumption is untested.**

Your proposal rests on a single critical assumption: *That the root cause of demurrage penalties is a lack of real-time visibility which, if solved, allows for successful re-routing.*

Currently, you have evidence of the symptom ($45k in penalties, 6-hour delays) but no evidence confirming the cause. If the delays are caused by track congestion (an external constraint) or rigid rail provider contracts that forbid re-routing, a tracking dashboard will provide perfect visibility of an unavoidable disaster. It will add cost without reducing fines.

**Required Action: Run the "Cheapest Disconfirming Test" First**
Before writing code or allocating Q3 budget to a dashboard, you must validate whether "visibility" actually leads to "avoidable penalties."

**The Test: Manual Concierge Simulation (1 Week)**
Do not build software. Simulate the outcome manually using your existing phone/spreadsheet workflow but with heightened frequency.
1.  **Select a Sample:** Pick 10 high-risk rail cars scheduled for next week.
2.  **Increase Visibility Manually:** Have a dispatcher call the rail provider every 4 hours for these specific cars to get exact location/ETA updates (simulating the "real-time" data).
3.  **Attempt Intervention:** When a delay is detected, immediately attempt to re-route or negotiate a slot change based on that new data.
4.  **Record the Result:** Did the rail provider allow the change? Did the change actually reduce the delay?

**Numeric Kill Threshold**
*   **Metric:** Percentage of detected delays where intervention was *possible and effective*.
*   **Threshold:** If, in your sample of 10 cars, you find that **fewer than 3** delays could have been avoided even with perfect information (i.e., <30% success rate), **drop the dashboard idea**.
*   **Reasoning:** If you cannot fix the problem manually with the data, automating the data collection will not fix it either. The bottleneck is likely contractual or infrastructural, not informational.
