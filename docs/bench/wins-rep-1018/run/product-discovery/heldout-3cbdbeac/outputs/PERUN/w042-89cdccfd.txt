**Status:** **UNVERIFIED.** A final procurement decision cannot be made yet because the "unknown factor" (3-year maintenance cost) is the riskiest assumption driving total cost of ownership (TCO). Proceeding without this data violates the riskiest-assumption gate.

#### 1. The Riskiest Assumption Gate
*   **Assumption:** The hardware durability and software update costs of 12 standalone kiosks are lower (or comparable) to 12 sites of shared tablets over 3 years, justifying the upfront efficiency gain.
*   **Required Test:** **Vendor-Specific TCO Pilot.** Before full procurement, you must obtain binding 3-year service level agreements (SLAs) or historical failure rate data from specific vendors for both form factors in high-traffic clinical settings.
*   **Kill Threshold:** If the projected 3-year maintenance cost of kiosks exceeds the monetary value of the time saved (4 mins/patient × patient volume × staff hourly rate) by more than 15%, **drop the kiosk option**.
*   **Action:** Do not sign a full fleet contract. Request pilot units (1 kiosk, 1 tablet station) at your highest-volume site for 30 days to track actual breakage incidents and IT ticket volume.

*   **Not building** a custom software solution; this is a procurement decision between existing commercial options.
*   **Not solving** for 100% patient autonomy immediately; if the pilot shows >20% of patients cannot use the chosen method without staff help, the "efficiency" claim is invalid.
*   **Not finalizing** the budget until the maintenance cost variable is replaced with a vendor quote.
