### Risk Assessment: Legacy Reporting Module Deprecation

**1. Financial Impact (Revenue & Cost)**
*   **Current Net Loss:** The module costs $60,000/year to maintain ($5k/mo) but generates only $40,000/year in revenue. You are currently losing **$20,000 annually** by keeping it running.
*   **Risk Scenario (Migration Failure):** If both clients churn due to migration friction:
    *   **Immediate Revenue Loss:** $40,000/year.
    *   **Net Financial Shift:** You stop the $20k loss, but you also lose the $40k top-line revenue. The net positive impact of deprecation vanishes if you lose the customers entirely.
    *   **Hidden Cost:** If the clients are enterprise accounts with wider usage of DataStream, losing their trust could jeopardize revenue beyond the $40k attributed to this module.

**2. Reputation Impact**
*   **Compliance Risk:** If the legacy tool contains unique fields required for regulatory compliance (e.g., specific audit logs or data formats) that the new dashboard lacks, forcing a migration could render your clients non-compliant. This creates immediate legal liability for them and a breach-of-contract scenario for you.
*   **Trust Erosion:** Unresponsive outreach combined with a hard deadline (Dec 1) signals inflexibility. If the non-responsive client discovers the shutdown post-deadline without a working alternative, it validates a narrative that DataStream does not support its legacy enterprise users, potentially stalling sales to similar large accounts.

**3. Critical Unknowns**
*   **Dependency Gap:** You do not know if the "unique fields" are actually used or if they are compliance-mandatory.
*   **Silent Churn Signal:** The lack of response to the October 5 email is a high-risk indicator. It suggests either the contact is no longer relevant, the client is ignoring you hoping the problem goes away, or they are internally blocked from responding.

### Immediate Validation Step: The "Compliance Audit" Call

Do not send another email. The assumption that "clients can migrate with minimal friction" is the **riskiest assumption** because it relies on unstated technical dependencies. You must disconfirm this before December 1.

**Action:** Conduct a targeted, 15-minute "Compliance & Workflow Audit" call with a technical stakeholder at each of the two accounts.

**The Script (Mom Test Approach):**
*   *Do not pitch the new dashboard.*
*   *Do not ask "Would you be willing to migrate?"* (They will say yes to be polite).
*   **Ask:** "Can you walk me through the last time you generated a report for [Specific Compliance Regulation]? Which specific fields did you have to export manually because they weren't in the standard view?"
*   **Ask:** "What happens internally if you cannot produce that exact file format by December 2nd?"

**Success Metric (Go/No-Go):**
*   **Go:** The client confirms they use only standard fields available in the new dashboard, or they explicitly confirm a migration plan with a technical owner.
*   **No-Go (Kill/Pivot):** The client identifies a unique field or format required for compliance that the new dashboard cannot currently replicate.
    *   *Pivot:* Pause deprecation for that specific client until the new dashboard supports that field, or build a lightweight middleware bridge *only* for that data point.

**Handling the Non-Responsive Client:**
If the second client does not answer a direct phone call to a known technical user within 48 hours, you must treat them as a **blocked dependency**. Do not shut them off on Dec 1. Instead, extend their legacy access automatically for 90 days while escalating the contact chain to find a decision-maker. Shutting off a silent, paying enterprise client is a guaranteed reputation incident.

**Summary for CEO:**
We are currently losing $20k/year on this module. However, shutting it down carries a risk of losing $40k in revenue and damaging enterprise trust if hidden compliance dependencies exist. We will validate these dependencies via direct technical interviews this week. If unique compliance fields are found, we will delay deprecation for affected accounts until the new dashboard bridges the gap.
