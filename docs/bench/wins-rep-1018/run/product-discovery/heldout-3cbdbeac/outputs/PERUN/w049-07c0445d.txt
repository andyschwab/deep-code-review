### Decision Framework: Video Library vs. Interactive Checklist

**Status:** UNVERIFIED. No build recommendation can be made until the "visual preference" assumption is tested with your specific demographic (adults 65+). The following structures the evidence you must gather to fill the blanks.

#### 1. Riskiest Assumption Gate
*   **Assumption:** Patients over 65 prefer and effectively use video for post-surgery instructions over text/checklists.
*   **Impact:** High (determines entire Q3 roadmap).
*   **Uncertainty:** High (no data for this demographic).
*   **Cheapest Test:** Concierge/Wizard-of-Oz test with 20 patients from your pilot group.
    *   *Action:* Manually send 10 patients a single high-quality video link and 10 a refined text checklist for the same procedure.
    *   *Metric:* Completion rate (did they watch/read all?) and follow-up confusion tickets.
    *   **Kill Threshold:** If <70% of the video group watches >80% of the content, or if their confusion ticket rate is not lower than the text group, **drop the video library idea**.
    *   *Deadline:* Run before any engineering scope is defined.
