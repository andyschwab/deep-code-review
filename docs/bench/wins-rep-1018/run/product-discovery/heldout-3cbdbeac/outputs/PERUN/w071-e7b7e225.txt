### Priority Assessment: Dedicated Photo Tracker

**Status:** **UNVERIFIED / DO NOT BUILD YET**
**Reason:** The riskiest assumption (viability/cost) and the core problem validation (behavioral evidence vs. stated intent) have not been tested. Building now risks high infrastructure costs and delayed billing revenue without confirmed value.

#### 1. The Riskiest-Assumption Gate
Before allocating 300 engineering hours, the following assumptions must be tested. The most critical is **Viability (Cost)**:
*   **Assumption:** Storing high-resolution images for 500 patients will not double current cloud storage costs.
*   **Cheapest Test:** Calculate exact storage requirements (MB/patient/month × 500 × retention period) and multiply by current cloud provider rates. Do not guess.
*   **Kill Threshold:** If projected monthly cost > 15% of the estimated revenue saved by the 10-minute time reduction, **drop the feature** or pivot to low-resolution/compressed storage immediately.
*   **Action:** Finance/DevOps to provide exact cost projection by [Date]. No code written until this number is known.

The second critical assumption is **Desirability (Workflow Fit)**:
*   **Assumption:** Nurses need a *dedicated tracker* inside the app, not just a secure way to upload photos they already take.
*   **Cheapest Test:** "Concierge" test. Have 5 nurses upload photos via a secure, temporary web form (no app build) for one week. Measure if they actually do it and if it saves time.
*   **Kill Threshold:** If fewer than 3 of 5 nurses use the manual workaround consistently, the app feature will likely fail adoption. **Do not build.**

#### 2. Evidence Gap (Interviews vs. Behavior)
*   **Current Data:** 18/20 nurses *said* personal devices are a problem. This is stated intent, not behavioral proof.
*   **Missing Data:** We do not know if they currently *avoid* taking photos due to privacy fears, or if they take them anyway and hide the files. We do not know if the "friction" is the photo taking or the *logging* of the photo.
*   **Required Next Step:** Run a "Mom Test" follow-up with 3 nurses. Ask: "Walk me through the last time you documented a wound. What specific step made you worry about privacy?" Do not pitch the solution.

#### 4. Non-Goals for This Stage (Prototype/Validation)
*   **Not Building:** Automated image analysis. This is a future bet contingent on the basic tracker working.
*   **Not Building:** High-resolution storage by default. Start with compressed images to validate cost assumptions.
*   **Not Doing:** Delaying the Billing Module update until the photo tracker is fully built.

1.  **Pause** the 300-hour build estimate.
