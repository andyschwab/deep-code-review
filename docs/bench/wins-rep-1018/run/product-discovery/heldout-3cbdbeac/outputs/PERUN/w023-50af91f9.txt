### Strategic Comparison: MetroPharm Partnership vs. Independent Logistics

**Current Evidence Status:**
*   **Validated:** 98% on-time delivery rate (Pilot: 5 independent pharmacies, June).
*   **Unverified (Critical Blocker):** MetroPharm legacy IT integration feasibility within 90 days.
*   **Unverified (Financial):** Actual per-delivery fee reduction required by MetroPharm; accuracy of the $1.2M independent build estimate.
*   **Unverified (Market):** Whether "rapid scale" via MetroPharm actually converts to retained volume if service levels drop due to integration friction.

**Decision Framework: The Riskiest-Assumption Gate**
Your decision cannot be made on current data because the highest-impact variable (IT integration) is untested. Building the independent network assumes the partnership fails; partnering assumes integration succeeds. Both are guesses without a specific test.

**1. The Partnership Path (Scale Focus)**
*   **Hypothesis:** Integration succeeds within 90 days, enabling immediate access to 200k customers despite lower margins.
*   **Risk:** If integration fails, you lose the quarter and the partnership.
*   **Missing Data:** You do not know if their legacy systems can talk to your routing software.
*   **Required Test (Before Commitment):** A **technical spike** (not a full build) targeting the specific API or data exchange point with MetroPharm's IT team.
    *   *Success Metric:* A successful data handshake (e.g., one prescription order routed and acknowledged) within 14 days.
    *   *Kill Threshold:* If a handshake cannot be established in 14 days, the 90-day window is statistically unreachable. **Do not sign the deal.**

**2. The Independent Logistics Path (Margin Focus)**
*   **Hypothesis:** $1.2M upfront cost yields higher long-term margins and avoids integration risk.
*   **Risk:** Slower scale contradicts investor pressure; capital burn may outpace revenue growth.
*   **Missing Data:** Validation that the unit economics hold at 45 locations, not just 5. The pilot (5 pharmacies) may not reflect the complexity of a regional chain's volume or the cost of a full fleet build-out.
*   **Required Test:** A **financial stress test** using the pilot's actual cost-per-delivery vs. the projected $1.2M capex amortization.
    *   *Success Metric:* Proof that cash runway survives the $1.2M spend while achieving investor-desired growth rates.

**Recommendation: Do Not Choose Yet**
You are attempting to make a strategic choice between two paths where the primary constraint for the "Scale" path (integration) is unknown.

**Immediate Next Step:**
Execute the **Integration Spike** with MetroPharm immediately.
*   **If Pass:** The partnership becomes the viable path for "rapid scale." You then negotiate fees based on confirmed technical viability.
*   **If Fail:** The partnership is dead. The decision collapses to a single option: raise the $1.2M for independent logistics or shrink the growth target.

**Non-Goals for This Stage:**
*   **Do not** sign a binding partnership agreement contingent on a 90-day integration window without a prior technical handshake.
*   **Do not** commit the $1.2M capital expenditure until the partnership is definitively ruled out by the integration test.
*   **Do not** present a final "Go/No-Go" to investors until the integration risk is retired. Present the *test plan* instead.

**Summary for Investors:**
"We have validated operational excellence (98% on-time). We are currently de-risking the single largest threat to scale: legacy IT integration. We will have a binary go/no-go signal on the MetroPharm partnership within 14 days, ensuring we do not waste the quarter on an unfeasible integration."
