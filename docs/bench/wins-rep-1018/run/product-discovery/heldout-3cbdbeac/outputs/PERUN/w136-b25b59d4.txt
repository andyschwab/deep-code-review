**SUBJECT:** Decision Required: Build vs. Buy for Luggage Tracking (Riskiest Assumption Unverified)

**Recommendation: DO NOT BUILD yet. DO NOT commit to a vendor yet.**
**Immediate Action:** Run a 14-day "Wizard-of-Oz" manual test to validate the core assumption before spending capital on software development or long-term contracts.

**The Critical Gap**
We currently have evidence of a problem (4 incidents in 45 days; 6-hour resolution time), but **zero evidence** that a custom software tool is the correct solution or that the volume justifies the fixed cost. The assumption that "barcode scanning + driver app = <30 min resolution" is untested. Building now risks creating expensive infrastructure for a process flaw that might be solved by better training, simpler checklists, or a low-cost existing app. Furthermore, with a 60% seasonal drop, a custom build may sit idle and incur maintenance costs without proportional value during the off-season.

**The Cheapest Test (Riskiest Assumption Gate)**
Before writing code or signing a vendor contract, we must disconfirm the riskiest assumption: *That digital tracking is required to achieve <30 minute resolution.*

**Test Plan: The Manual "Concierge" Pilot**
*   **Duration:** 14 days (covering peak arrival windows).
*   **Method:** Use existing tools (WhatsApp group + shared Google Sheet + printed barcode stickers from a local printer). No new software development.
    *   Staff manually scan/print stickers.
    *   Drivers photo-confirm handoff in the WhatsApp group.
    *   Manager tracks resolution time on the Sheet.
*   **Success Metric (Kill Threshold):** If manual tracking does not reduce average resolution time to **under 45 minutes** within the first 7 days, the hypothesis that "process visibility" is the bottleneck is likely false, or the staff adoption barrier is too high for a custom app to fix.
*   **Decision Rule:**
    *   **Pass (<45 mins):** Proceed to evaluating low-code/no-code vendors vs. custom build based on off-season cost modeling.
    *   **Fail (>45 mins):** Stop. The issue is likely behavioral or operational, not technological. Do not build software.

**Build vs. Buy Analysis (Pending Test Results)**
*   **Build (Custom Software):** Only justified if the manual pilot succeeds *and* the calculated cost-per-bag saved during the 40% off-season is lower than vendor fees. High risk of over-engineering for seasonal volume.
*   **Buy (Third-Party):** Viable only if a vendor offers a "pay-per-active-season" or highly flexible scaling model. Most enterprise luggage tools have fixed annual minimums that may not make sense given the 60% volume drop.

**Non-Goals for This Stage**
*   Developing a permanent mobile app interface.
*   Integrating with the Property Management System (PMS).
*   Solving "lost luggage" for non-transfer scenarios (e.g., internal resort moves).
