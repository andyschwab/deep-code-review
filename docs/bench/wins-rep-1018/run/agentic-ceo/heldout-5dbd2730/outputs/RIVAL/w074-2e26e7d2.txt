### Work Session Agenda: Patient Feedback Sentiment Analysis Rollout

**Objective:** Finalize rollout order, assign decision authority, and execute the pre-activation accuracy verification within budget and timeline constraints.

#### 1. Rollout Order Strategy
Given the constraint of only one pilot department running parallel to the legacy process for 48 hours, and the high stakes of the irreversible real-time alert system, the rollout order must prioritize risk mitigation and data representativeness.

*   **Pilot Department Selection:** Choose the department with the highest volume of survey responses and the most diverse range of sentiment drivers (e.g., Emergency Department or Outpatient Clinic). This ensures the pilot stress-tests the system under maximum load and varied conditions.
*   **Phased Activation Plan:**
    *   **Phase 1 (Pilot - 48 hours):** Activate the tool in the selected pilot department alongside the manual process. Do *not* enable the real-time alert system yet. Use this window to compare automated categorization against manual validation by the two department heads.
    *   **Phase 2 (Validation & Tuning):** Analyze discrepancies from the 48-hour pilot. Engineers adjust categorization logic based on department head feedback.
    *   **Phase 3 (Full Rollout):** Deploy to remaining five departments sequentially or in small batches, enabling the real-time alert system only after successful validation in each phase.

#### 2. Decision Authority Matrix
Clear delineation of responsibilities is critical to avoid bottlenecks and ensure accountability.

| Decision Area | Owner | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pilot Department Selection** | **You (Requester)** | Requires strategic alignment with hospital priorities and risk tolerance. |
| **Categorization Logic Thresholds** | **Department Heads** | They possess the domain expertise to define what constitutes a "service gap" or "negative spike." |
| **Technical Configuration & Integration** | **Engineers** | They understand the system architecture, API limits, and deployment mechanics. |
| **Go/No-Go on Real-Time Alerts** | **You (Requester)** | This is the irreversible step with operational consequences (paging managers); final accountability rests with leadership. |
| **Discrepancy Resolution Protocol** | **Joint (Heads + Engineers)** | Requires collaboration to balance clinical context with technical feasibility. |

#### 3. Immediate First Step: Pre-Activation Accuracy Verification
Before any department goes live, you must verify the tool's accuracy against a known dataset.

*   **Action:** Extract a sample of 500 historical survey responses from the chosen pilot department that have already been manually categorized and scored by staff.
*   **Execution:** Run these 500 records through the new sentiment analysis tool in a sandbox environment (no live alerts).
*   **Validation Metric:** Compare the tool's output against the existing manual classifications. Calculate precision, recall, and F1-score for negative sentiment detection.
*   **Success Criteria:** The tool must achieve at least 90% agreement with manual categorization on negative sentiment flags before proceeding to the 48-hour parallel pilot. If accuracy is below 90%, engineers must retrain or adjust the model before any live deployment.

**Timeline Check:** Complete the sandbox verification by end-of-day Wednesday to allow Thursday/Friday for adjustments, ensuring the 48-hour pilot can start Monday as planned. This keeps the project on track for the December 15th board review.

**Note:** The 4-hour reboot penalty for the alert system mandates that the "Go/No-Go" decision be based strictly on the 48-hour pilot data, not assumptions. Ensure department heads are available during the pilot window for real-time validation.
