### Migration Work Session Plan: ClearSight Optics

**Immediate First Task**
Execute a read-only snapshot backup of the entire legacy database immediately. Verify the integrity of this backup file before proceeding to any cleansing or mapping activities. This is your only safety net once the system goes read-only in 48 hours.

**Order of Operations**
1.  **Data Cleansing & Standardization (Hours 0–24):** Identify and fix formatting inconsistencies, null values in critical fields (e.g., Sphere, Cylinder, Axis), and duplicate patient records. Map legacy field names to the new lens ordering schema.
2.  **Validation & Dry Run (Hours 24–36):** Run the migration script against a staging environment using the cleansed data. Perform row-count verification and random sample audits against the legacy source. Test the new system's ability to generate valid insurance billing codes from the migrated data.
3.  **Final Cutover (Hours 36–48):** Halt all non-essential access. Execute the final data swap. Perform immediate post-migration sanity checks (total record count, critical field integrity).
4.  **Post-Migration Review (Post-Cutover):** Conduct a full audit of 100% of records for critical fields and 10% random sampling for complex prescription history. Monitor the new system for 24 hours before declaring success.

**Delegation to Junior Developer**
Assign the junior developer to:
*   **Data Profiling:** Run scripts to identify nulls, outliers, and formatting errors in the 50,000 records.
*   **Cleansing Execution:** Apply the standardized cleaning rules to the staging dataset under your supervision.
*   **Documentation:** Log every error found and fixed, creating a "cleaning ledger" for the audit trail.
*   **Staging Validation:** Perform the row-count checks and basic field comparisons during the dry run.

**Risks Requiring Your Personal Evaluation**
*   **Data Loss During Mapping:** Verify that complex prescription histories (e.g., progressive lens additions, prism corrections) are not truncated or misinterpreted by the new schema.
*   **Insurance Billing Compatibility:** Confirm that the migrated data generates valid billing codes for the October 1st cycle; a single format error could delay claims for thousands of patients.
*   **Rollback Feasibility:** Since the swap is irreversible without manual re-entry, define the exact "point of no return" and ensure your backup restoration process is tested and documented.
*   **Timeline Buffer:** Assess if the 48-hour window allows sufficient time for unexpected cleansing complexities; if the dry run fails, do you have a contingency to extend the read-only period?

**Note:** This plan prioritizes data integrity over speed, given the irreversible nature of the cutover. Ensure your team communicates blockers immediately to avoid last-minute surprises.
