### Preparation Session Workflow: Prism Ledger Migration

**Priority Decision:** You must prioritize **simulating the failover procedure** before writing final validation scripts. While validation logic is critical, the failover simulation reveals the structural weaknesses in your rollback plan and timing constraints. If you cannot successfully roll back the schema changes within the window while preserving the last six hours of transaction history, no amount of balance validation will save the migration. The simulation dictates the specific data points and speed requirements your validation scripts must meet.

**Order of Operations:**

1.  **Constraint Mapping & Risk Modeling:** Define the exact "point of no return" where schema changes become irreversible without data loss. Map the 28-hour window into strict 15-minute increments, accounting for the 8 engineers and 5 DBAs.
2.  **Failover Dry-Run (Simulation):** Execute a full-scale simulation on a production-sized clone. Specifically test the rollback scenario where reconciliation fails at T-minus 1 hour. Measure the time required to revert schema changes and restore service.
3.  **Validation Script Finalization:** Based on simulation latency data, write and optimize the reconciliation scripts to ensure they complete well within the safety margin identified in step 2.
4.  **Role Assignment & Communication Drill:** Assign specific engineers to specific ledger shards. Conduct a communication drill using the exact channels intended for the live event to ensure clear status reporting under pressure.
5.  **Pre-Migration Snapshot:** Schedule the final immutable snapshot of the legacy system immediately preceding the window.
6.  **Execution:** Execute the migration in phased batches rather than a single "big bang" if the architecture allows, validating each batch before proceeding.

**Personal Verification Checkpoints:**
As the lead, you must personally verify only these three critical gates:
1.  **The Rollback Viability Report:** Confirm the simulated rollback completed successfully with zero data loss for the trailing six-hour transaction window.
2.  **The Reconciliation Threshold:** Sign off on the exact decimal precision match (down to the cent) required to trigger the traffic switch.
3.  **The "Stop" Authority Protocol:** Verify that every team member knows the single specific metric that triggers an immediate abort, and that you are the sole decision-maker for that call.

**Single Most Important Action Right Now:**
**Freeze the legacy schema and initiate a full-volume failover simulation immediately.** Do not write another line of validation code until you have proven that a failed migration can be safely reversed within the allotted time. The ability to undo the change is the only safety net you have; if that net has holes, the migration cannot proceed.
