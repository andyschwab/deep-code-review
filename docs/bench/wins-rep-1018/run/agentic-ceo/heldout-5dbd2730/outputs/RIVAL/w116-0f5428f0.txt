**Strategy Session Structure for Northwind Agri Biosecurity Protocol**

**1. Order of Operations**
*   **Phase 1: Risk Definition & Triage Logic (Immediate):** Define the specific clinical signs and data points that constitute an automatic "reject" versus "hold for secondary review." This must be finalized before staffing models are created.
*   **Phase 2: Zoning & Flow Design:** Map the physical arrival path into three distinct zones: Hot (unscreened), Warm (secondary vet review), and Cold (cleared for entry). This ensures that if an infection is found, it does not contaminate the screening team or the facility.
*   **Phase 3: Staffing Allocation:** Assign the four veterinarians exclusively to the "Warm" zone for secondary reviews and final sign-off. Delegate initial visual triage and data logging to trained field managers to maximize vet efficiency.
*   **Phase 4: Data Integration:** Establish the logging protocol that links each animal ID to its screening result instantly, creating an audit trail required for liability protection.
*   **Phase 5: Staff Training & Simulation:** Conduct dry runs of the flow with staff before the July 25 deadline.

**2. Decisions Requiring Your Explicit Approval (Liability Focus)**
*   **Rejection Criteria Thresholds:** You must personally approve the specific medical criteria that trigger an automatic herd quarantine versus individual rejection. This defines the legal boundary of "due diligence."
*   **Liability Waivers & Escalation Paths:** Approve the protocol for who is notified immediately if a positive case is found (legal, insurance, upper management) and the exact wording of any documentation field managers sign regarding their limited diagnostic authority.
*   **Final "Stop-Work" Authority:** Define the specific scenario under which operations halt completely, a decision that carries massive financial liability and rests solely with you.

**3. Delegatable Tasks to Field Managers**
*   **Initial Triage Execution:** Conducting the first-pass visual checks based on the checklist you approve.
*   **Physical Zone Management:** Directing truck drivers and managing the physical barriers between Hot, Warm, and Cold zones.
*   **Data Entry:** Logging animal IDs and initial check results into the system.
*   **Staff Coordination:** Managing shift schedules and ensuring personal protective equipment (PPE) compliance among non-vet staff.

**First Concrete Step to Prevent Irreversible Quarantine**
The immediate first step is to **draft and approve the "Binary Triage Checklist"** by tomorrow. This document must list the specific, observable symptoms that allow a field manager to instantly separate an animal from the main group before a veterinarian ever touches it. Without this binary filter, your four veterinarians will become bottlenecks, forcing mixed-status animals to wait together, which is the primary vector for cross-contamination that leads to total herd quarantine. This checklist is the foundation upon which the zoning, staffing, and liability protections are built; until it is signed off, no other planning can effectively mitigate the risk of a $200,000 loss.

*Note: Ensure the checklist includes a "gray area" protocol that defaults to isolation rather than release.
All training materials must reference the approved checklist version number to maintain legal defensibility.*
