### Surgical Robot Patch Deployment: Coordination Session Plan

**Session Objective:** Determine the optimal deployment sequence and safety protocol for the irreversible haptic feedback patch across five trauma centers.

**1. Deployment Strategy Decision (Order of Operations)**
*   **Recommendation:** Do not start with the lowest volume center nor the most experienced staff exclusively. Adopt a **Phased Canary Approach**.
    *   **Phase 1 (Pilot):** Select **one** robot at the center with the *most experienced staff* but *low immediate surgical volume* for the next 48 hours. This maximizes the chance of successful recovery if the irreversible install fails, while minimizing patient impact.
    *   **Phase 2 (Expansion):** Roll out to remaining units at the pilot center.
    *   **Phase 3 (Full Rollout):** Proceed to other centers based on surgeon availability, not just volume.
*   **Decision Point:** Confirm which specific center meets the "Experienced Staff + Low Immediate Volume" criteria for the Phase 1 pilot.

**2. Pre-Clearance Safety Checks (Mandatory Before Surgery)**
Before any updated robot is cleared for a procedure, the following checks must be signed off by a Software Architect:
*   **Latency Verification:** Confirm haptic feedback latency is within the new specified tolerance (<5ms deviation) under load simulation.
*   **Fail-Safe Trigger Test:** Verify the system correctly enters "safe mode" if haptic data stream is interrupted.
*   **Calibration Integrity:** Run full kinematic calibration to ensure the patch did not alter spatial mapping.
*   **Rollback Contingency Validation:** Although the software is irreversible, confirm the physical service kit and technician are on-site and ready for immediate hardware intervention if the unit fails post-install.

**3. Scheduling Conflicts: Decision Matrix**
*   **Team Can Handle Independently:**
    *   Coordinating the 48-hour offline window for non-critical units.
    *   Managing the physical presence of the 15 field engineers during installation windows.
    *   Executing the technical installation and initial diagnostic scripts.
*   **Requires Your Decision (Escalation Points):**
    *   **Surgeon Pushback:** If a lead surgeon refuses the 48-hour downtime for their primary unit, you must decide whether to delay their cases or deploy a reserve unit (if available).
    *   **Emergency Override:** If a trauma surge occurs during a scheduled patch window, you must authorize the immediate halt of the deployment and potential reversion of schedule.
    *   **Failure Response:** If the Phase 1 pilot fails, you must decide whether to pause the entire global rollout or proceed with modified parameters.

**4. Session Agenda Flow**
1.  **Risk Review (15 mins):** Architects present failure modes of the irreversible install.
2.  **Site Selection (20 mins):** Map the 5 centers against current surgeon schedules and staff expertise to select the Phase 1 Pilot site.
3.  **Protocol Definition (25 mins):** Finalize the 4-point Pre-Clearance checklist and assign Architect sign-off authority.
4.  **Conflict Simulation (20 mins):** Walk through a "Surgeon Refusal" and "Trauma Surge" scenario to define your escalation thresholds.
5.  **Resource Allocation (15 mins):** Assign the 15 engineers to specific centers based on the phased timeline.
