### Migration Plan: Serenity Hills to CompassionFirst

**Immediate First Task:** Assign the Lead Nurse and IT Manager to jointly conduct a **Critical Data Inventory Audit** immediately. This involves identifying the top 10% of patient records containing active, time-sensitive care plans and medication schedules that cannot tolerate even a minute of unavailability. This specific subset dictates the migration wave order and ensures patient safety is the primary filter for all subsequent steps.

#### Phase 1: Preparation & Segmentation (Now – Tuesday, Oct 1st)
*   **IT Specialists:** Execute the technical schema mapping between the legacy system and CompassionFirst. Develop a script to extract data in prioritized waves based on the Critical Data Inventory. Set up a isolated "sandbox" environment in CompassionFirst for validation.
*   **Nurses:** Categorize the 80 active patients into three tiers: Tier 1 (Critical/Active changes), Tier 2 (Stable/Regular updates), and Tier 3 (Archival/Historical only). Nurses must flag any physical paper notes not yet digitized.
*   **Joint Action:** Define the "Rollback Protocol"—the exact steps to revert to the legacy system if the migration fails mid-stream.

#### Phase 2: Validation & Simulation (Wednesday, Oct 2nd)
*   **IT Specialists:** Perform the migration of Tier 3 (lowest risk) data into the sandbox. Verify data integrity, checking for missing fields or formatting errors. Fix extraction scripts based on results.
*   **Nurses:** Conduct a **Targeted Staff Training Simulation** using the migrated Tier 3 data in the sandbox. Nurses should attempt to retrieve care plans and enter mock vitals. This validates both the data accuracy and the usability of the new interface under pressure.
*   **Decision Point:** If the simulation reveals critical data loss or usability blockers, halt and refine. If successful, proceed to final extraction prep.

#### Phase 3: Execution & Go-Live (Thursday, Oct 3rd)
*   **IT Specialists:**
    *   **08:00:** Begin full extraction of Tier 1 and Tier 2 data.
    *   **12:00:** Complete data transfer and run automated integrity checks.
    *   **14:00:** Lock the legacy system to read-only mode.
    *   **16:00:** Final sign-off on data completeness.
*   **Nurses:**
    *   **08:00 – 12:00:** Continue patient care using legacy system; document any urgent changes on temporary secure paper logs if the system is locked for extraction.
    *   **14:00 – 17:00:** Verify Tier 1 patient records in CompassionFirst. Confirm medication lists and care plans match the paper logs exactly.
    *   **18:00:** Official Go-Live. All new documentation enters CompassionFirst.

#### Roles and Responsibilities Summary

| Responsibility Area | Owned By | Specific Duties |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Data Integrity & Security** | **IT Specialists** | Schema mapping, script execution, backup creation, rollback execution, technical troubleshooting. |
| **Clinical Accuracy** | **Nurses** | Patient tiering, verifying care plan accuracy post-migration, identifying missing clinical context, final sign-off on patient records. |
| **Process Coordination** | **Joint** | Timeline adherence, communication of status to stakeholders, managing the "war room" during go-live. |

**Why Audit Before Training?**
You must begin with the **Data Integrity Audit** (specifically the Critical Data Inventory) rather than staff training. Training is useless if the underlying data structure is flawed or if the most critical patient records are not prioritized for the first migration wave. The audit defines *what* you are moving and *in what order*, which creates the dataset required for a meaningful training simulation.

**Note:** Ensure the "Rollback Protocol" is printed and physically available to the nursing station in case of digital failure during the transition.
