### Structured Migration Plan for ChainShip Global

**1. Data Subset for Initial Validation**
Validate the **"Active In-Transit" subset** first. This includes all shipments currently moving between nodes that have had a status update within the last 72 hours.
*   **Reasoning:** This subset represents the highest volatility and immediate business value. Validating static, completed archives (90% of your 2M monthly volume) is lower risk; validating active, changing data proves the migration logic handles real-time state transitions correctly before committing the genesis block.

**2. Critical Strategic Choice: Integrity vs. Completeness**
You must decide between **Strict Cryptographic Integrity** (migrating only data with verifiable, unbroken audit trails) and **Total Historical Completeness** (migrating all records, including those with gaps or legacy formatting errors).
*   **Recommendation:** Choose **Strict Cryptographic Integrity**. For the genesis block to be trusted by your 15 enterprise clients, the chain must be immutable and mathematically sound from block zero. Any record with ambiguous history or unverified legacy data must be excluded from the genesis block and stored in a separate, linked "legacy archive" off-chain or in a side-car structure. Do not compromise the genesis block's purity to achieve 100% volume migration; a smaller, perfect ledger is superior to a large, disputed one.

**3. Precise First Action: The Read-Only Shadow Snapshot**
The team must immediately execute a **Read-Only Shadow Snapshot** of the current production database into an isolated staging environment, strictly prohibiting any write operations to the live system.
*   **Execution:** Configure the ten developers to build a parallel extraction pipeline that pulls data from the live DB into a sandboxed instance without altering source timestamps or statuses. This creates a "frozen" mirror for mapping and hash generation.
*   **Safety Mechanism:** Implement a database-level lock on the snapshotting user account that grants `SELECT` permissions only, ensuring zero risk of corrupting live tracking information during the mapping phase. All genesis block calculations and hash verifications must occur exclusively against this shadow copy.
