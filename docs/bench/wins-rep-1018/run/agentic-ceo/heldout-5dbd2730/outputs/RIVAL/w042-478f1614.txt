**Strategic Deployment Plan: QuietZone Noise Monitoring System**

**Immediate Decision: Structural Survey First**
Do not begin with a dry-run in the new wing. The irreversible risk lies exclusively in the historic brick walls of the older wing. A dry-run elsewhere validates the equipment, not the safety of the drilling protocol. You must prioritize the structural survey of the old wing to eliminate the risk of hitting hidden conduits or compromising structural integrity before any physical installation attempts are made.

**Phase 1: Validation and Site Prep (Days 1–5)**
*   **Action:** Conduct non-invasive wall composition analysis on the historic wing.
*   **Sign-off Required:** Approval of the scanning methodology and the "No-Drill" zones identified by the survey.
*   **Technician Managed:** Scheduling the survey team within the 2 AM – 5 AM window and organizing equipment logistics.

**Phase 2: Pilot Installation (Days 6–10)**
*   **Action:** Execute a limited installation (2–3 wards) in the *historic* wing only, using the validated safe zones.
*   **Sign-off Required:** Final authorization to proceed to full-scale rollout after reviewing the pilot for any micro-fractures or conduit near-misses.
*   **Technician Managed:** Execution of the drilling, mounting, and initial cabling during the night shift.

**Phase 3: Full Rollout (Days 11–18)**
*   **Action:** Deploy systems across the remaining 37 wards (mix of historic and new wings).
*   **Sign-off Required:** None, provided the pilot phase was successful.
*   **Technician Managed:** Daily coordination of the five technicians to maximize the 3-hour nightly window. The lead technician will assign specific wards per night to ensure the December 20th deadline is met with a buffer.

**Phase 4: System Calibration and Testing (Days 19–20)**
*   **Action:** Network integration, sensor calibration, and noise floor baseline establishment.
*   **Sign-off Required:** Final acceptance of system accuracy and data integrity.
*   **Technician Managed:** Running diagnostic scripts and adjusting sensor sensitivity.

**Authority Matrix**
*   **Your Direct Sign-off:**
    1.  Acceptance of the structural survey results and defined safe drilling zones.
    2.  Authorization to move from Pilot Installation to Full Rollout.
    3.  Final system acceptance before the holiday surge.
*   **Lead Technician Authority:**
    1.  Nightly ward assignment and staff scheduling.
    2.  Execution of drilling and mounting within pre-approved zones.
    3.  Routine troubleshooting and cable management.
    4.  Coordination with hospital security for night access.

**First Concrete Step: Non-Invasive Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR) Scan**
Before any tool touches the surface, you must deploy a certified structural engineer equipped with Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR) and multi-spectrum stud/conduit detectors. This step must occur during a daytime inspection (no need to wait for the 2 AM window) to map the internal composition of the historic brick walls. This scan will identify rebar, electrical conduits, and structural load-bearing points, generating a "heat map" for each ward that explicitly marks forbidden drilling zones. Only once this map is generated and reviewed can the drilling templates be created for the night team.

*Note: Ensure the GPR operator is familiar with historic masonry to distinguish between original brickwork and later patches.*
*Delaying this scan to start a dry-run elsewhere risks catastrophic structural damage and project termination.*
