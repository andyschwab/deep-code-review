### Installation Sequence by Cargo Sensitivity
1.  **Priority 1 (Immediate):** Pharmaceuticals and biologics (strict 2–8°C range; irreversible spoilage risk).
2.  **Priority 2:** Fresh produce and dairy (rapid degradation above 4°C).
3.  **Priority 3:** Frozen goods (buffer time exists but risk of thaw-refreeze cycles).
4.  **Priority 4:** Dry goods requiring cool storage (highest tolerance for temporary fluctuation).

### Calibration Failure Protocol
**Technicians Can Resolve Independently:**
*   Connectivity timeouts during initial pairing.
*   Battery voltage warnings before sealing.
*   Physical mounting alignment issues.
*   Software firmware update stalls (if a retry resolves it).

**Requires Engineering Review (Do Not Seal):**
*   Temperature reading drift exceeding ±0.5°C against the master standard.
*   Intermittent signal loss after three retry attempts.
*   Physical damage to the sensor housing or wiring harness.
*   Inconsistent data logging intervals.
