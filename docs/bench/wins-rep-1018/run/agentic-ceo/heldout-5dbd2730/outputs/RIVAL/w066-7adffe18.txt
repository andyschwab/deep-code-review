### Deployment Plan: Pediatric Wing Video Service

**1. Work Session Sequence**
*   **Phase A: Content Curation (Immediate Start):** Content managers must finalize the 200-hour library first. Technical deployment cannot begin until the content payload size is known to calculate bandwidth requirements.
*   **Phase B: Pilot Technical Deployment:** Network engineers will update firmware on a single control group (e.g., 10 rooms) during the first approved window.
*   **Phase C: Validation & Rollout:** Only after confirming the pilot units reboot successfully and stream content will the remaining 590 units be scheduled for updates.

**2. Required Deployment Windows for Approval**
You must approve updates strictly during off-peak bandwidth periods to avoid network congestion and ensure update integrity.
*   **Approved Window 1:** 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM (Daily)
*   **Approved Window 2:** 8:00 PM – 6:00 AM (Daily)
*   **Strictly Prohibited:** 6:30 AM – 7:30 AM and 6:30 PM – 7:30 PM (Shift change bandwidth drops).

*Note: Do not attempt to stagger updates during shift changes; the bandwidth drop risks incomplete firmware writes, causing black screens.*

**3. Content Manager Autonomy**
Content managers have full authority to decide the following without further approval, provided all material meets pediatric safety standards:
*   **Genre Mix:** The ratio of entertainment vs. educational health videos.
*   **Thematic Grouping:** How videos are categorized (e.g., "Pre-Surgery Prep," "Fun Distractions," "Health Camp Specials").
*   **Scheduling Logic:** The order in which videos play within specific age-group playlists.

**4. First Immediate Action to Avoid Blackout**
**Isolate and test the firmware update package on a single, non-critical TV unit immediately.**

Do not push the update to any patient rooms yet. You must verify that the specific firmware version interacts correctly with the current hospital network handshake and that the "auto-reboot" function works as expected after the update completes. If this test fails, you avoid a hospital-wide blackout. If it succeeds, you proceed to the 10-room pilot during the next approved window (10:00 AM).

***

*Ensure the firmware package is stored on a local server segment with high bandwidth to the TVs to minimize transfer time during the approved windows.*
*Confirm with facilities that the "manual reboot" labor estimate of 40 hours accounts for travel time between rooms on different floors.*
