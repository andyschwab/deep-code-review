### Deployment Workflow: Legacy Scanner Replacement

**1. Warehouse Grouping Strategy**
To maximize the efficiency of the 25 technicians against the 3,500 device target within the five-day window, warehouses are grouped into **Regional Clusters** based on travel time and device volume.
*   **Cluster Composition:** Group warehouses so that a single technician team can complete one full warehouse per day. With 50 warehouses and 5 days, we must complete 10 warehouses daily.
*   **Team Allocation:** Deploy technicians in pairs (12 teams of 2, with 1 team of 1 or a floaters). Each pair is assigned one warehouse per day.
*   **Throughput Math:** Each pair handles ~70 devices daily (3,500 devices / 50 warehouses / 5 days = 14 devices per warehouse average; however, to meet the 10-minute pairing constraint, teams must operate in parallel).
*   **Revised Allocation:** Since pairing takes 10 minutes, one technician can pair 48 devices in an 8-hour shift. To clear 70 devices/day per warehouse, assign **two technicians per warehouse** working simultaneously. This covers the volume with a buffer for travel and setup. The 25th technician acts as a "Swing Lead" to handle logistics, device transport between nearby clusters, and cover for breaks.

**2. Administrator Escalation Protocol**
The three server administrators will monitor the central pairing dashboard. They are authorized to resolve the following errors immediately without escalating to you:
*   **Network Time-Outs:** Retrying pairing sequences caused by transient Wi-Fi packet loss.
*   **Credential Mismatches:** Resetting device authentication tokens when the error code indicates "Invalid Key" but the device ID exists in the master inventory list.
*   **Firmware Version Conflicts:** Pushing mandatory over-the-air firmware updates if the pairing fails due to version incompatibility.
*   **Escalation Trigger:** You must be notified only if: (a) More than 15% of devices in a single warehouse fail pairing after admin intervention, (b) The central server shows latency >2 seconds, or (c) A device reports "Hardware ID Not Recognized," indicating a potential supply chain mismatch.

**3. Immediate Pilot Test Protocol (First 10 Devices)**
Execute this validation within the hour before dispatching teams:
*   **Step A (Baseline):** Select two technicians and one admin. Wipe and attempt to pair two legacy devices and two new devices in a controlled environment mimicking a warehouse Wi-Fi zone.
*   **Step B (Stress Test):** Simulate a network interruption during the 10-minute pairing window on three new devices to verify if the process resumes or corrupts.
*   **Step C (Rollback Verification):** On the final three devices, intentionally fail the pairing post-wipe to confirm the "critical risk" mitigation: verify if the wiped legacy devices can be restored to a functional state within 15 minutes.
*   **Go/No-Go Decision:** If the rollback fails on any of the three test units, halt full deployment immediately. If pairing success is <90% after admin intervention, refine the protocol before spreading to all 25 technicians.

*Note: Ensure all 3,500 new devices are pre-charged to at least 80% before distribution to avoid pairing delays due to low battery.*
*Note: Technicians must log every failed pairing attempt timestamped to the minute for admin pattern analysis.*
