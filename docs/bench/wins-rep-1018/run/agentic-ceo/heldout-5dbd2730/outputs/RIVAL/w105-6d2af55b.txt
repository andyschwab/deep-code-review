### Migration Strategy Session Structure

**1. Order of Operations**
*   **Phase A: Critical Path Validation (Immediate):** Verify the new site's power infrastructure and sorting machine compatibility *before* any disconnection occurs. This is the absolute gatekeeper; if the new site cannot accept the machines immediately, the power cut is forbidden.
*   **Phase B: Bottleneck Simulation & Layout Adjustment:** Address the 30% conveyor reduction before moving inventory. Map the new workflow to identify exactly where the peak-hour bottleneck will form and pre-define manual workarounds or staging zones to compensate.
*   **Phase C: Phased Inventory Migration:** Do not move all 50,000 sq. ft. at once. Execute a wave migration based on SKU velocity, ensuring high-turnover items are positioned in the new layout first to test the reduced conveyor capacity under load.
*   **Phase D: Staff Reconfiguration:** Retrain the 25 associates on the new manual workarounds identified in Phase B before the first pallet moves.
*   **Phase E: Disconnection & Transport:** Only after Phases A–C are signed off, cut power to the old sorting machines for transport.
*   **Phase F: Site Cleanup:** Final sweep of the old facility for the June 30 sale.

**2. Decision Required: Temporary Staffing Levels**
You must decide whether to authorize a **20% temporary staff increase (5 additional associates)** specifically for the peak-hour window during the first two weeks of operation.
*   *Context:* The 30% reduction in conveyor length creates a hard physical constraint that software cannot fix. Without extra hands to manually shuttle goods around the bottleneck or manage overflow staging areas, your current 25 associates will likely fail to meet peak throughput targets, causing shipment delays. You need to approve this budget now to have temps hired and trained by June 15.

**3. First Concrete Task (Due Before Noon)**
**Dispatch a senior facilities engineer to the new site immediately to physically verify and document that the electrical feed and floor mounting points for the incoming sorting machines are live, compatible, and ready for immediate connection.**
*   *Deliverable:* A signed "Ready-to-Connect" certificate from the engineer.
*   *Reasoning:* If this verification fails, cutting power at the old site becomes a catastrophic error that halts all operations. No other planning can proceed until this physical reality is confirmed.

*Note: The June 15 operational deadline is aggressive given the layout constraints; consider delaying the final old-site cleanup if bottleneck testing fails.*
