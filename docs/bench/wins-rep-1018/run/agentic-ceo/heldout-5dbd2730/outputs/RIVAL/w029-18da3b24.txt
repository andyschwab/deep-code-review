**Pre-Launch Coordination Plan: CareCompass GPS Rollout**

**Immediate Priority: Legal Review & Consent Verification**
The absolute first activity must be a formal legal review of the consent forms and the mechanism for capturing verified consent before any code is deployed or tested with real employees. Because violating labor laws in three major states exposes the company to massive fines and union backlash, no technical testing (beta or otherwise) can occur until the legal framework is certified as compliant. If the consent mechanism is flawed, a beta test merely generates evidence of liability.

**Sequence of Activities**

1.  **Legal Certification (Days 1–2)**
    *   **Task:** Legal counsel must review the current consent forms against the specific statutes of the three high-risk states.
    *   **Task:** Define the exact technical workflow required to capture "explicit, verified consent" prior to the first login (e.g., mandatory checkbox + digital signature + timestamp).
    *   **Founder Decision Required:** You must approve the final legal language and the specific consent workflow. Do not proceed until you sign off on this.

2.  **Development of Manual Override & Consent Gate (Days 3–5)**
    *   **Task:** The development team must build the "Consent Gate" that blocks app access until legal consent is recorded.
    *   **Task:** Simultaneously, build the "Manual Override Switch" to instantly disable GPS tracking globally. This is your emergency brake.
    *   **Constraint:** Do not integrate real GPS data yet; use mock coordinates to test the logic.

3.  **Limited Beta Test (Days 6–9)**
    *   **Task:** Recruit 20 aides in a single county who have already signed the newly certified consent forms.
    *   **Task:** Deploy the app with GPS enabled only for this group. Monitor the consent gate functionality and the override switch responsiveness.
    *   **Goal:** Validate that the legal safeguard works technically under load, not just on paper.

4.  **Full Rollout Preparation (Days 10–Launch)**
    *   **Task:** Verify all 200 day-one aides have completed the consent workflow in the system.
    *   **Task:** Conduct a final load test simulating 500 concurrent sessions to ensure the consent check does not create a bottleneck.
    *   **Founder Decision Required:** Final "Go/No-Go" based on beta success and 100% consent verification rate.

**Specific First Tasks for the Team**
*   **Legal Team:** Deliver a written memo confirming the consent form language meets all state-specific requirements by end of day tomorrow.
*   **Dev Lead:** Architect the database schema to store consent records immutably and draft the API logic for the global GPS kill-switch.
*   **Operations:** Begin drafting the communication plan to educate the 200 aides on *why* they must provide consent before accessing the app, framing it as a legal protection for them.

**Critical Founder Decisions**
1.  **Approval of Consent Workflow:** You must explicitly approve the method of capturing consent.
2.  **Authorization of Beta:** You must authorize the beta test only after legal certification is complete.
3.  **Launch Authorization:** You must confirm that 100% of the 200 day-one users have verified consent records before the January 10th switch is flipped.

**Note:** Do not attempt the beta test before the legal review; the risk of creating a record of non-compliance outweighs the benefit of early technical feedback. The manual override switch is mandatory infrastructure, not an optional feature.
