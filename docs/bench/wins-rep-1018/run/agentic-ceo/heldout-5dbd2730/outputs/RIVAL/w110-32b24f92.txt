### Northwind Home: White-Glove Delivery Launch Plan

**1. Optimal Order of Operations (6-Week Timeline)**
*   **Week 1: Capacity & Constraint Validation.** Quantify the exact throughput loss from manual overrides and stress-test the five coordinators against projected volume.
*   **Week 2: Financial Modeling & Risk Assessment.** Map the 40,000 credit budget against the 30% efficiency penalty to determine break-even volumes.
*   **Week 3: Contract Definition.** Draft the exclusive transport agreement terms based on validated capacity data.
*   **Week 4: Go/No-Go Decision Point.** Final review of metrics; sign contract only if criteria are met.
*   **Week 5: Operational Dry Run.** Execute a simulated launch with the team to refine manual override protocols.
*   **Week 6: Soft Launch & Marketing.** Begin limited service offering to gauge real-world response before the March boom.

**2. Initial Lead Expert**
The **Operations Analyst** must take the lead immediately. Before any contracts are signed or marketing begins, the physical reality of the warehouse bottleneck (the 30% slowdown) must be quantified. A strategic planner or sales lead cannot accurately assess the viability of the service without hard data on how the automation limitation impacts the five coordinators' ability to fulfill orders within the budget.

**3. Go/No-Go Criteria for the Transport Contract**
You must define these three specific metrics before signing the irreversible 12-month agreement:
*   **Maximum Sustainable Volume:** The exact number of premium units per week the team can process *including* the 30% manual override delay without exceeding labor budgets.
*   **Cost-Per-Unit Threshold:** The maximum delivery cost per item that allows profitability given the reduced throughput efficiency.
*   **Penalty Exit Clause Feasibility:** A legal confirmation of whether any performance-based exit clauses can be negotiated; if the contract is strictly "locked" with no out, the volume projections must have a 20% safety margin below the team's maximum calculated capacity.

**4. Immediate First Task**
Execute a **"Manual Override Simulation"** within the next 48 hours. Have the five coordinators process a batch of 20 mock premium furniture orders using the current warehouse system. Measure the exact time delay introduced by the manual override for each item and calculate the total labor hours consumed. This data will validate whether the 30% slowdown assumption is accurate or optimistic, forming the foundation for all subsequent budgeting and contracting decisions.

*Note: Do not approach the transport fleet until the simulation data confirms your team can handle the projected volume within the 40,000 credit cap.*
*The March deadline is fixed; any delay in validating the warehouse bottleneck risks signing a contract you cannot fulfill.*
