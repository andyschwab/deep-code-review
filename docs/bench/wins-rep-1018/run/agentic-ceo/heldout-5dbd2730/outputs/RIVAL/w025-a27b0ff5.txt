**Recommended Action Sequence for Veridian Health Outbreak Model**

**Phase 1: Immediate Data Triage (Execute Immediately by Data Team)**
Before any simulation or communication drafting, the integrity of the input data must be addressed to prevent garbage-in-garbage-out scenarios.
1.  **Isolate Unverified Records:** The team must immediately flag and segregate the specific data points originating from unverified private clinics within the northern district dataset.
2.  **Run Dual-Path Simulations:** Execute two parallel model runs:
    *   *Run A:* Includes all current data (status quo).
    *   *Run B:* Excludes the unverified private clinic reports (sanitized inputs).
3.  **Confidence Delta Analysis:** Compare the prediction confidence levels between Run A and Run B. Determine if the 88% confidence score collapses significantly without the unverified data.

**Phase 2: Leadership Review & Approval (Requires Your Direct Approval)**
Once the dual-path simulation results are generated, you must intervene before proceeding further.
1.  **Review Risk Thresholds:** Assess whether the prediction in Run B still warrants concern. If the threat disappears in Run B, the risk of false alarm is confirmed.
2.  **Authorize Audit Scope:** Based on the delta, approve the scope of a rapid manual audit. If the threat remains high in Run B, authorize a targeted audit of only the high-impact verified sources. If the threat vanishes, authorize a full hold on alert generation pending full verification.
3.  **Greenlight Communication Drafting:** Only after reviewing the simulation delta should you authorize the drafting of communication strategies. The content of the communication depends entirely on whether the threat persists in the sanitized model.

**Phase 3: Strategic Execution (Team Execution Post-Approval)**
1.  **Targeted Manual Audit:** The team executes the specific audit scope you approved, focusing on verifying the private clinic reports or confirming the trend via alternative verified sources (e.g., public hospital admissions, pharmacy sales data).
2.  **Draft Communication Strategy:**
    *   *Scenario High Risk (Sanitized):* Draft an urgent but measured alert for the Ministry, highlighting the verified trend.
    *   *Scenario Low Risk (Sanitized):* Draft a briefing note explaining the data anomaly and the decision to withhold alert to prevent economic disruption.
3.  **Final System Lock:** Integrate the audit findings into the final model presentation for the November 14th deadline.

**Summary of Responsibilities**
*   **Team Executes Immediately:** Data segregation, dual-path simulations, confidence delta calculation.
*   **You Must Approve:** The interpretation of the simulation delta, the specific scope of the manual audit, and the final go-ahead to draft external communications.

Do not draft communication strategies or run single-path simulations before understanding the impact of the unverified data. The sequence must be: Isolate Data → Simulate Variants → Leader Decision → Audit/Communicate.

*Note: This sequence prioritizes preventing reputational damage by validating the signal before amplifying it.*
