### Serenity Wellness Center: Digital Migration Planning Agenda

**Session Objective:** Define a zero-loss migration strategy for 5,000 historical appointments and 8 therapist schedules by August 1st.

**Agenda Flow & Stages**

1.  **Data Preparation & Entry (Weeks 1–3)**
    *   **Delegated Tasks:** Administrative staff will manually transcribe physical logs into the vendor's standardized CSV template. This includes client names, contact info, appointment dates/times, therapist assigned, and service codes required for loyalty tracking.
    *   **Validation Loop:** A secondary admin must perform a blind double-entry check on 10% of records daily to ensure accuracy before final compilation.

2.  **Staged Import Testing (Week 4)**
    *   Execute test imports using the vendor tool during the allowed window (Midnight – 4 AM).
    *   Start with a pilot batch of 50 records to verify field mapping and loyalty point calculation.
    *   Proceed to full batch imports in chunks of 500 records, verifying data integrity after each chunk before proceeding to the next.

3.  **Parallel Verification Phase (Week 5)**
    *   Run the digital system alongside physical books for one week.
    *   Confirm that new appointments book correctly and historical loyalty points reflect accurately for a sample of active clients.

4.  **Final Authorization & Archiving (Week 6)**
    *   **Required Authorization:** You must provide written sign-off only after verifying three specific conditions: (1) 100% of the 5,000 records appear in the digital system with correct loyalty totals; (2) The 8 therapists confirm their upcoming schedules are accurate; (3) A full, encrypted backup of the digital database is stored on a separate, offline drive.
    *   **Action:** Once signed, physical books are scanned for digital image backup, then immediately shredded to meet privacy protocols.

**First Concrete Action for Data Integrity**
Before any data entry begins, you must generate and lock a "Golden Master" spreadsheet template with strict data validation rules (e.g., date formats, therapist ID drop-downs, required fields). This prevents administrative staff from entering inconsistent data that the vendor's import tool might reject or corrupt during the midnight upload window.

***

*Note: Ensure the vendor's import tool is tested for timeout limits during the 4-hour window to prevent partial uploads. All admin staff handling data must sign confidentiality agreements prior to accessing physical logs.*
