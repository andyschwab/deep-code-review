**Vendor Scorecard Implementation Work Session Structure**

**Session Objective:** Determine the critical path for deploying the vendor scorecard system before the November 15th renewal deadline while mitigating the risk of premature supplier conflict due to data inaccuracies.

**Recommended Sequence of Operations**
You must **define the new weighting criteria first**, then clean the historical data.
*   **Reasoning:** Data cleaning is dependent on the specific metrics selected. Cleaning five years of data without knowing which variables (e.g., on-time delivery vs. cost variance) will be weighted heavily in the new formula risks wasting analyst hours on irrelevant data points or missing critical anomalies in the metrics that matter most. Defining the formula first acts as the filter for the cleaning process.

**Decision Authority Matrix**

| Decision Area | Requires Your Direct Approval | Can Be Handed Off to Analysts |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Weighting Formula** | • Final percentage allocation per metric (e.g., 40% Quality, 30% Cost).<br>• Definition of "critical failure" thresholds that trigger automatic penalties.<br>• Selection of which historical years carry the most weight (recency bias). | • Statistical correlation analysis of past metrics.<br>• Drafting proposed weight scenarios based on industry benchmarks.<br>• Testing formula sensitivity against historical data samples. |
| **Data Integrity** | • Acceptable error margin thresholds before publishing scores.<br>• Protocol for handling missing data points (exclude vs. impute). | • Executing the actual data cleaning and normalization.<br>• Identifying outliers and flagging anomalies for review.<br>• Running validation tests on the cleaned dataset. |
| **Risk Mitigation** | • Final go/no-go decision on publishing scores to the dashboard.<br>• Communication strategy for suppliers disputing their scores. | • Creating a "shadow mode" report to verify scores internally before dashboard integration.<br>• Drafting FAQ documents for internal stakeholders. |

**Immediate Next Step**
Convene a 90-minute kickoff meeting with the four analysts **today** to draft three distinct weighting scenario models. Do not touch the historical data yet. The output of this meeting must be a one-page proposal outlining three different weighting strategies (e.g., "Cost-Heavy," "Reliability-Heavy," "Balanced") for your review. Once you select the preferred strategy, the team can immediately begin cleaning only the specific data columns required for that model.

**Note:** This approach prevents the team from cleaning unnecessary data and ensures the weighting logic aligns with your strategic goals before technical execution begins.
**Note:** Given the six-week timeline, delay any dashboard integration until the "shadow mode" validation confirms 100% data accuracy to prevent premature supplier conflicts.
