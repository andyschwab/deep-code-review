**Planning Session Structure: Riverbend Diabetes Prevention Portal**

**1. Immediate Priority: Data Security Auditing & Migration Strategy**
Do not begin with user interface prototyping or marketing material creation. The irreversible migration of 5,000 paper records represents the single point of failure for this project. If this data is corrupted, historical health trends are lost permanently, rendering the portal useless regardless of its design or enrollment numbers. You must validate the migration protocol before any visual or promotional work begins.

**2. Workflow Ownership and Decision Rights**

*   **Team-Owned (Execution & Recommendations):**
    *   **Design Team (3 internal):** Responsible for drafting UI wireframes and user flow diagrams *after* data requirements are locked. They will propose the visual structure but cannot finalize it until data fields are confirmed.
    *   **External Contractors:** Tasked with executing the technical build of the database schema and the initial digitization script. They must provide the risk assessment report for the data migration.
    *   **Joint Output:** The team will produce a "Migration Safety Protocol" draft and a preliminary timeline for the 200-participant enrollment target.

*   **Product Manager Final Calls (Your Authority):**
    *   **Migration Sign-off:** You must personally approve the final data migration strategy and the backup verification process before a single record is moved. This is non-delegable due to the irreversible risk.
    *   **Budget Allocation:** With the $120,000 grant requiring full utilization by year-end, you must approve the spending schedule to ensure contractor payments and resource allocation align with the December 1st deadline.
    *   **Scope Trade-offs:** If the data audit reveals complexities that threaten the timeline, you alone decide whether to reduce features or request a timeline extension (though the grant deadline is fixed).

**3. Immediate First Step for This Session**
Initiate a **"Pre-Migration Risk Walkthrough."** Dedicate the first 45 minutes of this session to having the external contractors map out the exact steps of the paper-to-digital transfer on a whiteboard. Identify every potential point of data loss or corruption. Do not discuss colors, logos, or ad copy until this map is complete and every risk has a documented mitigation strategy. Only once the team agrees that the data is safe should you pivot to discussing the user interface or marketing launch.

**Note:** This approach prioritizes asset preservation over speed, ensuring the grant is not wasted on a flawed foundation.
**Note:** All subsequent planning phases (UI, Marketing) are contingent on the successful completion of this risk walkthrough.
