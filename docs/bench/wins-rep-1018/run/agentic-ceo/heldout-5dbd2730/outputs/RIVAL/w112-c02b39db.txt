### SwiftStock Voice-Picking Migration Kickoff Plan

**Phase 1: Assessment & Gap Analysis (Weeks 1-2)**
Map the existing 20% network dead zones against high-traffic picking paths. Validate the six technicians' familiarity with the new hardware frequency requirements. Quantify the exact volume of outbound shipping at risk during the transition window.

**Phase 2: Pilot & Network Remediation (Weeks 3-5)**
Deploy new devices in a controlled zone within one distribution center. Install temporary network extenders or access points to cover the identified 20% gap in critical areas only. Test the "point of no return" configuration wipe on a non-production batch to verify data backup integrity.

**Phase 3: Staged Rollout (Weeks 6-7)**
Migrate Distribution Center A fully. While monitoring stability, begin DC B. Keep DC C on the legacy system as a fallback buffer until DC A and B are stable. Train staff on workaround procedures for the remaining 20% uncovered areas if permanent fixes are delayed.

**Phase 4: Go-Live & Audit Prep (Week 8)**
Complete migration across all three centers. Conduct a full stress test simulating peak audit inventory counts. Finalize documentation for the March 15th audit.

**Critical Go/No-Go Decisions**
1.  **Network Coverage Threshold:** Do not proceed to full rollout if critical high-volume picking lanes remain in the 20% uncovered zone without a verified temporary workaround (e.g., portable hotspots or manual paper-backup protocols). **Decision Point:** If temporary coverage cannot guarantee 99% uptime in high-traffic zones by Week 4, halt rollout and reallocate budget to emergency network infrastructure.
2.  **Configuration Wipe Authorization:** Do not authorize the irreversible wipe of old device configurations until the pilot phase proves the new system can handle at least 4 hours of continuous peak load without failure. **Decision Point:** If the pilot failure rate exceeds 1%, delay the wipe and revert to root-cause analysis before touching production devices.

**First Concrete Action**
Immediately dispatch two technicians to physically walk the floor of the largest distribution center with a spectrum analyzer to map the exact coordinates of the 20% network gap, overlaying this data with the top 50 highest-velocity SKU locations. This data is required before any budget is spent or schedules are finalized.

*Note: The 80% network coverage constraint is the single biggest threat to the March 15th deadline; prioritize this over hardware configuration.*
