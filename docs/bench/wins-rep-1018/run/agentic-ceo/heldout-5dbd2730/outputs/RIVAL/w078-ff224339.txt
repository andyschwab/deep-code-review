**Last-Mile Carrier Integration & Negotiation Workflow**

**Phase 1: Technical Validation (Immediate Action)**
Before any legal review or contract discussion, you must validate the carrier's technical capacity.
*   **First Concrete Action:** Request immediate access to the carrier's staging environment to execute a load test simulating 80,000 requests per day (providing a 6.6% buffer over your 75k peak). Do not proceed to legal review until this test confirms latency stays under 200ms and error rates remain below 0.1%.

**Phase 2: Parallel Legal & Financial Review**
Once technical capacity is proven, initiate parallel tracks for legal and financial alignment.
*   **Financial Terms Requiring Your Direct Approval:**
    *   The $50,000 non-refundable deposit trigger (specifically tying the payment date to *successful* DNS cutover verification, not just signature).
    *   Penalty clauses for downtime exceeding 99.9% availability during the peak season.
    *   Volume tier pricing adjustments if daily packages exceed 85,000.
*   **Legal Team Authorized Finalization:**
    *   Indemnification language and liability caps.
    *   Data privacy compliance (GDPR/CCPA) wording.
    *   Standard termination notice periods and dispute resolution mechanisms.
    *   Service Level Agreement (SLA) definitions, provided they match your financial penalty requirements.

**Phase 3: Integration & Staging**
*   Update tracking APIs in a isolated staging environment.
*   Perform end-to-end testing with real package IDs but routed to the carrier's sandbox.
*   Conduct a "dry run" DNS propagation test using a low-traffic internal subdomain to verify resolution without impacting customers.

**Phase 4: Final Sign-off & Execution**
*   **Go/No-Go Meeting:** Scheduled for October 25th (one week prior to hard deadline).
*   **Condition for Signature:** All technical tests passed, financial terms approved by you, and legal redlines closed.
*   **Execution Sequence:**
    1.  Digital signature of the contract.
    2.  Release of the $50,000 deposit (only if contract ties release to post-signature provisioning confirmation).
    3.  Carrier confirms backend provisioning complete.
    4.  **Irreversible Step:** Flip DNS records to point to new carrier servers.
    5.  Monitor real-time error rates for 1 hour.

**Critical Constraints & Deadlines**
*   **Hard Go-Live:** November 1st.
*   **Latest Safe DNS Flip:** October 28th (allows 3 days for rollback if catastrophic failure occurs before peak season).
*   **Risk Mitigation:** Maintain the old carrier's DNS records as a low-priority fallback for 48 hours post-cutover.

**Notes**
The $50,000 deposit must be contractually contingent on the carrier passing your initial load test; do not pay it upon signature alone.
If the load test fails, halt all negotiations immediately to avoid sinking costs into an unviable technical partner.
