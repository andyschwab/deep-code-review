### Session Roadmap: Package Dimensioning System Deployment

**Phase 1: Mechanical Fit & Calibration (Hours 0–2.5)**
*   **0:00–0:30:** Verify conveyor belt speed stability and mount laser scanner brackets. Perform dry-fit alignment without power.
*   **0:30–1:30:** Power up scanners and run static calibration using certified reference cubes. Adjust laser angles to match belt velocity vectors.
*   **1:30–2:30:** Execute dynamic calibration using test parcels at full belt speed. Iterate adjustments until measurement variance is within tolerance.
*   **2:30–2:45:** **CRITICAL GATE:** Final verification of calibration data. Once confirmed, seal the mechanical housing. This step is irreversible without a full facility shutdown ($10k/hr cost).
*   **2:45–3:00:** Restart conveyor line and secure cabling.

**Phase 2: Software Integration (Hours 3:00–3:30)**
*   Map scanner output fields to the Warehouse Management System (WMS).
*   Validate that dimensional weight calculations trigger the correct carrier rate brackets in the staging environment.
*   Confirm error-handling protocols for unreadable barcodes or anomalous dimensions.

**Phase 3: Live Volume Testing (Hours 3:30–4:00)**
*   Run a controlled batch of 500 mixed-parcel types through the line.
*   Compare system-calculated rates against manual verification for a 10% sample.
*   Sign off on go-live status before the installation window closes.

### Accuracy Thresholds for Approval
You must personally approve the following before the housing is sealed:
1.  **Dimensional Variance:** Measurements must remain within ±0.25 inches of certified reference standards across three consecutive dynamic runs.
2.  **Rate Bracket Accuracy:** 100% of test parcels must calculate to the correct shipping bracket; any deviation triggering a "next highest bracket" charge is unacceptable given the $35k monthly risk.
3.  **Throughput Sync:** The scanner must maintain accuracy at 110% of maximum rated belt speed to ensure stability during peak surges.

### Decision Authority: Warehouse Supervisors
Supervisors are authorized to decide on:
*   **Operational Pacing:** Adjusting the feed rate of parcels during the testing phase to prevent jams, provided the belt speed remains constant for calibration validity.
*   **Parcel Selection:** Choosing which specific SKU mix constitutes the 500-parcel live test batch, ensuring it represents typical daily volume.
*   **Immediate Halt:** Stopping the test run immediately if physical safety hazards or equipment collisions are observed. They *cannot* authorize recalibration or housing unsealing.

### First Measurement to Verify
**Verify the exact conveyor belt speed in feet per minute (FPM) right now.**
The laser scanner calibration algorithms are directly dependent on this velocity vector. If the belt speed fluctuates or differs from the specification used in the software configuration, all subsequent optical calibration will be invalid, leading to immediate failure upon sealing the housing.

*Note: Ensure certified reference cubes are physically present on the line before starting Phase 1.*
*Do not proceed to housing sealing without written sign-off on the dynamic calibration results.*
