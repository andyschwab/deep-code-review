### Launch Work Session Structure

**1. Pre-Launch Prerequisite (The Very First Task)**
Before enabling the public link or sending any notifications, you must **execute a dry-run simulation with all eight counselor accounts logged in simultaneously.** This validates the "50 concurrent sessions" limit against your actual hardware/network setup and confirms that the routing logic correctly distributes test messages without exceeding the per-account cap. Do not proceed until this technical ceiling is proven safe under load.

**2. Counselor Account Activation Sequence**
Activate accounts in **staggered waves** rather than all at once to monitor stability:
*   **Wave A (30% Capacity):** Activate 3 accounts immediately upon dry-run success. These handle the initial influx of the first ~150 sign-ups.
*   **Wave B (Remaining Capacity):** Activate the remaining 5 accounts only when Wave A counselors reach 40 concurrent sessions each (leaving a 10-session buffer per account).
*   **Rotation Protocol:** Implement a hard rule where no single account exceeds 45 concurrent sessions. If a counselor hits this limit, they enter a "cool-down" status for 15 minutes while new chats route to available accounts.

**3. Routing Logic: Automated vs. Manual**
*   **Automated Routing (Default):** Use a "Least-Loaded" algorithm. The system must automatically assign incoming chats to the counselor with the fewest active sessions. This is critical for meeting the 15-minute response SLA with 500 sign-ups. Manual assignment is too slow for initial triage.
*   **Manual Assignment (Exception Only):** Counselors may manually claim a chat only if:
    *   The automated system flags a "high-risk" keyword (e.g., "relapse," "severe withdrawal").
    *   A patient explicitly requests a specific counselor they have spoken to before.

**4. Escalation Thresholds Requiring Direct Approval**
You must personally approve any action that alters the standard care protocol or exceeds operational limits:
*   **Wait-Time Breach:** If the average wait time exceeds 12 minutes (approaching the 15-minute hard limit), you must approve the temporary pause on new sign-ups or the activation of emergency backup staff.
*   **Clinical Deviation:** Any response deviating from the pre-approved script for high-risk patients (e.g., advising medical intervention beyond standard cessation support).
*   **Platform Instability:** If error rates on the chat platform exceed 2%, you must approve a full halt of the welcome push notification campaign.

**5. The Irreversible Action Checkpoint**
The welcome push notification to the 2,000 pilot patients is the point of no return. Establish a **"Go/No-Go" gate** immediately preceding this send:
*   Confirm Wave A accounts are active and stable.
*   Verify the automated router is functioning.
*   Confirm all counselors are logged in and at "Ready" status.
*   **Only then** authorize the push notification.
