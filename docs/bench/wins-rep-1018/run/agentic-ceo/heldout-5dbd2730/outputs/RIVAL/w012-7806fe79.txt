### Rollout Strategy: Phased Deployment Plan

To meet the 72-hour window while adhering to the <0.1% failure tolerance (max 10 failures), we will execute a four-phase "Canary-to-General" rollout. This approach limits exposure at every step, allowing an immediate halt if anomalies appear.

**Phase 1: Internal & Synthetic Validation (Hours 0–4)**
*   **Target:** 50 devices (0.5% of tolerance cap).
*   **Action:** Deploy to engineering team benches and a controlled lab environment simulating network instability.
*   **Go/No-Go Criteria:** 100% success rate required. Any failure triggers an immediate code freeze and root cause analysis.

**Phase 2: Ultra-Low Risk Canary (Hours 4–12)**
*   **Target:** 500 devices (5% of tolerance cap).
*   **Selection Criteria:** Devices with strong signal strength, recent successful update history, and located in regions with immediate field support availability.
*   **Action:** Push update to this segment.
*   **Go/No-Go Criteria:** Zero bricked devices allowed. Max 1 transient error permitted (must resolve on retry). If >0 devices brick, halt immediately.

**Phase 3: Expanded Regional Rollout (Hours 12–36)**
*   **Target:** 4,500 devices (45% of total).
*   **Selection Criteria:** Geographic segmentation (e.g., one specific time zone) to align with support team waking hours.
*   **Action:** Deploy in batches of 500 every 2 hours.
*   **Go/No-Go Criteria:** Cumulative failures must remain <5. If the count hits 5, pause and assess before proceeding to the next batch.

**Phase 4: Global Completion (Hours 36–60)**
*   **Target:** Remaining 5,000 devices.
*   **Action:** Deploy in larger batches of 1,000 every 4 hours.
*   **Buffer:** Hours 60–72 reserved exclusively for emergency remediation or manual interventions if Phase 3 encountered minor friction.

### Required Risk Mitigation Approvals

You must explicitly approve the following three protocols before Phase 1 begins:

1.  **The "Hard Stop" Threshold:** Authorization for the lead engineer to unilaterally abort the entire rollout if *any* device bricks in Phase 1 or Phase 2, or if cumulative failures reach 6 devices (60% of tolerance) in Phase 3. No escalation meetings allowed; the kill switch is automatic.
2.  **Physical Response Teams:** Pre-approval to dispatch field technicians to the specific geographic zones of Phase 2 and 3 immediately upon failure detection. Since devices cannot be recovered remotely, a "swap-out" protocol must be active and funded now.
3.  **Patient Communication Template:** Approval of a pre-drafted notification to be sent within 15 minutes of a confirmed failure, instructing patients on how to identify a bricked device and assuring them of immediate replacement, to maintain trust and compliance.

### First Monitoring Dashboard Specification

Build a real-time dashboard displaying only these four critical metrics, refreshed every 60 seconds:

1.  **Brick Rate (Critical):** Count of devices that failed to boot post-update. **Alert Threshold:** >0.
2.  **Rollback/Retry Success Rate:** Percentage of devices that failed the initial push but succeeded on automatic retry. **Alert Threshold:** <95%.
3.  **Deployment Velocity:** Number of devices successfully updated per hour vs. the planned schedule.
4.  **Geographic Failure Heatmap:** Visual plot of any failures by region to identify localized infrastructure issues (e.g., a specific cell tower causing packet loss).

***

*Note: This plan assumes your telemetry pipeline can report device status within 5 minutes of an update attempt. Ensure your engineering team validates the "kill switch" mechanism in Phase 1.*
