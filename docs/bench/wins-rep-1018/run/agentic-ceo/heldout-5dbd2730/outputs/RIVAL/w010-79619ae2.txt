### Immediate Planning Structure: Audit-First Protocol

**1. Primary Approach: Reverse-Engineered Compliance Mapping**
Do not begin with feature development or user testing. Your first approach must be a "Reverse-Engineered Compliance Map." Start with the specific regulatory statutes applicable to mental health data in your jurisdiction (e.g., HIPAA, GDPR, local mental health acts) and map every single required control directly to your current system architecture. Create a binary matrix: if a regulation requires "data minimization," mark exactly where in your pipeline this is enforced. If it cannot be marked, it is a critical failure point. This audit trail creation is not a documentation task; it is the architectural blueprint for the remaining 10 days. Every decision made from this moment must be logged with a timestamp, the decision-maker, and the specific regulatory justification to satisfy the legal review.

**2. Specific Compliance Gaps Requiring Personal Resolution**
As the leader, you must personally resolve three high-liability gaps that automation cannot fix:
*   **The "Irreversible Weight" Liability:** Since publishing training weights is irreversible, you must personally sign off on a "Static Risk Assessment." This document must certify that the current weights contain no biased outputs regarding self-harm or crisis intervention that could violate duty-of-care laws. You must verify that the "stop sequences" and hard-coded safety rails are decoupled from the weights so they can be patched without re-certification.
*   **Data Sovereignty & Segregation:** You must confirm exactly where the 500 participants' data will reside during testing. If the model processes data in a way that mixes test inputs with training logs, you are violating privacy protocols before the audit even begins. You must mandate a "clean room" environment where test data is ephemeral and never touches the production weight storage.
*   **The Human-in-the-Loop Escalation Path:** Regulations for crisis chatbots almost universally require a verified handoff to human care when risk thresholds are met. You must personally validate the latency and reliability of this handoff. If the bot delays a human connection during a simulated crisis, the system is legally non-compliant regardless of code quality.

**3. Immediate Next Step: The Data Handling Lockdown**
Before feature freezing begins, you must execute a "Data Flow Interdiction." Halt all current data pipelines. Implement a temporary, read-only mirror of your production environment for the 500-user test group. Ensure that no data written by these users can flow back into any logging system that touches the core training weights or the live audit trail until the legal team signs off on the data schema. Generate a cryptographic hash of the current database state to prove to auditors that no unvetted data has contaminated the pre-launch system. Only once this isolation is verified should you announce the feature freeze.
