### NutriFlow Launch Work Session Plan (10:00 AM – 12:00 PM)

**Critical Action for the Next 10 Minutes (10:00 – 10:10 AM)**
Immediately halt any automatic background sync processes and manually verify the integrity of the current production database snapshot against the latest allergy master list. Do not push the update yet. Confirm that the 15 kitchen staff accounts are pre-registered and that the system can authenticate at least 20 devices simultaneously in a isolated test environment before touching the live tablets.

**Order of Operations**

1.  **10:10 AM – 10:40 AM: Staged Connectivity & Load Simulation**
    *   Connect exactly 20 tablets to the kitchen Wi-Fi to simulate peak load.
    *   Attempt a dummy data sync for 50 patient trays focusing strictly on allergy flags and calorie totals.
    *   If latency exceeds 5 seconds or connections drop, reduce the concurrent sync batch size in the configuration immediately. Do not proceed until 20 devices can sustain a connection for 10 minutes without failure.

2.  **10:40 AM – 11:15 AM: QA Specialist Verification Protocol**
    Before the final go-ahead, the QA specialist must successfully execute and sign off on these three specific tests:
    *   **The "Allergy Hard-Stop" Test:** Input a tray order containing a known severe allergen for a specific patient ID. Verify the system explicitly blocks the tray confirmation and alerts the user. A silent failure here is a launch abort.
    *   **The "Offline Resilience" Test:** Disconnect a tablet from Wi-Fi mid-sync. Ensure the app caches the data locally without crashing and correctly queues the upload for when connectivity is restored.
    *   **The "Rollback Dry-Run" Test:** Simulate a failed push on a single non-critical tablet. Verify the team can manually revert that device to the previous stable version within 3 minutes.

3.  **11:15 AM – 11:45 AM: Phased Production Push**
    *   **Batch A (11:15 AM):** Push the update to 5 tablets used by head chefs only. Monitor for 15 minutes.
    *   **Batch B (11:30 AM):** If Batch A is stable, push to the remaining 10 tablets.
    *   **Verification:** Have staff visually confirm that the patient count matches 300 and that the "Lunch Service" mode is active.

4.  **11:45 AM – 12:00 PM: Final Safety Buffer**
    *   Stand down from technical updates.
    *   Kitchen lead performs a visual spot-check of 5 random digital trays against printed dietary sheets (if available) or verbal confirmation with nursing staff for high-risk patients.
    *   Declare system "Go" for meal service initiation.

**Contingency Protocol**
If the database sync fails at any point before 11:30 AM, abort the digital launch immediately. Activate the pre-defined manual protocol: distribute printed allergy lists generated from the *last known good backup* to the head chef and station leads. Do not attempt to fix the software during the lunch window; prioritize feeding patients safely using analog methods over a partially functioning digital system.

***

*Note: This plan assumes the "printed allergy lists" mentioned in the contingency are available from yesterday's data; if not, verbal verification with nursing stations becomes the primary backup.*
*Strict adherence to the 20-device Wi-Fi limit is mandatory; exceeding this will likely cause a total network collapse.*
