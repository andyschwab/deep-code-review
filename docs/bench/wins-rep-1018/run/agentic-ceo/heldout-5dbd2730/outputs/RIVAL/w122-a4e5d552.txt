### MedCold Logistics Strategy Session Agenda

**1. Critical Data Validation (Pre-Meeting Requirement)**
Before the session begins, the following data points must be aggregated to validate feasibility:
*   **Zone Gap Analysis:** A precise map comparing the current 12% coverage against the specific locations of the three contracted hospitals and the remaining 88% of target zones.
*   **Fleet Capacity Modeling:** Calculation of maximum daily delivery radius per van versus the total square mileage required to hit 90% coverage with only 25 units.
*   **Lease Liability Breakdown:** The exact monthly cost of the central hub multiplied by 60 months, contrasted against the $200,000 penalty risk if coverage targets are missed.
*   **Time-to-Operationalize:** The lead time required to recruit/train additional route planners and acquire/lease additional specialized vans if the current fleet is insufficient.

**2. Decisions Requiring Founder Direct Approval**
Your direct sign-off is mandatory on only one critical path item during this session:
*   **The "Go/No-Go" Trigger for the Hub Lease:** You must define the specific coverage percentage or contractual milestone that justifies signing the five-year lease. Do not approve the lease based on hope; approve it only if the data confirms the current fleet and team can physically achieve 90% coverage by November 30th *without* the hub, or if the hub is the single bottleneck preventing it. All other operational adjustments (shift changes, temporary staffing) should be delegated.

**3. First Hour Structure: Risk Mititation Focus**
Do not start with brainstorming expansion ideas. Structure the first 60 minutes strictly as follows:

*   **Minutes 0–15: The Hard Constraint Review.**
    Present the fleet capacity math. If 25 vans cannot physically cover 90% of the metro area by November 30th regardless of the hub location, the expansion plan is impossible without immediate asset acquisition. Stop all other planning if this math fails.

*   **Minutes 15–30: The Lease Stress Test.**
    Analyze the worst-case scenario: Signing the lease, failing to hit 90% coverage, and incurring both the lease burden and the $200,000 penalty. Determine if the company survives this financial hit. If not, the lease cannot be signed until coverage is proven via alternative means (e.g., third-party logistics partners).

*   **Minutes 30–45: The "Stop-Loss" Definition.**
    Establish the exact date by which you must see specific coverage metrics achieved. If these metrics are not met by this date, the lease signing is automatically cancelled, and a contingency plan (such as partnering with existing cold-chain providers) is activated immediately.

*   **Minutes 45–60: Resource Allocation for Validation.**
    Assign the eight route planners exclusively to stress-test the coverage model for the next 48 hours. No new hiring or marketing discussions occur until this validation is complete.

**Note:** Do not sign the lease until the fleet capacity math proves 90% coverage is physically possible.
**Note:** Prioritize avoiding the double-loss scenario (lease costs + penalties) over speed of expansion.
