**SwiftSend Cross-Border Remittance Strategy Session Plan**

**Core Decision:** Start by **updating the compliance framework**. Re-engineering currency logic before confirming regulatory interpretations risks building features that may be illegal or require immediate rework. Compliance defines the constraints; engineering executes within them.

**Sequence of Stages:**

1.  **Regulatory Triage (Days 1-3):** Deploy the four legal consultants to dissect the new requirements for Brazil, India, and Nigeria. They must produce a "Gap Analysis Report" identifying exactly which current practices violate new rules and what specific data points or calculation methods are now mandated.
2.  **Framework Update & Validation (Days 4-7):** Product Managers and Legal collaborate to rewrite the compliance framework based on the Gap Analysis. This document becomes the single source of truth for what the system *must* do.
3.  **Founder Decision Gate (Day 8):** You must review and approve the interpreted regulatory requirements, specifically where laws are ambiguous or offer multiple compliance paths. Your sign-off here prevents the six-month ban risk.
4.  **Technical Specification (Days 9-12):** Engineers translate the approved compliance framework into technical specifications for the currency calculation logic. No code is written until this spec is frozen.
5.  **Parallel Execution (Days 13-45):**
    *   *Track A:* Engineers re-engineer the currency logic.
    *   *Track B:* Legal prepares license applications using the updated framework data.
6.  **Internal Audit & Dry Run (Days 46-50):** Simulate the license application process using the new logic outputs to ensure zero discrepancies before official submission.
7.  **Final Submission & Launch Prep (Days 51-60):** File applications immediately upon internal audit success. Proceed with launch preparations contingent on filing receipt.

**Regulatory Interpretations Requiring Your Final Decision:**
*   **Ambiguous Definitions:** Where Brazil, India, or Nigeria use vague terms (e.g., "fair market value" vs. "interbank rate") that allow for multiple compliant interpretations.
*   **Risk Appetite:** If a requirement creates a conflict between speed and strict adherence (e.g., a temporary waiver process vs. full immediate compliance), you must decide the acceptable risk level.
*   **Data Residency:** If new rules conflict regarding where transaction data must be stored, you must choose the architectural compromise.

**First Concrete Step:**
Convene an emergency 2-hour working session tomorrow morning with the four legal consultants and the lead Product Manager. The sole output of this meeting must be the **Scope of Work for the Gap Analysis Report**, assigning each consultant a specific country and setting a hard deadline for their findings 48 hours later. Do not involve engineers until the legal scope is defined.

*Note: This plan prioritizes avoiding the six-month ban over speed of development, as a delayed launch is preferable to a banned operation.*
