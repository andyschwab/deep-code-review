### Deprecation Planning Session Structure

**1. Order of Operations**
1.  **Risk Assessment & Segmentation:** Map the five partners to their specific usage patterns, transaction volumes, and known internal change-management cycles.
2.  **Communication Strategy Design:** Draft the notification hierarchy (executive-to-executive vs. technical-to-technical) and the timeline for escalating urgency.
3.  **Technical Safeguard Implementation:** Design the "soft fail" mechanisms (logging, warning headers, traffic mirroring) that precede any hard shutdown.
4.  **Migration Support Protocol:** Establish a dedicated "tiger team" workflow to assist partners with testing and validation on the new API version.
5.  **Contingency Planning:** Define the specific criteria and procedure for a "circuit breaker" rollback if critical failures occur post-sunset.

**2. Strategic Choices (Your Domain) vs. Technical Execution (Team Domain)**

*   **You Must Decide (Strategic):**
    *   **Escalation Path:** Whether to bypass technical contacts and initiate direct C-level outreach immediately for laggard partners.
    *   **Hard vs. Soft Sunset:** Whether to enforce a strict cutoff on June 30th or implement a graded reduction in service quality (throttling) leading up to it.
    *   **Liability Communication:** How explicitly to articulate the legal and financial risks of non-compliance in official correspondence.
    *   **Exception Policy:** Whether to grant temporary extensions to specific partners and under what strict conditions.

*   **Team Executes (Technical):**
    *   **Traffic Analysis:** Mining logs to identify exactly which endpoints each bank uses and their error rates.
    *   **Warning Injection:** Implementing HTTP headers (`Deprecation`, `Sunset`) and response body warnings on all v2 calls.
    *   **Dashboard Creation:** Building real-time visibility into v2 usage per partner to track migration progress.
    *   **Shadow Testing:** Routing copies of v2 traffic to v3 endpoints to validate compatibility without affecting live transactions.
    *   **Rate Limiting:** Configuring gradual throttling rules if a soft sunset is chosen.

**3. First Hour Action Plan (Immediate Risk Mitigation)**

*   **Minutes 0–15: Freeze & Audit.** Halt any non-critical changes to the v2 codebase. Instruct the team to pull the last 30 days of access logs specifically for the five banking partners to establish a baseline of active dependencies.
*   **Minutes 15–30: Draft the "Red Alert" Communication.** Compose the initial notification template. It must explicitly state the June 30th hard stop, the $200M volume at risk, and the immediate requirement for a migration timeline from them. Do not send yet; prepare for your review.
*   **Minutes 30–45: Implement Passive Monitoring.** Direct the team to deploy a non-intrusive logging tag on all v2 requests from these five partners. This ensures that starting *now*, every single call is flagged for later analysis without altering behavior or risking breakage.
*   **Minutes 45–60: Stakeholder Alignment.** Convene a 15-minute stand-up with the eight-person integration team. Assign two people to log analysis, two to the warning header implementation, and four to prepare migration documentation. Confirm that no one touches the "disable" switch until further notice.

**Note:** Do not attempt to contact the banks until you have reviewed the drafted communication and the initial log audit confirms current activity levels.
**Note:** The primary goal of the first hour is visibility and preparation, not enforcement.
