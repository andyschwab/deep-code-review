**2. Strategic Choices: Data Retention vs. Cost**
You must make a binary decision on **Historical Depth vs. Migration Complexity**.
*   **Option A (Full Retention):** Migrate all 24 months of raw data. This maximizes legal protection but increases migration cost, time, and risk of data corruption during transfer. It requires your technical team to write complex transformation scripts.
*   **Option B (Tiered Retention):** Migrate only the last 6 months of raw, high-frequency data (critical for recent claims) and archive the older 18 months as read-only static reports (PDF/CSV) stored locally. This drastically reduces migration risk and cost while maintaining a defensible legal position for older claims via static records.
*   **Recommendation:** Given your team's bandwidth constraints, choose **Option B**. Insurance audits rarely require raw telemetry older than 12 months if summarized reports are available. This reduces the technical burden on your four-person team.
