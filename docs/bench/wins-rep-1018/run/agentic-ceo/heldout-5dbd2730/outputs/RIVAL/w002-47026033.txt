### Critical Care Database Migration: Planning Session Agenda

**Session Objective:** Define the irreversible cutover sequence, assign explicit go/no-go authority, and finalize the "point-of-no-return" contingency protocols for the 30TB patient record migration.

**Proposed Agenda (90 Minutes)**

**1. Risk & Constraint Alignment (15 min)**
*   **Lead:** Chief Medical Information Officer (CMIO)
*   **Focus:** Reconfirm the absolute constraint: Zero tolerance for loss of ER admissions data during the 64-hour window.
*   **Output:** Signed acknowledgment of risk parameters by all eight database specialists and five clinical liaisons.

**2. The Cutover Sequence & Verification Gates (30 min)**
*   **Lead:** Lead Database Architect
*   **Focus:** Map the exact linear order of operations. Identify the specific "Verification Gates" where data integrity must be mathematically proven before proceeding.
*   **Critical Verification Steps (Pre-Cutover):**
    *   **Checksum Validation:** Full 30TB hash comparison between source and target.
    *   **Referential Integrity Scan:** Automated script confirming zero broken links between patient records and clinical notes.
    *   **ER Data Shadowing:** Confirmation that the last 6 hours of ER admissions are replicated in a read-only staging area independent of the main migration stream.
*   **Output:** A minute-by-minute runbook with binary (Pass/Fail) criteria for each gate.

**3. Go/No-Go Authority Matrix (20 min)**
*   **Lead:** Project Director
*   **Focus:** Assign single-point accountability for every milestone. No committee votes; one named owner per decision.
*   **Decision Owners:**
    *   *Pre-Migration Start:* IT Director (Go/No-Go based on backup success).
    *   *Post-Data Transfer:* Lead DB Specialist (Go/No-Go based on checksums).
    *   *Live Traffic Cutover:* CMIO (Go/No-Go based on clinical liaison sign-off).
    *   *Abort Decision:* Incident Commander (Sole authority to trigger rollback or contingency).
*   **Output:** A signed RACI chart specifically for decision authority during the crisis window.

**4. Partial Failure & Contingency Protocols (20 min)**
*   **Lead:** Incident Commander
*   **Focus:** Define the "Partial Failure" state. Since full rollback risks ER data loss, the strategy must shift to "Forward Recovery" or "Hybrid Mode."
*   **Contingency Plans:**
    *   **Scenario A (Migration Halt at 50%):** Activate read-only mode on legacy system for historical data; route all new ER admissions to a temporary, isolated high-availability cluster until the primary target is repaired.
    *   **Scenario B (Data Corruption Detected):** Isolate corrupted blocks; switch traffic to legacy system for unaffected patients while manual reconciliation begins for affected records.
*   **Output:** Approved playbooks for Scenario A and B, including communication templates for clinical staff.

**5. Immediate Next Steps (5 min)**
*   **Focus:** Assign the very first concrete action to ensure readiness before Saturday 2 AM.

**First Concrete Action:**
The Lead Database Architect must execute a **full-scale dry run of the ER data shadowing protocol** on a non-production environment within the next 24 hours to prove that the last 6 hours of admissions can be captured and accessed independently of the main 30TB migration stream. Results must be reviewed by the CMIO before the team disperses.

***

*Note: This agenda prioritizes the protection of ER admissions data above all other migration metrics. All participants must arrive with their specific module's failure modes pre-documented.*
