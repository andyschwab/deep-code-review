**Urgent Generator Failover Testing Workflow**

**Immediate Action (Next 15 Minutes):**
Convene the two senior engineers and the authorizing lead immediately to perform a "pre-mortem" risk assessment. Specifically, verify the current thermal baseline of the main server room and confirm the secondary cooling systems are in a ready state *before* any disconnect occurs. Do not touch physical switches until this baseline is documented and signed off by the authorizer. This is the single most important step to ensure that if the test fails, you have data to justify an emergency abort or mitigation strategy before irreversible overheating begins.

**Order of Operations by Wing:**
Given the three-hour window and the irreversible nature of the manual override, test the wings in ascending order of criticality to contain potential failure:
1.  **Administrative Wing (Lowest Criticality):** Initiate the first failover here. This validates the ten-second engagement rule with the lowest risk to patient care or core infrastructure.
2.  **Outpatient Services Wing:** Proceed only if Unit 1 engages successfully and cools within parameters.
3.  **Emergency Department & ICU (Highest Criticality):** Test these last. If a failure occurs in earlier wings, you preserve the integrity of the life-support systems for the duration of the storm.

**Technician-Led Checks (No Authorization Required):**
The three technicians can immediately execute the following without waiting for the physical disconnect approval:
*   **Visual Inspection:** Verify fuel levels, oil pressure, and coolant levels on all six backup units.
*   **Control Panel Verification:** Confirm that all automatic transfer switches (ATS) are set to "Auto" and that no active fault lights are present on the local interfaces.
*   **Load Bank Preparation:** Ensure load banks are connected and ready to simulate the required electrical load for each wing.
*   **Communication Check:** Establish a dedicated radio channel between the server room, the generator yard, and the command center to ensure instant reporting of the ten-second engagement window.

**Authorizer Intervention Point:**
You, as the sole person authorized to approve the physical disconnect, must intervene **immediately after the technicians complete their pre-checks and before the first manual override is thrown.**
*   **The Moment:** Once the technicians report "Green" on all pre-flight checks, you must physically verify the server room thermal baseline.
*   **The Action:** Only then do you sign the "Initiate Irreversible Cycle" log and verbally authorize the senior engineer to throw the first manual disconnect switch.
*   **Constraint:** Because the cycle cannot be stopped once begun, your authorization is the hard gate. Do not delegate this signature. If the thermal baseline is higher than expected, you must halt the entire operation regardless of the storm timeline, as a failed test under high heat guarantees permanent server damage.

**Execution Timeline:**
*   **T+0 to T+15 mins:** Pre-mortem and thermal baseline verification (Authorizer + Senior Engineers).
*   **T+15 to T+45 mins:** Technician pre-checks and load bank setup.
*   **T+45 mins:** **Authorizer Intervention** (Sign-off).
*   **T+50 to T+140 mins:** Sequential testing of wings (approx. 30 mins per wing including reset and data logging).
*   **T+140 to T+180 mins:** Final system restoration and storm-ready lockdown.

Note: If any unit fails to engage within ten seconds during the Administrative Wing test, abort the sequence for the remaining wings immediately to preserve capacity for the actual storm event.
Ensure all communication logs are timestamped to the second for post-incident analysis.
