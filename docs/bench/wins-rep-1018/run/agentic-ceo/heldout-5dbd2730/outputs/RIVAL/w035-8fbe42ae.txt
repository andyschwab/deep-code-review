### Implementation Session Structure: Riverdale Medical Center Medication Reconciliation Bot

**1. Critical Decision: Override Feature Status**
**Do not enable the override feature immediately.** Given the two near-misses during the pilot involving 25 patients (an 8% error rate), activating irreversible automatic overrides on December 1st poses an unacceptable safety risk. The system must launch in **Advisory Mode** for the first month. In this mode, the bot flags conflicts and suggests corrections, but the human discharge planner retains final authority to accept or reject the recommendation. This builds trust among the 40 planners and creates a safety net while collecting data to refine the logic before enabling automation.

**2. Training Materials Requiring Immediate Approval**
You must approve the following three documents today to ensure staff readiness and safety:
*   **"The Human-in-the-Loop" Protocol:** A step-by-step guide detailing exactly how planners should review bot alerts, verify discrepancies against original physician orders, and document the final decision in Advisory Mode.
*   **Incident Reporting & Escalation Flowchart:** A clear visual guide on what to do when the bot flags a conflict, including immediate contacts for pharmacy verification and the specific form for logging "near-miss" data to improve the algorithm.
*   **Trust-Building Case Studies:** A briefing document containing anonymized examples from the pilot (including the two near-misses) explaining *why* the bot flagged them and how the human planner correctly resolved them, demonstrating the value of the partnership rather than replacement.

**3. Required Stakeholder Sign-offs on Safety Protocol**
Before any live interaction, the following stakeholders must formally sign the safety protocol:
*   **Chief Medical Officer (CMO):** To accept clinical liability and validate that the advisory workflow meets standards of care.
*   **Director of Nursing/Discharge Planning:** To represent the 40 planners, confirming the workflow is practical and does not create unsafe bottlenecks.
*   **Hospital Legal Counsel & Compliance Officer:** To certify that the "human-in-the-loop" process satisfies regulatory requirements and mitigates liability regarding the bot's recommendations.
*   **Chief Information Security Officer (CISO):** To verify data integrity and access controls during the transition from pilot to full deployment.

**4. First Live Simulation Design**
Before touching real patient records, conduct a **"Shadow Discharge" Simulation** with the following parameters:
*   **Participants:** All 40 discharge planners divided into shifts, paired with senior pharmacists.
*   **Scenario:** Use 10 complex, historical patient cases known to have medication conflicts (including the specific scenarios that caused the pilot near-misses).
*   **Process:** Planners process the discharges using the bot in Advisory Mode. The bot will generate alerts; planners must execute the verification protocol.
*   **Success Criteria:** The simulation is only successful if 100% of conflicts are identified by the team, and the documented resolution matches the gold-standard outcome established by the pharmacy team. Any failure to identify a conflict or a procedural error requires an immediate pause and protocol revision.
*   **Output:** A "Go/No-Go" report based on simulation accuracy, not just system uptime.

***

**Note:** The 15% readmission reduction goal should be treated as a secondary metric until the error rate drops below 1%; safety precedes efficiency.
**Note:** Schedule a weekly review of advisory data for the first month to determine the specific date for transitioning to limited override capabilities.
