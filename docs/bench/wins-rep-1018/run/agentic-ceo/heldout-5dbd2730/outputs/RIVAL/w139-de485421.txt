### Northwind Robotics Firmware Deployment Session Plan

**Objective:** Successfully deploy firmware to 12 assembly line robots before the November 15th audit while eliminating the risk of bricking units or disrupting peak production.

#### Phase 1: Safety & Rollback Safeguards (Pre-Build)
*Before any code is touched, the safety net must be woven.*
1.  **Define "Brick" Recovery:** Since flashing is irreversible, establish the physical replacement protocol. Confirm spare robot inventory levels and the estimated time-to-swap for a failed unit.
2.  **Rollback Strategy:** Because the update cannot be reverted via software, the "rollback" is strictly a hardware swap procedure. Document the exact steps to isolate and replace a bricked unit within 30 minutes.
3.  **Safety Lead Review:** Present the recovery protocol and risk assessment to the Safety Lead. Obtain written sign-off that the physical replacement strategy meets all operational safety standards.

#### Phase 2: Update Package Construction
*Only after safety sign-off.*
1.  **Environment Isolation:** Set up a sterile build environment disconnected from the production network.
2.  **Build Execution:** Compile the new firmware version.
3.  **Verification Hashing:** Generate cryptographic hashes (SHA-256) of the build artifact to ensure integrity during transfer.
4.  **Staging Transfer:** Move the verified package to a secure, isolated staging server accessible only by the deployment team.

#### Phase 3: Communication & Scheduling
*Coordinate human elements around the technical constraints.*
1.  **Maintenance Window Announcement:** Notify floor managers of the specific downtime window required. Since peak production tomorrow is off-limits, schedule the deployment for the immediate off-peak window following tomorrow's shift or the upcoming weekend, ensuring completion well before November 15th.
2.  **Team Briefing:** Assign specific roles to the five engineers:
    *   2 Engineers: Flashing operators (one primary, one verifier).
    *   1 Engineer: Monitoring throughput metrics post-update.
    *   1 Engineer: Standby for physical replacement (if bricking occurs).
    *   1 Team Lead: Overall coordination and "Stop Work" authority.

#### Phase 4: Execution (The Deployment Window)
1.  **Pre-Flight Check:** Verify all 12 robots are in a safe, idle state.
2.  **Pilot Update:** Flash **one** robot only. Wait for a full cycle test.
3.  **Validation:** Confirm the pilot robot operates without errors and throughput metrics improve.
4.  **Full Rollout:** If the pilot succeeds, proceed to update the remaining 11 robots in batches of two to manage risk.
5.  **Post-Deployment Audit:** Run a full system diagnostic on all units to confirm the 18% throughput lag is resolved.

---

### Immediate Action: The First Ten Minutes

**Do not touch the build tools.** In the first ten minutes of this session, the team must execute a **"Hard Lockdown & Protocol Declaration"**:

1.  **Physically Disconnect:** The Team Lead must verbally confirm that the build workstation is physically disconnected from the factory network and the robot control interface. No cable or wireless connection to the robots is permitted at this stage.
2.  **The "No-Go" Declaration:** The Safety Lead must explicitly state aloud: *"The recovery protocol is not yet signed. No build, transfer, or flash attempt is authorized."*
3.  **Whiteboard Commitment:** Write the date "November 15th" and the phrase "Irreversible Action" on the main whiteboard. Below it, draw a blank box labeled "Safety Sign-Off." The session cannot proceed to Phase 2 until the Safety Lead signs inside that box.

This ensures that the irreversible step is mechanically and culturally impossible to trigger until the safety logic is reviewed and approved.

*Note: This plan prioritizes the irreversible nature of the failure mode over speed. Do not compress Phase 1.*
*Ensure the scheduled maintenance window allows at least 4 hours of buffer before the audit deadline.*
