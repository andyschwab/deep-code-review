### PureFlow Inc. Cleaning Station Overhaul Plan

**1. Exact Sequence of Steps**

1.  **Pre-Shutdown Verification (Immediately):** Confirm the 48-hour shutdown window is locked in with production scheduling to ensure no conflicts arise before Monday, October 14th. Verify that all necessary parts for the new reverse osmosis system are on-site and inspected.
2.  **Personnel Briefing & Role Assignment:** Conduct a mandatory safety meeting with all four technicians. Explicitly assign the two certified technicians to all high-pressure connection tasks. Assign the remaining two technicians to support roles (logistics, cleaning, non-pressurized assembly) under the direct supervision of the certified staff.
3.  **Drainage Procedure Finalization:** Before any physical draining occurs, the specific step-by-step protocol for draining the main reservoir without cross-contaminating the secondary tanks must be written down, reviewed, and signed off. This includes identifying isolation valves and verifying seal integrity.
4.  **Controlled Shutdown & Isolation:** On Monday, execute the line shutdown. Isolate the cleaning station from the rest of the plant utilities.
5.  **Reservoir Draining (Critical Path):** Execute the approved drainage procedure. Monitor pressure levels and flow paths continuously to ensure zero backflow into the 10,000-liter secondary tank holding the finished product.
6.  **System Swap:** Once drained and verified safe, disconnect the old system. Install the new reverse osmosis unit, ensuring only certified technicians handle high-pressure fittings.
7.  **Testing & Sanitization:** Pressure test the new system with water (not product) to check for leaks. Perform a full sanitization cycle of the new unit and the connected lines.
8.  **Final Quality Check:** Test water output quality from the new system against PureFlow standards.
9.  **Restart & Monitoring:** Reconnect to the main line, restart operations, and monitor closely for the first 24 hours for any anomalies.

**2. Final Authority on Drainage Procedure**

The final call on the drainage procedure must be made by the **Plant Operations Manager** or the **Senior Lead Technician** (provided they are one of the two certified individuals), but only after a joint review with the Quality Assurance (QA) Lead. Since the risk involves contaminating 10,000 liters of finished product ready for shipment, QA must validate that the proposed isolation steps guarantee product safety. No draining occurs without both operational and quality sign-offs.

**3. The Very First Concrete Action**

Before touching any valves or scheduling the shutdown, the **immediate creation and validation of the written Drainage and Isolation Protocol** is the first concrete action. You must document the exact valve sequence, verify the physical condition of the isolation barriers between the main reservoir and secondary tanks, and have this document approved by both the Operations Manager and the QA Lead. Do not proceed to the Monday shutdown until this document is signed and distributed to the team.
