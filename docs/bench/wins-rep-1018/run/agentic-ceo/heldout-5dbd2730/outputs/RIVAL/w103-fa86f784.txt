**Decision: Execute a Limited Pilot on a Subset of Instances**

Do not attempt a full deployment; the risk of irreversible failure across 5,000 instances is unacceptable without live data. Do not halt entirely; manual code review alone cannot guarantee the 90% accuracy target within four hours. A controlled pilot on a statistically significant but small subset (e.g., 50–100 non-critical customer instances) offers the only viable path to validate the 90% claim while containing potential damage.

**Three-Hour Execution Sequence**

**Hour 1: Validation and Isolation (Minutes 0–60)**
*   **Action:** Isolate 100 low-priority customer instances and deploy the update strictly to this sandboxed group.
*   **Decision Authority:** Technical Lead authorizes the specific instance selection and deployment script.
*   **Required Approval:** You must explicitly approve the "Go/No-Go" criteria for the pilot metrics (e.g., "If accuracy < 88% or latency increases by >5%, abort immediately").

**Hour 2: Monitoring and Analysis (Minutes 60–120)**
*   **Action:** Engineers monitor real-time inference logs from the pilot group. Compare new model output against the legacy model's historical baseline for the same inputs.
*   **Decision Authority:** Technical Lead manages the engineering team's focus and triages any immediate errors.
*   **Required Approval:** You must decide whether to expand the pilot to 500 instances if the initial 100 show >90% accuracy, or prepare the contingency narrative if metrics lag.

**Hour 3: Final Strategy and Communication Prep (Minutes 120–180)**
*   **Action:** Consolidate pilot results. If successful, prepare a statement highlighting "successful initial rollout of next-gen engine." If failed, prepare a statement focusing on "rigorous validation protocols ensuring long-term stability" while omitting specific accuracy percentages until resolved.
*   **Decision Authority:** Technical Lead finalizes the technical summary of results.
*   **Required Approval:** You must approve the final talking points for the earnings call based on the pilot outcome.

**First Concrete Action (Next 10 Minutes)**

Instruct the Technical Lead to immediately identify and flag 100 non-critical customer instances suitable for the pilot and to prepare the deployment script for *only* those instances. Simultaneously, you must define the specific numerical threshold that constitutes a "failed" pilot (e.g., accuracy below 88%) so the team has a clear abort signal before a single line of code is executed.
