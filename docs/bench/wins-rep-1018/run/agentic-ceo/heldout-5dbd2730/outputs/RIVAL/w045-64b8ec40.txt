### Pre-Work Session Plan: Overnight HVAC Upgrade

**Phase Priority**
The **Safety Briefing and Isolation Ward Protocol** takes absolute priority over all physical installation tasks. No technician may enter a room until this is complete. Following safety clearance, the **Pediatric Wing Damper Disconnection** is the critical path item; it must be the first physical installation task attempted to maximize the window for recalibration or rollback if sensors fail.

**Instructions for the Shift Supervisor**
1.  **Muster at 9:45 PM:** Conduct roll call and verify all eight technicians are present.
2.  **Safety First (10:00 PM - 10:45 PM):** Deliver the mandatory isolation ward safety briefing. Verify understanding before issuing access badges.
3.  **Pediatric Wing Deployment (11:00 PM Start):** Deploy your two most senior technicians to the pediatric wing immediately after briefing. They must disconnect dampers and attempt calibration on the first three rooms only.
4.  **Stop/Go Decision Point:** At 12:30 AM, halt all pediatric work. If the first three rooms are not calibrated and stable, do not proceed to the remaining pediatric rooms. Escalate immediately to engineering leadership.
5.  **Wing Sequencing:** If pediatric stabilization is successful, release the remaining six technicians to the other three wings in parallel, leaving one senior tech dedicated solely to monitoring the pediatric wing.
6.  **Hard Stop at 5:00 AM:** Cease all installation work. Use the final hour (5:00 AM – 6:00 AM) exclusively for system-wide testing and nursing handover preparation.

**Critical Choices to Resolve Now**
*   **Rollback Protocol:** Define the exact manual procedure to restore original HVAC damper positions if the new sensors fail to calibrate in the pediatric wing. Who authorizes this, and what tools are required on-site?
*   **Emergency Contact Chain:** Identify the specific on-call engineer authorized to make real-time decisions if the pediatric calibration fails. Standard support lines may be too slow.
*   **Isolation Ward Access List:** Confirm exactly which rooms are currently active isolation wards to avoid unnecessary briefing delays for non-isolation rooms.

**Concrete First Steps**
1.  **Draft the Safety Briefing:** Create a one-page checklist for the isolation ward briefing and have it signed off by the Infection Control Officer before the shift starts.
2.  **Pre-Stage Pediatric Tools:** Ensure the specific calibration devices and manual override tools for the pediatric dampers are physically staged outside the pediatric wing entrance before 10 PM.
3.  **Map the Technician Teams:** Assign specific pairs of technicians to specific wings now. Do not wait until the night of the install to decide who goes where.
4.  **Print the "Stop/Go" Criteria:** Provide the shift supervisor with a written card detailing the 12:30 AM decision criteria for the pediatric wing to remove ambiguity during the night.
5.  **Notify Nursing Leadership:** Inform the night nursing supervisors of the exact 5:00 AM handover time so they can prepare their staff for the system demonstration.

*Note: The 12:30 AM checkpoint for the pediatric wing is the single most important control point to prevent infant safety risks.*
*Ensure manual override tools are physically verified before the team arrives.*
