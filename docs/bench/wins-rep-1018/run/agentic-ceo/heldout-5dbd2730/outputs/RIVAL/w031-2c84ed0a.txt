**Decision on Sequence:** Train the staff on the new interface first, then validate the hardware. Training is low-risk and can begin immediately without shutting down operations. Hardware validation requires the 48-hour irreversible shutdown; performing this before staff are proficient wastes the critical window if users struggle with the interface later.

**Approval Authority:**
*   **You Must Approve:** The exact start time of the 48-hour autoclave shutdown (due to surgical schedule impact), the final vendor sign-off document by Tuesday the 12th, and the official "Data Freeze" declaration.
*   **Delegate to Shift Supervisors:** Daily training attendance logs, individual technician competency assessments, physical hardware installation checks, and the execution of the dry-run drills.
