### BlueOcean Imports Shipping Provider Cutover Plan

**Phase 1: Vendor Validation & Compatibility (Immediate)**
Before any hardware changes, verify the new labels work with existing scanning infrastructure at distribution hubs.
*   **Action:** Conduct a pilot scan test with major partners using the new label stock.
*   **Decision Authority:** Warehouse Manager approves test logistics; You approve pilot scope and partner engagement.

**Phase 2: Hardware Procurement & Staging**
Once compatibility is confirmed, source and prepare the 400 new printers.
*   **Action:** Order printers matching the new paper/adhesive specs; set up a staging area for firmware updates and test prints.
*   **Decision Authority:** Warehouse Manager selects specific printer models within spec; You approve total procurement budget and expedited shipping costs.

**Phase 3: Parallel Run & Staff Training**
Run the new system alongside the old one without cancelling the old contract.
*   **Action:** Train staff on new hardware; process 5% of daily volume (400 packages) through the new system to verify end-to-end flow.
*   **Decision Authority:** Warehouse Manager determines training schedule and shift rotations; You approve overtime budgets required for parallel processing.

**Phase 4: The Cutover (Pre-January 10th)**
Execute the full switch during the lowest volume window.
*   **Action:** Swap all 400 printers; cancel old service; monitor first 4 hours of full-volume (8,000 packages) output closely.
*   **Decision Authority:** Warehouse Manager executes the physical swap; You give the final "Go/No-Go" command to cancel the old contract.

**Decision Matrix: Budget vs. Operations**
*   **Your Decisions (Budget/Strategic):** Any cost exceeding the approved procurement cap by >5%; costs associated with expedited shipping to meet the Jan 10th deadline; financial penalties for potential SLA breaches during transition; final authorization to terminate the legacy contract.
*   **Warehouse Manager Decisions (Operational):** Specific printer brand selection (provided it meets technical specs); staff shift assignments for training and installation; daily workflow adjustments during the parallel run; troubleshooting minor hardware jams or alignment issues.

**First Concrete Task**
The absolute first task is to **ship 50 test packages labeled with the new provider's stock through your primary distribution hub** and confirm successful scans at every checkpoint. Do not order a single printer or schedule staff until you have written confirmation from the hub that these 50 packages were processed without error. This validates the adhesive and paper size against the scanners before you incur any hardware costs.

*Note: Ensure the test includes peak-hour conditions to mimic real stress. Keep the old contract active until the full 8,000-package day is successfully completed on the new system.*
