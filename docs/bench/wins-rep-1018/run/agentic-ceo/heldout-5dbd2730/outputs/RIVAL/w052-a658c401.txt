**Session Plan: Oakwood Campus Wheelchair Tracking Deployment**

**1. Building Deployment Order**
To minimize risk to patient care equipment, the deployment must proceed from the lowest-risk zones to the highest-risk zones. This ensures that if a calibration error occurs, it affects non-critical areas first, allowing the team to refine the process before approaching the ICU.

*   **Phase 1 (Low Risk - Administrative/Outpatient):** Buildings 1–4 (Admin, Outpatient Clinics, Cafeteria, Library). These areas have no life-support equipment.
*   **Phase 2 (Medium Risk - General Wards):** Buildings 5–9 (General Medical/Surgical Wards, Pediatrics, Maternity). These areas have monitoring equipment but less sensitivity than ICU.
*   **Phase 3 (High Risk - Critical Care):** Buildings 10–12 (ICU, CCU, Emergency Department). **The ICU is the final zone.**

**2. Critical Decision Point**
The mandatory approval gate occurs **after Phase 1 completion and before entering Phase 2**.
*   **Action:** The team will calibrate and test one base station in Building 1 (Admin) and one in Building 5 (General Ward) as pilots.
*   **Approval Required:** You must physically verify with biomedical engineering that signal levels in the Phase 2 pilot (Building 5) cause zero interference with standard monitors. Only upon your written approval of these specific signal strength settings may the team proceed to calibrate the remaining Phase 2 buildings and eventually the ICU.
*   **Constraint:** Do not calibrate any base station in Buildings 10–12 (ICU zone) until all 9 previous buildings are fully tagged and verified.

**3. First Concrete Action**
The logistics team of six must immediately **segment into two teams of three** and begin **physical tagging of wheelchairs in Building 1 (Admin)** only.
*   **Team A (3 people):** Inventory and affix tags to wheelchairs located in Building 1.
*   **Team B (3 people):** Inventory and affix tags to wheelchairs located in Building 2.
*   **Restriction:** No base stations are to be powered on or calibrated today. The sole objective of the first shift is reducing the pool of untagged assets in the lowest-risk zone. Power outlets remain unused until the calibration protocol is reviewed tomorrow morning.

**Operational Notes**
*   Since only one zone can be active at a time due to power outlet limits, base station calibration for a specific building must occur strictly during the wheelchair tagging phase for that same building to ensure immediate verification.
*   If any interference is detected during the Phase 2 pilot, halt all operations immediately; do not attempt to adjust signal strength live—reboot the system as per protocol before retrying.
