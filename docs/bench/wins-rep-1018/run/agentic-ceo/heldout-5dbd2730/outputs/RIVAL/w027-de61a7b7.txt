**MindBridge Encryption Deployment: Strategy Meeting Workflow**

**Phase 1: Immediate Parallel Initiation (Start Today)**
*   **Engineering Squad:** Begin drafting the **real-time rollback script** immediately. This is the highest priority technical safeguard. The team must define the trigger conditions for automatic reversion if a device fails to handshake with the new encryption layer. No sign-off required to start coding; sign-off required only on the final "trigger logic" definition before integration.
*   **Operations Lead:** Draft the **user communication plan** template focusing on "scheduled maintenance windows" rather than "potential downtime" to prevent panic. This draft must be ready for review within 24 hours.

**Phase 2: Critical Decision Point (Requires CTO Sign-off)**
*   **Pilot Program Approval:** You must explicitly approve the **closed group pilot of ten providers** before any code touches a production device.
    *   *Recommendation:* Do not skip this. Select ten non-critical providers (low session volume) for a 48-hour test window.
    *   *Action:* Identify the ten providers by end-of-day today.
    *   *Sign-off:* You must sign off on the specific list of providers and the exact 48-hour window.

**Phase 3: Execution Sequence (Post-Sign-off)**
1.  **Deploy to Pilot Group:** Push the update to the ten selected providers.
2.  **Monitor & Validate:** Engineering monitors handshake success and session stability in real-time.
3.  **Go/No-Go Decision:** If the pilot succeeds without incident for 48 hours, proceed to full rollout. If failures occur, the rollback script executes automatically, and the team analyzes logs before retrying.
4.  **Staggered Full Rollout:** Do not update all 3,000 devices simultaneously. Deploy in batches of 300 devices per day over ten days to limit exposure.
5.  **Communication Release:** Send the finalized communication plan to all therapists 24 hours before their specific batch update window.

**Summary of Responsibilities**
*   **CTO Sign-off Required:** Selection of the 10 pilot providers, the specific pilot time window, and the final "Go" command to move from Pilot to Full Rollout.
*   **Team Immediate Action:** Coding the rollback script, defining trigger logic, and drafting communication templates.

**Note:** The 45-day window allows for a cautious, staggered approach; rushing a full deployment risks the exact outage we must avoid.
**Note:** Ensure the rollback script is tested in a sandbox environment mimicking the field devices before the pilot begins.
