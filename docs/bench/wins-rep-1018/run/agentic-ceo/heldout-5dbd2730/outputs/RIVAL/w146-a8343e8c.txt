### Session Plan: Data Sovereignty Compliance & Re-encryption Strategy

**Phase 1: Assessment & Architecture (Hours 0–2)**
1.  **Inventory Verification:** Confirm the exact distribution of the 50TB across the three clusters and identify specific table schemas containing transaction logs.
2.  **Encryption Standard Selection:** Define the end-to-end encryption algorithm (e.g., AES-256) and key management hierarchy compliant with the new law.
3.  **Resource Allocation:** Assign the four security specialists to key architecture and audit logging, and the two DBAs to cluster performance modeling and backup integrity checks.

**Phase 2: The Dry Run & Backup Strategy (Hours 2–4)**
1.  **Snapshot Protocol:** Establish a procedure for taking immutable, pre-re-encryption snapshots of all three clusters.
2.  **Pilot Execution:** Select a non-critical 1TB subset from one cluster to perform a full re-encryption cycle. This validates the timeline and identifies bottlenecks without risking the entire dataset.
3.  **Rollback Validation:** Verify that the backup restoration process works within the "hours" window before data obsolescence occurs.

**Phase 3: Execution Planning (Hours 4–6)**
1.  **Sequencing Logic:** Determine the order of cluster migration to minimize downtime (e.g., staggered rolling updates vs. maintenance window blackout).
2.  **Key Rotation Protocol:** Draft the exact command sequence for master key rotation, ensuring the "point of no return" is clearly marked.
3.  **Compliance Audit Trail:** Define how encryption success will be logged and reported for legal verification.
