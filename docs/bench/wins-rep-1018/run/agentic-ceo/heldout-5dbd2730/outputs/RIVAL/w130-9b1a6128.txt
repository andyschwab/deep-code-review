### Cold Storage Alarm System Implementation Plan

**Objective:** Fully deploy and test the new cold storage alarm system across four warehouses before Friday, 6 PM.

**Immediate Action:** Schedule the **First Physical Inspection** for tomorrow morning (Day 1, 08:00). This inspection must verify power conduit accessibility and network drop locations in all four warehouses to confirm technician staging areas. Without this confirmation, hardware installation cannot proceed safely.

#### Phase 1: Preparation & Staging (Day 1 – Day 2)
*   **Technicians (Team of 6):** Divide into two teams of three. Team A stages hardware and pre-configures sensor IDs for Warehouse 1 & 2; Team B does the same for Warehouse 3 & 4.
*   **Facility Manager Ownership:**
    *   Secure explicit written authorization for the legacy network shutdown.
    *   Coordinate with the utility provider to confirm the exact voltage fluctuation window during the generator switch.
    *   Ensure backup power is available at the central monitoring station during the cutover.

#### Phase 2: Parallel Installation (Day 3 – Day 4)
*   **Execution:** Teams deploy simultaneously to minimize downtime exposure.
    *   Team A installs sensors and gateways in Warehouses 1 & 2.
    *   Team B installs sensors and gateways in Warehouses 3 & 4.
*   **Constraint:** Do **not** disable the legacy network yet. Run both systems in parallel to validate data alignment.

#### Phase 3: Calibration & Integration (Day 5, Morning)
*   **Technicians:** Calibrate all new sensors against manual thermometer readings. Verify signal strength and latency to the central dashboard.
*   **Facility Manager Ownership:** Review the calibration logs and sign off on data accuracy.

#### Phase 4: Failover Testing & The Go/No-Go Decision (Day 5, 14:00)
*   **Test Protocol:** Simulate a voltage fluctuation event while the legacy system is still active but muted. Confirm the new system triggers alarms within the 2-hour spoilage window requirement.
*   **CRITICAL GO/NO-GO DECISION POINT:**
    *   **Time:** Friday, 14:00 (4 hours before maintenance).
    *   **Decision Maker:** You (Project Lead), based on Technician data validation and Facility Manager's safety sign-off.
    *   **Criteria for GO:** All 4 warehouses reporting stable data; failover test successful; Facility Manager confirms generator readiness.
    *   **Criteria for NO-GO:** Any sensor offline, latency >5 minutes, or unverified power stability. If NO-GO, abort the cutover and maintain legacy monitoring (accepting the 12% risk) rather than risking total blindness.

#### Phase 5: The Irreversible Cutover (Day 5, 15:30)
*   **Condition:** Only proceed if the decision is **GO**.
*   **Action:** Technicians disable the legacy monitoring network to prevent signal interference.
*   **Verification:** Confirm new system is the sole active monitor for 30 minutes prior to the 6 PM grid switch.

**Note:** The 48-hour factory reset constraint makes the Go/No-Go decision at 14:00 the single most critical control point; do not compress this timeline.
**Note:** Facility Manager must remain on-site from 14:00 until the generator switch is complete to authorize emergency reversion protocols if catastrophic failure occurs immediately post-cutover.
