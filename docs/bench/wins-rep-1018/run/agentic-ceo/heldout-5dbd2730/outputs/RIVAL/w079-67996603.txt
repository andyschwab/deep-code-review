**Deployment Strategy & Immediate Action Plan**

**1. Deployment Sequence: Staggered Regional Waves**
To minimize total network downtime and mitigate the risk of a central handshake failure, do not deploy to all 4,200 vehicles simultaneously. Instead, execute a **Staggered Regional Wave** strategy:
*   **Wave 1 (Pilot):** Select the region with the lowest volume of urgent deliveries (approx. 350 vehicles). Deploy to 10% of this region first (35 trucks) as a canary test. If successful, complete the rest of the region.
*   **Wave 2–12 (Rolling Deployment):** Proceed region-by-region, ensuring only one region is offline at any given time. With 12 coordinators, assign one to each remaining region.
*   **Timing:** Begin Wave 1 immediately. Each subsequent wave starts only after the previous region's vehicles have fully rebooted and confirmed connectivity. This ensures that if the central handshake fails, only one region (max 350 trucks) is affected, preventing a national halt and avoiding the 48-hour manual rollback scenario. Total theoretical downtime per truck remains 20 minutes, but network availability is preserved.
