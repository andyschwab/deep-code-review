### Kickoff Session Agenda & Work Sequence

**Recommended Sequence of Work:**
1.  **Define Strategic Parameters (Day 1):** Before writing code or designing screens, you must define the accuracy threshold and risk tolerance. This dictates the data model's complexity and the UI's warning logic.
2.  **Build the Data Model (Days 2–20):** Once the threshold is set, engineering can ingest weather data and train the prediction algorithm. This is the longest lead-time item and the core technical risk.
3.  **Design User Interface & Communication Tone (Days 10–25):** With a working prototype of the model, designers can create interfaces that clearly convey uncertainty without causing panic. The tone should be empathetic but factual, developed in parallel with UI drafts.
4.  **Integration & Testing (Days 25–35):** Combine the model, UI, and support line integration.
5.  **Soft Launch & Calibration (Days 35–40):** Test with a small user segment to fine-tune the threshold before the October 5th deadline.

**Strategic Parameters (Owner: You/Leadership):**
*   **Accuracy Threshold:** The specific probability percentage (e.g., 70% vs. 90%) required to trigger a delay notification. This is the primary trade-off between false alarms and missed warnings.
*   **Risk Appetite:** A clear directive on whether the business prioritizes avoiding customer anger (fewer false alarms) or preventing logistical failures (fewer missed warnings).
*   **Budget Allocation:** How the $30,000 cloud budget is split between heavy computation for model training versus real-time inference costs.
*   **Escalation Protocol:** At what point a predicted delay automatically triggers a human support outreach versus staying digital-only.

**Execution Tasks (Owner: Product Team):**
*   **Data Pipeline Construction:** Sourcing, cleaning, and normalizing historical weather and shipment data.
*   **Algorithm Selection & Training:** Choosing the machine learning approach and iterating on model performance against the defined threshold.
*   **UI/UX Implementation:** Building the customer-facing dashboard and internal agent view.
*   **Copywriting:** Drafting notification templates based on the approved communication tone.
*   **Cloud Infrastructure Setup:** Configuring servers within the $30,000 limit to ensure scalability during storm spikes.

**First Concrete Action for Today:**
Convene a 60-minute decision meeting with yourself and the lead engineer to mathematically define the **minimum acceptable precision and recall rates** for the estimator. You must sign off on a specific "cost of error" formula (e.g., "One missed warning equals ten false alarms in severity") before the team can select a model architecture. Without this number, the development window will be wasted iterating on the wrong target.

Note: This sequence ensures the highest risk (the model's decision logic) is resolved before resources are spent on design or integration.
Delaying the threshold decision even by 48 hours jeopardizes the October 5th launch due to the required model training time.
