### Mercy Care Gift Shop Inventory Replacement: Planning Session Flow

**1. Recommended Stage Order**
To meet the November 25th deadline and manage the high-risk data purge, the planning session must follow this strict sequence:
1.  **Risk Mitigation & Rollback Strategy:** Define the exact protocol for the database purge and the "fail-safe" procedure if the sync fails. This is the critical path item; if this isn't solved, the project cannot proceed.
2.  **Data Transfer Architecture:** Map the specific fields between the old stock database and the new point-of-sale (POS) terminals to ensure consignment goods are tracked accurately.
3.  **Vendor Reconciliation Plan:** Schedule the review of current stock levels with vendors to establish a baseline before the switch.
4.  **Interface Build Scope:** Define the minimum viable features required for the holiday rush, deferring non-essential updates.
5.  **Staff Communication & Training:** Draft the announcement and training schedule for the new checkout process.
6.  **Cutover Rehearsal:** Plan the 30-minute downtime window, including a minute-by-minute script for the switchover.

**2. Store Manager Ownership**
The two store managers must own all tasks involving physical inventory, vendor relationships, and staff coordination. Specifically:
*   **Vendor Coordination:** Leading the review of stock levels and confirming consignment terms with suppliers.
*   **Staff Training:** Executing the announcement of the new checkout process and managing the training roster.
*   **Physical Verification:** Validating that the digital stock counts match physical shelves during the reconciliation phase.
*   **Cutover Execution:** Managing the floor during the 30-minute switchover to ensure no sales attempts occur while systems are offline.

**3. Decisions Requiring Your Sign-off**
As the project lead, you must explicitly approve:
*   **The Rollback Protocol:** The specific technical steps to restore the old database if the new sync fails during the purge.
*   **The Data Mapping Schema:** Final approval on how consignment data fields translate from the legacy system to the new POS.
*   **The Cutover Window:** Confirmation of the exact date and time for the 30-minute downtime to ensure it aligns with low-traffic periods.
*   **Scope Freeze:** Approval of the feature list for the November 25th launch, rejecting any additions that threaten the timeline.

**4. First Concrete Action (Today)**
Convene an immediate 30-minute meeting with the three software developers to draft the **Data Purge and Rollback Logic**. You must produce a written step-by-step procedure that details exactly how the system will detect a sync failure and revert to the old database without losing consignment tracking data. Do not discuss interface design or staff training until this document is drafted and reviewed, as this is the single point of failure for the entire project.

*Note: The November 25th deadline allows only a narrow margin for error; prioritize the risk protocol over feature completeness. Ensure the developers document the rollback logic in writing before leaving today.*
