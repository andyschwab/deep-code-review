### IronHaul Logistics Fuel System Rollout: Kickoff Structure

**Phase 1: Controlled Validation (Days 1–5)**
*   **Pilot Group:** Select 5 non-critical vehicles for initial firmware flashing.
*   **Stress Test:** Simulate simultaneous updates on exactly 5 units to verify the "brick" threshold.
*   **Data Verification:** Confirm digital logs match physical movement for 100% of pilot routes.

**Phase 2: Regional Staggered Deployment (Days 6–14)**
*   **Batching Strategy:** Update vehicles in groups of 4 per depot per night. Never exceed 4 simultaneous updates across the entire fleet to maintain a safety margin below the 5-vehicle failure limit.
*   **Driver Training:** Mandatory 30-minute digital input workshops for drivers transitioning from paper logs.
*   **Hybrid Buffer:** Maintain paper log backups for updated trucks for 48 hours post-install as a fail-safe.

**Phase 3: Final Sweep & Audit Prep (Days 15–18)**
*   **Remaining Fleet:** Complete updates on the final 20% of vehicles.
*   **System Lock:** Disable paper log entry options in the software.
*   **Audit Simulation:** Run a full data integrity report to ensure federal compliance.

**Decision Authority Matrix**

| Decision Category | Requires Your Direct Authorization | Field Supervisor Authority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Risk Management** | Approval to proceed to next batch if *any* engine bricking occurs. | Halting updates immediately if anomalies are detected. |
| **Technical Scope** | Changing the firmware version or update protocol. | Scheduling specific truck downtime within their depot. |
| **Personnel** | Authorizing overtime pay for emergency rollback teams. | Assigning drivers to training sessions and pairing mentors. |
| **Compliance** | Signing off on the final pre-audit data report. | Verifying individual driver adoption of digital logs. |

**The Single Most Important Action (First Hour)**
Establish a **hard technical interlock** that physically or software-limits the firmware update tool to process a maximum of **four vehicles simultaneously**. Before discussing training or schedules, you must configure the deployment server or update dongles to reject any attempt to flash a fifth unit concurrently. This removes human error from the equation and guarantees that the critical failure threshold (5+ simultaneous failures) cannot be breached, regardless of field enthusiasm or scheduling pressure.

***

*Note: This plan assumes the "5 vehicle" limit is a hard technical constraint; if it is probabilistic, the batch size should be reduced further.*
*Ensure all field supervisors receive the updated "Stop Work" authority protocol immediately.*
