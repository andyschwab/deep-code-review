### Warehouse Slotting Optimization Migration Plan

**Objective:** Ensure the automated warehouse slotting system is fully optimized and deployed for 15,000 SKUs (including 3,000 irregular new items) before the physical racking configuration is locked on November 14th.

#### 1. Proposed Work Session Agenda
To determine the precise sequence of data cleansing, simulation, and deployment, convene a 4-hour intensive workshop with stakeholders from Logistics, Data Engineering, and Robotics. The session must follow this sequence:

*   **Hour 1: Data Gap Analysis & Cleansing Strategy.** Review the dimensional data for the 3,000 new irregular SKUs. Define the cleaning protocol for missing or outlier dimensions that currently break the logic.
*   **Hour 2: Simulation Scenarios & Risk Modeling.** Run "what-if" scenarios in the sandbox environment. Specifically test edge cases where irregular items force suboptimal spacing to quantify the potential 20% capacity loss.
*   **Hour 3: Deployment Sequence Definition.** Map the critical path from final data lock to the November 14th calibration deadline. Identify the exact handoff point between software finalization and robotics calibration.
*   **Hour 4: Decision Matrix & Role Assignment.** Finalize risk tolerance levels and assign ownership for the execution phase.

#### 2. Critical Decisions Required from You
You must make the following strategic decisions during the session:

*   **Risk Tolerance for Irregular Items:** Define the acceptable threshold for storage efficiency loss on the new 3,000 SKUs. Must the system achieve 100% theoretical density, or is a 5-10% buffer acceptable to guarantee stability and prevent system crashes?
*   **Fallback Protocol:** Decide the manual override procedure if the automated logic fails to place a specific irregular SKU during the initial live run. Will these items go to a designated "quarantine" zone, or will human intervention reroute them immediately?
*   **Data Cut-off Date:** Establish the hard deadline after which no new dimensional data can be ingested into the model before the November 14th lock.

#### 3. Robotics Team Responsibilities
The robotics team owns the physical translation of the digital map. Their specific deliverables are:

*   **Calibration Execution:** Translate the final optimized slotting map into machine-readable coordinates for the automated movers by November 13th.
*   **Collision Testing:** Verify that the new irregular dimensions do not create physical collision risks within the aisles during high-speed retrieval.
*   **Lock Confirmation:** Formally sign off on the physical racking configuration on November 14th, triggering the irreversible infrastructure lock.

#### 4. First Immediate Task
**Initiate the "Irregular SKU Data Audit" immediately.**
Extract the raw dimensional data for the 3,000 new SKUs and run a script to flag any entries with missing, zero, or physically impossible dimensions (e.g., negative volume or sizes exceeding bay limits). Do not wait for the work session to begin this; the quality of the upcoming simulation depends entirely on the baseline integrity of this dataset. Deliver a summary report of flagged items to the Data Engineering lead within 24 hours to inform the cleansing strategy.

*Note: This plan assumes the simulation environment is currently accessible and mirrors production logic. Any delays in the data audit will directly compress the testing window before the November 14th deadline.*
