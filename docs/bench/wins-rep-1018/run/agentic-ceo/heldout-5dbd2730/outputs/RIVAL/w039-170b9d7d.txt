### SterilTrack Deployment Strategy & Workflow

**Recommended Strategy: Single-Site Pilot Followed by Parallel Rollout**
Do not attempt a parallel rollout immediately. Given the high risk of bricking devices during the firmware update and the critical four-day window, you must execute a **Single-Site Pilot** on Monday morning at the center with the highest technician availability. This validates the firmware update process on a small batch (5–10 units) before committing the entire fleet. If the pilot succeeds, switch to a parallel rollout for the remaining five sites Tuesday through Thursday. If it fails, you contain the damage to one site and preserve time for remediation.

**Workflow Breakdown (Monday – Thursday)**

1.  **Site Assessment & Staging (Monday 06:00 – 08:00)**
    *   **Engineers:** Verify network connectivity at the pilot site and stage the firmware on a local server.
    *   **Local Technicians:** Physically isolate the first batch of 10 autoclaves and confirm power stability.
    *   **Safety Check (Sign-off Required):** You must approve the "Go/No-Go" decision for the firmware flash after engineers confirm the backup image is ready and rollback procedures are tested.

2.  **Pilot Execution & Validation (Monday 08:00 – 12:00)**
    *   **Engineers:** Perform firmware updates and sensor calibration on the pilot batch.
    *   **Local Technicians:** Assist with physical sensor mounting and cable management under engineer supervision.
    *   **Safety Check (Sign-off Required):** You must validate the data integrity on the central dashboard for the pilot batch before authorizing full-scale deployment.

3.  **Parallel Rollout (Tuesday – Thursday)**
    *   **Engineers:** Split into teams of one engineer per two sites (remote support) or rotate physically if travel time permits. They oversee the firmware logic and dashboard integration.
    *   **Local Technicians (Delegated Tasks):** Technicians can independently handle physical sensor installation, cabling, and basic power cycling for the remaining 190 units, provided they follow the checklist validated during the pilot.
    *   **Engineers:** Remotely monitor the firmware push and intervene only for errors.

4.  **Final Validation (Thursday 14:00 – 17:00)**
    *   **Joint Effort:** Engineers and technicians verify connectivity for all 200 units.
    *   **Safety Check (Sign-off Required):** You must sign off on the final certification report confirming 100% device uptime and data accuracy before the maintenance shutdown begins.

**Specific First Action for Supply Chain Security**
Immediately contact your hardware vendor to **place a standing order for 15 replacement autoclave control boards** with an expedited shipping guarantee for delivery by Monday morning. Do not wait for the pilot result; these units are your insurance policy against bricking. Request a temporary loaner pool if full replacements cannot be sourced within 24 hours.

***

*Note: Ensure local technicians are briefed on the "stop work" protocol if they encounter physical resistance during sensor mounting to prevent hardware damage.*
*The success of this plan relies entirely on the stability of the firmware update tested in the Monday pilot.*
