**Preparation Session Plan: Apex Credit Scoring Engine Rollout**

**Primary Starting Point: Historical Back-Test**
You must begin the preparation session with a **historical back-test** of the new model against the 50,000 pending applications. Stakeholder communication is premature and potentially damaging before you quantify the impact. If the back-test reveals that the new thresholds reject 40% of applicants compared to 15% under the old rules, your communication strategy changes entirely. You cannot communicate what you do not yet know. The back-test provides the empirical data required to shape the narrative for regulators, customers, and internal teams.

**Sequence of Validation, Approval, and Deployment**

1.  **Shadow Validation (Weeks 1–2):** Run the new engine in "shadow mode" alongside the legacy system on live traffic without making actual decisions. Compare outcomes specifically for edge cases and protected demographic classes to detect bias.
2.  **Threshold Impact Analysis (Week 3):** Data scientists to produce a variance report showing the delta in approval rates, average loan size, and projected risk exposure between the old and new models.
3.  **Executive & Risk Approval (Week 4):** Present the variance report to the Risk Committee. This is the gate before any code touches production.
4.  **Phased Deployment (January 3–9):** Deploy to 1% of traffic, then 10%, monitoring for latency and error rates. Do not flip the switch for 100% of traffic until January 9th.
5.  **Go-Live (January 10):** Full activation aligned with fiscal planning.

**Threshold Adjustments Requiring Explicit Authorization**
As the Division Lead, your explicit written authorization is required for:
*   **The Final Cut-Off Score:** The specific numerical score that separates "Approve" from "Reject." Even a 5-point shift can alter thousands of decisions.
*   **Override Protocols:** The criteria under which a human underwriter can override the model's automatic rejection.
*   **Fairness Tolerance Limits:** The maximum acceptable disparity in approval rates across protected classes. If the back-test exceeds these limits, deployment must halt until you authorize a model retrain or threshold adjustment.

**First Concrete Step**
Immediately instruct your six data scientists to **extract the raw data of the 50,000 pending applications and run them through the new scoring engine in an isolated sandbox environment.** Do not deploy code, do not draft emails, and do not schedule stakeholder meetings until this dataset is scored and the distribution of new scores is visualized. This single action validates whether the model behaves as expected in your specific environment before any further resources are committed.

*Note: Ensure legal counsel reviews the back-test results for regulatory compliance before the Executive Approval phase. Delaying the go-live date is preferable to deploying a biased model that causes irreversible financial loss.*
