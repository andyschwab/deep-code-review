### Drone Delivery Pilot Session Plan: Sector 4

**Phase 1: Pre-Launch Verification & Simulation (Friday)**
1.  **Safety Certification Verification:** Before any software interaction, you must personally verify the **Airworthiness Certificate** for all drone units and the **Remote Pilot Certificate (Part 107 equivalent)** for all eight pilots. Additionally, confirm the **Certificate of Waiver or Authorization (COA)** specifically covering the dense urban Zone 4 no-fly exemptions.
2.  **Critical First Simulation:** Run a **"Geofence Boundary Stress Test" in a closed-loop sandbox environment.** This simulation must ingest the exact coordinate set intended for upload and attempt to fly virtual drones across every defined boundary edge at maximum speed. The success criterion is binary: the virtual drones must refuse to cross the line 100% of the time without triggering a permanent ground lock. Do not proceed to live uploads until this simulation passes with zero errors.

**Phase 2: Software Upload & Validation (Saturday, 07:00 – 08:30)**
*   **07:00 – 07:45:** Execute the one-way geofence coordinate push. Due to the permanent lock risk, this must be performed on a single "canary" unit first. Verify the unit accepts the coordinates and remains operational.
*   **07:45 – 08:30:** Upon successful canary verification, proceed with the remaining fleet. Immediately follow with a static "handshake" validation where each drone reports its active geofence version ID to the command center. Any unit failing to report the correct ID is grounded immediately.

**Phase 3: Flight Path Validation (Saturday, 09:00 – 11:00)**
*   **Window:** 09:00 – 11:00 (Within noise ordinance).
*   **Action:** Deploy two drones on pre-cleared, short-range loops (0.5 miles) within Sector 4.
*   **Objective:** Validate real-world GPS drift against the new geofence and confirm obstacle avoidance sensors interact correctly with urban canyon effects. No deliveries are attempted in this window.

**Phase 4: Emergency Protocol Drills (Saturday, 11:00 – 12:00)**
*   **Scenario A:** Loss of Command Link. Pilots practice automated return-to-home (RTH) within the geofenced area.
*   **Scenario B:** Forced Landing. Simulate a battery critical event requiring an immediate descent to a pre-designated safe zone.
*   **Constraint:** Since no ground vehicle fallback exists, drills must verify that the drone can safely hold position or land autonomously without external intervention.

**Phase 5: Operational Ramp-Up (Saturday, 12:00 – 16:00 & Sunday/Monday 09:00 – 16:00)**
*   **Saturday Afternoon:** Begin low-volume deliveries (20 units) to test logistics flow.
*   **Sunday/Monday:** Scale to full capacity. To achieve 200 deliveries in 48 hours with only 14 total flight hours available (7 hours/day), the fleet must average ~14.3 deliveries per hour collectively. With 8 pilots, this requires a turnover time of under 4 minutes per delivery per active drone.
*   **Schedule:**
    *   Saturday (12:00–16:00): 40 deliveries.
    *   Sunday (09:00–16:00): 80 deliveries.
    *   Monday (09:00–16:00): 80 deliveries.
    *   *Note: This schedule assumes 100% uptime; any technical halt jeopardizes the 48-hour goal.*

**Required Safety Certifications for Personal Verification:**
1.  **Unit Airworthiness Certificates:** Ensuring hardware integrity for urban flight.
2.  **Pilot Remote Licenses:** Validating legal authority for all 8 operators.
3.  **Urban Operations Waiver (COA):** Specific authorization for Sector 4 density and no-fly proximity.

**First Simulation Requirement:**
The **Geofence Boundary Stress Test** in a sandbox environment is the mandatory first step. It isolates the coordinate data from physical hardware to prevent the catastrophic "permanent ground" scenario caused by erroneous one-way pushes.

*Given the irreversible nature of the geofence update, the "canary" device test is non-negotiable before fleet-wide deployment.*
*The tight 48-hour window leaves no margin for error; if Saturday's validation fails, the target is mathematically unreachable.*
