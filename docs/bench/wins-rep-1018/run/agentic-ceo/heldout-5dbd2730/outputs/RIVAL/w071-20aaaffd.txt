### Strategy Session Agenda: Mailroom Sorting Algorithm Transition

**1. Immediate Priority: Create a Manual Override Protocol**
Do not prioritize stress-testing or communication first. While testing is vital, your constraint states that once legacy rules are deactivated, misrouted claims are irretrievable. Therefore, the absolute priority is establishing a safety net that functions *after* the switch fails. Stress-testing cannot guarantee 99% accuracy in a live environment with 10,000 daily pieces; only a human-in-the-loop fallback can recover revenue post-failure. Communication with billing is secondary and should occur only after the safety protocol is defined.

**2. Division of Responsibilities**

**Engineering Team Ownership (The "How"):**
*   **Shadow Mode Deployment:** Run the new algorithm parallel to the legacy system for 48 hours without routing actual mail. Compare outputs to identify divergence points.
*   **Exception Flagging Logic:** Build the technical trigger that identifies low-confidence sorts (e.g., anything below 99.5% certainty) and automatically routes these to a "quarantine" queue rather than final delivery.
*   **Rollback Switch:** Develop an immediate "kill switch" to revert to legacy rules if the error rate exceeds 0.5% within the first 4 hours of go-live.

**Founder Critical Choices (The "What" and "When"):**
*   **Threshold Definition:** You must define the specific criteria for manual review. Decide the exact confidence score below which a claim is held for human verification.
*   **Resource Allocation:** Authorize the temporary reassignment of two staff members from non-critical tasks to man the manual override queue during the first 72 hours.
*   **Risk Acceptance:** Explicitly approve the trade-off between speed and accuracy. If the manual queue grows, you must decide whether to delay sorting or risk a backlog.

**3. The Single Most Important Action**
**Define and staff the "Quarantine Queue" workflow immediately.**

You must leave this meeting with a signed protocol detailing exactly how pieces of mail flagged as "uncertain" by the new algorithm are physically handled, who reviews them, and the maximum time allowed for that review. Without this specific workflow defined and staffed before October 28th, any algorithmic error results in immediate, unrecoverable revenue loss. The technology will fail at the margins; your revenue depends entirely on how you handle those margins.
