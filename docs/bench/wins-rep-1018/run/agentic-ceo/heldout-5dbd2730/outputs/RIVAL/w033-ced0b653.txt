**Rollout Strategy: Staged Pilot Deployment**

Do not go live everywhere simultaneously. Given the irreversible nature of the intake form changes, the recent legal flags on edge cases, and the critical risk of missing preeclampsia indicators, a single-clinic pilot is mandatory. Deploying to all 12 clinics at once creates an unacceptable failure mode where 150 staff members could be utilizing flawed logic before the three legal edge cases are resolved. A pilot allows us to validate the algorithm's real-world performance against the 92% historical accuracy rate and confirm the legal fixes work in a live environment before scaling.

**Kickoff Session Plan**

**Objective:** Align leadership on the pilot strategy, finalize the resolution of legal edge cases, and establish the communication protocol for the October 1st deadline.

**First Concrete Actions (Morning Of):**

1.  **Convene Emergency Triage:** Assemble the four training specialists, the lead developer, and a legal representative immediately. The sole agenda is to review the three flagged edge cases. Do not proceed with any training material distribution until these cases are either resolved in the code or explicitly documented as known limitations with a manual workaround.
2.  **Select Pilot Site:** Designate the clinic with the most experienced nursing staff and the strongest IT support liaison as the pilot location. This minimizes variables when troubleshooting potential logic failures.
3.  **Freeze Training Content:** Halt any distribution of training modules that do not include the specific manual override procedures for the three legal edge cases. The training specialists must update their scripts to include these exceptions before touching the wider staff roster.

**Workflow Diagrams Requiring Your Personal Review:**

You must personally review and sign off on two specific diagrams before the pilot launches:
*   **The "Exception Handling" Flowchart:** This must detail exactly what a nurse does when the system flags a patient in a way that matches one of the three legal edge cases. It must show the manual verification step and the escalation path to a senior clinician.
*   **The "Data Integrity" Loop:** This diagram must illustrate how data entered during the pilot is audited daily. It needs to show the feedback loop from the clinic back to the development team to ensure that if the algorithm misses a high-risk indicator, it is caught within 24 hours.

**Instructions for Clinic Managers (Temporary Pause on Paper Forms):**

Send the following directive to all 12 clinic managers immediately:

"Effective immediately, all clinics must maintain a dual-system protocol. While we prepare for the digital rollout, **do not destroy or archive existing paper intake forms.** For the duration of the pilot phase at [Pilot Clinic Name], all other clinics will continue using standard paper forms.

At the pilot site, nurses will enter data into the new system, but a **mandatory secondary check** using the paper form logic is required for any patient presenting with hypertension or proteinuria symptoms until further notice. If the digital tool's risk score contradicts the paper form's manual calculation, the paper form's assessment takes precedence, and the discrepancy must be logged instantly. We are pausing the full retirement of paper forms until the pilot confirms the digital logic handles all legal edge cases without error."

**Note:** This approach sacrifices speed for safety, ensuring the October 1st deadline is met with a verified system rather than a broken one. The dual-system requirement during the pilot is non-negotiable given the preeclampsia risks.
