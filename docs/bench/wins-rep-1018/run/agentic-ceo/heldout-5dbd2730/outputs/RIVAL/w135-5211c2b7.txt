### FlashDrop Launch Work Session Structure

**1. Strategic Sequence of Stages**
To meet the September 30th deadline while mitigating the September 10th pricing lock-in, execute work in this strict order:
*   **Phase 1: Pricing & Revenue Model (Immediate – Sept 9):** Finalize tier structures and elasticity simulations. This must be completed before the app store submission window closes to avoid refund cascades.
*   **Phase 2: Route Optimization & Fleet Logistics (Sept 10 – Sept 20):** Deploy algorithms for the 60-scooter fleet based on the finalized pricing zones. Validate battery swap cycles and peak-hour coverage in the financial district.
*   **Phase 3: Merchant Onboarding & Integration (Sept 15 – Sept 25):** Activate merchant portals using the confirmed pricing API. Focus on high-volume lunch and dinner spots first.
*   **Phase 4: Marketing Announcement & Public Launch (Sept 26 – Sept 30):** Roll out communications to the 2,500 beta users and the general public, ensuring support staff are briefed on the new rates.

**2. Decision Authority Matrix**
*   **Requires Your Final Decision (CEO/Lead):**
    *   **Base Rate & Surge Multipliers:** The specific dollar amounts for the "Standard" and "Flash" tiers.
    *   **Subscription Cap:** The maximum monthly fee for unlimited deliveries.
    *   **Loss Leader Strategy:** Approval on whether to subsidize specific merchant zones to drive adoption.
*   **Product Team Can Finalize:**
    *   **UI/UX Presentation:** How the prices are displayed within the app interface.
    *   **Algorithm Parameters:** The specific weightings for route efficiency vs. battery conservation.
    *   **Merchant Contract Terms:** Standard legal clauses and onboarding workflows (excluding the final rate card).
    *   **Marketing Copy:** The wording of the announcement, provided it reflects the approved prices.

**3. First Three Actionable Items**
1.  **Conduct Pricing War-Game (Today):** Convene a 90-minute session with finance and product leads to simulate three pricing scenarios (Conservative, Aggressive, Balanced) against current beta usage data. Select the single model to submit by September 10th.
2.  **Lock Geographic Fences (Tomorrow):** Define the exact operational boundaries for the 60 scooters in the financial district. Map these zones to the chosen pricing tiers to ensure the route optimization engine has fixed parameters to begin testing.
3.  **Draft "No-Rollback" Certification (Day 3):** Create a sign-off document listing the exact JSON payload for the app store update. Require signatures from you and the Lead Engineer confirming that these rates are final, preventing last-minute changes that could trigger the refund cascade.

*Note: The September 10th deadline is the critical path; all other timelines depend on this submission occurring without error.*
*Ensure the 60-scooter maintenance schedule is integrated into the route optimization phase immediately after pricing is locked.*
