### Kickoff Session Structure: Volunteer Coordination Platform

**1. Priority Stage: Role Definition & Capacity Mapping**
We must tackle **Role Definition** first. Before any scheduling logic can be built or assignments reviewed, we must explicitly define the headcount and skill requirements for each of the ten medical stations. Without a clear "demand" baseline (e.g., Triage needs 20 volunteers, Vaccination needs 30), any scheduling algorithm or recruitment drive will be inefficient. This stage establishes the target numbers required to hit the 200-volunteer goal.

**2. Delegable Administrative Duties (Junior Coordinators)**
Assign the following execution-heavy tasks to your four junior coordinators to maximize their bandwidth while you retain strategic oversight:
*   **Recruitment Outreach:** Execute organic recruitment campaigns via community boards, local university networks, and partner non-profits, adhering to the "no paid advertising" constraint.
*   **Data Entry & Verification:** Manage the incoming registration data, verifying credentials and availability against the defined role requirements.
*   **Draft Scheduling:** Populate the initial scheduling logic based on volunteer availability once the role definitions are set.
*   **Communication Drafts:** Prepare initial welcome packets and shift reminder templates for your review.

**3. Strategic Choices Requiring Your Personal Decision**
As the lead, you must resolve these high-impact, irreversible variables:
*   **Critical Station Thresholds:** Define the minimum staffing floor for critical care areas (Triage, Vaccination). Determine the specific contingency protocol if these floors are not met by March 15th.
*   **Assignment Finality Policy:** Establish the hard deadline for the "lock-down" of shifts. You must decide the exact date when the registration portal closes and no further swaps are permitted to prevent care gaps.
*   **Escalation Authority:** Determine who has the final sign-off on exceptions (e.g., a last-minute volunteer swap in a critical zone) after the portal closes. This authority must rest solely with you or one designated senior lead.

**Workflow Sequence**
1.  **Define Roles:** Quantify needs per station.
2.  **Build Logic:** Configure scheduling rules based on those needs.
3.  **Review:** Station leads validate draft assignments against operational reality.
4.  **Announce:** Publish the final, locked roster.

***

**First Action Required:**
Immediately convene the five coordinators to finalize the **Volunteer Headcount Matrix**, specifying the exact number of volunteers and required skill sets for each of the ten medical stations to establish the recruitment target.
