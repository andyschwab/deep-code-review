**Strategy Session Plan: Autonomous Forklift Deployment**

**1. Critical Sequence of Operations**
To meet the December 1 deadline while mitigating the risk of irreversible downtime, the project must follow this strict linear progression:
*   **Phase 1: Digital Simulation & Layout Finalization (Now – Nov 10):** All floor markings, traffic lanes, and charging station locations must be modeled digitally and simulated for conflict with human operators. No physical changes occur in this phase.
*   **Phase 2: Stakeholder Approval Gate (Nov 10):** A hard stop for final sign-off on the digital layout. Once passed, physical reconfiguration begins.
*   **Phase 3: Physical Installation (Nov 11 – Nov 24):** Execution of floor marking reconfiguration and charging station installation. This is the "point of no return."
*   **Phase 4: Integrated Operator Training (Nov 25 – Dec 1):** Training occurs *on the new layout* with the actual units. Human operators (40) and autonomous units (12) practice shared workflows.

**2. Decision Authority Matrix**
To prevent bottlenecks and ensure accountability, decisions are categorized as follows:

*   **Require Your Personal Approval (Irreversible/High Cost):**
    *   **Final Floor Geometry:** The exact coordinates of new traffic lanes and exclusion zones. Changing these after Nov 10 triggers the two-week reversal penalty.
    *   **Charging Station Infrastructure:** Permanent placement of high-voltage charging docks, as moving them involves electrical rewiring and floor patching.
    *   **Go/No-Go Decision for Phase 3:** The explicit authorization to break ground on floor markings on Nov 11.

*   **Safety Team Validation (Compliance/Risk Mitigation):**
    *   **Sensor Blind Spot Analysis:** Verifying that the 12 units' sensors detect humans in all proposed traffic patterns.
    *   **Emergency Stop Protocols:** Validating that emergency stops function correctly at the intersection of human and machine paths.
    *   **Signage and Lighting:** Ensuring new visual cues meet occupational safety standards before installation begins.

**3. First Concrete Action**
**Initiate a "Digital Twin" Walkthrough immediately.**
Before any meeting or purchase, gather the floor manager, safety lead, and two senior forklift operators to review a digital overlay of the proposed 12-unit paths against current human workflows. Do not discuss hardware yet. The sole objective of this session is to identify *one* potential conflict point between the autonomous fleet and the 40 human operators in the proposed layout. Identifying this conflict now prevents the $50,000/day downtime risk later.

**Note:** The November 10 deadline for layout finalization is the single most critical date; missing it jeopardizes the December 1 operational start.
Ensure the safety team signs off on the digital simulation results, not just the physical installation.
