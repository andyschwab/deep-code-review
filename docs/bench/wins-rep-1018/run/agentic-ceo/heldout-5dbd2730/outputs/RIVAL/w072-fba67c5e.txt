**Deliverable: Initial Work Session Plan for Haven Grief Services**

**Primary Focus: Content Moderation Workflows**
You must begin the session by establishing content moderation workflows. Given that the irreversible publication of private data poses the highest risk of emotional harm, security configurations and user experience mapping are secondary until the rules governing what can be published are strictly defined. You cannot secure a platform or design a user journey without first knowing exactly what data is entering the system and the specific protocols for handling it.

**Decision-Making Authority**
*   **Content Team Autonomy:** The four writers may autonomously draft stories, conduct initial interviews, and format text according to style guides. They can also flag potential red flags (e.g., mentions of minors, traumatic details) for review.
*   **Program Director Intervention (Mandatory):** You must personally intervene to approve any material containing photos, names of deceased individuals under 18, specific location data, or contact details. You are the final gatekeeper for the "50-family" dataset. No story moves from "draft" to "ready for development" without your explicit sign-off on privacy clearance.

**Specific First Action**
The specific first action to undertake in this session is to **co-create the "Red Line" Checklist and Approval Matrix.**

Do not start writing or designing. Instead, gather the team and define the absolute non-negotiables. Create a physical or digital checklist that every single story must pass before it reaches you. This checklist should include items such as:
*   "Are all phone numbers/email addresses removed?"
*   "Do we have written consent for every photo used?"
*   "Are last names of living relatives obscured?"

Once the checklist is defined, map the approval workflow: Writer → Self-Check against List → Director Review → Final Approval. This ensures that trust and safety are baked into the process before a single line of code is written or a pixel designed.

**Note:** With a $30,000 budget and a January 10th deadline, this upfront clarity prevents costly rework and legal liability later.
**Note:** The expected 500 visitors is a low volume, so prioritize manual review accuracy over automated scaling solutions.
