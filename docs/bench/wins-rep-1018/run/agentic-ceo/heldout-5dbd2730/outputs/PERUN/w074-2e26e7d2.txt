**Decision: Staggered Rollout with Manual Gate Before Real-Time Activation**

**The Call:** Do not activate the real-time alert system on Monday. Proceed with a 48-hour parallel pilot in **Emergency Services** only, keeping alerts in "log-only" mode. Full real-time paging activation is deferred until after the board review data baseline is established, unless the pilot shows zero false positives over 48 hours.

**Figures:** 1 pilot dept (Emergency) x 48 hours = ~3,300 responses (based on 50k/mo volume); 0 real-time pages sent during pilot; 4-hour reboot cost avoided if logic fails.

**Rollout Order & Rationale:**
1.  **Emergency Services (Pilot):** Highest volume and most critical sentiment spikes. Validates the system under maximum load.
2.  **Outpatient Clinics:** High volume, lower immediate risk than ER.
3.  **Inpatient Wards:** Moderate volume, complex context.
4.  **Surgery:** Low volume, high stakes (requires highest accuracy).
5.  **Pediatrics:** Specialized language models needed.
6.  **Administration:** Lowest volume, internal focus.

**Configuration Decisions:**
*   **Owner (You) Must Decide:**
    *   **Sentiment Thresholds:** Define the exact score that triggers a "negative" flag (e.g., <0.3 vs <0.4). This dictates alert fatigue.
    *   **Escalation Protocol:** Who specifically gets paged? (Define the on-call roster).
    *   **False Positive Tolerance:** Acceptable rate before halting rollout (Recommendation: >2% false positives = stop).
*   **Engineers Can Finalize:**
    *   **Data Ingestion Pipeline:** Connection to survey database.
    *   **Logging Format:** How "log-only" results are stored for review.
    *   **Reboot Script:** Automated procedure for the 4-hour recovery if needed.
    *   **Dashboard Visualization:** Displaying the pilot data for department heads.

**Immediate First Step (Accuracy Verification):**
Run a **historical back-test** immediately using last month's 8,000 verified survey responses. Compare the tool's categorization against the manual tags already in the system.
*   **Success Criteria:** >90% alignment on negative sentiment identification.
*   **Action:** If alignment is <90%, engineers must retrain the model before the Monday pilot. If >90%, proceed to the 48-hour Emergency Services parallel run.

**Halt Trigger:** If the historical back-test shows >10% false positives OR if the 48-hour pilot generates >5 erroneous pages to on-call managers.
**Fallback Trigger:** If the parallel pilot reveals a data ingestion lag >15 minutes, revert to manual processing for Emergency Services and delay rollout to Outpatient by 2 weeks.

**Gap to Headline:** The goal to "identify service gaps before December 15th" is at risk if the back-test fails. Conservative estimate adds 5 days for model retraining, pushing the earliest full rollout to December 20th unless the pilot passes flawlessly.

**Sign-offs Required:**
*   **Clinical Operations Director:** Approval of sentiment thresholds.
*   **IT Lead:** Confirmation of reboot script readiness.
*   **Department Head (Emergency):** Agreement to participate in the 48-hour parallel validation.

Note: The 4-hour reboot constraint makes the "log-only" pilot phase non-negotiable for risk mitigation.
Do not authorize real-time paging until the 48-hour parallel run completes with owner sign-off on the error rate.
