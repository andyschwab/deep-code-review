**Decision:** Validate hardware first, then train staff. You cannot train effectively on an unverified system, and a 48-hour irreversible shutdown demands confirmed hardware readiness before committing the window.

**Figures:** 14 days total; 48-hour shutdown window; 200 surgeries at risk; Vendor sign-off due Tuesday the 12th.

**Owner Approvals (Do Not Delegate):**
1.  **Shutdown Authorization:** Explicit written approval for the 48-hour line stop.
2.  **Surgery Rescheduling:** Final sign-off on the list of 200 affected procedures and their new dates.
3.  **Vendor Contract:** Final acceptance of the Tuesday the 12th sign-off terms.
4.  **Go/No-Go Call:** The final decision to initiate the 48-hour window based on pre-shutdown validation results.

**Delegated to Shift Supervisors:**
1.  Staff scheduling for the training sessions.
2.  Physical inventory counting and boxing of current sterile supplies.
3.  Coordination of vendor technicians for hardware installation.
4.  Execution of the dry-run drills once the plan is approved.

**First Four Hours: War Room Session Plan**

**Hour-by-Hour Agenda:**

*   **Hour 1: Risk & Schedule Alignment.** Review the 200 surgeries at risk. Head of Scheduling presents the rescheduling draft. You approve the rescheduling strategy. Define the exact start and end timestamps for the 48-hour shutdown.
*   **Hour 2: Hardware Validation Protocol.** Lead Biomedical Engineer and Vendor Lead define the "Pass/Fail" criteria for hardware validation. Establish the test script to be run *before* the shutdown begins. If hardware fails this pre-test, the shutdown is cancelled.
*   **Hour 3: Training Curriculum Review.** Shift Supervisors review the training modules. Confirm that training can be completed in shifts without halting current operations prior to the shutdown. Set training completion deadlines (must be 100% complete before shutdown starts).
*   **Hour 4: Communication & Contingency.** Draft the internal announcement to surgical staff. Define the "Abort Criteria" during the 48-hour window (what specific failure triggers a rollback to manual tracking?). Assign a single point of contact for the vendor during the shutdown.

**Halt Trigger:** If hardware pre-validation fails any single critical test by Day 10, halt the shutdown plan and revert to manual audit trails for the Joint Commission inspection.

**Fallback Trigger:** If vendor sign-off is not received by Monday the 11th (day before deadline), delay deployment and prepare a manual compliance briefing for the Joint Commission instead of risking an incomplete system launch.
