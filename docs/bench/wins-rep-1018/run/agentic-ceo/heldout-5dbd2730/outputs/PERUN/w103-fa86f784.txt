**Decision: Halt full deployment. Execute a controlled, time-boxed pilot on 0.5% of traffic (25 instances) immediately.**

Do not deploy to all 5,000 instances. The risk of irreversible failure before the market opens outweighs the potential gain of hitting 90% accuracy globally. A full deployment is a binary gamble; a pilot is a measurable experiment. If the pilot fails, we contain the blast radius to 25 customers and retain the ability to claim "system maintenance" rather than "catastrophic model failure." If it succeeds, we have live data to reference in the call, even if global rollout happens post-call.

**Three-Hour Execution Sequence**

**Hour 1: Containment and Baseline (T-minus 180 to 120 mins)**
*   **Action:** Isolate 25 non-critical customer instances. Deploy the new neural network update *only* to this subset.
*   **Decision Authority:** Technical Lead authorizes the selection of instances and the deployment command.
*   **Required Approval:** You must explicitly authorize the "Pilot Scope" (the specific list of 25 customers) to ensure no VIP accounts are impacted.
*   **Outcome:** Establish a live baseline. If accuracy drops below 82% or latency spikes >10%, the system auto-reverts these 25 instances immediately.

**Hour 2: Measurement and Stabilization (T-minus 120 to 60 mins)**
*   **Action:** Monitor the 25 instances. Aggregate accuracy metrics in 15-minute windows.
*   **Decision Authority:** Technical Lead manages the monitoring dashboard and prepares the "Go/No-Go" recommendation based on pre-defined thresholds (Accuracy >88%, Latency stable).
*   **Required Approval:** You must approve the "Call Narrative" at T-minus 75 mins. If the pilot shows >88% accuracy, the narrative is "Early results confirm trajectory to 90%." If the pilot is flat or volatile, the narrative shifts to "Optimization phase ongoing; current stability at 82% is the conservative baseline."
*   **Outcome:** A verified data point. Either a confirmed upward trend or a confirmed containment of failure.

**Hour 3: Narrative Lock and Contingency (T-minus 60 to 0 mins)**
*   **Action:** Finalize earnings call script. Prepare the "Pause" statement if the pilot failed.
*   **Decision Authority:** Technical Lead certifies the final metric from the pilot.
*   **Required Approval:** You approve the final script. No further code changes are permitted after T-minus 30 mins.
*   **Outcome:** A defensible position for investors. You are either reporting verified early success or demonstrating rigorous risk management by avoiding a catastrophic rollout.

**Decision Matrix for Approval**
1.  **Pilot Scope (List of 25 customers):** You approve. (Prevents VIP churn).
2.  **Deployment Command:** Technical Lead authorizes. (Speed).
3.  **Abort Trigger:** Automated if pilot accuracy <82%. No human approval needed to save the platform.
4.  **Call Narrative:** You approve. (Investor relations strategy).

**First Concrete Action (Next 10 Minutes)**
Instruct the Technical Lead to generate a list of 25 low-volume, non-VIP customer instances and present it to you for immediate sign-off. Do not discuss the model architecture, do not review the code, and do not debate the 90% target. Your sole focus for the next ten minutes is validating that the 25 chosen customers will not cause reputational damage if the update fails. Once you sign that list, the pilot begins.

*Note: This approach prioritizes capital preservation over optimistic forecasting. The market penalizes crashes far more severely than it rewards unverified promises.*
