**Decision:** Do not sign the digital compliance ledger tonight. Submitting a ledger based on procedures updated and staff retrained within a 10-hour window for 300 compounds and 60 staff creates a provable discrepancy between the documented protocol and operational reality, guaranteeing the shutdown order you seek to avoid. The single most critical document to draft in the next hour is a **Formal Notice of Pending Compliance Update**, filed with the inspecting agency, stating that while physical safeguards are active, the digital ledger and formal training records are undergoing final verification to ensure accuracy, requesting the audit commence at 10 AM instead of 8 AM to allow for this mandatory quality gate.

**Prioritization of Chemical Categories:**
Address categories in this exact order based on immediate physical risk and audit visibility:
1.  **Volatile/Reactive Compounds:** Any substance with flash points below ambient temperature or potential for exothermic reaction. These pose immediate physical danger if handling is flawed; inspectors will check these first.
2.  **High-Toxicity/Carcinogenic Agents:** Substances requiring PPE and exposure monitoring. Discrepancies here carry the highest legal liability and fines.
3.  **Corrosives and Oxidizers:** High visibility in storage and handling; errors here are easily spotted during a walk-through.
4.  **General Hazardous Materials:** Lower immediate risk; update these only if time permits after the top three are secured.

**Training Delegation Strategy:**
*   **You Must Certify Personally:** Only the **Emergency Response and Spill Containment protocols** for the top two chemical categories (Volatile and High-Toxicity). Your signature here attests to the facility's readiness to prevent catastrophe. This limits your direct liability to life-safety events.
*   **Delegate to Shift Supervisors:** Routine handling, storage stacking, PPE donning/doffing, and waste segregation for all 300 compounds. Have supervisors conduct "toolbox talk" style briefings and sign off on attendance logs immediately. These logs serve as interim proof of training until formal modules are completed. The digital ledger can note "Training in progress, supervised by [Name]" for these items without claiming full certification.

**Execution Plan for Remaining Time:**
1.  **Hour 1 (Now):** Draft and transmit the Formal Notice of Pending Compliance Update to delay the audit start time.
2.  **Hours 2–5:** Safety officers update handling procedures for the top 3 chemical categories only. Documentation specialists format these for the ledger.
3.  **Hours 3–6 (Parallel):** Shift supervisors conduct delegated training briefings for routine handling. You conduct the critical emergency response briefing for the top 2 categories.
4.  **Hours 6–9:** Documentation specialists input the top 3 categories into the ledger. You review and digitally sign only those entries.
5.  **Hours 9–10:** Final walk-through of the top 3 category storage areas to ensure physical alignment with the new documents.
6.  **Midnight:** Submit the ledger with the top 3 categories marked "Compliant" and the remaining 297 marked "Update Pending – Scheduled for [Date]." This is legally defensible; claiming full compliance for all 300 is not.

**Note:** Attempting to force 300 compound updates and 60 full retrainings in 10 hours guarantees errors that inspectors will find. Partial, accurate compliance is safer than total, fraudulent compliance.
