**Decision: Keep the override feature in ADVISORY mode for the first 30 days.**
The single deciding factor is the pilot failure rate: 2 near-misses in 25 cases equals an 8% error rate. At this rate, automatic overrides would guarantee patient harm. The 15% readmission reduction goal cannot be pursued if the safety floor is breached. We will re-evaluate automatic overrides only after the error rate drops below 0.1% in a controlled simulation.

**Figures:**
*   Pilot Error Rate: 2 / 25 = 8.0% (Stated goal for safety: <0.1%)
*   Required Safe Volume for Validation: 300 error-free simulated cases before live auto-override consideration.
*   Timeline Adjustment: Go-live date remains December 1st for *advisory* mode; auto-override target moved to January 15th contingent on metrics.

**Required Training Materials for Approval Today:**
1.  **"The Human-in-the-Loop Protocol":** A one-page checklist detailing exactly when a planner must ignore the bot's suggestion and how to log that discrepancy without penalty.
2.  **"Anatomy of a Near-Miss":** A redacted case study of the two pilot failures, explaining specifically why the logic failed and how the human planner caught it.
3.  **"Escalation Pathway":** A clear flowchart showing who to call (clinical lead vs. technical lead) when the bot flags a conflict the planner cannot resolve in under 2 minutes.

**Required Stakeholder Sign-offs on Safety Protocol:**
1.  **Chief Medical Officer (CMO):** Must sign off that the advisory workflow does not violate clinical standards of care.
2.  **Director of Nursing/Discharge Planning:** Must certify that the 40 planners have completed the simulation and understand the "ignore" protocol.
3.  **Legal/Compliance Officer:** Must acknowledge the risk acceptance of the advisory period and the specific conditions required to switch to auto-override.
4.  **IT Security Lead:** Must confirm that all bot recommendations and human overrides are immutably logged for audit.

**First Live Simulation Design (Before Touching Real Records):**
*   **Scenario:** 50 historical discharge cases known to have complex medication conflicts (including the 2 pilot near-misses).
*   **Participants:** All 40 discharge planners, rotated in shifts.
*   **Setup:** The bot runs in "Shadow Mode" (seeing real data but affecting nothing) while planners perform manual reconciliation.
*   **Success Criteria:**
    1.  Planners identify 100% of the known conflicts the bot missed in the pilot.
    2.  Planners correctly override the bot in 100% of the cases where the bot was wrong.
    3.  Average time-to-discharge does not increase by more than 5 minutes per patient compared to baseline.
*   **Fail Condition:** If any planner accepts an incorrect bot recommendation during the simulation, the go-live is halted for retraining.

**Halt Trigger:** If more than 1% of live advisory recommendations are overridden by planners due to perceived error in the first week, auto-override development stops immediately.
**Fallback Trigger:** If the simulation reveals a third distinct category of logic error not seen in the pilot, revert to 100% manual reconciliation until patched.

*Note: This plan prioritizes patient safety over the December 1st automation target. The "advisory" status is a safety gate, not a delay.*
*All training and simulation data must be stored in a HIPAA-compliant environment; do not use real patient names in training materials.*
