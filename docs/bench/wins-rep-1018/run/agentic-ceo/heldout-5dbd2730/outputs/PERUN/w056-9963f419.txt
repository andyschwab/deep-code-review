**Decision:** Launch Building 4 (Emergency Department) first at 06:00 tomorrow; defer full campus cutover to a phased schedule over 72 hours to mitigate the risk of total system failure against the Tuesday compliance deadline.

**Figures:** 200 staff / 3 rooms = 67 staff per room. At 15 minutes per briefing, one room processes 4 staff/hour. Three rooms process 12 staff/hour. To train 200 staff requires 16.7 hours of continuous room usage. This exceeds the available time before the "hard" Tuesday deadline if done sequentially per building. The corrected approach is "Train-the-Trainer": train 12 Floor Managers (1 hour total), who then train their teams on-site during the rollout.

**Rollout Structure & Delegation:**
1.  **First Building:** Building 4 (Emergency Department). It has the highest volume of stretcher requests and the lowest tolerance for phone-line delays. Success here proves the system under pressure; failure here is contained to one zone rather than paralyzing the whole campus.
2.  **Floor Manager Tasks:**
    *   Conduct 10-minute "huddle" training for their specific shift teams using the provided one-page cheat sheet (no room booking required).
    *   Act as the local "super-user" to manually log any failed digital requests on paper backups immediately.
    *   Verify that all tablets/kiosks in their zone are logged in and connected to the new platform by 05:45 tomorrow.
3.  **Leader (Your) Go/No-Go Decisions:**
    *   **Go/No-Go 1 (Tonight 20:00):** Confirm 100% of Floor Managers have completed the "Train-the-Trainer" session. If <100%, delay Building 4 launch.
    *   **Go/No-Go 2 (Tomorrow 05:45):** Confirm connectivity in Building 4. If any kiosk is offline, authorize the manual paper fallback for that specific zone only.
    *   **Go/No-Go 3 (The Switch):** Only flip the main switch to disable the old phone line after the first 10 successful digital dispatches in Building 4 are confirmed. Do not flip based on time; flip based on verified transactions.

**First Three Actions (Next Hour):**
1.  **Convene the 12 Floor Managers immediately** for a 45-minute emergency briefing to designate them as trainers and distribute the one-page cheat sheets.
2.  **Draft and distribute the "Manual Fallback Protocol"** to all managers, defining exactly how to record a transport request if the system crashes (this is your compliance safety net).
3.  **Schedule the Building 4 "Soft Launch" window** (06:00–08:00) and notify the compliance officer that the full campus cutover is now a phased rollout to ensure data integrity, securing their buy-in before the deadline pressure peaks.

**Halt Trigger:** If more than 3 transport requests fail to route digitally in Building 4 within the first 30 minutes, halt the rollout and revert Building 4 to phone dispatch immediately.
**Fallback Trigger:** If any Floor Manager reports unable to access the training materials by 18:00 today, pause their building's inclusion in the Phase 1 rollout.

*Note: The original "all buildings tomorrow" plan is mathematically impossible given the training bottleneck; the phased approach protects the Tuesday deadline by ensuring the first building works perfectly before expanding.*
