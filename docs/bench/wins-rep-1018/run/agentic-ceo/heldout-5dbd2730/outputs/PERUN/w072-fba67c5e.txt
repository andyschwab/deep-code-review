**Decision:** Begin immediately with **Content Moderation Workflows**. Do not start with platform security configuration or user experience mapping.

**Rationale:** Your primary risk is irreversible emotional harm from data exposure, not system intrusion or navigation friction. Security configuration protects the perimeter, but moderation workflows protect the specific data instances (photos, contact details) inside. If the workflow for approving a story is flawed, a perfectly secured server will still publish harmful content. UX mapping is secondary until the safety gate is defined.

**Autonomy vs. Intervention Matrix:**

| Decision Area | Content Team Autonomy | Program Director Intervention Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Story Editing** | Grammar, tone, narrative flow, formatting. | **Mandatory.** Final approval of every story before it enters the "ready" queue. |
| **Privacy Scrubbing** | Initial redaction of obvious names/phones using a checklist. | **Mandatory.** Spot-check audit of 100% of stories for hidden metadata and background photo details. |
| **Photo Selection** | Cropping, resizing, alt-text writing. | **Mandatory.** Explicit sign-off on every image file to ensure no unauthorized individuals or locations are visible. |
| **Publication Timing** | Scheduling draft uploads to the staging environment. | **Mandatory.** The "Publish" button for the live site requires your direct credential or digital signature. |
| **User Feedback** | Drafting responses to general inquiries. | **Mandatory.** Any response involving a specific family's story or complaint. |

**Specific First Action for This Session:**
Draft the **"Four-Eyes Publication Protocol"** document. This single-page rule set must define that no asset (text or image) moves to the live site without two distinct approvals: one from a writer (checking narrative) and one from you (checking privacy/sensitivity). Include a specific checklist item for "Background Scan" in photos to catch accidental exposures of addresses or other families.

**Note:** With a $30k budget and Jan 10th deadline, do not custom-build complex permission software; use your existing CMS's draft/review states enforced by this manual protocol.
**Note:** The 500-visitor estimate is irrelevant to the safety protocol; the risk magnitude is determined by the 50 families, not the audience size.
