**Decision: Deploy to one pilot clinic first.**
The single deciding factor is the unresolved legal flag on three edge cases combined with the irreversible nature of the intake form change; a full rollout risks missing high-risk preeclampsia indicators across 150 staff members immediately, whereas a pilot limits exposure while validating the fix.

**Figures:** 150 staff / 4 specialists = 37.5 staff per specialist (unmanageable for simultaneous go-live training by Oct 1); 92% accuracy on 500 cases implies ~40 missed/high-risk misclassifications if applied broadly without fixing the 3 flagged edge cases.
