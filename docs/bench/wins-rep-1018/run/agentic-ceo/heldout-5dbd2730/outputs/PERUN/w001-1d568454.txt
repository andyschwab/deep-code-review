**Decision:** Do not attempt a global rollout in a single four-hour window. The risk of an irreversible failure affecting 45,000 patients is unacceptable. The strategy must be a **staged, canary deployment** starting with a internal/control group, extending the effective timeline beyond the initial four-hour regulatory window for the full fleet.

**Critical Data Points to Verify Before Session:**
1.  **Transfer Duration:** Exact time required to push the update to a single device under varied network conditions.
2.  **Failure Rate Baseline:** Historical success/failure rates of previous OTA (Over-The-Air) updates for this specific device model.
3.  **Rollback/Recovery:** Since the update is irreversible, define the specific manual intervention required if a device "bricks" during transfer (e.g., physical dock replacement).
4.  **Battery Threshold:** Minimum battery percentage required on the device to *start* the update to prevent mid-cycle power loss.

**Proposed Session Sequence:**

**Phase 1: Risk Quantification (First 45 mins)**
*   **Action:** Engineering leads present the "worst-case scenario" math. If 1% of devices fail during the 4-hour window, that is 450 patients without pumps.
*   **Autonomous Decision:** Engineers define the technical constraints (bandwidth limits, server load).
*   **Owner Sign-off Required:** Acceptable failure rate threshold (likely must be 0% for the first batch).

**Phase 2: Segmentation Strategy (Next 45 mins)**
*   **Action:** Define the "Canary" group. Recommend selecting 50–100 devices from internal staff or highly monitored clinical partners, not random patients.
*   **Decision Logic:** Do not segment by region initially; segment by **risk profile**. The first batch must be recoverable within minutes.
*   **Owner Sign-off Required:** Approval of the specific criteria for the Canary group and the communication plan sent to them.

**Phase 3: The "Stop" Protocol (Next 30 mins)**
*   **Action:** Establish hard gates. If *any* device in the Canary group fails to acknowledge receipt within X minutes, the global push aborts automatically.
*   **Autonomous Decision:** Engineers set the technical triggers for the abort sequence.
*   **Owner Sign-off Required:** Authority to halt the entire rollout if minor anomalies appear (safety over speed).

**Phase 4: Timeline Reconciliation (Final 30 mins)**
*   **Action:** Map the staged rollout against the deadline. The 4-hour window applies to the *initiation* of the Canary phase. Full global deployment may need to span 48–72 hours in micro-batches to ensure monitoring capacity.
*   **Owner Sign-off Required:** Approval to request a regulatory amendment or notification that full deployment will extend past Friday if the Canary phase requires analysis time.

**Immediate Owner Sign-off Summary:**
1.  Authorization to delay full global completion past Friday if the Canary phase indicates instability.
2.  Approval of the "Zero-Tolerance" abort policy.
3.  Selection criteria for the initial 100-device test group.

**Engineering Lead Autonomous Scope:**
1.  Technical implementation of the abort triggers.
2.  Server load balancing calculations.
3.  Drafting the technical script for the data transfer.

**Note:** The irreversible nature of this update dictates that speed is secondary to verifiable success in the first batch.
