**Decision:** Proceed with a **Phased Pilot Rollout** starting immediately, targeting 10 pharmacies today, 40 tomorrow, and the remainder by Friday noon. Do not attempt a simultaneous "big bang" deployment to all 200 sites; the risk of synchronized legacy counter lockout and unmanageable support volume is too high.

**Figures:** 200 pharmacies / 5 coordinators = 40 sites per coordinator. At 30 minutes per site (conservative estimate for verification), total human-hours required = 100 hours. With 3 days remaining (approx. 24 working hours per coordinator), capacity is 120 hours. **Margin is tight (20 hours buffer); any rework triggers failure.**

**Order of Operations & Delegation:**

1.  **Immediate Action (Owner Approval Required):** Define the **Rollback Trigger Threshold**. You must explicitly approve: "If >2 of the first 10 pharmacies report data mismatch or legacy lockout, halt all deployment and execute rollback."
2.  **Delegated to Field Coordinators (Prep Phase):**
    *   Contact assigned 40 pharmacies each to confirm a specific 30-minute maintenance window before Friday.
    *   Verify local workstation OS versions and network static IP assignments against the central whitelist.

3.  **Delegated to DBAs (Config Phase):**

    *   Validate stock count alignment immediately post-deploy.
5.  **Full Rollout:** Only upon successful validation of the pilot group.

*   *Success Criteria:* Round-trip latency <200ms and successful authentication token exchange. If this fails, no deployment occurs.

2.  **Revert:** DBAs restore the legacy inventory counters from the pre-deployment snapshot taken at step 3.
3.  **Notify:** Field coordinators inform the remaining 190 pharmacies that the window is cancelled.
4.  **Analyze:** Isolate the error log from the failed pilot sites before attempting a fix.

1.  **Rollback Threshold:** Confirm the "2 out of 10" failure rule.
