**Decision:** Halt all feature development and user testing immediately; the only permissible next action is a full "Data Handling & Output Safety" audit before any weights are published or the 10-day legal window closes.

**Figures:** 10 days (legal window) - 0 days (audit time required) = **0 days remaining for fixes** if audit does not start today. The stated launch date (15th next month) is unreachable unless the audit clears within 72 hours; otherwise, the launch must slip by at least 3 weeks to accommodate re-certification.

**Approach First:**
Adopt a "Compliance-First Freeze." Do not freeze features based on functionality; freeze them based on regulatory risk. Your immediate priority is constructing the **Audit Trail Artifact**, a living document that maps every data input, model decision, and output to a specific compliance requirement (e.g., HIPAA, GDPR). This document must be signed off by your 3 compliance officers before a single line of code is locked.

**Specific Compliance Gaps You Must Resolve Personally:**
As the leader, you cannot delegate these three high-liability decisions:
1.  **Irreversibility of Weights:** You must personally sign off on the exact snapshot of training data weights being submitted. Once published, any bias or hallucination found later requires full re-certification. You need a formal "Risk Acceptance" document stating that the current weights have been vetted for PII leakage and harmful output patterns.
2.  **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** For a crisis chatbot, fully automated responses to high-risk keywords (suicide, self-harm) are likely non-compliant without a human escalation path. You must define the threshold where the bot stops answering and routes to a human, and verify this logic is hard-coded, not learned.
3.  **Data Sovereignty & Retention:** You must explicitly authorize the data retention policy. Since you cannot expose 500 testers to unvetted responses, you must decide if their data is deleted immediately post-session or stored for audit. If stored, you must confirm encryption standards and access logs are active *now*, not post-launch.

**Immediate Next Step to Secure Data Handling:**
Execute a **"Dry-Run Audit"** on a closed subset of data (no live users).
1.  Isolate a non-production environment.
2.  Run the 3 compliance officers through a scripted simulation of 50 high-risk crisis scenarios.
3.  Record every system action, data touchpoint, and response generated.
4.  Compare this log against your regulatory checklist.
5.  Only when this log is clean do you proceed to feature freezing.

**Halt Trigger:** If the Dry-Run Audit reveals *any* unlogged data transmission or a single unvetted high-risk response, stop all progress. Do not proceed to feature freeze until the specific failure mode is patched and re-audited.

**Fallback Trigger:** If the legal review window (10 days) closes without a signed audit trail from all 3 officers, the launch date automatically shifts to "TBD." Do not attempt to launch with a partial sign-off; the liability of a crisis chatbot failure outweighs the cost of a delay.

*Note: This plan assumes your legal team has provided the specific regulatory checklist; if not, request it immediately as a blocker. Do not fabricate compliance standards.*
