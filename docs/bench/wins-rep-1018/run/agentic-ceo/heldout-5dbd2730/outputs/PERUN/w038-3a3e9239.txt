**Decision:** Begin immediately with a **Targeted Data Integrity Audit** of the top 20% of active patients (those with complex, high-frequency care needs), not a staff training simulation. Training is useless if the data being migrated is incomplete or corrupted; you cannot train on missing records.

**The Plan: Stages, Ownership, and Timeline**

**Stage 1: Critical Data Triage & Validation (Now – Tuesday EOD)**
*   **Goal:** Ensure the "irreversible risk" is mitigated for the most vulnerable patients first.
*   **IT Specialists Own:** Writing scripts to extract legacy data for the top 16 patients (20% of 80); comparing record counts and field completeness against a checksum; flagging discrepancies.
*   **Nurses Own:** Manually verifying the *clinical accuracy* of those 16 extracted summaries. They must confirm that the last 48 hours of notes, current medication lists, and active care plans match the system output.
*   **Why:** This validates the migration mechanism on the highest-stakes data before scaling.

**Stage 2: Full Extraction & Dry Run (Wednesday AM – Wednesday PM)**
*   **Goal:** Execute the full migration in a sandbox environment.
*   **IT Specialists Own:** Running the full extraction for all 80 patients into the CompassionFirst staging environment. Fixing any format errors identified in Stage 1.
*   **Nurses Own:** Spot-checking a random sample of 10 additional patients (non-critical) to ensure standard records transfer cleanly.
*   **Constraint:** No patient care disruption. This happens in the background.

**Stage 3: Just-in-Time Micro-Training (Thursday AM)**
*   **Goal:** Equip nurses to use the new system for the go-live.
*   **IT Specialists Own:** Providing a one-page "Cheat Sheet" specific to CompassionFirst (how to view a chart, how to enter a vitals check, how to flag an alert). No long simulations.
*   **Nurses Own:** A 30-minute huddle to review the cheat sheet and log into the test environment to perform one dummy entry.
*   **Why:** Nurses are stretched thin; deep simulation is a luxury you cannot afford. Focus only on the critical path tasks they will perform Thursday night.

**Stage 4: Go-Live & Parallel Safety Net (Thursday PM – Midnight)**
*   **Goal:** Cutover with a safety net.
*   **IT Specialists Own:** Executing the final delta sync (capturing changes made Wednesday/Thursday) and switching the DNS to CompassionFirst.
*   **Nurses Own:** Entering real-time care data into CompassionFirst.
*   **Critical Safety Measure:** Until midnight, keep the legacy system open in "Read-Only" mode on a separate monitor or tablet for the Charge Nurse only. If a discrepancy arises, they check the legacy read-only view immediately. Once the clock strikes midnight and the billing cycle aligns, the legacy system is archived.

**The Very First Task to Assign**
Assign the **Charge Nurse** and the **Lead IT Specialist** to jointly identify the list of the **16 highest-acuity patients** within the next 60 minutes.
*   **Action:** IT pulls the data for these 16; the Charge Nurse reviews it against their physical/mental logs.
*   **Reason:** If the migration fails for these 16, patient safety is compromised. Validating this subset proves the pipeline works before you risk the remaining 64.

**Note:** Do not attempt to train all 12 nurses on the full system; focus only on the specific screens they need for tonight's shift.
**Note:** If the Stage 1 audit reveals >5% data corruption, halt the full migration and escalate immediately; do not proceed to Stage 2.
