**Decision:** Proceed with a phased "Parallel-Run" migration strategy, deferring the physical archive destruction until after a successful billing cycle reconciliation in August. The single governing constraint is the vendor's 00:00–04:00 upload window, which dictates all data preparation timelines.

**Figures:** 5,000 appointments ÷ 60 days (now to Aug 1) = ~83 records/day. With 8 therapists, this averages 10 records/therapist/day, well within administrative capacity without overtime.

**Planning Session Agenda & Flow**

1.  **Data Schema Mapping (Day 1-3):** Match physical log columns (Client, Date, Service, Therapist, Notes) to the digital platform's required fields. Identify gaps where physical notes cannot be digitized.
2.  **Pilot Batch & Validation (Day 4-10):** Manually enter 50 records (one week per therapist). Run a test upload during the 00:00–04:00 window. Verify data integrity and loyalty point calculation.
3.  **Staggered Data Entry (Day 11-45):** Administrative staff enter historical data in reverse chronological order (newest first). Most recent data is critical for current billing; older data is secondary for loyalty.
4.  **Parallel Operations (Day 46-55):** Run both physical books and digital scheduling simultaneously for two weeks. Discrepancies are resolved daily.
5.  **Final Reconciliation & Cutover (Day 56-60):** Compare August 1st billing projections from both systems.
6.  **Archival Authorization & Destruction (Post-August 1):** Only after billing matches, authorize shredding.

**Delegation Matrix**

*   **Administrative Staff (Delegated):**
    *   Transcribing appointment details from physical logs to the staging spreadsheet.
    *   Formatting files to meet the vendor's specific CSV/JSON schema.
    *   Executing the file upload during the 00:00–04:00 window.
    *   Flagging illegible handwriting or missing data for manager review.
*   **Owner/Manager (Reserved):**
    *   Defining the data mapping schema.
    *   Resolving ambiguous entries (illegible notes, missing client IDs).
    *   **The Shred Authorization:** Signing the "Data Integrity Certificate" confirming the digital system matches the physical ledger for the current billing period.

**Required Authorization Before Shredding**

Loyalty points have been verified for a random sample of 50 clients. I authorize the destruction of physical logs corresponding to this verified set."* Without this specific sign-off, the physical books remain secure storage, not waste.

**First Concrete Action for Data Integrity**
Initiate a **"Golden Record" audit** immediately. Select 20 random appointments from the physical logs across different therapists and dates. Manually enter these into the new platform as a test batch. Attempt to upload this file during the next available 00:00–04:00 window. Verify that these 20 records appear correctly, including loyalty points. If this micro-test fails, the entire 5,000-record plan halts for format correction before any further data entry begins.

**Halt Trigger:** If the pilot upload fails twice due to format errors, stop all data entry and engage the vendor's technical support before proceeding.
**Fallback Trigger:** If the digital platform is unavailable on August 1st, revert to physical logs for billing and delay the loyalty program launch by 30 days.
