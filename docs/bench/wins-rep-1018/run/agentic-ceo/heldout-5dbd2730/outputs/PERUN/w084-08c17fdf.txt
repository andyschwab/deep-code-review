**Decision:** Proceed with a staggered regional rollout starting immediately, contingent on the pilot validating a <5% failure rate. The single constraint is the 250-hour pairing capacity versus the 58-hour window; parallelization is mandatory.

**Figures:** 3,500 units × 10 mins = 35,000 mins (583 hours total work). 25 technicians × 8 hours/day × 5 days = 1,000 available tech-hours. Capacity exists (583 < 1,000), but only if work is perfectly parallelized across all 5 days. If the pilot fails, the 583-hour requirement cannot be met by the 25 technicians alone before the count.

### 1. Warehouse Grouping Strategy
Group the 50 warehouses into **five regional waves of 10 warehouses each**, deployed sequentially over the five days.
*   **Logic:** Do not split technician crews. Assign all 25 technicians to one region per day. This ensures that if a site-specific issue arises (e.g., Wi-Fi interference), the entire team encounters it simultaneously and can pivot, rather than stranding small teams in failed locations.
*   **Daily Target:** 700 units per day (10 warehouses × ~70 units each).
*   **Efficiency:** Technicians work in pairs (12 pairs + 1 float). Each pair handles ~58 units/day. At 10 mins/unit, this is 580 mins (9.6 hours) of pure pairing time. Overtime or shift staggering is required to hit 700 units/day with 25 people unless the 10-minute estimate includes travel/setup. If 10 mins is *only* pairing, you must add 20% buffer for logistics. Assume 12 hours shifts to absorb logistics.

### 2. Admin Escalation Protocol
Server admins (3 total) act as a mobile triage unit, not a helpdesk. They rotate among the 10 active warehouses.
*   **Admin-Fixable (No Escalation):**
    *   Timeout errors during handshake (retry pairing).
    *   Incorrect server IP/Port configuration on the device.
    *   Local Wi-Fi authentication failures (credential re-entry).
    *   Device firmware mismatch resolvable via local cache.
*   **Escalate to You Immediately:**
    *   Central server database connection loss (affects all units).
    *   Security certificate rejection (indicates a root CA issue).
    *   Failure rate exceeds 10% in a single warehouse after admin intervention.
    *   Any scenario requiring a rollback to legacy devices (since they are wiped).

### 3. Immediate Pilot Test (First 10 Devices)
Execute this within the hour on a isolated bench simulating the warehouse environment:
1.  **Wipe & Swap:** Fully wipe one legacy unit and activate one new unit.
2.  **Pairing Attempt:** Perform the 10-minute pairing process.
3.  **Failure Injection:** Intentionally disconnect Wi-Fi mid-pairing to verify error handling and recovery time.
4.  **Verification:** Scan a test asset and confirm real-time sync with the central server.
5.  **Stop/Go Criteria:**
    *   **GO:** 9/10 devices pair successfully on the first try; recovery from injected failure takes <2 mins.
    *   **NO-GO:** >1 device fails to recover; or sync latency >30 seconds. Halt deployment and investigate server logs before touching warehouse inventory.

**Halt Trigger:** If the pilot yields >10% failure or central server instability is detected.
**Fallback Trigger:** If Day 1 regional completion is <80% by 6:00 PM, pause Day 2 deployment and re-calculate staffing needs.

*Note: The 10-minute estimate is tight for a full swap including physical mounting; verify if this includes logistics. Ensure legacy devices are not wiped until the new unit in that specific zone is confirmed active.*
