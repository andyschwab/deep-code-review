**Decision:** Execute a 4-stage canary rollout over 68 hours, holding the final 4-hour buffer for emergency rollback. Do not push to 100% of devices.

**Figures:** 10,000 devices × 0.1% failure tolerance = 10 max failures. 4 engineers × 17 hours active monitoring each (staggered shifts) = 68 hours coverage within the 72-hour window.

**Rollout Phasing (Time-Boxed):**
1.  **Stage 1 (Hours 0–4): Internal & Synthetic Canary.** Push to 50 devices (0.5%). These must be lab-controlled or staff-owned units simulating patient physiology. *Goal:* Verify binary integrity and basic handshake.
2.  **Stage 2 (Hours 4–20): Low-Risk Patient Canary.** Push to 450 devices (4.5% total). Select patients with stable historical data and non-critical conditions. *Goal:* Detect edge-case physiological interactions. Max allowable failures: 1.
3.  **Stage 3 (Hours 20–50): Regional Batch.** Push to 4,500 devices (50% total). Group by geographic region to isolate network carrier issues. *Goal:* Stress test OTA infrastructure. Max cumulative allowable failures: 5.
4.  **Stage 4 (Hours 50–68): Remainder.** Push to remaining 5,000 devices only if Stage 3 failure rate is <0.05%.

**Risk Mitigations Requiring Your Explicit Approval:**
*   **The "Hard Stop" Threshold:** Authorize an immediate global halt if *any* single stage exceeds 50% of its allocated failure budget (e.g., 1 failure in Stage 1, 3 in Stage 2). Do not wait for the full stage to complete.
*   **Physical Response Protocol:** Since bricked devices require physical replacement, approve the dispatch of 15 replacement units to the regions covered in Stage 2 and 3 *before* Stage 3 begins. We cannot wait for failures to ship hardware.
*   **Kill-Switch Authority:** Designate one specific engineer (not a committee) with the sole authority to trigger the global halt without seeking further permission during the window.

**First Monitoring Dashboard Requirements:**
Build a real-time view displaying only these three metrics, refreshed every 60 seconds:
1.  **Installation Success Rate (%):** Aggregated per stage. Alert threshold: <99.9%.
2.  **Post-Update Heartbeat Latency (ms):** Time from device wake to server ack. Alert threshold: >200ms increase from baseline (indicates firmware loop).
3.  **Critical Error Count:** Count of devices reporting "boot loop" or "sensor mismatch" flags. Alert threshold: >0.

**Halt Trigger:** Cumulative failures reach 6 devices (60% of total budget). Action: Freeze all queues, initiate physical replacement protocol for affected regions.
**Fallback Trigger:** Stage 2 failure rate exceeds 0.2% (1 device). Action: Revert Stage 2 devices immediately; do not proceed to Stage 3.

*Note: This plan assumes your team can sustain 17-hour shifts; if not, reduce stage sizes to match available monitoring eyes. Do not automate the "proceed" decision between stages; require a manual sign-off after each phase.*
