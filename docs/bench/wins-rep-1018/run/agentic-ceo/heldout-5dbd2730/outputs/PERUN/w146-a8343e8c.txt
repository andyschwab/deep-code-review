**Decision:** Proceed with a "Backup-Verify-Pilot-Rotate" sequence; do not attempt a direct live migration. The single governing constraint is the irreversibility of key rotation without an immediately restorable backup.

**Figures:** 50 TB across 3 clusters = ~16.7 TB/cluster. With 6 specialists (4 security, 2 DBA), parallel pilot on one cluster is feasible while others prepare. Deadline March 1st creates a hard stop; any delay in the pilot phase pushes the compliance date beyond the legal limit.

**Sequence of Operations**

1.  **Immutable Backup & Verification (Hours 0–4):** Before touching encryption, create full snapshots of all three clusters. Crucially, perform a restore test on a sandbox environment to prove the backup is valid. *Failure here stops the entire project.*
2.  **Pilot Re-encryption (Hours 4–24):** Select the smallest or least critical cluster for the first full re-encryption run using new keys. This validates the throughput, identifies performance bottlenecks, and confirms the encryption logic without risking the entire 50 TB estate.
3.  **Staggered Rollout (Days 2–5):** Apply the validated process to the remaining two clusters sequentially or in parallel depending on pilot performance metrics.
4.  **Key Rotation & Final Audit (Day 6):** Once all data is re-encrypted and verified, rotate the master keys permanently. Generate the compliance audit trail immediately.

**First Immediate Actions**

**Halt Trigger:** If the backup restore verification fails, halt all operations. Value: Data integrity. Date: Immediate. Action: Engage disaster recovery team.
