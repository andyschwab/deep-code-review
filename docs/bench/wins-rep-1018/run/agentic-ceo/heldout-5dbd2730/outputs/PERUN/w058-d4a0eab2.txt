**Decision:** Proceed with launch only after completing the single mandatory pre-flight task below; activate accounts in three staggered waves; automate initial triage but manually assign active counseling; escalate only on safety flags or queue breaches.

**Figures:** 8 counselors × 50 sessions = 400 max concurrent capacity vs. 500 expected sign-ups (deficit 100); 500 users / 15 min response window = 33.3 users/min arrival rate vs. 8 counselors × 4 chats/hour (conservative) = 32 chats/hour capacity (bottleneck confirmed).

**1. The Very First Task (Hard Gate)**
Before enabling the public link or sending any notifications, you must **simulate the "501st User" scenario**. Log in all eight counselors to reach 400 active sessions, then attempt to connect 101 additional test accounts. Verify that the system either queues these users with a clear "wait time" message or fails gracefully without crashing the active sessions. If the 500th user cannot connect instantly, the welcome push notification **must not be sent**.

**2. Account Activation Sequence (Staggered Waves)**
Do not activate all eight counselors simultaneously. Stagger logins to monitor platform stability and session distribution:
*   **Wave A (T-Minus 30 mins):** Activate 2 lead counselors. Verify login success and session counting accuracy.
*   **Wave B (T-Minus 15 mins):** Activate 3 additional counselors. Confirm total concurrent count reaches 250 without error.
*   **Wave C (Launch Moment):** Activate final 3 counselors. Total capacity 400 active sessions ready.
*   *Note:* Keep one "floater" account credential ready for immediate swap if a primary account hits the 50-session hard limit unexpectedly.

**3. Routing Logic: Automated vs. Manual**
*   **Automated:** Initial greeting, consent confirmation, and urgency triage (e.g., "Are you having a medical emergency?"). The bot must instantly filter out non-cessation queries and emergency medical cases before they enter the counselor queue.
*   **Manual:** All active counseling interactions. Once a user passes triage, a human must claim the session. Do not auto-assign based on round-robin; allow counselors to pull from a shared "Available" queue to prevent uneven load distribution where one counselor is stuck with a complex case while others sit idle.

**4. Escalation Thresholds Requiring Your Approval**
You are to be alerted immediately only for:
*   **Safety Critical:** Any user indicating immediate self-harm or severe adverse reaction to cessation aids (bypass queue, direct handoff to emergency protocols).
*   **Queue Breach:** If the wait time for a new user exceeds 12 minutes (leaving a 3-minute buffer before the 15-minute SLA breach).
*   **Platform Failure:** If any counselor account is locked or the concurrent session count freezes.

**Fallback Trigger:** If the queue breach occurs twice in the first hour, halt the welcome push notification to the remaining 1,500 patients immediately.

**Halt Trigger:** If the "501st User" simulation fails to queue gracefully, do not launch.

*Note: The 100-user capacity deficit requires either reducing the initial push batch size to 400 users or extending the response SLA expectation.*
