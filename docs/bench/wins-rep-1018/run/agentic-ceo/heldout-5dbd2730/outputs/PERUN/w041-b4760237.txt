**Decision:** Do not start any automated cleansing or manual spot-checks yet. The absolute first action is to **execute a Data Privacy Impact Assessment (DPIA) checkpoint and secure a temporary, isolated staging environment** before any data moves. Moving 5,000 sensitive health records without verifying the encryption and access controls of the destination violates privacy laws immediately, regardless of speed.

**Figures:** 5,000 files / 3 staff / ~6 hours available (assuming 8 AM start to 5 PM deadline) = ~278 files per hour per person if manual. This confirms manual cleaning of all records is impossible; automation is required, but only after safety gates.

**Sequence of Events & Approval Gates:**

1.  **Isolation & Legal Gate (Immediate – 30 mins):**
    *   **Action:** Provision a secure, air-gapped staging database. Verify encryption at rest and in transit.
    *   **Approval Required:** You must explicitly confirm the staging environment meets HIPAA/GDPR (or local equivalent) standards before extraction begins.
    *   **Risk:** Skipping this exposes the company to immediate regulatory fines exceeding the audit penalty.

2.  **Extraction & Profiling (30 mins – 1.5 hours):**
    *   **Action:** Extract a read-only copy of all 5,000 records into the staging environment. Run a non-destructive profiling script to identify duplicate rates, null values, and format errors. Do not alter source data.
    *   **Deliverable:** A "Data Health Report" showing exact counts of duplicates and corruption types.

3.  **Threshold Definition & Strategy Sign-off (1.5 hours – 2 hours):**
    *   **Action:** Based on the report, define the "Clean Enough" threshold.
    *   **Approval Required:** You must approve the specific duplicate handling logic (e.g., "Keep most recent record, archive others") and the acceptable error rate (e.g., "<0.5% critical fields missing") before any cleaning script runs.
    *   **Decision:** We will use an automated cleansing script configured with your approved logic. Manual effort is reserved *only* for records the script flags as "unresolvable."

4.  **Automated Cleansing & Dry Run (2 hours – 4 hours):**
    *   **Action:** Run the cleansing script on the staging copy. Perform a dry-run import into ComplianceHub using a 10% sample (500 records).
    *   **Verification:** Confirm no duplicates were created and no records were locked out.

5.  **Full Migration & Verification (4 hours – 5 PM):**
    *   **Action:** Execute full import. Run a post-migration count verification (Source Count vs. Destination Count + Archive Count).
    *   **Final Approval:** You sign off on the verification report to close the audit requirement.

**Halt Trigger:** If the Data Health Report shows >20% of records lack a unique employee identifier, halt immediately. Automated merging will fail, and manual repair of 1,000+ files by 5 PM is mathematically impossible. We would need to request an audit extension based on data integrity risks.

**Fallback Trigger:** If the dry-run import locks even one record due to a duplicate constraint, halt the full migration. Re-tune the deduplication logic and re-test on a new sample before proceeding.

**Note:** Your compliance officer must oversee the "Archive" step to ensure discarded duplicates are retained for legal discovery, not deleted. Do not allow the analysts to delete raw data under time pressure.
