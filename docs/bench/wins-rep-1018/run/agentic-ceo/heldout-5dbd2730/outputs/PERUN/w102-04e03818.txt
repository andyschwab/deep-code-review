**Decision:** Prioritize building the **matching engine** before the user registration flow. The single most important first step to take today is to **define and freeze the "Minimum Viable Match" criteria** (the specific data points required to successfully pair a farmer load with a carrier) and write the automated tests that verify this logic under high concurrency.

**Rationale:** Your constraint is trust, not traffic. If you open registration first, you risk populating the marketplace with users who cannot transact due to broken logic, causing immediate reputational damage that cannot be reset. With only seven weeks until peak season and a history of 20% spoilage, the core value proposition is reliable transport, not just user accounts. A functional matching engine ensures that when you do open the gates, the first interactions are successful, securing the trust required for network effects to take hold.

**Work Session Plan (7-Week Sprint):**

*   **Weeks 1–3 (Core Build):** Focus 100% of the seven-person product team on the matching engine, load posting, and carrier acceptance workflows. Implement strict concurrency testing immediately to simulate the first week of harvest loads. User registration remains a simple, manual, or invite-only backend function during this phase.
*   **Weeks 4–5 (Onboarding & Integration):** Shift focus to carrier onboarding tools and farmer outreach preparation. The marketing lead begins executing the delegated outreach activities (detailed below) to build a waitlist, not open registration. The product team integrates the finalized matching engine with a streamlined, secure registration flow.
*   **Week 6 (Soft Launch):** Invite a controlled cohort of 20 farmers and 10 carriers to test the full loop. Verify match success rates and system stability. No public marketing yet.
*   **Week 7 (Hard Launch):** Open public registration and activate the full marketing push just days before September 1st.

**Go-Live Criteria Requiring Your Approval:**
1.  **Match Success Rate:** Verification that the engine successfully matches at least 85% of test loads in the simulation environment.
2.  **Concurrency Stability:** Confirmation that the system handles 10x the expected peak load without latency exceeding 2 seconds.
3.  **Data Integrity:** Assurance that no load or carrier data is lost or corrupted during high-volume transactions.
4.  **Rollback Capability:** A verified, one-click mechanism to pause new matches if critical errors appear post-launch.

**Delegated Outreach Activities (Marketing Lead):**
*   **Waitlist Campaign:** Execute a targeted campaign to collect commitments from farmers and carriers, emphasizing the "September 1st Launch" deadline to create urgency without opening the platform.
*   **Carrier Vetting:** Pre-screen and verify the credentials of the top 50 target carriers so they are ready for instant activation upon launch.
*   **Educational Content:** Prepare "How to Use" guides and success stories from the soft-launch cohort to deploy immediately upon public opening.
*   **Budget Allocation:** Manage the $100,000 budget, reserving 70% for the final two weeks of the launch window to maximize impact when the platform is fully open.

**Note:** This plan assumes the current team has the necessary technical expertise to build a high-concurrency engine in three weeks; if not, external specialized help must be sourced immediately. All marketing activities must strictly avoid promising features not yet validated by the engineering lead.
