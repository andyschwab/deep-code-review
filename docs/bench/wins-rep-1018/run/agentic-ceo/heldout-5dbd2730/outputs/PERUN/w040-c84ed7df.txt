**Decision:** Prioritize **validating the data migration scripts** before load testing. A fast server cannot fix corrupt data, and a dosing error from bad mapping is irreversible and life-threatening, whereas slow performance is a manageable friction.

**Roadmap: Prototype to Public Announcement**

**Phase 1: Data Integrity & Safety Gate (Weeks 1–2)**
*   **Product Team (Autonomous):** Build the mapping logic and execute the "First Concrete Step" (defined below) on a sanitized copy of the data. Develop the rollback mechanism.
*   **Clinic Directors (Decision Required):** Sign off on the **Data Mapping Specification**. They must verify that the sample output correctly reflects patient history and medication units before any bulk processing is authorized.

**Phase 2: Infrastructure Stress & Throttling (Weeks 3–4)**
*   **Product Team (Autonomous):** Simulate peak traffic from 15 clinics on the government servers. Implement queueing systems and bandwidth throttling to prevent crashes during low-bandwidth windows.
*   **Clinic Directors (Decision Required):** Approve the **Access Schedule**. Based on stress test results, they must agree to staggered login windows for their staff to accommodate server limits.

**Phase 3: Pilot Onboarding (Weeks 5–6)**
*   **Product Team (Autonomous):** Onboard 500 patients across three diverse clinics. Monitor for data drift and system latency.
*   **Clinic Directors (Decision Required):** Authorize **Full Bulk Import**. This is the final go/no-go based on pilot success rates and staff readiness.

**Phase 4: Full Launch & Announcement (Weeks 7–8)**
*   **Product Team (Autonomous):** Execute the bulk import for the remaining 19,500 patients using the validated scripts and approved schedule. Monitor system health.
*   **Clinic Directors (Decision Required):** Coordinate the **Public Announcement** timing to ensure support staff are fully manned before patients are notified.

**First Concrete Step: The "Shadow Import" Verification**
Do not touch the live database yet. Execute a **Shadow Import** using a sanitized extract of 100 high-complexity patient records (those with multiple medications and historical changes).
1.  Run your migration script against this subset in an isolated environment.
2.  Generate a "Diff Report" comparing the source data to the migrated result, specifically highlighting medication dosages, units (mg vs. mcg), and frequency.
3.  Have a senior clinician manually audit these 100 records against the Diff Report.
4.  **Stop Condition:** If even one dosage value or unit is misinterpreted, the script is rejected. No further work proceeds until the mapping logic is corrected and re-verified.

**Note:** Given the January 10th deadline and server constraints, staggered data ingestion during off-peak hours is mandatory to avoid failure. All decisions by Clinic Directors must be documented in writing to satisfy state health department compliance requirements.
