**Decision Memo: Launch Plan & Risk Mitigation**

**Call:** Proceed with a "Key-First" locked scope; defer all non-essential UI features to post-holiday to guarantee the December 1st date. The single governing number is **48 hours**: the maximum time allowable between key generation and successful backup verification before the project halts.

**Figures:**
*   **Audit Capacity:** 2 auditors × 5 days = 10 auditor-days. (Stated: "only two external security auditors"). *Match.*
*   **Integration Scope:** 450 families × 1 record sync = 450 data mappings. (Stated: "450 current families"). *Match.*
*   **Timeline:** Today to Dec 1st ≈ 9 weeks. (Stated: "hard deadline of December 1st"). *Match.*
*   **Risk Correction:** The prompt implies key generation is a future step; this is incorrect. Key generation infrastructure must be built and tested *before* any patient data is touched. The "irreversible step" is actually the *first* step in the technical timeline, not the last.

**Decisions Made:**
1.  **Order of Operations:**
    *   **Phase 1 (Weeks 1-2):** Key Management Infrastructure (KMI) build and dry-run. No patient data connected.
    *   **Phase 2 (Weeks 3-5):** Database integration and end-to-end encryption (E2EE) wiring on test data.
    *   **Phase 3 (Weeks 6-8):** External Security Audit (focused strictly on KMI and E2EE).
    *   **Phase 4 (Week 9):** Final deployment and holiday monitoring.
2.  **Owner Sign-off Required (You):**
    *   Approval of the **Key Custody Protocol** (who physically/digitally holds the backup shards).
    *   Authorization to **halt the project** if the dry-run fails to restore keys within 4 hours.
    *   Final go/no-go after the external audit report.
3.  **Engineer Authority (No Sign-off Needed):**
    *   Selection of cryptographic libraries (must be industry standard, e.g., Signal Protocol or libsodium).
    *   Implementation of the database sync logic.
    *   Configuration of the audit environment.

**Failure Modes & Triggers:**
*   **Key Loss:** If the backup restoration test fails >2 times, the launch date slips. *Trigger:* Restoration time > 4 hours. *Action:* Halt deployment, engage third-party key recovery specialist (budget contingency).
*   **Audit Blocker:** If auditors find a critical flaw in the KMI <14 days before launch. *Trigger:* Critical severity finding. *Action:* Defer launch to Q1; do not launch with known critical encryption flaws.
*   **Integration Failure:** If >5% of the 450 family records fail sync during testing. *Trigger:* Error rate >5%. *Action:* Manual data entry fallback plan activated.

**Triggers:**
*   **Halt Trigger:** Value: Failed key restoration test. Date: End of Week 2. Action: Stop all integration work; re-architect KMI.
*   **Fallback Trigger:** Value: Audit critical finding. Date: Nov 15. Action: Cancel holiday launch, communicate delay to stakeholders.

**First Immediate Task:**
**Execute a "Paper Cut" Key Ceremony Dry-Run.** Before writing code for the portal, the three backend engineers must simulate the generation, storage, and restoration of master keys on an isolated machine. They must prove they can destroy a test key and recover it from backups within 4 hours. This validates the irreversible risk vector immediately.

**Note:** This plan assumes the compliance officer has already approved the choice of encryption standard; if not, that is the absolute first blocker. Do not integrate the patient database until the Key Ceremony Dry-Run is successful.
