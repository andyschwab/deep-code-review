**Decision:** Do not sign the central cold-storage hub lease until zone coverage math validates the fleet's ability to hit 90% without it; the lease is your single point of failure, not your accelerator.

**Figures:** 25 vans covering 88% more zones (from 12% to 90%) implies a 7.3x capacity increase. Unless each van's efficiency triples or you subcontract 60% of the volume, the current fleet cannot physically cover the required area by November 30th, making the $200,000 penalty probable regardless of the hub lease status.

**Strategy Session Sequence (First Hour: Risk Mitigation Focus)**

**Minutes 0–15: Validate the Capacity Constraint (The "Hard No" Check)**
*   **Data Needed:** Average daily stops per van in the current pilot vs. the density of the remaining 88% of zones.
*   **Action:** Calculate the maximum theoretical coverage of 25 vans in the new zones. If the math shows <80% coverage even with perfect efficiency, the discussion shifts immediately from "where to put the hub" to "how to acquire temporary fleet capacity."
*   **Goal:** Prove whether the bottleneck is storage (hub) or mobility (vans). Do not discuss real estate until this is resolved.

**Minutes 15–30: Stress-Test the "Irreversible" Lease**
*   **Data Needed:** The specific clause in the lease allowing subletting or early termination for cause, and the cost of short-term cold-storage alternatives (refrigerated containers, third-party logistics hubs) for 6 months.
*   **Action:** Model the P&L of signing the 5-year lease vs. a 6-month bridge solution.
*   **Goal:** Determine if the "irreversible" step is actually necessary for Day 1 launch. If 3 hospital contracts can be served from a temporary micro-hub, delay the central lease until adoption rates are proven.

**Minutes 30–45: Define the Founder's Approval Gate**
*   **Your Direct Approval Required:** The decision to **defer the central hub lease** and authorize emergency budget for **third-party fleet augmentation** or **short-term storage**.
*   **Why:** Only you can accept the operational complexity of a hybrid logistics model to avoid the financial rigidity of a 5-year lease. The team cannot approve this trade-off; they will naturally optimize for the "perfect" central hub rather than the "survivable" launch.

**Minutes 45–60: Draft the Contingency Trigger**
*   **Action:** Set a hard date (e.g., October 15th) where, if subcontracted fleet partners are not secured, you revisit the hub lease as a last resort.
*   **Goal:** Create a forced decision point that prevents drifting into the lease by default.

**Immediate Next Step:** Instruct your route planners to model the "Max Coverage" scenario with 25 vans today. Do not let them plan routes based on the assumption that the new hub will exist.
