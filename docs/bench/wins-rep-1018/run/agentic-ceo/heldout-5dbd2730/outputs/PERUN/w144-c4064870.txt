**Decision:** Begin immediately with a **historical back-test** of the new model against the 50,000 pending applications. Do not start with stakeholder communications.

**Reasoning:** You stated that activation causes "immediate, irreversible financial loss or regulatory fines" due to bias or error. Communicating rejections based on an unverified model risks contacting customers under false pretenses, creating immediate reputational and legal liability before the model's safety is proven. The back-test provides the factual basis required to draft accurate communications.

**Figures:**
*   **Scope:** 50,000 pending applications × 1 new model = 50,000 simulation runs required.
*   **Timeline:** Go-live January 10th. If today is early December, you have ~30 days. 50k runs on 6 data scientists allows ~833 runs/scientist/day, feasible within 3–5 days for initial results, leaving 3+ weeks for remediation.
*   **Risk Exposure:** 1% error rate on 50k apps = 500 wrongful decisions. At an average loan value of $20k, potential exposure = $10M plus regulatory fines.

**Sequence of Validation, Approval, and Deployment:**

1.  **Shadow Mode Simulation (Days 1–5):** Run the new engine against the 50k pending apps in a read-only environment. Compare outcomes against current approvals. Flag all divergences.
2.  **Bias & Drift Audit (Days 6–10):** Risk analysts review divergences for protected class bias and statistical drift. Generate the "Impact Report."
3.  **Threshold Calibration (Days 11–15):** Data scientists adjust thresholds to meet risk appetite while minimizing divergence spikes.
4.  **Executive Authorization Gate (Day 16):** You review the final Impact Report and calibrated thresholds.
5.  **Communication Drafting (Days 17–20):** Legal and Comms draft notices *only* for the specific cohort identified in step 4.
6.  **Staged Deployment (Jan 3–9):** Deploy to 1% of live traffic (canary) for 48 hours, then 10%, then 100% on Jan 10.

**Threshold Adjustments Requiring Your Explicit Authorization:**
You must sign off on any change that alters the approval rate by more than **±2%** compared to the legacy baseline, or any adjustment that shifts the denial rate for any single demographic segment by more than **5%**. These are your "circuit breakers." No code moves to production without your signature on the final Impact Report confirming these metrics are within bounds.

**First Concrete Step:**
Instruct your six data scientists to **ingest the 50,000 pending application records into an isolated sandbox environment and execute the new scoring engine in "shadow mode" by 5:00 PM tomorrow.** The deliverable must be a raw count of approvals/denials under the new logic versus the old, with no customer contact initiated until this data is reviewed.

**Halt Trigger:** If the shadow run reveals a denial rate increase >5% overall or >10% in any protected class segment, stop all deployment planning immediately and revert to model retraining.

**Fallback Trigger:** If the sandbox environment cannot be secured and data-isolated within 48 hours, delay the January 10th go-live date; do not risk data leakage or contamination of the live pipeline.
