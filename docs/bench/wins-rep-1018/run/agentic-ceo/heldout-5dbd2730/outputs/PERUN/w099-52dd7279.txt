**Decision:** Prioritize frontend driver interface testing before backend alert logic.

**Figures:** 40 days total minus 10-day buffer for holiday freeze equals 30 days execution. 200 trucks × 1 firmware update = 200 simultaneous points of failure. 30% theft spike is the cost of inaction; 100% fleet silence is the cost of a failed update.

**Rollout Strategy:**
Adopt a "Silent Canary" approach. Do not push the firmware to all 200 trucks simultaneously. The irreversible step requires a phased rollout: 5 trucks (Day 1), 20 trucks (Day 3), 50 trucks (Day 7), and the remainder only after 48 hours of stability at each stage. This limits the blast radius of a crash to 5% of the fleet initially, preserving communication for the other 95%.

**Risk Allocation:**
*   **Risks You Must Accept Personally:**
    1.  **Residual Theft Exposure:** By choosing a phased rollout, you accept that 195 trucks remain unprotected during the first week of testing. You own the decision to delay full protection to ensure fleet connectivity.
    2.  **Schedule Slip:** If the 5-truck canary fails, the December 10th launch date may slip. You must authorize holding the date firm or extending it based on canary results.
*   **Tasks Assigned to Security Team:**
    1.  **Update Integrity Verification:** Cryptographically sign the firmware and verify the chain of trust before any device accepts the packet.
    2.  **Rollback Mechanism Validation:** Prove the "undo" switch works on the canary group before wider deployment.
    3.  **Threat Modeling:** Assess if the alert system itself creates a new attack vector (e.g., spoofing theft alerts to disrupt logistics).

**Execution Plan:**
Days 1–10: Frontend stability and rollback testing on 5 devices.
Days 11–20: Backend logic integration and expanded canary (20 devices).
Days 21–30: Full fleet deployment and monitoring.

**Immediate Action:**
Isolate five non-critical trucks immediately and attempt the firmware update on them alone to validate the rollback mechanism before writing a single line of alert logic.
