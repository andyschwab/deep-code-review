**Decision:** Prioritize the Pediatric Wing safety protocol and calibration verification above all installation speed; if the pediatric pilot fails, halt the entire hospital-wide rollout immediately.

**Figures:** 300 rooms / 8 technicians = 37.5 rooms per tech. 8 hours (10 PM–6 AM) = 1.6 hours per room including travel, install, and test. This leaves zero margin for error or rework.

**Critical Choices to Resolve Now:**
1.  **Pediatric Safety Gate:** Who is the single authorized clinical lead (not IT, not facilities) to sign off on temperature stability in the pediatric wing before any other wing begins? If this person is not identified and present by 9:45 PM, the project stops.
2.  **Isolation Ward Access:** Which specific technicians have completed the required safety briefing? If fewer than two technicians are cleared, isolation wards must be excluded from tonight's scope to prevent bottlenecks.
3.  **Fallback Protocol:** If a pediatric sensor fails calibration, does the room revert to manual HVAC control immediately, or is there a backup heating unit on standby? This decision must be made before disconnection begins.

**Instructions for the Shift Supervisor:**
*   **Sequence:** Start exclusively in the Pediatric Wing (Phase 1). Do not deploy crews to other wings until the first three pediatric rooms are installed, calibrated, and signed off by the clinical lead.
*   **Staffing:** Assign your two most senior technicians to Pediatrics. Assign technicians with verified safety briefings to Isolation Wards only. Split the remaining six technicians between the two general wings.
*   **Communication:** Establish a 15-minute check-in cycle. Any failure in calibration triggers an immediate "Stop Work" order for that specific zone until resolved.
*   **Handover:** Prepare a simplified one-page status sheet for nursing staff by 5:30 AM listing: Room Number, New Thermostat Location, Emergency Override Procedure, and Who to Call if Temp Drifts.

**Concrete First Steps for Pre-Work Session:**
1.  **Verify Credentials:** Cross-reference the 8 technician names against the safety briefing roster. Reassign or bench anyone missing clearance.
2.  **Confirm Clinical Lead:** Secure the name and direct cell number of the pediatric nurse manager who will perform the safety sign-off.
3.  **Dry-Run Calibration:** Perform one full install-and-calibrate cycle on a non-patient mock-up or an empty room right now to time the process. If it exceeds 90 minutes, the 300-room goal is impossible; reduce the scope immediately.
4.  **Print Emergency Overrides:** Ensure physical cards with manual override instructions are printed and staged at every nursing station before 10 PM.
5.  **Stage Backup Heat:** Move portable heating units to the pediatric wing corridor as a precaution before work begins.

The success of this deployment depends entirely on the pediatric pilot. Do not prioritize speed over the safety of the infants.

*Note: This plan assumes all hardware is pre-staged and tested; if hardware verification is incomplete, delay the launch. No external legal or compliance review was performed; consult hospital counsel for liability protocols.*
