**Decision:** Proceed with a "Shadow-Mode First" deployment strategy. Do not enable the "override all manual overrides" logic on Day 1. The risk of a total shipping halt (100% revenue stoppage) outweighs the current fine risk ($5k/incident). The irreversible step must be converted into a gated transition: run the new automation in parallel with manual entry for 5 business days, validate 100% agreement, then flip the switch.

**Figures:**
- Current Exposure: 600 shipments/month × ~error rate (unknown) × $5,000 = Variable monthly fine.
- Deadline: 20 business days from today.
- Team Capacity: 8 people × 20 days = 160 person-days available.
- Risk Re-base: Assuming a conservative 2% current error rate, monthly fines are ~$60,000. A total halt costs significantly more per day than this. The "irreversible" override must be delayed until validation proves <0.1% discrepancy.

**Risk Tolerance Parameters to Set:**
1.  **False Positive Limit:** Set the maximum acceptable rate of valid shipments incorrectly blocked by the new logic to 0%. Any block requires immediate human review during the shadow phase.
2.  **Discrepancy Threshold:** The automation cannot go live until its declarations match manual entries on 100% of a 3-day sample batch.
3.  **Rollback Trigger:** If the shadow mode reveals >5 discrepancies in 100 shipments, the deployment halts immediately for logic review.

**Workflow Sequence:**
1.  **Days 1–5 (Data & Logic Freeze):** Map current manual fields to new regulatory requirements. Hard-code the validation rules. Do not write integration code yet.
2.  **Days 6–12 (Shadow Build):** Build the automation to generate forms but *not* submit them. Output goes to a staging queue.
3.  **Days 13–17 (Parallel Run):** Team processes shipments manually as usual. The system generates forms in the background. Compare every single form. Investigate mismatches.
4.  **Days 18–19 (Gate Review):** If discrepancy rate is 0%, prepare the "override" switch. If >0%, fix logic and extend shadow run (request regulatory extension if absolutely necessary, citing safety validation).
5.  **Day 20 (Controlled Go-Live):** Enable automation with a "circuit breaker" that reverts to manual queue if error rates spike above 0% in the first hour.

**First Concrete Actions for Today:**
1.  **Assign Roles:** Designate 2 developers to map regulations to data fields, 2 to build the shadow comparator tool, and 4 to document the current manual process step-by-step (the "baseline").
2.  **Isolate the Kill Switch:** Architect the deployment so the "override" logic is behind a feature flag that defaults to OFF. Verify this architecture before writing validation code.
3.  **Sample Extraction:** Pull the last 50 hazardous material shipment records to use as the initial test dataset for the shadow mode.
4.  **Define "Block" Protocol:** Write the exact procedure the logistics team will follow if the system blocks a shipment during the shadow phase (e.g., "Escalate to Lead Developer within 15 mins").

Note: This plan prioritizes continuity of operations over immediate automation compliance, as a total halt violates the core business constraint.
Note: Regulatory fines are financial; a total shipping halt is existential. The timeline allows for a 5-day shadow validation period without missing the Oct 10 deadline.
