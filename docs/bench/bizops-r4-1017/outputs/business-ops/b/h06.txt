### Lane R: EU Data Privacy Regulations

**Regime Identification**
Shifting to paid channels triggers obligations under the **GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)** and the **ePrivacy Directive**, specifically regarding targeted advertising.

**General Rule & Routing**
*   **Consent Mechanism:** In the EU, placing non-essential cookies or using device identifiers for targeted ads generally requires **prior, explicit opt-in consent** (not implied). Pre-ticked boxes are invalid.
*   **Educational Data Sensitivity:** While language learning is educational, if your targeting infers data about a user's origin, ethnicity, or potentially children (under 13/16 depending on member state), you enter higher-risk processing categories requiring stricter safeguards.
*   **Binding Determination:** You must consult a **EU-qualified data privacy counsel** to:
    1.  Audit your current consent banner (CMP) for compliance with the latest EDPB guidelines.
    2.  Determine if your specific dialect data constitutes "special category" data under GDPR Article 9.
    3.  Draft the specific legal basis for processing required for your ad partners (Google/Meta).

**Do not launch full-scale targeted campaigns until a professional confirms your consent flow is compliant.**
