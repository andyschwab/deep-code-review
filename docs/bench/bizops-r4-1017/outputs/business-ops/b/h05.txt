### Lane R: Legal Regime & Routing

**Regime:** UK Contract Law and Professional Indemnity standards.

**General Rule:** In the UK, limitation of liability clauses in terms of service are subject to the **Unfair Contract Terms Act 1977 (UCTA)**. For business-to-business (B2B) contracts, liability for negligence can be limited only if the term is "reasonable." For business-to-consumer (B2C) contracts, you generally cannot exclude liability for death, personal injury, or negligence causing other loss if it is deemed unfair under the **Consumer Rights Act 2015**. Courts often scrutinize caps on liability for professional services; a cap that is disproportionately low compared to the fee (e.g., capping liability at £2k on a £2k fee when risk is £250k) may be struck down as unreasonable.

**Routing Required:**
You must engage a specialist UK commercial attorney immediately to:
1.  Draft terms that define the scope of "authentication" clearly (opinion vs. guarantee).
2.  Structure a liability cap that has a higher probability of being upheld as "reasonable" under UCTA.
3.  Advise on whether your current insurance policy excludes "forgery" specifically, as this dictates your actual residual risk.

**Do not attempt to draft these clauses yourself; a voided clause leaves you fully exposed to the £250k.**
