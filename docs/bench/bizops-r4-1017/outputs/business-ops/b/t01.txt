### Lane A: Arithmetic Application & Sensitivity Analysis

**1. Current Model (Flat Fee)**
*   **Formula:** `Revenue = Subscribers × Monthly Price`
*   **Inputs:** 120 users × $29
*   **Projected Revenue:** **$3,480/month** (assuming constant headcount).
*   **Volatility Driver:** Churn (8% monthly). If 8% cancel during slow seasons and aren't replaced, revenue drops to ~$3,202 next month, compounding downward.

**2. Proposed Model (Per-Word)**
*   **Formula:** `Revenue = Subscribers × Avg Words/User × Price Per Word`
*   **Inputs:** 120 users × 15,000 words × $0.002
*   **Projected Revenue (at average):** **$3,600/month**.
*   **Volatility Driver:** Usage variance. You noted unknown variance during holidays.
    *   *Scenario A (50% drop in volume):* 120 × 7,500 × $0.002 = **$1,800**.
    *   *Scenario B (20% drop in volume):* 120 × 12,000 × $0.002 = **$2,880**.

**3. Break-Even & Flip Input Analysis**
*   **Fixed Costs:** $450/month.
*   **Current Contribution Margin:** $3,480 - $450 = $3,030.
*   **Usage Flip Point:** To match current revenue ($3,480) under the new model, your average user must process **14,500 words** ($3,480 ÷ 120 ÷ $0.002).
*   **Risk:** If holiday volume dips below 14,500 words/user average, your revenue falls *below* the flat-fee baseline, exacerbating cash flow issues rather than smoothing them.

**4. Decision Aid**
*   **Read:** The per-word model only stabilizes cash flow if usage is *less* volatile than subscriber count. If translators stop working entirely during slow seasons (volume → 0), revenue collapses faster than with flat fees (where they might stay subscribed for potential future work).
*   **Threshold Rule of Thumb:** If LTV:CAC < 3 or payback > 12 months, fix pricing before scaling. Here, the critical threshold is **volume variance**. If standard deviation in word count exceeds 20%, the per-word model introduces *more* volatility than the 8% churn risk.
*   **Next Step (This Week):** Do not switch billing yet. Instrument your system to track the **standard deviation of word counts** per user over the last 6 months. If the variance is high, consider a **hybrid model** (e.g., $15 base + $0.001 per word) to capture upside while guaranteeing baseline coverage of your $450 fixed costs.
