### Decision Aid

**One-line read:** The loyalty program generates an extra **$1,480 per month** in total contribution margin, which is the maximum amount you can spend on *incremental* customer acquisition to break even on the program itself.

**The Formula & The Flip Input**
Your capacity to fund marketing is defined by:
`Max Incremental CAC Budget = (New Volume × New Margin) − (Old Volume × Old Margin)`

*   **Your Slots:** (920 × $19) − (800 × $20) = **$1,480**
*   **The Flip Input:** The **repeat purchase rate increase**. If the volume increase is only 10% (880 orders) instead of 15%, your total margin drops to $16,720, leaving only $720 for marketing. If volume stays flat at 800, you lose $800/month immediately due to the margin erosion.

**Thresholds (Rules of Thumb)**
*   **Viability Check:** If your blended CAC to acquire/retain these additional 120 customers exceeds **$12.33 per new order** ($1,480 ÷ 120 incremental orders), the program destroys value rather than creating it.
*   **LTV:CAC Context:** Ensure this incremental spend does not push your overall LTV:CAC below 3:1.
