### Lane R: Regulatory Boundary (The "What" and "Who")
Your core question involves **cross-border withholding tax**, **tax treaty application**, and **entity classification** (central entity vs. independent contractors). These are strictly regulatory matters.
*   **The Regime:** You are operating under UK tax law (HMRC) for the payer, and the domestic tax laws of Germany, France, Spain, and Poland for the recipients, mediated by the **UK's Double Taxation Treaties** with those specific nations.
*   **The Risk:** Treating international creators as UK residents when they are not triggers incorrect withholding (often defaulting to the highest rate) and potential penalties for failure to deduct. Conversely, failing to withhold when required shifts the liability to your organization.
*   **The Routing:** You cannot legally determine the correct withholding rate or the validity of a "central entity" structure without a licensed tax adviser. Specifically, you need a specialist in **international entertainment tax** or **cross-border royalties**. Do not attempt to self-certify residency status based on general knowledge.
