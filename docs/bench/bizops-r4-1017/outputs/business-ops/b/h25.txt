### Lane A: Unit Economics Evaluation

**Current State Analysis**
Based on your inputs, here is the arithmetic for your current transaction fee model:
*   **Average Order Value (AOV):** $8.50
*   **Your Gross Revenue per Transaction:** ($8.50 × 1.5%) + $0.30 = $0.1275 + $0.30 = **$0.4275**
*   **Estimated Payment Processor Cost:** Assuming a standard industry rate of ~2.9% + $0.30 (since you did not provide your specific processor cost, this is an assumption you must verify). Cost = ($8.50 × 2.9%) + $0.30 = $0.2465 + $0.30 = **$0.5465**.
*   **Net Revenue per Transaction:** $0.4275 (Your Rev) - $0.5465 (Proc Cost) = **-$0.119** (Loss per transaction).

*Note: If your "payment processor fees" mentioned in your prompt are the *only* costs and you are not paying a third party (i.e., you are the processor), the math changes. However, assuming you pay a standard processor, you are currently losing money on every single order before even accounting for your $1,200 fixed server costs or developer payroll.*

**Break-Even for 20% Gross Margin**
To achieve a 20% gross margin on the transaction itself (excluding fixed costs), we must solve for the Average Order Value (AOV) where your net revenue covers the processor cost plus the margin.

*Formula:*
Let $R$ = Your Revenue Rate (1.5% + $0.30)
Let $C$ = Processor Cost Rate (Assumed 2.9% + $0.30)
Let $P$ = Price (AOV)
Target: $(R(P) - C(P)) / R(P) = 0.20$ ?
*Correction:* Gross margin is typically calculated on *your* revenue, not the GMV.
Target: $(Your Revenue - Processor Cost) / Your Revenue = 0.20$
$Your Revenue = 0.015P + 0.30$
$Processor Cost = 0.029P + 0.30$ (Using assumed 2.9% rate)

Equation:
$((0.015P + 0.30) - (0.029P + 0.30)) / (0.015P + 0.30) = 0.20$
$(-0.014P) / (0.015P + 0.30) = 0.20$

*Result:* This yields a negative number. **Under the current fee structure (1.5% + $0.30), it is mathematically impossible to achieve a positive gross margin if your processor charges a higher percentage (e.g., 2.9%) than you do.** You are arbitraging a negative spread.

Even if your processor cost is lower (e.g., 1.0% + $0.30), let's solve for the break-even AOV where Net Revenue = $0:
$0.015P + 0.30 = 0.01P + 0.30 \rightarrow 0.005P = 0 \rightarrow P = 0$.
Wait, if the fixed fees match ($0.30), you only profit on the percentage difference.
If Processor is 2.9% + $0.30 and You charge 1.5% + $0.30:
You lose 1.4% on every dollar. No AOV fixes this.

**Required Pivot for Viability**
To make the % + fixed model work, your take rate must exceed the processor take rate.
If you must keep the 1.5% + $0.30 model, you must negotiate a processor rate significantly below 1.5%, which is unlikely for small tickets.
