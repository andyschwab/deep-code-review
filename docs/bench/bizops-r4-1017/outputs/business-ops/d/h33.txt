Focus on institutional clients for long-term stability, but do not abandon individual subscribers yet; the math shows institutions are 4x more valuable per dollar of acquisition cost, but their 6-month sales cycle creates a cash-flow gap you must bridge with current individual revenue.

**The Arithmetic**
Your institutional Lifetime Value (LTV) is effectively infinite relative to the contract term since churn is near zero. Assuming a conservative 3-year horizon:
*   **Institutional LTV:** $2,500/year × 3 years = **$7,500**.
*   **Institutional CAC:** **$1,200**.
*   **Institutional LTV:CAC Ratio:** **6.25:1**.

For individual musicians, high churn drastically reduces value:
*   **Individual Monthly Churn:** 8% implies an average lifespan of 1/0.08 = 12.5 months.
*   **Individual LTV:** $15/month × 12.5 months = **$187.50**.
*   **Individual CAC:** You stated sign-ups are instant via the website. If we assume your marketing spend to generate these is low (e.g., $20 per acquired user via organic/SEO), the ratio is healthy. If you are paying for ads, an 8% monthly churn makes paid acquisition dangerous unless CAC is under ~$60.
*   **Individual LTV:CAC Ratio:** Highly sensitive to acquisition cost, but likely **3:1 to 9:1** if organic.

**The Trade-off**
The institutional model offers superior unit economics per sales dollar spent (6.25 return vs. variable individual return). However, the **payback period** differs wildly.
*   **Institutional Payback:** $1,200 cost / ($2,500/12 months) = **~5.8 months**. You wait nearly 6 months to recoup costs, coinciding exactly with your sales cycle length. This means you fund the entire sales cycle out of pocket before seeing a dollar back.
*   **Individual Payback:** If CAC is $20, payback is **1.3 months**. Cash returns almost immediately.

**Decision Aid**
Shifting *exclusively* to institutions jeopardizes short-term cash flow because you will incur 6 months of expenses ($1,200 per prospect) before any revenue arrives. With 300 institutions, you have a stable base of $750,000 ARR. Adding more requires upfront capital.

*   **Flip Input:** If your cash runway is less than 9 months of operating expenses + new sales commissions, you cannot pivot exclusively.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If LTV:CAC > 3 and Payback < 12 months, the channel is viable. Institutions pass this, but the *timing* of the cash flow is the constraint.
