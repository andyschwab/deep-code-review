**The Arithmetic of Leakage vs. Rate Reduction**

Your current situation presents a clear trade-off between **rate** and **volume retention**.
*   **Current State:** $42,000 Gross Revenue (GMV) implies a total transaction volume of $210,000 ($42k / 20%). Your take is $5 per $25 lesson.
*   **The Leakage Signal:** A 15% month-over-month decline in repeat bookings for top tutors suggests that for every 100 lessons booked last month, only 85 are being booked this month on-platform. If this trend continues for three months, your on-platform volume drops by ~33%, reducing your revenue to ~$28,000 even if the commission rate stays at 20%.
*   **The Hypothetical Cut:** If you drop to 10% commission ($2.50 per lesson), you need to **double** your on-platform transaction volume just to match your current $42,000 revenue. However, you do not need to double *total* market volume; you only need to recapture the leaking volume.

**Decision Framework: The Break-Even Retention Rate**

To decide if lowering the fee works, calculate the **Break-Even Retention Rate**. This is the percentage of currently leaking transactions you must recapture to make a fee cut revenue-neutral.

*   **Formula:** `Required Volume Increase % = (Old Rate - New Rate) / New Rate`
*   **Application (20% → 10%):** `(20 - 10) / 10 = 100%`. You need 100% more on-platform volume.
*   **Application (20% → 15%):** `(20 - 15) / 15 = 33.3%`. You need 33.3% more on-platform volume.

Given your current 15% monthly churn in repeat bookings, recovering 33% of lost volume by moving to a 15% fee is mathematically more probable than recovering 100% of lost volume by moving to 10%. The 15% monthly decline indicates tutors already feel the 20% fee exceeds the value of your lead generation.

**Sensitivity Analysis & Thresholds**

The "compelling threshold" for tutors is not a fixed number but a function of **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)替代**.
*   **Tutor Logic:** If a tutor can acquire a student independently for less than the commission fee, they will leave.
*   **Your Value Metric:** Your fee is justified only if `Commission Fee < Tutor's Independent CAC`.
*   **Assumption:** If a tutor spends $50 on ads to find a student who books 10 lessons ($250 GMV), their effective CAC is 20%. Your 20% fee matches this. If they can find students for $25 (10% CAC) via social media or word-of-mouth, your 20% fee is double their alternative cost, driving the leakage you see.
