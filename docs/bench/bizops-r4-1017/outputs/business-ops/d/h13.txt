**Verdict:** Your current unbundled model creates a margin deficit for clinics at last month's volume; you must restructure to a bundled per-unit price or a tiered volume-based software fee to align with the $185 insurance cap.

**The Arithmetic (Lane A)**
We apply your figures to the clinic's unit economics to test sustainability.

*   **Clinic Revenue per Pair:** $185 (Insurance Cap)
*   **Clinic Cost per Pair (Current):** $210 (Hardware) + Software Allocation
*   **Current Software Allocation:** $12,000 monthly fixed cost ÷ 1,200 pairs = $10/pair.
*   **Total Clinic Cost:** $210 + $10 = $220 per pair.
*   **Clinic Margin:** $185 - $220 = **-$35 per pair.**

At 1,200 units, clinics lose $42,000 monthly on your product alone. They cannot sustain this.

**Sensitivity Analysis: The Break-Even Volume**
For your current unbundled model ($210 unit + $12k fixed) to work within the $185 cap, the software allocation must drop to zero, which is impossible, or the unit price must drop. If we assume the clinic *must* make at least $0 margin:
*   Max allowable total cost = $185.
*   If Unit Price stays $210, the model fails immediately regardless of volume.
*   **Flip Input:** To make the *current* pricing work, the unit price must drop to at most $175 (assuming software is free), or the software fee must be absorbed by you.

**Restructuring Options**

1.  **Bundle Everything (Recommended for Adoption):**
    Fold the $12,000 software cost into the per-unit price.
    *   Target Clinic Margin: Let's assume clinics need $25/pair to cover their own overhead.
    *   Target Price to Clinic: $185 (Cap) - $25 (Margin) = $160.
    *   Your New Revenue: $160 × 1,200 = $192,000.
    *   Your Costs: ($45 × 1,200) + $12,000 = $66,000.
    *   Your Margin: $126,000.
    *   *Result:* You survive, but your per-unit revenue drops from $210 to $160. Clinics accept this because the cost is fully reimbursable as a "medical device" expense rather than a non-reimbursable "software fee."

2.  **Separate SaaS Fee (High Risk):**
    If you keep the $210 hardware price, the clinic is already $25 over the cap before software. Adding a SaaS fee guarantees rejection.
    *   *Threshold:* Clinics will likely reject any separate fee if the hardware price exceeds ~$150–$160.
    *   *Decision Aid:* If you separate fees, you must lower the hardware price to ~$150 and charge up to $35/pair equivalent in SaaS. However, since SaaS is often not reimbursable, clinics prefer the bundled $160 all-in price.
