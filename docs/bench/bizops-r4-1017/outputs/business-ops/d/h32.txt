Absorb the premium increase only if you can immediately implement a value-based pricing tier for high-value items; otherwise, the current flat-fee model is mathematically unsustainable at your current volume.

**The Arithmetic (Lane A)**
Your unit economics have shifted critically due to the fixed cost increase.
*   **Revenue per unit:** $75
*   **Variable cost per unit:** $40
*   **Previous Fixed Cost (Insurance):** $30,000 / 8,000 units = $3.75/unit
*   **New Fixed Cost (Insurance):** $48,000 / 8,000 units = $6.00/unit
*   **New Contribution Margin:** $75 - $40 - $6.00 = **$29.00 per coin** (down from $31.25).

While a $2.25 drop per unit seems small, it represents a **7.2% reduction in your bottom-line profit per coin**. More dangerously, your break-even volume has risen. Assuming your other fixed costs (rent, salaries) remain constant, you now need to grade approximately **533 more coins** just to cover the extra $18,000 in insurance premiums.

**The Pricing Restructure Analysis**
Your flat fee of $75 creates a mismatch between risk and revenue. Grading a $50 coin costs you the same labor as grading a $50,000 coin, but the liability exposure (and thus the insurance driver) is exponentially higher for the latter.
*   **Current State:** You are subsidizing high-risk, high-value items with low-risk, low-value items.
*   **Proposed Solution:** Introduce a "High-Value" tier.
    *   *Threshold:* Coins declared over $5,000.
    *   *Mechanism:* Charge a base fee ($75) + a percentage of declared value (e.g., 1% or 2%) or a flat surcharge (e.g., +$150).
    *   *Impact:* If 10% of your volume (800 coins) are high-value and you add a $100 surcharge, you generate $80,000 in new revenue, covering the insurance hike 4x over while aligning price with risk.

**Decision Thresholds & Next Step**
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If your insurance cost exceeds **5-7% of total revenue**, your pricing model is misaligned with your risk profile. Your new ratio is $48k/$600k = **8%**, signaling immediate action is required.
*   **Flip Input:** The % of your volume that is high-value. If >5% of coins are valued over $5k, a tiered model is mandatory.
*   **Action:** Do not reduce coverage; the liability risk of misgrading a rare coin exceeds any premium savings. Instead, draft a "Declared Value Surcharge" schedule this week and apply it only to *new* submissions to test price elasticity without alienating existing customers.

*Note: Industry benchmarks for insurance-to-revenue ratios vary widely by niche and claims history; the 5-7% figure used above is a general operational heuristic, not a verified industry standard for numismatics.*
