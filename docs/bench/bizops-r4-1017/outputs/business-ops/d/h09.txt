**Financial Model (Monthly)**

*Proposed Tiered State (with 15% volume reduction assumption):*
- New Luxury GMV: $200,000 × (1 - 0.15) = $170,000
- Standard GMV: $1,800,000 (unchanged)
- New Revenue: ($170k × 25%) + ($1.8M × 15%) = $42,500 + $270,000 = **$312,500**

**Analysis & Sensitivity**
The model shows an increase of $12,500 in monthly net contribution under your specific assumptions. The "flip input" here is the volume elasticity of your luxury sellers. If the volume drop exceeds ~23% (where luxury revenue falls below $30,000), the strategy destroys value compared to the flat rate.
- *Break-even Volume Drop Formula:* $30,000 = $200,000 × (1 - X) × 25%. Solving for X yields ~40% drop to match current luxury revenue alone, but since standard revenue is constant, the total revenue break-even occurs when the lost luxury revenue equals the gain. Actually, since the rate increases, you can afford a volume drop.
- *Correction on Break-even:* Current Luxury Rev = $30k. New Rate = 25%. To get $30k at 25%, GMV must be $120k. This implies a 40% drop in GMV ($200k -> $120k) just to maintain current luxury revenue levels. Since standard revenue is untouched, total revenue remains $300k if luxury volume drops 40%. Your assumed 15% drop is well within the safe zone.

**Decision Aid**
1.  **Read:** The 15% volume reduction assumption yields ~$12.5k extra monthly profit, plus potential savings from reduced authentication disputes.
2.  **Formula:** `Max Tolerable Volume Drop = 1 - (Current Rate / New Rate)`. Here: `1 - (0.15 / 0.25) = 40%`. As long as luxury listings drop less than 40%, gross revenue increases.
3.  **Threshold:** If luxury volume drops >20%, investigate seller churn causes immediately; if >40%, revert the change.
