**Strategic Recommendation**
While adding 0.3 jobs/day is mathematically sufficient to break even on the fuel hike, it leaves no buffer for errors or further price spikes.
1.  **Fuel Surcharge (Preferred):** Add a $1.50–$2.00 "fuel adjustment" line item. This passes the cost directly to the consumer without requiring operational changes.
2.  **Hub-and-Spoke:** If you switch to drop-off, you eliminate the "drive-between" fuel cost entirely, likely saving more than the 30% increase. This also allows higher density (more than 12 jobs) since drive time is removed.
3.  **Reduce Radius:** Only do this if density in the core zone allows >12 jobs/day to compensate for lost revenue from cut areas.

**Decision Trigger:** If diesel averages >$3.80 for two consecutive weeks, activate the surcharge. If it hits $4.00, pilot the hub-and-spoke model in one zone.
