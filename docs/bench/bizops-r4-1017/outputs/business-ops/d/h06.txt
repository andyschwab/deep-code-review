**Regulatory Triage (Lane R)**
You asked about EU data privacy for targeted advertising. This falls under the **GDPR** and the **ePrivacy Directive**.
*   **The Rule:** Targeted advertising generally requires prior, explicit consent (opt-in) for cookies/tracking, especially for sensitive data. While "educational app" isn't a special exemption, if you target minors (likely for language learning), the bar for consent is higher (often requiring parental consent).
*   **The Risk:** Shifting your *entire* budget to paid channels implies heavy reliance on tracking pixels (Meta, Google). If you process data without valid consent, fines can reach 4% of global turnover.
*   **Action:** Do not launch aggressive retargeting campaigns until a qualified EU privacy counsel reviews your cookie banner flow and data processing agreements. Specifically, ask them: "Does our current consent mechanism satisfy GDPR Article 7 for behavioral advertising targeting users under 16?"
