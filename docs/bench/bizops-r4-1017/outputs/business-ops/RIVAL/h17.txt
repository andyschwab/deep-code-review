### Strategic Pricing & Product Mix Analysis for PureSuds

**Immediate Recommendation:** Do not reformulate with cheaper oils or reduce bar size. As an artisanal brand selling on "organic essential oil blends," changing the recipe or shrinking the product constitutes a breach of trust that will permanently damage brand perception and justify delisting by partners. Instead, execute a **Temporary Surcharge Strategy** combined with a **Product Mix Pivot**.

#### Option Analysis

**1. Reformulate with Cheaper Oils (Reject)**
*   **Cash Flow Impact:** Neutral to positive immediately.
*   **Brand Perception:** Catastrophic. Your value proposition is "artisanal" and "organic." Switching to synthetic or lower-grade oils invalidates your core promise. Customers and retail partners will view this as deception, likely leading to mass returns and loss of shelf space permanently.

**2. Reduce Bar Size (Shrinkflation) (Reject)**
*   **Cash Flow Impact:** Moderate improvement. Reducing size by ~45% to match costs would be required, which is visually obvious.
*   **Brand Perception:** High negative. Artisanal consumers are highly sensitive to value integrity. Obvious shrinkflation signals desperation and greed, eroding loyalty faster than a price increase.

**3. Absorb Costs Temporarily (Reject)**
*   **Cash Flow Impact:** Fatal.
    *   Current Margin: $8.50 - $7.40 = $1.10 per bar.
    *   Monthly Contribution: 15,000 units × $1.10 = $16,500.
    *   Monthly Loss: $16,500 (contribution) - $45,000 (overhead) = **-$28,500**.
    *   Runway: $120,000 / $28,500 ≈ **4.2 months**. This confirms your fear; you will be insolvent by month five.

#### Recommended Strategy: The "Transparency & Surcharge" Approach

**Step 1: Implement a Temporary Supply Chain Surcharge**
Instead of raising the base MSRP from $8.50 to $14.00 (a 65% jump), keep the base price at $8.50 and add a visible **"Temporary Essential Oil Surcharge" of $4.50**, bringing the consumer price to $13.00.
*   **Why this works:** It separates the volatile cost component from your brand value. It signals to retail partners and consumers that this is an external, temporary market force, not a permanent greed-based price hike.
*   **Financials:** New Margin = $13.00 - $7.40 = $5.60.
    *   Monthly Contribution: 15,000 × $5.60 = $84,000.
    *   Monthly Profit: $84,000 - $45,000 = **+$39,000**.
    *   Result: You stop the bleed immediately and rebuild cash reserves.

**Step 2: Pivot Product Mix (The "Blends" Strategy)**
Lavender is the bottleneck. Immediately launch and promote limited-edition bars using abundant, lower-cost organic oils (e.g., citrus, peppermint, or eucalyptus) priced at $9.50 (no surcharge).
*   **Action:** Incentivize retail partners with a 5% higher margin on these non-lavender bars to encourage shelf placement.
*   **Goal:** Shift 30-40% of volume to these SKUs to reduce exposure to the lavender shortage while maintaining average transaction value.

**Step 3: Partner Communication Script**
Provide your retail partners with a one-page explainer: *"Due to a global lavender harvest failure, we are applying a temporary pass-through surcharge to maintain our organic standards. We refuse to dilute our formula. This surcharge will be removed once harvest prices stabilize."*
*   Most artisanal retailers prefer a transparent, temporary surcharge over a competitor who secretly lowers quality.

**Projected Outcome:**
By adopting the surcharge model, you preserve your brand integrity, avoid the "sticker shock" of a permanent price reset, and generate ~$39k/month in profit, extending your runway indefinitely while the market corrects.

*Note: Monitor lavender futures monthly; plan to remove the surcharge within 6-9 months to reward customer loyalty.*
*Ensure your website and packaging clearly date the surcharge to reinforce its temporary nature.*
