### Recommended Pricing Strategy for Lumina Casting

To protect your margins against gold volatility without complicating the customer experience, you should adopt a **"Locked Price via Deposit"** model combined with a **short quote validity window**. This approach shifts the commodity risk from you to the market timeline while maintaining a premium, trustworthy client experience.

**1. Implement a 48-Hour Quote Validity Window**
Explicitly state on all estimates: *"This price is guaranteed for 48 hours from the time of issue."*
*   **Why:** Gold swings of $50/oz represent a $25 risk per ring (0.5 oz). A 48-hour window significantly reduces the probability of a massive spike occurring before the client commits. It creates gentle urgency without being aggressive.
*   **Customer Communication:** Frame this as standard industry practice for precious metals, similar to how currency exchange rates work. Most bespoke clients understand that raw material costs fluctuate.

**2. Require a Non-Refundable, Metal-Weighted Deposit**
Change your payment terms to require a deposit equal to **110% of the current melt value** of the gold required (approx. $220–$250 depending on spot) to commence production.
*   **The Mechanism:** The moment the client pays the deposit, the gold price is "locked." You immediately purchase the metal or allocate it from inventory at that specific cost. The remaining balance (your labor fee + any remaining metal cost if you under-collected) is due upon pickup.
*   **Why 110%?** This covers the exact metal weight plus a tiny buffer for casting loss or minor spikes during the 24 hours it takes to process the payment and buy the metal.
*   **Policy:** Clearly state that if the client cancels after the deposit, the metal portion is non-refundable because the materials have been purchased specifically for their bespoke design. The labor portion of the deposit can be refundable or credited toward a future redesign, depending on your goodwill policy.

**3. Absorb Minor Fluctuations in the Labor Fee**
Do not build a large volatility buffer into the base price for every customer, as this makes you uncompetitive during stable markets.
*   **Strategy:** Your $400 labor fee should absorb minor daily noise (e.g., $5–$10 swings). Only the extreme volatility (the $50 swings you mentioned) threatens the business. The deposit + validity window handles the extreme cases; your margin handles the noise.

**Sample Client Script:**
*"To ensure we secure the exact gold quality and weight for your custom ring, we require a deposit covering the current metal value to lock in your price. This allows us to purchase your specific gold immediately, protecting you from any market increases while we craft your piece. Quotes are valid for 48 hours to account for daily market shifts."*

**Summary of Actions:**
1.  Update quotes to expire in 48 hours.
2.  Collect a deposit equal to 110% of the melt value before ordering materials.
3.  Treat the deposit as the "price lock" moment.

This structure ensures you never buy metal at a loss, keeps your upfront pricing clean, and aligns with standard practices for high-end custom jewelers dealing in volatile commodities.

*Note: Consult with a local attorney to ensure your deposit cancellation language complies with New York consumer protection laws.*
