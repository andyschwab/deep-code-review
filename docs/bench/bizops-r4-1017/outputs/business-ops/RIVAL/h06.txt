Your current unit economics are broken under the new traffic mix. With organic traffic halved, your blended Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) has likely risen from €12 to approximately **€18–€19**. While this is below your €25 paid CAC, it threatens your runway if growth targets remain static.

*   **Current State:** €35k MRR with 4% churn implies a monthly loss of €1,400 in revenue. To maintain current growth, you likely need to replace churned users plus add net new users.
*   **The Shift:** Previously, 60% of users came at €0 marginal ad cost (organic). Now, that portion is cut in half, meaning 30% of your previous volume must be replaced by paid ads at €25/user.
*   **Blended CAC Impact:** If you previously acquired 1,000 users (600 organic, 400 paid @ €25 = €10k spend), your CAC was €10. Now, acquiring 1,000 users requires 300 organic and 700 paid (€17.5k spend). Your blended CAC jumps to **€17.50**. If your conversion rates on paid traffic are lower than organic (common), this figure rises further toward €20+.
*   **Runway Projection (Maintaining Growth):**
    *   To maintain growth against 4% churn, you must reinvest heavily. Assuming you need ~1,500 new users/month to grow:
    *   Cost at new mix: ~€28,000/month in ad spend.
    *   Budget Gap: Your €10k budget covers only ~35% of the required volume.
    *   Result: You cannot maintain current growth targets with the current budget. Attempting to do so by exhausting the €10k budget yields fewer users, causing net contraction or stagnation while burn rate remains high. At current burn, the 8-month runway is optimistic if revenue dips due to slower user intake.

*   **Runway Projection (Slowing Growth):**
    *   Strategy: Cap user acquisition to match the €10k budget strictly.
    *   Volume: ~400–500 new users/month (mixed channel).
    *   Outcome: You will likely fail to offset the 4% churn (€1,400 revenue loss) plus grow. Revenue will slowly decline.

This extends the runway beyond 8 months by reducing the pressure to raise immediate capital, buying time to fix SEO or improve retention.

**Recommendation:** Immediately pause aggressive growth targets. The math dictates that pouring €10k into paid channels at €25 CAC cannot replicate the volume previously driven by organic search. Focus the budget on retaining existing users (lowering the 4% churn) rather than expensive acquisition. A 1% reduction in churn saves €350/month instantly, equivalent to acquiring 14 new users via paid ads.

**EU Data Privacy Regulations**

Yes, strict regulations apply before shifting to targeted ads:
1.  **GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation):** Educational apps often process data regarding minors. Targeted advertising based on user behavior or location (dialect) requires explicit, granular **opt-in consent**. Pre-ticked boxes are illegal.
2.  **DSA (Digital Services Act):** Prohibits targeted advertising based on "special category data" (which can include inferred data about language habits linked to ethnic origin or regional identity) without explicit consent.
3.  **Age Appropriateness:** If your app targets students under 18, many EU member states require parental consent for data processing for marketing purposes.
