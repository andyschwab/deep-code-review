**Runway Analysis: ViewPoint VR**

Based on your current financials, **you will not survive a full six-month sales cycle with zero revenue.** You must take immediate action to extend your runway or accelerate cash inflow.

**The Math**
*   **Current Cash:** $350,000
*   **Monthly Burn:** $65,000
*   **Runway:** $350,000 / $65,000 = **5.38 months**

**The Verdict**
You will hit **$0 cash in approximately 5 months and 12 days** (mid-way through the 6th month). Since your sales cycle is expected to take six months from pitch to signed contract, you will run out of money roughly two weeks before the first deal closes. Furthermore, even if you sign on day 180, commercial contracts rarely pay 100% upfront; net-30 or net-60 payment terms would push actual cash receipt to month 7 or 8, guaranteeing insolvency under the current model.

**Immediate Recommendations**

1.  **Reduce Burn Immediately (Priority: Critical)**
    To survive a 6-month cycle plus a 30-day payment lag (7 months total), you need a runway of at least 7–8 months.
    *   **Target Burn:** $45,000/month (to reach ~7.7 months runway).
    *   **Action:** You need to cut costs by **$20,000/month** immediately. With a team of eight, this likely requires reducing headcount by 2–3 roles or implementing significant salary deferrals/equity swaps for leadership. Delaying product features is secondary; labor is your primary cost driver.

2.  **Restructure Deal Terms (Priority: High)**
    Do not wait for the "signed contract" to generate cash.
    *   **Action:** Introduce a "Mobilization Fee" or "Deposit" of 20–30% ($9,000–$13,500) due upon signing to cover initial scan costs.
    *   **Action:** Push for milestone billing (e.g., 50% upon project kickoff, 50% upon delivery) rather than 100% upon completion. Closing two deals with a 30% deposit ($27,000 total) in Month 4 or 5 could bridge the gap without drastic layoffs.

3.  **Accelerate the Cycle**
    *   **Action:** Offer a "Fast-Track" discount (e.g., 10% off) for deals signed and deposited within 30 days. Losing margin is preferable to losing the company.

**Conclusion**
Your current trajectory leads to insolvency in Month 5. You cannot afford to wait for the standard sales cycle to play out without modifying either your cost structure or your payment terms. Cut burn to $45k/month immediately or secure upfront deposits on the pending negotiations to survive.

*Note: This analysis assumes constant burn and no unexpected expenses. Any delay in the sales cycle beyond 6 months worsens the outlook significantly.*
