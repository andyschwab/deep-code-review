### Monthly Net Profit Comparison: BeanRoute Loyalty Program

**Current State (Before Loyalty Program)**
*   **Monthly Orders:** 800
*   **Revenue per Order:** $42
*   **Cost per Order:** $22
*   **Margin per Order:** $20
*   **Total Monthly Revenue:** $33,600
*   **Total Monthly COGS:** $17,600
*   **Current Monthly Net Profit:** **$16,000**

**Projected State (After Loyalty Program)**
*   **Projected Order Volume:** 920 orders (800 + 15% increase)
*   **Revenue per Order:** $42
*   **Adjusted Cost per Order:** $24.10 ($22 base + ~$2.10 redemption cost representing the 5% margin reduction)
*   **Adjusted Margin per Order:** $17.90
*   **Total Monthly Revenue:** $38,640
*   **Total Monthly COGS (incl. rewards):** $22,172
*   **Projected Monthly Net Profit:** **$16,468**

**Financial Verdict**
The loyalty program increases your total monthly net profit by **$468**. While your margin per unit drops from $20 to $17.90, the 15% volume increase (120 additional orders) generates enough gross profit to offset the lower margins and the cost of the rewards.

**Marketing Budget Implications**
You currently generate $16,000 in profit. Under the new model, you generate $16,468. This means the program creates a **$468 monthly surplus** compared to your current state.

To determine if this funds your acquisition costs:
1.  You need to acquire **120 additional orders** to hit the projected volume.
2.  If you currently acquire customers organically or with existing spend, any *new* marketing spend required to generate these 120 orders must be less than $468/month to remain profitable relative to today.
3.  This allows for a new Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) of roughly **$3.90 per incremental order** ($468 surplus / 120 new orders).

If your current marketing efficiency allows you to acquire these additional 120 orders for less than $3.90 each, the program successfully funds its own growth. If acquiring these specific incremental orders costs more than $3.90 each, the program will erode your total net profit below the current $16,000 baseline, even though the volume is higher.

**Note:** This analysis assumes the 5% margin reduction accurately captures all redemption liabilities; ensure your point expiration policies are strict to prevent liability creep.
Monitor the "break-even CAC" of $3.90 closely; if paid ads exceed this, rely on email/SMS channels to drive the repeat volume.
