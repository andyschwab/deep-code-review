### Warranty Reserve Strategy & Structure

**1. Recommended Reserve Allocation**
To maintain your required **25,000 CHF** operational liquidity from your current **40,000 CHF** reserves, you can allocate a maximum of **15,000 CHF** specifically for the new warranty liability.

Based on your volume of **120 repairs/month** (1,440 annually), here is the per-job reserve calculation:
*   **Total Allocatable Reserve:** 15,000 CHF
*   **Annual Repair Volume:** 1,440 jobs
*   **Reserve Per Job:** **~10.40 CHF**

You should internally set aside **10.50 CHF per repair** into a dedicated warranty ledger. This creates a self-funding pool that grows with volume. If catastrophic failures exceed this pool in a given year, the excess is treated as an operational loss rather than a pre-funded liability, protecting your core capital.

**2. Tiered vs. Flat Fee Model**
A **Tiered Warranty Model** is strictly superior to a flat fee for your business case.

*   **The Risk:** A flat "one-year comprehensive" warranty exposes you to unlimited liability on rare part failures (up to 2,000 CHF). Since you cannot accurately predict the frequency of these events under the new one-year term, a flat model forces you to hoard cash to cover worst-case scenarios.
*   **The Solution:** Structure the warranty to cap your exposure while offering perceived value.

**Proposed Tiered Structure:**

*   **Tier 1: Standard Guarantee (Included in Price)**
    *   **Coverage:** 6 months on labor and standard adjustments.
    *   **Exclusions:** Rare part replacements, water damage, or user impact.
    *   **Purpose:** Maintains your current risk profile (3% follow-up rate) without additional reserve requirements.

*   **Tier 2: Premium Protection (Optional Add-on: ~45 CHF)**
    *   **Coverage:** Extends labor guarantee to 12 months.
    *   **Parts Cap:** Covers parts up to **250 CHF**.
    *   **Logic:** This covers the vast majority of genuine defects without exposing you to the 2,000 CHF catastrophic events. The 45 CHF fee immediately funds the reserve (4x your calculated 10.50 CHF need), creating an instant profit center for low-risk extensions.

*   **Tier 3: Elite Comprehensive (High-Value Watches Only)**
    *   **Eligibility:** Watches with original repair bills >1,500 CHF.
    *   **Coverage:** 12 months labor + parts.
    *   **Condition:** Requires a specific inspection of the "rare parts" in question before approval.
    *   **Strategy:** By limiting this to high-bill repairs, you increase the margin buffer to absorb potential 2,000 CHF hits. You only accept this risk where the initial revenue justifies the reserve hit.

**Implementation Note:** Do not market the "One-Year Warranty" as a blanket promise. Market the "12-Month Labor Guarantee" as standard, with "Parts Protection" as an upgrade. This shifts the psychological framing from "insurance against failure" to "extended care," allowing you to price the risk accurately without tying up your 25,000 CHF operational buffer.

*Note: Review the specific failure modes of the "rare parts" historically; if they are age-related rather than repair-related, exclude them explicitly to prevent moral hazard.*
