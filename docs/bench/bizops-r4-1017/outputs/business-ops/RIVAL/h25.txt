### Unit Economics Evaluation: TruckEats

**Current Model Viability**
Your current transaction fee model is **not viable** at an $8.50 average order value (AOV).

*   **Revenue per Transaction:** ($8.50 × 1.5%) + $0.30 = **$0.4275**
*   **Estimated Payment Processor Cost:** Standard rates are ~2.9% + $0.30.
    *   Cost: ($8.50 × 2.9%) + $0.30 = **$0.5465**
*   **Net Revenue per Transaction:** $0.4275 - $0.5465 = **-$0.119** (Loss)

At 5,000 transactions, you are losing approximately **$595/month** purely on payment processing spread, before accounting for your $1,200 fixed server costs or developer salaries. You are effectively subsidizing every transaction.

**Break-Even Analysis for 20% Gross Margin**
To achieve a 20% gross margin on the transaction fee revenue (assuming the 2.9% + $0.30 processor cost remains constant and you do not pass the fee to the consumer), the math requires a significant increase in order size.

Let $X$ = Required Average Order Value.
*   Your Revenue: $0.015X + 0.30$
*   Processor Cost: $0.029X + 0.30$
*   Target: Your Revenue must be 125% of Processor Cost (to leave a 20% margin on your revenue).

Equation: $0.015X + 0.30 = 1.25(0.029X + 0.30)$
$0.015X + 0.30 = 0.03625X + 0.375$
$-0.075 = 0.02125X$

**Result:** This equation yields a negative number, meaning **it is mathematically impossible** to achieve a positive margin, let alone 20%, with a 1.5% fee when your cost basis (processor fee) is 2.9%. You are charging less than half of what it costs you to process the payment.

**Revised Break-Even (Covering Costs + 20% Margin)**
If we assume you meant covering your *fixed costs* ($1,200) plus a 20% margin on total revenue:
1.  Total Monthly Cost Needed: $1,200 (servers) + Processor Costs.
2.  To simply cover the $1,200 fixed cost with 5,000 transactions, you need $0.24 net profit per transaction *after* processor fees.
3.  Since you are currently losing $0.12 per transaction, you need to swing revenue by $0.36 per transaction.
4.  Because your fee percentage (1.5%) is lower than the processor cost percentage (2.9%), increasing volume or AOV under this specific fee structure **never** solves the problem. The gap widens as volume grows.

**Strategic Recommendation**

**Pivot Immediately.** Do not attempt to influence user behavior to solve this.

1.  **The Fatal Flaw:** You cannot build a business where your take rate (1.5%) is lower than your cost of goods sold (payment processing ~2.9%). No amount of order volume or slight AOV increases will fix a negative unit margin structure.
2.  **Recommended Model:** Switch to a **Flat Monthly Subscription** per truck.
    *   Food trucks prefer predictable costs.
    *   Charge **$49–$79/month** per truck.
    *   At 50 trucks @ $50/mo = $2,500 MRR. This covers your $1,200 server cost immediately, leaving $1,300 for partial developer coverage, with zero variable loss per transaction.
3.  **Hybrid Alternative:** If you must keep transaction fees, charge the food truck a **monthly base fee ($29)** to cover platform costs, and pass the **full payment processing fee (2.9% + $0.30)** directly to the customer or the truck as a "convenience fee." Do not absorb processing costs with a lower markup.

Attempting to raise AOV from $8.50 to a level where a 1.5% fee covers a 2.9% cost is impossible; the percentage gap ensures you lose money on every single sale regardless of size.
