### Revenue Projection & Volatility Analysis

**Current State (Flat $29/mo):**
*   **Monthly Revenue:** $3,480 (120 users × $29)
*   **Costs:** $450
*   **Profit:** $3,030
*   **Volatility:** Low revenue variance, but high churn risk (8%) due to perceived lack of value during slow months.

**Proposed State (Per-Word @ $0.002):**
*   **Baseline Scenario (Average 15k words):** $30/user → $3,600 total revenue.
*   **Peak Season (+50% volume to 22.5k words):** $45/user → $5,400 total revenue.
*   **Slow Season (-60% volume to 6k words):** $12/user → $1,440 total revenue.
*   **Costs:** $450 (fixed).
*   **Profit Range:** $990 (slow) to $4,950 (peak).

**Verdict: Do Not Switch to Pure Per-Word Pricing.**

While your intuition correctly identifies that flat pricing causes churn during slow seasons, switching entirely to a usage metric ($0.002/word) introduces **dangerous revenue volatility** that threatens your fixed costs.

In the slow season scenario above, your revenue drops to $1,440. While this covers your $450 server costs, it leaves only $990 for your own compensation and growth. More critically, if your user base shrinks slightly or usage drops further (e.g., -70%), you approach a break-even crisis. Pure usage pricing transfers all market risk from the customer to you; when translators don't work, you don't get paid, making your cash flow *more* unpredictable, not less.

Furthermore, billing complexity will increase significantly. You will face disputes over word counts, need robust tracking infrastructure, and deal with "bill shock" anxiety from users who suddenly owe $60 instead of $29. This friction often *increases* churn among price-sensitive freelancers.

**Recommended Strategy: Hybrid "Base + Overage" Model**

To stabilize cash flow while aligning with value, adopt a tiered hybrid model rather than pure usage:

1.  **Base Plan ($19/mo):** Includes up to 10,000 words/month.
    *   *Why:* Lowers the entry barrier during slow seasons, reducing the incentive to cancel. Even at 50% usage, users pay something.
2.  **Overage Fee ($0.003/word):** Charged only on words exceeding the 10k limit.
    *   *Why:* Captures extra value during peak seasons when users are earning more and less price-sensitive.

**Projected Hybrid Outcome:**
*   **Slow Season (6k words):** User pays $19. Revenue: $2,280. (Better than $1,440 pure usage).
*   **Average Season (15k words):** User pays $19 + (5k × $0.003) = $34. Revenue: $4,080.
*   **Peak Season (22.5k words):** User pays $19 + (12.5k × $0.003) = $56.50. Revenue: $6,780.

This approach guarantees a baseline revenue floor well above your costs while allowing upside capture during busy periods. It directly addresses the churn driver (paying $29 for little work) without exposing you to the extreme downside of pure usage pricing.

*Note: Implement hard caps or email alerts at 80% of limit to prevent bill shock. Consider an annual pre-pay option for a 15% discount to secure cash flow upfront.*
