**The Call:** Do not raise the price to $14.00 immediately; instead, implement a "Good-Better-Best" product mix shift by launching a temporary "Lavender Blend" bar at $9.50 (reformulated) while keeping the original "Pure Lavender" at $11.50, and reduce the pure bar size by 15% to protect cash flow without a shocking price hike.

**The Numbers:**
*Current State (Crisis):*
Price: $8.50 | Variable Cost: $7.40 | Margin: $1.10/unit
Monthly Contribution: ($1.10 × 15,000) - $45,000 = **-$28,500 burn**.
Runway: $120,000 / $28,500 ≈ **4.2 months** (matches your estimate).

*Option A: Raise Price to $14.00 (Maintain 60% Margin)*
Target Margin Check: ($14.00 - $7.40) / $14.00 = 47% (Note: $14 does not actually yield 60% margin on $7.40 cost; you would need ~$18.50).
Assumption: Sales volume drops 40% due to delisting/price shock (Industry rule of thumb for >50% price hikes in CPG).
New Volume: 9,000 units.
Contribution: (($14.00 - $7.40) × 9,000) - $45,000 = **+$14,400 profit**.
*Risk:* High probability of permanent retail partner loss.

*Option B: Reformulate ("Lavender Blend") & Right-Size*
New Product: 70% Lavender, 30% cheaper oils (e.g., Litsea). New Cost: $4.50.
New Size: 85% of original weight (perceived as "new edition").
Price: $9.50.
Assumption: 60% of customers switch to new blend; 40% stay with Pure (at reduced volume).
Pure Bar Strategy: Raise to $11.50, reduce size 15% (Cost drops to ~$6.30).
Mixed Volume: 15,000 total (assuming retention).
Contribution: [(9,000 × ($9.50-$4.50)) + (6,000 × ($11.50-$6.30))] - $45,000
= ($45,000 + $31,200) - $45,000 = **+$31,200 profit**.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The critical input is **price elasticity of demand** for your specific brand loyalty. If more than 55% of customers refuse the reformulated blend, Option B fails.
*Trigger:* If Month 1 sales of the "Blend" are below 7,500 units, immediately pivot to shrinking the pure bar size further or negotiating temporary cost-sharing with retail partners.
*Rule of Thumb:* In CPG, a 10-15% "shrinkflation" is less noticeable than a 60% price hike.
*Step this week:* If you can source a complementary oil under $2/oz, consider launching the "Blend" as a limited "Harvest Rescue" edition to frame the change as transparent rather than deceptive.

Confirm with your **retail partners** (Tier 2/3 boundary) whether a temporary SKU change requires new slotting fees or contract amendments. Confirm with a **product liability attorney** (Tier 3) if changing the ingredient list requires new labeling compliance or allergen re-testing before sale.
