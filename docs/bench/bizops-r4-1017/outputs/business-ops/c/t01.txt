**The call.** Reject the switch to pure per-word pricing as structured; it replaces predictable churn volatility with severe usage volatility that likely lowers revenue for light users while failing to capture enough value from heavy users to offset the loss, given your current 8% churn.

**The numbers.**
*Current Model (Flat):*
Revenue = Subscribers × Price
Base: 120 × $29 = **$3,480/mo**.
*Assumption:* If churn hits 8% immediately without replacement, revenue drops to ~$3,200 next month. Fixed costs are $450, leaving ~$3,030 contribution.

*Proposed Model (Per-Word):*
Revenue = Subscribers × Avg Words × Rate
Base Case (Your Assumption): 120 × 15,000 × $0.002 = **$3,600/mo**.
Low Usage Scenario (Holiday/Slow Season - ASSUMED 40% drop): 120 × 9,000 × $0.002 = **$2,160/mo**.
High Usage Scenario (Peak Season - ASSUMED 150% spike): 120 × 22,500 × $0.002 = **$5,400/mo**.

*Comparison:* The flat model varies only by subscriber count (churn). The per-word model varies by both count *and* individual usage. Your "slow season" hypothesis suggests usage drops 40-50% for freelancers. Under the per-word model, a 40% usage drop crashes revenue to $2,160, which is 38% lower than your current stable baseline of $3,480. To match current revenue at $0.002/word, every user must process at least 14,583 words. If variance is high (some do 5k, some do 50k), your revenue becomes unpredictable.

**What flips it, and the trigger.**
The deciding input is the **standard deviation of word counts** across your 120 users.
*Trigger:* If more than 30% of your users process fewer than 10,000 words/month during slow periods, the per-word model will reduce total revenue compared to the flat fee.
*Rule of Thumb:* Usage-based pricing stabilizes cash flow only if usage is counter-cyclical to churn (e.g., users stay but use less) AND the rate is set high enough that low-usage revenue covers fixed costs. Here, low usage coincides with low willingness to pay.
*Step this week:* Export the last 6 months of word-count logs. If the coefficient of variation (standard deviation / mean) is >0.5, **consider** a hybrid "Good-Better-Best" tier (e.g., $19 for up to 10k words, $39 for up to 30k, $0.001 overage) rather than pure utility pricing.
