**The Call**
Adopt a tiered warranty model (Standard vs. Premium) rather than a flat fee; this caps your exposure on catastrophic part failures while remaining competitive. Immediately reserve **28 CHF per repair job** for the new warranty liability, which protects your 25,000 CHF operational liquidity floor while covering expected historical claims.

**The Numbers**
*Formula:* Monthly Reserve Need = (Volume × Follow-up Rate × Avg Cost) + (Volume × Catastrophic Rate × Max Cost).
*Current Baseline (6-month, informal):*
- Volume: 120 repairs/month.
- Follow-up: 3% (3.6 jobs). Assuming average follow-up cost is modest (e.g., labor adjustment), let's estimate 150 CHF/job → **540 CHF/month**.
- Catastrophic events: Unknown frequency under 1-year term.
*Scenario Analysis for 1-Year Warranty (Annualized Liability):*
To maintain 25,000 CHF liquidity from your 40,000 CHF reserves, you can allocate **15,000 CHF** total to warranty reserves.
- **Base Case (Catastrophes remain rare):** If catastrophic events occur at 0.5% (approx. 1 every 17 months currently, potentially doubling to 1 every 8 months under 1-year coverage).
  - Est. Monthly Catastrophic Cost: 120 jobs × 0.5% × 2,000 CHF = **1,200 CHF**.
  - Total Monthly Liability: 540 (follow-up) + 1,200 (catastrophic) = **1,740 CHF**.
  - Required Reserve Buffer (6 months coverage): 1,740 × 6 = **10,440 CHF**. (Well within the 15,000 CHF limit).
  - *Reserve per job:* 1,740 / 120 = **14.50 CHF**.
- **Stress Case (Catastrophes double due to extended term):** Frequency rises to 1.0% (1.2 jobs/month).
  - Est. Monthly Catastrophic Cost: 120 × 1.0% × 2,000 = **2,400 CHF**.
  - Total Monthly Liability: 540 + 2,400 = **2,940 CHF**.
  - Required Reserve Buffer (6 months): 2,940 × 6 = **17,640 CHF**. (Exceeds 15,000 CHF limit).
  - *Reserve per job:* 2,940 / 120 = **24.50 CHF**.
- **Decision:** To stay safely under the 15,000 CHF cap even if frequency spikes slightly, round up the reserve to **28 CHF/job** (3,360 CHF/month accumulation rate), allowing you to build the buffer quickly or absorb a shock without dipping into operational cash.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes this call is the **frequency of catastrophic part failures** under the extended 12-month term.
*Trigger:* If you experience **more than 2 catastrophic part failures (≥2,000 CHF) in any rolling 90-day period** after launch, pause the unlimited Premium tier and switch to a "parts-excluded" warranty or cap the payout at 1,500 CHF.
*Rule of Thumb:* If the claim rate exceeds 1.5% of total volume, the flat reserve model breaks.
*This Week's Step:* If your historical data shows zero catastrophic failures in the last 24 months, consider launching with a **6-month Premium trial** instead of 12 months to validate the risk before committing to the full year.

**What to Confirm, and with Whom**
You must confirm whether your proposed warranty terms constitute an **insurance contract** under Swiss law (FINMA regulations) or a standard service guarantee. Selling a warranty that covers random, high-cost future events for a fee can sometimes trigger insurance licensing requirements. Bring your draft warranty terms to a **Swiss commercial attorney** specializing in consumer protection or insurance law to verify you are not inadvertently acting as an unlicensed insurer.
