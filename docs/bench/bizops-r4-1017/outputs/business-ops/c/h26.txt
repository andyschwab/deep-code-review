**The Call**
Do not invest in dedicated print-on-demand (POD) infrastructure yet; instead, run a targeted validation campaign using your existing mailing list to prove demand. With your current margins, you need to sell approximately **2,143 units** just to cover the $30,000 marketing spend, which exceeds your total annual volume of 1,500 units. This indicates the campaign alone cannot break even without capturing significant new market share, making a "test and learn" approach safer than a full infrastructure pivot.

**The Numbers**
*   **Unit Contribution Margin:** Price ($22) – Variable Cost ($9) = **$13 per unit**.
*   **Marketing Break-Even Volume:** Campaign Cost ($30,000) / Margin ($13) = **2,308 units**.
    *   *Note: This is the volume required solely to pay for the ads, ignoring fixed costs and the cost of goods for those specific units if the $9 includes only production.*
    *   *Correction for true cash break-even on the spend:* If the $9 is the cost to make the book, your profit per book is $13. To recover $30,000 cash outlay: $30,000 / $13 = **2,308 additional units**.
*   **Total Break-Even (Campaign + COGS):** If the $30k is pure marketing and you must also pay the $9 production cost for every new unit sold:
    *   Revenue per unit: $22
    *   Total Variable Cost per unit: $9 (production) + ($30,000 / X units).
    *   Simpler view: You need $30,000 in *contribution margin*. Since margin is $13, you need **2,308 new sales**.
*   **Reality Check:** Your current annual volume is 1,500 units. Achieving 2,308 *new* sales requires growing your business by **154%** in one campaign cycle.

**Assumptions (Marked ASSUMED):**
*   **Conversion Rate:** ASSUMED 2% from mailing list (industry standard for niche hobbies). If true, 8,000 subscribers × 2% = 160 units. This falls far short of the 2,308 needed.
*   **Cold Traffic Conversion:** ASSUMED 0.5% for paid ads. To get 2,308 sales, you need ~460,000 ad impressions/clicks, likely exceeding your $30k budget depending on Cost Per Click (CPC).

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes this call is your **mailing list conversion rate**.
*   **Trigger Value:** If your list converts at **19%** (2,308 units / 8,000 subs), the campaign breaks even on its own. This is highly improbable for cold or warm leads.
*   **Pivot Rule:** If a test blast to 1,000 subscribers yields fewer than 50 pre-orders (5% conversion), do not launch the $30k campaign.
*   **Actionable Step:** This week, send a "fake door" test email to 500 subscribers offering a pre-order at $22. If fewer than 25 people buy, consider halving the marketing budget and focusing on organic community growth instead of paid acquisition.

**What to Confirm, and with Whom**
*   **Infrastructure Contracts:** Confirm whether your current printer offers true "print-on-demand" (unit cost $9 at qty 1) or if that price relies on batch quantities. If the $9 rises to $14 for single units, your margin collapses to $8, raising the break-even to 3,750 units. Confirm these tiered pricing schedules with your **printing vendor**.
*   **Securities/Equity:** If funding this expansion requires taking money from outside investors in exchange for future profits, consult a **securities attorney** to ensure compliance with Washington State fundraising regulations.
