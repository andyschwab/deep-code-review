**The call.** Do not pivot exclusively to institutional clients; the six-month sales cycle creates a cash-flow valley that individual subscriptions currently bridge, so pursue a hybrid strategy where institutional growth is prioritized but individual acquisition continues to fund the wait.

**The numbers.**
*Formula:* LTV = (ARPA × Gross Margin) / Churn Rate. (Assuming 100% gross margin for digital goods).
*Institutional LTV:* $2,500 / ~0% churn (using 1% annualized for math) = ~$250,000.
*Individual LTV:* ($15 × 12) / (8% monthly churn) = $180 / 0.08 = $2,250 annualized value, but strictly monthly: $15 / 0.08 = $187.50.
*Payback Period:*
Institutional: $1,200 CAC / ($2,500 / 12 months) = ~5.8 months to recover cost.
Individual: $0 CAC (organic) / $15 = Instant.

*Assumptions (marked ASSUMED):*
1. Institutional churn is 1% annually (ASSUMED low, based on "nearly zero").
2. Individual CAC is $0 (ASSUMED, as you cited only commissions for institutions).
3. Sales cycle is exactly 6 months with no revenue recognized prior (ASSUMED conservative).

*Results:*
One institutional client ($2,500/yr) equals the annual value of ~14 individual subscribers ($15/mo). However, acquiring that one client takes 6 months and $1,200. In those 6 months, you forego the cash flow of potentially hundreds of instant individual sign-ups. If you stop individual marketing today, you lose ~$4,800/month in recurring revenue (assuming 320 new sign-ups needed just to offset 8% churn of 4,000 users) while spending $7,200 (6 months × $1,200) to close just one deal that pays $2,500 upfront. You would burn cash immediately.

**What flips it, and the trigger.**
The flip input is **Individual Churn**. If you can lower individual churn from 8% to 4%, your Individual LTV doubles to $375, making the segment significantly more efficient to scale than the slow institutional motion.
*Trigger:* If institutional sales pipeline value does not exceed 3× your monthly burn rate by **Q3**, halt exclusive B2B focus.
*Rule of Thumb:* Never let committed recurring revenue (ARR) from new logos drop below 20% of total ARR in a single quarter during a pivot.
*Step:* If individual churn remains >6% next month, consider implementing a "annual pre-pay" discount for individuals to lock in cash flow before chasing large deals.
