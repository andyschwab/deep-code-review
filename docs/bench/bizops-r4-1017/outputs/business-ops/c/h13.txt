**The Call**
Reject the current unbundled model immediately; at 1,200 units, clinics operate at a loss ($-25/pair before software), guaranteeing churn. Switch to a fully bundled per-unit price of $235 to align clinic costs with insurance caps, eliminating the non-reimbursable SaaS friction.

**The Numbers**
*Clinic Unit Economics (Current Model)*
Formula: `Clinic Margin = Reimbursement - (Hardware Price + Allocated Software Cost)`
Inputs: Reimbursement $185 (Given); Hardware Price $210 (Given); Volume 1,200 (Given); Fixed Software Cost $12,000 (Given).
Allocated Software Cost: $12,000 / 1,200 = $10/pair.
Total Clinic Cost: $210 + $10 = $220/pair.
Result: $185 - $220 = **-$35/pair loss**.
*Verdict:* Clinics lose $35 on every pair printed, excluding their own labor/overhead. They cannot sustain this.

*Your Unit Economics (Bundled Model)*
Formula: `Your Contribution = (Bundled Price - Variable Cost) * Volume - Fixed Costs`
Inputs: Variable Cost $45 (Given); Fixed Costs $12,000 (Given).
Scenario A (Current Separated): ($210 - $45) * 1,200 - $12,000 = $198,000 - $12,000 = **$186,000 Monthly Contribution**.
Scenario B (Bundled at $235): ($235 - $45) * 1,200 - $12,000 = $228,000 - $12,000 = **$216,000 Monthly Contribution**.
*Note:* The bundled price ($235) exceeds the insurance cap ($185). This forces the clinic to absorb a $50 gap or charge the patient directly. If the cap is a hard legal ceiling with no patient co-pay allowed, the market size is zero at current costs.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is the **allowable patient co-pay or private pay percentage**.
*Trigger:* If clinics can collect >$50/pair from patients (or private insurance) above the $185 cap, the bundled model works. If the $185 cap is absolute and non-negotiable for all patients, your variable cost ($45) plus required clinic margin makes the current value proposition impossible.
*Rule of Thumb:* If >20% of cases are private pay (uncapped), proceed with bundling. If 100% are capped at $185, you must reduce your price to <$140/pair to leave the clinic any margin.
*This Week's Step:* If >20% of volume is private pay, propose the $235 bundled rate immediately; if not, pause sales and model a price reduction to $140 to test volume elasticity.
