**The Call:** Adopt a non-refundable, metal-weighted deposit required at the time of quoting, paired with a strict 48-hour price validity window. This approach secures your material cost immediately without embedding a large, invisible buffer that might deter customers or complex clauses that confuse them.

**The Numbers:**
Your current risk exposure per ring is calculated as:
`Risk = (Gold Weight) × (Price Swing)`
Using your figures: `0.5 oz × $50/oz = $250` potential loss per order.
With a fixed labor fee of $400, a $250 swing consumes **62.5%** of your margin.

*Option A: Validity Period Only*
If you simply set a 48-hour expiry, you lose the sale if the client hesitates, but you lose $0 margin on accepted quotes.
*Assumption:* Clients decide within 48 hours (Base case). If they take 5 days (Low case), you must re-quote, risking customer friction.

*Option B: Embedded Buffer*
Adding a $250 buffer to every quote raises the price to $2,050.
*Result:* You protect margin 100% of the time, but you may reduce conversion rates if competitors do not surcharge.

*Option C: Metal-Weighted Deposit (Recommended)*
Require a deposit equal to the current spot value of the gold (approx. $1,000–$1,100 depending on market) + 50% of labor ($200) upfront.
*Formula:* `Deposit = (0.5 oz × Current Spot) + $200`
*Result:* You purchase the metal immediately upon deposit. The remaining balance is fixed labor. Your margin is locked regardless of future spot swings.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The deciding factor is your **client conversion rate**. If requiring an upfront metal deposit causes your close rate to drop below 80% of current levels, the strategy is too friction-heavy.
*Trigger:* Track your next 20 quotes. If fewer than 16 convert with the new deposit requirement, pivot to a "Price Lock Fee" model (charge a small non-refundable fee to hold the price for 7 days) instead of full pre-payment.
*Rule of Thumb:* In high-volatility commodity businesses, passing raw material risk to the consumer via deposits is standard; absorbing it via buffers is rare for custom work.
*This Week's Step:* Draft a one-sentence addendum to your quote: "To secure this estimate, a deposit covering the current gold weight is required; this amount is applied to the final balance and ensures your price is locked against market fluctuations."
