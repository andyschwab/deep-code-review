**The Call**
Reject the venture debt proposal as structured; the monthly debt service combined with your operating burn will deplete cash before reaching 150 subscribers. The hardware subsidy model creates a fatal timing mismatch where upfront capital requirements ($200k) exceed early recurring revenue accumulation. Pivot immediately to a "Bring-Your-Own-Device" (BYOD) model or require full hardware purchase upfront to eliminate the $160,000 cash drag.

**The Numbers**
*Formula:* Monthly Cash Flow = (Subscribers × $99) − Operating Expenses − Debt Service.
*Debt Service Estimate:* On $250,000 at 12% annual interest, interest-only payments are ~$2,500/month; principal + interest over 3 years is ~$8,300/month. We will use the conservative interest-only figure for this stress test.
*Fixed Monthly Burn:* $35,000 (Ops) + $2,500 (Debt Interest) = $37,500.
*Break-even Subscribers:* $37,500 / $99 ≈ 379 subscribers.

*Scenario Analysis (Months 1–6):*
Assumption: Subscriber growth is linear from 0 to 150 over 6 months (Average ~75 subscribers).
- **Revenue (Avg):** 75 subs × $99 = $7,425/month.
- **Total Outflow:** $37,500 (Burn) + $33,333 (Amortized Hardware/fit-out cost recovery needed).
- **Net Cash Flow:** $7,425 − $37,500 = **($30,075) monthly deficit**.
- **Cumulative Gap (6 months):** ~$180,450 deficit, excluding the initial $200,000 upfront outlay.
- **Result:** You run out of cash well before month 6. Even at 150 subscribers (Month 6 run rate), revenue is $14,850, covering less than 40% of your monthly cash obligations.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is the **Upfront Hardware Cost per User**.
*Trigger Value:* If the upfront cost per user drops to $0 (BYOD) or is fully paid by the user ($800+), the $160,000 hole disappears, and the break-even subscriber count drops to ~354 (still high, but manageable with equity).
*Rule of Thumb:* If Customer Acquisition Cost (including hardware subsidy) exceeds 12 months of Lifetime Value (LTV), the unit economics are broken. Here, subsidy ($750) + CAC assumptions likely exceed the first-year margin ($688).
*Action:* If you cannot secure non-dilutive grants or pre-sales covering 100% of hardware costs by next Friday, consider pausing the subsidized hardware rollout and launching a BYOD pilot.

**What to Confirm, and with Whom**
**Tier 3 (Route):** The specific terms of the venture debt (covenants, warrant coverage, personal guarantees) and whether offering a subscription tied to a subsidized asset constitutes a secured transaction or lease requiring specific UCC filings.
**Bring to:** A venture debt specialist or securities attorney. Do not sign term sheets without verifying if the hardware collateralization triggers additional compliance costs or changes the effective APR significantly above 12%.
