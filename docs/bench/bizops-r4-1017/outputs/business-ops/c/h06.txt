**The Call:** The current shift to paid ads is unsustainable at your growth targets; you will exhaust cash in under five months. Immediately slow growth to preserve runway and diversify acquisition channels beyond paid search.

**The Numbers:**
Your blended Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) has shifted due to the traffic drop.
*   **Old Blended CAC:** €12 (given).
*   **New Scenario:** Organic traffic (60% of volume) dropped by 50%. Your new mix is roughly 43% organic and 57% paid.
    *   *Formula:* `(Organic % × €0) + (Paid % × €25)`. *Note: Organic cost is treated as €0 for marginal cash outflow, though labor costs exist.*
    *   *New Blended CAC:* ~€14.25 (assuming organic remains free) to €25 (if all new growth must be paid).
    *   *Conservative Assumption:* To maintain *growth targets*, you must replace the lost organic volume with paid users. New Blended CAC ≈ **€21–€25**.

**Unit Economics & Runway:**
*   **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR):** €35,000.
*   **Churn:** 4% monthly (€1,400 lost revenue).
*   **Net Growth Target:** To grow, you must replace churn (€1,400) plus add new revenue.
*   **Marketing Budget:** €10,000/month.
*   **Users Acquirable at €25 CAC:** 400 users/month.
*   **Revenue from New Users:** Assuming similar value to existing base, 400 users might generate ~€2,300–€3,000 MRR (estimated).
*   **Net Burn:** If you spend the full €10k to acquire ~€3k in recurring revenue, you are burning significant cash against immediate returns.
*   **Runway Projection:**
    *   *Current Path (Aggressive Paid):* High burn rate. If total monthly burn (ops + marketing) exceeds €50k (implied by 8-month runway on current cash), shifting to higher CAC accelerates this. At a blended CAC of €25, your efficiency drops sharply.
    *   *Slow Growth Path:* Cap paid spend to only replace churn (€1,400 revenue needed / ~€25 CAC ≈ 56 users). Spend ~€1,400 on ads. Save €8,600/month. This extends runway significantly, potentially doubling it.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The **Paid CAC** is the flip input. If you can lower paid CAC below €15 through creative testing or channel diversification, aggressive growth becomes viable again.
*   **Trigger:** If blended CAC exceeds €18 for two consecutive weeks, pause all non-churn-replacement spending.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** LTV:CAC should remain above 3:1. With 4% monthly churn, average lifespan is ~25 months. If Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) is ~€8.75 (€35k/4k users est.), LTV is ~€218. A €25 CAC is mathematically sustainable (ratio ~8:1), *but* the cash flow timing of paying €25 today for revenue over 25 months causes the runway crisis.
*   **Step this week:** If paid CAC stays above €20, consider reallocating 50% of the ad budget to retention campaigns to lower the 4% churn, which improves LTV instantly without new acquisition costs.

**What to Confirm, and with Whom:**
Regarding EU data privacy: Shifting to paid ads involves processing personal data for targeting. The **GDPR** and the **ePrivacy Directive** apply. Specifically, using data for behavioral targeting in educational contexts may require explicit consent (opt-in) rather than legitimate interest, especially if minors are involved. The **Digital Services Act (DSA)** may also impose transparency obligations on ad targeting.
*   **Action:** Consult an EU-based privacy counsel to review your ad-tech stack and consent mechanisms before scaling paid spend. Do not rely on platform defaults for compliance.
