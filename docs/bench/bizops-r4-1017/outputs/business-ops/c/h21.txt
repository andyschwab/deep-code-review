*Break-even Analysis on Orders vs. OpEx:*
You have a $4,500 buffer to cover Operating Expenses ($O$) while waiting for the next batch payment.
If your two builders and part-time admin cost more than $3,000/month (a conservative estimate for Portland labor + overhead), your 45-day OpEx is ~$4,500.
*   **Base Case (OpEx = $4,500):** You break even on cash flow with **zero** new orders. Any new order adds liquidity.
*   **Risk Case (OpEx = $6,000):** You are short $1,500 immediately. You need **one** upfront customer ($2,400) or **two** installment customers ($1,600) just to cover the gap, ignoring the production cost.
