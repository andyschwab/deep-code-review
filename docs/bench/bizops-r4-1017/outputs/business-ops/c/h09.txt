The decision flips if the luxury listing volume contraction exceeds **36%**.

However, because we are comparing total profit, the specific flip point where total profit equals the current $240k occurs when luxury revenue falls to $30,000 (impossible at 25% on remaining volume) or when standard volume erodes due to seller sentiment.
*   *Refined Trigger:* Monitor the **monthly luxury listing count**. If it drops by more than 36% in the first 60 days post-implementation, the revenue gain from the higher rate is fully erased.
