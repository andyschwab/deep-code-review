**The Arithmetic**

**The Trade-off Analysis**
The institutional model offers superior unit economics (6.25x ratio vs. a capped $187 lifetime value). However, the **cash flow timing** is the critical constraint.
*   **Institutional Cash Flow:** You spend $1,200 today and wait ~6 months to receive $2,500. During those 6 months, that capital is tied up.
*   **Individual Cash Flow:** You receive $15 immediately upon sign-up.
