**The Decision Trigger: The Tiered Model**
A flat one-year warranty exposes you to the unknown frequency of 2,000 CHF failures. If just **two** such events occur in a month, your liquidity drops below the 25,000 CHF threshold immediately.

*   **Tier 1 (Standard - 6 Months):** Covers labor and minor adjustments (your historical 3% risk). Reserve **25 CHF/job**.
*   **Tier 2 (Premium - 1 Year):** Covers parts up to a **500 CHF cap**. Anything above (the 2,000 CHF catastrophes) is excluded or requires a separate "insurance" fee.
*   **Why this works:** It shifts the "unknown variable" (catastrophic frequency) off your balance sheet. You stop hoarding cash for events that happen once a year by capping your exposure per ticket.
