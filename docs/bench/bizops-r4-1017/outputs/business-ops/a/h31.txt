Your solvency depends on bridging the gap between paying for materials/labor and receiving customer payment. Previously, a two-week supplier lead time allowed a tight cash conversion cycle. With a three-month (12-week) lead time, you must pre-fund all inputs without incoming cash from new orders.

3.  **Working Capital Requirement:** The additional capital needed is roughly: `(Monthly Fixed Costs × 3) + (Cost of Goods Sold for 3 months of inventory)`.

**The Flip Input & Solvency Trigger**
The single input that flips this from a liquidity squeeze to insolvency is your **Monthly Burn Rate**.
*   **Liquidity Squeeze:** If Monthly Burn < $50,000, your $150k covers the 3-month gap, though tightly.
*   **Solvency Issue:** If Monthly Burn > $50,000, you will run out of cash before completing and billing the delayed units.
