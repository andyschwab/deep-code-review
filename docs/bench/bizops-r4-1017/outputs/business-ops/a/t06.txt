**The Arithmetic (Lane A)**

We compare your current monthly contribution margin against the projected post-program scenario. Note that "net profit" here refers to contribution margin (Revenue minus Variable Costs), as fixed operating expenses were not provided.

*Current State:*
*   **Volume:** 800 orders/month
*   **Margin per Order:** $42 (Price) - $22 (COGS) = $20
*   **Total Monthly Contribution:** 800 × $20 = **$16,000**

*Projected State (with Loyalty Program):*
*   **New Volume:** 800 × 1.15 (15% increase) = 920 orders/month
*   **New Margin per Order:** The program reduces effective margin by 5%.
    *   Calculation: $20 × (1 - 0.05) = $19 per order.
*   **Total Monthly Contribution:** 920 × $19 = **$17,480**

*The Delta:*
*   **Incremental Profit:** $17,480 - $16,000 = **+$1,480 per month.**

**The Marketing Constraint (The Missing Input)**
You asked if this survives while funding marketing. The program generates 120 *additional* orders (920 - 800). To determine if you can afford acquisition, we calculate the break-even CAC for these new customers.

*   **Available Budget for Acquisition:** The total incremental profit is $1,480.
*   **Break-even CAC Formula:** `Total Incremental Profit ÷ New Customers Acquired`
*   **Calculation:** $1,480 ÷ 120 = **$12.33**

*Correction for Organic vs. Paid:*
The 15% increase includes both organic repeat lifts and new paid acquisition. If *all* 120 new orders are paid acquisitions, your maximum allowable CAC is **$12.33**. If only half (60 orders) are paid and the rest are organic retention lifts, your allowable CAC doubles to **$24.66**.

**Decision Aid**
1.  **Read:** The program adds $1,480/month to your bottom line assuming the volume target is hit.
2.  **Flip Input:** The decision flips if the actual repeat rate increase is less than **10.5%**. Below this threshold, the margin loss outweighs the volume gain.
3.  **Threshold Rule:** If your actual blended CAC to generate these extra orders exceeds **$12.33** (assuming all new volume is paid), the program destroys value.
4.  **Next Step:** Before full launch, run a 4-week pilot tracking the *actual* redemption rate. If redemptions exceed 5% of order value, recalculate the margin per order immediately.
