**The Sustainability Test (Lane A Application)**
You asked if the economics hold if this traffic level (45,000 sessions) becomes the new normal. The answer depends entirely on the **conversion rate** (Sessions → Paid Postings), which you identified as unknown.

*   **Current Event Conversion:** 2,500 postings ÷ 45,000 sessions = **5.56%**.
*   **Baseline Conversion (Assumed):** If your usual 300 postings come from standard traffic (let's assume a typical niche site gets ~5,000 sessions/month normally), your baseline conversion is ~6%. If your normal traffic is lower, your baseline conversion is higher.

**The Break-Even Trigger**
To sustain the $3,800 extra infrastructure cost permanently (assuming auto-scaling remains active at this volume), you must maintain a minimum posting volume.
*   *Formula:* `Minimum Postings = Extra Monthly Infra Cost ÷ Price Per Posting`
*   *Calculation:* $3,800 ÷ $99 = **38.4 postings**.
*   *Analysis:* Since you normally sell 300, covering the *extra* server cost is trivial. The real risk is if the **conversion rate drops** significantly when traffic is non-viral.

If 45,000 sessions become normal but the "viral" intent disappears, conversion might fall to 1% (industry average for general job boards).
*   *Scenario B (1% Conversion):* 45,000 sessions × 1% = 450 postings.
    *   Revenue: $44,550.

*   *Scenario C (0.5% Conversion):* 45,000 sessions × 0.5% = 225 postings.
    *   Revenue: $22,275.
    *   Result: This is **lower** than your normal baseline revenue ($29,700), yet you are paying higher server costs. This is the failure state.

**Decision Aid**
1.  **Read:** The event was a net positive windfall ($243k surplus). Do not use this single month to set permanent pricing yet.
2.  **Flip Input:** The variable that breaks your model is **Conversion Rate**. If sustained traffic yields fewer than **300 total postings** (a <0.67% conversion rate on 45k sessions), you are worse off than before the spike despite higher traffic.
3.  **Threshold Rule:** If LTV (here, single transaction value) remains $99, do not commit to permanent infrastructure upgrades that exceed $3,800/month until you verify a sustained conversion rate above 1% at this volume.
