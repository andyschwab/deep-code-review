**The Arithmetic**
Your current monthly fixed operational costs are £8,500. With 15 weddings/month, your fixed cost per wedding is £567.

The critical change is the equipment depreciation.
*   **Asset Base:** 4 drones × £3,000 = £12,000 total value.
*   **Old Assumption (4-year life):** £12,000 / 48 months = £250/month depreciation.
*   **New Reality (2-year life):** £12,000 / 24 months = £500/month depreciation.
*   **Impact:** Your monthly burden increases by £250.

**New Monthly Burn:** £8,500 (ops) + £500 (depreciation) = £9,000.
**New Cost Per Wedding:** £9,000 / 15 weddings = £600.

To achieve a 25% net margin, your price must cover the cost plus the margin on the final price (Price = Cost / (1 - Margin)).
*   **Break-even Price:** £600.
*   **Target Price Calculation:** £600 / (1 - 0.25) = £600 / 0.75 = **£800**.
