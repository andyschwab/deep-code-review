**The Arithmetic of Inaction vs. Action**
Your current trajectory is mathematically unsustainable. At a $8.50 price and $7.40 cost, your contribution margin is $1.10 per bar.
*   **Monthly Contribution:** 15,000 units × $1.10 = $16,500.
*   **Net Burn:** $16,500 (contribution) − $45,000 (fixed overhead) = **−$28,500/month**.
*   **Runway:** $120,000 cash ÷ $28,500 burn = **4.2 months** (optimistic) to **3.6 months** (conservative).

If you raise the price to $14.00 while maintaining the current formula:
*   **New Margin:** $14.00 − $7.40 = $6.60 per bar.
*   **Break-even Volume:** To cover the $45,000 overhead, you need to sell only 6,818 units ($45,000 ÷ $6.60).
*   **Risk Threshold:** You can afford to lose up to 54% of your current volume (dropping from 15,000 to ~6,800 units) and still stop burning cash. If volume drops less than 54%, you become profitable.

**Analysis of Strategic Options**

1.  **Reformulate (Cheaper Oils):**
    *   *Financial Impact:* Potentially restores margins without a price hike.
    *   *Brand Risk:* High. For an "artisanal organic" brand, changing the core scent profile of a best-seller violates the trust equation. If customers notice, churn becomes permanent, not temporary.
    *   *Verdict:* Only viable if marketed transparently as a temporary "Harvest Blend" distinct from the flagship product.

2.  **Reduce Bar Size (Shrinkflation):**
    *   *Financial Impact:* Linear reduction in material cost. To offset the $4.20 cost increase, you would need to reduce material content by ~57%, resulting in a comically small bar that damages perceived value.
    *   *Brand Risk:* High perception of "cheating" the customer.
    *   *Verdict:* Mathematically insufficient and brand-damaging.

3.  **Absorb Cost (Status Quo):**
    *   *Financial Impact:* Guaranteed insolvency within 4 months.
    *   *Verdict:* Reject immediately.

4.  **Dual-Track Pricing (Recommended):**
    *   *Strategy:* Keep the original "Pure Lavender" at $14.00 (positioned as premium/scarcity) and introduce a "Lavender & Local Botanicals" blend at $9.50–$10.00 using lower-cost local fillers to subsidize the expensive imported oil.
    *   *Cash Flow:* The $14.00 tier protects margin on loyalists. The $9.50 tier keeps retail partners happy with a sub-$10 SKU.
    *   *Brand Perception:* Frames the change as supply-chain transparency and innovation rather than a simple price gouge.

**Decision Trigger**
Implement the price increase and dual-SKU strategy within 14 days. If retail partners threaten delisting, present the $9.50 "Blend" option as a collaborative solution to keep shelf presence without selling at a loss. Monitor weekly sell-through; if the $14.00 SKU volume drops below 7,000 units by Month 2, re-evaluate the marketing narrative, not the price.
