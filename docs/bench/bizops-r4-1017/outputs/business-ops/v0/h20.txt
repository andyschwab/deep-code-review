### Lane A: Unit Economics & Decision Aid

Your model requires shifting from a recurring "Lifetime Value" (LTV) metric to a **Customer Lifetime Contribution (CLC)** that includes immediate margin plus attributed referral value. Since repeat purchases are rare, the "lifetime" is effectively the single transaction window plus the viral coefficient.

**1. Core Unit Economics (Single Transaction)**
*   **Revenue per Student:** $1,200
*   **Variable Costs:** $0 (Assumed included in fixed operating expenses; if there are per-student costs like certificates, subtract them here).
*   **Gross Margin per Student:** $1,200
*   **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** $450
*   **Contribution Margin (before referrals):** $1,200 - $450 = **$750**

**2. Incorporating Referral Value**
To factor in referrals, we calculate the **Viral Coefficient (k)** and the **Referral Lift**.
*   **Referral Rate:** 15% (0.15) of students refer a friend.
*   **Assumption:** You did not specify how many friends each referrer brings. Standard modeling assumes 1 successful referral per referrer for conservative estimates. If your data differs, adjust the multiplier below.
*   **New CAC Effective Reduction:** If 15% of students bring in 1 new student each, you acquire 0.15 "free" students per paid student.
*   **Effective CAC Formula:** $CAC_{effective} = CAC \div (1 + k)$
    *   $450 \div (1 + 0.15) = 450 \div 1.15 \approx \$391$
*   **Revised Contribution Margin:** $1,200 - \$391 = \mathbf{\$809}$

**3. Profitability & Runway Check**
*   **Monthly Volume:** 200 students
*   **Total Monthly Contribution:** 200 students $\times$ $750 (base margin) = **$150,000**
    *   *Note: This uses the base margin to be conservative; referrals are a bonus buffer.*
*   **Fixed Operating Expenses:** $95,000
*   **Net Monthly Profit:** $150,000 - $95,000 = **$55,000**

**Decision Aid & Thresholds**
*   **The Read:** Your current model is profitable. You generate $55k/month in net income with a healthy contribution margin ratio (~62%).
*   **The Flip Input:** The sustainability of your $450 CAC depends entirely on the **conversion rate** of your ads. If your ad costs rise such that CAC exceeds $1,200, you lose money on every signup.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** In low-repeat models, aim for a Contribution Margin of at least 3x CAC to cover fixed costs and risk. Your current ratio is $1,200 / $450 = **2.66x**. This is slightly below the ideal 3x safety buffer but sustainable because your fixed costs are currently low relative to volume.
*   **Referral Impact:** The 15% referral rate improves your margin by ~$59/student, acting as insurance against rising ad costs.
