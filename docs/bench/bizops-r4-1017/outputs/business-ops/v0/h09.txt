### Lane R: Regulatory Routing (Legal Determination)

**Regime Identified:** Oregon Unlawful Trade Practices Act (UTPA) and potential contract law regarding "unconscionability" or discriminatory fee structures in consumer/marketplace contracts.

**Route to Professional:** You must discuss this with an Oregon-licensed attorney specializing in consumer protection or platform liability.
*   **Why:** While merchants generally have freedom to set prices, disparate fee structures for "similar services" can sometimes trigger scrutiny under state consumer fairness laws if deemed deceptive or unfairly discriminatory, especially if the service (authentication/support) is the differentiator but the fee is applied broadly to the price point.
*   **Preparation for Counsel:** Bring your current Terms of Service, the specific definition of "luxury" you intend to use, and data correlating the higher fees to the specific cost of authentication disputes. Do not implement the tiered structure until counsel confirms the classification method does not violate Oregon's specific statutes on trade practices.

**Note:** The math assumes the 15% volume drop applies strictly to GMV; if average order value also shifts, recalculate using the provided formula. Legal routing is mandatory before changing fee schedules based on item value.
