**Regime Identification**
The relevant regime is the **EU VAT framework for electronically supplied services**. Under current rules, B2C sales of digital services to customers in the EU generally require the supplier to charge VAT at the rate of the customer's location. The **One Stop Shop (OSS)** mechanism allows non-EU businesses to register in a single EU member state to remit VAT for all EU sales, potentially avoiding multiple registrations. However, determining whether your specific volume triggers immediate obligations, how "organizer fees" versus "ticket sales" are classified (principal vs. agent), and the penalties for non-compliance are legal questions.
