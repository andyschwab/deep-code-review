### Lane A: Unit Economics & Stability Analysis

We can apply your provided numbers to standard unit economics formulas to compare the two segments. We will calculate Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR), Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), and a simplified Lifetime Value (LTV) based on your churn data.

**1. Current Revenue Snapshot**
*   **Institutional:** 300 clients × $2,500 = **$750,000 / year**
*   **Individual:** 4,000 users × $15 × 12 months = **$720,000 / year**
*   **Total ARR:** $1,470,000
*   *Observation:* Your revenue is currently split almost 50/50 between the two segments.

**2. Unit Economics Comparison**

| Metric | Institutional (B2B) | Individual (B2C) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Annual Revenue per Unit** | $2,500 | $180 ($15 × 12) |
| **CAC** | $1,200 | ~$0 (Organic/Instant) |
| **CAC Payback Period** | ~5.8 months ($1,200 ÷ ($2,500/12)) | Immediate (No upfront CAC) |
| **Monthly Churn** | ~0% | 8% |
| **Avg. Lifetime (Months)** | Indefinite (Assume 3+ yrs for calc) | ~12.5 months (1 ÷ 0.08) |
| **Est. LTV (Gross)** | $7,500+ (3 yrs) | ~$2,250 ($180 × 12.5) |
| **LTV : CAC Ratio** | > 6.25 : 1 | N/A (CAC is near zero) |

*Formulas used:*
*   `Avg Lifetime = 1 ÷ Monthly Churn Rate`
*   `LTV = Annual Revenue × Avg Lifetime (in years)`
*   `Payback Period = CAC ÷ Monthly Revenue`

**3. The Trade-off Analysis**
*   **Efficiency:** The institutional model is highly efficient per dollar spent on sales. For every $1 spent on commission, you secure $2.08 in first-year revenue. The LTV:CAC ratio (>6:1) is exceptionally strong by SaaS standards (where 3:1 is often the benchmark).
*   **Volume vs. Stability:** While individuals generate similar total revenue today, they require a constant influx of new users to replace the 8% monthly loss (approx. 320 users/month). You must acquire ~320 new individuals just to stay flat, whereas institutional revenue is sticky.
*   **Cash Flow Risk:** Shifting *exclusively* to institutional creates a "trough" risk. With a 6-month sales cycle and $1,200 upfront cost per deal, you must fund 6 months of operations before seeing return. If you stop marketing to individuals today, your $720k ARR will decay rapidly (losing ~8% of the base monthly) while new institutional revenue takes half a year to materialize.

**Decision Aid**
*   **Read:** Institutional deals offer superior long-term value and stability (LTV:CAC > 6), but individual subscriptions provide immediate cash flow and zero acquisition friction.
*   **Flip Input:** The critical variable is your **cash runway**. Can you survive 6–9 months with declining individual revenue while paying $1,200 upfront for deals that won't close until next quarter?
*   **Threshold Rule:** If your cash runway is < 9 months, an exclusive pivot is mathematically dangerous. If runway is > 12 months, you can afford the sales cycle dip.
*   **Next Step:** Do not abandon the individual channel yet. Instead, calculate the exact monthly burn rate if individual revenue drops 8% monthly. If that burn exceeds your current cash on hand, consider a hybrid approach: raise individual prices to reduce churn or lower CAC, while slowly building a dedicated institutional sales team.
