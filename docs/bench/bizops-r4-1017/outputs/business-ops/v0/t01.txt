### Lane A: Arithmetic Application & Sensitivity Analysis

**Current Model (Flat Rate)**
*Formula:* `Revenue = Subscribers × Monthly Fee`
*Inputs:* 120 users × $29
*Projected Revenue:* **$3,480/month**
*Volatility Driver:* User count (churn). At 8% monthly churn, you lose ~10 users/month unless acquisition matches it exactly. Revenue drops linearly with every cancellation.

**Proposed Model (Per-Word)**
*Formula:* `Revenue = Subscribers × Avg Words/User × Rate`
*Inputs:* 120 users × 15,000 words × $0.002
*Projected Revenue:* **$3,600/month** (at exact average usage)

**Volatility & Variance Analysis**
You noted missing data on word-count variance during holidays. This is the critical unknown.
*   **Scenario A (Low Variance):** If all users process ~15k words, revenue stays near $3,600. Churn might drop because users pay less in slow months (e.g., 5k words = $10 bill), reducing cancellation pressure.
*   **Scenario B (High Variance):** If usage swings wildly (e.g., some users do 50k words, others 1k), revenue becomes unpredictable.
    *   *Heavy User Risk:* A power user processing 100k words pays $200. They may churn due to "bill shock," replacing steady $29 revenue with volatile high-value accounts that are prone to canceling when their own work slows.
    *   *Light User Risk:* A user processing 2k words pays $4. While they stay, they no longer cover their share of your $450 fixed server cost ($450/120 = $3.75/user break-even on server costs alone, ignoring other expenses).

**Break-Even & Margin Check**
*   *Current Contribution:* $3,480 - $450 (server) = $3,030 gross contribution.
*   *Per-Word Break-Even Volume:* To cover the $450 server cost at $0.002/word, the total platform volume must be 225,000 words/month.
    *   Current estimated total volume: 120 × 15,000 = 1,800,000 words.
    *   *Result:* You are well above the server break-even even if volume drops 80%. The risk is not covering server costs; the risk is **revenue compression** if average usage falls below 12,083 words/user ($29 / $0.002).

**Decision Aid**
1.  **Read:** The per-word model projects slightly higher revenue ($3,600 vs $3,480) *only if* average usage holds at 15k words.
2.  **Flip Input:** The model flips from "profitable growth" to "revenue loss" if the average user processes **fewer than 12,083 words** per month.
3.  **Threshold Rule:** If >20% of your users fall below 12k words during slow seasons, total revenue will likely drop below the current $3,480 flat baseline, even if churn improves.
4.  **Next Step:** Before switching, export the last 3 months of word-count data. Calculate the standard deviation. If the variance is high, consider a **hybrid model** (e.g., $15 base + $0.001 per word) to ensure minimum coverage of fixed costs while offering usage-based scaling.

**Note:** You cannot calculate the true volatility impact without the historical word-count variance data; do not switch based solely on the average.
**Note:** Billing complexity for per-word models requires robust metering infrastructure; ensure your engineering cost to build this doesn't outweigh the stabilized cash flow benefit.
