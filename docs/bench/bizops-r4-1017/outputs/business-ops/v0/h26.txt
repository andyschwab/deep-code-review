### Lane A: Arithmetic & Market Sizing Method

You cannot calculate a Total Addressable Market (TAM) for "niche sci-fi graphic novels in the Pacific Northwest preferring print-on-demand" without external data you do not yet possess. Industry averages are indeed too broad. Instead, apply a **Bottom-Up Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)** method using your own conversion data as the anchor.

**1. The Formula for Conservative SOM**
Do not guess the total population. Estimate based on your known audience and a conservative expansion factor.
$$SOM = (Mailing List \times Conversion Rate) + (Organic Reach \times Cold Conversion Rate)$$

*   **Known Input:** Mailing List = 8,000.
*   **Known Input:** Current Conversion (Flagship) = $1,500 \text{ units} / 8,000 \text{ subs} = 18.75\%$.
*   **Assumption Required:** A new service launch typically converts lower than an established flagship. You must test this. If you assume a **conservative 2%** conversion on the list for a *new* service:
    $$Base Demand = 8,000 \times 0.02 = 160 \text{ units}$$
*   **Unknown Input:** "Similar niche products" or cold traffic. Without a specific competitor's public sales data, this input is **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent it. To get this, run a small ($500) ad test to a landing page describing the service and measure click-to-signup rate.

**2. Break-Even Analysis on Marketing Spend**
To determine if the $30,000 campaign is viable, calculate the units needed to cover that specific cost using your contribution margin.

*   **Price (P):** $22 (Assumed same as flagship; verify if POD commands a premium).
*   **Variable Cost (V):** $9 (Production). *Note: Ensure this includes POD-specific shipping if passed to you, or clarify if customer pays shipping.*
*   **Contribution Margin (CM):** $P - V = \$22 - \$9 = \$13$ per unit.
*   **Marketing Spend (F):** $30,000.

$$Break\text{-}Even Units = \frac{Fixed Marketing Cost}{Contribution Margin} = \frac{30,000}{13} \approx 2,308 \text{ units}$$

**Read:** You must sell **2,308 units** just to pay back the marketing budget, excluding all other business overheads (salaries, rent, software).

**3. Infrastructure Decision Aid**
Compare the Break-Even Units (2,308) against your conservative SOM (160 units from list alone).
*   **Gap:** 2,148 units must come from outside your current list.
*   **Threshold Rule of Thumb:** If your required break-even volume is >10x your conservative organic estimate, the risk of over-investment is high.
*   **Flip Input:** The variable that changes this answer is the **Price**. If POD allows a higher price (e.g., $28), your CM becomes $19, dropping break-even to ~1,579 units.

**Next Step:** Before committing the full $30,000, spend $500 to validate the "Cold Conversion Rate." If you cannot generate 50 pre-orders from cold traffic in a two-week test, the math suggests the full campaign will not break even.
