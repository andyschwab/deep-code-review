### Lane R: Regulatory Routing

**Regime:** California Labor Law, Intellectual Property (Copyright), and Right of Publicity.

**Routing Required:** You must consult with an entertainment attorney licensed in California before proceeding.

**Reasoning:** Retroactive ad insertion in archived episodes raises complex legal issues that cannot be resolved via arithmetic:
1.  **Contractual Rights:** Your existing creator contracts likely govern "baked-in" ads. Modifying archived content to insert new ads may require explicit consent or renegotiation, potentially triggering breach of contract claims.
2.  **Right of Publicity:** California has strict laws regarding the commercial use of a person's voice or likeness. Inserting new ads into old content where the host discusses specific topics could create false endorsements or violate their right of publicity if not contractually cleared.
3.  **Union Regulations:** If any creators are affiliated with unions (e.g., SAG-AFTRA), there may be specific residual payment structures or rules regarding "new uses" of archived material that mandate additional compensation.
