### Recommendation
**Do not delay the launch.**
Delaying does not solve a cash flow crisis because **you are not currently facing one**. Even with an immediate expansion, your cash reserve never drops below $45,000. Delaying simply postpones revenue generation without providing a necessary safety buffer.

**Action Plan:**
1.  **Proceed with immediate expansion.** Your current liquidity can absorb the $85k hit.
2.  **Do not seek a line of credit yet.** You have sufficient cash reserves. Taking on debt now incurs unnecessary interest costs when you are solvent.
3.  **Monitor the $45k floor.** Ensure your Denver location's revenue remains stable. If Denver revenue drops by more than 33%, you could risk dipping too low.

### Overlooked Financial Risks
1.  **New Location Operating Costs:** Your projection assumes the new location has **$0 operating expenses** (rent, utilities, instructor payroll) during the 6-month recruitment phase. If you must pay rent or staff before the cohort starts, your monthly burn will increase, lowering that $45k safety floor significantly.
2.  **Revenue Timing:** You assumed the full cohort revenue hits instantly in Month 7. If tuition is paid monthly or recruitment slips slightly, cash inflow will be slower than projected.

*Note: This analysis assumes no additional operating expenses for the new location prior to launch. If rent or salaries are due during the recruitment phase, recalculate immediately.*
*Consider securing a small line of credit only as an emergency backup for unexpected operational costs, not for the initial investment.*
