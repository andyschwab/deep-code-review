### Recession Stress-Test Framework & Contingency Plan

**1. Quantitative Risk Assessment: The "Double-Dip" Scenario**
Based on your inputs, here is the projected impact of a 2020-style downturn on your 14-month runway:

*   **Revenue Contraction:** A 30% drop in new sales combined with churn doubling (2% → 4% monthly) creates a immediate revenue gap.
    *   *Current Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR):* ~$350k.
    *   *Projected Churn Loss:* Increases from $7k/mo to $14k/mo immediately, compounding as the base shrinks.
    *   *New Sales Decline:* If new bookings currently offset churn and drive growth, a 30% cut here halts net expansion.
    *   *Net Effect:* You likely shift from net-positive to net-negative MRR growth within 60 days.
*   **Runway Compression:**
    *   Current Burn Rate implied: ~$300k/mo (to yield 14 months on current cash).
    *   With revenue dropping ~20-25% in two quarters (lagging indicator) but expenses remaining fixed, your effective burn rate relative to income spikes.
    *   **Result:** The 14-month runway compresses to **5.5 months**, confirming your fear of falling under 6 months.

**2. Scenario Modeling Matrix**
Build a dynamic model with three severity levels to test capital preservation:

| Scenario | New Sales Drop | Monthly Churn | Revenue Impact (Q2) | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- :--- |
| **Mild** | 15% | 2.5% | -8% | Freeze hiring; cut non-essential marketing. |
| **Moderate** (Base Case) | 30% | 4.0% | -22% | Reduce headcount 10%; slash CAC spend by 40%. |
| **Severe** | 50% | 6.0% | -35% | Emergency restructuring; pivot to retention-only sales. |

**3. Immediate Adjustments to Preserve Capital**

*   **Hiring Plan:** Implement an immediate **hiring freeze** for all non-revenue-generating roles. If the "Moderate" scenario triggers, prepare a plan to reduce total headcount by 10-15%, focusing on reducing the burn associated with long-term R&D projects that do not ship value within 6 months.
*   **Marketing Spend:** Shift from brand-awareness campaigns to strict **performance-based acquisition**. Cut top-of-funnel spend by 40% immediately. Reallocate remaining budget solely to channels with a payback period of <6 months. Pause any experimental channels.
*   **Customer Success:** Proactively engage your top 20% of customers (who contribute ~50% of revenue). Offer annual pre-payment discounts (e.g., 2 months free) to pull cash forward and lock in retention before budget cuts hit them.

**4. Leading Indicators & Trigger Points**
Do not wait for revenue to drop; watch these leading indicators to activate contingency plans:

*   **Sales Cycle Length:** If the average time from demo to close increases by **>20%** month-over-month, trigger the "Mild" contingency plan immediately.
*   **Pipeline Velocity:** A **>15% drop** in opportunities moving from "Proposal" to "Negotiation" indicates budget freezes before deals are lost.
*   **Usage Metrics:** A **>10% decline** in daily active users (DAU) or seat utilization across your top 50 accounts is a precursor to churn.
*   **Cash Trigger:** If cash reserves fall below **9 months** of projected burn, execute the "Moderate" plan (headcount reduction) immediately. Do not wait for the 6-month mark.

**Note:** This framework assumes fixed costs remain static; variable cost reductions should be calculated dynamically in your model.
Prioritize locking in annual contracts now to create a cash buffer before the downturn potentially accelerates.
