### Defensible Market Sizing Model for BugProtein

To construct a defensible Total Addressable Market (TAM) without direct insect protein data, use a **Proxy-Based Fermi Estimation**. This approach derives your market size by applying conservative adoption rates from adjacent, mature sectors to the global sports nutrition baseline. Investors trust this method because it relies on observable behaviors in similar categories rather than speculative hype.

**Step 1: Establish the Global Baseline**
Start with the verified total global sports nutrition market. Current industry consensus places this between **$85B and $90B USD** annually. For a conservative pitch, use **$85B** as your denominator.

**Step 2: Select and Validate Proxies**
Since specific insect protein data is missing, use penetration rates from two analogous "alternative protein" shifts:
*   **Proxy A (Plant-Based Dairy/Meat):** Alternative proteins currently hold ~3–5% of the total protein market in mature regions (US/EU).
*   **Proxy B (Sustainable Seafood/Organic):** Niche sustainable categories often stabilize at 1–2% of their broader category within 5 years of mainstream entry.

**Step 3: Apply a "Sustainable Niche" Adoption Rate**
Insect protein faces higher psychological barriers than plant-based but offers superior sustainability metrics. Therefore, apply a conservative **0.5% to 1.0%** penetration rate against the global baseline for your initial 5-year horizon. This aligns with your goal of capturing 0.5% but validates *why* that number is achievable based on historical precedents in organic and plant-based shifts.

**Step 4: Calculate the Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) Revenue**
Using the $85B baseline and your target capture:
*   **Global Insect Protein Opportunity (1% penetration):** $850M
*   **Your Target (0.5% of Global Sports Nutrition):** $425M Annual Revenue Potential.

*Note: This $425M figure represents the revenue opportunity if the entire global market behaved like your current niche. It is your theoretical ceiling.*

**Step 5: Sanity Check with Unit Economics**
Validate this top-down number with your bottom-up reality to ensure defensibility:
*   **Current Price:** £35 (~$44 USD) per 500g tin.
*   **Units Required for 0.5% Share:** $425M / $44 = ~9.66 million units annually.
*   **Daily Volume:** ~26,500 units/day globally.
*   **Comparison:** You currently sell 2,000 units/month (~66/day). Scaling to 26,500/day is a massive operational leap, suggesting your 0.5% global target is a long-term "North Star" rather than a 3-year goal.
