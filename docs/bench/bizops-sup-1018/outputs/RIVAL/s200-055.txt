### Unit Economics Analysis

Your current unit economics are under severe pressure but remain positive.
*   **Current Revenue per Repo:** $15.00
*   **Current Cost per Repo:** $7.50 (up from $4.00)
*   **Current Margin per Repo:** $7.50 (50% margin)
*   **Monthly Net Profit:** ($7.50 × 2,000 repos) - $18,000 OpEx = **-$3,000**

**Critical Finding:** You are currently operating at a **net loss** of $3,000/month despite having positive unit margins, because your contribution margin ($15,000) does not cover your fixed operating expenses ($18,000).

**Break-Even Thresholds:**
1.  **Negative Unit Margin:** Your margin turns negative when costs exceed $15. Since cost scales linearly with size ($4 at 50MB → $0.08/MB), you hit negative unit economics at **187.5 MB** per repository.
2.  **Company Profitability:** To cover your $18,000 OpEx, you need a total contribution margin of $18,000. At the current $7.50 margin per repo, you need **2,400 repositories** to break even. Alternatively, if repo count stays at 2,000, you need a margin of $9/repo, requiring a price increase to **$16.50** or a cost reduction.

### Strategic Recommendation: Tiered Pricing

You must introduce a tiered model immediately. A flat fee fails when usage (storage/compute) varies wildly.

**Proposed Structure:**
*   **Standard Tier ($15/mo):** Includes up to 75 MB storage and standard commit history. This covers your baseline costs with a healthy margin.
*   **Pro Tier ($29/mo):** Includes up to 250 MB storage and deep history analysis.
*   **Overage Fee:** $0.10 per MB for storage exceeding tier limits.

This aligns your revenue with your cost driver (storage volume). Customers with small repos subsidize nothing; customers with large binary assets pay for the infrastructure they consume.

### Communication Strategy

Do not frame this as a "price increase" due to your costs. Frame it as a **fairness adjustment** to ensure light users aren't subsidizing heavy users.

**Narrative for Existing Customers:**
"To ensure ReviewBot remains sustainable and fast for everyone, we are updating our pricing to reflect actual usage. Repositories under 75 MB will see no change. Larger repositories utilizing significant compute resources will move to our new Pro tier. This ensures those who use the most resources contribute fairly to the infrastructure."

**Mitigation Tactics:**
1.  **Grandfathering (Limited):** Offer current customers a 6-month grace period at their current rate if they commit to an annual plan now. This locks in cash and delays churn.
2.  **Usage Alerts:** Before charging overages, notify users when they approach the 75 MB limit, giving them a chance to clean up binaries or upgrade voluntarily.
3.  **Value Add:** Bundle the price change with a new feature (e.g., "Security Vulnerability Scanning") to increase perceived value.

Your $110,000 cash reserve gives you roughly 36 months of runway at the current burn rate, but you must fix the unit economics before scaling, as adding more customers at this margin profile will only deepen your losses.
