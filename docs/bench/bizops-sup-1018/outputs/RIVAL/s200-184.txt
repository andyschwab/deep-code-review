### Financial Reality Check: SilverCode Dojo

**Current Monthly Unit Economics**
*   **Revenue:** 4 classes × 5 students × $400 = **$8,000**
*   **Variable Costs (Instructors):** 4 classes × $350 = **$1,400**
*   **Fixed Costs (Rent):** **$2,000** (Software licenses excluded as amount unknown)
*   **Current Monthly Profit:** $8,000 - $1,400 - $2,000 = **$4,600**

**Correction:** Your unit economics are **not broken**; you are currently profitable by approximately $4,600 per month before software costs and taxes. The perception of "razor-thin margins" stems from looking at the per-class margin ($2,000 revenue - $350 cost = $1,650) without fully accounting for the volume across four classes covering the fixed rent.

**Break-Even Analysis**
To cover only your Instructor and Rent costs ($3,400 total):
*   You need **$3,400** in monthly revenue.
*   At $400/student, you need **8.5 students total per month**.
*   With 4 classes, you need an average of **2.2 students per class** to break even.
*   **Current Status:** You are operating at 5 students/class, which is **227% of your break-even volume**.

**Path to Optimization (Without 200% Price Hikes)**
You do not need to raise prices to $1,200. You have three mathematical levers to increase profitability while staying accessible:

1.  **Maximize Capacity (Immediate Impact):**
    You have a waitlist of 30. Filling your existing 4 classes to the 6-student cap adds 4 students per cycle.
    *   New Revenue: 4 classes × 6 students × $400 = $9,600.
    *   New Profit: $9,600 - $1,400 - $2,000 = **$6,200/month**.
    *   *Result:* A **35% profit increase** with zero price hike.

2.  **Utilize the Waitlist (Volume Growth):**
    With 30 people waiting, you can add 5 more classes immediately (hiring 5 more instructors).
    *   Total Classes: 9.
    *   Total Revenue: 9 × 6 students × $400 = $21,600.
    *   Total Instructor Cost: 9 × $350 = $3,150.
    *   New Profit: $21,600 - $3,150 - $2,000 = **$16,450/month**.
    *   *Constraint:* Do you have physical space for 9 concurrent or sequential classes? If space is limited to one room, you must run classes sequentially (evenings/weekends).

3.  **Alternative Revenue (Grants/Sponsorships):**
    Since your mission serves retirees (a demographic often eligible for senior grants) and focuses on digital literacy (a common corporate CSR goal), you should pursue non-dilutive funding.
    *   **Goal:** Secure $2,000/month in grants to cover rent entirely.
    *   **Result:** Every dollar of tuition becomes profit or instructor surplus, allowing you to potentially lower prices or increase instructor pay without touching the bottom line.

**Conclusion**
Your model is viable. The priority is not a price hike, but **filling existing seats** and **scaling class count** using your waitlist. Your current "thin margin" is actually a healthy 57% gross margin ($6,600 gross profit on $8k revenue).

*Note: Ensure software license costs are calculated; if they exceed $4,600/month, the model fails, but this is unlikely for standard coding tools. Use your $15k savings to market the waitlist conversion, not to subsidize operations.*
