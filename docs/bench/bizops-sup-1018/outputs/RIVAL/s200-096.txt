**Analysis of Your Options**

**Option A: Cutting Marketing Budget by 50%**
This is a necessary but insufficient step. Since marketing accounts for 60% of operating expenses, halving it would reduce your monthly burn significantly, potentially extending runway to 12–14 months. However, this assumes your current sales cycle and conversion rates remain static. If your nine-month sales cycle relies heavily on top-of-funnel marketing activity to sustain pipeline volume, cutting spend may cause revenue to collapse in two quarters, accelerating the crisis. Furthermore, this approach treats the symptom (cash burn) without addressing the disease (inefficient capital allocation).

**Option B: Pivoting to Smaller, Faster-Deals (Recommended)**
Your current model—enterprise sales with a nine-month cycle and high acquisition costs—is mathematically incompatible with your remaining capital. You cannot afford a sales cycle that consumes nearly your entire remaining runway per deal. A pivot to a product-led growth (PLG) motion or a "land-and-expand" strategy targeting smaller teams (Head of ML, Data Science Leads) rather than C-level enterprise procurement is essential.
*   **Speed:** Smaller deals close in weeks, not months, generating immediate cash flow.
*   **Efficiency:** Lower customer acquisition cost (CAC) improves the burn multiple rapidly.
*   **Validation:** Faster feedback loops allow you to refine the product before running out of money.
