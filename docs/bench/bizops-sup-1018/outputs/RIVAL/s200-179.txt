### Pricing Scenarios for LinguoTravel

To address the $1,500/month administrative increase and cash flow squeeze caused by 9-week visa delays, here are three modeled scenarios. These assume a baseline of 20 students per month across your cohorts.

**Scenario A: Base Price Increase (The "New Normal" Approach)**
*   **Action:** Increase the base price from $4,500 to $4,650 (+3.3%).
*   **Financial Impact:** On 20 students, this generates an extra $3,000/month. This covers the $1,500 admin cost and adds $1,500 to profit.
*   **Customer Acceptability:** Moderate. Since visa delays are industry-wide, customers may accept a modest increase if framed as "ensuring program stability." However, it risks making you less competitive against programs not yet raising prices.
*   **Best For:** Long-term stability if you believe visa delays will persist indefinitely.

**Scenario B: Optional Rush Processing Fee (The "Segmented" Approach)**
*   **Action:** Keep base price at $4,500. Introduce a "$400 Rush Visa Guarantee" for students needing confirmation within 4 weeks (using expedited services or priority filing).
*   **Financial Impact:** If 40% of students (8 students) opt-in, you gain $3,200/month. This covers the $1,500 cost and yields $1,700 profit. Students who wait the standard 9 weeks pay nothing extra, reducing friction for price-sensitive buyers.
*   **Customer Acceptability:** High. It gives control to the customer. Those anxious about delays pay for speed; budget-conscious students accept the wait.
*   **Best For:** Maintaining conversion rates while monetizing urgency. *Note: Ensure you can legally/operationally deliver the "rush" promise.*

**Scenario C: Revised Payment Terms (The "Cash Flow" Approach)**
*   **Action:** Keep price at $4,500. Change terms from "50% deposit, 50% at arrival" to "100% due 10 weeks prior to departure" (aligning with the new 9-week visa window).
*   **Financial Impact:** Immediate cash flow improvement. You hold the full capital during the visa wait, earning interest or reducing borrowing needs. It does not directly generate new revenue to cover the $1,500 admin cost unless invested aggressively, but it solves the liquidity crunch.
*   **Customer Acceptability:** Low to Moderate. Asking for full payment 3+ months in advance is a significant friction point and may increase cancellation requests or require a stricter refund policy.
*   **Best For:** Solving the immediate cash flow crisis without raising sticker prices.

**Recommendation: The Hybrid Model**
Do not rely on a single lever. Combine **Scenario B** and a modified **Scenario C**:
1.  **Shift Payment Terms:** Require 75% upfront upon acceptance (covering your hard costs earlier) and 25% at departure. This eases the cash flow strain without demanding 100% immediately.
2.  **Add a Rush Fee:** Introduce a $350–$450 "Expedited Processing" add-on.
3.  **Result:** The rush fees from impatient customers cover the $1,500 admin overrun. The earlier 75% payment solves the cash flow gap. Your base price remains competitive at $4,500.

**Implementation Note:** Clearly communicate that the "Expedited" fee covers third-party courier and priority filing costs, not just internal admin, to maintain trust.

**Legal Note:** Consult counsel before altering refund policies tied to the new payment schedule, as visa denials after full payment create higher liability.
