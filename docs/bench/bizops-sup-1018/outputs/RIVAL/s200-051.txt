*   Max Peak Cost per Million = $0.78 / 0.15 (peak volume %) = **$5.20 per million**

**Conclusion:** Your infrastructure costs during peak events could rise from the current $3.80 to as high as **$5.20 per million** before you dip below a 60% gross margin. You have a significant safety buffer.
