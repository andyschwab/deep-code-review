### Pricing Scenario Model for Acme Labs

**Current Baseline:**
*   **Fixed Costs:** $35,000
*   **Upfront Staffing (Due Pre-May):** $72,000 (60% of $120k)
*   **Target Cash by April 1:** $50,400 (70% of $72k)
*   **Last Year Volume:** 450 campers @ $650 = $292,500 Revenue
*   **Last Year Profit:** $137,500 ($292.5k - $35k fixed - $120k staff)

To meet your goal, you must generate **$50,400 in cash by April 1**. The models below assume an "Early Bird" deadline of April 1st. We will test three discount tiers against your baseline price of $650.

#### Scenario A: Conservative Discount (10% Off)
*   **Price:** $585
*   **Required Early Sign-ups to hit $50.4k:** 87 campers
*   **Break-even Enrollment (Total Season):** 238 campers
    *   *Calculation:* ($35k Fixed + $120k Staff) / $650 blended avg (assuming mix). Strictly at $585 price point: 265 campers.
*   **Cash Flow Impact:** Low friction for parents, but requires selling 87 units immediately.
*   **Risk:** If you only sell 50 early bird spots, you raise $29,250, missing your cash target by ~$21k.

#### Scenario B: Aggressive Discount (20% Off) — **Recommended**
*   **Price:** $520
*   **Required Early Sign-ups to hit $50.4k:** 97 campers
*   **Break-even Enrollment (Total Season):** 248 campers (at mixed price), or 317 if *everyone* pays $520.
*   **Cash Flow Impact:** The lower price point ($520) is psychologically easier for parents to commit to early. Selling 97 spots is feasible (22% of last year's volume).
*   **Margin Protection:** Even with a 20% cut, you retain $520 per camper against a variable cost of roughly $266 per camper ($120k/450). Your contribution margin remains strong at $254/camper.

#### Scenario C: Deep Discount (30% Off)
*   **Price:** $455
*   **Required Early Sign-ups to hit $50.4k:** 111 campers
*   **Break-even Enrollment (Total Season):** 342 campers (if all pay $455).
*   **Cash Flow Impact:** Dangerous. You need to sell 25% of your total historical volume just to cover April bills. This erodes margins significantly, requiring 342 total campers just to break even, which is a 76-camper increase over last year.

### Strategic Recommendation

**Adopt Scenario B (20% Off / $520 Price Point).**

1.  **Sales Target:** Market heavily to secure **100 early-bird registrations** by April 1. This generates **$52,000**, covering 72% of your upfront staffing bills and clearing your cash crunch.
2.  **Break-even Safety:** Your total season break-even is approximately **240–250 campers** (assuming a mix of early-bird and full-price late registrants). Since you enrolled 450 last year, this provides a massive safety buffer.
3.  **Profit Maximization:** Cap the early-bird quantity at 100 spots. Once sold, revert to the full $650 price. This creates scarcity, protects margins on the remaining 350+ expected campers, and maximizes August profit.

**Marketing Instruction:** "Register by April 1st to save $130 per week. Only 100 spots available at this rate."

*Note: Ensure your payment processor allows immediate charging for early-bird rates to guarantee cash arrival by April 1.*
*Fixed costs are covered after roughly 68 campers enroll at the $520 rate.*
