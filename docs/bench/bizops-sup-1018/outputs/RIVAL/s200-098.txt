**Executive Summary: Your current model is a "leaky bucket" that will drain your $900k reserve in approximately 11 months if unchanged. A viral coefficient of 1.4 is dangerous when the vast majority of viral growth is in your loss-leading segment.**

**The Math Behind the Leak**
You are currently losing $20,000 every month ($60k burn - $40k revenue). Your free tier costs $7,500/month to support but generates $0. With 50,000 free users growing at a 1.4 viral coefficient, your infrastructure costs will scale linearly or exponentially while revenue remains stagnant.

At a 4% conversion rate (2,000 paid / 52,000 total), you are below the SaaS benchmark for self-serve products (typically 5–10%). More critically, your viral growth is amplifying your losses. If your user base grows by 40% next month purely through viral channels, and the conversion rate holds, you will add ~20,000 new free users costing you an additional $3,000/month in hard costs, while only adding ~800 paying users (assuming perfect conversion of the new slice, which is unrealistic).

**Verdict on Viral Coefficient**
A viral coefficient of 1.4 is exceptional for consumer apps but toxic for your current unit economics. You are effectively paying $0.15 to acquire a user who has a 96% chance of never paying you. You are not building a network effect; you are subsidizing a hobbyist community with your runway.

**Immediate Strategic Recommendations**

1.  **Restrict the Free Tier Immediately:** Do not wait. You must decouple viral growth from infrastructure cost.
    *   **Action:** Introduce hard usage limits (e.g., bandwidth caps, session time limits, or number of concurrent tunnels) that align with the $0.15 cost. The free tier should be a "try before you buy" demo, not a permanent hosting solution.
    *   **Goal:** Reduce the cost per free user from $0.15 to <$0.02, or force heavy users to upgrade.

2.  **Repackage for Conversion:** Your 4% conversion suggests the jump from "free" to "paid" is too steep or the value isn't clear.
    *   **Action:** Create a "Hobbyist" tier at $5–$9/month. Capture the students and hobbyists who love the product but can't afford pro rates. Even converting 10% of your free base to a $5 plan generates $25,000 MRR, covering your entire infrastructure bill and halving your burn.

3.  **Shift the Viral Loop:** Change what goes viral.
    *   **Action:** Instead of viralizing the *usage* (which costs you money), viralize the *invitation*. Give free users credits for inviting paying customers, not just new signups.

**Conclusion**
Do not rely on organic conversion to save you. With $900k in the bank and a $20k monthly loss, you have a 45-month runway only if growth stops. If your viral coefficient holds, your costs will rise faster than your revenue, cutting that runway to under 12 months. You must restrict free usage now to ensure every new viral user contributes marginally positive or neutral economics, not negative.
