I cannot provide a legal determination on whether CashWise constitutes a regulated financial entity or requires a banking license. Regulatory classification in the UK depends on specific interpretations of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (FSMA) and the Payment Services Regulations 2017 by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). Only a qualified UK financial services lawyer or compliance consultant can analyze your specific API integration depth, data handling protocols, and user flow to give a definitive answer.

However, I can outline the general regulatory concepts and immediate steps you should take to address this risk before your funding round:

**1. The Core Regulatory Distinction**
In the UK, the line between an unregulated "app" and a regulated "institution" often hinges on whether you are merely providing *information* or actively *facilitating payment services*.
*   **Likely Unregulated (Information Society Services):** If your app only displays read-only data via Open Banking APIs and redirects users to the bank's own environment to complete account opening or transfers, you are typically acting as an Account Information Service Provider (AISP) or a simple referrer. AISPs require FCA registration but generally do not need to hold significant reserve capital like a bank.
*   **Potentially Regulated (Payment Initiation):** If your integration allows users to initiate transfers or complete account opening *within* your interface without being redirected to the bank's secure environment, you may be acting as a Payment Initiation Service Provider (PISP) or even an Electronic Money Institution. This triggers stricter capital requirements (which could include the $50k reserve mentioned) and audit obligations.

**2. Immediate Action Plan**
*   **Engage Specialized Counsel:** Do not rely on general corporate lawyers. Hire a firm specializing in UK FinTech regulation (e.g., those experienced with Open Banking implementations). They will perform a "regulatory perimeter assessment."
*   **Audit Your User Flow:** Document exactly where the user is when the legal contract with the bank is signed and when funds move. If the user never leaves your app during these critical moments, your risk profile is significantly higher.
*   **Clarify the Bank's Concern:** Request a written explanation from the compliance officer specifying which part of your integration triggered their concern. Is it the data aggregation, the referral mechanism, or the transfer capability? Their specific objection will guide your legal defense.
*   **Prepare for Due Diligence:** For your next funding round, investors will require a legal opinion letter stating your regulatory status. Proactively obtaining this now prevents deal collapse later.

**3. Strategic Mitigation**
If a full license is required, consider pivoting your product architecture to a "redirect model" where all regulated actions (account opening, transfers) occur on the bank's domain, keeping CashWise strictly as a marketing and data visualization layer. This often lowers the regulatory burden to a simpler registration rather than full authorization.

Do not attempt to self-diagnose this issue; the cost of a legal review is negligible compared to the risk of enforcement action or losing your banking partners.
