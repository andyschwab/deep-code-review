### LittleSprouts Cash Flow Forecasting Framework

To manage the mismatch between your $300,000 upfront revenue and $22,000 monthly burn, you must shift your mindset from "cash available" to "earned revenue." Your $6,000 tuition is not profit on Day 1; it is a liability (Deferred Revenue) that you earn linearly over 12 months ($500/student/month).

**1. The Core Model Structure**
Build a spreadsheet with the following columns for Months 1–12:
*   **Opening Cash Balance:** Cash in the bank at the start of the month.
*   **Cash Inflows:** New tuition collected (likely $0 after Month 1 unless you have rolling admissions).
*   **Total Cash Outflows:** Sum of rent, salaries, and utilities ($22,000 baseline).
*   **Net Cash Flow:** Inflows minus Outflows.
*   **Closing Cash Balance:** Opening Balance + Net Cash Flow.
*   **Restricted Cash Reserve (Critical):** A calculated row showing funds *legally/operationally owed* to future months. Formula: `(Remaining Months in Term) × (Monthly Burn Rate)`.
*   **True Available Liquidity:** Closing Cash Balance minus Restricted Cash Reserve. This is the only number you should use to make hiring or expansion decisions.

**2. The Baseline Projection (Current State)**
*   **Start:** $300,000 Cash.
*   **Monthly Burn:** $22,000.
*   **Annual Burn:** $264,000 ($22k × 12).
*   **Projected Year-End Surplus:** $36,000 ($300k - $264k).

While a $36,000 surplus looks healthy, it represents only ~1.6 months of operating expenses. This is your safety margin, not expansion capital.

**3. Implementing the "Lockbox" Method**
To prevent overspending, visually or physically segregate your funds:
*   **Operating Account:** Keep exactly 2 months of burn rate here (~$44,000) for immediate bills and payroll.
*   **Reserve Account:** Move the remaining ~$256,000 here.
*   **Monthly Transfer:** On the 1st of every month, transfer exactly $22,000 from Reserve to Operating.
*   **Rule:** If the Reserve Account drops below the required threshold for the remaining contract months, hiring and expansion are frozen.

**4. Decision Rules for Hiring & Expansion**
Do not use the initial $300,000 to justify a second location. Use the **True Available Liquidity** metric:
*   **Hiring:** Only hire if your projected year-end surplus exceeds 3 months of *new* total burn (including the new hire's cost) after the expansion. Currently, your $36k surplus is insufficient to support significant fixed cost increases.
*   **Expansion:** Delay the second location until you have retained earnings from previous years or secured separate financing. Funding expansion from upfront tuition of the *current* cohort risks insolvency if enrollment dips next year.

**5. Monitoring Cadence**
Review this forecast monthly. If enrollment drops below 50 students next year, your upfront cash will decrease while fixed costs (rent) likely remain, accelerating a cash crunch. Your model must include a "Sensitivity Analysis" tab showing cash flow at 40 and 45 students to stress-test your resilience.

***

*Note: This framework treats upfront tuition as a liability to be earned, ensuring you never spend next month's rent money on today's growth.*
*Consult a local CPA to align this with Ontario's specific accounting standards for deferred revenue in educational institutions.*
