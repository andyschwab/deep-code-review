### Viability Analysis: 40% Price Increase

**Verdict:** A universal, immediate 40% price hike is **not viable**. It carries an unacceptably high risk of triggering the 8% churn scenario, collapsing your runway to 7 months. The concentration of 30% of revenue in discounted early adopters makes them a single point of failure if handled aggressively.

**The Break-Even Model**
To offset a price increase with potential churn, you must calculate the "Churn Tolerance Limit."
*   **Current Monthly Revenue:** ~£160,000 (200 nodes × ~£800 avg/node/mo derived from ACV, or simply based on burn/growth context). *Correction based on your data:* 200 startups × £9,600 ACV = £1.92M ARR → £160k MRR.
*   **Target Revenue (40% increase):** £224k MRR.
*   **The Math:** If you raise prices by 40% but lose customers, your new revenue is: `(Remaining Customers) × (Old Price × 1.4)`.
*   **Break-Even Churn Rate:** To maintain current revenue (£160k), you can afford to lose **28.5%** of your customer base immediately (`1 - (1/1.4)`).
*   **Profitability Threshold:** To cover the £45k burn and reach profitability (assuming current growth isn't covering it), you need net new revenue. If your goal is purely to increase *yield* from the existing base by 40%, you can tolerate up to **28% churn** just to stay flat. However, your constraint is **5% max churn** to survive 12 months.

**The Risk:** Early adopters on beta rates are price-sensitive. A sudden 40% hike on them could easily trigger 10-15% churn alone, pushing you toward the danger zone.

### Strategic Recommendation: Segmented Rollout

Do not apply the increase universally. Use a **Segmented Grandfathering Strategy** to protect cash flow while capturing value.

1.  **New Customers (Immediate):** Apply the +40% price increase to all new logos effective immediately. This tests willingness-to-pay without risking existing relationships.
2.  **Early Adopters (The 30%):** **Grandfather temporarily, not permanently.**
    *   Offer them a "Loyalty Lock": They keep their beta rate for 6 more months *if* they switch to an annual contract now.
    *   **Why:** This secures 6 months of guaranteed cash upfront (improving immediate runway) and delays the price shock. After 6 months, migrate them to the new pricing gradually (e.g., +20% then +20%).
3.  **Standard Customers (The 70%):** Implement a **staggered increase**.
    *   Announce a 20% increase now, tied to a specific value milestone (e.g., new feature release).
    *   Plan the remaining 20% increase in 6 months.
    *   This keeps total annual churn likelihood under the 5% threshold by avoiding "sticker shock."

### Communication Strategy

**Narrative:** Frame the increase as an alignment with **value delivered**, not a correction of underpricing.

*   **Subject Line:** "Updates to CloudScan pricing and our roadmap to enterprise security."
*   **Key Message:** "Over the past year, we've added [X, Y, Z capabilities]. To sustain this innovation and ensure the security reliability you depend on, we are adjusting our pricing structure."
*   **For Early Adopters:** "As a thank you for building with us, you are eligible for a 6-month extension of your current rates if you annualize by [Date]."
*   **The "Why":** Be transparent that this ensures long-term viability. Customers prefer a sustainable vendor over a cheap one that might disappear.
