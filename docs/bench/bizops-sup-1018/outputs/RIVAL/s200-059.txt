### Financial Projection: 12-Month Runway Impact

**Current Baseline**
*   **Revenue:** $100,000/mo (2M mins @ $0.05)
*   **Variable Costs:** $40,000 (2M mins @ $0.02)
*   **Fixed Costs:** $45,000
*   **Net Profit:** $15,000/mo
*   **Current Runway:** Infinite (profitable), growing cash by $15k/mo.

**Scenario A: 15% Across-the-Board Price Cut**
*   **New Price:** $0.0425/min
*   **Assumption:** Volume remains static (2M mins) to test pure yield impact.
*   **New Revenue:** $85,000/mo
*   **Variable Costs:** $40,000 (unchanged)
*   **Fixed Costs:** $45,000
*   **Net Result:** **-$5,000/mo loss**
*   **12-Month Impact:** You burn $60,000. Cash drops from $300k to $240k.
*   **Risk:** To break even, you must increase volume by ~18% immediately just to match current profits. If volume doesn't spike instantly, you accelerate toward cash depletion without solving the retention issue.

**Scenario B: Tiered Model with Volume Discounts**
*   **Strategy:** Maintain $0.05/min for low-volume users; discount top 10 customers (1.2M mins) by 20% to match competitor.
*   **Top 10 Revenue:** 1.2M mins @ $0.04 = $48,000
*   **Remaining Revenue:** 0.8M mins @ $0.05 = $40,000
*   **Total Revenue:** $88,000/mo
*   **Variable Costs:** $40,000 (unchanged)
*   **Fixed Costs:** $45,000
*   **Net Result:** **+$3,000/mo profit**
*   **12-Month Impact:** You generate $36,000 profit. Cash grows to $336,000.
*   **Benefit:** You retain high-volume clients without subsidizing smaller users who are less price-sensitive.

**Recommendation**
Do **not** match the competitor's aggressive pricing across the board. A blanket 15% cut turns your profitable business into a loss-making one immediately, burning $60k over the next year.

Instead, **implement the tiered model immediately.** Your top 10 customers account for 60% of usage; they are your primary risk. Offering them a targeted 20% discount matches the competitor specifically where it matters while preserving margin on the remaining 40% of usage. This approach keeps you profitable (+$3k/mo) and extends your runway indefinitely, whereas the blanket cut shortens it.

Regarding your fear of confusing smaller users: Simplicity wins. Structure it as "Standard" ($0.05) and "Enterprise Volume" (custom pricing starting at 100k mins). Do not clutter the public page with complex math; hide the deep discounts behind a "Contact Sales" or automated volume calculator. Double down on parallel testing features for the Standard tier to justify the higher rate for mid-market users who value speed over raw cost.

*Note: This projection assumes volume remains constant; if the price cut triggers a volume war, variable costs will rise proportionally, worsening the loss in Scenario A.*
