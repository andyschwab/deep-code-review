### Cash Flow Projection & Strategic Recommendation

**Current State:**
*   **Cash Reserves:** $1,200,000
*   **Current Burn:** $75,000/mo
*   **Current Revenue:** $90,000/mo (growing 10% MoM)
*   **Net Cash Flow (Current):** +$15,000/mo (Profitable)

#### Scenario A: Immediate Aggressive Expansion
*   **Upfront Cost:** $600,000 (Month 0)
*   **New Burn Rate:** $135,000/mo
*   **Revenue Lag:** Assume 6 months before new hires generate significant incremental revenue in Europe.
*   **Projection (Months 1–6):**
    *   Starting Cash: $1,200,000 - $600,000 = $600,000.
    *   Monthly Deficit: $135,000 (burn) - $90,000 (base revenue) = -$45,000 (ignoring growth for conservative estimate).
    *   6-Month Cash Drain: ~$270,000.
    *   **Cash Remaining at Month 6:** ~$330,000.
*   **Runway Analysis:** With $330,000 remaining and a burn of $135,000, you have **2.4 months** of runway left if European revenue does not kick in immediately by Month 7. Even with 10% organic growth, the margin for error is non-existent. One delayed enterprise deal or hiring snag leads to insolvency within 9 months total.

#### Scenario B: Delayed Expansion (Status Quo)
*   **Upfront Cost:** $0
*   **Burn Rate:** $75,000/mo
*   **Projection:**
    *   Monthly Surplus: +$15,000 (increasing as revenue grows 10% MoM).
    *   **Cash Position in 6 Months:** ~$1,300,000+ (accumulating surplus).
    *   **Runway:** Indefinite at current burn; highly capital efficient.

#### Verdict: Do Not Proceed Without a Bridge Round
Your current financial position **does not support** the aggressive move without external capital. While you are currently cash-flow positive, the $600,000 upfront commitment combined with the near-doubling of your burn rate creates a "death valley" between spending the cash and realizing European revenue.

If you proceed immediately:
1.  You deplete 50% of your reserves in Day 1.
2.  You reduce your safety net to less than 3 months if the new market adoption lags by even 30 days.
3.  You lose all negotiating leverage with investors if you approach them while burning cash rapidly with <6 months runway.

**Recommendation:**
Secure a **bridge round of at least $1.5M** before signing leases or offer letters. This validates the expansion thesis with external capital rather than risking existing reserves. If a bridge round is not immediately available, **delay the physical office launch**. Hire the 5 engineers remotely first to validate the European revenue stream, then open the office once the new revenue covers the incremental burn. Losing first-mover advantage is risky, but running out of cash is fatal.

*Note: This projection assumes a 6-month revenue lag for new hires; shortening this window improves but does not solve the structural risk.*
*Consider a "soft launch" strategy to test the Paris market without the $600k fixed cost commitment.*
