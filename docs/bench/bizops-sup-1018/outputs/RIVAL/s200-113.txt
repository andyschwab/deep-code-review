### Revised Unit Economics & Break-Even Analysis for HeartGuard

**Current Unit Economics (Per Device)**
*   **Selling Price:** $299
*   **Hardware Cost:** $85
*   **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** $60
*   **Gross Margin per Unit:** $154 ($299 - $85 - $60)

**Financial Runway Reality Check**
Your current cash position ($750,000) and monthly burn ($83,000) provide a hard runway of **9.04 months**. Since your development timeline has extended to **12 months**, you face a **cash deficit of approximately $246,000** ($83k × 3 months) before you can fully launch, assuming no revenue comes in during development.

**The Missing Variable: Third-Party Audit Cost**
Let $A$ = Cost of the third-party security audit.
Let $L$ = Additional labor cost for the 6 extra months of engineering compliance work.

*Estimated Additional Labor ($L$):*
Assuming an average fully loaded cost of $15,000/month per engineer (salary + benefits + overhead) for 8 engineers:
$L = 8 \text{ engineers} \times \$15,000 \times 6 \text{ months} = \$720,000$.
*(Note: If your actual loaded cost differs, adjust this multiplier accordingly. This is likely already reflected in your current $83k burn, but represents new capital required to finish.)*

**Revised Break-Even Volume Calculation**
To maintain your original margin targets and cover the *new* costs introduced by the delay, you must cover the additional cash gap.

1.  **Total New Capital Needed:**
    $\text{Cash Deficit} (\$246,000) + \text{Audit Cost} (A) + \text{Additional Labor} (L)$
    *If $L$ is already included in the burn rate projection, the immediate gap is just the 3-month deficit + Audit.*
    Let's assume the $83k burn continues for 12 months. Total cash needed for 12 months = $996,000.
    Current Cash = $750,000.
    **Immediate Funding Gap = $246,000 + A (Audit Cost).**

2.  **Additional Units Required to Cover Gap:**
    $\text{Additional Units} = \frac{\$246,000 + A}{\$154 \text{ (Margin per Unit)}}$

    *   **Scenario A (Audit = $50k):**
        Gap = $296,000.
        Additional Units = **1,922 units**.
        Total Year 1 Target = 5,000 + 1,922 = **6,922 units**.

    *   **Scenario B (Audit = $100k):**
        Gap = $346,000.
        Additional Units = **2,247 units**.
        Total Year 1 Target = 5,000 + 2,247 = **7,247 units**.

    *   **Scenario C (Audit = $200k):**
        Gap = $446,000.
        Additional Units = **2,896 units**.
        Total Year 1 Target = 5,000 + 2,896 = **7,896 units**.

**Strategic Implications**
You originally projected selling 5,000 units. To simply break even on the *extended timeline costs* (excluding the original R&D recoupment), you now need to sell between **7,000 and 8,000 units** in Year 1, representing a **40–60% increase in sales volume**.

Given you only have 3 sales staff, achieving ~650 units/month post-launch is a significant operational stretch. You must either:
1.  **Secure Bridge Funding:** Raise at least $300k–$500k to cover the gap without inflating sales targets.
2.  **Increase Price:** Raising the price to $349 increases margin to $204/unit, reducing the additional unit requirement by ~25%.
3.  **Reduce Burn:** Cut non-essential costs immediately to extend the 9-month runway closer to 12.

**Action Item:** Obtain the exact quote for the third-party audit ($A$) immediately to finalize the specific unit target.

*The labor cost estimate above assumes standard Silicon Valley loaded engineering costs; adjust if your team's cost basis differs significantly.*
