**Deliverable: Regulatory Clarification and Budget Impact Analysis**

**1. Mandatory Compliance Standard**
For a UK government contract of this nature, you are legally mandated to meet the **Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) (No. 2) Accessibility Regulations 2018**. This regulation strictly requires adherence to **WCAG 2.1 Level AA** standards. There is no "lower standard" acceptable for digital training modules procured by central government bodies. While the regulations allow for "disproportionate burden" exemptions, these are narrowly defined, must be formally documented in an accessibility statement, and rarely apply to core deliverables like training content where the primary purpose is inclusion. Bidding with a lower standard (e.g., Level A) creates an immediate risk of disqualification during the technical evaluation or constitutes a breach of contract upon delivery.
