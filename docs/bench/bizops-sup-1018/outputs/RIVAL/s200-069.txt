Present your unit economics using a **Range-Based Margin Model** anchored to "Test Density" rather than a single static percentage. This approach validates the volatility as a managed variable rather than an uncontrolled risk.

**1. Redefine the Unit Economic**
Stop reporting margin as a single line item. Instead, present three distinct scenarios based on **Cloud Utilization Efficiency**:
*   **Base Case (60% Utilization):** Your historical average. Show a gross margin of ~60%. This is your operational baseline.
*   **Stress Case (30% Utilization):** Occurs during massive, irregular spikes where you must provision expensive on-demand capacity instantly. Show a gross margin of 35-40%. Explicitly label this as "Peak Load Penalty."
*   **Optimized Case (85% Utilization):** Achieved when clients schedule tests with lead time, allowing you to use spot instances or reserved capacity. Show a gross margin of 80-85%.

**2. The Forecasting Mechanism: Weighted Average**
For the four-quarter board deck, do not smooth the data into a fake constant. Instead, forecast revenue based on **committed volume** from your top 6 clients and apply a **Weighted Average Margin** that shifts over time.
*   **Q1-Q2:** Model a conservative weighted margin (e.g., 50%) assuming current irregular scheduling.
*   **Q3-Q4:** Model an improved weighted margin (e.g., 65%) contingent on specific operational changes (see below).

**3. The Path to Profitability (The "Control" Narrative)**
Investors fear volatility they cannot control. You must demonstrate how you will shift the mix from "Stress Case" to "Optimized Case." Present these three levers as your strategy to stabilize margins:
*   **Incentivized Scheduling:** Introduce a pricing tier where clients receive a 15% discount if they schedule tests 48 hours in advance. This allows you to secure cheaper cloud infrastructure, directly protecting your margin.
*   **Reserved Capacity Blocks:** Sell "Guanteed Load Blocks" (e.g., 100k users/hour guaranteed monthly) at a premium. This creates predictable baseline revenue that covers your fixed cloud commitments, insulating you from usage swings.
*   **Spot Instance Automation:** Highlight your engineering roadmap to automate the use of spot instances for non-critical test phases, mathematically lowering your "Stress Case" cost floor from 65% of revenue to 50%.

**4. Visual Presentation**
Replace the single margin line in your deck with a **shaded band chart**.
*   The top of the band represents the Optimized Case.
*   The bottom represents the Stress Case.
*   The solid line through the middle is your Weighted Average Forecast.
*   Add a footnote: *"Margin variance is directly correlated to client lead-time. Our Q3 pricing update incentivizes 48h advance booking, projected to narrow this band by 40%."*

This tells the board: "We know exactly why margins fluctuate, we have modeled the extremes, and we have a pricing product specifically designed to flatten the curve."

*Note: Ensure your "Stress Case" still shows positive contribution margin to prove scalability even in worst-case spikes.*
*Do not hide the 35% margin; frame it as the cost of "instant-on" liquidity which you are actively pricing to mitigate.*
