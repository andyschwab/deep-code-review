**The Call:** Do not frame the nonprofit tier as "zero revenue"; frame it as a verified B2B2C distribution channel that lowers your blended CAC and validates engagement, but you must model a clear path to monetizing the remaining 60% or introducing ancillary revenue streams to satisfy growth-focused VCs.

**The Numbers:**
Your current runway is a hard constraint before any Series A narrative matters.
*   **Formula:** Runway = Cash Reserves / Monthly Burn Rate
*   **Inputs:** $600,000 / $30,000
*   **Result:** 20 months.

Regarding the "distorted" unit economics, investors will not look at "Total Revenue" alone; they will look at **LTV:CAC** and **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)** on the *paid* segment.
*   **Assumption (Low):** If the 40% free users generate $0 indirect value and churn is high, your effective CAC rises because you are servicing users who don't pay.
*   **Assumption (Base):** If the 40% nonprofit users drive viral adoption (bereaved individuals invite others), reducing your paid CAC by 30%, the model strengthens.
*   **Assumption (High):** If you can upsell 10% of the free nonprofit organizations to a "Premium Admin" tier at $500/month within 18 months, that creates a $240k ARR bridge.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call from "viable strategy" to "fatal flaw" is the **Paid Conversion Rate** of the non-nonprofit segment.
*   **Trigger Value:** If your paid conversion rate from the remaining 60% of potential users falls below 2% (industry benchmark for niche video platforms) by Month 12, investors will view the free tier as a drain rather than a strategy.
*   **Stop/Pivot Date:** Month 14 (6 months before your targeted Series A).
*   **Rule of Thumb:** VCs prioritize **efficiency of growth** over raw user count in Canada's current market. A smaller, paying base with 120% NRR is valued higher than a massive free base with 0% monetization.
*   **This Week's Step:** If your current paid conversion is unknown, consider running a "freemium-to-trial" A/B test on 500 non-nonprofit users to establish a baseline conversion rate before finalizing the pitch deck narrative.
