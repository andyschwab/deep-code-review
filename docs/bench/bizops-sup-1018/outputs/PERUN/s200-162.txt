**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that flips this from "contractor" to "employee" is **Control over Method**.
*   **The Trigger:** If your platform mandates specific teaching materials, sets fixed lesson times that instructors cannot change without penalty, or handles customer refunds without instructor consent, you cross the line.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Contractors control the *means* (how/when they teach); employers control the *means* and only care about the *result*. If you control the means, you are an employer.
*   **This Week's Step:** If your terms of service currently allow TuneMatch to unilaterally refund students or dictate lesson curriculum, consider revising them to require instructor approval for refunds and framing quality standards as "outcome-based" (e.g., student retention rates) rather than "process-based" (e.g., must use Method X).
