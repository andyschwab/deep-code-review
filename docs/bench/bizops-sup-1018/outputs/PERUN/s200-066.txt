**The Call:** Do not aggressively shift revenue to direct channels before your Series A if it slows overall growth; instead, frame your marketplace strategy as "capital-efficient distribution validation" while presenting a low-cost plan to diversify. Investors prefer a narrative of "strategic leverage with a mitigation roadmap" over a forced pivot that burns cash on an unproven direct sales motion.

**The Numbers:**
Your current concentration risk is high but quantifiable.
*   **Marketplace Revenue:** 20% of $800k ARR = **$160,000/year** ($13,333/month).
*   **Direct Revenue:** $800k - $160k = **$640,000/year** (implied, though you noted a prior year test of only $30k).
*   **Burn Rate:** $45,000/month × 12 = **$540,000/year**.
*   **Runway:** Assuming no new revenue and $800k cash (hypothetical), runway is ~17 months. With current burn vs. revenue ($800k/12 ≈ $66k/mo), you are cash-flow positive by ~$21k/month.

*Assumption (ASSUMED):* You have sufficient cash reserves to cover the $45k burn independent of immediate fundraising.
*Assumption (ASSUMED):* The $30k direct revenue figure from last year represents a minimal effort test, not a fully funded team.

**Sensitivity Analysis:**
If a marketplace delists you (Loss of $13.3k/mo):
*   New Monthly Revenue: ~$53.3k.
*   New Monthly Profit/Loss: $53.3k - $45k = +$8.3k (still profitable).
*   **Result:** The business remains viable even with a total loss of the channel, provided the remaining 80% is stable. This is your strongest defense.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The narrative flips from "smart leverage" to "dangerous dependency" if marketplace revenue exceeds **30-40% of total ARR** or if your **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) via marketplaces** rises due to fee hikes.
*   **Trigger Value:** If marketplace revenue hits $240k/year (30% of a growing $800k+ base) without a corresponding increase in direct pipeline.
*   **Stop/Pivot Date:** Re-evaluate in **6 months** (approx. Q3 2024). If marketplace share grows faster than direct organic inbound, you must allocate budget to direct sales.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Investors tolerate high channel concentration if the unit economics are undeniable and the "loss scenario" doesn't kill the company.
*   **This Week's Step:** If your churn rate on marketplace customers is lower than direct customers, consider doubling down on the channel; if higher, consider a small pilot to improve direct retention.
