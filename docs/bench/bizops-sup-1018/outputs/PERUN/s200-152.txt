**The Numbers**

To extend runway by 18 months at a $40,000/month burn, you need **$720,000** in *collected* cash.
*Deals Required:* $720,000 / $25,000 per contract = **29 contracts**.
*Pipeline Reality:*
You have 10 qualified leads with a 20% close rate.
Expected closes = 10 leads × 20% = **2 contracts**.
Expected Revenue = 2 × $25,000 = **$50,000**.
*Cash Flow Timing (The Critical Failure):*
Current Cash: $240,000.
Monthly Burn: $40,000.
Months to Zero: 6 months.
Sales Cycle: 4 months.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes this outcome is **Cash Collection Timing**, not the number of deals signed.
*Trigger:* If you do not secure non-dilutive funding or a credit line covering 6 months of burn ($240,000) within the next **30 days**, you will run out of cash before recognizing significant revenue.
*Rule of Thumb:* In B2B enterprise sales, "revenue" on a contract does not equal "cash in bank" until 30–90 days post-signature. Your runway is determined by cash, not bookings.
*Action:* If you cannot expand the pipeline to 50+ leads immediately, consider pausing developer hiring or switching to a lower-cost contractor model to reduce the $40,000 burn to $20,000, doubling your organic runway to 12 months.
