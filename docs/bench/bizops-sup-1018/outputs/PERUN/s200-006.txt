**The Call**
A $200M TAM does not make a $10M post-money valuation impossible, but it caps your narrative; you must justify the round by proving you can capture a dominant share (10%+) of this niche quickly, rather than relying on massive market expansion. Reject the premise that small TAM automatically dictates high dilution; instead, frame the raise as buying dominance in a profitable, defensible segment where unit economics outperform mass-market competitors.

**The Numbers**
*Equity Dilution Arithmetic (Tier 1)*
Formula: `Equity % = Raise Amount / Post-Money Valuation`
Your Figures: $1.5M Raise / $10M Post-Money = **15% Equity Dilution**.

*TAM Context Analysis (Tier 2)*
Standard Seed Dilution Range: Typically 15%–25%.
Your Proposed Deal: 15% sits at the favorable (low) end of the standard range.
*Assumption (ASSUMED):* Investors accept the $10M valuation premise.
*Scenario Analysis:*
- **Base Case (Valuation Holds):** You give up 15%. This is standard for seed rounds regardless of TAM if growth metrics are strong.
- **Downside Case (Valuation Compressed to $7.5M due to TAM fears):** $1.5M / $7.5M = **20% Dilution**.
- **Upside Case (Valuation Expanded to $12M via metric leverage):** $1.5M / $12M = **12.5% Dilution**.

*Realistic Equity Range:* Given the niche constraint, expect investors to push for a lower valuation, resulting in a realistic dilution range of **15% to 22%** unless you prove high velocity.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is **Revenue Velocity (Month-over-Month Growth)** relative to your niche cap.
*Trigger:* If you can demonstrate you are on track to capture $20M (10% of TAM) within 3–4 years with healthy margins, the $10M valuation is justified.
*Rule of Thumb:* In niche hardware, investors accept smaller exits if the path to $10M–$20M ARR is clear and capital-efficient.
*Actionable Step:* If your current monthly recurring revenue (or monthly unit sales growth) is below 15% MoM, consider delaying the raise until you hit that threshold to negotiate the $10M cap firmly.

**Metrics to Highlight (Counteracting Small TAM)**
To justify the valuation despite the $200M cap, prioritize these metrics in your deck:
1.  **LTV:CAC Ratio:** Aim for >3.0x. In a capped market, efficient acquisition proves you can saturate the niche without burning cash.
2.  **Net Revenue Retention (NRR):** If >100% (via accessories or upgrades), it shows you can grow within the existing customer base, mitigating the "new customer" ceiling.
3.  **Gross Margin Stability:** Your 40% margin is healthy; prove it stays stable or improves at scale, unlike mass-market competitors who compete on price.
4.  **Payback Period:** Show CAC is recovered in <12 months. This reduces risk for investors who fear the market saturates before they get their money back.
