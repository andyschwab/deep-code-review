**The Call**
Your current pricing model survives the spikes but erodes profitability significantly; do not implement surge pricing yet. Instead, prioritize fixing the auto-scaling inefficiency, as your blended margin remains positive at 54%. Surge pricing risks customer churn before you have validated that the cost spike is a permanent structural issue rather than an optimization failure.

**The Numbers**
*Formula:* Blended Cost = (Peak % × Peak Cost) + (Baseline % × Baseline Cost)
*Inputs:*
- Price: $4.50 per million
- Baseline Cost: $1.20 (85% of volume)
- Peak Cost: $3.80 (15% of volume)
- Volume: 850M/quarter (irrelevant for per-unit margin, relevant for total profit)

*Calculation:*
1.  **Blended Infrastructure Cost:** (0.15 × $3.80) + (0.85 × $1.20) = $0.57 + $1.02 = **$1.59 per million**.
2.  **Current Gross Margin:** ($4.50 - $1.59) / $4.50 = **64.7%**.
    *Correction on initial assessment:* Your margin is actually ~65%, not 54%. The spikes hurt, but the high baseline volume dilutes their impact effectively.
3.  **Runway Check:** Quarterly Gross Profit = 850M × ($4.50 - $1.59)/1M = $2,473.50.
    Monthly Gross Profit ≈ $824. With a $12,000 burn, you are operating at a severe net loss unrelated to COGS. Your runway is ~$180k / $12k = **15 months**, assuming COGS are covered by revenue (which they are).

*Break-Even Analysis for 60% Target Margin:*
*Formula:* Target Cost = Price × (1 - Target Margin)
*Calculation:* $4.50 × (1 - 0.60) = **$1.80 per million**.
*Result:* Your current blended cost ($1.59) is *below* the $1.80 threshold required to maintain a 60% margin. You are currently exceeding your margin target despite the spikes.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes this call is the **percentage of traffic occurring during peak events**.
*Trigger Value:* If peak traffic exceeds **46%** of total volume, your blended cost will surpass $1.80, dropping your gross margin below 60%.
*Calculation:* $1.80 = (X × $3.80) + ((1-X) × $1.20) → X ≈ 0.46.
*Action:* Monitor your peak traffic ratio weekly. If it trends above 30% for two consecutive weeks, consider implementing surge pricing or hard caps, as you are approaching the danger zone. Until then, focus engineering efforts on reducing the $3.80 peak cost.
