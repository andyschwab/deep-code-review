**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The trigger is the **offer or sale** of the franchise. In California and Illinois, selling an unregistered franchise is a violation of state securities/investment laws, granting franchisees the right to rescind (cancel) the contract and demand a full refund of their $45,000 fee plus damages. The "small franchisor" or "fractional" exemptions rarely apply to active expansion plans involving new independent operators paying significant fees. If your legal spend exceeds $20,000, consider delaying the launch in the strictest states (CA, IL, NY, MD) until additional capital is secured, or explore "business opportunity" laws which may have different, though still complex, requirements.
