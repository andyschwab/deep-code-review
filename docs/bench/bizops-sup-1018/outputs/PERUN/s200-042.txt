**What flips it, and the trigger:**
The audit risk flips from "acceptable" to "flagged" if you cannot map a specific sold item to a specific purchase receipt.
*   **Trigger:** If more than 5% of your sold items lack a verifiable acquisition date and cost basis in your records, an auditor will likely reject your COGS calculation.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Unique, high-value items (> $500 cost) generally require Specific Identification under GAAP/IRS rules rather than flow assumptions like FIFO.
