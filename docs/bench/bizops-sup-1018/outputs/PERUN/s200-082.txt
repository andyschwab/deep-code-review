**The Criteria and The "Human-in-the-Loop" Myth:**
Under the EU AI Act, AI systems intended for use in recruitment (specifically filtering, ranking, or evaluating natural persons) are explicitly categorized as high-risk. The regulation targets the *purpose* of the system, not just the finality of its decision.
*   **Automated Decision-Making:** Your system filters 60% of applicants before human review. This constitutes a substantial influence on the hiring process.
*   **Human Override:** The fact that a human can override the decision **does not** lower your risk profile from "high-risk" to "limited/minimal." The law recognizes that human reviewers often suffer from automation bias (over-trusting the algorithm). Therefore, the strict obligations regarding data governance, technical documentation, transparency, and human oversight apply regardless of the final human sign-off.

1.  **Internal Gap Analysis (Weeks 1-4):** Conduct an internal review against the specific requirements for high-risk systems (Annex III of the Act). Map your current data sets, training logs, and model architecture against the mandated standards for accuracy, robustness, and cybersecurity.

Once your documentation is mature, engage a notified body to perform the conformity assessment. Premature engagement will result in high billable hours spent fixing basic documentation gaps you could have addressed internally.
