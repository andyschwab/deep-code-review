*Results vs. Common Thresholds (Illustrative Only):*
Many states use a threshold of $100,000 in gross sales **OR** 200 separate transactions.

*   Under Scenario B ($12k sales, 141 transactions): You likely remain **under** both thresholds ($12k < $100k and 141 < 200).

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is the **revenue concentration per state**. If any single state exceeds either the dollar threshold (often $100k) or the transaction count threshold (often 200), you have economic nexus.
*Trigger:* If your Q1 or Q2 reports show >$25,000 in sales or >200 transactions in *any single* state, you must consider that state a nexus state immediately.
*Rule of Thumb:* Do not aggregate sales across states; nexus is determined state-by-state.
*Step this week:* Export your last 12 months of sales data, filter by "Ship To" or "Billing" state, and count the transactions and sum the gross revenue for Florida, Texas, and Washington individually. If either number approaches the common limits ($100k/200 tx), stop selling to that state until you have a plan.
