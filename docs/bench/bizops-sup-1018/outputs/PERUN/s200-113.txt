**The Call**
Do not attempt to maintain your original margin targets by simply selling more units; with only 9 months of runway and a 12-month development cycle, you will run out of cash before reaching the market. Your immediate priority is not a revised sales target, but securing a bridge extension or reducing burn to survive the compliance delay.

**The Numbers**
Your current unit economics per device are:
*   **Price:** $299
*   **Variable Cost (Hardware):** $85
*   **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** $60
*   **Contribution Margin:** $299 - $85 - $60 = **$154 per unit**

Your cash constraint is the binding factor:
*   **Cash Remaining:** $750,000
*   **Monthly Burn:** $83,000
*   **Runway:** $750,000 / $83,000 ≈ **9.04 months**

To calculate the new break-even volume including the unknown audit cost, we use this formula:
$$Break\text{-}Even\ Units = \frac{Fixed\ Costs\ (including\ Audit)}{Contribution\ Margin\ per\ Unit}$$

Since you cannot quantify the increased labor costs or the audit fee yet, we must bracket the **Total Additional Compliance Cost (TACC)** as an assumption.
*   **Assumption (Low):** TACC is $100,000 (minimal audit, no extra labor).
    *   Additional units needed: $100,000 / $154 ≈ **650 units**.
    *   Total Year 1 Target: 5,650 units.
*   **Assumption (Base):** TACC is $250,000 (moderate audit + 3 months extra engineering labor).
    *   Additional units needed: $250,000 / $154 ≈ **1,624 units**.
    *   Total Year 1 Target: 6,624 units.
*   **Assumption (High):** TACC is $500,000 (rigorous audit + full year delay labor).
    *   Additional units needed: $500,000 / $154 ≈ **3,247 units**.
    *   Total Year 1 Target: 8,247 units.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes your outcome is the **Total Additional Compliance Cost (TACC)**. However, the true trigger is your **cash runway expiration date**.
*   **Trigger Date:** 9 months from today.
*   **The Problem:** Even if you hit the "Low" assumption target of 5,650 units, you cannot sell them if you cease operations in month 9 while your product launches in month 12.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If your development timeline exceeds your cash runway, unit economics are irrelevant until liquidity is solved.
*   **Actionable Step:** If your estimated TACC exceeds $150,000, consider pausing non-essential engineering features to focus solely on the minimum viable compliance documentation required for FDA submission.
