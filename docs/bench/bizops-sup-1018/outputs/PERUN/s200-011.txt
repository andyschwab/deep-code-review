**The Call:** Your 15,000€ budget is sufficient to validate demand for 300 units if you strictly target high-intent search traffic and local sustainable events, but you cannot size the total addressable market (TAM) accurately without primary data collection; proceed with a bottom-up validation sprint before committing to the full 500-unit run.

**The Numbers:**
To determine if the market supports 300 units, we calculate the required conversion efficiency against your budget.
*   **Formula:** Required CAC = Marketing Budget / Target Units Sold.
*   **Your Figures:** 15,000€ / 300 units = **50€ CAC target**.
*   **Unit Economics:** Price (Assumed 325€, mid-range of competitors) - COGS (85€) = **240€ Gross Margin**.
*   **Result:** A 50€ CAC represents ~15% of your selling price, which is healthy for luxury DTC if your conversion rate holds.

**Sizing Method (Bottom-Up):**
Do not use top-down reports. Build your model using these specific proxies:
1.  **Search Intent Volume:** Use Google Keyword Planner (free tier) for exact match terms in FR/DE: "vegan leather handbag luxury," "cactus leather bag," "mushroom leather purse." Filter by location (Paris, Berlin, Munich, Lyon). Multiply monthly volume by a conservative 0.5% capture rate to estimate organic reach potential.
2.  **Event Footfall:** Scrape Eventbrite and Meetup for "sustainable fashion pop-up" in Paris/Berlin (last 12 months). Take average attendance per event. Assume a 2% conversion rate from event visitor to email lead, and 5% from lead to sale for high-touch pop-ups.
3.  **Competitor Social Proof:** Manually count new followers and engagement on the 12 identified competitors' Instagram/TikTok over 30 days. Estimate their monthly new customer acquisition by dividing new followers by an assumed 10:1 follower-to-customer ratio (industry heuristic for niche luxury).

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is your **Click-Through Rate (CTR) on paid ads**. If your CTR on "vegan luxury" keywords falls below 1.5%, your effective CAC will spike above 50€, making the 300-unit goal unattainable with 15,000€.
*   **Trigger:** Run a 2,000€ test ad campaign in Month 1. If CAC > 60€ after 20 clicks, pause scaling and pivot to organic influencer seeding.
*   **Step:** This week, set up the Google Ads test targeting only the top 3 high-intent keywords identified in your research.

**Build vs. Buy Decision:**
*   **Internal (Do it yourself):** Search volume analysis, competitor social scraping, and basic event attendance counting. Your team of two can execute this in 40 hours.
*   **External (Hire a firm):** Only hire a firm if you need verified demographic income data for "sustainable fashion attendees" to prove bankability to investors. For operational planning, free proxies are sufficient.
