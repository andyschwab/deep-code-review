**The Call**
Treat your current architecture as high-risk for forced disclosure until a licensed attorney confirms the license scope; do not proceed with fundraising or new client contracts in this state. The "viral" nature of many open-source licenses (like GPL) often extends to statically linked binaries, meaning your single-binary distribution likely triggers the requirement to open-source your proprietary add-ons.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is the **specific license type** (e.g., MIT/Apache 2.0 vs. GPL v2/v3 vs. AGPL) and the **linking method** (static vs. dynamic).
*   **Trigger:** If the core engine is licensed under **GPL or AGPL** and you are **statically linking**, you are likely non-compliant.
*   **Stop/Pivot Rule:** If you cannot produce a written legal opinion stating your add-ons are "independent works" within 14 days, halt sales of the proprietary module to new EU clients immediately.
*   **This Week's Step:** If the license is GPL-family, consider refactoring the architecture to communicate via network APIs (microservices) rather than static linking, as this often creates a stronger argument for separate works, then engage counsel to validate.
