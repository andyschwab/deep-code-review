**Specific Regulatory Red Flags & Prohibited Activities:**
1.  **Fee Sharing:** In California, lawyers generally cannot share legal fees with non-lawyers. A single bundled invoice where the price covers legal services may be interpreted as the company (a non-law firm entity) sharing in legal fees.
2.  **Triage as Practice:** Non-lawyers cannot analyze legal facts to determine the appropriate legal remedy. Care coordinators must avoid:
    *   Assessing the merit of a legal claim.
    *   Recommending specific legal strategies (e.g., "sue," "settle," "file a complaint").
    *   Interpreting statutes or contracts for the employee.
    *   Completing legal forms on behalf of the employee.
3.  **Corporate Practice of Law:** California strictly prohibits corporations from practicing law. Only licensed individuals can do so. If the "product" is sold by the corporation as a legal service, it risks violating this doctrine.
