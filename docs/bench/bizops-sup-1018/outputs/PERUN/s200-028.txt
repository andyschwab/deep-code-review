**The Numbers**
Your effective margin per unit under a percentage-rent model is calculated by treating the concession fee as a variable cost rather than a fixed overhead.

*Formula:*
`Effective Contribution Margin = Sales Price - COGS - (Sales Price × Concession %)`

*   Concession Fee (18% of $8.50): $1.53
*   **Effective Contribution Margin:** $8.50 - $2.20 - $1.53 = **$4.77 per bag**

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that destroys this model is a **Minimum Annual Guarantee (MAG)** clause common in airport concessions. If the contract states you must pay the *greater* of 18% of sales OR a fixed minimum (e.g., $10,000/month), your cost structure shifts.
*   *Trigger:* If the MAG implies a monthly payment > $7,200 (your projected 18% fee), your effective margin drops disproportionately at lower sales volumes.
*   *Rule of Thumb:* If seasonal traffic dips below $25,000/month, verify if the MAG kicks in.
*   *Action:* If the MAG exceeds $7,200/month, consider negotiating a lower base percentage with a higher MAG cap, or delay launch until peak travel season.

**Routing Note**
Airport concession agreements often include complex "Common Area Maintenance" (CAM) charges, marketing fees, and strict termination clauses tied to sales thresholds; have an attorney review the specific definition of "Gross Sales" to ensure credit card processing fees and returns are excluded before signing.
