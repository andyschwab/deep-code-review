**What Flips It, and The Trigger**
The single input that flips the valuation from "hardware" to "subscription" is **Gross Retention (Churn)**.
- **Trigger:** If your monthly churn exceeds **2.5%**, buyers will revert to hardware multiples (3x–4x) because the "recurring" nature is unstable.
- **Rule of Thumb:** To command 6x+, you must demonstrate <1.5% monthly churn and >100% Net Revenue Retention (upsells/cross-sells).
- **Actionable Step:** If your churn is currently unknown or untracked, **this week** implement cohort analysis to prove retention stability; if churn is >2%, consider a price increase or commitment discounts to lock in revenue before negotiations deepen.
