**The Call:** Do not mix bulk powders into capsules on-site; this activity almost certainly classifies as "manufacturing" under FDA guidelines, triggering facility registration, GMP compliance, and potential enforcement that threatens your core business. Sell only pre-manufactured, sealed bottles from established suppliers until you engage regulatory counsel.

**The Numbers:**
*   **Projected Revenue:** 50 bottles/week × $45 × 52 weeks = **$117,000/year**.
*   **Risk Exposure:** Potential fines for unregistered manufacturing and misbranding can range from **$10,000 to $50,000+ per violation**, plus seizure of product and injunctions.
*   **Assumption (ASSUMED):** You currently have no FDA facility registration or Current Good Manufacturing Practice (cGMP) protocols in place.
*   **Break-even on Compliance:** The cost to retrofit a lab space to cGMP standards and register typically exceeds **$50,000–$100,000** initially, far outweighing the projected $117k revenue for a side venture.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that changes this from retail to manufacturing is **mechanical alteration**. If you open bulk containers and perform any processing (mixing, encapsulating, bottling), you are a manufacturer.
*   **Trigger:** The moment you weigh, mix, or encapsulate ingredients on-site.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Retailers sell sealed units; manufacturers create units.
*   **Step this week:** If you intend to sell supplements, **consider** sourcing fully finished, labeled products from a third-party cGMP-certified manufacturer and reselling them sealed. Do not touch the bulk powder.
