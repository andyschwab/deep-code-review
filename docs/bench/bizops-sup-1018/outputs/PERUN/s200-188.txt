**The Call:** Reject the dedicated sales team; the market math shows a maximum theoretical revenue of $90,000/year, which cannot cover the $180,000 sales cost plus your $120,000 annual operating baseline. Proceed immediately with a self-serve online model to preserve your $100,000 runway.

**The Numbers:**
*Formula:* Annual Revenue = (Total Addressable Market × Conversion Rate) × Price Per Unit.
*Inputs:* 1,200 candidates × 5% conversion × $1,500 price.
*Result:* **$90,000** maximum annual revenue.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes this call is the **Conversion Rate**. To break even on the sales team model alone (ignoring operating costs), you would need a conversion rate of **20%** (generating $360,000 revenue), which is four times your current 5% estimate and highly improbable for a cold market.
*Trigger Rule:* Do not hire a sales representative until you have organically validated a conversion rate above 15% *and* expanded your total addressable market to at least 3,000 candidates.
*Action Step:* If your current funnel data shows a conversion rate below 10% after spending $5,000 on ads, consider pivoting the product to include adjacent maritime certification exams rather than hiring staff.
