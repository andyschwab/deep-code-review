**The Numbers**
*Formula:* `Net Profit = Contract Value - (Base Cost + Compliance Uplift)`
*Inputs:*

- Base Cost (17 modules @ £4k): £68,000 (Derived from £85k / 1.18 margin implies cost is ~84.7% of revenue).
- Compliance Uplift: £1,000 per module × 17 modules = £17,000 (ASSUMED: 17 modules based on £68k base / £4k unit).

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The deciding input is the **number of modules** and the **actual uplift percentage**. If the module count is lower than estimated or the uplift is less than 25%, you may retain slight profit.
*Trigger:* If the RFP clarification confirms "WCAG 2.1 AA" (the standard for UK public bodies under the Public Sector Bodies Accessibility Regulations 2018), you must pivot immediately.
*Rule of Thumb:* Public sector digital services in the UK generally mandate WCAG 2.1 AA. Training materials hosted on a government domain usually fall under this.
*Step this week:* Submit a formal clarification question to the procurement officer asking: "Does this contract require adherence to WCAG 2.1 AA, and is an accessibility statement mandatory upon delivery?" If yes, do not sign at £85,000 without a change order.
