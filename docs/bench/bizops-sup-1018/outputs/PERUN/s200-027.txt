**The Call:** Treat foot-scan data as "special category" biometric data requiring explicit, separate consent and a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) before launch; do not rely on standard terms of service. While GDPR does not strictly mandate local EU storage, it imposes such high barriers on cross-border transfers that hosting within the EU (or using a provider with EU-only processing) is the only viable path for a £50k budget to avoid rebuilding later.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The classification of the data flips the requirement from "standard notice" to "explicit consent." Under GDPR Article 9, biometric data processed for the purpose of uniquely identifying a natural person is sensitive. Since your app creates a unique 3D model for bespoke manufacturing, regulators will likely view this as unique identification.
*   **Trigger:** The moment you capture the first image for processing.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If the data creates a unique template of a physical characteristic, assume Article 9 applies.
*   **Step this week:** If you have not yet finalized your cloud architecture, consider mandating EU-region-only storage in your infrastructure-as-code to eliminate transfer complexity immediately.
