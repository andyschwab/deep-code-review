**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The trigger is the **actual shipping cost reduction**. If shipping does not drop to near-zero, the margin gain shrinks.
*Trigger Value:* If new shipping costs remain above $2.00/meal, the new margin ($26-$11-$7.50-$2.00 = $5.50) falls below the old margin ($6.50), requiring new subscribers.
