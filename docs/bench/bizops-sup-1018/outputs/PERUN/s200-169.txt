**The Call**
Do not halt your core feature yet; displaying data via API and earning referral fees typically classifies you as an introducer or technical provider, not a regulated bank, but you must immediately commission a formal "regulated activities" assessment from UK counsel to confirm you are not inadvertently performing "payment initiation" or "account information" services that require FCA registration before your next funding round.

**The Numbers**
Your current unit economics rely on referral volume, not regulated status, but the potential compliance cost creates a binary risk to your runway.
*   **Current Monthly Revenue:** 800 openings × $10 = $8,000.
*   **Potential One-Time Compliance Cost:** $50,000 (reserve capital) + Audit fees (ASSUMED $15,000–$25,000 annually).
*   **Runway Impact:** If forced to hold reserves immediately, you would need ~6.25 months of current gross revenue just to meet the capital threshold, effectively freezing operational cash.
*   **Trigger Value:** The definition of your API activity. If your API *initiates* transfers, you likely fall under PSD2 (Payment Services Directive 2) as a Payment Initiation Service Provider (PISP). If you only *read* data, you may be an Account Information Service Provider (AISP). Both require FCA authorization in the UK, but neither requires you to be a "bank" holding deposits.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is whether your system sends a command to move money or merely displays information.
*   **The Rule of Thumb:** Reading balances = AISP (regulated, lighter touch); Moving money = PISP (regulated, stricter); Holding money = Bank/E-Money (heaviest regulation).
*   **The Trigger:** If your legal review confirms your API calls constitute "payment initiation" under the UK's Payment Services Regulations 2017, you must cease transfer functions until registered.
*   **Actionable Step:** This week, extract the exact API method logs for one transfer flow and send them to a UK fintech solicitor with the question: "Does this specific technical implementation constitute 'payment initiation' requiring FCA authorization, or is it a exempt technical service?"
