**The Call:** Do not cut marketing by half immediately; instead, pivot your sales strategy to target smaller, faster-declosing deals while reducing marketing spend by 20% to extend runway. A 4.5 burn multiple is a critical warning sign for a company with only $1.5M remaining and an 18-person team, regardless of being "deep-tech," because your current cash runway (~8.3 months) is insufficient to survive another full 9-month sales cycle without new revenue.

**The Numbers:**
*   **Runway Formula:** Cash Balance / Net Burn
    *   $1,500,000 / $180,000 = **8.3 months** (Base Case).
*   **Burn Multiple Context:** 4.5x (Net Burn / Net New ARR). While early-stage deep-tech often sees multiples of 2.0–3.0x, 4.5x indicates inefficient capital deployment relative to growth, especially with a 9-month sales cycle.
*   **Scenario A (Cut Marketing 50%):**
    *   *Assumption (ASSUMED):* Marketing is 60% of OpEx. Cutting 50% of marketing reduces total OpEx by ~30%.
    *   *New Net Burn:* ~$126,000 (assuming revenue stays flat initially due to lag).
    *   *New Runway:* $1,500,000 / $126,000 = **11.9 months**.
    *   *Risk:* Halving marketing may starve the top of the funnel, making the 9-month cycle even harder to close, potentially stalling revenue growth entirely.
*   **Scenario B (Pivot to Smaller Deals + 20% Marketing Cut):**
    *   *Assumption (ASSUMED):* Smaller deals reduce sales cycle to 3–4 months and require less marketing spend per closed dollar.
    *   *Target:* Reduce burn to $140,000 while accelerating revenue recognition.
    *   *Result:* Extends runway to **10.7 months** while increasing the probability of closing deals within the remaining cash window.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes this call is the **Sales Cycle Length**. If you cannot compress the sales cycle from 9 months to under 4 months within the next 60 days, austerity (deep cuts) becomes the only option to avoid insolvency.
*   **Trigger Rule:** If you do not have at least 3 qualified opportunities in the "final negotiation" stage with a projected close date within 90 days by [Date: 2 months from today], you must execute a 40%+ reduction in operating expenses immediately.
*   **Actionable Step:** If your average contract value (ACV) is above $50k, consider launching a "starter" tier under $10k ACV this week to test faster conversion; if conversion on the starter tier exceeds 5% in 30 days, shift 50% of sales effort there.
