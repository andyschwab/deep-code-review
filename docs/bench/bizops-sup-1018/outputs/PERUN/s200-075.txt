**The Call**
Do not establish a German subsidiary yet; for a single $60k contract, the cost ($40k) destroys unit economics. Proceed with a German AWS region (eu-central-1) and a robust Data Processing Agreement (DPA), but treat the "local entity" requirement as a specific legal negotiation point, not an automatic regulatory threshold. The binding decision on whether your Delaware entity can legally sign this specific German public-sector or highly regulated enterprise contract rests with counsel, not a general rule.

**The Numbers**
*   **Deal Value:** $60,000/year.
*   **Compliance Cost (Subsidiary):** $40,000 one-time + ongoing admin.
*   **Compliance Cost (Hosting + DPA):** ~$0 incremental setup (assuming AWS region switch is architectural, not legal) + legal review fees (estimated $2k–$5k).
*   **Runway Impact:** Spending $40k represents **27%** of your $150k cash reserve.
*   **Break-even on Subsidiary:** At $60k revenue, you would need ~8 months just to recoup the setup cost, ignoring taxes and local payroll.
*   **ASSUMED:** Your gross margin remains >80% regardless of hosting region; the customer does not mandate local invoicing from a local VAT ID as a hard condition of purchase.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes this call is the **customer's procurement policy regarding "Permanent Establishment" (PE) risk and invoicing**.
*   **Trigger:** If the German customer's legal team states that their internal compliance or tax department *requires* invoices to originate from a German VAT ID to avoid withholding tax complexities or PE risks for *them*, you must reconsider.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Data residency (GDPR) usually requires data *location*, not corporate *location*. Corporate presence is usually triggered by "doing business" (sales agents, offices), not just remote data hosting.
*   **Step this week:** If the customer insists on a local entity, consider negotiating a "reseller" model where a local German partner signs the contract and resells your software, shielding you from entity setup while satisfying their procurement.
