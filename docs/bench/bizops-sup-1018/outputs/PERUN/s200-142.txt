**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The deciding input is the **FDA's feedback on your proposed predicate device and clinical endpoints** during the Pre-Submission.
*   **Trigger:** If the FDA indicates in the Q-Sub that your software requires a full Premarket Approval (PMA) rather than 510(k), the cost and timeline explode, making the $250k pilot insufficient.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Never commit >15% of total cash reserves to clinical evidence generation before the regulatory pathway (Class I, II, or III) is explicitly confirmed by the agency.
