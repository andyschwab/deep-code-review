**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The deciding factor is the specificity of the word "Certified."
*   **Trigger Value:** The presence of the word "Certified" or an official seal logo without a current certificate number.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If you cannot produce a certificate number upon request by a retailer or regulator on Day 1, the claim is legally indefensible.
*   **Pivot Trigger:** If your legal counsel confirms that your proprietary polymer has already passed ASTM D6400 or D6868 testing (even if the formal certificate is pending), you may be able to use "Meets ASTM Standards for Home Compostability" immediately.
