**The Call:** Freeze all non-essential hiring immediately and cut discretionary marketing spend by 40% now to extend your baseline runway beyond 12 months; do not wait for churn to confirm the downturn.

**The Numbers:**
Your current Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) is $4.2M ($350k MRR).
*Baseline Burn:* With 14 months runway, your monthly net burn is ~$300k ($4.2M / 14).
*Stress Scenario (20% Budget Cut + 4% Churn + 30% Sales Drop):*
1.  **Churn Impact:** At 4% monthly churn on 350 customers, you lose 14 customers/month. At $1k Average Revenue Per Account (ARPA), that is **-$14k/month** in lost MRR immediately, compounding monthly.
2.  **Sales Impact:** A 30% drop in new sales reduces inflow. If current new sales cover churn plus growth, a 30% cut likely turns net-new revenue negative.
3.  **Contraction:** If existing customers cut spend by 20%, ARPA drops from $1k to $800. This is an immediate **-$70k/month** revenue shock (20% of $350k).
*Result:* Combined revenue shock (~-$84k/month initially, worsening with churn) against a fixed $300k burn shrinks runway rapidly. If revenue drops 25% overall while burn stays flat, runway collapses from 14 months to ~10.5 months. To hit your feared <6 months, burn must also increase or revenue collapse must exceed 50%.

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)**. If NRR drops below 90% (due to contraction + churn), the business is no longer compounding and is in survival mode.
*Trigger Rule:* If two consecutive months show NRR < 95% or new sales pipeline velocity drops >20% vs. last quarter, execute the "Contingency Plan."
*Actionable Step:* This week, segment your top 50 customers by cash flow health; if 3+ signal budget freezes, activate the hiring freeze and marketing cut immediately.

**Routing Note:**
While this model uses your provided figures, determining the legal implications of layoffs (WARN Act thresholds in Washington State) or restructuring vendor contracts requires immediate consultation with employment counsel and a CPA before executing cuts.

**Leading Indicators to Watch:**
1.  **Payment Delays:** Invoices paid >5 days late often precede cancellation requests by 45 days.
2.  **Seat Utilization:** A drop in active users >10% within a key account predicts down-sells.
3.  **Sales Cycle Length:** If time-to-close extends by >20%, assume the 30% sales drop scenario is active.

**Hiring & Marketing Adjustments:**
*   **Hiring:** Move to "backfill only" status. Any role not directly tied to retaining existing revenue (CS/Support) or critical security/compliance is paused.
*   **Marketing:** Shift 100% of budget to retention campaigns and low-CAC content marketing. Pause all brand-awareness and high-cost paid acquisition channels until LTV:CAC stabilizes above 3:1 under the new churn assumptions.
