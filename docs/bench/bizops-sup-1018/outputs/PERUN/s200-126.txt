**The Call:** Do not pivot to the Medicare Advantage (MA) channel yet; the $75,000 upfront compliance cost and nine-month sales cycle create a cash-flow valley your current burn rate cannot safely bridge without verified contract volume. Stick to the Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) model to extend runway while you secure a Letter of Intent (LOI) from an MA plan that explicitly guarantees volume sufficient to amortize the compliance cost within 12 months of revenue recognition.

**The Numbers:**
*Current Runway:* $90,000 monthly burn implies ~2.2 months of runway if you stop all revenue immediately. With current DTC revenue, we must estimate volume to determine actual runway.
*Assumption:* You did not provide current monthly unit sales. I will assume a base case of **200 units/month** via DTC to illustrate the comparison. *If your actual volume differs, recalculate using the formulas below.*

**Scenario A: DTC Only (Status Quo)**
*   **Unit Margin:** $199 (price) - $15/mo sub (recurring) - $85 (CAC).
    *   *Note:* Hardware margin is negative ($199 - $85 = $114 gross profit on hardware, assuming $0 COGS for this math, which is likely optimistic). If hardware COGS exists, margin shrinks.
    *   *LTV (18-month horizon):* $114 (hardware) + ($15 × 18 months) = $384 per customer.
    *   *Contribution Margin:* $384 - $85 (CAC) = **$299 per customer**.
*   **Monthly Contribution (at 200 units):** 200 × $299 = $59,800.
*   **Net Burn:** $90,000 (burn) - $59,800 (contribution) = **$30,200/month**.
*   **18-Month Capital Need:** $30,200 × 18 = **$543,600**.

**Scenario B: Medicare Advantage Pivot**
*   **Unit Margin:** $140 (plan pay) + ($10 × 18 months) = $320 LTV.
*   **CAC:** $0 (plan sells it), but **$75,000 fixed compliance cost**.
*   **Break-Even Volume on Compliance Cost:** $75,000 / ($320 - DTC Equivalent Variable Cost).
    *   *Comparison:* MA LTV ($320) vs DTC Contribution ($299). MA yields ~$21 more per unit over 18 months *if* CAC is truly zero.
    *   *Units needed to cover $75k:* $75,000 / $21 ≈ **3,572 units** just to match DTC efficiency, ignoring the time value of money and the 9-month delay.
*   **Cash Flow Gap:** You incur $75,000 immediately + 9 months of $90,000 burn ($810,000) before the first dollar of MA revenue arrives.
*   **18-Month Capital Need:** $75,000 (compliance) + $810,000 (burn during delay) - (Revenue months 10-18). Even with high volume, the upfront capital requirement is drastically higher than DTC.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The decision flips only if the MA plan guarantees **minimum annual volumes exceeding 4,000 units** in the first contract year.
*   **Trigger:** Secure a signed LOI with a minimum purchase commitment of 4,000 units/year before spending any of the $75,000.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** If the compliance cost exceeds 20% of your projected first-year gross profit from the channel, the risk is too high for a pre-revenue/startup stage without external non-dilutive funding.
*   **Step this week:** If you cannot get an LOI with volume guarantees from a plan administrator within 30 days, consider pausing the MA track to preserve cash.
