*   *Formula:* (Average Revenue per Free User) − (Average Infrastructure Cost per Free User).
*   *Current Reality:* $0 − ($30,000 / 5,000 users) = **−$6.00 per user/month**.
Every free user currently costs you $6/month directly. You need a conversion rate high enough to cover this drag.

Your current conversion rate is 2.4% (120/5,000).
