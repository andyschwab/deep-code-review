**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The deciding factor is whether a specific state accepts a **blanket resale certificate** (one document covering all future transactions) versus requiring a **transaction-specific certificate** (a new document for every order).
*   **Trigger:** If any of your five target states mandate transaction-specific verification or have strict "due diligence" laws requiring active validation of the buyer's status, your current "collect once" plan fails immediately.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Most states allow blanket certificates for recurring buyers, *but* they require the seller to verify the certificate's validity periodically (e.g., every 3–5 years) and ensure the goods sold match the buyer's business type.
