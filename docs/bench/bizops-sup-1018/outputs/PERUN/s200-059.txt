**The Call**
Do not match the competitor's flat rate broadly; instead, immediately pilot a tiered volume-discount model for your top 10 customers while maintaining the current per-minute rate for the rest. A blanket 15% price cut destroys your contribution margin too aggressively without a guaranteed volume spike, whereas a tiered approach protects yield from smaller users and directly addresses the retention risk of your largest accounts.

**The Numbers**
*Current State:*
Revenue: $100,000 (2M mins × $0.05)
Variable Cost: $40,000 (2M mins × $0.02)
Contribution Margin: $60,000
Fixed Costs: $45,000
Net Cash Flow: +$15,000/month
Runway: $300,000 / $15,000 = **20 months**

*Scenario A: Blanket 15% Price Cut (Price drops to $0.0425)*
Assumption: Volume remains flat at 2M minutes (labeled **ASSUMED: Flat Volume**).
New Revenue: $85,000
Variable Cost: $40,000
Contribution Margin: $45,000
Fixed Costs: $45,000
Net Cash Flow: $0/month
Runway: **Indefinite only if growth is zero; any dip creates immediate burn.**
*Note:* If volume drops 5% due to churn, you burn $2,250/month.

*Scenario B: Tiered Model (Targeted Discount)*
Assumption: Top 10 customers (60% of usage = 1.2M mins) get a 20% discount ($0.04/min); others (0.8M mins) stay at $0.05. Labeled **ASSUMED: 20% discount for top tier**.
New Revenue: (1.2M × $0.04) + (0.8M × $0.05) = $48,000 + $40,000 = $88,000
Variable Cost: $40,000 (unchanged)
Contribution Margin: $48,000
Fixed Costs: $45,000
Net Cash Flow: +$3,000/month
Runway: $300,000 / $3,000 = **100 months** (under flat volume assumption).

*Scenario C: Tiered Model with Volume Uplift*
Assumption: The discount retains the top 10 and attracts 10% more volume from them (1.32M mins), while small users remain stable. Labeled **ASSUMED: 10% volume growth in top tier**.
New Revenue: (1.32M × $0.04) + (0.8M × $0.05) = $52,800 + $40,000 = $92,800
Variable Cost: (2.12M × $0.02) = $42,400
Contribution Margin: $50,400
Net Cash Flow: +$5,400/month
Runway: **55+ months**.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The single input that changes the call is the **churn rate of the top 10 customers**. If you lose even two of these clients (approx. 12% of total volume) because you refuse to discount, revenue drops by ~$12,000, turning your current +$15k cash flow into +$3k, slashing runway to 100 months but risking a death spiral if more leave.
*Trigger:* If any of the top 10 customers request a formal quote review in the next 30 days, activate the tiered discount immediately.
*Rule of Thumb:* Never sacrifice more than 50% of your contribution margin ($30k) to chase volume unless you are certain of >50% volume growth.
*Step this week:* If a top-10 customer hints at leaving, consider offering a provisional 20% discount tied to a 12-month commitment.
