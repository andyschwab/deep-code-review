**The Call:** Immediately run a two-week A/B test pitting your current complex tiers against a simplified three-band usage model; do not roll out a flat-rate change to all 5,000 users until you validate that Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) does not drop below $1,850.

**The Numbers:**
Your current leakage is driven by a 43% drop in conversion (8% → 4.5%) while CAC remains fixed at $1,200.
*   **Current Cost Per Paid Customer:** $1,200 / 0.045 = **$26,666**.
*   **Previous Cost Per Paid Customer:** $1,200 / 0.08 = **$15,000**.
*   **Monthly Waste:** With 20% more traffic but half the conversion, you are acquiring roughly 34 fewer customers than before (assuming a baseline of ~100 signups previously), resulting in ~$40,800 in immediate wasted spend, plus the opportunity cost of lost Lifetime Value (LTV). Your $90k estimate implies a higher traffic volume; if true, the burn is critical.

To justify simplification, the new model must lift conversion back toward 6-7% without slashing ARPU.
*   **Break-even Conversion Rate:** If simplification causes ARPU to drop from a hypothetical $2,500 blend to $2,000, your conversion rate must rise to **5.4%** just to maintain the same revenue efficiency.
*   **ASSUMED (Low):** Simplification lifts conversion to 5% but drops ARPU by 20%. Result: Revenue neutral, cash flow improves slightly due to lower support friction.
*   **ASSUMED (Base):** Simplification lifts conversion to 6.5% with flat ARPU. Result: **$45,000/month recovered** in efficiency.
*   **ASSUMED (High):** Simplification lifts conversion to 8% (restoring baseline) with 10% ARPU uplift due to reduced "sticker shock" on overages. Result: **$120,000/month value creation**.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is **ARPU elasticity**. If the simplified model causes ARPU to dip below **$1,850** (a 26% drop from a hypothetical $2,500 blended average), the increased volume will not cover the loss in margin, accelerating your runway burn despite higher signups.
*   **Trigger Rule:** If the A/B test shows ARPU < $1,850 after 1,000 trial exposures, abort the simplification and revert to tiered pricing with capped overages instead of variable ones.
*   **Action This Week:** If your engineering team can instrument usage bands, consider launching a "Pro Simplified" beta to 10% of incoming traffic tomorrow, tracking only "Signup Completion" and "Estimated First Month Bill."
