**The Call:** Reject the unlimited fixed-fee model as currently structured; it exposes you to catastrophic margin erosion if a single client scales to 1,000 seats. Instead, adopt a "Good-Better-Best" tiered fixed-fee model with a hard cap or overage charges to protect unit economics while offering predictability.

**The Numbers:**
To find the break-even point where Fixed Fee equals Per-Seat revenue, we compare the fixed contract value against the variable per-seat price.
*Formula:* Break-Even Seats = Fixed Fee / Per-Seat Price
*Your Figures:* €45,000 / €150 = **300 seats**.

At 300 seats, both models generate identical revenue. However, profitability diverges immediately after due to your variable costs (€40/seat).
*   **Per-Seat Model Profit:** (€150 - €40) × Seats = €110 × Seats.
*   **Fixed-Fee Model Profit:** €45,000 - (€40 × Seats).

*Scenario Analysis (ASSUMED client growth):*
1.  **Small Client (200 seats):**
    *   Per-Seat Profit: €22,000.
    *   Fixed-Fee Profit: €45,000 - €8,000 = **€37,000**. (Fixed fee wins by €15k).
2.  **Average Client (250 seats):**
    *   Per-Seat Profit: €27,500.
    *   Fixed-Fee Profit: €45,000 - €10,000 = **€35,000**. (Fixed fee wins by €7.5k).
3.  **Break-Even Profit Point (300 seats):**
    *   Per-Seat Profit: €33,000.
    *   Fixed-Fee Profit: €45,000 - €12,000 = **€33,000**. (Equal).
4.  **Large Enterprise (1,000 seats - ASSUMED risk scenario):**
    *   Per-Seat Profit: €110,000.
    *   Fixed-Fee Profit: €45,000 - €40,000 = **€5,000**. (Per-seat wins by €105k; margin collapses to ~11%).

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that flips the recommendation is **client headcount growth beyond 300 employees**.
*Trigger Rule:* If any client's active seat count exceeds 300, the fixed-fee model destroys value compared to per-seat pricing.
*Decision Threshold:* As a rule of thumb, unlimited fixed fees only work if you can contractually guarantee the client stays under 300 seats or if your variable costs drop near zero at scale (which they do not; materials/instructor time remain linear).
*Actionable Step:* If you proceed with fixed fees this week, consider implementing a "Base + Overage" structure: €45,000 covers up to 300 seats, with every additional seat billed at €110 (your net margin rate) or a discounted €120 to share savings, ensuring you never lose money on growth.
