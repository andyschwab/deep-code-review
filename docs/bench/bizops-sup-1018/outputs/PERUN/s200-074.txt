**The Call:** Do not finalize the $200k marketing budget yet; your current $1.2M revenue projection is a "top-down" guess that ignores the critical "monorepo" filter, so you must switch to a "bottom-up" validation method to determine if the reachable market justifies the spend.

**The Numbers:**
Your current formula is: *Total Companies × Capture Rate × ACV = Revenue*.
*   **Total Companies:** 5,000 (Given)
*   **Capture Rate:** 2% (Assumption)
*   **ACV:** $12,000 (Given)
*   **Result:** $1.2M

However, this misses the **Monorepo Prevalence Factor (MPF)**. The corrected formula is:
*Revenue = 5,000 × **MPF** × 2% × $12,000*

Since **MPF** is unknown, we must bracket it with assumptions to see if the budget holds:
*   **Low Case (MPF 10%):** 5,000 × 0.10 × 0.02 × $12,000 = **$120,000** (Budget unjustified; burn rate too high).
*   **Base Case (MPF 30%):** 5,000 × 0.30 × 0.02 × $12,000 = **$360,000** (Budget risky; CAC likely exceeds LTV).
*   **High Case (MPF 60%):** 5,000 × 0.60 × 0.02 × $12,000 = **$720,000** (Budget still aggressive; requires >2% capture or higher ACV).

*Note: Even in the High Case, revenue ($720k) does not cover a $200k marketing budget if your Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) exceeds $6,600 per customer (assuming 60 customers).*

**What Flips It, and The Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is **Monorepo Prevalence (MPF)** among companies with 50+ engineers.
*   **Trigger Value:** If validated MPF is below **45%**, the projected revenue cannot support a $200k marketing spend with a healthy LTV:CAC ratio (assuming standard SaaS margins).
*   **Decision Rule:** If you cannot confirm at least 45% monorepo adoption in your target segment within 30 days, pivot the marketing budget toward expanding the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) to include polyrepo teams with specific sync pain points, or reduce the budget to $50k for experimental channels.
*   **Step this week:** If you have access to 10 engineering leaders in your network, consider asking them directly: "Does your 50+ person team use a single monorepo, multiple repos, or a hybrid model?" to get an immediate directional sample.
