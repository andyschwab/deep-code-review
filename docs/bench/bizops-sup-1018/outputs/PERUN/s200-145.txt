**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The single input that changes the call is the **technical narrative** distinguishing "scientific uncertainty" from "routine application."
- **Trigger:** If your nutritionists are applying established nutritional science to known ingredients, it is routine (0% eligible). If they are experimenting to solve a problem where the outcome is not deducible by a competent professional (e.g., a new delivery mechanism for unstable nutrients), it is R&D.
- **Rule of Thumb:** "Could a competent professional in the field have solved this using existing knowledge?" If yes, it is not R&D.
- **This Week's Step:** If your project logs do not explicitly record failed experiments and hypotheses regarding scientific/technical uncertainty, consider halting the claim preparation until you reconstruct this evidence trail.
