**The Numbers**
To calculate accurate runway, we derive the **Net Cash Contribution per Unit** and subtract fixed costs to find the **Adjusted Monthly Burn**.

*Formula:*
`Net Cash Inflow = (Units Sold × Price × (1 - Return Rate)) - (Units Sold × Return Rate × Reverse Logistics Cost)`
`Adjusted Burn = Fixed Expenses - Net Cash Inflow`
`Runway = Cash on Hand / Adjusted Burn`
