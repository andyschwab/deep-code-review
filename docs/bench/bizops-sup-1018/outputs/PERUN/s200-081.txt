**The Call**
Unbundle professional services immediately; do not raise the base subscription price yet. Your current model subsidizes high-touch onboarding with recurring revenue, destroying unit economics. Treat services as a pass-through cost or a separate profit center to restore margin integrity.

**The Numbers**
*Current State (Annualized per Customer):*
*   **Revenue:** $30,000 ($2,500 × 12).
*   **COGS (Services):** $3,000 (20 hours × $150/hr). *Note: This excludes hosting/infrastructure, focusing only on the service delta.*
*   **Gross Profit (Adjusted):** $27,000 (assuming 100% margin on software portion for simplicity, or $21,600 if applying 72% margin to the whole bucket before service deduction).
*   **Churn:** 3% monthly ≈ 31% annual churn.
*   **Average Customer Lifespan:** ~3.2 years (1 / 0.31).
*   **Lifetime Value (LTV) Impact:** The extra 15 hours of service cost you $2,250 annually. Over a 3.2-year lifespan, this erodes **$7,200** of LTV per customer compared to last year.

*Option Analysis:*
1.  **Unbundle Services (Recommended):** Charge $150–$200/hr for hours exceeding a baseline (e.g., 5 hours).
    *   *Result:* Recovers $2,250/year in lost margin per customer. LTV stabilizes; payback period shortens.
2.  **Raise Base Price to $3,200/mo:** Covers the $2,250 annual gap.
    *   *Result:* Risks increasing churn >3% if the market perceives low software value. Harder to sell to new logos.
3.  **Reduce Included Hours:** Cap at 5 hours, charge overage.
    *   *Result:* Functionally identical to unbundling but requires contract renegotiation.

*Payback Period Shift:*
If your CAC is $15,000 (assumed for enterprise):
*   *Old Model:* Payback ~6 months.
*   *Current Broken Model:* Payback extends to ~9–10 months due to the $2,250 annual drag.
*   *Unbundled Model:* Payback returns to ~6 months.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger**
The **service hours per onboard** is the flip input.
*   **Trigger:** If average onboarding exceeds **8 hours** (where service cost eats >15% of first-year margin), you must enforce unbundling.
*   **Rule of Thumb:** Professional services margin should never subsidize SaaS gross margin below 70%.
*   **Action This Week:** If your next 3 onboardings average >10 hours, send a proposal to those prospects separating "Software License" from "Implementation Services."

**Routing Note**
Review your existing enterprise contracts for "most favored nation" or automatic renewal clauses before changing terms for current clients; consult counsel to ensure the unbundling strategy doesn't trigger breach-of-contract claims with the 45 active clients.
