**The Call:** Do not pass the full €7.50 increase to the customer; the projected 30% volume drop destroys profitability. Instead, adopt a hybrid strategy: absorb approximately €4.00 of the tax (reducing your net margin to ~15%) and pass only €3.50 to the customer, keeping the retail price at €68.50. This minimizes volume churn while preserving positive unit economics. Regarding subscriptions and exemptions: these are jurisdiction-specific determinations that require immediate validation before finalizing any pricing model.

**What Flips It, and the Trigger:**
The critical input is **Price Elasticity of Demand**. If raising the price to €68.50 causes volume to drop by more than **18%**, Scenario B (absorbing cost) becomes superior.
*Trigger:* Run a localized A/B price test in one French region within 14 days. If conversion drops >18% at the €68.50 price point, pivot to absorbing more cost or reducing pack size to stay under the €50 threshold.
