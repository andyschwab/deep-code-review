**Recommendation: Do not proceed with the current plan; execute a capped, time-boxed pilot first.**

The proposal relies on a fatal assumption: a 15% conversion rate (18,000 subscribers) is industrially unprecedented for general news archives without an existing paywall track record. Industry benchmarks for hard paywalls typically range from 1–5%. If conversion lands at a realistic 3%, monthly revenue drops to $43,200 ($518k annually), failing to cover the $750k upfront investment or the loss of $300k in ad revenue. Furthermore, excluding legal compliance costs for GDPR/CCPA creates immediate liability risk when handling user data for payments.

**Main Failure Modes:**
1.  **Conversion Delusion:** Achieving even 5% conversion (6,000 users) yields only $864k/year, resulting in a net loss when combined with ad decay and implementation costs.
2.  **Unpriced Compliance:** Regulatory fines or mandatory legal retrofits could exceed the initial technical budget, halting operations post-launch.
3.  **Churn Cliff:** Without historical churn data, a high exit rate after month three could reduce the subscriber base faster than acquisition can replace it, collapsing the recurring revenue model.

**Kill Criteria & Pivot Trigger:**
Launch a 45-day pilot targeting 5,000 users (not the full base) with a hard cap of $50k on spend.
*   **Kill Condition:** If Month-One recurring revenue is below **$18,000** (implying <1.5% conversion) or if legal counsel identifies unmitigable compliance gaps.
*   **Pivot Action:** If conversion is between 1.5% and 4%, pivot to a "freemium" model (limited free articles) rather than a hard lock.
*   **Leading Metric:** Track "paid conversion rate" weekly; review on Day 30.

**Note:** The current plan assumes zero friction in user behavior and zero regulatory cost; both must be validated before committing capital.
