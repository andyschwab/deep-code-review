**Recommendation: Proceed with a capped, conditional pilot ("Go-If"), not the full launch.**

Approve the $45k site build immediately, but **hold** the $150k marketing spend and the 25% production shift until a 6-week pilot validates unit economics. The current plan contains a fatal margin error: your estimated $12 shipping + $3 packaging = $15 variable cost per unit. On a $250k revenue target with 20% margin ($50k gross profit), you are assuming an average order value (AOV) that likely cannot absorb $15/unit logistics without turning the 20% margin negative. If your average vase sells for $100, logistics alone consume 15% of revenue, leaving only 5% to cover the $8k 3PL contract, marketing, and overhead.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Margin Collapse:** The plan ignores that DTC margins (20%) are lower than wholesale (30%) despite higher revenue potential, because logistics ($15/unit) and customer acquisition costs (CAC) were under-modeled. If CAC exceeds $40 per customer, the $150k campaign will burn cash without reaching the $250k sales target.
2.  **Channel Cannibalization:** Assuming wholesale customers remain "unchanged" while shifting 25% of production is risky. If high-value wholesale buyers discover lower DTC prices or face stockouts due to DTC prioritization, you could lose the stable $1.2M base to chase the risky $250k online revenue.
3.  **Conversion Delusion:** A 15% conversion rate from a "limited beta" is likely inflated compared to cold traffic. If real-world conversion drops to the industry standard of 2–3%, you will need 5–7x more traffic to hit sales targets, exploding the marketing budget.

**Kill/Pivot Criteria:**
After the 6-week pilot (spending max $20k on marketing):
*   **KILL** if: Net contribution margin per unit (Price minus COGS, Shipping, Packaging, and actual CAC) is less than **$10**.
*   **PIVOT** if: Cart abandonment exceeds **70%** (indicating shipping shock) or if wholesale order volume drops by more than **5%** during the pilot.
*   **GO** to full $150k spend only if: The pilot achieves a verified net margin of >15% and wholesale stability holds.

Do not commit the full $150k or shift production volume until these specific unit economics are proven with real money.
