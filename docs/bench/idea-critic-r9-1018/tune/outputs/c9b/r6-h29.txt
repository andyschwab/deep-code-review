**Recommendation: GO-IF**
Proceed with a capped pilot in the **North zone only** starting 1 Oct 2025, limited to the first 500 subscribers. Do not launch across all three zones until the pilot validates that the refund rate remains under 4% and labor costs do not exceed the 4% operational increase assumption.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**
1.  **Adverse Selection & Frequency Spike:** The historical 2.8% refund rate is based on one-off transactions, not a "all-you-can-eat" subscription. Subscribers may strategically book three cleans monthly and claim dissatisfaction on the least perfect visit to effectively receive free services, causing refund costs to skyrocket beyond the 4% buffer.
2.  **Labor Cost Underestimation:** The model assumes only a 4% cost rise, but "deep-clean" visits triggered by dissatisfaction complaints often require double-labor (re-cleaning) and senior staff intervention to manage unhappy premium clients, likely pushing hourly effective costs well above the $13.50 baseline.
3.  **Competitor Price War:** If competitors match the guarantee within 60 days, they may undercut the $49 price point. Since your margin relies on low refund rates, a price war forces you to either absorb losses or break the guarantee promise, damaging brand trust immediately.

**Kill Criterion**
Halt or pivot the program immediately if **refund costs exceed 5% of subscription revenue** or if **average labor hours per subscriber exceed 1.2x the baseline** for any single month during the pilot.

*Note: The projected $882k revenue assumes 15% conversion without accounting for churn if the guarantee is abused; monitor net retention closely.*
