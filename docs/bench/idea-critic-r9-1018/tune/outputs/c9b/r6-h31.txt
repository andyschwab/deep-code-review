**Biggest Backfire Risks:**
1.  **Margin Trap:** If tutor sign-ups rise by less than 10% (below your 15% estimate) while the payment processor fee doubles due to volume spikes, net margins could collapse by over 20%, failing to offset the 8% per-session margin drop.
2.  **Operational Overload:** The unmodeled rise in dispute-resolution costs could erode profits faster than the increased session volume generates them, especially if new, lower-quality tutors enter via the eased barrier.

**Kill Criterion:** Halt the rollout immediately if **net margin dips below 14% for two consecutive months** during the pilot, or if the payment processor fee increase exceeds 50% of the projected incremental revenue.
