**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Discovery Collapse:** The 5% conversion assumption is irrelevant if the demo never reaches enough players. Without a publisher's existing wishlist networks, your $80k marketing buy may yield insufficient traffic to hit the revenue target, regardless of conversion quality.
2.  **Revenue Math Error:** Your $500k gross revenue projection likely ignores platform fees. Steam takes 30%. To net $500k *gross* (before dev costs), you actually need ~$715k in sales. If your $500k figure is pre-fee, your net is only $350k, resulting in a negative ROI once the $250k dev + $80k marketing costs are deducted.
3.  **Cash Flow Mismatch:** Revenue arrives 30–60 days post-sale. With a hard October launch, you will have zero revenue during the critical Q3 marketing spend, creating a liquidity crunch if the $250k budget is strictly for development and not working capital.

**Abort/Pivot Trigger:**
If the free demo (launched June 1) achieves less than **3.5% conversion to wishlist/purchase** (not just views) after **14 days** of paid traffic, immediately halt the self-publishing track and initiate partner discussions.

**Critical Flaws in Numbers & Assumptions:**
*   **The "Gross" vs. "Net" Trap:** A seasoned reviewer will immediately flag the $500k gross revenue claim. If this is top-line sales, Steam's 30% cut leaves $350k. Subtracting $330k in total costs ($250k dev + $80k marketing) leaves only $20k profit, which is an ~6% ROI, not 100%. To achieve 100% ROI on a $330k investment, you need $660k in *profit*, requiring roughly $945k in gross sales. Your current math appears to ignore the platform tax entirely.
*   **Market Growth vs. Share:** A 15% YoY market growth does not guarantee your specific title grows. In a growing market, competition often intensifies faster than demand, diluting individual share.
*   **Hidden Fees:** Beyond the 30% platform cut, budget for payment processing fees (~1-2%), potential refund rates (average 5-10% for sims), and influencer contract overages. These are rarely "hidden" but frequently unallocated in initial budgets.

*Note: The 5% conversion rate from a free demo is aggressive for a space-sim; industry average often hovers closer to 2-3% for niche genres. Ensure your $80k marketing budget is weighted heavily toward pre-launch wishlists, not just day-one visibility.*
