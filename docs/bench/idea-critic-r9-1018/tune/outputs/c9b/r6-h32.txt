**Three Biggest Failure Points**

Industry standard for niche subscription boxes is 1–3% of an existing list; even a heroic 5% yields only 250 members. Banking on 30% without a pre-launch waitlist validation will result in wasted ad spend and insufficient sign-ups to cover fixed costs.
2.  **The Shipping Math Gap:** Your P&L assumes $9,000 revenue - $8,000 ads = $1,000 profit, implying zero cost for the plant, pot, soil, box, labor, and shipping. If the "unknown" shipping/packaging cost is even $15/unit (conservative for live plants), fulfilling 200 boxes costs $3,000, turning your $1,000 profit into a $2,000 loss before counting inventory costs.
