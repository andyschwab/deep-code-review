**Recommendation: Proceed with a capped, six-month pilot (Go-If).**
Launch the advisory desk only if regulatory clearance is confirmed within 10 weeks and total upfront capital expenditure is capped at $95,000 (covering lease, marketing, and partial staffing). Do not hire both senior advisors immediately; start with one senior lead and one junior associate to reduce fixed burn while validating the conversion model.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Conversion Overestimation:** The 12% conversion assumption (120 clients) relies on existing loan customers wanting *advisory* services, not just capital. If the actual adoption rate is below 6% (60 clients), revenue drops to ~$174,000, failing to cover the $180,000+ in fixed staff and lease costs, resulting in an immediate operational loss.
2.  **Regulatory & Timeline Drag:** The 8–12 week clearance window is a critical path risk. If clearance slips to 16 weeks, lease costs accrue with zero revenue, and the $20,000 marketing spend loses potency due to delayed launch momentum, eroding the first-year ROI by approximately 15%.
3.  **Hidden Compliance Burden:** The proposal explicitly excludes compliance costs. If regulatory requirements mandate specialized reporting or insurance not budgeted, the margin per client ($2,900) could be negated entirely, turning every new client into a net loss rather than a profit center.

**Pre-Agreed Stop/Pivot Trigger:**
If the desk acquires fewer than **40 paying advisory clients** by the end of Month 6, immediately halt the second senior hire and pivot the model to a virtual-only advisory service using existing staff, terminating the physical lease upon expiration of the initial term.
