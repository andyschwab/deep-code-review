**Recommendation: Proceed with a capped, time-boxed pilot (Go-If).**

Launch the refrigerated service immediately, but restrict the rollout to **one** van instead of three. This reduces upfront capital exposure from $150,000 to $50,000 while validating the operational model. The unit economics are viable only if the 30% volume lift (360 parcels) is achieved immediately; at current projections, the margin is dangerously thin before accounting for the unknown insurance increase. By deploying a single vehicle, you preserve $100,000 in cash to absorb higher-than-expected insurance premiums or maintenance costs without jeopardizing the core ambient business.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Insurance Shock:** The premium increase for temperature-sensitive cargo exceeds the projected net margin, turning the 5% target into a loss.
2.  **Volume Miss:** Hospitals do not switch vendors quickly enough, leaving the dedicated refrigerated fleet underutilized while fixed maintenance costs ($5k/month) continue to burn cash.
3.  **Temperature Breach:** A single cooling failure damages a medical shipment, creating liability that outweighs the revenue of the entire pilot.

**Pre-Agreed Stop/Pivot Trigger:**
Execute a hard stop or pivot if, after the first quarter (90 days), either of the following conditions is met:
*   Refrigerated volume is **under 600 parcels per month** (averaged over the quarter).
*   Net margin falls **below 5%** after accounting for actual insurance and maintenance costs.

If triggered, immediately list the van(s) for resale and revert to ambient-only operations.
