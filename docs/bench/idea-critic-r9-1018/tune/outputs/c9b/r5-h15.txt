1.  **Logistics Bankruptcy in Remote Zones:** If remote pickup costs are truly 30% higher than average, the $49 shipping fee will not cover expenses in these areas, turning every remote sale into a net loss despite the high order value.
2.  **Conversion Overestimation:** Assuming a 15% conversion rate (up from the pilot's 12%) without validating price sensitivity against the competitor's $30 shipping could result in significantly lower revenue, failing to cover the $200k marketing spend.
3.  **Cash Flow Mismatch:** The $5,000/month logistics budget is likely insufficient if pickup volume spikes early; if the $49 fee doesn't fully cover variable costs upfront, you may face a cash crunch before conversion revenue realizes.

If **conversion falls below 11%** or **average pickup cost per unit exceeds $55** after the first 2,500 sign-ups (approx. 3 weeks), immediately **pause nationwide expansion** and pivot to a zone-restricted model excluding high-cost remote ZIPs.
