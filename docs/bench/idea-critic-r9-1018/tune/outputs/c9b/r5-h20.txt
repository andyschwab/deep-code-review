**Main Failure Modes:**

2.  **Operational Bottleneck:** Achieving 24-hour turnaround requires more than faster machines; it demands immediate lab technician handoff. If your current staff cannot pivot instantly, the "premium" promise fails regardless of machine speed.
3.  **Cannibalization:** Existing customers switch from the $180 to the $250 option without new volume growth, inflating revenue but failing to cover the massive fixed cost of the new machines.

**Kill Criteria:**
Terminate the project if, after **two months** of operation, the **net new volume** (excluding cannibalized orders) does not increase by at least **5% month-over-month** or if **verified insurance reimbursement** is not secured for 60% of your target provider network within the first 30 days.
