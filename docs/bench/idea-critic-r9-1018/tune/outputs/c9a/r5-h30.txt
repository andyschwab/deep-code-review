The financial model contains a critical contradiction that invalidates the revenue projection. If the $6,750 figure is the realistic target, it implies an effective collection rate of only ~33% (due to unemployment, underemployment, or defaults), not the 80% success rate assumed. This threatens operational solvency before accounting for increased administrative costs.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Revenue Collapse:** The discrepancy between the calculated $20,250 and the cited $6,750 suggests unmodeled default rates or salary floors that drastically reduce yield.
2.  **Cash Flow Mismatch:** Upfront costs (instruction, facilities) must be paid immediately, while ISA revenue trickles in over three years, creating a working capital hole not covered by the reduced upfront tuition mix.
3.  **Regulatory & Legal Risk:** ISAs face evolving scrutiny regarding usury laws and consumer protection; a pilot without legal counsel approval risks future litigation or forced restructuring.

**Stop/Pivot Trigger:**
