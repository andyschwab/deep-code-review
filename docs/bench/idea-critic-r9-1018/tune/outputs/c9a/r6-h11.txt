**Recommendation: Delay**

Do not authorize the full fleet replacement yet. Proceed only after securing a fixed-price quote for the 240V electrical upgrades and validating the first-year energy consumption on a two-mower pilot. The current proposal lacks the critical installation cost data required to calculate true ROI, and the five-year battery life assumption carries unquantified risk beyond year three.

**Top Three Failure Modes**
1.  **Hidden Infrastructure Costs:** Site-specific electrical upgrades (transformers, trenching, panel swaps) could exceed hardware costs, destroying the payback period.
2.  **Battery Degradation:** If capacity drops below 80% before year three, replacement costs (~$4,000/unit) would erase accumulated operational savings.
3.  **Utility Rate Volatility:** A rise in commercial electricity rates above $0.22/kWh or the loss of off-peak pricing tiers would significantly narrow the projected $8,520 annual savings per unit.

**Kill Criteria (Stop/Change Triggers)**
*   **Installation Cost:** If total site upgrade quotes exceed **$25,000**, halt the project for re-evaluation.
*   **Energy Pricing:** If verified blended electricity costs exceed **$0.20/kWh** during the pilot, suspend further rollout.
*   **Battery Health:** If pilot units show >15% capacity loss or charging time increases by >20% within the first **18 months**, terminate the contract and revert to gas.

*Note: The current $136,800 hardware estimate excludes installation, making the "go" decision financially unsupported. A small pilot mitigates the battery degradation unknown before committing capital.*
