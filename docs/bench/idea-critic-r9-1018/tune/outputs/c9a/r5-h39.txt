**Recommendation: Proceed with a conditional "Go-If" pilot, not a full launch commitment.**
Do not authorize the $4.5M capital outlay yet. Proceed only if the Q4 2025 pilot validates that logistics costs do not exceed $18/unit and confirmed demand from apartment renters reaches at least 40,000 units annually. Your current revenue math ($30M) is correct, but the plan relies on unverified switching behavior and incomplete cost modeling that could erase the projected $14.4M gross margin.

**Top Three Failure Modes:**

1.  **Cannibalization Without Net Growth:** Your model assumes 30% of existing customers will switch to flat-pack. If these customers simply trade down from the $250 assembled unit to the $250 flat-pack (which costs you less to make but sells for the same price), you gain no new revenue while risking brand dilution. Worse, if the switch rate is high but *new* apartment-renter acquisition is low (due to lack of market research), you effectively shrink your total addressable market by alienating customers who value assembly services, failing to hit the 120,000 unit volume target.
2.  **Logistics and Waste Cost Overruns:** You compared your $130 production cost to Competitor X's price but ignored their lower shipping fees. Flat-pack savings often vanish in last-mile delivery complexity and damage rates. If real logistics costs exceed $20/unit or MDF waste disposal fees are higher than modeled, your unit margin collapses from $120 to below $80, extending the break-even date well past Q3 2027 and missing the 18% ROI target.
3.  **Supply Chain Fragility:** The plan depends on veneer sheets with known disruption risks. A 10% shortage in veneer supply halts the high-volume flat-pack line entirely, whereas your current assembled line might have more buffer. Without a secondary supplier or inventory buffer, a single supply shock stops revenue generation while the $4.5M CNC equipment sits idle, accruing depreciation without output.

**Kill Criteria (Tripwires):**

*   **Pilot Unit Cost > $155:** If the fully loaded cost (production + logistics + waste) per unit in the Q4 2025 pilot exceeds $155, terminate the project. This leaves insufficient margin to cover the capital payback within 3 years.
*   **Confirmed Pre-orders < 45,000 Units:** If the pilot fails to secure binding commitments or pre-orders for at least 45,000 units from the target apartment demographic by Q1 2026, halt the full launch. This indicates the 120,000 unit annual target is unrealistic without acquiring entirely new customer segments you haven't validated.
*   **Cannibalization Rate > 40% with Zero Net New Customers:** If more than 40% of flat-pack sales come from existing customers who would have bought assembled units, and net new customer acquisition is below 5,000 units, kill the project. This proves the line is merely a costly substitution, not an expansion.
