**Recommendation: GO-IF**
Proceed with a limited pilot in the **North zone only** starting 1 Oct 2025, rather than a full three-zone launch. Cap the pilot at 500 subscribers (approx. 28% of your projected conversion) to validate the "deep-clean" refund behavior before scaling. A full launch assumes the 2.8% refund rate will hold for a recurring, high-frequency service, but historical data from one-off guarantees is likely a dangerous underestimate for a subscription model where users can strategically cycle visits. If the pilot confirms costs remain under control, expand to East and West in Q1 2026.

**Top Three Failure Modes**
1.  **Refund Rate Explosion:** The 2.8% historical rate fails because subscribers learn to game the system (e.g., using the "100% satisfaction" clause to get free cleans every month), turning the refund liability from $24k/year to over $400k/year, wiping out all margin.
2.  **Labor Cost Underestimation:** The 4% operating cost increase ignores the administrative burden of processing refunds, re-cleaning, and managing churn, which typically scales non-linearly with dispute volume, eroding the $13.50/hr labor efficiency.
3.  **Competitor Price War:** If a competitor launches a similar guarantee at $39/month within 60 days, your 15% conversion assumption collapses, leaving you with high fixed labor costs and insufficient revenue density in the North zone.

**Kill Criterion**
Halt or pivot the program immediately if **refund costs exceed 3.5% of subscription revenue** in the North zone pilot for any single month, or if **customer acquisition cost (CAC) rises by more than 20%** due to competitive response within the first 90 days.

*Note: Your current revenue model ($882k) appears to assume 100% retention and no prorated cancellations; stress-test the cash flow impact if 10% of subscribers churn monthly.*
*Ensure your terms of service explicitly define "100% satisfaction" to prevent subjective abuse (e.g., weather-related outdoor cleaning issues).*
