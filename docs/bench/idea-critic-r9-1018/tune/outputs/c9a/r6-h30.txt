**Recommendation: Do not proceed in current form; execute a "Go-If" pilot.**

The proposal relies on a fatal assumption: a 15% conversion rate on a hard paywall. Industry benchmarks for local news archives typically range from 1–3%, not 15%. Your model projects $2.9M annually based on 20,000 subscribers; at a realistic 2% conversion (2,400 users), revenue drops to ~$345k/year. This fails to cover the $750k upfront cost, let alone ongoing operations, while simultaneously sacrificing $300k in ad revenue. The math only works if your content is uniquely indispensable, which remains unproven.

**Main Failure Modes:**
1.  **Conversion Delusion:** The 15% target is unsupported by data. If actual conversion is <5%, the initiative generates negative cash flow immediately.
2.  **Unpriced Liability:** Excluding GDPR/CCPA compliance costs is dangerous. Retroactively gating user data or managing right-to-be-forgotten requests for archived content could incur legal fines exceeding the project's budget.
3.  **Churn Cliff:** With no churn data, the model assumes static revenue. If 50% of early adopters cancel after month three (common for one-off research needs), the recurring revenue base collapses before breaking even.
4.  **Resource Bottleneck:** Three developers handling integration, digitization, and compliance without external support risks delaying launch, increasing the "cost of delay" while burning salary capital.

**Kill Criteria & Pivot Triggers:**
Launch a limited beta to 5% of traffic first. Proceed to full rollout **only if**:
*   **Metric:** Beta conversion rate exceeds 4% (not 15%) AND month-one net revenue (subscriptions minus lost ads) exceeds $40k.
*   **Tripwire:** If month-one net revenue falls below $20k, **kill** the hard paywall immediately.
*   **Pivot Condition:** If traffic drops >30% but conversion is <2%, pivot to a "freemium" model (recent articles free, deep archive paid) or a donation-supported archive rather than a hard gate.

*Note: You must obtain a legal cost estimate for GDPR/CCPA before any code is written; this is a potential showstopper independent of revenue.*
