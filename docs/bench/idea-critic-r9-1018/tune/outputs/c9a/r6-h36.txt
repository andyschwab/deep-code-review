**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" conditional approval, not a full immediate rollout.**

Approve the procurement and initial installation only if the project is structured as a phased rollout with a hard stop mechanism. The current proposal relies on unverified assumptions regarding pipe-age variance and hidden labor costs that threaten to erase the projected net benefit. Specifically, the omission of the estimated $150k annual labor cost reduces the first-year net benefit from $1.1M ($1.4M - $300k) to roughly $950k, extending the payback period significantly. Furthermore, assuming a uniform 8% reduction across districts with varying infrastructure ages is a critical vulnerability; older districts may see higher leak detection but also disproportionately higher false positives or repair complexities.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Labor Cost Overrun:** If the false-positive rate hits the vendor's upper bound (6%) rather than the optimistic lower bound, field crew costs could exceed the $150k estimate, potentially consuming the entire margin generated by the premium alert package.
2.  **Adoption Shortfall:** The financial model hinges on 5% adoption of the premium package. If actual adoption falls to 3%, revenue drops by 40%, making the data-service fees disproportionate to the value gained.
3.  **Infrastructure Variance:** If the 8% loss reduction does not hold in older districts due to non-leak related water loss (e.g., metering errors vs. actual leaks), the core value proposition of "avoided treatment costs" fails to materialize in those zones.

**Pre-Agreed Kill Criteria:**
Establish a review gate after the first 10,000 meters are installed (approx. Month 3). The project must be paused or pivoted if:
*   **False-Positive Rate:** Verified field data shows a false-positive alert rate exceeding **3.5%** (midpoint of vendor range, weighted for labor cost sensitivity).
*   **ROI Threshold:** The calculated annualized ROI based on actual adoption and labor costs falls below **4%** (adjusted from your 3% suggestion to account for the newly identified labor risk).
*   **Adoption Rate:** Premium package uptake is less than **3.5%** after the initial marketing push in the first phase.

If any of these thresholds are breached, halt further installation and renegotiate the data-service fee structure or shift to a targeted rollout only in districts with verified pipe conditions matching the pilot parameters.
