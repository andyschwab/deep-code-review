**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" pilot, not a full launch.**
Approve the hiring and lease only if a binding pre-commitment secures regulatory clearance within 10 weeks and a signed letter of intent from at least 50 existing loan customers confirms interest before any offer letter is issued. Do not commit the full $180,000+ annual burn based solely on the 12% conversion assumption without these two leading indicators.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Regulatory Deadlock:** The 8–12 week clearance estimate slips to 6+ months due to undefined compliance scope, burning cash on salaries and rent while advisors sit idle, destroying the first-year ROI before the first client is seen.
2.  **Conversion Illusion:** The 12% conversion (120 clients) relies on "existing loan customers" wanting *advisory* services rather than just capital; if the actual adoption is below 5% (50 clients), revenue drops to ~$145,000, failing to cover the $180,000 in direct costs plus the unquantified compliance overhead.
3.  **Unit Economics Erosion:** The $2,900 revenue per client assumes low touch-time; if senior advisors require 4x the expected hours to navigate complex small-business needs, the effective hourly yield drops below the $75,000 salary cost, making every additional client a loss leader.

**Pre-Agreed Stop/Pivot Trigger:**
If the desk acquires fewer than **40 paying advisory clients** by the end of Month 6, immediately freeze hiring for the second advisor, sublease the excess office space, and pivot the remaining advisor to a remote-only, transaction-based model rather than a dedicated physical desk.

*Note: The current model excludes the cost of compliance labor and potential regulatory fines, which could materially alter the break-even point. The 12% conversion rate must be validated against a small sample before scaling to 1,000 customers.*
