**Recommendation: Proceed with a capped, six-month pilot (Go-If).**
Launch the desk only if regulatory clearance is confirmed within 10 weeks and total upfront capital (lease, hiring, marketing) is capped at $115,000. Do not commit to the full 12-month lease or second hire until the pilot validates demand.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Conversion Overestimation:** The 12% conversion rate (120 clients) assumes existing loan customers want advisory services. If actual adoption is half that (6%), revenue drops to $174,000, failing to cover the $200,000+ in fixed staff and lease costs, resulting in an immediate operational loss.
2.  **Regulatory & Compliance Drag:** The 8–12 week clearance window is uncosted. If clearance slips to 16 weeks, you incur ~$58,000 in rent and salaries with zero revenue, extending the break-even point beyond the first year and burning the marketing budget before launch.
3.  **Unit Economics Erosion:** The $2,900 revenue per client assumes high-margin advisory work. If complex small-business cases require more senior advisor hours than anticipated, the effective hourly rate may drop below the cost of delivery, making every additional client a net loss despite hitting the volume target.

**Pre-Agreed Kill Trigger:**
Stop or pivot the initiative if **fewer than 35 paying advisory clients** are secured by the end of Month 6. This threshold accounts for a slower ramp while ensuring the path to covering the $180,000 annual fixed costs remains viable. If this metric is not met, terminate the lease at the earliest break clause and redeploy the advisors to existing loan operations.
