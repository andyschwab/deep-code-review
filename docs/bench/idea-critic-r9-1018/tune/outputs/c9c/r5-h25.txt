**Recommendation: Proceed only as a capped, time-boxed pilot (Go-If), not a full launch.**

Do not commit to the July 1 full-scale launch or the $195,000 total spend yet. Instead, authorize a **6-week pilot** starting July 1 with a hard budget cap of **$60,000** (covering initial production and a reduced marketing test). The condition to proceed to the full six-month plan is achieving **2,500 active subscribers** by August 15 with a verified **content licensing cost under $1.50 per subscriber**. This approach validates the missing licensing variable and the aggressive 30% conversion assumption before exposing the organization to the full financial risk.

**Three Most Likely Failure Modes:**

1.  **The Licensing Margin Crush:** You have budgeted $0.02/minute for bandwidth but **$0 for licensing**. If rights holders demand standard streaming royalties (often 15–20% of revenue or a flat fee per view), your unit economics flip immediately. At $9.99/month, even a $2.00 per subscriber licensing fee destroys nearly 20% of your top line before paying for platform or marketing, making the 12,000-subscriber target financially insufficient to cover the $150k production cost.
2.  **The Conversion Fantasy:** The plan assumes converting 30% of 8,000 in-person buyers (2,400 subscribers) plus significant new growth to hit 12,000. In-person theatergoers often value live liveness over digital archives; a 30% conversion rate is exceptionally high for digital add-ons (industry standard is often 2–5%). If conversion lands at a realistic 5%, you secure only 400 foundational subscribers, requiring impossible viral growth to hit your target, leaving the $45k marketing spend wasted on trying to bridge an unrealistic gap.
3.  **Churn Acceleration:** Relying on a 5% monthly churn rate is dangerous for niche content. If the "regional theatre" audience treats the pass as a "watch once then cancel" service for specific hits, churn could spike to 15–20% after the first quarter. High churn combined with low conversion creates a "leaky bucket" where marketing spend only maintains flatline growth rather than compounding toward 12,000.

**Pre-Agreed Kill Condition:**

**Stop the initiative on September 1, 2024, if:**
*   **Net New Subscribers < 1,500** AND **Total Content Licensing Costs > 15% of Gross Revenue.**

If both conditions are met, the unit economics are proven broken for this model, and the remaining budget must be reverted to live production reserves.
