**Recommendation: Proceed with a capped, time-boxed pilot (Go-If), not a full immediate rollout.**

Launch the 12% commission rate on July 1 only for new tutors in two specific geographic markets for 90 days. Do not apply the cut to existing tutors or all regions yet. This tests the 15% acquisition lift and 8% volume increase assumptions while limiting exposure to the unmodeled support costs and the potentially doubling $120k payment processor fee.

**Biggest Risks of Backfire:**
1.  **Margin Collapse:** If tutor sign-ups rise <10% or session volume rises <5%, the 8% per-session margin drop will not be offset, causing an immediate net-margin dip.
2.  **Cost Spike:** A volume surge could trigger the payment processor fee to double to $240k annually, eroding profits faster than anticipated if support costs also scale linearly with user count.
3.  **False Elasticity:** The core assumption that tutor price elasticity mirrors student elasticity is unverified; tutors may not respond to the fee cut as predicted, leaving you with lower revenue and no growth.

**Kill Criteria:**
Halt the rollout and revert to 20% if **net margin falls below 12% for two consecutive months** during the pilot, or if the payment processor notifies us in writing that the annual fee will exceed $180k based on projected pilot volume.

*Note: The "unknown" support costs must be tracked weekly during the pilot; do not wait for quarterly reviews to assess operational strain.*
*Ensure the pilot contract with the payment processor explicitly caps fee adjustments during the test window.*
