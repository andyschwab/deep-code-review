**Recommendation: Proceed only as a capped, time-boxed pilot (Go-If), not a full launch.**
Do not commit to the March 1st full-scale launch. Instead, treat Q1 as a "validation sprint" capped at 200 members (not 500) to verify the shipping variable and churn assumptions before scaling capital expenditure. The current plan risks negative unit economics if shipping exceeds $2.50/box or if churn remains above 5%.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Shipping Cost Variance:** Your model assumes a fixed $1.50 shipping cost, but "mixed-size parcels" and variable distances often spike rates 40–60% higher than base quotes. If actual shipping hits $2.40, your margin collapses from $5.50 to ~$4.60 per unit, threatening viability at lower volumes.
2.  **Churn Underestimation:** Assuming churn drops from 30% to <5% after three months is aggressive for a paid book club. Industry benchmarks for niche subscriptions often stabilize between 8–12%. If churn stays at 10%, your net growth stalls despite acquisition efforts, inflating your Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) payback period indefinitely.
3.  **Author Chat Logistics vs. Value:** Virtual author chats are high-effort coordination points. If author cancellations occur or attendance is low (<20% of members), the perceived value of the $15 fee drops, directly accelerating churn. This operational risk is unpriced in your current budget.

**Pre-Agreed Stop/Pivot Trigger:**
Stop or pivot if, by the end of Month 2, the **blended cost per shipped box exceeds $2.25** OR if **net member growth is negative** (new sign-ups minus churn) for two consecutive weeks. This forces a decision before the $3/book wholesale inventory commitment becomes sunk for 800 users.

**Arithmetic & Logic Slips:**
*   **Margin Math:** Revenue ($15) - Books ($3) - Packaging/Shipping ($1.50) = **$10.50 gross margin per user**, not $5.50 (if you previously calculated lower). However, this ignores payment processing fees (~3% + $0.30 = ~$0.75), bringing real margin to ~$9.75.
*   **Volume Scaling:** Scaling from 500 to 800 members by Month 6 requires a net addition of 50 members/month *after* churn. With a 5% monthly churn on a growing base, you need ~60–70 new sales/month just to hit 800, not a simple linear add.
*   **Date Check:** Launching March 1st, 2025, means Q1 ends March 31st. You cannot have "first quarter" data to prove churn stabilization before you need to order books for April. Your "30% then 5%" assumption is unverified by the time you must spend cash for Month 2 inventory.

*Note: Secure a hard cap on shipping rates with your carrier before ordering any inventory. Validate the "author chat" value proposition with a pre-launch survey to ensure it drives sign-ups rather than just being a cost center.*
