**Main Failure Modes:**
1.  **Supplier Capacity Illusion:** The assumption that fabric capacity scales linearly from 80k to 150k yards without cost is unverified; if the supplier hits a bottleneck or demands price hikes, lead times will blow out beyond the 7-day promise, triggering the unpriced $3 surcharge on >30% of orders and eroding the projected $250k savings.

3.  **Surcharge Underpricing:** If the actual operational cost to expedite the 30% of late orders exceeds the passed-on $3 fee due to complex logistics, the unit economics will flip from profitable to loss-making per transaction.

**Kill Criteria (Stop/Pivot Triggers):**
*   **Stop immediately** if, during the 90-day pilot, the **order fill rate within 7 days drops below 85%** (indicating the supplier cannot meet linear scaling or logistics are failing).
*   **Pivot or Halt** if the **measured churn rate among pilot customers exceeds 4%** (annualized) or if the **net cost per unit (including surcharges) exceeds the current MTO model projection by more than $1.50**.
