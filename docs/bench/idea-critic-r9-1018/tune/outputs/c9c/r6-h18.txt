**Recommendation: Proceed with a Hybrid Approach (Self-Publish with Contingency)**
Do not commit the full $80,000 marketing budget upfront. Instead, allocate $20,000 for the June demo launch to validate conversion, holding the remaining $60,000 in reserve. This preserves cash flow while testing the core assumption before the October full release.

**Three Biggest Failure Points**
1.  **Conversion Collapse:** The 5% demo-to-buy conversion is optimistic; industry average for space sims often hovers near 1–2%. If traffic is high but sales are low, the revenue model breaks immediately.
2.  **Discovery Blackout:** Without a publisher's existing relationships, securing premium Steam featuring or influencer coverage is difficult. The $80,000 may burn quickly on paid ads with diminishing returns if organic discovery fails.
3.  **Revenue Math Error:** Projecting $500,000 gross revenue requires selling ~25,000 units at $20. After Steam's 30% cut, net revenue is $350,000. Against a $250,000 dev + $80,000 marketing spend ($330,000 total), your net profit is only $20,000, not the $250,000 implied by a "100% ROI."

**Abort/Pivot Triggers**
*   **Trigger 1:** Demo conversion rate is below **2.5%** after two weeks of live data (June 1–15, 2025). *Action: Pivot to seeking a publishing partner for marketing support.*
*   **Trigger 2:** Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) on paid ads exceeds **$15** during the demo phase. *Action: Halt paid ad spend immediately.*
*   **Trigger 3:** Wishlist-to-sale ratio on launch day is below **10%**. *Action: Activate contingency plan to reduce post-launch content scope to preserve runway.*

**Critical Flaws in Numbers & Assumptions**
*   **ROI Miscalculation:** You claimed a 100% ROI ($250k profit on $250k spend). However, if Gross Revenue is $500k, Steam takes $150k (30%), leaving $350k net. Total costs are $330k ($250k dev + $80k marketing). Your actual profit is $20k, an **8% ROI**, not 100%. To achieve 100% ROI (netting $580k), you need ~$830k in gross revenue, requiring ~41,500 unit sales.
*   **Market Growth Reliance:** Basing revenue on a "15% YoY market growth" assumption is dangerous. Revenue depends on *your* share of the market, not the total market size. A growing tide does not guarantee your ship floats if visibility is poor.
*   **Hidden Fees:** You noted "hidden platform fees" as an unknown. Beyond Steam's 30%, account for payment processing fees (~1-2%), potential refund rates (typically 5-10% for early access), and VAT/taxes which may reduce net further depending on your corporate structure.
