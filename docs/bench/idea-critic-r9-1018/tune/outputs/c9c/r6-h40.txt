**Top Three Failure Modes:**
1.  **Regulatory & Supply Chain Halt:** Unpriced customs delays for battery packs or inability to secure aviation insurance grounded the fleet, rendering the $800k software integration and drone procurement a sunk cost before operations begin.
2.  **Operational Throughput Collapse:** Actual weather windows fall below the 80% assumption or maintenance costs exceed the $15k/month/drone estimate (likely given remote salt-air corrosion), causing the cost-per-delivery to surpass current road freight rates.
3.  **Capacity Mismatch:** The 150kg payload limit per sortie proves insufficient for urgent safety kits or bulky spare parts, forcing dual flights that double energy consumption and halve effective range, breaking the economic model.

Terminate or fundamentally re-scope the pilot if, by **31 August 2025**, the fully loaded operating cost (including amortized hardware, software, maintenance, and actual insurance) exceeds **$45,000 per week** while achieving fewer than **40 successful deliveries**.
