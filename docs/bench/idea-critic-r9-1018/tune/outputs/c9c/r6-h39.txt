**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **capped, time-boxed pilot** targeting 1,250 passes (25% of goal) by March 31, 2027, with a hard marketing spend cap of $37,500. Do not commit to the full $150k campaign or 5,000-pass goal until this pilot validates the conversion rate and attendance frequency.

**Critical Flaws in Your Numbers:**
1.  **Revenue Math Error:** You stated 5,000 passes generate $600,000, but 5,000 × $120 = **$600,000**. However, your later calculation says "3 × $120 × 5,000 = $1.8M." This is double-counting. The revenue is **$600,000 total**, not per show. If a patron attends 3 shows, the theater still only receives the single $120 pass fee.
2.  **Break-Even Miscalculation:** With $600,000 total revenue and fixed production costs of $8,000/show, you need to know the total number of shows to calculate true break-even. If you produce 20 shows, costs are $160,000. Profit would be $440,000 (ignoring marketing). Your claim that break-even is 3,200 passes implies a cost structure not supported by the $8,000/show figure alone unless you are assuming variable costs per attendee which were not listed.
3.  **Conversion Assumption:** A 10% conversion (5,000 sales) from a cold/warm email list for a *new* $120 product is highly optimistic. Industry standard for cold/warm email is often 1-3%. At 2% conversion, you sell 1,000 passes ($120k revenue), likely failing to cover the $150k marketing budget alone.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Cannibalization:** Existing patrons who would have paid $50 × 3 = $150 switch to the $120 pass, reducing average revenue per head by 20% without bringing in new volume.
2.  **Over-Attendance:** If pass holders attend 5+ shows instead of the projected 3, marginal costs (seating, staffing, utilities) may spike without additional revenue, eroding margins.
3.  **Marketing Burn:** The $150k budget is exhausted before reaching the 3,200 break-even volume if the conversion rate stays below 4%.

**Kill Criteria:**
Terminate the program immediately if, after 90 days: (1) Net profit (Pass Revenue minus Marketing Spend minus Production Costs) is negative, AND (2) Verified email conversion rate is below 3%.
