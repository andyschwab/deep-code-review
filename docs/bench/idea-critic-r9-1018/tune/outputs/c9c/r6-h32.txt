**Recommendation: GO-IF**
Proceed with the pilot only if you immediately cap the ad spend at $2,000 and secure a firm shipping quote before June 1. Do not commit the full $8,000 or launch until the per-box cost is known, as the current math guarantees a loss if shipping exceeds $17.50 per unit.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**
1.  **Arithmetic & Margin Collapse:** Your projection assumes $1,000 profit on $9,000 revenue after $8,000 ad spend, leaving only $1,000 to cover COGS (plants, pots, soil) and shipping for 200 boxes. This implies a total fulfillment budget of $5 per box. Given typical plant costs, this is impossible; if fulfillment costs $20/box (a conservative estimate), you will lose $3,000 in Q3 alone, not make a profit.
2.  **Conversion Delusion:** You assume 30% of existing customers (1,500 people) would sign up, yet you are only targeting 200. If your conversion rate is actually 4% (a more realistic cold/warm list average), you will fail to reach the 200-member goal even with the full 5,000 list, rendering the revenue projection invalid.
3.  **Operational Blind Spot:** The "unknown" packaging and shipping cost is not a minor variable; it is the primary driver of unit economics. Launching without this figure means you are betting on a business model that may be negative by definition. Additionally, a 1,200 sq ft greenhouse has limited climate control; scaling to 200 unique monthly shipments may create logistical bottlenecks in picking and packing that delay shipments, increasing churn immediately.

**Pre-Agreed Halt Trigger**
Halt the program immediately if the **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)** exceeds $25 or if the **fully loaded cost per box** (plant + packaging + shipping + labor) exceeds $22.50. At $22.50 cost and $45 revenue, you have $22.50 gross margin; if CPA is $25, you lose money on every subscriber before accounting for overhead.
