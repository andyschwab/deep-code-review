1.  **Negative ROI Baseline:** Even with perfect execution, the service costs 3x more than the maximum projected revenue lift.

Your suggested trigger (net lift <3% after six months) is too lenient and too late. The fundamental error is treating a $54,000 fixed cost as viable against a $18,000 maximum variable gain. The project loses money even in the "success" scenario you described.
