**Recommendation:** Proceed with a capped, time-boxed pilot launch in 40 stores (not 120) by Q1 2025 to validate distribution capacity and cannibalization rates before committing the full $250k budget or targeting the June 1 date.

**Top Three Failure Modes:**
1.  **Distribution Bottleneck:** Your current partner cannot absorb a 40% volume increase without service degradation to existing flagship lager accounts, causing net revenue loss.
2.  **Cannibalization Overhang:** The "Summer Sunset" blend steals more than 30% of its sales from higher-margin keg accounts in the same regions, resulting in a net margin decline despite the higher unit price.
3.  **Margin Illusion:** The projected 20% higher margin evaporates because cocktail-specific compliance, packaging, or slotting fees were excluded from the $8 unit cost basis.

**Kill/Pivot Criteria:**
Pull the plug if the pilot shows net margin growth of less than 5% after 90 days or if the distribution partner fails to meet 95% on-time fill rates for the pilot stores. Pivot to a direct-to-consumer model if retail partner capacity is confirmed as the blocking constraint.

*Note: The assumption that a existing beer distributor can seamlessly handle 40% volume growth in a new category (cocktails) is the highest risk; verify their legal licensing for spirits in target states immediately. The June 1 launch date is likely unrealistic if regulatory approval for the new blend is required in each new jurisdiction.*
