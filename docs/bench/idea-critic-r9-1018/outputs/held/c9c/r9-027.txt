The current plan relies on a fatal assumption: that the AR SDK royalty fee is negligible. A 15% uptake is an outlier that requires validation before committing capital.

3.  **Fragmentation Debt:** The "six-month sprint" underestimates the cost of supporting diverse iOS/Android hardware, causing timeline slippage that misses the book launch window.

**Kill Criterion:**
Terminate the project immediately if the **paid conversion rate is below 4%** after the first 30 days of the pilot, or if the **SDK royalty exceeds 5% of gross revenue**.

The plan compares this to a standard digital product launch, ignoring the **hardware friction tax**. Unlike buying a book, users must download a separate engine, grant camera permissions, and possess a relatively modern device. This friction alone typically reduces conversion by 50% compared to standard in-app purchases, a factor not reflected in your 15% assumption.
