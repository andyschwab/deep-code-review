**Recommendation: Proceed with a capped, 90-day pilot under a "go-if" condition.**

Launch the pilot immediately only if the cooperative secures a written commitment from at least 60% of the target farmer group to participate before hardware procurement begins. Without this pre-commitment, the project risks becoming a sunk cost in unused hardware. The budget allocation is viable, but the pilot must be strictly time-boxed to validate the regulatory and adoption risks you identified before scaling to the full 25% volume target.

**Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Adoption Collapse:** Farmers reject the RFID tags due to perceived surveillance or lack of immediate cash incentives, leaving the system with data gaps that invalidate the "traceability" premium.
2.  **Regulatory Shock:** New data sovereignty laws or export documentation requirements emerge mid-pilot, rendering the current blockchain schema non-compliant and requiring expensive re-architecture.
3.  **Unit Economics Breach:** If the actual spoilage reduction fails to hit the 1% target, the ROI disappears, as the 5% price premium alone may not cover the $0.12/tag + transaction fee overhead.

**Kill Criteria & Metrics:**
*   **Adoption Threshold:** Terminate or pause if active farmer participation drops below **60%** within the first 45 days.
*   **Cost Cap:** Halt immediately if pilot implementation costs exceed **$287,500** (15% overrun of your $250k budget).
*   **Spoilage Metric:** Re-evaluate the approach if end-of-pilot spoilage remains above **1.4%** (failing to achieve at least half the projected improvement).
