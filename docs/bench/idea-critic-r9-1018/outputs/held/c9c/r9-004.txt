**Recommended Course of Action:** Proceed with a **capped, phased pilot** at two locations only by 15 March 2025, rather than an eight-store rollout. Cap the total capital outlay at 400k PLN (2 stores × 200k max) and defer the remaining six locations until the Ministry's regulation is finalized and the supplier's price lock is contractually secured for 24 months.

**Principal Failure Modes:**
1.  **Regulatory Ambiguity:** The Ministry delays or alters compliance fees, rendering the current biodegradable film non-compliant or subject to unexpected levies, eroding the projected 12% margin lift.
2.  **Labor Cost Underestimation:** Retraining bakers disrupts production schedules, causing overtime costs or waste that exceeds the 8% landfill fee savings, turning the net impact negative in Year 1.
3.  **Supply Chain Price Shock:** The polymer supplier invokes a force majeure or market adjustment clause, increasing film costs and destroying the gross margin assumption.

**Abandonment Trigger (Kill Criterion):**
Abort the expansion to the remaining six stores immediately if, after the first quarter of the pilot:
*   The **total cost per pilot store exceeds 200k PLN** (including unanticipated labor or compliance fees), OR
*   The **actual gross margin lift is below 3%** (adjusted for labor and material variances).

**Note:** The current plan lacks a verified labor baseline and exposes 1.2M PLN to unconfirmed regulatory outcomes; a two-store pilot limits downside risk to 400k PLN while validating the core economic assumptions.
**Note:** Secure a written price-lock addendum from the supplier before committing even the pilot capital.
