**Recommendation: Do not proceed with the proposed pilot.** The current plan exposes the city to a high probability of operational failure during peak winter conditions while hiding a net financial loss in the first year.

**Toughest Objection: The Winter Viability Gap**
Your model assumes 60% driver replacement, yet Helsinki's average February snow depth (45 cm) presents a critical, unvalidated failure mode for autonomous navigation. If the trucks cannot operate safely or efficiently in snow, you achieve 0% labor savings during the months of highest collection demand, while still incurring full capital and insurance costs. A six-month pilot starting March 1st deliberately misses the primary stress test (winter), rendering the pilot data useless for a full-year rollout decision. You are effectively paying to test the system only in ideal conditions.

**Stronger Option: A "Winter-First" Stress Test**
Instead of a standard six-month commercial pilot, negotiate a **3-month, winter-only proof-of-concept** with EcoCrate involving just two units, deployed specifically during January–March 2025.
*   **Cost:** Drastically lower upfront capital exposure (~€1.7M vs €8.5M).
*   **Value:** Directly validates the single biggest technical risk (snow navigation) before committing to fleet-wide integration.
*   **Financial Reality:** Even if labor savings hold, your current math is flawed.
    *   *Gross Savings:* 18 drivers (60% of 30) × €45k = €810k/year.
    *   *Hidden Costs:* 10 trucks × (€120k insurance + €30k software) = €1.5M/year recurring.
    *   *Net Result:* €810k savings - €1.5M costs = **€690k annual loss**, not a €1.5M gain. The project loses money immediately unless labor costs rise drastically or hidden fees are waived.

**Kill Criteria & Trigger Metric**
You must pull the plug or rethink the plan entirely if either of the following occurs:
1.  **Operational Trigger:** During the winter stress test, autonomous intervention (human takeover) is required more than **15% of the time** during snowfall events >20 cm. This threshold proves the technology cannot handle Helsinki's core climate challenge.
2.  **Financial Trigger:** EcoCrate refuses to cap the recurring software and insurance premiums at a level where total operational costs are at least **20% lower** than the current manual fleet. Without this margin, the risk premium destroys the business case.

**Note:** The current proposal omits a net-negative cash flow in Year 1 and ignores the primary environmental constraint.
