**Recommendation: Proceed with a capped, time-boxed pilot under strict "go-if" conditions.**
The proposal aligns with the budget and timeline, but the reliance on a 70% enrollment rate to achieve a 30% waste reduction is a single point of failure. The plan is viable only if treated as a validated learning experiment rather than a guaranteed service rollout. Commit the full $2.3M only in tranches, releasing subsequent funds only upon hitting interim milestones.

**Top Failure Modes:**
1.  **Enrollment Cliff:** The 70% household enrollment assumption is aggressive for a new app-driven service. If actual enrollment dips below 45%, the volume required to hit the 1,500-ton target becomes mathematically impossible regardless of participant compliance.
2.  **Integration Cost Black Hole:** Unknown ERP integration costs could consume the operational budget. If integration exceeds the 15% contingency, funds for community outreach (the driver of enrollment) will be cannibalized, causing a secondary failure in adoption.
3.  **Baseline Data Flaw:** The 30% reduction target relies on 2023 city-wide data applied to a specific subset of 2,500 blocks. If these blocks have different waste profiles than the city average, the 1,500-ton target is misaligned from day one.

**Kill Criteria & Triggers:**
Stop the pilot immediately and revert to standard collection if **either** of the following occurs:
*   **Diversion Threshold:** Actual verified waste diversion is **below 12%** (40% of the target trajectory) after the first three months (by 1 June 2025). This indicates the service model or enrollment is fundamentally broken.
*   **Cost Threshold:** Total confirmed integration costs exceed the allocated budget line by **more than 15%**. This signals unsustainable unit economics for a city-wide scale-up.

**Leading Metric to Watch:**
Weekly active app users as a percentage of total households in the pilot zone. Review this metric every Friday; a stagnation below 50% of the projected curve for two consecutive weeks requires an immediate strategy pivot before the 3-month financial kill switch triggers.

*Note: The 12% diversion trigger is set at 40% of the goal to account for seasonal variance while still providing an early warning signal. Ensure the ERP integration contract includes a hard cap before signing.*
