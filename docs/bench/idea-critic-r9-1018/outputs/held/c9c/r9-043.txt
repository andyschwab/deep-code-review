1.  **Cost Underestimation:** Remote delivery costs push the per-unit expense above the $35 revenue, creating negative margin on every sale.
2.  **Conversion Miss:** Sign-ups fall short of 430, failing to cover fixed operational overhead and the increased variable costs.
3.  **Churn Underestimation:** If churn exceeds 5% in the first month due to delivery delays or product issues, the lifetime value drops below the acquisition cost, making the subscription model unsustainable.
