3.  **Density Dilution:** The model assumes uniform delivery efficiency. In reality, low-density rural drops in the Bay of Plenty will push the cost per drop well above $6, destroying the margin established in dense areas.

This eliminates the $3.5M capex, removes the need for a custom app initially, and leverages their existing MPI compliance, reducing the risk to purely marketing and customer retention.
