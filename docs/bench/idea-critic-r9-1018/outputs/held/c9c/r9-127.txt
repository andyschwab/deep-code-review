**Recommendation: No-Go in current form; pivot to a capped, single-site pilot with a hard kill criterion.**

The strongest objection is that the business case relies on unverified variables that could invert the ROI within the first year. You are assuming a "negligible" per-record transaction fee without a number; for 3.5 million kg of cod, even a $0.005 fee per record (if one record equals one kg or batch) creates an annual operational cost of $17,500 to $175,000+, which directly erodes the projected $355,000 total benefit. Furthermore, the assumption that all distributors will accept QR logs within three months is a critical external dependency outside your control; if even one major distributor refuses integration, the "premium price uplift" vanishes for that volume, breaking the revenue model. The six-month parallel rollout also concentrates execution risk; if the vendor's node maintenance ($144,000/year) is fixed regardless of throughput, a delayed rollout or partial adoption means you pay full price for fractional value.

**Cheaper/Faster Alternative:**
Reject the three-plant parallel launch. Instead, run a **single-plant pilot in Tromsø (40% volume)** for three months. This reduces the initial implementation exposure to roughly $70,000 (prorated) and limits monthly maintenance to $4,000. Use this window to negotiate the exact per-record fee based on real volume data and force-test distributor acceptance with your largest partner before committing to Ålesund and Bergen. This approach validates the "negligible" fee claim and the distributor adoption hypothesis with only 40% of the capital at risk.

**Kill Criterion:**
Abandon the project immediately if, after the 90-day Tromsø pilot, the **net margin per kg does not increase by at least $0.03**. This metric accounts for the spoilage reduction and price uplift minus the actual verified transaction fees and operational overhead. If the net gain is below $0.03/kg, the platform fails to deliver sufficient value to justify the complexity and fixed costs across the remaining 60% of production.

**Note:** Do not present the $355,000 annual benefit to the CEO until the per-record fee is contractually defined. The current "negligible" description is a financial blind spot that will be challenged immediately.
