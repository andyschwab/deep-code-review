**Recommendation: Proceed with a capped, time-boxed pilot (Go-If), but revise the financial model and device assumption before CEO approval.**

**Primary Risks & Unrealistic Expectations:**
1.  **Device Assumption Critical Failure:** The claim that "80% of target users have AR-capable smartphones" is likely inflated for a general K-12 demographic. If the actual addressable market is <50%, the €500k investment cannot yield the projected €150k/quarter revenue, extending payback beyond the budget window.
2.  **Revenue Math Mismatch:** A 30% subscription boost generating only €150k/quarter implies a current quarterly run rate of €500k. Verify this baseline; if current revenue is lower, the absolute gain will miss targets even if the percentage growth is hit.
3.  **Hidden Costs:** The budget lacks line items for Apple App Store/Google Play AR review compliance delays, increased customer support for hardware-specific bugs, and potential data privacy legal reviews for recording minors in real-world settings.

**Better Alternative:**
Before committing to full AR development, validate the core "gamified pronunciation" mechanic via a **non-AR mobile web prototype** using standard microphone input. This tests the pedagogical value and engagement loop at <10% of the cost. If engagement doesn't lift without the AR novelty, the expensive AR layer will not save the product.

**Kill Criteria (Pull the Plug):**
Abandon the project immediately if **either** of the following occurs during the pilot phase (by 31 March 2026):
*   **Adoption Threshold:** Less than **15%** of eligible pilot students activate the AR feature at least once per week (invalidates the 80% device/interest assumption).
*   **Conversion Efficiency:** The pilot fails to generate a **5% conversion lift** from free-to-paid tiers among AR users compared to the control group (proves the feature does not drive the modeled 30% total boost).

**Note:** The €30k UI kit should be withheld until the device compatibility audit confirms the 80% figure. Recalculate the ROI using a conservative 50% device penetration rate to stress-test the €500k cap.
