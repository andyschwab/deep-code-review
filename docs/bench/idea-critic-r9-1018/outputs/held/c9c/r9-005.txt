**Executive Summary & Decision Framework**

Your current model contains a critical arithmetic flaw: a 25% reduction in total water loss (not just the 3% unaccounted portion) on a 12,000 km network implies saving ~0.75% of total volume. If your 1.5M DKK saving assumes reducing the *entire* loss by 25%, you are likely overestimating revenue recovery unless the 3% figure represents the *total* loss, not just "unaccounted" water. Verify if the 3% is Net Night Flow (NNF) or total system input; if it's only the unaccounted portion, the absolute volume saved may be too small to cover the 500k DKK integration plus ongoing AI licensing, extending payback well beyond four years.

**Hidden Flaws & Costs**
1.  **The False-Positive Tax:** You identified the false-positive rate as unknown. In predictive maintenance, a 10% false-positive rate on a sensitive model can generate hundreds of unnecessary dispatches. At ~2,000 DKK per dispatch (crew + truck), 200 false alarms annually erase 400k DKK of your projected savings immediately.
2.  **Integration Debt:** The 500k DKK quote likely covers API handshakes, not data cleansing. If your SCADA history has gaps or noise, the AI will underperform until you invest in sensor calibration or data engineering, a cost rarely included in vendor quotes.
3.  **Repair Bottleneck:** Your assumption of a 30-day repair window is risky. If the pilot flags 50 leaks in month one, do you have the capacity to dig 50 holes without delaying other critical maintenance? Delays increase water loss duration, eroding the "quick win" metrics needed for board approval.

**Better Way / Alternative**
Before committing to a full AI overlay, run a **targeted acoustic correlation pilot** on your highest-loss DMA (District Metered Area) only. This requires no SCADA integration, has near-zero false positives for metallic pipes, and costs <50k DKK. It validates whether your leak density justifies the repair spend before layering on complex AI prediction.

**Kill Criteria & Pivot Triggers**
Do not proceed to full rollout unless the pilot meets these specific conditions by March 2025:
*   **Stop Condition:** If the ratio of *verified leaks* to *dispatched crews* drops below 60% (i.e., >40% false positives), terminate the contract. The operational cost of chasing ghosts outweighs the water savings.
*   **Pivot Trigger:** If the average time from "AI flag" to "repair completed" exceeds 45 days, pivot from a "predict-and-repair" model to a "priority-list" model where AI only ranks existing known issues rather than generating new tickets.
*   **Financial Gate:** If net savings (water saved minus dispatch costs) do not exceed 150k DKK in the first 90 days of the pilot, the 4-year payback is illusory; halt expansion.

*Note: Recalculate your 1.5M DKK saving using the actual cost per cubic meter of treated water, not just the retail price, to ensure you aren't double-counting energy savings already captured elsewhere.*
*Ensure the vendor contract includes a penalty clause for false-positive rates exceeding 15%.*
