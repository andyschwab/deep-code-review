**Recommendation: GO-IF**
Proceed only if the pilot validates that at least 45% of existing customers voluntarily adopt the service within the first 60 days, and the total cost per acquired booking (development amortization + AI fees) remains under $120. Do not commit to the full $420,000 spend until this threshold is met; treat the initial phase as a capped, time-boxed experiment.

**Principal Failure Modes**
1.  **Adoption Delusion:** The plan assumes 80% of existing customers will switch to an AI-driven service. This is historically unprecedented for high-value travel ($4,400 avg) without a massive price cut or forced migration. If customers value human curation, adoption may stall below 20%, making the $1.1M revenue target impossible.
2.  **Unit Economics Trap:** At 2,000 itineraries/month, the variable cost is only $160/month, which is negligible. However, the $420,000 fixed cost requires ~95 additional bookings/month (at $4,400 each) just to break even on the investment within 12 months, ignoring the unpriced EU compliance costs.
3.  **Regulatory Blind Spot:** The "unknown" EU compliance cost for AI personalization could be substantial (legal review, data sovereignty, explicit consent flows). If this requires rebuilding the data pipeline, the timeline and budget will collapse.

**Kill Criteria & Trigger**
*   **Metric:** Voluntary adoption rate of existing customers.
*   **Threshold:** If <45% of the invited base activates the service by August 1, 2024 (60 days post-launch).
*   **Action:** Immediately halt further development spend and pivot to a "human-in-the-loop" hybrid model or cancel the project.
*   **Leading Metric to Watch:** Cost Per Acquired Booking (CPAB) including the pro-rated development cost. Review date: July 1, 2024.

**Notes**
The rollout date (June 1, 2024) contradicts the current date (Oct 2026); ensure the timeline reflects a future launch, not a past one. The 80% switching assumption is the single most fragile number in this model and requires immediate validation before any code is written.
