**Recommendation: GO-IF (Conditional Go)**
Proceed only if a binding cap on the USSD transaction fee is secured at ≤8% before development begins, and a 2-week pilot in one district validates that mobile coverage exceeds 90% for actual user transaction times, not just signal presence. Without these two verified constraints, the project shifts to NO-GO due to uncapped margin erosion and revenue overestimation.

**Principal Failure Modes**
1.  **Margin Collapse:** The "low-double-digit" fee estimate (potentially 15–19%) could consume 50–60% of your interest income on $300 loans, rendering the portfolio unprofitable even with a perfect 12% default rate. Your current model treats this variable cost as negligible; it is likely the primary driver of unit economics.
2.  **Coverage Illusion:** A 95% *signal* coverage rate does not equal 95% *transaction success*. USSD sessions are fragile; network congestion during market hours in Accra/Kumasi could cause timeout failures, leading to abandoned applications and reputational damage before the loan is ever booked.
3.  **Default Rate Mismatch:** Applying a 12% branch-based default rate to a remote, mobile-first cohort is risky. Without physical collateral or face-to-face pressure, digital defaults often spike higher initially until behavioral scoring models mature.

**Kill Criteria & Pivot Triggers**
*   **Pull the Plug:** If the finalized USSD fee exceeds 10% of the loan value, or if the pilot reveals a transaction failure rate >8% during peak trading hours (6 AM – 10 AM).
*   **Pivot:** If the fee is manageable but coverage is spotty, pivot immediately to an "Agent-Assisted" model where traders transact through verified local kiosks with stable connectivity, rather than direct-to-consumer USSD.

**Smarter Path Alternative**
Before building a custom integration, negotiate a revenue-share pilot using the telecom's existing micro-lending API wrapper. This shifts the technical burden and fee negotiation to the vendor for the first $500k of volume, allowing you to validate the true default rate and effective transaction costs with only $20k of your budget rather than the full $150k build.
