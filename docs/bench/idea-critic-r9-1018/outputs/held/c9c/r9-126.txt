**Primary Failure Points & Model Errors:**

Achieving 70% penetration in 12 months is industrially unprecedented for a maintenance add-on; a more realistic 5–10% conversion yields $420k–$840k annually, not $5.6M.

You would need ~9 full-time technicians immediately, not "a small service van" crew. If refurbishment takes 60 minutes (a 33% slip), you need 12 technicians, destroying your margin.

If labor costs scale linearly (approx. $40/hour fully loaded), 270 hours/month of work costs ~$10,800. However, the $250k upfront plus fixed overhead means the "10-month payback" relies entirely on the impossible 70% uptake. At 10% uptake, you likely never break even on the initial capital.
