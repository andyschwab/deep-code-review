**Recommendation: GO-IF**
Proceed only if a written regulatory pathway is confirmed by Q1 2025; otherwise, halt. The project fails the "cost of delay" and "reversibility" tests without this gate. While the unit economics ($1.5M annual savings vs. $1.2M opex) are positive, they rely entirely on volume assumptions that cannot be met without regulatory clearance. The $5M sunk cost is irrelevant to the decision; the risk is exposing an additional $1.2M+ annually to a stalled launch.

**Primary Failure Modes**
*   **Regulatory (Critical):** The "no firm date" for Canadian securities approval is a single point of failure. If the tokenized settlement layer is classified as a security requiring prospectus-level review rather than a sandbox exemption, the timeline could slip indefinitely, burning the $1.2M annual budget with zero revenue.
*   **Financial:** The model assumes $8M monthly volume immediately. If adoption ramps at 50% of forecast due to friction in user onboarding or liquidity gaps, the $1.2M operating cost exceeds the fee savings, creating a net loss versus the legacy system.
*   **Technical/Market:** The 2% volatility band assumption is fragile. If currency fluctuations exceed this during the 5-second settlement window without automated hedging mechanisms explicitly built and tested, the platform could incur losses greater than the 1% fee revenue on specific transactions.

**Kill Criteria & Tripwires**
1.  **Regulatory Stop:** If full regulatory approval (or a binding conditional exemption) is not received by **March 31, 2025**, cancel the project. Do not spend further development funds beyond maintenance.
2.  **Volume Pivot:** If live transaction volume is below **$2,000,000/month** three months post-launch, trigger an immediate operational review to reduce burn or sunset the platform.
3.  **Volatility Circuit Breaker:** If exchange rate volatility exceeds **3%** in any 24-hour period prior to full hedging automation, pause new transactions automatically.

**Leading Metric to Watch:** *Regulatory Approval Status* (Weekly check-in).
**Review Date:** January 15, 2025 (to assess Q1 deadline feasibility).
