**Strongest Objection (The Latency Trap):**
The entire predictive model collapses if legacy PLC latency exceeds the model's sampling window. If the PLCs cannot feed data fast enough, the "predictive" alerts will arrive after the failure occurs, rendering the 3.7M RUB license a sunk cost for a reactive dashboard. The proposal assumes the data-science team can tune models to overcome hardware latency, which is often physically impossible. Without verifying this first, you are buying a solution for a data stream you do not yet have.
