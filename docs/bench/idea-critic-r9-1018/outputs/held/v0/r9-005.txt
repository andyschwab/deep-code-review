**Sanity Check & Decision Framework for AquaPulse Nordic Pitch**

Your math holds only if the vendor's 90% capture rate applies to *your* specific pipe material and pressure zones, not just their aggregate case studies. The hidden flaw is the **integration latency cost**: if the AI flags a leak but your SCADA data refreshes slowly or requires manual validation, the "30-day repair" assumption slips, eroding the 1.5M DKK saving. Furthermore, a 25% reduction in physical loss does not automatically equal 25% budget savings if your repair costs are fixed-price contracts rather than variable; verify if the 8M DKK budget includes overhead that won't decrease with fewer leaks.

**Better Way / Alternative**
Before committing to the 500k DKK integration, run a **offline shadow pilot**. Feed the vendor three months of historical SCADA data *now* without connecting live systems. Ask them to return flagged leaks so you can cross-reference them with actual repairs made during that period. This validates the false-positive rate and accuracy on *your* network for near-zero cost and zero operational risk, removing the "integration effort" unknown from your initial decision.

**Kill Criteria & Pivot Triggers**
During the six-month pilot, pull the plug immediately if:
1.  **False Positive Ratio > 3:1**: If crews investigate three dry holes for every real leak, operational fatigue will cause them to ignore future alerts, rendering the system useless regardless of detection accuracy.
2.  **Time-to-Verify > 48 Hours**: If it takes more than two days to distinguish a real leak from a sensor glitch after an alert, the water loss saved is negligible compared to the status quo.

Pivot to a **targeted zone rollout** (rather than full network) if the integration effort exceeds 750k DKK or if the pilot shows high accuracy only in high-pressure zones, suggesting the model cannot generalize across your entire 12,000 km network.

**Note:** Ensure your board deck explicitly separates "water saved" from "cash saved," as these diverge if energy/pumping costs aren't the primary driver of your 8M DKK budget.
