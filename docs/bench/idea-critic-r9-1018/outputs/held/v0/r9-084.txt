**Critical Failure Points & Blind Spots**
1.  **The "Unchanged Default" Assumption:** Your plan hinges on the default rate remaining at 5% while expanding to new user segments. This is the highest-risk assumption. New segments often behave differently than the training population; if the model approves riskier borrowers to achieve the 15% lift in approval rates, the default rate will likely rise, eroding margins immediately.
2.  **Regulatory & Compliance Volatility:** You noted uncertain regulatory response to alternative data in Singapore. If regulators restrict specific data inputs mid-pilot, the model's predictive power could collapse, or you could face fines that exceed the $90k API estimate.
3.  **Hidden Infrastructure Costs:** The $45k for data validation is likely an underestimate. Integrating real-time alternative data often requires significant latency management and error-handling logic not captured in simple licensing fees. If data feeds fail silently, you may make lending decisions on stale or incorrect information.

**The Signal to Pull the Plug (Kill Criterion)**
Stop the pilot immediately if the **rolling 30-day default rate exceeds 6.5%** (a 30% buffer over the baseline 5%) for any single new user segment. Do not wait for the three-month conclusion. This threshold balances statistical noise with catastrophic loss.
