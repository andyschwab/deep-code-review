**Recommendation: Proceed with the pilot only if converted to a "Go-If" experiment with strict kill criteria; do not commit to the full rollout based on current assumptions.**

The math only works if you capture 100% of the assumed $0.25 premium on every bottle sold through the new system. Your current plan treats an unverified survey response as guaranteed revenue while locking you into a $150,000 sunk cost and higher variable fees than a competitor's solution ($0.23 vs $0.12). The biggest failure modes are: (1) Distributors refuse to pass the premium to consumers or keep the margin for themselves, leaving you with higher costs and no offset; (2) Adoption friction causes distributors to bypass your branded bottles for cheaper alternatives, shrinking the volume base needed to absorb the implementation fee; (3) The "blockchain" value prop proves invisible to the end consumer, making the premium unsustainable past the initial novelty.

*   **Distributor Participation:** Fewer than 40% of target distributors actively scan/register the bottles (indicating operational rejection).
*   **Net Margin:** The blended cost per bottle (implementation amortized + variable fee) exceeds the realized premium income for two consecutive months.
