**Recommendation: Go-if**
Proceed with the pilot only if a binding pre-commitment from the Texas state regulator confirming air-space clearance timelines is secured before funds are released. Without this, the project faces immediate immobilization.

**Principal Failure Modes**
1.  **Regulatory Paralysis:** The 45-day clearance assumption is a single point of failure. If the regulator delays beyond 60 days, the $850,000 capital sits idle while fixed costs (software maintenance, trained staff salaries) burn without offsetting labor savings, destroying the Year-1 ROI.
2.  **Environmental Mismatch:** The 12% historical downtime estimate may be optimistic for specific Texas corridors prone to sudden high-velocity gusts. If actual grounding exceeds 20%, the required inspection frequency cannot be met, forcing a reversion to expensive manual crews and negating the projected $420,000 labor saving.
3.  **Detection Risk:** A false-negative rate above 5% creates unacceptable liability. Missing a pipeline defect is far costlier than the inspection itself; if initial calibration flights show rates approaching 4%, the trust required for operational reliance collapses.

**Kill Criteria (Pull the Plug)**
Terminate or pivot the program immediately if:
*   Regulatory clearance is not granted within **50 days** of application.
*   Operational data shows wind-related grounding exceeds **18%** over the first 60 flight-days.
*   Controlled testing reveals a false-negative detection rate greater than **3.5%** (providing a safety buffer below the 5% limit).
