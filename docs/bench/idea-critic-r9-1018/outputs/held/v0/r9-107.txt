**Principal Failure Modes & Hidden Costs**

3.  **Default Rate Mismatch:** Applying a 12% branch-based default rate to mobile-first borrowers is risky. Without physical collateral or face-to-face pressure, digital defaults often spike higher initially until behavioral scoring matures.
4.  **Timeline Risk:** A January 15, 2025 go-live for a new USSD integration is aggressive. Telecom vendor integrations often face bureaucratic delays (testing, certification) that slip launches by weeks, burning budget without revenue.
