**Strongest Objection: The Regulatory-Integration Mismatch**
Your timeline assumes regulatory clearance (6 months) and technical integration can proceed linearly, but this is likely false. De-identified clinical data on a public or permissioned blockchain often triggers re-identification risks under evolving GDPR and HIPAA interpretations, potentially stalling the IND amendment indefinitely. If clearance is delayed to 18 months—a common scenario for novel data architectures—your $4.5M capital burn extends significantly while hospital partners (Northside, Eastpoint, West Ridge) face shifting internal compliance landscapes. The "unknown" integration cost is not merely a budget variance; it is a critical path dependency. If hospitals require custom, non-standard APIs to bridge legacy warehouses to your ledger, the 18-month window collapses, making the March 2026 launch impossible without slashing scope or exploding costs.

**Better Alternative: Federated Data Trust with Off-Chain Storage**
Instead of a full blockchain ledger for data upload, propose a **Federated Data Trust**. Keep raw data within each hospital's existing secure warehouse (solving the integration cost and latency issue) and use blockchain *only* for immutable audit logs of access requests and consent verification. This reduces the attack surface, simplifies regulatory approval by avoiding "data on-chain" scrutiny, and lowers the initial investment by leveraging existing hospital infrastructure rather than building new upload pipelines.

**Three Ways This Could Unravel**
1.  **Regulatory Gridlock:** The IND amendment is rejected or stalled due to novel blockchain storage concerns, freezing the project before a single byte is transferred.
2.  **Integration Cost Explosion:** The "unknown" cost to map heterogeneous hospital data schemas to a unified blockchain standard exceeds 50% of the budget, forcing a mid-project halt.
3.  **Participant Revocation:** A high-profile re-identification incident in the broader industry causes participants to withdraw consent en masse, rendering the marketplace empty and the ROI calculation void.

If the **regulatory feedback loop exceeds 9 months** without a written pathway to approval, or if the **pilot integration with Northside (the first partner) reveals custom development costs exceeding $750k per site**, you must halt or pivot immediately.
