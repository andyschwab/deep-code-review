**Recommendation: HOLD**

Do not move forward with the full rollout plan or the €1.3M revenue projection yet. The proposal contains a critical, unverified assumption regarding operational capacity that invalidates the financial model. You cannot commit to a March 2025 pilot or lock in 12-month contracts without first quantifying the labor hours and material yield for the fiberglass re-coating process. If the actual labor time exceeds your implicit assumptions, the €15 refurbishment cost will collapse, turning every subscription into a loss-making unit.

**Principal Failure Modes**
1.  **Unit Economics Collapse:** The €15/board refurbishment estimate is a guess. If re-coating takes 2+ hours of skilled labor or requires expensive materials, the margin on the €79/month fee evaporates, especially given the heavy upfront discount (€216 for 3 months vs. €237 standard) which delays cash flow recovery.
2.  **Conversion Overestimation:** A 22% conversion rate (990 customers) in year one is exceptionally high for a hardware-as-a-service pivot. If conversion lands at a more typical 5–8%, the volume is insufficient to cover the fixed costs of setting up the refurbishment line, leaving you with stranded inventory and labor.
3.  **Churn Underestimation:** The 7% annual churn assumption ignores the "novelty drop-off" common in subscription boxes. If surfers realize they prefer owning their specific board shape or get injured, churn could spike to 20%+, making the 12-month contract lock-in difficult to enforce or damaging to brand reputation if forced.

**Superior Option**
Run a **"Concierge Pilot" with 20 users** in Lisbon only, before setting a date or price. Manually service these boards and track the *actual* time and cost per re-coat. Use this data to recalibrate the €15 cost assumption and the pricing model. Do not offer the 3-month discount until you know your true break-even point; instead, test price elasticity with a month-to-month pilot to gauge real retention without long-term contract friction.

**Kill Criterion (Stop Signal)**
Stop the initiative immediately if the **fully loaded labor and material cost per re-coat exceeds €45** (3x your estimate) during the concierge pilot, or if **monthly active usage of the swapped boards drops below 4 times per month** per subscriber, indicating the service does not solve a frequent enough pain point to justify a recurring fee.

*Note: Your revenue math appears slightly off; 990 users at €79/mo is ~€940k ARR, not €1.3M, unless you are counting the upfront cash as full-year revenue, which distorts the unit economics view.*
*Secure a definitive labor estimate before presenting any date-specific rollout to the CEO.*
