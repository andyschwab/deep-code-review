Launch the six-month pilot on 1 July 2024 only if the vendor provides a fixed-cost GDPR audit quote and a signed integration timeline with your CRM before 15 June. Without these, the risk of budget overrun and data compliance failure outweighs the potential savings.

1.  **Adoption Miscalculation:** Your 80% smartphone assumption may be technically true but functionally false; if tenants lack data plans, privacy trust, or language support, the 5% adoption target will miss, leaving the £250k spend stranded on unused infrastructure.

Terminate or fundamentally re-architect the pilot if **active monthly users do not reach 2.5% (half your target) by the end of Month 2**.
