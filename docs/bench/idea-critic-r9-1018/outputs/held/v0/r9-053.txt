**Recommendation: GO-IF**
Proceed with a strictly limited 6-week pilot to validate the core assumption before committing to the full $120,000 build. Do not start the custom Unity development or sign the studio retainer yet.

**How This Plan Could Collapse**
1.  **The Hardware Fallacy:** The projection of an $180,000 lift relies on 70% of donors owning compatible VR headsets. This is almost certainly false; global VR penetration is significantly lower, and your specific donor demographic (likely older, high-net-worth individuals) may have even lower adoption. If the actual ownership is <10%, the addressable market vanishes, and the ROI becomes negative.
2.  **The Rural Bandwidth Bottleneck:** Streaming 4K content to a rural audience (both donors and potentially on-site visitors) faces severe latency and data cap issues. If the experience buffers or fails to load, engagement drops to zero, damaging brand trust rather than enhancing it.
3.  **Friction vs. Empathy:** Even if donors own headsets, the friction of setting up hardware to view a charity pitch is high. Donors prefer low-friction giving (click-to-donate); forcing a hardware workflow may reduce total conversion rates despite higher individual engagement.

**Trigger Metric & Kill Criteria**
*   **Primary Trigger (The Pivot Point):** Conduct an immediate email survey to a random 10% sample of your donor list asking two questions: "Do you own a VR headset?" and "Would you use it for a sanctuary tour?"
    *   **Kill Condition:** If reported ownership is **<25%** (not 70%), **STOP** the custom build immediately. The math cannot work. Pivot to a web-based 360° video experience accessible on phones/tablets.
*   **Secondary Trigger (The Board's Rule):** As requested, if live usage in the first quarter is **<5%** of the *total* donor list, terminate the project. However, given the hardware barrier, a more sensitive leading metric is **"% of headset-owning donors who activate the experience."** If fewer than 40% of those who *can* use it *do* use it within 30 days, the content or distribution strategy is flawed, and you should cut the studio retainer.

**Note:** The July 15, 2025 launch date is unrealistic if the pilot reveals you need to switch from a native VR app to a web-based solution to reach your audience.
**Note:** Do not spend the $45,000 on hardware bundles for the sanctuary unless you have confirmed on-site visitor demand; most donations will come from remote donors using their own devices (if they have them).
