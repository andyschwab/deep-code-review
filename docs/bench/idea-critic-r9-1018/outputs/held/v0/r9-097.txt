If actual churn hits 20%, your active subscriber base collapses to ~4,000 users by month six, reducing 18-month revenue from $8.4M to roughly $2.5M, turning your $1.2M cost assumption into a loss-making venture once marketing spend is included. Use this pilot to gather real data on churn and server load rather than relying on static assumptions from a different game phase.
