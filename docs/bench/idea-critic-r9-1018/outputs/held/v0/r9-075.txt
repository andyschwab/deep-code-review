**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **3-month, €15,000 pilot** using 5 headsets and a university partnership, rather than the proposed 12-month, €120,000 full rollout.

**Strongest Objection (The Adoption Cliff):**
The proposal assumes 80% of monthly visitors (5,760 people) will try the experience. This is physically impossible given your staffing constraints. With only 200 staff hours allocated over 12 months, you have roughly 16 hours per month for setup, troubleshooting, and hygiene. Even if a session lasts only 15 minutes, your team can facilitate at most ~64 sessions monthly. To hit your target of 5,760 users, you would need to process 192 users *per day* without staff intervention, which contradicts the complex nature of VR onboarding, especially for the 30% of patrons aged 65+ who may require significant assistance. The project fails not because the content is bad, but because the operational math limits actual usage to ~1% of the projected adoption, destroying the ROI case for ancillary sales.

**Smarter Alternative:**
Instead of purchasing 30 headsets (€25,500) and bearing full maintenance liability, partner with a local university's media or computer science department. They often seek real-world deployment sites for student projects and may provide hardware, technical support, and content customization in exchange for case study rights. Replace the high-friction VR requirement with a lower-cost Augmented Reality (AR) app that patrons can run on their own smartphones. This removes the hardware bottleneck, eliminates hygiene/staffing overhead, and allows simultaneous usage by hundreds of visitors, genuinely testing the "15% footfall increase" hypothesis without the €120,000 capital risk.

**Kill Criteria (Stop/Change Condition):**
Do not wait three months. Implement a **2-week "Friction Gate"**: If the average daily completed sessions per headset fall below 4 during the first 14 days of the pilot (indicating setup time or user confusion is throttling throughput), immediately halt the VR expansion and pivot to the AR app strategy. This measures operational viability before significant funds are consumed.

**Note:** The current budget allocates €43,500 for hardware alone; the proposed pilot reduces this exposure by over 80%.
