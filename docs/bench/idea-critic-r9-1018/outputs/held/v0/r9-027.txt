**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **Go-If** condition: secure a fixed-cost or capped-royalty SDK contract before authorizing the $195,000 development spend. Without this, the project carries unchecked downside risk that invalidates the revenue forecast.

**Top Three Failure Modes:**
1.  **Royalty Escalation:** If the SDK vendor imposes a per-unit fee >$2.00 or a revenue share >15%, the unit economics collapse, turning the projected $300k revenue into a net loss after costs.
2.  **Conversion Overestimation:** The 15% uptake assumption (6,000 users) is aggressive for a niche AR add-on; if conversion lands at industry-standard 3–5%, revenue drops to $60k–$120k, failing to cover the $195k initial outlay.
3.  **Technical Fragmentation:** AR performance varies wildly across device models; if the "reading layer" causes battery drain or crashes on >20% of user devices, refund requests and brand damage will outweigh sales.

**Pull-the-Plug Metric:** Halt development if the **pilot conversion rate is below 6%** (half the forecast) within the first 30 days of the soft launch, or if the finalized SDK contract pushes the **break-even point beyond 18 months**.

**Hidden Risk:** The plan compares this to a standard digital book launch, ignoring the **support burden**. AR layers require active troubleshooting (camera permissions, OS updates, device compatibility) that standard ebooks do not. You have budgeted for assets and marketing, but allocated **zero operational budget** for customer support or bug fixes, which could silently erode margins post-launch.
