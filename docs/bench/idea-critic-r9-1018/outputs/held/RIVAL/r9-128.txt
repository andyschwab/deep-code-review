**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **phased pilot launch** limited to non-sensitive client inquiries for the first six months, rather than a full immediate rollout. This approach secures the operational efficiency gains while deferring the $30,000 cybersecurity insurance premium increase and mitigating compliance risk until the conversion rate is validated.

**Principal Failure Modes:**
1.  **ROI Erosion via Hidden Costs:** The projected $144,000 annual reclaimed revenue assumes a clean 12-hour weekly recovery. However, if the $30,000 cybersecurity premium is incurred immediately without proportional new matter conversion, the net first-year benefit drops to $114,000. If the implementation drags or requires unexpected customization, the $25,000 service fee could balloon, pushing the break-even point beyond the three-year horizon.
2.  **Compliance & Liability Exposure:** Storing client data in the cloud triggers the insurance hike and potential ethical wall conflicts. If the chatbot inadvertently provides unauthorized legal advice or hallucinates precedent in a live session, the firm faces malpractice exposure that the standard policy may not cover, regardless of the "automation" label.
3.  **Adoption Stagnation:** The model relies on 800 monthly sessions. If the interface creates friction or clients prefer human intake for complex matters, session volume will miss targets, rendering the 30% automation goal unachievable and leaving attorney hours unchanged while fixed costs rise.

**Early Indicator to Pull the Plug or Pivot:**
Monitor the **New Matter Conversion Rate** and **Escalation Frequency** at the 90-day mark.
*   **Pull the Plug:** If the conversion rate remains statistically zero or if the escalation rate (cases the bot cannot handle and must pass to humans) exceeds 50%, the tool is failing to generate new revenue or save time.
*   **Pivot:** If the conversion rate is healthy but the cybersecurity cost threatens margins, pivot to a localized, on-premise deployment model to avoid the cloud insurance surcharge. If attorneys report spending *more* time correcting bot errors than the 12 hours saved, immediately pause automation and revert to a "draft-only" assistant mode.

**Note:** Do not commit to the full cloud architecture until the pilot validates that the conversion rate justifies the $30,000 annual risk premium. Frame the pilot to Maya as a "fiduciary safeguard" rather than hesitation.
