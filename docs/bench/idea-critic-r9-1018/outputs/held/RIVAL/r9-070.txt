**Recommendation: Do not proceed to the July 15 pilot without a fixed, capped sequencing cost and a regulatory pre-clearance letter.** The current plan treats the two largest binary risks—unit economics and legal permission to sell—as variables to be solved later. This is not a launch plan; it is a research proposal disguised as a product rollout.

**The Toughest Objection: The "Variable Cost" Trap**
Your financial model collapses if the DNA sequencing fee exceeds $1.80 per pack. At a $12 price point, with standard CPG COGS (ingredients, packaging, labor, logistics) likely consuming $6.50–$7.50, and your allocated R&D/Marketing amortization, you have almost no buffer. If sequencing costs scale linearly or spike due to supply constraints, your margin turns negative on day one. Furthermore, banking on regulatory approval within six months is a critical path failure point; if approval takes 12 months, your $250k marketing budget burns away while inventory sits in a warehouse, destroying cash flow before revenue starts.

**A Better Route: The "Concierge" Validation**
Stop the 30,000-unit pilot. Instead, run a "Concierge MVP" with 500 units.
1.  **Manual Processing:** Sequence the DNA using a partner lab on a spot-market basis to establish a hard cost floor.
2.  **Pre-Sale Validation:** Sell these 500 units at the full $12 price point to verify willingness to pay *before* building inventory.
3.  **Regulatory Probe:** Submit the filing immediately with this smaller batch data. If the regulator pushes back on the nutrigenomics claims, you have lost $80k, not $1.1M + production costs.
This route delays the "big launch" but guarantees that when you do scale, the unit economics are proven and the regulatory path is clear.

**Primary Ways This Plan Could Collapse**
1.  **Margin Inversion:** Sequencing costs + COGS > $12. You sell more units and lose more money.
2.  **Regulatory Block:** The FDA (or equivalent) classifies the DNA claims as "drug-like" rather than "food-like," requiring clinical trials that cost millions and take years.
3.  **Market Apathy:** Dog owners care about ingredients/brand trust, not genomic data, resulting in <2% market capture instead of 15%.

**The Pivot/Pull-the-Plug Metric**
**Trigger:** If the fully loaded Cost Per Unit (including sequenced DNA) exceeds **$8.50**, or if regulatory clearance is not granted within **5 months**.
*   **Action:** If costs exceed $8.50, pivot to a non-genomic premium line using the same branding. If regulatory clearance fails by month 5, pull the plug entirely; the core value proposition is illegal to market.
