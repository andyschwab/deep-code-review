### Toughest Pushback: The Unit Economics & Churn Reality
Maya's sharpest challenge will target your **$0.15/user/month** infrastructure assumption and **5% quarterly churn**. Live-service models incur exponentially higher costs than static updates due to real-time synchronization, database write-heavy operations for inventory/progress, and 24/7 moderation. A realistic cloud cost is **$0.45–$0.60/user/month**, tripling your OpEx to **~$3.6M** over 18 months, not $1.2M.

Furthermore, maintaining <5% quarterly churn for a subscription is exceptionally rare in gaming without massive content velocity. If churn hits a standard 15% quarterly, your active subscriber base collapses by >50% within a year, turning the projected $8.4M revenue into ~$3.5M. Combined with the missing marketing spend (typically $20–$40 per converted subscriber in this sector, implying a **$2.4M–$4.8M** hole in your budget), the project shifts from profitable to a significant cash-burn risk.

### A More Viable Path: The "Founder's Pass" Hybrid
Do not pivot the entire studio to a high-risk subscription model immediately. Instead, propose a **hybrid monetization layer**:
1.  **Launch a "Founder's Pass" ($7/mo)** as an optional premium tier rather than a gatekeeper. Include exclusive cosmetics, early access to zones, and a monthly currency stipend.
2.  **Target a 5% conversion** (4,000 users) initially. This lowers infrastructure strain and validates the recurring revenue model without betting the company on 15% adoption.
3.  **Reinvest 100% of Pass revenue** into a dedicated monthly content drop. This creates a visible feedback loop where subscribers directly fund the content they consume, reducing churn justification.
4.  **Marketing Strategy:** Leverage existing community channels (Discord, in-game mail) for zero-cost conversion pushes before spending on external ads.

This approach caps downside risk while proving the Live-Service engine works.

### The Plug-Pull Signal
Define this hard metric before launch:
**If Month 3 net subscriber growth is <2% or Month 4 churn exceeds 12%, pause all further Live-Service development.**

This indicates the content velocity cannot sustain the subscription value proposition. At that point, revert to your successful buy-to-update model and sunset the recurring billing infrastructure to prevent bleeding cash on retention marketing.
