**Strongest Objection: The "Negligible" Transaction Fee Trap**
The vendor's refusal to disclose the per-record cost is a critical red flag. In high-volume processing, "negligible" often means $0.005–$0.01. At 3.5 million kg annually, if you generate just one traceability record per kg, that is 3.5 million transactions. At $0.01/record, your variable cost is **$35,000/year**, eroding nearly 10% of your projected $180k spoilage savings. If records are generated per batch or per box, the cost drops, but without this number, the ROI is unverifiable. Furthermore, the $144k/year fixed maintenance cost against $355k/year total benefit leaves a razor-thin margin for operational friction or adoption delays.

**Cheaper/Faster Alternative: Hybrid QR + Centralized DB**
Before committing to blockchain, test a centralized database with signed QR codes. This provides identical customer-facing audit trails and spoilage tracking at a fraction of the cost (likely < $20k setup, minimal maintenance). Blockchain adds immutability, but ask: *Who are we preventing from rewriting history?* If the threat is internal fraud, blockchain doesn't stop bad data entry ("garbage in, garbage out"). If the threat is external distrust, a centralized system with third-party audit access often suffices for distributors. Validate the need for distributed consensus before paying the blockchain premium.

**Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **Distributor Rejection:** Your assumption that distributors will accept new QR logs within three months is optimistic. If major buyers refuse to scan or integrate these logs, the $175k price uplift vanishes instantly.
2.  **Operational Drag:** If scanning slows down the packaging line by even 5%, the labor cost overtime will exceed the spoilage savings.
3.  **Data Integrity:** If plant staff batch-scan items to save time rather than scanning individual units, the traceability granularity collapses, making the system useless for pinpointing spoilage sources.

**Concrete Kill Trigger**
Abandon the project immediately if the **pilot plant (Ålesund) fails to demonstrate a net positive cash flow within 90 days**. Specifically, if the sum of (actual spoilage reduction + realized price premium) minus (vendor fees + added labor costs) is < $0, do not roll out to Tromsø or Bergen. Do not proceed to full rollout until the vendor provides a written cap on the per-record transaction fee.
