The proposal fails on unit economics and hidden dependency risks. Your three-year cost estimate of CHF 800k is mathematically incorrect; the actual outlay is **CHF 950k** (CHF 350k build + CHF 450k upkeep), creating an immediate CHF 150k budget hole before a single line of code is written. More critically, the business case relies on an 8% price premium to justify a feature where the primary comparable (GemCrest) required a CHF 1.2m consulting engagement to succeed—a cost you have excluded. Without that heavy-handed implementation support, achieving the required retailer adoption and consumer trust is speculative. You are betting CHF 2.5m of launch capital on a 35% adoption rate (60% of markets × ~60% retailer uptake) without a verified distribution strategy for the scanning app.

**Primary Collapse Vectors:**
1.  **Adoption Fracture:** If boutique retailers refuse the QR app (high friction, low perceived value for them), the "verified" chain breaks at the point of sale, rendering the ledger useless for authentication while still incurring the full CHF 150k/year upkeep.
2.  **Premium Erosion:** If the market perceives the blockchain as marketing fluff rather than tangible value, the 8% premium collapses. At a 4% premium, the project burns cash indefinitely without ever reaching break-even.
3.  **The GemCrest Illusion:** Replicating their 70% counterfeit reduction likely requires their level of consulting spend. Attempting to achieve this on a shoestring budget will result in a "ghost ledger"—technically functional but operationally ignored.

*   **ABORT** if pre-launch retailer surveys indicate **<40% willingness** to actively use the scanning app during the sales process.
*   **ABORT** if pilot pricing data shows the price premium settling below **5%**.
