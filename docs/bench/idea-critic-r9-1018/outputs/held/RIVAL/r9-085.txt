**Decision Recommendation: Do Not Proceed As Outlined.**

The current plan relies on a "best-case" regulatory timeline that contradicts the risk profile of building adjacent to a heritage-listed park. Proceeding now exposes the institution to unacceptable schedule slippage and cost escalation. You should authorize a **90-day Feasibility & Risk Mitigation Phase** before committing capital.

**Principal Failure Modes**

1.  **Regulatory Gridlock (Highest Probability):** Heritage approvals for adjacent developments rarely conclude within six months. A delay here creates a domino effect: construction starts late, pushing the completion date past the 30-month window. This delays revenue onset while fixed costs (loan interest, project management) continue to accrue.
2.  **Construction Cost Escalation:** A $12M budget for 1,800 seats implies ~$6,666 per seat. Given current inflation in materials and labor, this is tight. A 15% overrun (common in heritage-adjacent sites requiring specialized foundations or aesthetic concessions) would push costs to $13.8M, eroding the ROI margin entirely.
3.  **Demand Elasticity Miscalculation:** The model assumes a 15% enrollment rise alongside a 3% tuition hike. If the local market is price-sensitive, the tuition increase could suppress demand, leaving the new seats underutilized. Fixed operating costs for empty seats will drain the surplus.

**Arithmetic & Operational Gaps**

*   **Missing Operating Expenses (OpEx):** The brief mentions capital expenditure (CapEx) but ignores the incremental OpEx. Adding 1,800 seats requires additional faculty, administrative support, utilities, and maintenance. If these recurring costs are not fully funded by the projected tuition hike, the project will operate at a loss even if enrollment targets are met.
*   **Timeline Arithmetic:** If planning takes 9 months instead of 6, the 30-month construction clock starts later. Even if construction remains efficient, the *total* time to revenue extends by 25%, significantly impacting the Net Present Value (NPV) of the project.

**Trigger Conditions for Stop-Loss or Re-scope**

Establish these hard gates immediately. If any are breached, the project must pause for Board re-approval:

*   **Regulatory Trigger:** If final heritage consent is not secured within **9 months** of application submission.
*   **Financial Trigger:** If revised tender estimates or mid-project change orders push total capital spend above **$13.5 million** (a 12.5% contingency buffer rather than the implied 0%).
