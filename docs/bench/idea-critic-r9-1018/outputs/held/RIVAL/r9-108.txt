**Recommendation: Proceed with a conditional pilot, not a full rollout.**

Approve the $225k development and pilot spend immediately, but structure the $30k ad boost and the assumed $200k partner marketing contribution as tranches released only upon hitting specific performance gates. Do not commit to the full 9-week rollout until Week 4 data validates the conversion lift. The potential upside (doubling conversion rates and increasing order value) justifies the risk, but only if you cap the downside exposure before the anecdotal data becomes expensive reality.

**Critical Failure Points:**
1.  **The AOV Assumption:** Your entire ROI model likely hinges on the unverified $12 Average Order Value (AOV) lift. If AR drives traffic but users only buy low-cost items to "try on," your conversion rate could hit 5% while total revenue stagnates, failing the 1.5× return requirement.
2.  **SDK License Lock-in:** The $45k third-party SDK cost is a sunk cost upfront. If the user experience is clunky or latency is high on older devices (common in AR), adoption will flatline, and you cannot recover this license fee to pivot to a different tech stack easily.
3.  **Marketing Dependency:** The model assumes NordicHome provides $200k in marketing. If they reduce this spend due to internal budget shifts, your volume target of 12k sessions/week becomes mathematically impossible regardless of product quality, causing the pilot to fail on distribution rather than merit.

You must halt or pivot immediately if **Week 4 data shows a blended conversion rate below 3.5% OR an AOV lift of less than $4.**

Do not wait for Week 12. By Week 4, you will have sufficient session data to extrapolate the final result. If the conversion lift is under 3.5%, the "immersive" premise is not translating to sales, and continuing to Week 12 will guarantee missing the 1.5× ROI target. Similarly, if the AOV lift is under $4, the unit economics cannot support the $225k fixed cost within the $250k total spend cap. Triggering the halt at Week 4 preserves roughly $100k of the planned ad boost and prevents further sunk costs into a failing value proposition.
