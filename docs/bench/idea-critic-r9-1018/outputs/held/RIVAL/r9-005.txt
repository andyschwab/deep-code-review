**Executive Sanity Check: AquaPulse Nordic Pitch**

Your core math holds water, but the risk profile is skewed by two unverified assumptions: the false-positive rate and the integration "long tail."

**1. Hidden Costs & Flaws**
*   **The False-Positive Tax:** If the model generates even 5 false alarms per day across 12,000 km, and each requires a 2-person crew visit (2 hours @ ~800 DKK/hr), you burn **~230,000 DKK/year** in wasted labor. This directly erodes your 1.5M DKK saving. Without a guaranteed False Positive Rate (FPR) < 10%, your net savings could drop by 15–20%.
*   **Integration Creep:** The 500k DKK quote likely covers API handshakes, not data normalization. Legacy SCADA systems often require custom middleware to clean noisy telemetry before the AI can ingest it. Budget an additional **200k–300k DKK** for data engineering contingencies.
*   **The Repair Bottleneck:** Assuming repairs happen within 30 days is optimistic if you lack spare parts inventory for specific pipe materials identified by the AI. Delayed repairs extend the loss window, delaying ROI.

**2. Reliable Alternatives**
Before committing to full AI predictive modeling, consider a **Hybrid Acoustic + Flow Step-Change** approach.
*   *Why:* It is less dependent on historical data quality (which often plagues AI startups) and offers immediate, localized verification.
*   *Strategy:* Deploy fixed acoustic loggers in high-loss districts first. This validates the *existence* of leaks with higher certainty before layering on the predictive AI, reducing the risk of "garbage in, garbage out."

**3. Pilot Kill/Pivot Criteria (Sept 2024 – Feb 2025)**
Do not wait for the full six months to decide. Set these gates at Month 3:
*   **PULL THE PLUG if:**
    *   **False Positive Rate > 15%:** If crews are digging dry holes more than 1 in 7 times, operational trust will collapse, and labor costs will exceed savings.
    *   **Integration Stability < 98%:** If the data pipeline drops packets or requires manual resets more than twice a month, the system is not scalable.
*   **PIVOT if:**
    *   **Detection Latency > 7 Days:** If the AI identifies a leak a week after it starts, you are missing the "quick win" savings. Pivot to using the system only for monthly auditing rather than real-time response.
    *   **Repair Conversion < 60%:** If the AI flags leaks that your teams cannot verify or fix within the pilot window, the issue is likely data granularity, not the algorithm. Pivot to a manual survey of those specific zones.

**Final Note:** Ask the vendor for a "Silent Period" clause in the contract—penalties if the system is offline or generating unusable data for more than 48 hours consecutively. This shifts the integration risk back to them.
