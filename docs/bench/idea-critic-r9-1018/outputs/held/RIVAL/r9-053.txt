**Recommendation: Do not move forward in the current form.**

The plan relies on a fatal assumption: that 70% of your donor base owns a compatible VR headset. Industry data suggests high-end VR penetration is typically under 5% of the general population, and likely lower among an older demographic typical of wildlife sanctuary donors. If even 90% of your donors lack the hardware, your projected $180,000 revenue lift evaporates immediately, leaving you with a $120,000 sunk cost and no return.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Hardware Barrier:** The "bring your own headset" model excludes the vast majority of donors. Unlike mobile apps, VR requires significant upfront investment by the user.
2.  **Infrastructure Reality:** Streaming 4K content to rural areas (both your sanctuary location and potentially rural donors) will face severe latency and bandwidth bottlenecks, causing motion sickness and drop-offs rather than immersion.
3.  **Adoption Friction:** Even donors with headsets rarely download niche apps. The friction of installing a custom Unity build will crush conversion rates well before they hit your 5% stop-loss metric.

**Pivot Trigger & Metric:**
Your current stop-loss (5% usage in Q1) is too late; you will have already spent the $120,000. You need a **pre-development validation trigger**.

*   **New Metric:** Before writing code or buying headsets, create a simple landing page describing the experience and ask donors to "Reserve a Spot" or "Request an Invite."
*   **The Pivot Rule:** If fewer than 10% of your email list clicks through to the landing page, or if fewer than 2% explicitly confirm they *own* a compatible headset in a follow-up survey, **pivot immediately** to a 360-degree web-based video tour accessible on smartphones. This captures 95%+ of your audience rather than chasing the <5% VR niche.

**Note:** The $15,000 studio retainer should be contingent on hitting the hardware-ownership validation milestone, not just time elapsed.
**Note:** Consider using the hardware budget to loan headsets to top-tier donors or set up on-site kiosks at fundraising galas instead of relying on home ownership.
