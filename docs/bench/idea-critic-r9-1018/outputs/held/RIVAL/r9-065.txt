**Recommendation:** Do not approve the full $12M rollout yet. Instead, authorize a **$1.5M "Spectrum-Contingent" Pilot** limited to 20km of the highest-density corridor. This pilot must validate the 70% fiber reuse assumption and secure a binding delivery guarantee from Apex Networks before any further capital is committed.

**Core Collapse Vectors:**
1.  **The Spectrum Cliff:** Your entire unit economics model assumes the license arrives by Q3 2025. If approval slips to Q1 2026, you burn $4M in idle infrastructure and vendor retainers with zero revenue, blowing the 18-month ROI window.
2.  **The Fiber Illusion:** Re-using 70% of existing fiber is a high-risk assumption. If legacy ducts are damaged or capacity is insufficient (requiring new trenching), your $4.2M savings evaporates, pushing capex toward the $18M Rwanda benchmark while targeting half the revenue.
3.  **Vendor Overload:** Apex's Rwanda success was 4G. 5G requires denser node placement and higher power precision. Assuming identical timelines despite higher technical complexity is a classic planning fallacy that will delay launch by at least 6 months.

**Pull-the-Plug/Pivot Triggers:**
*   **Regulatory Delay:** If the spectrum license is not physically in hand by **October 15, 2025**, halt all hardware procurement immediately. Pivot to a neutral-host model or delay launch to preserve cash.
*   **Civil Works Variance:** If the pilot reveals that fiber reuse is below **50%** (requiring significant new trenching), pivot the strategy to a wireless backhaul solution or reduce the coverage footprint to match the original budget.
*   **ARPU Shortfall:** If the pilot yields an ARPU below **$12** after 3 months, the $15 target is unrealistic for this corridor; pause expansion and rework the pricing/product bundle.

**First Leading Indicator to Watch:**
Monitor the **Civil Works Discovery Rate** in the first 5km of the pilot. Specifically, track the ratio of "usable existing ducts" vs. "new trenching required" per kilometer. If this ratio dips below 2:1 within the first 30 days of digging, your cost basis is already broken, and you should stop before deploying a single radio unit.
