**Subject: LitLink Proposal – Critical Risks & Recommendation**

Cian,

While the projected €45,000 monthly uplift is attractive, the strongest objection that could sink this plan is the **unverified integration risk with our existing POS combined with hidden compliance costs.** The vendor's €120,000 upfront fee and 3% transaction cost assume a "plug-and-play" reality that rarely exists in retail. If LitLink cannot seamlessly sync with our current POS without expensive custom middleware, or if GDPR compliance for 8,000 member profiles requires legal remediation we haven't budgeted for, the actual ROI could turn negative within the first quarter. The vendor's reliance on a 35% adoption rate (2,800 users) rather than our conservative 30% estimate further suggests their financial model is fragile; a miss here delays break-even significantly given the heavy upfront capital outlay.

**Smarter Route:**
Do not sign the full €120,000 contract yet. Instead, propose a **single-store pilot** for the June 15th launch. Negotiate a reduced upfront fee (e.g., €15,000) for one location to validate the POS integration, measure actual member adoption, and quantify compliance overhead. This caps our downside while proving the 20% revenue lift claim before rolling out to all 12 stores.

**Abandonment Trigger:**
We should abandon the idea entirely if, after a 60-day pilot, **actual app adoption falls below 20% of that store's loyalty members** or if **integration maintenance costs exceed 5% of generated ticket revenue.** These metrics directly test the vendor's core value proposition; failing them indicates the platform cannot scale profitably across the wider chain.
