**Recommended Course of Action**
Proceed with a **Phased Pilot Launch** at two locations only by 15 March, delaying the full eight-store rollout. This approach caps immediate capital exposure at 300 k PLN rather than 1.2 million PLN. Use the pilot to empirically measure the unquantified baker retraining hours and validate the supplier's ability to deliver the biodegradable film at current rates under live production stress. Make the remaining six locations contingent on the pilot achieving the projected 12% margin lift without exceeding a 10% labor efficiency drop.

**Principal Failure Modes**
1.  **Supplier Price Shock:** The polymer supplier invokes a force majeure or cost-pass-through clause due to the new Ministry regulations, erasing the projected 12% margin lift.
2.  **Labor Bottleneck:** Retraining takes 3× longer than anticipated, causing production delays that outweigh the 8% landfill fee savings.
3.  **Regulatory Whiplash:** The Ministry introduces a specific compliance fee for "certified biodegradable" materials not currently in the budget, turning the solution into a net cost liability.

**Abandonment Triggers**
You should abandon the plan entirely if either of the following occurs during the pilot phase:
*   **Cost Trigger:** The total cost per store (capital + retraining + compliance fees) exceeds **210 k PLN** (a 40% overrun on the 150 k base).
*   **Performance Trigger:** The net gross margin lift falls below **4%** after accounting for all new operational costs and labor inefficiencies.

**Note:** This strategy converts your unknown variables (labor and regulation) into measured data before committing the full 1.2 million PLN. It protects the company from a systemic rollout failure while keeping the zero-waste initiative alive.
