**Recommendation: NO-GO in current form.**

The plan contains a critical financial blind spot that threatens solvency before launch. Your $150k budget and revenue projections rely on a "low-double-digit" transaction fee guess. In African mobile money ecosystems, USSD and telco fees often range from 15% to 25% per transaction. If the fee is 20% on a $300 loan, you lose $60 immediately per loan, leaving only $240 to service. With a 12% default rate, your loss on defaults is $36 per loan. Your gross margin per loan becomes negative ($240 - $300 cost of funds - $36 default - $60 fee = -$156 loss per loan). You are effectively paying customers to borrow money.

**Principal Failure Modes:**

1.  **Fee Structure Catastrophe:** The unknown telco fee is not a variable; it is the business model. If it exceeds 8%, your unit economics likely collapse given the 12% default assumption. You cannot build software to fix a broken unit economics equation.
2.  **Coverage Illusion:** 95% network coverage does not equal 95% transaction success. USSD sessions time out frequently during peak hours or power outages common in the region. A 40% failure rate in transaction completion will destroy user trust and increase support costs, which are not budgeted.
3.  **Default Rate Underestimation:** Branch-based loans rely on social pressure and physical collateral. Digital-only loans to traders with no credit history often see default rates spike to 25-30% in the first cohort. At 25% defaults, the portfolio value drops to $1.125M, insufficient to cover operational costs and capital loss.

**Specific Outcomes to Pull the Plug or Pivot:**

*   **Pull the Plug:** If the formal telco quote confirms a per-transaction fee above 10%.
*   **Pull the Plug:** If a pilot of 100 loans shows a default rate exceeding 18% within 60 days.
*   **Pivot:** If the fee is high but manageable, pivot from a direct lender to a "lead generator" model where you charge banks a fee for verified leads, offloading the balance sheet risk and telco fee negotiation to established partners.

**Smarter Path:**

Do not build from scratch. Partner with an existing Mobile Network Operator (MNO) like MTN or Vodafone Cash who already has the rails. Offer your underwriting algorithm as a white-label service on their existing platform. This shifts the telco fee burden to them (as they own the pipe) and leverages their existing agent networks for cash-in/cash-out, reducing your CapEx from $150k to roughly $40k for integration and pilot marketing.
