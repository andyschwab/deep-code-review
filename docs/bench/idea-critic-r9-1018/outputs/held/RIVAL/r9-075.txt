**Recommendation:** Pivot from a hardware-heavy VR pilot to a **phased Augmented Reality (AR) partnership**. Instead of purchasing 30 headsets (€25,500 CAPEX) and risking low senior adoption, partner with a local university's computer science department to co-develop a "Bring Your Own Device" AR literary trail. This eliminates hardware maintenance, reduces the budget to primarily content licensing and staff training, and leverages patrons' existing smartphones, which have higher familiarity among the 65+ demographic.

**Primary Objection (The Deal-Breaker):**
The proposal relies on a **demographic mismatch that threatens ROI**. With 30% of your visitors aged 65+, assuming an 80% overall adoption rate is mathematically precarious. VR headsets often present barriers for seniors (motion sickness, weight, interface complexity). If the senior cohort opts out entirely, you must capture **114% of your remaining non-senior visitors** to hit the 80% total target. A more realistic adoption rate of 40–50% would fail to justify the €120,000 investment or the predicted footfall increase, leaving the library with expensive, unused hardware.

**How It Could Fail:**
1.  **Adoption Collapse:** Seniors avoid the experience due to usability issues, and general visitors treat it as a novelty rather than a repeat driver, missing the 15% footfall target.
2.  **Hidden Operational Drag:** The 200 allocated staff hours will likely be insufficient for sanitization, troubleshooting, and re-stocking, diverting resources from core library services and creating staff resentment.
3.  **Obsolescence Risk:** Dedicated VR hardware depreciates rapidly; if the pilot succeeds but requires a full hardware refresh in Year 2, the ongoing costs will exceed the €18,000 ancillary revenue gain.

**Stop/Change Trigger:**
Implement a **hard stop clause at Month 3**: If average weekly usage does not reach **15% of total visits** (adjusted from 5% to account for the novelty window) OR if **negative incident reports** (user discomfort, technical failures) exceed 2% of sessions, immediately halt expansion. Convert remaining budget to the AR partnership model or extend the license only if a clear path to breaking even by Month 6 is demonstrated.

*Note: This approach preserves the innovative "literary experience" goal while removing the massive financial risk of hardware ownership.*
