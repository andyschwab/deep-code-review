**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **conditional pilot** rather than the full $150k rollout. Commit only $25,000 immediately to scan 10 flagship artworks and secure a month-to-month platform trial. Require a firm, capped per-gigabyte streaming rate in writing before releasing funds for the remaining 110 artworks or the annual license. Present this to Maya as "de-risking the technology bet while validating demand."

**How this could collapse:**
1.  **The Bandwidth Trap:** If high-resolution streaming costs exceed $0.15/GB, your $20,000 hosting budget evaporates after roughly 1,300 user sessions, making the unit economics negative before you hit break-even.
2.  **Volume Delusion:** Selling 2,500 tickets at $12 requires converting 8.3% of your current revenue base into VR buyers immediately. If conversion mirrors standard digital museum rates (often <1%), you will sell ~300 tickets, generating $3,600 instead of the required $30,000 annually.
3.  **Concurrency Cap:** If the $70,000 license limits you to 500 *total registered users* rather than 500 *concurrent* users, you cannot scale beyond a tiny niche, capping revenue permanently.

**Arithmetic & Cost Gaps:**
*   **Revenue Shortfall:** Your break-even math assumes $30,000 from tickets (2,500 × $12). However, a 15% boost on $300,000 physical sales is $45,000. Even with a 3% commission ($1,350), total projected new revenue is $31,350. This leaves only $1,350 buffer against the $150k investment's operational costs, implying a payback period far longer than Q4 2026 unless operational costs are near zero (unlikely).
*   **Scan Cost Efficiency:** $45,000 for 120 scans is $375/artwork. If the pilot reveals complex pieces take 3x longer, this line item could double to $90,000.
*   **Marketing Burn:** $15,000 for a new product launch is extremely thin. Acquiring 2,500 paying users implies a Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) of $6. If industry CAC for cultural experiences is $20+, you can only afford 750 users, breaking the model.

**Concrete Pivot Metric:**
Pull the plug or pivot if **Month 3 ticket sales are below 180 units** (averaging 60/month) OR if the **verified streaming cost exceeds $0.10/GB**. The 800-ticket threshold you suggested is too high for a "kill" signal; by the time you miss 800 in a single month, you have likely already burned 6 months of runway. Fail fast at 180.
