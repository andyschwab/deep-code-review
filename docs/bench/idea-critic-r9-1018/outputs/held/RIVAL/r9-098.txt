**Recommendation: Do not proceed with the full €1.15M commitment yet.** Instead, approve a capped €250k "Phase 0" to validate the core assumptions before signing the €800k licensing deal. The current plan relies on two unproven variables that could turn a projected profit into a significant loss: the assumption of uniform conversion uplift and the undefined cloud compute costs for processing 2.5 million high-resolution assets.

**Principal Failure Modes:**

1.  **The "Uniform Uplift" Fallacy:** An 8-percentage-point lift (from 3.2% to 11.2%) is exceptionally aggressive for e-commerce. It is highly unlikely to be uniform. If the AI performs well on dresses but fails on complex items like structured blazers or shoes, the aggregate lift may drop to 2–3 points. At a lower uplift, the ROI may not cover the €800k licensing fee, especially if the model requires expensive human-in-the-loop corrections for failed generations.
2.  **Compute Cost Explosion:** You have allocated €150k for compute but have no pricing model for generating 500k shopper avatars and rendering try-ons for 2M images. Generative inference is costly. If the cost per render exceeds €0.10 due to latency requirements or model complexity, your operational burn could exceed €250k annually, eroding margins faster than the conversion lift can recover them.
3.  **Return Rate Cannibalization:** Virtual try-ons often increase consumer confidence to buy, but they can also increase returns if the digital fit does not match physical reality perfectly. If return rates climb from an industry standard of ~20% to 35% because the avatar simulation was slightly off, the logistics and restocking costs will wipe out the revenue gain from the higher conversion rate.

**Specific Abort Signal:**

**Abort the rollout immediately if the Pilot A/B test (Oct 1–15, 2025) shows a return rate increase of >5% relative to the control group, regardless of conversion performance.**

While low conversion is a revenue problem, a spike in returns is a margin killer that scales with success. If the AI drives more sales but those sales are unprofitable due to returns, you are effectively paying €800k to lose money faster. Do not wait for the full Q3 data; set this as a hard kill-switch in your dashboard for the first two weeks of traffic.
