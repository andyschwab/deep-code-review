**Recommendation: NO-GO (Proceed only with conditions)**

Do not approve the €2.5M investment in its current form. The proposal relies on unverified efficiency claims and ignores critical integration costs that threaten the 5-year payback window.

**Principal Failure Modes**

*   **Technical (Efficiency Mismatch):** The quoted 18% net efficiency is benchmarked against a competitor's 20% system running on a different fuel mix. If the actual capture rate drops to 14–15% due to our specific exhaust composition or duct geometry, annual savings could fall below €350k, extending payback beyond 7 years.
*   **Financial (Hidden Downtime):** The model assumes continuous 85% capacity but ignores retrofit downtime. If duct modification requires even 10 days of partial shutdown, the immediate production loss could exceed €200k, effectively adding 40% to the capital cost in year one.
*   **Regulatory (Carbon Credit Volatility):** The financial model likely treats carbon credits as a bonus. If credit prices stagnate or regulatory methodologies change regarding "avoided emissions" vs. "efficiency gains," this revenue stream could vanish, leaving the project reliant solely on fuel savings.

**Abort Trigger**

Establish a hard stop after the first 12 months of operation: **If verified net cash flow (fuel savings + credits – maintenance) is below €300,000, initiate immediate decommissioning or asset sale.**
