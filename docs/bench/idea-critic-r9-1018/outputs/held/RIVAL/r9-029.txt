**Recommendation:** Do not proceed to a full six-month pilot yet. Instead, commission a fixed-price, four-week **Technical & Compliance Feasibility Sprint** (£15k–£20k cap) to resolve the GDPR audit cost and CRM integration timeline before committing the remaining £230k.

**Devil's Advocate: How This Rollout Could Flop**
1.  **The "Smartphone" Illusion:** Assuming 80% capability ignores data poverty. Tenants with older devices or capped data plans may avoid voice interactions due to bandwidth costs, skewing feedback toward wealthier households and violating your "fair service" mandate.
2.  **The Integration Cliff:** If the vendor's voice engine cannot write back to your legacy CRM in real-time, you create a "data silo." Staff will ignore the bot's insights because they aren't in their daily workflow, rendering the £250k investment a standalone toy rather than an operational tool.
3.  **The GDPR Bill Shock:** If the compliance audit reveals you need a UK-hosted data enclave or complex consent re-engineering, costs could spike from £20k to £80k instantly, blowing the pilot's ROI before a single call is answered.
4.  **Adoption Friction:** Tenants hate navigating menus. If the voice AI misunderstands local dialects or requires perfect enunciation, frustration will drive *more* calls to the center as users seek human help to fix bot errors, increasing rather than decreasing volume.

**The Pull-the-Plug Metric**
**"Resolution Containment Rate" < 60% by Week 6.**
Do not wait for the full six months. If, after six weeks, the bot fails to resolve tenant queries without human escalation 60% of the time, terminate the pilot. A voice bot that merely triages problems without solving them adds friction without reducing call-center load, failing the primary economic thesis.

**Note:** Your comparison of £20/tenant vs. £150/paper survey is flawed; the true comparator is the marginal cost of a digital form or email, which is near-zero. Ensure the pilot measures *incremental* value over existing low-cost digital channels, not just paper.
