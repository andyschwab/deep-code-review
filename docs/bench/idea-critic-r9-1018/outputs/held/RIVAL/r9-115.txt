**Recommendation: Pitch a 90-Day "Lite" Pilot, Not the Full 12-Month Rollout**

Do not pitch the $120,000, 12-month plan tomorrow. The satellite backhaul cost is an unpriced existential risk, and assuming 80% download adoption for a niche utility in remote areas is dangerously optimistic. Instead, propose a **$25,000, 90-day proof-of-concept** limited to 200 users on two specific high-value trails. Use existing guide tablets to stream sightings to a simple web view (no app store download required) to validate the connectivity costs and actual user engagement before committing to full hardware procurement or custom app development.

**Principal Failure Modes**
*   **Connectivity Cost Blowout:** If real-world usage spikes to 2GB/user/month due to map tiles or image caching, your $0.10/GB estimate could become a $60,000 annual overrun, destroying the unit economics.
*   **Adoption Friction:** Requiring tourists to download an app, create an account, and enable location services *before* reaching signal-less zones will likely result in <20% adoption, not 80%.
*   **Hardware Fragility:** Rugged tablets in Fiordland's wet, cold environment may fail faster than anticipated, creating support nightmares when guides cannot access sightings.

**Trigger Points to Pull the Plug**
*   **Connectivity:** If the pilot reveals average data costs exceed **$0.25/GB** or latency prevents "live" updates (>5 minute delay).
*   **Adoption:** If **<30%** of targeted guests actively use the tracking feature during the 90-day trial.
*   **Value:** If booking uplift attributable to the app is **<5%** after the trial period.

**What You Are Overlooking & Better Alternatives**
You are overlooking the **friction of the "last mile."** Tourists in Fiordland care about the view, not troubleshooting app permissions. A better alternative is a **Guide-Centric SMS/WhatsApp Bot**. Guides carry the rugged hardware; they receive satellite pings and broadcast simplified text updates ("Kakapo spotted 200m North") to guests via local Wi-Fi hotspots or pre-trip SMS. This eliminates the need for guest smartphones to have satellite data, removes the app download barrier entirely, and caps your data costs to the guide's device only. It tests the core value proposition (live sightings drive bookings) without the $120k capex risk.
