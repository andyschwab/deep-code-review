**Recommendation: NO-GO (in current form).** Proceed only as a tightly constrained pilot until three critical assumptions are stress-tested. The current plan confuses *capacity* with *demand* and underestimates regulatory friction.

**Chief Ways This Could Flop:**

1.  **The Demand Fallacy:** You assume clinics *can* handle 500 extra consults, not that patients will *pay* for them. Filling 500 slots/month requires ~17 new paying patients per clinic, every single day. Without a proven customer acquisition engine, you are building infrastructure for empty rooms. Revenue is likely overstated by 60–80% in Year 1.
2.  **The "95% Uptime" Mirage:** In Nairobi and Mombasa, "internet reliability" isn't just uptime; it's power stability and last-mile latency. Tele-diagnostics fails violently at 80% bandwidth. Your $2.5M budget likely lacks a dedicated line-item for redundant power (generators/solar) and Starlink/4G failovers per site. One outage during a diagnostic session creates massive liability.
3.  **Regulatory Black Box:** Operating without Ministry clearance is a shutdown risk, not just a delay. If the Ministry imposes data-localization rules or bans remote diagnosis by non-residents after you've scaled, your tech stack becomes stranded assets. The "pilot" starting Jan 3rd may already be in technical violation if final clearance is pending.
4.  **Unit Economics Drift:** At $12/consult, your margin is razor-thin. You haven't accounted for payment processing fees, patient no-shows (often 20%+ in public-facing health), or the cost of medical indemnity insurance for remote errors. The break-even point is likely 350+ consults, not 200.

**The Precise Signal to Pull the Plug:**
Your proposed signal (200 consults) is too lagging; by then you've burned months of OpEx. Use this **dual-trigger kill switch**:
*   **Trigger A:** Average revenue per clinic < $1,800/month (approx. 150 *paid* consults) for **two consecutive months**.
*   **Trigger B:** Ministry of Health clearance not received in writing by **Month 4**.
*   **Action:** If *either* triggers, freeze expansion immediately and pivot to a service-bureau model or shut down.

**What Would Change My Mind:**
I would flip to a conditional **GO** only if you:
1.  **Pre-sell Demand:** Show signed LOIs from payers (insurers/corporates) guaranteeing 200 consults/month across the pilot clinics *before* full rollout.
2.  **Harden Infrastructure:** Allocate 15% of the budget ($375k) specifically for offline-first tech and power redundancy, proving the system works at 40% bandwidth.
3.  **De-risk Regulation:** Secure a "letter of non-objection" from the Ministry allowing the pilot to operate legally while full clearance is processed.

**Note:** Your arithmetic holds ($12 × 500 × 150 ≈ $900k/month potential), but the model assumes 100% utilization which never happens in healthcare.
