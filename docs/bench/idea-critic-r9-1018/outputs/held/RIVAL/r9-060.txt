**Recommendation: Proceed with Conditional Approval**

Move forward with the Eco Spiral pilot, but immediately freeze the "70% enrollment via new app" assumption as a critical risk rather than a planning baseline. The $2.3M budget and 30% diversion target are ambitious but defensible *only* if you decouple service success from app adoption. Treat the app as a secondary channel; rely on low-friction, non-digital enrollment (e.g., opt-out mailers, curbside signage) to secure the volume needed for the waste reduction math to hold.

**Top Failure Modes**

1.  **Enrollment Cliff:** The 70% household enrollment rate is the plan's single point of failure. If the app launch stalls or user friction is high, enrollment could cap at 30–40%, rendering the 1,500-ton diversion target mathematically impossible regardless of operational efficiency.
2.  **Integration Cost Overrun:** Unknown ERP integration costs threaten to cannibalize operational funds. If custom middleware is required to track waste streams against billing, the pilot could burn through capital before collecting meaningful data.
3.  **Baseline Data Flaw:** The 30% reduction relies on 2023 city-wide data (5,000 tons). If the 2,500 targeted blocks have different waste profiles than the city average, the absolute tonnage goal will be misaligned, making the pilot appear to fail even if performance is strong relative to its specific zone.

**Exact Plug-Pull Triggers**

Execute the stop-work order immediately if **either** of the following occurs:

*   **Diversion Lag:** Actual verified diversion falls below **12%** (40% of the 30% target) after the first **three months** (by 1 June 2025). This indicates the service model itself is not resonating, not just a slow start.
*   **Budget Breach:** Confirmed ERP integration costs exceed the allocated software budget by more than **15%**. This signals hidden technical debt that will make post-pilot maintenance financially unsustainable.

*Note: Secure a fixed-price cap for the ERP integration before March 1 to eliminate the cost unknown. Do not pilot the technology and the behavior change simultaneously without a manual fallback for enrollment.*
