**Recommendation: NO-GO for the pilot in its current structure.**

The pilot is financially negative from day one. You are paying €18,000 to save a projected €11,700, creating an immediate net loss of €6,300 before accounting for the 2% transaction surcharge. Unless the 2% surcharge is capped or waived, it will likely exceed the vendor fee itself given your volume, turning this into a massive cost center. Furthermore, the projected savings rely entirely on a 15% incident reduction; if the actual reduction is only 10%, your savings drop to €7,800, widening the loss gap significantly. Do not proceed until the vendor fee is reduced to at least €8,000 (to break even at 15% reduction) or the success metric is contractually guaranteed with a refund clause.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Behavioral Backfire:** Drivers with low ratings may become defensive or quit the platform, increasing churn beyond the current 5% and reducing fleet availability.
2.  **Surcharge Explosion:** The 2% transaction fee on *all* rides (not just accidents) could cost CarCoop far more than the accident savings if ride volume is high.
3.  **False Positives:** If the algorithm incorrectly flags safe drivers, you risk legal liability and member revolt.

**Tripwire Condition:**
Halt the project immediately if the **cost per prevented accident** exceeds **€1,500**. Calculate this weekly: *(Vendor Fee + Surcharge Costs) / Number of Accidents Prevented vs. Baseline*. If this ratio crosses €1,500, the system is more expensive than simply absorbing the accident costs.
