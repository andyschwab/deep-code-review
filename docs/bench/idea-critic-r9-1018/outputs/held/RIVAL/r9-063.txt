If members downgrade to the digital tier rather than adding it, your net revenue will collapse. You have not modeled churn, yet digital services typically see 5–10% monthly churn, which would drastically reduce your month-six revenue run rate.

You should abandon or fundamentally rethink the concept if you achieve **less than 25% conversion of the existing membership base (300 subscribers) by the end of Month Two.**

Hitting only 25% early on indicates that the value proposition does not resonate with your warmest audience. If your current members won't buy it at a discount, new users certainly won't buy it at full price. Proceeding beyond this point would simply burn cash validating a product-market fit that does not exist.
