**Strongest Objection:** The proposal commits a category error by treating a **hardware-constrained environment** as a software-only fix. Your legacy PLCs likely operate on scan cycles (e.g., 500ms–2s) that may be too slow to capture the high-frequency vibration or thermal signatures required for *predictive* (vs. preventive) maintenance. If the PLCs cannot sample fast enough, no amount of cloud modeling will predict failures; you will simply be building an expensive dashboard for data that is already too stale to act upon. The 18% downtime reduction assumes the data exists to make predictions, which is unproven.

**Main Ways This Plan Could Fail:**
1.  **The Data Fidelity Trap:** The pilot reveals PLC latency smears critical failure signals, rendering the AI models inaccurate. You end up paying 3.7M RUB/year for false positives that erode technician trust.
2.  **The Hidden CAPEX Cliff:** The 7.2M RUB sensor upgrade is just the floor. Legacy integration often requires gateway hardware, network segmentation, and significant DS team overtime, potentially doubling the upfront cost to >10M RUB.
3.  **Inventory Mismatch:** Reducing spare parts from 2,500 to 1,850 units relies on perfect prediction. If the model misses a failure mode, you face critical downtime waiting for parts you deliberately deleted from inventory.

**Trigger Condition to Walk Away:**
 Halt the project immediately if the **4-week pilot demonstrates that PLC scan rates exceed 50% of the lead time required to intervene** on your top 3 most costly failure modes. If the data arrives too late to order the part or schedule the repair before the break, the predictive premise is dead.

**Alternative to Consider:**
Before signing the OrbitForge license, run a **"Shadow Mode" audit**. Install the sensors on one critical line, stream data locally (bypassing the cloud), and have your DS team attempt to replicate just *one* historical failure using the legacy PLC data alone. If they cannot reconstruct the failure signature from existing logs, do not buy the software. Prove the signal exists before buying the predictor.
