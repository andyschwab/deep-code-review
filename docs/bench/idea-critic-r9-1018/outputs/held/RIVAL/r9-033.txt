**Recommendation:** Do not approve the rollout in its current form. The financial model relies on an unverified assumption about data consumption that could inflate your five-year operating cost by 300%, turning a manageable pilot into a fiscal liability. You must mandate a "Data Consumption Cap" pilot phase before committing to the full 120,000-user license.

**Strongest Objection: The Data Cost Black Box**
Your budget assumes stable data rates, but it ignores volume volatility. Cold-chain alerts and automated reminders are data-intensive. If each of the 120,000 patients consumes just 50MB extra per month due to rich-media alerts, your annual data bill jumps from the projected ~21 million KES to over 90 million KES. The license fee (18 million KES over five years) is negligible compared to the potential data overrun. You are betting the clinic's operational budget on a network provider's per-megabyte rate remaining static while your volume explodes.

**Better Alternative Missed: USSD and SMS-First Hybrid**
You assumed 80% smartphone ownership is sufficient, but this excludes the most vulnerable 20% who often have the highest vaccine dropout rates. Instead of a pure mobile app rollout, adopt a hybrid model: use USSD (unstructured supplementary service data) for appointment confirmations and basic tracking, which works on every mobile phone ever made and costs a fraction of data. Reserve the expensive tablet/app infrastructure only for health workers managing cold-chain logistics, not for patient-facing reminders. This reduces your tablet requirement from 150 to perhaps 30 and eliminates the patient data cost variable entirely.

**Failure Modes**
1.  **Cost Spiral:** Data usage per patient exceeds 20MB/month, causing the total cost of ownership to exceed 150 million KES over five years, diverting funds from actual vaccine procurement.
2.  **Adoption Cliff:** The 20% without smartphones are systematically excluded, creating a two-tier health system and lowering overall herd immunity metrics despite high app usage.
3.  **Hardware Obsolescence:** The 150 tablets selected today may not support OS updates required by the app in Year 3, forcing a premature, unbudgeted hardware refresh.

**The Kill Trigger**
Stop the rollout immediately if a 30-day stress test with 5,000 users shows average monthly data consumption per user exceeding **15MB**. At that volume, the projected five-year data cost surpasses 60 million KES, violating the principle of fiscal sustainability. If this threshold is breached, pivot immediately to the SMS/USSD hybrid model or renegotiate a fixed-cost bulk data package with the provider before spending another shilling on licenses.
