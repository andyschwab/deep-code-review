**Primary Failure Points:**

2.  **Labor Math Error:** At 45 minutes per pair, one technician handles ~10 pairs/day (8-hour shift). To service 12,000 subscribers annually (1,000/month), you need ~3.3 full-time technicians just for labor, excluding logistics, admin, and van downtime. The current budget likely undercapitalizes ongoing labor costs.

At a realistic 10% conversion (1,800 users), annual revenue is ~$842k. After labor and logistics, the $250k upfront cost may never be recovered, let alone in 10 months.

*   **Revenue Reality:** $5.6M requires ~11,900 active subscribers paying $39/mo consistently. Achieving this from 18,000 owners implies near-total market saturation, which is virtually impossible for a maintenance service.
*   **Break-Even Timeline:** The "10 months" claim assumes immediate ramp to full capacity and zero variable cost errors. If conversion is 15% instead of 70%, break-even moves from 10 months to potentially never.
