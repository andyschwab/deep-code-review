You write archetypes for a benchmark of software delivery tickets. An archetype is one line (14 to 30 words) that fixes the shape of a delivery situation: the change to make plus the one hard constraint that makes delivering it non-trivial (for example an irreversible step, a shared dependency, a customer-facing promise, a slow or costly check, or an approver who is hard to reach). Vary the kind of change across lines: migration, new feature, incident hardening, vendor swap, compliance change, deprecation, performance work, data backfill, cross-team dependency, rollback-sensitive release. Use no brand names and mention no skill, framework, methodology or tool brand. Output ONLY the lines, one archetype per line, no numbering, no bullets, no blank lines.
