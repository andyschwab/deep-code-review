# Delivery Plan: Identity Verification Sync

## Lanes and Parallelism
Work is divided into three serialized phases with one parallel track.
1.  **Preparation Lane (Digital Governance Team):** Isolate the `council-thresholds.json` modification and biometric handler updates. This must occur first to prevent audit log mismatches.
2.  **Parallel Validation Track:** While code is prepared, the Senior Systems Administrator prepares the manual deployment trigger script, and the Security Lead pre-authorizes the specific false positive signatures expected from the new biometric handlers. This prevents the 55-minute CI pipeline from failing due to known security flags.
3.  **Execution Lane (Joint Officials):** The Mayor, City Clerk, and Council Chair operate in a single, synchronized window. No work proceeds here until the Preparation Lane is complete.

## Gates and Checks
*   **Gate 1 (Code Freeze):** The Digital Governance Team locks the `citizen-services` module on a specific release candidate revision. No further commits are accepted until the joint sign-off is complete.
*   **Gate 2 (Pre-Flight CI):** The CI pipeline runs on the frozen revision in a isolated sandbox *before* the officials convene. This verifies the build succeeds and the Security Lead's overrides are correctly applied. If this fails, the meeting is cancelled to save the 400-credit cost and avoid delaying officials.
*   **Gate 3 (Staging Verification):** The three officials access the staging environment simultaneously. The system must present a unified dashboard showing the updated validation logic. Approval requires three distinct digital signatures on the change request form within a 30-minute window. If any signature is missing, the deployment is immediately aborted; staggered approval is forbidden.
*   **Gate 4 (Production Trigger):** Only after all three signatures are cryptographically verified does the Senior Systems Administrator manually trigger the production deployment.

## Review Strategy
*   **Technical Review:** Conducted by the Digital Governance Team Lead prior to Gate 1. Focuses strictly on the integrity of `council-thresholds.json` and the logic of the document validator.
*   **Security Review:** Conducted by the Security Lead prior to Gate 2. Validates that the manual overrides for biometric handlers are scoped correctly to prevent broader vulnerabilities.
*   **Compliance Review:** Conducted jointly by the Mayor, City Clerk, and Council Chair during Gate 3. They verify the functional behavior in staging against the tri-council mandate. This review is independent of the technical team; the officials do not rely on developer assurances but on their own observation of the staging environment.

## Landing and Rollback
*   **Merge Strategy:** The release does not use a standard pull request merge. It utilizes a "Promotion" model where the verified staging artifact is promoted to production only after the triple-signature gate passes.
*   **Human Decision Point:** The Senior Systems Administrator holds the final "Go/No-Go" key. Even with three signatures, if the pre-flight health checks (latency, error rates) on the production edge nodes are abnormal, the Administrator must halt the trigger.
*   **Rollback Protocol:**
    *   **Trigger:** If residents report lockouts or validation errors within 15 minutes of deployment, or if audit logs show data mismatches.
    *   **Action:** Immediate reversion to the previous stable artifact. Do not attempt to patch the live system.
    *   **Testing:** The rollback procedure must be dry-run in the sandbox environment during the Preparation Lane to ensure the previous version restores access instantly.
    *   **Constraint:** Because reverting mid-process risks locking out residents, the rollback script includes a "circuit breaker" that maintains read-only access to existing permits while the verification service restarts, ensuring no user is stranded during the transition.

This plan ensures financial efficiency by gating the expensive CI run, enforces the strict simultaneous approval requirement, and prioritizes resident access through a tested, immediate rollback capability.
