### Delivery Plan: Policy Engine v4.2 Rollback & Audit Preservation

**1. Lanes & Parallelism**
*   **Lane A (Coordination & Approval):** Incident Commander secures dual approval from Security Lead and Platform Architect. This is a strict serial gate before any code movement.
*   **Lane B (Audit Safeguard):** Database Engineer isolates the shared append-only audit ledger. They configure read-only snapshots for the compliance team and disable all non-essential write-heavy validation queries to prevent budget triggers. This runs parallel to approval gathering but must complete before rollback initiation.
*   **Lane C (Deployment Prep):** Release Engineer prepares the v4.1 deployment package in the 'BlueFin' pipeline. They explicitly disable the automated smoke test suite for the secondary region to bypass known flakiness.

**2. Gates & Checks**
*   **Gate 1 (Authorization):** Deployment halts until dual approval tokens are present in the pipeline.
*   **Gate 2 (Audit Integrity):** Before rollback, Lane B confirms the audit ledger snapshot is queryable and contains the last six hours of data. No schema reversal attempts will be made due to irreversibility risks; the schema remains at v4.2 while the application logic reverts.
*   **Gate 3 (Primary Region Health):** Post-deployment, the only reliable gate is manual verification of the primary region. An engineer must confirm legitimate user access is restored and permission denials have ceased. Secondary region status is monitored but does not block success declaration due to flaky tests.

**3. Review Strategy**
*   **Independent Reviewer:** A Senior SRE not involved in the original v4.2 deployment reviews the rollback execution plan and the audit preservation steps.
*   **Review Timing:** Occurs immediately after Gate 1 (Approval) but before Lane C triggers the pipeline.
*   **Focus Areas:** Verification that audit log queries are disabled during the write-heavy rollback window and confirmation that the secondary region's smoke tests are muted.

**4. Landing & Merge Strategy**
*   **Execution:** Once approved and reviewed, the 'BlueFin' pipeline executes the v4.1 rollback across all three regions simultaneously.
*   **Success Criteria:** Manual confirmation of restored access in the primary region + audit ledger accessibility verified by the compliance team.
*   **Human Decision Points:**
    *   If primary region manual verification fails after 15 minutes, the Incident Commander decides whether to attempt a second rollback or initiate emergency maintenance mode.
    *   Compliance team must sign off that the audit trail is complete before closing the incident.
*   **Rollback of Rollback:** Not possible via standard pipeline due to schema constraints. Any further issues require full backup restore procedures involving Infrastructure Leadership.
*   **Post-Incident:** The v4.2 code remains frozen. A separate ticket will address the schema migration reversal once a full backup strategy is confirmed. Audit ledger cost monitoring resumes normal operations only after rollback writes stabilize.

**Critical Constraints Enforced:**
*   Zero audit log truncation or archiving during the process.
*   No schema reversal attempts.
*   Secondary region automated tests ignored for success determination.
*   Budget trigger avoidance via limited log validation during high-write periods.

This plan prioritizes immediate service restoration while guaranteeing audit trail preservation for compliance requirements, acknowledging the technical constraints of the shared ledger and database schema.
