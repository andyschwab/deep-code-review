### Delivery Plan: Urgent Waitlist Slot Refill Automation

#### 1. Work Lanes & Parallelization
*   **Lane A (Core Logic - Serialized):** Develop the urgency-tier sorting algorithm and slot-matching engine. This must be completed before Lane B can integrate.
    *   *Constraint:* Must strictly enforce Level 1 > Level 2 > Level 3 logic, ignoring join-time for higher tiers.
*   **Lane B (Audit & Logging - Parallel to A):** Implement the immutable audit log schema and write events for every decision (slot ID, candidate ID, urgency tier, timestamp, reason for selection/rejection).
*   **Lane C (Trigger Mechanism - Parallel to A):** Build the cancellation event listener to trigger the refill process within seconds.
*   **Lane D (Safety & Dry-Run - Serialized after A/B/C):** Implement the "dry-run" mode that simulates the logic against production data snapshots without sending notifications or booking slots. This is critical due to the lack of real urgency data in staging.

#### 2. Gates & Checks
*   **Gate 1: Unit Test Suite (Local/Pre-Commit):**
    *   *Revision:* Feature branch head.
    *   *Check:* Must cover all urgency tier permutations and the specific race condition scenario in the notification sender.
    *   *Constraint:* Tests must be deterministic; flaky tests are forbidden due to the 45-minute CI cost.
*   **Gate 2: Dry-Run Verification (Staging):**
    *   *Revision:* Release candidate tagged `rc-1`.
    *   *Check:* Execute against a production data snapshot in dry-run mode. Verify logs show correct tier prioritization over join-time.
    *   *Owner:* QA Engineer + Product Owner (to validate clinical logic).
*   **Gate 3: CI Pipeline (Automated):**
    *   *Revision:* Same `rc-1`.
    *   *Check:* Full suite pass. If failed, immediate halt; no partial merges allowed due to shared database schema with billing.
*   **Gate 4: Senior Lead Approval (Manual):**
    *   *Constraint:* Must occur Monday–Thursday. David Chen must personally approve the production deploy. No Friday deploys.

#### 3. Review Strategy
*   **Code Review:**
    *   *Reviewer:* Senior Backend Engineer (not the author, not David Chen).
    *   *Focus:* Concurrency handling in the notification sender (to prevent duplicate alerts), strictness of the urgency comparator, and audit log completeness.
    *   *Independence:* Reviewer must not have contributed to Lane A or C.
*   **Logic Audit:**
    *   *Reviewer:* Compliance Officer or Clinical Lead.
    *   *Focus:* Review the dry-run logs to certify that no Level 1 patient was bypassed for a Level 3. This is a non-code sign-off required before Gate 4.

#### 4. Landing & Rollback
*   **Merge Strategy:**
    *   Merge to `main` only after Gates 1–3 pass and the Logic Audit is signed off.
    *   Deploy to production immediately after merge *if* it is Monday–Thursday and David Chen is available. If Friday, hold the deployment until Monday.
*   **Human Decision Points:**
    *   David Chen's explicit approval for the production deploy command.
    *   Compliance sign-off on the dry-run audit logs.
*   **Rollback Plan:**
    *   *Trigger:* Any report of incorrect tier prioritization or duplicate notifications post-deploy.
    *   *Action:* Immediate feature flag toggle to disable automation (reverting to manual process).
    *   *Data Remediation:* If incorrect bookings occurred, execute the manual database validation script (2-hour process) to undo changes. This script must be pre-tested in the dry-run environment before launch day.
    *   *Communication:* Alert clinical staff immediately to resume manual calling until the fix is deployed.

This plan prioritizes clinical safety and auditability over speed of delivery, acknowledging the high cost of errors and the constraints of the deployment window.
