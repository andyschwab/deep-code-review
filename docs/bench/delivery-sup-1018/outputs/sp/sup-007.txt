### Delivery Plan: Offline-First Fuel Telemetry Module

**Lanes & Parallelism**
*   **Lane A (Code Finalization):** Engineering integrates the updated local storage logic and sync mechanism into the `northstar-fleet` monorepo. This lane runs strictly against the `vehicle-protocol` library's current version-locked state; no updates to this library are permitted in this deployment.
*   **Lane B (Staging Environment Prep):** DevOps provisions the staging environment to mirror the northern sector's connectivity gaps. This includes configuring network shapers to simulate total signal loss for 12+ hours.
*   **Parallel Execution:** Lanes A and B proceed simultaneously. Lane A cannot merge until Lane B is ready for manual verification.

**Gates & Checks**
*   **Gate 1: Unit & Integration Suite (Automated):** Runs on the primary CI runner immediately upon commit. The flaky `offline-sync` test on the secondary runner is bypassed automatically but flagged for manual review.
*   **Gate 2: Heavy-Load Simulation (Automated + Cost Control):** Triggered only after Gate 1 passes. This $450, 50-minute simulation must run on the specific commit hash intended for production. Failure here aborts the pipeline immediately to prevent wasted spend.
*   **Gate 3: Manual Staging Verification (Human-in-the-Loop):** Mandatory due to CI flakiness. Engineers deploy the Gate 2-passed build to staging. They must physically verify:
    1.  Continuous logging during a simulated 12-hour blackout.
    2.  Graceful sync of large backlogs upon reconnection.
    3.  Zero data loss in billing audit logs.

**Review Process**
*   **Technical Review:** Performed by a senior engineer *not* involved in Lane A coding. This review occurs after Gate 1 but before Gate 2 to avoid simulation costs on flawed code. It focuses on the local storage implementation and sync queue logic.
*   **Compliance Review:** Conducted by the Logistics Engineering lead to ensure the `vehicle-protocol` version lock was respected and no unauthorized dependencies were introduced.

**Landing Strategy**
*   **Approval Gate:** Before any firmware signing, explicit written approval must be obtained from Jane Smith. This approval must reference the specific commit hash that passed Gate 2 and Gate 3.
*   **Deployment Window:** The Over-The-Air (OTA) push is scheduled for a low-activity window to monitor initial rollout.
*   **Irreversibility Constraint:** Since the OTA action is irreversible for 48 hours, the rollout uses a canary strategy:
    1.  Deploy to 5% of the fleet (non-critical routes).
    2.  Monitor telemetry ingestion and billing audit integrity for 2 hours.
    3.  If stable, expand to 50%, then 100%.
*   **Rollback Protocol:** True rollback is impossible for 48 hours. If critical failure occurs in the canary phase:
    1.  Halt expansion immediately.
    2.  Isolate affected units from the billing pipeline to prevent audit corruption.
    3.  Prepare a hotfix build that bypasses the new logic (feature flag off) if available, or wait out the 48-hour lock for a corrective push.

**Human Decisions Required**
1.  **Jane Smith:** Written approval to proceed to OTA signing.
2.  **Logistics Lead:** Sign-off on manual staging verification results.
3.  **Ops Commander:** Go/No-Go decision to expand from 5% canary to full fleet based on the 2-hour monitoring window.

This plan prioritizes data continuity over speed, acknowledging the irreversible nature of the deployment and the specific risks of the northern sector coverage gaps.
