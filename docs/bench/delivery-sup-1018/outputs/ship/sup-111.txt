**Delivery Plan: Premium Gift Wrap Tri-Partner Sync**

**Lanes & Parallelism**

*   **Lane B (Core Logic):** Implement synchronous validation in `OrderService`. Logic: Call Partner 1, 2, 3 sequentially or in parallel (await all); if any fail/timeout, throw specific error, log Partner ID, abort transaction. No partial states allowed.
*   **Lane C (Staging Hardening):** Replace mock responses in staging with deterministic fault-injection scripts to eliminate historical flakiness. Ensure timeouts trigger exactly at 12s. Runs parallel to Lane B but must finish before G5.

*   **Rollback Plan:** If production logs show unexpected "Validating" stuck states or high timeout rates post-launch, immediate feature flag flip (if available) or pipeline rollback to previous SHA. Database hotfix prepared for stuck orders.
