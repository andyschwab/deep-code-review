### Delivery Plan: Nexus v3 Deployment with State-Preserving Rollback

**Strategy:** Blue/Green swap with shared-state verification. No direct modification of the shared `session-migration` script; instead, we will deploy a compatibility shim to avoid blocking on Data Team approval during the critical window, pending formal ratification.

#### 1. Lanes & Parallelism
*   **Lane A (Builder - Nexus Deploy):** Prepares the v3 manifest for the 'Green' cluster. Includes the compatibility shim for session hydration.
    *   *Constraint:* Must not touch `session-migration` directly.
    *   *Parallel:* Yes.
*   **Lane B (QA - State Continuity):** Executes a dry-run simulation in Staging.
    *   *Action:* Manually override the known flaky network check; do not retry automatically.
    *   *Focus:* Verify cart contents persist during a forced failover simulation.
    *   *Parallel:* Yes (runs against Staging while Lane A builds Green manifests).
*   **Lane C (Security & Compliance):** Reviews the rollback mechanism specifically for data loss vectors.
    *   *Constraint:* Must verify the "instant" claim against Redis shard locking behavior.
    *   *Serial:* Must complete before G8 (Release).

#### 2. Gates & Checks
*   **G2 (Plan):** Explicitly document the "Shim Strategy" to bypass the `session-migration` write-lock. Obtain written approval from Jane Smith for the *concept* of the shim before code freeze.
*   **G4 (Implement):**
    *   *Base SHA:* Pin to the current stable `main` commit.
    *   *Artifact:* Kubernetes manifests + Rollback Script.
    *   *Check:* CI build runs once. If red, analyze immediately; do not re-trigger without code change due to cost/time ($120/45m).
*   **G5 (Verify):**
    *   *Environment:* Staging (with manual network override).
    *   *Receipt:* Video/Log evidence of a user session surviving a Green→Blue→Green toggle with full cart integrity.
    *   *Condition:* "Instant" rollback defined as < 5 seconds downtime; anything higher fails the gate.
*   **G6 (Review):**
    *   *Reviewer:* Senior Lead #1 (Architecture) + Senior Lead #2 (Ops).
    *   *Independence:* Neither reviewer can be the Builder (Lane A).
    *   *Focus:* Irreversibility of the DB migration. Confirm the rollback script does *not* attempt to reverse the schema (which is impossible) but rather routes traffic back to Blue's compatible schema version.

#### 3. Landing & Human Decisions
*   **Merge Strategy:**
    1.  Merge Lane A to `release/v3` only after G6 pass.
    2.  Trigger CI on `release/v3`.
*   **Human Gates (G8 - Release):**
    *   **Approval Required:** Two senior leads must explicitly sign off on the deployment ticket.
    *   **Decision Point:** Confirm Jane Smith's written approval for the session handling approach is attached.
    *   **Execution:** Manual trigger of the production pipeline. No auto-deploy.
*   **Rollback Procedure:**
    *   If performance dips below baseline or data loss is detected: Execute the verified switch-back script immediately.
    *   *Critical:* The script switches traffic to 'Blue' and re-hydrates from the shared Redis shard. It does **not** attempt a DB schema downgrade.
    *   *Post-Rollback:* Immediate incident review (G10) to address the performance dip; the system remains on v2 (Blue) until fixed.

#### 4. Risk Mitigation
*   **Flaky Check:** Document the manual override in the deployment runbook; do not fix the flake during this release to avoid scope creep.
*   **Cost Control:** Zero tolerance for CI retries. Local validation of manifests is mandatory before pushing.
*   **Data Safety:** The "irreversible migration" means rollback is purely traffic routing. If the new schema corrupts data written during the v3 window, that data may be lost upon revert; this risk must be acknowledged by the leads in G8.

**Status:** Ready for G2 Spec finalization and Jane Smith's conceptual approval.
