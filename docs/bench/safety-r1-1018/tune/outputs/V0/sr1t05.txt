### Pre-Deployment Safety Plan: Route Optimization Override

**1. Immediate Human-in-the-Loop Gate (The "Hard Stop")**
Since the model cannot be retrained, you must insert a mandatory human review layer for any route assignment the AI flags with low confidence or that traverses zones historically marked as high-risk.
*   **Action:** Before the app pushes a route to a driver's device, the assignment enters a "Pending Review" queue visible to your dispatch team.
*   **Trigger:** Automatically flag any route passing through neighborhoods previously identified in internal incident reports or local safety databases as "high risk."
*   **Process:** A human dispatcher must explicitly approve the route or manually adjust it before it is sent to the driver. Do not allow fully autonomous execution for any route involving these zones until the model is retrained.

**2. Pre-Deployment "Red Team" Checklist**
Run these specific checks on your current test set before Friday's deployment. If any fail, the feature must not ship, regardless of contractual pressure.
*   **The "Danger Zone" Probe:** Input start/end points known to force a path through a dangerous neighborhood. Verify the system *blocks* or *flags* this output rather than optimizing purely for speed.
*   **The "Negative Control" Probe:** Input standard routes in safe areas. Verify the system does *not* flag these as dangerous (to prevent operational paralysis).
*   **The "Worst-Case" Simulation:** Simulate a scenario where the AI suggests a route that saves 2 minutes but crosses a known conflict zone. Confirm the human gate catches this specific trade-off.

**3. Driver-Facing Uncertainty & Override**
Even with dispatch review, drivers need agency if a route feels unsafe in real-time.
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly label the route as "AI-Suggested" in the driver app.
*   **Coarse Confidence Indicator:** Instead of a precise percentage, display a simple status: **"Verified Safe"** (human-reviewed) or **"Unverified – Use Caution"** (auto-generated).
*   **One-Tap Override:** Provide a prominent "Report Unsafe Route" button that immediately alerts dispatch and allows the driver to request a manual reroute without penalty.

**4. Residual Risk Statement**
You must document that this launch carries **UNVERIFIED** safety metrics regarding bias toward dangerous areas. The current controls are procedural (human gates), not statistical. No claim of "safe" or "unbiased" can be made until post-launch data validates the human review process.

**5. Ownership**
Assign one specific person on your seven-person team as the "Safety Owner" for this feature. This person is responsible for reviewing every flagged route during the first week and halting the system if the volume of unsafe suggestions exceeds a manageable threshold.

***

**Note:** Contractual obligations do not override the necessity of a human gate on high-stakes safety decisions; shipping without one creates liability far exceeding delay penalties.
