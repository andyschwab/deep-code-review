### Manual Override Strategy and Pre-Deployment Safety Plan

**1. Immediate Pre-Deployment Checks (Static & Simulation)**
Before the Friday deadline, execute these non-training interventions to filter unsafe routes:
*   **Geofenced Blacklist Integration:** Manually compile a list of high-risk coordinates or neighborhood polygons in São Paulo based on recent incident reports and driver feedback. Configure a hard rule in the dispatch layer: if any segment of an AI-generated route intersects with these zones, the route is automatically rejected before reaching the driver.
*   **"Speed vs. Safety" Simulation Sweep:** Run the current model against a fixed dataset of 50 known high-risk origin-destination pairs. Flag any route where the estimated time savings are marginal (e.g., <5 minutes) but the path deviates into flagged zones. This establishes a baseline for false positives.
*   **Deterministic Fallback Verification:** Ensure that when the AI route is blocked by the blacklist, the system defaults to a standard, non-optimized mapping provider (e.g., standard Google Maps/Waze API calls without custom weighting) rather than failing or retrying the unsafe path.

**2. Real-Time Manual Override Protocol**
Since retraining is impossible, implement a human-in-the-loop gating mechanism for the initial launch period:
*   **The "High-Risk" Queue:** Configure the dispatcher dashboard to highlight any route assignment that touches a blacklisted zone or exceeds a specific speed-to-distance ratio. These assignments must be manually reviewed and approved by a shift lead before being sent to the driver.
*   **Driver "Panic" Override:** Empower drivers to reject any assigned route without penalty via a single-button interface labeled "Unsafe Route." When triggered, the system must immediately generate a standard alternative route and log the incident for later analysis. No questions asked during the first two weeks.
*   **Shift Lead Authority:** Designate one team member per shift as the "Safety Officer" with the exclusive ability to override the AI's assignment in real-time. This person monitors the "High-Risk" queue and handles driver rejection callbacks.

**3. Post-Assignment Monitoring & Feedback Loop**
*   **Live Deviation Alerts:** If a driver deviates significantly from the assigned route (suggesting they are avoiding a dangerous area the AI missed), trigger an alert to the Safety Officer to check on the driver's status.
*   **Incident Logging:** Create a simple form for drivers to tag *why* a route was rejected (e.g., "active conflict," "poor lighting," "blocked street"). This qualitative data will be crucial for the eventual model retraining once compute resources are available.

**4. Launch Governance**
*   **Phased Rollout:** Do not enable the optimization AI for 100% of orders immediately. Start with 20% of traffic in lower-risk zones only. Increase volume by 20% daily only if zero safety incidents occur.
*   **Contractual Communication:** Inform logistics partners that the launch includes a "Safety Validation Phase" where manual reviews may add 2-3 minutes to dispatch times. Frame this as a quality assurance measure to ensure long-term reliability, which protects their assets as much as your drivers.

This approach prioritizes immediate physical safety through hard rules and human oversight, satisfying the launch deadline while containing the specific risk of unsafe neighborhood traversal.

*Note: Ensure all drivers are briefed on the new "Unsafe Route" button before their first shift. Keep the blacklist updated daily based on driver feedback.*
