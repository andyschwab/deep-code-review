### Immediate Action Plan: Transparency & Fallback

**1. Communication Strategy (Launch within 24 hours)**
*   **Reframe the Display:** Immediately add a visible disclaimer near inventory counts: *"Stock levels are estimated based on demand forecasts and may vary. Final availability confirmed at checkout."* This shifts the promise from a guaranteed count to a probabilistic estimate, managing user expectation before they reach the cart.
*   **Proactive Email/SMS:** If you have a list of users who viewed high-risk items (those with volatile forecast data), send a brief note: *"Due to unprecedented demand, some items shown as available may sell out quickly. We are updating stock in real-time."*
*   **Checkout Clarity:** Ensure the final "Place Order" button triggers a real-time API check. If stock is zero, block the transaction immediately with a friendly message: *"Oops! This item just sold out. Here are similar in-stock alternatives."* Do not let the user pay for out-of-stock items.

**2. Fallback Mechanism (No Infrastructure Changes Required)**
*   **The "Soft Reserve" Logic:** Since you cannot change the forecasting model or database infrastructure, implement a lightweight application-layer check. When a user clicks "Add to Cart," do not deduct inventory yet. Instead, perform a synchronous check against the *actual* warehouse database (not the forecast cache) only at the moment of payment initiation.
*   **Manual Override Gate:** Designate one team member as the "Stock Sheriff" for the holiday sale duration. They will have access to a simple dashboard (or even a shared spreadsheet updated hourly by warehouse staff) listing true stock levels. If the automated system allows an oversell, this person manually flags the order within 1 hour for immediate cancellation and refund before the item is picked for packing.
*   **Compensation Protocol:** Pre-authorize a standard compensation package for any customer who experiences a stock-out after checkout. Example: *"We failed to fulfill your order for [Item]. We have issued a full refund plus a $15 credit for your next purchase."* This turns a trust violation into a service recovery moment.

**3. Execution Timeline**
*   **Day 1:** Update UI copy (disclaimers) and configure the real-time checkout check. Draft compensation email templates.
*   **Day 2:** Train the "Stock Sheriff" and establish the hourly true-stock update rhythm with the warehouse.
*   **Day 3:** Dry-run the cancellation and compensation flow with a test order.
*   **Day 4:** Launch sale with monitoring focused on the delta between displayed and actual stock.

**Note:** This plan treats the forecast error as a known limitation rather than a fact, preventing the system from making false promises while working within your fixed budget. The "Stock Sheriff" acts as the necessary human-in-the-loop gate for high-stakes financial transactions until the model is fixed.
