### Contingency Protocol: Rapid Human Review & Customer Communication for Account Freezes

**1. Immediate Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Pre-Deployment Requirement)**
Do not deploy fully autonomous freezing for high-stakes actions (locking funds) without a human gate. Given the constraint of using only existing internal tools:
*   **Action:** Configure the staging deployment to switch the "Freeze" action to a "Flag for Review" status for the first 30 days or until a specific false-positive threshold is met.
*   **Workflow:** When the model flags an account, the system must trigger an immediate alert to a designated internal compliance officer via your existing monitoring dashboard. The account remains active but marked "Under Review" unless the threat level is critical (e.g., confirmed breach), in which case a senior manager must approve the freeze within 15 minutes.
*   **Owner:** Head of Compliance. **Stop Condition:** If no human reviewer acknowledges the alert within 15 minutes during business hours, the system must escalate to the on-call engineer immediately.

**2. Rapid Review Triage Process**
*   **Tier 1 (Automated Pre-Screen):** Use existing internal logs to auto-populate a review ticket with: user tenure, transaction history variance, and the specific model confidence score.
*   **Tier 2 (Human Decision):** A reviewer must manually verify the flag against known legitimate patterns (e.g., travel notifications, large but expected transfers).
*   **SLA:** All freeze flags must be resolved (unfrozen or confirmed frozen) within **60 minutes** of detection during business hours and **4 hours** outside business hours.

**3. Customer Communication Protocol**
*   **Immediate Notification:** Upon any freeze or "Under Review" status, trigger an automated SMS and email via existing channels stating: "Your account security is being reviewed. This is an automated precaution. A human agent is currently reviewing your case."
*   **Transparency & Uncertainty:** Do not state the account is "permanently locked" or assign a specific probability of fraud (e.g., "87% likely fraud"). Use defined tiers: "Status: Under Verification."
*   **Appeal Channel:** Provide a direct, prioritized support link or phone number specifically for "Frozen Account Appeals" that bypasses general queues.
*   **Resolution Message:**
    *   *If False Positive:* "Review complete. Your account is fully restored. We apologize for the inconvenience; this was a precautionary measure."
    *   *If Confirmed Fraud:* "Review complete. Account access remains restricted due to verified security concerns. Please contact our fraud specialist team."

**4. Monitoring & Rollback Plan**
*   **Metric:** Track the "False Positive Freeze Rate" daily.
*   **Threshold:** If >2% of flagged accounts are confirmed as false positives within the first week, or if the average review time exceeds the SLA by 20%, **halt automatic flagging immediately**. Revert to manual review for all suspicious activities until the model is re-tuned.
*   **Documentation:** Log every freeze, review time, and outcome in a central spreadsheet accessible to leadership for post-launch review.

**Note:** This protocol treats the freezing of funds as a high-stakes irreversible action requiring a human gate; fully automating this without review is the primary failure mode to avoid. Legal counsel should review the specific wording of customer notifications to ensure compliance with Japanese financial regulations regarding account access.
