### Pre-Deployment Safety Plan: Route Optimization Override

**1. Immediate Human-in-the-Loop Gate (The "Hard Stop")**
Since the model cannot be retrained, you must insert a mandatory human review layer for any route assignment the system flags as "high efficiency" but lacks safety validation.
*   **Action:** Configure the dispatch dashboard to hold any route passing through predefined "high-risk zones" (see Step 2) in a "Pending Approval" state.
*   **Process:** A senior dispatcher must manually verify the route against local knowledge before it is sent to a driver. No route touching a high-risk zone goes to a driver without this explicit click-to-confirm.
*   **Ownership:** Assign one specific team member as the "Safety Gate Owner" for the first two weeks post-launch to ensure accountability.

**2. Static Geofence Blocklist (Pre-Deployment Check)**
Create a static lookup table of known dangerous neighborhoods or specific streets in São Paulo based on your team's existing local knowledge and historical incident data.
*   **Implementation:** Before the AI output reaches the driver, run the suggested coordinates against this blocklist.
*   **Logic:** If *any* segment of the proposed route intersects with the blocklist, the system must automatically reject the assignment and trigger the Human-in-the-Loop Gate described above. Do not rely on the AI to "learn" this; hard-code the exclusion.

**3. Driver "Panic Override" Protocol**
Empower drivers to reject unsafe assignments without penalty.
*   **Feature:** Add a prominent "Report Unsafe Route" button in the driver app.
*   **Policy:** Explicitly communicate to drivers and logistics partners that using this button will never result in deactivation or reduced pay. If triggered, the route is immediately revoked, and a human dispatcher manually assigns a safe alternative.
*   **Feedback Loop:** Log every rejection. These are your real-time "adversarial probes" to identify gaps in your static blocklist.

**4. Transparency & Disclosure**
*   **Internal Disclosure:** Clearly label these assignments in the dispatcher view as "AI-Generated – Requires Safety Verification."
*   **Driver Disclosure:** Inform drivers that the route is an optimization suggestion and that they have final authority to deviate if they feel unsafe.
