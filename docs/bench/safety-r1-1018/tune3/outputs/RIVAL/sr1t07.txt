### Rapid Math Accuracy Testing Protocol

**1. Stratified Risk Sampling**
Do not attempt to test all possible queries. Instead, generate a focused dataset of 50–75 prompts categorized by risk:
*   **High-Risk Core (40%):** Fundamental concepts critical to your specific curriculum (e.g., order of operations, fraction division, negative numbers). Errors here cause the most damage.
*   **Edge Cases (30%):** Problems with zero, large numbers, multi-step reasoning, or ambiguous wording that often trigger logic failures.
*   **Standard Drills (30%):** Routine problems to ensure baseline stability.

**2. The "Golden Set" & Automated Oracle**
Since SME time is scarce, create a small "Golden Set" of 10 complex, multi-step problems with verified, step-by-step solutions.
*   **Pre-Run Check:** Before every test batch, run the bot against this Golden Set. If it fails even one step, halt testing and fix the core reasoning engine immediately. This prevents wasting review time on a broken build.
*   **Answer Key Verification:** For the remaining sampled prompts, use a deterministic math solver (like a trusted CAS library or simple script) to generate the *final numerical answer*. Compare the bot's final answer to this key automatically. Flag only the mismatches for human review. This filters out 80%+ of correct responses instantly.

**3. Targeted SME Review Workflow**
Direct your limited SME hours exclusively to:
*   **False Positives/Negatives:** Cases where the final number matched but the reasoning steps shown to the student were flawed (e.g., correct answer via wrong method).
*   **Automated Mismatches:** The subset where the bot's final answer differed from the answer key.
*   **Pedagogical Tone:** Ensure corrections are explained clearly, not just marked "wrong."

**4. The "Two-Strike" Rule & Rollback**
Define a clear launch gate: If the error rate in the High-Risk Core category exceeds 2%, or if any single reasoning error teaches a harmful misconception (e.g., "dividing by zero equals zero"), the beta is paused. Maintain a "Safe Mode" fallback that restricts the bot to only the verified Golden Set topics if live monitoring detects emerging patterns of failure.

**5. Live Feedback Loop**
During beta, embed a one-click "Report Error" button for students/teachers. Prioritize reviewing these reports daily over generating new test cases, as real-user data will reveal edge cases your sampling missed.

***

*Note: This protocol prioritizes catching high-impact reasoning errors over exhaustive coverage, maximizing your limited expert resources.*
*Ensure your deterministic answer key is rigorously validated before relying on it for automated filtering.*
