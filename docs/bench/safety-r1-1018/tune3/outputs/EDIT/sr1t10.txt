**Plan: Mitigate Inventory Discrepancies for Holiday Sale (4-Day Timeline, Zero Infrastructure Change)**

**1. Immediate Communication Strategy (Transparency & Expectation Setting)**
*   **Homepage & Product Page Banner:** Deploy a static, non-technical banner visible site-wide: *"High demand expected! Inventory counts are estimates based on forecasting. Final availability is confirmed at checkout."* This shifts the promise from "in stock now" to "likely available," managing expectations before the click.
*   **Checkout Clarification:** Add a bold, mandatory acknowledgment checkbox next to the "Place Order" button: *"I understand that item availability is subject to real-time verification and my order may be partially fulfilled or refunded if stock is unavailable."* This creates explicit consent, reducing chargeback risk and frustration.
*   **Email Template Prep:** Draft a "Stock Shortage" email template now. If an order cannot be fulfilled, send it within 2 hours of the failed pick. Offer a sincere apology, a full immediate refund, and a guaranteed 15% discount code for a future purchase. Speed of notification is more critical than the bad news itself.

**2. Fallback Mechanism (Process-Based, No Code Changes)**
*   **The "Human Gate" for High-Value/High-Volume Orders:** Since you cannot change the code to stop automated overselling, insert a manual review step for the highest risk transactions.
    *   **Rule:** Any order containing >3 units of the same item or totaling >€200 is flagged internally.
    *   **Action:** Your 5-person team manually verifies physical stock *before* sending the "Order Confirmed" email. If stock is missing, trigger the "Stock Shortage" email immediately. Do not let the system auto-confirm these high-risk orders.
*   **Triage Protocol:** Assign one team member solely to monitor inventory discrepancies during peak sale hours (09:00–21:00). Their only job is to catch failed fulfillments early and execute the apology/discount workflow.

**3. Post-Sale Trust Repair**
*   **Proactive Outreach:** If the model over-sold a specific popular item by a wide margin, proactively email *all* affected customers before they ask where their item is. "We underestimated demand" is better than "We lost your order."
*   **Root Cause Note:** Document the specific forecasting error for post-holiday analysis. The model is currently presenting unverified predictions as facts (hallucination surfaced as fact); future iterations must display confidence tiers or require human validation for low-stock items.

**Notes:**
This plan adheres to your fixed budget and timeline by relying on process changes and clear communication rather than engineering fixes.
Legal counsel should review the new checkout disclaimer language to ensure it meets local consumer protection laws in Berlin.
