### Emergency Pricing Override Protocol

**1. Emergency Zone Identification (Data Feeds & Triggers)**
Implement a deterministic pre-processing layer that ingests the following public data feeds to flag "Emergency Zones" before the pricing model runs:
*   **Official Alerts:** RSS/API feeds from local emergency management (e.g., Toronto Emergency Management Office) for declared states of emergency, evacuation orders, or shelter-in-place mandates.
*   **Geospatial Anomalies:** Real-time traffic API spikes indicating speeds <5 km/h across contiguous zones combined with a sudden drop in active driver supply (indicating drivers are fleeing or stuck).
*   **Keyword Filtering:** Scan local news/social media APIs for high-frequency co-occurrence of "evacuation," "disaster," or specific event names paired with "emergency" within a 2km radius.

**Logic:** If *any* official alert is active OR *two* secondary signals trigger simultaneously within a zone, mark the zone as `EMERGENCY_ACTIVE`.

**2. Rule-Based Pricing Caps (The Override)**
When a zone is marked `EMERGENCY_ACTIVE`, bypass the dynamic pricing model's output and enforce these hard rules:
*   **Hard Cap:** Set the surge multiplier to a maximum of **1.0x** (base fare only) or a fixed flat rate determined by average historical base fares for that time of day. Do not allow any surge calculation to exceed this.
*   **Service Availability:** To prevent driver starvation while preventing gouging, offer a flat "Emergency Response Bonus" paid entirely by the platform (not the rider) to drivers who accept rides in these zones, decoupling driver incentive from rider cost.
*   **Duration:** The override remains active until the official alert is cleared or manual review confirms signal dissipation.

**3. Monitoring & Human Gate**
*   **Real-Time Dashboard:** Create a visible alert panel showing all `EMERGENCY_ACTIVE` zones, the triggered data source, and the current enforced cap.
*   **Stop-Condition Alert:** If the system detects >10% of city-wide zones triggering emergency flags simultaneously (potential data feed error or city-wide crisis), automatically pause *all* dynamic pricing city-wide and revert to base fares, triggering an immediate page to the on-call engineering lead.
*   **Manual Override:** Designate one senior data scientist as the "Safety Gate" with the authority to manually expand/reduce emergency zones or lift caps if false positives occur.

**4. Rollout Plan (5-Day Constraint)**
*   **Day 1-2:** Build the data feed connectors and the boolean logic for zone flagging. Hard-code the 1.0x cap logic.
*   **Day 3:** Simulate historical disaster scenarios against the new logic to ensure the cap triggers correctly without crashing the service.
*   **Day 4:** Dry run with the "Safety Gate" owner; verify the dashboard and paging system.
*   **Day 5:** Deploy with the "City-wide Pause" stop-condition active by default.

**Note:** This protocol treats the potential for exploitative pricing as a high-stakes irreversible harm; the hard cap acts as the mandatory human-aligned gate since full model retraining is not feasible.
**Note:** Legal counsel must review the specific definition of "emergency" used in the data feeds to ensure alignment with local consumer protection regulations before launch.
