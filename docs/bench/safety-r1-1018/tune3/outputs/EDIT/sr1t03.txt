### Contingency Protocol: Rapid Human Review & Customer Communication for Account Freezes

**1. Immediate Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Pre-Deployment)**
Do not deploy fully autonomous freezing for high-stakes actions (locking funds) without a human gate. Given the constraint of using only existing internal tools:
*   **Action:** Configure the deployment to flag any "freeze" decision as a **pending action** rather than an executed one for the first 14 days.
*   **Workflow:** Alerts route to a dedicated Slack/Teams channel monitored by two senior staff members on rotating shifts. No account is frozen until a human clicks "Confirm" in the internal admin panel.
*   **Fallback:** If no human responds within 15 minutes during business hours (JST), the system defaults to **no freeze** and instead triggers a step-up authentication challenge (e.g., SMS code) for the user. It is safer to let a potentially fraudulent transaction proceed than to lock a legitimate user out of funds without review.

**2. Rapid Review Triage Process**
*   **Trigger:** When a fraud flag is raised, the internal dashboard must display: (a) the specific rule triggered, (b) the user's transaction history volume, and (c) a "One-Click Override" button.
*   **SLA:** Maximum 10-minute review time during operating hours.
*   **Escalation:** If the reviewer is uncertain, the protocol mandates releasing the hold and marking the case for post-transaction forensic analysis. The cost of a false positive (locked funds) outweighs the cost of a delayed fraud investigation in this rollout phase.

**3. Customer Communication Template (If a Freeze Occurs)**
If a freeze is confirmed and executed, immediate transparency is required to mitigate harm and over-reliance on the automated decision.
*   **Channel:** SMS and Email simultaneously.
*   **Message Draft:**
    > "Alert: Your account access has been temporarily paused by our automated security system due to unusual activity. This is not a final determination. A human specialist is reviewing your case now. Expected resolution time: <30 minutes. Do not reply to this message. For immediate assistance, call our priority line at [Number]. We apologize for this inconvenience."
*   **Disclosure:** Explicitly state that an **automated system** initiated the pause. This manages expectations and clarifies that the decision is provisional, not a verdict on their character.

**4. Post-Incident Recovery**
*   **Unfreezing:** Once cleared, funds must be accessible instantly. No additional "cooling off" periods.
*   **Compensation:** Automatically waive any fees incurred during the freeze period. For freezes lasting longer than 1 hour, offer a standardized goodwill credit (e.g., ¥1,000) without requiring a customer request.
*   **Logging:** Record every false positive in a shared spreadsheet with columns for: Trigger Rule, Review Time, Decision (Override/Confirm), and Customer Impact. This serves as your manual "harm inventory" to refine the model post-launch.

**5. Stop Condition**
If more than 3 legitimate users are frozen within the first 48 hours, **pause the automated freezing feature entirely** and revert to manual review only until the model thresholds are recalibrated.

***

**Note:** This plan treats account freezing as a high-stakes irreversible action requiring a human gate; fully automating this without review is the primary failure mode to avoid.
**Note:** Legal counsel should verify if specific Japanese financial regulations mandate additional disclosure language for automated adverse actions.
