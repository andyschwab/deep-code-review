### Pre-Launch Anti-Cheat Review Strategy

**1. Harm Inventory & Correlation Logic (MAP)**
Given the constraint of using existing telemetry without new fields, immediately map current data points to specific false-positive risks:
*   **Risk:** Legitimate latency misidentified as "glitchy" movement or teleportation.
    *   **Correlation:** Cross-reference movement anomalies with existing packet-loss or ping-spike metrics in your current logs. If a movement flag coincides with a network stability dip (>150ms or >5% loss), downgrade the confidence of the cheat detection.
*   **Risk:** Approved accessibility tools (e.g., macro-assist, screen readers) triggering "unauthorized software" signatures.
    *   **Correlation:** Isolate input patterns known to be generated by your approved accessibility list. If a flagged pattern matches an approved tool's signature exactly, suppress the ban flag automatically.

**2. Graduated Response System (MANAGE)**
Do not issue immediate permanent bans for any case lacking 100% certainty or involving irreversible account loss before the tournament. Implement a three-tier gate:
*   **Tier 1 (Low Confidence/Borderline):** If the anomaly score is above the threshold but correlates with network instability or matches an accessibility pattern, issue an **in-game warning** only. Log the event for post-match review. No gameplay restriction.
*   **Tier 2 (High Confidence but Reversible):** For strong signals that do not match known false-positive profiles, apply a **temporary suspension (e.g., 24 hours)** rather than a permanent ban. This creates a window for human review before irreversible damage occurs during the tournament.
*   **Tier 3 (Confirmed Malice):** Reserve immediate permanent bans only for cases with multiple corroborating signals (e.g., known cheat signature + impossible physics + no network latency).

**3. Human-in-the-Loop Gate (GOVERN)**
Establish a "Tournament Rapid Review" squad from your eight-person team.
*   **Workflow:** All Tier 2 suspensions and any ban appeals filed within 48 hours of the tournament launch must queue for manual review.
*   **Constraint Adherence:** Since you cannot add logging fields, the reviewer must view the raw telemetry correlation (Movement Flag vs. Ping Log) in your existing dashboard.
*   **Owner:** Assign one specific engineer to own the "False Positive" metric daily until the tournament concludes.

**4. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Run a retrospective simulation on the last 30 days of telemetry before launch:
*   **Probe:** Re-process historical data applying the new correlation logic.
*   **Metric:** Count how many previously banned accounts would now be flagged as "Network Instability" or "Accessibility Match."
*   **Stop Condition:** If the simulation shows >1% of your active player base would have been falsely flagged under the new rules, tighten the correlation thresholds further before go-live. Do not guess the false positive rate; calculate it from this simulation.

**Transparency Note:** Clearly disclose to players that the system uses automated analysis and that borderline cases result in warnings first. Publish a simple "Model Card" internally detailing that network latency is a known limitation and how the system adjusts for it.

**Legal & Ethics Note:** Route the final definition of "irreversible harm" and any regulatory disclosure requirements regarding automated bans to your legal counsel; do not assert compliance internally. Never certify the system as "unbiased"; report the residual risk from your simulation instead.
