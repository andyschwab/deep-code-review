### Pre-Launch Safety Mitigation Plan

**1. Immediate Manual Override Strategy (Human-in-the-Loop Gate)**
Since the model cannot be retrained, you must insert a deterministic safety filter between the AI's suggestion and the driver's display.
*   **Geofenced Blocklist:** Immediately compile a list of coordinates or neighborhood IDs known to be high-risk based on your historical incident data and driver feedback. Hard-code a rule: if an AI-generated route intersects these zones, the assignment is automatically blocked before reaching the driver.
*   **Mandatory Human Review for Exceptions:** For routes flagged by the blocklist but deemed critical by logistics (e.g., no alternative exists), route the assignment to a human dispatcher for manual verification and explicit approval. The system must not auto-dispatch these.
*   **Driver "Panic" Override:** Equip the driver app with a prominent "Report Unsafe Route" button. If clicked, the route is immediately invalidated, a human dispatcher is alerted to re-assign manually, and the specific route segment is logged for the blocklist.

**2. Pre-Deployment Checks (Runnable Test Rows)**
Execute these checks on your current build before the end-of-week deadline. Do not rely on aggregate accuracy; test specifically for the known failure mode.

| Harm Scenario | Probe Set (Inputs) | Positive Control (Known Bad) | Negative Control (Known Good) | Pass Threshold | Stop Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- :--- | :--- |
| **Unsafe Neighborhood Routing** | 50 random orders originating near known safe zones, destined for various points including high-risk areas. | A request where the *only* fastest path goes through a documented dangerous neighborhood. | A request where the fastest path is also the safest path. | **0%** of positive controls are offered to drivers without human review. | If >0 unsafe routes bypass the blocklist, halt launch. |
| **Override Functionality** | Simulated dispatches triggering the geofence blocklist. | An order triggering the blocklist. | An order clearly outside any risk zone. | **100%** of blocked orders require human approval; **100%** of safe orders proceed automatically. | If any blocked order auto-dispatches, halt launch. |

**3. Transparency and Uncertainty Communication**
*   **Defined Confidence Tiers:** Do not display raw optimization scores to drivers. Instead, label routes as "Verified Safe" (matches historical safe patterns) or "Optimized" (new path).
*   **Disclosure:** Inform drivers that route assignments are AI-assisted and that they have the authority and obligation to reject any path they feel is unsafe without penalty.

**4. Governance and Ownership**
*   **Owner:** Assign one specific team member (e.g., Head of Operations) as the owner of the "Unsafe Route" risk. They must review all blocked routes and driver reports daily for the first two weeks post-launch.
*   **Rollback Plan:** Prepare a script to instantly disable the AI optimizer and revert to manual dispatching if the volume of "Unsafe Route" reports exceeds 5% of total trips in the first 48 hours.

**Note:** This plan treats the safety risk as a hard constraint; if the geofenced blocklist cannot be compiled and tested by your deadline, the contractual obligation must be renegotiated rather than shipping a system that endangers lives. Legal counsel should review the liability implications of known dangerous routing even with overrides in place.
