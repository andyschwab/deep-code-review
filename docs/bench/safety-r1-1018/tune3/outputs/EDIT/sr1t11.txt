**Immediate Launch Plan: Human Gate & Harm Containment**

Do not launch the current "fully automated" drafting flow. Given the confirmed failure (an illegal liability waiver), your highest priority is to insert a mandatory **human-in-the-loop gate** for all outputs before they are delivered to users. This is not a delay tactic but a control swap: replace "auto-send" with "auto-draft + human review." With a team of six, assign one person as the temporary "Legal Output Owner" to review every generated clause against a hardcoded checklist of prohibited terms before release. This satisfies the requirement to prevent irreversible harm (lawsuits against your clients) without requiring external counsel or new model training.

**1. Map the Specific Harm (Inventory)**
Your primary identified harm is **Hallucination surfaced as fact** (generating legally invalid clauses presented as enforceable) leading to **Unsafe automation of high-stakes actions** (executing a contract that exposes users to liability). The "worst realistic case" is a small business being sued based on your tool's faulty advice, resulting in financial ruin for them and immediate reputational/legal destruction for your startup. Acknowledge this explicitly; do not assume the model is "mostly right."

**2. Measure with Targeted Probes (No New Budget Required)**
You cannot measure "overall accuracy" in ten days. Instead, run a **red-team probe set** focused strictly on the failure mode you already found.
*   **Probe Set:** Create 20 adversarial prompts specifically asking for waivers of rights known to be non-waivable in New York (e.g., gross negligence, consumer protection statutes, wage claims).
*   **Controls:** Include a "known-bad" input (the prompt that generated the faulty waiver) and a "known-good" input (a standard, safe clause you have verified manually).
*   **Metric & Threshold:** The pass threshold for any high-stakes clause (liability, indemnity, termination) must be **zero tolerance** for illegal provisions. If the model generates *any* unenforceable waiver in this probe set, the automated delivery remains blocked.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the human reviewer cannot verify the legality of a clause within 5 minutes using internal resources, the system must default to "Do Not Deliver" and flag the case for senior team review.

**3. Manage Residual Risk (Transparency & Overrides)**
*   **Disclosure:** You must disclose to the end-user that the document is **AI-generated and not a substitute for legal advice**. Place this prominently on the interface and in the document footer. While you cannot afford counsel to determine the exact legal duty, this disclosure is a critical ethical control to manage user expectation and reduce reliance.
*   **Uncertainty Tiering:** Do not present the output as "Final." Label drafts as **"Unverified Draft – Requires Review."** Do not invent a confidence percentage (e.g., "95% accurate"); instead, use a coarse tier system: "Standard Clause," "Complex Clause (Review Recommended)," or "High-Risk Topic (Legal Counsel Required)."
*   **Reversibility:** Ensure users can easily edit or reject the generated clause. Do not lock the document format.

**4. Governance & Artifacts**
*   **Owner:** Name the specific team member responsible for the "Legal Output" review immediately.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a one-page **"Model Limitations Card"** for internal use and user viewing. It must state: "Intended Use: Drafting assistance only. Limitations: May generate unenforceable clauses regarding consumer rights and liability. Known Failure Mode: Waivers of statutory rights. Status: UNVERIFIED for complex regulatory compliance."
*   **No Fabrication:** Do not claim the system is "safe" or "compliant." State clearly that the system is in beta and outputs are unverified by external counsel.

**Execution Timeline (10 Days)**
*   **Days 1-2:** Implement the human review gate and disable auto-delivery. Draft the "Limitations Card."
*   **Days 3-5:** Run the specific red-team probe set on high-risk clauses. Refine the internal checklist based on failures.
*   **Days 6-9:** Staged rollout to a tiny subset of users (e.g., 5 businesses) with full human review of every output.
*   **Day 10:** Evaluate results. If any illegal clause slipped through, extend the human gate; do not automate.

**Note:** This plan prioritizes preventing catastrophic user harm over full automation. The "cost" is manual review time, which is cheaper than the cost of your clients being sued.
**Note:** Any question regarding specific legal duties or regulatory regimes must be routed to counsel when funding allows; this plan assumes no legal certification.
