**Urgent Audit Plan and Containment Strategy**

**1. Immediate Containment (Effective Immediately)**
Do not wait for the audit to complete. Execute a "circuit breaker" configuration change to disable all outbound data flows to third-party advertising partners and activity recommendation services.
*   **Action:** Set the feature flag or environment variable controlling third-party integrations (e.g., `ENABLE_THIRD_PARTY_SHARING`) to `false` across all environments (production, staging, dev).
*   **Verification:** Confirm via network monitoring logs that zero packets are leaving your infrastructure destined for known ad-tech domains or IP ranges.
*   **Impact:** This temporarily suspends the "localized activity recommendations" feature. Communicate this limitation to stakeholders as a necessary safety measure to protect minor data.

**2. Targeted Data Flow Audit (Days 1–4)**
Given the constraint against full refactoring, perform a surgical trace on the specific data routing error rather than the entire pipeline.
*   **Trace Method:** Inject synthetic, uniquely identifiable test payloads (canary tokens) representing minor location data into the ingestion point. These tokens should contain distinct markers (e.g., specific latitude/longitude combinations or user IDs) not present in production.
*   **Monitoring Points:** Monitor logs at every egress point, specifically focusing on the legacy code modules identified as potential leak sources. Look for the presence of these unique markers in outbound HTTP requests, message queue topics, or database replication streams intended for external partners.
*   **Validation Criterion:** The system passes this check only if the canary tokens are observed strictly within internal boundaries and never appear in logs or network captures associated with external advertising endpoints.

**3. Human Gate for Launch (Day 5)**
Do not automate the launch decision.
*   **Requirement:** A designated senior engineer and the product owner must manually sign off on the audit results.
*   **Condition:** Launch is permitted *only* if the canary trace confirms 100% isolation and the circuit breaker remains active for ad-tech flows until positive validation is achieved. If any leak is detected, the launch date must slip; shipping leaked minor location data is an irreversible harm that cannot be mitigated post-factum.

**4. Transparency and Disclosure**
*   **Internal:** Document the discovered routing error, the specific legacy module involved, and the residual risk of the temporary fix in a brief incident report.
*   **External:** If any real user data was exposed prior to this discovery, legal counsel must be engaged immediately to determine notification duties. Do not guess the legal requirement; route this specifically to your Data Protection Officer or external counsel given the involvement of minors.

**Notes**
*   This plan prioritizes harm prevention over feature completeness; the recommendation engine will remain offline until the pipeline is proven safe.
*   No metric of "safety" is certified here; the manual sign-off and successful canary trace are the minimum gates required to proceed.
