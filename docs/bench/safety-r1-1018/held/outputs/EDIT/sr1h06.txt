**Pre-Launch Safety Protocol: Emergency Vehicle Exemption Verification**

**Critical Constraint:** Do not activate city-wide automated fine issuance for restricted zones by the end of the month without a mandatory human-in-the-loop gate for all initial enforcement actions. Fully autonomous issuance of fines that may impact emergency response operations constitutes an unsafe high-stakes automation. The deadline does not override the requirement to prevent irreversible harm to public safety.

**Harm Inventory & Worst Case**
*   **Harm:** False Positive Enforcement (Bias/Unfair Outcome). The system incorrectly identifies an active emergency vehicle as a standard violator.
*   **Affected Group:** Ambulance crews, fire fighters, police units during active crises.
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** An automated fine or tow order distracts a driver during a life-saving maneuver, delays response time due to administrative confusion, or creates a legal liability that discourages future emergency parking, resulting in loss of life or property.

**Residual Risk Reporting**
*   **Metric Status:** Any error rate derived from limited simulations is **UNVERIFIED** for real-world city-wide deployment. Do not publish a specific accuracy percentage (e.g., "99% safe") on safety documentation.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** A Model Card must be published before launch detailing:
    *   **Intended Use:** Traffic congestion reduction via enforcement.
    *   **Out-of-Scope:** Determining active emergency status without human verification.
    *   **Limitations:** Performance on obscured markings, night conditions, and non-standard vehicle configurations is unmeasured due to compute limits.
    *   **Owner:** Name the specific city planner accountable for exemption failures.

**Management Controls**
1.  **Human Gate:** All fines generated by the system for vehicles matching *any* emergency profile (marked, shape-based, or contextually suspicious) must route to a human reviewer for explicit confirmation before issuance.
2.  **Uncertainty Display:** If the model confidence is low or the input is ambiguous, the system must default to "No Action" or "Review," never "Fine." Do not display raw confidence percentages (e.g., "87%") to operators; use defined tiers: **Confirmed Violation**, **Corroborated**, **Unverified (Do Not Fine)**.
3.  **Override Mechanism:** Emergency services must have a direct, immediate channel to reverse any issued fines without bureaucratic delay.

**Legal & Regulatory Routing**
*   The determination of whether automated fines constitute a "regulated automated decision" under local municipal law or the EU AI Act (given the consequential impact on emergency services) must be routed to legal counsel immediately. Do not assert compliance internally.

**Note:** This protocol assumes the "end of month" deadline is fixed; if the Human Gate cannot be implemented by then, the automated issuance feature must be descoped.
**Note:** Limited computing resources preclude full simulation; therefore, the "Unverified" status of real-world performance must be explicitly stated in all public communications.
