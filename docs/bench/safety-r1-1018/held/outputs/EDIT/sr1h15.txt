### Testing Plan: Geofence Drift & Contamination Risk

**Objective:** Quantify maximum coordinate deviation using consumer-grade GPS under agricultural conditions and define operational buffers to prevent cross-contamination of neighboring organic farms.

**Simulation Scenarios:**
Run these tests in a controlled environment (e.g., a large open field with marked "organic" zones) before live deployment. Do not test over actual neighboring crops initially.

1.  **Static Hover Drift Test:**
    *   **Setup:** Place the drone at a fixed point exactly 5 meters from a simulated organic boundary.
    *   **Action:** Engage hover mode for 30 minutes (typical spray duration).
    *   **Measurement:** Log GPS coordinates every second. Calculate the maximum radial deviation from the starting point.
    *   **Variable:** Repeat under three conditions: clear sky, partial tree cover (signal multipath), and near metal structures (interference).

2.  **Boundary Approach & Turn Test:**
    *   **Setup:** Program a flight path that runs parallel to the organic boundary, then executes a 180-degree turn at the row end.
    *   **Action:** Execute the path at standard spraying speed.
    *   **Measurement:** Record the closest point of approach (CPA) to the boundary line during the turn and straightaway. Consumer GPS often lags during turns, causing "corner cutting" or overshoot.
    *   **Variable:** Test at varying wind speeds (0–15 km/h) to simulate drift compensation errors.

3.  **Signal Loss Recovery Test:**
    *   **Setup:** Simulate temporary GPS signal degradation (using software simulation or physical obstruction).
    *   **Action:** Trigger a "return-to-home" or "hover" fail-safe while near the boundary.
    *   **Measurement:** Measure the deviation during the transition from active navigation to fail-safe mode.

**Operational Buffer Recommendations:**
Based on typical consumer GPS accuracy (2–5 meters horizontal error) and the high stakes of organic certification loss, program the following hard constraints:

*   **Static Buffer:** Set the internal geofence **10 meters inside** the actual property line. This accounts for the maximum observed drift in the Static Hover Test plus a safety margin. If the neighbor is closer than 20 meters total width, **do not automate** spraying in that zone; switch to manual control.
*   **Dynamic Buffer:** Increase the buffer to **15 meters** during turning maneuvers at row ends, where deviation is highest.
*   **Hard Stop Logic:** Program the flight controller to immediately cease pump output and ascend 2 meters if the GPS position reports within 8 meters of the internal geofence, regardless of the planned path.

**Critical Constraint:**
Because consumer GPS cannot guarantee sub-meter accuracy, **full autonomous execution near boundaries is unsafe.** You must implement a "Human-in-the-Loop" gate: the system should pause and request explicit pilot confirmation before entering any zone within 15 meters of a neighbor's property. No automated decision should override this check.

**Note:** This plan identifies residual risk; it does not certify the system as "safe" or "drift-free."
Legal requirements for pesticide application and organic buffer zones in NSW must be verified by counsel, not this technical plan.
