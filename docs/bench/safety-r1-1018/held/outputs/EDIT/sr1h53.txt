### Temporary Manual-in-the-Loop Workflow for Canal Boat Rentals

To prevent unlicensed operators from accessing vessels while meeting your end-of-month deadline without custom engineering, implement the following **staged verification workflow** effective immediately. This approach gates high-risk actions behind human review while preserving speed for low-risk customers.

**1. Risk-Based Triage Rules (Deterministic Filters)**
Configure your current bot to automatically **approve** only applications that meet *all* of the following "Low-Risk" criteria:
*   Identity document matches the application name exactly.
*   Applicant declares "Recreational Use" only.
*   Rental duration is under 48 hours.
*   No prior rejection history in your database.

Any application failing *any* of these checks is instantly **flagged for Human Review** and paused. This specifically catches potential commercial operators (who often require longer terms or have complex identities) without slowing down typical holidaymakers.

**2. The Human Gate Process**
For flagged applications, route the data to a dedicated Slack channel or shared spreadsheet accessible by your five-person team.
*   **Action:** A designated team member must manually verify the applicant's license status against the government registry website (manual lookup) before approving.
*   **Constraint:** The bot is configured to **never** send access codes or unlock doors for flagged applications until a team member toggles a "Manually Verified" status in the system.
*   **Ownership:** Assign one team member per day as the "Verification Lead" to ensure accountability.

**3. Uncertainty & Disclosure**
*   **User Communication:** Update your booking interface to state: *"For safety and compliance, some bookings require brief manual license verification. You will be notified within 2 hours if further checks are needed."* This manages expectations and reduces perceived friction.
*   **Internal Confidence:** Do not rely on the bot's "confidence score." Treat any application requiring manual review as **Unverified** until a human explicitly confirms the license number against the official registry.

**4. Emergency Stop Condition**
If the volume of flagged applications exceeds your team's capacity to review them within 2 hours, **pause all automated approvals** immediately. Switch to 100% manual review until the backlog clears. It is safer to delay legitimate rentals than to release a vessel to an unlicensed operator.

**5. Transition Plan**
This workflow is a temporary control. Once the automated government registry bridge is built, these manual gates should be replaced by API checks, but the rule that "high-risk flags require human confirmation" should remain as a fallback safety layer.

***

**Note:** This plan treats the issuance of a boat as a high-stakes action requiring a human gate for any non-trivial risk, adhering to safety floors regardless of resource constraints.
**Note:** Legal requirements regarding specific licensing disclosures should be confirmed with counsel; this workflow assumes the duty to verify exists.
