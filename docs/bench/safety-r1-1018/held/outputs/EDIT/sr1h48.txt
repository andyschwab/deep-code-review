**Plan: Software Safety Layer for Book Return Bins**

Given the fixed mechanical constraint and the imminent rollout, we will implement a three-tier software safety layer focused on detection, gating, and transparency. This approach treats the crushing of fragile items as a high-stakes irreversible action requiring a human-in-the-loop gate.

**1. Detection & Inventory (MAP)**
We cannot rely on the bot to perfectly identify every fragile book via software alone, as this risks "hallucination as fact" (false confidence). Instead, we will map specific risk indicators:
*   **Input Signals:** Utilize existing sensors (weight distribution, dimensions, barcode metadata) to flag potential large-print or older editions. Large-print books often have distinct dimensions or ISBN prefixes.
*   **Harm Inventory:** Explicitly record that "false negatives" (failing to detect a fragile book) result in physical damage to property belonging to vulnerable patrons (elderly users). The worst-case scenario is the destruction of a unique or sentimentally valuable edition.

**2. The Human Gate & Uncertainty Tiers (MANAGE)**
Since the mechanical design cannot change, the software must enforce a "default safe" state for ambiguous cases.
*   **Coarse Confidence Tiers:** The software will not output a percentage (e.g., "87% fragile"). Instead, it will classify items into three defined tiers:
    *   **Safe to Compact:** High confidence based on standard paperback/hardcover metrics.
    *   **Unverified/Review Required:** Any item matching large-print dimensions, unusual weight, or missing metadata.
    *   **Do Not Compact:** Items explicitly flagged via barcode as "Special Collection" or "Fragile."
*   **The Gate:** The crushing mechanism is **disabled by default** for the "Unverified" and "Do Not Compact" tiers. These items are routed to a holding bin for staff inspection. Only items in the "Safe to Compact" tier are processed automatically. This prevents automation bias where staff might assume the bot is infallible.

**3. Measurement & Rollout (MEASURE)**
Before the school year rollout, we must validate this logic without claiming the system is "safe."
*   **Adversarial Testing:** Run a red-team exercise using a dataset of known fragile large-print books and standard books. Measure the "false negative" rate (fragile books marked as Safe).
*   **Stop Condition:** If the false negative rate exceeds 0% during beta testing, the autonomous mode for the "Safe" tier is paused, and all items require manual sorting until the detection logic is tuned.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Publish a simple "System Card" for library staff detailing: intended use (standard books only), limitations (cannot guarantee detection of all fragile items), and the procedure for handling "Unverified" bins.

**4. Governance**
Assign one team member as the owner of the "Fragility Detection" metric. Any update to the book database or sensor firmware triggers an immediate re-review of the detection thresholds.

**Note:** We cannot certify the system as "unbiased" or "harm-free"; we can only report the residual risk after testing. If legal regulations regarding automated decisions affecting public property apply, this determination must be routed to legal counsel immediately.
