### Pre-Launch Safety Audit Plan: Automated Pool Cleaning Bot

**Critical Constraint Acknowledgement:** You stated a strict budget cap prevents installing *additional physical safety switches*. However, draining a pool with a person inside is a **high-stakes, irreversible, and potentially fatal action**. Relying solely on software detection (AI vision/sensors) without a physical interlock is an unacceptable risk that cannot be "audited" into safety. Software can hallucinate, lag, or fail due to lighting/reflection. **The floor for this feature is a human-in-the-loop gate or a physical hard-stop.** If you cannot install physical switches, you must implement a **mandatory human confirmation step** via a mobile alert or onsite siren/light sequence that requires explicit override before draining begins. Full autonomous execution of draining is the failure mode this audit seeks to prevent, not verify.

#### 1. Harm Inventory (MAP)
*   **Harm:** Unsafe automation of high-stakes action (draining with occupant).
*   **Affected:** Swimmers (risk of entrapment, injury, drowning).
*   **Worst Case:** Fatality due to AI failing to detect a stationary or submerged person during irregular usage.
*   **Secondary Harm:** Over-reliance (staff assuming the bot "knows" the pool is empty).

#### 2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)
Do not assert safety; measure failure modes. Execute the following test rows before any launch:

| Harm Probe | Input Scenario (Positive Control) | Expected Result | Pass Threshold | Stop Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- :--- |
| **Occlusion Failure** | Mannequin/person partially submerged or behind ladder/filter housing during scheduled drain time. | System **ABORTS** drain; triggers alarm. | 0% false negatives. | Any failure to detect = **IMMEDIATE LAUNCH BLOCK**. |
| **Low-Light/Reflection** | Person in water during dusk/dawn or with high surface glare (simulating Sydney sun angles). | System **ABORTS** drain. | 0% false negatives. | Any failure = **BLOCK**. |
| **Irregular Usage** | Person enters pool 5 minutes before scheduled drain (breaking usage pattern). | System **ABORTS** drain; resets schedule. | 0% false negatives. | Any failure = **BLOCK**. |
| **Sensor Blind Spot** | Person located in known camera/sensor blind spots. | System **ABORTS** drain (fails safe). | 0% false negatives. | Any failure = **BLOCK**. |
| **Adversarial Input** | Simulated sensor noise or spoofed "empty pool" signal. | System **ABORTS** drain; requires human reset. | 0% successful overrides by noise. | Any success = **BLOCK**. |

**Metric:** False Negative Rate (FNR) for human presence.
**Threshold:** **Zero tolerance.** One missed detection is one potential fatality.
**Transparency Artifact:** Publish a "Safety Data Card" detailing these test conditions, the specific lighting/weather scenarios tested, and the explicit limitation: "System relies on [Sensor Type]; does not replace physical supervision."

#### 3. Risk Management (MANAGE)
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Given the inability to install physical switches, you **must** introduce a procedural gate. The AI should *request* permission to drain via a verified channel (e.g., site manager app confirmation) rather than executing autonomously. If the budget prevents physical switches, it must fund this operational step.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** If the AI confidence in "pool empty" is anything less than absolute (e.g., due to glare, turbidity), the system must default to **NOT DRAINING** and flag for human review. Do not show a percentage (e.g., "98% empty") to operators; show **"STATUS: UNVERIFIED - DO NOT DRAIN."**
*   **Fallback:** If sensors are obscured or malfunctioning, the default state must be "Idle," not "Drain."

#### 4. Governance (GOVERN)
*   **Owner:** Name a specific individual responsible for the "Drain Authorization" decision.
*   **Re-review:** Mandatory re-testing if camera angles change, water chemistry affects clarity, or lighting conditions shift seasonally.

**Note:** This plan identifies that your current constraint (no physical switches) creates an unmitigable residual risk for autonomous operation. The only safe path forward without new hardware is removing full autonomy for this specific action.
**Legal Routing:** Consult counsel regarding Australian workplace safety laws and liability for automated machinery in public/residential spaces; do not rely on this audit as a legal shield.
