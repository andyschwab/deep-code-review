### Testing Roadmap: London Bridge Toll Waiver System

**Phase 1: Data Integrity & Logic Verification (Days 1–3)**
*   **Static Logic Audit:** Manually verify the decision matrix against the legal statute. Confirm that `Disaster Declaration = Active` AND `Vehicle Type = Emergency` strictly results in `Fee = 0`. Ensure no "fuzzy" logic allows partial charges during declared states.
*   **Synthetic Dataset Creation:** Generate a controlled image set of 500+ license plates covering:
    *   **Valid Exempts:** Ambulances, fire engines, police cars (London-specific markings).
    *   **False Positives:** Private cars with similar decals, non-emergency government vehicles, motorcycles.
    *   **Edge Cases:** Mud-splated plates, damaged frames, non-standard fonts, and foreign diplomatic plates.
*   **Unit Testing:** Run the AI against the synthetic dataset in a sandbox. Target 99.9% precision on exempt vehicles. Any false negative (charging an emergency vehicle) is a critical block.

**Phase 2: Environmental Stress Testing (Days 4–6)**
*   **Condition Simulation:** Test recognition accuracy under specific London-relevant chaotic conditions:
    *   **Lighting:** Heavy rain, fog, dawn/dusk glare, and night-time headlight reflection.
    *   **Occlusion:** Vehicles partially blocked by debris, other cars, or emergency equipment (ladders/hoses).
    *   **Motion:** High-speed approach vs. stop-and-go traffic jams typical of disaster evacuations.
*   **Disaster Declaration Latency:** Simulate the time gap between an official disaster declaration and system updates. Verify the system defaults to "Waive" or "Safe Mode" if the connection to the declaration feed is lost, rather than defaulting to "Charge."

**Phase 3: Integrated Field Trials (Days 7–9)**
*   **Controlled Live Run:** Coordinate with a local fire or ambulance station for a 4-hour window on a non-operational bridge lane or test rig.
    *   Drive 20+ real emergency vehicles through the gantry at varying speeds.
    *   Introduce "decoy" civilian vehicles to ensure they are still charged correctly.
*   **Failover Drills:** Physically disconnect the disaster feed during the test to observe system behavior. The system must log the error but not issue fines to identified emergency plates if the last known state was "Disaster Active."

**Phase 4: Go/No-Go Decision (Day 10)**
*   **Metrics Review:**
    *   **False Negative Rate (Emergency Vehicle Charged):** Must be 0%.
    *   **False Positive Rate (Civilian Waived):** Acceptable threshold < 0.1% (recoverable via audit).
    *   **Processing Latency:** Must not cause traffic bottlenecks.
*   **Final Sign-off:** If any emergency vehicle was charged during testing, halt launch. If only civilian errors occurred, approve launch with a manual reimbursement protocol ready.

**Critical Success Factor:** The system must prioritize "False Waiver" (letting a civilian through for free) over "False Charge" (billing a responder). In a disaster, speed of passage outweighs revenue loss.

**Note:** Ensure all test data complies with UK GDPR; anonymize any real license plates captured during field trials immediately.
**Note:** Prepare a manual override hotline for emergency dispatchers to report billing errors in real-time post-launch.
