### Pre-Launch Safety Review Process: Autonomous Street Sweeper Pilot

**Phase 1: Static Route Policy Audit**
Before any physical deployment, audit the AI's objective function. Ensure "human safety" and "egress preservation" are hard constraints, not weighted variables against efficiency. The algorithm must be mathematically prohibited from generating a route that completely encircles a block or seals a cul-de-sac without maintaining a minimum clear width (e.g., 4 meters) at all times. Verify that the map data includes explicit "no-block" zones for driveways and emergency access points.

**Phase 2: Simulation-Based Stress Testing**
Run high-fidelity simulations using historical traffic and emergency call data for the specific Toronto pilot neighborhoods. Do not rely on average conditions; simulate peak congestion combined with random emergency events.
*   **Test Case A (The Cul-de-Sac Trap):** Simulate a sweeper entering a dead-end street while a resident attempts to exit. Verify the AI detects the trapped geometry and either refuses entry or positions itself to leave the exit clear.
*   **Test Case B (The Gridlock Seal):** Simulate multiple sweepers operating on adjacent parallel streets. Verify they do not coordinate in a way that simultaneously blocks all perpendicular intersections, effectively trapping a whole neighborhood sector.
*   **Test Case C (Dynamic Emergency Override):** Inject a simulated "ambulance approaching" signal into the system while a sweeper is positioning across a driveway. The system must demonstrate an immediate abort-and-retract maneuver within seconds, prioritizing the clear path over route completion.

**Phase 3: Physical "Tabletop" and Controlled Field Trials**
Before full residential deployment, conduct controlled trials on closed courses mimicking residential layouts.
*   **Driveway Blockade Test:** Place static obstacles representing cars in driveways. Run the sweeper to ensure its path planning logic identifies these as permanent obstructions and never plans a maneuver that would compound the blockage.
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Emergency Drill:** Have safety officers act as residents signaling distress. Test the latency and reliability of the emergency stop mechanism and the remote operator's ability to manually override and retract the vehicle.

**Phase 4: Operational Safeguards and Monitoring**
*   **Geofenced Exclusion Zones:** Program permanent digital barriers around known high-risk locations (e.g., homes with registered medical equipment users, if privacy laws permit, or simply all driveways).
*   **Real-Time Egress Monitoring:** Equip sweepers with sensors to continuously verify driveway clearance. If a driveway becomes blocked (by the sweeper or another object), the vehicle must alert a human monitor immediately.
*   **The "30-Minute Rule":** Implement a hard timeout where no sweeper can remain stationary blocking any potential access point for more than a few minutes without human authorization.

**Specific Medical Emergency Test Cases for Validation**
1.  **Scenario:** Resident signals for ambulance while sweeper is mid-maneuver across their driveway.
    *   **Success Criteria:** Sweeper detects emergency signal (or simulated siren proximity), halts immediately, and reverses to clear the full driveway width within 10 seconds.
2.  **Scenario:** Ambulance needs to turn onto a street currently being cleaned.
    *   **Success Criteria:** Sweeper yields right-of-way automatically, pulling to the curb or returning to a designated safe zone, never forcing the ambulance to wait or detour.
3.  **Scenario:** Power failure or system crash while positioned near a driveway.
    *   **Success Criteria:** Default fail-safe mode ensures the vehicle does not lock its wheels in a blocking position; it must default to a non-obstructive stance or be easily manually pushed aside.

**Note:** This review must be documented and signed off by an independent safety engineer before the pilot begins.
**Note:** Establish a 24/7 rapid-response human team during the 30-day pilot to physically intervene if digital safeguards fail.
