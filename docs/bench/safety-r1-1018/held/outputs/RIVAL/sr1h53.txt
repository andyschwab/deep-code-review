### Temporary Manual-in-the-Loop Safeguard Workflow

To prevent unlicensed operators from renting vessels while awaiting the government registry integration, implement the following three-tiered workflow immediately. This approach isolates high-risk applications for human review without introducing friction for the majority of legitimate customers.

**1. Automated Pre-Screening (The "Fast Lane")**
Configure your existing bot to perform immediate, low-latency checks against data the applicant *already* provides in the standard form. If an application meets **all** of the following criteria, the bot approves the rental instantly (under 30 seconds):
*   **License Format Validity:** The entered license number matches the strict regex pattern for valid UK boat licenses (e.g., correct length, alphanumeric structure).
*   **Expiry Date Check:** The uploaded license image or entered date shows an expiry date at least 30 days in the future.
*   **Low-Risk Profile:** The rental duration is under 48 hours AND the vessel power is below a specific threshold (e.g., under 15HP), as shorter, lower-power rentals statistically present lower liability risks.
*   **Clean History:** The user's account (if returning) or device fingerprint has no prior flags or cancellations.

**2. The "Hold & Review" Queue (The Safety Net)**
Any application failing *any* of the above criteria is automatically moved to a "Pending Review" status. The bot sends an immediate automated response: *"Your application requires a quick manual verification to ensure safety compliance. A team member will review your documents within 2 hours. You will be notified immediately upon approval."*

Triggers for manual review include:
*   License number format mismatch or missing digits.
*   Expiry date within the next 30 days.
*   Requests for high-power vessels or long-duration rentals (>48 hours).
*   First-time users with no historical data.
*   Uploaded images that are blurry, cropped, or fail basic OCR confidence scores.

**3. Rapid Human Verification Protocol**
With a team of five, distribute the "Pending Review" queue via a shared dashboard (e.g., a simple internal web view or shared spreadsheet updated by the bot's database).
*   **Assignment:** Rotate one staff member as the "Verification Lead" during peak hours to focus solely on this queue, while others handle customer support.
*   **Action:** The verifier visually compares the uploaded license image against a public reference guide of valid UK boat licenses (available on the government website). They check for photo matching, hologram presence (if visible), and date consistency.
*   **Decision:**
    *   **Approve:** Click "Verify" in the dashboard; the bot instantly sends the confirmation email and unlocks the vessel.
    *   **Reject:** Click "Reject" with a standardized reason (e.g., "License expired," "Image unreadable"). The bot sends a polite rejection email inviting re-application with correct documents.
    *   **Escalate:** If a license looks potentially forged, flag it for senior review and temporarily block the user account.

**Operational Guardrails**
*   **SLA:** Set a hard internal Service Level Agreement of 2 hours for queue clearance. If the queue exceeds 20 applications, trigger an alert to mobilize all five team members to clear the backlog.
*   **Audit Log:** Every manual decision must be logged with the reviewer's ID and timestamp to ensure accountability and train the future automated system.
*   **Default Deny:** The system defaults to "Pending" for any ambiguity. No vessel is released without a binary "Approved" status from either the bot (fast lane) or a human (review queue).

This workflow ensures that 100% of rentals are vetted, with ~80-90% likely passing instantly via the fast lane, while the remaining high-risk cases are caught by human judgment before any keys are handed over.
