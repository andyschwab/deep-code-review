### Software-Only Wind Interference Verification Checklist

To ensure safe drop aborts without new hardware, simulate the following scenarios using your existing telemetry logs. Each scenario must verify that the bot triggers an "Abort Drop" command when calculated risk exceeds the safety threshold.

**1. Historical Extreme Event Replay**
*   **Action:** Isolate telemetry logs from days with recorded high wind gusts or turbulence.
*   **Simulation:** Replay these exact wind vectors against your current drop-zone algorithm.
*   **Success Criteria:** The bot must flag these historical moments as "No-Go" zones. If the original logs show a successful drop during these conditions, the new logic must override and simulate an abort.

**2. Micro-Burst and Shear Simulation**
*   **Action:** Synthesize data patterns representing sudden wind direction shifts (shear) or rapid vertical downdrafts (micro-bursts) that occur between standard telemetry sampling intervals.
*   **Simulation:** Inject these synthetic spikes into stable flight logs at varying altitudes (10m, 30m, 50m).
*   **Success Criteria:** The bot must detect the instability trend before the package release point and abort. Verify the system does not "average out" the spike, which would falsely indicate safety.

**3. Urban Canyon Turbulence Modeling**
*   **Action:** Map your delivery routes against building heights to identify "urban canyons."
*   **Simulation:** Apply computational fluid dynamics (CFD) approximations or empirical urban wind models to your logs to simulate erratic airflow caused by building wakes.
*   **Success Criteria:** The bot must automatically increase the safety margin or abort drops in these specific geofenced areas when wind speed exceeds a lower threshold than in open fields.

**4. Sensor Latency and Drift Stress Test**
*   **Action:** Artificially introduce latency (delay) and drift (error) into the wind speed/direction inputs within the logs.
*   **Simulation:** Run the algorithm with "stale" data to mimic processing delays during high-load operations.
*   **Success Criteria:** The bot must assume a "worst-case" wind scenario when data freshness degrades and abort the drop rather than relying on outdated safe readings.

**5. Payload Aerodynamic Variance**
*   **Action:** Adjust the drag coefficient inputs in your logs to represent different package shapes and weights (lightest vs. heaviest loads).
*   **Simulation:** Test borderline wind conditions where a heavy package might be safe, but a light, high-drag package is not.
*   **Success Criteria:** The abort logic must be dynamic based on the specific payload ID. A "one-size-fits-all" wind limit is insufficient; the bot must abort for lighter payloads in conditions where heavier ones might pass.

**6. Battery-Voltage Coupled Wind Resistance**
*   **Action:** Correlate low-battery telemetry states with high-wind events.
*   **Simulation:** Simulate scenarios where battery voltage sag reduces motor responsiveness to wind correction.
*   **Success Criteria:** The bot must lower its wind tolerance threshold as battery levels drop. If the drone cannot guarantee a hover-and-hold position due to power constraints, the drop must be aborted.

**7. The "Edge-of-Envelope" Boundary Test**
*   **Action:** Generate thousands of synthetic wind vectors that sit exactly 1% below and 1% above your defined safety limit.
*   **Simulation:** Run a binary search style simulation to find the precise breaking point of your algorithm.
*   **Success Criteria:** Verify there is no "flickering" state where the bot oscillates between "Go" and "No-Go." The system must decisively abort on the unsafe side of the line every time.

**Deliverable for Investors:** Compile the pass/fail rates of these seven simulations into a "Virtual Flight Safety Report," highlighting that the software logic proactively rejects risky conditions that previous hardware-only checks might have missed.

*Note: This process relies entirely on the fidelity of your existing logs; garbage-in will result in garbage-out.*
*Ensure all synthetic data generation is documented to maintain audit trails for regulatory review.*
