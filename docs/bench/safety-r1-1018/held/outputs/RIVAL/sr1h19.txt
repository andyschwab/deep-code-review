**Pre-Launch Safety Protocol: Parking Garage Access Control**

**1. Simulated Environmental Stress Tests**
Conduct these tests during off-peak hours (2:00 AM – 4:00 AM) to prevent traffic disruption.
*   **Low-Light Simulation:** Temporarily dim or cover existing garage lighting to simulate bulb failure or night-time glare. Run 50 exit cycles using standard tickets. Record the system's response time and failure rate when contrast is reduced by 40%.
*   **Damaged Ticket Simulation:** Prepare a batch of 20 test tickets with common defects: coffee stains obscuring 15% of the code, creases across the barcode/QR area, and faded thermal print (achieved by exposing tickets to direct sunlight for 30 minutes prior). Attempt to exit with these tickets sequentially.
*   **Angle and Distance Variance:** Have testers hold tickets at non-optimal angles (30 degrees off-axis) and distances (2 inches too close/far) to mimic driver error combined with low-resolution camera limitations.

**2. Manual Override Verification Process**
Before Friday's release, validate the human-in-the-loop fallback mechanism.
*   **Trigger Protocol:** Define the exact condition that triggers the override (e.g., "No read after two attempts within 10 seconds").
*   **Alert Latency Test:** Measure the time between a failed automated read and the arrival of an alert at the security station. The target is under 5 seconds.
*   **Remote Release Drill:** Have a security operator manually trigger the barrier open from the control room upon receiving a "failed read" alert. Perform this drill 10 times to ensure the software does not lock out manual commands during error states.
*   **Physical Intercom Check:** Verify that the voice intercom automatically connects to an operator if the barrier remains closed for more than 15 seconds after a failed scan.

**3. Deployment Readiness Checklist**
*   **Signage:** Install temporary high-visibility signs at all exits stating: "New System Testing: If barrier does not open, press Call Button for immediate assistance."
*   **Staffing:** Schedule two additional floor staff members for the first 48 hours post-launch to physically assist drivers if the remote override experiences latency.
*   **Rollback Plan:** Document the specific hardware switch or software command to revert to the legacy "always-open" or previous verification mode if the failure rate exceeds 5% during the first hour of live traffic.

**4. Success Criteria for Go/No-Go Decision**
*   Automated read success rate must be ≥ 95% under simulated poor lighting.
*   Manual override must engage within 10 seconds of a failed read in 100% of drill attempts.
*   Zero instances where the system crashes or requires a reboot after processing a damaged ticket.

If any of the above criteria are not met during tonight's testing, the Friday release must be paused until the OCR threshold settings are recalibrated or the manual override latency is resolved.

*Note: Ensure all test tickets are clearly marked "VOID" to prevent accidental re-use as valid parking passes. Retain all log files from these stress tests for liability and engineering review.*
