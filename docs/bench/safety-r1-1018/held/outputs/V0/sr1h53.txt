### Temporary Manual-in-the-Loop Workflow for Canal Boat Rentals

To prevent unlicensed operators from accessing vessels while maintaining speed for legitimate customers, implement a **Risk-Tiered Triage System** effective immediately. This approach gates only high-risk applications behind human review, allowing low-risk transactions to proceed instantly.

**1. Define Automatic "Green Lane" Criteria (Instant Approval)**
Configure your current bot to bypass human review only if an application meets **all** of the following strict criteria:
*   **Document Match:** The uploaded ID name matches the application name with 100% string equality.
*   **License Presence:** A field explicitly marked "Boating License Number" is populated with alphanumeric data (not empty, not "N/A").
*   **Self-Declaration:** The user checks a box stating, "I hold a valid UK boat license and accept liability," with a timestamped log.
*   **Low-Value/Duration:** The rental is under 4 hours or below a specific monetary threshold (reducing the stakes of error).

If any single criterion fails, the application is automatically routed to the "Amber/Red" queue.

**2. Establish the "Amber Queue" (Human Review Required)**
Applications failing the Green Lane criteria are paused instantly. The user receives an automated message: *"Your application requires a quick manual verification step. Our team will respond within 60 minutes."*

Your five-person team will manually verify these flagged applications using this 3-step checklist:
*   **Visual Inspection:** Does the uploaded license image look authentic? (Check for blurriness, obvious Photoshop artifacts, or mismatched expiry dates).
*   **Registry Spot-Check:** Since the automated bridge is not ready, assign one team member per shift to manually search the government registry website using the provided license number. Take a screenshot of the match and attach it to the booking record.
*   **Call-Back Verification:** For any application where the license number is missing, illegible, or the photo ID looks suspicious, trigger a mandatory 2-minute phone call to the applicant before approval.

**3. The "Red Lane" (Automatic Rejection)**
Configure the bot to instantly reject and block any application containing:
*   Blank license fields.
*   IDs expired by more than 30 days.
*   Blacklisted email domains or phone numbers previously associated with fraud.
These users receive a generic message: *"We are unable to process your rental at this time due to verification requirements."* Do not provide specific reasons to avoid helping bad actors game the system.

**4. Operational Guardrails**
*   **Shift Rotation:** With a team of five, rotate one person exclusively as the "Verification Officer" during peak hours (09:00–17:00) to ensure the Amber Queue never stalls.
*   **Default to Deny:** If the Verification Officer cannot confirm the license status within 15 minutes of review, the default action is to deny the rental. It is safer to lose a sale than to launch an unqualified operator.
*   **Audit Log:** Maintain a simple spreadsheet logging every manual override, the officer's name, and the screenshot of the registry check. This creates accountability and aids the future automated integration.

This workflow ensures that no boat leaves the dock without a verified license, either by strict automated rules or human confirmation, satisfying the safety requirement without halting all operations.

*Note: This manual process is a temporary control; document its limitations clearly for your legal team regarding the upcoming government integration deadline.*
*Ensure all staff know that "speed" never overrides the "Default to Deny" rule when license validity is unclear.*
