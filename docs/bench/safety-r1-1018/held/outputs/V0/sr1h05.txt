### Simulation Plan for Allergen Safety in Skincare Recommendations

**1. Construct the Synthetic Harm Inventory**
Before generating data, define the specific failure modes your simulation must catch. For your context, the critical harms are:
*   **Bias/Exclusion:** The system fails to recognize severe allergies in specific skin types or demographics.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The engine recommends a product containing an allergen while claiming it is "safe" or "hypoallergenic."
*   **Unsafe Automation:** The app pushes a recommendation with high confidence despite conflicting allergen data, leading to potential physical harm.

**2. Build the Synthetic Dataset (The Probe Set)**
Since real-world trials are impossible, your synthetic data must be adversarial, not just representative.
*   **Positive Controls (Known Bad):** Create 500+ user profiles with explicit, severe allergies (e.g., "severe nickel allergy," "anaphylactic reaction to shea butter"). Pair these with products known to contain those exact allergens. The expected output is a hard block or explicit warning.
*   **Negative Controls (Known Good):** Create profiles with no allergies or mild sensitivities paired with safe products. The expected output is a standard recommendation.
*   **Edge Cases:** Include profiles with conflicting data (e.g., user lists "sensitive skin" but no specific allergens) and products with obscure ingredient synonyms for common allergens.
*   **Subgroup Slicing:** Ensure the dataset covers diverse skin tones and types to test for bias in how conditions are recognized from photos.

**3. Define Metrics and Stop Conditions**
Do not measure "accuracy" generally. Measure specific safety failures.
*   **Metric:** "Allergen Violation Rate" = (Number of recommendations containing a known user allergen) / (Total number of allergen-specific test cases).
*   **Threshold:** Set a **zero-tolerance threshold** for severe allergen violations. Any single instance of recommending a known severe allergen is a failure.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the violation rate > 0% for severe allergies, the launch is blocked. Do not proceed to "soft launch" until this is resolved.

**4. Execute the Simulation and Report Residual Risk**
Run the recommendation engine against the synthetic dataset.
*   **Document Failures:** Log every instance where the system suggested an allergen. Analyze *why* (e.g., did the photo scan miss the condition? Did the ingredient parser fail on a synonym?).
*   **Report Status:** Clearly label the safety status of the feature. If testing reveals any unmitigated risk, mark the metric as **UNVERIFIED** rather than inventing a "99% safe" claim. You cannot certify safety without real-world validation; you can only report the results of this specific simulation.

**5. Create the Transparency Artifact (Model/Data Card)**
For your internal compliance records, draft a **Model Card** that includes:
*   **Intended Use:** Personalized skincare recommendations for general consumers; explicitly state it is *not* a medical diagnostic tool.
*   **Limitations:** Acknowledge that testing was performed on synthetic data only and that real-world allergic reactions may vary.
*   **Measured Performance:** Report the "Allergen Violation Rate" from your simulation, broken down by allergy type.
*   **Owner:** Name the specific team member responsible for monitoring post-launch feedback.

**6. Implement a Human-in-the-Loop Gate**
For any recommendation involving a user profile flagged with "severe allergy" or "medical condition," the system must not auto-deliver the final advice. Instead, it should trigger a mandatory disclaimer requiring explicit user confirmation or route the case for human review if your operations allow. Fully autonomous execution of high-stakes health advice is the failure mode you must prevent.

**Note:** This plan identifies risks but does not certify the system as safe; the final launch decision rests with your leadership based on the residual risk reported. Legal requirements for AI disclosure in health contexts should be routed to counsel immediately.
