### Pre-Launch Monitoring Plan & Temporary Circuit Breakers

**1. Vulnerable Segment Identification (Monitoring Rules)**
Before the quarterly review, configure your legacy reporting to flag accounts meeting *any* of the following criteria for manual review rather than automatic reduction:
*   **Tenure vs. Volatility:** Accounts open >5 years with a sudden <30% drop in inflows lasting <45 days (indicates temporary shock vs. structural decline).
*   **Utilization Spike:** Customers whose credit utilization jumped >40% month-over-month but who made at least one minimum payment in the last 60 days.
*   **Essential Spend Ratio:** Accounts where spending on identified essential categories (utilities, grocery, healthcare) exceeds 60% of total outflows, suggesting limited discretionary cutback ability.
*   **Recovery Signals:** Accounts showing a downward trend in negative balance days over the last 14 days despite the overall monthly dip.

**2. Temporary Circuit Breakers (Hard Stops)**
Implement these logic gates immediately to prevent irreversible harm without requiring core code changes (use configuration tables or pre-processing filters if possible):
*   **The "Loyalty Lock":** Automatically suspend any limit reduction for accounts with >7 years tenure and zero missed payments in the last 24 months, regardless of the model's risk score.
*   **The "Hardship Cap"::** If the model recommends a reduction >25% of the current limit, cap the action at 10% and route the case to a human agent.
*   **The "Life-Event Pause":**: If an account has triggered a "hardship assistance" flag or forbearance request in the last 90 days, bypass the automated reducer entirely.
*   **The "Volume Threshold":** Do not execute reductions on accounts with a current limit under a specific subsistence level (e.g., <$2,000), as the absolute impact is disproportionately high.

**3. Human-in-the-Loop Gate**
For any account flagged by the monitoring rules or hit by a circuit breaker, the output must be a "Pending Review" status, not a finalized decision. A human agent must validate the context (e.g., checking for recent large one-off expenses like medical bills) before any limit change is applied.

**4. Measurement & Stop Conditions**
*   **Metric:** Track the percentage of flagged accounts that would have been reduced under the original logic but were saved by the circuit breakers.
*   **Stop Condition:** If >15% of all proposed reductions are blocked by these rules, halt the automated rollout entirely and extend the manual review period. This indicates the model's baseline logic is too aggressive for the current economic climate.

**Note:** These controls address the risk of unsafe automation and over-reliance on unverified model outputs; they do not certify the model as unbiased.
Legal counsel must verify if these automated decisions trigger specific regulatory disclosure duties under local banking laws before launch.
