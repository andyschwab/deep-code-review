### Internal Bias Audit Plan for AI Hiring Tool

**Phase 1: Construct the Harm Inventory**
Before testing, explicitly define the risk: **Systematic Exclusion**.
*   **Harm:** The tool rejects qualified candidates with non-traditional paths (gaps, career pivots, freelance work, non-degree education).
*   **Affected Group:** Candidates with nonlinear careers, often including caregivers, career changers, and self-taught engineers.
*   **Worst Case:** The team homogenizes, missing top talent, and exposes the company to discrimination liability.
*   **Status:** This inventory is now open. Do not proceed to launch until measured.

**Phase 2: Build the "Shadow" Test Suite (No External Tools Required)**
Use your historical data to create a controlled evaluation set. You do not need expensive software; you need rigorous manual construction of test cases.

1.  **Extract Positive Controls (The "Should Pass" Set):**
    *   Query your historical database for employees currently rated as "High Performers" who entered via non-traditional paths (e.g., bootcamps, gap years, industry pivots).
    *   Anonymize their real resumes. These are your **Known-Good** cases. If the tool rejects these, it is broken.

2.  **Construct Negative Controls (The "Should Fail" Set):**
    *   Take resumes of candidates who were historically rejected for clear lack of qualification (e.g., missing critical hard skills entirely).
    *   Ensure these do *not* have non-traditional markers that could confuse the test. These are your **Known-Bad** cases. The tool must reject these to prove it isn't just accepting everyone.

3.  **Create Adversarial Probes (The "Edge Case" Set):**
    *   Take standard, highly qualified resumes and artificially inject "non-traditional" signals: a 2-year gap labeled "Caregiving," a job title change from "Teacher" to "Project Manager," or freelance blocks instead of corporate tenure.
    *   Create pairs: **Resume A** (Traditional path) vs. **Resume B** (Identical skills/experience, Non-traditional path).

**Phase 3: Define Metrics and Stop Conditions**
Run the tool against these three sets. Record the output strictly as Pass/Fail (Invite to Interview/Reject).

*   **Metric 1: False Rejection Rate on Positive Controls.**
    *   *Threshold:* If the tool rejects >0% of your "High Performer" non-traditional profiles, **STOP**. This is a critical failure.
*   **Metric 2: The Pairwise Disparity Gap.**
    *   Compare acceptance rates of Resume A vs. Resume B pairs.
    *   *Threshold:* If the Non-traditional version is rejected at a rate >5% higher than the Traditional version, **STOP**.
*   **Metric 3: False Acceptance on Negative Controls.**
    *   Ensure the tool still filters out unqualified candidates. If it accepts all negative controls to avoid bias, it is useless.

**Phase 4: Management and Governance**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Until the metrics above are stable, **no candidate with a non-traditional marker can be auto-rejected.** Any resume triggering a "reject" based on gaps or pivots must be routed to a human recruiter for review.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a one-page "Model Card" for internal use. List:
    *   Intended Use: Initial screening only.
    *   Limitations: "Untested on [specific demographic] until Phase 3 completes."
    *   Owner: Name the specific engineer/recruiter pair responsible.
    *   Status: Mark bias metrics as **UNVERIFIED** until the test suite runs. Do not invent a safety percentage.

**Phase 5: Execution**
Run the tests manually or with simple scripts. If the tool fails the Positive Controls or shows a significant Pairwise Disparity, do not deploy. Retrain or adjust weighting rules, then re-test.

**Note:** This plan measures behavior, not code correctness; a bug-free system can still be biased.
**Note:** If this tool makes final hiring decisions without human review, pause deployment immediately; high-stakes decisions require a human gate by default.
