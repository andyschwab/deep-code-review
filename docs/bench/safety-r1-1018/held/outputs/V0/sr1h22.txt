### Pre-Launch Safety Plan for SeineAR

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before launch, explicitly document the specific risks your dynamic generation poses. For SeineAR, the critical harms are:
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The AI inventing controversial or offensive events and presenting them as historical truth.
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** Narratives that systematically disadvantage or misrepresent specific cultural groups.
*   **Missing AI Disclosure:** Users assuming the overlay is curated, verified historical data rather than machine-generated interpretation.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** In this context, "high-stakes" means irreversible reputational damage to the museum or severe offense to visitors. There is no "undo" button once a tourist reads an offensive fabrication on the gallery floor.

**2. Measurement Strategy (MEASURE)**
Do not assert the system is safe; test the outputs directly.
*   **Build Output-Harm Evals:** Create a test suite of adversarial prompts targeting known weak points (e.g., controversial wars, colonial history, religious artifacts). Include "positive controls" (inputs known to trigger hallucinations) and "negative controls" (safe inputs).
*   **Define Stop Conditions:** Establish a pass threshold for launch. If the model generates *any* unverified offensive claim in the high-risk category, the launch must be blocked or the feature disabled.
*   **Report Residual Risk:** If you cannot measure a specific harm due to budget constraints, label it `UNVERIFIED` in your internal docs. Do not invent a "0% hallucination rate."
*   **Create a Model Card:** Draft a one-page transparency artifact for your team and stakeholders. List intended uses, known limitations (e.g., "may hallucinate obscure dates"), and the fact that content is AI-generated. This is mandatory for a shipped AI feature.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Critical):** Since you cannot hire more consultants, you must re-allocate existing resources. **Fully autonomous generation of historical narratives is too risky for launch.** Implement a gate where *any* generated response regarding controversial topics requires explicit human approval before being served, or restrict the AI to only answer questions from a pre-vetted, static knowledge base.
*   **Show Uncertainty:** Never display AI confidence as a precise percentage (e.g., "95% sure"). Instead, use defined coarse tiers if you must show confidence, such as "Verified by Curators" vs. "AI Interpretation." Better yet, add a visible disclaimer: "Narratives generated by AI; verify with official guides."
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly label all dynamic overlays as "AI-Generated Interpretation" to manage user expectations and prevent over-reliance.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   Name a specific owner on your team of twelve responsible for reviewing hallucination reports post-launch.
*   Establish a rollback plan: If a harmful narrative is reported, there must be an immediate switch to disable dynamic generation for that exhibit.

**Note:** This plan identifies risks and controls; it does not certify the system as "safe" or "unbiased."
**Note:** Legal requirements for AI disclosure in the EU (e.g., EU AI Act) must be verified by counsel; this plan recommends disclosure as a safety control, not a legal verdict.
