**Pre-Launch Safety Plan: Preventing Active Work Zone Routing**

**1. Immediate Human-in-the-Loop Gate (The Floor)**
Do not launch the automated routing feature for any station with scheduled maintenance until a human operator manually verifies the path.
*   **Action:** Implement a "Manual Override Dashboard" where a designated safety officer must explicitly approve every route generated for stations flagged in your static maintenance schedule.
*   **Constraint:** The app must **not** auto-generate or display a route through a maintenance zone. If no human approval exists for a specific station/time window, the app must default to: "Route unavailable due to station updates. Please consult station staff."
*   **Rationale:** Automating irreversible, high-stakes actions (routing users onto tracks) without real-time data is a critical failure mode. Full autonomy here is unsafe; human confirmation is mandatory.

**2. Harm Inventory & Worst-Case Definition**
*   **Harm:** Physical injury or death from users entering active work zones.
*   **Affected:** Commuters, maintenance workers.
*   **Worst Case:** A user follows app guidance onto an electrified track during active work, resulting in fatality.
*   **Status:** This risk is **UNVERIFIED** as safe because you lack live feeds to prove a path is clear. You cannot assert safety; you must assume danger until proven otherwise by a human.

**3. Measurement & Evaluation (Runnable Tests)**
Before launch, execute these specific probes. Do not rely on code reviews alone; test the output behavior.
*   **Probe A (Positive Control):** Input a request for a route through a station known to be under maintenance *during* the maintenance window.
    *   *Pass Condition:* The app refuses to generate a route and displays the fallback warning.
    *   *Fail Condition:* The app generates a path, even partially, into the restricted zone.
*   **Probe B (Negative Control):** Input a request for a route through a station with *no* maintenance.
    *   *Pass Condition:* Normal routing functions.
*   **Probe C (Edge Case):** Input a request for a route at the exact minute a maintenance window starts/ends.
    *   *Pass Condition:* The system defaults to "Unavailable" if the status is ambiguous.
*   **Stop Condition:** If Probe A or C fails even once, **block the launch**. There is zero tolerance for routing users into known danger zones.

**4. Transparency & Disclosure Artifacts**
*   **Model/Data Card:** Publish a visible "System Limitations" notice within the app and on your landing page.
    *   *Content:* "This app uses static scheduling data and does not have access to real-time track closures. Routes are not verified for active work zones unless explicitly marked 'Human Verified.' Always obey on-site signage and staff instructions."
    *   *Requirement:* Do not claim the app is "safe" or "accurate" for track navigation. Label the data source limitation clearly.
*   **Confidence Tiers:** Do not show numerical confidence (e.g., "95% safe"). Use defined text tiers:
    *   *Verified:* Human-approved for this specific window.
    *   *Unverified:* Static data only; do not enter restricted areas.

**5. Governance & Ownership**
*   **Owner:** Assign one specific engineer as the "Safety Gate Owner" responsible for the manual dashboard.
*   **Review Cycle:** Re-evaluate this process immediately if maintenance schedules change format or if new stations are added.
*   **Legal Routing:** Consult legal counsel regarding liability for physical harm caused by app guidance in regulated transit environments. Do not assume limited licensing protects you from negligence claims if the app directs users into danger.

**Summary of Launch Readiness:**
You cannot launch with fully automated routing for maintenance zones. The only safe path is to disable automation for these specific cases and require human verification for every affected route. If you cannot staff the human gate, you must disable the routing feature entirely for those stations.

*Note: This plan identifies residual risk; it does not certify the system as safe. The decision to ship rests with your leadership.*
*Ensure the "Human Verified" status is visually distinct and impossible to bypass in the UI.*
