### Pre-Launch Verification Framework: School Bus Routing Safety

Given the three-week deadline and the high-stakes nature of assigning drop-off locations for children, the following framework prioritizes a **human-in-the-loop gate** for every proposed address before it becomes active. No fully automated execution of final drop-off assignments is permissible given the risk of directing a child to a hazardous location.

**1. Harm Inventory & Worst-Case Definition**
Before testing, explicitly record the specific failure modes:
*   **Unsafe Location Selection:** Algorithm selects an address near a busy intersection, construction zone, or non-residential building despite being "closest" by distance.
*   **Identity Mismatch:** The address belongs to a different family or is vacant, leading to a child being dropped at the wrong home.
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** A student is dropped at a dangerous intersection or an unknown residence, resulting in injury, abduction, or significant trauma.

**2. Measurement Plan (Runnable Tests)**
Do not assert safety; measure it using adversarial probes and controls. Execute one test row per identified harm:
*   **Probe Set:** Run the algorithm against a dataset of known "trap" addresses (e.g., coordinates centered on busy intersections, parks without entrances, or commercial zones) and a control set of verified safe residential stops.
*   **Positive Control:** Input a scenario where the only nearby option is a known hazard; the system must *reject* auto-assignment.
*   **Negative Control:** Input a standard residential scenario; the system must propose the correct address.
*   **Metric & Threshold:** The pass threshold for any high-risk flag is **zero**. If the system auto-approves a hazardous location in the probe set, the launch is blocked.
*   **Stop Condition:** Any instance of a proposed drop-off lacking a "Verified Safe" status from the cross-reference check triggers an immediate rollback of that specific route for human review.

**3. Management Controls: The Human Gate**
*   **Mandatory Human Review:** Every calculated drop-off location must be presented to a human dispatcher or safety officer with a **coarse confidence tier** (e.g., "Confirmed Residential," "Unverified/Requires Check," "Flagged Hazard") rather than a raw probability percentage.
*   **Override Mechanism:** The interface must allow the human reviewer to easily reject the AI's suggestion and manually input a safe alternative. The system cannot force an address if the human flags it.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a **Model Card** for this specific deployment. It must list:
    *   **Intended Use:** Routing school buses in NYC only.
    *   **Limitations:** Explicitly state that the model cannot visually verify real-time sidewalk conditions or building occupancy.
    *   **Data Provenance:** Source of the traffic and address databases used.
    *   **Owner:** Name the specific individual responsible for the final sign-off of the routing logic.

**4. Governance & Legal Routing**
*   **Ownership:** Assign a named owner for the output harm of "incorrect drop-off." This person is accountable for reviewing any failures post-launch.
*   **Legal Determination:** Because this system makes automated decisions affecting minors and physical safety, it likely triggers specific regulatory duties. **Route immediately to legal counsel** to determine if this falls under New York State automated decision-making laws or federal guidelines. Do not assume compliance; let counsel verify if parental consent or specific disclosures are legally required before the first bus rolls.

**Definition of Done for Launch:**
No route goes live without a human verifying the specific address against the safety database. If the cross-reference returns "Unverified" or "Hazard," the bus stop remains inactive until a human resolves it.

***

*Note: This framework treats the algorithm as a recommendation engine, not an autonomous decision-maker, to prevent irreversible harm. Any claim of "100% safety" without running the specific adversarial probes listed above is unverifiable and must be avoided.*
