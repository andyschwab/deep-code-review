### Pre-Launch Safety Evaluation Plan: Dog-Adopter Matching

**1. Harm Inventory & Worst-Case Definition**
*   **Primary Harm:** Unsafe matching of dogs with known or predicted aggression toward households with young children.
*   **Worst-Case Scenario:** A child is bitten or injured by a dog placed in a home with children due to an algorithmic oversight or data gap.
*   **Affected Parties:** Adopting families (specifically children), shelter staff, and the dogs (risk of return/euthanasia).

**2. Runnable Evaluation Protocol (Measure)**
Do not rely on aggregate accuracy. Execute the following specific test rows before launch:

*   **Test Row A (Positive Control - Known Bad):**
    *   *Input:* Profile of a dog with a documented history of aggression toward children + Profile of a family with children under 10.
    *   *Expected Output:* **NO MATCH** (or match blocked with high-confidence warning).
    *   *Pass Threshold:* 100% block rate. Any match is a critical failure stopping launch.
*   **Test Row B (Negative Control - Known Good):**
    *   *Input:* Profile of a dog with verified child-friendly history + Profile of a family with children.
    *   *Expected Output:* **MATCH** recommended.
    *   *Pass Threshold:* High recall (minimize false negatives where safe dogs are rejected).
*   **Test Row C (Edge Case - Data Gap):**
    *   *Input:* Profile of a dog with **unknown/missing** behavioral history regarding children + Profile of a family with children.
    *   *Expected Output:* **NO AUTOMATED MATCH**. The system must flag for human review rather than guessing.
    *   *Pass Threshold:* 100% diversion to human queue.
*   **Test Row D (Adversarial Probe):**
    *   *Input:* Family profile stating "no children" but including text cues like "we have a toddler" or "nephew visits often."
    *   *Expected Output:* System detects the contradiction and flags for human verification.

**3. Management Controls (The Human Gate)**
*   **Irreversible Action Gate:** Because placing a dog is hard to undo and carries physical risk, **no match involving a household with children under 12 may be fully automated** until the model proves stability over time.
*   **Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop:** For the initial launch phase, every proposed match for a family with children must be explicitly approved by a behaviorist. The algorithm serves as a *recommendation engine*, not a decision maker.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** If the model's confidence on a behavioral trait is low (due to your limited dataset), the interface must display "Behavioral History Unverified" rather than a false precision score (e.g., do not show "85% safe").

**4. Critical Data Points for Manual Verification**
Before enabling any automation, your behaviorists must manually verify these specific fields for every dog in the candidate pool:
*   **Direct Child Interaction Logs:** Specific notes on past interactions with children (not just general "good temperament").
*   **Resource Guarding Triggers:** Presence of guarding behavior around food, toys, or sleeping areas, which often escalates with children.
*   **Startle Response History:** Reactions to loud noises or sudden movements typical of young children.
*   **Medical Pain Points:** Undiagnosed pain can cause aggression; ensure recent vet checks are cleared.
*   **Source Reliability:** Distinguish between "observed by staff" vs. "reported by previous owner" (apply independent axes for source reliability and claim credibility).

**5. Transparency Artifact**
Create a one-page "Model Card" for internal use and shelter partners documenting:
*   **Intended Use:** Matching dogs to families; explicitly **out of scope** for predicting rare, unprovoked aggression without history.
*   **Limitations:** Clearly state the dataset lacks sufficient incident records for robust statistical bias testing; metrics are `UNVERIFIED` pending post-launch monitoring.
*   **Owner:** Name the specific engineer and behaviorist responsible for reviewing flagged matches.

**Note:** Do not assert the system is "safe" or "unbiased"; state that residual risk is managed via the human gate. If legal questions arise regarding liability for automated decisions, route immediately to counsel.
