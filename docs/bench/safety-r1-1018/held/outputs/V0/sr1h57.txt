### Testing Approach: Synthetic Stress-Test for Facial Recognition

**1. Harm Inventory & Scope**
The primary harm is **over-reliance/automation bias** and **unsafe automation**, where the system incorrectly labels residents as intruders, causing distress and nuisance alarms. Since collecting new biometric data is prohibited, the validation strategy relies entirely on **synthetic image variations** derived from the existing, consented resident dataset.

**2. Synthetic Data Generation Plan**
Generate adversarial test sets by applying programmatic transformations to the existing authorized resident images. Do not use real photos of non-residents.
*   **Accessory Simulation:** Synthetically overlay diverse eyewear (prescription, sunglasses, rimless) and headwear (caps, beanies, hoods) at varying angles and lighting conditions.
*   **Environmental Stressors:** Apply noise, blur, and low-light filters to simulate suburban evening conditions common in Canada during the holiday season.
*   **Pose Variation:** Rotate facial vectors to test recognition limits when residents are not facing the camera directly.

**3. Measurement Protocol (One Row Per Harm)**
Execute the following evaluation loop for each synthetic category:
*   **Probe Set:** 500 synthetic variations per resident (totaling 4,500+ images), split into "known-good" (resident with accessory) and "known-bad" (synthetic intruder patterns).
*   **Metric:** False Positive Rate (FPR) specifically for *authorized users* labeled as *intruders*.
*   **Threshold:** Set a zero-tolerance stop condition for any resident being flagged as an intruder more than 1% of the time in "known-good" scenarios.
*   **Stop Condition:** If the FPR exceeds the threshold for any accessory category, the feature launch is blocked until the model is retrained or the threshold logic is adjusted.

**4. Managing Residual Risk**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** Until the false positive rate is proven negligible via the synthetic tests, disable automatic police dispatch or loud siren activation for "intruder" events. Route these alerts to a mobile app confirmation step first.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** If the model confidence drops below a defined tier (e.g., "Corroborated") due to occlusion (hats/glasses), the system must display "Identity Unverified" rather than "Intruder Detected." Do not show raw confidence percentages (e.g., "87%") to users, as this implies false precision.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** documenting that testing was performed using synthetic variations due to privacy constraints. Explicitly list "heavy occlusion" as a known limitation if the synthetic tests reveal persistent gaps. Mark any untested demographic slices as `UNVERIFIED`.

**5. Governance**
Assign a specific owner to review the synthetic test results before the holiday launch. Re-evaluate the harm inventory if the physical camera placement changes or if new accessory trends emerge post-launch.

***

**Note:** This plan measures residual risk but does not certify the system as "safe" or "unbiased"; the final ship decision rests with the product owner.
**Note:** Ensure legal counsel reviews whether synthetic data generation complies with Canadian biometric privacy laws before execution.
