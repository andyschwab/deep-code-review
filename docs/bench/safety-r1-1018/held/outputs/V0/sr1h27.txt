### Pre-Launch Audit Plan: Automated Fining & Dispute Resolution

**1. Harm Inventory & Worst-Case Definition**
Before testing, explicitly record the primary harm: **Unsafe automation of irreversible financial actions**.
*   **Affected Party:** Library patrons.
*   **Worst Realistic Case:** A patron is wrongly fined due to the known 5% sensor error, cannot easily dispute the charge, suffers financial loss, and is blocked from future library services.
*   **Constraint Acknowledgement:** With a 5% sensor error rate, a fully automated fine system without a human gate guarantees wrongful charges. The audit must verify that the system *prevents* automation in these ambiguous cases, not just that it calculates fines correctly when sensors are perfect.

**2. Measurement Plan: Runnable Test Rows**
Do not assert safety. Execute the following specific test rows against your dispute workflow before launch. Each row must have a "Stop Condition" that blocks launch if failed.

*   **Test Row A: The Known-Error Probe (Positive Control)**
    *   **Input:** Simulate sensor data triggering the known 5% ambiguity (e.g., two similar books detected where only one should be, or a book detected but no checkout record exists).
    *   **Expected Behavior:** The system **must not** issue a fine or charge a payment method automatically. It must flag the event for human review or auto-generate a "dispute ready" ticket.
    *   **Metric:** 0% automated fines issued on ambiguous inputs.
    *   **Stop Condition:** If *any* fine is charged automatically during this probe, launch is blocked.

*   **Test Row B: The Dispute Reversal Probe (Negative Control)**
    *   **Input:** Process a confirmed wrongful fine (simulated) through the dispute workflow.
    *   **Expected Behavior:** The user can initiate a dispute without barriers (no hidden defaults, no manufactured urgency). The reversal of the charge and restoration of account status happens within a defined, short SLA (e.g., <24 hours) via human intervention.
    *   **Metric:** 100% successful reversal rate; time-to-resolution < SLA.
    *   **Stop Condition:** If a user cannot easily find the dispute button, or if reversal takes longer than the SLA, launch is blocked.

*   **Test Row C: Over-Reliance & Disclosure Check**
    *   **Input:** Review the user-facing notification for a fine.
    *   **Expected Behavior:** The notice explicitly states the fine was generated by an automated sensor system with a known error rate and provides immediate instructions on how to contest. It does not present the fine as an indisputable fact.
    *   **Metric:** Qualitative pass/fail on clarity and ease of override.
    *   **Stop Condition:** If the language implies infallibility or hides the dispute path, launch is blocked.

**3. Management Controls: The Human Gate**
Given the 5% error rate and the financial nature of fines:
*   **Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop:** Any fine triggered by sensor data that lacks a 100% correlated digital checkout record must be routed to a staff member for verification *before* any money is moved or account privileges are restricted. Fully autonomous execution of fines is the failure mode you must prevent.
*   **Uncertainty Disclosure:** Do not show patrons raw sensor confidence scores (e.g., "87% sure"). Instead, use defined tiers: "Confirmed Match" (auto-process) vs. "Ambiguous Detection" (requires review).

**4. Governance & Artifacts**
*   **Owner:** Name a specific product owner responsible for reviewing every failed test row and any disputed fine post-launch.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Create a one-page "System Card" for internal staff and public view detailing: the 5% sensor error rate, the types of errors known to occur, the exact steps to dispute, and the guarantee that no money is taken without human verification on ambiguous reads.

**Note:** Do not fabricate a "0% error" metric for your safety report; explicitly state the residual risk is managed via the human gate. If legal counsel is required to determine if these automated fines constitute a regulated consumer decision under local law, route that question immediately; do not assume compliance.
