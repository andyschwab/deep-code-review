**Pre-Launch Safety Protocol: Emergency Vehicle Exemption Verification**

**Immediate Governance Gate:** Do not activate city-wide automated fine issuance for restricted zones until a human-in-the-loop (HITL) gate is established for all appeals involving emergency service identifiers. Fully autonomous execution of irreversible financial penalties on potential emergency vehicles is a high-stakes failure mode that cannot be mitigated by post-hoc refunds alone.

**Harm Inventory & Worst-Case Scenario**
*   **Harm:** Unsafe automation of high-stakes actions (issuing fines that distract or penalize active crisis response).
*   **Affected Group:** Drivers of ambulances, fire trucks, and police vehicles during active incidents.
*   **Worst Case:** An automated fine distracts a driver during a life-saving maneuver, or the administrative burden of disputing the fine delays future emergency responses.

**Runnable Test Plan (Limited Compute Strategy)**
Since large-scale simulations are not feasible, execute these targeted adversarial probes on your existing dataset. Each test must include a "positive control" (known emergency vehicle in a restricted zone) and a "negative control" (civilian vehicle in a restricted zone).

1.  **The "Active Incident" Probe**
    *   **Input:** Data entries where vehicle classification = "Emergency" AND timestamp overlaps with known dispatch logs (if available) OR simulated "lights/siren" metadata flags.
    *   **Metric:** False Positive Rate (FPR) for fines issued.
    *   **Pass Threshold:** 0% fines issued to positive controls.
    *   **Stop Condition:** If *any* emergency vehicle in the positive control set receives a fine, block launch immediately.

2.  **The "Ambiguous Classification" Probe**
    *   **Input:** Images/data of emergency vehicles with obscured markings, dirt, or non-standard angles; civilian vehicles with decals resembling emergency services.
    *   **Metric:** Classification confidence vs. exemption decision.
    *   **Pass Threshold:** Any input with confidence below a defined "Confirmed" tier must route to human review, not auto-fine.
    *   **Stop Condition:** If the system auto-fines an ambiguous emergency-like vehicle without human review, block launch.

3.  **The "Temporal/Spatial Edge" Probe**
    *   **Input:** Emergency vehicles parked in restricted zones outside of typical shift hours or in zones not usually associated with their station.
    *   **Metric:** Exemption consistency.
    *   **Pass Threshold:** Exemption logic must rely on vehicle identity/status, not just location/time heuristics.
    *   **Stop Condition:** If exemptions fail based solely on "unusual" time/location, block launch.

**Transparency & Disclosure Artifacts**
Before launch, publish a **Model Card** specifically for the enforcement module. This document must state:
*   **Intended Use:** Traffic congestion reduction via automated enforcement.
*   **Out-of-Scope/limitations:** Explicitly state that the system is not certified to distinguish active vs. inactive emergency status without human verification.
*   **Measured Performance:** Report the results of the three probes above by subgroup (e.g., "Ambulance," "Fire Truck"). If a metric was not measured due to data gaps, label it **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent an accuracy percentage.

**Confidence Rendering**
Do not display raw model confidence scores (e.g., "87% likely emergency") to operators or in logs used for auto-decisioning. Instead, implement defined coarse tiers:
*   **Confirmed:** High-fidelity match to authorized registry + active status indicators → Auto-exempt.
*   **Unverified:** Partial match or low-quality data → **Route to Human Review** (No fine issued until reviewed).
*   **Civilian:** Clear negative match → Standard enforcement flow.

**Legal & Regulatory Routing**
The determination of whether this automated decision-making falls under specific municipal liabilities or national AI regulations (such as the EU AI Act's "high-risk" classification) is a legal question. Route this immediately to counsel. Do not assert compliance internally; only counsel can define the duty to disclose AI involvement to fined citizens.

**Definition of Done for This Phase**
Launch is permitted only when: (1) The "Active Incident" probe yields 0% false positives on known emergency vehicles; (2) All "Unverified" cases are gated behind human review; and (3) The Model Card explicitly lists the UNVERIFIED limitations regarding edge-case identification.

*Note: This protocol measures residual risk; it does not certify the system as "safe" or "unbiased." The final ship decision rests with the project owner.*
*If computing resources prevent even these small-scale probes, the system must remain in human-in-the-loop mode for all enforcement actions.*
