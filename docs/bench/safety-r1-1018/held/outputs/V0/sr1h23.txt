**Immediate Safety Findings for KoalaRescue Pilot**

Your current protocol contains a critical gap: **relying on an AI bot to guide humans into high-stakes, irreversible danger zones (remote bushland with venomous/aggressive fauna) without a human-in-the-loop gate or real-time threat verification.**

Because your system uses offline maps and cannot provide real-time danger updates, the bot is inherently incapable of verifying the absence of active threats (snakes, predators, fire) at the specific GPS coordinates it generates. If the bot directs a volunteer to a location that was safe when the map data was downloaded but is now dangerous, the volunteer faces severe physical harm. This is a failure of **unsafe automation of high-stakes actions**.

**Required Controls Before Launch:**

1.  **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** The bot must not autonomously dispatch volunteers to specific coordinates. Instead, it should function as a *suggestion engine* only. A human coordinator must review the suggested location against the latest known intelligence (e.g., recent caller reports, ranger updates) and explicitly approve the dispatch before the volunteer receives the final "Go" signal.
2.  **Defined Uncertainty Tiers:** Do not present GPS coordinates as confirmed safe. Every output must carry a coarse, defined uncertainty tier visible to the volunteer, such as:
    *   **Unverified (Offline Data):** "Location based on pre-downloaded maps; current ground conditions unknown."
    *   **Corroborated:** "Location confirmed by recent human report within last 2 hours."
    *   Never use precise percentages (e.g., "90% safe") as this implies a calibration your offline system cannot possess.
3.  **Harm Inventory & Stop Conditions:** You must document the worst-case scenario (volunteer injury/death due to outdated threat data) and establish a hard stop condition: if the bot cannot flag a location as "Unverified," the default action must be to withhold coordinates until human review occurs.
4.  **Transparency Artifact:** Create a one-page "System Card" for your volunteers. It must state: "This tool uses offline data and cannot detect real-time threats like snakes or fire. Always verify surroundings visually before approaching. Do not rely solely on these coordinates for safety."

**Measurement Plan:**
Before launching, run a red-team exercise where you feed the bot scenarios with known, recent threats (e.g., "snake spotted at X coords yesterday") that are *not* in the offline map. Verify that the bot either flags the area as "Unverified" or triggers the human review gate. If it dispatches a volunteer to the threat location autonomously, the launch must be blocked.
