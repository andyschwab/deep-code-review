### Pre-Launch Review Checklist: Shark Warning Bot (Sydney Coast Watch)

**1. Harm Inventory & Worst-Case Mapping**
*   **Identify Specific False Positives:** List the top three benign marine life types (e.g., dolphins, rays, schools of fish) currently triggering alarms.
*   **Define Worst-Case Impact:** Document the specific cost of a false alarm (e.g., "2-hour beach closure," "$X tourism loss," "public trust erosion").
*   **Verify Human Gate:** Confirm that *no* automated alert triggers a beach closure without explicit human verification by a lifeguard or operator. Full automation of closures is prohibited at this stage.

**2. Output Measurement Plan (No Retraining Required)**
*   **Adversarial Probe Set:** Curate a dedicated test set containing *only* the identified benign species in various lighting/sea states. Do not rely on general accuracy metrics.
*   **Define Pass Thresholds:** Set a strict "zero tolerance" threshold for automated closures based on false positives. If the model flags a benign object with high confidence, the system must fail this check.
*   **Confidence Tiering:** Replace raw probability scores (e.g., "87% shark") with defined coarse tiers for human reviewers:
    *   *Confirmed:* Multiple corroborating frames/features.
    *   *Unverified:* Single-source detection or low-clarity footage (Default action: Monitor, do not close).
*   **Injection & Edge Checks:** Test the system against "noise" inputs (glare, shadows, birds diving) to ensure they do not trigger high-confidence alerts.

**3. Risk Management & Transparency**
*   **Uncertainty Display:** Ensure the operator dashboard clearly highlights "Unverified" detections differently from "Confirmed" ones, preventing automation bias.
*   **Model Card Draft:** Prepare a one-page transparency artifact listing:
    *   *Intended Use:* Drone footage analysis for shark presence.
    *   *Known Limitations:* Specific species prone to confusion; performance in rough seas.
    *   *Owner:* Name the specific team member responsible for reviewing flagged events.
*   **Legal Routing:** Flag the question of "mandatory AI disclosure to the public" or "liability for missed sharks" to legal counsel. Do not assert compliance internally.

**4. Governance & Stop Conditions**
*   **Rollback Trigger:** Define the exact metric that halts launch (e.g., "More than 2 false positives in a 4-hour simulated run").
*   **Review Cadence:** Schedule a re-evaluation of the harm inventory if the drone camera angle, time of day, or marine migration patterns change.

**Notes**
*   Do not invent a "false positive rate" for marketing materials; label any unmeasured metric as **UNVERIFIED**.
*   The goal is not to certify the system as "safe," but to prove the human gate effectively catches the model's known errors.
