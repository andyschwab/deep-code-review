### Risk Assessment and Mitigation Strategy: Parking Enforcement Bot

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
*   **Primary Harm:** Unsafe automation of a high-stakes, irreversible action. Towing an ambulance during a critical patient drop-off creates immediate risk to life and disrupts emergency care.
*   **Secondary Harm:** Bias/Unfair Outcome. The system systematically disadvantages emergency services due to visual limitations (camera angles) that obscure markings.
*   **Root Cause:** Over-reliance on imperfect visual data without a human gate for high-consequence decisions. The current design treats all vehicles in the zone identically, ignoring the critical distinction between violators and emergency responders.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
*   **Eval Focus:** Do not measure general "accuracy." Measure the **false positive rate specifically for emergency vehicles**.
*   **Test Protocol:** Run adversarial probes using images of ambulances from the specific restricted camera angles. Include "known-bad" controls (clear violators) and "known-good" controls (ambulances with partial occlusion).
*   **Metric & Threshold:** The pass threshold for autonomous towing of any vehicle resembling an emergency responder must be **zero tolerance**. Any detection of emergency markings (even partial) or vehicle shape consistent with an ambulance must trigger a "Unverified/Review Required" state, not a tow dispatch.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** stating the intended use (non-emergency enforcement), the known limitation (inability to distinguish obscured emergency vehicles), and the explicit out-of-scope use (autonomous action on potential emergency vehicles).

**3. Mitigation Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** Immediately disable fully autonomous towing for any vehicle detected in the emergency loading zone. Implement a strict rule: **No tow truck is dispatched without explicit human confirmation.**
    *   *Workflow:* The bot flags a violation → Sends image/alert to a human operator → Operator verifies it is *not* an ambulance → Operator authorizes tow.
    *   *Rationale:* Given the camera constraints, the AI cannot achieve the certainty required for irreversible actions involving potential life-safety risks. The human acts as the necessary sensor fusion for context the camera misses.
*   **Uncertainty Signaling:** Configure the system to output a coarse confidence tier rather than a percentage. If the model detects *any* feature resembling an emergency vehicle (shape, light bar, partial text), it must classify the output as **"Unverified – Requires Human Review"** rather than "Violator."
*   **Operational Override:** Establish a direct hotline or digital override for hospital staff to instantly halt any pending tow actions while the system is in pilot.

**4. Governance (GOVERN)**
*   **Ownership:** Assign a specific product owner responsible for reviewing every false positive involving emergency vehicles.
*   **Re-evaluation Trigger:** The system cannot move from "pilot" to "full auto" until the camera limitation is resolved or a new sensor modality (e.g., license plate recognition cross-referenced with emergency vehicle databases) is integrated and proven to have a zero false-positive rate for ambulances.
*   **Legal Routing:** Consult legal counsel immediately regarding the liability of automated enforcement against emergency vehicles and any regulatory duties to disclose this automation to the public. Do not assert compliance internally; route this determination externally.

**Definition of Done for Pilot Launch:**
The system launches *only* with the human-in-the-loop gate active for all towing decisions. No fully autonomous towing occurs until the harm inventory shows the risk to emergency vehicles is mitigated by technology, not just process.

***

*Note: Do not fabricate a safety metric (e.g., "99% safe") to satisfy the winter surge deadline; the risk to life dictates the control, not the schedule.*
*Ensure the "Model Card" is published internally before the pilot starts to document these known limitations clearly.*
