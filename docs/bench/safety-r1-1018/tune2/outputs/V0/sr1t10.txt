### Immediate Action Plan: Transparency & Fallback Protocol

**1. Communication Strategy (Launch Immediately)**
*   **On-Site Disclosure:** Place a visible, non-intrusive banner on product pages and the cart stating: *"Inventory counts are real-time estimates based on high demand. Final availability is confirmed only at checkout."* This shifts the promise from the display to the transaction point, managing expectations without hiding the issue.
*   **Email Pre-empt:** Send a brief note to your subscriber list before the sale starts. Acknowledge the high anticipation, explain that stock updates rapidly, and promise a "no-questions-asked" refund or priority restock notification if an item sells out after ordering.
*   **Support Scripting:** Equip your five-person team with a unified response: "We apologize for the discrepancy. Our system estimated availability based on forecast, but demand exceeded supply. We have issued a full refund and added a 15% discount code for your next purchase."

**2. Technical Fallback Mechanism (No Infrastructure Changes)**
*   **"Soft Reserve" Logic:** Modify the checkout flow (frontend or simple backend flag) to treat inventory as "soft reserved" only when the user hits the payment gateway, not when they view the page. If the database returns "0" at that exact second, trigger an immediate, friendly interruption: *"Oops! This item just sold out. Your card has not been charged."*
*   **Hard Cap Override:** Manually set a conservative "safety buffer" in your existing admin panel. If the model says 10 units, manually cap the display at 6. This reduces the surface area for error without new code.
*   **Post-Order Triage:** For orders that slip through and cannot be fulfilled:
    *   Issue refunds within 1 hour of discovery.
    *   Automatically email the customer with the refund confirmation and a goodwill gesture (discount code).
    *   Do not wait for the customer to notice; proactive communication preserves trust better than silence.

**3. Operational Triage for the 5-Person Team**
*   **Role Split:** Dedicate one person solely to monitoring stock discrepancies and triggering the "out of stock" manual overrides during peak hours. The other four handle support tickets using the pre-approved script.
*   **Decision Rule:** If an item is oversold, prioritize refunding first, then offering alternatives. Do not attempt to source stock last-minute given the budget constraint; focus on damage control.

**4. Post-Sale Review**
*   Document every instance of discrepancy. Use this data to recalibrate the forecasting model after the holiday rush.
*   Draft a "Model Card" summary for internal use detailing the known limitation (overestimation) and the manual controls applied, ensuring future launches include these guards by default.

***

*Note: This plan prioritizes radical transparency and rapid refund execution over technical fixes, adhering to your zero-budget and four-day constraints.*
