### Contingency Protocol: Rapid Human Review & Customer Communication for Account Freezes

**1. Immediate Trigger & Triage (0–15 Minutes)**
*   **Automatic Halt:** Upon any account freeze by the fraud model, the system must immediately pause any further automated actions (e.g., data deletion, permanent closure) and flag the case as "Pending Human Review."
*   **High-Priority Queue:** All freeze events enter a dedicated, high-priority queue visible to the entire 10-person team via existing internal monitoring dashboards.
*   **Initial Triage:** The first available team member acknowledges the alert within 5 minutes. No freeze remains unacknowledged for more than 15 minutes during business hours.

**2. Rapid Human Review Process (15–60 Minutes)**
*   **Reviewer Assignment:** The acknowledging team member becomes the case owner. If the freeze involves high-value accounts (>¥1M) or vulnerable user segments (e.g., elderly, small business payroll), a second team member must co-sign the review.
*   **Evidence Checklist:** Reviewers must manually verify three data points using existing internal tools before lifting or maintaining a freeze:
    1.  Transaction velocity vs. historical user baseline.
    2.  Device/IP reputation compared to known fraud patterns.
    3.  Recent user-initiated security changes (password resets, 2FA updates).
*   **Decision Threshold:** If evidence is ambiguous or the user has a long-standing clean history, the default action is to **lift the freeze** and flag for posterior analysis. Maintain the freeze only if clear, corroborated evidence of fraud exists.

**3. Customer Communication Protocol (Concurrent with Review)**
*   **Immediate Notification:** As soon as the freeze occurs, an automated but human-reviewed template message is sent via SMS and email.
    *   *Content:* "Your account access is temporarily paused for security verification. This is an automated precaution. A human specialist is reviewing your case now. Expected resolution time: under 60 minutes. Do not reply to this message; call our emergency line if urgent."
*   **Resolution Update:** Once the human review concludes:
    *   *If Lifted:* "Your account has been reactivated. We apologize for the inconvenience. No further action is needed."
    *   *If Maintained:* "Our review indicates potential unauthorized activity. Your account remains secured. Please contact our fraud specialist directly at [Phone Number] to verify your identity and restore access."
*   **Emergency Escalation:** Provide a dedicated phone line staffed by rotating team members for users claiming immediate financial hardship (e.g., inability to pay rent/medical bills).

**4. Post-Incident Analysis & Rollback**
*   **False Positive Log:** Every freeze, regardless of outcome, is logged in a shared spreadsheet with: timestamp, trigger reason, reviewer name, decision, and time-to-resolution.
*   **Daily Retrospective:** The team leads a 15-minute stand-up each morning during the rollout week to review false positives. If the false positive rate exceeds 5% of total freezes, the team must manually lower the model's sensitivity threshold until retrained.
*   **Compensation Policy:** For any freeze lasting longer than 2 hours caused by a system error, authorize a standard goodwill credit (e.g., ¥1,000 or fee waiver) without requiring user request.

**Note:** This protocol treats account freezing as a high-stakes, reversible action requiring a human-in-the-loop gate by default. The "default to lift" rule prioritizes minimizing user harm over maximizing fraud capture during the initial rollout.
