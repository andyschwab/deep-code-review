### Pre-Launch Safety Mitigation Plan: Route Optimization AI

**1. Immediate Deterministic Guardrails (No Retraining Required)**
Implement a hard-coded "Safety Filter" layer that runs *after* the AI generates a route but *before* it is assigned to a driver. This operates independently of the model:
*   **Geofenced Exclusion Zones:** Map known high-risk neighborhoods in São Paulo based on existing internal incident reports or public crime data. If an AI-generated route intersects these zones, the system automatically rejects the route.
*   **Fallback Logic:** When a route is rejected by the filter, the system must immediately revert to a deterministic, non-AI baseline algorithm (e.g., standard shortest-path avoiding flagged zones) or present the top 3 alternative routes that bypass the exclusion zones.
*   **Speed Cap Enforcement:** Hard-code a maximum allowed time-savings threshold. If the AI route saves less than X minutes compared to the safe baseline but passes through a flagged zone, default to the safe baseline.

**2. Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate for High-Stakes Assignments**
Since fully autonomous execution of potentially unsafe routes is the primary failure mode, institute a mandatory review step for edge cases:
*   **Trigger Conditions:** Any route flagged by the Safety Filter, any route entering a "borderline" zone (areas adjacent to exclusion zones), or any route where the AI confidence score is below a defined threshold.
*   **Review Process:** These specific assignments are routed to a dedicated operations dashboard for manual approval by a shift supervisor before being dispatched.
*   **Driver Override:** Equip drivers with a prominent "Report Unsafe Route" button in the app. Activating this immediately halts the current navigation, logs the incident, and switches the driver to the safe baseline algorithm for the remainder of the trip. No penalty applies to drivers who reject routes via this mechanism.

**3. Pre-Deployment Verification Checks**
Execute the following targeted tests before the end-of-week deadline:
*   **Adversarial Probe Set:** Run the new system against a curated list of 50 known "dangerous" origin-destination pairs in São Paulo. **Pass Condition:** Zero routes are assigned that traverse hard-coded exclusion zones without triggering the fallback or HITL gate.
*   **Negative Control Test:** Run 50 standard, low-risk routes. **Pass Condition:** The system does not unnecessarily block safe routes or degrade performance significantly on non-flagged paths.
*   **Kill Switch Validation:** Verify that the operations team can instantly disable the AI optimization module and revert 100% of traffic to the legacy deterministic algorithm within 2 minutes.

**4. Transparency and Monitoring**
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly label AI-assigned routes in the driver app as "Optimized Suggestion" rather than "Mandatory Path."
*   **Residual Risk Log:** Document that safety performance in borderline zones is currently `UNVERIFIED` due to the lack of retraining. Establish a daily review cadence for the first two weeks post-launch to analyze any "Report Unsafe Route" incidents and expand exclusion zones accordingly.

**Owner:** Head of Operations (must sign off on exclusion zone maps and HITL workflow).
**Launch Constraint:** If the Adversarial Probe Set fails (any unsafe route slips through), the launch must be delayed or the AI module disabled entirely.
