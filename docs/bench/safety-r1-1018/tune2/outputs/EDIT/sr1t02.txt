**BLUF:** Do not deploy the automatic denial mechanism in ten days. With an unverified algorithm, no diverse labeled data, and high-stakes irreversible outcomes (claim denial), full automation violates the safety floor. The only shippable plan within your constraints is a **human-in-the-loop gate** for all denials, paired with a deterministic fallback.

**1. Immediate Control: Human Gate & Fallback (Days 1–2)**
*   **Stop Condition:** Disable the "auto-deny" function. The system may *flag* inconsistencies, but a human adjuster must review and confirm every denial before notification.
*   **Fallback Logic:** If the algorithm's confidence is below a strict threshold (or if image quality metrics like lighting/noise are poor), default to "manual review required" rather than "approve." Never default to "deny" on uncertainty.
*   **Owner:** Lead Engineer to implement the kill-switch and routing logic by Day 2.

**2. Simulation & Stress Testing with Limited Resources (Days 3–7)**
Since you cannot collect new data, you must adversarially test existing assets to map failure modes:
*   **Synthetic Degradation:** Write scripts to programmatically alter your existing internal photos to simulate Mumbai-specific conditions:
    *   *Lighting:* Apply filters to simulate monsoon overcast, harsh noon sun, and night-time flash glare.
    *   *Occlusion:* Digitally add rain streaks, mud splatter, or common roadside debris to valid damage photos.
    *   *Angle/Resolution:* Downsample images to mimic low-end device cameras common in the region.
*   **Adversarial Probes:** Run these altered images through the algorithm.
    *   *Positive Control:* Valid claims that the system now incorrectly flags as "inconsistent" due to lighting/noise.
    *   *Negative Control:* Clearly fraudulent images that should still be flagged.
*   **Metric:** Track the "False Denial Rate" on these degraded valid claims. If the rate exceeds 0% for the gated high-stakes action, the model is not ready for automation.

**3. Transparency & Disclosure Artifacts (Days 8–9)**
*   **Model Card (Draft):** Create a versioned document stating:
    *   *Intended Use:* Triage only, not final decision.
    *   *Limitations:* Explicitly list "unverified performance on diverse lighting conditions" and "lack of diverse training data."
    *   *Status:* Mark accuracy metrics as **UNVERIFIED**. Do not invent a percentage.
*   **User Disclosure:** Add a UI notice informing users that "AI analysis is assisting our adjusters; final decisions are made by humans." Route the specific legal requirement for this disclosure to counsel immediately; do not assert compliance yourself.

**4. Deployment Strategy (Day 10)**
*   **Rollout:** Deploy the tool strictly as a **decision-support system**.
*   **Governance:** Assign a specific owner to re-evaluate the model once a diverse dataset becomes available. No autonomous denial is permitted until measured performance across subgroups (lighting, region, device type) is verified.

**Residual Risk:** The system may still exhibit bias against users with lower-quality cameras or in specific weather conditions. This risk is managed, not eliminated, by the human gate.

**Note:** This plan prioritizes preventing irreversible harm (wrongful denial) over full automation. Regulatory filing should reflect the "human-in-the-loop" reality, not an automated capability you cannot verify.
