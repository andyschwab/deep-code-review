**Immediate Action Plan: Holiday Sale Inventory Discrepancy Mitigation**

**1. Fallback Mechanism (Deterministic Rules & Kill Switch)**
*   **Cap Displayed Inventory:** Immediately override the forecasting model's output with a deterministic rule. For any item where actual physical stock is ≤ 20 units, display "Low Stock" or the exact verified count. Do not display forecasted numbers for low-inventory items.
*   **"Verify at Checkout" Gate:** Implement a hard stop at the cart/checkout phase. Before payment authorization, the system must query the live warehouse database. If the item is out of stock, block the transaction with a clear message: "Item just sold out. Please remove to continue." This prevents charging customers for unavailable goods.
*   **Kill Switch:** Designate one engineer to manually toggle a "Safe Mode" flag. If error rates spike, this switch forces all product pages to display "Check Availability at Checkout" instead of any specific number, effectively hiding the forecast entirely.

**2. Communication Strategy (Transparency & Defined Tiers)**
*   **On-Site Disclosure:** Add a static banner near the inventory count on product pages: *"Stock levels update in real-time; displayed counts are estimates during high demand."* This manages expectations without admitting a bug.
*   **Uncertainty Tiers:** Replace precise low numbers (e.g., "3 left") with coarse, defined tiers for items under 50 units:
    *   **Confirmed:** >50 units in warehouse.
    *   **Limited:** 10–50 units (display "Only a few left").
    *   **Critical:** <10 units (display "Almost gone – verify in cart").
    *   *Do not show percentages or model confidence scores.*
*   **Post-Failure Protocol:** If an order must be cancelled due to stock error:
    *   Send an immediate email apology from a human (not automated generic text).
    *   Offer a guaranteed 15% discount code for a future purchase.
    *   Explicitly state: "Our system overestimated availability. We failed to reserve this for you."

**3. Governance & Ownership**
*   **Owner:** Assign one specific team member as the "Inventory Truth Owner" for the duration of the sale. They monitor the "Verify at Checkout" failure logs hourly.
*   **Review Cadence:** Hold a 10-minute stand-up every morning of the sale to review cancellation rates. If cancellations exceed 2% of orders, the Owner triggers the Kill Switch to hide all specific counts.

**4. Residual Risk Note**
*   Some customer frustration is unavoidable if the physical stock is gone; the goal is to prevent *financial* harm (charging for nothing) and *deceptive* patterns (hiding the uncertainty).
*   This plan treats the forecast as "Unverified" for low-stock items, prioritizing hard database checks over model outputs.

**Note:** This approach adheres to your budget by using logic gates rather than new infrastructure. Legal counsel should review the specific wording of the on-site disclosure to ensure it meets local consumer protection standards in Berlin.
