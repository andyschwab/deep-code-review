**Immediate Launch Plan: Harm Containment Over Validation**

You cannot validate accuracy against all regulatory requirements in ten days without counsel, and claiming "validated accuracy" would be a fabrication. Your priority is not to prove the system is safe, but to prevent irreversible harm through strict containment and human gating.

**1. Implement a Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Effective Immediately)**
Stop all fully automated delivery of final contract clauses. The generation of a legally binding document that limits consumer rights is a high-stakes, irreversible action.
*   **Action:** Modify the workflow so the tool outputs a "Draft for Review" rather than a final document.
*   **Requirement:** Every generated clause must be explicitly reviewed and approved by a qualified human (internal team member with legal training or the business owner themselves with clear warnings) before it can be copied, signed, or saved as final.
*   **Constraint:** Do not launch with an "auto-send" or "auto-finalize" feature. If the code forces automation, disable the feature entirely until the gate is built. This is non-negotiable.

**2. Deploy Coarse-Grained Uncertainty Disclosure**
Do not present generated text as authoritative fact. The system must visually and textually distinguish between "AI Suggestion" and "Legal Advice."
*   **Action:** Append a prominent, unremovable disclaimer to every output: *"This is an AI-generated draft, not legal advice. It has not been reviewed by an attorney and may not comply with current laws. You must verify this clause with a qualified professional before use."*
*   **Confidence Tiers:** If your system calculates confidence, do not show percentages (e.g., "90% accurate"). Instead, use defined text tiers: *"Unverified Draft"* (default) vs. *"Human Reviewed"* (only after the gate in step 1 is passed).

**3. Narrow the Scope to "Safe" Templates Only**
Since you cannot test everything, stop generating high-risk clauses dynamically.
*   **Action:** Hard-code a blocklist of high-liability topics (e.g., liability waivers, indemnity clauses, non-competes) that the AI is forbidden to draft from scratch.
*   **Fallback:** For these blocked topics, serve only static, pre-vetted templates (if you have them) or return a message stating: *"This clause requires custom legal drafting. Please consult an attorney."* It is safer to refuse a task than to perform it poorly.

**4. Establish a "Stop Condition" and Rollback Trigger**
Define the specific failure that kills the launch.
*   **Metric:** If *any* user reports a generated clause that actively violates a known statute (like the liability waiver incident), the system must automatically pause generation for that clause type immediately.
*   **Protocol:** Do not wait for a statistical threshold. One confirmed instance of illegal advice is a critical failure requiring immediate manual intervention.

**5. Transparency Artifact (Internal Model Card)**
Create a one-page internal document listing:
*   **Known Limitations:** Explicitly state the system has not been vetted by counsel and fails on complex state-specific consumer protections.
*   **Intended Use:** "Drafting assistance only," never "final legal execution."
*   **Owner:** Name the specific team member responsible for reviewing flagged outputs.

**Critical Note:** You stated you cannot afford external counsel; however, distributing flawed legal documents exposing clients to lawsuits creates a liability far exceeding the cost of a limited-scope review by a lawyer. If you cannot implement the human gate (Step 1) before the funding runs out, **do not launch**. Releasing an un gated legal automation tool is not a "gap" in testing; it is a guaranteed harm vector.
