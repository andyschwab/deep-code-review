### Contingency Protocol: Rapid Human Review & Customer Communication for Account Freezes

**1. Immediate Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Pre-Deployment)**
Do not deploy fully autonomous freezing for high-stakes actions (locking funds) without a human gate. Given the constraint of using only existing internal tools:
*   **Staged Rollout:** Limit the automated freeze to accounts flagged with a "Critical" confidence tier only. All "Medium" or "Low" confidence flags must route to a manual review queue before any action is taken.
*   **Exposure Cap:** Restrict the automated freeze to a maximum of 5% of total daily transaction volume or 10 accounts per hour during the first week. If this threshold is reached, the system must automatically pause all further automated freezes and alert the engineering lead.
*   **Kill Switch:** Designate one engineer as the "Safety Owner" with the authority to instantly disable the freezing logic via feature flag if the false positive rate exceeds 1% in the first 24 hours.

**2. Rapid Human Review Workflow**
*   **Triage Queue:** Establish a dedicated internal channel (e.g., Slack/Teams) where freeze alerts are posted instantly.
*   **SLA:** Set a strict Service Level Agreement: Legitimacy checks must be completed within 15 minutes of the freeze during business hours (JST) and 45 minutes outside business hours.
*   **Verification Criteria:** Reviewers must check two specific data points before unfreezing: (a) recent login location/device consistency, and (b) transaction history pattern match against known fraud vectors. If either is inconclusive, default to "Unfreeze" and monitor rather than maintaining the lock.

**3. Customer Communication Protocol**
*   **Immediate Notification:** Upon freezing, trigger an automated SMS and email stating: "Your account access is temporarily paused for security verification. This is an automated precaution. A human reviewer is assessing your case now."
*   **Transparency on Uncertainty:** Do not state the account is "fraudulent." Use the phrasing: "We detected unusual activity requiring manual confirmation." This avoids definitive claims that may be hallucinated or incorrect.
*   **Resolution Update:** Once the human review is complete, send a second message within 5 minutes: "Review complete. Your account is [restored / requires further info]." If restored, include a direct link to resume activity immediately.
*   **Escalation Path:** Provide a dedicated phone number or priority chat queue for frozen users to speak to a senior agent if the 15-minute SLA is breached.

**4. Post-Incident Review**
*   Log every false positive with the specific trigger cause.
*   Conduct a daily retrospective for the first week to adjust the fraud model's sensitivity thresholds based on actual false positive data.

**Note:** This plan treats account freezing as a high-stakes irreversible action requiring a human gate by default; full automation of this action is the primary risk to mitigate.
**Note:** Legal requirements regarding automated financial decisions in Japan must be verified by counsel; this protocol recommends disclosure as a safety control but does not assert legal compliance.
