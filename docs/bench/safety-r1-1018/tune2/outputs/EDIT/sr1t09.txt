### Immediate Safety Plan: Rule-Based Guards and Human Gate

Given the critical risk of contraindicated prescriptions and the inability to retrain the model before launch, the following deterministic controls must be implemented immediately to block harmful outputs. Full autonomous prescribing is unsafe under current conditions; the system must shift to an "assistive draft" mode where the bot suggests, but rules and humans decide.

**1. Deterministic Rule-Based Guards (The Hard Block)**
Implement a post-generation filtering layer that runs *after* the bot generates a recommendation but *before* it is shown to any human or patient. This layer requires no model retraining, only logic integration with your existing electronic health records (EHR).
*   **Contraindication Matrix:** Hard-code a lookup table of known high-risk drug-condition and drug-drug interactions relevant to your formulary. If the bot's output contains a medication that conflicts with the patient's recorded history (allergies, current meds, diagnosed conditions), the system must **block the output entirely** and return a standard error message: "Recommendation withheld due to potential safety conflict. Escalating for review."
*   **Keyword Triggers:** Flag outputs containing specific high-alert medication classes (e.g., opioids, anticoagulants, insulin) or phrases indicating uncertainty ("might help," "consider trying") for mandatory human review, regardless of the contraindication check.
*   **Fallback Logic:** If the bot's confidence is low (if accessible) or if the input symptom description matches a "high-ambiguity" list (e.g., "chest pain," "severe headache"), bypass the bot's suggestion and trigger a direct alert to a clinician.

**2. Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate**
Until the model is retrained and validated, **no prescription generated by this bot may be sent to a patient without explicit clinician approval.**
*   **Workflow Change:** The bot's output becomes a "draft order" visible only to the prescribing doctor. The interface must clearly label this as "AI-Generated Draft – Requires Verification."
*   **Verification Step:** The clinician must actively click "Approve" or "Edit" before the prescription is finalized. There is no "auto-send" option for this feature in the current phase.
*   **Audit Trail:** Log every bot suggestion, every block by the rule-based guard, and every clinician decision (approve/reject/edit) to build a dataset for future model correction.

**3. Rollout Strategy & Kill Switch**
*   **Staged Exposure:** Do not roll out to all patients. Limit the feature to a small pilot group (e.g., 5% of traffic or specific low-risk clinics) for the first two weeks.
*   **Kill Switch:** Implement an immediate global toggle to disable the bot's suggestion engine entirely, reverting to standard manual entry, if the rate of blocked recommendations exceeds a defined threshold (e.g., >2% of drafts blocked) or if any adverse event is reported.
*   **Disclosure:** Clearly inform clinicians that they are interacting with an experimental AI assistant. Patient-facing materials must not imply the prescription was autonomously decided by AI.

**Residual Risk Note:** This plan mitigates immediate harm but does not fix the underlying model error. The "UNVERIFIED" status of the model's accuracy remains until retraining occurs. Legal counsel must review the disclosure language and liability implications of AI-assisted prescribing in your jurisdiction before launch.
