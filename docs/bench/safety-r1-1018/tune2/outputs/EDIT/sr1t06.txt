### Step-by-Step Audit and Monitoring Framework

**1. Map the Harm Inventory (Immediate)**
Before testing, explicitly document the risk: "Systematic exclusion of specific neighborhoods due to biased filters." Identify the affected groups (users seeking housing in those areas, landlords in those areas) and the worst-case realistic impact (reinforced segregation, financial loss for sellers, legal liability under fair housing laws). An empty inventory is not a pass; write this down now.

**2. Measure with Adversarial Probes (Days 1–5)**
Since you cannot retrain the model, you must test the current logic rigorously. Do not rely on aggregate accuracy.
*   **Construct Probe Sets:** Create synthetic user queries and listing profiles that are identical in price, size, and quality but differ *only* by neighborhood (targeting the suspected areas vs. control areas).
*   **Define Controls:** Use "known-good" inputs (listings in neutral areas that should pass) and "known-bad" inputs (listings that should legitimately fail due to price/rules) to validate your test harness.
*   **Execute Sliced Evaluation:** Run these probes against your current filter. Measure the pass/fail rate *per neighborhood*, not just overall.
*   **Set Stop Conditions:** Define a threshold for failure (e.g., "If visibility in Target Neighborhood A is >10% lower than Control Neighborhood B for identical listings, launch is blocked"). If you cannot measure this difference, the metric is `UNVERIFIED`, and you cannot claim fairness.

**3. Manage Residual Risk with Deterministic Rules (Days 6–8)**
You cannot fix the data bias in ten days, so you must gate the output.
*   **Implement a Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** For any listing in the flagged neighborhoods that the model recommends hiding or down-ranking, force a manual review by a human operator before the decision is finalized. Do not allow fully automated exclusion of these areas.
*   **Override Mechanism:** Ensure customer support or listing agents can easily override a filter decision if a listing is wrongly hidden.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a brief "Model Card" section for internal use stating: "Intended Use: Ranking listings. Limitation: Known data gaps in Neighborhood X. Mitigation: Human review gate active for Zone Y." Do not claim the system is "unbiased"; state the limitation and the control.

**4. Post-Launch Monitoring (Day 10+)**
*   **Continuous Slicing:** Automate the reporting of listing visibility rates by neighborhood daily.
*   **Feedback Loop:** Create a specific channel for users to report "missing listings" and tag these by location.
*   **Re-evaluation Trigger:** If the disparity metric exceeds your pre-defined threshold post-launch, trigger an immediate rollback of the filtering feature for the affected zones.

**Note:** I cannot certify this system as "fair" or "safe"; only your team can make the ship decision based on the residual risk reported above. If legal counsel determines these filters violate local housing regulations, that determination overrides this technical framework.
