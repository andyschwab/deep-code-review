I can't write that confirmation, and you shouldn't sign one. No test proves drafts are "unbiased," and whether the tool creates legal obligations for the customer is a legal determination, not an engineering one. A letter asserting either would be an unsupported claim sitting in a contract file, and it would be worst for you if a pay or promotion dispute ever cited it. Here is what I'd do in the three weeks instead.

**Harm inventory (who is hurt, worst case)**
- **Bias:** gendered or age-coded language, or different standards for equivalent ratings. Employees are affected, and the worst case is a systematically skewed pay or promotion record.
- **Hallucination as fact:** the draft invents incidents, achievements or quotes the manager never entered, and these land in a permanent record.
- **Over-reliance:** managers skim and send without real edits, so the model's phrasing becomes the record.
- **Missing disclosure:** employees can't tell the text was machine-drafted.
- **Unsafe automation:** a draft flows into the HR record, and from there into pay decisions, without a deliberate human act.

**Controls, enforced in code rather than the prompt**
- Nothing enters the employee-facing record until the manager explicitly confirms, and that confirmation is enforced server-side. Propose zero tolerance for any path that bypasses it.
- Flag any claim in the draft that isn't traceable to the manager's notes or ratings, and highlight it for review.
- Mark drafts as AI-assisted in the record. Whether disclosure is legally required is for counsel.
- Ship in shadow mode with the prospect first, with drafts logged but not used, then roll out in stages. Keep a kill switch and a named owner who monitors the same metrics afterward.

**Test rows, one per harm**
- **Bias:** build counterfactual pairs with identical notes and ratings, varying only gender cues (names, pronouns) and age cues (tenure phrasing, graduation years). Compare tone, length, competence versus warmth words, and consistency between rating and language, sliced by group. Use a deliberately skewed template as a positive control that the metric must catch. Rerun identical inputs as a negative control to measure noise from non-deterministic output. The threshold is owner-set, and the stop condition is that a failing slice blocks that customer's launch.
- **Fabrication:** probe with sparse notes and contradictory ratings, and count unsupported claims. Set the threshold with the owner and propose zero tolerance for invented incidents.
- **Injection and leakage:** put instructions in the notes field and check that no other employee's data appears.
- **Over-reliance:** in shadow mode, measure how often managers send drafts unedited.

Zero failures in n probes only bounds the true rate at roughly 3/n at 95% confidence, so size n to the tolerance you choose. Any "ground truth" taken from past reviews is itself selection-biased, so say so in the results.

**Three-week plan for four people**
- **Week 1:** finalize the inventory, write the confirmation gate and the unsupported-claim flag, build the probe sets, and send counsel the questions below on day one.
- **Week 2:** run the evals, fix what fails, and rerun. Draft the model card with measured results by subgroup. Anything unmeasured stays `UNVERIFIED`.
- **Week 3:** shadow-mode pilot with the prospect, kill switch tested, monitoring and owner named.
- **If time runs short:** ship narrower, with narrative sections only, opt-in for one department, and no pay-linked fields populated by the draft.

**Questions for counsel (applicability, thresholds and dates are theirs)**
- Do employment anti-discrimination rules for sex and age apply to AI-assisted evaluations?
- Do any state or local rules on automated employment decision tools apply, such as bias audits or notice to employees?
- Do privacy and employee-data rules apply?
- If any customers have EU staff, does the EU AI Act's treatment of employment systems apply?
- How should liability be allocated in the contract?

Counsel needs to know the feature's function, that managers can edit, that output feeds pay and promotion, and the customer locations. The cycle opening in three weeks is the time-sensitive fact.

**What you can send the HR director now**
"Tallow has inventoried the harms this feature could cause, is testing drafts for gender- and age-related differences using paired probes, requires manager confirmation before any draft enters the record, and will share measured results and limitations in a model card. We can't warrant that outputs are unbiased, and questions about your legal obligations should go to your counsel. We've asked ours to prepare answers on the regulatory questions."

If the prospect won't proceed without the original wording, that tells you something about the risk they're trying to hand to you. The ship decision is yours, made on the measured results.
