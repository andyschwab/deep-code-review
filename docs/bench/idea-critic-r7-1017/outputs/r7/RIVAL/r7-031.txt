**Recommendation:** Do not sign the contract yet. Proceed only after completing a 30-day pilot at one studio to validate bandwidth costs and securing written consent from instructors regarding broadcast rights. The current projection ignores a likely $300/month bandwidth expense and assumes a 20% conversion rate that may be optimistic without verifying legal restrictions first.

**Main Ways This Could Fail:**
1.  **Legal Block:** Existing instructor contracts may explicitly forbid digital broadcasting, requiring costly renegotiations or causing key staff to leave.
2.  **Margin Erosion:** Unmeasured bandwidth costs (~$300/month) could cut your projected $1,200 profit by 25%, extending the break-even point significantly.
3.  **Conversion Miss:** If only 10% of studio attendees convert instead of 20%, you will operate at a monthly loss of ~$1,400 even before bandwidth costs.
4.  **Integration Debt:** If the analytics do not integrate seamlessly with your CRM, manual data entry will consume staff hours, negating operational efficiency gains.

**Kill Metric:** Terminate the project if, after the 30-day pilot, the **net profit per digital subscriber falls below $8** (after accounting for bandwidth, support, and amortized setup) or if **legal counsel identifies unresolvable restrictions in more than 20% of active instructor contracts**.
