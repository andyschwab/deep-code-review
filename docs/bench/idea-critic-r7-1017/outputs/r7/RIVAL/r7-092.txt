**Recommendation: Proceed with a Phased Pilot, Not Full Adoption**

Do not switch all 12 hectares immediately. The math does not yet support a full rollout. While the projected revenue lift (€9,600) covers exactly half of the increased cost (€19,200), you are still operating at a net loss of €9,600 in the first year unless the "15% yield lift" is guaranteed. Given the regulatory uncertainty regarding EU organic certification, a full switch exposes the vineyard to unnecessary financial and compliance risk.

Instead, convert **2 hectares** as a control pilot for the 2024 season. This limits your additional upfront cost to €3,200 while allowing you to validate the yield claims and ensure Vineveil remains compliant under new EU rules before committing the entire block.

**Three Main Failure Modes**

1.  **Regulatory Decertification:** The upcoming EU organic changes ban or restrict Vineveil's active ingredient mid-season.
    *   *Result:* You lose the entire investment on treated grapes and face a costly emergency switch back to chemicals or loss of organic status for the vintage.
2.  **Yield Performance Gap:** The projected 15% yield lift fails to materialize due to higher-than-expected pest pressure or product inefficacy.
    *   *Result:* If the yield increase is less than 7.5%, the extra revenue will not even cover half the increased cost, deepening the net loss beyond the projected €9,600.
3.  **Pest Resistance or Outbreak:** Vineveil proves insufficient against a specific local pest variant not covered in general trials.
    *   *Result:* Crop damage exceeds the historical average, reducing total usable volume below the baseline of the chemical regimen.

**Stop-Loss Triggers**

Execute an immediate review and halt further expansion if either of the following occurs during the pilot:

*   **Financial Trigger:** Net revenue from the pilot block falls more than **€1,500** below the projected baseline (indicating the yield lift is underperforming by a critical margin).
*   **Agronomic Trigger:** Pest pressure exceeds **1.1 kg/ha** despite treatment, or EU regulators issue any notice of review regarding Vineveil's active ingredients.

*Note: Secure a written guarantee from the Vineveil supplier regarding yield performance before signing any contract. Monitor EU regulatory bulletins monthly starting January 2024.*
