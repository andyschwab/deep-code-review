**Top Three Failure Modes:**

1.  **Integration Quagmire:** The vendor's vague "few weeks" estimate is the single biggest threat. Legacy platforms often lack modern APIs, meaning the integration could require custom middleware that costs $100k+ and takes 6+ months, not weeks. If this happens, your migration deadline of 1 Dec 2025 will be missed, and the projected 30% efficiency gain will never materialize because staff will be dual-entering data.
2.  **Budget Overrun via Hidden Reallocation:** Your assumption that the $200k IT spend can absorb this without reallocation is unverified. If integration requires external consultants or unexpected downtime mitigation, you will likely need to pull funds from other critical projects. This creates internal friction and risks stalling other firm priorities, turning a "secure portal" win into a broader IT resource crisis.
3.  **Adoption Friction & Data Integrity:** Migrating 150 active accounts in six weeks (Oct–Dec) while integrating systems is aggressive. If data mapping fails during migration, you risk corrupting active client files or creating version control nightmares. Furthermore, if the UI differs significantly from the current system, senior partners may resist adoption, leaving the firm paying for a tool nobody uses while the old system runs in parallel (doubling costs).

**Kill Trigger:**
Terminate the project immediately if the **integration effort exceeds 8 weeks or costs more than $75,000** (including internal labor and external consultants). This cap is roughly double the vendor's implied "few weeks" but prevents the project from consuming the entire year's IT roadmap.
