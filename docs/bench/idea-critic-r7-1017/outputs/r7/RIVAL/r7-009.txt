**Recommendation: Do Not Proceed in Current Form**

The proposal contains a fundamental unit-economics error that renders the project unprofitable under realistic conditions. You are modeling an 8% gross margin (12% revenue minus 4% assumed costs), but local logistics run at 8%, which alone eliminates your entire profit buffer before accounting for the mandatory $50,000 compliance fee or the $120,000 marketing spend. With a $12 average resale price, an 8% logistics cost is $0.96 per item. On 150,000 items, that is $144,000 in logistics alone, leaving only $36,000 from the projected $180,000 gross profit to cover marketing, compliance, and build costs. The project burns cash on every transaction.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**

1.  **Negative Contribution Margin:** The 5% European vendor fee benchmark is irrelevant to the Delhi market. At 8% local logistics plus the unpriced $50,000 regulatory fee, the cost per item exceeds the revenue per item once marketing acquisition costs are included. You will scale losses, not profits.
2.  **Supply-Side Collapse:** The model assumes 20% customer participation (30,000 active sellers from 150k inventory) without the budgeted $120,000 marketing push. If participation drops to a conservative 5% (7,500 items), fixed build costs of $800,000 cannot be amortized, resulting in a cost-per-item of over $100, far exceeding the $12 price point.
3.  **Regulatory Stoppage:** Launching without the $50,000 compliance fee reserved creates an existential risk. If the Delhi regulator imposes this fee post-launch or halts operations for non-compliance, the platform faces immediate shutdown and reputational damage that could impact the core business.

**Kill/Redesign Trigger**

**Trigger:** If verified vendor logistics contracts exceed 6% of the transaction value OR if first-month active seller participation is below 4% (6,000 items listed).

**Action:** Immediately pause the build and redesign the unit economics. Do not wait for the full nine-month build to complete; this must be validated in a 4-week pilot before January 2025.

**Note:** You must re-baseline the financial model using local Delhi logistics rates (8%) and include the $50,000 compliance fee as a hard cost before approaching the board. The current European benchmark is misleading and dangerous to rely upon.
