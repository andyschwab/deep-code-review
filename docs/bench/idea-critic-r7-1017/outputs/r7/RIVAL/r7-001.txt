**Recommendation:** Proceed with the pilot, but cap the initial integration spend at $4,000 and treat the Q1 target as a validation milestone rather than a revenue goal. The unit economics are strong enough to justify the risk, provided churn stays within the modeled 5%.

**Top Three Failure Points:**
1.  **Churn Exceeding 7%:** At 5% churn, the model works. If early data shows monthly churn above 7%, the cost to acquire subscribers will outpace their lifetime value, making the 12,000-subscriber goal financially impossible.
2.  **Integration Cost Overrun:** Since vendors only quoted "low-four figures," an unexpected complexity spike could push costs toward $10k+. Without a hard cap, the pilot could burn cash before shipping a single bag.
3.  **Fulfillment Margin Compression:** The model assumes an $8/bag cost. If operational friction (packaging, labor, shipping errors) pushes the true cost above $9.50/bag, the 12% platform fee combined with COGS will erase net profit.

**Kill/Pivot Trigger:**
Terminate or pivot the project if the pilot achieves a **Net Profit Margin below 10%** or **Monthly Churn exceeds 6.5%** for two consecutive months.
