**Recommendation: Do not approve the full $12M rollout yet.** Instead, authorize a **$1.5M "Pre-Launch Validation Phase"** (July–September 2025) specifically to resolve the unpriced data integration and regulatory rebate uncertainty. Proceed with the remaining $10.5M only if the regulator confirms a rebate rate that sustains >10% ROI and the middleware integration cost comes in under $600k.

**Main Failure Modes:**
1.  **Regulatory Rejection:** If the regulator denies the anticipated rebate rate, the unit economics collapse, turning a projected profit into a loss on every active user.
2.  **Integration Cost Overrun:** The 5% budget allocation ($600k) for middleware is a guess; if legacy system complexity doubles this cost, it erodes the margin needed to subsidize wearables.
3.  **Adoption/Engagement Mismatch:** If the 10% enrollment target is met but the 80% app-usage assumption fails (e.g., due to poor UX or lack of incentives), the promised 3% acute-care reduction will not materialize, invalidating the core ROI thesis.
4.  **Hardware Margin Compression:** At $45/device, even a small increase in hardware costs or a lower-than-expected rebate creates a negative cash flow per enrolled member.

**Trigger to Halt or Pivot:**
**The "Unit-Economics Gate" at Month 3 (September 30, 2025).**
*   **Halt Condition:** If the regulator has not formally confirmed a rebate rate that guarantees a minimum 10% ROI *or* if the finalized middleware integration quote exceeds $750k (25% over budget).
*   **Pivot Condition:** If enrollment exceeds 10% but app usage is below 60% by Month 4, pause member acquisition and pivot budget entirely to engagement mechanics (gamification, support) before scaling further.

**Note:** The Veracruz pilot's 15% ROI is not directly transferable due to the difference in member pool scale and the unverified rebate assumption; treating it as a guaranteed baseline is the plan's greatest risk.
