**Recommendation: Proceed with a Controlled Pilot, Not a Full Rollout**

Do not commit to the full $150,000 annual contract yet. Instead, negotiate a 90-day pilot covering 20% of your portfolio (700 borrowers) at a capped cost of $30,000. The vendor’s arithmetic on savings is overly optimistic; reducing defaults from 5% to 3% represents a 40% improvement in risk performance, which is exceptionally rare for a first-generation model. A pilot allows you to validate the actual reduction in default rates and, crucially, test regulatory acceptance of alternative data before scaling. If the pilot demonstrates a default reduction of at least 1.5 percentage points (saving ~$600k annually at scale) without regulatory friction, then sign the full contract.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**

1.  **Regulatory Rejection of Alternative Data:** Regulators may deem mobile-money logs non-compliant for credit decisions due to privacy laws or "fair lending" concerns. If authorities block the use of this specific data mid-rollout, the model’s predictive power could collapse, rendering the $150,000 spend worthless while leaving you with a broken underwriting process.
2.  **Model Decay and Data Drift:** The vendor’s performance guarantee assumes static data quality. If mobile-money transaction patterns shift (e.g., due to new competitors or economic changes) or if the "alternative" data proves less correlated with repayment than the vendor’s training sets suggested, the model will fail to hit the 3% target. You risk paying for data points that add no signal over your current bureau feeds.
3.  **Implementation Lag and Operational Disruption:** Integrating real-time alternative data into your existing loan origination system may take longer than expected. If the integration delays the go-live past Q4 2025, or if the new scoring causes unexpected friction in loan approval times (angering customers), the projected savings will not materialize in the fiscal year, hurting your P&L immediately.

**Abort/Pivot Trigger**

**Trigger:** If, after six months of operation (including the pilot and initial scale-up), the **annualized bad-debt savings fall below $400,000** (equivalent to a default rate reduction of less than 1 percentage point) OR if a **formal regulatory inquiry is opened** regarding the use of mobile-money data.

**Action:** Immediately terminate the vendor contract (invoking the performance clause if possible) and revert to the legacy credit bureau scoring model for all new originations. Do not attempt to "tune" the model further; the cost of capital and compliance risk outweighs the diminishing returns.
