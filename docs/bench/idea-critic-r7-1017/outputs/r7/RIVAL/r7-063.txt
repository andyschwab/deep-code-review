**Recommendation: Proceed with Pilot, Conditional on Data Audit**

Approve the three-month pilot starting Q3 2025, but withhold commitment to the full two-year contract until a specific "Data Quality Gate" is passed in Month 1 of the pilot. The financial upside is compelling, but the assumption that legacy grinders will connect via OPC-UA without extra hardware or latency issues is a critical single point of failure.

**Financial Snapshot**
*   **Current Pain:** 1.44M PLN/year (120k/month).
*   **Target Savings:** ~432k PLN/year (30% reduction).
*   **Cost:** 141k PLN/year (subscription + amortized integration).
*   **Net Value:** ~291k PLN/year positive cash flow, assuming targets are met.
*   **Risk:** The unbudgeted 5k PLN training cost and potential hardware upgrades for old grinders could erode margins by 10–15%.

**Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **Technical (High Risk):** Legacy grinders lack the necessary sensor tags or suffer high latency on the existing OPC-UA network. If data arrives too slowly or incompletely, the predictive models will generate false positives, causing operators to ignore the system entirely.
2.  **Operational (Medium Risk):** Plant staff reject the alerts due to lack of trust or insufficient training. Without the budgeted 5k PLN training program, adoption will likely stall, rendering the software useless regardless of algorithmic accuracy.
3.  **Financial (Low Risk):** The vendor fails to integrate within the 45k PLN fee, citing unforeseen complexity with the old machines, leading to scope creep and budget overruns.

**Kill/Pivot Trigger**
Establish a hard checkpoint at the **end of Month 6** (3 months pilot + 3 months rollout):
*   **Condition:** If verified downtime reduction is **<10%** (vs. the modeled 30%) AND the root cause is identified as **data latency or missing tags** on the legacy grinders.
*   **Action:** **Terminate** the contract immediately. Do not attempt to patch the data pipeline; the hardware limitation is likely structural. Pivot budget toward targeted hardware replacement for the specific failing grinders rather than paying for a SaaS solution that cannot read them.

**Note:** Ensure the 5,000 PLN training budget is formally added to the pilot phase; skipping this invalidates the operational success metric.
**Note:** Require Gearwave to sign a clause stating the 45k PLN integration fee covers all necessary adapters for the current machine list.
