**Three Main Failure Points:**
1.  **Fatal Math Error:** You project 12,000 extra sales from a 5% lift, but 5% of your $1.2M revenue at a $10 ticket is only **6,000 extra sales**, not 12,000. Your projected ROI is inflated by 100%. Furthermore, assuming 70% (10,500) of regulars will download a crypto wallet is unrealistic; industry standard for non-custodial wallet adoption in loyalty is typically <5%. 2.  **Unbudgeted Compliance Risk:** Launching in Q3 2025 without a legal quote for digital asset regulations is reckless. If the state classifies these NFTs as securities or requires money-transmitter licensing, legal and restructuring costs could easily exceed your entire $120k development budget, turning the project into a net loss before a single token is minted.
3.  **Friction vs. Reward:** The friction of setting up a wallet, managing gas fees (unless you abstract this entirely, which increases dev cost), and securing keys vastly outweighs the perceived value of a loyalty discount for the average beer drinker. This will likely result in high drop-off rates and negative customer sentiment rather than increased loyalty.
