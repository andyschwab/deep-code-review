**Recommendation:** Proceed with a controlled scale-up to 2,500 weekly parcels immediately, but withhold full 10,000-parcel deployment until a 90-day wind-impact study is completed. The €0.20 per-package saving is compelling, but your assumption that battery costs are negligible and wind cancellations are unquantified represents unacceptable financial risk at full scale.

**Three Most Likely Failure Modes:**
1.  **Weather Volatility:** Unmodeled wind thresholds cause >15% flight cancellation rates, forcing expensive van fallbacks that erase the €0.20 margin.
2.  **Battery Degradation:** Assuming negligible replacement costs is dangerous; if battery life drops 20% faster than lab tests, capital expenditure could double within 18 months.
3.  **Regulatory Shift:** A single incident or policy change in the EU tightens BVLOS (Beyond Visual Line of Sight) rules, grounding the fleet despite operational readiness.

**Stop/Pivot Trigger:** Halt expansion if the **blended cost per package exceeds €0.38** (a 52% increase over pilot costs) over any rolling 30-day period. This threshold accounts for increased van fallbacks due to weather and initial battery maintenance, ensuring you never scale a loss-making operation.
