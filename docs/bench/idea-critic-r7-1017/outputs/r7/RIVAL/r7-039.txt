**Recommendation: Proceed with Pilot, Withhold Full Rollout Commitment**

Approve the NZ$1.65 million pilot for the three named farms effective 1 July 2025, but explicitly decouple this from the cooperative-wide rollout. The projected NZ$2.4 million annual return relies on an 80% adoption rate within six months, a assumption that is historically aggressive for cooperative structures without regulatory clarity. Treat the pilot strictly as a validation engine for the regulatory timeline and ERP integration costs, which currently represent unquantified liabilities potentially exceeding NZ$100,000.

**Top Three Failure Modes**

1.  **Regulatory Stalemate:** Data sovereignty laws may prohibit storing milk traceability data on a permissioned blockchain without specific exemptions. If the Ministry for Primary Industries does not grant provisional approval within 90 days of the start date, the system cannot legally scale beyond the pilot farms.
2.  **Integration Cost Explosion:** The undefined 5–7% ERP integration cost is a risk vector. If legacy middleware requires custom rewriting rather than standard API connection, costs could double, eroding the ROI before the first liter is traced.
3.  **Adoption Friction:** The 80% adoption target assumes voluntary cooperative buy-in. If farmer training reveals the hardware upgrades disrupt daily milking workflows, adoption will stall below the 50% threshold required to justify the premium pricing model.

**Kill/Pause Triggers**

Execute an immediate project pause if either of the following conditions is met:
*   **Adoption Failure:** Fewer than 50% of the cooperative farms have signed binding adoption agreements by 31 December 2025 (six months post-launch).
*   **Operational Inefficacy:** The pilot farms fail to demonstrate a minimum 5% reduction in recall identification time or product loss during the first quarter (ending 30 September 2025).

*Note: The total budget should be formally amended to NZ$1.65 million immediately to account for the likely ERP integration variance.*
