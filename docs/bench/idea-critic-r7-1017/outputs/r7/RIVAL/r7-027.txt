The vendor’s math relies on a "best-case" scenario that ignores your integration risk. 3.  **Adoption Friction:** A 90-day rollout is aggressive for training. If field managers find the interface complex or the alerts irrelevant during the critical planting window, they will revert to legacy methods, rendering the analytics useless regardless of their accuracy.
