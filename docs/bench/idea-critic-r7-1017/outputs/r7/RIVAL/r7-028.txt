**Recommendation: Do not sign the current term sheet; renegotiate to cap recoupment at the $500k advance only and require a fixed marketing spend commitment before signing.**

The current math contains a critical flaw in how the "net" revenue is calculated before the split. Your internal forecast projects $2M in *gross* sales. However, you must deduct the ~12% platform fees ($240k) and the $80k localization cost *before* determining the pool for recoupment and splitting, unless the contract explicitly states Pixel Forge absorbs localization.

If Pixel Forge recoups their $500k advance from the *gross* ($2M), they take $500k off the top. The remaining $1.5M is then split 70/30. You get $1.05M + the initial $500k (already spent) = $1.55M total value, minus your $80k localization = **$1.47M net to you.**

However, if "recouped before royalty split" means they take their $500k + their $350k marketing ($850k total) off the *gross* first, the pool shrinks to $1.15M. Your 70% share of that is only $805k. Plus you still pay the $80k localization. Total net to you: **$725k.** This is drastically lower than your optimistic view.

**Main Failure Modes:**
1.  **The "Marketing Black Box":** The deal says Pixel Forge covers marketing, but without a minimum spend clause or audit right, they could spend $50k on low-effort ads, claim the rest as "internal services," and leave your game invisible.
2.  **Recoupment Waterfall:** If the contract allows them to recoup *both* the advance and marketing costs before you see a dime, your upside is severely capped. With $850k off the top, you need massive sales just to break even on your time.
3.  **Localization Ambiguity:** If the $80k comes out of your share rather than being a dedicated budget line item funded by the advance, your margins evaporate.

**Walk-Away Trigger:**
If the projected **Net Revenue to Studio (after fees, localization, and split) is < $900k** based on conservative sales estimates (e.g., $1.2M gross instead of $2M), walk away or demand a higher split (80/20) after recoupment.

**Arithmetic Slip & Overlooked Risk:**
Your growth math is slightly off. Starting at $400k in Month 1 with 25% MoM growth yields ~$2.3M in Year 1, not $2M. While this looks good, it relies on perfect execution. The bigger risk is **cash flow timing**. The $500k advance is likely paid in tranches (e.g., on signing, alpha, beta, launch). If they withhold the final tranche pending "approval," you could be stranded paying the $80k localization and living expenses while waiting for a check they can delay indefinitely. Ensure the advance schedule is tied to dates, not subjective milestones.
