**The Three Biggest Failure Modes**

1.  **Hardware Fragmentation & Exclusion:** By targeting iOS only based on a generic "70% market share" statistic, you risk alienating the 30% of high-value customers using Android or older iPhones lacking LiDAR/ARKit support. If your core buyers are older demographics, their device turnover is likely slower than the general market, meaning the "addressable market" for the app could be less than 40% of your base, destroying the unit economics.
2.  **The "Novelty Drop-off" (Low Retention):** AR apps frequently suffer from high initial download rates followed by immediate churn once the "wow factor" fades. If the app does not solve a specific friction point (e.g., visualizing exact scale in a room) better than existing tools, users will not return. With a €30,000 annual support fee, a low-usage app becomes a sunk cost rather than a sales driver within six months.
3.  **Conversion Disconnect:** High download volume (20,000) does not guarantee the projected €120 average order value. Users may treat the app as a game or catalog viewer without completing purchases. If the integration between the AR visualization and the checkout flow is not seamless, you will incur development costs for a marketing tool that fails to move the needle on actual furniture sales.
