**Recommendation: Conditional Approval with Strict Performance Gates**

Proceed with the Phase 1 rollout only if Trackpulse contractually guarantees a **maximum false-positive rate of 5%** and agrees to a **clawback clause** if Year 1 net savings fall below **$4 million**. **Three Primary Failure Modes**

1.  **Technical (The "Boy Who Cried Wolf" Effect):** The pilot's 95% accuracy may collapse at scale due to data heterogeneity across different track ages and weather zones. If false positives exceed 10%, technicians will begin ignoring alerts, rendering the system useless and potentially causing missed catastrophic failures.
2.  **Financial (The Volume Mismatch):** The $12M savings projection assumes linear scalability. If the national network's baseline downtime is less volatile than the Midwest corridor, or if the $800k/hour cost estimate includes fixed costs that don't disappear with reduced downtime, actual cash savings could be 40–50% lower than projected, extending the ROI period from 6 months to over 2 years.
3.  **Operational (The Training Bottleneck):** Rolling out to 150 technicians simultaneously creates a high risk of inconsistent adoption. If even 20% of the workforce mistrusts or misinterprets the AI diagnostics, the system creates friction rather than efficiency, leading to delayed repairs and increased liability.

**Scrap Trigger Condition**

Terminate the project immediately if, after the first 12 months of operation, the **verified net cash savings (actual downtime reduction minus total operating costs) are less than $4.0 million.** This threshold accounts for a 33% underperformance against the optimistic $12M target while still covering the $3.5M license, $1.2M training, and $0.2M storage costs with a minimal safety margin.

**Hidden Risks & Arithmetic Slips**

*   **Arithmetic Slip (Annualization Error):** You stated the pilot saved "roughly 15 hours per year" based on a 6-month run. If you observed 15 hours of saved downtime *during* the 6-month pilot, the annualized run-rate is **30 hours**, not 15. This implies the potential annual savings could be **$24 million**, not $12 million. Conversely, if you only saved 15 hours *annualized* from the 6-month data, the pilot effect was negligible. Clarify this immediately; it swings the ROI by $12M.
*   **Hidden Risk (Liability Shift):** By adopting predictive AI, the rail network may inadvertently assume higher legal liability. If the AI predicts a fault is "safe" and a derailment occurs, plaintiffs will argue the railway was negligent in trusting a known fallible algorithm over traditional manual inspection protocols.
*   **Hidden Cost (Model Drift):** The budget includes storage but no line item for **model retraining**. Rail environments change (new rolling stock, track wear patterns); without an annual budget for data science labor to retrain the model, accuracy will degrade by ~5–10% annually, invalidating the savings thesis by Year 3.
