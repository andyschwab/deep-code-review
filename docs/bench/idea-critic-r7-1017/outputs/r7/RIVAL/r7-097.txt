**Recommendation: Proceed with a Phased Rollout (Conditional Go)**

Approve the full rollout to eight additional sites only after securing written premium agreements from at least 60% of target buyers (a "soft launch" threshold). The projected net annual benefit of ~$680,000 justifies the risk, provided we correct the budget baseline immediately. The pilot's 2.5% price uplift validates the market demand, but the unknown regulatory timeline requires us to treat integration as modular rather than monolithic.

**Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **Buyer Resistance:** The revenue model collapses if fewer than 50% of buyers accept the premium, leaving us with high fixed costs and no price recovery.
2.  **Regulatory Paralysis:** An undefined approval delay could stall the system indefinitely, turning the $75k integration budget into sunk cost with no operational utility.
3.  **Fee Erosion:** The 0.5% transaction fee, while small per unit, scales aggressively; if volume spikes without the expected price premium, margins will compress faster than anticipated.

**Pre-Agreed Kill/Pivot Triggers**
*   **Kill Switch:** Terminate the project immediately if cumulative costs (integration + fees) exceed the revised budget ($525,000 total rollout estimate) by **15%**, OR if verified premium uptake remains below **40%** after 90 days of full operation.
*   **Pivot Trigger:** If regulatory approval is not granted within **6 months**, pause all new site integrations and shift resources to a "light" version of the ledger that operates off-chain for internal tracking only, awaiting legal clarity.
