**Recommendation:** Proceed with the Bruges pilot **only if** the vendor contract includes a clause guaranteeing compliance with Belgium's 2026 radio spectrum regulations at no extra cost. Without this, the regulatory risk alone threatens the entire €295,000 investment.

1.  **Regulatory Obsolescence:** The hardware uses a radio spectrum that may become illegal in Belgium in 2026. If the vendor cannot upgrade firmware remotely or replace units cheaply, you face a forced, costly rip-and-replace shortly after full deployment.
2.  **Adoption Delusion:** The plan assumes 80% brewmaster adoption in six months. If actual adoption lags (e.g., hits only 40%), the data density drops, rendering the waste-reduction algorithms inaccurate and delaying ROI by over a year.
3.  **Hidden Integration Costs:** You have excluded the €10,000/year legacy POS integration fee from the ROI calculation. Over three years, this €30,000 gap, combined with the service fee, erodes the net savings from €300k to roughly €225k, extending the payback period significantly.
