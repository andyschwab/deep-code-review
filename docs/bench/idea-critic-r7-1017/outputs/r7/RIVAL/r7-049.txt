**Recommendation: Do not move forward with the current plan.**

The financial model contains a critical structural error that invalidates the revenue projection. You are calculating revenue based on **gross bookings** ($200 × 12,000 users = $2.4M) while ignoring **churn**. If 15% of users leave after three months, they do not generate annual revenue. Furthermore, if the $200 represents a *per-trip* value, the model assumes every user books exactly once a year; if it is an *annual* target, the churn calculation must reduce the active user count before multiplying by price.

Corrected, the math looks closer to: (10,200 retained users × $200) = **$2.04M**, not $2.5M. More dangerously, comparing yourself to OceanPath's *annual* revenue using your *gross booking volume* (ignoring that OceanPath likely has recurring retention you haven't modeled) is an apples-to-oranges comparison that will undermine credibility with Suriya Kham.

**Primary Failure Modes:**

1.  **The Licensing Black Box:** You have no cost data for the high-resolution satellite tiles. If this fee is per-user or per-download, it could obliterate margins on the $200 booking value. Launching without a signed term sheet or a hard cap on these costs is financially negligent.
2.  **Single-Point Dependency:** Relying entirely on the Thai Tourism Authority for free map data creates a single point of failure. If political shifts or budget cuts alter this partnership, your core product (offline navigation) breaks immediately.
3.  **Acquisition Cost Blindness:** The $150,000 marketing budget to acquire 12,000 users implies a Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) of $12.50. In the travel sector, this is aggressively optimistic. If CAC rises to $25, you need double the budget to hit your targets, delaying profitability by quarters.
4.  **Seasonality Ignorance:** Launching June 15 puts you directly into the low-season monsoon period. User acquisition and trip bookings naturally dip during this window, making the Q4 target of 12,000 users significantly harder to reach than a dry-season launch.
