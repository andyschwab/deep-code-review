**Recommendation: Proceed with a 90‑Day Pilot, Not a Full 12‑Month Commitment**

Do not sign the 12‑month, $144,000 contract yet. Instead, negotiate a 90‑day pilot covering two clinics for a fixed fee (capped at $25,000 including onboarding). This approach validates the vendor's 15% readmission reduction claim and your internal cost assumptions before exposing the network to full financial risk. The current proposal relies on unverified variables—specifically the average cost per readmission and patient adherence rates—making a full rollout a speculative gamble rather than a data‑driven investment.

**Primary Failure Modes**

1.  **Adherence Collapse:** The 80% broadband estimate measures capability, not willingness. If patients lack digital literacy or motivation, adherence will drop, rendering the technology useless regardless of connection speed.
2.  **Financial Mirage:** Without knowing your actual average cost per readmission, the projected $150k savings is theoretical. If your actual cost per stay is lower than the vendor assumes, the program will fail to cover its $144k annual price tag.
3.  **Hidden Operational Costs:** The contract excludes ongoing hardware and maintenance. If clinics must purchase tablets or hire staff to manage the platform, the ROI will evaporate.

**Kill/Pivot Triggers**

Establish these specific thresholds at the end of the 90‑day pilot to decide the future of the program:

*   **Kill Threshold:** If readmissions decrease by **less than 8%** or patient adherence falls below **60%**, terminate the project immediately. These results indicate the solution does not work for our specific demographic.
*   **Pivot Threshold:** If readmissions drop between **8% and 12%**, do not expand. Instead, renegotiate the contract to a per‑successful‑engagement pricing model to align vendor incentives with actual outcomes.
*   **Go Threshold:** Only sign the full 12‑month agreement if readmissions drop by **more than 12%** AND the calculated cost avoidance exceeds **1.5x the total program cost** based on your newly measured average readmission expense.

**Note:** You must calculate your current average cost per readmission before the pilot ends to accurately assess the financial data. Ensure the pilot agreement explicitly grants you ownership of all patient usage data for independent analysis.
