Proceed with a limited pilot launch in Q3 2025, but **do not** commit to the full 18,000-household target or bank on the $9.72M revenue projection until the pilot validates unit economics. The current plan ignores $6 per order in variable costs (packaging + shipping) and potential spoilage, which could erase the 12% net margin from your dry food line if applied broadly without adjustment.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**
1.  **Margin Collapse:** The unmodeled $6/order cost plus 5% spoilage risk turns the subscription into a loss leader, subsidizing every box sold and dragging down the profitable dry food business.
2.  **Churn Cascade:** If actual churn hits the researched upper bound (20%) rather than the hoped-for lower bound, the cost to acquire replacements exceeds the lifetime value of the subscriber, making growth mathematically impossible.
3.  **Inventory Spoilage:** Holding wet ingredients for a "stable base" that churns faster than expected leads to increased waste, compounding the margin erosion exactly when cash flow is needed most.

**Tripwire Metric**
If **actual churn exceeds 15% in the first quarter** post-launch, immediately **pause new subscriber acquisition** and pivot to a pre-order model to eliminate inventory spoilage risk while re-pricing the subscription to cover the verified $6 variable cost.
