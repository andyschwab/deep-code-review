**Recommendation: Proceed with a conditional pilot ("Go-If"), not a full rollout.**

Approve the vendor contract only if tied to a six-month pilot covering 20% of new originations, with a clause allowing immediate termination if regulatory guidance on alternative data remains unresolved by Q2 2025. Do not commit to the full $150,000 annual spend or enterprise-wide deployment until the pilot validates both the projected savings and the regulatory stance.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**

1.  **Regulatory Rejection:** Regulators classify mobile-money logs as intrusive alternative data, forcing a sudden halt to model usage. This renders the $150,000 spend sunk and creates compliance remediation costs, while leaving the portfolio exposed to the original 5% default rate.
2.  **Data Quality Mismatch:** The assumption that vendor data quality matches current credit bureau feeds proves false. If mobile-money data is noisier or less predictive than expected, the model may fail to reduce defaults to 3%, potentially increasing false rejections of good borrowers and shrinking loan volume without reducing losses.
3.  **Arithmetic Overestimation:** The projected $800,000 savings assumes a linear reduction in defaults (70 loans saved). If the model primarily filters out low-risk applicants who would have repaid anyway, or if the remaining high-risk pool is smaller than anticipated, the actual reduction in bad debt may fall significantly short of the 2% target.

**Abort/Pivot Trigger:**

Terminate the pilot and revert to the current scoring method if, after six months, the **actualized annualized savings in bad-debt provisions fall below $400,000** (50% of the projected benefit) OR if a formal regulatory warning is issued regarding the specific alternative data sources used.
