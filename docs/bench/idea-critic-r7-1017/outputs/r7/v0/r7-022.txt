**Recommendation: Modify**

Proceed with a three-month pilot on Silk Loom, but do not commit to the full $1,200 monthly shipping estimate or the 5% conversion assumption in your final revenue projection. Your current math overstates net profit because it ignores the margin erosion from the 12% commission plus fees on low-value items, and it unfairly disadvantages GlobalMarket by ignoring its higher average order value. Before presenting to Fatima, adjust the forecast to show a "conservative" scenario using a 2% conversion rate and a "global-adjusted" scenario that credits GlobalMarket for higher basket sizes. This protects the cooperative's credibility if the traffic surge does not materialize immediately.

**Three Most Likely Failure Points:**
1.  **Margin Collapse:** The combined 12% commission and $0.25 fee on a $30 item consumes 14.8% of revenue; if your current net margin is below 20%, the additional volume may generate cash flow but negligible profit after covering the new $1,200 operational cost.
2.  **Conversion Overestimation:** Boutique shoppers differ from marketplace browsers; if the actual conversion rate lands at 2% (industry standard for cold traffic) instead of your assumed 5%, you will only gain 60 sales, generating $1,800 in new revenue which barely covers the new monthly fees and shipping costs.
3.  **Channel Cannibalization:** The "new" visitors may simply be existing customers shifting their purchases from the fee-free boutique to the fee-heavy platform, resulting in higher costs for the same total unit volume.

**Kill Criteria (Stop/Change Triggers):**
Terminate or renegotiate the pilot if any of the following occur after 90 days:
*   **Conversion Rate:** Sustained conversion remains below 2.5% despite optimized listings.
*   **Net Contribution:** The channel fails to generate at least $1,500 in *net* profit (revenue minus COGS, commissions, fees, and allocated shipping) per month.
*   **Cannibalization:** More than 30% of Silk Loom sales are traced to existing customer emails without a corresponding increase in total cooperative unit volume.
