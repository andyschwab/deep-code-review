**Recommendation: NO-GO in current form; proceed only as a "Go-If" pilot.**

Do not approve the $800,000 build yet. The current financial model is materially flawed because it omits $170,000 in known potential costs (privacy compliance and marketing) and underestimates logistics expenses by comparing them to an irrelevant European benchmark. Approving this budget now guarantees a shortfall before the first item is sold. Instead, restructure the proposal as a **Go-If**: release only $150,000 for a 90-day Minimum Viable Product (MVP) targeting 5,000 items. Full funding is contingent on validating the 20% participation rate and confirming the regulatory fee structure within that pilot window.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**

1.  **Unit Economics Collapse:** The model assumes a 12% gross margin on a $12 price point ($1.44/item). However, shifting from a 5% to an 8% logistics fee immediately erodes 3% of revenue. When combined with the unpriced $50k–$170k overhead, the cost per item likely exceeds the margin, turning every resale into a net loss rather than a profit center.
2.  **Participation Rate Delusion:** The plan relies on 20% of Delhi customers listing garments. Without the newly identified $120,000 marketing push, this target is almost certainly unachievable. If actual participation lands at a conservative 2–5% (industry standard for organic adoption), the platform will launch with insufficient inventory to attract buyers, creating a "ghost town" effect that kills network effects immediately.
3.  **Regulatory Stoppage:** Building the full nine-month solution before resolving the Delhi data-privacy fee creates existential risk. If the regulator imposes the $50,000 fee (or stricter data residency rules) after development begins, the architecture may require expensive rework, or the business case may become negative, rendering the sunk build cost a total loss.

*   **Volume Trigger:** If first-month active listings fall below **800 items** (representing a <6% conversion of the initial inventory target adjusted for pilot scale), terminate the initiative.
