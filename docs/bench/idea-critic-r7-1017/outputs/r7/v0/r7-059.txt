**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" Conditional Approval**

Approve the EcoPulse pilot only if the vendor signs an addendum capping annual maintenance costs at $15 per sensor ($2,250/year total) and guarantees a minimum 10% reduction in collection trips within the first six months of operation. Without these written constraints, the unknown operational expenses and unverified integration risks outweigh the projected savings.

**Potential Failure Modes**

*   **Financial:** The undisclosed maintenance fees could exceed $20/sensor annually, eroding over 60% of the projected $55,000 yearly savings and extending the payback period beyond the three-year contract term. Additionally, if the SAP integration requires custom middleware not covered in the $45,000 quote, initial costs could spike by an additional $10,000–$20,000.
*   **Technical:** The assumption of "seamless" SAP integration is the single largest technical risk. If the sensor data format is incompatible with the current routing module, the system may revert to manual entry, nullifying the efficiency gains and increasing labor costs.
*   **Operational:** The projected 15% trip reduction relies on consistent fill-rate variability. If the district's waste generation is already highly optimized or static, sensors will provide data without enabling route consolidation, resulting in near-zero actual savings despite the hardware investment.

**Kill Criteria (Stop/Change Conditions)**

Terminate the contract or withhold final payment if, after the first two quarters of full operation (by Q2 2026):
1.  Actual fuel and labor savings are less than **$4,125 per quarter** (equivalent to a 10% annualized run-rate of the $55k target).
2.  The total cost of ownership (hardware + installation + first year maintenance) exceeds **$47,250**.
3.  Manual intervention is required for route planning in more than **5% of collection cycles** due to data integration failures.

**Note:** The current $45,000 quote likely excludes gateway hardware or cellular data plans; verify this immediately before signing.
**Note:** Ensure the "three-year service agreement" explicitly includes firmware updates to prevent obsolescence.
