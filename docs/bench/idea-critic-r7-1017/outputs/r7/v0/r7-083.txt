**Recommendation: No-Go.** Do not proceed with the current plan. The proposal relies on unverified assumptions regarding hardware penetration and conversion rates that inflate projected revenue by an order of magnitude, creating a high risk of capital loss without a viable path to profitability.

**Critical Flaws & Failure Modes:**
1.  **Flawed Revenue Math:** The $2.5M projection assumes 100% of attracted users (200,000) convert to paying customers at $12 each. In reality, even a strong 5% conversion rate yields only $120,000 in revenue against a $150,000 build cost, excluding marketing and operations.
2.  **Hardware Penetration Delusion:** Assuming 30% of your demographic owns VR headsets is unsupported; global VR penetration is significantly lower. This artificially inflates the addressable market.
3.  **Technology Adoption Risk:** Users may reject a niche artisan experience due to friction (headset requirement, motion sickness) compared to standard 2D video demos.
4.  **Cost Overruns:** Nine months for a persistent multi-user VR marketplace with live streaming is highly optimistic; scope creep could double the budget before launch.
5.  **Revenue Shortfall:** Without historical conversion data, the "unknown" variable is the primary driver of failure. If conversion is <1%, revenue covers less than 10% of development costs.

**Hidden Costs:**
*   High-bandwidth streaming infrastructure for 12 live feeds.
*   Ongoing content coordination with 12 master weavers (scheduling, tech support).
*   App store fees and VR-specific marketing channels.

**Kill Criteria (Pre-agreed Triggers):**
*   **Financial:** If month-six spend exceeds 60% of the budget ($90,000) without a functional, tested prototype supporting at least one live demo.
*   **Adoption:** If a pre-launch landing page waitlist yields fewer than 5,000 sign-ups (validating interest) within 60 days of announcement.
*   **Technical:** If average session duration in alpha testing is under 3 minutes (indicating usability/friction issues).

**Note:** The revenue model must be rebuilt using conservative conversion benchmarks (1-3%) before re-evaluation. A 2D interactive pilot should be tested first to validate demand at a fraction of the cost.
