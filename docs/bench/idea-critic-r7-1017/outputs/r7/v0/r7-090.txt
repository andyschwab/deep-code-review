**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **Go-If** condition: secure a written contract with the waste-to-energy plant guaranteeing zero fees and defined intake capacity before releasing any funds. Without this, the $150,000 annual saving is speculative, and the project risks becoming a cost center if disposal fees apply.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Revenue Reversal:** The verbal agreement with the waste plant collapses, imposing tipping fees that erase the projected $150k savings and turn spent grain into a liability.
2.  **Throughput Shortfall:** Unplanned maintenance exceeds the vendor's vague "low single-digit" estimate, preventing the line from reaching the critical 12,000 bottles/day throughput needed to justify the $2.5M capex.
3.  **Benchmark Gap:** The line achieves only 70–75% waste reduction (missing the 85% target), failing to meet the sustainability marketing claims used to justify the premium investment against the competitor's 90% standard.

**Halt/Redesign Triggers:**
*   **Waste Reduction < 75%** sustained over the first 6 months of operation.
*   **Unplanned Downtime > 5 days per quarter** in Year 1.
*   **Any positive cost per ton** charged for spent grain removal.
