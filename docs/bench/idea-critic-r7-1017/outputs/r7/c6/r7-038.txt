Do not commit to the €45k + €660k/year (€2.5k × 22 × 12) full rollout yet. Your extrapolation contains a critical arithmetic slip: an 8% revenue lift on five properties during a specific 12% weekend surcharge does not mathematically guarantee a 15% annual increase across 22 diverse properties. The pilot data is too narrow (weekends only, low season) to support an annualized forecast that ignores weekday dynamics, seasonal elasticity, and the massive fixed cost of the license.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Margin Erasure:** The €55,000 monthly licensing fee (€2.5k × 22) requires a consistent revenue lift of roughly €60k+ monthly just to break even. If the AI fails to optimize weekdays or off-peak seasons as well as it did weekends, you will pay more in fees than you gain in revenue.
2.  **Channel Conflict:** Integrating dynamic pricing with your existing channel manager during a 20% demand surge (September) risks rate parity violations or double-bookings if the API latency spikes, leading to immediate guest compensation costs and brand damage.
3.  **Guest Backlash:** Assuming constant 68% occupancy ignores price sensitivity. Aggressive weekend surcharging may depress repeat booking rates, lowering lifetime value even if short-term revenue ticks up.

**Kill Criteria:**
Terminate the project immediately if, after a 90-day full-portfolio trial:
*   The **net** revenue lift (gross lift minus licensing fees) is less than **5%** for two consecutive months.
*   Direct guest complaints regarding pricing fairness exceed **0.5%** of total bookings.
*   The system causes any rate-parity violation with major OTAs during the September surge.

**Hidden Risk:**
Your "unknown" regarding the September surge is the highest risk vector. Deploying an unproven algorithmic layer exactly when demand peaks creates a "black box" scenario where you cannot distinguish between natural demand revenue and AI efficacy. If the AI overprices during the surge, you lose volume; if it underprices, you leave money on the table. You must validate the system in a lower-stakes period first.
