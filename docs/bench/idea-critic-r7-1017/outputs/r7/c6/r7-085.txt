**Recommendation: Proceed with a conditional pilot (Go-If), not a full rollout.**

Approve the R45,000 integration only if tied to a 90-day capped pilot with pre-committed kill criteria. Do not commit to the full 1.2% ongoing fee or permanent adoption until the pilot validates local artist retention and net revenue after fees. The Durban data is promising but insufficient to assume Cape Town artists will tolerate price volatility without attrition, and the current math ignores the fee's impact on net margins.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Artist Attrition:** Local artists reject the 2% commission hike plus price volatility, causing roster participation to drop below 85%, reducing inventory and deterring visitors.
2.  **Net Revenue Contraction:** The 1.2% transaction fee and higher commissions erode the projected 10% price lift, resulting in flat or negative net revenue despite higher gross sales.
3.  **Traffic Elasticity Error:** The assumed 8% foot traffic growth fails to materialize because Cape Town visitors are more price-sensitive than Durban's, leading to fewer total sales at the higher price point.

**Kill Criteria:**
Terminate the project immediately if, after 90 days: (1) Active artist participation falls below 85% of the pre-pilot roster; (2) Net revenue (after all fees and commissions) does not exceed the baseline by at least 4%; or (3) Customer complaint volume regarding pricing exceeds 5% of total transactions.
