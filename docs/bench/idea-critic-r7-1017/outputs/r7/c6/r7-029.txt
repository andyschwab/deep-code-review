**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **conditional pilot (Go-If)**: Launch only 4 satellites initially to validate the 18% poaching reduction claim and the 24-hour latency impact before committing to the full $58 million constellation.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Latency Mismatch:** The assumed 24-hour data delay renders real-time anti-poaching interventions impossible, reducing the system to a forensic tool rather than a preventative one.
2.  **Flawed Benchmarking:** Basing coverage expectations on the "SkyGuard" system (different orbit, 30% more satellites) leads to significant blind spots in the 2.3 million km² target area.
3.  **Budget Creep:** The unpriced $200k annual maintenance for ground stations, combined with the initial arithmetic error, signals a risk of further hidden operational costs undermining the $1.2 million savings projection.

**Kill/Pivot Criteria:**
Terminate or pivot the project if, after 12 months of the 4-satellite pilot, illegal hunting incidents do not decrease by at least **5%** or if ranger response times to alerts worsen due to data latency.

*Note: The initial budget calculation contained a $400k arithmetic error ($57.6M vs $58M) that must be corrected in the final brief. The comparison to SkyGuard requires a revised coverage model specific to 550km LEO before full approval.*
