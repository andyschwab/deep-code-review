2.  **Integration Cost Explosion:** Undiscovered incompatibilities with the existing ERP cause integration costs to exceed the 7% contingency, eroding the ROI before the pilot concludes.
3.  **Adoption Collapse:** Farmers reject the hardware or workflow changes, resulting in less than 50% participation, which breaks the network effect required for traceability and invalidates the revenue model.

*   Regulatory approval is not granted or is deemed unfeasible by **30 September 2025**.
