**Recommendation: No-Go on the current plan; proceed only as a capped, six-week discovery pilot with a $25,000 budget.**

The proposal's financial model contains a critical flaw: it conflates "visitors" with "payers." You estimate 200,000 users and apply a $12 spend to *all* of them, generating $2.4M. In e-commerce and VR, conversion rates are rarely 100%; even a generous 5% conversion rate drops projected revenue to $120,000, which fails to cover the $150,000 development cost, let alone generate profit. Furthermore, the assumption of 30% VR hardware penetration among your current web demographic is likely inflated by an order of magnitude without specific market data, creating a massive distribution bottleneck.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
1.  **Revenue Collapse:** The model assumes 100% monetization. If fewer than 12,500 users (6.25% of 200k) buy at $12, the project operates at a loss.
2.  **Distribution Friction:** Requiring users to own VR headsets limits the addressable market significantly below the 45,000 monthly web visitors, making the 200,000 user target unrealistic without massive new user acquisition spend not included in the budget.
3.  **Technical Debt:** Building a persistent, multi-user VR bazaar with live streaming for 12 weavers in nine months for $150k is highly optimistic; latency and synchronization issues could degrade the "live demo" core value proposition.

**Kill Criteria & Triggers:**
Before committing to the full build, initiate the **Discovery Pilot**.
*   **Trigger 1 (Cost):** If the pilot spend exceeds $25,000 without a functional prototype demonstrating stable live-stream integration for at least one weaver, terminate immediately.
*   **Trigger 2 (Demand):** If a landing page test fails to secure 500 committed pre-orders or waitlist sign-ups from existing users expressing intent to use VR, terminate.
*   **Trigger 3 (Hardware Reality):** If internal surveying reveals less than 5% of the current 45,000 monthly visitors own a VR headset, terminate the VR-specific approach and pivot to a 360-degree web experience.

**Hidden Costs & Flawed Assumptions:**
*   **Content Production:** The budget ignores the cost of equipping 12 weavers with high-fidelity 360-capture gear and the ongoing bandwidth costs for live streaming.
*   **Platform Fees:** VR app stores typically charge 30% revenue shares, which would reduce the $12 average spend to $8.40 net, worsening the unit economics.
*   **Support Overhead:** VR applications require specialized customer support for hardware troubleshooting, a cost center not present in the current web model.

*Note: The revenue calculation error ($2.5M vs realistic <$150k) is the primary blocker. The pilot must validate hardware ownership rates before any code is written.*
