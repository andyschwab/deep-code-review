Assuming it is a one-time cost without written confirmation is a dangerous assumption.

3.  **Conversion Rate Sensitivity:** The plan relies entirely on a 30% conversion from trial to active mentee. In cross-border ed-tech, 30% is an exceptionally aggressive assumption (industry benchmarks often hover between 5–15%). If conversion lands at a more conservative 15%, you will have only 6,000 active mentees by Q4 2025, likely failing to generate the revenue required to sustain the $70k/month operational burn.
