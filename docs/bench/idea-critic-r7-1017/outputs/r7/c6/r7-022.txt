**Recommendation: Modify (Pilot First)**
Do not commit to the full integration yet. Launch a 90-day capped pilot with a hard spend limit of $1,500 for setup and initial marketing, targeting only 500 new visitors rather than the full 3,000. This reduces upfront risk while validating the actual conversion rate and hidden logistics costs before scaling.

**Three Most Likely Failure Points**
1.  **Conversion Overestimation:** Marketplace traffic is often less qualified than boutique visitors; if conversion falls below 2% (60 sales) instead of the projected 5% (150 sales), the net revenue gain vanishes after fees and shipping.
2.  **Margin Erosion:** The $1,200/month shipping/customs estimate may be too low for international individual orders compared to bulk boutique shipping; if per-unit logistics exceed $8, the 12% commission plus fees will render each scarf unprofitable.
3.  **Channel Cannibalization:** The "new" sales may simply shift existing boutique customers to the online platform without generating net new volume, meaning you pay the 12% commission on revenue you already captured at 0% marginal cost.

**Kill Criteria**
Stop or pivot the initiative immediately if, after three months:
*   The verified conversion rate is **under 2.5%** (fewer than 75 sales from 3,000 visitors).
*   The fully loaded cost per unit (commission + fees + shipping) exceeds **$12**, reducing net margin below 15%.
*   Total net profit from the channel (revenue minus all variable costs and amortized setup) is **negative for two consecutive months**.

*Note: Your comparison with GlobalMarket is currently flawed because it ignores Average Order Value (AOV); a lower traffic source with higher AOV might yield better absolute profit. Recalculate the projection using net profit per visitor, not just traffic volume.*
