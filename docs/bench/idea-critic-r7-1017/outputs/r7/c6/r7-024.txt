**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" Pilot, Not Full Launch**
Do not commit to the full October 1 launch or the €210,000 total spend yet. Instead, authorize only a **4-week technical pilot** capped at **€35,000** (subset of build budget) to validate the POS integration and conversion rate before releasing marketing funds.

**Primary Failure Modes**
*   **Technical (Critical):** Your assumption of "no extra licensing fees" for POS integration is unverified. Most legacy POS systems require paid middleware or specific API tiers. If this costs >€5k/month, the unit economics collapse.
*   **Financial:** Your math contains a hidden slip. You project 1,500 extra orders at a 10% lift (€2.50 extra per order), generating only **€3,750/month** in incremental revenue. At this rate, it would take **46 months** just to recover the €120k AI build cost, ignoring the €80k marketing. The project bleeds cash immediately unless the "10% lift" applies to *all* transactions, not just the 1,500 new ones.
*   **Market:** You have zero data on the conversion rate of recommendations. If the AI suggests beers users dislike, it may decrease basket size rather than increase it, actively damaging the customer experience.

**Pre-Agreed Kill Criteria**
Terminate the project immediately if, by the end of the 4-week pilot:
1.  **Integration Cost:** Verified POS middleware fees exceed **€2,000/month**.
2.  **Conversion Rate:** Fewer than **8%** of users who see a recommendation actually purchase the suggested beer.
3.  **Revenue Lift:** The net increase in average spend per customer is **<2%** (statistically significant below your 10% target).

**Note on Numbers:** Your current projection implies the AI only influences 1,500 total orders monthly; if you meant 1,500 *additional* orders on top of existing volume with a 10% lift across the board, please clarify, as the current math does not support the investment.
**Note on Timeline:** A six-week sprint to build, integrate, and test an AI model with three external brewery data sources is highly optimistic; buffer the launch date by at least 3 weeks to avoid a broken demo for the CEO.
