**Critical Failure Points**

2.  **Churn Miscalculation:** You rounded 15% of 12,000 to 1,800 lost users, but churn applies to the *active* base over time, not just a one-time subtraction. If churn is monthly, your Q4 active user count could be under 7,000, not 10,200.
3.  **Single Point of Failure:** Relying on one competitor (OceanPath) for benchmarking is dangerous. Their $1.8M revenue might include non-booking income (ads, data sales) your app lacks, making the "33% ahead" claim misleading.
4.  **Partnership Risk:** The Thai Tourism Authority partnership is verbal or informal. If they delay map data delivery by even two weeks post-launch, user retention will plummet due to broken offline navigation.
