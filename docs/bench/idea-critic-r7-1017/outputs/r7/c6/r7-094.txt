**Recommendation: Proceed with a capped pilot (Go-If), not full deployment.**
Approve the purchase of **20 drones** (not 200) and the software platform to run a 90-day operational pilot in a single permissive zone. Do not commit the remaining capital or training budget until the pilot validates that regulatory approval is secured and unit economics hold at scale. This converts a $1.35M irreversible bet into a ~$300k learning investment.

**Three Main Failure Modes:**
1.  **Regulatory:** The aviation authority delays permitting beyond Q2 2025 or restricts flight paths such that the 10km radius covers less than 40% of your order density, rendering the network unusable for the majority of shipments.
2.  **Financial:** The "five days, eight hours" utilization assumption fails due to weather, battery degradation, or maintenance downtime, causing the cost per delivery to creep back toward $6 instead of stabilizing at $3.
3.  **Technical/Operational:** The routing software cannot handle real-world exceptions (failed drop-offs, return-to-base emergencies) at the projected volume of 45,000 orders/month, leading to service failures that damage brand trust more than standard shipping delays.

**Kill Criteria:**
Terminate the project immediately if, after 90 days of the capped pilot: (a) formal regulatory approval is not granted; or (b) the fully loaded cost per delivery exceeds **$5.50** (adjusted for a 15% buffer over the $3 target); or (c) successful delivery completion rates fall below **95%**.

*Note: Your current math assumes 100% fleet utilization; even a 20% downtime rate doubles your unit cost. Verify the "2-week pilot" data included maintenance downtime and bad weather before finalizing the board deck.*
