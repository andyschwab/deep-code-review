**Recommendation: Proceed with a conditional pilot (Go-If), not full deployment.**

Approve a 3-month pilot on 15 bins (10% of the fleet) starting June 2025, contingent on EcoPulse providing a fixed annual maintenance cost per sensor in writing before any hardware is ordered. Do not commit to the full $45,000 rollout or the three-year service agreement until the pilot validates the integration and savings assumptions.

**Primary Failure Modes:**
*   **Financial:** The undisclosed annual maintenance fee exceeds the operational savings, turning a projected $10,000 net gain into a net loss within the first year. The $45,000 quote may also exclude necessary gateway infrastructure, causing immediate budget overruns.
*   **Technical:** The sensor data fails to integrate seamlessly with the existing SAP routing system as assumed, requiring costly custom middleware or manual data entry that negates labor savings.
*   **Operational:** The promised 15% reduction in collection trips is not realized due to route rigidity or sensor false positives, leaving fuel and labor costs unchanged while adding device management overhead.

**Kill Criteria (Stop/Change Condition):**
Terminate the expansion immediately if, after the 3-month pilot, the actual reduction in collection trips is less than 10% (two-thirds of the projected benefit) or if the disclosed annual maintenance cost exceeds $15 per sensor/year. These thresholds ensure the project remains financially viable even if performance is sub-optimal.

**Note:** The current math relies entirely on an unverified maintenance cost; without this figure, the ROI is speculative. The pilot caps exposure while forcing the vendor to reveal hidden costs.
