**Recommendation: Proceed with a capped pilot, not a full launch.**
Do not commit to the July 1 full-scale target of 1,200 households. Instead, authorize a "Go-If" pilot limited to the first 300 subscribers for Q3 2025. This caps your exposure to the unverified per-order delivery cost and the ignored seasonal churn spikes while validating the 30% conversion assumption.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Logistics Margin Collapse:** Your model uses a flat $1.20/mile estimate, but farm-to-table routes are fragmented. If average miles per household exceed projections by just 15% due to low density in the "Cottage Echo" area, the variable cost will erase the margin on the $45 price point before you hit 500 subscribers.
2.  **Conversion Overestimation:** Assuming 30% of retail customers will switch is aggressive without a dedicated marketing budget. You noted an $8,000 gap to acquire the first 300 users; if organic conversion lands at 10% instead of 30%, you will miss the July revenue target by over $200,000, leaving fixed logistics costs under-utilized.
3.  **Seasonal Churn Shock:** Your 5% quarterly churn model ignores seasonality. If produce variety drops in winter or prices rise, churn could spike to 15% in Q4. A simple 12-month average masks this cliff, potentially causing a cash flow crisis exactly when you planned to scale.
