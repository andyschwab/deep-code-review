**Recommendation: Proceed with a conditional pilot (Go-If), not full deployment.**

Authorize the $350,000 integration spend only if a binding regulatory approval letter is secured by Q1 2025 and a binding purchase agreement for the first 5,000 kWh is signed. Do not commit to the full 12,000-member rollout or the recurring $45,000 quarterly fees until these two conditions are met. The current proposal relies on unverified regulatory timelines and an assumed participation rate that, if off by just 4%, eliminates the projected profit margin.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**

1.  **Regulatory & Timeline Mismatch:** The "Q3 2025" approval window is vague; if regulatory clearance slips to 2026, the $350,000 integration cost sits idle while the $45,000 quarterly fees potentially accrue for maintenance of inactive systems, burning $180,000 annually with zero revenue.
2.  **Participation Rate Sensitivity:** The financial model breaks if participation drops from 15% to 11%. At 11% (1,320 members), generated credits fall to ~132,000 kWh/month, reducing annual revenue to ~$221,000, which fails to cover the $180,000 in annual operational fees alone, leaving no return on the capital integration cost.
3.  **Market Rate Volatility:** The $0.14/kWh rate is a snapshot; if the collective's market rate drops to $0.09/kWh due to oversupply from other cooperatives joining, annual revenue plummets to ~$1.6M, extending the payback period on the $350,000 investment from 2.8 years to over 5 years, missing the 12% ROI target.

**Abort Trigger (Kill Criterion):**

Abort the project immediately if, within 6 months of signing the integration contract, the net cash flow (Credit Revenue minus Operational Fees) remains negative for two consecutive quarters, or if final regulatory approval is not granted by December 31, 2025.

*Note: The current math assumes 180,000 kWh/month yields $2.5M/year; however, 180,000 kWh * $0.14 * 12 months equals ~$302,400, not $2.5M. There is a magnitude error in the revenue projection that must be corrected before CEO review.*
