**Decision needed: approve the vendor's $18k re-run by Friday to fix 38% metadata errors, or assign three cataloguers to an internal cleanup until end of Q3.**

**Key figure: 38% of 412k records have mismatched metadata.**

**The Problem**
Post-migration QA identified three critical data integrity issues:
*   **Metadata Mismatch:** 156,560 records (38%) contain incorrect metadata, concentrated in the children's and local history sections.
*   **Duplicate Call Numbers:** 14% of the collection has conflicting call numbers.
*   **Missing Records:** The legacy export omitted 6,000 bibliographic records, discovered only during QA.

The vendor attributes the metadata mismatch to a mapping error and offers to re-run the process.

**The Options**
1.  **Vendor Re-run**
    *   **Cost:** Additional $18,000.
    *   **Timeline:** Delays launch by two weeks.
    *   **Outcome:** Vendor resolves the mapping issue; internal team remains available for other tasks.
2.  **Internal Cleanup**
    *   **Cost:** No additional vendor spend; utilizes existing salary budget.
    *   **Timeline:** Extends work until the end of Q3.
    *   **Resource Impact:** Ties up three cataloguers exclusively for this duration.
    *   **Outcome:** Full control over the fix, but significantly longer resolution time.

**Recommendation**
The project team leans toward **Internal Cleanup** to avoid the unplanned $18k expense, despite the timeline extension to Q3. However, this requires pulling three cataloguers from other duties for several months.

**Required Action**
Marcus must select one option by **Friday** to present at the steering committee.
*   If choosing the vendor re-run, approval for the $18k change order is required immediately.
*   If choosing internal cleanup, authorization to reallocate three cataloguers through Q3 is required.

**Additional Context**
*   **Search Performance:** Branch managers have reported slow search speeds in the new system; this is under investigation but separate from the data migration errors.
*   **Training:** Attendance reached only 50% of the target; a make-up session plan is being drafted.
