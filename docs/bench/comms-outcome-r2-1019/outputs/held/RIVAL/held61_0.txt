**Executive Summary: Migration Data Quality and Path Forward**

Post-migration QA identified significant data integrity issues affecting 38% of the 412,000 uploaded records. The mismatches are concentrated in the children's and local history sections. Additional findings include 14% duplicate call numbers and 6,000 bibliographic records missing from the vendor's initial export.

The vendor attributes these errors to a mapping failure and has proposed a re-run of the upload. This option requires an additional $18,000 and will delay the project timeline by two weeks. Alternatively, the internal team can resolve the discrepancies without external cost. However, this approach requires three cataloguers to dedicate their time until the end of Q3.

A decision is required by Friday's steering committee to either authorize the vendor re-run or initiate the internal cleanup process.

**Operational Notes**
Branch managers have reported performance latency in the new search interface. Additionally, training attendance reached only 50% of the projected target, which may impact user adoption rates.
