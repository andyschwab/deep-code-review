**PR: Water Meter Replacement – Select Implementation Approach**

**Goal:** Secure approval for one implementation approach by Friday, June 14, to finalize capex figures for the June 18 board pack.

**Context:** We are replacing 12,400 meters district-wide. Current old-meter leak rate is 14.2% per year.

**Options:**
*   **Approach A:** Replace all 12,400 meters by end of Q3.
    *   Capex: $1.85M
    *   Opex saving: $0.42/unit/month
    *   Non-revenue water reduction: 8.3%
*   **Approach B:** Phase replacement over 18 months (retains old radio readers for one year).
    *   Capex: $2.1M
    *   Non-revenue water reduction: 5.1%
    *   Benefit: Less disruption to the high-rise block on Elm St.

**Recommendation:** Approach A delivers higher non-revenue water reduction (8.3% vs 5.1%) and lower capex ($1.85M vs $2.1M). Approach B offers reduced disruption for Elm St. but costs more and saves less water.

**Action Required:** Please approve either Approach A or Approach B with a short comment confirming the selected capex figure.

**Note:** Vendor demo is scheduled for June 12; findings may inform final execution details but should not delay this approval.
