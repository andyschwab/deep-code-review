*   **Projected Savings:** Switching suppliers will save approximately $31,500 annually on spoilage costs alone, excluding potential pricing improvements.

**Alternative Considered**
Renegotiating tighter delivery windows with FreshField was considered. However, given the significant gap in spoilage rates (23% vs. 9%) and the proven cold-chain compliance issues, a supplier change presents a more definitive solution to the revenue leak.

2.  Issue instruction to Tom to coordinate the first GreenRoots delivery with the new rack installation.
