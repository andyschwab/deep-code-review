**PR: M12 Curb Sensor – Vision-Based Ranging Algorithm**

**Summary**
This PR replaces the ultrasonic ranging algorithm with a vision-based implementation on M12 sensor nodes. The update addresses the 27% underreporting of parking occupancy observed in last month's pilot data. The diff adds approximately 9,000 lines of code, updates the calibration table to 38 entries, and modifies the power profile.

**Options for Review**
We have two implementation paths, each with distinct trade-offs regarding power, latency, and accuracy:

*   **Option A: Edge Inference**
    *   **Architecture:** Inference runs directly on the node.
    *   **Impact:** Reduces central compute load but increases power consumption.
    *   **Battery Life:** Projected 11% faster drain (falls short of the 14-month target).
    *   **Accuracy:** 1.8% false-positive rate in integration tests.

*   **Option B: Gateway Offload (Recommended)**
    *   **Architecture:** Inference is offloaded to the gateway.
    *   **Impact:** Introduces 0.2s latency but maintains current power efficiency.
    *   **Battery Life:** Meets the 14-month target.
    *   **Accuracy:** 1.2% false-positive rate in integration tests.

**Recommendation**
I recommend **Option B**. It preserves the required battery life while delivering the lower false-positive rate (1.2% vs 1.8%). The added 0.2s latency is acceptable given the gains in accuracy and power sustainability.
