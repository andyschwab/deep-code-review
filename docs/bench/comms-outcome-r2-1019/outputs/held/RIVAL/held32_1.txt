**PR: Select Picking-Zone Layout for New Mezzanine**

**Owner:** Dana (Tech Lead)
**Deadline for Approval:** Thursday, 2025-03-20
**Impact:** Missing this date delays the vendor lock-in scheduled for 2025-03-25.

**Context**
We have completed 62 hours of simulation comparing two layouts for the new mezzanine picking zone. The operations team is currently split on the direction. We need your decision to finalize the design and begin the permit application.

**Options**
The diff summary details the technical implementation for both approaches:

*   **Approach A: High-Density Carousel**
    *   **Throughput:** 4,800 picks/hour.
    *   **Infrastructure:** Requires an additional conveyor junction.
    *   **Cost Impact:** +$150k for the junction.
    *   **Trade-off:** Higher upfront cost and fixed infrastructure for maximum speed.

*   **Approach B: Pod-Based Mobile Robots**
    *   **Throughput:** 3,200 picks/hour.
    *   **Infrastructure:** No additional conveyor junctions required.
    *   **Cost Impact:** Lower initial infrastructure cost.
    *   **Trade-off:** Lower peak throughput, but easier to scale incrementally.

**Constraints**
Structural analysis confirms the mezzanine floor load can support either layout without reinforcement.

**Recommendation**
I recommend **Approach A**. Despite the $150k premium for the conveyor junction, the 50% increase in throughput (4,800 vs. 3,200 picks/hour) better aligns with our peak volume targets. Approach B's scalability is valuable, but the immediate capacity gap is a larger risk than the fixed cost of Approach A.

**Action Required**
Please review the attached diff summary and approve either Approach A or Approach B by **Thursday, 2025-03-20**. Once approved, we will confirm the decision with stakeholders and immediately start the permit application to meet the vendor lock-in deadline.
