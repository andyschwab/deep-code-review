**Context**
This PR swaps the ultrasonic ranging algorithm for a vision-based one to address the 27% underreporting of parking occupancy seen in last month's pilot. The diff adds ~9k lines, updates the calibration table to 38 entries, and modifies the power profile.

**Options**

*   **Option A: Edge Inference (On-node)**
    *   **Pros:** Reduces central compute load.
    *   **Cons:** Increases battery drain by a projected 11%, missing our 14-month target.
    *   **Performance:** 1.8% false-positive rate in integration tests.

*   **Option B: Gateway Offload (Recommended)**
    *   **Pros:** Maintains the current 14-month battery life target.
    *   **Cons:** Adds 0.2s latency; increases reliance on gateway availability.
    *   **Performance:** 1.2% false-positive rate in integration tests.

**Recommendation**
I recommend **Option B**. While it introduces minor latency, it preserves battery life and delivers better accuracy (1.2% vs 1.8% false positives).
