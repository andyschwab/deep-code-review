### Appointment Reminder Pilot: Approach Selection (A vs. B)

This PR implements the logic for the upcoming appointment-reminder pilot, including a minor front-end tweak to the patient portal across four files. The core change introduces a configurable strategy for handling no-shows, requiring a final decision between two approaches before the pilot launches on the 20th.

**Proposed Approaches**
We compared two distinct workflows:
*   **Option A:** Send an SMS 24 hours before the appointment.
*   **Option B:** Send an email 48 hours prior, followed by a phone call on the day of the appointment.

**Data & Trade-offs**
Testing indicates that **Option A** reduces the no-show rate to **11%**, a significant improvement over the current baseline of **18%**. However, **Option B** is more cost-effective per interaction. The estimated cost is **$0.80** per reminder for Option A, compared to **$1.20** for Option B.

While Option A carries a higher per-unit cost, the reduction in no-shows suggests a better overall outcome for clinic capacity. Option B saves money on the reminder itself but relies on a more labor-intensive process (phone calls) without matching the attendance improvement of the SMS approach.
