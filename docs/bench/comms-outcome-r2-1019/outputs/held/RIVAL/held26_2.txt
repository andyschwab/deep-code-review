**PR: Appointment Reminder Strategy and Portal Tweak**

This update compares two approaches for reducing patient no-shows ahead of the pilot starting on the 20th. The diff includes the logic for both options plus a minor front-end tweak to the patient portal across four files.

**The Options**
*   **Option A:** Send an SMS 24 hours before the appointment.
    *   **Cost:** $0.80 per reminder.
    *   **Result:** Reduces no-shows from the current 18% to **11%**.
*   **Option B:** Send an email 48 hours before plus a phone call on the day.
    *   **Cost:** $1.20 per reminder (30% higher than Option A).
    *   **Result:** Performance data pending; cost efficiency is the primary advantage.

**Recommendation**
Option A is the preferred approach. It delivers the lowest no-show rate (11%) while costing less per reminder than Option B.
