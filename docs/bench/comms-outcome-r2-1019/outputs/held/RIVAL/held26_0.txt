This update implements the logic for the upcoming appointment-reminder pilot, including a minor front-end tweak to the patient portal. The changes span four files and focus on selecting the optimal strategy to reduce no-shows before the pilot launches on the 20th.

**The Decision**
We compared two approaches for handling no-shows:
*   **Option A:** Send an SMS 24 hours before the appointment.
*   **Option B:** Send an email 48 hours before, plus a phone call on the day.

Current data indicates our no-show rate sits at **18%**. Testing shows Option A reduces this to **11%**. While Option B is cheaper per reminder ($0.80 for A vs. $1.20 for B), Option A delivers the superior reduction in missed appointments.
