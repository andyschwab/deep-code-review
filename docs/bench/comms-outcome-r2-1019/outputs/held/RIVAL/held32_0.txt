**PR: Select Picking-Zone Layout for New Mezzanine**

**Owner:** Dana (Tech Lead)
**Deadline for Approval:** Thursday, 2025-03-20
**Impact:** Missing this date delays the vendor lock-in scheduled for 2025-03-25.

**Context**
The ops team has completed 62 hours of simulation comparing two layout approaches for the new mezzanine. The team is currently split on the direction. We need your decision to finalize the design and begin the permit application.

**Options**

*   **Approach A: High-Density Carousel**
    *   **Throughput:** 4,800 picks/hour.
    *   **Infrastructure:** Requires an additional conveyor junction ($150k cost).
    *   **Load:** Compatible with current mezzanine floor load specs.
    *   **Trade-off:** Higher upfront cost and complexity for maximum throughput.

*   **Approach B: Pod-Based Mobile Robots**
    *   **Throughput:** 3,200 picks/hour.
    *   **Infrastructure:** No extra conveyor junction required.
    *   **Load:** Compatible with current mezzanine floor load specs.
    *   **Trade-off:** Lower initial throughput, but easier to scale incrementally.

**Recommendation**
I recommend **Approach A**. Despite the $150k cost for the extra conveyor junction, the 50% increase in throughput (4,800 vs. 3,200 picks/hour) better aligns with our peak volume targets. The mezzanine floor load supports this configuration without reinforcement.

**Action Required**
Please review the diff summary attached to this PR and approve either Approach A or B by **Thursday, 2025-03-20**. Once approved, we will immediately start the permit application to meet the vendor lock-in deadline.

Let me know if you need further details on the simulation data before making your call.
