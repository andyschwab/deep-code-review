Subject: Urgent: Q3 Floor Price Decision for Monday Board Meeting

DeShawn,

The Northfield growers' board rejected the $3.20/cwt floor price last night by an 11-4 vote. They are demanding we present one of two new options to the full membership by Friday to meet the 72-hour notice requirement for Monday's board meeting.

The board's options are:
1. A $3.45 floor price with a 2% member levy.
2. A $3.60 floor price with no levy.

Jake, our CFO, has projected the financial impact of both scenarios. The $3.45 option results in a $180K deficit for the season. The $3.60 option increases that deficit to $340K. These projections do not yet account for the increased hauling costs we are currently absorbing while routing 40% of our milk to the Dry Creek facility due to the Larkspur cheese plant flood outage.

Given the tighter deficit and the current strain on logistics costs, I recommend we proceed with the **$3.45 floor price with the 2% member levy**. This option limits our seasonal deficit to $180K while still meeting the board's demand for a higher floor.

Please confirm by end of day if you approve this recommendation and the associated levy structure so I can draft the membership notice immediately.
