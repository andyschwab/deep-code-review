**Marcus must decide by Friday: approve the vendor's $18k re-run (2-week delay) or authorize internal cleanup (end of Q3, 3 cataloguers) to fix 38% metadata mismatches.**

**The Situation**
Post-migration QA on 412k records revealed three critical data integrity issues:
*   **Metadata Mismatch:** 38% of records (primarily children's and local history) have incorrect metadata following the vendor upload.
*   **Duplicate Call Numbers:** 14% of call numbers are duplicated.
*   **Missing Records:** The legacy export omitted 6,000 bib records, identified only during QA.

**The Decision**
The vendor attributes the errors to a mapping issue and offers a re-run with the following terms:
*   **Cost:** Additional $18k.
*   **Timeline:** 2-week delay.
*   **Deadline:** Approval required by this Friday.

**The Alternative**
If the vendor re-run is not approved, the internal team will execute the cleanup:
*   **Timeline:** Completion by end of Q3.
*   **Resource Impact:** Ties up three cataloguers exclusively until finished.
*   **Recommendation:** The project team leans toward internal cleanup to avoid extra costs, though this extends the timeline significantly.

**Additional Context (Not part of the immediate decision)**
*   **Search Performance:** Branch managers report the new search function is slow.
*   **Training:** Attendance reached only 50% of the planned target.
