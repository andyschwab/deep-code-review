**Recommendation: MODIFY (Pilot First, Scale Later)**

Do not authorize the full $1.2M rollout immediately. Instead, approve a **90-day pilot** opening only **two** branches and hiring **five** officers ($540k initial outlay). Tie the remaining three branches and seven hires strictly to achieving **$450k in net new loan volume** by end of Q3 2025. This approach validates the "instant-approve" adoption rate and underwriting capacity assumptions before committing 100% of the capital and increasing fixed overhead by 30%.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**

1.  **Revenue Math & Rate Shock:** The projected $2M gain assumes 15% growth on the *starting* $12M balance. In reality, revenue derives from the *average* daily balance throughout the year. If growth is linear, the average balance is closer to $12.9M, yielding only ~$1.35M in new revenue (at 15%), not $2M. Furthermore, if interest rates rise even 0.5%, margin compression could erase 20–30% of this projected profit, turning the expansion net-negative.
2.  **Regulatory & Capacity Bottleneck:** The plan assumes current underwriting can handle a 30% volume spike without new compliance staff. This is a critical single point of failure. A 30% increase in applications typically creates a disproportionate backlog in complex files, slowing time-to-decision. If the "instant-approve" competitor captures customers due to our slower manual processing, the new branches become costly showrooms with no inventory. Regulatory exams also scale non-linearly; a sudden footprint increase often triggers enhanced scrutiny that existing staff cannot manage alongside origination.
3.  **Fixed Cost Trap:** Hiring 12 officers and leasing five locations creates immediate, rigid fixed costs. If the fintech competitor's product causes market share erosion greater than 5% in the Metro area, the new branches will burn cash daily. Unlike variable marketing spend, you cannot pause lease payments or salaries when adoption lags, potentially threatening the liquidity of the existing $12M book if losses mount.

**Pre-Agreed Abort Trigger**

**Abort the remaining rollout and freeze hiring if Net New Loan Volume is less than $300,000 by September 30, 2025.** This threshold represents 50% of the required pace for the full annual target; missing it by Q3 indicates the adoption rate or capacity assumptions are fundamentally broken, making the full-year goal mathematically impossible without excessive risk.

*Note: The revenue projection must be recalculated using average balance forecasts, not year-end targets, to present an accurate ROI to the CLO.*
*Ensure the pilot includes a specific stress test of the underwriting team's maximum daily throughput before scaling.*
