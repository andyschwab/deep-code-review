Proceed only if you can secure binding Letters of Intent (LOIs) from 15 solo practitioners (10% of the total market, not 30% of a converted free tier) within 30 days, committing to the $15k price point upon feature completion. **Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Conversion Delusion:** The 30% conversion rate from free to $15k/year is historically unprecedented for solo practitioners without a proven enterprise track record; if actual conversion is 5% (7.5 lawyers), revenue drops to $112.5k, failing to cover the $195k in immediate costs.

3.  **Unit Economics Inversion:** If lawyers use the tool for 5+ hours instead of the estimated 3 hours due to complex case needs, API costs will scale linearly while revenue remains fixed, potentially turning every new customer into a net loss.

Terminate the project immediately if, by **30 November 2024**, the pilot fails to generate **$25,000 in actual collected revenue** (not LOIs or promises) from at least **2 distinct paying customers**. This threshold proves both willingness to pay and the ability to deliver without catastrophic support overhead.
