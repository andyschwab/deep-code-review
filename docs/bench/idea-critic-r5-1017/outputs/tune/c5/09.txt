**How This Plan Could Fail:**
1.  **Revenue Math Error:** Your projection of $2M assumes 8,000 *steady* subscribers. With a 5% monthly churn, you must acquire ~400 new subscribers *every month* just to maintain size, requiring far more than the one-time $500k marketing spend to sustain growth.
2.  **Margin Collapse:** The 20% gross margin is likely overstated. You included packaging costs but excluded the $0.08/mile fuel surcharge for refrigerated trucks. If average delivery is 20 miles, that adds $1.60/box, slashing margins by ~32%.
3.  **Conversion Unknown:** With zero data on how many of the 22,000 existing customers will upgrade, the entire subscriber forecast is a guess. If conversion is below 1%, you cannot reach 8,000 subscribers without acquiring entirely new customers at a much higher cost.
4.  **Timing Risk:** A March 1 start leaves only ~8 weeks before the board meeting. If the pilot fails or logistics stall, you will face the board with sunk costs and no performance data.

**Kill Criteria (Stop/Pivot Triggers):**
*   **Churn > 8% per month** during the pilot (indicates product-market fit failure for this price point).
*   **Net Gross Margin < 12%** after accounting for fuel surcharges and actual packaging waste (indicates unit economics cannot support the model).
*   **Upgrade Conversion < 0.5%** from the existing customer base (indicates the "elite" segment is too small or uninterested).
