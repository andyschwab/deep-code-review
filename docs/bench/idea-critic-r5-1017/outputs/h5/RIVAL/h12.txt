**Recommendation: Launch with a capped pilot (50 customers) for Year 1, not the full 150.**

Your math holds ($30M gross), but the risk profile is too asymmetric for a full launch without validating the "unknown unknowns." Specifically, you are underwriting a 20-year liability with only Q1 data and zero post-warranty attrition history. A pilot limits exposure to the aluminum volatility and labor inflation while generating the churn data needed to underwrite Years 2–20 confidently.

**Top Three Failure Modes:**

1.  **The "Cliff" Churn Risk:** You assume <5% churn, but have no data on customer behavior after the manufacturer's warranty expires (typically year 10–12). If systems begin failing en masse as they hit year 15, and customers cancel rather than pay for major repairs, you retain the labor cost burden without the revenue. This turns the "last mile" service into a loss-leading liability.
2.  **Labor Inflation vs. Fixed Revenue:** Your $1.8M crew budget is a static line item in a 20-year contract with fixed revenue. If service technician wages rise at 4–6% annually (historically higher than general inflation), your margin evaporates by Year 7. Unlike the aluminum cost (which is variable), labor is your fixed operational backbone; you cannot easily cut it without breaching service level agreements.
3.  **Regulatory/Grid Obsolescence:** Solar policy and net-metering rates change frequently. If utility companies drastically reduce buy-back rates or change interconnection rules in Year 5, the economic value of the solar system drops for the homeowner. They may abandon the system entirely, defaulting on the maintenance contract, leaving you with stranded assets and unrecoverable mobilization costs.

**Pre-Agreed Pull-the-Plug Trigger:**

**Trigger:** If **Year 1 actual service costs exceed $2.4M** (a 20% variance over the pro-rated $1.8M budget) **OR** if **annualized churn exceeds 7%** in the first 12 months.

**Action:** Immediate freeze on new contract signings and a mandatory strategic review to reprice Year 2 contracts or restructure the service scope before expanding beyond the pilot cohort.
