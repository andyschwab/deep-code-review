**Recommendation: Proceed with a controlled pilot, not a full rollout.**

Do not shift the entire business to fixed-price bundles immediately. Instead, launch the $1,200 SAT bundle as a limited pilot for the next quarter targeting only 500 students (approx. 25% of your base). Your current revenue math contains a critical error: selling 150 bundles at $1,200 generates $180,000, not $200,000. More importantly, 150 bundles represents only $720,000 in annualized revenue, which is a 68% drop from your current $2.3 million run rate. Unless the "150 bundles per quarter" implies a massive increase in total student volume rather than a conversion of existing clients, this model shrinks the company. Even if you convert 80% of clients (1,600 students), you must verify if they will buy *one* bundle or multiple to match current consumption. The $50,000 integration cost is a secondary concern; the primary risk is revenue collapse due to volume miscalculation.

**Three Biggest Ways This Plan Could Fail:**

1.  **Revenue Collapse via Volume Mismatch:** The proposal assumes 150 bundles/quarter sustains the business, but this equals less than $1M annually. If the plan relies on converting 1,600 existing clients, you need to sell ~400 bundles per quarter, not 150. If the market only supports 150 sales, you will lose over $1.5 million in annual revenue.
2.  **Margin Erosion from Scope Creep:** You estimated an $800 cost per bundle. If tutors spend just 10% more time than anticipated helping students pass, the cost rises to $880, slashing your $400 margin by 20%. Unlike hourly billing, you absorb all inefficiency. Without strict curriculum guardrails, tutor behavior will naturally expand to fill the time, destroying profitability.
3.  **Churn from Price Sensitivity:** If 20% of your price-sensitive clients leave for the competitor's $1,100 offering, you lose that revenue entirely. Since you are assuming 80% conversion, a 20% dropout rate means you miss your volume targets immediately, leaving you with high fixed costs (the new booking system) and lower top-line revenue.

**Kill Trigger:**

Terminate the bundle initiative immediately if **first-quarter net new revenue (bundled sales minus lost hourly clients) is less than $150,000** or if the **actual cost-per-bundle exceeds $850**.
