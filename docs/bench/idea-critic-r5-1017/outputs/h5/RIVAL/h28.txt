**Recommendation:** Proceed with a phased rollout to 10 high-traffic locations first, rather than all 42 stores immediately. The pilot's 18% margin is promising but relies on stable resale prices; a limited launch caps your exposure to inventory volatility while validating the $185,000 investment thesis.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Inventory Obsolescence (Financial):** Niche genres sit unsold for months, turning liquid cash into dead stock and eroding the 18% margin through storage and opportunity costs.
2.  **Operational Bottleneck (Operational):** Store staff spend excessive time grading and cleaning books, reducing time for selling new inventory and increasing labor costs beyond the pilot's $2,340 baseline.
3.  **Brand Dilution (Brand):** An influx of low-quality or damaged trade-ins creates a "used bin" aesthetic that undermines Inkwell's premium positioning, discouraging full-price buyers.

**Stop/Redesign Triggers:**
*   **Net margin drops below 12%** for two consecutive months (indicates pricing or labor inefficiencies).
*   **Inventory turnover falls under 0.6** (books sitting >5 months) in any pilot location.
*   **Customer satisfaction scores regarding store cleanliness drop by 15%** due to trade-in processing clutter.

*Note: The March 2025 launch date allows time for this 10-store validation phase. If metrics hold, scale to the remaining 32 stores with confidence.*
