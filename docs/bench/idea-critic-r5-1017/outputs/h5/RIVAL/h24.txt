**Recommendation: Proceed with a Conditional Pilot, Not Full Build-Out**

Do not commit to the full $5.2M capital outlay yet. The math works only if the 15% YoY growth materializes immediately and the labor premium stays below 8%. Committing to a 48-month lease before validating the Boise labor market creates dangerous fixed-cost exposure while you are already operating 20% under capacity in Portland. Instead, negotiate a 12-month flexible lease or sublease arrangement to validate the $0.60 shipping cost and actual wage rates. If the pilot confirms unit economics within 6 months, exercise the expansion option; if not, you cap losses at operational setup costs rather than being trapped in a multi-year lease.

**Top Three Failure Modes**

1.  **Labor Cost Erosion:** The assumed 12% wage uplift is a guess. If the local Boise premium hits 18–20% to attract talent away from established logistics players, your $0.15 per-kit savings vanishes, turning the projected $1.2M profit into a net loss within 14 months.
2.  **Demand Timing Mismatch:** You are currently 20% under capacity in Portland. Adding 9,000 kits of new capacity before organic growth fills the Portland gap means you will pay for idle Boise capacity for 12–18 months, burning roughly $540k annually in lease costs alone before revenue catches up.
3.  **Fixed-Cost Trap:** The 48-month lease locks you into $2.16M in fixed obligations. If growth slows to 8% instead of 15%, you will be stuck paying for unused capacity with no exit ramp, crippling cash flow for other initiatives.

**Scrap/Pivot Trigger**

**Trigger:** If the confirmed all-in labor cost (wages + benefits + training) exceeds **$19.50/hour** (approx. 10% uplift over current baseline) OR if Portland utilization does not exceed **90%** by the time the Boise site is lease-signed.

If either condition is met, **scrap the lease** and pivot to a third-party logistics (3PL) partner in the Mountain West region to handle overflow. This preserves the geographic expansion strategy without the balance sheet risk of a long-term lease and capital build-out.
