**Recommendation:** Proceed with a **six-month pilot launch** rather than a full twelve-month commitment. The base-case math shows a strong potential return ($350k revenue vs. ~$200k direct costs), but the unquantified compliance costs and the unproven 12% conversion rate create excessive risk for a full-year rollout. A pilot limits exposure while validating the core adoption assumption.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Adoption Overestimation:** If the conversion rate is actually 6% (60 clients) instead of 12%, revenue drops to $174,000, which fails to cover the $180,000 in fixed staff and lease costs, let alone marketing and compliance.
2.  **Regulatory & Compliance Drag:** Unbudgeted compliance costs or delays exceeding 12 weeks could defer revenue recognition while burning cash, potentially turning a profitable year into a net loss before the desk even opens.
3.  **Cannibalization:** Advisory clients might shift existing loan balances to lower-margin products or require disproportionate service time, eroding the net value per client below the $2,900 assumption.

**Pre-agreed Stop/Pivot Trigger:**
If the desk acquires **fewer than 50 paying advisory clients by the end of Month 6**, immediately freeze hiring for the second advisor and pivot the existing staff to a remote-only, low-overhead model until unit economics are proven.
