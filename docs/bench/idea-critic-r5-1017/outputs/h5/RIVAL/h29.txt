**Recommendation: Do not proceed to full launch yet.** Run a limited regional pilot first. Your current financial model is flawed: a 10% volume drop on a 30% price hike yields ~$15.12M in revenue ($12M × 1.30 × 0.90), not $14M. However, once you include the $0.25/bag packaging increase and the $667k annual equipment amortization, your projected profit margin shrinks significantly. Without focus-group data, you are risking brand equity and $2M in capital on an unverified assumption that customers equate "single-protein" with "premium." Validate the acceptance rate with a low-cost pilot before committing to the full rollout.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **Volume Collapse:** The actual volume drop exceeds 23%, causing total revenue to fall below the current $12M baseline, making the new line less profitable than the status quo.
2.  **Margin Erosion:** The combined impact of higher packaging costs, equipment amortization, and lower-than-expected volume eliminates the premium margin, resulting in lower net profit despite higher prices.
3.  **Brand Alienation:** Loyal customers perceive the price hike as unjustified greed rather than a quality upgrade, leading to churn not just from the new line but from the existing mixed-protein range.

**Kill/Pivot Trigger:**
If the pilot shows **<15% repeat purchase rate after 90 days** or **total category volume (new + old) drops by >12%** in the test region, kill the national rollout immediately.
