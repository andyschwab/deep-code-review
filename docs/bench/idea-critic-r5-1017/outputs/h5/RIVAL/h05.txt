You are ignoring packaging waste and insurance entirely in the model, which are direct variable costs for physical shipping.
2.  **Operational Bottleneck:** Shipping 2,000 samples monthly (approx. 90 per business day) within 24 hours requires significant warehouse re-engineering. If pick-and-pack time exceeds current estimates, the $2,200 labor budget will be breached immediately, requiring overtime or new hires.

4.  **Competitive Response:** With zero data on competitor moves, a rival could undercut your $15 price point or offer free shipping, rendering your pilot uncompetitive before it gains traction.
