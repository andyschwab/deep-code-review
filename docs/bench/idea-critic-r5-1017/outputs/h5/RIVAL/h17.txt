**The Arithmetic Check**
Your break‑even estimate is incorrect; you will likely lose money in Year 1.
*   **Revenue:** 10 units × $550/day × 274 days = **$1,507,000** (Your forecast of $1.2M was too conservative, which is good, but costs are higher).
*   **Debt Service:** $1.48M loan at 4.2% over 36 months ≈ **$43,800/month** or **$525,600/year**.
*   **Operating Costs:**
    *   Maintenance: 5% of $1.48M = $74,000.
    *   Insurance: $12,000 × 10 = $120,000.
    *   Marketing: $30,000.
    *   *Total OpEx:* **$224,000** (Slightly higher than your $210k estimate).
*   **Year 1 Net Cash Flow:** $1,507,000 (Rev) - $525,600 (Debt) - $224,000 (OpEx) = **+$757,400**.

*Correction:* While the annual cash flow is positive, your **monthly** obligation is ~$43,800. In a bad month (60% utilization), revenue drops to ~$99,000. After paying debt and prorated OpEx (~$18,000), you net only ~$37,000. This leaves little buffer for unexpected repairs or sales cycles. You break even on *cash flow* immediately if utilization holds, but you are highly leveraged against any dip. The real risk is not annual profit, but **liquidity during the Q4 dip**.

**Three Biggest Failure Modes**
1.  **Utilization Cliff:** If utilization drops to 50% instead of 60% in Q4, monthly revenue falls to ~$82,500. After debt and costs, you are barely covering the loan, leaving zero margin for emergency repairs or downtime.
2.  **Hidden Damage & Downtime:** You assumed service contracts cover downtime. They rarely cover "rental abuse" (hydraulic leaks, undercarriage damage, operator error). One major repair could take a unit offline for 3 weeks, destroying that month's profit for that asset.
3.  **Residual Value Risk:** You are financing over 36 months. If the used market for mid-size tractors softens in 3 years, you could owe more on the loans than the fleet is worth, trapping you in the business.

**Pre‑Agreed Pivot Condition**
Stop or pivot if **average utilization falls below 65% for two consecutive months** OR if **cumulative net cash flow is negative after Month 6**. This forces a reassessment before the debt burden compounds.

**Hidden Risks Missed**
*   **Delivery/Logistics:** Moving 10 tractors to/from job sites costs $300–$600 per trip. Who pays? If you do, this eats 5–10% of revenue.
*   **Cleaning & Prep:** Tractors return muddy and damaged. Budget 4–6 hours of labor per rental turnaround ($500/unit/month).
*   **Sales Cycle Lag:** B2B rentals often have 30–60 day payment terms. You must service the loan monthly while waiting for checks. Ensure you have 6 months of debt service ($260k) in working capital.
