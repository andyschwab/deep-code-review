**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" Launch**
Launch the December 15 start date only if you secure a binding insurance quote under $3,500 by December 1. If the premium exceeds this, delay the launch until February 1 to reduce the exposure window.

**Primary Failure Modes**
1.  **Demand Seasonality Error:** Assuming winter occupancy (70%) matches spring is your largest risk. Winter daylight is shorter, weather is harsher, and tourist volume typically drops significantly compared to spring. If occupancy falls below 45% (14 guests), you will operate at a loss per trip.
2.  **Weather Cancellations:** Your model assumes consistent weekly trips. Winter storms could force cancellations on Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday without refunding the fixed upfront $15,000 gear cost or the high insurance premium, destroying unit economics.
3.  **Safety Liability:** Cold-water immersion risks are higher in winter. A single minor incident could spike future premiums or halt operations entirely, a risk not fully captured in the $2k–$5k estimate.

**Pre-Agreed Stop/Pivot Condition**
Stop or pivot immediately if the **average occupancy over the first four trips (Dec 15 – Jan 6) is below 50% (15 guests)**. This indicates the spring demand assumption is invalid for winter conditions. Do not wait until March; cut losses after the first month.

**Hidden Costs & Flawed Assumptions**
*   **Flawed Assumption:** "Demand similar to spring." This is unsupported. Winter whale watching is a distinct product with different constraints (cold, darkness, sea state) than spring. Treat the 70% figure as a best-case scenario, not a baseline.
*   **Hidden Cost:** **Cancellation Refunds & Rescheduling.** You budgeted for fuel/guide/gear per *trip*, but not the administrative cost or reputational damage of refunding 30 guests last minute due to weather.
*   **Hidden Cost:** **Gear Maintenance.** Saltwater and freezing temperatures degrade wetsuits and heaters faster than spring conditions. The $15,000 upfront may need a 20% contingency for mid-season repairs/replacements.
*   **Revenue Leak:** No budget for marketing specifically targeting *winter* tourists, who are a different demographic than spring visitors. Relying on spring awareness may result in empty seats.
