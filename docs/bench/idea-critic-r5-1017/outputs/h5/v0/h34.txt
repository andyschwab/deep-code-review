**Recommendation: Proceed with a "Go-If" Pilot, Not a Full Launch.**
Do not commit to the full $65,000 upfront spend or the 15-café target yet. Instead, secure the flagship café's 6-month trial first. **Three Biggest Failure Modes:**
1.  **The Anchor Tenant Collapse:** If the flagship café (12% of volume) declines the trial, the remaining volume required to cover fixed costs ($7,000/month rent/logistics) becomes disproportionately harder to secure, likely delaying break-even beyond 12 months.
2.  **Margin Erosion via Waste:** The 20% gross margin assumes <5% waste. In wholesale, order volatility is higher than retail; if waste hits 10%, margins drop to near zero, turning every additional sale into a cash-flow negative event.
3.  **Logistics Bottleneck:** The $2,000/month logistics budget assumes efficient routing for 3,000 units/month. Adding cafés without density (scattered locations) will spike delivery costs per unit, invalidating the $2.50 price point profitability before the 15-café goal is reached.

**Stop/Pivot Trigger:**
Halt expansion immediately if **net cash flow is negative for two consecutive months** after the first five cafés are active, OR if **waste exceeds 8% for any single month**. Do not wait for a quarterly review; monthly monitoring is required given the thin margin buffer.
