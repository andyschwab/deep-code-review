Pricing tiers for niche vertical SaaS (like beekeeping) are not determined by a single definitive scientific experiment. Instead, they are derived from:
1.  **General SaaS Pricing Principles:** Applying broad frameworks like *Good-Better-Best* packaging or *Van Westendorp* price sensitivity surveys to the specific willingness-to-pay of beekeepers.
2.  **Market Observation:** Analyzing competitors (e.g., HiveTracks, BeeKeepPal) to see what the market currently bears.
3.  **Unit Economics:** Calculating margins based on the specific cost-to-serve of features like swarm tracking, hive health logs, or local weather integration.
