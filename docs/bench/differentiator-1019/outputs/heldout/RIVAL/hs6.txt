There is **no single publicly available exact dollar amount** for the average revenue per account (ARPA) of solo-lawyer practice-management software specifically in Chile. SaaS companies treat this data as highly confidential competitive intelligence, and no market research firm publishes granular ARPA figures for such a narrow niche (solo lawyers) in a specific LATAM market (Chile) in free reports.

However, I can provide a **data-backed estimate** to serve as a benchmark for your pricing page, derived from regional SaaS pricing patterns and purchasing power parity (PPP) adjustments for the Chilean legal market.

### Estimated ARPA Benchmark: **$25 – $45 USD per month**

**Derivation Logic:**
1.  **US Baseline:** In the US, solo-legal practice management tools (e.g., Clio Solo, MyCase) typically range from **$39 to $49 USD/month**.
2.  **LATAM Adjustment:** Software pricing in Chile generally trades at a **30–45% discount** relative to US MSRP due to local purchasing power and competitive pressure from lower-cost regional alternatives.
3.  **Solo Segment:** Solo practitioners are the most price-sensitive segment. They often reject "per-feature" enterprise tiers in favor of flat-fee essentials.

### Strategic Context for Your Pricing Page

If you list a price significantly outside this range on your Chilean pricing page, you risk specific friction points:

*   **Below $20 USD:** You trigger the "too cheap" heuristic (Van Westendorp). Solo lawyers may perceive the tool as unstable, lacking local compliance (e.g., integration with Chilean courts or tax laws), or having poor support.
*   **Above $60 USD:** You enter "sales-led" territory. A solo lawyer will not self-serve at this price point; they will demand a demo, compare you against full-scale ERPs, or simply churn during the trial.

### Recommended Pricing Structure for Chile
Instead of a single number, structure your tiers to capture the variance within this average:

*   **Entry (Solo Basic):** **$19–$25 USD/mo**. Limited cases/storage. Captures the price-sensitive freelancer.
*   **Standard (Solo Pro):** **$35–$45 USD/mo**. The "anchor" tier. Includes local document automation and calendar sync. This is where your volume should sit.
*   **Small Firm (2-5 users):** **$60–$80 USD/mo**. Per-seat pricing kicks in here.

### How to Validate This Before Launching
Since exact data is unavailable, do not guess. Use the **"Pick a Price You Can Learn From"** approach:
1.  **Set your Standard tier at $39 USD.**
2.  Launch to a small cohort of 20–30 Chilean lawyers.
3.  **Measure the flinch:** If >40% convert immediately, you are too cheap (raise to $49). If <5% convert and cite price, you are too high (drop to $29 or add more perceived value).

**Final Note:** Ensure your pricing page displays prices in **CLP (Chilean Pesos)** alongside USD or as the primary currency to reduce cognitive load for local buyers, using a dynamic converter to maintain your USD margin targets.
