# MelodyStream Recommendation Engine Evaluation Plan

## Test Design
**Method:** A/B Split Test (50/50 traffic allocation)
**Control:** Current genre-tag based engine
**Variant:** New listening-history based engine
**Duration:** 4 weeks (28 days) to capture two full weekly listening cycles and account for novelty effects.

## Metrics Framework

### Primary Metric (Success Criteria)
*   **Average Daily Listening Time per User:** Target lift >5% over the 45-minute baseline.
    *   *Definition:* Total minutes streamed divided by unique active users (DAU) per day.

### Secondary Metrics (Mechanism & Quality)
*   **Skip Rate:** Percentage of tracks skipped within the first 30 seconds. (Lower indicates better relevance).
*   **Session Frequency:** Average number of distinct listening sessions per user per week.
*   **Return Rate:** Percentage of users who return to the platform within 24 hours of a session.
*   **Long-Tail Artist Share:** Percentage of total streams attributed to artists outside the top 1,000 global charts. (Monitors diversity).

### Guardrail Metrics (Stop-Loss Conditions)
*If any of these degrade by >3% with statistical significance, pause the test immediately.*

*   **Artist Churn Rate:** Number of unique artists streamed per user per week. (Prevents over-concentration on few artists).
*   **Catalog Concentration (Herfindahl Index):** Measure of how heavily listening is skewed toward a small subset of tracks. High concentration indicates "fatigue" risk.
*   **Small Label Stream Share:** Total streams generated by artists affiliated with independent/small labels vs. major labels. (Directly addresses content partnership concerns).
*   **Customer Support Tickets:** Volume of complaints related to "repetitive music" or "lack of variety."

## Analysis & Decision Logic

**Go/No-Go Criteria:**
1.  **Green Light:** Primary metric increases significantly (>5%) AND all guardrail metrics remain stable (within ±3%).
2.  **Yellow Light (Iterate):** Primary metric increases, but "Long-Tail Artist Share" or "Small Label Stream Share" drops >5%. *Action:* Adjust algorithm weighting to boost discovery before global launch.
3.  **Red Light (Stop):** Primary metric shows no significant lift OR any guardrail metric degrades >3%. *Action:* Do not launch; revisit model parameters.

## Reporting Cadence
*   **Daily:** Automated monitoring of guardrail metrics only.
*   **Weekly:** Full metric review for internal team.
*   **Final:** Comprehensive report at Day 28 including segment analysis (e.g., power users vs. casual listeners) to present to the CTO and Head of Content Partnerships.

---
*Note: Ensure user consistency (sticky bucketing) so users see the same engine throughout the 4-week period. Avoid peeking at primary metrics before Day 28 to prevent false positives.*
