# TalentFlow Performance Review Module: 60-Day Measurement Framework

## Executive Summary
This framework distinguishes between **technical adoption** (system usage) and **value realization** (successful review completion) across the five global enterprise pilots. The goal is to enable Customer Success (CS) and Implementation teams to triage support resources based on regional friction points rather than raw login volume.

## 1. Leading Indicators (Days 1–30)
*Focus: Detecting friction before it becomes a completion failure.*

| Indicator | Definition | Threshold for Alert | Action Trigger |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | : |
| **Manager-Initiated Session Ratio** | % of manager logins that result in launching a direct report's review vs. passive viewing. | < 60% of manager logins | CS to audit manager training completion; check for UI confusion in that region. |
| **Self-Assessment Drop-off Point** | Specific step where employees abandon the form (e.g., "Goal Selection" vs. "Competency Rating"). | > 25% drop at any single step | Product/UX to investigate specific field complexity; CS to issue targeted "how-to" guides for that step. |
| **Time-to-First-Action** | Time elapsed between manager notification and first employee login. | > 5 days post-notification | Indicates communication breakdown or timing conflict (e.g., quarter-end); CS to verify internal comms. |
| **Support Ticket Density** | Volume of tickets per 100 active users, categorized by "How-to" vs. "Bug." | > 5 tickets/100 users | "How-to" spikes indicate training gaps; "Bug" spikes indicate technical/regional latency issues. |

## 2. Lagging Indicators (Days 31–60)
*Focus: Validating value realization and cultural adoption.*

| Indicator | Definition | Success Target | Insight Provided |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Completion Velocity by Region** | Average days from review launch to final submission, segmented by country/culture. | < 14 days | Identifies if low completion is due to cultural hesitation (consensus cultures) or process bottlenecks. |
| **Quality Score (Manager Feedback)** | % of completed reviews where the manager adds written feedback (not just a rating). | > 75% | Measures if the tool is driving meaningful dialogue vs. box-ticking. |
| **Re-open Rate** | % of reviews sent back for revision after initial submission. | < 10% | High rates indicate unclear guidelines or misalignment on expectations, not tool failure. |
| **Net Promoter Score (NPS) - Admin** | Survey score from HR Administrators at the client site. | > 30 | Gauges overall client sentiment and likelihood of expansion. |

## 3. Qualitative Feedback Strategy (Low-Friction)
To avoid survey fatigue during the critical rollout, use a **Tiered Intercept Approach**:

*   **Tier 1: In-App Micro-Surveys (Automated)**
    *   *Trigger:* Immediately after a user completes a self-assessment or a manager submits a review.
    *   *Question:* Single emoji scale (😠 😐 😊) + optional one-line comment: "What was the hardest part of this step?"
    *   *Volume:* Captures 100% of completions with zero extra effort.

*   **Tier 2: The "5-Minute Pulse" (Targeted)**
    *   *Audience:* Random sample of 10 managers and 10 employees per client region (Total: 100 users).
    *   *Timing:* Day 21 and Day 45.
    *   *Format:* 3 open-ended questions via email/Slack focusing on *process* vs. *tool*: "Did the tool help or hinder your conversation?"

*   **Tier 3: Executive Debrief (Strategic)**
    *   *Audience:* Primary HR Contact at each of the 5 enterprises.
    *   *Timing:* Day 60.
    *   *Format:* 30-minute structured call reviewing the Lagging Indicators above. Focus on business outcomes, not software features.

## 4. Regional Cultural Context Matrix
CS teams must interpret data through these lenses:
*   **High Power Distance Regions (e.g., APAC, LatAm):** Low employee initiation may be normal; look for *Manager* initiation rates as the primary driver.
*   **Direct Communication Cultures (e.g., US, Northern Europe):** Low text quality in feedback fields is a red flag; expect higher "Re-open Rates" due to scrutiny.
*   **Consensus Cultures:** Longer "Time-to-First-Action" is expected; do not flag as a failure until Day 20.

## Resource Allocation Logic
*   **High Logins / Low Completion:** Deploy Training Specialists (Issue: Knowledge/Process).
*   **Low Logins / High Drop-off:** Deploy Technical Support (Issue: UX/Bugs/Integration).
*   **High Completion / Low Quality:** Deploy Change Management Consultants (Issue: Cultural Adoption).

***

*Note: Baseline metrics should be established per client during the first 48 hours to account for varying internal HR processes. All data must be anonymized before cross-client comparison.*
