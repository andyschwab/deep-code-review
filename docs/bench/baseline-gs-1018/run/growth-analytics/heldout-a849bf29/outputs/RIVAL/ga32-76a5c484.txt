### Proposed Multi-Tiered Funnel Structure for IronClad Security

To address the six-month lag between site interaction and closed-won deals, you must shift from a single-conversion model to a **Leading Indicator Funnel**. This structure separates "Volume Metrics" (top of funnel) from "Quality Signals" (middle of funnel) that historically correlate with your 12 closed deals.

**1. Define the Stages**
*   **Stage 1: Awareness (Volume)** – Page views, time on site. *Do not use for experiment success.*
*   **Stage 2: Micro-Conversion (Engagement)** – Whitepaper download, webinar registration, pricing page visit >30 seconds.
*   **Stage 3: Macro-Conversion (Intent)** – Demo request.
*   **Stage 4: Sales Qualified Lead (SQL)** – Demo attended + fit criteria met (e.g., enterprise domain, specific job title).
*   **Stage 5: Opportunity Created** – Sales team validates budget/timeline.
*   **Stage 6: Closed-Won** – Final revenue.

**2. The "Lookback" Analysis (Immediate Action)**
Before running new experiments, use your 18 months of CRM data to validate your hypothesis. Export all opportunities from the last 18 months and map them back to their first touchpoint.
*   **Cohort A (Closed-Won):** Did these 12+ historical deals download the whitepaper or attend a webinar *before* requesting a demo? Calculate the % attachment rate.
*   **Cohort B (Lost/No-Show):** Did the 438 demo requests that didn't close skip these intermediate steps?
*   **Result:** If 80% of winners downloaded the whitepaper but only 20% of losers did, "Whitepaper Download" becomes your **Primary Leading Indicator**.

**3. Evaluating Weekly Experiments**
You cannot wait six months to judge a landing page test. Instead, measure the **rate of progression to the Leading Indicator**.
*   **Old Metric:** Demo Requests / Visitors (Noisy, low correlation to revenue).
*   **New Metric:** (Whitepaper Downloads + High-Intent Demo Requests) / Visitors.
*   **Logic:** If Variant B increases whitepaper downloads by 20% compared to Variant A, and your lookback analysis proves whitepaper readers close at 5x the rate of non-readers, Variant B is the winner *now*, even before the deals close.

**4. Reporting for Q2 Budget Justification**
For Friday's meeting, present the funnel as a **Predictive Model**:
*   "We are optimizing for [Validated Leading Indicator], which historical data shows is present in X% of closed-won deals."
*   "Weekly experiment success is defined by lifting this specific intermediate metric, reducing our feedback loop from 6 months to 2 weeks."
*   Include a simple table showing the historical conversion rate from "Demo Only" vs. "Demo + Content Engagement" to prove the quality argument to the Head of Sales.

**Note:** Ensure your tracking plan explicitly tags users who complete multiple micro-conversions (e.g., whitepaper + webinar) as "High Propensity" in your analytics tool.
**Note:** If the lookback analysis shows no correlation between content consumption and closing, pivot the leading indicator to "Demo Show Rate" or "SQL Creation Rate" instead.
