**Referral Program Diagnostic & Recommendation Plan**

**Immediate Recommendation:** Do not kill the program yet; do not reduce the bonus yet. Your current data compares two different *cohorts* (Organic vs. Referral), not two *variants* of a single test. You cannot isolate whether the churn is caused by "low-quality users" or "poor onboarding" without a controlled A/B test. Reducing the bonus now is a guess that might simply lower acquisition volume without fixing retention.

**Step 1: Isolate the Variable (The "Onboarding vs. User Quality" Test)**
To determine if the issue is the *user* or the *experience*, you must run an A/B test exclusively on the **referred users**.
*   **Control Group (A):** Referred users receive the current standard onboarding + $10 bonus.
*   **Variant Group (B):** Referred users receive a modified onboarding flow (e.g., forced tutorial, delayed bonus unlock until Level 5) + $10 bonus.
*   **Hypothesis:** If Group B retains significantly better than Group A, the issue is **onboarding/experience**. If retention remains identical to Group A (and low), the issue is **user quality/incentive mismatch**.

**Step 2: Alternative Metrics for Referral Quality**
Stop reporting "Installs" or "Day 7 Retention" as primary success metrics for this program. Present these instead to the CFO:
1.  **Net LTV per Referral:** (Referee LTV + Referrer LTV) - (Bonus Cost + CAC). Currently -$4.50. Target: >$0.
2.  **Bonus Redemption Rate vs. Retention Correlation:** Do users who redeem the bonus immediately churn faster than those who wait? This identifies "bonus hunters."
3.  **Action-Based Conversion:** Track specific in-game actions (e.g., "Completed Tutorial," "Reached Level 3") rather than just time-based retention. This isolates engagement depth.
4.  **Referrer Quality Score:** Segment referrers by their own retention. Do high-retention players bring in high-retention friends? If yes, restrict bonuses to only high-quality referrers.

**Step 3: Strategic Options Based on Test Results**
*   **If Onboarding is the culprit:** Implement the Variant B flow permanently. Consider delaying the $10 payout until the referee completes a core game loop action (e.g., finishes Level 3). This filters bots/cash-seekers while keeping the incentive for genuine players.
*   **If User Quality is the culprit:** The $10 flat bonus is attracting the wrong crowd. Pivot to:
    *   **Tiered Rewards:** $2 instant, $8 after Day 7 retention.
    *   **In-Game Currency Only:** Replace cash with exclusive skins/items (lower marginal cost, higher perceived value for gamers).
    *   **Eligibility Gate:** Only allow users who have played for 7+ days to refer others.

**CFO Presentation Narrative:**
"We identified a -$4.50 LTV leak caused by a mismatch between our incentive structure and user intent, not necessarily the program itself. We are launching a controlled test this week to distinguish between onboarding friction and incentive-driven churn. Our immediate proposal is to shift from a 'Pay-per-Install' model to a 'Pay-for-Retention' model, aligning bonus payouts with actual gameplay milestones to protect margin."

*Note: Ensure your tracking distinguishes between the referrer's and referee's event streams to calculate true network LTV. Do not make final budget cuts until the A/B test on onboarding yields statistical significance (approx. 2-3 weeks).*
