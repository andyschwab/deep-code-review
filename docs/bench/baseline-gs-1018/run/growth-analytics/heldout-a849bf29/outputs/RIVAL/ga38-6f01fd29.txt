# GreenFuture Donor Sustainability Framework

## Primary North Star Metric: Sustainable Monthly Revenue (SMR)
**Definition:** The sum of all active recurring monthly donations plus an annualized equivalent of one-off gifts from donors who have given 3+ times in the past 12 months.
**Formula:** `(Active Monthly Subscriptions £) + (Repeat One-Off Donors Last 12mo £ / 12)`
**Why:** This isolates predictable cash flow from volatile campaign spikes, directly addressing your need for long-term sustainability without ignoring valuable one-off supporters.

## Supporting Metric 1: Recurring Conversion Ratio (RCR)
**Definition:** The percentage of total new donors in a period who select a monthly giving option.
**Formula:** `(New Monthly Donors / Total New Donors) × 100`
**Target:** Track weekly during the redesign rollout. A rising RCR indicates successful flow optimization, even if total donor count dips temporarily.

## Supporting Metric 2: Donor Lifetime Value Projection (dLTV)
**Definition:** An estimated 24-month value for new donors based on acquisition type.
**Formula:**
- *Monthly Donors:* `Average Monthly Gift × 18` (assuming 18-month average retention based on your historical data)
- *One-Off Donors:* `Average One-Off Gift × 1.4` (assuming 40% become repeat givers)
**Why:** This quantifies the long-term trade-off. It allows you to show the Board that 100 new monthly donors may equal 500 one-off donors in real value over two years.

## The "Trust & Health" Guardrails
To ensure donor trust remains paramount, monitor these two indicators weekly. If either drops below threshold, pause flow changes immediately.
1.  **Refund/Cancel Rate:** Must stay <2% of monthly subscriptions.
2.  **Support Ticket Volume:** Related to "billing confusion" or "cancel difficulty."

## Implementation Note for Volunteer Analysts
Create a single spreadsheet tab pulling raw export data. Use simple SUMIF formulas to categorize donors as "Monthly" or "One-Off" based on transaction frequency. Avoid complex modeling; the dLTV multiplier can be a static cell updated annually.

*This framework prioritizes clarity for non-technical stakeholders while rigorously tracking the shift to recurring revenue.*
