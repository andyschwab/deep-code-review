# PulseBand Gen 2 Pilot Measurement Strategy

## Primary KPIs: Health Behavior Change (Not Device Usage)

Track these three outcome metrics that indicate genuine behavior change rather than novelty usage:

1. **Sleep Insight Action Rate**: Percentage of users who modify bedtime/wake time within 48 hours of receiving a sleep stage insight. This measures whether data drives action, not just consumption.

2. **Weekly Sleep Consistency Delta**: Change in standard deviation of bedtime/wake time from Week 1 to Week 4. Habit formation shows as reduced variance, not just increased app opens.

3. **Retention-to-Insight Ratio**: Users retained at Day 30 who received ≥3 actionable insights in Week 2, divided by total users who received ≥3 insights. This isolates whether insights (not just device ownership) predict retention.

## Distinguishing Curiosity from Habit Formation

**Curiosity-driven usage patterns** (first 2-3 weeks):
- High app opens immediately after sync, declining sharply after Day 14
- Users view sleep stage breakdown but take no action (no bedtime changes, no goal adjustments)
- Sync frequency correlates with notification prompts, not self-initiated routine
- Session duration decreases week-over-week despite feature exploration

**Habit formation signals** (emerging Week 3-4):
- Consistent sync times within ±2 hours daily, regardless of notifications
- Users adjust goals or settings after viewing insights (tracked via settings-change events)
- App opens occur before bedtime or upon waking (contextual timing), not randomly
- Session duration stabilizes or increases as users establish review routines

## Essential Events to Log in Firmware

Log these events with timestamps to enable the above metrics:

| Event | Properties | Purpose |
|-------|------------|---------|
| `sleep_session_completed` | duration, sleep_stages_detected, confidence_score | Baseline sleep data |
| `insight_delivered` | insight_type, sleep_stage_reference, recommendation | Track what insights were shown |
| `user_action_taken` | action_type (bedtime_change, goal_set, alarm_adjusted), time_since_insight | Measure insight→action conversion |
| `sync_initiated` | trigger_type (manual, auto, notification), battery_level, hours_since_last_sync | Understand sync behavior vs. battery constraints |
| `app_session_started` | time_of_day, days_since_first_use, last_insight_viewed_days_ago | Contextualize engagement patterns |

## Pilot Success Criteria

By Day 30, the pilot validates the sleep features if:
- ≥40% of users demonstrate Sleep Insight Action Rate >25%
- Weekly Sleep Consistency Delta shows ≥15% improvement from Week 1 baseline for retained users
- Retention-to-Insight Ratio exceeds 0.6 (users who act on insights are 60%+ likely to retain)

If these thresholds are not met, the features are novelty-driven and should be simplified before mass production.

## Battery-Sync Tradeoff Monitoring

Track the correlation between `battery_level` at sync and `hours_since_last_sync`. If users consistently sync only when battery exceeds 70%, the 48-hour battery life creates a sync friction point. Target: users sync at least once per 24 hours regardless of battery level above 30%.
