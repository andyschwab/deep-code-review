# TrainPro Pilot Success Framework: Unified Metrics Dashboard

To satisfy the divergent requirements of your three manufacturing clients while securing enterprise contracts, report on a **Three-Tier Success Matrix**. This approach validates your internal hypothesis (80% completion) while explicitly addressing each stakeholder's unique definition of value.

### Tier 1: Adoption & Engagement (The "Usage" Metric)
*Addresses the client focused on completion rates and validates your internal 80% threshold.*

*   **Metric:** **Module Completion Rate (%)**
    *   **Definition:** Percentage of enrolled employees who finished 100% of assigned modules.
    *   **Target:** ≥80% per client site.
    *   **Secondary View:** Average % of curriculum completed per user (captures partial progress if the 80% binary is too harsh).
*   **Metric:** **Active Learner Velocity**
    *   **Definition:** Average days taken to complete a module from start date.
    *   **Why:** Demonstrates efficiency and integration into shift schedules.

### Tier 2: Competency & Knowledge (The "Skill" Metric)
*Addresses the client focused on assessment scores and proves training efficacy.*

*   **Metric:** **Post-Training Assessment Score Delta**
    *   **Definition:** Average percentage point increase between pre-training and post-training assessment scores.
    *   **Target:** ≥25% improvement average across all users.
    *   **Why:** Absolute scores can be misleading if tests are hard; the *delta* proves knowledge transfer regardless of baseline.
*   **Metric:** **First-Attempt Pass Rate**
    *   **Definition:** Percentage of users passing the final assessment on the first try.
    *   **Why:** Indicates clarity of content and immediate comprehension.

### Tier 3: Business Impact (The "ROI" Metric)
*Addresses the VP of Sales' need to justify enterprise pricing and satisfies the implicit "so what?" for all stakeholders.*

*   **Metric:** **Operational Efficiency Correlation**
    *   **Definition:** Self-reported or supervisor-verified reduction in time-to-task for trained procedures (survey-based if hard data isn't available yet).
    *   **Target:** ≥15% reported confidence increase in performing tasks.
*   **Metric:** **Stakeholder Satisfaction (NPS)**
    *   **Definition:** Net Promoter Score from site managers regarding the platform's ease of use and reporting.
    *   **Target:** NPS > +30.

## Reporting Strategy for April 12th Steering Committee

Do not present these as separate data points. Present them as a **funnel of value**:

1.  **Headline Statement:** "X% of employees completed the training (Tier 1), resulting in a Y% average increase in competency scores (Tier 2), correlating to Z% reported operational confidence (Tier 3)."
2.  **Client-Specific Highlights:** Dedicate one slide per client highlighting *their* primary metric first, followed by the supporting tiers.
    *   *Client A (Completion focused):* Lead with the 80%+ achievement.
    *   *Client B (Score focused):* Lead with the Assessment Delta.
3.  **The Enterprise Ask:** Conclude that because the pilot succeeded across *all three* dimensions (not just one), the enterprise contract should include full access to all modules, advanced analytics, and multi-site management features.

**Note:** Ensure all data cuts off strictly on April 10th to maintain pilot integrity; do not include any post-pilot activity in the core metrics. Prepare raw data appendices in case the committee questions the sample size of 200 users per site.
