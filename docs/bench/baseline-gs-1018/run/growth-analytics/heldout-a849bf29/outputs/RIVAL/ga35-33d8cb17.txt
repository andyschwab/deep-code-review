# NeoLiving South Korea Launch: KPI Dashboard Requirements

**Primary Success Metrics (Daily Monitoring)**
1.  **Mobile Payment Integration Rate:** Percentage of active devices linked to local payment platforms (KakaoPay, Naver Pay, Samsung Pay) within 24 hours of setup. *Hypothesis validation metric.*
2.  **Routine Automation Activation:** Count of unique users creating or triggering "scene" automations (e.g., "Good Morning," "Away Mode") per 1,000 active units. *Preferred over daily active interactions for measuring stickiness in this market.*
3.  **Setup Completion Time:** Median time from unboxing to first successful mobile app connection. *Critical for detecting friction with local network configurations or app localization issues.*
4.  **Retailer-Specific Activation Ratio:** Activated units divided by shipped units, segmented by the three partner retailers. *Identifies channel-specific distribution or demo issues.*

**Early Warning Indicators (Issue Detection)**
5.  **Connection Drop Frequency:** Average number of reconnection attempts per device per day. *Hardware/Network stability indicator.*
6.  **Feature Abandonment Rate:** Percentage of users who attempt but fail to complete specific high-value actions (e.g., setting a schedule, linking a payment method) within the first 3 sessions.
7.  **Support Ticket Trigger Events:** Volume of telemetry events correlating with customer support inquiries (e.g., repeated failed login attempts, firmware update failures).

**Cultural Context Metrics**
8.  **App Store Rating Velocity:** Daily average rating and review volume from Korean app stores (Galaxy Store, local Android stores), tracked alongside feature usage spikes.
9.  **Social Sharing Events:** Count of users utilizing "share status" or "gift mode" features, reflecting the high value placed on social proof and gifting in the region.

**Implementation Notes**
*   Prioritize event tracking for payment gateway handshakes and automation script executions over simple "device on/off" pings to validate the integration hypothesis.
*   Segment all data by retailer and device model immediately to isolate hardware vs. distribution channel issues within the first 72 hours.
