# Strategic Recommendation: Primary Metric & Guardrail for LocalLink

**Primary Metric to Optimize:** **Contribution Margin Dollars per Transaction**
*(Calculated as: Transaction GMV × Take Rate − Variable Costs)*

**Guardrail Metric:** **Provider Fill Rate by Category**
*(Percentage of homeowner requests successfully matched with a provider within 2 hours)*

### Rationale

At LocalLink's current stage ($8M GMV, 20% YoY growth) with a recent strategic decision to lower take rates, optimizing solely for **GMV** is dangerous. It incentivizes subsidizing low-margin jobs that increase top-line volume but erode unit economics, directly explaining why your revenue only grew 5% despite the GMV surge. This creates "hollow growth" that investors will penalize during your May funding round.

Conversely, optimizing strictly for **Take Rate %** is equally risky for a two-sided marketplace. Artificially inflating fees to boost margins can starve the marketplace of liquidity. If homeowners can't find providers (or vice versa) because prices are too high, GMV collapses, and the network effect reverses.

**Contribution Margin Dollars** is the superior north star because it forces the product team to balance volume and margin simultaneously. It answers the critical question: "Is this specific job actually profitable after accounting for the fee we charged?" This metric aligns product decisions with bottom-line health without sacrificing the liquidity needed for a marketplace to function. It shifts the focus from "how much money is moving through the app" to "how much value are we capturing from that movement."

The **Provider Fill Rate** serves as the essential guardrail. In the Midwest service market, reliability is the primary driver of retention. If your pursuit of contribution margin causes fill rates to drop (e.g., by pricing out smaller plumbing jobs or reducing incentives for electricians), you risk long-term churn. Monitoring this weekly ensures that margin optimization does not come at the cost of marketplace utility.

### Implementation for Investor Deck

For your May pitch deck, frame this shift as a maturation from "growth at all costs" to "sustainable unit economics." Highlight that while GMV grew 20%, the strategic pivot to optimize for Contribution Margin will decouple revenue growth from GMV growth, targeting a higher revenue CAGR in the next 18 months. Use your transaction logs to segment categories by current contribution margin; identify the bottom 20% of jobs that are likely subsidizing the rest, and present a product roadmap aimed either at improving their efficiency or re-pricing them to neutrality.

This approach demonstrates to investors that LocalLink has a sophisticated understanding of marketplace dynamics and is ready to scale profitably, not just volumetrically.

*Note: Ensure your variable cost calculation includes any provider subsidies, support costs per ticket, and payment processing fees.*
