### Recommended Engagement Metrics for StillMind Investor Deck

Given StillMind's low-frequency usage pattern (1.5 sessions/week) and the launch of "micro-sessions," traditional Daily Active Users (DAU) is indeed a misleading vanity metric that understates value. For your April 1st investor update, replace DAU with the following three metrics that align with your specific business context and product changes:

**1. Weekly Active Users (WAU) as Primary Volume Metric**
Shift your primary headline metric from DAU to WAU. With an average frequency of 1.5 times per week, a daily view fragments your user base artificially, making retention look volatile. WAU smooths this noise and captures the natural rhythm of your high-stress audience, who likely meditate sporadically based on workload rather than habit.
*   **Definition:** Unique users with ≥1 session in a 7-day rolling window.
*   **Investor Narrative:** "Our users engage on their own schedule; WAU reflects our true reach without penalizing intermittent usage."

**2. "Meaningful Session" Rate (Quality over Quantity)**
Since you assume one-minute sessions provide value, do not discard them, but distinguish them from deep engagement. Create a composite metric that validates your hypothesis that micro-sessions drive frequency without cannibalizing deep value.
*   **Definition:** The percentage of active users in a week who complete at least one session ≥5 minutes OR complete ≥3 micro-sessions (<2 mins).
*   **Investor Narrative:** "We track 'Meaningful Engagement' to ensure that while micro-sessions increase frequency, users are still deriving substantial calm, whether through one deep dive or several quick resets."

**3. 4-Week Retention Cohort Curve (Not Day-Retention)**
Daily retention charts will show massive drop-offs immediately after signup simply because users don't need the app daily. This looks like churn to investors but is actually natural behavior. Switch to a monthly cohort view.
*   **Definition:** Track the percentage of users who signed up in a specific month (e.g., March cohort) and returned at least once in Weeks 3 and 4. Compare the March cohort (post-micro-session launch) against January/February cohorts.
*   **Investor Narrative:** "Our 4-week retention shows that the March cohort returns more consistently than previous cohorts, proving micro-sessions successfully bridge the gap between high-stress events."

**Implementation Note for the Deck:**
Visualize the March 1st launch as an inflection point. Show a bar chart of WAU trending up post-launch, and a line graph comparing the 4-week retention curves of pre-launch vs. post-launch cohorts. Explicitly state in the footnotes: "Metrics adjusted for low-frequency, high-value usage patterns typical of high-stress professionals; DAU excluded as it misrepresents engagement frequency."

**Critical Data Warning:**
Ensure your "micro-session" definition has a floor (e.g., >30 seconds) to prevent accidental background opens from inflating these numbers. Investors will scrutinize whether "1-minute" sessions are intentional engagement or noise.
