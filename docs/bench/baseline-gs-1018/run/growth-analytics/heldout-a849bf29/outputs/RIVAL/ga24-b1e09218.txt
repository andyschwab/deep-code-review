**Prioritized Analytics Tasks for ThreadHub**

**1. Define and Manually Calculate "Meaningful Conversation" Rate (4–6 hours)**
Before building any dashboard, validate your core hypothesis using a simple spreadsheet export. Define "meaningful conversation" explicitly (e.g., a thread with 3+ replies from 2+ unique users within 48 hours). Export your last 14 days of user activity data. Manually calculate the correlation between this specific metric and Day-7 retention for your current 450 users.
*   **Action:** If the correlation is weak, pivot your metric immediately. If strong, you have your North Star. Do not code a dashboard yet; a weekly manual calculation is sufficient for now. This prevents you from automating the wrong metric.

**2. Implement Basic Event Tracking for the "Aha" Moment (2–3 hours)**
Once the metric is validated, implement lightweight event tracking only for the specific actions that constitute a "meaningful conversation." Use a simple, low-maintenance tool like PostHog (self-hosted or cloud) or even basic GA4 custom events if you already have it.
*   **Events to track:** `thread_created`, `reply_posted`, `user_mentioned`.
*   **Goal:** Automatically tag users who hit your "meaningful conversation" threshold within their first 3 days. This allows you to segment your upcoming event invitations: prioritize inviting users who have already hit this mark versus those who haven't, allowing for targeted messaging without complex infrastructure.

**3. Set Up a "Drop-off" Alert for New Signups (1 hour)**
With your event on the 25th approaching, your highest risk is new users signing up and failing to engage. Set up a simple daily email alert (via your analytics tool or a basic script) that flags the number of new signups from the previous 24 hours who have *zero* posts or replies.
*   **Action:** This gives you a daily "rescue list." As a solo founder, spend 15 minutes each morning personally welcoming or prompting these specific users. This high-touch intervention is more valuable than a dashboard for a community of this size and directly supports retention before your event.

**Allocation Recommendation:**
Spend **no more than 10 hours total** on these three tasks this week. Do not build custom dashboards; they are a distraction at 450 users. Your development hours should remain 80% focused on stability and features for the upcoming event, and 20% on this lightweight validation. The goal is to learn fast, not to build a data team.

*Note: Validate your hypothesis manually before automating. At your current scale, personal intervention based on simple data beats complex instrumentation.*
