### GreenLeaf Winter Retention Metric Framework

To distinguish between correlation (happy users logging in) and causation (care actions driving retention), and to address Scandinavian winter challenges, track these four metric categories. These move beyond "logins" to measure actual plant health and user efficacy.

#### 1. Plant Health & Survival Metrics (The Outcome)
*These measure if your service is actually working during the dark months.*
*   **Winter Survival Rate:** % of plants delivered in Sept/Oct still marked "Alive" or "Thriving" by March. This is your ultimate north star for the winter season.
*   **Distress Incident Rate:** % of users reporting "yellowing leaves," "drooping," or "pests" via the app or support tickets per 100 active subscribers.
*   **Recovery Success Rate:** % of plants flagged as "struggling" that are marked "recovered" within 14 days after a user engages with a specific care guide.

#### 2. Action-Based Engagement Metrics (The Causal Drivers)
*Replace "logins" with specific actions that prove users are caring for plants.*
*   **Light Adjustment Completion:** % of users who confirm they moved their plant to a brighter spot (critical for Nov-Feb in Oslo/Stockholm/Copenhagen).
*   **Watering Adherence Score:** Ratio of actual watering events logged vs. recommended frequency (adjusted for lower winter evaporation).
*   **Care Streak Length:** Average number of consecutive weeks a user logs a *care action* (not just a view).
*   **Guide-to-Action Ratio:** % of users who read a "Winter Care" article and then log a relevant action (e.g., reducing water frequency) within 48 hours.

#### 3. Satisfaction & Confidence Metrics (The Sentiment)
*Measure if users feel capable, not just satisfied.*
*   **Plant Parent Confidence Score:** A monthly 1-5 survey prompt: "How confident are you that your plant will survive the winter?"
*   **Value Perception Index:** Correlation between "Confidence Score" and likelihood to recommend (NPS).
*   **Support Ticket Sentiment:** % of incoming tickets expressing anxiety about plant death vs. general inquiries.

#### 4. Seasonal Context Metrics (The Scandinavian Variables)
*   **Light Deficit Days:** Track local daylight hours vs. user engagement. Does engagement drop when daylight falls below 6 hours?
*   **Heating Season Impact:** Monitor "Distress Incident Rate" against local heating start dates (dry air from radiators is a major killer in Nordic winters).

### Implementation Strategy for October Launch
To test causality, segment your 1,200 subscribers into two groups for the winter onboarding redesign:
1.  **Control Group:** Receives standard "Login to check your plant" reminders.
2.  **Treatment Group:** Receives action-oriented prompts: "It's dark outside. Move your Monstera 1 meter closer to the window today. Tap here when done."

Compare the **Winter Survival Rate** and **Recovery Success Rate** between these groups in February. If the Treatment Group has higher survival rates despite similar login frequencies, you have proven that *care actions* (driven by your content) cause retention, not just the act of logging in.

**Note:** Ensure your app allows one-tap logging for actions to reduce friction; high effort logging will artificially lower your action metrics.
**Note:** Baseline your "Distress Incident Rate" immediately in September before the light drops to ensure accurate winter comparison.
