**Primary Success Metric: Day-3 Guild Join Rate Correlated to Day-7 Purchase Conversion**

Do not track guild creation rates, chat volume, or mission completion as your primary success indicator. These are activity metrics that measure noise, not value. Your single most important success metric must be the **conversion lift of players who join a guild within their first three days compared to those who do not.**

**The Logic**

Your executive team needs to know if the social update drives revenue, not just activity. You stated a clear hypothesis: players joining a guild in their first three days are five times more likely to purchase. This hypothesis identifies a specific behavior (guild joining) within a specific window (Day 0–3) that predicts your financial goal (monetization).

Tracking "guild creation" is flawed because a high creation rate does not guarantee retention or spending; players can create empty guilds and churn. Tracking "chat volume" is a vanity metric; high message counts often indicate confusion or socializing without progression, neither of which guarantees an in-app purchase. Tracking "cooperative mission completion" is a lagging indicator of engagement but lacks the direct causal link to your monetization assumption.

The only metric that aligns social engagement with financial goals is the **conversion rate delta** between your two key cohorts:
1.  **Cohort A:** Users who joined a guild within 3 days of install.
2.  **Cohort B:** Users who did not join a guild within 3 days.

**Implementation Strategy**

To determine if the update is successful, you must measure the change in the size of Cohort A and the stability of their purchase probability.

1.  **Define the Event:** Track `guild_joined` with a property `days_since_install`.
2.  **Set the Observation Window:** Calculate the purchase rate for Cohort A vs. Cohort B over a 7-day window from install. Do not mix observation windows; ensure both cohorts are measured over the exact same timeframe to avoid skewing the data.
3.  **Calculate the Lift:** If your hypothesis holds, Cohort A should show a ~5x higher purchase rate than Cohort B.
4.  **Success Criteria:** The update is successful if the *percentage of total new users* entering Cohort A increases significantly without diluting the average revenue per paying user (ARPPU) of that group.

If the update drives more players into the "guild within 3 days" bucket, and that bucket maintains its high purchase probability, your total revenue will rise. If the update increases chat volume but fails to move users into the guild-joining bucket, it has failed to impact the bottom line.

**Recommendation for the Executive Team**

Present the roadmap success as: **"Increase the Day-3 Guild Join Rate from X% to Y%, maintaining a 5x purchase conversion lift over non-guilded players."** This ties the feature directly to the revenue model, ensuring the team optimizes for the behavior that actually drives money, not just the behavior that looks busy.

*Note: Ensure your analytics tool filters out bot traffic or spam guilds which could artificially inflate join rates without real engagement.*
*Note: Re-evaluate the "5x" assumption quarterly; if the lift drops below 3x, the guild mechanic may no longer be the primary revenue driver.*
