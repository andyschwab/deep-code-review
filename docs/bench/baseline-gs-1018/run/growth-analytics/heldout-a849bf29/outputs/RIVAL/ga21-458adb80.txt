### Board Recommendation: Prioritize Retention Before Pricing

**Decision:** Delay the price increase and immediately launch the content personalization initiative. The math confirms that fixing churn yields higher net revenue with lower risk than raising prices on a leaking bucket.

#### 1. The Trade-Off Analysis

**Scenario A: Raise Prices Now (The "Quick Win" Trap)**
*   **Assumption:** 5% price increase on current base.
*   **Risk:** With churn already elevated (4.2%), price sensitivity is likely high. A conservative estimate suggests a 0.5% spike in churn immediately.
*   **Net Result:** You gain revenue from the 95.8% who stay, but you accelerate the loss of the 4.2% who leave. If churn spikes to 4.7%, you lose ~590 subscribers/month. At an estimated €15/month ARPU, that is **€8,850/month in lost recurring revenue** just to offset the price hike gain. The net gain is negligible, and the growth trajectory flattens.

**Scenario B: Fix Retention First (The Compound Growth Engine)**
*   **Assumption:** Reduce churn from 4.2% to 2.7% (your 1.5% target) over two quarters.
*   **Impact:** Saving 1.5% of 12,500 subscribers equals **187 subscribers retained every month**.
*   **Revenue Value:** 187 subs × €15 × 12 months = **€33,660 in annual recurring revenue (ARR) saved per month of improvement.**
*   **Compounding:** Unlike a one-time price hike, retention improvements compound. Every subscriber saved stays in the base to be counted next month, reducing the number of new sales needed just to stand still.

#### 2. Essential Metrics to Track

To validate this strategy at the board meeting and during execution, track these four specific metrics:

| Metric | Why It Matters | Target Signal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cohort Retention Rate** | Measures if personalization works for specific signup months, isolating the initiative's effect from overall noise. | Month-2 retention improves by >5% vs. control cohort. |
| **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)** | The ultimate measure of long-term value. Retention changes impact LTV exponentially more than small price changes. | LTV increases by >15% within 6 months. |
| **Churn Reason Code: "Content Relevance"** | Directly validates your hypothesis that personalization is the lever. | % of churn citing "lack of relevant content" drops by 20%. |
| **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)** | Combines churn, downgrades, and upgrades. Shows if existing customers are becoming more valuable. | NRR moves from <100% to >105%. |

#### 3. Interpretation & Investor Narrative

**The Argument:**
Present retention not as a "delay" in revenue, but as **efficiency optimization**.
*   **Current State:** You are spending marketing budget to replace 4.2% of your base every month just to stay flat. This is inefficient capital allocation.
*   **The Pivot:** By lowering churn to 2.7%, you effectively increase your organic growth rate without spending €1 on acquisition.
*   **The Future Price Increase:** Once churn is stabilized at <3%, the base is "sticky." *Then* you can raise prices with minimal friction, capturing the full upside because users are getting personalized value.

**Definitive Recommendation:**
Approve the personalization roadmap immediately. Frame the delay in price increases as a strategic move to **maximize the valuation multiple** for the funding round. Investors pay for efficient, compounding growth (high retention), not fragile revenue spikes (price hikes on high churn). Fix the leak, then turn up the water pressure.

*Note: Ensure your analytics team sets up cohort tracking by signup date immediately to isolate the personalization impact from seasonal news cycles.*
