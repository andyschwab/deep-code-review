### Metric Framework for GreenFuture: Sustainable Impact Scorecard

**1. The North Star: Annualized Recurring Revenue (ARR) from Active Donors**
Reject "Total Number of Donors" and "Total Donation Volume" as primary success metrics. These are vanity metrics in your context; they rise with one-off campaign spikes but do not reflect sustainable capacity.
*   **Definition:** The sum of all active monthly recurring gifts, annualized, restricted to donors who have given within the last 90 days.
*   **Why:** This metric only rises when a donor commits to predictable, long-term support. It directly correlates to your ability to plan climate action projects, satisfying the Board's need for financial health without ignoring mission impact.

**2. Input Metrics (The Levers)**
Track these three specific inputs that move the North Star. Do not instrument anything else until these are stable.
*   **Recurring Conversion Rate:** The percentage of new donors who select "Monthly" at checkout vs. "One-off."
    *   *Question answered:* Is the new flow successfully shifting behavior?
*   **90-Day Active Retention (Recurring):** The percentage of monthly donors who remain active after 3 months.
    *   *Question answered:* Are we acquiring quality subscribers who trust us, or are they churning immediately?
*   **One-off to Recurring Upgrade Rate:** The percentage of existing one-off donors who switch to monthly within 60 days of a specific engagement.
    *   *Question answered:* Are we successfully migrating the legacy base?

**3. The Guardrail: Total Cash Flow Floor**
To address your fear of dropping total volume during the transition:
*   **Metric:** Rolling 3-Month Total Cash Inflow.
*   **Rule:** Set a hard floor based on your minimum operational burn rate. If the shift to recurring causes total cash inflow to dip below this specific number for two consecutive months, the acquisition strategy pauses. This protects liquidity while you build the recurring base.

**4. Reporting & Governance**
*   **The Composite Score:** Do not build a complex weighted algorithm. Present a simple "Sustainability Ratio": *ARR from Active Donors* divided by *Total Cash Inflow*.
    *   *Target:* Move this ratio from its current baseline (likely <20%) toward 60% over 18 months. A rising ratio proves the portfolio is becoming predictable, even if total cash fluctuates.
*   **Data Hygiene:** Ensure your analytics use a stable, pseudonymous donor ID to track users across one-off and recurring states. Do not use raw PII in analytics tools; route identity management to your compliance lead.
*   **Stage Appropriateness:** As you are redesigning the flow (MVP/Growth transition), ignore "Referral" metrics and deep funnel drop-offs for now. Focus entirely on the checkout choice (One-off vs. Monthly) and the first 90 days of retention.
