### North Star Metric
**Weekly Active Care Completers**: The count of subscribers who complete a verified "care action" (e.g., logging water, rotating the plant, or confirming soil moisture) within a 7-day window.
*Why:* This replaces "logins" (vanity) with verified value delivery. It aligns directly with your hypothesis that *caring* drives retention, not merely *visiting*.

### Input Metrics (The Levers)
Track these to move the North Star:
1.  **Care Action Conversion Rate**: % of users who open the app and actually log a care event. (Diagnoses if the UI is friction-heavy).
2.  **Seasonal Light Deficit Alert Response**: % of users who adjust plant location or add grow-light hours after receiving a "Low Light" notification (critical for Oct–March in Oslo/Stockholm/Copenhagen).
3.  **Onboarding Activation Depth**: % of new subscribers who log their first care action within 48 hours of delivery.

### The Funnel Read (Bottom-Up)
Do not optimize acquisition until you validate the retention link. Read in this order:
1.  **Retention (The Leak Check)**: Segment churn by "Weekly Active Care Completers" vs. "Non-Completers."
    *   *The Test:* If churn is ~4% for both groups, app engagement is a **symptom** of happy customers, not the **cause**. If churn is significantly lower (e.g., <2%) for Completers, the causal link holds.
    *   *Current Status:* **UNVERIFIED**. You have aggregate churn (4%) and aggregate engagement (25% monthly), but no cohort overlap. You cannot claim causality yet.
2.  **Activation**: Of the 1,200 subscribers, how many completed a care action in week 1? If this is low, winter onboarding will fail regardless of content quality.
3.  **Acquisition**: Only scale spend once the Retention step confirms that driving "Care Completers" lowers churn.

### Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only What Answers a Question)
Stop tracking generic "screen views." Instrument these specific events:
*   `care_action_logged` (Properties: `action_type`, `plant_id`, `season_tag`) → *Question: Are users actually caring for plants?*

*   `subscription_renewed` / `subscription_cancelled` (Properties: `last_care_action_date`, `care_streak_length`) → *Question: Does care frequency predict renewal?*

### Experiment Design: Winter Onboarding
To test your redesign for October, do not just "launch and watch."
*   **Pre-commitment**: Define the primary metric (`Weekly Active Care Completers`) and a guardrail (`Support Ticket Volume` – ensure new notifications don't confuse users).
*   **Method**: Run an A/B test on the onboarding email sequence.
    *   *Control:* Current generic care tips.
    *   *Variant:* Seasonal "Winter Light Survival" prompts with specific location-based advice.
*   **Rule**: Do not peek at results daily. Pre-calculate the sample size needed to detect a lift (e.g., moving care completion from 25% to 30%) and read the result **once** at the end of the cycle. A day-3 "win" is statistically invalid noise.
