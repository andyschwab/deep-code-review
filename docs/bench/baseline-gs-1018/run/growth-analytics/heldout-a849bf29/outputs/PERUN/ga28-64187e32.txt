**North Star Metric: Weekly Value Deliveries (WVD)**

Reject "Daily Active Users" and raw "Signups" as vanity metrics; they penalize your specific use case (high-stress, low-frequency) and rise regardless of value. Define your North Star as **Weekly Value Deliveries**: the count of unique users per week who complete at least one session meeting a validated "minimum effective dose" (MED).

**1. Validate the "Micro" Assumption (Do not guess the threshold)**
You stated an *assumption* that one-minute sessions provide value. An unverified assumption is `UNVERIFIED`. Before finalizing the deck:
*   **Action:** Run a cohort analysis comparing users whose *only* sessions were <2 mins against those with >5 mins over a 4-week window.
*   **Metric:** Compare their Week-4 retention rates.
*   **Decision Rule:** If the <2 min cohort retains at <50% of the >5 min cohort rate, your MED is not 1 minute. Set the WVD threshold to the shortest duration where retention correlates with long-term survival. Do not count sessions below this threshold as "active."

**2. Read the Funnel Bottom-Up (Retention before Frequency)**
Your current average (1.5 opens/week) is a baseline, not a failure. Increasing frequency without proving value is waste.
*   **Diagnosis:** Do not try to move users from 1.5 to 3.0 opens immediately. First, verify if the users currently opening 1.5 times are *returning* the following week.
*   **Metric:** **Week-over-Week Retention of WVD**. (Of the users who hit the MED this week, what % hit it next week?)
*   **Investor Narrative:** "We are not optimizing for mindless daily logins; we are optimizing for reliable stress relief. Our metric tracks users who successfully receive value weekly."

**3. Event Taxonomy (Instrument only what answers a question)**
Do not instrument "every click." Define these three events specifically for the April 1st deck:
*   `session_completed`: Properties: `duration_seconds`, `feature_type` (micro vs. standard). *Question: Did they hit the MED?*
*   `value_threshold_met`: Boolean flag triggered only if `duration` ≥ MED. *Question: Did we deliver value this week?*
*   `weekly_active_valued`: Aggregated count of unique users with `value_threshold_met` = true in a 7-day window. *This is your North Star.*
