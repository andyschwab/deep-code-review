**Emergency Analysis Plan: HomeHaven Checkout Conversion Decline**

**1. Immediate Quantitative Triage (Do this tonight)**
Since granular funnel data is missing, prioritize these three segmented comparisons (New Design vs. Old Design baseline) to isolate the leak:
*   **Device & Performance Split:** Segment conversion by **Mobile vs. Desktop** and by **Connection Speed/Page Load Time**. If the drop is isolated to mobile or correlates with load times >3s, the cause is likely technical performance, not UX anxiety.
*   **Step-Level Drop-off:** Instrument immediate event tracking for the three new steps. Identify exactly which of the three clicks has the highest exit rate. If abandonment spikes at the final "Confirm" button without a progress bar, your anxiety hypothesis gains weight. If it spikes at step 1, the issue may be trust or clarity, not duration.
*   **Cart Value Cohort:** Analyze conversion by **Order Value buckets**. Since AOV rose 5%, check if high-value orders are converting *better* while low-value ones are abandoning. This would suggest the new design filters for serious buyers but frustrates browsers, complicating a simple rollback.

**2. Rapid Qualitative Gathering (Do this tomorrow morning)**
*   **Session Replays:** Watch 20–30 recordings of mobile users abandoning at the identified drop-off step. Look specifically for "rage clicks," rapid back-button usage, or long pauses indicating confusion.
*   **Intercept Survey:** Deploy a one-question exit survey on the checkout page for abandoning users: "What stopped you from completing your purchase today?" with options for "Technical error," "Unsure of next steps," "Too slow," and "Other."
*   **Support Ticket Scan:** Review the last 48 hours of customer support chats/emails for keywords like "stuck," "broken," or "checkout."

**3. Decision Framework for the Review**
Do not decide based on conversion alone. Calculate the **Revenue Per Visitor (RPV)** impact:
*   *Formula:* (Conversion Rate × Average Order Value).
*   If the 15% conversion drop is offset by the 5% AOV increase such that RPV is flat or positive, a full rollback may be unnecessary. Instead, consider a targeted fix (e.g., re-adding the progress bar only on mobile) while keeping the streamlined flow for desktop or high-intent users.

**Recommended Immediate Action:**
If mobile conversion is down >20% and session replays show technical errors or confusion at step 1, **rollback mobile only** to the old design while keeping the new design on desktop. This stops the bleeding on your largest traffic source while preserving data on the AOV lift. If RPV is down overall and no technical errors exist, proceed with a rapid A/B test re-introducing the progress bar to validate the anxiety hypothesis before a full revert.

**Note:** Do not attribute the drop to "anxiety" without evidence; technical latency on mobile is a statistically more probable cause for sudden drops post-deployment. Ensure any A/B test uses a pre-committed sample size to avoid false positives from peeking.
