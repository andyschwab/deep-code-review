### Measurement Framework: TalentFlow Performance Review Module (Days 1–60)

**North Star Metric**
**Weekly Active Completers (WAC):** The count of unique employees who complete a meaningful step in the review cycle (e.g., submit self-assessment, give peer feedback, or finalize a manager review) within a 7-day window.
*Why:* Login frequency is a vanity metric; it rises regardless of value. WAC only rises when real work happens. Discard "manager login frequency" as a primary success indicator.

**Funnel Diagnosis (Bottom-Up)**
Do not pour resources into acquisition (training more managers) until the leak at the bottom is plugged. Diagnose in this order:
1.  **Retention (The Core Leak):** Of the 40% who started a self-assessment, how many return within 3 days to finish? If they drop off after opening, the issue is interface confusion or length, not training.
2.  **Activation:** Did the user complete the "Aha" moment (submitting the first draft)?
3.  **Acquisition:** Only after fixing retention, analyze if manager logins correlate with employee starts.

**Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only These)**
Do not track "everything." Instrument only events that answer specific questions:
*   `review_started` (Question: Are users initiating?)
*   `review_abandoned_mid_form` (Question: Is the interface confusing? Property: `step_number`)
*   `review_submitted` (Question: Did we deliver value?)
*   `help_doc_viewed` (Question: Is this a training gap?)

**Leading & Lagging Indicators (First 60 Days)**
*   **Leading (Predictive):**
    *   *Time-to-First-Submit:* Median time from module launch to first completed self-assessment per company.
    *   *Abandonment Rate by Step:* Specific form fields where >20% of users drop off.
    *   *Support Ticket Ratio:* Tickets per 100 active users (spikes indicate confusion, not just "crunch time").
*   **Lagging (Outcome):**
    *   *Cycle Completion Rate:* % of assigned reviews fully closed by Day 60.
    *   *Regional Variance:* Completion rates normalized by region to isolate cultural/timing factors (e.g., end-of-quarter impact in specific markets).

**Qualitative Feedback Strategy (Low Friction)**
Avoid surveys that overwhelm clients. Use targeted, context-aware intercepts:
1.  **Exit-Intent Micro-Survey:** Trigger a single-question popup only when a user abandons a form: "What stopped you from finishing? (Too long / Confusing / Not now)."
2.  **The "5-User" Rule:** Customer Success should interview exactly 5 completers and 5 non-completers per enterprise region. Do not scale this; look for pattern saturation, not statistical significance.
3.  **Manager Syncs:** Replace generic check-ins with a single data review: "We see a 40% completion rate; is this due to the quarter-end crunch or tool friction?" Let their answer validate the quantitative drop-off timing.

**This Week's Actions**
1.  **Define & Instrument:** Implement tracking for `review_abandoned_mid_form` with a `step_number` property to pinpoint exactly where the 60% drop-off occurs.
2.  **Segment Baseline:** Query current data to split completion rates by "Pre-Quarter-End" vs. "Quarter-End" weeks to isolate the timing confounder.
3.  **Deploy Intercept:** Launch the single-question exit survey on the review form for the two largest enterprise clients.

*Note: All metrics must be compared against your own Day 1–10 baseline, not external industry benchmarks which are unavailable and likely irrelevant to your specific enterprise context. Ensure user identifiers used for cohort analysis are pseudonymized and routed to your privacy compliance protocol.*
