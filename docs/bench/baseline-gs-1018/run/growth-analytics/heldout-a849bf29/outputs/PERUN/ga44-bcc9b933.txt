### Evaluation Plan: MelodyStream Recommendation Engine

**1. North Star & Primary Metric**
*   **Primary Metric:** **Total Listening Time per Active User (Daily).**
    *   *Rationale:* This directly measures the hypothesis that history-based recommendations increase engagement. It replaces vanity metrics like "number of recommendations clicked" with actual value delivered (time spent listening).
    *   *Target:* Increase from the current baseline of 45 minutes.

**2. Input Metrics (Drivers)**
*   **Session Depth:** Average number of tracks played per session.
*   **Return Rate:** Percentage of users returning within 24 hours of a session.
*   **Skip Rate (Early):** Percentage of tracks skipped within the first 30 seconds (indicates immediate relevance failure).

**3. Guardrail Metrics (Critical Constraints)**
To address concerns about artist diversity and label relationships, these metrics must not degrade beyond a pre-defined threshold. If they do, the test stops regardless of primary metric gains.
*   **Artist Diversity Index:** The count of *unique artists* listened to per user per week.
    *   *Tripwire:* If this drops >5% compared to the control group, the engine is creating a "filter bubble."
*   **Long-Tail Share:** Percentage of total listening time attributed to artists outside the top 1,000 most popular on the platform.
    *   *Tripwire:* If this share decreases, smaller labels are being harmed.
*   **User Fatigue Signal:** Ratio of "thumbs down" or "skip all" actions per session.
    *   *Tripwire:* If this rises significantly, users are experiencing repetition fatigue.

**4. Experiment Design & Duration**
*   **Method:** A/B Test (50% Control/Current Engine, 50% Treatment/New Engine).
*   **Duration:** **21 Days (3 full weeks).**
    *   *Reasoning:* Music listening habits exhibit strong weekly seasonality (weekday commutes vs. weekend leisure). A minimum of three full weekly cycles is required to normalize variance and capture a stable signal.
*   **Stopping Rule:** Do not peek at results daily. Pre-commit to reading the data only once at Day 21. Peeking before the sample size is reached inflates the false-positive rate, making any "win" unreliable.

**5. Decision Framework**
*   **Ship:** Primary Metric increases statistically significantly AND all Guardrail Metrics remain within acceptable bounds (no significant drop in diversity or rise in fatigue).
*   **Iterate:** Primary Metric increases, but Guardrails trip. The engine drives engagement at the cost of ecosystem health. Requires tuning the algorithm to re-weight diversity before re-testing.
*   **Stop:** Primary Metric shows no improvement or declines. The hypothesis that history-based recs drive time spent is incorrect for this user base.

**This Week's Actions:**
1.  Instrument the **Artist Diversity Index** and **Long-Tail Share** events immediately; verify they are calculating correctly on historical data before the test starts.
2.  Calculate the minimum sample size required to detect a 2-minute increase in listening time with 80% power, and lock the test duration to ensure this N is met by Day 21.

*Note: All baseline comparisons must use MelodyStream's own historical data; no external industry benchmarks for "good" diversity exist. Ensure user identifiers used for cohort tracking are pseudonymized to comply with privacy standards.*
