### Recommended North Star Metric: **Net Value of On-Time Repaid Principal**

**Definition:** The total Naira value of loan principal repaid by users on or before their due date within a rolling 30-day window, net of any defaults from that same cohort's originating period.

**Why this over your alternatives:**
*   **Vs. Loan Volume:** Volume ignores risk. Your 12% default rate proves volume alone is a vanity metric that masks business erosion.
*   **Vs. Repayment Rate (%):** A percentage can be gamed by issuing tiny, low-risk loans while ignoring high-value sustainable lending. It lacks scale context.
*   **Vs. Net Recovered Funds:** "Recovered" often includes late payments and collections costs, lagging real user behavior. Focusing on **On-Time** repayment drives the *behavior* (user welfare + cash flow) that prevents the need for costly recovery efforts later.

This metric rises only when users succeed (welfare) and the business retains capital (sustainability). It naturally balances risk: if you tighten criteria too much, volume drops and the metric stalls; if you loosen too much, defaults rise and the metric shrinks.

### Tracking Plan & Event Taxonomy

Do not instrument everything. Track only events that answer specific questions about the shift from volume to quality.

**1. Core Events (Instrument Immediately)**
*   `loan_disbursed`: Properties: `loan_id`, `user_id` (pseudonymous), `amount`, `tenor_days`, `risk_tier`.
    *   *Question:* What is the mix of loans being issued?
*   `repayment_made`: Properties: `loan_id`, `user_id`, `amount_paid`, `days_since_disbursement`, `is_on_time` (boolean).
    *   *Question:* Are users paying early, on time, or late?
*   `loan_defaulted`: Properties: `loan_id`, `user_id`, `outstanding_principal`, `days_overdue`.
    *   *Question:* When does a loan become a loss?

**2. The Unknown Correlation (Your Specific Gap)**
You stated you do not know the correlation between early partial repayments and full completion. Do not guess.
*   **Action:** Create a derived cohort analysis query in your analytics tool.
*   **Segment:** Users who made a partial payment within the first 20% of the loan tenor.
*   **Metric:** Compare their `full_repayment_rate` vs. users who made no early partial payment.
*   **Status:** Until this query is run, any claim about early payments predicting success is **UNVERIFIED**. Do not set policy based on assumptions.

**3. Funnel Diagnosis (Bottom-Up)**
Do not pour money into acquisition yet. Read your funnel in reverse:
1.  **Retention/Repayment:** Are existing borrowers repaying on time? (If no, tightening criteria is mandatory before growth).
2.  **Activation:** Do approved users actually draw down the loan?
3.  **Acquisition:** Only optimize this once the repayment bucket is not leaking.

**4. Economic Volatility Guardrails**
In volatile markets, external shocks mimic product failure.
*   **Guardrail Metric:** `Default Rate by Disbursement Week`.
*   **Usage:** If defaults spike across *all* risk tiers simultaneously, it is likely macroeconomic (inflation/currency), not a model failure. If spikes occur only in specific tiers, it is a selection issue.
