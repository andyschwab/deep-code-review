### Essential Event Taxonomy for ReWear Italy Launch (Days 1–30)

To validate market fit and the hypothesis that "speed to first sale" drives retention, instrument only these five events. Each answers a specific decision question; do not track additional data until these baselines are established.

**1. `listing_created`**
*   **Question:** Are sellers successfully onboarding and supplying inventory?
*   **Properties:** `user_id` (pseudonymous), `category`, `price`, `timestamp`, `device_type`.
*   **Why:** This is your acquisition funnel top. Without supply, there are no sales. Compare volume against the Spanish baseline (pro-rated for market size), but prioritize the *completion rate* of the listing flow over raw volume.

**2. `offer_made`**
*   **Question:** Are Italian users utilizing negotiation features differently than Spanish users?
*   **Properties:** `user_id` (buyer), `listing_id`, `offer_amount`, `original_price`, `timestamp`.
*   **Why:** This directly addresses your unknown variable. Track the ratio of `offer_made` to `listing_viewed` (if available) or `listing_created`. A significant deviation from Spanish behavior here signals a cultural difference in buying habits that may require product adjustment.

**3. `first_sale_completed`**
*   **Question:** What is the actual "speed to first sale," and does it correlate with retention?
*   **Properties:** `seller_id`, `listing_id`, `time_to_sale_hours` (calculated), `sale_price`, `was_negotiated` (boolean).
*   **Why:** This validates your core hypothesis. Measure the median time from `listing_created` to `first_sale_completed` for cohorts. If this time exceeds your Spanish benchmark, retention will likely fail regardless of acquisition spend.

**4. `seller_return_visit`**
*   **Question:** Are sellers returning after their first sale (or first week)?
*   **Properties:** `user_id`, `days_since_first_action`, `action_type` (listing, browsing, editing).
*   **Why:** This is your leading indicator of retention. Do not wait for monthly churn data. Track the percentage of sellers who log in and perform *any* action within 7 days of their first sale. If "speed to sale" is high but `seller_return_visit` is low, the hypothesis is incomplete.

**5. `listing_expired_unsold`**
*   **Question:** Where is the liquidity leak?
*   **Properties:** `listing_id`, `days_live`, `views_received`, `offers_received`.
*   **Why:** Identifies inventory that failed to convert. High views but no offers suggests pricing or trust issues; low views suggests discovery algorithm failures in the new market.

**Implementation Notes:**
*   **Identifier Seam:** Ensure `user_id` is stable across sessions to track cohorts but remains pseudonymous. Route any raw PII (emails, addresses) strictly to your privacy compliance system, not this analytics stream.
*   **Baseline Discipline:** Do not ask "What is a good conversion rate?" Compare Italy's Week 1 metrics only against Spain's Week 1 metrics at launch. Any other benchmark is invented noise.

**This Week's Step:** Define the exact schema for `offer_made` and `first_sale_completed` by Friday. Verify that the data pipeline can calculate `time_to_sale_hours` in real-time before September 1st. If these two events are not readable by Day 3, pause marketing spend until fixed.
