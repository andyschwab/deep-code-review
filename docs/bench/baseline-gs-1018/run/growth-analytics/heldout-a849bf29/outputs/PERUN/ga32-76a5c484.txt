### Proposed Funnel Structure: The "Qualified Intent" Model

Do not optimize for "Demo Request" volume. With a 2.6% close rate (12/450), this is a vanity metric that rewards low-quality traffic. Instead, restructure your funnel around **Lead Quality Signals** that correlate with your 18-month historical close data.

**1. Redefine the North Star**
Shift from "Demo Requests" to **"Sales Qualified Opportunities (SQO) Created."** An SQO is only counted when a lead meets both a behavioral threshold (e.g., whitepaper download + webinar attendance) and a firmographic threshold (enterprise tier). This metric rises only when real pipeline value increases.

**2. The Intermediate "Quality" Funnel**
Map these stages to predict the 6-month lag:
*   **Acquisition:** Unique Enterprise Visitors.
*   **Activation (The "Aha" Moment):** Consumption of High-Intent Content (Whitepaper Download OR Webinar Registration). *Hypothesis: This step correlates higher with closed-won than demo requests alone.*
*   **Conversion Request:** Demo Request *from* an Activated user.
*   **Validation:** Sales Accepted Lead (SAL) – Sales team confirms fit within 48 hours.
*   **Outcome:** SQO Created (The leading indicator of revenue).

**3. Solving the Lag Time for Weekly Experiments**
You cannot wait six months to read A/B test results. Use **Proxy Metrics** validated against your historical CRM data:
*   **Retrospective Cohort Analysis:** Immediately query your 18-month CRM data. Correlate "Closed-Won" deals with early behaviors. Did 80% of winners download the whitepaper? Did 90% attend a webinar?
*   **The Proxy Rule:** If historical data shows "Whitepaper Download" has a 5x higher conversion rate to "Closed-Won" than "Demo Request" alone, make "Whitepaper Download Rate" your primary experiment metric.
*   **Weekly Readout:** Run landing page tests to maximize the *rate* of high-intent content consumption, not total demo clicks. A win is defined as a statistically significant lift in qualified content consumption, which your historical data confirms leads to revenue.

**4. Immediate Execution Plan (For Friday's Budget Meeting)**
*   **Action 1 (Data Validation):** Run a SQL query on the 18-month CRM export today. Calculate the conversion rate to "Closed-Won" for three cohorts: (A) Demo Only, (B) Demo + Whitepaper, (C) Demo + Webinar. Present this correlation matrix to prove which intermediate step predicts revenue.
*   **Action 2 (Instrumentation):** Update your analytics taxonomy. Tag events as `content_download_whitepaper` and `webinar_register` with a `user_id` that persists to the CRM opportunity ID. Stop tracking "Demo Request" as a primary success metric in dashboards; demote it to a secondary guardrail.
*   **Action 3 (Experiment Design):** Launch one A/B test on the landing page this week.
    *   *Control:* Current "Request Demo" CTA.
    *   *Variant:* Gated "Download Technical Whitepaper" CTA above the fold.
    *   *Primary Metric:* % of Enterprise Visitors downloading the whitepaper.
    *   *Guardrail:* Total Demo Request volume (ensure it doesn't collapse >20%).
    *   *Decision Rule:* If the variant lifts whitepaper downloads by >15% without crashing demo volume, ship it. Rely on the historical correlation to justify that this drives Q4 revenue.

**Note:** Do not fabricate a "good" conversion rate; use your own historical baseline as the only benchmark. Ensure the `user_id` passed to analytics is pseudonymous to comply with privacy standards while allowing cross-session tracking.
