### Immediate Data Plan for StreamlineHR (3-Week Execution)

**1. Define Your Single North Star Metric**
Stop tracking "active companies" as a vanity cumulative count. Your North Star must be **Weekly Active Companies completing the Core Action**. For an HR platform, this is likely "Payroll Run Completed," "Employee Onboarded," or "Compliance Report Generated." If this number does not rise, your growth is illusory. Measure only this value delivery metric and the 2-3 input metrics that directly move it.

**2. The "Must-Have" Event Taxonomy (Bottom-Up)**
Ignore acquisition metrics for now; pouring ads into a leaky bucket wastes cash. Instrument only these four events to diagnose retention and activation. Do not instrument "just in case."

*   **`core_action_completed`**: The specific moment a company gets value (e.g., `payroll_processed`).
    *   *Properties:* `company_id`, `action_type`, `timestamp`.
    *   *Question Answered:* Are users actually getting value week-over-week?
*   **`activation_milestone_reached`**: The first time a user hits the "Aha!" moment (e.g., `first_employee_added`).
    *   *Properties:* `company_id`, `time_since_signup`.
    *   *Question Answered:* Does onboarding lead to the core action?
*   **`session_start`**: Replaces raw logins.
    *   *Properties:* `company_id` (pseudonymous), `device_type`.
    *   *Question Answered:* Are they returning? (Retention).
*   **`churn_signal`**: A negative indicator, such as `account_downgrade` or `support_ticket_critical`.
    *   *Properties:* `company_id`, `reason_code`.
    *   *Question Answered:* Who is at risk before they leave?

**3. Tooling Strategy: Buy, Don't Build**
Do not build custom infrastructure. Your bandwidth is too scarce.
*   **Collection:** Use a client-side SDK (e.g., PostHog, Mixpanel, or Amplitude) with a server-side library for critical financial events (`core_action_completed`) to ensure data integrity. This avoids the two-week custom build cycle.
*   **Identity Seam:** Generate a stable, pseudonymous `company_id` at signup. Pass this ID with every event. Do not use raw emails or PII in analytics tools; route PII handling to your privacy compliance protocol. This allows you to track cohorts without violating privacy norms or rebuilding ops observability stacks.
*   **Governance:** Create a single `INDEX.md` file listing these four events, their exact property names, and the business question each answers. If an event isn't in this doc, it doesn't get built.

**4. Fundraising Narrative Data**
For your Series A pitch, investors need to see **Retention Cohorts**, not just total growth.
*   Group companies by their signup month.
*   Plot the % of each group that performed `core_action_completed` in Week 1, Week 2, and Week 4.
*   If the curve flattens, you have product-market fit. If it drops to zero, you have a leakage problem regardless of your 5,000 company headline number.

3.  **Verify One Query:** Run one query next week: "What % of companies who signed up in January performed `core_action_completed` in their second week?" If you cannot answer this, the instrumentation is incomplete.
