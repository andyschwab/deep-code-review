### Measurement Framework for DesignMatch

**1. The North Star: Weekly Completed Projects**
Reject "cumulative signups" (500 designers, 200 clients) and "total contract value" as your North Star. These are vanity metrics that rise even if the marketplace is broken. Your North Star must be **Weekly Completed Projects**. This metric only increases when real value is exchanged on both sides. If this number is flat while signups grow, you have a liquidity or trust leak, not a growth success.

**2. Diagnose Bottom-Up (Retention & Activation First)**
Do not pour money into acquiring more designers or clients until you fix the conversion leak. With only 40 completed projects from 700 signups, your funnel is leaking at the top. Diagnose in this order:
*   **Liquidity Check:** Time from "Job Posted" to "First Proposal Received." If this lag exceeds 24 hours, clients lose trust and leave. This is your primary liquidity metric.
*   **Activation Check:** Proposal-to-Hire Rate. Are designers sending proposals that clients ignore? If proposals are sent but not accepted, the issue is pricing mismatch or quality, not liquidity.
*   **Retention Check:** Do clients who complete one project return to post a second within 30 days? If not, the service experience failed.

**3. Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only What Answers a Question)**
Do not track "everything." Instrument these four specific events to answer your core questions:
*   `job_posted`: (Properties: budget_range, category, client_id). *Question: Is the supply of jobs healthy?*
*   `proposal_sent`: (Properties: job_id, designer_id, bid_amount). *Question: Are designers engaging?*
*   `proposal_viewed`: (Properties: job_id, client_id). *Question: Are clients ignoring bids or just not seeing them?*
*   `contract_completed`: (Properties: job_id, final_value, duration_days). *Question: Is value actually delivered?*

**4. The Identifier Seam**
To track a user from "signup" to "completed project," you need a stable, pseudonymous user ID attached to all events. Do not use raw emails or PII in your analytics tool. Route any PII handling and consent logic to your privacy compliance protocol; keep the analytics ID strictly for cohort tracking.

**5. Immediate Actions (This Week)**
*   **Query the Lag:** Run a query on `job_posted` vs. `proposal_sent` to calculate the median time-to-first-bid. If >24 hours, your immediate fix is notifying designers of new jobs, not lowering prices.
*   **Measure the Drop-off:** Calculate the ratio of `proposal_sent` to `contract_completed`. If high volume/low conversion, the issue is pricing/quality (trust). If low volume, the issue is liquidity.
*   **Define the Funding Metric:** For your dashboard, show the trend of **Weekly Completed Projects** alongside **Median Time-to-First-Bid**. Investors need to see that you understand the *mechanism* of the marketplace, not just the headcount.

**Verdict on North Star:** Prioritize **Completed Projects** over message response rates. Responses are a means to an end; completed contracts are the value.

*Note: Do not invent industry benchmarks for "good" conversion rates; compare your current 5.7% completion rate (40/700) only against your own future weekly trends. Any metric without the events above defined is UNVERIFIED.*
