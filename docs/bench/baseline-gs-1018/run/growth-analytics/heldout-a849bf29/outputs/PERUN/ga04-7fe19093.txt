For DataBridge's five-client reality, discard aggregate trends and statistical significance testing; they are mathematically invalid and misleading at this scale. Your North Star is not "logins" or "total seats," but **Admin-Led Core Value Realization**: the frequency with which an administrator successfully executes the specific workflow that justifies the $120k contract (e.g., "successful shipment rule deployment" or "inventory reconciliation completed").

**1. The Measurement Framework: Per-Account Baselines, Not Aggregates**
Do not average data across clients. A 500-seat client logging in 2,000 times and a 40-seat client logging in 100 times cannot be compared on raw volume. Instead, instrument **relative deviation from each client's own baseline**.
*   **Define the "Core Action" per client:** During onboarding, identify the one workflow that delivers value for *that specific retailer*. Instrument only that event.
*   **The Metric:** Measure the **Weekly Active Admins (WAA) performing the Core Action** divided by **Total Licensed Admins** for that specific account. This normalizes the 500-seat vs. 40-seat disparity.
*   **The Signal:** A drop below 80% of that specific account's 4-week moving average is a churn risk, regardless of the absolute number.

**2. Qualitative Over Quantitative for Early Signals**
With N=5, qualitative depth outweighs quantitative breadth. Your dashboard should not be a graph; it should be a **traffic light system based on leading indicators**:
*   **Green:** Core action frequency matches or exceeds the client's historical baseline.
*   **Yellow:** Core action frequency dips >20% below baseline for two consecutive weeks OR a key stakeholder (identified in onboarding) has not logged in for 14 days.
*   **Red:** Zero core actions for 7 days OR explicit feedback indicating workflow blockage.

**3. Event Taxonomy: Answer Named Questions Only**
Do not "track everything." Instrument only events that answer: "Did the admin solve a logistics problem this week?"
*   *Event:* `core_workflow_completed` (Properties: `client_id`, `workflow_type`, `duration`).
*   *Ignore:* Generic pageviews, login counts, or feature clicks that do not result in a completed business outcome. These are vanity metrics in a high-value B2B context.

**4. The Health Score Logic**
Construct the health score as a weighted binary check, not a continuous algorithm:
*   **50% Weight:** Core Action Retention (Is the % of active admins holding steady against their own baseline?).
*   **30% Weight:** Stakeholder Engagement (Have the identified decision-makers logged in within 30 days?).
*   **20% Weight:** Support Sentiment (Qualitative tag from CS notes: "Blocked" vs. "Expanding").

*Note: Do not attempt A/B testing or significance calculations; with five clients, every data point is a census, not a sample. Route all PII and user identifiers to your privacy compliance protocol before ingestion.*
