**Recommendation: Track "Day-1 Unboxing Completion Rate" as your North Star input.**

Do not track time-to-first-open or raw support volume yet; both are vanity or noise without context. Time-to-open rises naturally with eco-materials even if users are happy, and support tickets lag behind actual churn. Instead, define a binary event: **Unboxing Success** (user opens box and accesses contents within 24 hours of delivery without abandoning the process).

**Why this metric?**
Your cancellation rate jump (4% → 9%) is a retention leak. If the packaging is the culprit, the failure happens immediately at the "Activation" step of the funnel. A user who cannot open the box never experiences the product value, guaranteeing churn. This metric isolates the packaging friction from price sensitivity (which usually causes churn after renewal billing) or product quality issues (which cause churn after usage).
