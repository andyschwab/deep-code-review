### Value Measurement Framework for DataRich

**North Star Metric: Verified Active Enrichments (VAE)**
Reject "Total API Calls" as a vanity metric; it measures traffic, not value. Adopt **Verified Active Enrichments** as your North Star. A VAE is an API call that returns a successful firmographic match *and* is associated with a client user session or downstream workflow trigger within a defined freshness window. This shifts the focus from "how much data we sent" to "how much usable data the client consumed."

**Input Metrics (The Drivers)**
Track these three inputs to move the North Star:
1.  **Freshness-Adjusted Match Rate:** Calculate match rate separately for data <30 days old vs. >30 days old. This directly addresses your latency concern without needing client CRM access.
2.  **Schema Utilization Depth:** Measure the percentage of available firmographic fields actually returned and consumed per call. High call volume with low field diversity suggests shallow integration.
3.  **Repeat Query Ratio:** The percentage of unique records (e.g., specific company domains) queried more than once within 30 days. High repetition indicates the data is being actively monitored, not just batch-loaded once.

**Addressing the Latency Variable**
Do not average latency across all clients. Instead, instrument a **Freshness Tag** on every response.
*   **Action:** Tag every successful enrichment with `data_age_days`.
*   **Analysis:** Correlate `data_age_days` with the **Repeat Query Ratio**. If clients stop re-querying records once `data_age_days` exceeds 30, you have internal proof of their freshness threshold without seeing their CRM.
*   **Pricing Tiering:** Use this data to define "Real-Time" vs. "Standard" tiers. If a client's usage drops off after 30 days, they are self-segmenting into a lower-value tier.

**Proxy for Downstream Revenue (Without CRM Access)**
Since you cannot see client revenue, use **Integration Depth** as the proxy.
*   **The Logic:** Clients only build complex, multi-field integrations (e.g., triggering sales alerts based on employee count *and* funding round) if the data drives revenue. Simple one-off lookups rarely justify enterprise pricing.
*   **The Metric:** Track the **Complexity Score** per account (average number of distinct firmographic fields requested per session). A rising Complexity Score correlates with higher stickiness and perceived value.

**Renewal Negotiation Playbook (Q3)**
Equip your teams with these three evidence points instead of raw volume:
1.  **Utilization Rate:** "You consumed 1M calls, but 40% were for data >30 days old which your usage patterns suggest you don't action. Our new plan optimizes for fresh matches."
2.  **Depth of Adoption:** "Your team utilizes 12 distinct data fields per query, indicating deep workflow integration, compared to the average of 4."
3.  **Freshness Sensitivity:** "Your repeat queries drop 80% when data age exceeds 25 days. Our premium tier guarantees <24h latency to capture that lost value."

**Immediate Next Steps**
1.  **Instrument `data_age_days` and `fields_returned_count`** on every API response log by next sprint.
2.  **Define the "Active User" seed:** Identify the top 10% of clients by retention and analyze their `Repeat Query Ratio` to set your baseline for "healthy" behavior.
3.  **Stop reporting total call volume** in executive dashboards; replace it with VAE to prevent misaligned incentives.

*Note: This framework relies entirely on your server-side logs; no client-side integration is required. Ensure all user identifiers used for cohorting are pseudonymized to maintain B2B privacy compliance.*
