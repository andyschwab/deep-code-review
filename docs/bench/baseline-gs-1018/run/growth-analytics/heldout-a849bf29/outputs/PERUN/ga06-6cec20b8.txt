**The Call: Stop, Then Redesign**
1.  **Stop the current cohort analysis.** Do not try to squeeze significance from 140 events.
2.  **Redefine the North Star for this experiment.** Move away from "total referrals" (a vanity metric if they don't ride) to "referees completing 3 rides within 7 days" (real value).
3.  **Redesign the instrument.**
    *   **Question:** "Does a €5 credit drive *retained* new riders among power users?"
    *   **Event Taxonomy:** Instrument `referral_sent` (by user segment), `referral_claimed`, and `referee_core_action_completed` (3 rides).
