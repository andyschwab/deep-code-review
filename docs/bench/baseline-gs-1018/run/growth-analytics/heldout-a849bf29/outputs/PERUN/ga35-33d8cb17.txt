**North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Households completing a "Seamless Routine" (defined as a multi-device automation triggered successfully via a local mobile payment or lifestyle app integration).

**Rationale:** Daily active device interactions are vanity metrics; a user turning a light on once does not prove value. Weekly routine automations measure the core promise of a "smart home": set-and-forget convenience. Given your hypothesis that Korean users prioritize integration over raw energy stats, the North Star must verify that the ecosystem is actually *working together* within the local mobile context, not just that devices are powering on.

**Input Metrics (The Levers):**
1.  **Integration Activation Rate:** % of new units linked to a local mobile payment/lifestyle platform within 24 hours of unboxing.
2.  **Routine Completion Success Rate:** % of triggered automations that execute all steps without error (detects hardware/latency issues).
3.  **Day-7 Retention of Core Action:** % of users who trigger at least one custom routine in their second week.

**Event Taxonomy (Instrument Only These):**
*   `routine_created` (Properties: `trigger_source`, `device_count`, `uses_local_payment_hook`) → *Answers: Are users building value?*
*   `routine_executed_success` (Properties: `routine_id`, `latency_ms`) → *Answers: Is the system reliable?*
*   `routine_executed_fail` (Properties: `failure_step`, `error_code`) → *Answers: Where is the friction?*
*   `mobile_platform_linked` (Properties: `platform_name`, `time_to_link`) → *Answers: Is the integration hypothesis true?*

**Funnel Diagnosis (Bottom-Up):**
Do not look at acquisition (10,000 units shipped) yet. First, check **Retention**: Are users running routines in Week 2? If no, the product isn't delivering value regardless of sales. Second, check **Activation**: Did they link a mobile platform and create a routine? If no, the onboarding or integration is broken. Only then look at **Acquisition**. Pouring marketing into a leaky retention bucket is waste.

**Stage Awareness:**
You are in the **MVP/Growth** transition. Do not instrument "everything." Ignore granular energy data for now unless it blocks a routine. Focus entirely on the *integration* and *automation* flow. If the data shows low linkage to mobile platforms, your assumption is wrong, and you pivot; if high, you double down.

**This Week's Steps:**
1.  **Define the Schema:** Finalize the JSON payload for `routine_executed_success` and `mobile_platform_linked` with the engineering lead to ensure the local payment hook ID is captured pseudonymously (route PII handling to privacy compliance).
2.  **Build the Cohort Query:** Create a dashboard view filtering for "Households active in Week 1" and tracking their "Week 2 Routine Execution" rate.
3.  **Set the Tripwire:** If `Integration Activation Rate` is <40% by Day 7 post-launch, halt paid acquisition campaigns and trigger a user interview sprint to diagnose the linkage friction.

*Note: No benchmark for "good" retention is provided as none exists for your specific local context; compare only against your own Week 1 baseline. Ensure user identifiers are stable across sessions but pseudonymized before storage.*
