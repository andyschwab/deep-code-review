**Recommendation: North Star Metric for MindMate**

**Selected North Star:** **Weekly Active Users completing a "Meaningful Support Cycle."**

Define a "Meaningful Support Cycle" as a session where a user engages with the bot for a minimum duration (e.g., >3 minutes) and explicitly acknowledges a coping strategy or resource provided (e.g., clicking "Try this exercise" or saving a resource link), rather than just exchanging pleasantries.

**Rationale:**
This metric aligns with your mission by measuring *value delivery* (students actually using tools) rather than *vanity* (total logins) or *unverifiable proxies* (sentiment). It respects privacy by relying on behavioral signals within the chat session, not clinical outcomes or external follow-ups.

**Trade-off Analysis: Engagement Volume vs. Self-Reported Mood**

1.  **Engagement Volume (Total Sessions/Messages):**
    *   *Pros:* Easy to instrument; indicates reach.
    *   *Cons:* High risk of vanity. A student spiraling in crisis may generate high volume without improvement. Conversely, a student finding a quick answer may generate low volume but high value. Rising volume with flat "Meaningful Cycles" indicates a retention leak—users are talking but not getting helped.
2.  **Self-Reported Mood Scores:**
    *   *Pros:* Directly attempts to measure well-being.
    *   *Cons:* High susceptibility to the "faking positivity" bias you identified. Users may rate mood highly just to exit the conversation. Without clinical validation, these numbers are `UNVERIFIED` proxies that can mislead the board into false confidence.

**Addressing "Crisis Escalations"**
Do **not** use crisis escalations as a North Star metric, nor as a simple "good/bad" binary.
*   **Interpretation:** A rise in escalations is ambiguous without context. It could mean more students are in crisis (external factor) OR that your detection logic is working better (success).
*   **Action:** Treat this as a **Guardrail Metric**, not a success metric.
    *   *Goal:* Maintain a stable or increasing rate of *successful handoffs* (user connected to human help) while monitoring the *false negative* rate (if measurable via user feedback).
    *   *Board Message:* "An increase in escalations indicates we are successfully identifying at-risk students who need human intervention, which is a core function of the safety net. We measure success here by the *speed and completion* of the handoff, not by suppressing the number of alerts."

**Implementation Plan for April 10th Meeting**

1.  **Instrument the "Meaningful Support Cycle":** Tag events where users interact with specific coping modules or resource links. This answers the question: "Are students actively using the support we offer?"
2.  **Establish Baseline Trends:** Compare the current rate of these cycles against the 2,000-user baseline. Do not invent industry benchmarks for "good" mental health app retention; use your own prior month's data.
3.  **Privacy Seam:** Ensure the identifier used to track these cycles across sessions is pseudonymous. Route any PII or consent logic to your privacy compliance protocols; do not store raw conversation text linked to student IDs for this metric.
