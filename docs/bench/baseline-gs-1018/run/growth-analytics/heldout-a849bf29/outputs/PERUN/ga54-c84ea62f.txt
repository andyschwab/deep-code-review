**Growth Analytics Framework for FinFlow (Pre-Launch / MVP Stage)**

**1. North Star Metric: "Weekly Funded Invoice Value"**
Reject "Time to Funding" as your North Star. While speed matters, it is an output, not the core value. Your North Star must be **Weekly Funded Invoice Value** (total USD successfully financed per week). This only rises when a customer receives real value (cash flow). "Time to Funding" becomes a critical *input metric* to optimize, but measuring it alone creates vanity if no one is actually funded.

**2. The Funnel: Read Bottom-Up (Retention First)**
Do not visualize a linear top-down funnel yet. With no live data, your priority is validating that users who *do* get funded return.
*   **Primary Focus:** Measure **Retention of the Core Action** (second invoice submitted within 30 days). If manufacturers do not return, speed is irrelevant.
*   **The Leak Diagnosis:** Before tracking acquisition, instrument the drop-off between "Application Started" and "Documents Uploaded." This is your highest probable friction point in Southeast Asia due to digital record gaps.

**3. Event Taxonomy: Answer Named Questions Only**
Do not "track everything." Instrument only these three events to answer specific questions:
*   `application_started` → *Question:* Do users hesitate before uploading? (Distinguishes trust issues from technical barriers).
*   `document_verification_failed` (with property `failure_reason`: e.g., "format_error", "bank_timeout", "missing_record") → *Question:* Is the drop-off technical (bank API) or user-side (lack of records)?
*   `funding_disbursed` → *Question:* Did the user actually receive value?

**Handling Variance & Technical Failures:**
You cannot "account for" banking variance in the metric itself; you must **segment** by it. Tag every transaction with `bank_partner_id` and `country`. Your dashboard must show "Time to Funding" as a distribution (median vs. 95th percentile) per bank, not a single average. An average of 2 days hides the 5-day outliers that kill trust.
*   **Distinguishing Failure Types:** If the event `document_verification_failed` fires with `failure_reason: bank_timeout`, it is technical. If the user session ends after `application_started` without triggering a verification attempt, it is likely hesitation or lack of records. You currently lack data to split "lack of records" vs. "trust"; the instrumented `failure_reason` on the first attempt will reveal this post-launch. Do not estimate these numbers now; they are `UNVERIFIED`.

**4. Repeat Borrowing Rates**
**No.** Do not track repeat borrowing rates as a *core success metric* this early. In the `mvp` stage, your sample size for "repeat" behavior will be statistically insignificant and noisy. Focus entirely on **Activation** (first successful funding) and **Retention** (returning to submit a second invoice). Repeat rates become a primary metric only in the `growth` stage.

**5. Experiment Rigor & Investor Readiness**
For your Series A review:
*   **Do not fabricate benchmarks.** Do not claim "industry standard conversion is X%." State clearly: "Baseline established at launch; success defined as a 20% week-over-week increase in Funded Invoice Value from our own Day 1 baseline."
*   **Visual Recommendation:** Present a **Cohort Heatmap** (Week of First Application vs. Week of Second Application) rather than a static funnel. This proves to investors you are watching retention, not just vanity signups.
