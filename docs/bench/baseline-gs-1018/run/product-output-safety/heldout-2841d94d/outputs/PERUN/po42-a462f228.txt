### Risk Assessment: Virtual Lawyer Assistant Output Safety

**Critical Finding: High-Stakes Automation Without Human Gate**
Your system provides immediate legal guidance for court or negotiations. This constitutes a high-stakes, irreversible action where incorrect output can cause direct financial loss, eviction, or legal prejudice. **Current Status: Unsafe to Ship.** The "tight deadline" and "small team" do not lower the risk; they increase the likelihood of catastrophic failure. You must implement a **human-in-the-loop gate** before any advice reaches a user. Fully automated legal advice in this context is the primary failure mode this assessment prevents.

**Harm Inventory & Specific Gaps**
Based on your description, the following harms are imminent:

1.  **Hallucination Surfaced as Fact:** With limited localized training data, the model will inevitably fabricate statutes, case precedents, or procedural steps. Users treating these fabrications as fact in court face immediate dismissal of their cases or sanctions.
2.  **Bias / Unfair Outcomes:** Housing laws vary wildly by municipality. Without a comprehensive dataset, the tool will likely default to major city codes, systematically disadvantaging tenants in smaller jurisdictions or specific zoning overlays.
3.  **Over-Reliance:** The "immediate" nature of the tool invites automation bias. Users may skip consulting a real lawyer because the AI sounds confident, even when the advice is wrong.
4.  **Missing AI Disclosure:** Users must know this is a machine-generated draft, not a licensed attorney's opinion. Absence of this disclosure is deceptive.

**Required Measurement Plan (Do Not Fabricate Metrics)**
You cannot claim a "99% accuracy" rate or a "0% hallucination" rate. These metrics are **UNVERIFIED** without the data you lack. Instead, execute the following:

*   **Adversarial Red-Teaming:** Create a test set of 50 ambiguous tenant scenarios (e.g., "landlord entered without notice but I didn't say no") known to have different outcomes in different jurisdictions.
    *   *Positive Control:* Inputs where the law is clear and universal.
    *   *Negative Control:* Inputs where the law is highly local or non-existent.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If the model provides specific statutory citations for the Negative Control set without flagging uncertainty, **block launch**.
*   **Uncertainty Rendering:** The system must not output bare confidence percentages (e.g., "87% sure"). Instead, implement defined coarse tiers:
    *   *Confirmed:* Verified against known statute.
    *   *Corroborated:* Matches multiple sources.
    *   *Unverified/General Principle:* Model is guessing based on general logic, not local law.
    *   *Action:* Any output marked "Unverified" must trigger a mandatory disclaimer and a referral to human aid, not specific legal steps.

**Transparency Artifact Requirement**
You must produce a **Model Card** before shipping. This document must explicitly state:
*   **Intended Use:** Drafting assistance only, not legal representation.
*   **Limitations:** "Training data lacks comprehensive coverage of [Specific Regions]; advice for these areas is UNVERIFIED."
*   **Owner:** Name the specific team member responsible for reviewing flagged outputs.

**Immediate Next Steps**
1.  **Disable Full Automation:** Change the workflow so the AI generates a *draft* for internal human review before it is shown to any tenant.
2.  **Route Legal Duty:** Consult counsel immediately regarding whether this tool constitutes the unauthorized practice of law in your target jurisdictions. This is a legal determination, not a technical one.
3.  **Label Unknowns:** In your risk document, mark all accuracy claims for under-represented regions as `UNVERIFIED`. Do not invent a safety score to satisfy the grant deadline.

**Note:** Shipping without a human gate on irreversible legal advice violates the core safety floor for high-stakes automation.
**Note:** The grant deadline does not override the requirement to prevent user harm; a delayed launch is safer than a liable one.
