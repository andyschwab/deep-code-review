### Manual Review Protocol for High-Risk Background Check Matches

**Objective:** Prevent wrongful housing rejections caused by false-positive criminal record matches due to name similarities before general launch.

**1. Automated Flagging Criteria (The "Stop" List)**
The system must automatically halt any report generation and route it to manual review if *any* of the following conditions are met:
*   **Name Match Only:** The algorithm matches based solely on first and last name without a matching Date of Birth (DOB) or partial SSN.
*   **Geographic Mismatch:** The jurisdiction of the criminal record has no logical connection to the applicant's known residence history (e.g., record in State A, applicant has never lived in or near State A).
*   **Common Name Trigger:** The applicant's name appears on a pre-defined "High-Frequency Name" list (e.g., "John Smith," "Maria Garcia"), requiring mandatory human verification regardless of other data points.
*   **Severity Threshold:** The matched record involves violent felonies or sexual offenses, triggering a "Highest Stakes" review tier.

**2. The Human-in-the-Loop Workflow**
No automated rejection notice may be sent for a flagged case. A trained human reviewer must execute the following steps:
*   **Step A: Triangulation:** The reviewer must attempt to correlate at least two additional data points beyond the name (e.g., middle initial, exact DOB, physical description) using the legacy database export and any available applicant-provided data.
*   **Step B: Uncertainty Classification:** If correlation fails, the reviewer must assign a confidence tier to the match using defined language, not percentages:
    *   *Confirmed:* Multiple unique identifiers match.
    *   *Unverified:* Name matches, but key identifiers (DOB/Location) conflict or are missing.
    *   *Excluded:* Identifiers definitively prove non-match.
*   **Step C: Decision Gate:**
    *   If *Unverified*: The system must generate a "Notice of Potential Match" to the landlord, explicitly stating the record is **unconfirmed** and requires further applicant dispute before action. **Do not** generate a standard "Criminal Record Found" report.
    *   If *Excluded*: Suppress the record entirely from the report.

**3. Disclosure and Applicant Rights**
*   **AI/Algorithmic Disclosure:** All reports containing matched records must include a clear statement: "This report includes potential matches generated by an automated system. Matches marked 'Unverified' have not been confirmed by a human reviewer as belonging to this specific applicant."
*   **Dispute Mechanism:** Every report with a flagged match must include a prominent, easy-to-access link or phone number for the applicant to dispute the finding immediately, prior to the landlord making a final decision.

**4. Documentation and Ownership**
*   **Review Log:** Every manually reviewed case must be logged with the reviewer's ID, the timestamp, the specific data points checked, and the final classification (*Confirmed/Unverified/Excluded*).
*   **Owner Assignment:** A specific Product Owner is named as responsible for the accuracy of this workflow. This owner must re-review the protocol if the legacy database structure changes or if the volume of flags exceeds 15% of total reports.

**5. Launch Constraint**
The system is prohibited from auto-sending any report containing a "High-Stakes" (violent/sexual) charge without the completion of Step 2. Full automation of these specific outputs is blocked until further notice.

***

**Note:** This protocol treats unconfirmed name matches as "Unverified" rather than fabricating a false sense of accuracy; legal counsel must verify if the "Notice of Potential Match" satisfies specific FCRA adverse action requirements.
**Note:** The "High-Frequency Name" list must be generated and updated by the data team before beta testing concludes.
