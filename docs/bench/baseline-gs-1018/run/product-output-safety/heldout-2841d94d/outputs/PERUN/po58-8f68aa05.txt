### Product Output Safety Review: WorkPulse Sentiment Monitor

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
Before refining detection logic, we must explicitly name the harms this feature could cause to employees:
*   **Bias/Unfair Outcomes:** The system systematically disadvantages grieving employees by misclassifying normal grief responses as "disengagement," triggering inappropriate performance management.
*   **Hallucination as Fact:** The tool presents a probabilistic sentiment score as a definitive diagnosis of "underperformance" without evidentiary support.
*   **Over-reliance/Automation Bias:** Managers may trust the "disengaged" flag over their own contextual knowledge of an employee's recent life events.
*   **Missing AI Disclosure:** Employees and managers may not realize the flag is an automated inference rather than a verified HR assessment.
*   **Unsafe High-Stakes Automation:** If the system automatically initiates performance improvement plans (PIPs) or alerts senior leadership without human verification, it executes an irreversible, costly action.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Do not assert the system is "fair." Instead, execute these specific evaluations:
*   **Adversarial Probe Set (Bereavement Scenario):** Construct a test dataset containing communication patterns typical of grief (e.g., reduced verbosity, delayed responses, specific lexical shifts) distinct from disengagement (e.g., active avoidance, negative sentiment toward work tasks).
    *   *Positive Control:* Known grieving employees (simulated) who are otherwise engaged.
    *   *Negative Control:* Known disengaged employees with no recent life events.
    *   *Metric:* False Positive Rate (FPR) specifically for the "grief" subgroup.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If FPR for the grief subgroup exceeds 0%, the feature cannot launch with automated flagging.
*   **Injection & Leakage Check:** Test if revealing a "death in the family" in a channel context suppresses the disengagement flag appropriately, ensuring the model uses context rather than isolated signals.
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a **Model Card** detailing:
    *   *Intended Use:* Supporting manager awareness, *not* making performance decisions.
    *   *Limitations:* Explicitly state the model cannot distinguish grief from disengagement with 100% accuracy.
    *   *Subgroup Performance:* Report the measured FPR for grief scenarios as `UNVERIFIED` until the above evals run. Do not invent a "99% accuracy" claim.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (Mandatory):** The system must **never** automatically trigger a performance review or notify senior leadership. The output must be a private, low-fidelity signal to the direct manager only, requiring explicit human confirmation and context entry before any formal HR process begins.
*   **Uncertainty Rendering:** Do not show a raw confidence score (e.g., "87% disengaged"). Display a coarse, defined tier such as **"Signal Detected – Context Required"** with a clear disclaimer: *"This signal may reflect personal circumstances including bereavement. Do not act without direct conversation."*
*   **Disclosure:** Ensure managers are informed that this is an AI-generated hypothesis, not a factual assessment of performance.

**4. Governance & Legal Routing**
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific product owner responsible for reviewing false positives monthly.
*   **Legal Determination:** Automated employment decisions involving consequential effects (like PIPs) may trigger regulations under the **EU AI Act** or local labor laws. **Route immediately to counsel** to determine if this tool constitutes a "high-risk" AI system requiring mandatory impact assessments. Do not assume compliance based on internal testing.

**Definition of Done:**
The feature ships only when the bereavement probe set is run, the false positive rate is documented (even if non-zero), the automatic triggering of HR actions is disabled, and a Model Card warning of grief-misclassification risks is published.

***

*Note: This framework identifies residual risk; it does not certify the system as safe or unbiased. The final ship decision rests with the product owner.*
*Legal obligations regarding automated employment decisions must be verified by counsel before deployment.*
