### Sensitivity Analysis Plan: Fraud Alert Tone & Timing

**1. Harm Inventory (Map)**
Before testing, explicitly record the specific risks to users:
*   **Psychological Harm:** Causing severe panic or paralysis in victims of actual identity theft via aggressive phrasing.
*   **Over-reliance/Confusion:** Users ignoring real threats because false positives look identical to high-severity alerts.
*   **Missing Context:** Failing to distinguish between "suspicious login" (low stakes) and "funds moving" (high stakes) in the message tone.

**2. Measurement Strategy (Measure)**
Since extensive user research is unavailable, execute a **Scenario-Based Red Team** using internal staff and synthetic personas. Create one test row per harm:

*   **Probe Set A (Tone Gradient):** Generate three variations of the same alert (Neutral, Urgent, Alarmist).
    *   *Input:* "Unusual login detected from new device."
    *   *Positive Control:* A message explicitly stating "Your money is being stolen now" (valid high urgency).
    *   *Negative Control:* A message stating "We noticed a login, please check when convenient" (valid low urgency).
    *   *Metric:* Internal reviewer rating of "Panic Inducement" vs. "Clarity of Action" on a 1–5 scale.
    *   *Stop Condition:* If >20% of reviewers rate an alert as "Paralyzing" or "Unclear on next step," block that template.

*   **Probe Set B (Timing & Frequency):** Simulate burst scenarios.
    *   *Input:* 5 alerts sent within 2 minutes vs. 1 digest alert.
    *   *Metric:* Reviewer assessment of "Harassment" vs. "Helpfulness."
    *   *Stop Condition:* Any scenario rated as "Harassing" triggers a mandatory cooldown logic review.

*   **Probe Set C (Vulnerability Check):** Test messages against users who *know* they are compromised.
    *   *Input:* "Is this you?" sent to a persona flagged as "Confirmed Victim."
    *   *Metric:* Does the message offer immediate help (e.g., "Call this number") or just a warning?
    *   *Stop Condition:* Alerts to confirmed victims that do not provide an immediate human support path are blocked.

**3. Risk Management (Manage)**
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Gate:** For any alert classified as "High Stakes" (e.g., money movement initiated), the system must not auto-send the final "Account Locked" notification without a verified human review or a distinct, confirmed user action, if technically feasible within the deadline. If full automation is required, the message must clearly state it is an automated precaution and provide an immediate, one-tap reversal or hotline.
*   **Defined Confidence Tiers:** Do not use percentages ("87% chance of fraud"). Use defined text tiers:
    *   *Verified:* "We confirmed suspicious activity."
    *   *Suspected:* "We noticed unusual behavior."
    *   *Informational:* "A new device logged in."
*   **Transparency Artifact:** Draft a lightweight "Alert Logic Card" documenting intended use (fraud prevention), limitations (may cause anxiety), and the owner of the alert logic. This must accompany the launch.

**4. Governance**
Name one owner responsible for reviewing alert logs weekly post-launch. If panic-related support tickets spike, the owner must trigger a rollback to the "Neutral" tone template immediately.

**Note:** Legal counsel must review whether these alerts constitute regulated automated decisions under local laws before launch; this plan does not assert legal compliance. Do not fabricate a "0% panic" metric; report residual risk based on the red-team findings.
