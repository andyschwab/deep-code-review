### Immediate Mitigation Strategy: Content Filtering & User Notification

**1. Content Filtering Rules (The "Hard Block" List)**
Implement a deterministic pre-bid filter that blocks any ad creative or landing page containing keywords or metadata associated with sensitive health categories before it enters the auction for shared devices. Do not rely on the AI model to "guess" sensitivity; use a static blocklist for the launch.

*   **Blocked Categories:** Sexual health, reproductive services, mental health conditions, addiction recovery, specific chronic diseases (e.g., HIV, cancer, diabetes), and weight loss surgery.
*   **Keyword Triggers:** Block terms like "clinic," "prescription," "symptoms," "treatment," "confidential," and specific drug names associated with the above categories.
*   **Contextual Override:** If the browsing history signal indicates a sensitive topic (e.g., a user visited a health forum), suppress *all* personalized ads for that session and serve only generic, brand-safe house ads (e.g., "Happy Holidays from HomeCircle") or non-behavioral contextual ads unrelated to the browsing history.

**2. User Notification & Interface Strategies**
Since the tracking infrastructure cannot change, the interface must manage user expectation and provide immediate control.

*   **Explicit "Shared Device" Warning:** Before the campaign launches, inject a non-dismissible banner on login or first ad load for the holiday period: *"Notice: Ads on this device are based on recent browsing activity. If this is a shared tablet or TV, personalized ads may reflect the interests of other users."*
*   **"Pause Personalization" Toggle:** Place a highly visible, one-click toggle in the ad corner or settings menu labeled *"Pause Personalized Ads."* When activated, it immediately switches the feed to generic, non-behavioral content for the remainder of the session.
*   **Ad Labeling:** Clearly label every personalized ad with *"Why this ad? Based on recent browsing"* linked to a simple explanation. Do not hide the fact that the ad is algorithmic.

**3. Operational Guardrail**
Assign one team member to manually review the top 50 highest-spend ad creatives scheduled for the holiday launch against the blocklist. Automated filters can miss nuance; a human spot-check provides a final safety net for the highest-visibility inventory.

***

**Note:** This strategy treats the risk as a high-stakes privacy failure requiring a human-in-the-loop review of top creatives and a deterministic blocklist, rather than relying on unverified model accuracy.
**Note:** Legal counsel must review the specific wording of the user notifications to ensure they meet regional privacy obligations before the three-day launch window closes.
