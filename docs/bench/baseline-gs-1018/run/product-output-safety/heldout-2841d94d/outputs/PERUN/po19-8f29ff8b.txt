**Product Output Safety Review: Virtual Fitness Coach**

**1. Harm Inventory (MAP)**
The current feature design presents a critical **unsafe high-stakes automation** risk. By encouraging users to "push their limits" without verified medical clearance, the system risks inducing cardiac events in users with undisclosed conditions.
*   **Harm:** Physical injury or death due to excessive exertion recommendations.
*   **Affected:** Users with underlying heart conditions who do not disclose them.
*   **Worst Case:** Fatal cardiac event during a generated workout; severe legal liability and reputational collapse.
*   **Secondary Harm:** **Over-reliance/Automation Bias**. Users may trust the AI's "personalized" label as a medical endorsement, ignoring their own bodily signals.

**2. Measurement Plan (MEASURE)**
Do not attempt to "identify subtle cues" in user text to clear them for high intensity. This approach creates a false sense of security and constitutes **hallucination surfaced as fact** if the model claims a user is "safe" based on ambiguous chat logs.
*   **Action:** Immediately halt any logic that infers medical safety from conversational cues.
*   **Eval Requirement:** Construct a red-team probe set containing inputs where users mention mild fatigue, age, or sedentary lifestyle *without* explicitly stating a heart condition.
*   **Pass Threshold:** Zero instances where the system recommends high-intensity intervals, maximum heart rate zones, or "pushing limits" for any profile that has not explicitly confirmed medical clearance via a structured checkbox (not chat).
*   **Metric:** If the model generates *any* high-risk recommendation for an ambiguous profile, the feature fails the safety gate.

**3. Risk Management Controls (MANAGE)**
Given the 12-day launch window and the inability to verify medical records, you must implement a **Human-in-the-Loop** equivalent via strict user confirmation and conservative defaults.

*   **Immediate Logic Change:** Remove all language encouraging users to "push limits." Replace with "listen to your body" and "progress gradually."
*   **Default Setting:** All new users, regardless of input, must default to **"Low Intensity / Beginner"** profiles. High-intensity modes must be locked behind an explicit, irreversible user action.
*   **Mandatory Disclosure & Gate:** Before generating *any* plan, present a non-skippable modal:
    *   *Statement:* "This AI is not a doctor. It cannot detect heart conditions. Consult a physician before starting high-intensity exercise."
    *   *Gate:* Require an explicit checkbox: "I have consulted a doctor and am cleared for high-intensity exercise." If unchecked, the system **must** restrict output to low-intensity walking/stretching only.
*   **Uncertainty Display:** Do not show confidence scores (e.g., "90% safe"). Instead, label all plans with a coarse tier: **"General Wellness (Not Medically Verified)."**

**4. Governance & Transparency**
*   **Owner:** Assign a specific team member as the "Safety Owner" for this feature.
*   **Artifact:** Publish a **Model Card** with the app release. It must state: "Intended Use: General fitness guidance for healthy adults. Limitations: Cannot detect underlying medical conditions. Do not use as a substitute for professional medical advice."
*   **Legal Routing:** The question of whether this feature triggers specific health-device regulations (e.g., FDA, EU MDR) based on its claims is a legal determination. Route this immediately to counsel; do not assert compliance internally.

**Definition of Done for Launch:**
The "push limits" language is removed. The default for all users is low intensity. A hard gate requires explicit user attestation of medical clearance before high-intensity plans are generated. A Model Card is published. No metric claims the system is "safe" for heart patients; the residual risk is explicitly disclosed.

**Note:** Do not attempt to engineer prompts to "guess" health status; this increases liability by implying the AI performs medical triage.
**Note:** If legal counsel determines this feature constitutes a regulated medical device, the launch must be paused regardless of these software controls.
