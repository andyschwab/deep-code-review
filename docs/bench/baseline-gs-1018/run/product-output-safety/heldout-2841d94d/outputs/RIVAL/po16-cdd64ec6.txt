**Risk Assessment and Operational Workaround Plan: Facial Recognition Gate System**

**1. Identified Failure Modes**
Given the constraint of a locked core model, the system will exhibit high false-negative rates for specific demographic and cultural groups. Primary failure modes include:
*   **Occlusion Rejection:** Individuals wearing religious head coverings (e.g., hijabs, turbans, kippahs) or medical face masks will likely fail the "high confidence" match threshold because key facial landmarks are obscured.
*   **Demographic Bias:** If the training data lacked diversity, individuals with darker skin tones or specific facial structures may experience higher rejection rates even without occlusions.
*   **Environmental Sensitivity:** Lighting changes at entry points may exacerbate recognition failures for already marginal cases, leading to inconsistent access throughout the day.

**2. Risk Analysis**
*   **Operational Risk:** High volume of false rejections will cause significant bottlenecks at peak hours, delaying work start times and causing frustration.
*   **Compliance & Ethical Risk:** Systematic denial of access based on religious attire constitutes potential discrimination, exposing Global Corp to legal liability and reputational damage.
*   **Security Risk:** If security guards become overwhelmed by manual overrides, they may rush verification steps, potentially allowing unauthorized entry (false positives) due to fatigue or pressure.

**3. Operational Workarounds (Immediate Deployment)**
Since model retraining is impossible, the solution must be procedural. Implement a "Graceful Degradation" protocol effective immediately:

*   **Tiered Access Protocol:**
    *   *Level 1 (Automated):* Standard facial recognition scan. Success grants immediate entry.
    *   *Level 2 (Assisted Manual Override):* Triggered automatically after one failed scan. The user is directed to a dedicated "Verification Lane" staffed by a trained security officer.
    *   *Level 3 (Escalated Verification):* If the officer cannot visually verify the employee against their ID badge due to obscuration, the employee is escorted to a private area for secondary verification (e.g., PIN code, mobile credential, or manager vouching) before entry is granted.

*   **Staffing Adjustments:**
    *   Deploy additional security personnel to entry points specifically for the "Verification Lane" during peak hours (7:00 AM – 9:30 AM).
    *   Assign a "Flow Manager" to triage rejected users and prevent queue spillback into public areas.

*   **Alternative Authentication Methods:**
    *   Immediately enable backup authentication methods for all employees, such as RFID badges with PINs or mobile app-based QR codes, ensuring these are equally convenient to prevent stigma against using them.
    *   Publicly communicate that using backup methods due to recognition errors is a standard, expected procedure, not a security flag.

**4. Mitigation of Security Posture**
To maintain security while increasing manual throughput:
*   **Audit Logging:** Every manual override must be logged with the timestamp, guarding officer ID, and method of verification. Random audits of these logs will deter collusion.
*   **Visual Verification Training:** Train guards on rapid, respectful visual verification techniques that do not require removing religious headwear, focusing on visible facial features and badge photo comparison.
*   **Threshold Adjustment (If Configurable):** While weights are locked, check if the "confidence threshold" slider can be slightly lowered (e.g., from 95% to 85%) to reduce false negatives, accepting a slight increase in manual reviews as a trade-off for better flow.

**5. Implementation Timeline**
*   **Day 1-2:** Draft and distribute new Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) to security vendors; configure backup auth methods.
*   **Day 3-4:** Conduct rapid training drills with security staff on the new Tiered Access Protocol.
*   **Day 5:** Soft launch with a pilot group including employees with head coverings to test flow.
*   **Day 7:** Full deployment mandatory for the security upgrade deadline.

This approach prioritizes inclusivity and operational continuity without violating the vendor's technical constraints, ensuring the security upgrade meets the deadline while mitigating discrimination risks.

*Note: This plan assumes security personnel can be rapidly trained and that backup authentication infrastructure (badges/mobile) is already provisioned.*
