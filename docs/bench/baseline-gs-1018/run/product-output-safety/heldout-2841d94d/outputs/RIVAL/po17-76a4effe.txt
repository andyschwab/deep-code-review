### Mandatory Information Checklist for Disaster Summaries

Before authorizing the launch of the automated summarizer, every summary related to natural disasters (typhoons, earthquakes, floods, etc.) must be validated against the following mandatory information types. If any item below is missing, the summary must be flagged for human review or rejected.

**1. Event Classification and Severity**
*   **Specific Hazard Type:** Explicitly state the disaster (e.g., "Typhoon," "Earthquake," "Landslide").
*   **Official Signal/Intensity:** Include the specific warning level (e.g., "Signal No. 3," "Magnitude 6.2," "Red Alert"). Generic terms like "strong" or "big" are insufficient without the official metric.

**2. Geographic Specificity**
*   **Affected Areas:** List specific provinces, cities, or municipalities under warning. Do not generalize to "parts of Luzon" or "the region" if specific locations are available in the source.
*   **Exclusion Zones:** If applicable, clearly state areas *not* affected to prevent unnecessary panic.

**3. Temporal Urgency**
*   **Time of Impact:** Specify when the event is expected to hit or when the warning expires (e.g., "within 24 hours," "until 5:00 PM").
*   **Recency:** Indicate if the warning is new or an update to a previous alert.

**4. Actionable Directives**
*   **Mandatory Orders:** Include explicit government orders such as "Forced Evacuation," "Suspension of Classes/Work," or "Stay Indoors."
*   **Prohibited Actions:** Clearly state what residents must *not* do (e.g., "Do not cross bridges," "Avoid coastal areas").

**5. Critical Safety Warnings (Non-Negotiable)**
*   **Secondary Hazards:** Mention imminent secondary risks explicitly (e.g., "Flash floods," "Storm surge," "Aftershocks," "Lahar flow"). This is the most common failure point in brevity-focused summarization.
*   **Vulnerable Populations:** Include specific instructions for high-risk groups if mentioned (e.g., "Pregnant women prioritize evacuation").

**6. Source Authority**
*   **Issuing Agency:** Identify the official source (e.g., "PAGASA," "PHIVOLCS," "NDRRMC") to establish credibility and distinguish from rumors.

**Implementation Note for the Team:**
Since you cannot increase character limits, program the summarizer to prioritize these six data points above all other context (such as background stories, political reactions, or historical comparisons). If the algorithm cannot fit all six categories within the character limit, it should output a "Critical Alert: See Full Report" flag rather than a partial summary that omits safety warnings.

Given the five-day timeline, immediately run your existing test cases through this checklist; any summary failing even one item represents a potential life-safety risk that must be resolved before launch.
