# Manual Review Protocol for High-Risk Background Check Matches

**Objective:** To intercept and resolve potential false positives caused by name similarities before reports are finalized, ensuring renters are not wrongfully rejected due to system limitations.

### 1. Automated Triage & Flagging Criteria
The system must automatically route any match meeting the following "High-Risk" criteria to a manual review queue rather than generating an immediate report:
*   **Name Match Only:** The match relies solely on first and last name similarity without a matching Date of Birth (DOB) or Social Security Number (last 4 digits).
*   **Geographic Discrepancy:** The criminal record's jurisdiction (county/state) has no historical link to the applicant's known residence history (past 7 years).
*   **Age Variance:** The calculated age of the subject in the criminal record differs by more than 3 years from the applicant's age.
*   **Common Name Trigger:** The applicant's name appears on a pre-loaded "High Frequency Name" list (e.g., "John Smith," "Maria Garcia").

### 2. The Manual Review Workflow
Once flagged, a trained reviewer must execute the following four-step verification process within 2 hours:

**Step A: Document Collection**
The reviewer requests additional verification documents from the landlord partner if not already present in the file. Required items include:
*   Government-issued photo ID (to verify DOB and physical description).
*   Signed consent form authorizing a deeper manual search.

**Step B: The "Three-Point" Verification**
The reviewer manually compares the applicant's verified data against the criminal record entry using three distinct anchors:
1.  **DOB Exact Match:** If the DOB does not match exactly, the record is presumed unrelated unless a middle name/initial provides a unique link.
2.  **Physical Descriptors:** Compare height, weight, eye color, and sex listed in the legacy database entry against the applicant's ID. Significant discrepancies result in immediate exclusion.
3.  **Alias Cross-Check:** Review the applicant's provided aliases. If the criminal record lists an alias not provided by the applicant, flag for senior review.

**Step C: Jurisdictional Context Check**
Since the legacy database cannot be updated, the reviewer must perform a spot-check on the specific county court website (if available) or call the clerk of court for the jurisdiction listed in the hit.
*   *Query:* "Does record #[ID] belong to an individual with DOB [DOB]?"
*   If the court confirms the record belongs to a different individual, document the confirmation ID.

**Step D: Determination & Documentation**
The reviewer selects one of three outcomes and logs the rationale in the "Review Notes" field:
*   **Confirmed Match:** All three points align. Report proceeds with standard adverse action workflow.
*   **False Positive (Cleared):** Discrepancy found in DOB, physical description, or court confirmation. The match is suppressed from the final report.
*   **Inconclusive:** Insufficient data to rule out a match. The case is escalated to a Senior Compliance Officer for a final decision before any report is released.

### 3. Quality Assurance & Feedback Loop
*   **Dual-Signoff:** During the beta phase, 10% of all "Cleared" cases must be randomly selected for a second review by a Senior Officer to ensure consistency.
*   **Error Logging:** Every confirmed false positive must be logged with the specific failure mode (e.g., "System ignored 5-year age gap"). This log will be used to refine the automated triage rules post-beta.
*   **Turnaround Time:** No report containing a high-risk match is released until the manual review status is marked "Complete."

***

*Note: This protocol assumes reviewers have access to external court contacts and applicant IDs; if these are unavailable, the "Inconclusive" escalation path becomes the default safety valve.*
*Strict adherence to the "Three-Point" rule is critical given the inability to patch the legacy database.*
