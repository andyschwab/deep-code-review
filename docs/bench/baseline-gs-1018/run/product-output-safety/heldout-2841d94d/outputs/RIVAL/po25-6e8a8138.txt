### Content Filtering Rules

1.  **Sensitive Category Blacklist**: Immediately hard-block ad delivery for any user profile containing signals related to: reproductive health, mental health conditions, substance abuse recovery, HIV/STI treatments, domestic violence resources, and bankruptcy/foreclosure proceedings. No amount of optimization score can override this block.
2.  **Contextual Decoupling**: Disable the use of "browsing history" as a direct input for ad selection on shared device IDs (tablets, smart TVs). Switch these devices to "Context-Only" mode, where ads are selected based solely on the content currently being viewed (e.g., a cooking video shows kitchenware), not the user's past history.
3.  **Inference Suppression**: Prohibit the use of probabilistic inferences. If the system calculates a 80% likelihood of a condition based on search patterns, treat it as 0% for ad targeting purposes. Only serve ads related to explicitly searched terms within the last 24 hours, and only if those terms are not on the Sensitive Category Blacklist.
4.  **Creative Sanitization**: Scan ad creatives for visual or textual cues that imply sensitive conditions (e.g., images of medical clinics, specific medication packaging, or copy like "struggling with..."). Reject any creative that requires knowledge of a private condition to make sense to the viewer.

### User Notification & Interface Strategies

1.  **The "Shared Device" Prompt**: Upon detecting a login or session start on a known shared device (tablet/TV), display a non-intrusive but clear modal: *"This device is used by multiple people. We have paused personalized history-based ads to protect your privacy. You will see general interest ads instead."* This manages expectations and signals care.
2.  **One-Click "Private Mode" Toggle**: Add a visible toggle in the player interface labeled "Private Browsing Mode." When active, it visually confirms that no data from this session will be saved or used for future targeting. Default this to "On" for guest profiles.
3.  **Transparency Receipt**: Instead of a complex privacy policy link, offer a simple "Why am I seeing this?" icon on every ad. If the ad is context-based, it should say: *"Shown because you are watching [Current Program]."* If it is history-based (on personal devices), it should say: *"Shown based on your recent interest in [General Topic]."* Never reveal the specific sensitive query that triggered the interest.
4.  **Holiday Campaign Specific Disclaimer**: For the upcoming launch, include a brief footer in the campaign landing page: *"HomeCircle Ads prioritizes family privacy. We do not use sensitive health or financial data to target ads on shared household screens."*

**Note**: These measures rely on software configuration changes to your targeting logic and frontend display layers, avoiding changes to your underlying tracking infrastructure. Implementing the "Context-Only" switch for shared devices is the highest-impact immediate fix.
