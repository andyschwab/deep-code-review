### Validation Workflow: Grant Writer Credibility Guard

**Phase 1: Input Constraint & Prompt Engineering**
Modify the system prompt to explicitly forbid the invention of specific metrics, dates, or past award titles unless provided in the user input. Instruct the model to adopt a "conservative drafting" persona: if a specific eligibility criterion (e.g., "5 years of operation") cannot be confirmed by the user's input, the model must insert a clearly marked placeholder like `[CONFIRM: Years of Operation]` rather than estimating a number.

**Phase 2: The "Claim Extraction" Pass**
Before generating the final draft, run an intermediate automated step where the model analyzes its own draft to extract every factual claim into a structured list (e.g., "Claim: Organization has operated since 2018"; "Source: User Input"). Any claim where the "Source" is identified as "Inferred" or "Generated" is automatically flagged.

**Phase 3: Human-in-the-Loop Review Interface**
Present the draft to the user with a dedicated "Verification Mode." In this view:
*   **Green Text:** Facts directly quoted or derived strictly from user inputs.
*   **Yellow Highlights:** Claims flagged as uncertain or inferred. These sections are locked for editing and require the user to click "Confirm" or "Reject."
*   **Action:** If a user rejects a yellow highlight, the system reverts that section to a blank template or a question prompt, preventing the fabrication from remaining in the text.

**Phase 4: The "Donor Lens" Simulation**
Run a final automated check where a second AI instance acts as a "Skeptical Donor Reviewer." Feed it the draft and ask: "Which specific claims in this text lack evidence based on the provided inputs?" If this reviewer identifies specific fabricated qualifications, the system blocks submission and returns the document to the user with a warning: "Potential hallucination detected in Section X. Please verify."

**Phase 5: Mandatory Attestation**
Require the human user to digitally sign off on a checklist before the final PDF export or submission. The checklist must state: "I have verified all highlighted claims regarding our organization's history, financials, and past performance." This shifts the liability of factual accuracy to the human operator who possesses the ground truth, while the AI serves only as a drafting assistant.

**Implementation Note:** Given your two-week deadline, prioritize Phase 1 (Prompt Engineering) and Phase 3 (Yellow Highlighting) as they require the least infrastructure changes but offer the highest immediate risk reduction.

**Operational Note:** Train your remote team to treat any specific number or date not present in the initial user form as a "system error" until manually verified, fostering a culture of skepticism toward the AI's specificity.
