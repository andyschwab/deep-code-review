### Delivery Plan: SMS Trigger Update (24h → 48h)

#### 1. Lanes & Parallelism
*   **Lane A (Configuration & Logic):** Modify the reminder trigger interval from 24 to 48 hours. Implement the "future bookings only" logic gate to ensure existing 24-hour notifications are not duplicated or altered. Verify cancellation notification logic remains untouched.
*   **Lane B (Test Environment Prep):** Provision the lower environment mirror. Manually patch the known flaky timezone check script to bypass daylight savings false positives during this specific run.
*   **Lane C (Compliance Prep):** Draft the deployment justification document required for the Chief Medical Officer (CMO) and VP of Engineering.

*Parallel Execution:* Lanes A, B, and C begin immediately. Lane A cannot merge until Lane B confirms the test pass. Lane C must be complete before the final merge request.

#### 2. Gates & Checks
*   **Gate 1: Code Freeze on Shared Config:** All edits to the shared message template configuration must occur outside business hours to prevent lock conflicts with Marketing/Clinical Ops.
*   **Gate 2: Lower Environment Verification:**
    *   **Revision:** Deploy Lane A's output to the lower environment mirror.
    *   **Check:** Run the full synthetic data replay (approx. 55 mins).
    *   **Specific Validation:** Manually inspect the timezone conversion log lines to confirm no false positives from the known flaky check.
    *   **Logic Proof:** Verify via database query that appointments booked *before* deployment retain the 24-hour trigger, while those booked *after* utilize the 48-hour trigger. Confirm cancellation triggers remain unchanged.
*   **Gate 3: Compliance Sign-off:** Submit the passing test results and the drafted justification (Lane C) to the CMO and VP of Engineering. Deployment is blocked until both provide explicit written approval.

#### 3. Review Strategy
*   **Primary Reviewer:** A senior engineer from the Patient Engagement team *not* authoring the code. They must verify the "future bookings only" logic implementation against the ticket requirements.
*   **Independent Validation:** The QA lead must independently verify the synthetic data replay logs, specifically signing off on the manual timezone log inspection.
*   **Stakeholder Review:** Clinical Operations must review the change in the lower environment to confirm the message content and timing appear correct in a patient-facing simulation.

#### 4. Landing & Rollback
*   **Merge Condition:** Merge to the release branch only after Gate 2 (Test Pass) and Gate 3 (Dual Executive Approval) are satisfied.
*   **Deployment Window:** Schedule deployment during a low-traffic window (e.g., late evening) to minimize impact if issues arise, and to ensure the shared config file is unlocked.
*   **Human Decision Point:** The final "Go/No-Go" button press requires the VP of Engineering's presence or delegated authority due to the production database sensitivity.
*   **Rollback Plan:**
    *   **Trigger:** If duplicate messages are detected or cancellation notifications fail post-deployment.
    *   **Action:** Immediate reversion to the previous container image in all three regional clusters.
    *   **Communication:** Automated alert to Patient Engagement team and Clinical Operations.
    *   **Data Remediation:** If duplicates occurred, a script must be ready to flag affected patient records for manual apology outreach by the support team.

This plan ensures strict adherence to healthcare compliance, mitigates the risk of duplicate patient communication, and accounts for known infrastructure fragilities.
