### Delivery Plan: Refund Policy Wording Update

**1. Lanes & Parallelism**
*   **Lane A (Implementation):** Single developer task. Extract `en.json`, locate the refund timeline string, swap "5-7 business days" to "3-5 business days", and save. No parallel work is possible or necessary; this is a single-point change.
*   **Lane B (Verification):** Local-only verification. Since staging is unstable, the developer must manually inspect the rendered checkout page locally using the modified locale file. No automated CI integration tests will run; the commit message must strictly start with `docs:` to bypass the 45-minute suite.

**2. Gates & Checks**
*   **Gate 1 (Local Visual Check):** Before pushing, the developer must confirm the local build displays "3-5 business days" correctly on the checkout confirmation view. This replaces the staging gate.
*   **Gate 2 (Commit Convention):** The commit message must be prefixed with `docs:` exactly. Failure to do so triggers the full integration suite, delaying the timeline by 45+ minutes.
*   **Gate 3 (SRL Approval):** The production deploy requires manual approval from the Site Reliability Lead (SRL). Since the SRL is offline until 16:00 UTC, the merge to `main` can happen earlier, but the *deployment* is blocked until this window opens.

**3. Review Process**
*   **Reviewer:** One senior engineer independent of the implementer (e.g., a backend lead if the implementer is frontend).
*   **Scope:** Review is limited to the `en.json` diff and the commit message format.
*   **Constraint:** The reviewer must explicitly verify the string change matches the legal requirement exactly ("3-5 business days") and confirm the `docs:` prefix. No design or functional regression review is required per ticket constraints.
*   **Timing:** Review must occur before 15:30 UTC to allow time for the SRL approval window opening at 16:00 UTC.

**4. Landing Strategy**
*   **Merge Window:** Merge to `main` immediately after review approval. Do not wait for the SRL to merge; wait only for the deploy step.
*   **Deployment Trigger:** Auto-deployer triggers on merge to `main`.
*   **Human Decision Point:** The SRL must manually approve the production promotion upon returning at 16:00 UTC. The implementer must notify the SRL immediately upon their return.
*   **Irreversibility:** Once the SRL approves, static assets push immediately to production with no staging gate. There is no automatic rollback.
*   **Rollback Plan:** If the text is incorrect post-deploy, a immediate hotfix commit reverting the string is required, followed by the same SRL approval process. No automated revert exists.

**Risk Summary:** The primary risk is the irreversible production push if the local check fails to catch a formatting error. The secondary risk is missing the "end of day" deadline if the SRL is delayed beyond 16:00 UTC. Mitigation relies entirely on the accuracy of the local visual check and the precision of the commit message.
