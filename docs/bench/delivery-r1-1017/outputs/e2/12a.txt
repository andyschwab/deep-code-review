**Risk Assessment & Constraints**
This is a **high-risk, low-complexity** change. While the diff is trivial (one sentence), the deployment is **irreversible** for static assets and bypasses staging due to instability. The "docs-only" CI skip is dangerous here because it bypasses the build verification for a user-facing legal claim. The Site Reliability Lead (SRL) being offline until 16:00 UTC creates a hard **Human Gate** at G8 (Release). We cannot meet the "end of day" live requirement if "end of day" precedes 16:00 UTC.

**Landing Strategy**
1.  **Builder** opens PR immediately.
2.  **QA** verifies locally and approves.
3.  **Reviewer** approves code.
4.  **Conductor** marks the PR as "Ready to Merge, Pending Human Gate."
5.  **Merge:** Do **not** merge to `main` until 16:00 UTC when the SRL is available. Merging earlier triggers the auto-deployer, pushing an unapproved legal change to production.
6.  **Deployment:** At 16:00 UTC, notify SRL. Upon SRL approval in the deployment tool, merge the PR.
7.  **Rollback:** Since assets are immutable and irreversible once cached/pushed, rollback requires an immediate hotfix commit reverting the text and a forced cache invalidation. Prepare the revert PR now, but do not open it unless the deploy fails or legal recalls the change.

**Summary of Decisions**
*   **Parallelism:** Builder and QA work sequentially on the same commit (QA waits for Builder's push).
*   **CI Strategy:** Use `docs:` prefix to skip the 45-min suite, assuming build/lint remain active. If `docs:` skips build, omit prefix and accept the cost.
*   **Critical Block:** The merge is physically delayed until the SRL returns. "End of day" live deployment is impossible if the deadline is before 16:00 UTC.
*   **Verification:** Local-only due to staging instability. Double-verification (Builder + QA) is mandatory to compensate for lack of staging.
