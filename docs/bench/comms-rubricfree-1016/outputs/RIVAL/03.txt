**Subject: Lumen outage on October 15: what happened and what you need to do**

Hello,

Yesterday, October 15, Lumen was unavailable from 09:12 to 13:20 Eastern, about four hours. During that time, users could not log in or load their calendars. We apologize for the disruption.

**What happened**
A security certificate expired on an internal service that handles login. Certificates need to be renewed regularly, and the automatic renewal job had been failing since September without alerting us. When the certificate expired, login stopped working and calendars could not load.

**Is your data affected?**
No data was lost. Bookings made through your public booking page during the outage were queued and processed once service recovered. We are still checking whether about 300 of those bookings sent duplicate confirmation emails. If any of your customers mention getting two confirmations for one booking, that is the likely reason, and the booking itself is fine. We will tell you if our check shows your account was affected.

**Do you need to do anything?**
If your organization uses single sign-on (SSO), your users may need to sign in again. Everyone else does not need to do anything.

**What we are doing to prevent a repeat**
We have added alerting for certificate expiry, so a failed renewal will be flagged before it causes an outage. We are also auditing all of our other certificates this week.

**Service credits**
Customers on the Business plan are eligible for an SLA credit for this outage. We have not yet decided whether the credit will be applied automatically or will need to be requested. We will confirm how it works in a follow-up message.

If you have questions or see anything unusual in your account, please contact our support team and mention this incident.

Thank you for your patience.
