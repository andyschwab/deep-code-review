**Subject: Action needed before Nov 9: Ledgerly CSV export format is changing**

Hi,

**On November 9, Ledgerly CSV exports switch to a new format. If a spreadsheet, script, or integration reads your exports, update it before then. The old format will be retired on November 30.**

What changes:
- **Dates** use ISO format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **Amounts** no longer include currency symbols.
- **A new "Tax category" column** is added immediately after the Amount column. Anything that reads columns by position will shift from that point onward.

Timeline:
- **November 9:** the new format becomes the default.
- **November 30:** the old format is retired and no longer available.

If you only open exports by hand, nothing needs to be done, though dates and amounts will look different.

Questions about updating an integration? Reply to this email and we'll help.

The Ledgerly Team
