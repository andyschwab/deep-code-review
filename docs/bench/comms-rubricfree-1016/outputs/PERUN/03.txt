**Lumen was unavailable for about four hours yesterday, and no data was lost. Most customers don't need to do anything.**

**What happened**
From 09:12 to 13:20 Eastern on October 15, users couldn't log in or load calendars. The cause was an expired security certificate on an internal service that handles sign-in. The automatic renewal had been failing since September, and we didn't catch it. That's our failure, and we're sorry for the disruption.

**Is your data affected?**
No data was lost. Bookings made through your public booking page during the outage were held and processed once service came back.

We're still checking whether about 300 of those bookings sent duplicate confirmation emails. If your customers mention receiving two confirmations for one booking, that is the likely reason, and the booking itself is valid.

**What you need to do**
- **If your team signs in with SSO:** some users may need to sign in again.
- **Everyone else:** nothing.

Business plan customers are eligible for an SLA credit for this outage. We haven't yet decided whether it will be applied automatically or on request. We'll confirm the process in a follow-up message, so you don't need to contact us yet.

- We're auditing all our other certificates this week.
